FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2024 
Requirements on the Use of Flexible Connectors for 
Connecting Single Sprinklers to Range Pipes in Sprinkler System 
This Circular Letter serves to announce the applicability and modified 
specification for the use of flexible connectors to connect single sprinklers to range 
pipes ("Flexible Connectors"). It is supplementary to the Technical Guidance 
published in FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2020 "Specification for Automatic Sprinkler 
Installations" issued on 10 September 2020, which addresses the adoption with local 
modifications of the Loss Prevention Council Rules for Automatic Sprinkler 
Installations 2015 incorporating BS EN 12845 ("LPC Rules"). 
Currently, the specification for the use of Flexible Connectors as stipulated in 
Clauses TB227.2.4 to TB227.2.5 in the Technical Bulletins supplementary to the LPC 
Rules is stated as "not mandatory and for reference only" in the Technical Guidance. 
A Sub-working Group under the Fire Safety Standard Advisory Group was 
formed and tasked to look into the feasibility and applicability of Flexible Connectors 
in Hong Kong. After thorough review and consultation with the trade and respective 
parties of interest, the Sub-working Group has modified the specification for the design 
and installation ofFlexible Connectors locally. 
The modification is detailed in the amendment sheet attached to this letter and 
should be read in conjunction with the Technical Guidance, the LPC Rules including 
the Technical Bulletins, Notes, Commentary and Recommendations and the relevant 
requirements of the Codes quoted in these documents. The relevant entries in the 
Technical Guidance will be replaced by this modification, effective from 1 May 2024.
