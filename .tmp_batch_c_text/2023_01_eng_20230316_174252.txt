FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2023 
Sprinkler System for Car Ports 
This Circular Letter serves to announce the addition of sprinkler system 
requirement for car ports in the Code ofPractice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and 
Equipment, September 2022 (FSI Code). 
In view of the intensifying car fires and the consequential severity of damage 
notable worldwide, a comprehensive review pertaining to the adequacy of fire safety 
requirements for car ports had been conducted. The review reveals that, with the surging use 
ofplastic, fiberglass or other lightweight materials in vehicles and the more commonly use of 
electric car batteries as the major power source, the burning behaviour of modem vehicles has 
become more extreme and the magnitude of fire has become more severe. 
To better protect lives and properties at car ports, the review concludes that 
additional fire safety requirement, i.e. provision of sprinkler system for car ports with total 
floor area exceeding 230m2, has to be imposed. A consultation exercise which extensively 
engaged relevant professional bodies, trades and government departments has been conducted 
with positive feedback received. The revised requirement in the relevant paragraphs of the 
FSI Code is outlined in the enclosed amendment sheet. 
The revised requirement shall take effect from 1 April 2023. In relation to 
submission of building plans involving car ports, the following types of build1ng plans 
received by this Department on or after the effective date shall observe the revised 
requirement: 
· (i) new submission of building plans for new building; and 
(ii) new submission of building plans for existing building of which not less than 
one half measured by volume is rebuilt or which is altered to such an extent as 
to necessitate the reconstruction of not less than one half of the superficial area 
of the main walls, or where a new occupation permit shall be issued. 
Notwithstanding the above, voluntary compliance with the revised requirement 
before the effective date is strongly encouraged.
