Fire Services Department Circular Letter No. 7/99 
Installation and Maintenance of Audio/Visual Advisory System, 
Emergency Generator, Emergency Lighting and Exit Sign 
   The captioned works are classified as fire service installations and equipment.  Please be reminded 
that according to the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations no fire service installation or 
equipment shall be installed, maintained, inspected and repaired by any person other than a registered fire 
service installation contractor.  Any person who contravenes the provisions of these Regulations shall be guilty 
of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine of $5,000.-  
 
   You are advised to take note of the contents of this Circular Letter when drawing up your 
construction and maintenance contracts for the captioned works.
