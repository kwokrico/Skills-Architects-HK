                               FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2009
               Rules for Fire Detection and Fire Alarm Systems for Buildings

              This Circular Letter announces the adoption, with modifications, of British
     Standard BS 5839-1: 2002 + A2: 2008 - Fire detection and fire alarm systems for
     buildings - Part 1: Code of practice for system design, installation, commissioning and
     maintenance.

               Subsequent to the release of the 2002 edition of BS 5839-1, the Fire Safety
     Standards Advisory Group (FSSAG) looked into the feasibility and practicability of
     adopting this new edition for local application. After comprehensive discussions and
     extensive consultation with the industry and respective interested parties, the FSSAG
     has completed its study and recommended, subject to modifications as specified in
     Lists One to Three annexed to this Letter, to adopt BS 5839 – 1: 2002 + A2:2008 for
     local use. I now endorse the recommendations.

               The new standard will therefore take effect from 1 September 2009 for all
     initial building plan submissions received by this Department and the reference of BS
     5839: Part 1: 1988 in the current Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service

      Dear Sirs,

                                               FSD Circular Letter No. 2 / 2000
                                                  Exit and Directional Signs


                The following changes to the provisions on exit and directional signs shall take
      effect from the date of this Circular Letter:-

                    i) The use of self-luminous signs as both exit and directional signs is permitted in buildings or
                       premises which are under single ownership or a central management;

                    ii) To address the environmental concern over the disposal of the “radioactive” self-luminous
                        signs, labels providing information on the hazard level, expiry date and method of disposal are
                        required to be affixed to the signs; and

                    iii) Having regard to the provisions in BS 5499:Part I:1990 the combination of green/black as
                         one of the colour codes for exit/directional signs is deleted.

      2.        The changes shall have no retrospective effect. Regarding the green/black colour code, a grace
      period until 1st October 2000 is allowed, after which it will no longer be accepted.

                                FSD Circular Letter No. 5 /2005

       Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment
       and Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment
                                    (July 2005)


                This Circular Letter announces that the 1998 edition of Codes of Practice
    for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection, Testing and
    Maintenance of Installations and Equipment has been revised. The new edition (July
    2005) has incorporated with, inter alia, various provisions of fire service requirements
    promulgated in existing Circular Letters. In this connection, a summary table
    showing those existing Circular Letters which have been incorporated in the new
    edition of Codes is attached at Appendix I for reference.

              The above new edition of Codes will be implemented with effect from
    16.12.2005. They are fully applicable to all new submissions of building plans
    received by Buildings Department from the implementation date inclusive and
    subsequently endorsed by this Department.

                In respect of building projects with building plans approved by this
    Department before the implementation date, the fire service requirements prevailing at
    the time of approval are still applicable.

                                               F.S.D. Circular Letter No. 8/97
                                        Specification for Portable Fire Extinguishers


                      Subsequent to the withdrawal of the BS 5423: 1987 ‘Specification for Portable Fire
         Extinguishers’ which was replaced by BS EN3: Part 1 to 6 on 1.1.1997, this Department now accepts test
         reports for the testing of fire extinguishers in accordance with BS EN3: Part 1 to 6.


                    This Department will also continue to accept test reports for the testing of fire extinguishers in
         accordance with BS 5423: 1987 provided that the test reports are within 10 years from the date of issue.
