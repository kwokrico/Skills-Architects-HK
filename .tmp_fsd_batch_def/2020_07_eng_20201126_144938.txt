FSD Circular Letter No. 7/2020 
Certification of Fire Service Installations and Equipment required under the 
Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance, Cap. 636, Laws of Hong Kong 
The Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance (hereafter referred to as the 
Ordinance) has come into operation on 19.6.2020. The purpose of the Ordinance is 
to provide better protection from the risk of fire for occupants and users of, and 
visitors to, industrial buildings constructed or with building plans submitted on or 
before 1.3.1987. 
The certification for alteration and addition to fire service installations and 
equipment required under the Ordinance by Fire Service Installation Contractors will 
follow the existing procedure as promulgated in paragraph 4 of Part VI -Inspections 
of Fire Service Installations contained in "FSD Circular Letters 1996". 
The enclosed newly devised form FSI/314 D shall be used for all submissions 
on industrial buildings which fall within the ambit of the Ordinance. The form, 
which can be downloaded from the FSD website, shall be completed and returned to 
the Building Improvement Division 3 of the Fire Safety Command with effect from 
25.11.2020.
