BUILDINGS  ORDINANCE OFFICE PRACTICE NOTE FOR REGIST ERED CONTRACTORS 3
Cease Works Orders
To
 ensure that registered contractors are fully aware of the powers of the staff of the
Buildings Ordinance Office, the following advice is given :
(a) any instruction or order issued under powers conferred by  the
Buildings Ordinance, to stop any 
building works, street works, lift
works or escalator works in progress or to demolish any part of such
works either under construction or completed, or to alter any such
works under construction or completed, must be issued in writing;
and
(b) such an instruction or order must be signed on behalf of the Building
Authority by an officer holding one of the following posts :
Principal Government Building Surveyor
Government Building Surveyor
Government Structural Engineer
Chief Building Surveyor
Chief Structural Engineer
Senior Building Surveyor
Senior Structural Engineer
2. No me mber of the staff of the Buildings Ordinance Office has po wers un der the 
Buildings Ordinance to issue, verbally, instructions or orders requiring building works, street works, 
lift works or escalator works to be stopped, demolished or altered.
3. If a ny pe rson claiming to be a government official acting under  the Buil dings 
Ordinance gives any such ins truction or order verbally, registered contractors are advised to ask to  
see the officer's Warrant Card and having obtained information on his identity, to report the matter to 
the Building Authority without delay.
