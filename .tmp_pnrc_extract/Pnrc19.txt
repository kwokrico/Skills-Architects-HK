Regulation 45 of the  Building (Administration) Regulations requires that 
every registered contractor shall in the specified form notify the Building Authority of any 
change in the address at which he carries on business, within 14 days thereof. 
2. The form specified for such purpose is Form BA 24 (cf PNRC 2) .  An 
Electronic Forms Submission System has been set up to facilitate online submission of 
the form via the website of the Buildings Department (www.bd.gov.hk).  The form may 
also be sent to the Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, 
West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowl oon, Hong 
Kong.
