 
Buildings Department Practice Note for Registered Contractors 32
Display of Site Information 
 
   
 
To enable members of the public to bring to the attention of registered 
contractors (RC) any undesirable or unsafe conditions on building sites, you are 
requested to provide relevant information to facilitate contact.  It is desirable that the 
following information be displayed in a prominent position on site for public 
information : 
 
a) Address and/or lo t num
ber of the site; 
b) Names of authorized person, registered structural engineer 
and RC; 
c) Contact telephone number(s). 
  
2. While display of advertisement is not permitted on any hoarding or covered 
walkway, there is no objection under Build ing (Planning) Regulation 66(4) for the 
display of the basic developm ent information such as proposed works, use, height, 
number of blocks and the anticipated project completion date. 
 
3. For information displayed in locations other than the hoarding and covered 
walkway, you should ensure that such information is displayed within lot boundaries and 
does not interfere with the site activities. 
 
4. A similar practice note has been issued  to authorized persons and registered 
structural engineers.
