Buildings  Department Practice Note for Registered Contractors 23
Certification  of Plant Operators
for Lifting Appliances and Lifting Gear
On 24th July 1994, new legislation will come into force which requires that all
lifting appliances and lifting gear shall be operated only by persons who have been appropriately
---- trained and have the r
elevant and  current certificate in the attached format, issued by the
Construction Industry Training Authority or such other authority as may be approved by the
Commissioner for Labour.
2. Failure to comply will be an offence under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings
Ordinance, Cap. 59.  You should therefore ensure that all plant operators under your control receive
the necessary training and obtain a certificate.  Please do so in good time  : leaving training to the
last moment may result in putting too many candidates to the training authorities just before the
effective date.
