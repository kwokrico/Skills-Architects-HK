Buildings Department Practice Note  for Registered Contractors 7 
H
ong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance, Cap 301 
 The
 Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance restricts the 
height of any "building" to that prescribed.  I n this Ordinance, the word "building" 
includes any mast or pole or any pile driver, scaffolding, hoist or crane or other structure 
projecting skywards. 
2. With
 construction projects, it is important that tower cranes and any item of 
temporary plant or equipment used should not exceed the prescribed height restrictions.  
However, under certain closely monitored conditions, the Director -General of Civil 
Aviation may be prepared to grant temporary exemption from the Ordinance to enable 
construction equipment to be erected above the statutory height limits. 
3. Applications for such temporary exemption should be made on a standard 
form which   can be obtained from the Airport Standards Division of the Civil Aviation 
Department (CAD) on Level 5, Office Building, Civil Aviation Department Headquarters, 
1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport, Lantau, Hong Kong ; by fax on 
2795 8469 or by downloading from the website of CAD (http://www.cad.gov.hk).
