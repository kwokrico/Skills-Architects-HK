This Practice Note announces the publication of the 'Code of Practice on

2. The Code of Practice gives guidance on the inspection and maintenance of
buried water-carrying services affecting slopes.  It puts together relevant notes and guidelines to
form a comprehensive document for the use of G overnment D epartments, professionals,
contractors and building managers.  The Code of Practice focuses its guidelines on the
following aspects :
(a) Planning for an effective inspection and maintenance programme;
(b) Examining available options for detecting, inspecting and repairing
buried services affecting slope; and
(c) Recommendations for permanent measures to minimize impacts of
leakage from buried services on slope safety.
3. U nder Buildings O rdinance section 27C, the Building A uthority may require a
building owner to carry out investigation and any repair work to buried water-carrying services
laid in or near a slope.  A uthorized Persons, Registered Structural Engineers and Registered
Contractors should make reference to this Code of Practice when appointed in respect of the
design, construction and/or maintenance of buried services so to assure safety of slopes in their
vicinity.
4. A  similar practice note has been issued to A uthorized Persons and Registered
Structural Engineers.
