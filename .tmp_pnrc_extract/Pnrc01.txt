Practice Notes in Force 
 
2. To afford instant updating of information and for environmentally 
friendliness, all Practice Notes for Registered Contractors (PNRCs) will be disseminated 
via BD’s website and issue of hardcopies thereof will cease forthwith.  In this connection, 
we have revamped the webpage of PNRCs adding more features which aim to enhance the 
search function and navigation.  Premised on these built-in new functions, the indexes 
indicated in the footnote of the extant PNRCs will become redundant and will be deleted as 
and when the PNRCs are revised in future. 
 
3. The list of PNRCs in force woul d be kept updated on BD’s website 
(http://www.bd.gov.hk).  
 
4. PNRCs have proven to be useful platforms in our dissemination of 
information to the RCs.  I hope you will find the aforesaid new arrangements for issue of 
PNRCs both user and environmentally friendly.  With your support, we can help contribute 
towards a more sustainable society.
