# Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Contents last updated 28 April 2023 | Commissioner means the Commissioner for Labour (section 2)

> Scope note: This Ordinance decides when a workplace is an industrial undertaking or a notifiable workplace, and it gives the Commissioner power to make safety regulations and to prohibit use of an unsuitable notifiable workplace. It does not set scaffold, excavation, or hoist dimensions. Those site rules are in the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 59 sub. leg. I).

## Regulatory Overview

An industrial undertaking includes any factory, any construction work, and the premises or site and the machinery, plant, tools, gear, and materials with which any listed undertaking is carried on (section 2). The Ordinance does not apply to an undertaking which is not carried on by way of trade or for purposes of gain, or to any agricultural operation (section 2(3)).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When the site is construction work (section 2 and Schedule 3)

Construction work means the construction, erection, installation, reconstruction, repair, maintenance (including redecoration and external cleaning), renewal, removal, alteration, improvement, dismantling, or demolition of any structure or works specified in Schedule 3; any work preparing for that, including laying foundations and excavating earth and rock before foundations; and the use of machinery, plant, tools, gear, and materials in connection with those operations.

Schedule 3 specified structures and works:

| Item | Structure or works |
|---|---|
| 1 | Any building, edifice, wall, fence, or chimney, whether wholly or partly above or below ground level |
| 2 | Any road, motorway, railway, tramway, cableway, aerial ropeway, or canal |
| 3 | Any harbour works, dock, pier, sea defence work, or lighthouse |
| 4 | Any aqueduct, viaduct, bridge, or tunnel |
| 5 | Any sewer, sewage disposal works, or filter bed |
| 6 | Any airport or works connected with air navigation |
| 7 | Any dam, reservoir, well, pipeline, culvert, shaft, or reclamation |
| 8 | Any drainage, irrigation, or river control work |
| 9 | Any water, electrical, gas, telephonic, telegraphic, radio, or television installation or works, or any other works designed for the manufacturing or transmission of power or the transmission or reception of radio or sound waves |
| 10 | Any structure designed for the support of machinery, plant, or power transmission lines |

A contractor, in relation to construction work, is any person or firm engaged in carrying out construction work by way of trade or business, on his own account or under a contract or arrangement, including with the State or any public body.

A factory is any premises or place, other than a mine or quarry, in which articles are manufactured, altered, cleansed, repaired, ornamented, finished, adapted for sale, broken up, or demolished, or in which materials are transformed, and within the close, curtilage, or precincts of which any machinery other than machinery worked entirely by hand is used, or 20 or more persons are employed in manual labour.

**SD takeaway:** Building, foundation, demolition, and the Schedule 3 civil works are construction work, so the site is an industrial undertaking and the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations apply to that work.

### 2. Notifiable workplace, and what is not one (sections 2 and 9)

A notifiable workplace means any factory, mine, or quarry, and any premises or place in which a dangerous trade (Schedule 1) or scheduled trade (Schedule 2) is carried on or is proposed to be carried on. It does not include a construction site within the meaning of the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 59 sub. leg. I).

| Duty | Section | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| First use | 9(1) | The person having management or control shall, before the first occasion on which any industrial process is commenced or any industrial operation is carried on, notify the Commissioner in the prescribed form |
| Change of location, name, or nature | 9(2) | Notify the proposed change in the prescribed form before it takes effect |
| Change of the person in management or control | 9(3) | That person shall notify the Commissioner within 21 days after the change |

**SD takeaway:** A construction site is not a section 9 notifiable workplace. A factory, or premises where a dangerous or scheduled trade is proposed, must be notified before the process starts, and a change of use or location must be notified before it takes effect.

### 3. Prohibition notice if the workplace is not suitable (section 9A)

If the Commissioner considers that a notifiable workplace, whether or not notified under section 9, is not suitable for use as a factory, mine, or quarry, for a dangerous or scheduled trade, or for an industrial process or operation or any part of it, he may issue a prohibition notice in the prescribed form. Suitable means, for a workplace designed and constructed for industrial use, suitable in respect of the safety, health, and welfare of persons employed therein; for a workplace not so designed and constructed, suitable in respect of the safety, health, and welfare of persons generally, other than in respect of noise affecting persons not employed therein.

The notice must state the reasons and a date by which it is to be complied with. If the matter is remedied, the Commissioner may, and if the proprietor requests shall, cancel the notice, and may then give written directions. A proprietor aggrieved by the issue, a refusal to cancel, or a direction on cancellation may, within 28 days of being notified, appeal to the Administrative Appeals Board.

**SD takeaway:** A premises not designed and constructed for industrial use can still be prohibited if it is not suitable for the safety, health, and welfare of persons generally, so do not plan a notifiable process into a building that cannot meet that test.

### 4. Proprietor’s duty, safety-training certificate, and where the design rules sit (sections 6A, 6BA, 7, and 7A)

Section 6A requires every proprietor of an industrial undertaking to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health and safety at work of all persons employed by the proprietor there. The duty includes safe plant and systems of work, safe means of access and egress from any part under the proprietor’s control, and a safe working environment. The proprietor is the person for the time being having the management or control of the business, and includes the occupier and the occupier’s agent (section 2).

Schedule 4 names construction work, and every person carrying out construction work, as a specified industrial undertaking and class of persons. Section 6BA lets the Commissioner recognize a safety training course for that class. On and after the appointed day, the proprietor shall not employ at the undertaking a relevant person who has not been issued a relevant certificate or whose certificate has expired. A certificate expires on the day specified in it, not less than 1 year and not more than 3 years after issue, or if no day is specified, 3 years after issue.

Section 7 lets the Commissioner by regulation provide, among other things, for means of ensuring the safety of persons in industrial undertakings, precautions against fire and means of escape from fire, and duties of proprietors, contractors, and persons employed. Section 7A codes of practice give practical guidance. Failure to observe an approved code does not of itself cause criminal liability, but in criminal proceedings the code may be relied on as tending to establish or negative the alleged contravention or failure.

**SD takeaway:** Layout safety for a construction site is not in this Ordinance. Read Cap. 59 sub. leg. I for falls, excavations, and site welfare, and treat a section 6BA certificate as a staffing gate for every person carrying out construction work once an appointed day is in force.
