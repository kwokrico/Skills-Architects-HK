# Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 59 sub. leg. I)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Contents last updated 17 July 2026 | Made under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59)

> Scope note: These regulations set site safety duties for construction work, places of work, scaffolds, excavations, electricity, falling materials, fire escapes, latrines, and welfare. They are not a structural design code, and they do not print the member sizes of a working platform. A working platform, guard-rail, barrier, toe-board, fence, covering, gangway, or run provided under regulation 38B must comply with the Third Schedule applicable to it. A builder’s lift and a tower working platform are outside the definition of hoist and sit in the Builders’ Lifts and Tower Working Platforms (Safety) Ordinance (Cap. 470). A suspended working platform sits in Cap. 59 sub. leg. AC.

## Regulatory Overview

The regulations apply to all construction work, all construction sites, and the machinery, plant, tools, gear, and materials with which any construction work is carried on (regulation 3). A construction site is a place where construction work is undertaken and also any area in the immediate vicinity used for the storage of materials or plant used or intended to be used for that work (regulation 2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who is responsible (regulation 2)

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Contractor responsible for a construction site | A contractor is responsible if he is undertaking construction work there or, where more than one contractor is undertaking construction work at the site, if he is the principal contractor undertaking work there |
| Contractor responsible for plant | The plant is located at a construction site for which he is responsible |
| Direct control | Control over the way the construction work, process, excavation, operation, or erection, substantial addition, alteration, or dismantling of scaffold is carried out |
| Place of work | Any place used for construction work, or for any work activities arising from or in connection with construction work, including any place to which such a person has access whilst at work |
| Scaffold | Any temporarily provided structure on or from which persons perform work, or which enables access or materials to be taken to that place, including any working platform, gangway, run, ladder, or step-ladder (other than an independent ladder or step-ladder which does not form part of such a structure) together with any guard-rail, toe-board, or other safeguards and all fixings, but not including a lifting appliance or a structure used merely to support such an appliance or to support other plant or equipment |

**SD takeaway:** Draw the site boundary to include the immediate storage area. The principal contractor carries the site-wide duties, and any contractor with direct control of the work carries the same fall, excavation, and electricity duties for that work.

### 2. Falls from 2 metres, edges, and scaffolds (regulations 38A, 38AA, 38B, 38C, and 38D)

Hazardous conditions that may give rise to a risk of persons falling from a height include an unprotected edge or opening, improper design and construction of a place of work, inadequate or insecure support or anchoring, improper maintenance, and any working platform (other than a suspended working platform) that fails to comply with the Third Schedule applicable to it (regulations 38A(4A) and 38AA(5)).

| Duty | Who | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| Safe access and egress | Contractor responsible for the site, and any contractor with direct control of the work | Suitable and adequate safe access to and egress from every place of work, so far as is reasonably practicable (regulations 38A(2) and 38AA(2)) |
| Keep people out | Same | No person gains access to a place where hazardous conditions are present, except a person rectifying them if reasonably practicable steps have been taken for that person’s safety |
| Prevent falls | Same | Adequate steps to prevent any person falling from a height of 2 metres or more (regulation 38B(1) and (1A)) |
| What adequate steps include | — | Provision, use, and maintenance of one or more of: working platforms; guard-rails, barriers, toe-boards, and fences; coverings for openings; gangways and runs |
| Third Schedule | — | Every such working platform (other than a suspended working platform), guard-rail, barrier, toe-board, fence, covering, gangway, or run shall comply with the Third Schedule applicable to it |
| Demolition edges | — | Regulation 38B(1) and (1A) do not apply to an opening, corner, break, or edge exposed in demolition if adequate precautions prevent exposure to the risk of falling |
| Work off the ground | Contractor responsible and any contractor with direct control | If work cannot be safely done on or from the ground or from part of a permanent structure, provide and ensure the use of a scaffold, ladder, or other means of support safe for the purpose (regulation 38C) |
| Before use | Contractor responsible for that scaffold, ladder, or means of support, and any contractor with direct control of work which uses it | It shall not be used unless it is so designed and constructed that it does not collapse, overturn, or move accidentally; of suitable and sound materials of sufficient strength and capacity; and properly maintained and every part kept so securely supported or suspended as to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that it is stable (regulation 38D) |

**SD takeaway:** Any edge or opening from which a person can fall 2 metres or more needs a working platform, guard-rail, barrier, toe-board, fence, covering, gangway, or run that meets the Third Schedule, unless the work can be done safely from the ground or from part of a permanent structure.

### 3. Excavations (regulations 39, 40, 41, and 41A)

| Rule | Regulation | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| Support | 39(1) | As soon as may be necessary after commencement, erect a structure of suitable timber or other suitable material so workmen are not endangered by a fall or displacement of earth, rock, or other material, including waste and debris, adjacent to or forming the side |
| Examination | 39(2) | A competent person examines every part where workmen are employed at least once in every period of 7 days until the excavation or earthwork is completed or abandoned. No further work until a report in the approved form states that the excavation or earthwork and every structure erected under paragraph (1) is safe and secure |
| When support is not required | 39(4)(a) | No fall or dislodgment is liable to bury or trap a person employed in or near it, or to strike such a person from a height of more than 1.2 metres, having regard to the nature and slope of the sides and other circumstances |
| Fence or cover | 40(1) | Where a workman or other person lawfully on the site is liable to fall a distance of more than 2 metres, so far as practicable either erect a suitable barrier as close as is reasonably practicable to the edge, or securely cover the excavation, shaft, pit, or opening |
| When the barrier may be absent | 40(2) | While and to the extent access of persons or movement of plant, equipment, or materials makes it necessary, or while it has not yet been practicable to erect the barrier or covering since formation of that part |
| Stacking | 41 | No material placed or stacked close to the edge so as to endanger any person working in it, and no load or plant placed or moved near the edge if it is likely to cause the side to collapse |
| Escape | 41A | Where there is reason to apprehend danger from rising water or from an irruption of water or material, adequate means, so far as practicable, for persons employed therein to reach positions of safety |

**SD takeaway:** An excavation into which a person can fall more than 2 metres needs a barrier at the edge or a secure cover, and side support is required unless a fall cannot bury someone or strike someone from more than 1.2 metres.

### 4. Electricity, falling materials, drowning, fire escapes, and light (regulations 47, 49, 50, 52A, and 54)

| Hazard | Regulation | What the plan must allow |
|---|---|---|
| Live cable or apparatus | 47(1) and (1A) | Before commencement and during progress, measures, whether by rendering the cable or apparatus electrically dead or otherwise, so workmen liable to come into contact are not endangered |
| Overhead charged cable or apparatus | 47(2) and (3) | Adequate and suitably placed barriers or other means so the cable or apparatus is not a source of danger, including from a lifting appliance coming into contact |
| Falling material | 49(1) and (1A) | Precautions necessary to prevent a workman being struck |
| Lowering | 49(2) | Scaffolding materials, tools, or other objects and materials are not thrown, tipped, or shot down from a height where they are liable to cause injury, and where practicable are properly lowered by a lifting appliance or lifting gear |
| Demolition debris | 49(3) and (4) | Where proper lowering is impracticable, or part of a building or other structure is being demolished or broken off, all reasonable steps to protect workmen from falling or flying debris |
| Lighting | 50 | The place of work, the approach, any part where raising or lowering is in progress, and the vicinity of any dangerous opening, adequately and suitably lit to the extent necessary for that workman’s safety |
| Water | 52A | On or adjacent to water into which a workman is liable to fall with risk of drowning: suitable rescue equipment kept efficient, and measures for prompt rescue. Where there is a special risk of such a fall from land, from a structure adjacent to or above the water, or from a floating stage, secure fencing. Fencing may be removed or remain unerected for the time and to the extent necessary for access or movement of materials |
| Fire | 54 | Maintain in good condition and free from obstruction all means of escape in case of fire and all fire-fighting appliances provided on the site, and in the place where the construction work is carried out. No person shall wilfully alter, damage, obstruct, or otherwise impair them |

**SD takeaway:** Keep existing means of escape and fire-fighting appliances clear for the whole of the works, and set crane and scaffold zones off live overhead cables unless the cable is dead or a barrier keeps the lifting appliance clear.

### 5. Welfare and notification (regulations 55, 56, and 66)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Latrines | The contractor responsible for the site shall provide sufficient and suitable latrine and washing conveniences. Where persons of both sexes are or are intended to be employed, the conveniences shall afford proper separate accommodation for persons of each sex (regulation 55) |
| Shelter | A room or covered area where workmen can shelter during inclement weather, and a supply of wholesome drinking water (regulation 66(1)) |
| Meals | If facilities for obtaining meals are not available in the vicinity, or if the Commissioner so directs, a room or covered area where workmen can take meals and one or more stoves. That room may also serve as the shelter (regulation 66) |
| Notice to the Commissioner | Within 7 days after commencement, furnish in writing the contractor’s name and address, partners if a firm, every subcontractor’s name and address, the location, the nature of the work, the commencement date, whether mechanical power is or will be used and its nature, and the expected duration (regulation 56(1)) |
| When notice is not required | The contractor has reasonable grounds for believing the work will be completed in less than 6 weeks; or information has already been furnished for other construction work at the same site; or not more than 10 workmen are or will be employed at any one time (regulation 56(2)) |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve site area for separate latrines where both sexes will be employed, and for a covered shelter with drinking water, before the programme assumes the whole site is building footprint.
