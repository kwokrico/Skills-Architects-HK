# TPB PG-No. 23A Kindergarten and Child Care Centre in Kowloon Tong Garden Estate
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
March 2011 [First issued: June 2001] | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: Planning criteria for a section 16 application for a kindergarten or child care centre in Kowloon Tong Garden Estate, the area bounded by Cornwall Street, Waterloo Road, Boundary Street and the East Rail line, mainly zoned “Residential (Group C) 1” on the Kowloon Tong Outline Zoning Plan. It is not an Education Bureau or Social Welfare Department registration, and it is not a kindergarten standard for sites outside that estate.

## Regulatory Overview
The estate is primarily for low-density residential development, and the interchange of the MTR (Mass Transit Railway) Kwun Tong Line and East Rail Line near Kent Road and Suffolk Road already attracts a high volume of traffic, so a new kindergarten or child care centre is assessed on the traffic it adds. A new proposal should be supported by a traffic impact assessment, and the Town Planning Board would only favourably consider it if parking and loading/unloading inside the premises allow setting down and picking up to be carried out safely within the premises, so that on-street loading/unloading and illegal parking could be minimized.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. On-site parking and loading (paras 3.1–3.2)

The Board seeks advice from the Transport Department on the vehicular access arrangements and the provision of parking and loading/unloading facilities. In designing those facilities, the following requirements should be observed:

| Facility | Requirement within the premises |
|---|---|
| Car parking | **0–1** space per **4 to 6** classrooms |
| Taxi / private-car lay-by | **1** lay-by for every **5 to 8** classrooms |
| School bus | A **minimum of 2** lay-bys of the size **3 m × 12 m** |
| Substitution for those two school-bus lay-bys | **5** lay-bys of the size **3 m × 7 m** for minibus / nanny van, providing a total number of seats that is **at least the same** as that provided by 2 school buses |
| Length and gear | School buses or minibuses of **minimum 10 m** and **6.5 m** in length respectively should be able to enter and depart in **forward gear** |
| Paths and students | The manoeuvring paths of any vehicles within the site **should not cross over any parking spaces**. Due regard should also be paid to students’ safety, avoiding possible conflict between vehicular manoeuvring and students’ activities |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze a forward-in, forward-out layout with the classroom-based car and taxi lay-bys and either two 3 m × 12 m school-bus lay-bys or five 3 m × 7 m van lay-bys with at least the same number of seats.

### 2. Other planning criteria (para 3.3)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Compatibility | Should be compatible with the surrounding developments |
| Environment | Should not be susceptible to adverse environmental impacts such as noise impact from Waterloo Road and trains; otherwise adequate environmental mitigation measures **must** be provided |
| Infrastructure | Should be sustainable in terms of the capacities of existing and planned infrastructure such as drainage, sewerage, roads, water supply and utilities in the locality and its surrounding areas |
| Trees | Tree felling to make way for the kindergarten or child care centre **would not normally be allowed** |
| Structure and means of escape | Should be to the satisfaction of the Buildings Department |
| Fire | Access for fire appliances and fire service installations should be to the satisfaction of the Fire Services Department |
| Kindergarten | Internal layout and provision of indoor/outdoor play area should be to the satisfaction of the Education Bureau |
| Child care centre | Internal layout and provision of indoor/outdoor play area should be to the satisfaction of the Social Welfare Department |
| Other departments | All other statutory or non-statutory requirements of Government departments should be met |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the trees, and where the site is exposed to Waterloo Road or train noise the scheme must include mitigation; play area is for the Education Bureau or the Social Welfare Department according to the use.
