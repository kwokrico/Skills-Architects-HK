# TPB PG-No. 34D Renewal of Planning Approval and Extension of Time for Temporary Use
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
August 2021 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: Procedure guideline on renewing a planning approval for a temporary use or development, and on extending the time for compliance with its planning conditions. It does not authorise a permanent use, and it does not set plot ratio or building height. The renewal period and the compliance period are tied to the previous temporary approval.

## Regulatory Overview
A temporary approval lapses at the end of its approval period, and continuation requires a fresh section 16 application for renewal, filed not less than 2 months and normally not more than 4 months before expiry. An extension of time to comply with a time-limited condition is a Class B amendment under section 16A, and the total compliance period, including the extension sought, should not exceed half the duration of the temporary approval.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Renewal filing window and the streamlined submission (paras 2, 3.1–3.5)

The approval lapses on expiry. The Board is under no obligation to renew if there are new planning circumstances. A renewal is a section 16 application under the extant statutory plan, following the Guidance Notes and Town Planning Board Guidelines for section 16 applications, including the owner’s consent and notification requirements.

Where there is no major change in planning circumstances, such as a change in planning policy or land-use zoning, new technical assessments are not required. Updated assessments may still be required. Doubt on that need is referred to the relevant District Planning Office or the Government departments concerned.

| Submission item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| (a) | Reasons for the application |
| (b) | The renewal period sought. **The period requested cannot exceed the duration of the previous approval** |
| (c) | Whether the previous conditions on technical assessments and provision of facilities have been complied with to the satisfaction of the departments |
| Conditions already complied with, and no change in the proposed use or layout | The accepted proposals, plus correspondence and photos of the implemented facilities. The date of each photo must be shown |
| Earliest filing | Normally **no more than 4 months** before expiry. An earlier application is considered only on individual merits and exceptional circumstances. The applicant must justify it, for example renewal of a permit or licence, continued community services, or a longer relocation if renewal is refused. Convenience of the applicant is not accepted |
| Latest filing | **No less than 2 months** before expiry, so the application can be published for inspection and comment. An application filed less than 2 months before expiry may not be processed. A fresh section 16 application is then required |

**SD takeaway:** Lodge the renewal section 16 inside the window that opens **4 months** before expiry and closes **2 months** before expiry, with owner’s consent or notification and dated photos of the complied facilities.

### 2. How a renewal is assessed, and how long it may run (paras 4.1–4.2)

| Criterion | What the Board looks at |
|---|---|
| (a) | Any material change in planning circumstances since the previous approval, such as planning policy or land-use zoning, or a change in surrounding land uses |
| (b) | Adverse planning implications of renewal, such as pre-emption of planned permanent development |
| (c) | Whether the previous planning conditions were complied with to the satisfaction of the departments within the specified time limits |
| (d) | Whether the approval period sought is reasonable |
| (e) | Any other relevant considerations |

Under normal circumstances the renewal period should not be longer than the original validity period of the temporary approval. In general the Board is unlikely to grant a period **exceeding three years** unless there are strong justifications and the period is allowed under the relevant statutory plans. The Board may grant a shorter period than the one requested.

**SD takeaway:** Ask for no longer than the previous approval, and do not expect a renewal **longer than three years** unless the statutory plan allows that period and the justification is strong.

### 3. Extension of time to comply with conditions (paras 5.1–5.6, 6.1–6.2)

Temporary permissions are generally granted with time limits for compliance. An extension is granted only with good justifications. It falls within Class B amendments and is made under section 16A, following the Guidance Notes and the Town Planning Board Guidelines for Class A and Class B Amendments.

| Rule | Limit or content |
|---|---|
| When to apply | **No less than 6 weeks** before the specified time limit expires |
| Filed inside 6 weeks | May not be processed |
| Condition missed by the specified time limit | The planning permission is revoked |
| Permission already revoked when the Board considers the application | The Board will not consider the extension, even if the application was submitted before expiry. A fresh section 16 application is required |
| What to submit | Reasons; the extension period sought; and an account of everything done to implement the conditions since permission was granted, including evidence of proposals submitted and works undertaken or completed |

| Assessment criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| (a) | Whether there is a full justification for missing the prescribed time limit |
| (b) | Whether reasonable action has been taken on all conditions or on the outstanding ones |
| (c) | Whether the extension has adverse planning implications |
| (d) | Whether the extension sought is reasonable |
| (e) | Any other relevant considerations |

If the total compliance period, including the extension sought, **exceeds half the duration of the temporary approval**, extension is normally not granted. The examples printed are **6 months** for a temporary use of one year, and **18 months** for a temporary use of three years. Under no circumstances should the extension of time for compliance exceed the original validity period of the temporary approval.

**SD takeaway:** File the section 16A extension at least **6 weeks** before the condition deadline, and keep the total compliance time within **half** of the temporary approval and inside the original validity period.
