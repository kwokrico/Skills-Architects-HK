# TPB PG-No. 29C Submission and Processing of Representations and Further Representations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
September 2023 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: Procedure guideline on how representations and further representations are lodged and processed in plan-making under the Town Planning Ordinance (Cap. 131). It does not grant a permission, and it does not set plot ratio or building height.

## Regulatory Overview
A plan or a plan amendment exhibited under section 5 or section 7 is open for representation for 2 months, and a Board amendment proposed to meet a representation is open for further representation for the first 3 weeks of its public inspection. The plan, with schedules of representations, further representations and Board amendments, must be submitted to the Chief Executive in Council within 5 months after the expiry of the last plan exhibition period, with the extensions stated in paragraph 2.6.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Statutory time limits (paras 2.1, 2.4, 2.6, 3.2–3.3, 4.1; Annex 1)

| Step | Limit printed in this guideline |
|---|---|
| Exhibition of a plan or amendment under section 5 or section 7 | **2 months** for public inspection |
| Representation, in support or in opposition | **2 months from publication of the Plan** |
| Further representation on a proposed amendment | **3 weeks from publication of the proposed amendment(s)**, being the first 3 weeks of that public inspection |
| Submission of the plan to the Chief Executive in Council | **Within 5 months after the expiry of the last plan exhibition period** |
| Extension of that 5-month limit | **A period of 2 months**, and in exceptional circumstances **two further periods of 2 months each**, by the Secretary for Development |
| Annex 1 label of the same submission | **5 months from the end of the 2-month exhibition period** (sections 8(4)–(5)), with the same Secretary for Development extensions (sections 8(6)–(8)) |
| Further information after the representation or further-representation limit | **No provision.** A late representation or further representation is treated as not having been made |

The date of submission is the receipt date if sent by hand, the date of the postal chop if sent by post, or the receipt date of an online, fax or e-mail submission. During the 2-month exhibition the Board publishes a notice once a week in two daily Chinese newspapers and one daily English newspaper, on the Board’s website, and in each issue of the Gazette. For a proposed amendment, newspaper and website notices follow the same pattern during the first 3 weeks.

**SD takeaway:** Put the whole representation in before the **2-month** exhibition ends; nothing can be added after that limit.

### 2. What the Board can treat as not made (paras 3.1, 3.4, 3.7–3.9)

Representations use Form No. S6 and further representations use Form No. S6D. They go in writing to the Secretary, Town Planning Board, by hand, post, fax or e-mail.

| Missing or excluded content | Consequence printed in this guideline |
|---|---|
| No full name as on the Hong Kong Identity card or passport, an incomplete or illegible name, or no identity-card or passport number (first four alphanumeric characters) | May be treated as not having been made. An authorized agent must also provide the original signed authorization letter. A company, organisation or group submission needs the same particulars for its authorized representative |
| Representation without the matter in the Plan, whether it supports or opposes, the reasons, and any amendment proposed | The Board may refuse to process it and treat it as not having been made (sections 6(2)(a) and 6(2)(b)) |
| Further representation without the proposed amendment it relates to, whether it supports or opposes, and the reasons | Same refusal (sections 6D(2)(a) and 6D(2)(b)) |
| Reasons about compensation or assistance from resumption, acquisition, clearance or obtaining vacant possession | To that extent, may be treated as not having been made (sections 6(3A) and 6D(3B)) |
| More than 20 pages, or any page larger than A4 | **4 hard copies and 1 soft copy**, plus an e-mail address. Provide a summary in the other language. Each searchable PDF must not exceed **200 MB** |

**SD takeaway:** A representation that argues compensation, or that omits the matter, the support-or-oppose position and the reasons, can be treated as not made.

### 3. Representation hearing (paras 2.2, 2.5, 3.5, 5.1–5.5)

There is a hearing for representations. There is no hearing for further representations. Under section 2A(1) the Board may appoint Representation Hearing Committees. A representer who gave no correspondence address or e-mail address is taken as not exercising the right under section 6B to attend and be heard. Paragraph 5.2 still allows that person to contact the Secretariat before the deadline and attend subject to identity verification.

| Hearing step | Practice printed in this guideline |
|---|---|
| Tentative hearing date | Told about **4 weeks** before the hearing |
| Attendance form | Return within **10 calendar days** from the date of the invitation |
| Time, arrangements and Board paper | About **7 days** before the hearing. The same notice states whether consent to an authorized representative is granted |
| Natural person | Must attend in person. An authorized representative needs prior consent, granted only for exceptional circumstances. The Board has delegated that consent to the Secretary under section 2(5)(c). The criteria sit in a separate Guidance Notes document |
| Company, organisation or group | May authorize a natural person to attend |
| Absence | The Board may proceed in the representer’s absence or adjourn |

**SD takeaway:** A natural-person representer returns the attendance form within **10 calendar days** and attends in person.

### 4. How a proposed amendment is decided (paras 2.3–2.5, 6.1–6.2; Annex 1)

After the representation hearing the Board may propose amendments to meet or partly meet the representations, in the manner proposed or in another manner it considers appropriate. Only planning-related considerations are taken into account: the nature of the submission, planning intention, land-use compatibility and impacts (environment, ecology, traffic, infrastructure, landscape, visual, air ventilation and the local community), and other considerations the Board considers appropriate.

| Further representations received | What the Board then does |
|---|---|
| None | Amends the plan by incorporating the proposed amendment(s) (Annex 1: section 6G) |
| Received, and not opposing | Holds a meeting, considers them, and amends the plan by the proposed amendment(s) (Annex 1: section 6F(9)) |
| Opposing | Seeks departmental comments and written responses where appropriate, then decides whether to amend by the proposed amendment(s) or by those amendment(s) as varied (Annex 1: section 6F(8)) |

The amendment forms part of the plan (Annex 1: section 6H(1)). If no amendment is proposed, the plan proceeds to submission to the Chief Executive in Council.

**SD takeaway:** An opposing further representation is decided on the papers, with departmental comments, and can vary the amendment the Board had proposed.

### 5. Development Scheme Plans (paras 7.4, 8.1–8.4)

The ordinary notification in section 7 does not apply to a Development Scheme Plan under the Urban Renewal Authority Ordinance (Cap. 563).

| Decision | When it is released |
|---|---|
| Decision on the Development Scheme Plan, and an amendment to it under section 7 | Kept confidential for **3 to 4 weeks** after the meeting, and released when the plan is published under section 5 |
| Decision after the representation hearing | Kept confidential for **3 to 4 weeks**, and released when any proposed amendment is published under section 6C(1). The same practice applies after further representations |
| No amendment after the Board’s consideration | Also released **3 to 4 weeks** after the meeting |
| Before release | No gist, confirmed minutes, verbal advice or interim reply |

**SD takeaway:** On a Development Scheme Plan, do not treat the meeting day as the release day; the decision stays confidential for **3 to 4 weeks**.
