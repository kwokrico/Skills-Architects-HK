# TPB PG-No. 35E Extension of Time for Commencement of Development
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
MARCH 2026 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: How the Board decides whether an approved development has commenced, and how it assesses an application under section 16A of the Town Planning Ordinance to extend the time for commencement. The time limit does not apply to a permission for regularisation of uses or to a permission granted on a temporary basis. The Board does not have power under the Ordinance to extend time for a planning permission that has lapsed and ceased to have effect.

## Regulatory Overview
Any planning permission granted by the Board, except those for regularisation of uses and those granted on a temporary basis, is subject to a time limit that the permission shall cease to have effect on a specified date unless, prior to that date, the approved development has commenced or an extension of time for commencement is granted. The time limit is imposed so that the approved development would be implemented within a reasonable period; with good justifications and the assessment criteria in paragraph 4, the Board may grant an extension under section 16A, but should there be new planning circumstances governing the application, the Board is under no obligation to approve the application.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What counts as commencement (paras 2.1–2.2)

The determination should be considered on the facts and circumstances of each case.

| Situation | What may constitute commencement |
|---|---|
| In general | The approval of building plans would constitute a commencement of development |
| A land transaction with the Government is required | The Board may consider that the approved development has commenced as at the date of acceptance of the formal basic terms offer. That offer is the basic terms, excluding provisional basic terms, with or without premium, fee or rent, offered by the Lands Department. The land document generally includes a land grant (including land sale and small house grant), a lease modification (including a short-term or temporary waiver), a land exchange, a short-term tenancy agreement, and any other document issued by the Lands Department which is required to effect the approved development |
| Building-plan submission or land transaction is not applicable | Issuance of relevant documents for undertaking the approved development, use or works by the relevant authorities may constitute commencement, for instance an excavation permit for land excavation, or a certificate of exemption in respect of site formation works (for example proposed land filling for a permitted New Territories Exempted House) |
| Government project or use | The Board may also consider the grant of the relevant government land allocation, or the approval of funding for carrying out detailed design/building works |
| Urban Renewal Authority development scheme | The approval of the resumption of land by the Chief Executive in Council would also constitute a commencement of development |
| More than one structure or premises, with more than one approval of building plans, land transaction or government land allocation covering different parts of a site | The facts and circumstances of the case decide whether the approved development has “commenced” |
| Comprehensive development which is not under single ownership and involves different implementation phases specified in the approved scheme | Phased commencement could be allowed provided that the approved scheme has demonstrated that phased development will not undermine the planning intention. Comprehensive development means development within a zone or zones on a statutory plan, for example a “Comprehensive Development Area” zone, intended for comprehensive development or redevelopment, or permitted in the form of a comprehensive development scheme. Reference will generally be made to the criteria on “Allowance for Phased Development” in Town Planning Board Guidelines No. 17A. The circumstances in para 2.1 apply. Project proponents of comprehensive developments with phased commencement **shall** make due effort to implement the remaining phases within the validity period, or otherwise the remaining phases of the approved schemes yet to be commenced **shall cease to have effect** |

**SD takeaway:** Treat approval of building plans, or acceptance of the formal basic terms offer where a land transaction is required, as the commencement event, and commence every remaining phase of a comprehensive scheme within the validity period or that phase shall cease to have effect.

### 2. Extension period, submission deadline, and lapse (paras 3.1–3.4)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Aggregate period | Any extension or extensions of time for commencement **shall not** result in an aggregate extension period longer than the original duration for commencement of the approved development proposal. The guideline states no separate number of years |
| Class of amendment | The application falls within Class B amendments published by the Board and **shall** be made under section 16A. The procedures in the Town Planning Board Guidelines for Class A and Class B Amendments to Approved Development Proposals **shall** be followed |
| Lodging date | The applicant **shall** submit the application **no less than 6 weeks** before the expiry of the specified time limit |
| Not commenced by the specified time limit | The planning permission **will lapse** |
| Permission already ceased at the time of consideration | The Board does not have power under the Ordinance to extend time for a planning permission that has lapsed and ceased to have effect, despite an application submitted before expiry. A fresh section 16 planning application in accordance with the extant statutory plan will be required |
| Material the applicant is required to provide | (a) reasons for the application; (b) the time period for which an extension is sought; (c) an account of all actions taken to implement the development since the granting of planning permission, including evidence and documentation on the submitted proposals and any works undertaken or completed to fulfil any approval conditions |

**SD takeaway:** Lodge the section 16A application no less than 6 weeks before expiry, and keep the aggregate extension no longer than the original duration, because a permission that has ceased to have effect cannot be extended.

### 3. Assessment criteria (paragraph 4)

The criteria for assessing an application for extension of time for commencement may include:

| | Criterion |
|---|---|
| (a) | Whether there has been any material change in planning circumstances since the original permission was granted, such as a change in the planning policy or land-use zoning for the area |
| (b) | Whether there are any adverse planning implications arising from the extension of time |
| (c) | Whether commencement is delayed due to technical or practical problems beyond the control of the applicant, for example delays in land administration procedures, technical issues in respect of vehicular access and drainage works, or difficulties in land assembly |
| (d) | Whether the applicant has demonstrated that reasonable action has been taken for implementation of the approved development, for example submission of building plans for approval and/or application for a Small House or land exchange, where applicable |
| (e) | Whether the applicant has demonstrated that reasonable action has been taken to the satisfaction of relevant government departments in complying with any approval conditions, for example submission and/or implementation of proposals, where applicable |
| (f) | Whether the applicant has demonstrated that there is a good prospect to commence the proposed development within the extended time limit |
| (g) | Whether the extension period applied for is reasonable |
| (h) | Any other relevant considerations |

**SD takeaway:** Provide the reasons, the period sought, and the account of actions since the permission, and expect the Board to test a material change in policy or zoning and whether there is a good prospect of commencing within that period.
