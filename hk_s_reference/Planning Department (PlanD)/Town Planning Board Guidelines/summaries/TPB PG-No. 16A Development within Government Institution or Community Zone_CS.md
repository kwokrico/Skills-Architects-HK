# TPB PG-No. 16A Development within Government Institution or Community Zone
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
March 2026 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: These guidelines apply to applications under section 16 of the Town Planning Ordinance (Cap. 131) for development and for redevelopment for Column 2 uses within a “Government, Institution or Community” (“G/IC”) zone, and, wherever appropriate, to in-situ conversion of an existing “G/IC” building, or part of it, to Column 2 uses. “G/IC” zones reflect existing government, institution and community (GIC) uses and reserve sites for future GIC facilities and other public uses in accordance with the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines (HKPSG), or to meet the requirements of government bureaux and departments. Some low-rise, low-density GIC developments serve as breathing space and/or visual relief in a high-rise, high-density setting, and some sites are reserved for unforeseen future demand with no specific GIC use designated yet.

## Regulatory Overview
Sites zoned “G/IC” are intended to be developed or redeveloped for GIC uses unless it is established that GIC provision would not be jeopardised and the concerned Government departments have no objection to releasing the site, or part of it, for other uses. A Column 2 proposal is considered on its individual merits against the criteria in paragraph 2, including reprovisioning, compatibility, scale, air ventilation and visual impact, infrastructure, parking, environment, landscape and any building of historical or architectural value.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When these guidelines apply (paras. 1.1–1.5 and para. 3.1)

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Why a “G/IC” site may be released | Changing demographic structure and revisions to GIC standards may leave facilities surplus, obsolete or under-utilised, while others need in-situ expansion or reprovisioning. Opportunities then exist for beneficial uses |
| Column 2 uses | May be permitted with or without conditions on application to the Board under section 16 |
| Development and redevelopment | Both are within the scope of the guidelines |
| In-situ conversion | The guidelines also apply, wherever appropriate, to proposed in-situ conversion for Column 2 uses of an existing “G/IC” building, or part of it |

**SD takeaway:** Treat every Column 2 use, including a conversion of part of an existing “G/IC” building, as a section 16 application that still has to protect long-term GIC provision.

### 2. Releasing “G/IC” land without harming GIC facilities (paras. 2.1–2.2)

The applicant should provide justifications and satisfactorily demonstrate all of the following that apply.

| Case | What must be demonstrated |
|---|---|
| Site designated with specific uses | The application site is no longer required for the designated GIC uses, **or** adequate reprovisioning of the designated GIC uses is provided either in-situ or elsewhere; **and** there is adequate provision of other GIC facilities in the district |
| Undesignated “G/IC” site | The application site is no longer required to be reserved for any GIC uses |
| Either case | The proposed development/redevelopment would not adversely affect the provision of GIC facilities and open space in the district on a long-term basis |
| Existing and planned facilities on the site | The proposal **should not** adversely affect the normal operation of the existing GIC facilities, nor delay the implementation of the planned GIC facilities, if any |
| Temporary reprovisioning | If necessary, it **should** be provided **prior to the completion** of the proposed development |

**SD takeaway:** Do not take “G/IC” land for a Column 2 use until designated facilities are either no longer required or reprovided in-situ or elsewhere, district GIC and open space remain adequate on a long-term basis, and any temporary reprovisioning is in place before the new development is completed.

### 3. Land use, intensity, massing and ventilation (paras. 2.3–2.5)

| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Land-use compatibility | Should be compatible with the GIC uses on the site, if any, and with the surrounding areas |
| Scale and intensity | Should be in keeping with that of the adjacent area |
| Controls taken into account | Development restrictions stipulated on the statutory plan for similar developments in the locality, and the prevailing development restrictions administratively imposed by the Government on nearby similar developments, for example restrictions in the outline development plans/layout plan, and plot ratios in accordance with the density zones under the HKPSG |
| Massing and townscape | Scale and design should take into account the character and massing of buildings in the surrounding areas |
| Air and views | **Should not** cause significant adverse air ventilation and/or visual impact on the townscape and ventilation of the area |

**SD takeaway:** Match the massing to the adjacent area and to the statutory-plan and administrative restrictions that apply to similar nearby developments, including HKPSG density-zone plot ratios, and test air ventilation and visual impact before the height is fixed.

### 4. Infrastructure, traffic and environment (paras. 2.6–2.8)

| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure | Should be sustainable in terms of the capacities of existing and planned drainage, sewerage, roads, water supply and utilities in the locality and surrounding areas |
| Parking and loading/unloading | There **should** be adequate provision to serve the development in accordance with the HKPSG and to the satisfaction of the Transport Department |
| Vehicular access | Adequate arrangements **should** be provided to the satisfaction of the Transport Department |
| Environmental effect on surroundings | The development **should not** cause, directly or indirectly, the surrounding areas to be susceptible to adverse environmental impacts |
| Pollution sources nearby | The development should not be susceptible to adverse environmental impacts from pollution sources nearby, including a heavily trafficked road |
| If it is susceptible | Adequate environmental mitigation, monitoring and audit measures **must** be provided |

**SD takeaway:** Size parking, loading and access to the HKPSG and the Transport Department, confirm drainage, sewerage, roads, water and utilities can carry the scheme, and where a heavily trafficked road or other pollution source affects the site lock in mitigation, monitoring and audit.

### 5. Landscape, heritage buildings, planning gain and other requirements (paras. 2.9–2.12)

| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Mature trees, vegetation, or high landscape or amenity value | Design and layout should be compatible and should blend in well with the surrounding areas |
| Landscape submission | A Landscape Assessment according to Annex B of the Guidance Notes for Application for Permission under Section 16 of the Ordinance should be submitted as necessary |
| Historic or architectural value | Design and layout should have regard to the preservation of any existing buildings of historical or architectural value on or adjoining the application site |
| Planning gain | In general, the Board may give favourable consideration to a proposed development that may bring planning gain to the community |
| Other departments | All other statutory or non-statutory requirements of relevant government departments should be met |

**SD takeaway:** On a treed or high-amenity “G/IC” site, blend the layout into the landscape and submit the section 16 Landscape Assessment where it is necessary, and set the plan to preserve any building of historical or architectural value on or next to the site.
