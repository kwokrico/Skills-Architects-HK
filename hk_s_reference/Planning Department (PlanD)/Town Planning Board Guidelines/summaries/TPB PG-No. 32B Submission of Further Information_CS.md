# TPB PG-No. 32B Submission of Further Information
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
September 2023 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: Procedure guideline on further information that supplements a section 12A amendment-of-plan application, a section 16 planning application, or a section 17 review of a section 16 decision. It does not set plot ratio or building height. A change in those parameters can decide whether the further information is refused as a material change or is published again and restarts the decision period.

## Regulatory Overview
The Board accepts further information only inside the two periods specified in Gazette Notice 5200 of 1 September 2023, and only where it does not materially change the nature of the application. Accepted further information on a section 16 application or a section 17 review is published for public comment unless the Secretary of the Board exempts it, and the statutory decision period restarts unless an exemption from recounting is granted.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The only periods in which further information is accepted (paras 2.1–2.2, 3.1–3.2; Appendix A)

Under section 2(5)(c) the Board has delegated to its Secretary the power to accept further information, to exempt it from publication for public comments, and to exempt it from recounting the statutory time limit. The publication requirement does not apply to a section 12A application. Further information given outside the specified periods must not be accepted (sections 12A(13A), 16(2JA) and 17(2HA)).

Appendix A is Gazette Notice 5200, which took immediate effect on 1 September 2023. For a section 12A application (section 12A(24A)), a section 16 application (section 16(7A)) and a section 17 review (section 17(8)), the specified periods are:

| Window | Period in Gazette Notice 5200 |
|---|---|
| First | **Within two months after the day of the receipt of the application.** For the avoidance of doubt, sections 12A(14)(c)(ii), 16(2K)(c)(ii) and 17(2I)(c)(ii)(A) do not apply to this window |
| Second | **Within two months after the day** on which the Board decides to defer its decision so that the applicant can give further information |

Paragraph 2.2 states the counting the Board applies: the first **2-month** period starts from the day after the application is made (the worked example is an application made on 1 September 2023, accepted from 2 September 2023 to 1 November 2023 inclusive). The second **2-month** period starts from the day after the deferment decision (the worked example is a deferment on 1 December 2023, accepted from 2 December 2023 to 1 February 2024 inclusive).

Assessments are submitted in one go, not in batches. When a decision is deferred for further information, the Secretary may schedule the meeting on a date after that specified period and before the statutory time limit expires.

**SD takeaway:** Further information is accepted only in the **two-month** window after lodgement and, if the Board defers for that purpose, in a second **two-month** window after the deferment.

### 2. Further information that is refused as a material change (paras 3.3–3.4, 4)

Further information inside the specified periods is accepted when it does not result in a material change of the nature of the application. What is material is a matter of fact and degree.

| Application | Generally a material change |
|---|---|
| Section 12A | A major change in the site area or boundary, the proposed zoning, the uses, or the development restrictions |
| Section 16 and section 17 | A major change in the area and configuration of the site (for example enlargement to include an additional lot), the proposed use (for example office to hotel), the design and layout (for example a substantial change in built form and disposition of blocks), or the nature of the approval (for example temporary to permanent) |
| Any of the above | A change in the proposed site area, plot ratio, gross floor area, site coverage or building height **exceeding 10%** is generally “material”, particularly an increase. The circumstances of the case are still taken into account |

Material further information is not processed. Proceeding with it requires a fresh application. Proceeding with the original application means the further information is ignored and the statutory time limit runs from the original application date.

**SD takeaway:** Keep any revision of site area, plot ratio, gross floor area, site coverage or building height to **10% or less**, or lodge a fresh application.

### 3. What is published again, and what restarts the decision period (paras 3.3, 3.5–3.8, 5, 6)

If accepted further information is not exempted, the statutory time limit restarts automatically: **2 months** for a section 12A application and a section 16 application, and **3 months** for a section 17 review, unless an exemption is granted under sections 12A(15), 16(2L) and 17(2J). The applicant must say at the outset whether the original application should proceed if the further information is refused, or is accepted but not exempted from publication and/or recounting.

| Further information | Publication | Decision clock |
|---|---|---|
| Section 16 or section 17, accepted, not exempted | Published for **three weeks** for public comments. This is the further information that restarts publication | Statutory time limit **restarts**, unless the applicant has already elected to proceed with the original application |
| Section 16 or section 17, accepted and exempted from publication | Not published for public comments. Considered on the original meeting date | Original schedule kept |
| Section 12A, accepted, not exempted from recounting | Publication for public comments does not apply | Statutory time limit **restarts**, unless the applicant has elected to proceed without it |
| Section 12A, exempted from recounting | Publication for public comments does not apply | Original schedule kept |
| All accepted further information | Available on the Board’s website and at the Planning Enquiry Counters until the Board has considered the application, whether or not it was exempted from public comment | — |

An exemption is assessed case by case for both temporary and permanent schemes. In general it may be granted for:

| Type | What may be exempted |
|---|---|
| (a) Minor scheme change that does not change site area, plot ratio, gross floor area, site coverage or building height | Internal layout or disposition; location of open space and ancillary major utility installation; form of the building blocks; an increase in open space; a reduction in car parking spaces with no change in the car parking ratio; location and size of non-building area; a Landscape Master Plan that preserves trees and/or plants more trees; private indoor recreational facilities; phasing and implementation; operational details such as operation hours. The Secretary may refer to the Schedule of Class A Amendments |
| (b) Clarification | Background and supporting information, including identity, site area or boundary, lot number, existing conditions, owner’s consent, notification, operations, lease, application history, compliance with other legislation, surrounding uses and implementation schedule |
| (c) Responses | Technical clarification or responses to departments and public comments, without changing the scheme |
| (d) Revised technical assessment | No major change in assumptions, methodologies, findings and proposed mitigation measures |
| (e) Corrections | Editorial and transcription errors and miscellaneous minor information |
| (f) Other | Information the Secretary considers could be exempted |

Unless the applicant has clearly elected to proceed with the original application, an accepted but non-exempt submission is recounted and the applicant is told the rescheduled meeting date. Further information that is not accepted leaves the original application running, unless the applicant indicated otherwise.

**SD takeaway:** A section 16 or section 17 supplement that is accepted and not exempted is published again for **three weeks** and restarts the **2-month** or **3-month** decision period.

### 4. Consolidated sets, and representations (paras 7, 8)

Where several accepted further-information submissions cover the planning statement or technical assessments, the applicant submits one consolidated version to supersede them, preferably **at least two weeks** before the scheduled meeting. That version contains no new information. A consolidated version with no new information is not treated as new further information.

A representation on a new draft plan or on an amendment, and a further representation on amendments proposed under section 6B(8), must be made within the statutory time limits. There is no provision for the Board to accept further information on a representation or further representation after those limits. A late representation or further representation is treated as not having been made.

**SD takeaway:** Do not rely on a later supplement to a representation; the Ordinance gives no route for further information after the statutory limit.
