# TPB PG-No. 20A Compliance with Approval Conditions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
March 2026 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: Governs the general timing for complying with conditions imposed by the Town Planning Board, split into conditions before building-plan approval and conditions after building-plan approval. It is not the condition list on a particular permission.

## Regulatory Overview
Conditions imposed by the Board, in particular those related to the development itself, should be complied with before the use applied for actually comes into place, except conditions with a prescribed time limit, because permission is granted only subject to complete fulfilment of the imposed conditions. Other than time-limited conditions, which are usually for temporary uses, conditions divide into those required before building-plan approval and those required after building-plan approval, normally before occupation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Time-limited conditions (paragraphs 1–3)

| Type | When it is complied with |
|---|---|
| Conditions related to the development itself, other than those with a prescribed time limit | Should be complied with **before the use applied for actually comes into place**. |
| Conditions imposed with a prescribed time limit, usually temporary uses | Should be complied with **before the expiry of the time limit**. |

**SD takeaway:** Put every time-limited condition on the programme against its own expiry, separate from the building-plan and occupation sequence.

### 2. Before building-plan approval (paragraphs 4–6)

The following conditions concern or would affect detailed design and should be complied with **before the approval of the building plans**:

| Condition | Why it is before building-plan approval |
|---|---|
| Design, disposition or layout, and facilities forming part of the building, such as car parking spaces, footbridges and other community facilities within or forming part of the building development | The subject matter can be shown on the building plans. |
| Landscaping proposal for a development in a “Comprehensive Development Area”, or in an area with special design significance | The conceptual landscaping proposal may affect the building design. To satisfy the condition, the applicant is **only required to submit a conceptual landscape proposal rather than a detailed landscape plan**. |
| New or revised impact assessments, for example traffic impact assessment, environmental assessment and drainage impact assessment | The results will affect the building design, so they should be completed before approval of the building plans. |

| Related rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| How landscaping and assessments are submitted | Requirements in paragraphs 4(b) and 4(c) may not be reflected in general building plans, so a **separate submission** to the relevant departments is necessary. The applicant may instead submit the landscape proposal and assessments together with the general building plans. That choice is the applicant’s. |
| Revised Master Layout Plan | If compliance with the other approval conditions would not result in any **major changes** in the design, disposition or layout, submission of the revised Master Layout Plan **is not a pre-requisite** for approval of the building plans. |

**SD takeaway:** Clear design, disposition, layout, on-building facilities, the conceptual landscape proposal and the impact assessments before building-plan approval, and do not hold the building plans for a revised Master Layout Plan unless the other conditions cause a major layout change.

### 3. After building-plan approval, normally before occupation (paragraphs 7–9)

These conditions may not need to be complied with at building-plan approval. They are expected to be complied with **before occupation**. Non-compliance before occupation may cause significant adverse impacts on the development and the surrounding area.

| Condition | Timing |
|---|---|
| On-site facilities: vehicular access, landscaping, drainage, and sewage treatment and disposal | Expected before occupation. |
| Implementation of proposed mitigation for environmental, drainage and sewage impacts | Expected before occupation. |
| Development **should not be occupied** before off-site works or facilities such as road improvements and trunk-sewer improvement | Before occupation. |
| Facilities which require Government funding, such as public transport facilities, external footbridge links, and government, institution and community and other off-site facilities | Should **tie in with the programmes** of the concerned departments. |
| Effect on detailed design | Paragraph 9 states that these conditions have no direct impact on the detailed design, so they **need not be complied with before building-plan approval**. Exact timing depends on individual circumstances. |
| Advance design | The design of these facilities should be submitted **well in advance** to the relevant departments. |

**SD takeaway:** Do not occupy before on-site access, landscape, drainage, sewage, mitigation and the stated off-site works are in place, and submit the design of Government-funded off-site facilities early enough to meet that department’s programme.
