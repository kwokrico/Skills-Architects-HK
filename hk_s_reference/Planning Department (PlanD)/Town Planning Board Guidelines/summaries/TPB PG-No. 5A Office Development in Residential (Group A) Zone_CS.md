# TPB PG-No. 5A Office Development in Residential (Group A) Zone
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
December 2023 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: These guidelines set the main planning criteria for section 16 applications for office development and redevelopment in the “Residential (Group A)” (“R(A)”) zone, including schemes that incorporate commercial uses on the lower floors. Office use requires planning permission from the Town Planning Board except on the lowest three floors of a building, or in the purpose-design non-residential portion of an existing building. Each application is considered on its individual merits.

## Regulatory Overview
The planning intention of the “R(A)” zone is primarily for high-density residential development, and the permission system keeps planning control over those sites while allowing an office only where there are strong justifications. A proposal is assessed on location and compatibility, local office demand, on-site parking and loading, accessibility, traffic, site size, and whether the building is purposely designed for office and commercial use, with favourable weight where the scheme produces specific environmental or planning gains.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where office use needs permission (para. 1, paras. 2.1–2.2)

| Situation | Planning position |
|---|---|
| Office use in an “R(A)” zone | Requires planning permission from the Board, except the two cases below |
| Lowest **three** floors of a building | Excepted from that permission requirement |
| Purpose-design non-residential portion of an existing building | Excepted from that permission requirement |
| Development or redevelopment for office, which may include commercial uses on the lower floors | Within the scope of these guidelines |

The permission system is intended to keep adequate planning control and to allow flexibility only where there are strong justifications.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze any office above the lowest three floors, and any office outside an existing purpose-design non-residential portion, as a section 16 application that needs strong justification.

### 2. Location, demand and access (para. 3.1(a), (b) and (d))

| Criterion | What the Board looks for |
|---|---|
| Residential character | The office **should not** be located in a predominantly residential area |
| Compatibility | Should be compatible with the existing and planned land uses of the locality, for example a site close to the core business centre or office hub that forms an extension of the commercial area |
| Demand | There is a demand for office space in the particular area or district |
| Access | The site should be easily accessible, for example close to the Mass Transit Railway Station or well served by other public transport facilities |

**SD takeaway:** Place the office as an extension of a commercial or office hub on a site close to a Mass Transit Railway station or other public transport, outside a predominantly residential area, and with evidence of office demand in that district.

### 3. Parking, traffic and site size (para. 3.1(c), (e) and (f))

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Parking and loading/unloading | There **should** be adequate provision within the site in accordance with the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines and to the satisfaction of the Transport Department |
| Narrow frontage | Where the on-site loading/unloading requirement cannot be met, the applicant should demonstrate alternative locations for loading/unloading facilities to the satisfaction of the Transport Department |
| Traffic flow | The office **should not** cause congestion and disruption to the traffic flow of the locality |
| Congested areas | The applicant may be required to submit a traffic impact assessment to demonstrate no adverse traffic impact in the area |
| Site size | The site should be of adequate size to achieve a properly designed office building. The minimum site area varies with site configuration and with loading/unloading requirements in the particular locality |

**SD takeaway:** Size the site so a properly designed office can provide parking and loading/unloading to the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines and the Transport Department, and keep a traffic impact assessment in the drawing set where the locality is prone to congestion.

### 4. Purpose design and planning gains (para. 3.1(g), para. 3.2)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Building brief | The office **should** be purposely designed for office/commercial uses so that there is no risk of subsequent illegal conversion to substandard domestic units or other uses |
| Environmental gain the Board may favour | The site is near major sources of air and noise pollution, such as a major road, and the office is equipped with central air-conditioning and other noise mitigation measures which make it less susceptible to pollution than a residential development |
| Other planning gain | Provision of public open space and community facilities catering to the needs of the district |

In general, the Board may give favourable consideration to office developments which produce specific environmental and planning gains of those kinds.

**SD takeaway:** Purpose-design the floor plates for office and commercial use only, and where the site is next to a major road lock in central air-conditioning, noise mitigation, and any public open space or district community facilities as the planning gain.
