# TPB PG-No. 36C Class A and Class B Amendments to Approved Development Proposals
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
December 2023 | Town Planning Board

> Scope note: This guideline classifies amendments to a scheme already permitted under section 16, 17 or 17B of the Town Planning Ordinance (Chapter 131) into Class A and Class B, and sets the section 16A procedure. It is not a zoning bulk table. A change outside both classes needs a fresh section 16 application, and the extant statutory plan and any approval condition still cap what Class A or Class B can do.

## Regulatory Overview
Class A amendments need no further application to the Town Planning Board; Class B amendments need approval under section 16A(2); anything outside both classes needs a fresh section 16 application. The Annex schedule, agreed by the Board on 17 November 2023 in substitution for the schedule published on 2 March 2018 and published on 8 December 2023 with immediate effect, sets 19 categories, and a Class B application is assessed only against the scheme approved under section 16, 17 or 17B.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Classification, who decides, and the anti-stacking rule (ss. 2–8)

| Rule | What the guideline prints |
|---|---|
| Class A | No further application. Highlight the change on the building plans for checking by the Planning Department |
| Class B | Application under section 16A(2) by the person to whom the permission was granted, on Form No. S16A. A later owner may be appointed as authorized representative. Owners’ consent, owner notification, and publication for public inspection do not apply |
| Outside both classes | Fresh section 16 application |
| More than one accepted Class B application (s. 16A(7)) | The permission is the original approval as amended by **one** accepted Class B application. The applicant may implement the original permission, or that permission with any one accepted Class B application. All Class B changes should go in one application |
| What the Class B test looks at (s. 5) | Only the scheme previously approved under section 16, 17 or 17B. No account is taken of Class A amendments already allowed, Class B amendments already approved under section 16A, or minor amendments approved by a public officer under delegated authority before the Town Planning (Amendment) Ordinance 2004. Aggregate change beyond Class B is a fresh section 16 application |
| Who decides Class B | Delegated to the Director of Planning, and to the Deputy Director and Assistant Directors of Planning in the District Planning Branch. If concerned departments consider the application unacceptable, it goes to the Board |
| Deletion of previously proposed Government, institution or community facilities | Even if the deletion is initiated and agreed by the relevant department, and even if the facility is an approval condition, the matter goes to the Board. If the deletion is not initiated by the relevant department, a fresh section 16 application is required |
| Fulfilling an approval condition (s. 8) | No separate section 16A(2) application for amendments made in order to fulfill a condition. Disagreement with the department goes to the Board |
| Time for a delegated decision | Normally within **6 weeks** |
| Time if departments consider it unacceptable | Submitted to the Board within **two months** from receipt |
| Review under section 17 | Within **21 days** of notification. The review is not published for public inspection |
| Appeal under section 17B(1) | Within **60 days** of notification of the Board’s review decision, to the Secretary of the Town Planning Appeal Board |
| When to submit | Preferably before building plans. If the Class B change appears only at building-plan stage, the form and highlighted extracts may go to the Secretary of the Board at the same time as the plans go to the Building Authority. Pre-application advice does not pre-empt the decision |
| Commencement and condition dates (s. 6) | The time limit on the original permission stays unless extension of time is itself part of the section 16A amendment. Details are in the Board’s guidelines on extension of time for commencement, and on renewal and extension of time for compliance for a temporary use |

**SD takeaway:** Measure every Class B change from the original permitted scheme only, and put the whole Class B package in one section 16A application.

### 2. Site area, gross floor area, plot ratio and number of units (Categories 1–3)

Both Class A and Class B site changes must not add land in a different zone that itself needs planning permission, and must not make a material change to the approved proposal.

| Category 1 — site area and boundary | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Setting-out of the boundary; land-grant processing as required by the department; and/or general building plans as required by the department | A | No percentage printed |
| Alignment of public utility pipelines, and boundary or extent of filling or excavation, as required by the department | A | No percentage printed |
| Reduction of gross site area with a corresponding reduction in gross floor area | A | No percentage printed |
| Other changes | B | **Not exceeding 10%** of the gross site area |

Gross floor area of Government, institution or community facilities is not this category. It is Categories 7 and 9.

| Category 2 — total gross floor area and plot ratio | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction in gross floor area or plot ratio | A | Any reduction |
| Increase from additional plot ratio permitted by the Building Authority under Regulation 22(1) or (2) of the Building (Planning) Regulations, where the Notes of the extant plan permit it | A | As permitted under those Notes |
| Increase arising from Category 1 Class A item (a) (setting-out, land grant, or general building plans as required by the department) | A | Only if total gross floor area or plot ratio is **not** an approval condition, and the result does **not exceed** the maximum gross floor area or plot ratio on the extant plan |
| Increase arising from a Category 1 Class B site change | B | **Not exceeding 6,000 m² or 10%** of the approved total gross floor area, or the equivalent plot ratio, **whichever is the less**. Only where the extant plan has **no** plot ratio or gross floor area restriction, and the approval condition, if any, has **none** either |

| Category 3 — number of units | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction in the number of units | A | Any reduction |
| Increase | A | **Not exceeding 200 units or 10%** of the approved provision, **whichever is the less**, and only if the maximum number of units is not an approval condition |
| Increase because site area grew under Category 1 Class A, with the same average flat size as the approved scheme | A | No separate unit cap printed for this item. If average flat size changes, this item does not apply; items (a) and (b) and the Class B band do |
| Other increases | B | **Not exceeding 500 units or 20%** of the approved provision, **whichever is the less** |

“Units” means domestic units (houses or flats), units in a residential institution, and hotel guestrooms. For Class A items (a) and (b) and for Class B, the matching change in unit size is always permitted.

**SD takeaway:** A Class B gross floor area increase is only the lesser of **6,000 m²** and **10%**, and only when it comes from a Class B site-area change on a permission and a plan that set no gross floor area or plot ratio cap.

### 3. Building blocks, building height and site coverage (Categories 4–6)

| Category 4 — building blocks | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Change in building form, including podium height and podium design | A | The affected part is not the subject of an environmental mitigation measure, or the department agrees the related change |
| Reduction in the number of blocks | A | Disposition changes to the remaining blocks are minor, and the deleted block is not the subject of an environmental mitigation measure, or the department agrees. The schedule does not print a percentage for “minor” |
| Minor change in disposition of blocks or houses other than a New Territories Exempted House or a Small House | A | Same environmental-mitigation proviso. No percentage is printed |
| Minor change in disposition of a New Territories Exempted House or Small House | A | Must **not reduce** the percentage of the house footprint inside the “Village Type Development” zone and/or the village environs of a recognized village, plus the same environmental-mitigation proviso |
| Increase in the number of building blocks | B | No percentage cap is printed. A guard house, plant room or similar minor ancillary block may be left out of the block count for a Class A reduction and for this Class B increase |

| Category 5 — building height (absolute height, storeys, and metres above Principal Datum) | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction in absolute building height and/or number of storeys | A | Any reduction |
| More storeys, with **no** increase in absolute building height above ground | A | Must not exceed any storey restriction on the extant plan |
| More storeys, **no** increase in absolute height above ground, but the storey restriction on the extant plan is exceeded | B | Class B item (c) |
| Increase in the height of a block, where maximum building height is **not** an approval condition | A | Any one of: (i) not exceeding the building height restriction on the extant plan; or (ii) if the plan has **no** building height restriction, **not exceeding 10%** of the approved absolute height above ground or storeys above ground, **excluding refuge floors**; or (iii) for a building **not exceeding 30 m** above ground or **10 storeys** above ground, if the plan has **no** building height restriction, **not exceeding 20%** of that approved height or those storeys; or (iv) green features covered by the Joint Practice Notes of the Buildings Department, Lands Department and Planning Department, if the plan has **no** building height restriction |
| Increase other than Class A, and the plan has **no** building height restriction | B | **Not exceeding 20%** of the approved absolute height above ground or storeys above ground, **excluding refuge floors** |
| Same, for a building **not exceeding 30 m** above ground or **10 storeys** above ground, and the plan has **no** building height restriction | B | **Not exceeding 30%** of the approved absolute height above ground or storeys above ground. This item does not repeat the refuge-floor exclusion |

“Building height (above ground)” excludes basement floors. If the extant plan requires planning permission for additional basement floors, Class A and Class B of this category do not apply.

| Category 6 — site coverage | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Reduction | A | Any reduction |
| Increase | A | Not exceeding the site coverage restriction on the extant plan; or, if the plan has **no** site coverage restriction, **not exceeding 10%** of the approved site coverage |
| Increase from Joint Practice Note green features; from additional site coverage permitted by the Building Authority under Regulations 20, 22(1) or (2) of the Building (Planning) Regulations; or from a smaller site under Category 1 Class A item (a) | A | Only if the plan has no site coverage restriction, or the change does not take the scheme above the plan’s site coverage restriction |
| Other increases | B | **Not exceeding 20%** of the approved site coverage, and only if the plan has **no** site coverage restriction |

**SD takeaway:** Where the extant plan already restricts building height or site coverage, the **10% / 20% / 30%** uplifts in Categories 5 and 6 are not available.

### 4. Type and mix of uses, and internal layout (Categories 7–8)

Other than public utilities and Government, institution or community uses, an approved scheme or Master Layout Plan is grouped into four categories: (i) residential; (ii) hotel; (iii) office; (iv) other commercial uses, including retail, eating places, recreation, entertainment, sports, culture, kindergarten, child care centre and public car park. Location changes for Government, institution or community facilities are Category 9. Class A and Class B mix changes must not contravene gross floor area or plot ratio restrictions on the extant plan.

| Category 7 — type and mix | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Change of type or mix inside the same one of the four categories | A | No area cap printed |
| Relocation of approved non-domestic uses, excluding Government, institution or community uses, inside the non-domestic part | A | No area cap printed |
| Gross floor area moved from one non-domestic category to another, both already in the approved scheme, and agreed by the relevant department | A | No numeric cap printed on this item |
| The same category-to-category move, both categories already in the approved scheme | A | **Not exceeding 2,000 m² or 10%** of the approved non-domestic gross floor area, **whichever is the less** |
| Gross floor area moved between domestic and non-domestic, both already in the approved scheme | A | **Not exceeding 5%** of the approved domestic or non-domestic gross floor area |
| Category-to-category non-domestic move, both categories already in the approved scheme | B | Changes that **exceed 2,000 m² or 10%** but **do not exceed 4,000 m² or 20%** of the approved non-domestic gross floor area, **whichever is the less** |
| Domestic to non-domestic, or the reverse, both already in the approved scheme | B | **Not exceeding 10%** of the approved domestic or non-domestic gross floor area |
| Non-domestic gross floor area moved from Government, institution or community use to another non-domestic category in the remarks | B | **Not exceeding 2,000 m² or 10%** of the approved non-domestic gross floor area, **whichever is the less**, agreed by the department, and not exceeding any gross floor area or plot ratio restriction on the extant plan |

| Category 8 — internal layout and disposition of premises | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Change of internal layout or disposition | A | The affected part is not the subject of an environmental mitigation measure, or the department agrees the related change. That part may be an environmental buffer or subject to environmental nuisance |
| Class B | — | **Not applicable** |

**SD takeaway:** A use category that is not already in the approved scheme is outside the Category 7 bands; the domestic or non-domestic shift that stays Class A is **5%**, and **10%** is the Class B ceiling.

### 5. Government, institution or community facilities, open space and recreation (Categories 9–11 and 15)

| Category 9 — Government, institution or community facilities | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Addition initiated by the relevant department | A | No floor-area cap printed |
| Change in type, location and/or floor area | B | No floor-area cap printed |
| Deletion initiated by the relevant department | B | Must still be submitted to the Board |
| Addition not initiated by the relevant department | B | No floor-area cap printed |
| Deletion not initiated by the relevant department | Outside Class A and Class B | Fresh section 16 application |

| Category 10 — public open space (including open space in a private development for public use) | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Increase in total area | A | No cap printed |
| Change of location, where location is not an approval condition | A | **Not more than 5%** of the public open space is on a different street, level or floor, and it is not subject to an environmental mitigation measure, or the department agrees |
| Active to passive, or the reverse | A | No area cap printed |
| Reduction in total area | B | Must not fall below the minimum provision on the extant plan, if the plan states one |

| Category 11 — private open space | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Increase in total area | A | No cap printed |
| Reduction | A | The result is **not less than** the minimum standard in the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines |
| Change of location | A | Not the subject of an environmental mitigation measure, or the department agrees |
| Other changes of area or location | B | No further percentage printed |
| Open space in a private development for public use | — | Category 10, not Category 11 |

| Category 15 — recreational facilities | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Change in private recreational facilities | A | No floor-area cap printed |
| Change in public recreational facilities, including location, layout, type and floor area, as agreed by the department | A | No floor-area cap printed |
| Class B | — | **Not applicable** |

**SD takeaway:** Do not delete a Government, institution or community facility unless the department initiated the deletion, and even then the deletion goes to the Board; a public open space location change stays Class A only if **no more than 5%** of it moves to another street, level or floor.

### 6. Parking and transport, non-building areas, trees, utilities and phasing (Categories 12–14, 16 and 17)

| Category 12 — car parking, loading and unloading, and other transport facilities | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Change in number or type of parking or loading and unloading spaces | A | Provision is **not less than** the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines, or the department agrees. This schedule prints no parking ratio of its own |
| Layout of internal roads, car park, loading and unloading, and emergency vehicular access | A | As agreed by the department |
| Number and/or location of ingress or egress, and location of footbridges or subways (including connection points), public passageway, public transport terminus, car park, loading and unloading, and lay-bys | A | As agreed by the department |
| The same parking, ingress or egress, or transport-facility changes where the Class A proviso is not met | B | No percentage printed |

| Category 13 — non-building area, setback and building gap | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Increase in size | A | No percentage printed |
| Change in location and/or size initiated by the department | A | No percentage printed |
| Change in location and/or **reduction** in size not initiated by the department | B | No percentage printed |

These are areas designated for a purpose, including road widening and mitigation of environmental, air ventilation, visual and/or landscape impact. Class A and Class B changes must not conflict with the related restriction on the extant plan.

| Category 14 — tree preservation and landscape master plan | Class | Limit as printed |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve and/or plant more trees | A | No cap printed |
| Soft or hard landscape design, or the implementation programme, as agreed by the department | A | No cap printed |
| Remove trees of an undesirable species, trees with a health or structural problem, dead trees, and trees in imminent danger | A | No percentage printed |
| Change in individual trees identified for preservation | B | Preservation here does not include transplanting |
| Increase in trees to be felled, other than the Class A removals above | B | **Not exceeding 10%** of the approved level |
| Decrease in the number of preserved trees | B | **Not exceeding 10%** of the approved level |

Class A and Class B tree changes must not affect an Old and Valuable Tree on the Register of Old and Valuable Trees kept by the Development Bureau. Preservation under Class A, and under Class B items (a) and (c), does not include transplanting.

| Category 16 — ancillary utility installation | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Change of location inside the building or development, as agreed by the department | A | Examples printed: refuse collection point, sewage treatment facilities, electricity substation, liquefied petroleum gas compound, utility pipelines |
| Deletion, as agreed by the department | A | Same examples |
| Other location changes | B | No percentage printed |

| Category 17 — phasing and implementation | Class | What is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Change with no Government, institution or community facility or public open space involved | A | No time shift printed |
| Change that does involve those, as agreed by the department | A | No time shift printed |
| Other minor change that affects provision of those facilities or that open space | B | “Minor” is not given a percentage |

**SD takeaway:** A smaller designated non-building area, setback or building gap is Class B unless the department initiated it, and felling or loss of preserved trees beyond **10%** of the approved level is outside Class B.

### 7. Extension of time (Categories 18 and 19, and s. 6)

| Category | Class A | Class B |
|---|---|---|
| 18 — extension of time for commencement | **Not applicable** | The period of extension, or the **aggregate of all periods of extension, not exceeding the original duration** for commencement of the approved proposal |
| 19 — extension of time for compliance with approval conditions | **Not applicable** | Extension of time for compliance. No length is printed in this category |

Unless the section 16A application itself includes the extension, the commencement date and the condition-compliance dates on the original permission stay as approved. The schedule points to the Board’s separate guidelines on commencement extensions, and on renewal and extension of time for a temporary use, for the detailed tests.

**SD takeaway:** Commencement can be extended only as a Class B amendment, and the extension, or all extensions added together, must not be longer than the original commencement period.
