# Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Index (English)

**Issued by:** Planning Department
**Scope:** Chapters 1 to 12 (12 chapters in this library). This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The chapter number links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF link in the same cell opens `source_reference/Chapter N (English).pdf`.
**Titles:** The official title is the chapter subject in the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Chapter 1 states that the standards and guidelines are neither statutory nor rigid. Before a submission, check the current Planning Department text, the outline zoning plan, and the lease.

| [Chapter 9](<./summaries/Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 9 Environment (English)_CS.md>) · [PDF](<./source_reference/Chapter 9 (English).pdf>) | Environment | March 2014 Edition | Gives planning guidance on air, noise, water and waste for public and private development, permanent or temporary. |
| [Chapter 10](<./summaries/Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 10 Conservation (English)_CS.md>) · [PDF](<./source_reference/Chapter 10 (English).pdf>) | Conservation | March 2020 Edition | Explains conservation from the planning perspective, covering nature conservation and biodiversity and the conservation of declared monuments, historic buildings, sites of archaeological interest and other heritage items, including zoning on town plans. |
| [Chapter 11](<./summaries/Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 11 Urban Design Guidelines (English)_CS.md>) · [PDF](<./source_reference/Chapter 11 (English).pdf>) | Urban Design Guidelines | December 2025 Edition (restructured and reformatted from the November 2015 edition) | Leads a thinking process for responsive urban design as a best practice guide, not as regulatory requirements or a one-size-fits-all code, and leaves the project team to decide which guidelines are relevant. |
| [Chapter 12](<./summaries/Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 12 Miscellaneous Planning Standards and Guidelines (English)_CS.md>) · [PDF](<./source_reference/Chapter 12 (English).pdf>) | Miscellaneous Planning Standards and Guidelines | December 2025 Edition (Section 2 updated to align with the cavern development strategy) | Gives planning standards and guidelines for rock cavern development, petrol filling stations, potentially hazardous installations, vehicle repair workshops, port back-up and open storage, and the use of land beneath flyovers and footbridges, which do not fall within the other chapters. |
