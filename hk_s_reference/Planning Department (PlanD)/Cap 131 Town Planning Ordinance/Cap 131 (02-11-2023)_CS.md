# Cap 131 — Town Planning Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 2 November 2023 | Cap. 131

> Scope note: Cap 131 is the **plan-making, planning-permission, and planning-enforcement statute**. It creates layout / OZP / DPA plans, makes **Notes, diagrams and Schedules part of the plan**, runs **s.16 / s.12A / s.16A / s.17 / s.17B**, gates Cap 123 via BO s.16(1)(d)/(da), and criminalises unauthorised development in **DPAs** and **regulated areas**. It does **not** itself print SC/PR/BHR tables — those live in the **plan Notes**. At SD: freeze **latest plan + Notes** → classify use → CDA/MLP if needed → pick s.16 vs s.12A vs redesign → only then mass for BD / lease.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 131 applies wherever the Chief Executive directs the Town Planning Board (TPB) to prepare **draft layout / Outline Zoning Plans** (s.3(1)(a)) or **Development Permission Area plans** (s.3(1)(b)). It covers the full chain from draft exhibition and representations, through CE in Council approval (including **partly approved** plans), to planning applications, reviews, appeals, and Director of Planning enforcement.

For schematic design it is the statute that decides whether the proposed **use, intensity envelope and building works** are always permitted under the plan, need **s.16** permission, need **s.12A** rezoning, or are blocked until a **CDA master lay-out plan (MLP)** is approved — and whether BD will refuse Cap 123 plans for Cap 131 conflict.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Core definitions — SD scope traps (s.1A)

| Term | Schematics implication |
|---|---|
| **development** | Building, engineering, mining or other operations **in, on, over or under** land, **or** a **material change** in the use of land or buildings. Changing use without “building” can still be development |
| **material change in the use of land or buildings** | Includes **depositing matter** on land — even if already used for deposit — if **area, height or amount** of the deposit increases. Critical for open storage / filling / brownfield |
| **building** | Includes a **structure or part of a structure** (wider than ordinary language) |
| **container** | Includes containers converted for accommodation, storage or any other use — treat conversion as potential development |
| **Authority** | Director of Planning (enforcement lead) |
| **Board** | Town Planning Board |
| **land owner** | Same meaning as *owner* in Cap 123 s.2(1) |
| **occupier** | Includes tenant (rent or not), person residing in a building, person carrying on full-time occupation in a building |
| **development permission area (DPA)** | Area designated on a plan under ss.3(1)(b) and 20 — **not** land that is only on an interim DPA plan |
| **existing use** (DPA) | Use of building or land in existence **immediately before** Gazette of the draft DPA plan |
| **interim development permission area (IDPA)** | Area on a plan under s.26 (legacy — no new IDPAs after 1991 amending Ordinance) |
| **unauthorized development** | On DPA land, s.20(7) land, or a **regulated area**: development in contravention of Cap 131. In ss.22–23 (IDPA context): development other than IDPA-permitted development undertaken on/after IDPA Gazette |
| **regulated area** | Area designated / modified under s.21A for environmental / nature-conservation protection (NT OZP land; not HK Island / Kowloon / New Kowloon; not DPA / s.20(7) land) |
| **material date** | **9 December 2022** |
| **pre-material-date use** | Use of building or land in existence **immediately before** 9 Dec 2022 (regulated-area baseline) |
| **approved plan** | Whole plan approved under s.9, **or** all parts are approved parts |
| **partly approved plan** | Has at least one **approved part** but is not yet an approved plan |
| **approved part / outstanding part** | 2023 machinery: CEIC can approve part of a plan while the rest remains outstanding; refused parts drop out of the plan from refusal date (s.1A(6)) |
| **property** (enforcement) | Includes contents of a vehicle or container; excludes immovable property |

**SD rule:** If you change use, intensify open storage, convert containers, excavate, or build under/over land — ask “is this *development*?” before assuming Column 1 saves you.

### 2. TPB machinery you design around (ss.2–2C, 3)

#### 2.1 Who decides what

| Body | Quorum / composition | What architects meet |
|---|---|---|
| **TPB (s.2)** | Quorum **5**, including chairman or vice-chairman | Plan-making, s.16 / s.12A / s.16A / s.17 (unless delegated) |
| **CE-appointed committees (s.2(3))** | Quorum **5**: chair/vice-chair + **≥3 unofficial** members | May receive delegated powers under ss.3, 4(1), 4A, 5, 7(1)–(3), 8, 12A, 16, 16A, 20(1) |
| **Board-appointed committees (s.2A)** | ≥5 members, ≥3 non-public-officers; majority present must be non-public-officers | Representation / further-representation work under ss.6B–6H |
| **Delegation to public officers (s.2(5)(b))** | Limited | **s.16A(2)** Class B applications; and short-life DPA permissions (discontinue + reinstate within **6 months**) |
| **Secretary to the Board** | Administrative | Further-info acceptance windows under ss.6B(4A)(b), 12A(13)/(15), 16(2J)/(2L), 17(2H)/(2J) |

#### 2.2 Meetings — open vs closed (s.2C)

- Default: Board / committee meetings **open to the public**.
- **Closed for deliberation** after hearing persons entitled to be heard, when deciding under ss.6B(8), 6F(8)/(9), 12A(23), 16(3), 16A(5), 17(6).
- Other closed-meeting grounds: public interest, premature release prejudicing Board/Government/CE/CEIC, confidentiality, LPP, legal proceedings.
- Board may fix speaking-time limits for s.6 representers at s.6B hearings.

#### 2.3 Papers circulation (s.2B)

Business may be done by circulating papers (including electronic) unless a meeting is required by the Ordinance. Any member can force a meeting by written notice within the period in the papers — then written resolutions already approved become void.

#### 2.4 Board’s plan-making duty (s.3)

TPB must systematically prepare:

1. **Draft layout plans** for areas CE directs — including **types of building** suitable for erection; and  
2. **Draft DPA plans** for areas CE directs.

Board may take inquiries / arrangements (including occupancy / thoroughfare censuses) while preparing drafts.

### 3. What an OZP / layout plan can control (s.4) — the real design envelope

Draft layout plans may show or provide for:

| s.4(1) item | Typical SD read (detail is in Notes) |
|---|---|
| (a) Streets, railways, main communications | Road reserves, setbacks, building lines, rail protection |
| (b) Residential / commercial / industrial / other specified uses | Column 1 / Column 2 / prohibited uses |
| (c) G/IC reserves | Do not build into G/IC without plan / permission path |
| (d) Parks, recreation, open space | Open-space zoning ≠ private recreational GFA free-for-all |
| (e) Undetermined | Usually needs plan amendment or special permission logic — high risk |
| (f) **Comprehensive development areas** | MLP track — see §4 |
| (g) Country parks, CPA, SSSI, green belts, conservation / environment uses | Strict conservation / recreation bias |
| (h) Village-type development, agriculture, rural uses | V-zone / AGR constraints; NTEH still need Cap 121 / lease overlay |
| (i) Open storage | Often Column 2 / temporary permission culture |

**Critical statutory rule (s.4 closing words):** diagrams, illustrations, **Notes** and descriptive matter the Board thinks appropriate **are part of the plans**.  

→ Plot ratio, site coverage, building height restriction (BHR), non-building areas, building gaps, parking remarks, “always permitted” lists, and Column 2 lists are **Cap 131 plan law** via the Notes — not optional guidance.

**Resumption (s.4(2)):** Board may recommend Cap 124 resumption of land that interferes with a draft / approved / partly approved plan **or** an approved MLP.  

**No zoning compensation (s.4(3)):** Being zoned under s.4(1)(b) alone generally attracts **no** compensation.

### 4. Comprehensive Development Areas — hard stop before massing (ss.4A, 19)

| Rule | Practical reading |
|---|---|
| **CDA note may prohibit Cap 123 “building works”** | Except works specified in the note, **or** works with TPB permission |
| **Permission may be by reference to an MLP** | TPB may require a **master lay-out plan** with building dimensions, floor area **for each use**, development programme, and any other matter TPB requires |
| **Approved MLP is public** | Chairman-certified copy deposited in Land Registry — free inspection |
| **s.19 validation** | Historic “(OU) Comprehensive Development Area” / “(OU) Comprehensive Redevelopment Area” labels and related TPB/BA decisions treated as valid |

**SD implication:** Inside CDA, freeze **MLP geometry and use mix** before BD concept. Piece-meal Cap 123 submissions that fight the MLP invite BO refusal under Cap 123 s.16(1)(da). Budget planning application + MLP + assessments before locking towers, basement, podium, or phasing.

### 5. Plan-making pipeline — when the constraint sheet can still move (ss.5–11)

Architects need this for **programme risk** when a site sits under a draft or amendment draft.

#### 5.1 Exhibition (s.5)

- Draft suitable for publication must be exhibited for public inspection for **2 months**.
- During that period: advertise **weekly** in **2 Chinese + 1 English** daily newspapers; notify in **each Gazette** issue; publish inspection info on **TPB website**.
- Copies available on payment of Board-determined fee.

#### 5.2 Representations (s.6)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | **Any person** |
| Window | Only within the **2-month** exhibition |
| Late | Treated as **not having been made** |
| Content | Must state the particular matter, nature & reasons, and any proposed amendments; manner as Board requires |
| Compensation / resumption-assistance reasons | Board may treat representation (to that extent) as not made (s.6(3A)–(3B)) — do not rely on “pay me” arguments as planning representations |
| Inspection | Representations available for public inspection until CEIC decides under s.9 |

#### 5.3 Hearing & proposed amendments (ss.6B–6H) — 2023 streamlined path

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **s.6B** | ASAP after the 2-month period, Board holds meeting to consider representation; representer entitled to attend and be heard (natural person may authorise another in limited cases) |
| **s.6B(8)** | Board may propose amendments to the plan after considering representations |
| **s.6C** | Proposed amendments made available for public inspection |
| **s.6D** | **Further representations** may be made on those proposed amendments |
| **s.6E** | Withdrawal of s.6 representations |
| **s.6F** | Board considers further representations; may amend / decide accordingly |
| **s.6G** | If no further representation on a proposed amendment — Board proceeds on that basis |
| **s.6H** | Amendments made under s.6F or 6G take effect as part of the draft plan machinery |

**SD tip:** If your site is under amendment exhibition, monitor both **s.6 representations** and **s.6D further representations** — either can rewrite Notes that your massing assumes.

#### 5.4 Board’s own draft amendments (s.7)

Board may amend a draft plan under s.7 (subject to the Ordinance’s exhibition / consideration sequence as applied). Do not treat a “frozen” draft sheet as permanent while Board amendment powers remain live.

#### 5.5 Submission to CE in Council (s.8) and CEIC powers (s.9)

After consideration, Board submits the draft (or relevant parts) to **Chief Executive in Council**. CEIC may (as provided in s.9):

- approve the plan **in whole**;
- approve **part** only (**partly approved plan** machinery — 2023);
- refuse approval of the plan or part;
- refer back / require further action as the section provides.

Related machinery:

| Section | SD relevance |
|---|---|
| **s.9A** | Amendment of draft / submitted part after certain s.9(1)(c)/(2)(c) outcomes |
| **s.9B** | Submission for approval of **outstanding part** |
| **s.9C** | Correction of errors in approved / partly approved plans |
| **s.9D** | Exhibition of approved / partly approved plans + copies |
| **s.10** | Refusal to approve under s.9 — refused plan/part treated as never prepared / not part of plan (s.1A(6)) |
| **s.11** | Deposit of approved / partly approved plans in **Land Registry**; public notice of deposit |

#### 5.6 Draft amendments bind BD immediately (s.12(5))

Any **draft plan showing amendment** prepared under ss.3–4 (including amendments to approved plans referred under s.12) is taken to be a draft plan for Cap 123 **s.16(1)(d)** purposes.  

→ BD conflict testing includes **live amendment drafts**, not only the last CE-approved sheet.

#### 5.7 Approved / approved parts as standards (s.13)

Approved plans and approved parts of partly approved plans **must be used by all public officers and bodies as standards for guidance** in exercising their powers. PlanD, LandsD and BD policy posture starts here.

#### 5.8 Roads / Railways deemed approved (s.13A)

Works/use authorised under Cap **370**, and schemes authorised under Cap **519**, are **deemed approved under Cap 131** even if not shown on the s.9 plan — coordinate with gazetted road/rail schemes early.

### 6. Changing an approved plan (s.12) vs applying to change it (s.12A)

#### 6.1 Government-led revocation / replacement / amendment (s.12)

| Power | Effect |
|---|---|
| **CEIC revoke** (s.12(1)) | Whole approved plan, or approval of a part of a partly approved plan |
| **S for Dev refer** (s.12(1A)) | Refer approved plan for **replacement** or **amendment**; or refer partly approved plan to amend an approved part |
| **Process** | New / amendment plan goes through exhibition → representations → CEIC as if a fresh draft (ss.3–11 applied with modifications) |
| **Partial refusal salvage (ss.12(3B)–(3H))** | Complex 2023 rules preserving approved parts when amendment parts are refused — always read the **latest approved part** with the original |
| **s.12(5)** | Amendment draft counts as Cap 123 conflict draft |

#### 6.2 Applicant-led plan amendment (s.12A) — rezoning track

Use when the Notes do **not** allow the scheme even via s.16.

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Eligible person** | (a) LR-registered owner of **non-Government** land in the specified part; (b) person with that owner’s written consent; (c) person with **D of Lands** written consent for **Government land**; (d) public officer; (e) Cap 201 public body |
| **Government land** | Unleased Cap 28 land, or STT under Cap 150 |
| **Owner consent / notify** | Same “current land owner” consent / written notify / Board-required reasonable steps gate as s.16 — Board may refuse to consider |
| **Conflict with live amendment** | If original approved plan is a referred approved plan and a relevant draft already covers the area, proposal must not relate to matters covered by that draft amendment |
| **Further info** | Only in Board-gazetted **specified periods**; acceptance can restart the receipt clock unless exempted |
| **Decision clock** | Meeting within **2 months** of receipt; applicant entitled to attend and be heard |
| **Outcomes** | Accept in whole/part, or refuse |
| **If accepted** | Does **not** instantly rezone — Board must trigger s.12 referral / draft amendment / s.7 amendment path. Scheme waits on **plan-making**, not a permission letter |

**Programme:** Treat s.12A as **months-to-years**, not a 2-month permit.

### 7. s.16 planning permission — Column 2 / “permission may be granted”

Applies only where the draft / partly approved / approved plan **provides for the grant of permission** for a purpose (classic Column 2 / remark permission). Not a general variance beyond the plan.

#### 7.1 Filing gates (s.16(1)–(2B))

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Form | Written to secretary to the Board; Board-required particulars |
| Fee | Prescribed fee (if any) under s.14(2) |
| Owner trail | Within reasonable period before filing: written consent of each **current land owner**, **or** written notification, **or** Board-required reasonable steps — with particulars set out in the application |
| Verification | Board may require verification by statutory declaration or otherwise |
| Refuse to consider | Non-compliant form/fee/owner trail → Board may refuse to consider (even before merits) |

**current land owner:** person registered in LR as owner at the start of the Board-gazetted look-back period before application.

#### 7.2 Publicity & comments (s.16(2C)–(2I))

| Step | Clock |
|---|---|
| Public inspection of application | From filing until Board meeting under s.16(3) |
| Notice | Site notice on/near land **or** newspaper (2 Chinese + 1 English weekly for first 3 weeks) **and** website for first 3 weeks |
| Public comments | Only within **first 3 weeks** of inspection period — late = not made |
| Comments published | After the 3 weeks, until Board meeting |

#### 7.3 Further information (s.16(2J)–(2L), (7A)–(7B))

- Accepted only if given in a Board-gazetted **specified period** and does not materially change the nature of the application.
- Unless exempted, acceptance → re-inspection / re-notification and **restarts** the “receipt” clock for the 2-month decision period.

#### 7.4 Decision (s.16(3)–(7))

| Rule | Implication |
|---|---|
| **2-month** consideration at a meeting | Programme milestone |
| Must take public comments into account | Design / assessment package must anticipate objections |
| **s.16(4)** — permission only to the extent shown/provided/specified in the plan | Cannot s.16 past a hard Notes prohibition that is not permission-enabled |
| **s.16(5)** — conditions as Board thinks fit | Time limits, GFA mix, design, landscaping, traffic, drainage, phasing — bake into brief |
| Written notification + s.17 review rights if refused | Start review clock immediately |
| **s.16(7) Cap 123 bridge** | Anything permitted under Cap 131 s.16 is **not** a contravention of approved / partly approved / draft plans for Cap 123 **s.16(1)(d) and (da)** |

### 8. Amending a s.16 permission — Class A / Class B (s.16A)

| Class | Effect | SD discipline |
|---|---|---|
| **Class A** | Classes/descriptions gazetted by Board under s.16A(10)(a). Permission may be read as including those amendments **without** fresh application | Keep optioneering inside Class A where possible |
| **Class B** | Gazetted under s.16A(10)(b). Permission-holder applies; Board decides within **2 months**; may accept with conditions | Tower shifts, GFA mix changes, condition-sensitive moves often leave Class A |
| **Stacking rule (s.16A(7)–(9))** | Accepted Class B amendments can be read into the permission; when construing the base s.16 permission for this section, disregard amendments taking effect under s.16A | Track the **operative** permission set carefully for BD / lease drawings |

Refusal of Class B → s.17 review rights.

### 9. Review and appeal (ss.17, 17A–17C)

#### 9.1 Board review (s.17)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Applicant aggrieved by s.16 or s.16A decision |
| Deadline | **21 days** from notification |
| Form | Writing + **grounds** — no grounds → Board **must refuse** the review application (s.17(1B)) |
| Hearing day | Not more than **3 months** from receipt; **14 days’** notice to applicant |
| Public process (s.16 reviews) | Inspection + 3-week comments (similar to s.16 publicity) |
| Further info | Specified-period rules; can reset clocks |
| Rights at hearing | Applicant / authorised representative may attend and make representations |
| Outcome (s.17(6)) | Confirm, reverse, or substitute any decision Board could have made under s.16 / s.16A |

#### 9.2 Appeal Board (ss.17A–17B)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who sits | CE-appointed panel; **cannot** include TPB members, public officers (with judicial carve-outs), or Justices of Appeal |
| Constitution | Chairman/Deputy + 4 others; ≥3 present including Chairman to hear/determine |
| Appeal deadline | **60 days** after notification of s.17(6) decision |
| Powers | Confirm / reverse / vary; award costs; summons witnesses; admit evidence whether or not court-admissible |
| Finality | Appeal Board decision is **final** (s.17B(9)) |
| Offences (s.17C) | Fail to appear on summons / produce documents / be sworn / comply with orders → fine at **level 5** |

### 10. Development Permission Areas (ss.20–21)

| Rule | Practical reading |
|---|---|
| **Designation** | On draft plan under s.3(1)(b) as CE directs |
| **Cannot designate** | Land already/previously included in a Cap 131 plan, **except** IDPA plans under s.26 |
| **Contents** | May include s.4(1) matters; ss.4(3), 4A–13A, 16, 17 apply as for layout plans |
| **Life** | **3 years** from first s.5 Gazette; CEIC may extend once by up to **1 year** (Gazette before expiry) |
| **While DPA effective (s.21)** | No development unless: **existing use**, **or** permitted under **latest** DPA plan, **or** **s.16** permission |
| **After land absorbed into OZP (s.20(7))** | Ban continues unless: existing use relative to DPA plan, **or** permitted under **latest** s.3(1)(a) plan, **or** s.16 permission (before or after inclusion) |
| **Pending apps (s.20(6A))** | s.16 / s.17 / s.17B rights survive DPA expiry for applications filed during the effective period — decided against the DPA plan’s extent |
| **Penalty (ss.20(8), 21(2))** | First conviction **$500,000**; subsequent **$1,000,000** (subject to Cap 630 private columbaria where applicable) |

**SD for NT / ex-rural lots:** Prove “existing use” with dated evidence (aerials, photos, occupation records). Cap **24A**: LandsD aerial-photo documents are prima facie evidence in proceedings.

### 11. Regulated areas — 2023 enforcement layer (ss.21A–21F)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Applies to** | Land already on a Cap 131 plan |
| **Does not apply to** | HK Island / Kowloon / New Kowloon; DPA land; s.20(7) land |
| **Who designates** | Secretary for Development by Gazette — for environmental degradation protection and/or nature conservation |
| **Plan** | Regulated area plan prepared & signed under s.21B; Land Registry deposit; free inspection |
| **Effect dates** | Designation / modification / revocation take effect on **Gazette publication** date |
| **Baseline use** | **pre-material-date use** = use immediately before **9 Dec 2022** |
| **Offence (s.21F)** | While designation effective: no development unless pre-material-date use, **or** permitted under latest s.3(1)(a) plan, **or** s.16 permission |
| **Penalties** | Same $500k / $1M band as DPA |

**SD:** On NT OZP land, check regulated-area Gazette + plan even if Column 1 looks clear — new operations after the material date / designation can still be unauthorised development.

### 12. Enforcement — design and title risk (ss.22–25, 24A)

#### 12.1 Inspection & information (s.22)

- Authority may enter land/premises (and access land) at reasonable time to ascertain unauthorised development, post notices, verify discontinuance/reinstatement — with **reasonable suspicion** threshold for ascertainment entries.
- Domestic premises: occupier consent **or** magistrate’s warrant.
- May require identity details and assistance from persons present.
- Written notice requiring **relevant information**; failure / false / reckless / material omission → fine at **level 6**.

#### 12.2 Notices (s.23)

| Notice type | What it can require |
|---|---|
| **Discontinuance (s.23(1))** | Stop the “relevant matters” by a specified date — served on land owner, occupier, and/or person responsible |
| **Accelerated + steps (s.23(2))** | If continuance could be health/safety hazard, adversely affect environment, or make timely reinstatement impracticable/uneconomic — earlier date + mandatory steps |
| **Reinstatement (s.23(3))** | Date **≥ 30 days** after service. DPA / s.20(7) land → condition immediately before DPA effective (or more favourable). Regulated area → condition immediately before **material date** (or more favourable) |
| **IDPA special baseline (s.23(4))** | Certain IDPA→DPA sequences reinstate to pre-IDPA Gazette condition |
| **Compliance / post-compliance notices** | Further notices when Authority satisfied; Land Registry registration |
| **Self-help (s.23(7)–(8))** | Authority may enter, discontinue, reinstate, take possession/remove/detain/dispose of property; recover expenses as civil debt |
| **Non-compliance offence (s.23(6))** | First: $500,000 + **$50,000/day**; subsequent: $1,000,000 + **$100,000/day** |
| **LR registration (s.23(4B), (8B))** | Notices registrable as instruments affecting land — **title due diligence must scan for planning enforcement** |

#### 12.3 Defences (s.23(9)) — what you must be able to prove

Defendant may prove, among other things:

- took all reasonable steps to comply;
- the alleged UD was **not** in fact a development;
- it was an **existing use** / pre-IDPA use / **pre-material-date use**;
- it is permitted under the **latest** relevant plan;
- **s.16** permission had been granted.

Prosecution need **not** prove that the Authority’s opinion was factually correct that a development / UD existed (s.23(9A)) — evidentiary burden is skewed; design and record-keeping matter.

#### 12.4 Review of reinstatement decisions (s.24)

- Within **30 days** of s.23(3) service → apply in writing to **Secretary for Development**.
- Parties not entitled to be present; paper review with chance to respond to Authority materials.
- Secretary may confirm / reverse / substitute.
- **Operation of the reinstatement decision is suspended** until review disposed.

#### 12.5 Evidence & delegation

- **s.24A:** LandsD aerial-photo image documents prima facie evidence.
- **s.25:** Authority may authorise public officers in writing.

### 15. Cap 131 ↔ Cap 123 ↔ lease — never conflate

| Instrument | Controls | Schematic use |
|---|---|---|
| **Cap 131 plan + Notes** | Use, planning intensity in Notes, CDA/MLP, permission pathways | First filter |
| **Cap 131 s.16 / s.12A / s.16A** | Discretionary permission / plan change / amendment classes | When Notes require TPB |
| **Cap 123 (+ 123F etc.)** | Building works approval, statutory SC/PR regimes, OP | Second filter — still applies with planning permission |
| **Lease / Conditions of Grant** | Contractual use, GFA, height, D&D | Third filter — Cap 131 does **not** waive lease |

Cap 131 s.16(7) cures Cap 123 **plan-contravention** grounds under s.16(1)(d)/(da) only. It does **not** waive BO construction standards, FSI, or lease breaches. Cap 123 approval/consent also does **not** confer planning permission.

**Also remember Cap 123 s.16(1)(d)/(da) kill switches:** conflict with Cap 131 draft / approved / partly approved plan, or with TPB-approved **MLP** in a CDA.

### 17. Pre-schematic checklist (Cap 131)

1. Confirm plan sheet(s): draft / amendment draft / partly approved / approved — download **Notes + ES + MLP** if any.
2. Classify every proposed use against Notes (always permitted / s.16 / prohibited / need s.12A).
3. Extract Notes controls: **PR / SC / BHR / NBA / setbacks / building gaps / parking / GIC floors / non-building areas**.
4. If **CDA** (or OU CDA/CRA) → start MLP + TPB path before BD concept freeze.
5. Screen **DPA / s.20(7) legacy** and **regulated area** (material date 9 Dec 2022).
6. Decide track: redesign to always-permitted / **s.16** / **s.12A** — and price the programme honestly.
7. Prepare **owner consent / notification** trail before any TPB filing.
8. Align Cap 123 submission narrative with Cap 131 s.16(7) / MLP so BO s.16(1)(d)/(da) cannot bite.
9. Parallel-check **lease** and Cap **123F** — Cap 131 alone never clears the project.
10. For brownfield / NT: evidence **existing use** / pre-material-date use; search LR for **s.23 notices**.
11. If site under amendment exhibition: freeze massing only after checking live draft Notes + representation risk.
12. Keep Class A / Class B amendment strategy if a s.16 permission will need post-approval flexibility.
