# Master Schedule of Notes Index (English)

**Issued by:** Planning Department, for Notes prepared for the Town Planning Board
**Scope:** 2 covering-notes sets and 29 zone schedules in this library. This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The code links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF keeps its download filename in `source_reference/`.
**Titles:** The official title is the zone heading printed on the PDF.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. These are master templates, not the Notes of any one Outline Zoning Plan. Before a scheme, read the adopted plan Notes, the Town Planning Ordinance (Cap. 131), and the lease. A use marked * on a master is added only where that plan includes it. Plot ratio and building height apply only where the master prints a figure or the adopted plan adds one.
