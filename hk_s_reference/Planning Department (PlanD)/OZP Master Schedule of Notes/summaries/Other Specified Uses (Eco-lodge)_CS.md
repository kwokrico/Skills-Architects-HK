## Regulatory Overview

Other Specified Uses annotated Eco-lodge is for low-rise, low-density resort accommodation. Nature reserve and nature trail are Column 1, and hotel and holiday camp are Column 2 inside a comprehensive scheme at plot ratio 0.2 and 1 storey.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended to provide sustainable-based tourism in the form of an eco-lodge for low-rise, low-density resort-type accommodation. The development shall not have an adverse impact on the local environment and shall adopt best practice, green architecture, and sustainable principles in design, construction, and operation. Uses in support of the resort, and active or passive recreation, may be permitted subject to planning permission.

**SD takeaway:** Do not draw a hotel as of right. The section 16 scheme is comprehensive, at plot ratio 0.2 and 1 storey.
