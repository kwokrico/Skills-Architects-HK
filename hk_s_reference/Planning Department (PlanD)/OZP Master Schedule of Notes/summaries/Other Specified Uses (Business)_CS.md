Cargo handling may be Column 1 in a newly planned area where infrastructure and traffic capacity permit.

## Regulatory Overview

Other Specified Uses annotated Business allows a mix of information technology and telecommunications, non-polluting industry, office, and other commercial uses in a new business building. An existing industrial or industrial-office building keeps a tighter Column 1 list.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for general business uses. A mix of information technology and telecommunications industries, non-polluting industrial, office, and other commercial uses are always permitted in new business buildings. Less fire-hazard-prone office use that would not involve direct provision of customer services or goods to the general public is always permitted in existing industrial or industrial-office buildings.

**SD takeaway:** A new business building can take office and non-polluting industry as Column 1. An existing industrial building cannot take customer-serving office without staying inside the Schedule II rule.

### 4. Existing industrial or industrial-office building

For a building without industrial undertakings involving offensive trades or the use or storage of dangerous goods, Office is always permitted.

In the purpose-designed non-industrial portion on the lower floors of an existing building, except basements and floors used wholly or mainly for car parking, loading or unloading, or plant, separated from industry above by a buffer floor and with no industry in that portion, these uses are always permitted: Commercial Bathhouse/Massage Establishment, Eating Place, Educational Institution, Exhibition or Convention Hall, Institutional Use (not elsewhere specified), Library, Off-course Betting Centre, Office, Place of Entertainment, Place of Recreation, Sports or Culture, Private Club, Public Clinic, Religious Institution, School (excluding kindergarten), Shop and Services, and Training Centre. Social Welfare Facility, excluding residential care, may be permitted there on application.

**SD takeaway:** Customer-serving uses in an old industrial building need the buffer floor. Office that does not serve customers is Column 1 where the building has no offensive trades or dangerous goods.
