## Regulatory Overview

Open Space is for outdoor public space. Park, playground, and sitting-out area are Column 1, and eating place, shop, and place of entertainment are Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for outdoor open-air public space for active and/or passive recreational uses serving local residents and the general public.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the zone open-air. A building for shop or eating place is an s.16 case.
