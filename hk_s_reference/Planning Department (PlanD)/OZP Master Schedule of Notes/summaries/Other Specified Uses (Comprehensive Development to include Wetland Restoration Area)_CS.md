## Regulatory Overview

This annotation is an incentive to restore degraded wetlands next to existing fish ponds through comprehensive residential and/or recreational development, and to phase out sporadic open storage and port back-up uses. Column 1 is agriculture and passive recreation, flat and hotel are Column 2, and the printed cap is plot ratio 0.4 and 6 storeys including carport.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended to provide an incentive for the restoration of degraded wetlands adjoining existing fish ponds through comprehensive residential and/or recreational development to include a wetland restoration area. It is also intended to phase out existing sporadic open storage and port back-up uses on degraded wetland. Any new building should be located farthest away from xxxxx/Deep Bay.

**SD takeaway:** A new flat is not Column 1. If it is pursued, it is a comprehensive scheme at plot ratio 0.4 and 6 storeys including carport, with the building farthest from Deep Bay.

### 4. Remarks printed on this master

Except as specified for uses annotated #, the application is a comprehensive development scheme including a wetland restoration proposal. The layout plan covers land uses and building positions and heights; a wetland restoration and/or creation scheme with buffer, long-term management, and monitoring; an environmental impact study including ecological and visual impact; landscape; traffic impact; drainage and sewerage impact; programming and phasing including the wetland scheme; and any other information the Town Planning Board requires. For a use annotated #, developed on its own and not as part of a comprehensive scheme, the wetland restoration proposal and the layout plan may be exempted. No new development or redevelopment, except to a New Territories Exempted House, shall exceed a maximum plot ratio of 0.4 and a maximum building height of 6 storeys including carport, or the plot ratio and height of the building existing on the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan, whichever is greater. The master says minor relaxation of the plot ratio and building height restrictions stated in paragraph (b) may be considered under section 16. Paragraph (b) on this master is the # exemption. The plot ratio and height are stated in paragraph (c). Car park, loading or unloading, plant room, caretaker’s office and quarters, and ancillary recreational facilities for all owners or occupiers of the domestic building or domestic part may be disregarded. Diversion of stream, filling, or excavation, including to effect a Column 1 or Column 2 use or a covering-notes use, needs section 16 permission, except public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government and maintenance, repair, or rebuilding works.

**SD takeaway:** Lock plot ratio 0.4 and 6 storeys including carport. A use marked # can be applied for without the wetland layout plan only if it is not part of the comprehensive scheme.
