## Regulatory Overview

Other Specified Uses annotated Industrial Estate reserves land for an estate whose industries are admitted by the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation. Industrial use and office are Column 1, and offensive trades and an oil depot are Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended to provide or reserve land for an industrial estate for industries admitted by the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation according to the Corporation’s criteria. Industries included would normally not be accommodated in conventional industrial buildings because of their specific requirements.

**SD takeaway:** The estate operator’s admission criteria sit in front of the Column 1 list. Do not treat the zoning as an open industrial permission outside that estate.
