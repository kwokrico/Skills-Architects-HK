## Regulatory Overview

This annotation allows low-density private residential or passive recreation in exchange for long-term conservation of the remaining fish ponds or wetland, under a private-public partnership and a no-net-loss in wetland principle. Column 1 is fish pond culture, nature reserve, and wetland habitat, and flat is Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended for conservation and enhancement of the ecological value and functions of the existing fish ponds or wetland through an application for development or redevelopment under the private-public partnership approach. Low-density private residential or passive recreational development in exchange for committed long-term conservation and management of the remaining fish ponds or wetland within the site may be permitted subject to the no-net-loss in wetland principle and planning permission. Any new building should be located farthest away from xxxxx/Deep Bay.

**SD takeaway:** Do not site the building on the pond edge. The application is a comprehensive scheme with a wetland conservation and enhancement scheme, and the intensity number is filled on the plan, not on this master.

### 4. Remarks printed on this master

The application, except as the Note otherwise provides, is a comprehensive development scheme with minimum pond filling and no decline in wetland function of the fish ponds within and near the site. The layout plan covers: (i) land uses, development parameters, and the nature, position, and heights of all buildings; (ii) a wetland conservation and enhancement scheme, including design, wetland buffer, long-term maintenance and management, and monitoring; (iii) an environmental impact study, including ecological and visual impact; (iv) landscape proposals; (v) a traffic impact study; (vi) a drainage and sewerage impact study; (vii) programming, phasing, and implementation, including the wetland scheme; (viii) any other information the Town Planning Board requires. No new development or redevelopment shall exceed a maximum gross floor area or plot ratio of (xxx). In that figure, car park, loading or unloading, plant room, caretaker’s office and quarters, and ancillary recreational facilities for all owners or occupiers of the domestic building or domestic part may be disregarded. Diversion of stream, filling of land or pond, or excavation, including to effect a Column 1 or Column 2 use or a covering-notes use, needs section 16 permission from the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan, except public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government and maintenance, repair, or rebuilding works.

**SD takeaway:** The intensity is a blank (xxx). Do not invent it. The scheme lives or dies on no-net-loss in wetland and minimum pond filling.
