# Country Park
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Master schedule template. No edition date is printed on this PDF. | Planning Department — Master Schedule of Notes to Statutory Plans

> Scope note: This master does not set Column 1 or Column 2. It states that Town Planning Board approval is not required. The PDF header is (Country Park.rtf). Control sits with the Country and Marine Parks Authority under the Country Parks Ordinance (Cap. 208).

## Regulatory Overview

The Country Park note is a single rule, not a use schedule. Consent of the Country and Marine Parks Authority is required for uses and developments, and the Town Planning Board does not approve them.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The only rule on this master

Country Park means a country park or special area as designated under the Country Parks Ordinance (Cap. 208). All uses and developments require consent from the Country and Marine Parks Authority and approval from the Town Planning Board is not required.

**SD takeaway:** Do not file a section 16 application for the use. Obtain Country and Marine Parks Authority consent, and design to that permit.
