## Regulatory Overview

Residential (Group B) is the medium-density residential schedule, and flat and house are always permitted. Commercial uses that serve the neighbourhood are Column 2, on an urban, New Town, or rural wording.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

For an urban area or New Town, the zone is primarily for medium-density residential developments where commercial uses serving the residential neighbourhood may be permitted on application. For a rural area, the wording is sub-urban medium-density residential development, with the same application route for neighbourhood commercial uses.

**SD takeaway:** Shop and eating place on the lowest floors need an s.16 application.
