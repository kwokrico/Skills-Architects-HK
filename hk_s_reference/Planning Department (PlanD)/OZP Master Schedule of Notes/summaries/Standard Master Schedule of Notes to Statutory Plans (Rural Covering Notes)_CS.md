# Standard Master Schedule of Notes to Statutory Plans (Rural Covering Notes)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Header on the PDF: Rural_CN_Eng_8-4-05 | For areas previously covered by DPA plans | Planning Department — Master Schedule of Notes to Statutory Plans

> Scope note: These are the covering Notes for an Outline Zoning Plan whose area **was** previously covered by a Development Permission Area plan. They are part of the plan. They are not the Notes of any one plan. Paragraphs and phrases marked * are added only where that plan needs them. Temporary use is not the five-year urban rule. It is two months as of right for listed events, and three years only with permission.

## Regulatory Overview

The rural covering Notes carry forward uses that existed immediately before the Development Permission Area plan, and uses effected under an earlier plan. Any other use needs to be always permitted or to have permission, and a use that the Notes permit must still meet other legislation, the Government lease, and other Government requirements.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Uses that continue from the Development Permission Area (paragraphs 3 to 6)

| Paragraph | Rule |
|---|---|
| (3) | No action to make a use conform if it existed immediately before the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan and has continued. A material change, or any other development, must be always permitted or must follow a permission. Minor alteration of an always-permitted use is excepted |
| (3) if the plan area has been extended | The same protection uses the first draft plan, including the interim or draft development permission area plan, that covered that land |
| (4) | A use or development permitted under an earlier draft or approved plan, including an interim or development permission area plan, and effected during that plan, is always permitted. A material change must still be always permitted or permitted on application |
| (5) | Except where (3) or (4) applies, a use inside both this plan and the interim or draft development permission area plan shall not be undertaken or continued after the first gazette of that area plan without permission, unless it is always permitted |
| (6) added where the plan was extended once onto land never in an IDPA or DPA | A use on that extended land shall not be undertaken or continued after the first gazette of the draft outline zoning plan without permission, unless always permitted. If the plan was extended more than once, the trigger is the date the land was first included in this plan or an earlier outline zoning plan. The Remarks of the zones are amended to match |

**SD takeaway:** A use that has continued since the Development Permission Area gazette can stay. A new use, or a material change, cannot.

### 2. Permissions lapse (paragraph 7)

When a use, material change of use, development, or redevelopment is carried out as always permitted or under a permission, all permissions for that site lapse, unless the Town Planning Board specifies otherwise.

**SD takeaway:** Redevelopment under an always-permitted use spends the earlier permissions.

### 3. Uses always permitted on any land (paragraph 9)

Always permitted anywhere, except where the use is in Column 2 of a zone, and except in Site of Special Scientific Interest, Conservation Area, Coastal Protection Area, or Other Specified Uses annotated Comprehensive Development and Wetland Enhancement Area under paragraph (10):

| Group | Uses |
|---|---|
| (a) | Maintenance, repair, or demolition of a building |
| (b) | Provision, maintenance, or repair of plant nursery, amenity planting, open space, rain shelter, refreshment kiosk, footpath, bus or public light bus stop or lay-by, cycle track, taxi rank, public utility pipeline, electricity mast, lamp pole, telephone booth, telecommunications radio base station, automatic teller machine, and shrine. Light rail is added where appropriate |
| (c) | Maintenance or repair of road, watercourse, nullah, sewer, and drain. Railway track is added where appropriate |
| (d) | Geotechnical works, local public works, road works, sewerage, drainage, environmental improvement, marine related facilities, waterworks excluding a service reservoir, and other public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government |
| (e) | Rebuilding of a New Territories Exempted House |
| (f) | Replacement of an existing domestic building by a New Territories Exempted House. The existing building is one that existed on the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan, or, if the plan area was extended, on the first gazette of a draft plan covering that building |
| (g) | Provision, maintenance, or repair of a grave of an indigenous New Territories villager or a locally based fisherman and his family members, for which Government permission has been obtained |

**SD takeaway:** Rebuilding a New Territories Exempted House, and replacing an existing domestic building with one, is as of right. A new road is not. Only maintenance or repair of a road is.

### 4. Conservation zones, including wetland enhancement (paragraph 10)

Added for Site of Special Scientific Interest, Conservation Area, Coastal Protection Area, and Other Specified Uses annotated Comprehensive Development and Wetland Enhancement Area.

| Always permitted | Needs permission |
|---|---|
| Maintenance or repair of plant nursery, amenity planting, sitting-out area, rain shelter, refreshment kiosk, road, watercourse, nullah, public utility pipeline, electricity mast, lamp pole, telephone booth, shrine, and grave | Provision of plant nursery, amenity planting other than by Government, sitting-out area, rain shelter, refreshment kiosk, footpath, public utility pipeline, electricity mast, lamp pole, telephone booth, and shrine |
| Geotechnical works, local public works, roads, sewerage, drainage, environmental improvement, marine related facilities, waterworks excluding a service reservoir, and other public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government | |
| Provision of amenity planting by Government | |

**SD takeaway:** On these zones, new planting other than Government amenity planting, and a new footpath, need permission.

### 5. Temporary use (paragraph 12)

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| (a) Not more than two months, no site formation (no filling or excavation), and only the listed structures | Always permitted, except in SSSI, Coastal Protection Area, Conservation Area, and OU (Comprehensive Development and Wetland Enhancement Area). Listed structures: carnivals, fairs, film shooting, festival celebrations, religious functions, or sports events |
| (b) Not more than three years, other than (a) | Needs permission. The Town Planning Board may permit up to three years even if the use is not in the plan, or may refuse. Temporary open storage and port back-up are prohibited in SSSI, Conservation Area, Coastal Protection Area, and OU (Comprehensive Development and Wetland Enhancement Area) |
| (c) More than three years | Permission in accordance with the plan |

**SD takeaway:** Do not use the urban five-year rule here. Two months, no fill, and only the listed events, are as of right. Three years needs permission. Open storage as a temporary use is prohibited on the conservation zones.

### 6. Road, Undetermined, ancillary uses, and definitions (paragraphs 8, 11, 13 to 15)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Road junctions, road alignment, railway tracks, zone boundaries | May be adjusted as detailed planning proceeds. Marked items are added where appropriate |
| Land shown as Road | Needs permission except paragraphs (9)(a) to (9)(d) and (9)(g), and, where added, road, toll plaza, on-street vehicle park, railway station, and railway track |
| Undetermined, where added | Needs permission except paragraphs (9) and (12)(a) |
| Ancillary | Operations incidental to, and uses directly related and ancillary to, a permitted use in the same zone are always permitted |
| Terms | Unless the Notes say otherwise, section 1A of the Town Planning Ordinance applies |
| Existing building | Physically existing, and in compliance with relevant legislation and the Government lease |
| New Territories Exempted House | A domestic building other than a guesthouse or hotel, or a building primarily for habitation other than a guesthouse or hotel, whose ground floor may be Shop and Services or Eating Place, exempted by a certificate of exemption under Part III of Cap. 121 |

**SD takeaway:** Land shown as Road still needs permission for a use that is not maintenance, demolition, a grave, or the short list of road and rail items the plan adds.
