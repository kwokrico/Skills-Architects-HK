## Regulatory Overview

The Sports and Recreation Club annotation has two always-permitted uses, place of recreation, sports or culture, and private club. Eating place, shop, and a vehicle park need permission, and the PDF prints no planning intention and no plot ratio.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
