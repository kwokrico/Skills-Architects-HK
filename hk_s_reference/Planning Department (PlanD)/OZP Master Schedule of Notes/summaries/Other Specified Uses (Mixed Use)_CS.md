## Regulatory Overview

Other Specified Uses annotated Mixed Use is for non-industrial mixed uses, stacked in a building or laid out across a site, under Schedule I for a non-residential portion, Schedule II for a residential portion, and Schedule III for an existing building before conversion. New development must physically separate residential and non-residential uses, and must not mix them on the same floor.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for mixed non-industrial land uses. Flexibility for development, redevelopment, or conversion to residential or other uses, or a combination of compatible commercial, residential, educational, cultural, recreational, and entertainment uses, either vertically in a building or horizontally over a site, is allowed to meet changing market needs. Physical segregation has to be provided between the non-residential and residential portions within a new or converted building so that non-residential uses do not cause nuisance. Some commercial uses are always permitted in an existing mixed-use building before its redevelopment or conversion.

**SD takeaway:** Decide which schedule the floor sits in before assigning Column 1. Do not mix flat and shop on the same floor.

Remarks (a) and (c), and the italic phrases, are added where appropriate. Remark (b) is added only when plot ratio restrictions are imposed.
