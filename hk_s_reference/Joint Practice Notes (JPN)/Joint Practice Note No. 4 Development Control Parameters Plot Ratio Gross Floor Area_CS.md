# Joint Practice Note No. 4 — Development Control Parameters: Plot Ratio / Gross Floor Area
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue November 2008 | Last revision March 2017 | This revision October 2021 (revamped) | Effective **18 October 2021** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GR/1-125/6/1/0 (III) · LandsD LD BP/7-4/3 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Aligns max PR/GFA stipulation, GFA accountability, and compliance checking across BD, LandsD, PlanD. Applies to new GBP / major revisions submitted on/after **18 Oct 2021**. Plans approved before “January 2011” version → refer to “November 2008” version (BO s.16(3)(d)). **Not** for Cap. 121 NT-exempt buildings. Read with LandsD LAO PN 4/2014A. Cross-check B(P)R First Schedule, APP-151, JPNs 1/2/8, LAO PN 3/2018.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 4 makes statutory-plan PR/GFA (for new/amended plans under the streamlined regime) the shared intensity reference, with BD as **sole verifier** of GFA computation under B(P)R 23(3)(a) for GBP purposes across all three regimes. PlanD and LandsD follow BD’s verified arithmetic.

At schematic design, build one GFA schedule from BD practice (Groups I–III), then test against OZP and lease — never invent a parallel GFA system for planning or Lands.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Why three regimes exist (and how they interact)

| Regime | Instrument | Intensity control |
|---|---|---|
| Building | B(P)R (Cap. 123F) First Schedule | Permissible PR by site class / height / use |
| Planning | OZP / DPAP Notes | Max PR/GFA as needed; works **with** B(P)R — more stringent of the two historically applied |
| Land | Modern leases | Max GFA clause reflecting statutory plan (or PlanD advice if no plan restriction) |

Historically each department checked GBP with its own site-area and GFA-accountability interpretations. JPN 4 streamlines that.

---

### 2. Stipulating maximum PR/GFA — streamlined rule

Applies to sites under **new** PR/GFA control on new statutory plans, or **new/amended** PR/GFA on amended plans gazetted on/after this JPN’s effective date.

| Situation | Controlling number |
|---|---|
| Statutory plan more stringent than First Schedule | **Statutory plan** is sole reference (save exceptions) |
| First Schedule more stringent than statutory plan | BA may, on application, favourably exercise **BO s.42** to allow PR up to planning maximum |
| Site-area interpretation differs (PlanD/LandsD vs BO) | BA prepared to s.42 modify toward planning max PR/GFA |
| LandsD | Continues to write lease max GFA from planning restriction |

#### Exceptions where streamlined “planning as sole reference” may not apply

| Exception | Note |
|---|---|
| Sites without site classification under B(P)R **reg. 19(3)** | Special handling |
| Sites carved out from parent site whose development intensity already used up under building regime | Special handling |

#### What does **not** get streamlined PR stipulation

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Buildings already with OP | Streamlined para. 4 arrangements **not** applicable |
| Multi-phase | **No transfer of GFA** between completed building and other phases / new buildings yet to complete |

---

### 3. Compliance checking — who does what

| Department | Role |
|---|---|
| **BD** | Sole agent verifying GFA computation per B(P)R **23(3)(a)** for facilities/features/structures for GBP under all three regimes |
| B(P)R 23(3)(a) | GFA = area within external walls at each floor + balconies + thickness of external walls |
| Verification scope | Curtain walls, atrium voids, carparking, green features, amenity features, innovative features — per B(P)R, PNAPs, JPNs |
| **PlanD** | Uses BD-verified GFA for statutory plan / planning approval compliance |
| PlanD special accountability wording | If plan or approval has specific GFA accountability for certain facilities → BD figure is **base**; PlanD processes its overlay separately |
| PlanD no special wording | Follows BD practice in GFA calculation **and** granting of GFA concessions |
| Exceptions | Building Committee of BD or town planning mechanism |
| **LandsD** | Uses BD-verified GFA for max PR/GFA under existing and new leases |
| Lands exception | Site-specific max and/or min GFA for specific uses (retail, office, welfare) → LandsD still leads verification per **LAO PN 3/2018** (BD not normally required to split those uses) |

### 4. Group I — normally **not** GFA-accountable under all three regimes

Subject to compliance with exemption criteria in PNAPs/JPNs. **Unless** statutory plan, planning approval, or lease **explicitly** says these would not be exempted. Some items (e.g. green features, larger lift shafts) apply to **new developments/redevelopments only**, not all A&A.

| # | Item | Key criteria / PNAP |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elevated structures (elevated garden areas, terraces) | BA satisfied covered areas underneath would not be abused |
| 2 | Enclosed open areas (light wells, yards, pipe wells); 3-side enclosed open flat roofs / gardens / roofs / terraces | BA satisfied enclosed areas would not be abused |
| 3 | Horizontal areas of staircases, lift shafts, vertical ducts solely serving floors accepted as non-GFA | APP-2 |
| 4 | Open flat roofs | — |
| 5 | Open private carparks | — |
| 6 | Open staircases serving as main access to residential units/houses | BA satisfied covered areas under staircases would not be abused |
| 7 | Outdoor swimming pools | — |
| 8 | Rooftop staircase hoods (residential) | Size not excessive; **no other** roof facilities/features/structures GFA-accountable |
| 9 | Claddings | APP-2 |
| 10 | Refuge floors incl. refuge floors cum sky gardens | APP-2, APP-122 |
| 11 | Air-conditioner boxes / platforms | APP-19, APP-151 |
| 12 | Covered areas under large projecting / overhanging features | APP-19 |
| 13 | Covered landscaped and play areas (residential) | APP-42, APP-151 |
| 14 | Horizontal screens / covered walkways / trellises (residential) | APP-42, APP-151 |
| 15 | Minor projecting features (entrance canopies, projecting windows, window cills/surrounds, similar) | APP-19 |

**SD:** Group I is the “safe” concession list if PNAP criteria met and no hostile plan/lease wording — still verify each PNAP before claiming.

### 5. Group II — exempt under all three **only if** lease has specific GFA exemption provision

Without the lease clause on existing leases → lease modification, assessment, and usually **premium** (unless LandsD separately promulgates nil-premium policy for a category).

#### 5.1 Group II(A) — often nil premium (+ admin fee if appropriate)

| Item | PNAP / JPN |
|---|---|
| Caretaker’s quarters and caretaker’s offices (residential) | APP-42, APP-151 |
| Wider common corridors and lift lobbies (res) | JPN 1 / APP-151 list |
| Communal sky gardens | JPN 1 / 2 |
| Communal podium gardens for non-residential | JPN 1 |
| Acoustic fins | JPN 1 |
| Wing walls, wind catchers, funnels | JPN 1 |
| Owners’ corporation / owners’ committee offices (residential) | APP-42, APP-151 |

> Note: JPN 1 para. 9 currently **prevails** over JPN 4 on green-feature GFA/SC under existing leases until JPN 4 is updated — check both.

#### 5.2 Group II(B) — premium assessment typical

| Item | PNAP / JPN |
|---|---|
| Curtain walls | APP-2 |
| Balconies for residential buildings | JPN 1 |
| Utility platforms for residential buildings | JPN 2 |
| Non-structural prefabricated external walls | JPN 2 |
| Noise barriers | JPN 2 |
| Buildings adopting Modular Integrated Construction | JPN 8 |
| Residential recreational facilities | APP-104, APP-151, LAO PN 4/2000(B) |

**SD / feasibility:** Flag Group II(B) early in development appraisal — curtain wall and balconies are frequent premium items on older leases.

### 6. Group III — case-by-case; pre-aligned in **new** leases

| # | Item | SD note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Public transport terminus (PTT) | Confirm exempt vs accountable in lease / planning brief before basement strategy |
| 2 | Government accommodation (hand-over) other than PTT | Same |
| 3 | Covered public passageways and walkways required by Government | Same |
| 4 | Covered private carparking, L/UL bays/areas, ancillary driveways, ramps, lift shafts & lobbies of car/passage lifts, staircases etc. **solely serving** the private car park | Largest GFA swing on many sites — settle before SD freeze |

| New leases | Three departments try to align Group III position and **specify in the lease** (exempt or not). Further relaxation beyond lease text → separate premium treatment. |
| Existing leases | Case-by-case among three departments |

---

### 7. SD workflow

1. Extract OZP max PR/GFA + First Schedule + lease max GFA (and any use-specific GFA).  
2. Apply streamlined hierarchy (planning vs BO s.42 path).  
3. Classify every claimed item into Group I / II(A) / II(B) / III.  
4. For Group II, audit whether lease already has the exemption clause.  
5. Build GFA schedule **exactly as BD would** — PlanD/LandsD will follow.  
6. Never transfer GFA from OP’d phases into later phases.  
7. For use-specific lease GFA (retail/office/welfare), run LandsD LAO PN 3/2018 path in parallel.
