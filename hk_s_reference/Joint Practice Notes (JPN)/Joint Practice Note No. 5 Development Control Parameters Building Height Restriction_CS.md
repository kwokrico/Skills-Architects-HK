# Joint Practice Note No. 5 — Development Control Parameters: Building Height Restriction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issued April 2019 | Effective **15 May 2019** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GP/BL/TP/28 · LandsD LD BP/7-4/16/2 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Streamlines **BHR as a development-control parameter** under statutory town plans (PlanD lead). Does **not** change BD’s B(P)R building-height interpretation for development intensity or fire safety. Applies to new GBP / major revisions on/after **15 May 2019**. **Not** for Cap. 121 NT-exempt buildings. Cross-check OZP Notes, PNAP APP-5 (storey heights / transfer plates), APP-126 (signboards).

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 5 makes statutory town plans the primary home for BHR; new/modified leases generally **omit** BHR except in special circumstances. Height for BHR is measured to the highest level of the **main roof** (roof over highest usable floor space), unless OZP/lease says otherwise.

At schematic design, lock the OZP BHR form (mPD / metres / storeys), establish mean site formation level per tower, then size roof plant, architectural features, and signboards inside the free-roof envelopes — or they count toward BHR.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Why BHR exists (and what this JPN does / does not cover)

| Purpose of BHR | Visually compatible urban form; visual quality; natural air ventilation; control building bulk in vertical dimension |
|---|---|
| Imposed on | Statutory town plans and (historically) Government Leases |
| BD role | BD does **not** impose BHR as development control — but building height under B(P)R still controls intensity and fire safety minima |
| This JPN | Does **not** apply to BD’s B(P)R height interpretation |

---

### 2. How BHR is expressed — pick the right measuring stick

| Form | Typical planning intent | How to measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Metres above Principal Datum (mPD)** | Protect views to ridgelines / visual features; varying height profile / permeability | Absolute level — top of main roof (unless OZP includes roof structures) |
| **Metres** | Local character / natural topography where fixed mPD less appropriate | From **mean site formation level** |
| **Number of storeys** | Character / intensity contexts | B(P)R definition of “storey” unless lease specifies otherwise |

---

### 3. Mean site formation level (metres-form BHR)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Definition | Average of the **sum of the highest and lowest** formation levels of the land on which **any part of the building** stands, **including basement floors** |
| Multi-building projects | If buildings sit on different formed platforms → measure mean formation **per building** on its own formed site portion — **not** the entire development site |

**SD:** On stepped / sloping lots, each tower may have a different height budget in metres even under the same “max Xm” OZP clause.

---

### 4. Storeys-form BHR — what counts as a storey

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Definition | B(P)R reg. 2: space between upper surface of a floor and upper surface of floor next above; for top storey — to mean height of ceiling or roof |
| Basements | Count toward storey (and metres) BHR **unless** OZP/lease says otherwise |
| Future OZPs | May disregard **fully submerged** basement floors (no visual impact) — check Notes |
| Basement definition | B(P)R reg. 41C(5): storey below ground storey with required exit route in an **upward** direction |

---

### 5. Carport levels referenced in BHR

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Predominant use | ≥ **50%** of carport-level area dedicated to car parking **including** associated driveways and ramps (unless lease says otherwise) |
| Remainder | May hold other ancillary equipment/facilities serving the building if **reasonable size** vs development scale |

---

### 6. Top of building for BHR

| Default | Highest level of the **main roof** = roof over the highest **usable floor space** |
|---|---|
| Usable floor space (B(P)R reg. 2) | Floor space **other than** staircases, staircase halls, lift landings, WC/urinal/lavatory-basin space, and space occupied by machinery for lifts, A/C or similar services |
| OZP override examples | Explicit Notes e.g. not to exceed level of named roads (Tai Tam Road, Island Road, Repulse Bay Road) — then **total** building height **including roof-top structures** must not exceed stipulated BHR |

---

### 7. Roof-top structures normally **not** counted toward BHR

Subject to failing the triggers in §8 below.

| Category | Examples / notes |
|---|---|
| **(a) Building works on/above main roof** | Per BO definition — shown on GBP for B(P)R + BHR compliance |
| Roof-top ancillary structures | Plant rooms, water tanks, lift machine rooms, E&M (chiller, cooling tower), sustainable/renewable energy (solar panel, wind turbine), structures/features **housing and screening** facilities or plants |
| Telecom RBS | Follow OZP and/or lease separately — not a free pass |
| **(b) Roof-top architectural features** | Feature crowns, screens, sculptures as building works |
| **(c) Roof-top signboards** | Includes **LED screens**; still subject to BO safety, lease, other authorities, **PNAP APP-126** |
| Labour safety M&R kit (open-air) | Working platforms, cat-ladders, parapets, gondolas (+ screens, plinths, railway) — if **reasonable size** vs scale |
| Lightning pole | Reasonable size |

---

### 8. When roof-top structures **do** count (fail either trigger)

#### Trigger I — enclosed/covered roof coverage

| Fail if | Total areas of **all enclosed (and covered)** roof-top structures, **regardless of height**, exceed **50%** of the roof area of the floor below |
|---|---|

#### Trigger II — height limits (apply whether enclosed, covered, or open)

##### (a) Roof-top ancillary structures — all building types

| Building height | Max free roof structure height |
|---|---|
| ≤ **30 m** | **3 m** (or **5.2 m** for lift provision) |
| > **30 m** | Lesser of **10%** of building height or **15 m** |
| Lift provision note | Same **5.2 m** lift provision also applies to buildings **> 30 m but ≤ 52 m** |

##### (b) Roof-top architectural features

| Building type | Building height | Max free feature height |
|---|---|---|
| Residential and composite (C&R) | Any | Lesser of **15%** of BH or **7 m** |
| All other types | ≤ **35 m** | **7 m** |
| All other types | > **35 m** | **20%** of BH; **plus** optional slender **spire/antenna** further **20%** of main BH if cross-section ≤ **5 m × 5 m** |

##### (c) Roof-top signboards — all building types

| Building height | Max free signboard height |
|---|---|
| ≤ **30 m** | **3 m** |
| > **30 m** | Lesser of **10%** of BH or **15 m** |

#### Worked examples (SD)

| Case | Check |
|---|---|
| 120 m residential tower; feature crown 8 m | 15% of 120 = 18 m, but hard cap **7 m** → **8 m fails** → counts toward BHR |
| 40 m office; plant 4.5 m high; coverage 40% | Height OK (≤ 10% or 15 m → 4 m? Wait: 10% of 40 = 4 m) → **4.5 m fails** height trigger even if coverage OK |
| 25 m building; lift machine room 5.0 m | ≤30 m band allows 5.2 m for lift → **OK** if coverage ≤50% |
| Feature crown OK height but enclosed plant + features cover 55% of roof | **Trigger I fails** → structures count |

### 9. Storey heights and transfer plates (contribute to BH)

| Item | Authority |
|---|---|
| Range of permissible storey heights (esp. residential) | **BD** — PNAP **APP-5** |
| Acceptable transfer-plate designs / thickness | BD — APP-5 |
| Purpose | Maintain safety/health; prevent abuse at plan approval |

These eat the BHR budget even when roof features are free.

---

### 10. Authority matrix

| Party | Role |
|---|---|
| **PlanD** | Authority on BHR as development-control parameter; administers OZP BHR; recommends BA refuse plans under BO **s.16(1)(d)** or **s.16(1)(da)** |
| Ambiguity / special cases | Enquire PlanD — assessed on individual merits |
| **BD** | B(P)R height for intensity/fire; APP-5; reasonableness of M&R safety facilities |
| **LandsD** | Follows this JPN **except**: (a) lease explicitly different; or (b) lease has **no other** intensity control (e.g. no max GFA) — then Director of Lands final |
| Existing lease BHR | May apply to LandsD to remove via lease mod (+ premium if assessed + admin fee) |

---

### 11. SD checklist

1. Quote BHR form from OZP (mPD / m / storeys) and whether Notes include roof structures.  
2. Confirm whether basements count.  
3. Establish mean site formation level **per tower** on stepped sites.  
4. Design roof plant + screens + feature crown against **both** 50% coverage and height free-envelopes.  
5. Coordinate APP-5 storey heights / transfer plate with remaining BH budget.  
6. Check lease for leftover BHR stricter than OZP; consider lease-mod strategy early.
