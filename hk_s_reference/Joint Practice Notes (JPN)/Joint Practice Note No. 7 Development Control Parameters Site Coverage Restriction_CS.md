# Joint Practice Note No. 7 — Development Control Parameters: Site Coverage Restriction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue August 2021 | Effective **23 August 2021** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GR/1-125/6/1/0 · LD BP/7-4/16/7 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Streamlines SC restriction across BO, OZP, and lease. **Not** for Cap. 121 NT-exempt buildings. Read with LandsD LAO PN **3/2020A** (Design, Disposition and Height / Site Coverage under lease). Streamlined lease paragraphs apply only where lease contains max **GFA and/or PR**. Cross-check B(P)R First Schedule, regs 18A / 19(3) / 20(3), APP-19.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 7 keeps **B(P)R First Schedule SC** as the default bulk control; OZP may impose **more stringent** SC for selected sites or levels; **BD** is the single agency for SC accountability of facilities/features. New/modified leases generally **will not** restate SC except in special circumstances.

At schematic design, size podium and tower footprints to First Schedule (plus any OZP overlay), treating underground structures as **SC-free** under BD practice unless plan/lease says otherwise.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What “site coverage” means

| Source | Definition |
|---|---|
| B(P)R reg. 2 | Area of the site covered by the building erected thereon; for part of a composite building — area of site covered by that part |
| Roofed-over area (old leases) | De-facto SC restriction on some leases — this JPN’s streamlined arrangements apply to such leases **subject to** para. 5 / LAO PN 3/2020A path |

---

### 2. SC under the three regimes (current logic)

#### 2.1 Building regime

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Instrument | B(P)R First Schedule max SC |
| Depends on | Site classification under reg. **18A** (Class A/B/C); building **height**; **domestic / non-domestic** use |
| Unclassified sites | Reg. **19(3)** — BA determines height, max SC, and max PR |
| Low non-domestic bulk | Reg. **20(3)** — up to **100% SC** for non-domestic part of a building to a height not exceeding **15 m** above ground level |

**SD:** The 15 m / 100% non-dom podium allowance is a primary massing tool — keep retail/parking podium ≤ 15 m if claiming full site coverage at low levels.

#### 2.2 Planning regime

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| When used | Selected sites — preserve local character, enhance air ventilation, visual quality, permeability |
| Interaction | Imposed **in tandem** with B(P)R SC |
| Streamlined OZP drafting | New/amended plans gazetted on/after effective date should state clearly if stricter SC applies only to **particular levels**; remainder follows B(P)R |
| Scope of streamlining | Only sites with new/amended SC control on plans gazetted on/after effective date |

#### 2.3 Land administration regime

| Lease type | Practice |
|---|---|
| Old leases | Max SC or roofed-over area **plus BH** often used to control intensity (before modern GFA/PR clauses) |
| Modern leases with GFA/PR | SC may still appear if another B/D needs it for specific objectives |
| This JPN paras 8–9 | Apply **only** to leases containing max GFA and/or PR |
| Leases **without** max GFA/PR | Use LandsD LAO PN 3/2020A — do **not** assume JPN 7 lease streamlining |

---

### 3. Streamlined control hierarchy

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | B(P)R max SC continues to apply as general guideline |
| 2 | If PlanD needs stricter SC → specify clearly on new/amended statutory plan (e.g. level-specific) |
| 3 | **BD** = single agency for accountability of facilities/features for SC calculation |
| 4 | For new/amended plans on/after effective date → PlanD adopts BD’s SC treatment when formulating max SC |
| 5 | For existing statutory plans → PlanD generally follows BD’s SC treatment unless plan provides otherwise |
| 6 | With B(P)R + statutory plans as lead regimes → SC **not** set out in new/modified leases except special circumstances |
| 7 | If special-case lease includes max SC → LandsD adopts BD accountability when drafting and checking |
| 8 | Existing leases with SC → LandsD follows BD accountability unless explicit lease conditions prohibit |
| 9 | Wish to remove lease SC → lease mod to LandsD (+ premium if assessed + admin fee) |

---

### 4. Accountability practices PlanD/LandsD will follow

Unless statutory plan and/or lease state otherwise:

| Item | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Underground structures** | BD does **not** count for SC → PlanD and LandsD adopt same |
| **Minor projecting features** (APP-19 list) | LandsD may disregard under **existing** lease **without** lease modification if BA also disregards |
| **Non-minor projections** disregarded by BA via BO **s.42** modification | LandsD prepared to follow BD practice, subject to lease modification (if necessary) + premium + admin fee |
| No intensity increase under lease | Minor lease modification normally applicable |

**APP-19 minor projections typically include:** canopies over entrances, projecting windows, window cills/surrounds, and similar minor projections (see also JPN 4 Group I).

---

### 5. SD massing implications

| Strategy | Why |
|---|---|
| Put parking / plant underground | SC-free under BD practice |
| Keep non-dom podium ≤ 15 m | Unlock up to 100% SC under reg. 20(3) |
| Check OZP for level-specific SC | Tower SC may be tighter than podium |
| Separate APP-19 minor projections from large overhangs | Large features need s.42 + possible lease mod |
| Old lease without GFA/PR | Intensity may still be controlled by SC/roofed-over + BH — run LAO PN 3/2020A |

---

### 6. SD checklist

1. Classify site (A/B/C or unclassified) and read First Schedule SC vs height/use.  
2. Confirm reg. 20(3) 15 m / 100% non-dom opportunity.  
3. Read OZP for any stricter SC and which levels it hits.  
4. Audit lease: GFA/PR present? SC/roofed-over present? Apply correct PN path.  
5. Keep underground bulk SC-clean; list projections as minor vs modification-needed.  
6. If removing obsolete lease SC, programme lease mod early.
