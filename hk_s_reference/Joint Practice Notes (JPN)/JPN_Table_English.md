# Joint Practice Notes Index (English)

**Issued by:** Buildings Department, Lands Department, and Planning Department
**Scope:** JPN 1 to JPN 8 (8 joint practice notes in this library). This library was not checked against the live departmental websites.
**How to use:** The number links to the critical summary beside this file. The PDF is in `source_reference/` under the same filename stem.
**Titles:** The official title is the PDF cover heading where the text layer has one. Two-line cover headings are joined with an em dash. For JPN 1 and JPN 7 the text layer does not print the subtitle as a cover heading, so those rows use the library title.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current joint note, the outline zoning plan, and the lease.

## Green and innovative buildings

| No. | Official title | This revision / effective | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [JPN 1](<./Joint Practice Note No. 1 Green and Innovative Buildings_CS.md>) | Green and Innovative Buildings | This revision June 2025 (first issue February 2001; last revision September 2019) | Exempts residential balconies, wider common corridors and lift lobbies, residential sky gardens, non-residential podium gardens, acoustic fins, and wing walls, wind catchers or funnels from gross floor area and/or site coverage on new projects that do not yet have an occupation permit, subject to PNAP APP-151. |
| [JPN 2](<./Joint Practice Note No. 2 Second Package of Incentives for Green and Innovative Buildings_CS.md>) | Second Package of Incentives to Promote Green and Innovative Buildings | This revision July 2022 (MiC provisions removed; see JPN 8) | The July 2022 revision removes the modular integrated construction incentives and points to JPN 8, while still exempting non-structural prefabricated external walls, residential utility platforms, noise barriers, and non-residential communal sky gardens from gross floor area and/or site coverage. |
| [JPN 8](<./Joint Practice Note No. 8 Enhanced Facilitation Measures for Modular Integrated Construction_CS.md>) | Incentive to Promote Green and Innovative Buildings — Enhanced Facilitation Measures for Buildings Adopting Modular Integrated Construction | First issued July 2022; effective 1 August 2022 | For plans submitted on or after 1 August 2022, disregards 10% of the modular integrated construction floor area from gross floor area and from site coverage, and supports up to 4% extra building height on the storey height of the MiC floors. |

## Development control parameters

| No. | Official title | This revision / effective | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [JPN 4](<./Joint Practice Note No. 4 Development Control Parameters Plot Ratio Gross Floor Area_CS.md>) | Development Control Parameters — Plot Ratio / Gross Floor Area | This revision October 2021; effective 18 October 2021 | Aligns the maximum plot ratio and gross floor area, how floor area is counted, and how the three departments check compliance, for new general building plans and major revisions submitted on or after 18 October 2021. |
| [JPN 5](<./Joint Practice Note No. 5 Development Control Parameters Building Height Restriction_CS.md>) | Development Control Parameters — Building Height Restriction | First issued April 2019; effective 15 May 2019 | Streamlines building-height control on statutory town plans, measured to the main roof unless the plan or lease says otherwise, and does not change the Buildings Department's reading of building height for development intensity and fire safety. |
| [JPN 7](<./Joint Practice Note No. 7 Development Control Parameters Site Coverage Restriction_CS.md>) | Development Control Parameters — Site Coverage Restriction | First issue August 2021; effective 23 August 2021 | Keeps the Building (Planning) Regulations First Schedule as the default site-coverage limit, allows a more stringent outline zoning plan restriction, and makes the Buildings Department the single agency for how facilities and features count toward site coverage. |

## Landscape and sustainable building design

| No. | Official title | This revision / effective | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [JPN 3](<./Joint Practice Note No. 3 Landscape and Site Coverage of Greenery_CS.md>) | Landscape and Site Coverage of Greenery | This issue April 2019; effective 15 May 2019 | Separates landscape submissions, handled by the Planning Department or the Lands Department, from site coverage of greenery, which the Buildings Department checks under PNAP APP-152, and records that the Buildings Ordinance does not empower the Buildings Department to impose landscape requirements. |
| [JPN 6](<./Joint Practice Note No. 6 Sustainable Building Design Guidelines Building Separation and Building Setback_CS.md>) | Sustainable Building Design Guidelines — Building Separation and Building Setback | First issued September 2019; effective 15 October 2019 | Makes the Buildings Department the sole authority to interpret, accept, and check building separation and building setback under PNAP APP-152, whether required by the lease or as a gross-floor-area concession pre-requisite. |
