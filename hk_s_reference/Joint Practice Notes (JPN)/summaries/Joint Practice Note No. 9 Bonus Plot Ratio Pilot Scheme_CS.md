# Joint Practice Note No. 9 — Bonus Plot Ratio Pilot Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 2026 | Buildings Department / Lands Department / Planning Department
Ref: BD GR/1-55/11/4 · LAO/URS-PM-002-004 · UR U/URR/6 (C)

> Scope note: Sets out what an applicant must lock before applying under the Bonus Plot Ratio (PR) Pilot Scheme for private redevelopment in seven old districts (2025 Policy Address). It is an administrative joint note on eligibility, the 20% bonus, and two mutually exclusive ways to use it. **Not** a grant of planning permission, lease modification, or Buildings Ordinance (BO) approval, and **not** a statement of the development intensity the Town Planning Board (TPB) will allow. The Appendix method calculates the bonus only; it does not set the intensity of the scheme.

## Regulatory Overview

During the five-year pilot (1 September 2026 to 31 August 2031), an owner who pulls down an eligible old building with residential elements and redevelops it mainly for residential use may obtain an additional 20% land-premium-free PR / gross floor area (GFA), either built in situ with TPB approval or encashed to offset premium.

Acceptance is by lease modification (including land exchange), which binds the owner to complete within a building covenant, among other terms.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Pilot window and application sequence (paras 2–4, 16(a))

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bonus | Additional **20%** PR/GFA, land-premium-free |
| Pilot period | **1 September 2026 – 31 August 2031** |
| Entry | Lease modification application to the District Lands Office (DLO) |
| If s.16 Town Planning Ordinance (Cap. 131) permission is required | Obtain it **before** the lease modification application |
| Extension of the five-year window | Lands Department (LandsD), with Development Bureau (DEVB), may extend a particular case only if reasonable action to ready the lease-modification submission was taken inside the window |
| “Lease modification” | Includes a land exchange; “lease modification letter” includes Conditions of Exchange |
| Execution deadline | Lease modification letter executed within **three years** of a valid application; no extension unless LandsD with DEVB finds strong and valid justification |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze the path before design: if “Flat” is not always permitted, the section 16 permission has to exist before the lease-modification file, and the letter must still be executed inside three years.

### 2. Eligible site — all four tests (para 5 and footnotes)

| Test | Threshold |
|---|---|
| (a) Location | Inside one of the seven designated areas (footnote 1): the Schedule to the Land (Compulsory Sale for Redevelopment) (Specification of Lower Percentage) Notice (Cap. 545A), being the areas of these Outline Zoning Plans (OZPs) |
| (b) Existing building | Building(s) with residential elements, Occupation Permit (OP) issued by the Building Authority (BA) **at least 50 years** before the planning application, or before the lease modification application if no planning application is required |
| (c) New use | Redeveloped **mainly for residential use**, whatever the OZP zone. More than **50%** of total GFA in residential use meets this test |
| (d) Site area | At least **700 m²**, one or more lots |

**Seven OZPs named in footnote 1**

| OZP | District named in the note |
|---|---|
| Cheung Sha Wan OZP No. S/K5/39 | Sham Shui Po |
| Ma Tau Kok OZP No. S/K10/30 | Kowloon City and To Kwa Wan |
| Mong Kok OZP No. S/K3/36 | — |
| Sai Ying Pun & Sheung Wan OZP No. S/H3/34 | — |
| Tsuen Wan OZP No. S/TW/35 | — |
| Wan Chai OZP No. S/H5/31 | — |
| Yau Ma Tei OZP No. S/K2/24 | — |

**“Building with residential elements” (footnote 3).** Wholly or partly for domestic purpose as approved by the BA, including composite buildings. Excludes (i) hotels and guesthouses; (ii) residential facilities managed or operated by an organisation for people who meet eligibility criteria (boarding houses, dormitories, residential institutions, staff quarters, student hostels, residential care homes for the elderly or persons with disabilities, and similar); (iii) New Territories Exempted Houses.

Age and use are read from the OP. If the OP and/or approved plans are missing, an Authorized Person must demonstrate use and/or age (footnote 4).

**What counts toward 700 m², and what earns the 20% (footnote 6 and the historic-building note)**

| Land | Counts toward 700 m² | Included in the 20% bonus base |
|---|---|---|
| Government land to be granted in the lease modification | No | No — not eligible for bonus PR |
| Non-building area, garden reserve, or other land with no development right under the lease | Yes | No |
| Redevelopment already commenced before the five-year period: (i) building demolished or demolition commenced; (ii) lot already sold under a Lands Tribunal compulsory-sale order (Cap. 545); (iii) lease modification already executed for redevelopment of the existing or last building | Yes | No |
| Site area of graded historic building(s) to be retained | Yes | Yes |

**SD takeaway:** Test 700 m² and the bonus base as two different areas — government land, non-building area, and pre-pilot demolition or sale drop out of the 20% even when they help the size test; retained graded historic buildings stay in both.

### 3. How the 20% is calculated (para 6 and Appendix)

The Appendix principles calculate the bonus only. They are not a promise of intensity. TPB decides each section 16 application on its merits.

Base PR/GFA is the **maximum on the plan**, not the existing building, even where the Notes allow redevelopment up to an existing PR/GFA that is higher (Appendix footnote 2).

| Site situation | Base used for 20% × site area |
|---|---|
| “Flat” always permitted, and Notes state maximum domestic PR (Dom PR) / domestic GFA | That Dom PR/GFA |
| “Flat” always permitted, and Notes state only a maximum total PR/GFA | That total PR/GFA |
| “Flat” always permitted, and the plan has no PR or GFA restriction | Maximum permissible PR for domestic buildings **over 61 m** in the First Schedule of the Building (Planning) Regulations (Cap. 123F) (B(P)R), by site class |
| Planning permission required for “Flat”, and the zone’s own Notes state a maximum Dom PR/GFA (Appendix footnote 4: e.g. “Residential (Group E)” and “Comprehensive Development Area”) | That zone’s own Dom PR/GFA |
| Planning permission required for “Flat”, and the zone has no Dom PR/GFA | Maximum Dom PR of the **“R(A)”** zone on the same OZP |
| Same, and that “R(A)” zone also has no PR/GFA restriction | B(P)R First Schedule, domestic buildings over 61 m, by site class |

Zones where “Flat” is always permitted (para 7 / Appendix footnote 1): “Residential (Group A)” (“R(A)”), “Residential (Group B)” (“R(B)”), “Residential (Group C)”, “Other Specified Uses” annotated “Mixed Use” (“OU(MU)”).

Zones cited as needing permission for “Flat” (para 6 and Appendix footnote 3): “Commercial” (“C”), “Government, Institution or Community” (“G/IC”), “Residential (Group E)” (“R(E)”).

**Appendix worked examples (illustrative arithmetic in the note, not a substitute for the current OZP or First Schedule)**

| Example | Base | Bonus domestic GFA |
|---|---|---|
| 700 m² “R(A)” on Mong Kok OZP, max Dom PR 8.5 | OZP Dom PR | 700 × 8.5 × 0.2 = **1 190 m²** |
| 700 m² “R(B)” on Ma Tau Kok OZP, max PR 5 | OZP total PR | 700 × 5 × 0.2 = **700 m²** |
| 700 m² “OU(MU)” on Wan Chai OZP, no Dom PR; Class A; example First Schedule Dom PR 8 | B(P)R | 700 × 8 × 0.2 = **1 120 m²** |
| 700 m² “G/IC” on Yau Ma Tei OZP; “R(A)” Dom PR on that OZP 8.5 | “R(A)” on the same OZP | 700 × 8.5 × 0.2 = **1 190 m²** |
| 700 m² “C” on Wan Chai OZP; “R(A)” has no PR; Class B; example First Schedule Dom PR 9 | B(P)R | 700 × 9 × 0.2 = **1 260 m²** |

Land premium **value** of the bonus is a standard rate issued and updated annually by LandsD, one rate per designated area. No appeal against the rate or the value (para 7).

**SD takeaway:** Compute bonus GFA as 0.2 × the plan maximum (or the “R(A)” / over-61 m First Schedule fallback), on the bonus base only — never on the existing building’s higher PR, and never on land footnote 6 excludes.

### 4. Option (i) — build the bonus in situ (paras 8–12)

Choose (i) or (ii), not both. No hybrid. If the in-situ building cannot absorb the full 20%, the unused bonus is not encashed, not transferred, and not paid out in cash (footnote 8).

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Planning application | State expressly that the bonus PR will be used in kind. Build the 20% **in addition to** the original 100% permissible under the OZP, subject to TPB approval under section 16 |
| After approval | Lease modification to LandsD, letter within the five-year period |
| Lease terms | Minimum necessary: building covenant (a compulsory-sale order is taken into account), user, minimum and maximum PR or GFA, alienation restrictions before compliance including pre-sale consent, deed of mutual covenant, plus TPB land-related conditions where appropriate. Bureaux/departments add other terms only on a minimum-necessary basis (e.g. public safety) |
| Premium | Assessed on existing lease value versus modified conditions, then **deducted** by the bonus value at the standard rate on the date of the Binding Basic Terms Offer (BBTO). A revised BBTO uses the rate on the revised-offer date |
| Above B(P)R First Schedule | Section 42 BO application. BA will favourably consider these with reference to Joint Practice Note No. 4 |
| Permission zone, and TPB-approved PR exceeds the zone cap, the “R(A)” cap, or B(P)R, as the case may be | Treated as having used the bonus in kind; the 20% standard-rate value still offsets in-situ premium (footnote 12) |
| TPB conditions | May require the lease modification to be executed before building plans are submitted under the BO |

**SD takeaway:** If the scheme needs the extra 20% inside the envelope, say so in the section 16 application, then test the total against B(P)R and keep a section 42 / JPN 4 path ready; unused in-kind bonus dies on this site.

### 5. Option (ii) — encash the bonus (paras 13–15)

For applicants who will not build the 20% in situ, including where TPB does not approve that in-situ use (footnote 13).

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Application | Lease modification for the in-situ redevelopment, stating the encashment intention. If the zone needs permission for “Flat”, section 16 approval still comes first |
| Lease terms | Same minimum-necessary list as Option (i) |
| Value | LandsD states the 20% value at the BBTO-date standard rate. It may offset premium on the original 100% in-situ redevelopment and/or other transactions, including government land sale or lease modification in the Northern Metropolis and elsewhere, until exhausted |
| Record | On execution, LandsD issues documentation of the value (or the remainder if part was used on the in-situ premium) and keeps a register |
| Life | Unused value valid **10 years** from the lease modification letter for the eligible site. No interest, redemption, or compensation after expiry |
| Transfer | Non-transferable, except to associated companies under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), on documentary proof. Government’s decision on that relationship is final |

**SD takeaway:** Encashment is a 10-year premium credit tied to this acceptance, not extra floor area — do not draw an in-situ massing that depends on the 20% if Option (ii) is the choice.

### 6. Ownership, other concessions, and failure (para 16)

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Section 16 timing | Full site ownership is **not** required when the section 16 application is made; owners’ consent or TPO notification is |
| Lease modification or building-plan submission | Full land ownership is required. Joint ownership is allowed for granting the bonus |
| Split of value | Multiple owners: value may be split by Land Registry shares at the application date. Separate joint-venture ownership without unified title: split by written agreement given to LandsD |
| Early enquiry | Before committing to planning and title work, DLO will advise on eligibility and the estimated encashment value. Planning Department (PlanD) advice is available where a planning application is involved |
| Voluntary gains | Encouraged, especially when the bonus is built in kind: socially beneficial facilities of the applicant’s choice (examples: community living room, makerspace) and/or design merits the applicant owns (setback beyond the Sustainable Building Design Guidelines, pedestrian canopy, public passageway). District Studies by the Urban Renewal Authority may be used as a reference. Not stated as a condition of the 20% |
| Other GFA concessions | Still available (green and innovative buildings; bonus GFA supported by the Commissioner for Heritage). Those concessions are **not** part of the base for this bonus PR |
| Transfer of Plot Ratio (TPR) | A sending or receiving site under the TPB Guidelines for Transfer of Plot Ratio under section 16 may also seek this bonus in kind or by encashment if para 5 is met. TPR limits still apply, including the maximum GFA the site can accommodate |
| Non-compliance | If the bonus has been used and the in-situ redevelopment is not built in accordance with the lease, Government may recover the full value of the bonus used from the applicant and its parent company (if applicable), backed by a Deed of Undertaking on LandsD’s terms |
| Fees | LandsD administrative fee; Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office fee where the modification is an in-situ land exchange |
| Merits | Each application on its own merits. The JPN is not a representation that TPB, LandsD, or BA will approve |

**SD takeaway:** Keep heritage and green-feature concessions off the bonus-PR base, and do not stack this 20% on top of a TPR receiving-site cap without checking the TPR Guidelines’ maximum GFA.
