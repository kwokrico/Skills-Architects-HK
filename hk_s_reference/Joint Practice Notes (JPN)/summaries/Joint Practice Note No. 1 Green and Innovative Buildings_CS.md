# Joint Practice Note No. 1 — Green and Innovative Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue February 2001 | Last revision September 2019 | This revision June 2025 | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GP/ENV/8 · LD 2/1020/00 · TPB/C/BLC/2

> Scope note: Optional GFA and/or SC exemptions for listed green features under BO, Government Lease, and TPO. Applies to **new projects without OP**. Hotels and non-domestic portions of composite buildings are **not** “residential” for JPN 1. All concessions subject to **PNAP APP-151** (pre-requisites; overall GFA cap where stated). Cross-check OZP BH/SC, lease height/storey limits, PNAP APP-104 (RRF), APP-123 (balcony fire), CoP on Access for External Maintenance (balcony/AC), DM:BFA (elderly facilities).

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 1 is the first BD–LandsD–PlanD package promoting green buildings by exempting six feature types from GFA and/or SC: residential balconies; wider residential common corridors and lift lobbies; communal residential sky gardens; communal non-residential podium gardens; acoustic fins; and wing walls / wind catchers / funnels.

At schematic design, these are **massing and GFA-budget levers** — balcony/UP disposition, corridor width strategy, sky-garden count and vertical spacing, greenery “L” zones, and APP-151 concession-cap allocation must be fixed before plate freeze. BA exemption is the gateway; lease and OZP follow with their own traps.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Occupancy filters (read before claiming any concession)

| Rule | Implication |
|---|---|
| “Residential” incentives | Do **not** apply to non-domestic portion of a composite building |
| Residential accommodation for commercial use | Out of scope |
| Hotels | Treated as **non-residential** → no balcony, wider corridor/lobby, or residential sky-garden concessions |
| Application window | New projects only — OP not yet issued |

### 2. Feature menu — what each exempts

| Feature | GFA | SC | APP-151 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balconies (res) | Yes (50% / capped) | Yes (same) | Pre-requisites **+ overall cap** |
| Wider common corridors / lift lobbies (res) | Yes (width band) | Yes | Pre-requisites |
| Communal sky gardens (res) | Yes | — | Pre-requisites; RRF-connected voids **outside** overall cap |
| Communal podium gardens (non-res) | Yes | — | Pre-requisites |
| Acoustic fins | Yes | Yes | Pre-requisites |
| Wing walls / wind catchers / funnels | Yes | Yes | Pre-requisites |

### 3. Residential balconies — full geometry checklist

#### 3.1 Location and conflicts

| Rule | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Host room | Living room, dining room, or bedroom **only** |
| Projecting window | **Forbidden** in the same room |
| Utility platform | ≥ **1.5 m** away (unless combined design per CoP Access for External Maintenance App. B) |
| Re-entrant | Not in re-entrant with unobstructed width < **2,300 mm** |
| Light well | Not permitted |
| Lot boundary | Must not project beyond |

#### 3.2 Openness, level, exhaust

| Rule | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Open perimeter | ≥ **40%** of balcony perimeter faces **open air** (B(P)R reg. 2) and is **not enclosed** above safe parapet height |
| Level drop | ≥ **150 mm** from the room leading onto the balcony |
| Exhaust | If B(P)R reg. 36 modification granted for internal bathrooms/lavatories → exhaust must **not** open into balcony or covered area underneath |

#### 3.3 Area maths (per residential unit)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum balcony size (including exempted part) | ≥ **2 m²** |
| Maximum portion exemptible | ≤ **50%** of balcony area |
| Exempt area cap | Greater of **1 m²** or **2.5%** of Usable Floor Space of the unit, subject to max **3 m²** |

**Worked implication:** A 40 m² UFS flat → 2.5% = 1.0 m² → exempt = 1 m² (the 1 m² floor applies). A 160 m² UFS flat → 2.5% = 4 m² → hard-capped at **3 m²**. Design balcony ≥ 2× intended exempt area (because only 50% exempts).

#### 3.4 Covered area under the lowest balcony

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Full GFA **and** SC exemption | Allowed for covered area under the **lowest** balcony |
| Openness | ≥ **40%** of that covered area’s perimeter not enclosed above safe parapet and faces open air |

#### 3.5 Enforcement / OP blockers

| Condition | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Enclosure above safe parapet | Misuse → enforcement / prosecution |
| Exhaust into balcony / covered area | Same |
| Undertaking not registered in Land Registry | **OP will not be issued** |

### 4. Wider common corridors and lift lobbies

#### 4.1 Eligibility

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Floors | Residential floors **other than entrance hall(s)** |
| Driver | Need must **not** result from any statutory width requirement |
| Service corridors / service lift lobbies | Separated by door(s) from main circulation and not normally used by residents to reach flats → **disregarded** in exemption assessment |
| Lease exclusion | Detached / semi-detached / terraced house with **no common staircase** → **not** eligible for lease exemption (Lands decision final) |

#### 4.2 Exempt width bands (structural dimensions)

| Element | Continuous uninterrupted width eligible for exemption |
|---|---|
| Corridor | **1,200 mm – 2,200 mm** |
| Lift lobby | **1,650 mm – 2,500 mm** |

Only the continuous band along the **whole length** qualifies. Local widenings that are not continuous throughout cannot be exempted (App. B Figures 1–3).

#### 4.3 Corridor vs lift lobby — measurement (App. B)

| Arrangement | How to classify |
|---|---|
| Straight | **Lift lobby** = floor space immediately outside internal sides of lift shafts. Where it extends beyond those sides by ≥ clear depth **“D”** of the lift shaft → space beyond up to flat entrances = **corridor** |
| L-shaped | Lobby may turn and extend up to max length = **D**; beyond that = corridor |
| L- or T-shaped (extended) | Same D-rule: beyond D from internal sides of shafts → corridor to flat entrances |
| Wheelchair space | 1.5 m × 1.5 m manoeuvring space shown in figures — indentations smaller than 1.5 m × 1.5 m disregarded when measuring greenery distance “L” on gardens (App. C), and lobby diagrams flag non-continuous widenings as non-exempt |

**SD:** Fix core geometry and “D” early; chasing exemption by locally bumping corridor width without continuity fails.

### 5. Communal sky gardens (residential)

#### 5.1 Purpose and count

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Natural ventilation + greenery + recreational garden space for communal use |
| Microclimate | Wind tunnel and/or CFD recommended for location and dimensions |
| Max number | In addition to any podium garden: ≤ (number of **residential storeys**) ÷ **15** |
| Example | 45 residential storeys → max 3 sky gardens (+ podium garden if any) |

#### 5.2 Floor-plate geometry

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Cross-ventilation | Open-sided above safe parapet on ≥ **two opposite** sides |
| Net garden area | ≥ **50%** of floor-plate area |
| Multi-parcel gardens | Parcels on same floor must be connected by **communal circulation** on that floor |
| Clear height | ≥ **4.5 m** |
| Access | From **common area only** |
| Exhaust | No ventilating-system exhaust facing the garden |
| Fixed elements | All planters, furniture, equipment, facilities fixed to floors/walls/permanent construction; design for typhoon |

#### 5.3 Greenery within net garden (critical for SD elevations)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum greenery | ≥ **15%** of floor-plate area |
| Planting | Vegetated (native trees/shrubs + adequate soil depth recommended) |
| Accountable zone | Within horizontal distance **“L”** measured perpendicular from outer edge of any external wall that is open above safe parapet and faces open air |
| Definition of L | Clear vertical distance between **top of that external wall opening** and **finished floor level** of the garden |
| Lateral extension | Zone may extend laterally by **1.5 m** beyond the wall opening if opening width ≥ **1.5 m** |
| Indentations | Indentation < **1.5 m × 1.5 m** disregarded when measuring L (App. C Fig. 1) |

#### 5.4 Parapet rules for cross-vent and greenery assessment (footnote 5)

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Solid parapet | No solid portion above **1,250 mm** from garden FFL |
| Opening height | Top of external wall opening ≥ **3,000 mm** above top of solid parapet |
| Planters/benches on inside of parapet | Parapet above 1,250 mm must be highly permeable (≥ **2/3 free area**) so ventilation/lighting not blocked |

#### 5.5 Vertical placement

| Rule | Constraint |
|---|---|
| First sky garden (if >1 garden and **no** podium garden) | ≤ **10 storeys** above lowest ground storey (street-level microclimate) |
| Spacing | ≥ **10 storeys** above any lower sky garden or podium garden, unless exceptional environmental justification |
| PlanD | No concern on overall building height |
| Refuge floors | Required refuge at roof **or** coupled with sky garden(s); design per fire codes |
| Refuge count example | 55-storey building needing 2 refuge floors → may have 2 refuge-cum-sky-gardens **+** 1 independent sky garden eligible for GFA exemption |

#### 5.6 Notices, maintenance, commercial ban

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Notice | Prominent notice: communal use; commercial activities (café, shops) **strictly prohibited** |
| Maintenance | Financially viable; maintenance & management manual for greenery for end-users |
| Lease (para. 9) | Sitting-out only; recreational facilities limited to elderly-friendly equipment/facilities in App. A §1(c)(xvi); commercial prohibited |

### 6. Sky garden + RRF package (App. A §1(c)(xvi) — June 2025)

Use this when stacking recreational floors with a sky garden to unlock **void GFA outside APP-151 cap**.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Connection | Sky garden directly accessible from / connected with RRF on **same floor** or **next consecutive floor** |
| RRF compliance | Must meet PNAP APP-104 |
| Stack limit | Sky garden + RRF together ≤ **3 consecutive** storeys |
| Elderly toilet | ≥ 1 elderly-friendly toilet + elderly fitness equipment (DM:BFA) |
| If toilet = required accessible toilet | Provide **another** elderly-friendly facility in addition to fitness equipment |
| Indication on plans | Elderly toilet, facilities, fitness equipment must be shown |
| Demarcation | Sky garden vs RRF areas clearly demarcated on plans |
| Rec facilities in sky garden | **Only** elderly fitness / elderly facilities — **no** APP-104 Appendix A recreational list items |
| Enclosure of elderly kit | Toilets/changing stations may be enclosed; other elderly facilities / fitness = **open design**, no enclosure |
| Toilet area vs net garden | Elderly-friendly toilet/changing station areas **excluded** from net garden % under §1(c)(iv); may be GFA-exempt and **not** under APP-151 overall cap if not excessive and do not obstruct natural/cross ventilation |
| Refuge | Required refuge floor must **not** be coupled with this sky garden |
| Voids over sky garden | GFA-exempt; **not** subject to APP-151 overall cap |

#### Same-floor RRF connection — relaxed garden %

| Criterion | Relaxed value |
|---|---|
| Net garden area | ≥ **30%** of floor plate (instead of 50%) |
| Greenery | ≥ **8%** of floor plate (instead of 15%) |

#### Consecutive-floor RRF (Annex to App. C Example 2)

Sky garden on one floor; RRF on next consecutive floor; voids over sky garden still outside APP-151 cap. Net/greenery stay at standard 50% / 15% unless same-floor connection applies.

### 7. Communal podium gardens (non-residential)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Location | Under footprint of a **non-residential tower** |
| Use of floor | Sitting-out only; **no occupied accommodation** on same floor |
| Access | Common area only |
| Clear height | ≥ **4.5 m** |
| Cross-vent | ≥ two opposite sides open above safe parapet (same parapet footnotes as sky gardens) |
| Service lifts | Where cargo/service lifts exist → garden **not** served by them |
| Exhaust | Must not face garden |
| Net area | ≥ **50%** of **tower footprint** (not whole site); multi-parcels connected by communal circulation |
| Greenery | ≥ **15%** of tower footprint in “L” zone (same L / 1.5 m lateral rules) |
| Notice / commercial ban | Same as sky gardens |
| Maintenance manual | Required |
| PlanD BH | No concern |
| Lease extras | Commercial prohibited; **no shop front** directly abutting communal podium garden |
| APP-151 | Pre-requisites apply |

### 8. Acoustic fins

| Rule | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Function | Mitigate unwanted / excessive sound |
| Structure | **Non-load-bearing** — self-weight + wind on own surface only |
| Lot boundary | Must not project beyond |
| Justification | Quantitative assessment — effective and reasonable size |
| APP-151 | Pre-requisites apply |

---

### 9. Wing walls, wind catchers and funnels

| Element | Design intent |
|---|---|
| Wing wall | Fin channelling wind into the building; non-load-bearing (self-weight + wind on own surface) |
| Wind catcher | Captures cooling breeze; deliberate use of buoyancy |
| Funnel | Uses building height for stack/chimney effect (thermo-syphonic ventilation), especially windless hours; hybrid mechanical + natural stack systems encouraged where unmodified hot humid air would not comfort/conserve energy |
| Common | Must not project beyond lot; quantitative assessment of provision and size; APP-151 pre-requisites |

### 10. Triple-regime application (design-critical procedure)

#### 10.1 Under the BO

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Application | Modification with supporting documents **and undertaking** |
| Timing | Exemptions may be given under BO before lease mod / premium completion, subject to TPO compliance |
| Enquiry | Respective Chief Building Surveyor |

#### 10.2 Under the Government Lease

| Scenario | Treatment |
|---|---|
| New auction/tender sites with GFA/SC restrictions | Lease may include clause allowing same BA exemptions **without premium**, if App. A conditions met and design/disposition acceptable to Director of Lands |
| Existing leases with GFA/SC restrictions | Same BA exemptions generally available; if lease limits **storeys or height** and feature breaches → **lease modification required** |
| Balconies on existing leases | May use lease exemption provision **or** lease mod + premium + admin fee + Lands conditions |
| Other JPN 1 features (not balconies) | Often exemptible if BA-exempt and Lands design/disposition OK — subject to corridor house exclusion, sky-garden sitting-out/elderly/commercial limits, podium commercial/shopfront limits |
| Conflict with JPN 4 | Para. 9 of **this JPN prevails** over JPN 4 on GFA/SC exemption of green features under existing leases until a new JPN 4 is promulgated |
| Pre-requisite | Lease GFA/SC exemption requires **prior BA grant** |
| Parallel track | Submit lease mod to DLO with BD submission early; BD alerts DLO on in-principle no-objection; after BA approval send approved plans + demand-note receipt to DLO |
| Enquiry timing | DLO reply normally ~30 days; or via centralised processing 8 weeks (non-BC III) / 10 weeks (BC III) |

#### 10.3 Under the TPO

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Criteria | PlanD adopts same App. A criteria, subject to no contravention of OZP/DPAP **BH and/or SC** |
| Exceedance | If GFA exemption pushes BH/SC above OZP → TPB **minor relaxation** (if OZP provides); case-by-case merits |
| Previously approved schemes | Class A / Class B amendments per TPB Guidelines |
| Enquiry | Respective District Planning Office |

### 11. Conditions BA will impose (App. A §2) — design & DMC hooks

| Condition | Content |
|---|---|
| Undertaking | Letter of undertaking registered in Land Registry **before OP application** |
| DMC designations | (i) Balconies + covered areas beneath = **non-enclosed areas**; (ii) sky/podium gardens = **common areas**; (iii) AC platforms on balconies (CoP Ext. Maint. App. B) = **areas for air-conditioning** — individual AC platforms must **not** be erected on external walls |
| No DMC | Same designations into SPA / Assignment / Tenancy |
| Ongoing | Binding conditions for control, operation, financial support, maintenance |
| Balcony enclosure ban | Must not enclose above safe parapet |
| Exhaust ban | Must not open into balcony / covered area |
| Garden use | Exclusive use of owners, tenants, visitors only; no other purpose/person without BA prior consent |

**SD takeaway:** Budget APP-151 cap before stacking balcony + corridor + sky garden. Prefer RRF-connected sky-garden voids (outside cap) on tall residential. Never assume hotel or podium non-dom can take “residential” concessions. Freeze balcony vs UP vs projecting-window strategy per room before façade freeze.
