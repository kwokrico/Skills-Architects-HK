# Joint Practice Note No. 8 — Enhanced Facilitation Measures for Modular Integrated Construction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issued July 2022 | Effective **1 August 2022** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD/GR/1-125/126 (IV) · LD BP/7-4/5/3 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Dedicated MiC incentives (third green/innovative JPN after JPN 1 & 2). Supersedes MiC content formerly in JPN 2 / repealed **PNAP APP-161** for new submissions. Applies to new GBP / major revisions on/after **1 Aug 2022**. Pre-JPN 8 approved MiC plans may reference Sept 2019 JPN 2 + repealed APP-161. Cross-check APP-151 (MiC GFA disregard is **outside** the 10% cap), APP-19, JPN 4 Group II(B), JPN 5 BHR.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 8 facilitates MiC by (a) granting **10% of MiC floor area** disregarded from GFA and from SC per floor, and (b) supporting **up to 4%** additional building height based on total storey height of qualifying MiC floors — across BO, planning (s.16 minor-relaxation support), and lease (fast-track modification).

At schematic design, decide MiC module grid, wall/slab build-ups, and which floors qualify as “MiC floors” (≥50% MiC area) before locking PR, SC, and BH budgets. Concessions revoke if MiC is not adopted or MiC area shrinks.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What MiC is (and why concessions exist)

| Definition | Freestanding volumetric modules (finishes, fixtures, fittings, etc.) manufactured off-site, transported, assembled on site |
|---|---|
| Benefits cited | Better QC; shorter programme; less neighbourhood nuisance; less waste; simplified process |

| MiC design penalty | Concession response |
|---|---|
| Repetitive **double walls** between modules | GFA/SC disregard |
| Thicker enclosure walls for rigging/hoisting | GFA/SC disregard |
| Lengthened common corridors / internal doorways | GFA/SC disregard |
| Double / thickened **slabs** → higher storey height | BH relaxation up to 4% |

---

### 2. GFA and SC concessions under the BO (s.42)

| Concession | Quantum | APP-151 interaction |
|---|---|---|
| GFA | **10%** of MiC floor area disregarded from development GFA | **Not** subject to overall GFA cap of 10% in APP-151 |
| SC | **10%** of MiC floor area at **each floor level** need not be counted for SC | Per floor |

#### 2.1 Defining MiC floor area

| Include | Exclude |
|---|---|
| Floor area within external walls of the **combined MiC modules** | Minor projecting features (APP-19 para. 3) |
| Floor areas of associated **construction joints** | Acoustic fins |
| Thickness of such walls | Noise barriers |
| If MiC used for plant rooms / similar services | Curtain walls |
| If MiC used for green/amenity features already GFA-exempt (balcony, UP, common corridor/lobby, non-structural prefab external wall) — those areas **may still be included** in MiC floor area for working out the 10% | Cladding |
| | **AC platforms** under APP-19 — exclude even if combined with balconies/UPs |

#### 2.2 Revocation / adjustment

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| MiC not adopted in the proposed building | GFA and/or SC concessions **revoked** |
| Subsequent reduction in MiC floor area | Concessions **adjusted** downward |

---

### 3. Planning and lease follow-through on GFA/SC

| Regime | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **PlanD** | Follows BD exemption of MiC floor area from GFA and/or SC, subject to no contravention of specific restrictions on the statutory plan |
| **Lease with** green/innovative GFA/SC exemption clause (e.g. many Conditions of Sale) allowing BA-exempt green features without premium/admin fee | Approval following BD’s MiC exemption given at building-plan checking stage |
| **Lease without** such clause | Director of Lands absolute discretion; may allow exemptions on terms; **fast-track** lease mod and/or consent; premium (where applicable) at **standard rates**; LandsD to issue practice note |

---

### 4. Building height facilitation — up to 4%

#### 4.1 Core rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligible uplift | Increase of BH up to **4%** of the **total storey height of MiC floors** |
| Qualifying “MiC floor” | Floor where MiC floor area ≥ **50%** of the **total area on that floor** |
| Total area on that floor | Area of the site covered by that floor; **exclude** from denominator: APP-19 minor projections, acoustic fins, noise barriers, curtain wall, cladding |
| Storey height of MiC floor | Measured from **lowest level of the MiC module** to **highest level of the MiC module** on that floor, **including** thickness of slab(s) + associated construction joints below |
| Support quantum | Up to 4% of total MiC-floor storey heights — **irrespective** of actual extra vertical space MiC consumes |

#### 4.2 Worked example (App. A format)

| Floor | % MiC floor area | Storey height contribution |
|---|---|---|
| G/F | 100% | 5 m |
| 1/F | 100% | 5 m |
| 2/F–3/F | 70% | 4 m × 2 = 8 m |
| 4/F–10/F | 50% | 4 m × 7 = 28 m |
| 11/F–15/F | 30% | **N.A.** (below 50% — not MiC floors) |
| **(a) Total storey height of MiC floors** | | **46 m** |
| **(b) Max eligible BH relaxation** | 4% × 46 m | **1.84 m** |

**SD:** Partial-MiC upper floors that fall below 50% contribute **nothing** to the BH budget — either push them over 50% or don’t rely on them for BHR relief.

### 5. Planning path for BH relaxation

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| If BH exceeds OZP BHR | s.16 application for **minor relaxation** (if OZP provides); TPB case-by-case merits |
| PlanD stance | Prepared to **support** MiC-driven applications |
| Evidence — already zoned; MiC-only reason | Simple **visual appraisal** sufficient |
| AVA | Generally **not** required; major breezeways may need simple air-ventilation review |
| Traffic / env / infra assessments | Normally **not** required if no increase in PR/GFA |
| GFA disregarded under this JPN / other JPNs | Does **not** constitute increase in PR/GFA |
| Approved schemes | PlanD supports minor amendments via **s.16A**; TPB Guidelines Class A/B apply |
| Class A tip | If max BH was **not** an approval condition, adopting MiC is Class A if change does not exceed extant OZP BH and planning brief BH |
| Class B | Considerations in this JPN relevant |
| What to identify in application | MiC floor(s) + corresponding storey height(s) |
| Support ceiling | Up to 4% of total MiC-floor storey heights |

#### GBP stage certification (App. A)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Authorized Person |
| To whom | PlanD DPO and/or LandsD via centralised processing |
| With | GBP submission |
| Content | Certify MiC floors and storey heights true and complete (form in App. A); demonstrate on plans to BA |
| False certification | May attract legal action |

---

### 6. Lease path for BH relaxation

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| If MiC BH breaches lease BHR | Lease modification to permit excess up to same **4%** quantum |
| Terms | As imposed by LandsD |
| Process | Fast-track; premium at standard rates where applicable |
| Certification | Same App. A-style certification + demonstrate on building plans; submit to LandsD via centralised processing |

---

### 7. Revocation of BH permissions

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| MiC not adopted as submitted | Planning permission for minor BH relaxation **no longer applicable** |
| Same for lease | Lease BH permission / modification benefit falls away |

---

### 8. Relationship to older MiC incentives

| Era | Instrument | GFA disregard |
|---|---|---|
| **Current** (GBP on/after 1 Aug 2022) | **JPN 8** | **10%** of MiC floor area (+ 10% SC per floor) |
| **Legacy** (BA-approved MiC plans before JPN 8) | JPN 2 (Sept 2019) + repealed **APP-161** | **6%** of MiC floor area (APP-161); not subject to APP-151 10% cap |
| JPN 2 update | July 2022 version removed MiC references | Use JPN 8 going forward |

---

### 9. Triple-regime reminder

BD, PlanD, and LandsD act in different capacities — action by one does not bind the others. JPN is general guidance; case-specific circumstances may differ. Lease rights reserved.

---

### 10. SD checklist

1. Decide full-MiC vs hybrid; map floors that clear the **50%** MiC-area test.  
2. Define MiC floor area carefully — include joints and MiC amenity modules; **exclude** AC platforms, CW, cladding, APP-19 minor projections.  
3. Budget **+10% MiC GFA** outside APP-151 cap; coordinate other concessions still under the cap.  
4. Apply **10% SC** disregard per MiC floor in podium/tower SC checks.  
5. Convert double-slab build-up into BH: Σ qualifying storey heights × **4%**.  
6. If OZP/lease BHR is tight, programme s.16 / lease mod with App. A certification.  
7. Warn client: drop MiC later → lose GFA/SC/BH facilitation.
