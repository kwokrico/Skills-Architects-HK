# Joint Practice Note No. 6 — Sustainable Building Design Guidelines: Building Separation and Building Setback
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issued September 2019 | Effective **15 October 2019** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GR/1-125/6/0 · LD BP/7-4/16/5 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Streamlines compliance checking for **building separation** and **building setback** under SBD Guidelines. Dimensional criteria live in **PNAP APP-152** (summarised below for SD). **SCG** streamlining is in **JPN 3**. SBD kicks in via lease, planning advisory clauses, and/or **APP-151** GFA-concession pre-requisites.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 6 designates **BD as the sole authority** to interpret, accept, and check building separation and building setback under APP-152 — whether triggered by lease or as a GFA-concession pre-requisite. Separate LandsD submissions for these two items are not required once BD accepts the GBP proposal and completed works match.

At schematic design, freeze continuous projected façade length (Lp), permeability gaps, and street setbacks to APP-152 (or any **higher** lease/B/D standard) before massing lock — failure blocks APP-151 concessions and lease compliance.

### 2. Who accepts what (JPN 6 process)

| Scenario | Process |
|---|---|
| New/modified leases | Director of Buildings named as approving authority; lease SBD clauses satisfied when BD accepts separation/setback on GBP **and** development completed per APP-152 to BD’s satisfaction |
| A&A after completion | Any A&A affecting the two SBD requirements must still satisfy BD — else lease SBD clauses fail |
| Existing leases (DoB not named) | LandsD prepared to treat BD-accepted GBP proposal as lease compliance; if proposal fails lease prescription → seek LandsD written approval/consent (setback consent may attract premium + admin fee) |
| Higher than APP-152 | Another B/D may impose stricter separation/setback for urban design — still compute/show APP-152 on GBP for BD comment; named B/D decides acceptance of higher bar at design and completion |
| AP duty | Demonstrate **and certify** on GBP compliance with separation and setback per APP-152 |
| Separate LandsD pack | **Not** required under streamlined arrangement |

### 3. Building separation — when it applies (APP-152)

Comply if **either**:

| Trigger | Threshold |
|---|---|
| (a) Large site | Site ≥ **20,000 m²** |
| (b) Long façade on smaller site | Site < 20,000 m² **and** building or group of buildings has continuous projected façade length **Lp ≥ 60 m** |

| Exemption | Standalone residential blocks with height ≤ **15 m** — exempt from separation requirements and disregarded in assessment of other buildings |

---

### 4. Building separation — Design Requirement (1): Lp

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Limit | Lp of a building or group of buildings along a street ≤ maximum permissible |
| Formula basis | Max Lp = **5 × mean width of street canyon (U)** |
| Detail method | APP-152 Appendix B |

**SD:** On narrow streets, U is small → max Lp is tight → break long podiums/towers early.

---

### 5. Building separation — Design Requirement (2): Separating Distance (S) and Permeability (P)

#### 5.1 Permeability composition

| Component | Share of P |
|---|---|
| Intervening Space (IS) | Minimum **2/3** of P |
| Permeable Element (PE) | Maximum **1/3** of P |

Assessed on **two vertical projection planes**.

#### 5.2 Minimum P (APP-152 Table 1)

| Height H of tallest building | Site < 20,000 m² and Lp ≥ 60 m — each plane | Site ≥ 20,000 m² — Plane 1 | Site ≥ 20,000 m² — Plane 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H ≤ 60 m** | **20%** | **20%** | **25%** |
| **H > 60 m** | **20%** | **20%** | **33.3%** |

#### 5.3 Separating distance S for Intervening Space

| Rule | Dimension |
|---|---|
| IS between projected façade and site boundaries **or** centreline of adjoining streets/lanes | S ≥ **7.5 m** wide |
| If IS not enough to make 2/3 of P | Provide additional IS with S ≥ **15 m** between two projected building façades |

Assessment zones and measurement method: APP-152 Appendix B. Minor noise barriers that are not extensive in height/extent may be disregarded in separation assessment (APP-152).

### 6. Building setback — when and how (APP-152)

#### 6.1 Trigger

Buildings fronting a street **< 15 m wide** must set back to comply with **one** of the following.

#### 6.2 Option (a) — 15 m × 15 m ventilation corridor

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Geometry | No part of building up to **15 m** above street level within **7.5 m** from **centreline** of the street |
| Effect | Maintains ventilation corridor min section **15 m × 15 m** |
| Varying street level | Keep minimum sectional area along full frontage following street profile |
| Figures | APP-152 Appendix C Figs C1–C2 |

#### 6.3 Option (b) — 45° inclined plane with podium garden

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Condition | Provide **cross-ventilated communal podium garden** with clear height ≥ **4.5 m** |
| Geometry | No part of building up to 15 m above street level may protrude above **45° inclined plane** with base at street level on **site boundary line on the opposite side of the street** |
| Figures | APP-152 Appendix C Figs C3–C4; examples C5–C9 |

#### 6.4 What BA may allow within setback

| Item | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Structures higher than 15 m above street | May build **over** setback area |
| Uncovered setback | Canopy complying with B(P)R **reg. 10** may be permitted |
| Minor projecting features / signboards | Project ≤ **600 mm** from external walls; clear height ≥ **2.5 m** above street |
| Footbridges | Single-storey footbridges across setback may be permitted |
| Columns | Supporting building above — per Fig C2 requirements |
| Setback ground use | Properly landscaped and paved; open without permanent building structures other than landscaped features, perforated balustrades, perforated boundary walls, structural columns |
| GFA tip | Setback area at ground under footprint of over-sailing structures / under canopy may be GFA-exempt if designated **common parts**, accessible by occupants, **no commercial activities** |

#### 6.5 Setback exemption

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Whole or part exemption | Possible where building height < **2 × mean width** of the street |
| Height measure here | From mean level of abutting street to mean height of roof over highest usable floor space |

---

### 7. Special considerations (APP-152)

BA takes flexible/pragmatic stance for genuine constraints (ferry piers, railway terminals, stadia; adaptive reuse of historic buildings where façades/layout are character-defining). Alternative approaches: APP-152 Appendix E.

---

### 8. SD checklist

1. Confirm SBD trigger: lease-mandatory, APP-151 pre-requisite, planning advisory, or combination.  
2. Measure site area vs 20,000 m² and Lp vs 60 m → separation on/off.  
3. On streets < 15 m: choose Option (a) 7.5 m from centreline **or** Option (b) 45° + 4.5 m podium garden.  
4. Design permeability: ≥2/3 true gaps (7.5 m / 15 m), ≤1/3 PE; meet Table 1 % on both planes.  
5. If lease/B/D is stricter than APP-152, design to the higher bar but still annotate APP-152 on GBP.  
6. Protect accepted gaps/setbacks through A&A and retail fit-out — lease compliance runs with completed works.
