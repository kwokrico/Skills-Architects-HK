# Joint Practice Note No. 3 — Landscape and Site Coverage of Greenery
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue August 2003 | This issue April 2019 (revamped) | Effective **15 May 2019** | BD / LandsD / PlanD  
Ref: BD GR/1-125/6/0 · LD BP/7-4/16/3 · TPB/C/TPB/35

> Scope note: Streamlines **who** processes landscape submissions (LMP/LP) vs **site coverage of greenery (SCG)** under PNAP APP-152. BO has **no** power for BD to impose landscape. Cross-check PlanD PN 1/2019, LandsD LAO PN 6/2003, LAO PN 7/2007 & 7/2007A (trees), APP-151, APP-152, JPN 6.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 3 separates two often-confused tracks: **landscape** (planning and/or lease — PlanD or LandsD / named B/D) and **SCG** (BD sole interpreter under APP-152, also often a lease condition and APP-151 GFA-concession pre-requisite).

At schematic design, freeze a Landscape Layout Plan that protects planting from bulk, and an SCG strategy meeting APP-152 Table 2 (or any higher lease %) before podium, roof, and streetscape are fixed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two tracks — never conflate

| Track | Definition | Legal home | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Landscape submissions** | LMP or LP — treatment of existing landscape resources, broad design of landscape provisions/features, info per PlanD PN 1/2019 | Planning regime and/or lease | PlanD and/or LandsD / named B/D |
| **SCG submissions** | Quantitative greenery % on GBP per APP-152; also under lease | Building regime (+ lease) | **BD** |

| Critical reminder | Detail |
|---|---|
| BO | No provision for BD to impose landscape requirements |
| Approvals don’t travel | PlanD landscape approval and/or BD SCG acceptance ≠ compliance with **other** lease conditions |
| Consistency | LMP/LP and SCG on GBP must **tally** with each other and not contravene lease |

---

### 2. Landscape — who checks (decision tree)

#### 2.1 Tied to planning application / planning conditions

| Factor | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Landscape in planning app or planning conditions | **PlanD** checks submission **and** implementation — even if lease also has landscape clauses |
| Landscaped areas **not** handed to Government after completion | Streamlined dual PlanD/LandsD procedure below applies from effective date |
| Rare case: landscape submitted with planning app but TPB approved **without** landscape conditions | Post-implementation lease landscape clause → **LandsD** checks |

**Deemed lease compliance:** PlanD approval of landscape = fulfilment of lease landscape clause **if** application for deemed approval submitted to LandsD per LAO PN 6/2003 (or successor).

#### 2.2 Lease-only landscape (no planning landscape)

| Factor | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Landscape as lease condition only | LandsD (or B/D named in lease) checks submission and implementation |
| Existing leases where “Director of Lands” is approving authority | LandsD lead |
| New/modified leases — PlanD or B/D requested LMP/LP | Lease names PlanD or that B/D as approving + compliance authority; procedures normally follow §4(b)–(d) |
| New/modified leases — no such request | **Simple landscape clause** only: landscape and plant trees/shrubs on unbuilt parts of lot and maintain — **no** landscape submission required |

#### 2.3 Areas outside private lot / hand-back

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Default | Procedures above apply to **private lots only** |
| Hand-back / outside lot | Concerned departments per lease clauses |
| Optional | If those departments agree, private-lot streamlined procedures may apply |

---

### 3. LMP processing (planning-connected cases)

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Preparer | **Registered Landscape Architect (RLA)** under Cap. 516 — **mandatory** for LMP and amendments |
| Submit to | Concerned District Planning Office (DPO) **before commencement of landscape works** |
| Completion | RLA **Self-Certification of Compliance (SCC)** + copies of latest approved LMP (or approved LMP with minor amendments duly coloured) to DPO **and** PlanD Landscape Unit |
| SCC content | Certifies works implemented per approved LMP; standard form in PlanD PN 1/2019 |
| Minor amendments | Generally **Class A** per TPB Guidelines No. 36B |
| After SCC | DPO acknowledges; Landscape Unit randomly selects for **full** compliance check |
| Selected + non-compliant | Discharge of planning condition and/or Certificate of Compliance under lease may be **withheld** |
| Not selected | Deemed satisfactorily completed **2 weeks** after PlanD receives SCC unless PlanD informs otherwise; DPO issues written compliance to RLA |

---

### 4. LP processing

| Path | Treatment |
|---|---|
| RLA appointed for LP preparation **and** implementation certification | Same procedures as LMP (§4(b)–(c)) |
| No RLA | Completed works subject to **full** compliance checking by PlanD Landscape Unit |

---

### 5. Timing gates vs construction (critical for programme)

| Lease / site condition | Landscape approval required before |
|---|---|
| Tree preservation requirement under lease | **Site formation works** |
| No tree preservation under lease | **Superstructure works** |
| Tree PNs | LandsD LAO PN 7/2007 and 7/2007A (or successors) |

**SD / programme:** Do not start site formation on tree-sensitive lots without approved landscape path.

---

### 6. Landscape Layout Plan vs GBP

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| When | Developments with landscape as **planning permission condition** |
| Timing | Submit to concerned DPO **prior to or at** GBP submission |
| Depth | **Not** as detailed as LMP/LP — basic landscape design concept only |
| Purpose | Prove landscape provisions will **not be unduly compromised** by building design |
| Role | Basis for later LMP/LP; details in PlanD PN 1/2019 |

**SD takeaway:** Landscape Layout Plan is a massing companion — protect ground-level planting, podium gardens, and tree zones from late plant rooms, driveways, and loading bays.

---

### 7. SCG — BD sole agent (plus APP-152 design numbers)

#### 7.1 Role split

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nature | One of three SBD key elements; **independent** of landscape requirements above |
| Triggers | Lease conditions; APP-151 GFA-concession pre-requisite where applicable |
| Authority | **BD** interprets SCG, accepts proposals, checks completion per **PNAP APP-152** |
| New/modified leases | Clause drafted so BD acceptance of SCG on GBP = lease satisfied; **no** separate LandsD SCG submission |
| Higher than APP-152 | Another B/D may demand higher % — still show on GBP; BD comments/computes within APP-152; named B/D decides if higher bar met at design and completion |
| Existing leases | LandsD approval normally required; **alternative:** show SCG on GBP for BD acceptance — LandsD prepared to regard BD acceptance as lease-compliant subject to any **prescriptive** lease wording |
| Lot/site area mismatch | If BD-accepted SCG fails lease prescription → lease modification (+ premium if assessed + admin fee) |

#### 7.2 APP-152 SCG thresholds (design numbers JPN 3 points to)

| Site area | Min SCG — primary zone | Min SCG — overall |
|---|---|---|
| **1,000 – <20,000 m²** | **10%** | **20%** |
| **≥ 20,000 m²** | **15%** | **30%** |
| Single family house only | SCG requirement **not** applicable | — |

Primary zone = pedestrian-level greenery emphasis under APP-152 Appendix D. Detail planting types, soil, vertical greenery counting rules live in APP-152 — not restated in JPN 3.

#### 7.3 Transparency

APP-152: after OP, SCG plans/details uploaded to BD website.

---

### 8. SD checklist

1. Map whether landscape is planning-driven, lease-only LMP/LP, or simple landscape clause.  
2. If trees under lease → sequence LMP approval **before site formation**.  
3. Appoint RLA early for LMP (mandatory) / LP (recommended to use SCC path).  
4. Issue Landscape Layout Plan with first GBP if planning landscape condition exists.  
5. Size SCG to the **higher** of APP-152 Table 2 and lease %; protect primary-zone greenery in massing.  
6. Cross-check LMP/LP vs GBP SCG vs lease — three documents must align.
