# Cap 311 — Air Pollution Control Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version last updated **11 April 2025** (TOC) | Cap. 311 | Authority: air pollution control authority (typically DEP) under s. 4

> Scope note: Cap. 311 is the primary statute for **air quality objectives (AQOs)**, **specified-process licensing**, **abatement / chimney control**, and **asbestos** (investigation, abatement, and ban on use/supply). It is **not** Cap. **499** EIA (which uses these AQOs as approval criteria), and it is **not** the Cap. 311 subsidiary regulations (construction dust, open burning, fuel restriction, furnaces/chimneys, asbestos control, etc.) — those implement detail under s. 43. Primary SD gates: **Schedule 5 AQOs** (EIA / EP variation), **Schedule 1** (will the brief trigger a specified-process licence?), and **Parts VIII–X asbestos** (survey before demolition / A&A / strip-out).

## Regulatory Overview

Cap. 311 abates, prohibits and controls atmospheric pollution: air control zones and statutory **AQOs** (Part II / Schedule 5); control of polluting processes via technical memoranda and **air pollution abatement notices** (Part III); **licensing of premises** used for **Schedule 1 specified processes** (Part IV), with **closure notices** for suspected unlicensed operation (Part VA from Ord. 5 of 2025); enforcement powers including chimney / plant modification (Part V); and a full **asbestos** regime plus a ban on use/supply/import/transhipment (Parts VIII–X).

Except as provided by s. **43(1)(p)**, the Ordinance does **not** apply to air pollutants from furnaces or engines used to propel vessels, railway locomotives or aircraft (s. **3**).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Air quality objectives — Schedule 5 (ss. 6–8, 7A)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Zones | CE in Council may declare **air control zones** (s. **6**) |
| AQOs | **Schedule 5** prescribes AQOs for **every** air control zone (s. **7A(1)**; Sch. 5 Pt 1 §2) |
| Review | Secretary must review AQOs at least once every **5-year** review period from **1 Jan 2014** (s. **7A(3),(6)**) |
| Authority duty | Aim to **achieve** relevant AQOs ASAP and thereafter **maintain** them (s. **8(2)**) |
| Reference conditions (gases) | Adjust to **293 K** and **101.325 kPa** (Sch. 5 Pt 1 §3) |

**Schedule 5 concentration limits (post–11 Apr 2025):**

| Pollutant | Averaging | Limit | Exceedance allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SO₂** | 10-min | **500 μg/m³** | ≤ **3** periods / year |
| **SO₂** | 1 day | **40 μg/m³** | ≤ **3** days / year |
| **RSP (PM₁₀)** | 1 day | **75 μg/m³** | ≤ **9** days / year |
| **RSP (PM₁₀)** | calendar year | **30 μg/m³** | — |
| **FSP (PM₂.₅)** | 1 day | **37.5 μg/m³** | ≤ **18** days / year |
| **FSP (PM₂.₅)** | calendar year | **15 μg/m³** | — |
| **NO₂** | 1 hour | **200 μg/m³** | ≤ **18** hours / year |
| **NO₂** | 1 day | **120 μg/m³** | ≤ **9** days / year |
| **NO₂** | calendar year | **40 μg/m³** | — |
| **O₃** | max daily 8-h mean | **160 μg/m³** | ≤ **9** days / year |
| **O₃** | peak season | **100 μg/m³** | — |
| **CO** | 1 hour | **30 000 μg/m³** | — |
| **CO** | 1 day | **4 000 μg/m³** | — |
| **CO** | max daily 8-h mean | **10 000 μg/m³** | — |
| **Lead** | calendar year | **0.5 μg/m³** | — |

**SD takeaway:** Lock Cap. **499** EIA / EP strategy against these AQOs before massing and plant-room decisions; Ord. **5 of 2025** transitional EP-variation rules sit in the Amendment Ordinance, not Cap. 311 itself.

### 2. Specified processes — Schedule 1 licence gate (ss. 11–15)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Licence | Owner **must not** use / permit premises for a **Schedule 1** process without a licence (s. **13(1)**), subject to s. **20** exemptions |
| Best practicable means | Owner of SP premises **shall** use BPM to prevent noxious/offensive emissions and render discharged emissions harmless/inoffensive (s. **12(1)**) |
| Public notice | Licence applications publicly notified; objections allowed if process would tend to inhibit attainment/maintenance of any relevant AQO (s. **14**) |
| Grant criteria | Authority must consider applicant’s BPM capability, purpose of attaining/maintaining AQOs, and whether noxious/offensive emissions would be / likely be **prejudicial to health** (s. **15(3)**) |
| Licence term | Reasonable period of **not less than 2 years** (s. **15(4)**) |
| Conditions | May cover Sch. **2** matters (place/times of emission, **chimney design/construction**, discharge rate, treatment plant, monitoring, records, access) |
| Electricity “specified licence” | Item **7** fossil-fuel generation **> 5 MW** (not stand-by only) also pulls Part **IVB** emission allowances (ss. **26G–26N**, Sch. **2A**) |

**Architect-relevant Schedule 1 triggers (thresholds abbreviated — check full item text):**

| # | Process | Key trigger for SD |
|---|---|---|
| **3** | **Cement Works** | Handle cement / produce or grind clinker **and** any of: bulk storage **> 50 t**; concrete mixer capacity **> 3 m³**; or hourly concrete output **> 20 m³/h**. Storage includes land **and ship/barge** facilities connected to operational equipment (Ord. **5 of 2025**) |
| **7** | Electricity Works | Fossil fuel burnt for generation; installed capacity **> 5 MW** |
| **10** | Metal Recovery | Scrap treated in furnace for metal recovery **> 50 kg/h** (primary object) |
| **11** | Mineral Works | Size reduction / grading / heating of slag, PFA or minerals **> 5 000 t/a** evolving dust |
| **12** | Incinerators | Installed capacity **> 0.5 t/h** for destroying wastes/refuse |
| **24** | Pathological Waste Incinerators | **> 50 kg/h** medical/hospital/pathological waste |
| **25** | Organic Chemical Works | Includes organic liquid / liquid-fuel tanks with installed capacity **> 100 m³** |
| **19** | Asbestos Works | Milling/blending raw asbestos or manufacturing asbestos products |

**Premises (s. 2):** includes place, building, relevant plant, and **any part** thereof; for **Cement Works (item 3)** also includes a **ship or barge** (or part).

**Owner (s. 2):** includes lessee/occupier, construction contractor in possession of a site, building management for common areas, person carrying on the SP, and ship master/controller.

**SD takeaway:** Before locking industrial / batching / energy / incineration briefs, screen Schedule 1 — unlicensed SP is an offence (s. **13**) and can trigger **closure notices** (Part VA).

### 3. Air pollution control plan — sensitive receptors (s. 14A)

Authority may require a licence applicant to submit an **air pollution control plan**, which may include:

| Plan element | Design implication |
|---|---|
| Chimney / plant particulars | Freeze flue locations, heights, and plant that evolves pollutants |
| BPM control equipment / measures | Space, access, and services for abatement plant |
| Maps of affected receptors | **Residential buildings, schools, hospitals, medical clinics** and other buildings/structures that may be affected |
| AQ / health risk assessment | Vicinity of premises **and** identified buildings |
| Monitoring scheme | Source and/or ambient monitoring hardware and access |

**SD takeaway:** Treat receptor mapping (schools, hospitals, residences) as a site-planning constraint equal to setbacks — not a post-tender compliance memo.

### 4. General air pollution control — TM, abatement, chimneys (ss. 9–10, 30)

| Instrument | Architect flag |
|---|---|
| Technical memorandum (s. **9**) | Secretary may set principles/standards for predicting, measuring and abating pollution from a **polluting process** |
| Abatement notice (s. **10**) | If emission causes/contributes to existing or **imminent** air pollution → cease, reduce, or take other abatement steps; may require **immediate** compliance |
| Abatement factors (s. **10(2)**) | Relative location of source vs affected place; locality; time/duration/frequency; dust/grit deposit; **objectionable odour**; staining/corrosion; sensory irritation; colour/opacity disturbance; public safety; other effects unreasonable for the public to suffer |
| Carve-out (s. **10(6)**) | Does **not** apply to licensed SP under s. **15**, or Cap. **132** licensed offensive trades (odour) |
| Chimney / plant notice (s. **30**) | Authority may require modify/replace/clean/repair, install control equipment, change operation method, or **prohibit** specified fuel/material — for unsuitable design, poor maintenance, wear, unsuitable fuel, or improper operation |

**Definitions that catch ordinary buildings (s. 2):**
- **Chimney** = any structure/opening through which air pollutant may be emitted, including **flues**; includes chimneys structurally separate from the premises they serve
- **Relevant plant** = furnace, engine, oven or industrial plant
- **Polluting process** = activity/process or operation of chimney, relevant plant, machinery or equipment that evolves air pollutant

**SD takeaway:** Kitchen exhaust, generator flues, and industrial plant discharges are Cap. 311 **chimneys** — design for modification/abatement space and neighbour sensitive-use distance early.

### 5. Closure notices for unlicensed specified processes (Part VA, ss. 30C–30N)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Authority has reasonable cause to believe s. **13** offence has been / is being committed (s. **30D(1)**) |
| Effect | Notice affixed conspicuously; service on person conducting the SP |
| Ongoing prohibitions | Conducting the SP on subject premises; entering/remaining (with limited permissions); interfering with notice / locks / seals |
| Design consequence | Unlicensed cement / industrial SP on site can be **physically closed** — programme risk for batching plants and industrial tenancies |

**SD takeaway:** For Schedule 1 uses (especially cement works post–2025 barge/ship expansion), treat SP licence as a **go/no-go** before construction logistics layout.

### 6. Asbestos — investigation, plans, registered parties (Parts VIII–IX, ss. 51, 69–79)

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Pre-work investigation (s. **69(1)**) | Owner of premises that contain / may reasonably be suspected to contain ACM **shall** engage a **registered asbestos consultant**, submit investigation report (s. **70**) and, if ACM found, abatement plan (s. **71**) at least **28 days before** intended asbestos abatement work or work using/handling ACM |
| Class exemptions | Secretary may Gazette classes of work exempt from report/plan (s. **69(2)–(3)**) or from registered contractor (s. **75(4)–(5)**) |
| Authority-specified premises (s. **69(4)–(8)**) | Unless certified free of ACM by ArchSD Director or Secretary-authorised officer → mandatory investigation; if ACM found → management plan + investigation report |
| Change of use / new purpose (s. **69(5)**) | Conversion or construction that would make premises “specified” under s. **69(4)** → owner must comply as if already specified |
| Notice to start work (s. **73**) | **≥ 28 days’** written notice to Authority before abatement / ACM-handling work |
| Who must be appointed | **Consultant** to supervise management/abatement plans (s. **74**); **contractor** (with supervisors continuously at abatement area) to carry out work (s. **75**); **laboratory** for sampling/analysis (s. **76**) |
| Containment (s. **2**) | Working area isolated to prevent asbestos fibre escape |
| Abatement notice (s. **79**) | Authority may force investigation, plans, registered parties, and control measures if premises/plant/activity contains or may release asbestos |

**Investigation report must assess (s. **70(f)**):** quantity/type/composition; friability; condition; accessibility; position relative to natural/artificial **ventilation paths**; population and activities nearby.

**SD takeaway:** For pre-1990s stock, demolition, strip-out or A&A: budget **28-day** asbestos survey + plan lead-in before works that disturb fabric; ventilation and occupancy adjacency drive abatement zoning.

### 7. Ban on asbestos use / supply (Part X, ss. 80–83)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Ban (s. **80**) | Must **not** use, supply, import or tranship asbestos or ACM (or cause/permit the same) |
| “Use” includes | Affixing, applying, spraying or **installing** on/in premises; adding/mixing into products; wrapping with asbestos/ACM |
| Penalty | Fine **$200,000** and imprisonment **6 months** (s. **80(2)**) |
| Non-application (s. **82**) | Goods in transit; certain proprietary Chinese medicines; use in work in an **industrial undertaking** (Cap. **59** meaning, with stated carve-outs) |
| Exemption (s. **83**) | Authority may exempt on application if warranted and no unacceptable risk |

**SD takeaway:** Do **not** specify asbestos-containing products in new work; treat any ACM discovery as a registered-party abatement problem under Part IX, not a materials substitution footnote.

### 8. What Cap. 311 delegates but does not detail here (s. 43 + CoPs)

| Topic | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Furnace / chimney installation approvals, dark smoke, fuel quality | Cap. 311 **subsidiary regulations** under s. **43** (and related EPD practice) |
| Construction dust, open burning, non-road mobile machinery, etc. | Separate Cap. 311 regs — cite those CS when authored |
| Codes of Practice | s. **37** — CoPs for chimney/plant construction, operation, maintenance, asbestos containment, etc. |
| Appeal | Part **VI** Appeal Board (s. **31** onwards) — licence refusal, notices, asbestos conditions, etc. |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 311 sets the **licence / AQO / asbestos / abatement** skeleton; day-to-day construction dust and chimney approval numbers come from the Cap. 311 regs and CoPs — pull those before detailing site logistics and flue geometry.
