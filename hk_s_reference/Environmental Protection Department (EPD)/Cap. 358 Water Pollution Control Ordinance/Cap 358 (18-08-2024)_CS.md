# Cap 358 — Water Pollution Control Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 18 August 2024 | Cap. 358 | Authority: **Director of Environmental Protection (DEP)**

> Scope note: Cap 358 is the **discharge / deposit control statute** for Hong Kong waters, inland waters (incl. groundwater), and **communal sewers / drains**. It is **not** the Technical Memorandum effluent number tables, **not** Cap. 358AL sewerage connection works detail, and **not** Cap. 466 dumping-at-sea. At SD: map Water Control Zone + receiving environment (foul sewer vs storm drain vs stream / sea / soakaway) → freeze trade / F&B / process effluent vs domestic sewage → size pretreatment / sampling / WWTP plant room against a future DEP licence and the s. 21 TM.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 358 lets the Chief Executive in Council declare **water control zones (WCZs)** and the Secretary for Environment and Ecology set **water quality objectives (WQOs)**; DEP must exercise licensing and enforcement to achieve those WQOs (ss. 4–6). Within a WCZ, **waste / polluting / poisonous / noxious** discharges to waters, flow-impeding discharges to inland waters, and most discharges to communal sewers / drains are **offences unless licensed or carved out** (ss. 8–9, 12).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who decides — Authority and key definitions (s. 2)

| Term | Meaning for SD |
|---|---|
| **Authority** | **Director of Environmental Protection** |
| **Secretary** | Secretary for Environment and Ecology |
| **Waters of Hong Kong** | All **inland waters** + **tidal waters** + **sea within HK boundary** (and, for selected ss., flora / fauna thereof) |
| **Inland waters** | River, stream, watercourse, lake, pool, pond (natural / artificial, above / below ground), dry beds, **groundwater** and ground containing it — **excludes** drains / sewers and isolated ponds that never reach a watercourse / HK sea (unless regs pull them in) |
| **Communal sewer / drain** | Not used exclusively by one discharger; communal sewer **includes a public wastewater treatment facility** |
| **Wastewater** | Water used / generated by human activity then discarded — **excludes unpolluted water** as defined in s. 9(3) |
| **Wastewater treatment facility** | Structure / plant / land intended to change wastewater characteristics — **includes Imhoff tank, septic tank, cesspit, soak-away pit** |
| **Discharge / deposit (extended)** | Causing or permitting matter to **enter** waters / sewer / drain; also depositing anywhere if matter (or a component) is **likely to enter** those waters / sewer / drain within a reasonably foreseeable time by fall, percolation, wind or water (**s. 2(2)–(3)**) |

**SD takeaway:** Site runoff, construction slurry, chemical storage spills, and soakaways that can reach a stream, groundwater, foul or storm network are **in-scope discharges/deposits** — not only a pipe outfall.

### 2. Water Control Zones and WQOs (Part II, ss. 4–6)

| Control | Rule |
|---|---|
| WCZ declaration | CE in Council order in Gazette after ACE consultation; plan / map deposited in Land Registry or other sufficient description (**s. 4**) |
| WQO | Secretary establishes (and may amend) quality that should be achieved / maintained in the public interest; published in Gazette; kept with public register (**s. 5**) |
| DEP duty | Exercise Cap 358 powers to achieve WQOs **as soon as reasonably practicable** and maintain them (**s. 6(3)**) |
| Secretary direction | May direct DEP on licence terms (**s. 20(4)**) or licence variation / cancellation (**s. 24**) where WQO achievement needs it — health danger or harm to sewage plant for s. 9 licences (**s. 6(4)–(5)**) |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm which **WCZ** and published **WQO** apply to the site / outfall before assuming trade-effluent quality or marine / stream discharge is feasible.

### 3. The two prohibition gates — ss. 8 and 9 (lock these before plumbing strategy)

#### 3A. Waters / inland waters — s. 8

| Offence | What is prohibited |
|---|---|
| **s. 8(1)(a)** | Discharge of **any waste or polluting matter** into the **waters of Hong Kong** in a WCZ |
| **s. 8(1)(b)** | Discharge of any matter into **inland waters** in a WCZ that tends (alone or with other matter) to **impede proper flow** in a way leading / likely to lead to **substantial aggravation of pollution** |
| **s. 8(1A)** | Discharge of **any poisonous or noxious matter** into the **waters of Hong Kong** (subject to s. 12(1A) defences) |
| **Occupier / vessel** | If discharge is from premises or vessel, **occupier** or person in command / charge also commits an offence (**s. 8(2)**) |

**s. 8 does not apply to (s. 8(3)):**

| Carve-out | Note |
|---|---|
| Discharge **by way of** communal sewer or communal drain | Controlled under **s. 9** instead |
| Normal vessel / equipment operation | — |
| Discharge needing **Dumping at Sea Ordinance Cap. 466** permit | Sister regime |
| Harbour works / moorings / aids with **Director of Marine** consent; Cap. **127** lease / authorized reclamation works | — |
| **Unpolluted water** as in s. 9(3) | — |

#### 3B. Communal sewers / drains — s. 9

Subject to s. 12, it is an offence to discharge **any matter** into a communal sewer or communal drain in a WCZ **except**:

| Allowed without that prohibition | Destination rule |
|---|---|
| **Domestic sewage** | Into foul communal sewer / drain **vested in and maintained by Government**; or one that conducts into such a foul system; or one that delivers to a WWTF whose **discharge is licensed**; **or** into a communal sewer / drain **Gazetted by DEP** as for foul or surface drainage (**s. 9(1)(a), (aa)**) |
| **Unpolluted water** | Into communal sewer / drain **for surface drainage water** (**s. 9(1)(b)**) |

**Domestic sewage (s. 9(3)) — include / exclude:**

| In | Out |
|---|---|
| Kind / quantity from domestic use of toilet, WC, bath, shower, sink, basin or other sanitary fitment by a person in a household **or at a place of work** | Solid residue from a WWTF |
| | Effluent from a WWTF that uses **electrical or mechanical** equipment |
| | Waste from a **food business** subject to Food Business Regulation (**Cap. 132X**) |

**Unpolluted water (s. 9(3)):** (a) **rain water** from any part of a building (incl. appurtenant area); or (b) water with **no** poisonous, noxious or polluting matter.

**Also outside s. 9 (s. 9(4)):** water used for **firefighting**, life / property emergencies, or **street / thoroughfare cleansing**.

**Occupier** of premises from which a prohibited sewer / drain discharge is made also commits an offence (**s. 9(2)**).

**SD takeaway:** Separate **foul**, **trade / F&B / process**, and **storm** at SD. Do **not** treat kitchen / food-business waste or mechanically treated effluent as free “domestic sewage.” Do **not** put polluted water into the storm drain as “unpolluted.”

### 4. Strict liability flavour and defences (ss. 10–12)

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Mental element | For causing entry / deposit under ss. 8–9, prosecution **need not** prove intention, knowledge or negligence (**s. 10**) |
| Primary defence path | Discharge / deposit made **under and in accordance with** a licence (ss. 15, 16, 20, 23A) or approved farming deposit order (**s. 12**) |
| Emergency | Discharge in emergency to avoid danger to life / property + notify DEP in writing ASAP (**s. 12(1)(e), (1A)**) |
| Cap. 354 overlay | No ss. 8–9 offence if discharge is **licensed under Cap. 354** or complies with Waste Disposal (Livestock Waste) Regulations Cap. **354A** (**s. 12(1B)**) |
| Other ordinances | Grant of other licences / permits **does not** authorize a ss. 8 / 9 breach (**s. 49(2)**) |

**Penalties (order of magnitude for brief risk):** ss. 8(1)/8(2)/9 ordinary — up to **6 months** + **$200,000** first / **$400,000** subsequent + **$10,000/day**; poisonous / noxious (s. 8(1A) or into sewer) — up to **1–2 years** + **$400,000 / $1,000,000** + **$40,000/day** (**s. 11**).

### 5. Licensing regime — when SD must plan for a DEP licence (Parts IV–V)

| Path | When | Design flags |
|---|---|---|
| **New / general licence (s. 20)** | For purposes of **s. 8(1)(a), 8(1)(b) or 9(1)** | Apply in prescribed form (**s. 19**); DEP may refuse if even with conditions discharge endangers health, harms drainage / sewerage / operators, or WQOs unlikely to be achieved (**s. 20(3)**) |
| **Public notification** | Applications for **s. 8** water / inland licences | Register entry + bilingual newspaper notice at applicant cost; **30-day** objection window on WQO grounds (**s. 19(3)–(4)**). **Exempt:** licence to discharge domestic sewage from a **separate household unit** (**s. 19(3A)**) |
| **Licence term** | Grant period **not less than 2 years** (**s. 20(5)**) | Domestic-sewage-only licence may be specified as **indefinite** (subject to s. 24) (**s. 20(6)**; same idea for s. 15 existing) |
| **Licence runs with the discharge** | Not personal | Extends to the discharge / deposit when made by **any** person (**s. 22(4)**) |
| **Technical memorandum (s. 21)** | Secretary may issue TM with **permissible limits** of physical / chemical characteristics | DEP **shall be guided** by applicable TMs when issuing / renewing licences under ss. 15, 20, 23, 23A — **numeric standards live in the TM, not in Cap 358** |
| **Small renewals** | Establishment consuming **≤ 10 m³ water/day** and applied flow ≤ that | Newspaper notification for renewal / s. 16 renewal **disapplied** (**ss. 23(3A), 23A(3A)**) |
| **Foul connection cancels prior licence** | When discharge first enters Government foul sewer / licensed WWTF path | Prior licence for that wastewater stream **deemed cancelled** (**s. 24(7)**) |

**SD takeaway:** Trade effluent, cooling bleed, process water, F&B grease / waste streams, and private WWTF effluent need a **licence strategy** (and plant space) at SD; household domestic to Government foul usually does not — but **process and F&B do**.

### 6. First Schedule — what DEP can lock into the licence (design brief)

Licence terms / conditions may cover (**First Schedule**):

| # | Condition topic | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Place and times / periods of discharge or deposit | Outfall location, operating hours |
| 2 | Design and construction of plant / equipment used to discharge | Pretreatment plant architecture / structure |
| 3 | Rate / total amount of discharge or constituents | Peak flow, tank sizing |
| 4 | Nature, composition, colour, temperature | Treatment performance |
| 5 | Treatment before discharge + plant / equipment to provide, maintain, use | Room / roof / basement for WWTP |
| 6–7 | Inspection, sampling, measuring equipment + maintenance / security | Sampling points, access, secure cabinets |
| 8 | Samples and analysis results to DEP | Lab / courier logistics |
| 9–10 | Records of rate / composition + **authorized officer access** | Meter / log space; access routes |
| 11 | Precautions so discharge does not enter **specified** waters / communal drain / sewer | Bunding, diversion valves, dual containment |

**SD takeaway:** Budget **accessible sampling points**, pretreatment / grease / pH / oil separators as use requires, and secure plant rooms — DEP can make them licence conditions.

### 7. Private WWTF and sewerage connection power (ss. 2, 46(2))

| Topic | Cap 358 hook |
|---|---|
| Private WWTF in definition | Septic / Imhoff / cesspit / soakaway / land disposal count as **wastewater treatment facilities** (**s. 2**) |
| Regulation power | CE in Council may regulate construction / maintenance / demolition of sewerage; **require owner / occupier** to build works to convey wastewater to a sewerage connection point; authorize DEP to build and **recover costs**; require **fill-in / demolish redundant WWTF** after sewer connection; force repairs / takeover of failing WWTFs (**s. 46(2)**) |
| Sister instrument | Detail is in **Water Pollution Control (Sewerage) Regulation** and related Cap. 358 subsidiary legislation — do not design “permanent septic” on sites likely to receive a connection order |

**SD takeaway:** On unsewered sites, size septic / package plant as **interim**, with a clear path to **Government foul connection** and demolition of redundant tanks.

| Power | Architect note |

### 10. SD checklist (Cap 358 only)

1. Confirm **WCZ** + receiving medium: Government **foul**, **storm**, **stream / sea**, **groundwater / soakaway**.
2. Split streams: **domestic sewage** vs **trade / F&B / process** vs **rain / unpolluted** — never mix polluted into storm.
3. If any non-exempt discharge to waters or communal network: plan **DEP licence**, **TM limits**, pretreatment, and **First Schedule** sampling / access.
4. If private WWTF: treat as licensed facility; plan eventual **sewer connection** and tank demolition.
5. Site drainage / storage: assume **s. 2(3)** — anything likely to percolate / wash into waters or sewers is a regulated deposit.
