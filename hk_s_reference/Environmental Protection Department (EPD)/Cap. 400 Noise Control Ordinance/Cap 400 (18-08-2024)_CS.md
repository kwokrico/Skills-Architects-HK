# Cap 400 — Noise Control Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 18 August 2024 | Cap. 400 | Noise Control Authority (typically DEP) + Police (neighbourhood noise)

> Scope note: Cap 400 is the **environmental noise control statute** — neighbourhood / construction / industrial-commercial / intruder-alarm / prescribed-product regimes. It is **not** Cap. 59 occupational noise, **not** Cap. 312 aircraft noise, and **not** a BO / FS Code design manual. Numeric Acceptable Noise Levels (ANLs) and CNP assessment methods live in **Technical Memoranda (TMs)** and subsidiary regs, not in Cap 400 itself. At SD: classify receivers (domestic vs non-domestic plant) → programme construction for **restricted hours / designated areas / percussive piling** → locate plant rooms, A/C, louvres and alarms clear of NSRs → assume **CNP** and possible **noise abatement notice (NAN)** risk on plant.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 400 controls noise from **domestic premises and public places** (ss. 4–5), **construction sites** via Construction Noise Permits (ss. 6–8A), **places other than** domestic / public place / construction site through NANs guided by TMs (ss. 10, 13), **intruder alarms** (ss. 13A–13B), and **prescribed noisy products** (Part III). Aircraft noise is wholly excluded (s. 36); road-vehicle travel noise is carved out of ss. 4–5 (s. 5A).

For schematic design the Ordinance freezes **when** powered plant and piling can run, **where** “designated area” rules bite harder, and **how** building services and commercial plant become NAN-exposed once occupied — without itself printing dB(A) tables.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Key definitions that flip the control regime (s. 2)

| Term | SD meaning |
|---|---|
| **domestic premises** | Premises used **wholly or mainly for residential purposes** as a **separate household unit**; also a hotel / boarding-house part let to a guest. Noise from the **non-residential** part of a mixed building is **not** ss. 4–5 — it falls under **s. 13** |
| **construction site** | Any place where **construction work** is carried out |
| **construction work** | Broad: build / demolish / alter / maintain / repair of buildings and civil works; earth extraction; reclamation; dredging; **piling**; prep works; use of machinery / plant / materials for those ops |
| **powered mechanical equipment (PME)** | Machine / device driven by electrical, chemical or thermal energy (incl. compressed air, steam, hydraulic) whose principal function is mechanical movement |
| **percussive piling** | Driving piles by hammering / other percussive means (drop, diesel, pneumatic, steam, etc.) — **excludes** hand-held portable devices |
| **designated area** | Area established by Gazette under **s. 8A** (densely built-up / NSR-rich zones — see Designated Areas Notice + TM) |
| **annoyance** | Annoyance that would **not** be tolerated by a **reasonable person** (ss. 4–5 / parts of s. 13) |
| **Authority** | Noise Control Authority appointed under **s. 3(1)** |

**SD takeaway:** Label every noise source as domestic / public-place / construction / “other place” **before** sketching plant rooms or night works.

### 2. Neighbourhood noise — dwellings and public places (ss. 4–5, 5A)

| Control | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **s. 4** night / holiday | **11 p.m. – 7 a.m.**, or **any time on a general holiday** | Making / causing noise in domestic premises or public place that is a source of **annoyance**; owner / tenant / occupier / person in charge who **knowingly permits** such noise from domestic premises |
| **s. 5(1)** anytime listed sources | **Any time** | Musical instruments / players / radio / TV; loudspeakers / megaphones; games / pastimes; trade or business — if annoyance |
| **s. 5(2)** A/C & ventilation | **Any time** | Operating (or causing / permitting) any **air-conditioning or ventilating system** (or part) in **domestic premises** that is a source of annoyance |
| **s. 5(3)–(4)** | **Any time** | Animals / birds; attracting attention to goods / trade near public places |
| Penalty (ss. 4–5) | — | Fine at **level 3** |
| **s. 5A** carve-out | — | Noise from trams; NW Railway vehicles (Cap. 372); **other road vehicles** travelling in a public place |

**SD takeaway:** Residential A/C, VRV, bathroom / kitchen fans and louvre orientation are Cap 400 exposure from **day one of occupation** — not only construction-stage risk. Mixed-use: podium commercial plant → **s. 13**, not s. 5(2).

### 3. Construction noise — restricted hours and CNP (s. 6)

| Activity | Time window | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **General construction using PME** (not percussive piling) | **7 p.m. – 7 a.m.**, or **any time on a general holiday** | Offence unless a **construction noise permit (CNP)** is in force **and** conditions complied with (s. 6(1)) |
| **Prescribed construction work in a designated area** | Same restricted hours / holidays | Same CNP gate (s. 6(2)) — prescribed activities set by notice / regs (e.g. formwork, hammering) |
| **Percussive piling** | **7 a.m. – 7 p.m.** on a day that is **not** a general holiday | Needs CNP (s. 6(3)) |
| **Percussive piling** | **7 p.m. – 7 a.m.**, or **any time on a general holiday** | **Absolute prohibition** — CNP cannot authorise (s. 6(4)) |
| Penalties (s. 6(5)) | — | First: fine at **level 6**; subsequent: **$200,000**; plus **$20,000 / day** continuing |

**Domestic DIY carve-out (s. 6(6))** — no CNP if **all** of:
1. Work done **only** by the owner / tenant / occupier; **and**
2. Only PME is **portable hand-held** (no other support); **and**
3. **Only one** PME item in use in the premises at any one time

**SD takeaway:** Night / Sunday / holiday PME works and weekday percussive piling are **programme and method-of-construction** decisions at SD — not “contractor later”. Prefer bored / non-percussive foundations near NSRs; assume **no** night percussive piling ever.

---

### 4. Construction noise permits — process gates (s. 8)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Who issues | **Authority** (s. 8(1)); may attach any conditions thought fit |
| Guidance | Authority **shall be guided** by TMs under **s. 9(1)** (s. 8(3), (5)) |
| Decision clock | Issue CNP **or** written refusal within **28 days** of receipt; silence → permit **deemed issued** (s. 8(4)) |
| Refusal | Allowed if issue would be contrary to TM principles / procedures / guidelines / standards / limits (s. 8(5)) |
| Typical conditions (s. 8(13)) | Display of CNP; **max noise levels** at specified locations / times; limits on when specified PME or construction work may run |
| Amend / cancel | For breach, false info, or (except percussive-piling CNPs) **public interest** (s. 8(10)–(11)) |
| Appeal (s. 18) | Only for **percussive piling** CNPs in the **7 a.m.–7 p.m.** non-holiday window — not for general PME night CNPs |

Sister TMs (issued under s. 9; not Cap 400 text):
- Technical Memorandum on Noise from **Percussive Piling**
- Technical Memorandum on Noise from Construction Work **other than Percussive Piling**
- Technical Memorandum on Noise from Construction Work in **Designated Areas**

**SD takeaway:** CNP is an **EPD noise-assessment product**, not a BD formality — lock NSR distances, plant inventory and working hours before claiming a night-works programme.

---

### 5. Designated areas — denser urban overlay (s. 8A)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| How created | Secretary for Environment and Ecology by **Gazette notice** (s. 8A(1)) |
| Effect | Separate / stricter TM may apply to one or more designated areas; on conflict with territory-wide TM, **designated-area TM prevails** (s. 8A(2)) |
| Construction bite | Triggers **s. 6(2)** CNP for **prescribed** construction work in restricted hours inside the area |

**SD takeaway:** Check whether the site is inside a **Designated Area** before promising evening formwork, breaking, or dump-truck cycles.

### 6. Industrial / commercial / building-services noise — NAN regime (ss. 10, 13)

Applies to noise from any place **other than** domestic premises, a public place or a construction site (factories, restaurants, offices, car parks, plant rooms, etc.).

Authority may serve a **noise abatement notice** if satisfied the noise (alone or with other such places):

| Gate (s. 13(1)) | Trigger |
|---|---|
| (a) | Source of **annoyance** to any person (other than persons in the emitting place) in a place treated as an **NSR** in the s. 10 TM |
| (b) | Does not comply with any **prescribed** standard / limit *(provision not yet in operation)* |
| (c) | Does not comply with any standard / limit in the **s. 10 TM** |

| NAN mechanics | Detail |
|---|---|
| Served on | Person making / causing / permitting the noise; and/or owner / tenant / occupier / person in charge of the place |
| May require | Abate by a date; keep emissions within specified limits; operate plant / machinery / vehicles / equipment / processes under stated conditions; notify compliance in writing |
| Time to comply | Period set with regard to nature / difficulty / complexity (s. 13(3)) |
| Effective date | Not less than **21 days** after service (s. 13(5)) |
| Breach | Level 6 / $200,000 + $20,000/day (s. 13(6)–(7)) |
| Appeal | s. 19 — notice generally **suspended** pending appeal unless Authority certifies public interest |

**SD takeaway:** There is **no** general duty to meet ANLs on day one of occupation — but once a complaint → measurement → **NAN**, plant, louvres, silencers and operating hours become **mandatory retrofit**. Design plant rooms, cooling towers, kitchen exhaust and generators as if ANLs will be enforced.

### 7. Intruder alarms (ss. 13A–13B)

| Installation | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Premises alarm (s. 13A) | Audible signal **must cease ≤ 15 minutes** after activation (controller’s duty) |
| Vehicle alarm (s. 13B) | Must not sound except from **direct physical contact** with the vehicle; signal **must cease ≤ 5 minutes** |
| Penalties | Premises: level 3 + **3 months** imprisonment; vehicle: level 3 |

**SD takeaway:** Spec security / BMS alarm cut-offs at **≤ 15 min** for premises; do not rely on continuous siren logic.

---

### 8. Prescribed products — plant that enters the site (Part III, ss. 14–17A)

Cap 400 criminalises import / manufacture / supply / use of **prescribed** products that fail prescribed noise standards, labelling, or Authority directions. Detail is in regs (e.g. hand-held percussive breakers; air compressors — Noise Emission Labels).

| Hook | SD relevance |
|---|---|
| ss. 14–15 | Trade supply of non-compliant prescribed plant for HK use |
| s. 17 | **Using** non-compliant prescribed product is an offence |
| s. 17A | Authority may direct use **per manufacturer noise instructions** |
| Penalties | Typically level 6 / $200,000 + daily fine |

**SD takeaway:** Site plant lists (breakers, compressors) need **Noise Emission Labels** / compliant models — not only CNP hours.

---

### 9. What Cap 400 expressly does **not** cover / softens

| Item | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Aircraft noise** | Entire Ordinance **does not apply** (s. 36) — see Cap. 312 |
| **Road vehicles in transit** (ss. 4–5) | Exempt under s. 5A (separate vehicle first-registration noise regs under s. 27(3A) / Cap. 374 linkage) |
| **Occupational (in-workplace) noise** | Outside Cap 400 — Cap. 59 / Labour Department |
| **MTRCL / KCRC** under NAN | s. 13 applies only so far as **practicable and compatible** with their statutory functions (s. 37) |
| **Government** | Ordinance binds Government, but criminal ss. mostly do not prosecute Government / public officers acting in duty — administrative escalation instead (s. 38). **s. 13B** does not apply to Government |
| **CE in Council exemptions** | Gazette order may exempt area / place / premises / activity (s. 35) |
| **Emergency defence** | s. 33 — life / injury prevention; unforeseeable property damage; unforeseeable serious public-transport disruption; or other Ordinance authorisation |

---

### 10. SD checklist — lock before massing / programme

| Gate | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Map **NSRs** (dwellings, schools, hospitals, etc. per TMs) within influence of plant and construction |
| 2 | Is site in a **Designated Area**? If yes, assume stricter night / holiday **prescribed works** CNP |
| 3 | Foundation method: avoid **percussive piling** near NSRs; never programme it in restricted hours |
| 4 | Construction programme: daytime PME OK without CNP; **7 p.m.–7 a.m. / general holidays** PME needs **CNP** |
| 5 | Permanent plant (A/C, ventilation, generators, kitchen exhaust, transformers): treat as **s. 13** NAN risk; attenuate at source / path |
| 6 | Domestic A/C / vent: **s. 5(2)** annoyance exposure to neighbours |
| 7 | Alarms: **≤ 15 min** premises cut-off |
| 8 | Cross-check sister instruments: Cap 400 TMs; Designated Areas Notice; product regs; EIAO noise assessment if designated project |

**SD takeaway:** Cap 400 fails projects through **construction programme**, **piling method**, and **plant-room / louvre placement** — freeze those three before schematic freeze.
