> Scope note: Allocations in this memorandum apply to each emission year from 1 January 2017 and supersede the Second Technical Memorandum for those years. The Fourth Technical Memorandum supersedes this one for each emission year from 1 January 2019. Quantities are printed without a unit word beside the figures. Nitrogen oxides are expressed as nitrogen dioxide. The subject is a specified licence for electricity works.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 1.2 sets the quantity of emission allowances for each specified pollutant for each specified licence, and the method of determining that quantity, for every emission year from 1 January 2017. Section 2.6 requires the Authority to allocate that quantity unless the Ordinance or subsidiary legislation provides otherwise, and the allocation is for electricity generation for local consumption.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing stations from 1 January 2017 (s. 2.1)

A is the aggregate net sent-out electricity, in GWh, from renewable energy systems into the grid of Lamma Power Station and its extension in the emission year. B is the same aggregate into the grid of Castle Peak Power Station. Renewable energy systems are solar, wind, biomass, hydro, tidal, wave, geothermal, or energy from waste, including landfill gas or sewage gas, that provide electricity to the grid. A and B are rounded up to the next whole number. The allowance itself is also rounded up to the next whole number.

| Station | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lamma Power Station and Extension | 5 200 + (2 − A) × 0.614 | 9 450 + (2 − A) × 0.941 | 250 + (2 − A) × 0.027 |
| Black Point Power Station | 1 440 | 4 140 | 110 |
| Castle Peak Power Station | 3 757 + (21 − B) × 0.367 | 12 358 + (21 − B) × 1.208 | 389 + (21 − B) × 0.038 |
| Penny’s Bay Gas Turbine Power Station | 2 | 2 | 1 |

**SD takeaway:** From 2017, renewable output sent out from Lamma or Castle Peak reduces that station’s allowance through the (2 − A) and (21 − B) terms, while Black Point stays at the 2015 figures of 1 440, 4 140 and 110.

### 2. New electricity works (s. 2.2)

C is the total installed capacity in MW, or 300, whichever is smaller. D is the number of months in the emission year after operation starts, and part of a month counts as a full month. E is renewable net sent-out electricity from that new works in the emission year, in GWh, rounded up to the next whole number.

| Pollutant | 2017 and thereafter |
| --- | --- |
| Sulphur dioxide | 90 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.047 |
| Nitrogen oxides | 230 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.120 |
| Respirable suspended particulates | 7 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.004 |

**SD takeaway:** A new station is scaled by the lesser of its capacity and 300 MW, by the months it operates, and downward by its own renewable sent-out units.

### 3. Review (s. 2.7)

The Secretary shall review the quantities not less than once every two years after commencement.

**SD takeaway:** The review interval printed in this memorandum is two years, shorter than the three-year interval in the first and second memoranda.
