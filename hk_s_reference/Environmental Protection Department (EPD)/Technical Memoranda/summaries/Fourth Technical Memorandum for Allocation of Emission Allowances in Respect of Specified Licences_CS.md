> Scope note: This is the fourth technical memorandum issued under section 26G. It applies to each emission year from 1 January 2019 and supersedes the Third Technical Memorandum for those years. The Fifth Technical Memorandum supersedes this one for each emission year from 1 January 2020. Quantities are printed without a unit word beside the figures. Nitrogen oxides are expressed as nitrogen dioxide. Emission-allowance memoranda six to ten are not in this library and are not summarised here.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 1.2 sets the quantity of emission allowances for each specified pollutant for each specified licence, and the method of determining that quantity, for every emission year from 1 January 2019. Section 2.6 requires the Authority to allocate that quantity unless the Ordinance or subsidiary legislation provides otherwise, and section 2.3 limits the allocation to electricity generation for local consumption.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing stations from 1 January 2019 (s. 2.1)

A is renewable net sent-out electricity, in GWh, into the Lamma grid in the emission year. B is the same quantity into the Castle Peak grid. Both are rounded up to the next whole number, as is the allowance.

| Station | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lamma Power Station and Extension | 4 250 + (2 − A) × 0.548 | 8 980 + (2 − A) × 0.973 | 200 + (2 − A) × 0.022 |
| Black Point Power Station | 290 | 4 140 | 110 |
| Castle Peak Power Station | 4 678 + (21 − B) × 0.418 | 12 358 + (21 − B) × 1.105 | 389 + (21 − B) × 0.035 |
| Penny’s Bay Gas Turbine Power Station | 2 | 2 | 1 |

**SD takeaway:** The 2019 step cuts Black Point sulphur dioxide from the 1 440 printed in the third memorandum to 290, and it lowers the Lamma base figures, while Penny’s Bay remains 2, 2 and 1.

### 2. New electricity works (s. 2.2)

C is installed capacity in MW, or 300, whichever is smaller. D is months after operation starts, with part of a month counted as a full month. E is renewable net sent-out electricity from the new works, in GWh.

| Pollutant | 2019 and thereafter |
| --- | --- |
| Sulphur dioxide | 90 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.047 |
| Nitrogen oxides | 250 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.131 |
| Respirable suspended particulates | 7 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.004 |

**SD takeaway:** Against the third memorandum, the new-works nitrogen-oxides factor rises from 230 to 250 and its renewable deduction from 0.120 to 0.131, while sulphur dioxide and particulates keep the same factors.

### 3. Review (s. 2.7)

The Secretary shall review in 2015 the quantity for each pollutant and each specified licence set or determined under this memorandum, after its commencement.

**SD takeaway:** The review year printed on this memorandum is 2015, which is before the 1 January 2019 emission years to which the formulae apply.
