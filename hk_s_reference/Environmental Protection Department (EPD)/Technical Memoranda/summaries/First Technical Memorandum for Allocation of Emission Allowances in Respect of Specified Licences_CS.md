# First technical memorandum on emission allowances for specified licences
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Special Supplement No. 5 to Gazette No. 45/2008. Signed by Edward T. W. Yau, Secretary for the Environment. Published under section 37B(1) of the Air Pollution Control Ordinance (Cap. 311) and effective under section 37C. | Environmental Protection Department

> Scope note: This is the first technical memorandum issued under section 26G of the Ordinance. It allocates emission allowances for specified licences of electricity works, which item 7 of Schedule 1 to the Ordinance defines. It is not a building-chimney standard and it does not set the health protection levels in the air-pollution abatement-notice memorandum. The Second Technical Memorandum in this library supersedes the allocation under this memorandum for each emission year from 1 January 2015. The pages print quantities of emission allowances and do not print a unit word beside those figures. Nitrogen oxides are expressed as nitrogen dioxide.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 1.2 sets out the total quantity of emission allowances for all of the specified licences, and the principles and methods for allocating a quantity to each specified licence for each emission year. Subject to the new-works and updating rules, section 2.1 fixes that total for each and every emission year from 1 January 2010, and section 2.7 requires the Authority to allocate the quantity determined under this memorandum unless the Ordinance or its subsidiary legislation provides otherwise.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The total from 1 January 2010 (s. 2.1)

| Specified pollutant | Total quantity for all specified licences, each emission year from 1 January 2010 |
| --- | --- |
| Sulphur dioxide | 25 120 |
| Nitrogen oxides, expressed as nitrogen dioxide | 42 600 |
| Respirable suspended particulates | 1 260 |

**SD takeaway:** These three figures are a territory total for licensed electricity works from 2010, not a limit that can be copied onto a building boiler or a commercial kitchen.

### 2. How an existing station’s share is calculated (s. 2.3, Annex 2)

Existing electricity works are those with a valid specified licence on the commencement date at Black Point Power Station, Castle Peak Power Station, Lamma Power Station and Lamma Power Station Extension, and Penny’s Bay Gas Turbine Power Station, at the lots and streets printed in section 1.3. Until the first update under section 2.4 takes effect, each existing works’ quantity is A × B / C. A is the section 2.1 total for that pollutant. B is that works’ electricity generation for local consumption from 1999 to 2003 inclusive. C is the sum of that generation for all existing works in the same years. Local consumption is gross generation minus sales for export outside Hong Kong, in GWh, whether or not the licence holder exports directly.

**SD takeaway:** Each existing station’s quantity from 2010 is its share of local generation in 1999 to 2003, applied to the territory total in section 2.1.

### 3. New electricity works (s. 2.2, Annex 1)

A new electricity works is any electricity works, other than those four stations, that comes into existence after commencement. For a full emission year, until the first update:

| Total installed capacity | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 300 MW | 2/3 × installed capacity in MW | 4/3 × installed capacity in MW | 1/30 × installed capacity in MW |
| Equal to or more than 300 MW | 200 | 400 | 10 |

If the licence does not start in January, the rest of that emission year is allocated pro rata by month, and part of a month counts as a full month. The quantity is rounded up to the next whole number.

**SD takeaway:** A new power station under this first memorandum is capped either by a fraction of its megawatts or, at 300 MW and above, by the fixed row 200 / 400 / 10, until the three-year update replaces that row.

### 4. The three-year update (ss. 2.4–2.6, Annex 3)

From 2010, and not less than once every three years, the Authority updates the split among existing works and among new works whose licence has been in force for 12 months or more. The update is made on 1 January and uses only shares of electricity generation, by the Annex 3 formula A × B / C. B and C use adjusted local-consumption generation from October of the sixth preceding year through September of the preceding year. The adjustment is D × 60 / E, where E is the number of consecutive calendar months from the first month of the licence to that September, or 60, whichever is smaller. Licence holders are notified in writing no less than four years before the updated quantity takes effect.

**SD takeaway:** A station’s allowance under this memorandum can be rewritten on generation share, but the holder must be told at least four years before the new figure applies.
