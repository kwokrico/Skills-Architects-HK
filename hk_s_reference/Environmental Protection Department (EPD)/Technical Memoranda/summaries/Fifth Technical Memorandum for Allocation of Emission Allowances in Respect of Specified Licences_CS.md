## Regulatory Overview

Section 1.2 sets the quantity of emission allowances for each specified pollutant for each specified licence, and the method of determining that quantity, for every emission year from 1 January 2020. Section 2.6 requires the Authority to allocate that quantity unless the Ordinance or subsidiary legislation provides otherwise, for electricity generation for local consumption.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing stations from 1 January 2020 (s. 2.1)

A is renewable net sent-out electricity, in GWh, into the Lamma grid. B is the same quantity into the Castle Peak grid. Section 1.3 defines a renewable energy system as solar, wind, biomass, hydro, tidal, wave, geothermal, or energy from waste, including landfill gas or sewage gas, that provides electricity to the grid. A and B, and the resulting allowance, are rounded up to the next whole number.

| Station | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lamma Power Station and Extension | 3 130 + (2 − A) × 0.563 | 6 350 + (2 − A) × 0.928 | 145 + (2 − A) × 0.019 |
| Black Point Power Station | 279 | 4 074 | 108 |
| Castle Peak Power Station | 4 259 + (32 − B) × 0.422 | 10 844 + (32 − B) × 1.073 | 331 + (32 − B) × 0.033 |
| Penny’s Bay Gas Turbine Power Station | 2 | 2 | 1 |

The Castle Peak renewable term uses 32 rather than the 21 printed in the third and fourth memoranda.

**SD takeaway:** From 2020 the latest memorandum in this library lowers Black Point nitrogen oxides from 4 140 to 4 074 and particulates from 110 to 108, and it lowers the Lamma base quantities again, while Penny’s Bay remains 2, 2 and 1.

### 2. New electricity works (s. 2.2)

C is installed capacity in MW, or 300, whichever is smaller. D is months after operation starts, with part of a month counted as a full month. E is renewable net sent-out electricity from the new works, in GWh.

| Pollutant | 2020 and thereafter |
| --- | --- |
| Sulphur dioxide | 36 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.018 |
| Nitrogen oxides | 55 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.028 |
| Respirable suspended particulates | 14 × (C/300) × (D/12) − E × 0.007 |

**SD takeaway:** A new station’s 2020 factors are 36, 55 and 14 times the capacity and month fractions, which is a lower sulphur-dioxide and nitrogen-oxides starting point than the 90 and 250 factors in the fourth memorandum, with a higher particulates factor of 14 instead of 7.
