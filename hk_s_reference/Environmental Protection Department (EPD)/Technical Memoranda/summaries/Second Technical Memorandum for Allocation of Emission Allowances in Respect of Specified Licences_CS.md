# Second technical memorandum on emission allowances for specified licences
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Special Supplement No. 5 to Gazette No. 41/2010. Signed by Edward T. W. Yau, Secretary for the Environment. Published under section 37B(1) of the Air Pollution Control Ordinance (Cap. 311) and effective under section 37C. | Environmental Protection Department

> Scope note: This is the second technical memorandum issued under section 26G. It replaces, for each emission year from 1 January 2015, the allocation set or determined under the first memorandum. The Third Technical Memorandum in this library supersedes this one for each emission year from 1 January 2017. Quantities are printed without a unit word beside the figures. Nitrogen oxides are expressed as nitrogen dioxide. The subject is a specified licence for electricity works, not a building chimney.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 1.2 sets the quantity of emission allowances for each specified pollutant allocated to each specified licence for every emission year from 1 January 2015, and states that the first memorandum’s allocation for those years is superseded. Section 2.4 requires the Authority to allocate the quantity set or determined in this memorandum unless the Ordinance or subsidiary legislation provides otherwise.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing stations from 1 January 2015 (s. 2.1)

Existing works are the same four stations as in the first memorandum, at the addresses printed in section 1.3, if a valid specified licence is in force on the commencement date. The allocation is for electricity generation for local consumption, defined as gross generation minus export sales outside Hong Kong.

| Station | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Black Point Power Station | 1 440 | 4 140 | 110 |
| Castle Peak Power Station | 4 260 | 13 390 | 420 |
| Lamma Power Station and Lamma Power Station Extension | 6 780 | 10 020 | 300 |
| Penny’s Bay Gas Turbine Power Station | 2 | 2 | 1 |

**SD takeaway:** From 2015 this memorandum replaces the generation-share split with a fixed quantity for each of the four existing stations, and Penny’s Bay is printed at 2, 2 and 1.

### 2. New electricity works (s. 2.2 and the Annex)

For a full emission year the Annex prints:

| Total installed capacity | Sulphur dioxide | Nitrogen oxides | Respirable suspended particulates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 300 MW | 12/30 of the total installed capacity in MW | 27/30 of the total installed capacity in MW | 8/300 of the total installed capacity in MW |
| Equal to or more than 300 MW | 120 | 270 | 8 |

A licence that does not start in January is pro-rated by month for the rest of that year, and part of a month counts as a full month. The Annex quantity is rounded up to the next whole number.

**SD takeaway:** A new station at or above 300 MW is printed at 120, 270 and 8 for a full year under this memorandum, which is lower than the 200, 400 and 10 row in the first memorandum.

### 3. Review (s. 2.5)

The Secretary shall review the quantity for each pollutant and each specified licence not less than once every three years after commencement.

**SD takeaway:** The 2015 station figures are not printed as permanent; the Secretary must review them at least every three years.
