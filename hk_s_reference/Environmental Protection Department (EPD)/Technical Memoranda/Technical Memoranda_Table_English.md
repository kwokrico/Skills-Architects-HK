# Technical Memoranda Index (English)

**Issued by:** Environmental Protection Department
**Scope:** Eleven technical memoranda in this library: four noise memoranda, the effluent-standards memorandum, the air pollution abatement notice memorandum, and the first to fifth technical memoranda on allocation of emission allowances for specified licences. Emission-allowance memoranda six to ten are not in this library. This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The short title links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF link in the same cell opens the file in `source_reference/`.
**Titles:** The official title is the title printed on the memorandum.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current memorandum, the ordinance it is issued under, and any later memorandum that supersedes the allocation year in question.
