# Gas Codes Index (English)

**Issued by:** Electrical and Mechanical Services Department (Gas Authority / Gas Standards Office)
**Scope:** The gas-pipe avoidance code and its supplementary information, LPG Modules 1 and 2, and GU 03, GU 04, GU 06, GU 12 and GU 21 (9 documents in this library). This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The short title links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF link in the same cell opens the file in `source_reference/`.
**Titles:** The official title is the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current code approved by the Gas Authority under section 9 of the Gas Safety Ordinance (Cap. 51).
