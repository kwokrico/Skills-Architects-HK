# Code of Practice GU 04 — Excess Flow Valves for Domestic Gas Cooking
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First Edition, June 2005 | The Gas Authority

> Scope note: GU 04 covers selection, listing and installation of an excess flow valve on a piped gas supply to a domestic gas cooking installation. A domestic gas cooking appliance, as a domestic gas appliance under Cap. 51C regulation 2, has a rated heat input up to **70 kW** and is of a type approved under Code of Practice GU 05 (section 1.2). This code does not apply to a cylinder LPG supply connected with a low-pressure regulator that already has an integral excess-flow device complying with the code of practice for low-pressure regulators supplying gas from LPG cylinders of less than 40 litres water capacity (section 1.1). It does not set kitchen ventilation or flue size.

## Regulatory Overview

The excess flow valve is installed as near as practical to the fitting that connects the service line to its source of piped gas, so that likely tubing damage occurs only downstream of the valve and does not separate the valve from the upstream piping (section 5.1.5).

Installation is gas installation work and is carried out by a registered gas installer employed by a registered gas contractor (section 5.1.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where the valve sits (section 5.1)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Position (s. 5.1.5) | As near as practical to the fitting connecting the service line to the piped-gas source. Damage downstream of the valve must not separate the valve from the upstream piping |
| Match to the appliance (s. 5.1.3) | The registered gas contractor checks that the model suits the appliance in gas type, rating and connection |
| Orientation (s. 5.1.6 and 5.1.7) | As the manufacturer's instructions specify, generally horizontal or vertical, and in the direction of flow marked on the body |
| Materials (s. 1.5) | The product also meets regulation 4(1) of the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations on construction and material of gas fittings |
| Inspection (s. 5.2.1) | The valve and the associated gas fittings connected to the domestic gas cooking appliance are inspected by a registered gas installer at least once every **18 months** |

**SD takeaway:** Show the excess flow valve at the start of the cooking supply, on the upstream side of the flexible or tubing run, not at the appliance end.
