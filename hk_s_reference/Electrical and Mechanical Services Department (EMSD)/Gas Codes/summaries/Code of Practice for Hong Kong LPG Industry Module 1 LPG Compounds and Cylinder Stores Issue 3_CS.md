# LPG Code of Practice, Module 1 — Compounds and Cylinder Stores (Issue 3)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Issue 3, May 2023 | Gas Standards Office, Electrical and Mechanical Services Department

> Scope note: Module 1 sets the recommended practice for LPG compounds and cylinder stores, including standby cylinder stores, and for the equipment inside them. It is read with the Gas Safety Ordinance (Cap. 51) and the subsidiary regulations. It does not design the underground vapour pipe from the compound to the user. That is Module 2. It does not set the kitchen chamber used for cylinders under 130 litres water capacity in a restaurant. That is Code of Practice GU 06. The preface states that the requirements are guidance and are not a set of rules that cannot be changed.

## Regulatory Overview

An LPG compound and a cylinder store that is a notifiable gas installation need construction approval and approval of use from the Gas Authority before construction and before gas is introduced, and the quantitative risk assessment for the site must be acceptable to the Gas Authority (sections 3.1 and 4.1.1.7).

Bulk tanks are underground or mounded, inside individual vessel chambers, and they are not installed in basements (sections 4.1.3.1 and 4.1.3.3).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Compound fence, sterile area, and tank position (section 4.1)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Perimeter fence (s. 4.1.2.1) | At least **1.8 m** high, and as open as possible for cross-ventilation |
| Exits (s. 4.1.2.3) | At least two, as far apart as possible. Gates open outwards and are not self-locking. The second exit is lockable only from the inside |
| Bund (s. 4.1.2.5) | The fence is bunded to **150 mm**. A speed bump at the tanker entrance maintains that bund |
| Warning letters (s. 4.1.2.6) | "NO SMOKING", "LPG HIGHLY FLAMMABLE" and "LPG STORE", in English and Chinese, letters and characters at least **120 mm** high, near each entrance |
| Main control valve (s. 4.1.2.7) | Accessible from outside the fence, with means to prevent unauthorised operation |
| Sterile area (s. 4.1.2.8) | At least **1 m** around the compound where the owner controls that land, paved and marked with yellow lines. Otherwise the sterile area may sit inside the fence if the safety distances are met inside the compound |
| Bulk tanks (s. 4.1.3.1 and 4.1.3.3) | Underground or mounded, in water-sealed vessel chambers. Not in basements |
| Other flammable liquid (s. 4.1.3.2) | Not closer than **6 m** to an aboveground vessel, or the bund wall of a vessel, containing another flammable liquid with a flash point below **60°C** |
| Public edge (s. 4.1.3.4) | Valve assembly on the manhole cover, and the fill-connection, at least **3 m** from the installation boundary where the public have legitimate access |
| Tank to installation boundary (s. 4.1.3.5) | At least **1.5 m**, and the vessel chamber wholly inside the installation boundary |
| Lot boundary or fixed ignition (s. 4.1.3.6) | Valve assembly and fill-connection at least **7.6 m** from the lot boundary or a fixed point of ignition |
| Pressure-relief vent (s. 4.1.3.7) | At least **1.5 m** from a building opening below the point of discharge, and at least **3 m** from a fixed source of ignition, an opening to a direct-vent appliance, and a mechanical ventilation intake |
| Drainage (s. 4.1.4.3) | If the tanker-bay drain connects to a public drain, an LPG trap is incorporated. Appendix C sizes the weir from the specific gravity of LPG |

Appendix B prints the same minimum safety distances for an underground or mounded tank as a table: to the lot boundary or a fixed point of ignition, **3 m** from the underground tank, **7.6 m** from the filling connection or tank valve assembly, and **3 m** from the pressure-relief vent; to an installation boundary with an industrial fence at least 1.8 m high, **1.5 m** from the tank and **3 m** from the filling connection or valve assembly; and **6 m** from the tank to a vessel or bund of other flammable liquid with a flash point below **60°C**.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the fill point 7.6 m from the lot boundary and from fixed ignition, 3 m from the public edge of the compound, and the tank itself 1.5 m inside the fence and 6 m from any other flammable-liquid tank.

### 2. Cylinder stores (section 4.2)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Level (s. 4.2.1.8) | Readily accessible at ground level, never in a cellar or basement. Floor level, free of depressions, paved with concrete |
| Palletised stacks (s. 4.2.1.9) | Gangway not less than **2.5 m**. Unpalletised gangway not less than **1.5 m**. Stack height not more than **1.6 m** |
| Electrical apparatus (s. 4.2.1.10) | Normally none inside the store or within the classified area in Appendix F |

**SD takeaway:** A cylinder store is a ground-level concrete room with a 1.5 m gangway, or 2.5 m if the cylinders are on pallets, and it is not a basement.

### 3. Overhead power cables (section 4.4.1)

An aboveground LPG tank or LPG cylinders must not be installed below overhead cables unless protected by a suitable screen bonded to earth (s. 4.4.1.1):

| Cable voltage | Stand-off from a vertical line dropped from the cable |
|---|---|
| Below **1.0 kV** | **1.5 m** |
| **1.0 kV** or above | **10 m** |

Telephone and data cables are not power cables for this code (s. 4.4.1.2). Layout also accounts for vehicle impact, falling trees, slope stability, and an unusually high tide at the waterfront (s. 4.4.2).

**SD takeaway:** Keep aboveground cylinders 10 m off the vertical line of a high-voltage overhead cable, or screen them with an earthed barrier, and do not fix the compound until the quantitative risk assessment is accepted.
