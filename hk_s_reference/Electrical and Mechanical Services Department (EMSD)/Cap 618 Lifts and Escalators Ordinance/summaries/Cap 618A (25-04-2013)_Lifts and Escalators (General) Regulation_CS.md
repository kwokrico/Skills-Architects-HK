# Cap 618A — Lifts and Escalators (General) Regulation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Last updated: 25 April 2013 | Cap. 618 sub. leg. A | Made under the Lifts and Escalators Ordinance (Cap. 618)

> Scope note: Cap. 618A sets duties of responsible persons and registered persons for log-books, incident notices, and the documents and time limits for use-permit and resumption-permit applications. It does not set shaft, pit, machine-room, or energy-efficiency dimensions. Commencement of the operative provisions cited below is 17 December 2012 in this consolidation.

## Regulatory Overview

The responsible person for a lift must keep a log-book in the specified form, and the same duty applies to an escalator.

An application for a use permit or a resumption permit must reach the Director within two months after the examination to which the accompanying certificate relates, with the prescribed fee.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Log-book (ss. 2, 17)

| Item | Lift (s. 2) | Escalator (s. 17) |
|---|---|---|
| Who keeps it | Responsible person for the lift | Responsible person for the escalator |
| Form | Specified form | Specified form |
| Contents | Description of the lift; name and contact details of every registered lift contractor who has undertaken maintenance; particulars of every incident and every other failure, including who was notified or attended; entries required of the registered contractor (s. 5) and the registered engineer (s. 11); any other particulars the Director specifies | Parallel entries for the escalator, including contractor entries under s. 20 and engineer entries under s. 25 |
| Removal of entries | Information in s. 2(2)(b)(ii) to (v) may be removed on or after the **3rd anniversary** of the date it was entered | Same 3rd-anniversary rule |
| Production | If an enforcement officer asks, the responsible person produces the log-book within **3 days** | Same duty |

**SD takeaway:** The log-book is a kept record in the specified form, produced within 3 days of an enforcement request; provide a place the responsible person can keep it for the life of the lift or escalator, with entries retained for three years.

### 2. Suspension notice and emergency-device failure (ss. 7, 8)

If the registered contractor who currently undertakes lift works (or, if none, the contractor who most recently did) becomes aware of an incident, and normal use is suspended before the end of **4 hours** after the contractor becomes aware and is unlikely to resume before the end of that period, the contractor must within that 4-hour period display a notice in a conspicuous part of the lift, or in a conspicuous place in the vicinity of the lift. The notice is in the specified form and states the reason for the suspension.

Section 8 places a parallel duty on the registered lift contractor for failure of certain emergency devices. The escalator equivalents are sections 22 and 23.

**SD takeaway:** Leave a conspicuous surface on the lift, or immediately beside it, where a suspension notice can be displayed within four hours of an incident.

### 3. Permit applications — two-month delivery (ss. 31–33)

| Application | Must include | Delivered to the Director |
|---|---|---|
| Use permit under s. 26(1)(a) of the Ordinance (s. 31(1)) | Specified form; certificate issued under s. 24(4) of the Ordinance; information specified in the form; prescribed fee | Within the **2-month** period beginning on the date the examination to which the certificate relates is completed |
| Use permit under s. 26(1)(b) (s. 31(2)) | Same, subject to s. 33 | Same 2-month period |
| Resumption permit under s. 28(1) (s. 32) | Specified form; certificate under s. 24(4) or s. 25(2) of the Ordinance; prescribed fee | Same 2-month period |
| Single application for both a s. 26(1)(b) permit and a s. 28(1) permit (s. 33) | Permitted for the same lift. Specified form and the certificates and fee the section requires | Within the 2-month period that applies to the examination certificate |

Escalator permit applications follow the parallel provisions later in Part 3 for the escalator sections of the Ordinance (ss. 56 and 58).

**SD takeaway:** Programme the registered engineer’s examination so the use-permit application, with its certificate and fee, can be delivered within two months after that examination is completed.

### 4. Records the contractor must keep (s. 6)

A registered lift contractor keeps records of lift works. For installation, the keeping period begins on the date of completion of the installation and ends at midnight on the date immediately before the **7th anniversary** of the completion date. For other works covered by s. 6(2), the period ends at midnight on the date immediately before the **3rd anniversary** of the completion date. The escalator contractor duty is section 21.

**SD takeaway:** Installation records are a seven-year contractor obligation measured from completion of the installation, so completion has to be a dated event in the handover set.
