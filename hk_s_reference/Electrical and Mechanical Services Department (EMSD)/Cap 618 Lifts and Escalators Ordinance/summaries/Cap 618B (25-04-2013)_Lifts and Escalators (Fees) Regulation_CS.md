# Cap 618B — Lifts and Escalators (Fees) Regulation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Last updated: 25 April 2013 | Cap. 618 sub. leg. B | Made under Cap. 618 s. 155

> Scope note: Cap. 618B prescribes the fees for use permits, resumption permits, duplicate permits, cancellation of orders, and registration of lift and escalator contractors, engineers and workers. It does not set safety standards or spatial design rules. The Regulation came into operation on 17 December 2012. The Schedule pages are marked last updated 17 December 2012.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 2 provides that the fee in column 3 of the Schedule is prescribed for the matter described opposite that fee in column 2.

The fees that affect a building project are the permit applications; the registration fees apply to the contractor, engineer and worker who carry out the works.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Lift permit fees (Sch. items 1–6)

| Item | Matter | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application for a permit under section 26(1)(a) of the Ordinance | $755 |
| 2 | Application for a permit under section 26(1)(b) of the Ordinance | $290 |
| 3 | Application for a permit under section 28(1) of the Ordinance | $800 |
| 4 | Single application for one lift for both a section 26(1)(b) permit and a section 28(1) permit | $940 |
| 5 | Application for a duplicate permit under section 29(1) of the Ordinance | $140 |
| 6 | Application for cancellation of an order under section 33(1) of the Ordinance | $300 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget $755 for the first lift use-permit application and $800 for a resumption permit after a major alteration, or $940 if both the renewal permit and the resumption permit are applied for together.
