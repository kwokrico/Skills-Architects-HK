# Cap 610B — Buildings Energy Efficiency (Registered Energy Assessors) Regulation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Last updated: 20 September 2025 | Cap. 610 sub. leg. B | Made under Cap. 610 s. 42

> Scope note: Cap. 610B sets who may be registered as a registered energy assessor, how long registration lasts, and the disciplinary route. It does not set lighting, air-conditioning, electrical, or lift energy-efficiency numbers. Those duties are in the Buildings Energy Efficiency Ordinance (Cap. 610) and the codes issued under it. Commencement in this consolidation is 21 March 2011.

## Regulatory Overview

A registered energy assessor is the person who certifies stage one and stage two declarations, issues a Form of Compliance, and carries out an energy audit under Cap. 610.

Registration is granted by the Director of Electrical and Mechanical Services only if one of the qualification routes in section 5 is met, and a certificate is valid for up to 10 years.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who can certify the design (s. 5(1))

The Director may allow an application under section 4 if satisfied of one of the following.

| Route | Qualification | Experience while holding that status |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Registered professional engineer under the Engineers Registration Ordinance (Cap. 409), registered in the electrical, mechanical, environmental, building services, or energy discipline | At least **2 years** practical experience in engineering works relating to energy efficiency in buildings |
| (b) | Corporate member of the Hong Kong Institution of Engineers in one of those disciplines, or an equivalent qualification the Institution recognises as not lower than that standard | At least **3 years** of that practical experience |
| (c) | Professional member of the Hong Kong Institute of Qualified Environmental Professionals, or an equivalent qualification the Institute recognises as not lower than that standard | At least **3 years** of that practical experience |

Each route also requires the knowledge needed to perform the duties and functions of a registered energy assessor under the Ordinance, and that the applicant is a fit and proper person.

**SD takeaway:** Appoint a registered energy assessor on one of these three routes before superstructure consent, because the stage one declaration must be certified by that person.

### 2. Comparable qualification route (s. 5(2), (2A), (3))

The Director may also allow an application if the applicant’s knowledge, qualification, education, experience and training, considered as a whole, are comparable to route (a), (b) or (c) and enable the applicant to perform the duties, and the applicant is fit and proper.

For that comparison the Director may accept attendance at or completion of any course the Director considers relevant.

Fit and proper may take into account a conviction, in Hong Kong or elsewhere, of an offence which if committed by a registered energy assessor may bring the profession into disrepute, a sentence of imprisonment whether suspended or not, and misconduct or neglect in a professional respect.

**SD takeaway:** A designer who is not on routes (a) to (c) can be accepted only if the Director finds the whole of that person’s background comparable; do not treat an unregistered consultant as able to sign the declaration.

### 3. Registration period and renewal (ss. 3–7, 12)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Application (s. 4) | Specified form, prescribed fee, and the documents specified in the form |
| If allowed (s. 5(4)) | The Director registers the applicant under section 30 of the Ordinance and issues a certificate of registration |
| First validity (s. 5(5)) | From the date the certificate is issued until the day before the **10th anniversary** of that date, or the day before removal under section 9, whichever is earlier |
| Register (s. 3) | Name, registration number, validity periods of all certificates issued under s. 5(4)(b) or s. 6(6)(b), and any other details the Director thinks fit |
| Renewal window (s. 6(3)) | At any time before expiry of the current registration, or within **28 days** after expiry |
| Renewal test (s. 6(5)) | The applicant must still meet the section 5 criteria of the route under which registration was granted |
| Renewed length (s. 7) | If the renewal application is submitted in the **4 months** immediately before expiry, or within the 28 days after expiry, validity runs to the day before the 10th anniversary of the **expiry date** of the current registration (or earlier removal). If submitted more than 4 months before expiry, validity runs to the day before the 10th anniversary of the **new certificate date** (or earlier removal) |
| Ceasing to hold the qualification (s. 12) | Where registration was under s. 5(1)(a), (b) or (c), the qualification is the one in that paragraph. Where registration was under s. 5(2), it is the submitted qualification |

**SD takeaway:** Check that the assessor’s certificate will still be valid on the day of the stage one certification, the stage two inspection, and any Form of Compliance, because each of those acts has to be done by a person who is registered at the time.
