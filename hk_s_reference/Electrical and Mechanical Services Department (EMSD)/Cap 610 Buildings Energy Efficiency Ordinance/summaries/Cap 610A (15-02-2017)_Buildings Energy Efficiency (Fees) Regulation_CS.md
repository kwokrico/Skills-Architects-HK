# Cap 610A — Buildings Energy Efficiency (Fees) Regulation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Last updated: 15 February 2017 | Cap. 610 sub. leg. A | Made under Cap. 610 s. 42

> Scope note: Cap. 610A prescribes the fees for declarations, certificate duplicates, and registered-energy-assessor applications under the Buildings Energy Efficiency Ordinance (Cap. 610) and the Registered Energy Assessors Regulation. It does not set energy-efficiency design standards, building types, or audit intervals. Commencement in this consolidation is 21 March 2011. The fee-table pages are marked last updated 21 March 2011.

## Regulatory Overview

Section 3 provides that the fee for a matter in column 2 of the Schedule is the fee in column 3 opposite that matter.

The Schedule has two parts: fees payable under the Buildings Energy Efficiency Ordinance, and fees payable under the Registered Energy Assessors Regulation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fees under the Ordinance (Sch. Part 1)

| Item | Matter | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submission of a stage two declaration under section 9 of the Ordinance | $760 |
| 2 | Application for renewal of a Certificate of Compliance Registration under section 13 of the Ordinance | $760 |
| 3 | Application for a duplicate of a Certificate of Compliance Registration under section 14 of the Ordinance | $155 |
| 4 | Application for a duplicate of a copy of a Form of Compliance under section 19 of the Ordinance | $155 |
| 5 | Application for a duplicate of a copy of an Energy Audit Form under section 24 of the Ordinance | $155 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget the $760 stage two declaration fee with the occupation-approval submission; the stage one design declaration is not a fee item in this Schedule.

### 2. Fees under the Registered Energy Assessors Regulation (Sch. Part 2, s. 2)

Section 2 defines the Registered Energy Assessors Regulation as any regulation made from time to time under section 42 of the Ordinance providing for registration, regulation, and disciplinary matters in respect of registered energy assessors.

| Item | Matter | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Application for registration as a registered energy assessor | $2,100 |
| 2 | Application for renewal of that registration | $1,100 |
| 3 | Application for a duplicate of the certificate of registration | $155 |

**SD takeaway:** The consultant who certifies the stage one declaration, stage two declaration, Form of Compliance, and energy audit must be a registered energy assessor; registration and renewal carry the fees in Part 2.
