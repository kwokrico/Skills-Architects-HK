# Code of Practice for Energy Efficiency of Building Services Installation (2024 Edition)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
BEC 2024 | Electrical and Mechanical Services Department | Issued under Part 9 of the Buildings Energy Efficiency Ordinance (Cap. 610)

> Scope note: This code (BEC 2024) sets the energy-efficiency design standards for lighting, air-conditioning, electrical, and lift and escalator installations in a prescribed building under Cap. 610. It applies to those installations irrespective of the form of electrical power supply (cl. 3.1). It does not apply to building categories outside Schedule 1 of the Ordinance, to buildings excluded by section 4 of the Ordinance, or to installations in Schedule 2 of the Ordinance (cl. 3.2). Requirements are standards at the corresponding design conditions (cl. 4.3).

## Regulatory Overview

For a prescribed building whose consent to commencement of superstructure works is given after commencement of Parts 2 and 3 of the Ordinance, the building services installations must comply with Sections 5 to 8 (prescriptive) or Section 9 (performance-based), and that duty covers new construction and all later retrofitting whether or not the works are major retrofitting works (cl. 4.1).

Where that consent was given on or before commencement of those Parts, major retrofitting works as defined in the Ordinance must comply with Section 10 (cl. 4.2). Installations must afterwards be maintained to the version of this code declared in the Certificate of Compliance Registration or Form of Compliance (cl. 4.4, citing ss. 12(3) and 18(2) of the Ordinance).

### 2. Air-conditioning load, air side, and water side (s. 6.1–6.10)

Clause 6.1.2 includes air-moving equipment that is part of a fire service installation but also provides normal air-conditioning, a unitary air-conditioner for a lift car, and equipment outside the building that is owned by the owner or responsible person and serves a space in the building. High-voltage equipment and Schedule 2 installations are outside (cl. 6.1.3).

Design conditions for human-comfort load calculations (Table 6.4):

| Condition | Application | Temperature and humidity |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor summer | Office and classroom | Minimum dry bulb **23°C**; minimum RH **50%** |
| Indoor summer | Other | Minimum dry bulb **22°C**; minimum RH **50%** |
| Indoor winter | Hotel | Maximum dry bulb **24°C**; maximum RH **50%** |
| Indoor winter | Other | Maximum dry bulb **22°C**; maximum RH **50%** |
| Outdoor summer | All | Maximum dry bulb **35°C** with wet bulb lower than **29°C**, or maximum wet bulb **29°C** with dry bulb lower than **35°C** |
| Outdoor winter | All | Minimum dry bulb **7°C** |

### 3. Insulation, equipment efficiency, metering, and plant control (ss. 6.11–6.14)

Chilled-water pipework, suction refrigerant pipework, ductwork carrying cooled air, and AHU casing handling cooled air need the minimum thickness in Tables 6.11a, 6.11b and 6.11c for the ambient condition and the insulation conductivity (cl. 6.11.1).

Worked row from Table 6.11a (chilled water, steel pipe, line temperature 5°C): pipe outer diameter **21.3 mm**, outdoor, thermal conductivity **0.024 W/m·°C**, surface coefficient **9**, insulation thickness **20 mm**. The same diameter at conductivity 0.038 and surface coefficient 13.5 is **15 mm** outdoors. Conditioned-space thickness for that diameter is **13 mm**. Outdoor and unconditioned-space thickness is based on a **27°C** dew point at 90% coincident relative humidity. Ceiling-void thickness depends on the conditions the registered energy assessor declares; the reference assumption in remark @5 is a **26°C** dew point at 85% coincident relative humidity. Full diameters are in the tables.

Minimum full-load COP, unitary air-conditioner, cooling, free air flow (Table 6.12a Part 1). Air-cooled: **2.8** at 7.5 kW and below for split and for non-split types outside the room-air-conditioner labelling scheme; **2.65** above 7.5 kW and below 40 kW; **2.6** from 40 to 200 kW; **3.3** above 200 kW. Water-cooled, all ratings: **3**, or **3.1** where refrigerant volume flow is varied to meet load. Heating-mode heat-pump COP: **3.1** air-cooled in every capacity band in that part of the table; **3.4** water-cooled.

Room air conditioners inside the Mandatory Energy Efficiency Labelling Scheme under the Energy Efficiency (Labelling of Products) Ordinance (Cap. 598) must be Grade 1 or Grade 2 in the Code of Practice on Energy Labelling of Products 2020 (cl. 6.12.2).

VRF full-load cooling COP, air-cooled modular top-discharge (Table 6.12a Part 2): **3.9** at 20 kW or below; **4.0** above 20 to 40 kW; **3.7** above 40 to 80 kW; **3.5** above 80 kW. Rating condition for the VRF table remark @3: equivalent refrigerant piping **5.0 m**, level difference **0 m**.

Air-cooled chiller minimum COP at full load, free air flow (Table 6.12b): reciprocating **2.8** below 400 kW and **2.9** at 400 kW and above; scroll **2.9** / **3.0** on the same split; screw **3.2** below 500 kW and **3.2** at 500 kW and above; VSD screw **3.1** (75% load **4.0**) below 500 kW and **3.1** (**4.1**) at 500 kW and above; centrifugal **3.2** all ratings; VSD centrifugal **3.3** (**4.2**) all ratings. A variable-speed chiller must also meet the 75% figure (cl. 6.12.1).

Water-cooled chiller minimum cooling COP (Table 6.12b): reciprocating/scroll **4.3** below 500 kW, **4.7** from 500 to 1 000 kW, **5.3** above 1 000 kW; screw **5.1** / **5.3** / **5.5** on those three bands; VSD screw **4.8** (**6.4** at 75%) below 500 kW, **5.2** (**7.0**) from 500 to 1 000 kW, **5.3** (**7.5**) above 1 000 kW; centrifugal **5.4** below 1 000 kW (remark @3: minimum COP for rated capacity below 500 kW; remark @4: **5.6** for 500 to below 1 000 kW), **5.8** from 1 000 to 3 000 kW, **5.9** above 3 000 kW; VSD centrifugal **5.5** (**7.3**) below 1 000 kW, **5.8** (**7.9**) from 1 000 to 3 000 kW, **5.9** (**8.0**) above 3 000 kW. Standard rating: air-cooled condenser ambient **35°C**, chilled water **12.5°C** in / **7°C** out; water-cooled condenser fresh water **32°C** in / **37°C** out, sea water **28°C** in / **33°C** out, same chilled-water temperatures. Excludes a heat-recovery chiller and a low-temperature chiller with design leaving fluid below **4.4°C**.

Heat pump full-load heating COP (Table 6.12c): air-to-water **2.8** at 100 kW or below, **2.8** above 100 kW up to 500 kW, **3.2** above 500 kW; water-to-water **4.4** at 500 kW or below, **4.5** above 500 kW. Heated water **40°C** in / **45°C** out. Air-to-water ambient **7°C** dry bulb and **90%** RH.

Open-circuit cooling tower (cl. 6.12.4–6.12.5): at least **1.7 L/s per kW** of fan-motor nameplate power for centrifugal fans, and **3.4 L/s per kW** for propeller or axial fans, at 37°C entering condenser water, 32°C leaving, and 28°C entering-air wet bulb. Out of scope (cl. 8.1.3): mechanized vehicle parking system, service lift, stairlift, industrial-truck-loaded freight lift, stage lift, powered lifting platform, a lift not on traction suspension ropes and not on a hydraulic piston, and Schedule 2 installations.

Traction-drive running active power at rated load, rated speed, upward, must not exceed Tables 8.4.1a (new building) and 8.4.1b (major retrofitting in an existing building) (cl. 8.4.1). Examples from Table 8.4.1a for a new building:

| Rated load | Vc < 1 m/s | 1 ≤ Vc < 1.5 | 2 ≤ Vc < 2.5 | 5 ≤ Vc < 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L < 750 kg | 5.6 kW | 7.8 kW | 12.6 kW | 24.4 kW |
| 1 000 kg ≤ L < 1 350 kg | 9.5 kW | 13.4 kW | 21.3 kW | 42.4 kW |
| 1 600 kg ≤ L < 2 000 kg | 13.1 kW | 19.4 kW | 30.1 kW | 61.1 kW |

Clause 8.4.1.1 does not apply to a lift with rated speed not less than **9 m/s** serving a zone of over **50 storeys** or over **175 m** between the top or bottom-most landing and the principal or ground landing, if it is designated as a fireman’s lift or a sky-lobby shuttle serving two principal stops; or to a lift with rated load at or above **5 000 kg** at a rated speed of **3 m/s** or above.

Hydraulic lifts, escalators and passenger conveyors have their own maximum electrical-power tables (Tables 8.4.2, 8.4.3 and 8.4.4). Escalator and passenger-conveyor allowances depend on nominal width and rise; width between 1 000 mm and 1 400 mm is interpolated.

| Utilization rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Power factor (cl. 8.5.1) | Not below **0.85** at rated load, rated speed, upward (lift), or at brake load, rated speed, upward where there is a rise (escalator or conveyor) |
| Decoration load (Table 8.5.2) | L < 1 800 kg: **0.405 × L**, or **445 kg**, whichever is smaller. L ≥ 1 800 kg: **0.2772 × L − 0.00001665 × L²**, or **915 kg**, whichever is smaller |
| Parking mode (cl. 8.5.3) | At least one lift in a bank parks in low traffic and does not answer calls until returned to normal operation |
| Car fan and air-conditioning (cl. 8.5.4) | Except an observation lift travelling through, or whose well is in, unconditioned space: ventilation off after **2 minutes** idle; air-conditioning off after **10 minutes** idle and not restarted sooner than **5 minutes**. Ventilation fan not above **0.53 W per L/s** |
| Regenerative braking (cl. 8.5.5) | Required for each lift with rated speed of **2.0 m/s** or above; power fed toward the incoming source of the driving controller |
| Car lighting (cl. 8.5.6) | Table 5.4 LPD (11.0 W/m²) unless total fixed lighting does not exceed **70 W**. The 150 W automatic-control exception does not apply. After **10 minutes** idle, lighting reduces to **50%** or less. Clause 5.5.1 control-point limits do not apply in the car |

**SD takeaway:** Any lift at 2.0 m/s or faster needs regenerative braking, decoration load must stay inside Table 8.5.2, and a bank needs a parking mode on at least one car.

### 6. Performance-based approach (s. 9)

Section 9 is an alternative to full compliance with Sections 5 to 8. Clauses 5.1–5.3, 6.1–6.3, 7.1–7.3 and 8.1–8.3 still apply.

Table 9.4 lists the requirements that may be traded: lighting LPD (5.4) and automatic lighting control (5.6); air-side fan power (6.7), variable flow (6.8), pipe friction (6.9), system control (6.10) and equipment efficiency (6.12); electrical distribution loss (7.4), motors (7.5) and power quality (7.6); lift and escalator electrical power (8.4), utilization (8.5) and THD (8.6); and overall thermal transfer value to the same requirements as the Building (Energy Efficiency) Regulation (Cap. 123M).

Any traded item must stay within **25%** of the corresponding prescriptive requirement (cl. 9.4.2). Design energy of the designed building must not exceed the energy budget of a reference building that meets Sections 5 to 8, both calculated by the same numerical method under Appendix A (cl. 9.5). An increase from missing a traded requirement may be offset by better performance on Sections 5 to 8, an energy-efficient feature, a better OTTV where Cap. 123M applies, or recovered or renewable energy captured or generated on site. Items in the trade-off must be under the same ownership and must actually be installed. On-site recovery or renewable systems need 15-minute metering kept **36 months**.

**SD takeaway:** A performance-based submission still has to beat or match the reference-building energy budget, and no traded prescriptive item may deviate by more than 25%.

### 7. Major retrofitting — which clauses switch on (s. 10, Table 10.1)

Major retrofitting is as prescribed in Schedule 3 of the Ordinance. Table 10.1 states which BEC clauses then apply. Floor area in item (a) is the internal floor area of the works area, which may be the hoarded area and need not be the area served by the installation. The 12-month period may be counted from the commencement of any one of the works in the series; if works in that period already reach **500 m²**, other works of the same series in the same unit or common area also comply even if they start outside that period. Ratings are of the newly installed equipment. A **4%** trade-off on the Table 6.12b minimum chiller COP is allowed for a refrigerant with global warming potential below **750**, with supporting information in the Form of Compliance.

| Works | Extra condition | Clauses that apply |
|---|---|---|
| Luminaires, works area ≥ 500 m², same series within 12 months, total circuit wattage of added or replacement luminaires **≥ 3 kW** | No existing luminaires, or the new circuit wattage is more than **50%** of the original luminaires in the area | LPD 5.4 |
