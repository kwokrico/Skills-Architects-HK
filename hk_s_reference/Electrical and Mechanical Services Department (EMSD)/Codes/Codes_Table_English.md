# Building Energy Codes Index (English)

**Issued by:** Electrical and Mechanical Services Department
**Scope:** The 2024 Building Energy Code, the 2024 Energy Audit Code, that audit code incorporated with Addendum No. 1/2025, and the Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations, 2025 Edition (4 documents in this library). This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The short title links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF is in `source_reference/` under the same filename stem. Where the PDF filename differs, the row also links the PDF.
**Titles:** The official title is the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current code issued under the Buildings Energy Efficiency Ordinance (Cap. 610), and, for the wiring code, the current Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations.
