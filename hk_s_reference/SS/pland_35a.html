        The Planning Department (PlanD) was established on 1 January 1990. Over the past 35 years, PlanD has brought together professionals from different fields and colleagues of various grades to undertake town planning duties and create land resources for Hong Kong,
        helping to shape it into the “Asia’s World City” it is today.

        Looking ahead, PlanD will continue to work with the people of Hong Kong to create more opportunities, and to develop a more liveable city together.

         

        Milestones

        1990

        Establishment of PlanD under the then Planning, Environment and Lands Branch, amalgamating all the planning functions previously carried out by the then Town Planning Office, the Strategic Planning Unit and various development offices of the then
        Territory Development Department

        Endorsed the Metroplan Selected Strategy, providing a planning and land use framework for restructuring the metro area

        1996
