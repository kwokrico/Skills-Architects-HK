# Active Design Guidelines
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
May 2023 | Planning Department consultancy report, Agreement No. CE 25/2019 (TP), Study on Active Design for Healthier Lifestyle

> Scope note: Advisory guidelines for a built environment that makes everyday physical activity easier. The report states that the guidelines are not prescriptive and do not include quantitative targets. Footpath widths stay in Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 8 and the Transport Planning and Design Manual.

## Regulatory Overview
The study takes forward the Hong Kong 2030+ direction to bring active design into planning and development. The guidelines are organised at neighbourhood scale and at building scale. Neighbourhood topics deal with destinations and the walk to them. Building topics deal with the position of uses, routes inside and around the building, and active frontages.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What an architect takes from the report
| Scale | Design point in the report |
|---|---|
| Neighbourhood | Provide destinations that can hold intentional and unintentional activity, and a walking route to those destinations |
| Building | Place uses, active spaces and routes so that people move during the day. An active frontage supports a safer pedestrian realm |
| Numbers | None. The report says users are not limited to quantitative restrictions |

**SD takeaway:** Use this report to ask whether the walk is a destination. Take every metre from Chapter 8, the Transport Planning and Design Manual, or the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access.
