## Regulatory Overview
Planning Department describes strategic planning as beginning with the Abercrombie Report in 1948, with a territorial development strategy reviewed about once a decade. The Civil Engineering and Development Department and the Government fact sheet describe the new-town programme. Planning Department's 35th-anniversary note dates the Metroplan decision, Hong Kong 2030, and Hong Kong 2030+.
