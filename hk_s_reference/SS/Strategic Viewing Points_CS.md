# Strategic Viewing Points
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Planning Department page saved 15 November 2026. The page is dated as current to 2026 on the saved copy.

> Scope note: The eight public viewpoints used with the ridgeline test in Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines Chapter 11. The page is a viewpoint list and a panorama set for visual impact assessment. It is not a view-corridor width.

## Regulatory Overview
The Planning Department publishes location plans, orthophotos and panorama photographs of eight strategic viewing points for visual impact assessment. The note on the page says to refer to the marked ridgeline and the marked 20% building-free zone, and to count rooftop structures other than an antenna when preserving views to the ridgeline. Chapter 11 states the building-free zone as 20% to 30% below the ridgelines when viewed from these points. Use both texts: the chapter gives the range, and the page identifies the points and the marked 20% line on the panorama.
