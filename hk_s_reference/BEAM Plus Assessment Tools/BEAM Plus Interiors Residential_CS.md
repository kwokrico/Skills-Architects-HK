# BEAM Plus Interiors — Residential
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Publication date: 27 November 2023 | Funded by HKGBC · Developed by BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: Label for greener **home** fit-outs. Users: homeowners, designers, builders; assurance for owners **and tenants**. It is **not** the non-residential interiors score table (no Platinum/Gold band), **not** whole-building NB/EB, and **not** a statutory fit-out substitute. Submit **after** works are complete; bake the standard into the concept on **day one**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus Interiors — Residential is a trusted standard of environmental sustainability for home fitting-out works: a sustainability and healthy-design guide for interior designers and builders, and a mark of assurance for homeowners and tenants. Rating is **checklist-based** (Green+ / Green), not the NB percentage ladder. Previously certified flats with certificates expired after **5 years** may re-enter.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Why certify (four value pillars)

| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Savings** | Save energy and water expenses |
| **Health and Wellbeing** | Occupant health and wellbeing |
| **Value** | Banks / institutions **may** offer preferential mortgage or insurance terms; property-value enhancement (verify with the lender — brochure claim, not a guarantee) |
| **Trusted** | Third-party certificate proves the home is truly green |

**SD takeaway:** Brief the client that the “value” pillar is a market claim to verify with their bank/insurer, not a BEAM-issued financial product.

---

### 2. Assessment aspects (checklist — no NR score table)

1. Integrated Design and Construction Management  
2. Materials and Waste  
3. Energy Use  
4. Water Use  
5. Health and Wellbeing  
6. Innovations and Additions  

**No Management aspect** (unlike Non-Residential v2.0). Brochure does not publish item lists or weights — the rating is a **count of checklist items achieved**.

**SD takeaway:** Do not import the Non-Residential Platinum 75-point table; this tool is a 45 / 30 item count.

---

### 3. Ratings and timing

| Rating | Minimum checklist items achieved |
|---|---|
| **Green+** (卓越) | ≥ **45** items |
| **Green** (優良) | ≥ **30** items |

- Day-one concept for the home fit-out.
- Submit after works complete.
- Interior designer or fit-out contractor may submit per contract.
- Existing homes / previously certified flats with 5-year-expired certificates welcome.

**SD takeaway:** Green+ needs **45** checklist items — if the fit-out is a light refresh, count items at concept; a 30-item Green is the floor, not a near-miss of Green+.
