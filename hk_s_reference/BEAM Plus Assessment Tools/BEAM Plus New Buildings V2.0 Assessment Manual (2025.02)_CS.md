# BEAM Plus New Buildings V2.0 Assessment Manual
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This Edition: February 2025 (First Issue September 2019; Second Edition May 2021; Third Edition October 2023) | Owned and operated by BEAM Society Limited (BSL); formal certification by Hong Kong Green Building Council (HKGBC)

> Scope note: This Assessment Manual is the credit criteria for **BEAM Plus New Buildings Version 2.0 (NB v2.0)** — demolition, planning, design, construction and commissioning of **new buildings**. It is **not** Existing Buildings, Interiors, Neighbourhood, Existing Schools, or Data Centres. It is **not** a Buildings Ordinance / BEC / FS Code substitute. BSL does not endorse self-assessed grading; only HKGBC formal certification may be promoted. Unauthorised buildings or portions are not assessed; reported unauthorised works can lead HKGBC and BSL to deprive the rating. Technical Circular Letters incorporated in this February 2025 edition amend individual credits — use this Manual edition, not an older credit map.

## Regulatory Overview

NB v2.0 covers design, construction, and associated testing and commissioning of all types of new buildings — commercial, educational, government, industrial, office, residential, hotels, shopping centres and similar — including shell and fitted-out premises within a development, with applicable credits varying by type. Assessment is not finalised until the building is completed, so that design features and construction practice are actually implemented, and is intended to dovetail with Existing Buildings and Interiors thereafter.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility, boundaries, and unauthorised works (s.1.1)

| Lock | Rule as printed |
|---|---|
| In scope | Design, construction, testing and commissioning of all types of new buildings from small single buildings to large developments (commercial, educational, government, industrial, office, residential, hotels, shopping centres, etc.) |
| Shell / fit-out | Assessment may cover shell and fitted-out premises; focus is what designer, builder and commissioning agent achieve; applicable credit count varies |
| Project boundary | Applicant defines the project boundary; need not match the site boundary, but must stay **consistent** throughout the assessment |
| Unauthorised works | Does not assess unauthorised buildings or portions (works not complying with the Buildings Ordinance); reported non-compliance → HKGBC and BSL may deprive the rating |
| Timing | Should be initiated in the **early stages** of project development; assessment covers demolition, planning, design, construction and commissioning |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze the BEAM project boundary and target grade at concept — changing the boundary mid-assessment breaks consistency across credits.

### 2. Assessment stages and conclusive credits (s.1.2)

| Stage | Submittal character (as printed) |
|---|---|
| **Provisional Assessment (PA)** | Drawings may be tender drawings or conceptual sketches conveying design intent; technical data may be specifications, catalogues, or preliminary calculations |
| **Compliance Assessment (CA)** | Shop drawings from the contractor (project name, title, date); detailed technical data (equipment schedules, approved catalogues, simulation reports) |
| **Remaining Final Assessment (RFA)** | As-fitted drawings; detailed as-installed technical data |

**Conclusive Credits** (marked with a specialised icon): if achieved at PA with evidence meeting CA requirements, no further documents are required at CA/RFA unless discrepancies, changes, or outstanding issues arise.

Certification process: Independent BAS or BSL in-house BAS assess; Technical Review Committee (TRC) of BSL reviews and endorses; HKGBC issues certification. Detail procedures sit in the BEAM Plus Project Assessment Procedures Manual (not this PDF).

**SD takeaway:** Treat PA as a real design-freeze milestone for conclusive credits — late massing or plant changes reopen those heads.

### 3. Category weights and scoring (s.1.2)

| Category | Weighting |
|---|---|
| Integrated Design and Construction Management (**IDCM**) | **18%** |
| Sustainable Site (**SS**) | **15%** |
| Materials and Waste (**MW**) | **9%** |
| Energy Use (**EU**) | **29%** |
| Water Use (**WU**) | **7%** |
| Health and Wellbeing (**HWB**) | **22%** |

| Scoring rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Prerequisites | All applicable prerequisites must be achieved; otherwise grade = **“Prerequisite(s) Not Achieved”** |
| BONUS / additional BONUS | Counted under their categories; factor **1.2** applied in score calculation. BONUS is independent of normal credits under the same item; **additional BONUS** requires the normal credit(s) first. Max possible score under each category = **100%** |
| IA | Up to **10** IA credits may be submitted toward the total qualifying for an award |
| Summary credit pool (IDCM) | Summary of Credits lists IDCM as **25 + 16 BONUS** (other category pools are credit-item based; confirm applicable set with BEAM Pro) |

**SD takeaway:** EU (29%) + HWB (22%) + IDCM (18%) = **69%** of the weighted pie — envelope, inclusive design, and integrated-team process are first-order SD locks.

### 4. Prerequisites (must all be achieved)

| ID | Title | Hard lock from this Manual |
|---|---|---|
| **IDCM P1** | Sustainability Champions – Project | Accredited **BEAM Pro** with valid credential for **NB v2.0** engaged as Project BEAM Pro (point of contact; key team member; checklist; guidance; advise Client). Project / Design / Construction BEAM Pros must be **different personnel** |
| **IDCM P2** | Environmental Management Plan | Prerequisite (EMP for construction impacts — detail in credit body) |
| **IDCM P3** | Timber Used for Temporary Works | **No virgin forest products** for temporary works (falsework, formwork, hoarding) unless exceptional circumstances; timber from sustainable forestry or re-used material; PA may use tender specs; construction needs monthly timber summary |
| **SS P1** | Minimum Landscaping Requirements | Sites **≥ 1,000 m²**: site coverage of greenery **≥ 20%** (measurement generally per PNAP APP-152, with Manual-stated exceptions); soil volume **≥ 12 m³** per tree; soil depths **≥ 1.2 m / 0.6 m / 0.3 m** for trees / shrubs / grass; irrigation and maintenance access |
| **MW P1** | Minimum Waste Handling Facilities | All buildings except listed single-family ≤3-floor / transient / plant-only exceptions: minimum recycle/waste facilities for collection, sorting, storage, recycling and disposal; strategy plan for organic, recyclable and non-recyclable streams with adequate floor and estate-level space and access |
| **EU P1** | Minimum Energy Performance | **BEC-governing buildings:** improve **2%** on code minimum COP/CSPF for AC **and** reduce **3%** on code maximum lighting power density. **Non-BEC-governing buildings:** demonstrate compliance with applicable BEC for those same two systems. Mixed projects: each building follows its status. Applicable BEC edition per latest BSL Circular Letter |
| **WU P1** | Minimum Water Saving Performance | Water-efficient flow devices → estimated annual potable saving of **10%** |
| **HWB P1** | Minimum Ventilation Performance | (a) On-site outdoor air pollutants measured before building design (CO, NO₂, O₃, PM₁₀ by IAQ CIB); (b) demonstrate minimum ventilation quantity for the designed ventilation mode |

If all applicable prerequisites are met but Bronze overall is not reached → **“Pre-requisites Achieved”** (not a grade).

**SD takeaway:** Appoint Project BEAM Pro at concept; reserve greenery ≥20% on sites ≥1,000 m²; size waste rooms and AC/lighting performance above BEC before GA freeze; schedule outdoor-air sampling before design locks the envelope strategy.

### 5. Rating gates (s.1.2 Determination of Overall Grade)

Requires: (i) all applicable prerequisites; (ii) overall score; (iii) minimum **20%** in **each** category.

| Grade | Minimum % each category | Total score |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 20% | ≥ 75% |
| Gold | 20% | ≥ 65% |
| Silver | 20% | ≥ 55% |
| Bronze | 20% | ≥ 40% |

**SD takeaway:** A Platinum overall score still fails if any weighted category is below **20%** — do not starve SS / MW / WU to chase EU.

### 6. SD process and space locks (selected heads)

| Credit | SD lock from this Manual |
|---|---|
| **IDCM 3** Integrated Design Process | 1 credit for whole-system thinking at conceptual design; **+1** for at least one multi-disciplinary design charrette in conceptual/schematic stages (glossary: charrette no later than design development, preferably schematic). Buildability before end of design development; O&M design consideration |
| **SS P1 / SS greenery** | Greenery geometry, soil depth/volume, and irrigation access are prerequisite on sites ≥1,000 m² |
| **MW P1** | Recycle/waste rooms on floors and at estate level with access for cleaners, users and collectors |
| **EU P1 / EU 1–2** | Envelope and plant path must clear BEC-plus thresholds; EU weighting is 29% |
| **WU P1 / WU 8** | Fixture schedule for 10% saving; plant/tank space if claiming harvesting/recycling |
| **HWB P1 / HWB 3** | Outdoor-air baseline before design; inclusive design credit path sits under HWB (22% weight) |
| Material time | Supporting information must be captured at the material time — cannot be done retrospectively |

**Certificate validity:** Defined in the glossary as the duration for which a BEAM Plus certificate and grading remain effective and officially recognised by HKGBC (numeric term not fixed in the framework body of this Manual — confirm on HKGBC materials for the project).

**SD takeaway:** Run the integrated-design charrette at schematic; freeze greenery, waste rooms, BEC-plus AC/lighting, and fixture flow rates before PA.
