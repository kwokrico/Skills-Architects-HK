# BEAM Plus New Buildings V2.0
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
HKGBC brochure (New Buildings V2.0) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: This tool rates **new construction** (demolition–planning–design–construction–commissioning) and **major renovation / A&A / wholesale or partial conversion**. It is **not** the O&M tool (Existing Buildings), **not** a fit-out tool (Interiors), **not** a masterplan tool (Neighbourhood), and **not** a Buildings Ordinance / BEC / FS Code substitute. Credit pool **varies by project type** — confirm with the consultant / BEAM Pro before locking an SD credit map.

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus New Buildings covers all new building types — small single buildings to large developments — whether residential, commercial, industrial or other, and can also be used for major refurbishment or alteration including wholesale or partial conversion. Overall rating is the weighted percentage of applicable credits in each aspect; all applicable prerequisites in all aspects must be achieved, and the Final Assessment certificate lasts **five years** after which owners are steered to Existing Buildings.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility and SD brief (brochure: “What buildings can be assessed?”)

| In | Out / caution |
|---|---|
| All new buildings: residential, commercial, industrial, community facilities, infrastructure | Do not use this tool alone for fit-out, school O&M, or data-centre-specific EU best-practice (those have dedicated tools) |
| Major renovation, A&A, wholesale **or** partial conversion | Credit count is typology-dependent — do not copy another project’s credit map |
| Lifecycle: demolition, planning, design, construction, commissioning | FA is not a perpetual label — plan the EB hand-off at year 5 |

**What a successful project is sold as (design brief drivers):** better indoor environment (health, wellbeing, productivity); less pollution to the external environment; energy-efficient buildings / systems / equipment including renewables; lower unsustainable consumption of scarce resources (**water**, **tropical timber**); more cost-effective sustainable design and process.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **target grade + typology credit pool** before massing; treat conversion / major A&A as NB, not EB.

### 2. V2.0 process changes (three upgrade features)

| Theme | What changed | Architect action at SD |
|---|---|---|
| **More human-centric** | Aspect rebranded to **Health and Wellbeing**; new healthy-living credits | Programme dual-aspect / daylight / inclusive design as core, not a late fit-out add |
| **More integrated green building design** | New aspect **IDCM** added | Run integrated design workshops (AP / RSE / BS / landscape / BEAM Pro) from concept |
| **More adaptable, certain, practicable** | Every credit reviewed; tool redesigned for clearer guidance | Use the current Assessment Manual — do not recycle v1.x credit maps |

**SD takeaway:** IDCM and HWB are first-order SD process and briefing items, not post-GA green add-ons.

---

### 3. Seven aspects and weights (brochure bar chart)

| Aspect | Weight | Subtopics (brochure) | SD freeze items |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Energy Use (EU)** | **29%** | Energy use reduction and control; renewable and alternative energy generation; energy-efficient equipment | Envelope / glazing / roof path; HVAC concept; metering and control; PV / alternative-energy area and structure |
| **Health and Wellbeing (HWB)** | **22%** | Design for green living; inclusive design; indoor environmental quality | Unit mix / dual aspect; BFA beyond DMBA; daylight, ventilation, acoustics, thermal strategy |
| **Integrated Design and Construction Management (IDCM)** | **18%** | Integrated Design Process; green construction practices; smart design and technologies | Team process plan; C&D waste / site green plan; smart-systems architecture |
| **Sustainable Sites (SS)** | **15%** | Neighbourhood integration; ecological responsive design; bioclimatic building design; climate resilience and adaptability | Massing vs neighbours; ecology / trees; bioclimatic form; flood / typhoon / heat resilience |
| **Materials and Waste (MW)** | **9%** | Materials use; green products and materials; waste reduction | Spec: timber policy, recycled content, low-impact products; demolition / C&D diversion targets |
| **Water Use (WU)** | **7%** | Water conservation; effluent discharges; rainwater harvesting and grey water recycling | Fixture flow rates; plant / tank / riser space for rainwater and greywater |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Outside the 100% pie | Innovative technology and techniques | Claim only if performance is truly beyond baseline credits |

EU + HWB + IDCM = **69%**. IA is listed as a seventh aspect but is not in the percentage bars.

**SD takeaway:** Lock envelope, orientation, inclusive layout, and integrated-team process at SD — those three aspects decide most of the grade.

---

### 4. Rating gates (Final Assessment)

Overall rating = weighted % of applicable credits under each aspect. **All applicable prerequisites in all aspects** required.

| Rating | Overall score | Min % of applicable credits **in each aspect** |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 75 | 20 |
| Gold | 65 | 20 |
| Silver | 55 | 20 |
| Bronze | 40 | 20 |

- Met all prerequisites but below Bronze → **“Prerequisites Achieved”** (not a grade).
- **FA certificate valid 5 years** → then pursue **Existing Buildings**.

**SD takeaway:** A Platinum overall score still fails if any aspect is below **20%** — do not starve SS / MW / WU to chase EU.
