# BEAM Plus Existing Buildings V2.0
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
HKGBC brochure (Existing Buildings V2.0) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited · Funding support noted: Construction Industry Council

> Scope note: This tool measures **actual operational performance** and facility-management practice of **existing buildings of all types and all ages**. Assessment covers only areas under **applicant control**; tenant involvement earns additional / bonus credits. It is **not** the new-build / major A&A tool (NB V2.0), **not** the school-specific tool, **not** the data-centre EDC tool, and **not** a statutory substitute. V2.0 flexibility is aimed at pulling in ~**42,000** existing HK buildings toward the Government Energy Saving Plan (brochure cites 2025).

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus Existing Buildings can start at any time during operational life and covers management, operation, maintenance and improvement for commercial, educational, government, industrial, office, residential, hotel, shopping-centre and similar stock. Applicants choose **Comprehensive Scheme A** (one-step), **Comprehensive Scheme B** (step-wise, remaining aspects within **3 years**), or **Selective Scheme** (per-aspect certificates, no prerequisites, lower threshold).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Scheme choice (registration decision)

| Scheme | Mechanism | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Comprehensive A** (one-step) | Assess **all** aspects → Comprehensive Certificate | NB-certified buildings; full upgrade push |
| **Comprehensive B** (step-wise) | Some aspects → **intermediate result** → remaining aspects **within 3 years** → Comprehensive Certificate | Budget / programme constrained |
| **Selective** | Any one or more aspects; **certificate per aspect**; **no prerequisites**; lower threshold | Partial capability; EU-only funding path |

```
Comp A:  [all aspects] ──────────────────────────► Comprehensive Certificate
Comp B:  [some aspects] → intermediate → [rest ≤3 yrs] → Comprehensive Certificate
Selective: [chosen aspect(s)] ───────────────────► Selective Certificate(s) per aspect
```

**Funding flag:** Registration under **Comprehensive** **or** Selective **Energy Use (EU)** may contribute to higher priority for **CLP Eco Building Fund** or **HK Electric Smart Power Fund**.

**SD takeaway:** Sign off scheme (A / B / Selective + which aspects) before writing the O&M / retrofit brief — it changes evidence, programme and fee path.

### 2. Aspects and weights (Comprehensive)

| Aspect | Weight | Subtopics (brochure) |
|---|---|---|
| **Management (MAN)** | **24%** | Green procurement; health, safety and environmental management; ESG disclosure; cleaning and pest control; **green lease** |
| **Energy Use (EU)** | **24%** | Annual CO₂ emissions or energy use; energy-efficient systems and equipment; energy management; **Retro-Commissioning** |
| **Water Use (WU)** | **14%** | Water audit; water conservation; effluent discharges |
| **Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ)** | **14%** | Safety, hygiene, IAQ and ventilation, thermal comfort, lighting, acoustics and noise |
| **Materials and Waste Aspects (MWA)** | **14%** | Green purchasing; waste disposal and recycling |
| **Site Aspects (SA)** | **10%** | Location and design of the building; emissions from the site; greenery and site amenities; building amenities |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Outside the pie | Performance gains from innovative design solutions — **N/A under Selective Scheme** |

Comprehensive = overall performance by aspect weighting. Selective = individual aspect assessment only.

**SD takeaway:** MAN + EU = **48%** of Comprehensive — green lease, retro-Cx and energy metering are the retrofit SD cores; IA cannot rescue a Selective path.

### 3. Rating gates

**Comprehensive** — all applicable prerequisites in all aspects required:

| Rating | Overall score | Min % MAN & EU | Min % SA, MWA, WU, IEQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 75 | 70 | 50 |
| Gold | 65 | 60 | 40 |
| Silver | 55 | 50 | 30 |
| Bronze | 40 | 40 | 20 |

**Selective** — no prerequisites; grade per assessed aspect:

| Grade | % of credits in assessed aspect |
|---|---|
| Excellent 卓越 | 70% |
| Very Good 優良 | 60% |
| Good 良好 | 50% |
| Satisfactory 滿意 | 40% |

**Record of Achievement** (all assessed aspect results) available on request after completing **≥ 2 aspects**.

**Validity:** both schemes **5 years**; Comprehensive Scheme B first intermediate certificate **3 years**. Renew by new assessment.

**SD takeaway:** Platinum Comprehensive dies if MAN or EU is below **70%**, even with a high overall score — do not treat site/IEQ cosmetics as a substitute for energy and management.

### 4. Volume certification (portfolio ≥ 5 properties)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Why | Pre-approved credit + portfolio assessment → lower cost / effort; synergise corporate sustainability policy; align with market disclosure reporting |
| Eligibility for reduced fee | **EB V2.0 only** (Comprehensive or Selective); must adopt a volume-certification assessment mechanism; enrol **≥ 5 properties**; consult **BSL** on feasibility **before** registration |
| Same scheme | All properties in the portfolio must opt for the **same** scheme (Comprehensive, or the **same** Selective aspect) |
| Fee basis | Discount by accumulated registered count under the same owner / developer / FM company |
| Output | HKGBC portfolio certificate + Project Online Account; optional individual certificates / plaques via HKGBC Secretariat |

**SD takeaway:** For a ≥5-asset FM client, lock **one scheme / one Selective aspect** across the portfolio before any single-building credit map.

### 5. Exemplars (ops / retrofit lessons)

| Project | Rating | Design / FM move |
|---|---|---|
| **Pacific Place** | Final Platinum, Comprehensive | Shopping mall + Grade-A office; proactive management + **monitoring-based HVAC retro-commissioning** → electricity reduction |
| **City One Shatin Phase 3C** | First residential Selective **Excellent (MAN)** | EMS since 1987; stakeholder engagement + ongoing facilities refurbishment; landscape greenery + community green activities |

**SD takeaway:** Retro-Cx of central HVAC and a live environmental-management system are the proven Platinum / Excellent moves — not façade-only upgrades.
