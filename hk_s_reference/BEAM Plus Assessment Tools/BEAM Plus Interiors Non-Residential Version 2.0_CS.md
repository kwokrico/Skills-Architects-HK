# BEAM Plus Interiors — Non-Residential Version 2.0
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Publication date: 27 November 2023 | Funded by HKGBC · Developed by BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: Label for greener **commercial and institutional fitted-out premises** — offices, retail, restaurants, clubhouses, industrial units and other non-residential premises. It is **not** whole-building NB/EB, **not** the residential interiors checklist tool, and **not** a statutory fit-out substitute (MWCS / FS Code / BEC still apply). Submit **after** fitting-out works are finished; bake the standard into the concept on **day one**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus Interiors Non-Residential v2.0 is a trusted standard for non-residential fitting-out works: a sustainability and healthy-design guide for interior designers and builders, and an assurance mark for premises occupiers. Ratings are Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze by overall score; certificates expire after **5 years**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who, what premises, why certify

| Item | Brochure rule |
|---|---|
| Users | Premises occupiers, designers, builders / fit-out contractors |
| Eligible premises | Offices, retail, restaurants, clubhouses, industrial units, other non-residential |
| Submitter | Occupier, or interior designer / fit-out contractor / environmental consultant per contract |
| Existing stock | Existing premises **or** previously certified premises with certificate expired after **5 years** welcome |

| Value pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Savings** | Save energy; minimise operating expenses |
| **Health and Wellbeing** | Occupant health, wellbeing, productivity |
| **Corporate Social Responsibility** | ESG responsibility |
| **Net Zero Commitment** | Optional path for premises pushing harder on de-carbonisation — **Net Zero certification option** |

**SD takeaway:** If the brief is a Net Zero tenancy, this tool (not residential Interiors, not whole-building EB) is the one that advertises a Net Zero certification option — lock that path on day one of the fit-out concept.

### 3. Ratings and timing

| Rating | Overall score |
|---|---|
| Platinum | 75 |
| Gold | 65 |
| Silver | 55 |
| Bronze | 40 |

- Take the green standard into account on the **first day** the fit-out is conceived.
- Submit **after** fitting-out works are finished.
- Certificate life implied by the 5-year expiry / re-entry rule.

**SD takeaway:** Concept-stage spec is the only recoverable moment; post-completion assessment cannot invent dual-flush, DC fan-coil or waste-diversion that was not in the contract.

### 4. Platinum exemplar moves (copy into SD / tender)

**CUHK Yasumoto International Academic Park 9/F (SRSDO) — Platinum (Beta)**

- First campus smart office at CUHK
- Merged two separate offices → flexible open-plan
- Careful materials selection + **reuse of furniture and appliances** → less waste / lower embodied carbon
- Colour palette of UN 17 SDGs; home to 30+ SRSDO staff

**Jardine House BaseHall 2 & 3 — Platinum (Beta)**

- **> 75%** construction waste recycled or upcycled
- Low-flow fixtures; low-flush urinals and WCs
- Brushless **DC fan coil**; programmable **MVAC timers** for non-business hours
- High-efficiency lighting → lower lighting power density
- **Smart meters** for real-time energy data: MVAC, lighting, small power, water heaters, servers, kitchen equipment

**SD takeaway:** Write waste-diversion **≥ 75%**, DC FCU + MVAC timers, low-flow sanitary, and end-use smart metering into the fit-out preliminaries if Platinum is the target.
