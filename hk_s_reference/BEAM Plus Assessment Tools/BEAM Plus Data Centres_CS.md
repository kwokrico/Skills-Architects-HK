# BEAM Plus Data Centres
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
HKGBC brochure (New Data Centres / Existing Data Centres) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: First BEAM Plus tool tailored to a **single project type**. Assessed data-centre area **≥ 500 m²**; data halls (IT racks) + related plant must be a **significant majority** of floor area. New construction / major A&A → **NDC**; operations and management → **EDC**. Consult a **BEAM Pro** before registration. This is **not** generic NB/EB (credits are DC-specific); **not** a statutory substitute (Cap. 123, FSI, BEC still apply).

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus Data Centres rates design and construction of new data centres and operations and maintenance of existing ones, with credit language aligned to practical DC build/operate conditions and a heavy emphasis on energy (servers + cooling). NDC and EDC certificates each last **five years**; NDC owners are then steered to EDC.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility, benefits, why not generic NB/EB

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Area | Assessed DC **≥ 500 m²** |
| Mix | Data halls + related plant = **significant majority** of floor area |
| Stage | New / major A&A → **NDC**; O&M → **EDC** |
| Registration | Consult **BEAM Pro** first |

**Benefits cited:** minimise construction/ops impacts; best practices and innovative tech for cost-effective DCs; organisational commitment to telecom infrastructure sustainability; support HK **carbon neutrality 2050**.

| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Specificity** | First BEAM Plus tool for one project type; credits aligned to practical DC conditions |
| **Best practice on energy use** | Servers + cooling dominate load — credits emphasise energy-efficiency best practices |
| **Emphasis on green management** | Unlike conventional Selective Scheme, **EDC Selective makes EU + MAN mandatory** |

**SD takeaway:** If halls + plant are not the majority of GFA, this tool does not apply — do not force a mixed office/DC podium into NDC.

### 2. New Data Centres (NDC) — 7 aspects

| Aspect | Weight | Subtopics |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy Use (EU)** | **40%** | Best practice on energy use; energy reduction and control; energy-efficient equipment; renewable and alternative energy generation |
| **IDCM** | **20%** | Integrated green building design process; green construction practices; smart and digitised project management process |
| **Sustainable Site (SS)** | **10%** | Bioclimatic building design; biodiversity enhancement; neighbourhood integration; climate resilience and adaptability |
| **Materials and Waste (MW)** | **10%** | Best practice on materials usage; green products and materials; green disposal |
| **Water Use (WU)** | **10%** | Water conservation; water harvesting and recycling; **smart water metering** |
| **Health and Wellbeing (HWB)** | **10%** | Inclusive design; indoor environmental quality |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Outside the pie | New and innovative technologies and techniques |

**NDC rating gates** — all applicable prerequisites in all aspects required:

| Rating | Overall score | Min % under **Energy Use** |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | ≥ 75% | 70% |
| Gold | ≥ 65% | 60% |
| Silver | ≥ 55% | 50% |
| Bronze | ≥ 40% | 40% |

Below Bronze but prerequisites met → **Pre-requisites Achieved**. Valid **5 years** → then pursue **EDC**.

**SD takeaway:** EU is **40%** of NDC and Platinum still needs **70%** of EU credits — freeze hall cooling concept, PUE path, renewables area and smart water metering at SD; envelope-only schemes will miss Platinum.

### 3. Existing Data Centres (EDC) — 8 aspects

| Aspect | Weight | Subtopics |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy Use (EU)** | **40%** | Energy metering and analysis; energy reduction and control; commissioning |
| **Management (MAN)** | **15%** | Health, safety and environmental management; O&M for site, building and building services; ESG disclosure |
| **IDCM** | **15%** | Integrated green building design process; smart and digitised management process |
| **Water Use (WU)** | **10%** | Water recycling; water conservation |
| **Health and Wellbeing (HWB)** | **10%** | IAQ and ventilation, hygiene, thermal comfort, lighting, acoustics and noise |
| **Materials and Waste (MW)** | **5%** | Green purchasing; green disposal |
| **Sustainable Site (SS)** | **5%** | Green building attributes of **host buildings**; heat island mitigation; amenities for O&M |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Outside the pie | Performance gains from innovative design solutions — **N/A under Selective** |

**EDC Comprehensive** — all applicable prerequisites in all aspects required:

| Rating | Overall score | Min % under **EU and MAN** |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum | ≥ 75% | 70% |
| Gold | ≥ 65% | 60% |
| Silver | ≥ 55% | 50% |
| Bronze | ≥ 40% | 40% |

**EDC Selective**

- Grade = % of credits in the assessed aspect(s)
- **EU and MAN are mandatory**
- All applicable prerequisites in assessed aspect(s) must be achieved
- Bands: Platinum **70%** / Gold **60%** / Silver **50%** / Bronze **40%**
- **Record of Achievement** on request after completing all **seven** Selective aspects (IA excluded)
- Valid **5 years**; renew by commissioning and submitting a new assessment

**SD takeaway:** EDC Selective is not a pick-and-mix like EB Selective — **EU + MAN must be in**; host-building greenery / heat-island (SS 5%) is a minority item, not the grade driver.
