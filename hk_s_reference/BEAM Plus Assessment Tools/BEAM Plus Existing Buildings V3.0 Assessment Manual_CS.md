# BEAM Plus Existing Buildings V3.0 Assessment Manual
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Copyright 2025 | Owned and operated by BEAM Society Limited (BSL); formal certification by Hong Kong Green Building Council (HKGBC)

> Scope note: This Assessment Manual is the credit criteria for **BEAM Plus Existing Buildings Version 3.0 (EB v3.0)** — management, operation, maintenance, and environmental performance of **existing buildings of all types and ages**. It is **not** New Buildings, Interiors, Neighbourhood, Existing Schools, or Data Centres. It is **not** a Buildings Ordinance / BEC / FS Code substitute. BSL does not endorse self-assessed ratings; only HKGBC formal certification may be promoted. Unauthorised buildings or portions are not assessed; reported unauthorised works can lead HKGBC and BSL to deprive the rating.

## Regulatory Overview

EB v3.0 covers commercial, educational, government, industrial, office, residential, hotel, shopping-centre and similar stock, whether or not previously green-certified, and normally assesses only areas under the Applicant’s control (tenant involvement can earn additional credit points under individual heads). Certification uses Comprehensive Scheme A (one-step) or Comprehensive Scheme B (step-wise within 3 years of the first intermediate result); there is no Selective Scheme in this Manual.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility, boundaries, and unauthorised works (s.1.1)

| Lock | Rule as printed |
|---|---|
| Typology | Management, operation and maintenance of all types and ages of existing buildings — small single building to large buildings, including commercial, educational, government, industrial, office, residential, hotels, shopping centres, etc. |
| Prior certification | Buildings without prior BEAM Plus or other green certificates are encouraged to participate |
| Local substitution | Where criteria reference local/national policies, applicants outside that context may substitute equivalent local requirements with evidence; if none exist, adopt the Manual as written |
| Unauthorised works | Does not assess buildings or portions unauthorised under the local building ordinance; reported non-compliance / unauthorised portions → HKGBC and BSL may deprive the awarded rating |
| Assessment boundary | Normally only areas under Applicant control; tenant involvement can earn credit points per individual credit heads |
| Neighbourhood | Seeks to reduce negative impacts on neighbours and reward “good neighbour” improvements to immediate surroundings |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm the assessment boundary and that no unauthorised portion sits inside it before locking an O&M or retrofit credit map.

### 2. Certification framework — Scheme A / B (s.1.2)

| Scheme | Mechanism | SD / programme lock |
|---|---|---|
| **Comprehensive Scheme A** (one-step) | All performance categories assessed in one go → one full certificate if requirements fulfilled | Optional **Provisional Assessment (PA)** only under Scheme A |
| **Comprehensive Scheme B** (step-wise) | Assess one or more categories → intermediate result → update assessed categories and submit remaining categories **within 3 years** of first intermediate result → full certificate; graded to the same standard as Scheme A | **PA not permitted** under Scheme B |

Process as printed: Independent BEAM Assessors (BAS) or BSL in-house BAS assess; Assessment Sub-committee (ASC) of BSL reviews and endorses; HKGBC issues certification. Detail procedures sit in the BEAM Plus Procedures Manual (Assessment) (not this PDF).

**SD takeaway:** Choose Scheme A vs B before writing the upgrade programme — Scheme B buys a 3-year staged path but blocks PA.

### 3. Performance categories and credit-point pool (s.1.2–1.3)

Absolute point-based scoring: total credit points across categories, **without category weighting or averaging**. Maximum attainable score: **100 credit points + 10 IA credit points**.

| Category | Credit points in Summary of Credits | Focus (Manual) |
|---|---|---|
| Management (**MAN**) | 38 | Sustainable management of occupied buildings; ESG; net-zero transition; resilience; staff/BIM/smart FM; green lease; tenant engagement |
| Sustainable Site (**SS**) | 17 | Public transport; equipment noise; light pollution; biodiversity; UHI; climate adaptation; neighbourhood; active commuting; EV charging |
| Materials and Waste (**MW**) | 29 | ODS; green products; life-cycle costing; waste handling/reduction; green purchasing |
| Energy Use (**EU**) | 78 | Energy audit; CO₂ / EUI; peak demand; metering; EPC; renewables; energy analysis; retro-commissioning |
| Water Use (**WU**) | 26 | Efficient devices/irrigation; leakage; effluent; recycling; smart metering; consumption reduction; water quality/safety |
| Health and Wellbeing (**HWB**) | 28 | Healthy living; biophilia; inclusive design; O&M amenities; ventilation; acoustics; IAQ/thermal/lighting/daylight; filtration; health protection |
| Innovations and Additions (**IA**) | up to 10 (adds to total; max 10) | Each successful IA credit adds 1 point |

Category pool figures above are the Summary of Credits totals as printed; the Manual caps overall score at 100 + 10 IA.

**SD takeaway:** Treat MAN + EU as the heavy credit pools for retrofit briefing, and plan IA only as a top-up (max 10), not a substitute for weak EU.

### 4. Rating gates (s.1.2 Determination of Overall Rating)

No category prerequisites are stated in this Manual. Rating needs **both** overall points **and** the per-category minima below (excluding IA):

| Rating | Overall credit points | Minimum in MAN, SS, MW, WU & HWB | Minimum in EU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 75 | 4 each | 15 |
| Gold | 65 | 4 each | 15 |
| Silver | 55 | 4 each | 15 |
| Bronze | 40 | 4 each | 15 |

If minima or Bronze overall are missed → **“Assessment Completed Without Any Rating.”**

**SD takeaway:** Platinum dies if EU is below **15** points or any of MAN/SS/MW/WU/HWB is below **4**, even with a high total — do not starve energy for site cosmetics.

### 5. Certificate validity and ongoing data (s.1.2)

| Lock | Rule as printed |
|---|---|
| Validity duration | Refer to HKGBC’s website (not printed as a fixed term in this Manual) |
| Ongoing data report | Must be submitted **annually** after certificate issuance for credits that require it (submittals marked “^”); only credits submitted for assessment need ongoing data |
| Failure to submit | Certificate becomes **invalid** until data is submitted; grading itself is not changed by that failure |
| Record buffer | Record-related submittals (building records, certificates, measurement reports) get a **6-month** buffer unless otherwise specified; the chosen 12-month assessment period must be **consistent across all credits** |
| Existing provisions | Pre-existing provisions that meet criteria are acceptable; new installations are not mandatory |

**SD takeaway:** Budget annual ongoing-data reporting into the FM appointment, or the certificate can go invalid even if the grade stands.

### 6. SD / retrofit space and systems locks (selected credit heads)

| Credit | Hard numbers / design locks from this Manual |
|---|---|
| **EU-00-01** Minimum Energy Performance | 1 credit point for an energy audit to the EMSD Code of Practice for Building Energy Audit |
| **EU-02-01** Renewables | On-site RE offsetting landlord energy: 0.2% → 1 pt up to 2.0% → 10 pts; off-site EAC/PPA path separately (10%–100% → 1–5 pts) |
| **SS-06-01** EV charging | Medium chargers (≥7 kW) for ≥2.5% or ≥5.0% of private-car / motorcycle / LGV spaces (1–2 pts); ≥2 quick chargers (≥50 kW) (1 pt); ≥1 fast charger (≥100 kW) for coach/light-bus/MGV–HGV spaces (1 pt) |
| **MAN-04-01** Green lease | Measurable KPIs/tasks in green lease (1 pt); green-lease coverage of leased area ≥5% / 10% / 15% (1–3 pts) |
| **MW-03-02** Waste handling | Collection/storage for 3 or 5 listed recyclable streams (1–2 pts); annual recycling by weight ≥10% / 15% / ≥20% (1–3 pts) |
| **WU-03-01** Water recycling | Feasibility study (1 pt); implementation (1 pt); recycled water ≥2.5% or ≥5% of annual fresh-water consumption (1–2 pts) |
| **HWB-02-01** Inclusive design | ≥5 or ≥10 applicable enhanced provisions from DMBA “Recommended Design Requirements” (1–2 pts); ≥3 family-friendly facilities from the Manual’s list (1 pt) |

**SD takeaway:** At retrofit SD, freeze EV charger capacity and count, waste-room stream count, water-recycling plant space, and DMBA enhanced provisions — those heads convert layout into credit points.
