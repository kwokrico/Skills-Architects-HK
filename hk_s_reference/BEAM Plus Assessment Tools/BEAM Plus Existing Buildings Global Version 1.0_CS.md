**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Brochure publication date: 30 August 2024 · Manual: *BEAM Plus Existing Buildings (Global Version) Version 1.0* (cover: 2024.05) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: Export tool for **existing buildings outside Hong Kong**. It is **not** local **EB V2.0** (do not apply HK energy / water / IAQ baselines unmodified). Brochure has **no rating table and no aspect weights** — those live in the Global V1.0 Manual. Not a statutory substitute in the host jurisdiction.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Hong Kong’s high-rise mixed-use facility and property-management practice (digital tools, smart central plant, retro-commissioning, user and community engagement) is packaged as **BEAM Plus Existing Buildings – Global Version** for the international market. Target regions include Macau, Mainland China, Southeast Asia, Belt and Road regions, Europe, and **any other regions**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Why a Global Version exists

Brochure rationale: Hong Kong is renowned for mixed-use skyscrapers and a mature FM industry; in digital tools, smart central-plant management, retro-commissioning, and user/community engagement, HK practice is among the first in the world. The Global Version is added to the BEAM Plus family **to export that expertise**.

**Reason to participate (brochure):** HK certification = independent third-party verification and robust quality management; BEAM Plus strength with **high-rise mixed-use** and mega-sized buildings lets owners showcase O&M maturity.

**SD takeaway:** Use this tool for **overseas** existing stock (especially high-rise mixed-use). Do not use local EB V2.0 baselines for a non-HK climate.

---

### 2. Six key features (brief / ops implications)

| Feature | Implication |
|---|---|
| **Flexible participation** | Comprehensive certificate **or** selective certificates in chosen aspects (e.g. management, energy use, health and wellbeing) |
| **Generalised baseline standards** | International **energy baseline adjustable for weather**; water-efficiency labelling and IAQ **may follow local standards** of the host region |
| **Carbon neutrality goals** | Encourage GHG emission-reduction targets; optional verification via **Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)**; option to procure **Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)** |
| **Expanded energy reduction scale** | Scoring range for energy reduction expanded from **8% to 45%** (recognises deep savings via new technology) |
| **Latest technology** | Digital FM practices; **BIM** to model as-built facilities for management efficiency |
| **Induce behavioural change** | IT platforms for users to showcase sustainable living; educational workshops for building users |

**SD takeaway:** At SD for an overseas O&M / retrofit: lock weather-adjusted energy baseline, local water/IAQ standards, BIM-as-built FM, and (if claimed) SBTi / REC path — those six features are the brochure’s entire technical content.
