# BEAM Plus Neighbourhood
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
HKGBC brochure (Neighbourhood) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: This is the **masterplanning / inception** tool for **new or planned** developments. A Neighbourhood certificate is **not** a New Buildings certificate — the project must reassess under NB when it moves from planning to building design / construction. It is **not** an O&M tool and **not** a statutory substitute for OZP, lease, or Cap. 123.

---

## Regulatory Overview

BEAM Plus Neighbourhood assesses sustainability at early / inception stage, lays an urban-sustainability framework for later stages, and is designed for Hong Kong’s range of “neighbourhood” scales. Certification is valid **up to five years**; after that (or when implementation starts) proponents are steered to New Buildings, then Existing Buildings in operation.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What counts as a neighbourhood (HK scale)

The physical context can be any of:

- A stand-alone high-rise with several hundred households
- A large multi-tower development
- A project with a couple of low-rise houses

Tool is for **new or planned** developments, having considered that scale / typology variation.

**Proponent opportunities cited:** demonstrate urban-sustainability commitment; create a sustainable neighbourhood with a **vibrant public realm**; save resources via **early effective planning**; engage community and key stakeholders; promote the project through third-party verification.

**SD takeaway:** Use this tool at inception — waiting until GA of the first tower wastes the public-realm credits.

---

### 2. Three design emphases

| Emphasis | Intent | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Space between buildings** | Start **public-realm** planning at the beginning of development | POS, streets, voids, podium open space are first-order geometry — not leftover |
| **Socio-economic elements** | New **Community Aspects** aspect forces a holistic view | Engagement, lifestyle, placemaking, CSR reporting are credit-bearing |
| **Be a good neighbour** | Positive impact on occupants **and** immediate neighbours | Wind permeability, visual link, deferred POS closure, shared amenities |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze block disposition, voids, pedestrian/vehicular segregation and neighbour impact **before** typical-floor efficiency studies.

---

### 3. Seven aspects and weights

| Aspect | Weight | Subtopics |
|---|---|---|
| **Site Aspects (SA)** | **25%** | Neighbourhood amenities; pedestrian-oriented and low-carbon transport; ecological value and cultural heritage; quality open space |
| **Community Aspects (CA)** | **20%** | Community engagement; sustainable lifestyle; placemaking and local character; CSR reporting |
| **Outdoor Environmental Quality (OEQ)** | **20%** | Outdoor thermal comfort and urban heat island; daylight access and visual quality; acoustics and air quality of open space |
| **Energy Aspects (EA)** | **16%** | Sustainable buildings and passive design; energy-efficient infrastructure |
| **Materials and Waste Aspects (MWA)** | **10%** | Minimised cut and fill; integrated waste management |
| **Water Aspects (WA)** | **9%** | Water environment and stormwater management; alternative water sources |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Credits (not % weight) | Innovative techniques; performance enhancements |

SA + CA + OEQ = **65%**. Public realm, socio-economics and outdoor environment dominate — not building EU.

**SD takeaway:** Cut-and-fill, stormwater and POS quality are masterplan locks; they cannot be recovered in NB.

---

### 4. Rating gates and lifecycle

All applicable prerequisites in all aspects required.

| Rating | Overall score | Min % **SA, EA, OEQ** | Min % **CA** | **IA** credits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum | 75 | 70 | 50 | 3 |
| Gold | 65 | 60 | 40 | 2 |
| Silver | 55 | 50 | 30 | 1 |
| Bronze | 40 | 40 | 20 | — |

**Validity:** up to **5 years**. Then register **NB** when the project moves from planning to implementation.

```
Masterplanning     Building Design     Construction     Post-Construction & Operation
     ND  ──────────────►  NB  ──────────────────────────►  EB
```

**SD takeaway:** Platinum needs **70%** of SA/EA/OEQ — wind, UHI, daylight to neighbours and passive block form are not optional if Platinum is the brief.

### 5. Pilot design moves (copy at SD)

**Fat Tseung Street West Subsidised Sale Flats (41-storey, 811 flats)**

- L-shaped block on the southern portion of the site
- **3-storey-high void** at main entrance → wind permeability + visual link
- Block disposition enables **deferred closure** of the existing playground → minimise disruption to public services
- Site abuts public roads on three sides; **vehicular access segregated** from pedestrian route
- Covered walkway → vehicle-free external areas
- Footbridge + lift tower at main entrance → POS, retail, community facilities, covered pedestrian network, transport hubs (NW Kowloon Reclamation Site 6)

**EMSD Headquarters transformation (ex–air cargo terminal)**

- First existing building converted to **district cooling** showcase
- Revamp Education Path → sustainable lifestyle for the public
- Piazza / outer area → staff + public recreation; quality landscape + ecological enhancement → ecological value + **UHI reduction**
- Pedestrian-oriented transport planning + renewables

**West Kowloon Cultural District (40 ha reclamation)**

- **23 ha** public open space + **2 km** harbourfront promenade
- Phased venue delivery around an integrated basement — public-realm continuity across phases is a masterplan credit risk if ignored

**SD takeaway:** Voids, deferred public-facility closure, pedestrian/vehicle split and phased POS continuity are the brochure’s own SD precedents — copy the geometry, not just the label.
