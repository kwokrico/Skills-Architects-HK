# BEAM Plus Existing Schools
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
BEAM Plus Existing Schools Version 1.0 (ES V1.0) | Certification body: HKGBC · Assessment body: BEAM Society Limited

> Scope note: Existing **primary and secondary** schools only. Scope is management, operation and maintenance **plus** education, health and wellbeing — not a generic EB scorecard. Applicant must have **≥ 1 school calendar year** of operational data **before** registration. It is **not** NB, **not** Interiors, **not** a statutory substitute, and **not** sized for mega-building credit maps.

---

## Regulatory Overview

ES V1.0 is a Hong Kong school-specific rating tool that certifies sustainability performance and emphasises top-management commitment, a **Plan–Do–Check–Act** continual-improvement cycle, and behavioural change through **education**. Certification lasts **five years**; a substantial registration-fee discount applies if the school registers for renewal **before** expiry.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility, benefits, design-relevant environment

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Occupancy | Existing **primary and secondary** schools |
| Data | **≥ 1 school calendar year** of operational data before registration |
| Emphasis | Top-management commitment; PDCA; education-led behavioural change |
| Environment upgrades cited | Lighting, ventilation, material usage, energy efficiency |

**Benefits cited:** greener / healthier place to learn and inspire + third-party recognition; behavioural change among teachers, students **and family members**; support HK **carbon neutrality by 2050**.

**SD takeaway:** Do not register a newly occupied school — wait for one full calendar year of ops data; treat lighting / ventilation / materials as the physical SD package, not a full NB envelope redo.

### 2. Two-book system (use both)

| Book | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Assessment Manual** | Framework, design intention, assessment criteria, submittal requirements per credit head; extended reading → green-building reference book for schools |
| **Technical Guidebook** | Further interpretation per credit; step-by-step calculation examples; calculation and document templates |

**Tool character (brochure):** Unique (HK school-specific) · Education-led · Simple versus mega-building tools.

**SD takeaway:** Credit calculations live in the Technical Guidebook — do not invent school-specific maths from NB/EB manuals.

---

### 3. Five performance categories

Framework assesses all-round performance: education, leadership, resource efficiency, indoor environment, wellbeing and innovations.

| Category | Subtopics |
|---|---|
| **Sustainable Leadership and Learning (SLL)** | High-level commitment; environmental learning; engagement |
| **Sustainable Campus Environment (SCE)** | Biophilia; neighbourhood integration; climate resilience |
| **Efficient Use of Resources (EUR)** | Decarbonisation actions; benchmarking and disclosure |
| **Health, Comfort & Happiness (HCH)** | Healthy indoor environment; healthy living; health protection |
| **Innovations and Additions (IA)** | Innovations and additions |

Brochure does not publish category **weights** — do not assume NB/EB percentages.

**SD takeaway:** Campus SD is SCE (biophilia, neighbour, climate) + HCH (indoor environment) — leadership/curriculum (SLL) is credit-bearing and must be in the client brief, not only the architect’s drawing set.

---

### 4. Rating gates

| Result | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **GREEN** 綠建學校 | Overall score **50** points |
| **Unclassified** | Overall score has **not reached 50 points** |
| **Outstanding Performance** (per category) | Category score **≥ 70%** — badge for that category (SLL / EUR / SCE / HCH) |

Brochure does **not** list Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze for this tool. Showcase projects in the brochure are **Green** (Beta Version).

**Validity:** **5 years**; re-assess every 5 years; **substantial registration fee discount** if renew before expiry.

**SD takeaway:** Target **≥ 50 overall** plus **≥ 70%** in the categories the school wants badged (SLL is the common Outstanding in the exemplars) — there is no Platinum ladder to chase.

### 5. Exemplars (Beta Green)

| School | Type | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| S.K.H. St. Clement's Primary | Aided primary | Energy and water conservation; waste and recycling; Outstanding in **SLL** |
| St. Paul's College | First DSS secondary Green | Senior-management environmental policy + upgrading works; Outstanding in **SLL, SCE, HCH** |
| Ying Wa Primary | DSS primary | Continuous stakeholder engagement on sustainability; Outstanding in **SLL, SCE, HCH** |

**SD takeaway:** Policy + stakeholder engagement (SLL) plus fabric/IEQ upgrades (SCE/HCH) is the documented path; energy/water/waste alone was enough for Outstanding SLL at St. Clement's but not for SCE/HCH badges.
