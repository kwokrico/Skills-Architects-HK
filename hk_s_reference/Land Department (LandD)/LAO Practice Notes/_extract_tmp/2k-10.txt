Lands Administration OfficeLands DepartmentPractice NoteIssue No. 10/2000Flexibility in Parking Provisions		Lease Conditions usually require parking, loading and unloading spaces to be provided in the respective lot at fixed ratios in relation to the gross floor areas or number of units.		To allow for design flexibility, I have decided, in addition to the fixed ratio provision, to incorporate a provision in future lease conditions allowing variations in the numbers of each type of parking, loading and unloading spaces by 5 percent or 50 spaces, whichever is the less, for parking, loading and unloading of the respective type of motor vehicles.  However, such flexibility (including without limitation the types of parking, loading & unloading spaces to which the flexibility applies) will only be incorporated on the advice of the Transport Department having regard to the merits of each individual case.		Those parking spaces or loading/unloading spaces provided in addition to the fixed ratios but pursuant to the flexibility stipulated in the lease conditions will not be taken into account for the purpose of calculating the total gross floor area and total site coverage, if any, stipulated in the lease conditions.		For existing lease conditions which do not contain the above-mentioned flexibility, a modification of the lease conditions to incorporate it will be considered upon application.	(R.
