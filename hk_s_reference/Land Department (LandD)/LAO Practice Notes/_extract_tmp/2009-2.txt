Temporary Occupation of Government Land to Facilitate _Building Maintenance
Building owners may wish to carry out regular maintenance to the exterior of their building or be required to carry out building maintenance works to their building in response to & repair order issued by the Buildings Department. Where there is adjacent vacant Government land which is not required for permanent development or temporary use, the Lands Department may consider approving an application for short term tenancy (STT) for temporary use of such Goverment land (e.g for erecting scaffolding and/or storage ofbuilding materials) for the purpose of facilitating building maintenance to be carried out by the building owner:  Normally; the STT is limited to a period of not longer than six (6) months and the area of the Government land to be granted is not larger than 180 m
An application for the STT for the above-mentioned purpose will have to be made by the owners' corporation (if formed) and if there is no owners' corporation, by owners - committee O estate manger of the building to which building maintenance works are t0 be carried out: The application should be submitted to the concerned District Lands Office (DLO). at least one calendar month in advance, together with the following supporting information
documents showing that maintenance contract has been awarded or is due to be called for carrying out the maintenance works;
(b) the estimated commencement date of the maintenance works and the anticipated date which the Government land is required for erection of the scaffolding and/or storage of materials;
the duration of the temporary occupation of the Government land under application; and
(d) resolution from the owners corporation (if formed) Or owners' meeting of the building   authorizing the application for the Government land and the execution of the STT
3 Upon consideration of the application by the DLO, a STT may be offered to the applicant for such duration as the DLO considers reasonable for carrying out of the maintenance work and on such terms and conditions as the DLO deems appropriate
