Practice Note 
Issue No. 5/2023A 
Incentive Scheme to Encourage Provision of  
Residential Care Homes for the Elderly Premises in New Private Developments  
With effect from 20 June 2026, this Practice Note ("PN") vanes and 
supplements Lands Department ("LandsD") Lands Administration Office ("LAO") PN No. 
5/2023. Except as varied and supplemented by this PN, all other information and provisions in 
LAO PN No. 5/2023 remain valid. 
2. 
To encourage the provision of residential care homes for the elderly by 
developers in new private developments, an incentive scheme has been launched in 2003 under 
LAO PN No. 4/2003 ("the Incentive Scheme"). 
Thereafter, enhancement measures are 
introduced to the Incentive Scheme for a three-year pilot period from 20 June 2023 to 19 June 
2026 under LAO PN No. 5/2023. The three-year pilot period will end on 19 June 2026. 
3. 
Upon a recent review from the policy perspective, approval has been given for 
the enhancement measures to continue after the end of the three-year pilot period. Starting 
from 20 June 2026, applications to LandsD under the Incentive Scheme will continue to be 
processed under LAO PN No. 5/2023 until further notice by the Government. All existing 
arrangements and requirements in relation to the enhancement measures as stated in LAO PN 
No. 5/2023 will remain unchanged. 
4. 
Interested parties are advised to visit the website of the Social Welfare 
Department and refer to the Guidance Note released by the Social Welfare Department in June 
2026. 
5. 
This PN is issued for general reference purposes only. All rights to modify the 
whole or any part ofthis PN are hereby reserved.
