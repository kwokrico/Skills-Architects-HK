Practice Note  
 
 
   
Issue No. 1/2024A 
 
 
 
Applications for Lease Modification 
including Land Exchange for Development of Land  
in Hung Shui Kiu/Ha Tsuen New Development Area 
 
 
 
 
With immediate effect, this Practice Note (“PN”) varies and supplements Lands 
Department Lands Administration Office PN No. 1/2024 (“PN 1/2024”).  Except as varied and 
supplemented by this PN, all other information and provisions in PN 1/2024 remain valid. 
 
2. 
The deadline for processing the accepted applications for designated development sites 
planned for commercial use among the Second Phase sites is proposed to be extended to 
31 December 2025.  For the avoidance of doubt, such extension of deadline does not apply to 
those Second Phase sites planned for commercial use but with no applications received by the 
earlier application deadline of 30 April 2024. 
 
3. 
The deadline for processing the accepted applications in respect of the Second Phase 
sites as indicated in paragraph 9 of PN 1/2024 is thus varied as follows: 
 
 
“Any accepted application for the Second Phase sites (except those designated 
development sites edged red on the Reference Plan1) will cease to be processed if a binding 
basic terms offer (with premium) cannot be issued and accepted on or before 31 March 2025.  
For those designated development sites edged red on the Reference Plan, any accepted 
application will cease to be processed if a binding basic terms offer (with premium) cannot be 
issued and accepted on or before 31 December 2025.”  
 
4. 
This PN is issued for general reference purposes only.  All rights to modify the whole 
or any part of this PN are hereby reserved.
