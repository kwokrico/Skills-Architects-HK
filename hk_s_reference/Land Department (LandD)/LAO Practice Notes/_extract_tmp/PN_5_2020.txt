Lands Administration Office Lands Department
Practice Note
Issue No. 5/2020
Payment of Professional Fees
in connection with Compensation Claims involving more than one professional
number of Ordinances relating to acquisition/resumption of land make provision for payment of costs or remuneration reasonably incurred by claimants in employing persons to act in professional capacity in connection with their claims for compensation:
In certain acquisition/resumption cases, input from more than one professional is required for assessment and submission of compensation claims to Government: The costs or remuneration reasonably incurred or paid by claimants in emploving these professionals are admissible_
3 The fees payable in employing more than one professional should be calculated on the claims from the different professionals involved with reference to the time, manpower and other resources engaged. Reasonableness as to quantum will be tested by the department: After the enactment of the Competition   Ordinance (Cap. 619) on 14.12.2015, the practice of  assessing professional fees claims based on the Scales of Professional Charges for General Practice Services in Hong Kong of the Hong Kong Institute of Surveyors is no longer applicable
The claimant may appoint one of the professionals to act as co-ordinator to handle the claim provided that the genuine need to appoint a co-ordinator can first be established. The costs or remuneration reasonably incurred or paid by the claimant in employing co-ordinator are payable in addition to the  fee(s) mentioned above_ The reasonableness as to the amount of claim will be tested by the department:
