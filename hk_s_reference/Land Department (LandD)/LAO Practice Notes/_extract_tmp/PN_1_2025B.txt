New Territories Exempted Houses 
With immediate effect, this Practice Note ("PN") varies and supplements Lands 
Department Lands Administration Office ("LAO") PN No. 1/2025 ("PN 1/2025") and 
supersedes PN No. 1/2025A. 
Except as varied and supplemented by this PN, all other 
information and provisions in PN 1/2025 remain valid. 
2.
Paragraph 13 of PN No. 1/2025 is varied as follows:
"Implementation of the Streamlined Arrangement in District Lands Offices,
New Territories
13.
The streamlined arrangement has been implemented as a pilot scheme by
the District Lands Office, Yuen Long and the District Lands Office, North since
23 January and 9 July 2025 respectively. 
With effect from 24 November 2025,
the streamlined arrangement will be implemented as a standard procedure by all
District Lands Offices, New Territories".
