Lands Administration Office 
Lands Department 
Practice Note 
Issue No. 4/2024 
Standard Rates for Premium Calculation for 
Exemption of Gross Floor Area and/or Site Coverage and 
Relaxation of Building Height Restriction 
arising from the adoption of Modular Integrated Construction 
Under Joint Practice Note No. 8 
This Practice Note ("PN") varies and supplements Lands Department Lands 
Administration Office· Practice Note No. 6/2022 ("PN No. 6/2022"). 
Except as varied and 
supplemented by this PN, other provisions and information contained in PN No. 6/2022 remain 
valid. 
2. 
With effect from 1 April 2024, the rates for calculation of premium for exemption 
of gross floor area and/or site coverage and relaxation of building height restriction arising from 
the adoption of Modular Integrated Construction as specified in Appendix III to PN No. 6/2022 
will be varied and replaced by the rates set out in Appendix I to this PN. 
The district 
boundaries will also be revised from six regions into five regions which will follow the relevant 
boundaries in Appendix II to this PN 
3. 
This PN is issued for general reference purpose only. 
All rights to modify the 
whole or any part of this PN are hereby reserved.
