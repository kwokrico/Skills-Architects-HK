Other points to note 
6. 
For the avoidance of doubt, IBs which have completed redevelopment under the 
industrial building revitalisation scheme promulgated in LAO PN Nos. 1/2010, 1/2010A and 
1/201 OB or the industrial building revitalisation scheme promulgated in LAO PN Nos. 2/2019 
and 2/2019A are not eligible for an application under the PNs. 
7. 
Every application submitted to LandsD pursuant to the PNs will be considered 
on its own merits by LandsD at its absolute discretion acting in its capacity as a landlord. The 
PNs shall not constitute any representation on the part of the Government or give rise to any 
expectation on the part of the applicant that any application submitted to LandsD will be 
processed or approved.
