# LAO Practice Note No. 9/2023 — Installation and Operation of Smart Lockers in Private Residential Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 December 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department

> Scope note: LandsD will **not enforce** the residential user restriction against **public-use smart lockers in common areas** of a residential development if **all** criteria in para 2 are met. It is **not** a waiver of the BO, TPO, other lease conditions, or the DMC. Director's decision on "common area" and "residential block" is final.

---

## Regulatory Overview

On a lot restricted to residential user under lease, smart lockers accessible to the **general public** (not solely residents) would breach the user restriction. This PN sets a non-enforcement envelope for lockers in DMC common areas. "Residential user restriction" includes "private residential purposes", "residential purposes", "residence(s)", "as a dwelling house" and "as a messuage or dwelling house". "Lease" includes Government Lease or Conditions of Sale/Grant/Exchange.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Non-enforcement criteria — all must be met (paras 2–3)

| Criterion | Hard lock |
|---|---|
| **(a) Floor area cap** | Aggregate floor area of smart lockers **≤ 3 m² × number of residential blocks** in the development |
| **(b) Height** | Lockers **≤ 2.75 m** |
| **(c) Location** | **No** lockers in common areas designated for **public passage**, or in **MOE / emergency escape routes or exits**; installation must not contravene any other lease conditions |
| **(d) Signage** | No advertising sign, bill, placard or notice **except** name and contact of the locker provider and information **essential** for operation |
| **(e) Fixing** | Securely attached to the **floor or the wall** |

There is **no** cap on locker count **per block**, provided the development-wide aggregate area stays within para 2(a). Director's decision on what constitutes a residential block, and on whether an area is common area (generally IO/manager control, designated as such in the DMC), is final.

**SD takeaway:** Size the locker room (or alcove) to **≤ 3 m² per residential block**, keep it **≤ 2.75 m** high, off MOE and public-passage common parts, and show it as DMC common area — public-use lockers outside that envelope remain a user-clause breach.

---

### 2. Who must comply; what this PN does not touch (paras 4–7)

Incorporated Owners / OC or the property manager must ensure compliance with para 2, other lease conditions, **and the DMC**. Government accepts no liability for loss from lockers. The PN does not vary or waive the lease, and does not affect the BO, TPO, other legislation, or DMC rights.

**SD takeaway:** Even inside the LandsD envelope, check DMC common-parts use and BA MOE — LandsD non-enforcement is not a DMC or BO clearance.
