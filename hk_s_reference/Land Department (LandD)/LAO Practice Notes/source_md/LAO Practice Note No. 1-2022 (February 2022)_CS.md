# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2022 — KTN/FLN Remaining Phase land exchange
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
16 February 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supersedes LAO PN Nos. **1/2014, 1/2014A and 1/2014D** (1/2014B and 1/2014C already superseded by 1/2014D)

> Scope note: In-situ lease modification / land exchange for **Remaining Phase** private-development sites on the Kwu Tung North and Fanling North ODPs (hatched on LandsD reference plans). It is **not** an OZP. Deadlines in para 13 were later varied by **PN 1/2022A**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Owners of private lots inside Remaining Phase hatched sites may apply for exchange for users permitted under OZPs **S/KTN/2** and **S/FLN/2** (or later amendments) or by TPB. Phase 1 application/BBTO deadlines (30 November 2014 / 30 September 2017) are historical. Lodge at CES/NDA, Landmark North, Sheung Shui. Accepted applications are published (plan of private land) and occupants notified.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. General criteria — Appendix III

| Criterion | Lock |
|---|---|
| Location | Sites planned for **private** development on adopted KTN and FLN **ODPs** (and later ODP amendments) |
| Size / ownership | Proposed surrender **≥ 4,000 m²**; all private lots under **single owner or JV** |
| Configuration | Reasonably regular; **contiguous**; **no intervening** lots not owned by applicant; fragmented lots not accepted |
| Confinement | Only lots inside a hatched planned private site; lots in road / GIC / OS / GB / public or subsidised housing **not normally** accepted |
| Access | Proper **vehicular access** feasible |
| OZP | Use and parameters comply with KTN and FLN OZPs |
| Residual of the ODP site | If not assembling **all** private land in the ODP development site: **layout plan** proving decent full-site utilisation **without loss of ODP-planned potential** (site boundaries may shift after NDA detailed design) |

**SD takeaway:** Assemble ≥ 4,000 m² inside one hatched ODP private site, keep the block contiguous, and if you leave residual private lots, prove the rest of the ODP site still yields its planned GFA.

---

### 2. Occupants — 4 July 2013 baseline (paras 8–10, App IV)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Eligible occupants | Individuals occupying surrender lots **as at 4 July 2013 or thereafter** as registered in LandsD’s **pre-clearance survey** |
| Applicant’s duty | Show they accepted **compensation or rehousing comparable** to Government NDA clearance (May 2018 non-means-tested option included). Applicant-paid package is **not premium-deductible** |
| Early Departure Scheme | Occupants may take Government early compensation/rehousing; then para 8 duty **drops** for that occupant but applicant **reimburses** Government (compensation + cash value of rehousing + admin cost) |
| Timing vs BBTO | After **PBTO**, before **BBTO**: either signed acceptances, **or** per-occupant **Undertaking** (keep offers open **3 years** from execution; reimburse if they later take Government EDS) **plus Deposit** equal to that reimbursement sum; plus evidence of **vacant possession** on a reasonable programme |
| Release of Undertaking/Deposit | Occupant accepted applicant’s offer; **or** reimbursement paid; **or** **3 years** from execution |
| Disputes | LandsD may **suspend** processing |

**SD takeaway:** Occupant clearance is on the critical path to BBTO — survey everyone on the 4 July 2013 register, price comparable rehousing, and allow 3-year EDS tail after execution.

---

### 3. Processing requirements — Appendix IV

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Title / VP | Unified titles; vacant possession by a specified deadline; **free of encumbrances** before execution |
| Time | Conclude **before** Government resumption; specified period unless Government extends |
| Government land | Re-grant **may** include adjoining/intervening GL **incapable** of reasonable separate alienation; GL that **can** be separately alienated is **not** normally thrown in to match the ODP blob |

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume the re-grant fills out the ODP site with GL — only leftover GL that cannot stand alone.

---

### 4. Submission pack — Appendix I (design-facing)

Must: 2 copies of land grants (LR/solicitor certified ≤ 1 month); 1:1000 plan; 2 copies historical+current ownership; company docs / mortgagee letters / solicitor share list if multi-owned; TPB letter if any; agent authorisation; property identity.

Facilitate: OZP extract; assignment/Deed Poll plans; **2 sets of sketch plans and development parameters**; occupant schedules (current **and** 4 July 2013-or-after who left); development programme.

**SD takeaway:** First pack needs parameter sketches plus the 4 July 2013 occupant schedules — not a late add-on.

---

### 5. Premium, vacant possession, original deadlines (paras 11–13, fn)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| After PBTO | Exchange **not processed further** until para. 8–10 occupant requirements are met |
| If approved | Terms including **premium** as LandsD demands |
| Vacant possession | Actual VP of private land **no later than acceptance of BBTO (with premium)** |
| Original clocks (this PN) | Apply by **15 August 2022**. Processing **stops** if BBTO with premium is not issued **and accepted** by **30 June 2023** (tied to Government resumption). Admin fee **not refunded**. Late applications **not considered** |
| Later variation | **PN 1/2022A** moves the BBTO (with premium) stop to **31 December 2023** |
| Standard-rate option | Government to announce NDA standard-rate premium **before end of March 2022**. IB Pilot **PN 1/2021** **does not** apply to these exchanges |

**SD takeaway:** VP must be ready at **BBTO acceptance**; treat **15 Aug 2022 / 30 Jun 2023** as the original clocks and **1/2022A** as the later BBTO extension — do not use the IB 1/2021 standard-rate pilot on these lots.
