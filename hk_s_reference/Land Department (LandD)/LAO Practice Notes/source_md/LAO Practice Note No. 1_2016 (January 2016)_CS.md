# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2016 — Waiver for testing laboratory use in industrial buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 January 2016 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 1/2016 | Applies from **1 February 2016** until LandsD otherwise determines

> Scope note: **Nil-fee waiver** to use part(s) of, or an **entire existing** industrial building, as **testing and/or calibration laboratory** (in addition to lease-permitted user) for the building’s lifetime or lease expiry/termination, whichever earlier. **Clinical** labs that require humans or animals to undergo checking **on the premises** are **not** eligible. Vacant sites cannot apply. Not a BO/FSD code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

An industrial building here is one lawfully erected on a lot restricted to **industrial and/or godown** purposes (excluding special factories: container-terminal storage, HA flatted factories, and special-industry lots such as cargo handling, ship building/repair, oil storage/refining and associated chemicals, polystyrene, chlorine/hydrogen/textile chemicals, etc.). Planning: testing-lab use must be always-permitted on the current statutory plan, **or** have **TPB permission before** LandsD filing (proof at application), **or** qualify as existing use under the statutory plans. Director’s decision on whether a use is “testing laboratory” is **final**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Waiver locks (paras 2–7)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Fee | **Nil waiver fee** (admin fee still payable on demand; non-refundable if withdrawn or not executed) |
| Term | Lifetime of **existing** IB at issue date **or** lease expiry/termination, whichever earlier |
| Clinical exclusion | Labs needing humans/animals to undergo checking **at the Industrial Premises** — **not eligible** for nil-fee waiver |
| Works | Any works for the permitted lab use must comply with **BO** |
| DMC | Waiver does not prejudice DMC rights/remedies |
| Parties | Executed by owner **and** mortgagees/chargees **and** ASP purchasers |
| What is waived | **User restriction** only vs testing-lab use; other lease changes need **separate** modification |
| Later mod / other waiver | Premium or waiver fee at **full market value without** benefit of this waiver |
| Vary area/conditions | Fresh LandsD application; premium/charge at discretion |
| Other authorities | FSD, BA and others still required |

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme **on-premises clinical** testing (human/animal subjects) onto this nil-fee track; get **TPB** first if the OZP requires permission, then treat the waiver as **user-only** for the life of this building.

---

### 2. Existing waivers and filing (paras 8–11)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Submit to | **Relevant DLO** (LandsD website contacts) |
| Existing waiver | May be replaced; terminated **on execution** of Testing Laboratory Waiver; **no** refund of waiver or admin fees; **no** termination notice |
| Discretion | Each case on merits as landlord |

**SD takeaway:** An existing unit waiver will be **killed without refund** when this lab waiver is executed — do not rely on stacking waivers.
