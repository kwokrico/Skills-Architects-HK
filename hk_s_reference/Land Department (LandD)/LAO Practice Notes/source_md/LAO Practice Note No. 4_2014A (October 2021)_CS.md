# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2014A — Accountable and non-accountable GFA under lease (JPN 4 overlay)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 October 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 4/2014A | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 4/2014**

> Scope note: Implements **revised Joint Practice Note No. 4** streamlined GFA/PR accountability among BD, LandsD and PlanD. It **does not** rewrite PN 4/2014 for every element; it substitutes JPN 4 (and BA’s ruling) for **certain** facilities/features/structures on plans first submitted (or major-revised) on or after the Effective Date. **Not** applicable to buildings exempted from Cap. 123 by **Cap. 121**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN No. 4 takes effect from **18 October 2021** (the Effective Date). Streamlined GFA accountability applies to **all new building plans or major revision of building plans** submitted to BA for approval **on or after** that date. For those plans, GFA accountability **under lease** for the covered facilities follows JPN 4 (and its exemption criteria, if any), **notwithstanding** PN 4/2014; in principle BA’s ruling is followed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When JPN 4 displaces PN 4/2014 (paras 1–3)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Effective Date | **18 October 2021** |
| Plans covered | New GBP **or major revision** submitted to BA **on or after** Effective Date |
| Accountability | For certain facilities, features and structures: treat GFA **under lease** per **JPN No. 4** (subject to JPN 4 exemption criteria) |
| BA | In principle, **BA’s ruling followed** for exemption from GFA |
| Cap. 121 | This PN **does not apply** to buildings exempted from Cap. 123 by Cap. 121 |
| Lease rights | Nothing fetters Government/Director as landlord; not a waiver of lease |

**SD takeaway:** For schemes whose first (or major-revision) BA submission is **on/after 18 October 2021**, size typical facilities to **JPN 4 + BA**, not the older PN 4/2014 tables — unless the building is Cap. 121-exempt.
