> Scope note: **Rate and district-map revision only** for premium on MiC GFA/SC exemption and BH relaxation under **JPN 8**. ## Regulatory Overview

From **1 April 2024**, the MiC premium rates in **Appendix III to PN 6/2022** are replaced by the rates in **Appendix I to this PN**. District boundaries are revised from **six regions to five**, following **Appendix II to this PN**.
