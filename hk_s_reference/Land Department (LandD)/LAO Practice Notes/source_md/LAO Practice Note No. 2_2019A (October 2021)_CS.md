# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2019A — Redevelopment of industrial lot — TPB window extension
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 October 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 2/2019A | Immediate effect | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 2/2019**

> Scope note: **Only** extends the Town Planning Board application period in PN 2/2019 para 2. All other 2/2019 provisions (3-year execution from TPB letter, 5-year building covenant, 20% non-domestic PR, outside-“R” geography, etc.) **remain valid**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Words **“During the three-year period commencing from 10 October 2018”** in PN 2/2019 paragraph 2 are varied to **“From 10 October 2018 to 31 October 2024 (both dates inclusive)”**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The only change (para 2)

| Item | As varied |
|---|---|
| TPB filing window for redevelopment above statutory-plan max intensity | **10 October 2018 – 31 October 2024** (both dates inclusive) |

**SD takeaway:** The **20% non-domestic PR** lease-mod track remains available only if TPB is applied to **on or before 31 October 2024** — execution is still **within 3 years of the TPB approval letter** per parent PN 2/2019.
