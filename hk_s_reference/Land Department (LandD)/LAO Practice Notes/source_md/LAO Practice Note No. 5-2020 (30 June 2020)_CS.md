# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2020 — Professional Fees on Compensation Claims
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 June 2020 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supersedes LAO PN 1/96 | Later varied by **PN 5/2020B** (and 5/2020A, superseded by 5/2020B)

> Scope note: **Admissible professional costs** when a claimant uses **more than one professional** on a land **acquisition/resumption** compensation claim. It is **not** a design or GBP PN. After Cap. 619 (14 Dec 2015), **HKIS scale charges are not used**.

## Regulatory Overview

Ordinances on acquisition/resumption often allow recovery of costs reasonably incurred employing professionals. Where several professionals are needed, fees are assessed on **time, manpower and resources** actually engaged; LandsD tests **reasonableness**. A **coordinator** among the professionals may be paid **in addition** if a genuine need is first established.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee basis (paras. 1–4)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Multi-professional | Admissible if reasonably required for assessment/submission of the claim |
| Quantum | Calculated from each professional’s claim vs **time / manpower / other resources**; department tests reasonableness |
| HKIS scales | **Not applicable** after Competition Ordinance **14.12.2015** |
| Coordinator | Optional; extra fee only if genuine need established first |

**SD takeaway:** On resumption, budget **time-based** multi-consultant fees — not HKIS scale percentages — and justify a coordinator separately.
