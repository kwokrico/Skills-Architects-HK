# LAO Practice Note No. 10/2025 — Offering Private Land to Offset Land Premium for ECNTA Land Exchange or LSLD Tender in Northern Metropolis NDAs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 December 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 10/2025

> Scope note: Lets ECNTA land-exchange applicants and LSLD tenderers in **Northern Metropolis NDAs** offset **premium balance** with the EGA value of **agricultural land** on a LandsD designated-projects list. It is **not** a planning or GFA PN. Land surrendered **in exchange for** the ECNTA site itself is **excluded**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Applicants for lease modification including land exchange under the Enhanced Conventional New Town Approach (ECNTA — **LAO PN 13/2023**) or tenderers for grant of land by tender under Large-scale Land Disposal (LSLD) in NM NDAs may offer private land that is planned to be resumed, or has been resumed with EGA not yet released, to offset the balance of land premium payable ("Premium Offsetting").

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What land can be offered (para 2)

| Test | Lock |
|---|---|
| Type | **Agricultural land** — old schedule and new grant lots **demised for agricultural use** |
| Location | Inside the **Northern Metropolis**; **excludes** any private land (agricultural or otherwise) to be **surrendered in exchange** for an ECNTA site |
| Owner as of the **relevant date** | ECNTA: date the PN inviting applications is promulgated. LSLD: date of tender invitation. Owner = applicant/tenderer **or** its **associated company** (Cap. 622). Land already resumed with EGA not yet released is **deemed** still owned by that former owner |
| Designated list | Must appear on LandsD's **list of designated projects** (land that **may be resumed within three years** of the relevant date, **or** already resumed with EGA outstanding). Published at landsd.gov.hk (land-acq-clearance / NDA page) |

Resumption ordinances cited: Cap. 124, Cap. 370, Cap. 370 as applied by s.26 of Cap. 358AL, Cap. 519.

**SD takeaway:** Only **NM agricultural** lots on the **designated list**, held by the applicant (or Cap. 622 associate) at invitation date, can offset premium — do not count the ECNTA surrender lots themselves.

---

### 2. Offer, title, resumption mechanics (paras 3–5)

Offers (ownership and particulars of each Offered Land) go in **with** the ECNTA application or LSLD tender. **No** later change of ownership or particulars. Prove title and area to LandsD; if owned by an associate, prove Cap. 622 association. Delay/failure may mean refusal of the Offered Land.

If as-yet-unresumed Offered Land is accepted: resumption notice gazetted/served/affixed; ownership reverts to Government **within one month** of the notice; Government clears households/businesses on prevailing NDA practice. Former owners must sign an **Agreement as to Compensation and Indemnity (ACI)** within **one month** of the resumption notice (or other period specified), agreeing the para 6 value for offsetting and warranting title free from encumbrances.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze Offered Land particulars **at application/tender** — post-offer transfers kill the offset.

---

### 3. Value and how it pays premium (paras 6–8)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Valuation basis | **Tier One or Tier Two** zone ex-gratia compensation rate for resumption of agricultural land, prevailing as of the date the resumption notice is **affixed** **or** the date of **reversion**, **whichever is the higher**. Rates depend on whether resumption is for development (e.g. NDAs) or non-development (e.g. wetland conservation) |
| Reference rates (from **1 October 2025**) | Tier One **$1,008 / sq ft**; Tier Two **$504 / sq ft** (subject to change) |
| Interest | Calculated value includes interest (if any) accrued before EGA release; for Premium Offsetting, payment is **deemed** on the date of **signing/execution of the ACI** |
| **ECNTA** | Offset must complete within a time stipulated in the **ECNTA invitation PN**. EGA in the executed ACI offsets premium **balance**; any shortfall is **cash** by LandsD's designated deadline |
| **LSLD** | Tenderers may opt **Staged Payment** instead of lump sum. **Only** the successful tenderer who opted Staged Payment may settle the remaining premium balance — **at most 75%** of total land premium (or as in Tender Documents) — by Premium Offsetting within **three years** from tender award (or as in Tender Documents). Parent-company written guarantee required |
| LSLD alienation | Until remaining premium is fully settled (offset or cash): **no alienation** of the tender-granted land, including underletting or pre-sale, **except building mortgage** for development |
| EGA cash | EGA accepted for offsetting is **not** paid in cash. Government may resume NM NDA land anyway; LandsD’s accept/reject of Offered Land is **final** |

**SD takeaway:** Size the offset against **Tier 1 $1,008/sf or Tier 2 $504/sf** (from 1 Oct 2025) of listed agri land; LSLD still needs **≥ 25% cash** up front unless the tender says otherwise, and **no pre-sale** until the remainder is paid.
