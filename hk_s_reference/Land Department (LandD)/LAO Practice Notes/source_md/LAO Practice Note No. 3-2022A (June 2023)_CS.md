# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2022A — KTN/FLN standard-rate premium (revised rates and deadline)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
June 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies and supplements LAO PN No. 3/2022

> Scope note: Re-sets **standard-rate land premium** figures and the **Option** deadline for Remaining Phase Kwu Tung North / Fanling North land exchanges, tracking PN 1/2022A’s BBTO date. It is **not** a new exchange window.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Paragraphs 2 and 3 of PN 3/2022 are replaced to match the PN 1/2022A processing deadline of **31 December 2023**. Applicants who accepted a PBTO before this PN without submitting the Option Form get a one-off opt-in until **28 July 2023**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Option mechanics (varied para 2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| BBTO (with premium) | Must be **accepted on or before 31 December 2023** or the application ceases |
| When to opt | Not later than acceptance of **PBTO**, via Appendix I Option Form |
| After PBTO accepted | Option is **irreversible**; **no premium appeal** under BBTO |
| Walking away | Application fees **forfeited**; **re-application will not be considered** |

**SD takeaway:** Standard-rate election must be on the table **before PBTO acceptance** — after that the GFA/use mix used in A×B−C×D−E is locked with no appeal.

---

### 2. Standard rates valid to 31 December 2023 (varied para 3)

All rates **per GFA** except agricultural use (**per site area**).

| NDA | Ag. use | Before: non-res. building^ | Before: residential building | After: non-res.# | After: residential |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kwu Tung North** | $4,000/m² | $15,000/m² | $30,000/m² | $27,500/m² | $40,000/m² |
| **Fanling North** | $4,000/m² | $15,000/m² | $30,000/m² | $22,500/m² | $37,500/m² |

| Note | Lock |
|---|---|
| ^ Non-res. building use before exchange | Includes **industrial**; LandsD type decision is final |
| # Non-res. use after exchange | Includes **commercial** and **modern industrial**; **excludes** columbarium, PFS, dangerous-goods storage, offensive trades; **hotel and hotel ancillary = commercial**; LandsD type decision is final |

**SD takeaway:** Price the scheme off **post-exchange GFA by use** (residential rate on all GFA not prohibiting private residential) using these KTN/FLN figures — they replace PN 3/2022’s higher table.

---

### 3. Late Option for pre-PN PBTOs (para 4)

| Situation | Lock |
|---|---|
| PBTO accepted **before** this PN, **no** Option Form yet | May opt at these revised rates by submitting the Option Form **on or before 28 July 2023** |
| Form not in by that date | Deemed to have chosen **case-by-case** premium |

**SD takeaway:** If PBTO is already accepted without an Option Form, 28 July 2023 is the last day to switch to standard rates.
