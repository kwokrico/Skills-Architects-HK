# LAO Practice Note No. 11/2023A — Standard Rates for the Agricultural-Land Pilot Scheme (supplements PN 11/2023)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2 April 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates effective **1 April 2024** | Supplements LAO PN No. 11/2023

> Scope note: Publishes the **standard rates** and the **A×B − C×D − E** formula for PN 11/2023. If the exchange also involves eligible **pre-1987 IBs** under **PN 12/2023**, the applicant may opt **only** these rates — **not** other PNs' rates. Does not restate PN 11/2023 geography except the overlap/pro-rata rules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 April 2024, standard rates for land exchange of NT agricultural land outside NDAs follow the zone table in paragraph 2, using the zones in paragraph 4 of PN 11/2023. Premium at standard rates is **not negotiable**. Negative calculated premium ⇒ **no premium charged**.

| Use label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| After: **residential** rate | All GFA **not prohibiting private residential use** |
| After: **non-residential** rate | Remainder GFA. Includes commercial and **modern industrial**; **excludes** columbarium, petrol filling station, storage of dangerous goods, offensive trades (standard rates **not applicable**). **Hotel and ancillary = non-residential** |

Overlapping zones: **higher** rates. Straddle two zones/sub-zones: after-GFA **pro-rata by site area**, GFA assumed evenly distributed.

**SD takeaway:** Price the scheme as **after-GFA × after-rate** minus **surrendered agri m² × $4,500** (and any building-land GFA × before-rate) — hotel counts as **non-res**; PFS / DG / OT / columbarium knock you out of standard rates.

### 2. Formula (para 3)

**Premium = A × B − C × D − E**

| Factor | What LandsD uses |
|---|---|
| **A** | Maximum **total GFA after** the exchange as stipulated in the land exchange document(s). **GA** floor space may be disregarded. If GA is stated as **NOFA**, convert **NOFA:GFA = 1:1.8**. Other floor space disregarded **only** if **expressly** GFA-exempted in the document. **If no maximum total GFA is stipulated, this PN does not apply** |
| **B** | After-use standard rate(s) for the lot's zone(s) |
| **C** | Total **site area of Agricultural land** surrendered **and/or** total **permissible GFA of Building land** surrendered |
| **D** | Before-use standard rate(s). Building land: residential rate if GFA does not prohibit residential; else non-res |
| **E** | Deductible **construction cost of Government Accommodation** the applicant must build, assign/hand back, or O&M for public use — assessed by the proponent department and, if practicable, told to the applicant **before** the Option deadline. If not conveyed, **E = 0** |

**Building land (fn 9):** land on which building structure(s) is allowed, including the building portion of mixed lots. Old schedule lots are Building land (residential — residential not prohibited) if the Block Government Lease Schedule term is one or more of: house, building, mat shed, boat hut, temple, nunnery, latrine, shop, cow shed, cattle shed, duck shed, cow byre, pigsty, school, or other uses LandsD determines. Permissible GFA = GFA specified in the lease; **if none, plot ratio 1.33**. Untraceable leases: use and site area at LandsD discretion.

**SD takeaway:** Put a **maximum total GFA** (and GA as NOFA if needed) in the exchange document or the standard-rate option dies; old-schedule "house" lots credit **PR 1.33** residential GFA as "C".

### 3. Survey pack and transitional option (paras 4–6)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| To fix "C" | **Survey Submission** by an **Authorised Land Surveyor** (Cap. 473): surveyed/registered areas and boundaries of lots wholly surrendered; proposed areas/boundaries of partial surrenders; proposed regrant site. Plus **title deeds** of all lots to be surrendered |
| Deadline for that pack | Not later than settlement of **initial administrative fee**, or **two months from 2 April 2024** for applications already accepted for further processing |
| If not provided and applicant still opts standard rates | LandsD fixes "C" from information known to it and issues BBTO |
| Transitional (PBTO already accepted or BBTO issued but premium **not** accepted as of this PN) | One-time Option Form (Appendix I) by **31 May 2024**; missing form ⇒ deemed case-by-case. **Does not** apply if BBTO (including premium) **already accepted** |
| After PBTO accepted, want standard rates later | **Withdraw and resubmit** the land exchange (fresh fees/timeline). Walking away from a case-by-case BBTO **forfeits** fees |

**SD takeaway:** Commission the ALS boundary survey **before** PBTO if you want a known "C"; on-going cases had a **31 May 2024** one-shot option.
