# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2025 — Self-certification of compliance for NTEHs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
January 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Pilot: DLO Yuen Long, **9 months** from 23 January 2025 (later extended by PN 1/2025B)

> Scope note: Optional **self-certification of compliance (SCC)** for **Certificate of Compliance** / **No Objection to Occupy** on Cap. 121 **New Territories Exempted Houses**. It is **not** a BO OP process and does **not** replace CoE for building/site formation/drainage works.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The PN applies only where a CoE for **building works** has been issued under Cap. 121 for the NTEH (site-formation/drainage CoEs need not also have been issued). DLO may, when issuing the land document, Approval Letter or CoE, rule the case **unsuitable** for SCC and force the conventional inspection path.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which applications (paras 2–4)

| Track | Typical locks in the land document / Approval Letter |
|---|---|
| New village house (land document) | Erect a building; **non-industrial** user; CoE required before BW/SF/drainage; **no occupation** until DLO CC |
| Rebuild (Approval Letter) | Non-industrial; CoE before works; DLO may issue **No Objection to Occupy** when Approval Letter conditions are met |
| In scope | CC under 2(d) **or** No Objection letter under 3(c), with building-works CoE |

**SD takeaway:** SCC is only for Cap. 121 NTEHs that already have a building-works CoE — conventional houses under Cap. 123 use PN 1/2026, not this PN.

---

### 2. Who may certify (para 6 and fn 2–3)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Registered professional (RP) | **RGE**, **RSE**, **RPE** (relevant discipline), or **AP** (architect / engineer / surveyor) as defined in Cap. 123 |
| Independence | RP submitting SCC **must not be or affiliated with** the person submitting Construction Completion Report (Forms CE/4 and CE/5) |
| Non-standard NTEH features | Seek **preliminary DLO advice** before CC application — standard forms cover usual features only |

**SD takeaway:** Appoint an independent RP (not the CE/4–CE/5 author) at SD if the village house will use the 10-week SCC stream.

---

### 3. Requisite Documents (para 6)

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Form 1 + App II + App III | SCC form; Standard Report on completed NTEH; Construction Completion Report |
| Ownership | Computer printout of current ownership |
| Form 2 (App IV) | Owner’s Statement |
| Others as required | Site plan(s), on-site photos, etc. |

**SD takeaway:** Build the as-built photo/plan pack into the completion programme — missing Requisite Documents stop the 2-week validity clock.

---

### 4. Processing times (paras 8–11)

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Valid application? | DLO decides within **2 weeks** of **all** Requisite Documents (no separate “valid” notice) |
| Invalid RP (not on Cap. 123 / Cap. 409 register) | Falls back to **conventional** processing |
| Streamlined CC | LandsD target: **10 weeks** from complete documents → CC **or** reasoned refusal |
| **Audit** selection (fn 5: then processed conventionally) | Clock extends to **14 weeks**; applicant is told |
| Pre-PN applications opting in | Processing **withheld** until Requisite Documents arrive; priority from **actual receipt date** |

**SD takeaway:** Programme NTEH occupation around **10 weeks** (14 if audited) from a complete SCC pack — not from first incomplete lodging.

---

### 5. After CC (para 12)

Issuance does not prejudice Government enforcement. Misconduct on SCC (including RP misconduct) may be referred to the Building Authority, professional bodies and other authorities.

**SD takeaway:** SCC is not a safe-harbour if the house does not match the certified parameters.
