# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2022 — Self-Certification of Sales Office and Show Flats
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
May 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Forms: Rev. May 2022

> Scope note: Streamlined **lease approval** for temporary **sales office, show flats and related marketing** in **private residential or composite C/R** developments under the “Provision of sales office and show flats” clause. It is **not** a BO occupation permit and does **not** waive other lease conditions. Guidance Notes live on landsd.gov.hk (`PN 5_2022 Guidance.pdf`).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Where the lease requires the Director’s written approval to erect separate temporary structures and/or use parts of buildings on the lot as sales office/show flats/marketing, approval is **normally** given if scale and period are **temporary and reasonable**. The lot owner may appoint a registered professional to submit a **self-certification of compliance** (Form 1 + Form 2) instead of a conventional consent application.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who, when, how long (paras 2–4; Form 1)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Eligible RP | **AP** / **RPS (General Practice)** / **RPS (Building Surveying)** / **Registered Architect** |
| DLO | Includes **Railway Development Section** |
| When to submit SCC | **After** issuance of **pre-sale consent** under the LandsD Consent Scheme |
| Validity of approval | Until the owner lodges the **Certificate of Compliance** application |
| Premises | Location and **GFA** as shown on **GBP approved by LandsD**; Form 1 states GFA in m² |
| Period | From the stated start date **to the date of CC application** |
| Attachments | Certified **full land search** + lease; **Consent Scheme** consent letter; plan of Premises GFA/location on the LandsD-approved GBP |

**SD takeaway:** Park sales-office/show-flat GFA on the **LandsD-approved GBP** and do not file the SCC until **presale consent** is in hand — approval dies when the **CC** application is lodged.

---

### 2. Random check vs deemed approval (paras 5–7)

| Outcome | Clock | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Valid SCC entered in register | Random full-check selection | RP told within **10 working days** of Receipt Date whether selected |
| **Not** selected | Letter of acknowledgement within **10 working days** | **Deemed approval** under the Clause, containing Form 2 para 2 terms |
| **Selected** | Result within **8 weeks** (check + **on-site inspection**) | If fail: fresh Form 1 + Form 2 after rectification; if pass: DLO **approval letter** (Director may add terms) |
| Deemed approval is not | Acknowledgement of **other** lease conditions | LandsD may **revoke/withdraw** if SCC is non-compliant or Form 2 is breached |

**SD takeaway:** Budget **10 WD** for a no-check deemed approval or **8 weeks** if drawn for full check — do not occupy/market on the SCC alone until the acknowledgement or approval letter is issued.

---

### 3. Owner’s locks on expiry (Form 2 para 2)

| Term | Design / lease lock |
|---|---|
| Statutory licences | Owner must obtain all licences/permissions; if not obtained, Approval is **deemed withdrawn** |
| End of Period / revocation | Premises **cease** sales/show use and revert to **all** GSC/SCs including **user and maximum GFA**; temporary structures **demolished** at owner’s cost |
| Other lease conditions | Remain in force; SCC/Approval does **not** prove other compliance |
| Re-entry | Lease re-entry proviso **extends** to Form 2 (a)–(e) breaches |
| Third parties | No Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Ordinance rights; DMC/mortgagee rights **not** prejudiced |
| Revocation | LandsD may revoke **at any time** without compensation |

**SD takeaway:** Treat sales office/show flats as **temporary GFA** that must vanish (and any temporary structure come down) by **CC application** — they cannot be left as permanent common or clubhouse space without a fresh lease path.
