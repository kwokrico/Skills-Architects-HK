# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2012B — High-tier data centre / data centre waiver (streamlining)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
May 2016 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 3/2012B | Immediate effect | Varies and supplements **LAO PN 3/2012** and **3/2012A**

> Scope note: Process and documentation update for the same two tracks as PN 3/2012. Caption (b) **includes part of, parts of, or an entire industrial building**. It does **not** restate the 3/2012 intensity or 15-year/zoning locks — those remain in 3/2012 / 3/2012A unless varied here. Appendix I here **supersedes** Appendix I to PN 3/2012.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Except as varied by this PN, all other provisions of PN 3/2012 and 3/2012A remain valid. The three notes are collectively “the Practice Notes”. The stated aim is to issue caption-(b) waivers acceptable to Government, on terms along the sample letter at Appendix II, **around two weeks** from receipt of a **valid** application with all required documents.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What this PN changes (paras 1–2, fn 1)

| Item | Change |
|---|---|
| Caption (b) coverage | Part of, **parts of, or an entire** industrial building |
| Form | Application **must** be in the form of **Appendix I** plus Checklist of Basic Requirements |
| Routing | **(a)** lease modification / exchange → **District Lands Officer**; **(b)** waiver → **Chief Estate Surveyor/Headquarters**, 20/F North Point Government Offices |
| Waiver target time | **~2 weeks** from valid receipt (documents complete) |
| Sample instrument | Appendix II sample **Data Centre Waiver** letter |

**SD takeaway:** Treat 3/2012B as the current **application pack and waiver form** — submit (b) to CES/HQ with the new Appendix I, not the 3/2012 form.

---

### 2. Application pack — design-facing extras vs 3/2012 (Appendix I checklist)

| Must-submit (beyond 3/2012 pack) | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Energy saving plan** | For the planned data centre; refer to OGCIO **“Green Data Centre Practices”** (fn 4) |
| Waiver floor plans | **4 copies**, not larger than **A3**, property edged **pink**, **internal floor area** specified; AP or other competent professional |
| Caption (b) declarations | Zoning **I / C / OU(B)** with OZP No.; latest **OP No.** and building age **≥ 15 years** from that OP |
| Caption (b) termination clause | Applicant accepts waiver may be terminated and **admin fee not refunded** even after grant |
| Lease-mod intensity | Still declare if proposed intensity is **less than** max, with GFA and **% of max** |
| High-tier proof | Conceptual design satisfying **fn 4 of PN 3/2012** |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **pink-edged IFA plans**, an **energy-saving / Green Data Centre** narrative, and OP-dated **≥15-year** age before filing — missing DVD-style completeness is not the issue here; missing pink plans and green plan are.

---

### 3. Sample waiver — design and works locks (Appendix II)

| Condition | Lock |
|---|---|
| Permitted purpose | **Data centre** as defined (computers, servers, telecom + E&M support in a secure controlled environment) |
| Warranties | Zoning I / C / OU(B); building age **≥ 15 years** from **latest OP** at application date — false/misleading → Government may terminate |
| Term | From letter date until **demolition of existing building**, lease expiry date, or **early lease termination** — whichever earlier |
| Exclusive user | Premises **only** for Permitted Purpose; Director’s determination **final** |
| Works | BO-compliant; complete within **3 years**; AP-certified proof **≤ 2 calendar months** after that 3-year period; fail → Government may terminate |
| Traffic | Before works start, supply TD-required information that road access, parking, and L/UL are adequate and no adverse traffic impact |
| Licences | All Government licences/permits **before** operation |
| Premium later | Subsequent redevelopment or lease modification assessed **without** taking the waiver into account |
| Resumption | Government may terminate on **≥ 1 calendar month** notice after resumption notice |
| DMC / mortgage | Waiver does not prejudice co-owners’ DMC rights or chargee/mortgagee rights; chargee consent to be endorsed if charged |
| Existing waiver | This waiver **supersedes** any existing waiver on the Premises from letter date |
| Not a lease mod | All other lease covenants remain; waiver is **not** a modification |

**SD takeaway:** Programme conversion works to **finish in 3 years** with AP proof, and size parking/L/UL for TD before start on site — the waiver is personal to this building’s life and is ignored in any later premium.
