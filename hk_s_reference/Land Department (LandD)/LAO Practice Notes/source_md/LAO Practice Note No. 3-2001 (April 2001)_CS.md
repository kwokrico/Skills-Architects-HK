# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2001 — Premium Assessment for Exemption of Balconies from GFA and SC under JPN 1
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 April 2001 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD 2/1020/00 II | Director of Lands (R.D. Pope)

> Scope note: How LandsD will **modify the lease and charge premium** so JPN 1 balconies can be excluded from **lease GFA and SC**. It does **not** set the BO/JPN 1 balcony design criteria — those stay with the Building Authority. Rates in the Appendix take effect **April 2001** and are reviewed from **1 April** each year.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 1 allows qualifying balconies to be exempted from GFA and SC under the lease **subject to lease modification and premium**. For balcony-only modifications, LandsD will not entertain applications **before BD plan approval**; a modification letter plus consent letter (with demand note) is the vehicle, with a single administrative fee then **$23,000**, targeted **1–2 months**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When to apply and what to state (paras 2, 6–7)

| Track | Lock |
|---|---|
| Balcony exemption **only** | Apply **after BD plan approval**. State: number of flats with exempted-balcony floor space; **total exempted balcony area per flat**; **maximum permissible** exempted balcony floor space per flat under JPN 1 |
| Instrument | Modification letter allowing the Director to issue **consent letters**; consent letter + demand note issued together; admin fee **$23,000** (then current); ~**1–2 months** |
| New mods / land exchanges (change of use or other issues) | Clause inserted so the Director may issue consent letters for balcony floor space already exempted by BA; consent still needs **premium + admin fee** |
| Subsequent changes | After first consent, changes to composition or permissible exempted floor space need a **further consent** (admin fee; possibly additional premium) |
| GBP | Provide a **schedule** on plans to BD **and** LandsD: permissible vs claimed exempted balcony space |
| Auction / tender sites | New “Definition of GFA” clause will let the Director exempt BA-exempted **green and innovative** floor space at **nil premium** |

**SD takeaway:** Do not lodge a balcony-only lease mod until BD has approved the plans — the premium is locked to **flat count × exempted m² band**, so freeze balcony sizes and the JPN 1 permissible cap on the GBP schedule.

---

### 2. Premium formula (para 4)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Inputs | Location (DLO district); number of flats with exempted balcony; **permissible** exempted floor space; **actual** exempted floor space |
| Rates | Appendix schedule; **non-negotiable**; reviewed from **1 April** each year |
| Basic sum | Premium = Σ (number of flats in a size band × premium per flat for that band) |
| Pro-rata | If **claimed** exempted area **<** JPN 1 permissible **and** permissible **> 2.59 m²**, assess **pro-rata** on the permissible-band rate |
| Rounding | Exempted balcony floor space rounded to nearest **0.1 m²** (2.54 → 2.5; 2.55 → 2.6) |
| Split lots | DLO district boundaries; lot straddling two districts → **higher** rate |
| Admin fee | **$23,000** in addition (then current) |

Worked example (HK Island; claimed = permissible): 30 flats @ 2 m² + 50 @ 4.1 m² + 20 @ 5 m² → 30×$4,100 + 50×$29,700 + 20×$38,600 = **$2,380,000**.

Pro-rata examples (HK Island, per flat): claimed 2.1 / perm 2.5 → **not** pro-rata, **$4,800**; claimed 2.1 / perm 2.6 → $15,800 × 2.1/2.6 = **$12,761.54**; claimed 2.8 / perm 4.0 → $28,700 × 2.8/4.0 = **$20,900**.

**SD takeaway:** Crossing **2.59 m² permissible** (the 2.6 m² band) roughly **doubles** the HK/KLN unit rate — keep claimed vs permissible on the same side of that cliff unless the extra area is worth the premium.
