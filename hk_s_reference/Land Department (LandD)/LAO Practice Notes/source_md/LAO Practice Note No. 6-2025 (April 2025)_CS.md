# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2025 — Standard Rates for JPN 1 & 2 Balcony / UP / NSPEWall Premium
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 April 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates effective **1 April 2025**

> Scope note: **Rate revision only** for premium on lease exemption of balconies, utility platforms and NSPEWalls from GFA and SC under **JPN 1** and **JPN 2**. Varies/supplements LAO PN **3/2001** (as supplemented by **6/2001**), **6/2002**, **3/2003**, **2/2011**, **4/2023** and **3/2024**; other provisions in those PNs remain valid. Districts follow **Appendix VII to LAO PN 3/2024** (five regions). It does **not** restate JPN dimensional rules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From **1 April 2025** the standard premium rates for those exemptions are revised. Which appendix applies still depends on the **JPN version** on the lease (October 2004 vs January 2011 JPN 1; February 2006 vs January 2011 JPN 2, or later variations); a lot straddling two districts uses the **higher** rate.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which table / which JPN vintage (para 2)

| Feature | JPN vintage | Appendix |
|---|---|---|
| Balcony | JPN 1 **October 2004** | **I** — per unit by total exempted balcony m² (2.0–5.0) |
| Balcony | JPN 1 **January 2011** | **II** — per unit by total exempted balcony m² (1.0–3.0) |
| Utility platform | JPN 2 **February 2006** | **III** — flat premium per residential unit |
| Utility platform | JPN 2 **January 2011** | **IV** — flat premium per residential unit |
| NSPEWall | JPN 2 **February 2006** | **V** — $/m² of BA-exempted area; thickness **> 300 mm** is **GFA-accountable** |
| NSPEWall | JPN 2 **January 2011** | **VI** — same method; thickness **> 150 mm** is **GFA-accountable** |

| Cross-cutting rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| District map | **Appendix VII to LAO PN 3/2024** (Hong Kong Island / Kowloon East / Kowloon West / NT South / NT North) |
| Straddling lots | **Higher** rate |
| Balcony rounding | AP to nearest **0.001 m²**; **2.544 → 2.5 m²**; **2.545 → 2.6 m²** |

**SD takeaway:** Use **PN 3/2024 Appendix VII** regions (not the old six-DLO map in PN 4/2023) and the lease’s JPN vintage — 2011 NSPEWall still caps exemption at **150 mm**.

### 4. NSPEWall — $/m² of BA-exempted area from 1.4.2025 (App V–VI)

Thickness from **approved building plans**; multiple thicknesses → **majority** principle. Average flat size = total residential GFA / total residential units; split at **104 m²(G)**. Mosaic/similar **tile cladding** that only beautifies is **not** in the premium calc; **curtain-wall-type** cladding on structural walls is GFA-countable unless exempted by lease modification + assessed premium.

**130 mm or less** (App V Feb 2006 and App VI Jan 2011), HK$/m²:

| District | ≤ 104 m²(G) | > 104 m²(G) |
|---|---|---|
| Hong Kong Island | 75,000 | 115,410 |
| Kowloon East | 56,000 | 86,170 |
| Kowloon West | 62,000 | 95,400 |
| New Territories South | 41,000 | 39,240 |
| New Territories North | 29,000 | 27,510 |

App V continues in 10 mm steps to **290–300 mm**. App VI (Jan 2011) stops at **140–150 mm**.

**SD takeaway:** Keep NSPEWall **≤ 150 mm** on 2011 leases (or **≤ 300 mm** on 2006) or the excess is **GFA**; decorative tile cladding stays out of the premium area.
