# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2002 — Processing Time for Master Layout Plan, General Building Plan and Landscaping Plan
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 June 2002 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L C Lau)

> Scope note: Shortens LandsD **pledged processing days** for MLP, GBP and landscaping plans after centralisation of BC III MLP/GBP. It does **not** change what those submissions must contain.

---

## Regulatory Overview

After centralisation of processing of master layout / general building plans (BC III cases), pledged processing times are reduced as tabulated. Time runs from **valid receipt** (no outstanding information required from the AP) to DLO approval/disapproval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised processing clocks

| Submission | Previous | Revised |
|---|---|---|
| MLP — **BC III** | within **12 weeks** (PN 2/94) | within **10 weeks** |
| MLP — **Non-BC III** | within 12 weeks | within **8 weeks** |
| GBP — **BC III** | within **15 weeks** (PN 1/94) | within **10 weeks** |
| GBP — **Non-BC III** | within 15 weeks | within **8 weeks** |
| Landscaping Plan | within **12 weeks** (PN 3/94) | within **8 weeks** |

**Non-BC III** (^) includes: Zone I straightforward cases; normal industrial/godown; normal electricity substation; hoarding plans; site formation plans; landscaping plans; drainage proposals; **NT exempted houses**.

Clock (#): from date of **valid receipt** (no outstanding AP information) to date of DLO approval/disapproval.

**SD takeaway:** Programme LandsD MLP/GBP/landscape at **10 weeks (BC III)** or **8 weeks (other)** from a **complete** submission — incomplete packs do not start the pledge.
