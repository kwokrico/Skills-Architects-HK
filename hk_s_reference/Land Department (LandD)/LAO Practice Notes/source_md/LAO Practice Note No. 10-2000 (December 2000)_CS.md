# LAO Practice Note No. 10/2000 — Flexibility in Parking Provisions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 December 2000 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (R. D. Pope)

> Scope note: Adds a **± flexibility band** on lease parking / loading-unloading counts, and says extra spaces within that band are **not** counted toward lease GFA and SC. It is **not** a Transport Department design standard; TD advice decides whether the flexibility is written into a given lease.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Lease conditions usually require parking, loading and unloading at **fixed ratios** to GFA or unit count. Future leases may, **in addition**, allow variation of each type by **5 percent or 50 spaces, whichever is the less**, but only on **Transport Department** advice and on the merits of each case (including which types the flexibility covers).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Flexibility band and GFA/SC (paras 2–4)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Variation | Numbers of **each type** of parking, loading and unloading spaces may vary by **5% or 50 spaces, whichever is the less**, for that type of motor vehicle |
| Who decides | Flexibility (including **which types** it applies to) is incorporated **only on TD advice**, case by case |
| GFA / SC | Spaces provided **in addition to the fixed ratios** but **pursuant to this flexibility** are **not** taken into account for **total GFA** and **total site coverage** stipulated in the lease |
| Existing leases | If the lease has **no** such flexibility, a **modification** to incorporate it will be considered on application |

**SD takeaway:** Treat the lease ratio as the base; extra bays within **min(5%, 50 spaces) per type** can be designed without eating lease GFA/SC — but only if TD has (or will) endorse that flexibility in the lease.
