# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2019 — Acceptable uses in ‘godown purposes’ premises
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 January 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 1/2019

> Scope note: Landlord view on whether **“cargo handling and forwarding facility”** and **“recyclable collection centre”** (TPB Definitions of Terms, with exclusions) may be accepted in premises restricted to **‘godown purposes’**. It does **not** interpret other users, waive lease, or replace FSD/BA licensing. Director’s decision on lease conformity is **final**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Godown Premises may only be used for **storage of goods**, with **movement** of goods expected and the operation involving **transient deposit and storage for delivery**. LandsD will generally accept the two named uses if there is **primarily storage** of goods/material with those attendant consequences. An overall view of all activities (nature and cumulative effect), the particular lease, proposed operation design, operation details and technology employed will be considered case by case.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What may be accepted — and what is excluded (paras 1–3, fn 2)

| Use (TPB DoT meaning, as adopted here) | Exclusion in this PN |
|---|---|
| **Cargo handling and forwarding facility** — inbound / transhipment / outbound cargo processing and/or freight forwarding, including logistics support such as packaging, labelling and final assembly for distribution | **Does not include** container freight station **or** free-standing purpose-designed logistics centre |
| **Recyclable collection centre** — community-based recycling: collection, storage, sorting, packing and baling of recyclable materials for recycling | **Does not include** sorting of **municipal solid waste** |
| Prerequisite | **Primarily storage** in the Godown Premises, with movement/transient deposit as in para 1 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the floor as **storage-led** godown with cargo-handling or recyclable-collection ancillary to that storage — do not design a **CFS, purpose-built logistics centre, or MSW sorting hall** onto a godown user.
