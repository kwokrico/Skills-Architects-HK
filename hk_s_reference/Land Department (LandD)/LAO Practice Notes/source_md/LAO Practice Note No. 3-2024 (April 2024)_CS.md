# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2024 — JPN 1/2 balcony, UP and NSP wall premium rates
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
April 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Effective **1 April 2024** | Varies LAO PN Nos. 3/2001 (as supplemented by 6/2001), 6/2002, 3/2003, 2/2011 and 4/2023

> Scope note: Annual revision of **standard premium rates** for GFA/SC exemption of **balconies, utility platforms and non-structural prefabricated external walls** under JPN Nos. 1 and 2. It is **not** a new exemption pathway — JPN 1/2 still govern what may be exempted.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Rates in the listed Practice Notes are revised after the annual review from 1 April 2024. District boundaries for the rates are reduced from **six regions to five**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where the new rates sit (para 2)

| Exemption | JPN version | Rate tables (this PN) |
|---|---|---|
| **Balconies** — GFA and SC | JPN 1 **October 2004** version (or later variations) | Appendix I |
| **Balconies** — GFA and SC | JPN 1 **January 2011** version (or later variations) | Appendix II |
| **Utility Platforms** and **non-structural prefabricated external walls** — GFA and SC | JPN 2 **February 2006** version (or later variations) | Appendices III and IV |
| Same UP / NSP walls | JPN 2 **January 2011** version (or later variations) | Appendices V and VI |
| District bands | — | **Five** regions (was six) |

The numbered rate tables (Appendices I–VI) are **not attached** to this library’s one-page PDF (`PN 3_2024.pdf`); use LandsD’s published appendices / **PN 6/2025** for current dollars.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze balcony / UP / NSP-wall exemption quantum against the **correct JPN vintage** and the **five-region** map before quoting GFA/SC — premium is the 1 April 2024 appendix rates, not the six-region predecessor.
