# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2000A — “House” Restrictions after Fully Profit
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 June 2014 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supplements LAO PN No. 3/2000 | CFA: *Fully Profit (Asia) Ltd v Secretary for Justice* FACV 17/2012

> Scope note: Extra test for lots with a **“house” restriction** after the CFA held that “house” means the **type and characteristics of the house existing when the Government lease was entered into**. It is a **guide** — each lease is construed on its own facts. It is **not** a BO house-count rule.

## Regulatory Overview

PN 3/2000A sits on top of PN 3/2000 (one main + one secondary entrance, etc.). LandsD will also compare the proposal to the **historic house on the lot at lease execution**, especially **building height**. A proposal that does not match that historic type/characteristics is **not permitted** without **lease modification + premium + admin fee**, unless the recognition / nil-premium path in para. 5 applies.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Historic house as the meaning of “house” (paras. 1–2)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| CFA holding | “House” = type and characteristics of the house **standing on the lot when the Government lease was entered into** |
| Extra to PN 3/2000 | Still apply PN 3/2000 entrance/MoE guidelines **plus** historic type/characteristics |
| Height | **Building height** is singled out as a characteristic that must match |
| Non-match | Not permitted under the house restriction → **modification + premium + admin fee** (unless para. 5) |

**SD takeaway:** Before SD on a “one house” / house-restriction lot, recover **what was on the lot at lease date** (height, type) — matching PN 3/2000 entrances is not enough after *Fully Profit*.

### 2. Sub-divided lots still on original Conditions (para. 3)

Where sub-lots remain on original Conditions of Grant/Sale/Exchange (no separate Government lease executed), but other sub-lots of the **same parent** have executed leases and had houses standing at that execution, LandsD looks at the **houses on those executed sub-lots** as the reference type/characteristics (houses having been erected under the original Conditions).

**SD takeaway:** On remaining Conditions land in a split parent lot, the “house” comparator may be the **sibling sub-lot’s lease-date house**, not a blank site.

### 3. When PN 3/2000 alone still governs (para. 4)

Use PN 3/2000 guidelines (not the historic-house overlay) if:

- **no house** existed on the lot at Government-lease execution; **or**
- no Government lease executed and the house was erected **only after** original Conditions — **except** the para. 3 sub-divided cases;

unless other evidence (correspondence, approved plans, pre-existing houses) establishes the context of “house”.

**SD takeaway:** Vacant-at-grant lots stay on the PN 3/2000 one-house definition unless the file shows a different historic meaning.

### 4. Recognition of a LandsD-approved redevelopment (para. 5)

If the lot (or para. 2–3 sub-lot) was **redeveloped** and LandsD **approved or raised no objection** to those building plans, LandsD may **recognise** that redevelopment’s **GFA and building height** as the permitted “house” type — **not** a waiver of the house restriction. Owner may be asked to (or may apply for) a modification spelling out that intensity + PN 3/2000 entrance requirements. Because it only records an already-approved redevelopment: **nil premium**, **admin fee** payable.

**SD takeaway:** A prior LandsD-cleared redevelopment can be the new baseline (GFA + height) via **nil-premium modification** — get that on title before the next redevelopment SD.
