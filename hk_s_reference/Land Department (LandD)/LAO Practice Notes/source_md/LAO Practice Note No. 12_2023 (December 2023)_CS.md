# LAO Practice Note No. 12/2023 — Charging Land Premium at Standard Rates on Lease Modifications for Redevelopment of Industrial Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 December 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Supersedes LAO PN Nos. 1/2021 and 1/2021A | These rates valid **until 31 March 2024** (later rates to be promulgated)

> Scope note: Optional **standard-rate premium** for lease modification / in-situ land exchange to **redevelop pre-1987 IBs** on industrial lots for **commercial, modern industrial and/or residential** use. **Not** for special uses (e.g. columbarium). IBs that completed **wholesale conversion** under the 2010 or 2018 IB revitalisation schemes are **not** pre-1987 IBs for this PN. **PN 2/2019** extra PR is **not** a prerequisite.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Applicants may stay on case-by-case premium or opt for standard rates **not later than PBTO acceptance** (Option Form, Appendix I). The Option is **irreversible**; no appeal against the BBTO premium; fees forfeited if the applicant then walks away. If no **maximum total GFA** is stipulated in the modification document, the scheme **does not apply**. Straightforward BBTO: normally **within five weeks** of PBTO acceptance.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligible stock (paras 1–3, fn 1–4)

| Term | Lock |
|---|---|
| **Industrial building** | Built for industrial and/or godown use on an industrial lot |
| **Industrial lot** | Land grant shall not be used other than for industrial or godown purposes or both. **Industrial** includes special industrial (leather tanning, garment manufacturing, food production, etc.). A lot **without** a specific user restriction is also an industrial lot for this PN. **Vacant** industrial lots that were occupied by pre-1987 IBs immediately before vacancy **are** eligible |
| **Pre-1987 IB** | Wholly or partly constructed **on or before 1 March 1987**, **or** constructed to building plans **first submitted** to BA **on or before** 1 March 1987 |
| **Not eligible as pre-1987** | IBs that completed wholesale conversion under the **2010 or 2018** IB Revitalisation Schemes |
| After-use | Commercial, modern industrial and/or residential. **Hotel and hotel ancillary GFA = commercial**. LandsD decides what is "special use" outside the scheme |

If the lot falls in an **NDA** or in the **agricultural-land pilot** areas, the applicant may opt **only** the standard rates in **those** PNs, not this PN.

**SD takeaway:** Confirm OP or first GBP submission **on/before 1 March 1987**, and that the IB was **not** wholesale-converted under 2010/2018 schemes, before banking this premium path.

### 2. Formula (para 5)

**Premium = A × B − C × D − E** (negative ⇒ no premium).

| Factor | Lock |
|---|---|
| **A** | Maximum total GFA after modification. **GA** may be disregarded; GA stated as NOFA ⇒ **NOFA:GFA = 1:1.8**. **Public Vehicle Park is GFA-accountable** unless the modification document exempts it. Only floor space **expressly** GFA-exempted in the document is otherwise disregarded |
| **B** | After-use rate for the region. **Residential** rates apply to all applications **not prohibiting private residential**, **except** sites in OZP **"CDA"** or **"OU"** for commercial-cum-residential (and/or related planning approvals): residential rate on GFA not prohibiting private residential; **commercial/modern industrial** rate on the remainder |
| **C** | GFA of the pre-1987 IB existing on the lot (or existing immediately before vacancy) per **latest BA-approved building plans**, **excluding** parking, L/UL, plant rooms and similar facilities **not normally GFA-accountable under the BO**, and any GFA on those plans that **does not comply with the lease**. Certify by **AP** or **Registered Professional Surveyor**. Alternative (irreversible): GFA as if redeveloped for industrial/godown **before** modification, as LandsD would use in a case-by-case assessment |
| **D** | Before-use rate: Industrial/Godown (except Special Industrial) — also where the grant has **no** user restriction — or **Special Industrial**, by region |
| **E** | GA construction cost (same logic as other standard-rate PNs); **E = 0** if not conveyed before the Option deadline |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **max GFA after** (PVP counts unless exempted) and AP-certify existing IB GFA **net of** non-accountable parking/plant; CDA/OU mixed sites split **B** between residential and commercial rates.

### 4. Enquiry Form and transitional (paras 7, 10–11)

| Tool | When |
|---|---|
| Enquiry Form (Appendix III) + supporting docs | Not later than settlement of **initial administrative fee** → LandsD states "C" with the PBTO |
| Transitional for **special industrial** lots with PBTO already accepted or BBTO issued but premium **not** accepted as of this PN | Opt by **28 February 2024**; Enquiry Form by **26 January 2024** if "C" needed first. **Not** if BBTO including premium already accepted |
| Opt after PBTO but before BBTO | **Resubmit** the lease modification (fresh fees/timeline) |

Option Form must include OP **or** BA letter showing first plan submission on/before 1 March 1987, plus the AP/RPS GFA Certificate and approved plans (unless using the alternative "C" basis).

**SD takeaway:** Lodge the Enquiry Form with the AP GFA certificate **with the admin-fee payment** so "C" is known before you irreversibly opt.
