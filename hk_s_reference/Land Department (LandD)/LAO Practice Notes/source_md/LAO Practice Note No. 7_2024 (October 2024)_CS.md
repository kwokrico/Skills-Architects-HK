# LAO Practice Note No. 7/2024 — Lease Modification (or a Land Exchange) for Redevelopment of an Industrial Lot (Special time-limited arrangement for application for relaxation of development intensity)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 October 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies/supplements LAO PN Nos. 2/2019 and 2/2019A

> Scope note: **Only** extends the Town Planning Board application window in PN 2/2019A and replaces the sample application form. It does **not** restate the industrial-lot intensity-relaxation scheme. Parent PNs remain valid except as varied here.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN varies LAO PN Nos. 2/2019 and 2/2019A for lease modification (or land exchange) to redevelop an industrial lot with a special time-limited relaxation of development intensity. All other provisions of those PNs stay in force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Extended TPB application window (para 2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| What changed | Words **"From 10 October 2018 to 31 October 2024 (both dates inclusive)"** in para 2 of **PN 2/2019A** |
| New window | **"From 10 October 2018 to 31 December 2027 (both dates inclusive)"** |
| What the window is | Time to submit application to the **Town Planning Board** for planning approval of redevelopment **above** the maximum development intensity permitted under the relevant statutory town plans (as specified in para 2 of PN 2/2019, as varied by PN 2/2019A) |

**SD takeaway:** File the TPB intensity-relaxation application on or before **31 December 2027** — the LandsD special arrangement still rides on that planning approval window.

---

### 2. Sample application form (para 3)

Appendix I to this PN **supersedes** the sample application in Appendix I to **PN 2/2019**. Use the new form for submissions under the parent scheme.

**SD takeaway:** Do not recycle the 2019 sample application — use Appendix I of PN 7/2024.
