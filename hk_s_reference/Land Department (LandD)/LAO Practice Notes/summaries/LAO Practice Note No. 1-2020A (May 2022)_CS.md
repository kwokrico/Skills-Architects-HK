# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2020A — Landscape clause: SCC at submission and faster completion checks
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
May 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies **LAO PN No. 1/2020**

> Scope note: Adds optional **self-certification at LMP/LP submission** (lease-only cases), early vetting during a binding land transaction, and **shorter** completion-audit clocks. It does **not** change JPN 3 planning-tied deemed approval, the simple plant-and-maintain clause, or Coloured-Area carve-out in PN 1/2020. Forms **1–5** and Figures **1–2** of PN 1/2020 are **superseded**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Except as varied here, PN 1/2020 remains valid. “DLO” includes Railway Development Section and other specialised LandsD sections. Deemed approval/compliance still does not certify other lease conditions and may be **withdrawn or revoked** if the LMP/LP or SCC fails LandsD requirements.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Optional SCC at submission — lease-only LMP/LP (paras 2–3; new Forms 6–7)

Lot owner **may** appoint an RLA to submit **Form 6** SCC + **Form 7** Owner’s Statement with the LMP/LP when **all** of the following are met:

| Condition | Lock |
|---|---|
| (a) | LMP/LP prepared by an **RLA** |
| (b) | LMP/LP incorporates the lot’s **latest TPRP approved by LandsD** |
| (c) | LMP/LP includes location, disposition and layout of greenery / greening features per **PNAP APP-152 Appendix D**, meeting the lease **prescriptive SCG** and **accepted by BD** |

| Audit path | Clock from valid LMP/LP + SCC receipt |
|---|---|
| Enter register; random full check with BPU **LAT** | RLA told within **10 working days** whether selected |
| **Not** selected | Acknowledgement = **deemed approval** of landscape **submission** within **4 weeks** |
| Selected | Result within **8 weeks**; if unacceptable, revise and re-submit |

**SD takeaway:** To use 4-week deemed LMP approval, freeze **LandsD-approved TPRP** and **BD-accepted SCG layout** into the same RLA drawings before lodging.

---

### 2. Early landscape vetting during land transaction (para 4)

If a land exchange / grant / modification is in process **and** the owner has entered a **binding contract and paid the required deposit**, owner or RLA may submit LMP/LP for vetting on the established timeframe. **Formal approval** is given only **after** the land transaction completes, and only if the submission is acceptable and PN 1/2020 criteria are met.

**SD takeaway:** Start LMP after PBTO/BBTO deposit — comments can flow, but do not treat them as lease approval until the new grant exists.

---

### 3. LAT comments (para 5)

LAT technical comments will be sent **to the RLA direct**, in advance of DLO’s consolidated reply.

**SD takeaway:** Route landscape queries to the RLA, not only the AP, so LAT notes are not sitting in a DLO letter.

---

### 4. Completion-stage clocks — revised para 11(c)–(d) of PN 1/2020 (para 6)

| Event | PN 1/2020 | This PN |
|---|---|---|
| Notice of random full check (LMP SCC) | 14 working days | **10 working days** from valid completion SCC; silence = deemed complied |
| Result if selected | Liaise within 14 working days; result **6 weeks after on-site check** | Result within **8 weeks from receipt** of completion SCC |
| Rectification + fresh SCC | Unchanged | Unchanged |

**SD takeaway:** Programme landscape CC around **10 working days / 8 weeks from SCC receipt**, not the old 14-day / 6-weeks-after-inspection pair.

---

### 5. Forms and flow charts (para 9)

| Superseded (PN 1/2020) | Replacement |
|---|---|
| Forms 1–5 | **1A, 2A, 3A, 4A, 5A** |
| Figures 1 and 2 | **Figure 1A** (submission), **Figure 1B** (submission with SCC), **Figure 2A** (completion) |
| New | **Form 6** (SCC on submission), **Form 7** (owner’s statement on submission SCC) |

Form 1A notes (design locks unchanged in substance): PlanD approval required before deemed-approval application; lot vs planning boundary; SCG vs BD plans; TPRP via **PN 2/2020**; Coloured Areas / other B/D still follow JPN 3 and **DEVB TC(W) No. 6/2015**.

**SD takeaway:** Use the May 2022 **A-series** forms and Figure 1B if submitting with SCC — old PN 1/2020 forms are dead.
