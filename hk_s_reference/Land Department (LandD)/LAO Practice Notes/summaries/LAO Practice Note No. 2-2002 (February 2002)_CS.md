# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2002 — Processing of Plans Submission before Completion of Land Exchange or Lease Modification
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
5 February 2002 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD BP/24 | Director of Lands (R. D. Pope)

> Scope note: When MLP and GBP may be **enquired on or scrutinised** while a land exchange, land grant or lease modification is still in train. It does **not** authorise **formal** plan approval before the land transaction is completed.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Where a land exchange, land grant or lease modification is in train, the applicant may submit enquiry MLP/GBP after **provisional basic terms** are offered, and a whole set of Plans for scrutiny after a **binding contract and deposit**. Formal approval of the Plans is given only after the land transaction is completed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two gates (body)

| Stage of the land transaction | What APs may submit | What LandsD will do |
|---|---|---|
| **Provisional basic terms** offered | **Enquiry** Plans to the DLO / Specialist Section on compliance with a **particular** basic term | Comments on the **specific issue / basic term** only |
| Applicant has entered a **binding contract** and paid the required **deposit** | A **whole set** of Plans for scrutiny | Scrutiny now; **formal approval** of the Plans **after completion** of the land transaction (if all is in order) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not expect GBP/MLP **approval** on the strength of offered terms — programme enquiry comments after PBT, full scrutiny after deposit, and **formal** LandsD approval only at completion.
