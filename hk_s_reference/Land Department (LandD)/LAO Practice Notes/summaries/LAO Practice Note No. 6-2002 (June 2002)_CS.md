# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2002 — Premium Assessment for Exemption of Non-structural Prefabricated External Walls and Utility Platforms from GFA and SC under JPN 2
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 June 2002 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L C Lau)

> Scope note: Lease-modification / consent / premium treatment of **JPN 2** non-structural prefabricated external walls (NSPE walls) and **utility platforms**. It implements JPN 2 paras **(6)(a) and (6)(b)**. It is **not** the BO exemption criteria (those stay with BA / PNAP 13).

---

## Regulatory Overview

LandsD explains how existing vs new leases treat NSPE walls (aligned with curtain-wall GFA measurement) and utility platforms (one per residential unit, **≤ 1.5 m²**, BA exemption under JPN 2). Utility-platform consent can be applied for **before** BA plan approval; extra units over the consent count pay more premium, **no refund** if fewer are built.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Non-structural prefabricated external walls (para 2)

| Lease | GFA/SC treatment |
|---|---|
| **No** SC for “Determination of GFA in buildings with curtain wall system forming external face of building” | LandsD uses **actual external faces** of external walls. If BA-approved GFA (PNAP 13, April 2001 Revision) **exceeds** lease GFA measured that way, **modification + premium** is required to permit the additional GFA and/or SC (**including** the NSPE walls) |
| **Has** that curtain-wall GFA SC | LandsD **follows BA’s** GFA and/or SC method (including NSPE walls) |
| **New** leases with GFA and/or SC restriction | GFA/SC definition will exclude the curtain-wall system **without premium**; that exemption **also applies to NSPE walls** (treated in the same category as curtain wall for lease interpretation) |

**SD takeaway:** On old leases without a curtain-wall GFA SC, NSPE walls count to the **outer face** and will need **modification + premium** if that exceeds lease GFA — new sales get the exemption **nil-premium**.

---

### 2. Utility platforms (paras 3–5)

Subject to BA GFA exemption under JPN 2: **one** utility platform **not exceeding 1.5 m²** per residential unit, exempted from lease GFA and/or SC, as follows:

| Lease / transaction | Instrument and money |
|---|---|
| Existing lease **without** Director’s discretion to exclude BA-permitted floor space (sub-clause (b) of Definition of GFA) | **Lease modification + consent letter**; **admin fee + premium** |
| Existing lease **with** that sub-clause (b) | **Consent letter**; **admin fee + consent fee** |
| JPN 1 GFA/SC definition (environmentally friendly / innovative features) | Intended to **also cover JPN 2** features — **need not be further amended** |
| **New auction/tender** sites with GFA and/or SC restriction | Existing GFA/SC definition included; utility platform **excluded without premium** |
| **Lease modification / land exchange / PTG** with GFA and/or SC restriction | Same GFA/SC definition; utility platform excluded **subject to premium** |

Application for the consent letter **may precede** BA plan approval. State **number of units** with UPs and **GFA of each** UP. Consent states the permitted unit count.

| Quantum vs consent | Money |
|---|---|
| More units with UPs than consent | **Additional premium** for the extra units |
| Fewer units than consent | **No refund** |

Standard premium **per residential unit** (non-negotiable; reviewed annually) for **4/2002–3/2003**:

| HK/KLN | Sha Tin / Sai Kung | Tai Po / North | Tuen Mun / Yuen Long | Kwai Tsing / Tsuen Wan | Islands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $3,100 | $1,800 | 0 | 0 | $2,300 | 0 |

DLO district boundaries; straddling lots → **higher** rate. Sample consent: Appendix I (Director may vary). Consent also caps aggregate UP GFA per unit at **1.5 m²**; register within **1 calendar month**; photocopy within **3 calendar months**.

**SD takeaway:** One UP **≤ 1.5 m²** per flat; freeze the **unit count** in the consent (over-count costs extra; under-count is not refunded) and check whether the lease already has the BA-exclusion discretion.
