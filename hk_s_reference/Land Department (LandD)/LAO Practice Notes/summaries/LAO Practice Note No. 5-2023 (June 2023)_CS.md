# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2023 — RCHE Premises Incentive (Time-limited Enhancements)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 June 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Pilot: **20 June 2023 – 19 June 2026** | During the Pilot, **LAO PN 4/2003** is **not** applicable

> Scope note: Time-limited **premium and lease-GFA** concessions to put **licensed RCHE** in **new** private developments (not industrial; not A&A/conversion of existing buildings). It is **not** a Cap. 459 licensing manual. SWD support is mandatory. Read SWD’s June 2023 Guidance Note.

---

## Regulatory Overview

During the three-year Pilot, LandsD may (i) **exempt eligible RCHE from land premium** on lease modification, land exchange or PTG for a new private development, and (ii) **exempt eligible RCHE from total permissible GFA under lease**. Applications received after the Pilot will be processed under the parameters of the Incentive Scheme (and **PN 4/2003** is not applicable during the Pilot).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is “new” and what BA/TPB will do (paras 1–2 and footnotes)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| New private development | **Excludes industrial** developments; **addition, alteration or conversion of existing building(s)** is **not** a new private development |
| Planning | Must comply with **TPO**; s.12A or s.16 if RCHE is not always permitted and/or **exceeds** OZP PR/GFA/BH |
| BO GFA | RCHE in private development is **GFA-accountable** under B(P)R |
| BA during Pilot | Prepared to grant **modification** for **higher PR/GFA** to accommodate the **additional RCHE GFA**, taking PlanD’s advice on OZP/planning-approval compliance when processing GBP |

**SD takeaway:** Only a **new** (non-industrial) scheme gets this; RCHE GFA is still BO-accountable unless BA grants the Pilot **PR/GFA modification** — start TPB if the OZP envelope is exceeded.

---

### 2. Concession cap and lease locks (para 3)

| Condition | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| SWD | Provision **must** have SWD support; **size and location of each** RCHE approved by SWD |
| GFA cap | **≤ 12,000 m²** total RCHE GFA **or 10%** of total permissible GFA under lease, **whichever is the greater** |
| How many RCHEs | **More than one** eligible RCHE per site may take the Concessions |
| Alienation | **No sub-division**; assignment, mortgage, underletting or other disposal **only as a whole unit** |
| Operation | Throughout the lease term, managed/operated as **licensed RCHE(s)** under Cap. **459** and subsidiary legislation, to SWD’s satisfaction |
| Lease | Above requirements **stipulated as lease conditions** as appropriate |
| After completion (para 4) | Developer may **lease or sell each RCHE as a whole unit** or **operate** it; unauthorised change of use or vacancy **> 12 months** (as identified by SWD) may trigger lease enforcement including **re-entry** and Government taking possession **without compensation** |

**SD takeaway:** Size the RCHE package to **max(12,000 m², 10% of lease GFA)**, get **SWD** to sign off location/size, and keep each RCHE as one **unsplittable** whole-floor (or whole-premises) unit — vacancy over **12 months** is an enforcement risk.

---

### 3. How RCHE GFA is measured for this PN (Annex)

**Included:** (a) RCHE(s) including **enclosing walls** and structures within the premises; (b) staircases, lift shafts, lobbies and corridors **solely serving** the RCHE(s), including horizontal areas of those stairs/lifts **passing through the RCHE floor** and serving RCHE **only** at that floor.

**Not included:** horizontal areas of stairs, lifts, lobbies and corridors **shared with other uses on the same floor**.

| Remark | Lock |
|---|---|
| Cap. 459 licensing floor space | Enclosing walls and internal structures are **excluded** from area-per-resident (different from this GFA) |
| Plant / parking / LUL | May be disregarded under the prevailing GFA-concession mechanism **except** remark (4) |
| Non-mandatory/non-essential plant rooms and vertical-duct horizontal areas **solely serving** RCHE | **Not** subject to the **10%** overall GFA-concession cap under **PNAP APP-151** |
| Authority | PlanD and LandsD rely on **BD’s verified GFA** on the GBP |
| Case-by-case | Whether an area is in or out of RCHE GFA is decided case by case |

**SD takeaway:** Give RCHE its **own** lifts/stairs/corridor if you want those areas inside the concession GFA; shared-floor cores stay out — and RCHE-only plant/ducts sit **outside** APP-151’s 10% cap.
