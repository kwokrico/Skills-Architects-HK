# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2011 — Standard Premium Rates for Balcony, UP and NSPE Wall
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 March 2011 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates **w.e.f. 1 April 2011** | Supplements LAO PNs 3/2001 (+ 3/2001A, 6/2001), 6/2002, 3/2003 | Supersedes rates in LAO PN 2/2010 from April 2011

> Scope note: **Non-negotiable standard premium rates** (by DLO district) for lease exemption of **balcony, utility platform (UP) and non-structural prefabricated external wall (NSPE wall)** GFA/SC under JPN 1 and 2. Rates at **date of assessment**, not date of application. Later PNs (e.g. 4/2023, 3/2024, 6/2025) **update the money** — use this CS only for the 2011 rate structure and JPN caps then in force.

## Regulatory Overview

From April 2011, Appendices I–III replace the 2/2010 rates for the **October 2004 JPN 1** / **February 2006 JPN 2** versions; Appendices IV–VI give rates for the **January 2011** JPN 1 and 2. After the January 2011 JPNs: exempt **not more than 50%** of balcony area and **50%** of UP area, **max 3 m²/flat** (balcony) and **0.75 m²/flat** (UP); NSPE wall max thickness **150 mm** under the 2011 JPN 2 (300 mm under the 2006 version).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. JPN 2011 exemption caps used for premium (para. 3)

| Feature | Cap used with Apps. IV–VI |
|---|---|
| Balcony | ≤ **50%** of balcony area exempt; **max 3 m² per flat** |
| Utility platform | ≤ **50%** of UP area exempt; **max 0.75 m² per flat** |
| NSPE wall | Max exempt thickness **150 mm** (Jan 2011 JPN 2). **>150 mm** GFA-accountable. Under Feb 2006 JPN 2, **>300 mm** accountable |

**SD takeaway:** Size balcony/UP so the **exempt slice** is the 50% / 3 m² / 0.75 m² cap — premium is charged on that exempted area, not the full terrace.

### 2. How balcony premium is assessed (para. 3)

Same as PN 3/2001 para. 4: round exempted balcony floor space to **nearest 0.1 m²**. If claimed exempt area **<** JPN permissible, and permissible **> 1.29 m²**, premium is **pro-rata**.

Worked examples in the PN (HK Island lot):

| Case | Claimed exempt (m²) | JPN permissible (m²) | Method | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.1 | 1.2 | Not pro-rata | $20,900 |
| 2 | 1.1 | 1.3 | $41,500 × 1.1/1.3 | $35,115.39 |
| 3 | 1.4 | (implied 2.0) | $81,200 × 1.4/2 | $56,840 |

**SD takeaway:** Claiming less than the JPN max does **not** always cheapen premium 1:1 — below 1.29 m² permissible the PN uses a different (non-pro-rata) rate band.

### 3. Rate tables and other locks (paras. 4–5 + Apps.)

| Lock | Detail |
|---|---|
| Which rates | Those **prevailing at assessment**, irrespective of application date |
| Negotiable? | **No**; subject to review |
| Districts | Sha Tin/Sai Kung; Tai Po/North; Tuen Mun/Yuen Long; Kwai Tsing/Tsuen Wan; HK/KLN; Islands. **Straddling lots → higher rate** |
| Mosaic cladding | Mosaic/similar tile finish **not** in premium; **curtain-wall-type cladding** on structural walls is GFA-countable unless modified + premium |
| Average flat size (NSPE) | Total residential GFA / total units; bands **not exceeding / exceeding 104 m²** |
| Majority thickness | If several NSPE thicknesses on approved plans, **majority** applies |

Full dollar schedules are in Apps. I–VI of `2011_2.pdf` (OCR). Do not copy stale 2011 dollars into a live premium — check the current LAO PN chain.

**SD takeaway:** District + JPN version (2004/2006 vs Jan 2011) pick the appendix; **current** LandsD rate PN overrides these figures.
