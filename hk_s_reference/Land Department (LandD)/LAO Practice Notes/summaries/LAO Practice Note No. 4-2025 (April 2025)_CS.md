# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2025 — Standard Rates for NT Agricultural Land Exchange (outside NDAs)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 April 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates effective **1 April 2025** | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 11/2023A**

> Scope note: **Rate and Option Form update** for the pilot scheme charging land premium at **standard rates** on lease modification / land exchange for **agricultural land in the New Territories outside NDAs**. It is **not** an NDA land-exchange PN and **not** a BO GFA code. Parent processing rules in **PN 11/2023A** (and **PN 11/2023** para 4 zones) otherwise remain valid.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From **1 April 2025**, standard rates for those exchanges follow the **zones in paragraph 4 of LAO PN 11/2023** where the lot(s) sit (agricultural-use rates **per site area**; all other listed rates **per GFA**). The Option Form in PN 11/2023A Appendix I is **replaced** by Appendix I to this PN.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How zones and uses are applied (para 2 notes)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Overlapping zones | **Higher** rates apply |
| Site partly in / partly out of a zone | **Whole site** eligible for standard rates; LandsD’s rates for the out-of-zone part are **final** |
| Site straddling two zones/sub-zones | Respective rates applied to **GFA after exchange on a pro-rata** of site area in each zone/sub-zone, assuming GFA **evenly distributed** |
| After-exchange **residential** rate | All GFA **not prohibiting residential use** |
| After-exchange **non-residential** rate | Remainder GFA; **not** applicable to **columbarium, petrol filling station, dangerous-goods storage, or offensive trades**; **hotel and ancillary** = non-residential |
| Before-exchange non-residential | Includes commercial and industrial; LandsD’s classification is **final** |
| New town (**#**) | OZP new town **plus fringe generally within 1 km** of its railway station(s); LandsD’s 1 km call is **final** |
| Railway catchment | Generally within **1 km** of the relevant station; LandsD’s 1 km call is **final** |
| Option | Irreversible on **PBTO** acceptance; **no premium appeal** at BBTO; fees **forfeited** if the applicant then drops the deal; completed standard-rate deals **published** on LandsD’s website |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **zone (and sub-zone)** and **residential vs non-residential GFA split** before opting — straddling sites get **pro-rata GFA**, overlapping zones take the **higher** rate, and hotel GFA is **non-residential**.
