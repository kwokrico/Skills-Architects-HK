# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2017 — Building Plans on Virtually Unrestricted Leases
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 April 2017 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department

> Scope note: **Simplified LandsD “no objection”** for GBP on a **virtually unrestricted** Government lease (no development/user restrictions other than **offensive trades**). It is **not** a BO concession PN and **does not** apply if ROW, rate-and-range, DDH/DD, or LandsD plan-approval clauses exist — those stay on the **8-week** PN 5/2002 path.

## Regulatory Overview

BD still refers plans via PNAP ADM-2. LandsD will issue a **no-objection letter** if: (a) the lease is virtually unrestricted (Director’s decision **final**); (b) **no encroachment** onto Government land; (c) proposed uses do not breach the **offensive-trade** restriction (or any offensive-trade **licence**). AP lodges Appendix II form + full lease pack at **first submission**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What “virtually unrestricted” means (para. 2 + fn. 1, para. 6)

| In | Out (usual processing; simplified approach **off**) |
|---|---|
| Lease with **no** specific development/user restrictions other than offensive trades (sample: App. I) | **Rights of way** clause |
| Offensive-trade clause may exist — see LAO PN **6/2007** | **Rate and range** clause |
| | **DDH or DD** clause |
| | Requirement for **Director’s approval of building plans** / erection other than existing buildings |
| | Site with **>1 lot or section** where **any** lease is **not** virtually unrestricted |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm the lease is **truly bare** (plus offensive trades only) before assuming a LandsD no-objection shortcut — one DDH or ROW clause kills it.

### 2. AP pack (paras. 3–5, Apps. II–III)

| Need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Form App. II Parts I–III | Virtually unrestricted declaration; offensive-trade licence compliance; **full certified lease set** (modifications, licences) + ownership printouts — certified by LR or solicitor **within 3 months** of submission |
| Parts IV–V | Confirm **no encroachment** on Government land (exception: architectural/amenity projections needing BD **and** LandsD approval) |
| Development schedule | Uses **per floor**; sample App. III (adapted from APSRSE **1/94**). Shaded fields (height SC, etc.) **not required** / may be deleted if lease is virtually unrestricted |
| ALS | If using land survey as site-parameter proof (PNAP ADV-33): ALS name, plan, report, record plan, SMF-0043 already at BD |

**SD takeaway:** Put a **floor-by-floor user schedule** on the drawings at first GBP — LandsD’s no-objection rests on it plus the certified lease bundle.

### 3. Street widening / voluntary surrender (para. 8)

If part of the lot is needed for **street widening**, AP may propose **voluntary surrender** in exchange for B(P)R **22(2)** concessions (PNAP APP-20). Show it on plans **and** Development Schedule; approach LandsD **Acquisition Section**. No-objection still needs para. 2 **plus** Acquisition Section / department acceptance of the surrender.

**SD takeaway:** Road-widening concessions on these lots are a **parallel Acquisition** track — do not assume no-objection includes the surrender.

### 4. Timing if simplified approach does not apply (para. 7)

Non-BCIII GBP under lease remains **8 weeks** per LAO PN **5/2002**.

**SD takeaway:** Mixed lots (one restricted lease in the assemblage) → full 8-week LandsD check.
