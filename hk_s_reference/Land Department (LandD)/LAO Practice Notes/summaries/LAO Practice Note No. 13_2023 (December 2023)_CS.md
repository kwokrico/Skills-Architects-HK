# LAO Practice Note No. 13/2023 — New Arrangements for Applications for Lease Modification including Land Exchange under the Enhanced Conventional New Town Approach
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 December 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect for designated-site broadening, 90% ownership option, and occupant Government-package option

> Scope note: Application rules for **ECNTA land exchange** in **all NDAs** — not a GFA/BO code. Sites must be **Government-designated** for private development. If exchange fails, Government resumes and disposes (e.g. public tender). Standard-rate premium is offered as an option; rates come in **separate PNs** per NDA.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Under ECNTA the Government generally resumes all private land in an NDA, but before resumption it allows lease modification including land exchange from lot owners of **designated development sites** if prescribed criteria are met. This PN extends ECNTA beyond KTN/FLN to **all NDAs** and sets general application requirements. LandsD acting as landlord may approve or reject any application.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What sites and uses (paras 3–6, Appendix I)

| Lock | Detail |
|---|---|
| Where | Only **designated development sites** earmarked for private developments in an NDA, announced by LandsD (plans on LandsD website). Timing varies by NDA |
| Uses now included | Not only **residential and commercial** — also **industries** and **privately-run community or welfare facilities**, as Government considers appropriate |
| After-use | As permitted under the statutory town plan(s) or TPB, or as advised by Government |
| Premium | Standard rates offered as an option (announced March 2022); **applicable rates per NDA** via separate PNs when applications are invited |
| Public facilities | Under certain circumstances (including the **90% ownership** option below), the grant may require the applicant to **construct public facilities** in the NDA (e.g. PTI, roads, open space, welfare/recreational). Construction cost **may** be premium-deductible if Government so decides. Standards and cost: relevant departments, **final** |
| Publication | LandsD publishes accepted applications (including a plan of the private lots); occupants are notified |

Appendix I general criteria: location inside a designated site; **no** surrender of lots **outside** the designated site (normally); **minimum size** (varies by site/NDA); single owner or joint venture; reasonably **regular**, **contiguous** (fragmented lots not accepted); OZP/ODP compliance; **proper vehicular access** feasible; no insurmountable planning/layout/urban-design harm and no jeopardy to adjacent lots' development potential or access. Applications meeting only the **minimum size** must submit a **layout plan** showing decent, practical development **fully utilising** the designated site **without loss of planned development potential**.

**SD takeaway:** Design to the **full designated-site** envelope (not just lots you own) and prove access and no leftover undevelopable fragments — a minimum-size application needs a layout that consumes the **whole** designated site.

### 2. Relaxed 90% ownership and 2-stage grant (paras 7–10)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Default | Minimum surrendered size (Appendix I para 3) plus other criteria |
| **Relaxed ownership** | If the applicant has acquired **≥ 90%** of **all private land** within a designated development site, may opt for Government to **resume the remaining private land** and grant the **entire** designated site (Government's absolute discretion) |
| Price of that option | Applicant **must construct public facilities** in the NDA (para 6) |
| Stage one | Surrender applicant's private land (+ Government land if any) → **Initial Land Grant** of a new lot; pay **Initial Premium** = pro-rata current market value of the **whole** designated site at BBTO. On accepting that BBTO, sign an Agreement to take up the remainder after resumption, and pay an **upfront deposit** = difference between whole-site CMV and Initial Premium |
| Stage two | After resumption, grant of resumed land **or** surrender of the Initial lot for a **Complete Land Grant**; **Top-Up Premium** or **partial refund** of deposit (no interest, within **30 days** of Complete Land Grant completion) to reflect the resumed land's pro-rata CMV at that BBTO. Top-up payable on accepting the Complete Land Grant BBTO |

**SD takeaway:** At **≥ 90%** assembled area you can design the **entire** designated site, but programme a **two-title** grant, an upfront deposit, and **public facilities** as a lease obligation.

### 3. Occupants, vacant possession, time limit (paras 11–15, Appendix II)

| Requirement | Lock |
|---|---|
| Comparable package | All eligible occupants (occupying as at LandsD **pre-clearance survey** or thereafter) must accept applicant offers **comparable** to Government NDA EGA (cash) or **non-means-tested rehousing** (in kind or cash-equivalent). Applicant's package is **not** premium-deductible |
| Occupant may choose Government | Eligible occupants may opt for **Government** compensation/rehousing. Applicant **reimburses** Government on a cost-recovery basis: compensation paid and/or cash value of rehousing based on **market value of land + construction cost of a comparable unit**, plus admin cost. Costing basis: a **non-means-tested subsidised sale flat in a dedicated rehousing estate**, **regardless** of the occupant's actual choice. Reimburse **with the land premium** |
| Before BBTO | Prove acceptances **or** give a LandsD-satisfactory **Undertaking** per hold-out occupant: keep offers open **3 years from execution**; reimburse if they later take Government's package; **deposit** with Government an amount equal to those Government costs. Deposit deductible if they accept Government's package (including **after** execution, within 3 years) |
| Release of Undertaking/deposit (no interest) | Occupant accepted applicant's comparable offer; **or** Government package fully reimbursed; **or** **3 years** from execution |
| Vacant possession | Documentary evidence that actual vacant possession **has been or will be** secured by a reasonable timing, before BBTO. Lots to be surrendered **free from encumbrances** before execution |
| PBTO is not enough | Exchange **not processed further** after PBTO until para 13 is met |
| Deadlines | Application-submission deadline **and** BBTO-acceptance deadline set by reference to Government's **resumption programme** — **final, non-negotiable**. Late applications not considered. If BBTO (with premium) cannot be issued and accepted by the deadline, processing **ceases**; admin fee **not refunded** |

Appendix II also: Government land adjoining/intervening the private land is included in the regrant only if **incapable of reasonable separate alienation or development** — Government will **not** normally add separately alienable GL just to fill the designated-site boundary.

**SD takeaway:** Programme **vacant possession and occupant settlements before BBTO**, and treat the NDA **resumption date** as a hard stop on the exchange — leftover Government land inside the designated site may **not** be in your lot.
