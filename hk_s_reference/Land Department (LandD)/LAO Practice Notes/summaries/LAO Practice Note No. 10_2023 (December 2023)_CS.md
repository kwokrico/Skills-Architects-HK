# LAO Practice Note No. 10/2023 — Incentive Scheme to Encourage Provision of Residential Care Homes for Persons with Disabilities in New Private Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 December 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Pilot Period: **20 December 2023 – 19 December 2026**

> Scope note: Premium and (during the Pilot Period) **lease GFA** concessions for **SWD-supported RCHD** in **new** private developments. **Industrial** developments are excluded. Addition, alteration or conversion of **existing** buildings is **not** a new private development. RCHD remains **GFA-accountable under B(P)R**; BA may grant modification for higher PR/GFA during the Pilot Period.

---

## Regulatory Overview

A time-limited pilot (the Pilot Period) lets LandsD grant "Time-Limited Concessions" on applications received **during** that window; applications after 19 December 2026 are not considered for those concessions. After the Pilot Period, a smaller premium-only Concession remains. Eligibility requires **Social Welfare Department** support. Planning application under TPO s.12A or s.16 is required if RCHD is not always permitted and/or exceeds OZP PR/GFA/BH.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Quantum of concession (paras 2–4)

| Period | Premium | Lease GFA |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** (applications **20 Dec 2023 – 19 Dec 2026**) | Exempt **one or more** eligible RCHD premises, total GFA **≤ 12,000 m² or 10% of total permissible GFA under lease, whichever is the greater**, per development project, from land premium on lease modifications, land exchanges and PTGs ("Land Transactions") | **Exempt** eligible RCHD premises from **total permissible GFA under lease** |
| **After 19 Dec 2026** | Exempt **one** eligible RCHD premises, GFA **≤ 5,400 m²** per development project, from land premium | **No** lease-GFA exemption |

**SD takeaway:** To bank both premium **and** extra lease GFA, lodge the Land Transaction application **inside the Pilot Period** and size RCHD to **max(12,000 m², 10% of lease GFA)**; after the pilot you get premium relief only, cap **5,400 m²**, and RCHD eats the lease GFA budget.

---

### 2. Eligibility locks (paras 5–6)

| Condition | Lock |
|---|---|
| SWD | Size and **location** of RCHD premises approved by SWD |
| Alienation | **No sub-division**; no assignment, mortgage, underletting or other disposal **except as a whole unit** |
| Operation | Throughout the lease term, managed and operated as **licensed RCHD(s)** under the Residential Care Homes (Persons with Disabilities) Ordinance (**Cap. 613**) and subsidiary legislation, to SWD's satisfaction |
| Developer choice | Subject to SWD approval: **lease or sell** each completed RCHD as a whole unit, **or** self-operate |
| Vacancy / change of use | Unauthorised change of use, or vacant **> 12 months** as identified by SWD, may trigger lease enforcement including **re-entry** and Government taking possession **without compensation** |

These requirements are to be stipulated as lease conditions as appropriate. Also see SWD Guidance Note (December 2023).

**SD takeaway:** Plan RCHD as a **single undivided premises** (location fixed with SWD) with a 12-month vacancy tripwire — not a subdivisible commercial floor.

---

### 3. How RCHD GFA is measured (Annex)

GFA of eligible RCHD includes:

| Included | Excluded |
|---|---|
| Areas of RCHD(s) including **enclosing walls** and structures within the premises | Horizontal areas of stairs, lift shafts, lobbies and corridors **shared** with other uses on the same floor |
| Stairs, lift shafts, lobbies and corridors **solely** serving the RCHD(s), including their horizontal areas on the RCHD floor where they serve RCHD only | — |

| Remark | Design lock |
|---|---|
| Cap. 613 licensing | Enclosing walls and internal structures are **excluded** from floor space **per resident** (different from this GFA) |
| Plant / parking / LUL | May be disregarded under the prevailing GFA-concession mechanism, **except** remark (4) |
| **Non-mandatory / non-essential plant rooms** and horizontal areas of **vertical ducts solely serving the RCHD** | **Not** subject to the **10% overall cap** of GFA concession under **PNAP APP-151** |
| Authority | PlanD and LandsD rely on **BD's verified GFA** on the GBPs |

**SD takeaway:** Give RCHD **dedicated** stairs/lifts/corridor if you want that circulation inside the concession GFA; shared mall circulation stays outside RCHD GFA. Sole-serving non-mandatory plant is **outside** the APP-151 10% cap.
