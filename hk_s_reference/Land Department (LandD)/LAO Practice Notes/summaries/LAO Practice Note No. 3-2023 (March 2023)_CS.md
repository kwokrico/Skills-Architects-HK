# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2023 — Offensive trades licences for specified food businesses
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supersedes LAO PN No. 3/2021 as varied by PN No. 1/2023

> Scope note: How old-lease **offensive trades** clauses interact with **hotel/food** users. Streamlines **deemed OT Licences** for eleven FEHD/LLB food-and-liquor licences. It is **not** a BO use code, **not** a DMC waiver, and **does not** permit hotel via the deemed route.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Many old Government leases ban named trades (including sugar-baker, oilman, butcher, victualler, tavern-keeper — “the five trades”) without an OT Licence. From the date of this PN, specified statutory food/liquor licences can **deem** an OT Licence when three criteria are met; applied-for licences remain available for whole-lot and unit cases.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. OT Licence on application — whole lot (paras 2–3, 5–6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Five trades unlocked | Sugar baker, oilman, butcher, victualler, tavern-keeper |
| **Hotel** (tavern-keeper lifted) | OT Licence will **restrict sale of any hotel except as a whole** and **letting of hotel rooms for not more than one year**; **delineate hotel portion on building plans** |
| Fee | **$32,600 per lot** (subject to review); LandsD aims to offer within **two months** for straightforward cases |
| Title printout | LR- or solicitor-certified, **not more than one month** old; hard or pdf |

**SD takeaway:** A hotel on an old OT-clause lot needs an **applied-for** OT Licence plus a **plan-delineated hotel portion** and whole-building sale / ≤1-year letting locks — not the deemed-licence stream.

---

### 2. Multi-ownership / single units (para 4)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Coverage | One or more units; registered owner of the premises |
| Hotel | OT Licence will **prohibit hotel use** of those premises |
| Fee | Same administrative fee as para 3(ii) |

**SD takeaway:** Unit-level F&B can be licensed; do not draw a hotel user on a unit OT Licence.

---

### 3. Deemed OT Licence for eleven statutory licences (paras 7–9)

Takes effect from this PN’s date. Deemed grant to the **owner** when **all three Criteria** are met:

| Criterion | Lock |
|---|---|
| (a) | Lot/premises **not** already covered by an OT Licence |
| (b) | Proposed use falls within one or more of the **five trades** |
| (c) | Lot/premises is **not part of a hotel development** and remains so during the statutory licence term |

**Statutory Licences (any one or more):**

| FEHD / LLB licence |
|---|
| General Restaurant; Light Refreshment Restaurant; Bakery; Food Factory; Fresh Provision Shop; Frozen Confection Factory; Milk Factory; Siu Mei and Lo Mei Shop; Composite Food Shop; Liquor Licence; Club Liquor Licence |

| Term | Lock |
|---|---|
| Duration | **Co-terminus** with the relevant Statutory Licence(s) |
| Auto-end | When all such licences expire/cancel, **or** any Criterion fails → full offensive-trades clause revives |

**SD takeaway:** Restaurant/bakery/FPS (etc.) on a non-hotel old-lease lot generally needs **no separate LandsD OT application** once the FEHD/LLB licence is granted; hotel and mixed hotel-podium F&B still need the applied-for route.

---

### 4. What an OT Licence does **not** override (paras 6, 12)

OT Licence (issued or deemed) does not displace other lease covenants, the Buildings Ordinance, the Town Planning Ordinance, other legislation, or **DMC** rights.

**SD takeaway:** Check OZP user, DMC, and BO restaurant fit-out independently of the OT stream.
