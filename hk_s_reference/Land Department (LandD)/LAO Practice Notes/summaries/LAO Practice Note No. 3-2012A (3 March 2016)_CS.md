# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2012A — Data Centre Applications after 31 March 2016
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 March 2016 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Varies and supplements LAO PN 3/2012 **from 1 April 2016**

> Scope note: **Extends the application window** for (a) lease modification / land exchange of an industrial lot for **high-tier data centre**, or (b) **waiver** for data-centre use in **part of** an industrial building, **after 31 March 2016 until further notice**. All other 3/2012 provisions remain. It is **not** the technical definition of “high-tier data centre” — that stays in PN 3/2012 / 3/2012B.

## Regulatory Overview

Without this PN, the 3/2012 scheme would have stopped taking applications after 31 March 2016. Caption (b) waiver processing is to be **streamlined toward about two weeks** from a valid application; details to follow by later amendment (delivered in **3/2012B**).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Window and status of 3/2012 (paras. 1–2)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Effect | From **1 April 2016** |
| Applications | May be made **after 31 March 2016 until further Government notice** |
| Parent | Except as varied here, **PN 3/2012 remains valid**; together = “the Practice Notes” |
| Waiver (caption b) | Aim: approval of acceptable cases **around two weeks** from valid application (details later) |

**SD takeaway:** Post-March 2016 IB data-centre conversion still uses the **3/2012 family** (now 3/2012 + 3/2012A + 3/2012B) — do not treat 31 March 2016 as a hard stop.
