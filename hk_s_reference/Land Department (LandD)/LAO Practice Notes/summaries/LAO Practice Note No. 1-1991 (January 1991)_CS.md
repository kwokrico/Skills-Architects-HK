# LAO Practice Note No. 1/1991 — Master Layout Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
January 1991 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
17 January 1991 | Director of Buildings and Lands (A. G. Eason)

> Scope note: Defines **why** Conditions of Sale require a Master Layout / Concept Plan on large-scale developments and **what information** the MLP must carry. It is **not** a Buildings Ordinance plan code and does **not** set GFA, SC, or parking numbers — those stay in the Conditions of Sale.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Conditions of Sale for large-scale developments usually require submission of Master Layout / Concept Plans so Government can accept the nature and layout of the scheme, the site is developed in an orderly composite manner, and the developer can get an early indication that the general nature and proposed layout are acceptable. All such plans (Master Plan, Layout Plan, Preliminary Concept Plan, Layout and Master Plan, General Layout Plan) will be called **Master Layout Plans** in future Conditions of Sale.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. MLP contents under Conditions of Sale (paras 1–3)

Information is specified in the Conditions of Sale and will usually include:

| Item | What the MLP must show |
|---|---|
| Site / roads | Formation levels; position, widths and levels of proposed roads, emergency accesses, footbridges and pedestrian ways |
| Buildings | Nature, **height** and **GFA**; disposition, location and distribution |
| Access | Vehicular ingress and egress |
| Parking | Indication of locations for parking of vehicles |
| Open space / landscape | Communal open space; indication of landscaping and recreational facilities |
| Phasing | Stages or phases of the development |

A sample MLP is attached at Appendix I (not in this extract).

**SD takeaway:** Freeze formation levels, road/EVA/footbridge geometry, building massing (height + GFA + disposition), ingress/egress, parking locations, communal OS/landscape, and phasing on the MLP — that is the set Lands will treat as the “general nature and layout.”
