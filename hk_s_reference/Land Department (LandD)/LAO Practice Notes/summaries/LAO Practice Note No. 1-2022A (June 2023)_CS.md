# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2022A — KTN/FLN Remaining Phase land exchange deadline
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
June 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies and supplements LAO PN No. 1/2022

> Scope note: Moves the **binding basic terms offer (with premium)** cut-off for accepted Kwu Tung North / Fanling North Remaining Phase land-exchange applications. It is **not** a new application window and does **not** change OZP/ODP design parameters in PN 1/2022.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN varies only paragraph 13 of LAO PN No. 1/2022. All other provisions of PN 1/2022 remain valid.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Processing cut-off (para 2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Varied para 13 wording | Any **accepted** application **ceases to be processed** if a binding basic terms offer (with premium) cannot be **issued and accepted on or before 31 December 2023** |
| Unchanged | Remainder of PN 1/2022 |

**SD takeaway:** Treat 31 December 2023 as the hard BBTO (with premium) deadline for Remaining Phase KTN/FLN exchanges already accepted under PN 1/2022 — massing and GA packages must be frozen in time for that offer.
