# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2004 — Site Coverage Control under the Lease Condition
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 March 2004 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L C Lau)

> Scope note: How LandsD will process developments that use **BD PNAP 280** flexibility on **site coverage and open space** where the **lease** also restricts SC / built-over / roofed-over / covered area. It is **not** PNAP 280 itself — BA still decides the BO SC exemption; LandsD charges **premium** for the lease-side relaxation.

---

## Regulatory Overview

LandsD will process applications that follow PNAP 280 on a **two-stage** path for existing leases with SC-type restrictions (insert the Director’s BA-exclusion discretion if missing, then premium on the approved GBP). New leases will include a similar discretionary clause so the purchaser may opt into PNAP 280 SC, still paying premium and admin fee case by case.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing leases with SC / built-over / roofed-over / covered-area restriction (A)

**Stage 1 — flexibility clause**

| Lease already has… | Action |
|---|---|
| Discretion: Director **may exclude** floor space or structures **excluded by BA** from SC under the BO, **subject to premium and admin fee** determined by the Director | **No** lease modification |
| **No** such clause | Modify the lease to add a similar clause allowing the Director to issue **consent letters**; pay **admin fee only** at this stage |

**Stage 2 — premium after BA approval**

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | When GBP based on the PNAP 280 SC/OS flexibility application is **approved by BD** |
| Submit to DLO | Approved GBP + letter stating clearly the **increase in SC** and other relevant PNAP 280 exemption information |
| Premium | **Case by case** on **enhancement in land value** from the SC relaxation |
| Consent | Issued after **admin fee + assessed premium** are settled |
| Later **increase** in SC | Subsequent amendment plans need Director’s approval; Director may impose conditions including **additional premium and admin fee** |
| Later **reduction** vs approved SC | **No refund** of paid premium |
| Admin fee on consents | First consent after Stage 1 modification: **no** admin fee; **further** consent letters: **fresh** admin fee |

**SD takeaway:** Programme Stage 1 modification (if the lease lacks the BA-exclusion discretion) **before** relying on PNAP 280 SC, then freeze the **approved SC** on the GBP — a later increase is a new consent; a later decrease is not refunded.

---

### 2. New leases (B) and fees (para 3)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| New leases with SC control | Discretionary clause similar to Stage 1 will be included so the grantee/purchaser **may** develop within PNAP 280 scope, **subject to premium and admin fee** case by case |
| If using PNAP 280 | Submit **approved GBP** to DLO as in Stage 2 |
| Admin fee (this PN) | **$23,000** (subject to annual review) for **either** lease modification **or** issue of consent letter |

Consent letter (Appendix I) locks SC / built-over / roofed-over / covered area to the **BA-approved drawing number and date**. Register within **1 calendar month**; photocopy within **3 calendar months**.

**SD takeaway:** On a new grant, PNAP 280 SC is an **option with a premium**, not a free lease right — the consent will cap SC at the specific approved GBP.
