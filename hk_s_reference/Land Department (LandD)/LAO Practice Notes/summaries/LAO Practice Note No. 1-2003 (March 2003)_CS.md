# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2003 — Transfer of Commitment Transfer Registers and Notifications for Land Exchange Entitlements (Letters A/B)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 March 2003 | Departmental Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L.C. LAU)

> Scope note: Administrative transfer of **Letters A/B** Commitment Transfer Registers (CTRs) and Notifications from the Land Registry to LandsD from **1 April 2003**. It is **not** a building-design, GFA, or lease-user instrument.

---

## Regulatory Overview

To give Letters A/B owners a one-stop service, Land Registry maintenance and storage of CTRs and Notifications for Land Exchange Entitlements transfer to Lands Department from **1 April 2003**. Search at the seven NT Land Registries was suspended **17–31 March 2003**; from 1 April 2003 records are centralised at LandsD Headquarters for search at the CTR Unit.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where to search and who may inspect (paras 2–4)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Search (from 1 April 2003) | CTR Unit, **21/F, North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road, North Point** |
| New notifications | Send to the CTR Unit at that address for registration |
| Who may inspect | **Only** the LEE owner or authorised agent, for CTRs/Notifications registered in **that owner’s name**; copies / certified copies on payment of fees |
| Third parties | Inspection or copies considered only on **written justification**; Director may refuse |

Enquiries (this PN): Customer Relations Manager Mr. Y C LI **2231 3233**; Assistant CRM Mr. Gary Lau **2231 3290**.

**SD takeaway:** This PN does not lock schematic design — if the project relies on **Letters A/B**, search and register notifications at LandsD’s North Point CTR Unit from 1 April 2003, not the NT Land Registries.
