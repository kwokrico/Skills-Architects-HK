# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2021 — Approval or Consent under Lease
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 December 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department

> Scope note: Classifies when LandsD **premium** is / is not charged for lease **approval or consent**, and which items can be granted at **GBP** checking. It is **not** a BO plan code and **does not apply** to Cap. 121–exempted buildings. Does not fetter the Director’s lease discretion.

---

## Regulatory Overview

A Government lease (including Conditions of Sale / Grant / Exchange) may require the lessor’s prior approval or consent; the Director may attach terms including premium. Premium is generally **not** charged where the consent does not relax lease restrictions, or (Part II of Appendix A) where it does relax them but the proposal is acceptable to the concerned bureau(x)/department(s); typical premium-chargeable cases are listed in Appendix B.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When premium is / is not charged (paras 1–4)

| Situation | Premium |
|---|---|
| Consent **pursuant to** lease clauses, **no** relaxation of requirements/restrictions | **No** premium — Appendix A **Part (I)** |
| Consent that **relaxes** lease requirements/restrictions | Director’s **absolute discretion**; premium **may** be charged if consent would **enhance development potential or land value** |
| Listed relaxations in Appendix A **Part (II)** | Premium **may not** be charged **if** the proposal is acceptable to the concerned B/D(s) |
| Typical premium cases | Appendix **B** (general reference) |

**SD takeaway:** Map every SD departure from lease (VAP, NBA/setback, parking numbers, MLP, GFA exclusions) to Appendix A vs B **before** locking the GBP — that is the premium path.

---

### 2. GBP-stage grant and 16-week premium track (paras 5–6; fn 2; PN 4/2018)

| Track | What | Timing / output |
|---|---|---|
| Streamlined (no separate application) | Appendix A **Part (I) specified clauses** + **all** Part (II) clauses | Generally assessed/granted at **GBP checking** |
| Fast-track **premium** | Specified Appendix **B** situations (marked **#**) | LandsD aims to advise premium within **16 weeks** of a **valid** application |
| After premium + admin fee paid | Consent letter **with** GBP approval | Permits development per the agreed GBP for the **lifetime of the building(s)** |
| Owner option | Apply for **lease modification** instead of lifetime approval/consent | Established modification mechanism |
| Lifetime of consent | Approval/consent under specific lease provision(s) | Effective for **lifetime of the building(s)** for which the GBP was approved (fn 2) |

**SD takeaway:** Put Part (I)/(II) items on the first GBP so consent rides the plan check; budget **16 weeks** + admin fee if any Appendix B **#** item is in the scheme.

---

### 3. Appendix A Part (I) — no-premium, no-relaxation consents

Typical clauses (no relaxation of lease requirements):

| Item | Clause type |
|---|---|
| 1 | Design and disposition (or design, disposition **and height**) |
| 2 | Recreational facilities |
| 3 | Office accommodation for watchmen and caretakers |
| 4 | Owners’ corporation / owners’ committee office |
| 5 | Quarters for watchmen and caretakers |
| 6 | Diversion/relocation of existing water mains, sewers, pipeworks, drainage, footpath, etc. **along the diverted route delineated in the lease** |
| 7 | Formation of green / yellow / brown / other **coloured areas** (areas **outside** lot, usually Government land on the lease plan, to be maintained or handed back) — EOT for coloured-area formation is Appendix B Item 8 |
| 8 | Deposit of car park layout plan |
| 9 | Landscaping |
| 10 | Preservation of trees (**except** retrospective consent for unauthorised felling/interference) |
| 11 | Natural terrain |
| 12 | Impact assessments (e.g. NIA) |
| 13 | Offensive trades (see LAO PN **3/2021**) |
| 14 | Provision of sales office and show flats |
| 15 | Restriction on alienation before compliance (**Presale Consent** under LandsD Consent Scheme) |
| 16 | DMC incorporating Management Agreement (if any) |
| 17 | **MLP** approval (including later amendments) submitted **after** lease execution to **comply with** lease — **not** if the amendment varies lease parameters or is an Appendix B Item 5 “control” MLP |

These Part (I) specified clauses **might generally** be assessed/granted at GBP checking.

**SD takeaway:** Recreational GFA, watchmen rooms, OC office, tree/landscape, car-park layout, and post-grant MLP-for-compliance are normally **GBP-stage, nil-premium** — do not treat them as modifications.

### 4. Appendix A Part (II) — relaxation, generally nil premium if B/D accepts (GBP-stage)

| Item | Relaxation | Preconditions in this PN |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Vehicular access | Vary or **widen** VAP along the **same street** | Ground level of the lot **not** permitted for commercial purposes; **does not include addition** of a VAP |
| 2 Noise barriers | Barriers within the lot **projecting beyond lot boundary over Government land** | “Noise Barrier” clause **plus** “NIA” clause in **residential** Conditions of Sale |
| 3 Building separation | Vary **projected façade length** from CoS prescription | **BD** satisfied with the proposal; **JPN 6** for SBD building-separation compliance |
| 4 Disabled persons’ parking | Vary **number and dimension** as BA may require and approve | Generally not premium-chargeable; inclusion of the clause in **old leases** may be by **technical modification** |
| 5 NBA / DRA / WRA / building setback | Listed structures **inside** those areas | Technically acceptable to the proponent department(s); **JPN 6** for SBD setback; noise barriers as required under lease or to implement approved NIA are included under fence/boundary wall |

**At-grade features allowed in NBA/DRA/WRA/setback (Item 5):** planter/curb; open driveway/ramp/road curb/EVA; open pavement/footpath/walkway/steps/stairs; open swimming pool/pool deck/jacuzzi; water feature; smoke vent / floor-type smoke outlet / basement air intake; surface channel / drainage outlets / draw pits.

**Common-use features:** fence/boundary wall (incl. lot-boundary utility cabinets); usual appurtenances (water/electricity/gas meter cabinets); garden fence/planter wall; entrance/metal/drop/crash gates; covered walkway at GF or podium / trellis; outdoor A/C plant; canopy covering **common** areas.

**Safety / other:** FS installations (street hydrant, sprinkler inlet, FS inlet, etc.); parapet of ramping driveways; vertical green features; minor structures holding pipes/drains; doors **outside** NBA/setback but **swinging over** it; temporary/supporting structures during construction.

All Part (II) consents **might generally** be assessed/granted at GBP checking.

**SD takeaway:** You can usually put planters, open EVA/driveways, FS inlets, meter cabinets, and swinging doors in NBA/DRA/WRA/setback **without premium** if the department accepts — **adding** a VAP, commercial-ground VAP moves, or extra parking numbers are **not** this track.

---

### 5. Appendix B — typical premium-chargeable consents

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **#1** | Relax setback-area restriction on the **existing lease plan** under the Building Setback Clause where the **Director** grants consent | **JPN 6** for SBD setback; Item 5 Part (II) structures in NBA/DRA/WRA/setback are **not** this premium item |
| **#2** | Exclude **aboveground ancillary parking** accepted by BD on technical grounds from GFA | — |
| **#3** | Vary **number and dimension** of parking or L/UL required under lease | Contrast disabled-parking variation in Part (II) Item 4 |
| **#4** | Vary VAP **other than** Part (II) Item 1 (same-street widen/vary, no commercial GF) | Includes **adding** a VAP |
| 5 | Amend approved **MLP or control drawings** that the lease **ties** the development to | Contrast Part (I) Item 17 compliance MLP |
| 6 | Cut away on **Government land** under Set Back Clause for **exceptional geotechnical** reasons | — |
| 7 | Divert existing pipework/drainage **or** vary alignment/layout/dimension of NBA, DRA, WRA | Contrast Part (II) Item 5 **structures** (nil premium) |
| 8 | **EOT** for formation of coloured areas if the expiry date **differs** from the Building Covenant | — |

**#** items: generally GBP-stage; LandsD aims to advise premium within **16 weeks** of a valid application.

**SD takeaway:** Aboveground car-park GFA exclusion, extra/fewer parking stalls, extra VAPs, and lease-tied MLP/control-drawing changes are **premium** — lock counts and access points against the lease before GBP.
