# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2003 — Premium Assessment for Exemption of NSPE Walls from GFA and SC for residential developments under JPN 2
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 June 2003 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L C LAU)

> Scope note: Standard **premium unit rates** (Appendix I — **not in this extract**) for lease modification to exempt **non-structural prefabricated external walls** from GFA/SC on **residential** leases that have **no** curtain-wall GFA special condition. It implements **PN 6/2002 para 2(i)**. Rates are **not** for curtain-wall schemes or the **commercial portion** of a composite development.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Where existing lease conditions have no special condition for determination of GFA in buildings with a curtain-wall external face, a lease modification (clause similar to that curtain-wall SC) is required if NSPE walls are to be exempted from GFA/SC. To speed processing and avoid premium negotiation, **non-negotiable** unit rates in Appendix I apply, subject to the conditions below; rates are reviewed annually.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When the standard rates apply (para 2)

| Condition | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use | Premium unit rates apply to lease modification for **residential use only** |
| Plans | Application **must** be accompanied by **BA-approved** building plans. **Not entertained** before BA approval |
| Application letter | State clearly: **average flat size**; **majority thickness** of NSPE wall; **GFA of the NSPE wall exempted** |
| Design change | Material changes that may change the calculated premium → **new** lease modification, **additional premium + admin fee** |
| Rate basis | Per **m²**, depending on **flat size**, **NSPE wall thickness**, and **location** (sample calc at Appendix II — not in this extract) |
| Admin fee | **$23,000** per application (subject to annual review) |
| District | DLO boundaries; straddling lots → **higher** rate |
| Out of scope | Rates **not** applicable to **curtain wall** development or the **commercial portion** of a composite development |

**SD takeaway:** Do not lodge the NSPE-wall mod until BA plans are approved — freeze **average flat size**, **majority wall thickness**, and **exempted NSPE GFA**; keep the commercial podium off this rate table.
