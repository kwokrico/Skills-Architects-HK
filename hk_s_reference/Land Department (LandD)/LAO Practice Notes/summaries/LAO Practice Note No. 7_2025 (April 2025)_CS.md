# LAO Practice Note No. 7/2025 — Standard Rates for Premium Calculation for Exemption of GFA and/or Site Coverage and Relaxation of Building Height Restriction arising from Modular Integrated Construction under Joint Practice Note No. 8
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 April 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Effective **1 April 2025** | Varies/supplements LAO PN Nos. 6/2022 and 4/2024

> Scope note: Replaces the **MiC GFA/SC exemption and BHR-relaxation premium rates** in Appendix I to **PN 4/2024**. It is **not** JPN 8 itself — JPN 8 still governs when BA disregards MiC floor area. Parent PNs remain valid except the rates table.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 April 2025, premium for lease exemption of GFA and/or site coverage and relaxation of building height restriction arising from Modular Integrated Construction (JPN 8) is calculated using Appendix I to this PN instead of Appendix I to PN 4/2024. All other provisions of PN 6/2022 and PN 4/2024 remain valid.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How premium is calculated (Appendix I notes)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Districts | **Hong Kong Island, Kowloon East, Kowloon West, New Territories South, New Territories North** — boundaries follow **Appendix II to LAO PN 4/2024** |
| Straddling lots | **Higher** of the two district rates |
| Rate basis | Unit rate per **exempted MiC floor area (m²)** |
| Quantum | Rate × exempted MiC floor area **approved by the Building Authority** |
| Rounding | Exempted MiC floor area(s) **rounded up to nearest 0.1 m²** |
| Negotiation | Premium determined by LandsD at absolute discretion; **not negotiable** |
| Domestic vs non-domestic | LandsD has absolute discretion on which rate type applies, per nature of development and the lease. If BA-approved GBPs have **not** specified whether exempted MiC floor area is domestic or non-domestic GFA, the **domestic** standard rate is used |

Appendix I also tabulates separate **Domestic** and **Non-Domestic** unit rates ($/m²) for the five districts. The extract’s grid is OCR-scrambled; use `PN 7_2025.pdf` Appendix I for the cell values (rates printed include $7,500, $6,200, $5,600, $4,800, $4,100, $4,000, $3,500, $2,900 and $2,000 per m²).

**SD takeaway:** Freeze BA-approved exempted MiC m² (domestic vs non-domestic tagged on GBP) and the PN 4/2024 district before quoting premium — LandsD will round up to 0.1 m² and default to the **domestic** rate if the GBP is silent.
