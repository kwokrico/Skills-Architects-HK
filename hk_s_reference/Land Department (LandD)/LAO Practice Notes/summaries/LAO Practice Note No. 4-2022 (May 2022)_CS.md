# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2022 — Installation of Solar Photovoltaic Systems in Private Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 May 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department

> Scope note: Lease facilitation for **ancillary** solar PV on private lots. It is **not** a BO structural code, **not** for dedicated commercial solar farms, and **not applicable** to Cap. 121–exempted buildings. NTEH roof/stairhood PV is pointed to a separate LandsD pamphlet.

---

## Regulatory Overview

LandsD will generally treat solar PV (panels, inverters, meters, boards, cables, supporting structures forming a complete grid-connected installation) as **ancillary** to the permitted lease user if the electricity is for the **sole use** of occupiers and of the building/accommodation **on the lot**, so a separate user application is generally **not** required. The installation must still comply with **all other** lease conditions; space **underneath** the PV **shall not be enclosed**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ancillary-use test (para 2)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| User | Generally accepted as ancillary to permitted lease use |
| Electricity | **Sole use** by occupiers in the building/accommodation **within the lot**; **not significantly in excess** of what those occupiers/building normally require |
| Under-croft | Space underneath the solar PV systems **shall not in any event be enclosed** |
| Other lease conditions | Still must be complied with |
| Separate LandsD user application | Generally **not** required if the above holds |

**SD takeaway:** Keep PV as a **lot-serving** array with **open** undercroft — do not enclose the space or size the array as an export farm.

---

### 2. BO / MWCS vs full GBP path (paras 3–4)

| Path | When | LandsD plans |
|---|---|---|
| **MWCS** simplified works | Supporting structure meets BD **Technical Guidelines on MWCS** (among other matters, **not higher than 1.5 m** — MW items **1.50** and **3.50**) | Separate building plans/drawings to LandsD **generally not** required |
| Full BA approval + consent to commence | Structure **does not** meet the Technical Guidelines / not under MWCS | Engage an **AP**; BD refers plans to LandsD under lease via **Centralised Processing** (**PNAP ADM-2**) |

**SD takeaway:** Design PV supports **≤ 1.5 m** under MWCS to avoid a LandsD GBP round-trip; taller frames go through BA → LandsD CPS.

---

### 3. Exclusions (paras 5, 7)

| Out of this PN | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dedicated **commercial solar farms** on private lots (whether or not buildings/structures are prohibited under lease) | Considered **separately**, case by case |
| Buildings exempted from the BO by **Cap. 121** | PN **not applicable** |
| NTEH roof or stairhood (new and existing) | Use LandsD pamphlet **“Building New Territories Exempted Houses”** (landsd.gov.hk) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not rely on this PN for a commercial PV farm or a Cap. 121 house — those are different tracks.
