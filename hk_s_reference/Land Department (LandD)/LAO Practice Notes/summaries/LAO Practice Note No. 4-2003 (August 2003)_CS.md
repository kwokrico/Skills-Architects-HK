# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2003 — Incentive Scheme to Encourage Provision of RCHE Premises in New Private Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 August 2003 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Patrick L C LAU)

> Scope note: LandsD may **exempt eligible purpose-built RCHE** from **land premium** on lease modifications, land exchanges and PTGs for residential/commercial developments, if SWD supports the premises. It is **not** an SWD licensing code — SWD’s August 2003 Guidance Note still governs operations.

---

## Regulatory Overview

To encourage purpose-built residential care homes for the elderly (RCHE) in new private developments, LandsD may grant concessions exempting eligible RCHE premises from land premium on modifications, exchanges and private treaty grants for residential/commercial developments. Eligibility requires **SWD support**; concessions, if granted, are subject to the lease conditions below.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility and lease locks (paras 2–3)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Quantum | **Only one** RCHE premises per development project; RCHE GFA **≤ 5,400 m²** |
| Alienation | **No sub-division**; assignment or subletting prohibited except as **one whole unit** |
| Standards | Must comply with **all SWD requirements** and all ordinances, by-laws or regulations in force from time to time |
| User | Throughout the lease term, managed and operated as an RCHE **to SWD’s satisfaction** |
| How locked | The above requirements are **stipulated as conditions in the Government lease** |
| Disposal / operation | Developer may **lease or sell** the completed RCHE or **operate** it; still as one whole unit |
| Vacancy / misuse | Unauthorised change of use, or leaving the RCHE vacant **> 12 months**, may trigger lease enforcement including **re-entry** and Government taking possession **without compensation** |

**SD takeaway:** If chasing the nil-premium RCHE concession, freeze **one** RCHE block/premises at **≤ 5,400 m² GFA**, unsplittable, SWD-backed, and design it so it cannot sit vacant or be converted without lease risk.
