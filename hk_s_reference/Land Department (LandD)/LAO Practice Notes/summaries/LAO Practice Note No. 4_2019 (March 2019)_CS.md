# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2019 — Waivers for industrial premises (non-data-centre / non-testing-lab)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 March 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 4/2019 | From **1 April 2019** | Target issue **≤ 2 months** from valid receipt | Supersedes **PN 5/2001, 5/2001A, 2/2003 and 1/2015** together with **PN 5/2019**

> Scope note: Streamlined **temporary waivers** for **non-industrial** use of existing **Industrial Premises** (part(s) of an IB on an industrial or industrial-and/or-godown lease). **Does not** cover data-centre waivers (**PN 3/2012 series**) or testing-laboratory waivers (**PN 1/2016**). Rooftop/external **radio base stations** → **PN 5/2019**. Not a planning permission.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Industrial Premises may only be used for purposes involving a **manufacturing process**. **Technology-based crop and aquaculture production** (controlled-environment production of crops/aquatic organisms; no direct customer services or goods) is **generally accepted as industrial** under lease. Standard-rate Schedule-of-Uses procedure **does not apply** to premises on **basement, ground or podium floors**, or to buildings with BA plans approved **on or after 1 April 2007** (or earlier plans with **major amendments** on or after that date) — those fees are individually assessed. Director’s decisions on user, floor location and major/minor amendments are **final**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two waiver tracks (paras 2–5, Appendix II)

| Track | Permitted uses | Fee basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Schedule of Permitted Uses** (Appendix II) | (1) HQ / back-office; (2) professional consultants’ offices (architects, engineers, surveyors, planning consultants, solicitors, accountants); (3) business-services offices (advertising, management consultants, PR, interior/graphic designers); (4) IT & telecom industries (see track 2 list; **excludes** rooftop/external RBS per PN 5/2019); (5) cargo handling and forwarding facilities; (6) recyclable collection centres; (7) other **non-residential** uses **not** involving **direct provision of customer services or goods to the general public** | Appendix III standard rates |
| **IT & telecom exclusive** (para 4) | Manufacture, design, development, production, operation, processing, assembly or **research** into: (i) electronic/micro-electronic systems, goods, components; (ii) IT products/services (computer HW/SW, contents, applications); (iii) telecom facilities/services as in **Cap. 106** — plus **ancillary office and storage** directly related. **No** customer/retail services with **physical attendance** of customers/public. Illustrations: gateways, exchanges, switching centres, RBS, paging centres, computer/data centres, telesites/carrier hotels. **Excludes** general commercial/office (trading, accounting, architecture, engineering consultancy, travel, estate agents, employment agencies, financial consultants, brokers, etc.) | Appendix IV standard rates |

**Godown vs industrial (App II fn 3):** Cargo handling / recyclable collection as in **PN 1/2019** on **godown-user** premises are generally accepted **without** this waiver. Those uses here **exclude** CFS / purpose-designed logistics centre and **MSW sorting**.

**SD takeaway:** Stay off **G/F, basement and podium** if you want standard rates; keep the user as **back-office / professional / IT-telecom / no-public-counter** — a shop or public-facing service is outside this PN’s Schedule.

### 2. Standard waiver fees from 1 April 2019 (Appendices III–IV)

**Appendix III — Schedule uses (not G/F, basement, podium; not post-1 Apr 2007 BA plans / major amendments)**

| Location | Annual fee (IFA) | Lump sum for **lifetime of building** (IFA) |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Area and Tsuen Wan / Kwai Tsing | **$718/m² p.a.** | **$6,540/m²** |
| NT other than Tsuen Wan / Kwai Tsing | **$549/m² p.a.** | **$4,980/m²** |

**Appendix IV — IT & telecom exclusive (RBS outside building/rooftop excluded)**

| DLO districts | Annual (IFA) | Lifetime lump sum (IFA) |
|---|---|---|
| HK West & South, HK East, Kowloon East, Kowloon West, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing | **$104/m² p.a.** | **$920/m²** |
| Tuen Mun, Yuen Long, North, Tai Po, Shatin, Sai Kung, Islands | **$69/m² p.a.** | **$610/m²** |

**IFA:** enclosed internal space for exclusive use including balconies, toilets and lift lobbies forming part of the unit; measured to interior face of external/separating wall; **include** internal partitions and columns.

Application may be for an initial term of years **or** lifetime of the existing building. Admin fee on demand, non-refundable. TPB / FSD / BA still required.

**SD takeaway:** Price the waiver on **IFA** (not GFA) and pick **annual vs lifetime lump sum** at SD; post-2007 IBs and G/F units are **individually assessed**, not these tables.
