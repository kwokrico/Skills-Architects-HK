# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2000 — Number of Houses Restrictions under Government Leases
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
April 2000 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (R.D. Pope)

> Scope note: LandsD interpretation of **“one house”** (and related phrases) for building-plan submissions where the lease or grant caps the number of houses. It is a **guide only** — each case is read on the lease as a whole. It is **not** a BO occupancy classification.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This note assists parties preparing building plans on lots restricted as to the number of houses that may be erected. The “one house” reading below applies **only** where the lease refers to “one house” **without qualification**; qualified phrases (Dwelling House / Private Dwelling House / European House) are interpreted separately.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What counts as “one house” (paras 2–3)

| Test | LandsD reading |
|---|---|
| **One house** | A building with **one main entrance** and **one secondary entrance**, plus such **MoE** as the Buildings Ordinance requires — provided those MoE are **exit-only** and **openable only from the inside** |
| Shops on GF of a multi-storey res/comm building, each with **its own street access** | **Not** one house |
| Terrace of town houses, each with **its own separate access** | **Not** one house |
| Joint development with “one house” character **over two lots**, each lease saying the owner **shall not erect other than one house** | **Not permitted** |
| **Free-standing outbuildings** | Also **houses**. A scheme that meets the para-2 building test **plus** e.g. a free-standing **guardhouse** or **E&M room** **breaches** the one-house restriction |

**SD takeaway:** On an unqualified “one house” lot, lock a single building with one main + one secondary door (BO MoE exit-only from inside) and put guardhouse / E&M **inside** that building — a free-standing outbuilding or shop-front street doors will fail.

---

### 2. Qualified phrases (N.B.)

| Lease words | Interpretation (guide only) |
|---|---|
| **Dwelling House** | Restricts **user to residential**. Residential **flats** inside a building that meets the “one house” criteria are **acceptable** |
| **Private Dwelling House** | A house designed and constructed to be occupied as a **single residential unit** |
| **European House** | Same criteria as **“one house”** |

**SD takeaway:** Read the exact lease words before assuming flats are allowed — “Private Dwelling House” is a **single unit**; “Dwelling House” can be flats if the building still reads as one house.
