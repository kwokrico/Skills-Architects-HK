# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2025B — NTEH self-certification rollout to all NT DLOs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
November 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies PN 1/2025; **supersedes PN 1/2025A**

> Scope note: Changes **where** the NTEH Certificate of Compliance / No-Objection-to-Occupy self-certification stream runs. It does **not** rewrite the SCC forms, RP rules, or 10/14-week targets in PN 1/2025.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Paragraph 13 of PN 1/2025 is replaced. All other provisions of PN 1/2025 remain valid.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Varied para 13 — implementation (para 2)

| Date / office | Status |
|---|---|
| DLO Yuen Long from **23 January 2025** | Pilot |
| DLO North from **9 July 2025** | Pilot |
| **All** District Lands Offices, New Territories from **24 November 2025** | Streamlined arrangement is the **standard procedure** |

**SD takeaway:** From 24 November 2025, NTEH CC/No-Objection applications in **every NT DLO** may use the PN 1/2025 self-certification stream (subject to DLO suitability) — no longer a Yuen Long/North-only pilot.
