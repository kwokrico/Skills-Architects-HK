# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2026 — Pilot “Pay for What You Build” (non-residential)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
May 2026 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Applications **1 June 2026 – 31 May 2029** (both inclusive) | Read with **LAO PN No. 2/2023**

> Scope note: Three-year pilot lease modification / land exchange so a lot owner pays premium only on an **Initial Phase** of **non-residential** GFA (≥ 60% of max), with a **ten-year** window after OP to buy the rest. It is **not** extra PR beyond OZP/BO, and **not** for residential GFA.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Applies territory-wide to non-residential developments on lots zoned Commercial, Industrial, OU (annotation), etc. On mixed zones (e.g. OU(Mixed Uses)), only the **independently developable non-residential portion** (no residential elements) may use the Pilot. Preferred use must be OZP-permitted or TPB-approved; LandsD decides the lease user wording.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Initial Phase gates (paras 4–5)

| Gate | Lock |
|---|---|
| Intensity | Proposed Initial Phase GFA ≥ **60%** of “maximum development intensity” = total permissible max **GFA of the whole lot** under the **statutory town plan or Cap. 123, whichever lesser**. If TPB permission covers the development, max = **TPB-approved GFA**. Show intended Initial Phase GFA in the planning application where permission is needed |
| User | **Well-defined** preferred non-residential use with supporting information (scope, occupation mode, business model — App I note) |
| Lease terms on approval | **Building covenant** for Initial Phase; **restriction on alienation except as a whole** for the whole lot (or the non-res carve-out in a mixed zone); user clause = approved preferred use; **max GFA** = approved Initial Phase intensity |
| Mixed-zone carve-out (fn 4) | If ≥ 60% GFA can be carved as a **standalone lot** under a separate lease, LandsD will consider separately |
| Premium | Difference in market value **before vs after** modification, based on **full MV of the Initial Phase GFA** (i.e. at least 60%) and the approved preferred use |

**SD takeaway:** Do not draw an Initial Phase below **60% of the lesser of OZP and BO GFA**, and expect a **no-strata-sale** lock on the whole (non-res) lot until Remaining Balance is taken up or the ten-year period expires.

---

### 2. Remaining Balance GFA — ten years from first OP (paras 7–10)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Remaining Balance | **40% or less** of total permissible max GFA |
| Clock start | **Fulfilment of BC** evidenced by date of **first Occupation Permit** for the Initial Phase |
| To take up residual | Further lease modification **within ten years**, subject to **then** OZP/BO; premium at **then** full MV. Whole-lot alienation restriction **may** be removed if Remaining Balance is **fully** realised |
| If unused after 10 years | Government **may redeploy** leftover intensity and infrastructure capacity to **other lots in the district** on those owners’ applications (planning permission/rezoning and updated IIAs may be required) |
| After 10 years, same owner wants residual | Approach **PlanD first**; then further modification under then OZP/BO; departments may require **updated infrastructure IAs** |
| Alienation restriction after 10 years | May apply to remove it even if residual unused; LandsD discretion; **full MV premium** + fees |

**SD takeaway:** Phase 1 must be a complete, BC’d, OP’d building at ≥ 60%; leftover GFA is a **10-year option**, not a reserved right — after that neighbours may absorb it.

---

### 3. Transparency (para 11)

LandsD will publish approved Pilot cases (location, user, Initial Phase intensity) and whether a further modification was filed in the Ten-year Period, so other owners can judge unused capacity.

**SD takeaway:** Assume Initial Phase GFA/user will be public; do not rely on unused residual remaining exclusive to the lot.

---

### 4. Application pack (Apps I–II) — skip privacy boilerplate

Must identify: OZP/DPAP zone; preferred non-res uses with GFA delineation sketches; total permissible max GFA (lesser of OZP/DPAP and BO) and **percentage** proposed; TPB letter if any; 1:1000 plan; ownership; OZP/DPAP extracts. Facilitate: assignment/Deed Poll plans; AP (or competent professional) sketch proposal; MLP / EIA / TIA / DIA.

Applicant must **acknowledge** that unused Remaining Balance may be redeployed after ten years.

**SD takeaway:** The first letter must state the **% of max GFA** and attach use-by-use GFA plans — that percentage is the Pilot eligibility test.
