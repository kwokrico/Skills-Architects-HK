# LAO Practice Note No. 11/2023 — Pilot Scheme for Charging Land Premium at Standard Rates on Lease Modification including Land Exchange for Development of Agricultural Land in the NT outside NDAs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 December 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates published separately (supplementary PN, first quarter 2024) | First rates review **no earlier than 1 April 2025**

> Scope note: Optional **standard-rate premium** for land exchange of **NT agricultural land outside NDAs**. It does **not** apply inside NDAs (KTN/FLN: **PN 3/2022** and **3/2022A**; other NDAs: PNs to follow) or inside **carved-out** strategic-study areas. Design GFA still comes from the OZP/lease — this PN only prices the exchange.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The Pilot Scheme extends standard-rate premium (as an alternative to case-by-case assessment) to development of agricultural land in the New Territories **outside** New Development Areas, by phases. Applicants may still choose conventional assessment. LandsD's decision on eligibility is final.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility and option timing (paras 2–3, 6)

| Test | Lock |
|---|---|
| Mix | **Not less than 50%** of the **site area of the private land under application** is agricultural land. That 50% is measured on the private land **proposed to be surrendered** |
| Agricultural land | Old schedule and new grant lots demised for agricultural use, **and** the agricultural portion of mixed lots (agriculture + building). May be whole lot, part, or multiple lots to match the OZP development-site boundary. LandsD determines areas from lease, land-boundary records and ground survey; **final** |
| Option | Opt **not later than acceptance of the Provisional Basic Terms Offer (PBTO)** on LandsD's specified option form (supplementary PN). Option is **irreversible** on PBTO acceptance — **no appeal** against standard-rate premium in the Binding Basic Terms Offer (BBTO). Fees **forfeited** if the applicant then walks away |
| Which rates | For each opted application, rates **prevailing at PBTO acceptance** apply |

**SD takeaway:** Do not opt unless **≥ 50% of surrendered site area** is agri land and you can live with an **unappealable** standard-rate BBTO.

---

### 2. Phase-1 geography (para 4, Appendix I)

First phase: **10 zones** in **Yuen Long, North and Tuen Mun** — four existing new towns and six railway catchments. LandsD's 1-km radius from a station (existing, or proposed with firm implementation commitment) is **final**. LandsD has absolute discretion whether a catchment is covered.

| No. | District | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yuen Long | Yuen Long Town |
| 2 | Yuen Long | Hung Shui Kiu Station |
| 3 | Yuen Long | Kam Sheung Road Station |
| 4 | Yuen Long | Tin Shui Wai Town |
| 5 | Yuen Long | San Tin Station |
| 6 | Yuen Long | Ngau Tam Mei Station |
| 7 | Yuen Long | Au Tau Station |
| 8 | North | Fanling / Sheung Shui Town |
| 9 | North | Kwu Tung Station |
| 10(a)(b) | Tuen Mun | Tuen Mun Town **Sub-zone 1** / **Sub-zone 2** |

| Overlay rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| **Carved-out** | NDAs and areas under on-going strategic development studies (Appendix I note) — Option **not applicable**. LandsD decision final. Indicative carve-outs on the plan include KTN NDA, FLN NDA, Hung Shui Kiu / Ha Tsuen NDA, San Tin Technopole, NTN New Town, Ngau Tam Mei LUR, Ma Tso Lung, Tsim Bei Tsui / Lau Fau Shan / Pak Nai, Tuen Mun East / GB, Tuen Mun West / Lung Kwu Tan |
| Overlapping zones | **Higher** rates apply |
| Site partly in / partly out of coverage | **Whole site** eligible; LandsD decides rates for the part **outside** coverage |
| Site straddling **two zones or two sub-zones** | After-exchange **GFA** split **pro-rata by site area** in each zone/sub-zone, assuming GFA evenly distributed |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm the lot is **outside** NDA/study carve-outs and which of the 10 zones (or TM sub-zones) it sits in **before** SD GFA mix — straddling sites get **pro-rata** after-GFA rates, overlaps take the **higher** rate.
