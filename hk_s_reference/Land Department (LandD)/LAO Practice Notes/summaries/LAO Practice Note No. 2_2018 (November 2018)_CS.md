# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2018 — Processing of general building plans under lease
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 November 2018 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 2/2018 | Supersedes **LAO PN 1/1994**

> Scope note: Landlord-side **GBP** processing through BD’s Centralized Processing System. Sets **rejection-without-scrutiny** gates (MLP, Development Schedule, colouring of amendments, separate lease GFA/SC calcs). It is **not** a BO submission PN. Sample schedule is **not** for virtually unrestricted redevelopment under **LAO PN 1/2017**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

LandsD comments and decides on GBP referred through the Centralized Processing System. A submission that fails paragraph 1(i)–(iv) is **rejected under lease without scrutiny**. Incomplete or incorrect Development Schedule may likewise be rejected without scrutiny. Preliminary comments should go via enquiry or workshop under **LAO PN 4/2018**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory submission gates (para 1)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| (i) MLP | **Prior approval** of Master Layout Plan **if required** |
| (ii) Development Schedule | Accompany GBP; sample Appendix I; Guidance Notes Appendix II |
| (iii) Re-submission / amendment | Amended parts **suitably coloured** |
| (iv) GFA / SC | **Separate** lease GFA and SC calculations unless they are **the same** as under the BO |

**SD takeaway:** Do not send GBP to LandsD until MLP (if any) is approved and the Development Schedule + lease GFA/SC (if different from BO) are complete — missing any gate is a **without-scrutiny reject**.

---

### 2. Development Schedule — what the AP must lock (Appendix I & II)

| Schedule block | Design lock |
|---|---|
| Location / lot / site area | Approx. m² |
| Height | Storeys, proposed height vs lease restriction, SC ref, drawing no. |
| Part D items | User; type of building; **GFA**; **SC**; building **separation**; building **setback**; **greenery**; DDH/DD (see **LAO PN 3/2014**); carpark; L/UL; vehicular access; caretaker office/quarters; OC/owners’ committee office; recreational facilities; NBA (e.g. drainage/waterworks reserve); formation areas (Green, Yellow, etc.); tree preservation; landscaping; other SCs (footbridge, open space, etc.) |
| MLP | Date of approved MLP if applicable |
| Lease documents | Use **latest** complete lease from Land Registry |
| Block plan | Mark lot boundary, vehicular access / footbridge points, NBAs, setback, coloured areas (Green / Yellow / Brown) **inside and outside** the lot per lease plan; site coordinates next to Block Plan (typically **1:1000**) |
| “Required/permitted” column | State stipulated parameters (e.g. **max and min GFA** under total GFA restriction) |
| “AP’s confirmation” | How GBP complies, citing SCs, PNs, and relevant-department agreement |
| Relevant departments | Use the list in LandsD’s post-sale/grant notification letter |
| Virtually unrestricted | Appendix I sample **does not apply** to PN 1/2017 redevelopment GBP |
| Status of schedule | Cursory summary only — **not** the lease; AP must supply further info on request |

**SD takeaway:** At SD, schedule every lease graphic (NBA, coloured formation areas, setback, access points) onto the Block Plan and quote **min/max GFA, SC, DDH, greenery, parking** against drawing numbers — LandsD will reject an incomplete schedule without reading the plans.
