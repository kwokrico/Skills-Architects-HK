# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2021 — Building Covenant Extensions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 June 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supersedes LAO PNs 4/2020, 5/2008, 5/1996 (APSS 4/96), 5/1994 (APSS 2/94)

> Scope note: How to **extend the building covenant (BC)** period on a new or modified lease. Completing the BC means finishing the development **and obtaining OP** within the BC period. **Does not apply** to indigenous-villager small houses under Cap. 121. It is **not** a BO occupation-permit PN.

## Regulatory Overview

Apply to the relevant DLO/section when OP will miss the BC date. LandsD generally processes extensions **closer to expiry** (early extension possible to support LACO pre-sale consent). Extensions are in **multiples of 3 months**, **premium** on a rising % of land value by year of extension. This PN adds a **1–3 year** streamlined multi-year extension and a **3-month deposit** path when OP is imminent.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Premium scale (para. 2)

| Extension year from original BC expiry | % of land value |
|---|---|
| 1st | **2%** |
| 2nd | **4%** |
| 3rd | **8%** |
| 4th | **14%** |
| 5th | **22%** |
| 6th and after | **32%** |

A few exceptions exist. Any approval is on LandsD terms including premium.

**SD takeaway:** Programme OP inside the original BC — each extra year **doubles-plus** the land-value levy.

### 2. Streamlined extension **>1 year and ≤3 years** (paras. 3–5)

With effect from this PN, LandsD will consider **one** application for **>1 year up to 3 years** (still in **3-month multiples**) instead of successive 1-year extensions. Lodge:

- AP certificate: **superstructure building contract awarded** (incl. commencement info);
- AP certificate: date OP is **reasonably expected**;
- copy of BA **consent to commence superstructure**.

If, at the end of that extended period, reasonable effort is **not** shown, LandsD may grant **shorter** further extensions, **refuse**, or take **lease enforcement**.

**SD takeaway:** A 3-year BC extension needs a **let superstructure contract + BA commencement consent + OP date** on the AP certificate — not a paper programme.

### 3. Three-month “OP imminent” extension (para. 6)

If the job is advanced and OP is expected **just after** BC (or extended BC) expiry: apply for **3 months** with AP certificate of **reasonable expectation of OP within those 3 months**. Premium is paid as a **deposit**; if OP issues **on or before** the 3-month end, the premium is **refunded without interest**.

**SD takeaway:** Use the 3-month deposit route only when OP is genuinely in the window — otherwise the deposit is kept as premium.

### 4. Other

- “Commence to operate” clauses may exist **in addition to or instead of** BC.
- Sample letters: Apps. II(a), II(b), III.
- Each case at LandsD’s **absolute discretion** as landlord.

**SD takeaway:** BC / commence-to-operate dates belong on the SD programme next to OP.
