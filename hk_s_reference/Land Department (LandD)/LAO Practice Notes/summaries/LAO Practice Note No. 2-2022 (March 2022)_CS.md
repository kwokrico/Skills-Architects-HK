# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2022 — COVID-19 building-covenant extension at nil premium
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Measure announced **14 March 2022**

> Scope note: One-off **up to six calendar months** BC (and CtO / coloured-area / GA completion) extension at **nil premium**. It is **not** a general BC-premium code — further time still follows LAO PN No. 2/2021. LandsD applies it **without application**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The Measure covers all development types with **unfulfilled BC as at 14 March 2022**, including Cap. 121 works and industrial-building conversion under special waiver. It does **not** apply to leases executed after 14 March 2022, to BCs not imposed by LandsD (e.g. Cap. 545 Lands Tribunal orders), or to cases already BC-fulfilled before **1 January 2022** with only formality outstanding.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who gets six months and from when (paras 2–3)

| Case | Nil-premium extension |
|---|---|
| BC (or already-extended BC) **not yet expired** on 14 March 2022 | LandsD offers **six calendar months from that expiry**; offer letter issued within **3 calendar months before** expiry |
| BC already **expired** on 14 March 2022 | Up to six calendar months commencing on the **expiry date or 1 January 2022, whichever later** |
| Premium already paid on earlier extensions | **Not refunded** |
| Application from owner | **Not required** |

Fn 1–2: “BC period” includes **commence-to-operate (CtO)** and associated formation/construction periods (coloured areas, Government Accommodation) even if those dates differ from BC.

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme a 14 March 2022-era unfulfilled BC as immediately in breach — LandsD will add up to six months at nil premium; still freeze GA/coloured-area completion dates against the same Measure.

---

### 2. Time beyond six months (para 4 and fn 6–7)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Further extension | May attract premium under established practice |
| Sliding scale (PN 2/2021 para 3) | The Measure’s up-to-six-month nil period is **disregarded** when counting the year of extension |
| Illustration (fn 6) | Original BC 31 March 2022, 1-year extension sought: **1 Apr–30 Sep 2022** nil; **1 Oct 2022–31 Mar 2023** charged at **2% p.a.** of land value |
| Further 6 months to 30 Sep 2023 (fn 7) | Still assessed as **1st-year** 2% p.a. because the nil six months are ignored |

**SD takeaway:** A programme that needs more than the Measure’s six months still pays PN 2/2021 premium, but the nil six months do **not** push you into a higher sliding-scale year.
