# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2026 — Standard rates for balcony, utility platform and non-structural prefabricated external wall exemptions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Issue No. 3/2026, signed 14 July 2026; rates from 1 April 2026 | Lands Department, Lands Administration Office

> Scope note: Revises the premium rates for exempting balconies, utility platforms and non-structural prefabricated external walls (NSPEWall) from gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage under Joint Practice Note (JPN) Nos. 1 and 2. It does **not** change the exemption geometry in those JPNs, and it is **not** a Buildings Department (BD) concession.

## Regulatory Overview
From 1 April 2026 this note supersedes Practice Note No. 6/2025 and revises the standard premium rates in Lands Administration Office Practice Note Nos. 3/2001 (as supplemented by 6/2001), 6/2002, 3/2003, 2/2011, 4/2023 and 3/2024. Everything in those earlier notes that this issue does not vary stays in force, and the new rates are for general reference only.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

**SD takeaway:** Lock the JPN edition the lease modification will follow before reading a rate, because October 2004, February 2006 and January 2011 do not share one table.

### 2. Balcony premium per unit (Appendices I and II)

Rates are premium per unit for total exempted balcony area per unit. The authorized person (AP) measures to the nearest 0.001 m², then the sheet rounds to 0.1 m²: 2.544 m² is read as 2.5 m² and 2.545 m² as 2.6 m². District boundaries follow Appendix VII to Practice Note No. 3/2024. A lot in two districts pays the higher rate.

Appendix I (October 2004 JPN 1) runs from 2.0 m² to 5.0 m² in 0.1 m² steps. Selected rows, premium per unit:

### 4. NSPEWall unit rates (Appendices V and VI)

The rate is per square metre of exempted NSPEWall area shown on building plans approved by the Building Authority. Thickness is the thickness on those plans; if more than one thickness is shown, the majority thickness applies. Average flat size is total residential GFA divided by the number of residential units, split at 104 m². Districts again follow Appendix VII to Practice Note No. 3/2024, and a straddling lot takes the higher rate.

Mosaic tiles or similar tile cladding that only finishes the wall are not counted for premium. Curtain-wall-type cladding fixed to the external structural wall is GFA-countable and can be exempted only by lease modification and a premium.

February 2006 JPN 2 (Appendix V): thickness over 300 mm is accountable for GFA. The sheet steps every 10 mm from “130 or less” to “more than 290–300”. Rates at 130 mm or less, $/m², for average flat size not exceeding 104 m² and exceeding 104 m²:

| District | ≤104 m² | >104 m² |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hong Kong Island | 84,000 | 129,260 |
| Kowloon East | 63,000 | 96,940 |
| Kowloon West | 70,000 | 107,710 |
| New Territories South | 46,000 | 44,030 |
| New Territories North | 33,000 | 31,310 |

January 2011 JPN 2 (Appendix VI): thickness over 150 mm is accountable for GFA, so the printed bands stop at “more than 140–150”. The “130 or less” rates on that sheet are the same six pairs as Appendix V.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the NSPEWall inside the JPN thickness cap (300 mm on the 2006 version, 150 mm on the 2011 version) or the extra thickness becomes accountable GFA, and price the exempted area from the thickness band and the 104 m² average-flat split.
