# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2025 — Yuen Long South Second Phase land exchange
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
February 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Read with **LAO PN No. 13/2023** (ECNTA)

> Scope note: Lease modification / in-situ land exchange for **two** designated **OU(Storage and Workshop)** sites in Yuen Long South NDA Second Phase. **PN 12/2023 / 12/2023A** (IB standard rates) and **PN 11/2023 / 11/2023A** (ag-land pilot standard rates) **do not apply**. NDA standard-rate premium “to be announced”.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Follow approved Tong Yan San Tsuen OZP **S/YL-TYST/14** (20 August 2021 or later amendments) and the Revised Recommended ODP (yls.hk). Only Site A (~3.7 ha, wholly OU(SW)) and Site B (~1.2 ha, largely OU(SW) plus a ‘Road’ strip now intended for storage/workshop) on Reference Plan App I are open. OZP Notes: OU(SW) is for **multi-storey buildings** housing brownfield operations that cannot sit in conventional flatted factories (bulky/heavy goods, high headroom).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The two sites (para 3 and fn 2)

| Site | Planning lock |
|---|---|
| **A** ~3.7 ha | Entirely **OU(SW)** |
| **B** ~1.2 ha | Mostly OU(SW); remainder shown as **‘Road’** on OZP — Government intends the **extended** site as OU(SW); Government will seek **TPB permission** for storage/workshop on the Road portion **if** an in-situ exchange is received. Expected ~**six calendar months** before BBTO; if permission is late, Government may **reject** or handle otherwise |
| Boundaries | Illustration only; Government may adjust |

**SD takeaway:** Design both sites as high-headroom multi-storey workshop; Site B’s Road strip is not a public road in the exchange scheme but needs TPB permission on Government’s application.

---

### 2. Government Accommodation on Site A (para 6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Site A only (larger site) | On completion, hand over **20% of floor space** as **Government Accommodation** for displaced brownfield operators |
| Use of the 20% | Government lets it; uses **may differ** from the remaining 80% (cluster/synergy considered) |
| Site B | This invitation’s 20% GA rule is stated for Site A |

**SD takeaway:** On Site A freeze **20% GFA as GA** with independent letting/services; the 80% private workshop can be a different operator mix.

---

### 3. Public facilities and 90% ownership (para 5, App IV)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Relaxed ownership (PN 13/2023 paras 8–10) | Applicant **shall** construct specified public facilities |
| Other applicants | Government **may** invite; construction cost **may** be premium-deductible |
| Assembly | Surrender **all** private land in the designated site (minor layout-neutral boundary tweaks OK). Planning intention = **one development per site**. **90%+** → relaxed criterion. Re-grant conforms to designated site; **GL not promised** |
| Access | Vehicular access feasible; no adverse impact on adjacent **vehicular and/or pedestrian** access |
| OZP | Use and parameters comply |

**SD takeaway:** These two sites are full-assembly (or 90%) single developments — no 4 000 m² residential carve-out option.

---

### 4. Deadlines (para 8)

| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Submit applications | **19 May 2025** |
| Accepted application: BBTO with premium issued **and** accepted | **19 May 2026** or processing ceases (fees not refunded) |
| Lodge at | CES/NDA, Landmark North, Sheung Shui |

**SD takeaway:** Workshop scheme and 20% GA package must be BBTO-ready by 19 May 2026.

---

### 5. Submission pack (App II) — design-facing

Must: letter with proposed user, **max GFA, SC, storeys/BH**; 1:1000 plan; TPB letter if any; ownership printout.

Facilitate: OZP extract; assignment/Deed Poll plans; sketches; MLP / EIA / TIA / DIA; occupant schedules (current **and** occupants at **10 July 2020** or after who left); programme; ALS survey to prove **90%** private-land threshold.

**SD takeaway:** First pack needs GFA/SC/BH, 90% survey, and the 10 July 2020 occupant trail.
