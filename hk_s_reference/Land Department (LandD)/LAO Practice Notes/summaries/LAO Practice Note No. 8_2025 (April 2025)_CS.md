# LAO Practice Note No. 8/2025 — Streamlined Arrangement on Phased GBP, DDH/DD and Landscaping Proposal Approval for Residential Units in Uncompleted Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 April 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect (paras 3–5)

> Scope note: Lets LandsD issue **phased** general building plan, DDH/DD, and landscaping approvals under lease so a **Cap. 621 "phase"** can go to pre-sale without whole-lot GBP/DDH. Other LACO CM 72F whole-lot approvals (MLP, carpark layout, DMC, etc.) still bind pre-sale. It is **not** a BO phasing code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Until this PN, GBP, DDH/DD and landscaping approvals under lease were issued on a **whole-lot** basis even for phased developments. LandsD will now consider phased approvals covering a specific part of the development that is a **"phase"** as defined in the Residential Properties (First-hand Sales) Ordinance (Cap. 621). "Lease" includes Government Lease or Conditions of Grant/Sale/Exchange.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Phased GBP and phased DDH/DD (paras 3–4)

| Lock | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who applies | **Authorised Person** for the applicant |
| When | At submission to LandsD of the **full set of up-to-date GBP at stage 2** of the Streamlined Building Plans Checking Process (**LAO PN 4/2018**) |
| Must attach | **Phasing Plans** approved by BA under **s.14(1) BO (Cap. 123)** |
| Purpose | Expedite residential units in a phased development; facilitate **pre-sale consent for part** of the development; leave some lease requirements to a later stage |

Conditions LandsD may impose include:

| (a) | Clear **delineation** in the Phasing Plans of the phase under application; Phasing Plans fulfil corresponding **lease** requirements |
| (b) | All relevant **lease requirements of that phase** complied with to LandsD's satisfaction — e.g. Consent Letter for exemption of **balconies and utility platforms** from GFA and/or SC; Consent Letter allowing BA-exempted **MiC** floor area to be exempted from GFA and/or SC |
| (c) | **AP certification** that there is **no insurmountable problem** for the applicant to design the development to comply with lease requirements for the **remaining phase(s)** |

**SD takeaway:** Draw Cap. 621 phases so the first-sale phase is lease-complete on its own (balcony/UP and MiC consent letters in hand) and the AP can certify the remainder is designable — otherwise LandsD will not phase the GBP/DDH.

---

### 2. Phased landscaping and residual whole-lot approvals (paras 5–6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Phased landscaping | Considered **only if** phased GBP or phased DDH/DD has been issued; **case-by-case** |
| Planning-condition landscaping | Where another department must approve landscaping **before** LandsD's phased landscaping approval (e.g. planning condition), LandsD **deems** phased landscaping approved for **pre-sale consent** once that other department has approved the **phased** landscaping proposal |
| Still whole-lot | After phased GBP / DDH/DD / landscaping, pre-sale for the phase still needs the other lease approvals in **paras 8.1 and 8.2 of Section III of the Annex to LACO CM No. 72F** (issued the same day), which remain **whole-lot** |

Para 2 of this PN still cites **LACO CM 72E** for the pre-sale list (DDH, MLP, landscaping, carpark layout, natural terrain hazard plans, DMC & MA, etc.).

**SD takeaway:** Phase the GBP/landscape for the sales phase, but programme **whole-lot** MLP, carpark layout, DMC and the rest of CM 72F — pre-sale will not wait on a partial DMC.
