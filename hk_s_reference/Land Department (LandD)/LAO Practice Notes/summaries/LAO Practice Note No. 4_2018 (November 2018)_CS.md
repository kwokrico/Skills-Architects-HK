# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2018 — Streamlined building plans checking process
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 November 2018 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 4/2018 | Streamlined Process applies to **new** GBP (incl. resubmissions / major revisions) received via Centralized Processing System **on or after 1 February 2019**

> Scope note: Three-stage **lease** GBP vetting plus optional **workshops**. LandsD will **not** check BO amendment submissions that have **no lease implications** (owner/AP must flag those that do). **Not** a substitute for BO approval. **Not** applicable to virtually unrestricted redevelopment under **PN 1/2017**, A&A to existing buildings, or technical plans (site formation, drainage, hoarding, green/yellow formation, landscape plan/LMP).

---

## Regulatory Overview

AP must ensure correctness of all arithmetic and area calculations (including GFA and SC) and demonstrate lease compliance in a **Development Schedule** with the GBP (**PN 2/2018**). “Major revision” (Director’s decision final) includes changes in disposition, number of blocks/storeys, floor-plan configuration; substantial changes in site area, formation levels and configuration; changes that seriously affect access. Pre-1 February 2019 receipts continue under previous practice.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Three-stage GBP checking (para 4)

| Stage | When | What LandsD does | AP must submit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** — full GBP before commencement | First full set | **Does not** check area calculations; checks **other** lease conditions. May give **approval in-principle** if acceptable | Full GBP + Development Schedule with **AP’s Certification** of arithmetic/area correctness and lease compliance. If computer used for calcs: **one set Coloured Building Plans** (**PN 3/2018**). **DVD-ROM not required** at Stage 1 |
| **2** — pre-sale | **Upon or prior to** Pre-sale Consent application to **LACO** | Full check **including area calculations**. Approve/accept if lease-compliant **or** irregularities rectified | Certify BA-approved up-to-date GBP (drawing nos. + version date); submit that full set **direct to DLO**; comply with **PN 2/2018 and 3/2018** including **DVD-ROM**; certify it is the same set referred to in the Pre-sale Consent application |
| **3** — OP | **Upon or prior to** OP application to BA | Same full check as Stage 2 | Same as Stage 2, but certify the set is the one referred to BA for **OP** |

**In-principle (Stage 1) condition:** up-to-date BA-approved full GBP must later be submitted for lease approval at Stage 2 and/or 3 as applicable.

**SD takeaway:** Stage 1 is **in-principle on everything except numbers**; freeze GFA/SC for **DVD + coloured plans at Stage 2 (pre-sale) and Stage 3 (OP)** — do not start sales or OP on Stage 1 alone.

---

### 2. Errors, modification window, and Stage 3 hard stop (para 4(II)–(III))

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rectify | Owner must rectify (including demolition/removal and GBP amendments) to LandsD satisfaction; keep BA/other departments informed and get their approvals as needed |
| Stage 2 escape | If not rectifiable, owner **may apply** for approval, consent or **lease modification** to relax parameters **for the lifetime of the buildings** under the GBP — **no guarantee**; premium + admin fee as imposed; GBP not approved until terms accepted, paid, and legal document executed |
| Stage 3 | **No** approval/consent/modification to relax parameters or paper over irregularities **would normally be entertained**; rectify. Failure may lead to lease enforcement and **re-entry** |
| Processing time | Performance pledges apply to all three stages; clock from **valid** receipt (no outstanding AP information) |

**SD takeaway:** Any lease GFA/SC or bulk overrun must be **fixed or modified at Stage 2** — Stage 3 is a rectify-or-enforcement gate, not a premium negotiation.

---

### 3. Workshops (paras 6–8, 10)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | All development types (rationalised from comprehensive-development presentations) |
| How | Written request stating purpose (concept presentation **or** fundamental issues) + drawings; **no walk-ins** |
| Cap | Generally **maximum two workshops per site** before a GBP submission, resubmission or major revision |
| Alternative | DLO may divert to enquiry submission or other means |
| What it is **not** | Not confirmation of lease compliance; **will not** interpret lease terms; comments **without prejudice / no commitment** |
| What it may do | Elaborate prevailing **LAO PN** guidelines on plan checking |

**SD takeaway:** Use at most **two** pre-GBP workshops for interfaces (Government/public facilities, access, bulk) — treat every comment as non-binding.
