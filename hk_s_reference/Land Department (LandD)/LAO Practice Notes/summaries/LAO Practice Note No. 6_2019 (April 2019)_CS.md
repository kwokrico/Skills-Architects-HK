# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2019 — Special waiver for conversion of an entire existing industrial building
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2 April 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 6/2019 | Valid applications received **on or before 31 March 2022** (extended to **31 October 2024** by **PN 6/2019A**)

> Scope note: **Nil waiver fee** special waiver to change use of an **entire existing IB** in **“I” / “C” / “OU(B)”** for the building’s lifetime or lease expiry/termination, whichever earlier. Requires a **≥10% Designated Portion** for Government-specified uses, **no GFA increase**, tightly limited envelope changes, **3-year** conversion completion, and **Director’s consent** to dispose (via **PN 2/2016**). **Not** concurrent with **PN 2/2019** lease modification. Vacant-site redevelopment is outside this PN.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Building age **≥ 15 years** from OP of the **entire** IB (if multiple OPs, from the **latest**). Application by lot owner, or **jointly by all** existing owners plus consent of all mortgagees/chargees/ASP purchasers. Proposed uses must be always-permitted on the OZP **or** have **TPB permission before** LandsD filing. If the zone leaves the Designated Zones **before execution**, the application is **rejected** (admin fee refundable at LandsD discretion). Special factories/lots excluded as in other IB PNs.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Designated Portion — 10% specified uses (para 3(a), 4)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Quantum | **Not less than 10%** of total **accountable GFA under the BO** of the existing IB **after conversion** |
| Uses | **Specified Uses** as Government determines, consistent with para 2(c) town planning; owners may propose GFA, location, layout, FF&E (suggested: arts/culture, creative industries, I&T, social service/community healthcare, sports/recreation, or other wider community benefits — e.g. small studios, young-entrepreneur workshops, I&T incubation, elderly service centres). If owners submit no preference, **Government decides** (final) |
| Commence and continue | Start using Designated Portion for Specified Uses within **6 calendar months** of conversion completion; continue throughout the waiver to policy bureaux/departments’ satisfaction |
| Cessation | Unused or inadequately used for **6 calendar months** = breach of commence-and-continue |
| Alienation | **No** assign, mortgage, charge, demise, part with possession, licence or right to occupy the Designated Portion, except the **Long Lease** below |
| Breach remedy | Government may require owner, at own cost, to underlet Designated Portion (or part) to **FSI** at **$1** p.a. if demanded, for residue of waiver period, and deliver **vacant possession** free of incumbrances |
| Cost | All wholesale-conversion costs of Designated Portion borne by owners; tenancy rent is owner–tenant (rental concession welcomed but not prescribed) |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve **≥ 10% BO accountable GFA** as a Government-controlled community/I&T/arts package that **cannot be sold** — locate it as a discrete, operable floor plate before conversion drawings.

---

### 2. Envelope, GFA, height, and works (para 3(b)–(c), 5)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| GFA cap | Total accountable GFA on **Last Approved Building Plans** (BA, before application) **must not be exceeded**; **no** BO additional SC or PR concession |
| External change generally | **No** change to external structures, **no** increase in height or bulk, **except**: |
| (I) Demolish GFA-accountable external structures | Up to **10%** of total accountable GFA on last approved plans |
| (II) Recover demolished GFA | Vertical and/or horizontal extensions **within lot** if: converted building SC **≤ BO** SC; and BH and/or SC **≤ OZP** (or TPB minor relaxation). **Does not** cover internal works that do not change the envelope; internal conversion within envelope remains permitted if no BH/bulk increase and GFA cap kept |
| (III) Roof-top plant | Machine rooms, A/C units, water tanks, stairhoods and similar on **main roof**, **BO GFA-exempt**, occupying **not more than 50%** of roof area of floor immediately below |
| (IV) Cladding / curtain wall | Added to outer face **within lot**, BO GFA-exempt. If they **protrude onto Government land**, LandsD **may** approve a modification with admin fee and **premium waived** |
| Lease BH | Must be observed, **save** LandsD **may** waive BH non-compliance in the special waiver (nil fee) **solely** for GFA recovery under (II) and/or rooftop structures under (III) |
| Other lease breaches | If proposal breaches lease **other than** user (or BH to the extent permitted under (II)/(III)), a **separate lease modification** (premium as appropriate) must be **completed before** the special waiver is executed |
| Completion | Conversion works BO-compliant; finish within **3 years** of special waiver letter; AP (or LandsD-accepted professional) certified proof (e.g. Form **BA 14** or works-contract completion) |

**SD takeaway:** Conversion is **GFA-neutral** on last-approved totals; you may **carve off ≤ 10% GFA** of accountable external structure and **rebuild it as vertical/horizontal extension** only if OZP BH/SC and BO SC still hold.

---

### 3. Disposal, user, existing waivers, and conflicts (paras 3(d)–(e), 6–17)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Disposal | Except Designated Portion, no sale/assignment/agreement without **Director’s prior written consent** — follow **PN 2/2016** |
| User | No part of building for purposes other than those specified in the special waiver |
| After expiry | Lot reverts to original lease user **and height** (if any); surviving non-compliant uses/structures need a **modification** |
| Full-market premium | Any later modification assessed **without** benefit of the special waiver |
| Multiple ownership | Joint execution; solicitors confirm ownership |
| Existing unit waivers | Terminated on execution; **no** refund |
| Concurrent **PN 2/2019** | Lease-mod under 2/2019 **and** this special waiver **will not be accepted** concurrently |
| Revert | Cancel special waiver (all owners + consents if multiple ownership) to return to original lease uses not specified in the waiver |

**SD takeaway:** Choose **wholesale conversion (this PN)** or **20% PR redevelopment (PN 2/2019)** — not both — and programme **3-year** works plus **PN 2/2016** disposal consents for every unit sale.
