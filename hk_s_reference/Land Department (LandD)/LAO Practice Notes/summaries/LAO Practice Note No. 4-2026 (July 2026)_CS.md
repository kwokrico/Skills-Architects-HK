# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2026 — Standard rates for modular integrated construction exemptions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Issue No. 4/2026, signed 14 July 2026; rates from 1 April 2026 | Lands Department, Lands Administration Office

> Scope note: Replaces the premium rates for gross floor area (GFA) and/or site coverage exemption, and for building-height relaxation, where modular integrated construction (MiC) is adopted under Joint Practice Note No. 8. It does not itself set the exemption geometry. That sits in Joint Practice Note No. 8 and in the practice notes this issue leaves in force.

## Regulatory Overview
From 1 April 2026 this note supersedes Practice Note No. 7/2025 and replaces the rates in Appendix I to Practice Note No. 4/2024. It varies and supplements Practice Note Nos. 6/2022 and 4/2024; every provision in those notes that this issue does not change remains valid.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is being repriced (para 2)

| Item | Rule on this note |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Adoption of MiC under Joint Practice Note No. 8 |
| Concession being charged | Exemption of GFA and/or site coverage, and relaxation of the building height restriction |
| Effective date | 1 April 2026 |
| Rate card replaced | Appendix I to Practice Note No. 4/2024 |
| New card | Appendix I to this note |

**SD takeaway:** Use this appendix for any MiC premium calculated on or after 1 April 2026; do not carry forward the 4/2024 or 7/2025 rates.

### 2. Unit rates (Appendix I)

The rate is per square metre of exempted MiC floor area. Premium is that rate times the exempted MiC floor area approved by the Building Authority (BA). Exempted area is rounded up to the nearest 0.1 m². Lands Department fixes the premium at its discretion and it is not negotiable. District boundaries follow Appendix II to Practice Note No. 4/2024. A lot in two districts pays the higher rate.

| Location | Domestic ($/m²) | Non-domestic ($/m²) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hong Kong Island | 8,400 | 4,200 |
| Kowloon East | 6,300 | 3,600 |
| Kowloon West | 7,000 | 3,500 |
| New Territories South | 4,600 | 3,100 |
| New Territories North | 3,300 | 1,700 |

If the general building plans approved by the BA do not say whether the exempted MiC floor area is domestic or non-domestic GFA, the domestic rate is used. Lands Department may still choose the rate type from the nature of the development and the lease.

**SD takeaway:** Label exempted MiC area as domestic or non-domestic on the general building plans, or the premium is calculated on the higher domestic rate, and round the exempted area up to 0.1 m² before multiplying.
