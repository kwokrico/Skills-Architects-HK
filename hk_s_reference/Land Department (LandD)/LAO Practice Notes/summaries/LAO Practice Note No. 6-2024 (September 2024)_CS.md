# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2024 — BIM for Area Calculation on GBP Submissions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 September 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect

> Scope note: LandsD **accepts BIM files** for **mathematical area calculations** in GBP submissions under lease, **in addition to** CAD files under **LAO PN 3/2018**. It is **not** a substitute for the GBP itself. Read with **PNAP ADM-19** para 24 and **PNAP ADV-34**, and LandsD **Guidance Notes on Use of BIM for Mathematical Calculation of Areas**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

When BIM is used to demonstrate area calculations, the AP must follow those Guidance Notes. Approval or disapproval under lease is given only to a GBP in hard copy or in the electronic format accepted by BD under **PNAP ADM-17**, and if GBP and BIM disagree the **GBP prevails**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How to submit BIM + coloured plans (paras 3, 6)

| Mode | BIM delivery | Also required |
|---|---|---|
| Hardcopy GBP | BIM on **non-rewritable DVD-ROM** diskette(s) | **Coloured Building Plans** (Guidance Notes paras 6–9): **identical** to GBP floor layouts; coloured to show **SC extent, transfer plate, GFA-accountable and non-accountable** areas under lease |
| GBP via **ESH** | Upload BIM **simultaneously** with the electronic copy through ESH | Same Coloured Building Plans |
| Non-compliance | Requirements above **or** Guidance Notes timeframe/technical requirements not met | GBP **may be rejected without further checking** |
| Coverage | Immediate; applies to **all** GBP submissions with BIM, including **re-submissions, major revisions, amendments** | — |

**SD takeaway:** BIM is extra evidence, not the approval drawing — still issue **Coloured Building Plans** that match the GBP, on DVD-ROM (paper) or **simultaneous ESH** upload, or the GBP can be **rejected unread**.

---

### 2. Government reuse of BIM (para 4)

Submission of BIM files is taken as **permitting** the Government to use the models to update the **3-Dimensional Digital Map** and other spatial data, and to keep them in the **Government BIM Data Repository** for smart-city uses (e.g. city planning and urban design).

**SD takeaway:** Assume submitted BIM is **not confidential vis-à-vis Government spatial/BIM repositories** — strip anything the client cannot have reused before issue.
