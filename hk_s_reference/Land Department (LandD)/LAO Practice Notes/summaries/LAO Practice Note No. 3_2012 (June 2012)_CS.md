# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2012 — High-tier data centre lease modification / data centre waiver
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 June 2012 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 3/2012 | Window: **25 June 2012 – 31 March 2016** (both dates inclusive)

> Scope note: Two time-limited tracks for **industrial lots / industrial buildings**: (a) lease modification or land exchange to develop the lot (wholly or in part) as **high-tier data centre**; (b) **nil-fee waiver** to use **part of an existing industrial building** as **data centre**. It is **not** a BO plan code, FSD circular, or a standing open window after 31 March 2016. Special industrial lots (industrial estates, container-terminal storage, HA flatted factories) are excluded.

---

## Regulatory Overview

An **industrial lot** is one that, under the Government lease, may not be used for any purpose other than **industrial or godown purposes or both**. Applications under this PN may be submitted only in the window in paragraph 1; postal chop (or LandsD receipt) is the submission date. Lease modification and Data Centre Waiver **may not be submitted concurrently**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. High-tier data centre lease modification — intensity and premium (paras 2–5, fn 4)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Intensity | Up to **or less than** max permissible under the statutory town plan, or **BO** if the plan is silent |
| Data-centre quantum | Data-centre part **not less than 40%** of applicable max intensity **or PR 2.5**, **whichever is the higher** |
| Ancillary office | **Not more than 30%** of total usable floor area of the data-centre part |
| Premium | Difference between land value under current lease vs proposed modified lease; data-centre part assessed as **“high-tier data centre”** use |
| Fees | Admin fee in **two instalments** (on demand at submission; balance on acceptance of binding basic terms) + LACO fee if applicable; non-refundable as set out in demand letters |

**High-tier data centre (fn 4):** designed for concurrent maintenance (uninterrupted planned maintenance) and high availability; typically redundant telecom access, redundant support components (generator, cooling), redundant power and cooling distribution paths, dedicated environmentally controlled computer-room space, dedicated cooling, dedicated generator and fuel tank. Redundant systems take over primary; dedicated plant **not shared** with non-data-centre uses.

**SD takeaway:** Size the data-centre GFA at **≥ 40% of max intensity or PR 2.5 (higher of the two)** and keep ancillary office **≤ 30% of DC usable floor area** — LandsD will write max intensity (including DC part) into the modification letter.

### 2. Data Centre Waiver — part of an existing IB (paras 6–12, fn 1, 5)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Zoning | IB in **“Industrial”**, **“Commercial”** or **“OU(B)”** on statutory town plans |
| Age | IB **not less than 15 years** at submission (from date of **last-issued OP**) |
| Fee | **Nil waiver fee** |
| Term | Lifetime of the IB **or** expiry/termination of current Government lease, **whichever earlier** |
| User during waiver | **No part** of the Industrial Premises may be used for any use **other than data centre** |
| Building works | Must comply with **BO**; complete within **3 years** of waiver letter; AP-certified proof (e.g. BA acknowledgement of **Form BA 14**) |
| DMC | Waiver does **not** prejudice DMC rights/remedies |
| Other lease terms | Waiver only lifts **user restriction**; other lease changes need a **separate** lease modification (premium as LandsD considers appropriate) |
| Revert / vary | Cancel waiver to revert to original lease user; change of waiver conditions or area needs a fresh LandsD application (premium/charge at discretion) |
| Later modification | Full market premium **without** benefit of the waiver |

**Data centre (fn 1):** facility housing computer systems, servers, telecom equipment and associated support (UPS, power distribution, standby generation, heat rejection, CRAC, fire suppression, environmental and security control) in a secure, controlled environment.

**Industrial building (fn 5):** lawfully erected and standing at submission date on a lot restricted to industrial or godown or both; same special-factory exclusions as industrial lots.

**SD takeaway:** For conversion of part of an existing IB, lock **≥15-year** building age, **I / C / OU(B)** zoning, and a **3-year** works-completion programme — the waiver dies with the building and does not credit later premium.

### 3. Process, existing applications, and existing waivers (paras 13–18)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Submit to | LandsD HQ, 19/F North Point Government Offices, 333 Java Road — **RCIB** |
| Professionals | Engage competent professionals |
| Original vs New Application | If a lease-mod application was already in hand **before 25 June 2012**, owner may (within the window) file a New Application under (a) or (b) and ask LandsD to **defer** the Original; if New is executed as (a), Original is treated as withdrawn; if New is (b), Original continues only on **written request** |
| Fees on switch | Original admin + LACO fees **not refunded**; New Application attracts its own fees |
| Existing waiver | May be replaced by Data Centre Waiver; existing waiver **terminated on execution**; **no** refund of waiver fees or admin fees; **no** termination notice |
| Concurrent tracks | Lease modification **and** Data Centre Waiver **may not** be submitted together |
| Other licences | FSD, BA and other authorities remain required |

**SD takeaway:** Pick **one** track (redevelopment modification vs in-situ waiver); do not run both, and do not assume an existing unit waiver survives or is refunded.
