# LAO Practice Note No. 7/2006 — Processing of General Building Plan Submissions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
6 December 2006 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD 4/1010/06 III (TC) | Immediate effect | Director of Lands (Patrick L C Lau)

> Scope note: How DLOs (and other LandsD offices handling GBPs, e.g. Land Supply and Redevelopment, Railway Development) treat **GBP amendment submissions** received while the first set is still under check, and when GBP will **not** be vetted because **MLP** or **lease modification** is still open. It is **not** a content checklist for GBP.

---

## Regulatory Overview

To streamline DLO handling of general building plans — especially amendments arriving while the first set is still under scrutiny — the arrangements below take **immediate effect**. “DLOs” includes other LandsD offices that handle building-plan submissions.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New cases — first set vs later amendments (para 1(i))

| Stage | Practice |
|---|---|
| First set (subject to (iii) & (iv)) | DLO will try to complete a **full check** of the **first** GBP referred through the Centralized Processing System **within the pledged time**, even if amendment plans arrive during that period |
| Acknowledgement | Within **one week** of receipt of the first set, a standard letter: amendments received during this period will be **separately handled** |
| After first-set check | DLO reviews any amendment plans received; AP may be asked to **consolidate all** relevant amendments on an **updated** set (**amendments in colour**); meetings may be invited |
| **Subsequent** submissions | The set under checking is **deemed superseded** by the **latest** amendment set received; the **pledge restarts** each time a new set is received |

**SD takeaway:** Do not drip-feed GBP amendments after the first set is in — each later set **restarts** the pledge; colour-consolidate if asked.

---

### 2. Transitional, MLP-first, and incomplete modification (paras 1(ii)–(iv), 2)

| Situation | Practice |
|---|---|
| **On-going** cases not yet approved | DLO concentrates on the GBP already received (first / re-submission / major revision) **before** this PN; then (i) applies |
| MLP required under lease, **not** yet approved or accepted subject to conditions | A full GBP received **before** that will **not be vetted** |
| After MLP approval/acceptance | DLO may require AP to consolidate previous BD-approved submissions/amendments into **one full GBP** for LandsD; that consolidated full set is vetted **within the pledged time** |
| Lease modification **not completed**, but GBP must be processed | Similar: after a **binding contract** (acceptance of basic terms), DLO may require the same BD-approved consolidation; that full set is vetted within the pledge |
| Direct to LandsD | For (i), (iii) and (iv), consolidated plans may go to LandsD **direct** if **BD approval is clearly shown on each plan**; LandsD copies its approval/disapproval letter to BD |

**SD takeaway:** Do not send a full GBP to LandsD until the **MLP** is approved/accepted (if required) and, if a modification is in play, until **basic terms are accepted** — then lodge **one** BD-stamped consolidated set.
