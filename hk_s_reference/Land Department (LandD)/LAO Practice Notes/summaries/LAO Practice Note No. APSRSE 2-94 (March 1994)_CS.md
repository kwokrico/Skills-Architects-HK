# LAO Practice Note No. APSRSE 2/94 — Processing of Master Layout Plan Submissions under Leases
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 1994 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (R. D. Pope)

> Scope note: Sets a **12-week** District Lands Office reply clock for lease-required MLP submissions and restates the information pack. It is **not** a GBP submission note — detailed floor plans and carpark layouts are **not** required on the MLP.

---

## Regulatory Overview

A substantive reply will be given to a master layout plan submission required under the lease within **12 weeks** from the date the submission is received by the District Lands Office concerned. The information list matches Issue No. 1/1991; the sample MLP attached to that earlier note may be used for illustration.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. MLP information pack (para 2)

| Item | Required on MLP |
|---|---|
| Site / roads | Formation levels; position, widths and levels of proposed roads, emergency accesses, footbridges and pedestrian ways |
| Buildings | Nature, height and GFA; disposition, location and distribution |
| Access | Vehicular ingress and egress |
| Parking | Indication of locations for parking of vehicles |
| Open space / landscape | Communal open space; indication of landscaping and recreational facilities |
| Phasing | Stages or phases of the development |
| **Not required** | Detailed layouts such as floor plans, carpark layout |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the MLP at master-plan grain (massing, GFA, roads, access, parking *locations*, OS/landscape, phasing) — do not load it with GBP-level floor or carpark layouts.
