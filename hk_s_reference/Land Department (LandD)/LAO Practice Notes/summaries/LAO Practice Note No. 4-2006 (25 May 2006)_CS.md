# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2006 — Car Parking in Residential Special Conditions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 May 2006 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD TI/1/99/03

> Scope note: New **residential parking SCs** tying spaces to **unit size bands** (HKPSG sliding scale) plus **± flexibility**, a **carparking provision schedule** on building plans, and sample dimensions / L/UL / bicycle / alienation rules. Rates in square brackets are **TD-confirmed per lot**. It is **not** a BO carpark GFA PN (see 4/2014 for accountability).

## Regulatory Overview

New leases require parking by **residential unit size**, with a plan schedule of exclusive unit GFA + apportioned residential common GFA. Developers may later seek modification (premium + admin fee) if they want a different provision. **Absence of the carparking provision schedule can cause building plans to be rejected without scrutiny.**

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Unit-size rates and flexibility (paras. 1–3, App. cl. 21, 24)

| Lock | Detail |
|---|---|
| Basis | HKPSG sliding scale — more spaces for larger units; **table in the lease**; rates **case-by-case** |
| Extra flexibility | Adjust up or down by **up to 10%** **or** **5% + 50 spaces**, **whichever is the less** (body para. 2) |
| Sample SC flexibility (cl. 24) | ±**5%** provided total change **≤50** spaces; plus a further ±**[ ]%** on residential + motorcycle spaces if inserted |
| Modification | Different provision than executed grant → apply **well before construction**; TD consult; **premium + admin fee** |

Unit-size bands in the sample SC (rates `[ ]` = TD):

| Size of each residential unit (GFA) | Provision |
|---|---|
| < 40 m² | 1 space per `[ ]` units or part |
| 40–<70 m² | 1 per `[ ]` units or part |
| 70–<100 m² | 1 per `[ ]` units or part |
| 100–<160 m² | 1 per `[ ]` units or part |
| ≥ 160 m² | 1 per `[ ]` units or part |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **flat-size mix** early — parking quantum (and later GFA of the car park) follows the **size bands**, not a single ratio.

### 2. How “size of each residential unit” is measured (para. 4–5, cl. 21(a)(ii))

| Component | Measurement |
|---|---|
| (I) Exclusive GFA | Exterior of enclosing walls/parapet; **mid-wall** between adjoining units; include internal partitions and columns; **exclude** GFA already excluded under the lease Definition of GFA SC |
| (II) Share of Residential Common Area | Pro-rata: exclusive GFA of the unit / exclusive GFA of all units × total Residential Common Area GFA (again excluding Definition-of-GFA exclusions) |
| Check | Exclusive GFA of all units + common area GFA = total GFA in Development Conditions (or residential portion of a composite) |
| Schedule | Building plans must show (i) exclusive GFA per unit and (ii) apportioned common GFA per unit |

**SD takeaway:** The parking schedule uses **lease GFA** (after exemption rules), not saleable area — keep the same measurement method as the GFA SC.

### 3. Visitors, disabled, motorcycle, L/UL, bicycle (sample SCs)

| Item | Sample lock (TD confirms numbers) |
|---|---|
| Visitors | If **> [75]** units in a **block**: **[1 to 5]** visitor spaces **per block**, **minimum 1** |
| Disabled | From the residential pool: **≥1 per [200]** resident spaces (or part **> [100]**), **min 1**; **plus 1** from the visitor pool; location/level **Director-approved** |
| Motorcycle | `[ ]%` of spaces under cl. 21(a); round **up** |
| Dimensions | Car **2.5 × 5.0 m**, headroom **2.4 m**; disabled **3.5 × 5.0 m**, **2.4 m**; motorcycle **1.0 × 2.4 m**, headroom **2.4 m** (or as approved) |
| L/UL | **1 per [800]** residential units or part, **min 1 per block**, **adjacent to or within** each block; **houses** (detached/semi/terrace single-family) **≠ a block**; bay **3.5 × 11.0 m**, headroom **4.7 m** |
| Bicycle | **1 per [15] or [30]** units **< 70 m²** (insert if TD requires) |
| Use | No storage/display/sale of vehicles |
| GFA/SC | Spaces under 21–24 **not counted** in total GFA/SC of the development SC |
| Alienation | Residential + MC spaces assignable **only with a residential unit** (optional cap: **≤3** spaces per unit); or whole portfolio to a **wholly-owned subsidiary** |
| Common areas | Visitor, L/UL, bicycle spaces → **Common Areas** |
| Layout plan | AP-certified layout **deposited** before most transactions; no parking except as shown; alter layout only with Director consent (fee/premium) |

**SD takeaway:** Budget visitor + disabled + L/UL **inside** the common-parts plan and deposit the **car-park layout** before assignment — it becomes the lease parking drawing.
