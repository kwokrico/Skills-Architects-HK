# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2001 — Premium Assessment for Exemption of Balconies from GFA and SC under JPN 1 (Supplementary to PN 3/2001)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 July 2001 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Atg.) (J.S. Corrigall)

> Scope note: Supplements **PN 3/2001**. If the lease GFA definition already lets the Director exclude BA-permitted floor space, **no modification** is needed — a **consent letter** (premium + admin fee) suffices. It is **not** a substitute for JPN 1 balcony design rules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Where the lease “Definition of GFA” already lets the Director exclude floor space the Building Authority may permit to be excluded, a **consent letter** within **1–2 months** of the owner’s application replaces the modification assumed in PN 3/2001. If that wording is missing, a modification (Appendix II sub-clause (b)(i)(II)) is still required and takes longer; apply after BA balcony exemption, because if no application has been received by **OP**, LandsD may act to protect Government’s interest.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Modification vs consent letter

| Lease position | Instrument |
|---|---|
| GFA definition includes: Director **may** exclude “any floor space which the Building Authority may permit to be excluded” | **Consent letter only** (sample Appendix I); **1–2 months** from application |
| That wording **absent** | **Modification** to add wording similar to Appendix II sub-clause **(b)(i)(II)**, **then** consent letter |
| Major modification / land exchange | Whole Appendix II GFA/SC definition clause is used; consent letter still needed (**premium + admin fee**) |
| Auction / tender sites | Extra sentence at end of (b)(i)(II): **no additional premium** for exclusion under that sub-clause — owner gets BA green/innovative GFA or SC exemption **without** premium or admin fee |
| Timing | Apply **as soon as possible after BA exemption**; letter issued only after **premium and admin fee** (where payable) are settled; no application by **OP** → LandsD may take action |

**SD takeaway:** Read the lease GFA clause first — if the BA-exclusion discretion is already there, skip modification and programme a **1–2 month** consent; if not, the modification path is the critical path before OP.

---

### 2. Consent-letter design locks (Appendix I)

| Condition | Lock |
|---|---|
| Unit count | Number of residential units with excluded balcony floor area **≤** aggregate of the Schedule’s first column |
| Area cap | Excluded balcony GFA/SC per unit is **restricted to the Schedule** (col. 3), provided max **permissible** balcony floor area (col. 2) is not exceeded |
| Permissible balcony | **4% of usable floor space** of the unit **or 5 m²**, **whichever is the less**; usable floor space as determined by BA |
| Registration | Register the letter by Memorial within **1 calendar month**; send photocopy of registered letter within **3 calendar months** |

**SD takeaway:** Size each balcony exclusion to **≤ min(4% UFS, 5 m²)** and freeze unit counts in the Schedule — the consent letter will cap both.

---

### 3. Standard GFA/SC definition (Appendix II) — SD-facing bits

| Theme | Rule |
|---|---|
| GFA | Area within external faces of external walls (or external perimeters) at each floor (including below ground), **plus** each balcony from **overall dimensions including thickness of sides** |
| Director may exclude (i)(I) | Floor space solely for parking or L/UL of motor vehicles; or occupied solely by lift / A/C / heating machinery or similar service; and space for **refuse disposal** |
| Director may exclude (i)(II) | Structure/floor space BA opines is a **balcony, corridor, lift lobby**, communal **sky garden** (res/composite) or communal **podium garden** (commercial/industrial), **acoustic fin, noise barrier, sunshade, reflector, wing wall, wind catcher or funnel** (or part) **and** excluded by BA; plus any other structure/floor space BA may exclude |
| Curtain wall (ii) | Outer face of **structural elements** may be taken as the external wall if a curtain wall forms the external face, provided the curtain wall projects **no more than 300 mm** from that outer face; Director decides what is a structural element |
| Common areas (c) | Communal sky/podium gardens and spaces under (b)(i)(I)–(II) shall, if the Director requires, be designated as **Common Areas** (lease SC or DMC) |

**SD takeaway:** For new grants/major mods, balcony and other JPN 1 features sit in (b)(i)(II); keep curtain-wall projection **≤ 300 mm** from structure if you want GFA measured to the structural face, and expect sky/podium gardens to be **common parts**.
