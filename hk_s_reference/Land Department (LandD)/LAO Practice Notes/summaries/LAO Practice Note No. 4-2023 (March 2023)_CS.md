# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2023 — Standard Rates for JPN 1 & 2 Balcony / UP / NSPEWall Premium
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 March 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates effective **1 April 2023** | Admin fee **$54,800** (additional, non-negotiable, subject to review)

> Scope note: **Rate revision only** for premium on lease exemption of balconies, utility platforms and non-structural prefabricated external walls from GFA and SC under **JPN 1** and **JPN 2**. Varies/supplements LAO PN **3/2001** (as supplemented by **6/2001**), **6/2002**, **3/2003** and **2/2011**; **supersedes** PN **3/2001A** and **1/2018**. It does **not** restate JPN dimensional rules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From **1 April 2023**, after annual review, the standard premium rates for those JPN 1/2 exemptions are revised **downwards**. Which appendix applies depends on the **JPN version** on the lease (October 2004 vs January 2011 JPN 1; February 2006 vs January 2011 JPN 2, or later variations).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which table / which JPN vintage (paras 2–3)

| Feature | JPN vintage | Appendix |
|---|---|---|
| Balcony GFA/SC exemption | JPN 1 **October 2004** | **I** — premium **per unit** by total exempted balcony m² (2.0–5.0) |
| Balcony GFA/SC exemption | JPN 1 **January 2011** | **II** — premium **per unit** by total exempted balcony m² (1.0–3.0) |
| Utility platform | JPN 2 **February 2006** | **III** — **flat** premium per residential unit |
| Utility platform | JPN 2 **January 2011** | **IV** — **flat** premium per residential unit |
| NSPEWall | JPN 2 **February 2006** | **V** — $/m² of BA-approved exempted area; thickness **≤ 300 mm** (excess **GFA-accountable**) |
| NSPEWall | JPN 2 **January 2011** | **VI** — same method; thickness **> 150 mm** is **GFA-accountable** |

| Cross-cutting rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Admin fee | **$54,800** on top of premium |
| Rates + fee | **Non-negotiable**; subject to review |
| District | Boundary of the **District Lands Office**; lot straddling two districts → **higher** rate |
| Balcony rounding | AP measures to nearest **0.001 m²**; **2.544 m² → 2.5 m²**; **2.545 m² → 2.6 m²** |

**SD takeaway:** Read the lease’s JPN 1/2 vintage first — 2011 tables are a different (generally lower) schedule and a tighter NSPEWall thickness cap (**150 mm** vs **300 mm**).

### 4. NSPEWall — $/m² of BA-exempted area from 1.4.2023 (App V–VI)

Thickness is as shown on **approved building plans**; if more than one thickness, **majority** principle. Average flat size = total residential GFA / total residential units; bands split at **104 m²(G)**. Mosaic/similar **tile cladding** that only beautifies is **not** in the premium calc; **curtain-wall-type** cladding on structural walls is GFA-countable unless exempted by lease modification + assessed premium.

**130 mm or less** (both App V Feb 2006 and App VI Jan 2011 share this band), HK$/m²:

| District | ≤ 104 m²(G) | > 104 m²(G) |
|---|---|---|
| Hong Kong / Kowloon | 103,220 | 158,820 |
| Sha Tin / Sai Kung | 67,290 | 64,440 |
| Tai Po / North | 45,150 | 43,000 |
| Tuen Mun / Yuen Long | 37,490 | 33,600 |
| Kwai Tsing / Tsuen Wan | 67,680 | 56,520 |
| Islands | 44,900 | 41,580 |

App V continues in 10 mm steps to **290–300 mm**. App VI (Jan 2011) stops at **140–150 mm** because thickness **> 150 mm** is GFA-accountable under that JPN 2.

**SD takeaway:** Keep NSPEWall **≤ 150 mm** on 2011 leases (or **≤ 300 mm** on 2006) or the excess is **GFA**, not a premium exemption — and do not count decorative tile cladding in the premium area.
