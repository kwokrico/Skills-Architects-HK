# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2019 — Redevelopment of industrial lot — time-limited PR relaxation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 February 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 2/2019

> Scope note: Special **time-limited** lease modification (or in-situ land exchange) for **pre-1987 industrial buildings** on industrial lots **outside “Residential” zone** in Main Urban Areas and New Towns, to take up TPB-approved **non-domestic plot ratio** of **not more than 20% above** the statutory-plan maximum (and not above **B(P)Reg** max non-domestic PR). It is **not** a wholesale-conversion waiver (see **PN 6/2019**) and not a standing open policy after the TPB window (later extended by **PN 2/2019A**).

---

## Regulatory Overview

A **pre-1987 IB** is wholly or partly constructed **on or before 1 March 1987**, or built to plans **first submitted** to BA **on or before 1 March 1987**. An **industrial lot** is restricted to industrial or godown or both; a pre-1987 IB on a grant **without** specific user restriction may still be treated as an industrial lot **for this PN**. **Main Urban Areas** (HKPSG): Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and New Kowloon, Tsuen Wan, Kwai Chung, Tsing Yi. **New Towns:** Sha Tin, Tai Po, Fanling/Sheung Shui, Tuen Mun, Yuen Long, Tin Shui Wai, Tseung Kwan O, Tung Chung.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning window, lease timing, and building covenant (paras 2–4)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| TPB window (as issued) | **Three years commencing 10 October 2018** (varied by **PN 2/2019A** to **10 October 2018 – 31 October 2024** inclusive) |
| Eligible sites | Pre-1987 IBs on industrial lots **outside “R”** in Main Urban Areas and New Towns |
| PR cap | TPB-approved increase **must not** exceed max non-domestic PR under **Building (Planning) Regulations** |
| Execute modification | Lease modification letter **no later than three years** from date of **TPB approval letter**; **no EOT** unless LandsD finds strong and valid justifications (LandsD decision final) |
| Building covenant | Normally **complete redevelopment within five years** from execution of the modification letter |
| Premium | Terms including **premium** and admin fee as LandsD imposes; max intensity and modified BC specified in the letter |
| Admin fee | **Two instalments** (on demand at submission; balance on acceptance of binding basic terms) + LACO fee if applicable |

**SD takeaway:** Do not start lease-mod drafting until **TPB PR** is in hand; then execute within **3 years of the TPB letter** and programme completion within **5 years of execution**.

---

### 2. Filing, multiple ownership, and switch from an in-hand application (paras 6–10)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Submit to | Units 07–11, 27/F CDW Building, 382–392 Castle Peak Road, Tsuen Wan — **IBRU** |
| Multiple ownership | Appoint solicitors to arrange execution by **all interested parties** and confirm up-to-date ownership, unless LandsD dispenses |
| Original Application already in hand | After TPB approval, may file **New Application** and ask LandsD to **defer** Original; if New rejected/not pursued or not executed within the 3-year TPB clock, Original resumes unless withdrawn; if New executed in time, Original **deemed withdrawn** |
| Fees on switch | Original admin + LACO **not refunded**; New Application has its own fees |

**SD takeaway:** Multiple-ownership lots need a **single solicitor-led execution set**; switching onto this 20% track kills the old application’s fees.
