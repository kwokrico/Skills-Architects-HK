# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2001 — “Other Specified Uses (Business)” Zones
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 February 2001 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (R.D. Pope)

> Scope note: Planning relaxation of **OU(Business)** on OZPs does **not** by itself change the lease. Industrial-lease holders still need **lease modification or waiver** (and premium / waiver fee) before they can use the new planning parameters. It is **not** a TPB guideline and does **not** list Column 1/2 uses.

---

## Regulatory Overview

TPB announced guidelines on 19 January 2001 for developments in newly designated **“OU(Business)”** zones. Territory-wide land-administration policy on lease modifications is **unchanged**; after OZP amendment procedures are complete, holders of existing **industrial** government leases who wish to use the new planning parameters must apply to the relevant DLO for modification or waiver in the usual way, with premium or waiver fee.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Lease still binds; intended new user (paras 2–4)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Planning vs lease | Statutory planning for OU(B) may be relaxed; **current lease-modification policy and practices still apply** |
| How to take advantage | After OZP amendment procedures are complete, submit **lease modification or waiver** to the DLO in the usual manner |
| Payment | Approval is subject to **modification premium or waiver fee**, depending on the permission |
| New user clause | For modifications giving effect to these zoning changes (and for **new government sites** sold in these zones), LandsD’s intention is a **“non-residential”** user (excluding certain other specific categories as appropriate) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not design an OU(B) mixed/office scheme on an industrial lease as of right — lock the **modification/waiver** (and the intended **non-residential** user) before assuming GFA mix or street-level uses.
