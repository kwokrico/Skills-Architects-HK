# LAO Practice Note No. 9/2025 — Gross Floor Area Exemption Arrangement for Aboveground Parking
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
4 November 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Supersedes LAO PN Issue No. 3/2025

> Scope note: Aligns **lease GFA** with BD's **PNAP APP-2** (Nov 2025) disregard of **aboveground** parking spaces and car-park ancillary areas. Applies to **new building plans (including major revision) for new developments submitted to BA on or after 1 November 2025** — **not** existing buildings. It is **not** a substitute for PNAP APP-2; BA still decides BO GFA.

---

## Regulatory Overview

PNAP APP-2 paras 15–18 and Appendix C let BA, under normal circumstances, 100% disregard **not more than two floors** of Aboveground Parking Spaces; in a **site-constraint** case (split levels / stacked multi-level parking because the site cannot be fully utilised), more than two aboveground carparking floors may be 100% disregarded if the **total floor area of the aboveground carpark does not exceed twice the site area**. This PN sets how that exemption is effected **under lease**, by lease type.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. BA envelope this PN rides on (para 1, fn 1–2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Ancillary areas (PNAP APP-2 App. C fn 5) | Reasonable-size driveways, ramps, lift shafts and lobbies of car/passenger lifts, and staircases serving the parking spaces — may be disregarded from GFA |
| Normal BA rule | **≤ two floors** of Aboveground Parking Spaces **100%** disregarded |
| Site-constraint BA rule | More than two aboveground carparking floors **100%** disregarded **if** total aboveground carpark floor area **≤ 2 × site area** |

**SD takeaway:** Design aboveground parking to **two full floors** (or, if the site cannot take two full floors, keep total aboveground carpark ≤ **2 × site area**) — that is the BA envelope LandsD will follow.

---

### 2. New land-sale leases (para 2(a))

Provisions will be **in the lease**; exemption is granted at **building-plan checking**; **no** separate LandsD application, **no** premium, **no** administrative fee.

| Lease GFA treatment | What |
|---|---|
| **100% exempt** | Parking **below ground** + ancillary; **and** Aboveground Parking Spaces on **any two floors** + ancillary |
| **100% exempt** (Director's written approval) | Aboveground Parking Spaces on **Additional Floors** (floors other than those two) + ancillary, **if** BA has 100% disregarded them |
| **50% exempt** | Any other Aboveground Parking Spaces + ancillary (not in the two rows above) |

**SD takeaway:** On a **new sale site**, two aboveground parking floors are 100% lease-exempt by the SC; extra floors need BA 100% disregard **plus** Director's written approval — still **nil** premium at plan-check.

---

### 3. Other new leases and lease modifications (not minor/technical) (para 2(b))

| Built into lease / modification letter | Extra (sub-clause) |
|---|---|
| **100%** below-ground parking + ancillary, **and** Aboveground Parking Spaces on **any two floors** + ancillary | Additional Floors 100% disregarded by BA → Director **may** grant 100% lease exemption, and **may require premium and administrative fee** |
| **50%** any other aboveground parking + ancillary | — |

| Site-constraint carve-out (para 2(b)(iii)) | If total aboveground parking + ancillary **≤ 2 × site area** and BA 100% disregards **more than two floors**, premium and administrative fee **may generally not be charged** for Director's written approval of 100% exemption of those Additional Floors |
| Subsequent increase (para 2(b)(v)) | If later GFA of Additional Floors (lease basis) **exceeds Specified Exempted GFA** in the approval letter → further approval, which may attract **additional** premium and admin fee |

At GBP stage, apply to LandsD for full exclusion of Additional Floors; **Specified Exempted GFA** is stipulated in the approval letter (difference between lease-accountable GFA before and after the Director's approval — e.g. moving from 50% accountable to 100% exempt ⇒ Specified Exempted GFA = **50%** of that floor area).

**SD takeaway:** On modification/new grant (not a sale site), budget **premium** for Additional Floors unless you stay inside the **≤ 2 × site area** site-constraint envelope; freeze Specified Exempted GFA and do not grow the carpark past it without a further application.

---

### 4. Public parking vs private; existing leases (paras 2(c)–2(e))

| Lease situation | Path |
|---|---|
| **(c) Public vehicle park** in a private development | Paras 2(a)–(b) apply to **private (ancillary)** parking; **may** apply to **public** parking on **PlanD advice** at lease preparation. If public parking is **100% GFA-accountable under the OZP** (above, at or below ground), the lease/modification will **explicitly** match that OZP accountability |
| **(d) Existing lease** with Appendix I Version 1 or 2 GFA-exemption SCs, **or** a flexibility SC (Appendix II) letting the Director exclude BA-excluded floor space subject to premium | Apply for **approval/consent letter** to exclude aboveground parking that is lease-accountable (full or 50%) but BA-100% disregarded. **Premium + admin fee** on Specified Exempted GFA. **Version 2:** unless BA opines that **at least two floors below ground fully utilising the lot** are provided for parking and L/UL, **all** Aboveground Parking Spaces are taken as **50% GFA-accountable** for the "before" case |
| **(e) Existing lease** with **no** such GFA-exemption provisions | Apply for **lease modification** to add an Appendix II-style clause **plus** approval/consent letter. Modification: **nil premium**; approval/consent: **premium** on Specified Exempted GFA; **one** administrative fee covers both. Later GFA growth: **no** further modification — further approval/consent with premium/admin fee |

**SD takeaway:** Match the parking GFA story to the **existing SC**: Version 2 lots should provide **two fully utilised basement parking/LUL floors** if you want a better "before" GFA; OZP-accountable public parks stay lease-accountable regardless of level.

---

### 5. Premium, admin fee, fast-track (paras 2(f), 3–4; Appendix III–IV)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Evidence | AP-certified GFA calcs of the aboveground parking + ancillary on the **lease** accountability basis (ancillary generally follows PNAP APP-2 App. C paras 9 and 12; BD's ruling generally followed). Copy GFA calcs to LandsD **BPU** |
| **Private** parking only | Standard rate **$8,900/m²** of Specified Exempted GFA (round up to **0.1 m²**) + admin fee **$90,750** (subject to review). Rate not reviewed **earlier than 1 November 2026**. Rate **prevailing at assessment** applies; **non-negotiable** |
| **Public** parking, or **mix** of public + private | Premium and admin fee **case-by-case** (existing mechanism) — standard rate **does not** apply |
| Fast-track aim | **10 weeks** (private only) / **16 weeks** (public or mix) from a valid application. Apply **after** BA approves the GBP for the GFA Exemption Arrangement |
| **Out of scope** | Fast-track and Appendix III rate **do not** apply if the modification also varies **other** SCs (including excluding **below-ground** private/public parking from total GFA) — then conventional holistic premium |

Checklist (Appendix IV): current ownership printout; agent authorisation; for approval/consent — BA-approved GBPs delineating the spaces + AP GFA calcs; for modification — application letter + 1:1000 location plan.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the application to **aboveground private** parking only if you want $8,900/m² and 10-week processing — mixing in a public park or a basement-GFA grab knocks you into case-by-case premium.
