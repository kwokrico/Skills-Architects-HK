# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2014 — Accountable and non-accountable GFA under lease
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
August 2014 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 4/2014 | Supersedes APSRSE **1/98(A)**, LAO **5/2000**, **7/2002**, and APSS **3/99**

> Scope note: LandsD practice (as landlord) on which building elements are **GFA-accountable or exempt under lease**. BA statutory GFA and LandsD lease GFA are **separate**; LandsD **usually follows BA** where the lease gives the Director discretion, but the Director may do otherwise in individual cases. It is **not** a BO GFA code. Later **PN 4/2014A** + **JPN No. 4** (from 18 October 2021) overlay accountability for certain facilities on new/major-revision plans.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Any building on the lot is measured for GFA unless the lease exempts it. Paras 4–38 are **guides only** to elements that may be exempted. Leases without an express exemption usually need **lease modification** before the same BA exemption can be taken under lease.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Default measurement and “follow BA” (paras 1–3, 39)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Default | All buildings measured for GFA unless lease-exempted |
| Discretionary exemptions | If lease reserves Director’s right to exempt, LandsD **usually** follows BA, Director retaining absolute discretion otherwise |
| Appeal | Director may consider GFA-exemption appeals; reasons required; Director’s decision **final** |

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume BA GFA exemption = lease GFA exemption — check the lease clause first; if silent, budget a **modification**.

---

### 2. Plant, vertical circulation, headroom, roofs (paras 4–10)

| Element | Lease GFA lock |
|---|---|
| E&M plant | Areas occupied **solely** by lift / A/C / heating (or similar) machinery may be exempt; BA usually followed |
| Stairs / lifts / lobbies serving **only** non-accountable GFA (e.g. basement/underground parking, mechanical floors) | May be exempt if **physically separated**, no access to GFA-accountable floors, design/disposition acceptable to Director |
| Same features serving basement **and** other floors | Only the **basement/underground parking portion** may be exempt; **entrance lift lobby and/or lift shaft at and above ground** serving the development **and** basement **included** in GFA |
| Composite: stairs/lifts/lobbies solely serving upper **domestic** | **Domestic GFA** even if they pass through non-domestic floors; if they also serve non-domestic GFA, count as **either** domestic or non-domestic GFA |
| Floor-to-floor | **3.5 m** flats / **4.5 m** houses generally acceptable; excess headroom **rejected or double (or more) counted** unless justified |
| High ceilings that may be accepted | Voids over living/dining/entrance foyer in duplex/house; mall atriums; banking halls; sporting halls; hotel main entrance lobbies — design must not invite abuse or lease breach; **BA approval** required; BA usually followed |
| Balconies / similar (cantilevered or on stilts) | **GFA accountable** |
| Flat roofs as natural set-back on roof of GFA-accountable areas | May be **non-accountable**; BA usually followed |
| Flat roof on GFA-**exempt** structure | Non-accountable if **DMC common**; private flat roof/garden may be non-accountable if area **≈ 1:1** with the flat/house it serves; if **> 1:1**, excess is GFA unless reduced to ~1:1 **or** excess designated **DMC common** |
| Open-sided covered landscape (residential, **not** single residence; or residential portion of composite) | BA usually followed if design, disposition and height acceptable to Director |

**SD takeaway:** Keep basement lift/stair cores **separated** from tower GFA, hold flat/house F-to-F at **3.5 / 4.5 m**, and cap private roof gardens at **~1:1** or put the excess in the DMC common parts.

### 3. Ancillary rooms, extra parking, walkways, yards (paras 11–20)

| Element | Lease GFA lock |
|---|---|
| Watchmen / caretakers’ office | Many existing residential / residential-portion leases permit and exempt; **new sale/tender** sites may use a **different** exemption quantum — read the lease. No clause → **modification**. BA (location/design) usually followed |
| OC / owners’ committee office | Same pattern; **not** applicable to **single residence**. No clause → **modification** |
| Extra parking / L/UL beyond lease min (incl. flexibility) + associated circulation/stairs; undesignated areas on car-park floors | **GFA accountable**; Director may exempt extras/undesignated space from **design constraints** if designated **DMC common** |
| Open-sided covered walkways linking residential blocks at GF or podium | May be excluded if location, alignment and width to Director’s satisfaction |
| Covered walkways **required under lease** within podium | Exempt **if** the lease so provides and they fully comply |
| Covered walkway **fronting shopping units** | **GFA accountable** even if open-sided, unless lease says otherwise |
| Uncovered light wells / yards / similar (not easy to abuse) | May be exempt; Director may follow BA. **3- or 4-side enclosed** open areas likely to be abused → generally **GFA** even if BA exempts |
| 3-side enclosed open flat roof / garden / roof / terrace | GFA if abuse-likely, unless **DMC common**. Private use more acceptable if facing common area (visible to residents/management) or between two flats with common parapet **≤ 1.5 m** |

**SD takeaway:** Shop-front covered walks and extra car-park voids are **in GFA** unless the lease or a DMC-common/design-constraint concession says otherwise — do not copy BA exemptions blindly.

### 4. Swimming pools and stair access (paras 21–30)

| Element | Lease GFA lock |
|---|---|
| Open pool on **garden-use** lots | Permissible **without** modification if **at grade** and ancillary structures do not exceed max GFA/SC or other lease conditions |
| Flatted / composite residential / estate houses — **communal** open pool | May be GFA-exempt if **DMC common**, reasonable size/design, all residents |
| **Private** open pools | Reasonable size/design; generally **one pool per block** (flatted/composite residential) or **one pool per house**, **in addition to** communal pool(s); “one per block/house” may be relaxed if **at grade**. Merits: type, size, design, disposition, height, environmental impact |
| No standard Recreational Facilities clause | Paras 22–23 principles still apply |
| Non-residential **other than hotels** | Open private/communal pool (at grade or not) + ancillaries **GFA accountable** unless lease specifically exempts |
| Maintenance void under pool/deck | **≤ 700 mm** clear headroom exempt if BA accepts; **> 700 mm** may be accepted with AP justification + BA. Maintenance corridor **≤ 1.1 m** clear (private) or **≤ 1.5 m** clear (communal) under/adjacent to deck generally exempt if BA accepts |
| Open external staircase as **main access** to unit/house (to save internal GFA) | **GFA accountable** even if BA exempts |
| Open **private** stair from top-floor unit to private roof (plus communal stair/stairhood in same block) | Normally **non-accountable**; BA usually followed |
| **Private stairhood** at roof (plus communal stair/stairhood) | **GFA accountable** |
| Communal stairhood (maintenance / fire escape) | Exempt if size and number reasonable; BA usually followed |

**SD takeaway:** Count private roof **stairhoods** and open **main-access** stairs in GFA; communal open pools need a **DMC common** designation and stay within one-private-pool-per-block/house unless at grade.

---

### 5. Envelope, green features, hotel BOH, clubhouse (paras 31–38)

| Element | Lease GFA lock |
|---|---|
| Curtain wall (modern lease with exemption in “Definition of GFA and SC”) | Usually follow BA / **PNAP APP-2** (projection limits from outer face of structure) |
| Curtain wall **without** lease exemption | Literal GFA from **actual external faces** — BA APP-2 measurement will **exceed** lease GFA → **modification** required |
| JPN 1 & 2 features (balcony, utility platform, non-structural prefab wall, noise barrier, communal sky/podium garden, etc.) | Modern leases usually exempt; **pre-requisite** = BA exemption first; BA usually followed. No lease clause → **modification** (after BA exemption) |
| Bay windows / A/C platforms | Exempt if lease so provides; BA usually followed |
| Canopies | Permitted if design acceptable, unlikely to be abused, and lease exemption exists; BA area usually followed |
| Hotel **back-of-house** | Unless lease **requires** BOH in overall/hotel GFA, BA exemption usually followed |
| Recreational facilities / clubhouse | Indoor, appropriate scale/composition, common use by residents and bona fide visitors under standard Recreational Facilities clause may be exempt; types per **LAO PN 4/2000(B)**; BA active/passive/ancillary mix usually followed |

**SD takeaway:** On older leases, **curtain wall and JPN features need modification** even if BA already exempted them — lock the GFA definition clause at SD before committing to a unitised curtain wall.
