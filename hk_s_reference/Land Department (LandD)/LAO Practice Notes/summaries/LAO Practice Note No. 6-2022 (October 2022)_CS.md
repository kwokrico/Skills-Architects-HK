# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2022 — MiC GFA/SC Exemption and BH Relaxation under JPN 8
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 October 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Admin fee **$54,800** (additional; subject to review)

> Scope note: How LandsD processes **lease modification, consent and approval** so that **MiC floor area** exempted by the BA under **JPN 8** can be excluded from lease **GFA/SC**, and how **BH** may be relaxed. It is **not** JPN 8 itself. Premium at **standard rates** (Appendix III; subject to review **not earlier than 1 April 2024**). Rates used are those **prevailing at assessment**, not at application date.

---

## Regulatory Overview

MiC floor area is the floor area within the external walls of combined MiC modules, plus associated construction joints, including wall thickness, as defined in **JPN 8 (July 2022) paragraph 6**. Whether exemption is free at GBP stage, needs a **consent letter + premium**, or needs a **lease modification then consent**, depends on which **Definition of GFA and/or SC** sub-clause is in the lease (Appendix I(a) Versions A / B / C).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Lease path by GFA/SC clause version (para 2; App I(a))

Leases entered into after JPN 1 (2001) and JPN 2 (2002) typically allow the Director to exempt BA-exempted green/innovative features from GFA/SC. MiC is one such feature under JPN 8.

| Lease text | What LandsD does | Premium / fee |
|---|---|---|
| **Version A** — Director may exclude BA-recognised environmentally friendly/innovative features (list includes balcony, UP, sky garden, NSPEWall **≤ 150 mm**, etc.) **without** a premium sub-clause | Exemption of MiC floor area **at building-plan checking**; **no** premium or admin fee | Nil |
| **Version B** — same exclusion but **subject to payment of premium and administrative fee** determined by the Director | **No** lease modification; apply for a **consent letter** to exclude BA-exempted MiC floor area | Premium + admin fee |
| **Version C** — Director may exclude floor space the BA **may permit to be excluded** | Same as B: consent letter | Premium + admin fee |
| **No** exemption provision in the GFA/SC definition | **Lease modification** to insert the exemption, **then** consent letter | Premium + admin fee on **both** modification and consent |

**SD takeaway:** Read the GFA/SC definition **before** committing to MiC — Version **A** is a free GBP-stage exemption; B/C or a silent lease is a **premium** consent (and possibly a modification).

### 2. Fast track — 10 weeks (para 3; App II)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Clock | LandsD **aims** to advise terms, premium and admin fee within **10 weeks** of a **valid** application |
| When to apply | As soon as possible **after BA has granted** MiC floor-area exemption |
| AP must supply | Certified schedule of **permissible** and **exempted** MiC floor area approved by BA (**total, domestic and non-domestic**) |
| If BH relaxation also needed | Additional AP certification of identified **MiC floor(s)** and corresponding **storey height** per **JPN 8 Appendix A** |
| Mandatory docs (GFA/SC and/or BH) | BA-approved **GBPs** per JPN 8 (exempted GFA/SC; BH info per JPN 8 App A if relevant); owner/purchaser **authorisation** if by agent; AP schedule above |
| Extra for modification | 2 copies of full land grant/modifications; location plan ~1:1000; 2 copies LR printout (historical + current); company docs / mortgagee letters / solicitor ownership list as applicable; documents certified **≤ 1 month** before submission |

**SD takeaway:** Do not file the LandsD MiC application until **BA exemption is on the GBP**, with the AP’s domestic/non-domestic MiC area schedule attached — that starts the **10-week** clock.

### 3. BH relaxation (para 4; App I(b))

| Lease height clause | Path | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Height clause **with** discretionary provision (App I(b) — Director may approve another height **subject to premium and admin fee**) | **Approval letter** | Premium + admin fee |
| Height clause **without** that discretion | **Lease modification** to insert it, **then** approval letter | Premium + admin fee |

**Fast track applies to BH only if** the excessive BH **does not exceed 4% of the total storey height of MiC floors** **and** the scheme is the subject of the **planning application referred to in JPN 8**. Otherwise BH is **not** on the 10-week track.

**MiC floor (this PN):** a floor whose MiC floor area is **≥ 50%** of the total area on that floor (total area = site area covered by that floor), per JPN 8 para 10 / fn 5. **Storey height of an MiC floor:** lowest to highest level of the MiC module, including slab thickness and associated construction joints below (JPN 8 fn 4).

**SD takeaway:** Keep extra height within **4% of total MiC-floor storey height** and on the JPN 8 planning track, or BH consent leaves the 10-week MiC GFA path.

### 4. Premium rates (para 5; App III) — until replaced

Unit rate **per exempted MiC floor area (m²)**; exempted area **rounded up to nearest 0.1 m²**. DLO district; straddling lots → **higher** rate. If GBPs do not split domestic vs non-domestic exempted MiC area, **domestic** rate is used. LandsD picks the rate type at its discretion.

| Location of lot | Domestic | Non-domestic |
|---|---|---|
| Hong Kong / Kowloon | $10,590/m² | $6,490/m² |
| Sha Tin / Sai Kung | $6,740/m² | $4,820/m² |
| Kwai Tsing / Tsuen Wan | $6,800/m² | $4,820/m² |
| Tuen Mun / Yuen Long | $4,110/m² | $2,290/m² |
| Tai Po / North | $5,090/m² | $3,040/m² |
| Islands | $4,930/m² | $2,880/m² |

Admin fee **$54,800**. Rates non-negotiable; review **not earlier than 1 April 2024**.

**SD takeaway:** Premium = district rate × BA-exempted MiC m² (round **up** to 0.1) + **$54,800** — unlabelled MiC area is charged at the **domestic** rate.

---

### 5. Later GBP changes (paras 6–7)

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Exempted MiC area and/or max eligible BH relaxation **exceeds** the consent/approval letter | Further consent/approval; admin fee + **additional premium** if appropriate |
| Later BA increase in BH and/or SC **with no increase** in exempted MiC GFA area | Additional premium **may not** be charged |
| Floor no longer qualifies as MiC floor (**< 50%** MiC area) **or** storey height of an MiC floor is **reduced** | BH relaxation under lease becomes **invalid** |
| MiC not adopted / BA GFA/SC concession revoked | Premium and admin fee **non-refundable** |
| Live land exchange / grant / modification | Applicant may ask for **Version A** GFA/SC text so later MiC (and other green-feature) exemptions need **no** consent letter |

**SD takeaway:** Do not drop an MiC floor below **50%** MiC area or shave its module height after consent — the BH relaxation **falls away**, and premium already paid is **kept**.
