# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2020 — Compliance of landscape clause under lease
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
January 2020 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Implements **JPN No. 3** (April 2019) | **Supersedes LAO PN No. 6/2003**  
Forms 1–5 and Figures 1–2 later superseded by **PN 1/2020A**

> Scope note: Landscape **submission and completion** for works **inside the private lot**. **Does not apply** to landscape for areas handed back to Government or **Coloured Areas** outside the lot. Deemed LandsD approval is **not** a waiver of other lease conditions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

“Existing lease” here means the landscape clause names **Director of Lands** as approving authority; “new lease” typically names **Director of Planning** or another B/D. TPRP is part and parcel of the landscape submission — use **LAO PN No. 2/2020** for the tree-preservation clause.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What clause will appear in a new or modified lease (paras 4–5)

| Situation | Lease / process lock |
|---|---|
| PlanD or other B/D requests landscape with stated objectives | **LMP clause or LP clause**; that B/D is named as authority for submission **and** implementation (JPN 3). **No separate** landscape submission to LandsD |
| No specific landscape request | **Simple** clause: landscape and plant trees/shrubs on any part of the lot **not built upon** and maintain. **No** landscape submission or compliance checking |

**SD takeaway:** Push at land-grant stage for a **simple plant-and-maintain** clause unless PlanD/B/D actually wants an LMP/LP — that is the difference between a full landscape package and none.

---

### 2. Landscape tied to a planning application (paras 6–8)

PlanD checks submission and implementation (JPN 3 paras 3–4). **No separate** LandsD landscape submission.

| Stage | Lock |
|---|---|
| Submission | PlanD-approved LMP/LP + **Form 1** from lot owner or **RLA** → LandsD acknowledgement within **10 working days** = **deemed approval** of the lease landscape **submission**. LandsD will not consider Form 1 until PlanD has approved |
| Completion | PlanD-confirmed implementation + **Form 2** → same **10 working days** deemed **compliance** |
| Lot vs planning site | Form 1 requires stating whether lease lot boundary is **the same as or different from** the planning application site |
| SCG on lease | Greenery provision/calculation must be **consistent with BD-accepted GBPs**; attach BD SCG approval letter |
| TPRP | If trees (including OVTs / trees of particular value / lease-designated trees) are involved, deemed TPRP approval follows **PN 2/2020** |
| Class B LMP/LP amendments | PlanD **PNPP No. 1/2019** para 10 |
| Coloured Areas / other B/D landscape | That B/D checks both stages (lease, JPN 3, **DEVB TC(W) No. 6/2015**) |

**SD takeaway:** Align lot boundary, PlanD LMP/LP, BD **APP-152** greenery, and TPRP as one pack — LandsD only rubber-stamps after PlanD (and BD SCG if leased).

---

### 3. Landscape to satisfy lease only (no planning submission) (paras 9–12)

LandsD checks both stages unless the lease names PlanD/another B/D (JPN 3 paras 5(a)–(b)).

| Item | LMP clause | LP clause |
|---|---|---|
| Who prepares | **RLA mandatory** | RLA **optional** |
| Processing | LandsD within **8 weeks** of a valid submission | Same |
| Amendments | Submit updated LMP/LP with changes **highlighted** | Same |
| Completion SCC | **Mandatory** RLA: Form 3 + dated colour photos + key plan + **Form 4** SCC + **Form 5** owner’s statement | SCC **optional**; without SCC, DLO does **on-site full check**. If RLA is used, LMP SCC procedures apply |

**LMP completion / random audit (para 11):**

| Event | Clock |
|---|---|
| Minor as-built deviations allowed in SCC | More trees preserved/planted; minor planting-location or compensatory size/species changes; **no diminishing effect** — highlight in colour on approved LMP |
| Random full check | RLA told within **14 working days** of SCC; **silence = deemed complied** |
| If selected | Site check date within those 14 working days; result within **6 weeks after** on-site check |
| Rectification | Fresh SCC after all rectification |
| Clean check | DLO letter confirming landscape clause complied |

**SD takeaway:** Lease-only LMP needs an RLA from SD and an 8-week LandsD approval; completion SCC is mandatory and “more trees / minor moves” is the only as-built tolerance without a formal amendment.

---

### 4. Site coverage of greenery (para 14)

Follow **JPN 3 paras 9–11**. Landscape submission must show **location, disposition and layout** of greenery areas and greening features per **PNAP APP-152 Appendix D**.

**SD takeaway:** Lease SCG is the same drawing set as BD SBD greenery — do not run two layouts.

---

### 5. Workshop with GBP (para 15)

**LAO PN No. 4/2018** workshop applies to associated LMP/LP. AP and RLA must state purposes (landscape vs building layout, GBP/LMP issues) and attach drawings. BPU Landscape Advisory Team may join.

**SD takeaway:** Book a PN 4/2018 workshop when podium greenery fights GBP/SCG, not after LandsD rejection.
