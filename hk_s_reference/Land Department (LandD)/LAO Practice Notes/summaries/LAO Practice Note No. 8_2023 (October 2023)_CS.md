# LAO Practice Note No. 8/2023 — Streamlined Requirement on Submission of Ownership and Land Grant Records
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 October 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Supersedes LAO PN 1/2023 | Varies the Relevant PNs listed below

> Scope note: Cuts paper from **lease-mod / waiver / IB / MiC / NDA** application packs: drop Land Grant Document copies and historical ownership printouts; lodge **one plain current-ownership computer printout** only. It does **not** change the design tests in the parent PNs.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN streamlines ownership/land-grant evidence for applications under LAO PN Nos. 4/2005, 3/2012B, 1/2016, 1/2017, 2/2019, 3/2019, 4/2019, 5/2019, 6/2019, 1/2021, 1/2021A, 1/2022, 5/2022, 6/2022, 2/2023 and 3/2023 (the Relevant PNs). Except as varied here (Annex), those PNs remain valid. LAO PN 1/2023 is superseded.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What to submit now (paras 2–3)

| Former requirement (dropped) | New requirement (immediate) |
|---|---|
| Complete set of Government lease / Conditions of Sale, Grant or Exchange, including executed modification and extension letters ("Land Grant Documents") | **No longer required** |
| Computer printout of **historical and current** ownership, often **certified** by Land Registry or the applicant's solicitor **within one month** before submission | **One plain copy** of a computer printout of **current** ownership particulars of the relevant property only (hard or soft copy per Annex Part B) |

**SD takeaway:** Application packs under the Relevant PNs no longer wait on certified Land Grant sets — attach a **current** ownership printout and proceed; LandsD already holds the lease.
