# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2000 — Car Parking Requirement — Mechanical Parking System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
February 2000 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (R.D. Pope)

> Scope note: How lease **car-parking numbers** may be met with a **mechanical** system instead of (or in addition to) conventional floors. It does **not** waive other car-parking or Conditions of Grant clauses — those still apply.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Lease conditions usually fix the number of parking spaces so occupants are served. A mechanical car-parking system may be accepted under the lease if the Director is satisfied on type, waiting area (no tail-back onto the public road), layout/elevations (including light-van-as-private-car spaces), and that provision does **not exceed** the lease minimum — with **not less than one-sixth** of all spaces in the conventional system.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Approval package for a mechanical system

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| (a) System | Type of mechanical parking system proposed |
| (b) Waiting | Waiting area + assessment that it is large enough for anticipated vehicles waiting to enter, **without tail-back onto the public road** |
| (c) Layout | Layout + relevant elevations showing the system can meet stipulated parking, including **space requirement for light vans categorised as private car** |
| (d) Quantum | Number of parking spaces provided **does not exceed the minimum** stipulated in the lease |
| Conventional share | **Always:** not less than **one-sixth** of the **total** number of spaces provided shall be in the **conventional** system |
| Other lease clauses | Car-parking proposal must still satisfy other car-parking or Conditions of Grant clauses |

**SD takeaway:** Size a conventional bank of **≥ 1/6** of total spaces, keep total spaces at the lease **minimum** (not above), and prove an on-site waiting bay that never queues onto the public street — including light-van private-car bays.

---

### 2. Waiting-area assessment report (annex)

Prime objective: **no car waits on the public street** to enter the car park. Waiting area is **inside** the car park.

| Factor | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Peak in-flow | Estimated **hourly traffic volume** at the entrance in peak hours; if mixed with conventional spaces, split volumes between the mechanical system and ordinary spaces |
| Cycle time | **Longest** time to park a car at the most remote mechanical location. Tower parks: time cycle between two cars leaving the waiting area when both arrive together and use the **same car lift / same tower**. Stackers: time to park at the **upper** space with the **lower** occupied |
| Peak wait | Average waiting period during peak hours |
| Failure | Contingency plan for **mechanical failure during peak hours** |
| Exit | A route **inside** the car park to divert traffic **back onto the public road** |

**SD takeaway:** The waiting bay and failure-diversion route are SD-critical: they must absorb peak cycle time (tower lift / stacker) without street queueing, and provide an internal escape path if the machine stops.
