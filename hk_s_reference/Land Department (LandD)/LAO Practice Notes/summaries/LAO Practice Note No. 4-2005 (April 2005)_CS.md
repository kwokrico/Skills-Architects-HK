# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2005 — Procedure on Processing of Waiver for Ground Floor of Industrial Building
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 April 2005 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD 3/1900/83 VIII | Director of Lands (Patrick L.C. LAU)

> Scope note: Waiver procedure for **ground-floor** change of use in industrial premises where the lease is restricted to **industrial** purpose. **PN 2/2003** covers **upper-floor** uses. It is **not** a TPB planning note and does **not** list acceptable GF trades.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Commercial use in an industrial building breaches an industrial-user lease and is liable to LandsD lease enforcement; the owner must apply for a change of use. This PN is for **ground floor only**; owners should apply promptly to reduce forbearance-fee exposure once enforcement has started.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two tracks — OZP Column I vs TPB (steps 1–4)

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| (1) Check OZP | Is the use in contravention of the outline zoning plan? |
| (2) **Column I** permitted use | Apply to the **DLO** with a plan of **location and size** and **ownership/title** particulars. DLO verifies, consults departments, and makes an offer |
| (3) Use **contravenes** the OZP | Apply to DLO **and** to **TPB**. DLO processes **only after TPB approval**. In normal circumstances DLO offers a waiver for the TPB-approved use within **4 months** of receiving TPB’s approval |
| (4) Fees (both tracks) | **Non-refundable administrative fee** before processing; plus **waiver fee** based on the **increase in market value** from the change of use |

**SD takeaway:** For a GF industrial conversion, check OZP first — Column I goes straight to DLO; any other use waits on **TPB** before the waiver offer (about **4 months** after TPB), and both tracks pay admin fee + value-based waiver fee.
