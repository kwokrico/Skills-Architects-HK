# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2019A — Entire-IB special waiver — application window extension
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 October 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 6/2019A | Immediate effect | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 6/2019**

> Scope note: **Only** extends the LandsD receipt deadline in PN 6/2019 paragraph 1. All other 6/2019 provisions (10% Designated Portion, GFA cap, envelope exceptions, 3-year works, PN 2/2016 disposal, etc.) **remain valid**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Words **“on or before 31 March 2022”** in PN 6/2019 paragraph 1 are varied to **“on or before 31 October 2024”**. Applications received after that date will not be considered for the purpose of PN 6/2019.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The only change (para 2)

| Item | As varied |
|---|---|
| Last date LandsD may receive a valid entire-IB special-waiver application under PN 6/2019 | **31 October 2024** |

**SD takeaway:** File the **whole-building conversion** pack **on or before 31 October 2024** — the 10% Designated Portion and GFA-neutral envelope rules in parent PN 6/2019 are unchanged.
