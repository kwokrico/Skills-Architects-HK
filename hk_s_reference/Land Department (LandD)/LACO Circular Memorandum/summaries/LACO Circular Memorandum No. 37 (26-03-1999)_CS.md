# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 37
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 March 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | HOS/PSPS conveyancing moves to HD from **1 April 1999**

> Scope note: Transfers most **HOS/PSPS conveyancing services** from LACO to Housing Department Legal Advice Division. **PSPS ASP/DMC and HOS DMC** approvals remain with Director of Lands. Routing circular — limited design impact.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 April 1999, HD Legal Advice Division takes over HOS/PSPS assignment/legal-charge approvals and HOS title requisitions formerly handled by LACO’s HOS Section. Pending LACO applications without approval by that date are forwarded to HD. Director of Housing may give Director of Lands approvals for HOS/PSPS conveyancing documents (other than ASP/DMC) and HA Deed of Guarantee legal charges under delegation.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What moves to HD vs what stays with Lands (paras. 2–5)

| Still Lands (LACO) | Now HD Legal Advice Division |
|---|---|
| PSPS **ASP** and **DMC** approval; HOS **DMC** approval under lease/grant | HOS assignment / assignment-form / legal-charge approvals; HOS title requisitions; PSPS assignment and legal-charge approvals |

**SD takeaway:** For PSPS/HOS schemes, keep ASP/DMC on the Lands consent path; route other conveyancing forms to HD LAD (Oi Man Commercial Complex contacts in CM).
