# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 38
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 April 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Up-front fees from **17 May 1999**

> Scope note: Requires **payment of CM No. 27 fees before** LACO starts processing; fees normally **non-refundable**. Administrative — no design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

For new applications received on or after 17 May 1999, all fees chargeable under CM No. 27 (as varied) must be collected before LACO begins processing. Fees are not normally refundable.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Up-front fee gate (paras. 1–2)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| When | New applications on/after **17 May 1999** |
| Payment timing | **Before** processing starts |
| Refund | **Not normally** refundable |

**SD takeaway:** Budget and pay consent/DMC fees with the application pack — incomplete fee payment delays the start of processing.
