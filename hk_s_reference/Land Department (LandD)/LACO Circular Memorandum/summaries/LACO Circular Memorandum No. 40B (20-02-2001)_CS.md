# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 40B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 February 2001 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | Further Consent Scheme relaxations from **16 Feb 2001** announcement

> Scope note: Trial-suspends **public balloting** (FCFS with Appendix I order measures), raises private-sale quota to **30%**, eases luxury exemption (price ≥ **$5M**; size/number caps removed), allows non-cashier-order deposits, named attorney for ASP, and **company** private purchasers. Sales-process locks — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Effective from the CM date following the Secretary for Housing’s 16.2.2001 announcement, CM No. 40B changes Consent Scheme rules for uncompleted private residential developments (ex PSPS/SCHS/FFSS). Projects that already started registration keep pre-existing ballot rules.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ballot suspension and private-sale quota (paras. (a)–(b))

| Measure | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Public balloting | Suspended on **6-month trial**; FCFS allowed if Appendix I public-order measures adopted (also for unsold units under existing consents) |
| Already-started registration | Pre-existing ballot/consent requirements **continue** |
| Private sale quota | Increased **20% → 30%** of units under each consent |

**SD takeaway:** Prefer FCFS only with Appendix I crowd-control measures locked; raise private-sale reservation ceiling to 30% while this CM applies (later removed by **40D**).

---

### 2. Luxury exemption, payment methods, attorneys, companies (paras. (c)–(g))

| Measure | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Luxury exemption | Size/number restrictions removed; registration-exemption price lowered to **≥ HK$5 million** |
| Preliminary deposit media | Cash, cheques, credit cards, electronic transfer allowed (not only cashier order) |
| ASP signing | Named attorney **without** power of substitution may sign for purchaser |
| Private sale purchasers | **Companies** permitted; prior notification of private-purchaser identities before PA **removed** |

**SD takeaway:** High-value unit sales and corporate private purchasers become administratively easier — still comply with remaining consent letter terms and brochure duties.
