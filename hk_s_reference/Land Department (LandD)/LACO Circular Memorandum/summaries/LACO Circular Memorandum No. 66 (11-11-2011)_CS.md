# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 66
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 November 2011 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 127 VI | Revised residential ASP (post-CM 63)

> Scope note: Replaces the CM No. 63 residential ASP with a technically amended standard form for all ongoing and new residential consent applications; existing consents may apply to switch. Form update — design locks remain those in the ASP (measurements, completion, plan change).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 66 updates the standard ASP for sale of residential units in uncompleted private developments previously issued under CM No. 63. The revised form must be adopted immediately for ongoing and new consent applications. LACO will consider applications to use it for units already under pre-sale consent.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Form replacement

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| New ASP | Adopt revised standard form attached to CM 66 |
| Replaces | ASP under **CM No. 63** |
| Existing consents | May apply to use revised form |

**SD takeaway:** Use the CM 66 ASP pack for residential consents from 11 Nov 2011 — do not lodge the superseded CM 63 form for new/ongoing apps.
