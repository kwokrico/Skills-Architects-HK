# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 31
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
5 June 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Immediate effect

> Scope note: Stops sending sales brochures/price lists to **LACO**; copies go to Consumer Council, Housing Bureau, and Estate Agents Authority instead. Distribution routing only — brochure content still driven by consent/LOCM 101 requirements elsewhere.

---

## Regulatory Overview

With immediate effect, developers need not provide LACO a copy of the sales brochure. Brochure and price list(s) must be sent to the Consumer Council, Housing Bureau, and Estate Agents Authority at the addresses stated in the CM.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New brochure recipients (body)

| Office | Role |
