# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 86
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 January 2024 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 86 adjusts 28 LACO fee items (cost recovery) from 1 February 2024 and publishes a full fee list.
Fees demanded from that date use the Appendix rates even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 1.2.2024** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM Nos. 84 and 84A** from 1.2.2024 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 86 Appendix until CM 87.
