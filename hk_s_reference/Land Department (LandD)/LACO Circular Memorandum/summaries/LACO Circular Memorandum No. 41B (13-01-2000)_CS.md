# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 41B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
13 January 2000 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | Slope Maintenance Manual in DMC

> Scope note: Amends CM No. 41 Guideline 28 so DMCs for developments with **slope structures** must annex a **Slope Maintenance Manual** (Geoguide 5) and require maintenance per that Manual. Architect lock at consent/DMC stage: Manual substantially final after site formation.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Following Standing Committee on Slope Safety recommendations, CM No. 41B revises Guideline 28 (App. A) and CM 41A Checklist Part C VIII (App. B). Future DMCs with slope structures must provide that the Manual is annexed and slopes are maintained accordingly.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Manual annexation (body; revised Guideline 28)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| When slopes/retaining walls exist | DMC must say a copy of the Slope Maintenance Manual **will be annexed**, and slopes maintained **in accordance with the Manual** |
| Timing | Manual more or less finalised on completion of site formation — layout knowable by pre-sale consent application |
| Submission to LACO | **No** Manual submission with DMC approval application; solicitors must covenant annexation on execution |
| Version | Manual attached on execution = latest version acknowledged/returned to AP by **Building Authority** |
| Chinese translation of Manual | LACO will **not** insist (optional) despite Manual forming part of DMC |

**SD takeaway:** After site formation, lock the BA-acknowledged Slope Maintenance Manual into the DMC execution pack — design slope inventory early enough for Geoguide 5 documentation.
