# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 3
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 August 1993 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Immediate effect | Triggered by Land Registry Trading Fund from **1 August 1993**

> Scope note: Requires Consent Scheme applicants to supply **Land Registrar–certified ownership records and the complete Government Grant** with the application, at applicant’s cost. It is a **title/lease document pack rule**, not a design code — but it forces early assembly of the full lease (all modifications/attachments) that drives SD lease-compliance checks.

---

## Regulatory Overview

After the Land Registry Trading Fund started, government departments (including LACO) pay for Land Registry services and face slower retrieval than public customers because of accounting procedures. To stop consent delays, CM No. 3 makes the certified ownership + Grant pack a **mandatory accompaniment** to every Consent Scheme application.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory documents with every Consent Scheme application (para. 3)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Ownership | **Urban Area:** computer printout of historical **and** current ownership particulars **OR** **New Territories:** copy of Deed Register / Record of Owners showing nature of instruments and memorial numbers of **all** documents registered against the property |
| Grant | **Complete** copy of the Government Grant **including all modifications and attachments** |
| Certification | Copies must be **certified by the Land Registrar** |
| Cost | Obtained **at the applicants’ expense** |

**SD takeaway:** Treat the certified full Grant (every modification) as a Day-0 SD input — user, BC, DDH, GIC, slope, DMC, car-park layout SCs all live there; incomplete Grant packs delay consent under this CM.

---

### 2. Extra set when FSI assignment required (para. 4)

| Condition | Extra submission |
|---|---|
| Any interest in the development must be assigned to **The Financial Secretary Incorporated** under the Land Grant | Submit an **extra set** of the para. 3 documents (may be plain copy of the certified set) for LACO’s Financial Secretary Incorporated Scheme Section to settle the draft DMC |

**SD takeaway:** GIC/GA schemes need a parallel document set for FSI DMC settling — align with CM 1A Annex 2 GA drafting, not only the main consent file.

---

### 3. Applications already with LACO (para. 5)

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Applications received before this CM | Applicants may still be required to provide the certified copies if LACO considers them necessary |

**SD takeaway:** Live consent cases can be asked for the pack mid-stream — keep Land Registry certified copies ready rather than assuming grandfathering.
