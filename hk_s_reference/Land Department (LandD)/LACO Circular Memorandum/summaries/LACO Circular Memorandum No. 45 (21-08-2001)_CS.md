# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 45
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 August 2001 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Tuen Mun & Tai Po office amalgamations

> Scope note: Amalgamates/relocates LACO **Tuen Mun** (into Yuen Long) and **Tai Po** (into North) offices from **14 September 2001**. Address/admin only — **no** design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

On 14 September 2001, LACO Tuen Mun Office relocates alongside Yuen Long Office (becoming LACO/Yuen Long and Tuen Mun Office), and Tai Po Office relocates alongside North Office (LACO/Tai Po & North Office). Services continue at the new locations.
