# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 34
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
14 August 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | GPA Guidelines & Checklist for GIC DMCs

> Scope note: Replaces **CM No. 11** GIC DMC “Provisions” with GPA’s **Guidelines and Checklist**. Incomplete checklist/docs → GPA will not scrutinise and LACO **rejects without priority number**. Strong SD locks on **AP-certified GFA/shares**, **GA plans**, and supporting document pack.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 14 August 1998, all new DMC approval applications involving Government Accommodation must use the attached GPA Guidelines and Checklist. Solicitors must send Part I supporting documents plus two completed checklists to GPA (31/F Revenue Tower) and a copy of the completed checklist to LACO. CM Nos. 3 (para. 4 only) and 11 are cancelled; CM 1A Annex 2 replaced; CM 21 para. 3 modified.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Hard gate — complete pack or rejection (paras. 2–3)

| Requirement | Consequence if missing |
|---|---|
| All Part I mandatory documents + Guidelines/Checklist completed to GPA’s satisfaction | GPA will **not** scrutinise DMC; LACO **rejects** application **without priority number** |
| Pending applications already with LACO/GPA | Checklist completion **not** required |

**SD takeaway:** Do not lodge a GIC DMC until the GPA checklist pack is complete — incomplete packs lose priority.

---

### 2. AP certificate content (Guidelines Part I item 4 — selected)

| AP must certify |
|---|
| GFA of GA; total GFA of all Cap. 123 “buildings” on the lot |
| Undivided shares allocated to GA; total undivided shares of the lot |
| Management shares (if any) to GA and total |
| Separate figures where more than one GA |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze GA GFA and share allocation with the AP certificate before DMC submission — these figures drive GPA scrutiny.

---

### 3. Document pack snapshot (Part I — selected)

| Documents typically required |
|---|
| 3 copies of DMC (revised pages in triplicate) |
| Certified land grant + modifications; updated LR search |
| AP certificate as above |
| Other supporting items listed in the Checklist (plans, assignments SCs, etc.) |

**SD takeaway:** Treat GPA Part I as a critical-path document list parallel to LACO DMC drafting — especially GA demarcation plans.
