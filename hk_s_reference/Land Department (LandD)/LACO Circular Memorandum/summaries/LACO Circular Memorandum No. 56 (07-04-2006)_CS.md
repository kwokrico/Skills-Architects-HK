# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 56
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 April 2006 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Revised DMC Guidelines (cancels CM Nos. 41, 41A, 41B)

> Scope note: Issues revised **DMC Guidelines** replacing the CM 41 series. SD locks emphasise **GFA-based undivided/management shares**, maintenance of major works/installations, **telecommunications** facilities principles, slopes, and green/innovative features. CM 34 GIC guidelines remain separate.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 56 revises DMC approval guidelines after government review and industry consultation (Law Society, REDA, HKIS, property management bodies). Guidelines attached to CM 41 are superseded; CM Nos. 41, 41A and 41B are cancelled. Guidelines cover building management structure for private developments under land-grant DMC approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Share allocation — GFA basis (Guideline 6 theme)

| Rule |
|---|
| Calculation of undivided shares and management shares standardised on a **gross floor area (GFA)** basis |

**SD takeaway:** Lock unit and common GFA schedules early — share matrices for DMC should follow GFA, not ad hoc value bases, unless LACO accepts otherwise under the guidelines.

---

### 2. Design-facing guideline topics (index snapshot)

| Guideline themes (selected) | SD relevance |
|---|---|
| Common areas / parking commons / no conversion | AP-certified common plans; carpark commoning strategy |
| Green and innovative features | DMC treatment of JPN green features |
| Slope maintenance | Slope structures + Manual pathway (successor to 41B) |
| Telecommunications network areas | Space/rights for telecom facilities |
| Maintenance of Works and Installations | Major plant/works maintenance regime |
| Retained Areas / structural alterations | Demise clarity; no structural works harming others |

**SD takeaway:** Treat CM 56 as the post-2006 DMC design handbook (later superseded by **CM No. 64**) — common parts, greens, slopes and telecom must be plan-ready for DMC annexation.
