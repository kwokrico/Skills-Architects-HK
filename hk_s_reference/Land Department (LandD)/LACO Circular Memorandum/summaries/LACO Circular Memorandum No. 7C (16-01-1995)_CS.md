# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 7C
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
16 January 1995 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Amends ASP pages from CM Nos. 7 / 7A

> Scope note: Issues **amended ASP pages** clarifying **BC vs full lease compliance**, **CoC as conclusive evidence**, and adding **Rainstorm Black Warning** to weather EOT / completion postponement. Design-facing for AP estimated dates and programme; still Consent Scheme conveyancing, not BO.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 7C amends selected clauses of the residential Consent Scheme ASP circulated with CM Nos. 7 and 7A (Clauses 4(2), 4(4)(c), 4(5)(a)(vi), 4(7), 14(1), 30 and Schedule 4). Solicitors must also heed CM No. 7B on amendments; pending applications are to use the revised pages.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cl. 4(1)(c) vs Cl. 4(2) — compliance vs Building Covenant (body (i))

| Concept | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Cl. 4(1)(c) date | AP’s genuine estimate of **actual compliance with all grant conditions** (not merely BC building completion / fit for occupation) |
| BC + 3 months | May be used in Cl. 4(1)(c) **only if** it is the AP’s genuine compliance estimate — **not** automatic fill-in |
| Cl. 4(2) | Vendor must complete per **building plans** by **BC expiry**; promptly obtain/pay for BC extension — protects purchaser from re-entry risk |
| Relationship | BC expiry (lease) and Cl. 4(1)(c) compliance estimate are **unrelated clocks** |

**SD takeaway:** AP Cl. 4(1)(c) date = estimated **full lease compliance**, not OP or BC alone; do not auto-set it to BC+3 months unless that is the real estimate.

---

### 2. Cl. 4(7) — conclusive evidence of completion (body (ii))

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fact of Cl. 4(1)(c) compliance | Question of fact; excludes further time waiting for Government CoC/consent-to-assign paperwork |
| Cut-off evidence | Issue of **Certificate of Compliance** or **consent to assign** is **conclusive** (but not sole) evidence of completion under Cl. 4 |

**SD takeaway:** Design/programme to **actual compliance**, then file CoC/consent-to-assign promptly — CoC/CTA seals the ASP completion dispute window.

---

### 3. Rainstorm Black Warning (Cl. 4(5)(a)(vi) & Cl. 30) (body (iii))

| Clause | Added trigger |
|---|---|
| Weather EOT (Cl. 4(5)(a)(vi)) | Inclement weather now includes **Rainstorm Black Warning** (plus >20 mm/24 h and Signal 8+ 08:00–17:00) |
| Completion day postponement (Cl. 30) | Also postponed if Rainstorm Black Warning issued **09:00–17:00** |

**SD takeaway:** Count Black Rainstorm days (with Signal 8 / >20 mm rain) into ASP weather EOT and completion-day calendars.
