# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 73B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
14 February 2014 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 73B revises selected non-statutory fee items in its Appendix from 17 February 2014.
Unlisted items remain per CM Nos. 73 and 73A; CM 73 is varied only to that extent.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 17.2.2014** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Varies **CM No. 73** (as varied by 73A) for Appendix items |

**SD takeaway:** Check CM 73B Appendix plus residual CM 73/73A items for fee budgets from mid-Feb 2014.
