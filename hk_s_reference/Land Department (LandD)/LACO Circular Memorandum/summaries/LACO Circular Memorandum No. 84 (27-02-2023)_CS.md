# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 84
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
27 February 2023 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 84 adjusts 16 LACO fee items (cost recovery) and publishes a full fee list from 6 March 2023.
Fees demanded from that date use the Appendix rates even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 6.3.2023** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM No. 83** from 6.3.2023 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 84 Appendix until later fee CMs.
