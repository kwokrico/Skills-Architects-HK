# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 49
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 November 2002 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Automatic consent to tenancy before lease compliance

> Scope note: Waives prior written consent for **leases/tenancies** (not amounting to assignment) before lease compliance if hard conditions met (term ≤10 years, starts after OP/TOP, no premium, rack rent, etc.). Future grants get a revised SC; existing grants get an immediate waiver on the same terms. Leasing programme lock — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Alienation restrictions traditionally required Director’s consent before underletting prior to compliance. CM No. 49 allows complying leases/tenancies automatically (no application) under stated conditions, and revises future Conditions of Sale/Exchange accordingly. Terms >10 years or other non-qualifying tenancies still need consent.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Auto-permitted tenancy conditions (para. 4)

| Condition | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Term | ≤ **10 years** total including renewals |
| Commencement | Not before BA **OP or TOP** covering the premises |
| Premium | **None** paid by tenant |
| Rent | Not exceed **rack rent**; not more than **12 months** rent in advance |
| User / terms | Must comply with / not contravene land grant |

**SD takeaway:** Pre-compliance leasing is possible only after OP/TOP and within the 10-year / rack-rent envelope — do not programme long commercial leases as “automatic”.

---

### 2. Pending applications (para. 5)

| Status | Option |
|---|---|
| Application in but fee unpaid | May withdraw |
| Fee already paid | Processed and formal approval issued as usual |

**SD takeaway:** No design lock — admin choice on pending tenancy-consent apps.
