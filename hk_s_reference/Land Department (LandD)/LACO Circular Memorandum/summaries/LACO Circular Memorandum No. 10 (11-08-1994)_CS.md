# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 10
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 August 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | New fee scale from **22 August 1994**

> Scope note: Updates **LACO non-statutory service fees** (Consent Scheme, Government lease, GBHS, CBS). Administrative fee schedule only — **no** design constraints. Architects use it only for consent/DMC programme cost awareness.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 10 revises LACO fees with effect from 22 August 1994. Appendix A covers Land Authority consent/DMC approval fees; B Government lease fees; C Government Built Housing Schemes administrative fees; D Cooperative Building Societies fees.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent Scheme fees that hit the programme (App. A — selected)

| Service | New rate (HK$) |
|---|---|
| Consent to ASP | **35,200** |
| Consent to assignments / deeds of exchange etc. | **17,600** |
| Consent to building mortgage/charge | **6,600** |
| Consent to ASP/DMC form amendments | **1,760** |
| Approval of DMC | **33,000** |
| Approval of sub-DMC / supplementary DMC / separate MA | **16,500** |

**SD takeaway:** Budget consent and DMC approval fees into the pre-sale programme; amendment consents are separate line items if forms change after approval.
