# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 16
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
29 December 1995 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Amends CM No. 7 Annex I

> Scope note: Relaxes residential Consent Scheme **consent timing** (9→**12 months**), **preliminary deposit band** (≈10%, 8–12%), and **post-ballot leftover sales**. Sales/consent programme instrument — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 16 amends CM No. 7 (Annex I para. 4) so presale consents may issue up to **12 months** before the AP’s estimated lease-compliance completion date. It also flexes deposit mechanics and allows first-come sales of leftovers after ballot/selection is exhausted. New and existing CM 7 residential consents may adopt these for unsold units without separate LACO variation approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent window (amends CM 7)

| Former (CM 7) | New |
|---|---|
| Consent ≤ **9 months** before AP estimated lease compliance | Consent ≤ **12 months** before that date |

**SD takeaway:** Residential pre-sale consent can start earlier (12-month window) — still anchored to AP lease-compliance estimate, not OP alone.

---

### 2. Preliminary deposit and 20% total (ASP Cl. 3(1) / Sch. 4)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Preliminary deposit | ≈ **10%** of average purchase price of units in that sale; for the unit concerned **not < 8%** and **not > 12%** |
| Within 14 days of formal ASP | Further instalment so preliminary + further = **20%** of that unit’s price |
| Unit switch after registration | May top up cashier order (cash/personal cheque if developer accepts) for a **different** higher-priced unit — not an additional unit |

**SD takeaway:** Price list banding must support 8–12% deposit per unit with a 20% cumulative gate at formal ASP.

---

### 3. Leftover units after ballot (flexibility)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| After ballot + selection finished and all registered applicants selected or declined | Remaining units may sell to general public **first come first served** |
| Channel | Developer or agent on developer’s instructions (not agent’s own account); still subject to consent conditions |

**SD takeaway:** Programme leftover release only after ballot/selection is truly complete — then FCFS is allowed without further ballot.
