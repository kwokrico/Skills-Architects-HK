# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 40A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 July 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Lands Department Consent Scheme — follow-on to CM Nos. 30B / 40

> Scope note: Implements **CM No. 30B** luxury ballot/registration exemptions into the CM No. 40 regime and revises the **monthly unaudited return** (App. XV) for exempted units. Sales admin — limited design impact.

---

## Regulatory Overview

After Secretary for Housing’s May 1998 relaxations and CM No. 30B (23.4.1999), LACO may on application exempt units from presale consent conditions on registration and balloting. CM No. 40A revises the CM 40 Appendix XV monthly return to capture sales of such exempted units.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Exempted units reporting (body)

| Change | Effect |
|---|---|
| Luxury / registration-ballot exemptions under CM 30B | Available on application within CM 40 framework |
| Monthly unaudited return (App. XV of CM 40) | **Revised** to reflect sale of exempted units |

**SD takeaway:** If seeking ballot/registration exemption for luxury stock, use the revised monthly return format — no massing change.
