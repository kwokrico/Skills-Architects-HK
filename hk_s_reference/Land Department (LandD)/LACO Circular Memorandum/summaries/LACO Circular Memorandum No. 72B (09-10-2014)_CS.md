# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 October 2014 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 Pt.2 | Lease-approval evidence dispensed; multi-firm SD; Cap. 622 updates

> Scope note: Streamlines residential pre-sale consent: LACO checks DLO for lease approvals (DDH/MLP/landscape/carpark) instead of requiring applicant evidence. Revises Annex/Appendices to CM 72A; Cap. 622 updates; new SD for multi-solicitor vendors.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72B revises the Annex and Appendices to CM No. 72A to speed residential pre-sale consent processing. Except as varied, CM Nos. 72 and 72A remain in force. Immediate effect for new and ongoing applications.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Lease approvals — LACO/DLO check (not applicant pack)

| Former prerequisite | New practice |
|---|---|
| Applicant produces evidence of lease approvals (e.g. DDH/DD, MLP, landscaping, carpark layout) | **Dispensed** — LACO checks with relevant **District Lands Office** before consent issues |

**SD takeaway:** Still obtain DDH/DD (and other grant approvals) on programme — LACO will verify internally, but missing approvals still block consent.

---

### 2. Multi-firm solicitors / Cap. 622

| Change |
|---|---|
| New standard SD covering **more than one solicitors firm** acting for the vendor |
| Updates necessitated by **Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)** |

**SD takeaway:** Administrative/forms update — AP certificates and measurement schedules remain in the revised Appendices attached to 72B.
