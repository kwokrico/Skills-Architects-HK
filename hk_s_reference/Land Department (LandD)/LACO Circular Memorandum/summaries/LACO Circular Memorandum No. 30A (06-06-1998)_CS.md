# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 30A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
6 June 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Revised residential ASP after CM No. 30

> Scope note: Issues the **revised standard residential ASP** reflecting CM No. 30 / 29 May 1998 policy (sub-sale etc.). Replaces the CM 7-series ASP for cases where consent has **not yet** been issued. Design-facing ASP locks (saleable area, 5% plan change, 3% shares, weather EOT) remain in the standard form.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 30A circulates (via Law Society) a revised standard ASP for residential Consent Scheme units incorporating CM No. 30. It replaces the ASP issued with CM No. 7 (as varied by 7A, 7C, 16 and 25) where consent has not yet been issued.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Form replacement (body)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| New standard residential ASP | Obtain from Law Society (hard/soft copy) |
| Applies where | Consent to sell **not yet issued** |
| Replaces | CM 7 ASP chain as varied by 7A, 7C, 16, 25 |

**SD takeaway:** For new residential consents after 6 Jun 1998, use the CM 30A ASP pack — do not lodge obsolete CM 7-series forms.

---

### 2. Continuing design locks in the standard ASP (attached form)

| Topic | Typical Consent ASP lock (unchanged pattern) |
|---|---|
| Saleable area | Exterior-wall / bay / CPS / roof rules; separate Sch. 3 items |
| Plan alteration | >**5%** saleable-area change → purchaser rescission window |
| Undivided shares | >**3%** total change needs Director of Lands consent |
| Completion / CoC | AP estimated compliance date; CoC/consent-to-assign mechanics |

**SD takeaway:** Unit envelopes, share matrix and compliance dating remain Consent ASP design locks even after the CM 30 policy rewrite.
