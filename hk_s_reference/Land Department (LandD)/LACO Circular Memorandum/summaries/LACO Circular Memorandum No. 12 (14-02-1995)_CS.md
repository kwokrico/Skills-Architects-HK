# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 12
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
14 February 1995 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Streamlined PSPS residential presale consent

> Scope note: Streamlines **PSPS residential** Consent Scheme processing with a specialised **ASP** (Appendix I) and **DMC guidelines** (Appendix II). LACO no longer checks development stage, lease approvals, or AP certificates for these residential PSPS consents. Architect-facing ASP locks (saleable area, BC, plan change) remain in Appendix I but sales are HA-nominated under HOS programme.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 12 applies to presale consent applications for **residential units in PSPS developments** received on or after 14 February 1995 (pending cases may opt in). Streamlining rests on HA nomination of purchasers, HOS sale programme control, and Monitoring Surveyor oversight — so usual purchaser-protection stage checks are relaxed for these units.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Why PSPS residential is treated differently (paras. 2–3)

| Special feature | Effect |
|---|---|
| Sales only to HA-nominated purchasers | No open-market speculation pathway |
| Must follow HA HOS sale programme | Sale timing set by HA |
| Progress monitored by HA Monitoring Surveyor | Construction monitoring outside LACO stage check |

**SD takeaway:** PSPS residential consent is process-light at LACO but still tied to HA sale date and Monitoring Surveyor — lock programme with Housing, not only Lands.

---

### 2. Streamlined LACO checks (paras. 3.1–3.3)

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| ASP | Must use specialised standard PSPS residential ASP (App. I); no stylistic amendments; D of H consulted only for strong deviations |
| DMC | Check against PSPS-specific guidelines (App. II); D of H only for strong deviations |
| Stage / lease approvals | LACO **will not** check stage reached or whether lease approvals obtained |
| AP certificate | **Not** required with the application |
| What LACO still approves | Form of ASP, DMC, and Statutory Declaration (**without** annexures) — solicitors responsible for accurate SD annexures at registration |
| Sale date confirmation | Application letter must confirm established HOS programme presale date; copy to D of H; D of H informs LACO within **7 days** if dates change |

**SD takeaway:** For PSPS residential, do not rely on LACO as a gate for foundations/lease SCs — still clear those for HA/Monitoring Surveyor and keep the HOS sale date confirmed.

---

### 3. Design-facing ASP hooks retained in Appendix I (selected)

| Topic | Hard rule (App. I ASP) |
|---|---|
| Saleable area | Exterior-wall / bay / CPS / roof measurement rules; separate Sch. 3 items |
| Plan change >5% saleable area | Purchaser rescission right (standard Consent ASP pattern) |
| Undivided shares | >3% total change needs Director of Lands consent |
| Completion / OP | ASP tracks development completion mechanics in the specialised form |

**SD takeaway:** Even under streamlined PSPS consent, unit area schedules and share matrix still follow Consent ASP measurement and 5%/3% locks in App. I.
