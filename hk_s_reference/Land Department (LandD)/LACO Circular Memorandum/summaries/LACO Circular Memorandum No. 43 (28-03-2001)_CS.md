# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 43
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

Fees review updating CM No. 27 scale

> Scope note: Revises selected **non-statutory LACO fees** from CM No. 27 and adds a Small House surrender/new-grant fee item. Administrative fee schedule — **no** design constraints beyond programme cost awareness.

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 43 completes a fee review: some CM 27 rates are revised and a new fee for surrenders of land in exchange for a new Small House grant in the NT is introduced. A complete current fee list is attached (effective per the CM’s stated commencement).
