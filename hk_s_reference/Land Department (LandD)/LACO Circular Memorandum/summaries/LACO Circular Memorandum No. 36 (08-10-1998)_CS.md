# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 36
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 October 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Preliminary agreements vs consent conditions

> Scope note: Clarifies permitted **PA practices** (registration by representative; adding close family members) and warns that unauthorised PA terms can trigger **consent withdrawal/suspension**. Sales-conduct compliance — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 36 addresses PA clauses that stretch consent-letter conditions. With immediate effect, LACO approves PAs/ASPs where (i) the original ballot registration name was submitted by an authorised representative, or (ii) a close family member not originally registered is added, provided the original registrant remains a party through Assignment.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Permitted PA flexibility (paras. 2–3)

| Practice | Status |
|---|---|
| Registration via authorised representative | Approved going forward; future consent letters to be amended |
| Add close family member to PA/ASP after ballot (with proof) | Approved if original registrant remains a party through Assignment |
| Speculative flips dropping original registrant | **Not** covered / not approved |

**SD takeaway:** Family-add / representative registration is fine — design/sales process must keep the original registrant on title through Assignment.

---

### 2. Breach consequences and solicitor duty (paras. 5–6)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Unauthorised PA variations | May cause **immediate withdrawal or suspension** of consent; reinstatement may require offering purchasers free rescission + full refund with interest |
| Future consent letters | Solicitors must ensure vendor understands sale-conduct conditions; check PA before preparing formal ASP; if breach found — **do not act** and notify LACO immediately |
| Retrospective suspension | LACO may suspend consent with retrospective effect; no further sales until LACO confirms reinstatement |

**SD takeaway:** Treat consent-letter sale rules as binding on PAs used by sales staff — PA breaches can stop the entire launch.
