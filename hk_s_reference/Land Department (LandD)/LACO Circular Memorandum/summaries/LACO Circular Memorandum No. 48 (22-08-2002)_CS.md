# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 48
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 August 2002 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Pre-consent advertising; drop 30% stake proof; ASP variation discipline

> Scope note: Allows **promotional activities before consent** (with strict no-reservation rules); dispenses with proving **30% stake** in the AP certificate (foundations + finance remain); tightens ASP variation requests. Architect-facing: show-flat accuracy; foundations still mandatory.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 48 amends CM No. 40 (as varied) for residential/commercial/industrial uncompleted developments (ex PSPS/FFSS). After formal consent application and fee payment, LACO may permit promotion before consent, subject to disclosure that consent is not yet obtained. The long-standing 30% investment proof in the AP certificate is dropped because premium/foundations typically already exceed 30%.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Pre-consent promotion (§I)

| Allowed after written permission | Hard bans before consent |
|---|---|
| Publicise details (**excluding price list**); issue CM 40-compliant brochures; show models/show flats **accurately representative** of actual size/layout; competitions/campaigns | No PA/reservation form; no taking names/IDs/unit choices to reserve; no reservation fee/deposit/consideration |
| Print ads | Copy to LACO within **3 days** of first appearance; must state consent **not yet** obtained |
| Breach / disorder / misrepresentation | Permission may be **withdrawn immediately** |

**SD takeaway:** Show flats must match actual unit size/layout; promotion ≠ consent — no soft reservations before consent letter.

---

### 2. 30% stake proof dispensed (§II)

| Dropped | Still required |
|---|---|
| Prove ≥30% investment in AP certificate | Foundations complete; sufficient finance for outstanding construction costs; other usual consent requirements |

**SD takeaway:** AP consent certificate no longer needs the 30% stake calculation — foundations completion remains a hard gate.

---

### 3. ASP variations (§III)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Default | Standard ASP without change |
| If variation unavoidable | Full justification per change; highlight deletions/additions in duplicate draft |
| Unapproved variation | May lead to **suspension** of consent |

**SD takeaway:** Avoid custom ASP clauses (aligns with CM 7B) — unapproved wording can suspend sales consent.
