# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2 July 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 | 30-month consent window vs AP material date

> Scope note: Amends CM No. 72 Annex — consent date must not be more than **30 calendar months** before AP-certified estimated **material date** (RPFSO s.2). Replaces CM 72 attachments with revised Annex/Appendices.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72A tightens the earliest timing of residential pre-sale consent relative to the AP-certified estimated material date. Except as varied, CM No. 72 remains in force; attachments to CM 72 are replaced by those attached to 72A.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. 30-month consent timing (amends CM 72 Annex Section III para. 10.5)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Consent for residential units / residential CPS | Granted for a date **not more than 30 calendar months** before the estimated material date |
| Estimated material date | As defined in **RPFSO s.2**; must be **certified by the Authorized Person** |

**SD takeaway:** Programme the AP-certified material date so consent can issue within the 30-month window — earlier consents will not be granted under this rule.
