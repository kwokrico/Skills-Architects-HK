# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 74D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 74D revises the communication-network lease approval fee from 26 September 2015.
Varies CM No. 74 as varied by 74A–74C for that item.

| Rule | Hard limit |

**SD takeaway:** Communication-network lease fee only.
