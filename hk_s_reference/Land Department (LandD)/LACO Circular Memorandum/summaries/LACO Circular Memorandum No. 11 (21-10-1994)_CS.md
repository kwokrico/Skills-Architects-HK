# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 11
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 October 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | GPA requirements for GIC in private developments

> Scope note: Mandates **DMC provisions for Government Accommodation** (to FSI) per the Annex, aligned with Government Property Agency requirements. Strong schematic locks on **GIC definition plans**, **easements/services**, **structure & shared services maintenance**, and **owners’ committee seats**. Not a BO fire/structural code, but lease/DMC design must accommodate these rights.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 11 applies to all DMC approval applications for developments that include **Government Accommodation**. Drafts must include the Annex matters; omission causes delay in DMC approval and forward-sale consent. Pending applications may amend drafts to match.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Definition and use flexibility (Annex §§1–3(i))

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Owner | **Financial Secretary Incorporated (FSI)** (Cap. 1015) |
| Government Accommodation | Precise definition; preferably by **plan(s)** annexed to DMC; include/exclude parts clearly |
| Use | FSI may **alter or vary use** of Government Accommodation at absolute discretion |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze a clear GA/GIC demise plan for the DMC early — ambiguous boundaries block DMC approval.

---

### 2. Rights, easements, and access (Annex §3(ii)–(iii))

| Right | Implication for design |
|---|---|
| Shelter, support, protection | Structure must support GIC permanently |
| Free passage of utilities/services | Routes through lot/development for gas, water, drainage, AC, telecom, etc. |
| Alter/divert exclusive GIC services | FSI may change exclusive services without developer charge (with care/precaution) |
| Use common parts/facilities | Access and benefit of commons for proper enjoyment of GIC |
| Entry for works | Reasonable-time entry with plant/vehicles for GIC maintenance/alteration |
| Free uninterrupted rights of way | Dedicated access strategy to GIC |
| Signs/advertisements | Exclusive right on GIC walls/columns/boundary elements |
| Lighting / FS / ventilation access | Access to services fixed on roof slabs, walls, structure |
| Additional services on structure | Right to run extra exclusive services on walls, beams, slabs, etc. |
| Manager entry to GIC | Only with prior reasonable notice; least disturbance |

**SD takeaway:** Design GIC access, service corridors, structural interfaces, and signage zones so FSI easements work without relying on private exclusive areas.

---

### 3. Maintenance split and cost contribution (Annex §§4–6)

| Topic | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Principle | FSI manages/maintains Government Accommodation |
| Other owners pay (“the Items”) | External finishes & structure of GIC; shared lifts/escalators/stairs; shared building services/FS plant; other shared commons; basement slab/drainage under GIC if any |
| Self-contained GIC | No contribution to remainder’s management charges; no capital equipment fund / debris fee etc. |
| Integrated GIC | Pays only for facilities/services that **actually serve** GIC; cap = GIC GFA / total building GFA; GPA (or Lands nominee) approval of amounts; starts from Assignment or takeover (earlier) |
| Integrated exemptions | No management deposits; limited capital fund; no GIC insurance premium; no debris fee; no late-payment penalty; FSI exempt from fitting-out regulations |

**SD takeaway:** Decide early whether GIC is **self-contained** or **integrated** — that choice locks DMC cost apportionment and shared-structure responsibility.

---

### 4. Owners’ committee seats (Annex §7)

| FSI undivided shares | Seat rule |
|---|---|
| **< 10%** | Right to **attend** usually adequate |
| **> 10%** | Seats in **direct proportion** to shareholding |
| Special large GIC (e.g. TI / cultural centre) even if <10% | May still need **≥ 1 seat** |

**SD takeaway:** Share allocation to GIC affects OC governance — coordinate undivided-share matrix with GPA expectations, not only private sales logic.
