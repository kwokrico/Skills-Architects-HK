# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 19
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 July 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Revised monthly sales return from **1 July 1996**

> Scope note: Redesigns the **monthly return on progress of sale** under standard residential consent letter condition (y), adding first-offer / re-offer / company-sale counts. Reporting only — **no** design locks.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 July 1996, the revised monthly return replaces the prior form for all residential sales consents (including existing consents still in force for units sold after that date). Housing Branch requested extra fields on units first offered, re-offered, and sold to companies.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Extra data fields (paras. 1–3)

| New information required |
|---|
| Number of units offered for sale **for the first time** |
| Number of units **re-offered** for sale |
| Number of units sold to **companies** |

**SD takeaway:** Sales admin must track first-offer vs re-offer and company purchases for the monthly consent return — no massing implication.
