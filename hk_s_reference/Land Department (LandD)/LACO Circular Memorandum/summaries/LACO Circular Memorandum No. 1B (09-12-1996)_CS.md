# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 1B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 December 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Immediate effect | Revises Checklist A from LACO CM No. 1

> Scope note: Splits pre-sale **Checklist A** into **A1** (residential consents subject to **LACO CM No. 7**) and **A2** (consents not subject to CM No. 7). Relaxes some submission timing (approved plans; executed superstructure contract). It is **not** a BO design code; it locks consent readiness, **ASP completion dating vs BC**, **stake >30%**, and lease approval items (DDH / MLP / car park / landscape) that sit on the SD programme.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM 1B applies to **Lands Department Consent Scheme pre-sale consent** applications from the date of the circular. Mixed developments that include both CM No. 7 residential units and other consented elements must submit **both A1 and A2**. Omitting completed A1/A2 (or Checklist B for DMC) delays consent/approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which checklist? (body paras.)

| Situation | Checklist |
|---|---|
| Residential developments **other than PSPS and HK Housing Society**, subject to measures in **LACO CM No. 7** | **A1** |
| Consents where CM No. 7 measures **do not** apply | **A2** |
| Mixed: CM 7 residential **plus** other consented elements | **Both A1 and A2** |

**SD takeaway:** Classify the consent package against CM No. 7 early — the ASP completion-date rule differs sharply between A1 and A2 (below).

---

### 2. Submission relaxations (body paras.)

| Former expectation | New rule under CM 1B |
|---|---|
| Full set of **approved building plans** with every application | **No longer required** at application; LACO may call for them in individual cases and will return when consent issues |
| Executed superstructure building contract + AP certificate of superstructure construction costs at application | **Not required at application**; submit AP-certified **estimate** of total construction costs (incl. superstructure); executed contract + precise costs **when available and in any event before consent is issued** |

**SD takeaway:** Consent can start earlier on estimates, but consent will not issue until contract/precise costs arrive — keep AP cost certificates on the consent critical path.

---

### 3. Hard rejection / 28-day gates (Notes to A1 & A2)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Missing **(C) 1–7** (relevant items) | Reject; **no priority number** until complete |
| Only **(C) 8–11** outstanding | Reject unless produced within **28 days** of LACO notice |

**Core documents (C) — both A1 & A2:** draft S/D & ASP (duplicate); BA plan-approval letter; **BD 103** (commencement); AP Certificate (S/D Cl. 4); financial-ability evidence (F 1–3); Land Registrar–certified Grant + ownership printout/Deed Register; then (as applicable) DDH, Master Layout Plans, Car Park Layout Plans, Landscaping; AP/Surveyor Certificate (S/D Cl. 5) where applicable; Memorandum of Agreement to Building Contract **before consent**.

**SD takeaway:** Lease-tied **DDH / MLP / car-park layout / landscaping** approvals remain consent blockers on a 28-day clock once LACO has checked the rest — schedule them before relying on priority.

---

### 4. Development progress & ASP dates — A1 vs A2

| Item | Checklist **A1** (CM 7 residential) | Checklist **A2** (non–CM 7) |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | Must be completed? **Yes/No** declared | Same |
| AP certifies | Stage; total / expended / outstanding building cost; **expected date of compliance** | Stage; costs; **expected date of completion** |
| Date in ASP Cl. 4(1)(c) | = AP expected compliance date; **must not exceed 3 months** from current **BC expiry** (B4) | Completion date **≤ 12 months** from current BC expiry **and not earlier** than AP expected completion |
| Stake (E) | Must **exceed 30%** | Must **exceed 30%** |

**SD takeaway:** Under CM 7 residential (A1), the contractual ASP compliance date is squeezed to **BC expiry + 3 months** — much tighter than the CM 1 / A2 **+12 months** rule. Lock BC status and construction programme before promising a pre-sale launch date.

---

### 5. Lease particulars architects must feed (B) — both checklists

| Field | Why it matters at SD |
|---|---|
| User SC + brief description | Use class vs OZP / GA |
| BC expiry / extensions / premium paid | ASP date envelope |
| DDH / Master Plans / Landscaping SC | Extra Lands approvals |
| GIC facilities on lot? | Triggers GA/DMC Annex 2 (CM 1A) |
| DMC & MA approval SC | Parallel DMC track |
| Car park layout deposit/registration SC | Layout must be deposited/registered if required |
| Sales office location (A1) | “Has C of P. approved the location?” |

**SD takeaway:** Extract every consent-related Special Condition (DDH, MLP, landscape, car park, GIC, DMC) into the SD constraints list; Checklist A will test them item-by-item.

---

### 6. Finance evidence (F) — design-adjacent only

| Means | Sufficiency test |
|---|---|
| Building Mortgage / earmarked deposit / bank–DTC undertaking | Undrawn / deposited / liability amount must cover **outstanding building cost**, or gap financing specified |
| Unsecured loan already spent on construction | Lenders must join ASP and confirm no call-back until completion (ASP Cl. 31 / relevant clause) |

**SD takeaway:** Outstanding cost figure comes from the AP certificate — keep cost-to-complete current when consent is live.
