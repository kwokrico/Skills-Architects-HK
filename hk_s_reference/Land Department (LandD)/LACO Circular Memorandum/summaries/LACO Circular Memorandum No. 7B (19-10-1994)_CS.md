# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 7B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
19 October 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Immediate effect

> Scope note: Stops **house-style / vendor-favouring amendments** to the standard Consent Scheme ASP. Process / drafting discipline for solicitors — **not** a buildings design code. Affects consent programme if the team expects non-standard ASP clauses (e.g. wide plan-alteration powers).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 7B applies to **all** pre-sale consent applications (residential and non-residential), including cases already submitted. With immediate effect LACO will **not entertain** ASP amendments to the standard form agreed with the Law Society (CM Nos. 1 / 7 / 7A), except rare cases where amendment is absolutely essential for purchaser protection or case-specific necessity (those will delay consent).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. No ASP amendments (paras. 1–4)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Default | No amendments to standard ASP for consent cases |
| Rejected motives | House style / cosmetic wording; clauses giving vendor very wide powers (e.g. future alterations) contrary to purchaser interests |
| Rare exception | Absolutely essential (e.g. better purchaser protection or specific circumstances) — considered on merits; **likely delays consent** |
| Existing applications | Practice applies to cases already submitted |

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme consent on custom ASP “flexibility” for late GA changes — use the standard form; expect queries/rejection of vendor-heavy alteration clauses.

---

### 2. How to propose lasting ASP changes (para. 5)

| Path | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Desired new standard clauses | Propose to **Law Society** or **LACO(HQ)** for general amendment of standard forms — not via individual consent apps |

**SD takeaway:** Systemic ASP design needs go through Law Society / LACO HQ, not project-by-project drafting.
