# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 23
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 March 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Early consent for named non-residential purchasers

> Scope note: Allows **early pre-sale consent** for specified **non-residential** portions to an **identified** purchaser before full Consent Scheme gates (stake, finance docs, DMC approval) are met — for large/special-design buyers. Strong lock: **no subsale**, near-standard ASP, purchaser **excluded from DMC share consultation**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 23 addresses occasional applications to pre-sell designated non-residential parts (or mixed-development non-residential premises) to a named purchaser before standard consent preconditions are complete. Consent may issue under strict conditions because such purchasers are usually corporate and separately legally/professionally represented.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When early consent is available (paras. 1–2)

| Typical incomplete gates that may be waived for this track | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Required investment extent not yet reached; finance-ability docs incomplete; DMC not yet approved | Let developers respond to large buyers or those with **special design/structural needs** |

**SD takeaway:** Use this track only for identified non-residential buyers with special design needs — not as a bypass for residential pre-sale.

---

### 2. Mandatory consent conditions (para. 3)

| Condition | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Scope | Specified **non-residential** portions only |
| Identity | Purchaser and premises **clearly specified** in consent letter |
| No subsale | Purchaser **prohibited** from subselling any part of the designated portion (or agreeing to) before Assignment |
| ASP form | As near as possible to **standard Consent Scheme ASP**, amended only as necessary for the case |
| DMC | Purchaser **not entitled** to take part in consultation on undivided shares or other DMC/MA provisions during LACO approval |

**SD takeaway:** Early non-res sale freezes the demise to a named buyer with no flip, and **blocks that buyer from DMC share negotiations** — keep private/GIC share matrix under developer/LACO control.

---

### 3. Strict interpretation (para. 4)

| Warning |
|---|
| Special consent terms strictly interpreted; attempts to avoid letter or spirit seriously regarded by Government |

**SD takeaway:** Do not stretch “named non-res portion” into multi-buyer or residential marketing strategies.
