# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 13
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 May 1995 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Industrial / godown / I-O DMCs and slopes

> Scope note: Clarifies that **LOCM 91 DMC Guidelines** (as expanded by **CM No. 4** slope provisions and applied universally by **CM No. 9**) apply to **industrial** (and godown / industrial-office / similar) DMCs requiring LACO approval. Design lock: **slope maintenance clauses and slope plans** unless LACO accepts there is no obvious slope.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 13 applies to all industrial (and similar) DMCs that require LACO approval under the lease. Guidelines including CM No. 4 must be followed insofar as relevant to industrial-type developments; slope clauses are mandatory where the lease has the usual cutting-away clause or specific slope obligations.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Industrial DMCs must follow Guidelines + slope rules (paras. (a)–(b))

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Guidelines | Adhere to LOCM 91 + CM No. 4 insofar as relevant to industrial type |
| Slope provisions | **Must** include CM No. 4 slope maintenance text if lease has usual “cutting away” clause or specific slope maintenance obligations |

**SD takeaway:** Industrial / godown / I-O schemes still need DMC slope maintenance drafting when the lease imposes cutting-away/slope duties — do not assume industrial user exempts CM No. 4.

---

### 2. Slope plan dispensation (para. (c))

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| LACO satisfied it is not practicable to show any slope on a plan (no obvious slope within or without the lot) | Annexed slope plan requirement under CM No. 4 **may be dispensed with** |

**SD takeaway:** If seeking plan dispensation, document that there is no obvious on-/off-lot slope — otherwise prepare the CM No. 4 slope plan for the DMC.

---

### 3. Scope of clarification

| Covered grant types |
|---|
| Industrial and/or godown, industrial/office, or other similar grants with slope maintenance and DMC obligations |

**SD takeaway:** Treat mixed industrial/office grants the same as pure industrial for these DMC/slope rules.
