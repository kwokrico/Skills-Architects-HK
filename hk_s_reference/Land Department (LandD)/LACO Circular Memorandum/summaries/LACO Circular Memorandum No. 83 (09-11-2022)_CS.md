# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 83
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 November 2022 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 83 adjusts seven LACO fee items and publishes a full fee list from 1 December 2022.
Fees demanded from that date use the Appendix rates even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 1.12.2022** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM Nos. 78, 78A, 78B and 78C** from 1.12.2022 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 83 Appendix until CM 84.
