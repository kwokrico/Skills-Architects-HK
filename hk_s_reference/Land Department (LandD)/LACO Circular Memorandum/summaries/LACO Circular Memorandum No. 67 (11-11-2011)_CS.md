# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 67
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 November 2011 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 127 VI | Non-residential ASP & SD forms

> Scope note: Issues revised standard **ASP** and **Statutory Declaration** for uncompleted **non-residential** units. AP must certify an **estimated completion date** for ASP Cl. 4(1)(c); AP Certificate para. 1(k) under CM 54 App. II is varied accordingly.

---

## Regulatory Overview

For cases where consent to sell has not yet been issued, CM No. 67 replaces non-residential ASP forms under the CM 1 chain and the SD form at App. A to CM 54A. Except as varied, other Consent Scheme terms remain in force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. AP estimated completion date (non-res)

| Requirement | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| ASP Cl. 4(1)(c) | Completion date per BA-approved plans — AP to provide **estimated completion date** for non-residential developments |
| AP Certificate | CM 54 App. II para. 1(k) varied to include that estimate |

**SD takeaway:** For non-res pre-sale consent, lock an AP-certified estimated completion date into the ASP/certificate pack before lodging.

---

### 2. Form replacements

| Former | Replaced by CM 67 forms |
|---|---|
| Non-res ASP under CM 1 as varied | Revised ASP (with companions `67_ASP.doc`) |
| SD App. A to CM 54A | Revised SD (`67_SD.doc`) |

**SD takeaway:** Use the CM 67 non-res ASP/SD pack for new non-res consents from 11 Nov 2011 (later consolidated under **CM No. 75** series).
