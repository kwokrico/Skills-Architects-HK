# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 43B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 December 2004 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Consent reinstatement fee

> Scope note: Adds a fee for **reinstatement of suspended/cancelled ASP consent**. Administrative — no design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

With immediate effect, CM No. 43B adds to the CM 43/43A fee list a charge for reinstating Consent to ASP for sale of uncompleted units before compliance where consent was suspended or cancelled for any reason: **HK$27,200**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New fee item

| Service | Rate |
|---|---|
| Reinstatement of Consent to ASP (suspended/cancelled) | **HK$27,200** (later revised by **CM No. 43D** to HK$26,800) |

**SD takeaway:** Budget reinstatement cost if consent is suspended — avoid PA/consent breaches that trigger suspension (see CM No. 36).
