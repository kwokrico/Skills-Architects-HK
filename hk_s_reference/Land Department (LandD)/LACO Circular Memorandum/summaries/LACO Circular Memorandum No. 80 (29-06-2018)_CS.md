# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 80
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
29 June 2018 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 10 | Each offer must cover at least **20%** of consented residential units

> Scope note: New Consent Scheme condition for residential pre-sale in uncompleted private developments: each offer for sale must include not less than **20%** of total residential units in the development/phase. Parking spaces sold with units do not count toward the 20%. Immediate effect.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 80 imposes new pre-sale consent conditions to improve market transparency and consumer protection. Whenever a developer offers residential units for sale under a pre-sale consent, the offer must meet the 20% threshold rules set out below.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. 20% offer rule

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Each offer for sale (auction, tender or otherwise) | Must offer **not less than 20%** of total residential units in the development or phase (as the case may be) |
| Parking spaces offered with residential units | **Not** counted toward the 20% |
| Previously offered / never offered | Director **not** concerned which units were previously offered; count is of units in the offer |
| Remaining unsold less than 20% | Then **all** remaining unsold residential units must be offered together |

**SD takeaway:** Sales phasing/batching must keep at least 20% of consented residential units in each offer — affects launch strategy more than building form, but flag early for multi-tower/phased schemes.

---

### 2. SRPA sales arrangements

| Requirement |
|---|
| Provide Sales of First-hand Residential Properties Authority a hard copy of the sales-arrangements document on the first day the arrangements must be made public under RPFSO s.47 |

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate brochure/sales-arrangement timing with the 20% offer batches.
