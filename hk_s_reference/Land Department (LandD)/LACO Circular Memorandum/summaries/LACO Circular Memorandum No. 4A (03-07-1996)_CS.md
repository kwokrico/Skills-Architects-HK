# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 4A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 July 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Immediate effect | Revises slope guideline from LACO CM No. 4; clarifies LACO CM No. 13

> Scope note: Updates Consent Scheme **DMC Guideline paragraph 27** so slope/RW maintenance cites **Geoguide 5 — Guide to Slope Maintenance** instead of the older GEO “Annual Maintenance Work…” pamphlet in CM No. 4. Also confirms CM No. 13 paras. 2(b)–(c) apply to **industrial** as well as non-industrial developments. Not a BO structural code — lock the DMC citation and slope identification plan at SD.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM 4A applies to draft DMCs submitted to LACO for approval under the Land Authority Consent Scheme where slope/retaining-wall maintenance provisions are required. From the date of this circular, paragraph 27 of the DMC drafting Guidelines must use the amended Geoguide 5 wording below. Obtain Geoguide 5 from GEO / Civil Engineering Department for reference.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Amended DMC Guideline paragraph 27 (immediate effect)

| Element | Hard rule under CM 4A |
|---|---|
| Owners’ duty | At own expense maintain and carry out all works on slopes, retaining walls or other structures **within or outside** the lot/estate as required by the Government Grant |
| Cited standard | **“Geoguide 5 — Guide to Slope Maintenance”** (GEO), as amended from time to time — **must be clearly specified in the DMC** |
| Identification plan | Plan showing these areas **annexed to the DMC** |
| Manager authority | Full authority (incl. Management Committee / Owners Corporation) to engage suitably qualified personnel to inspect, keep and maintain in good substantial repair per Grant and departmental guidelines |
| Payment | Adequate DMC provisions for **payment to the Manager by all Owners** of costs lawfully incurred or to be incurred |
| Manager liability | Not personally liable for Grant slope requirements if, despite all reasonable endeavours, costs cannot be collected from all Owners — responsibility remains with Owners |

**SD takeaway:** Swap any CM 4 / old-pamphlet DMC wording to **Geoguide 5** and keep a lease-accurate slope/RW identification plan ready for DMC annexation before LACO DMC approval.

---

### 2. Clarification of LACO CM No. 13 (para. 5)

| Clarification | Scope |
|---|---|
| Intention of CM No. 13 (23 May 1995) paras. **2(b) and 2(c)** | Confirmed to apply to **both industrial** (including industrial/godown and industrial/office) **and non-industrial** developments |

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume CM 13 slope/consent-related limbs are residential-only — industrial / I-O / I-G schemes are in scope for those paragraphs.
