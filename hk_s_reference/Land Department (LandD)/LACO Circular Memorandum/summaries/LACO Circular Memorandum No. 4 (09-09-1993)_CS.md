# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 4
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 September 1993 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Applies to current and new forward-sale consent applications where the Grant imposes private slope maintenance

> Scope note: Inserts **private slope / retaining-wall maintenance** into Consent Scheme **DMC guidelines** (LOCM 91 §27) and **sales brochure** requirements (LOCM 101 Appendix I Part A item 14). Critical for SD on lots with lease slope obligations. Later amended by **LACO CM No. 4A** (Geoguide 5 replaces the GEO annual-maintenance pamphlet cited here).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Many Government Grants require owners to maintain slopes; private flat owners often wrongly assume Government maintains all slopes in/adjoining private lots. CM No. 4 makes slope duty **visible in the DMC and sales brochure** before forward-sale consent where the Grant imposes owner-maintenance of slopes within and/or outside the lot. Consents **already issued** before this CM are not forced to reprint brochures; **current and new** applications must comply.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New DMC Guideline paragraph 27 (deemed added to LOCM 91)

| Element | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Who pays / works | **Owners** at their own expense maintain and carry out all works on slopes, retaining walls or other structures **within or outside** the lot/estate as required by the Government Grant |
| Standard (this CM) | In accordance with GEO **“Annual Maintenance Work on Slopes and Retaining Walls Within Private Property”** (as amended) — **annexed to the DMC** together with a **plan of such areas** for identification |
| Manager authority | Manager (incl. Management Committee / Owners Corporation) has **full authority** to engage suitably qualified personnel to inspect, keep and maintain in good substantial repair per Grant and government guidelines |
| Cost recovery | DMC must provide for Manager to collect from **all Owners** costs lawfully incurred or to be incurred |
| Manager liability | Manager **not personally liable** for Grant slope requirements if, having used all reasonable endeavours, costs cannot be collected from all Owners — duty remains with Owners |

**SD takeaway:** Identify every lease-required slope / RW / structure (on-lot and off-lot) at SD and produce an identification plan suitable for DMC annexation before consent drafting.

---

### 2. Sales brochure — LOCM 101 Appendix I Part A new item 14

| If Grant requires owner slope maintenance… | Brochure must include |
|---|---|
| Notice | Prospective purchasers informed of the lease requirement |
| Manager role | Under DMC, Manager has full authority to carry out necessary slope maintenance per government guidelines |
| Cost | Each owner obliged to contribute to costs of such works |
| Plan | **Plan showing slopes and any retaining walls or other related structures** already constructed or to be constructed **within and/or outside the lot** |

**SD takeaway:** Sales brochure slope plan is a consent item — same geometry as the DMC identification plan; coordinate AP / GEO / landscape early so marketing drawings match lease reality.

---

### 3. GEO annual maintenance pamphlet (enclosed) — operational minimums

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Responsibility | Private flat owners responsible for slopes/RWs within private lots and maintenance costs |
| Inspection window | Owner / IO / Building Manager inspect **every year between November and February** |
| Timing of works | Engage contractor to complete maintenance **before onset of wet season** |
| Typical annual works | Clear drains of debris; repair cracked/damaged drains; repair/replace cracked/damaged slope surfacing; remove surface debris and vegetation causing severe cracking; repair masonry pointing; unblock peepholes |

**SD takeaway:** Design access, drainage, surfacing and peepholes so annual GEO-style maintenance is practicable; CM 4A later points DMC text to the fuller **Geoguide 5** standard.

---

### 4. Application timing (para. 5)

| Consent status | CM 4 slope requirements |
|---|---|
| Consent **issued before** 9 Sep 1993 | Do not apply (avoids brochure reprint / consent delay) |
| **Current and new** forward-sale applications where Grant imposes slope maintenance | **Must** meet DMC §27 and brochure item 14 |

**SD takeaway:** Any live or new Consent Scheme case with slope SCs must carry DMC + brochure slope plans — no free pass for applications still in the queue.
