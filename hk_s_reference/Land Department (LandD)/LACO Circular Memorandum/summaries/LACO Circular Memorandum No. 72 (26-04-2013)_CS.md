# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 April 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 | Consent Scheme consolidated for RPFSO full operation (**29 Apr 2013**)

> Scope note: Revises/consolidates residential Consent Scheme requirements for compatibility with the **Residential Properties (First-hand Sales) Ordinance**. Applies to ongoing and new consents for residential units and residential CPS as separate units. **Supersedes** CM Nos. 40, 40A–D, 47, 54A, 60, 62, 63, 66, 68. Detailed rules in Annex/Appendices (companions).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72 is the post-RPFSO residential Consent Scheme handbook. Consent remains at the Director’s discretion; requirements may vary (e.g. subsidised schemes). Composite developments use starred appendices for both residential and non-residential units as indexed.

---

| Effect |
