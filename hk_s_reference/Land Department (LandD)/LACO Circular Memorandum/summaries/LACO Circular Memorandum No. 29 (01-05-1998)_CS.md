# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 29
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
1 May 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Streamlined Consent Scheme document pack

> Scope note: Streamlines consent/DMC processing: accept certified grant/owner records; drop separate submission of BA plan approval letter, BD103 and building-contract MoA (AP certifies instead; copies exhibited to SD); allow solicitor declaration in lieu of submitting draft ASP if standard form unused-varied. Architect lock: **AP certificate content expands**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 29 further streamlines Consent Scheme applications. LACO accepts solicitor- or Land Registry-certified land grant and owner records; no longer requires separate lodging of BA plan-approval letter, BD103, or building-contract MoA with the application (AP must confirm these in certificate; copies go on the Statutory Declaration exhibits). Unvaried standard ASP may proceed on solicitor declaration without prior draft ASP approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Documents LACO no longer requires with the application (para. 1(b))

| No longer separately submitted | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| BA building-plans approval letter | AP certificate confirmation + SD exhibit copies |
| Form BD 103 | Same |
| MoA of Building Contract | AP confirms contract entered and total construction costs; MoA exhibited to SD |

**SD takeaway:** AP consent certificate must expressly confirm plan approval, BD103, building contract existence and contract sum — keep evidence ready for SD exhibits.

---

### 2. Standard ASP without variation (para. 1(c))

| Case | Process |
|---|---|
| ASP = standard form (CM 1 as amended by 7/7A/7C/12/16/25 etc.) with **no** variation | Solicitor declaration of compliance sufficient — **no** draft ASP to LACO for prior approval |
| Any variation needed | Submit draft ASP in **duplicate** for approval as usual |

**SD takeaway:** Avoid non-standard ASP clauses (CM 7B) to skip draft-ASP approval delay.

---

### 3. Revised SD paras. (3)(a) & (4) (para. 2)

| SD change | Content |
|---|---|
| (3)(a) | Declare ASP accords with cited standard CM chain (except LACO-approved variations) |
| (4) AP certificate | Plans approved; foundations complete; BD103; superstructure commence date; contract total cost; cost expended/remaining; stage; compliance date (phased if applicable); GFAs for shares |

**SD takeaway:** Align AP certificate checklist with the revised SD para. (4) list before lodging consent.
