# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 75A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 June 2016 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 9 | Cap. 623 ASP for non-residential consents

> Scope note: Non-residential parallel to CM 72C — revises non-res ASP for **Cap. 623** Contracts (Rights of Third Parties). Immediate adoption for ongoing/new apps; existing consents may amend without LACO reference.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 75A updates the non-residential Consent Scheme ASP for Cap. 623. The revised ASP must be adopted for all ongoing and new applications for consent to sell uncompleted non-residential units.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cap. 623 ASP clause (non-res)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Ongoing / new consents | Adopt revised ASP with Cap. 623 clause **immediately** |
| Existing consents | May add/replace Cap. 623 clause **without reference to LACO** (deemed approved) |

**SD takeaway:** Form/legal update only — no new dimensional SD locks.
