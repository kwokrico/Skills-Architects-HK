# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 73A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
14 November 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 73A revises the fee for approval of lease for communication network areas.
From 18 November 2013 the revised rate is HK$10,100.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 18.11.2013** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Varies **CM No. 73** for this fee item only |

**SD takeaway:** Relevant only for communication-network lease approvals — otherwise no SD impact.

### 2. Revised rate

| Service | Rate (from 18.11.2013) |
|---|---|
| Approval of Lease for Communication Network Areas | **HK$10,100** |

**SD takeaway:** Fee-only item — no design constraint.
