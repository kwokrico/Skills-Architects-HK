# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 6
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 May 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Follows CM No. 5 on HOS/PSPS

> Scope note: Reminds solicitors of **mortgage / charge restrictions** on HOS and PSPS flats under the Schedule to the Housing Ordinance (Cap. 283). Conveyancing / financing compliance — **not** a Consent Scheme design or BO plan instrument. Relevant when a brief involves HOS/PSPS stock, secondary-market finance, or alienation feasibility.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 6 applies to mortgaging or charging flats acquired under the **Home Ownership Scheme (HOS)** and **Private Sector Participation Scheme (PSPS)**. It restates that Schedule para. 1 bars mortgage/charge before **10 years** from assignment plus premium, and that any mortgage/charge outside Director of Housing written approval and authorised terms is **unauthorised** and a Schedule breach (actionable under the ASP/Assignment).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Schedule bar on mortgage/charge (paras. 2–3)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Default prohibition | No mortgage/charge before **10 years** from assignment **and** payment of Schedule premium |
| Permitted exception (Sch. para. 4) | Only with **prior written approval** of the Director of Housing **and** on terms he authorises |
| Unauthorised mortgage/charge | Treated as breach of Schedule → breach of HOS/PSPS ASP and Assignment |

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume HOS/PSPS units can be charged like private stock — finance and exit strategy must track Housing approval and the 10-year/premium clock.

---

### 2. Deed of Guarantee form lock (paras. 5–6)

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Authorised mortgages under Deeds of Guarantee | Must **not differ** from the prescribed form between HA and participating lenders |
| Any variation | Needs **prior Director approval**; without it, no benefit of the Deed of Guarantee |
| Other authorised mortgages | Still need Director approval first |

**SD takeaway:** Flag early if project/finance narrative relies on HOS/PSPS chargeability — form and approval are Housing-controlled, not AP-design issues, but they constrain feasibility.
