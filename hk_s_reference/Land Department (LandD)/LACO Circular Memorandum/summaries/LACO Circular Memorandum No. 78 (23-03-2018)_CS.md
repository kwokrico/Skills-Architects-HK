# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 78
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 March 2018 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 78 issues a revised LACO fee list effective from 29 March 2018.
Fees demanded from that date use the Appendix rates even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 29.3.2018** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM Nos. 76, 76A, 76B, 76C and 76D** from 29.3.2018 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 78 Appendix until later fee CMs.
