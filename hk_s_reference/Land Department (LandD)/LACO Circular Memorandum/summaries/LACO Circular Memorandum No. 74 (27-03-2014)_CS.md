# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 74
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
27 March 2014 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 74 issues a complete LACO fee list effective from 28 March 2014.
Fees demanded from that date use the Appendix rates even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 28.3.2014** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM Nos. 73, 73A, 73B and 73C** from 28.3.2014 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 74 Appendix until later fee CMs.
