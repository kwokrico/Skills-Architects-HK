# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 79
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt 10 | Standard DMC clauses + revised DMC approval guidelines

> Scope note: Issues **standard DMC clauses** (Appendix I) and updated/consolidated **guidelines for approval of DMCs** to speed DMC processing. Architect-facing where DMC common-parts, manager powers, and green-feature covenants affect schematic briefing.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 79 provides standard DMC clauses for practitioners preparing Deeds of Mutual Covenant and consolidates LACO guidelines for DMC approval. Use of the standard clauses is intended to expedite approval.
