# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 77
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 July 2017 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 10 | Consent to assign before land-grant compliance

> Scope note: Director's consent for ASP/assignment **prior to Certificate of Compliance** under the standard restriction-on-alienation-before-compliance lease clause. Applies to residential, non-residential and mixed developments. Programme/CC sequencing impact.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Under the standard land-grant restriction on alienation before compliance, Director of Lands consent is required for ASPs and assignments before Certificate of Compliance. CM No. 77 states LACO will not process consent-to-assign in parallel with a CC application when the developer can (or should) apply for CC.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. No parallel consent-to-assign vs CC

| Situation | LACO practice |
|---|---|
| Developer has applied, or is in a position to apply, for **Certificate of Compliance**, or no good reason why CC cannot be applied for | Consent-to-assign application **not entertained / rejected**; fee **not refunded** |

**SD takeaway:** Do not run consent-to-assign and CC in parallel — finish lease-compliance works and apply for CC when ready.

---

### 2. Phased developments

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Phased construction | Consent to assign may be considered for **each phase** (not a part of a phase) |
| Last phase | Apply for **Certificate of Compliance** instead |

**SD takeaway:** Size assignable phases to match consent-to-assign phasing; last phase must go via CC.

---

### 3. OP without pre-sale consent

| Rule |
|---|
| Consent to assign may be applied for once **Occupation Permit** is issued, even if no pre-sale consent was sought or obtained |
| Pre-sale consent is **not** a prerequisite |
| If a pre-sale consent application is still under processing when consent-to-assign is lodged, the pre-sale application **stops** and its fee is **not refunded** |

**SD takeaway:** Post-OP assignment path exists without pre-sale consent — but lodging consent-to-assign kills any ongoing pre-sale application.
