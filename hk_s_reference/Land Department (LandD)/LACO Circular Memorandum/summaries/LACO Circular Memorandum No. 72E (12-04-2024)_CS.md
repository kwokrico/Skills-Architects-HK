# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72E
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 April 2024 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Electronic submission option for residential pre-sale consent

> Scope note: Allows **paper or electronic** lodging of residential consent applications; streamlines procedures. Certified copy of Government Grant no longer required from developer's solicitors at application. Admin/process focus.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72E introduces electronic submission as an alternative to paper for consent to sell residential units in uncompleted developments. Detailed paper vs e-submission procedures are in the attached Annex; supporting form pack is updated.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Paper vs electronic submission

| Mode | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Paper | Deliver original hard copies of all supporting documents at application |
| Electronic | Email electronic copies at application; deliver originals of **specified key** documents within the prescribed time limit |

**SD takeaway:** AP certificates and plans must still meet Annex content — only lodging channel changes.

---

### 2. Land grant / land search

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Certified copy of Government Grant with application | **No longer required** from developer's solicitors |

**SD takeaway:** Lease still governs design — but solicitor need not lodge certified grant copy at consent filing under this CM.
