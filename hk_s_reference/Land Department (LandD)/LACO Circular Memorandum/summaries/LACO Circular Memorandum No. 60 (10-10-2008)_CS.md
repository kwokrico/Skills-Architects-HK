# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 60
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 October 2008 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Revised ASP — Saleable Area vs Other Areas

> Scope note: Issues revised residential Consent Scheme **ASP** separating **Saleable Area** from **Other Areas** (bay, roofs, gardens, CPS, A/C plant room, etc.) with new Sch. 4 Measurements. Direct schematic measurement lock; Chinese term 實用面積 fixed for Saleable Area.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 10 October 2008, new and pending (not yet approved) pre-sale consent applications must use the Annex I revised ASP. App. XII (sales brochures) under CM 40C is revised. Variations and any new feature in Saleable Area or Other Areas need prior LACO approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Saleable Area vs Other Areas (paras. 3, 6)

| Concept | Content |
|---|---|
| Saleable Area (Cl. 1(1)(u); Sch. 4 Part A) | Unit SA including balcony / utility platform / verandah as defined |
| Other Areas (Cl. 1(1)(q); Sch. 4 Part B) | Separately measured: bay window, flat roof, roof, garden, cockloft, yard, terrace, car parking space, air-conditioning plant room, etc. |
| A/C platform (Sch. 4 Part C) | If sold with Property, measured in Part C (not Part A SA) |
| Chinese | “Saleable Area” = **實用面積** |
| Plan-change clauses | Cl. 10(d), 20(1)/(2) refer to **Measurements** of the Property, not only “saleable area” |

**SD takeaway:** Split brochure/ASP area schedules into SA (Part A) vs Other Areas (Part B) vs A/C platform (Part C) — do not dump bay/roof/garden into SA.

---

### 2. Brochure and variation discipline (paras. 4–5)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Brochures | Comply with revised App. XII for all new consents from this CM |
| ASP variations | Prior LACO approval; not normally entertained |
| New SA/Other Area features | Prior LACO approval before adding to Sch. 4 |

**SD takeaway:** Novel green features or area types need LACO buy-in before freezing Sch. 4 — programme consultation time.
