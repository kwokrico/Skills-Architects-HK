# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 40
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 May 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Consolidated Consent Scheme rules (ex PSPS / SCHS / FFSS)

> Scope note: **Consolidates** then-current Lands Department Consent Scheme instructions for sale of units in uncompleted **private residential** developments into one CM (with appendices). Primary architect locks: **≥30% stake**, AP certifications (foundations, BD103, costs, compliance ≤ BC+3 months), **CPS bundling (≤2 with unit)**, saleable-area brochure definition, and private-sale **≤20%** (later varied by 40B/40D).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 40 restates Consent Scheme purposes (ensure construction completion arrangements; fair pre-sale practice; healthy market policy) and gathers prevailing LOCM/LACO requirements so solicitors need not chase superseded circulars. It applies to private residential uncompleted sales requiring Director’s consent before Certificate of Compliance (excluding PSPS, Sandwich Class Housing and Flat for Sale Scheme).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Stake, progress and compliance dating (§§3–4)

| Gate | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Stake | Developer must have invested ≥ **30%** of total land + development cost (formulas for ordinary / Exclusion Order cases) |
| AP certificate | Approved plans; foundations complete; BD103; building contract/costs; stage; estimated compliance |
| Compliance vs BC | AP estimated compliance date generally **≤ 3 months** after current BC expiry; if later, **extend BC before consent** |
| ASP completion date | Not later than **3 months** after BC period expiry (per consolidated terms) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme consent until stake ≥30% and AP compliance date sits inside BC+3 months (or BC is extended).

---

### 2. Sale conduct — units, CPS, deposits (§11.1)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Units per purchaser | Limited to **one unit** per purchaser (later abolished by **CM No. 40D**) |
| Car parks | Max **2** CPS with each unit; CPS sold only with a residential unit (or to someone who already has ASP for a unit) |
| Preliminary deposit | ≈ **5%** of average purchase price of units in that sale |
| Stakeholder handover | Full preliminary deposit to stakeholders within **3 working days** |
| Cancellation forfeiture | Typically **5%** of purchase price (or actual preliminary deposit if less) |

**SD takeaway:** Design sales packaging around bundled residential CPS (≤2) and ~5% deposit mechanics in the then-current consent letter.

---

### 3. Private vs public sale (§§11.2–11.3; luxury exemption)

| Topic | Hard rule (as consolidated; later varied) |
|---|---|
| Private sale quota | ≤ **20%** of total residential units ex CPS (raised to 30% by **40B**; removed by **40D**) |
| Private buyers | Natural persons only under CM 40 (companies later allowed by **40B**) |
| Luxury ballot/registration exemption | Guidelines include ≤~100 units and price thresholds (later revised by **40A/40B**) |
| Brochure / saleable area | Appendix definition of Saleable Area + fittings/finishes list for sales brochure |

**SD takeaway:** Use CM 40 as the base Consent Scheme handbook, then overlay later 40-series amendments for quota/ballot/deposit payment methods.

---

### 4. Share allocation in Statutory Declaration

| Rule |
|---|
| Basis (Architect/AP/Surveyor-certified) for allocating undivided shares to common areas and management shares to units must be specified in the SD |

**SD takeaway:** Prefer clear GFA-based share matrices early — same practical lock as CM No. 8 “common basis”.
