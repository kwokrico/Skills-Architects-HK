# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 26
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
19 November 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Hotline from **1 November 1997**

> Scope note: Announces a public **24-hour hotline (2147 5475)** for consents to sell uncompleted residential units (issued in past six months and pending). Customer-service / transparency tool — **no** schematic design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 26 introduces Lands Department’s telephone enquiry hotline on consents to sell residential units. Callers can obtain summary/detailed reports on consents issued or pending, including development names/units, estimated completion, developer and solicitor firm, plus statistics.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Hotline content (body)

| Available info |
|---|
| Consents issued (past 6 months) and cases pending approval |
| Names and units; estimated completion; developer; solicitors’ firm |
| Statistics on total consents and units |
| Languages: English and Cantonese (User Guide at LACO district offices) |

**SD takeaway:** No design lock — use the hotline only as a market/consent-status check when programming competing launches.
