# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 39
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 May 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | ASP Cl. 3(3) and 12(2) amendments

> Scope note: Amends standard Consent Scheme ASP to allow **immediate full-balance payment** options and to bar objection where vendor’s interest on completion is **equitable only** (post consent-to-assign). Conveyancing form tweak — limited SD impact.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 39 amends approved ASP Cl. 3(3) (withholding balance until vendor can validly assign) so the withhold right does **not** apply where Schedule 4 opts for full balance within a stated days of signing. New Cl. 12(2) provides purchaser shall raise no objection if vendor’s interest is equitable rather than a legal estate. Usable for existing and future consents.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Immediate-payment option (Cl. 3(3))

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Default | Purchaser may withhold balance until vendor can validly assign + **14 days’** notice |
| Carve-out | If purchaser opted (Sch. 4) to pay full balance within stated days of signing → Cl. 3(3) **does not apply** |

**SD takeaway:** Early-payment ASP options can precede CoC — programme financing/sales terms accordingly; completion still needs valid assignability under other clauses.

---

### 2. Equitable interest on completion (Cl. 12(2))

| Rule |
|---|
| Purchaser shall raise no objection if vendor’s interest is equitable and not a legal estate |

**SD takeaway:** Consent-to-assign completions may convey equitable interest only — flag in sales/completion expectations, not in GA design.
