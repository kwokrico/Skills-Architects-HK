# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 33
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 July 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | M&A alterations under recreational leases

> Scope note: Routes applications to alter **Memorandum & Articles** under recreational leases (and Companies Ordinance referrals) to **district LACO Senior Solicitors**, with policy support from HAB Recreation and Sports Division (or other bureau). Corporate/lease consent process — **not** a buildings design code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Many recreational leases bar M&A changes affecting objects or lease obligations without Director of Lands written consent. LACO will process such applications (including those referred by the Registrar of Companies under Cap. 32 s.21(6)(b)), subject to case-by-case policy support; DLOs will no longer handle them.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Application routing (paras. 3–4)

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Address applications to | Senior Solicitors in relevant **district LACO** offices |
| Approval issuer | LACO on behalf of Director of Lands (or former RG(LO) as applicable) |
| Policy support | HAB Recreation and Sports Division or other appropriate bureau, case by case |
| DLO role | **Not** involved in future |

**SD takeaway:** Clubhouse/recreational-lease corporate changes are a LACO+bureau consent path — flag early if a project sits on such a lease and needs M&A changes.
