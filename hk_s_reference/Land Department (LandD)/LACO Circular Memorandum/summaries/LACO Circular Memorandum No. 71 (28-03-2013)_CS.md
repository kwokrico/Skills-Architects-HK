# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 71
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 March 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 | RPFSO transitional arrangements (operation **29 April 2013**)

> Scope note: Requires modification of **subsisting** pre-sale consents so sales on/after RPFSO operation date use Ordinance-compatible ASP/consent conditions. Vendors must apply via solicitors; no sale of unsold residential units under unmodified subsisting consent on/after 29 Apr 2013.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The Residential Properties (First-hand Sales) Ordinance comes into full operation on 29 April 2013. CM No. 71 sets transitional rules for consents issued before that date covering residential units and parking spaces constituting separate units.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Modification gate before post-Operation sales

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Sales on/after Operation Date under subsisting consent | Consent conditions must be modified; adopt new standard ASP with mandatory Ordinance provisions |
| Process | Vendor applies through solicitors for modification approval |
| Without approval | **Must not** proceed to sell unsold residential units under that consent on/after **29 Apr 2013** |

**SD takeaway:** Treat 29 Apr 2013 as a hard cut-over — unfinished launches need LACO modification approval before continuing sales.
