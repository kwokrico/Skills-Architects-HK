# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 7A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 July 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Supplementary to CM No. 7

> Scope note: Issues a **further revised residential ASP** after CM No. 7. Conveyancing form update — architect-facing locks are the **phased completion wording**, **14-day CoC/consent-to-assign application**, and clarified **cancellation / deposit forfeiture** rules. Not a BO design code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 7A supplements CM No. 7’s residential Consent Scheme ASP. It circulates a further revised ASP (via Law Society disk/hard copy) incorporating practitioner comments; significant design-facing changes are listed in the body paragraphs (i)–(v).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Phased development and CoC application (Cl. 4 amendments)

| Change | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Cl. 4(1)(c) option | For phased developments: complete Buildings and comply with grant **insofar as they relate to the Buildings** |
| New Cl. 4(6) | Vendor must apply for **Certificate of Compliance** or **consent to assign** within **14 days** of completing Building/Development as in Cl. 4(1)(c) |
| Former Cl. 4(6) → 4(7) | Clarifies when Building/Development is treated as completed for the clause |

**SD takeaway:** Phase sales only with consent-granted phase wording; programme CoC/consent-to-assign filing within **14 days** of the ASP compliance date.

---

### 2. Cancellation and deposit forfeiture (Cl. 11(3), 16(2)(a))

| Topic | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Agreed cancellation | Vendor may retain **5%** of purchase price as consideration; purchaser pays related legal costs |
| Purchaser default termination | Only sums paid as **deposit** (Sch. 4 item) may be forfeited — not all moneys paid |

**SD takeaway:** 5% cancellation retention is contractual; default forfeiture is deposit-only — coordinate sales terms with the revised ASP, not house forms.

---

### 3. Purchaser definition (Sch. 1)

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| “Purchaser” | Includes executors/administrators; **excludes** assignees, attorneys, nominees, trustees or other substitutes |

**SD takeaway:** Reinforces no ASP benefit transfer before Assignment — keep sales / nomination assumptions aligned with Consent Scheme ASP.
