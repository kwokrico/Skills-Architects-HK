# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 63
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
1 September 2010 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 V | Anti-speculation — 10% forfeiture; no sub-sale

> Scope note: For consents issued on/after **13 August 2010**, raises cancellation/forfeiture to **10%** of purchase price and **prohibits** nomination/sub-sale/transfer of ASP benefit before Assignment. Sales-contract lock — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Implementing Financial Secretary measures of 13 August 2010, CM No. 63 applies to all consents to sell uncompleted residential units issued on or after that date. Residential ASP form and CM 62 Annex I paras. B12 and B17 are revised accordingly. En bloc sales no longer need certain undertakings previously tied to sub-sale permission.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Hard sales locks (body)

| Measure | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Forfeiture / cancellation amount | Increased from **5% → 10%** of purchase price (fail to sign formal ASP after PA, or later cancel ASP) |
| Sub-sale / nomination / transfer of ASP benefit | **Prohibited** before completion and Assignment |
| Applies to | Consents issued on/after **13 Aug 2010** |

**SD takeaway:** Programme and sales terms must assume no ASP flipping and a 10% walk-away cost — not a 5% deposit world.
