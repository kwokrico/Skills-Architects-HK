# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 73
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 August 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 73 sets a complete list of LACO non-statutory fees effective from 19 August 2013.
Fees demanded from that date use the revised Appendix rates even if the application was made earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 19.8.2013** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM Nos. 69, 70 and 70A** from 19.8.2013 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to the CM 73 Appendix until later fee CMs (74/76/78/83/84/86/87 series).
