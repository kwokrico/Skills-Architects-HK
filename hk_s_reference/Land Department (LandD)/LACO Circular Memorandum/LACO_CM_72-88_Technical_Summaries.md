# LACO Circular Memoranda (Nos. 72–88) — Technical Design Summaries

**Scope:** LACO Circular Memoranda Nos. 72–88 (including lettered variants) from this library’s `source_md/*_CS.md`.
**Focus:** Consent Scheme / ASP / DMC / saleable-area measurement, lease-approval gates (DDH / MLP / landscaping), AP certificates, purchaser/rescission thresholds.
**Coverage:** 45 circulars (one section each).
**Caveat:** Advisory desk reference only. For **current residential** Consent Scheme use Nos. **72–72F**; for **non-residential** use Nos. **75–75D**. Verify the live LandsD LACO list and the project land grant before reliance. Individual `source_md/*_CS.md` files remain the per-instrument source; this file is the scan layer.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 April 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 | Consent Scheme consolidated for RPFSO full operation (**29 Apr 2013**)

> Scope note: Revises/consolidates residential Consent Scheme requirements for compatibility with the **Residential Properties (First-hand Sales) Ordinance**. Applies to ongoing and new consents for residential units and residential CPS as separate units. **Supersedes** CM Nos. 40, 40A–D, 47, 54A, 60, 62, 63, 66, 68. Detailed rules in Annex/Appendices (companions).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72 is the post-RPFSO residential Consent Scheme handbook. Consent remains at the Director’s discretion; requirements may vary (e.g. subsidised schemes). Composite developments use starred appendices for both residential and non-residential units as indexed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Regime replacement

| Effect |
|---|
| Supersedes listed pre-Ordinance consent CMs (40-series transparency/ASP chain, 47, 54A, 60, 62, 63, 66, 68) |
| Detailed criteria in attached **Annex** + Appendices (checklist, ASP, SD, AP certificates, etc.) |

**SD takeaway:** For residential pre-sale after RPFSO, use the CM 72 Annex pack (as later varied by 72A–72F) — not the superseded 40/62 brochure chain alone.

---

### 2. Companion forms (fold into this CM / lettered variants)

| Typical appendices (in source_reference) |
|---|
| Checklist, ASP, SD, AP Certificate, FU, IAPCQSC, MFRS, QFCAP, UPR, etc. |

**SD takeaway:** AP certificates and measurement schedules in the 72 appendices are the operative consent design locks — read with later 72A–F amendments.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2 July 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 | 30-month consent window vs AP material date

> Scope note: Amends CM No. 72 Annex — consent date must not be more than **30 calendar months** before AP-certified estimated **material date** (RPFSO s.2). Replaces CM 72 attachments with revised Annex/Appendices.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72A tightens the earliest timing of residential pre-sale consent relative to the AP-certified estimated material date. Except as varied, CM No. 72 remains in force; attachments to CM 72 are replaced by those attached to 72A.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. 30-month consent timing (amends CM 72 Annex Section III para. 10.5)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Consent for residential units / residential CPS | Granted for a date **not more than 30 calendar months** before the estimated material date |
| Estimated material date | As defined in **RPFSO s.2**; must be **certified by the Authorized Person** |

**SD takeaway:** Programme the AP-certified material date so consent can issue within the 30-month window — earlier consents will not be granted under this rule.

---

### 2. Form pack replacement

| Effect |
|---|
| Technical refinement also in Appendix VII (A)(3) |
| All CM 72 attachments replaced by revised Annex/Appendices to CM 72A |

**SD takeaway:** Use the CM 72A form pack (as later varied by 72B-F) for residential consents after 2 Jul 2013.

**SD takeaway:** Administrative/forms update — AP certificates and measurement schedules remain in the revised Appendices attached to 72B.

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72C updates the Consent Scheme ASP for Cap. 623 and sets internal processing aims for residential pre-sale consent applications. Revised ASP must be used for all ongoing and new applications immediately.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cap. 623 ASP clause

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Ongoing / new consents | Adopt revised ASP with Cap. 623 clause **immediately** |

**SD takeaway:** Keep consent applications active with timely responses — dormancy risks withdrawal.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
4 May 2020 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 10 | Combined Building Mortgage + FU; single FU rule

> Scope note: Amends Annex/Appendices to CM 72C to speed residential consent. Accepts **Combined Method** (Building Mortgage + Finance Undertaking) with new SD/FU forms; generally **one** FU only from an authorized bank/DTC.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72D updates residential Consent Scheme financing evidence and related forms. Combined Method financing is acceptable with the new standard SD and Finance Undertaking forms attached.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Combined Method financing

| Method | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Building Mortgage alone | Accepted (existing) |
| Finance Undertaking alone | Accepted (existing) |
| **Combined Method** (BM + FU) | Now accepted with dedicated standard SD / FU forms |

**SD takeaway:** Financing structure is solicitor/bank territory — AP certificates still track the Annex pack for the chosen method.

---

### 2. Single Finance Undertaking

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| FU or Combined Method | LACO accepts **only one** FU from a licensed bank or registered DTC under Banking Ordinance s.16 |
| Multiple FUs | Generally **not** acceptable without good justifications |

**SD takeaway:** Plan completion funding so only one FU is lodged with the consent pack.

**SD takeaway:** Lease still governs design — but solicitor need not lodge certified grant copy at consent filing under this CM.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72F
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 April 2025 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Phased DDH/DD + landscaping approvals for phase consent (LAO PN 8/2025)

> Scope note: Accepts **phased** DDH/DD and landscaping approvals under Lands Administration Office Practice Note No. **8/2025** as lease approvals for pre-sale consent of the relevant **phase** (RPFSO Cap. 621 meaning). Direct SD/lease-approval programming impact.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72F implements immediate revised requirements so phased lease approvals can support phase pre-sale consent. It aligns Consent Scheme practice with LAO PN 8/2025 streamlined phasing of DDH/DD and landscaping proposals.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Phased DDH/DD and landscaping

| Approval | Consent use |
|---|---|
| Phased **DDH** or **DD** covering a specific phase | May be accepted as the grant approval needed for that phase's pre-sale consent |
| Phased **landscaping proposal** | Likewise accepted for the relevant phase |
| "Phase" | As defined in **RPFSO (Cap. 621)** for the consent application |

**SD takeaway:** For phased launches, size DDH/DD and landscape submissions to the consent phase — full-site approvals are not mandatory if PN 8/2025 phased approvals are in place.
