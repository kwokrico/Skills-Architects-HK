## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 1
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 June 1993 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Effective for pre-sale consent applications from **1 July 1993**

> Scope note: This circular introduces the **revised Consent Scheme Agreement for Sale and Purchase (ASP)** and mandatory **Checklists A (pre-sale)** and **B (DMC & MA)**. It is a conveyancing / consent-processing instrument, **not** a Buildings Ordinance design code. For architects it locks **saleable-area measurement**, **completion / BC dating**, **plan-change rescission thresholds**, **undivided-share adjustment limits**, **stake >30%**, and **DMC guideline design hooks** that must be reflected in massing, common-parts plans, and sales materials before pre-sale consent.

---

## Regulatory Overview

LACO CM No. 1 applies to developments under the **Land Authority Consent Scheme** (ex–Land Office Consent Scheme) seeking **pre-sale consent** and/or **Deed of Mutual Covenant (DMC) & Management Agreement (MA)** approval. It binds all such applications lodged on or after 1 July 1993 (new ASP mandatory; incomplete applications without Checklists A/B are delayed or rejected without priority).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent application hard gates — Checklist A (paras. 4–5; Checklist A Notes)

| Gate | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| New ASP | Applications on/after **1 Jul 1993** must use the revised ASP; otherwise **rejected, no Priority Number** |
| Checklists | Pre-sale consent → **Checklist A**; DMC approval → **Checklist B**, both completed by solicitors |
| Reject if missing | Items **(C) 1–6** and **(D) 1** incomplete → reject, no priority |
| Approvals window | If only **(C) 7–9** outstanding → reject unless produced within **28 days** of LACO notice |
| Foundations | **(D) 1** — foundation works must be **completed** for consent |
| Stake | **(E)** — stake % must **exceed 30%** |
| ASP completion date | **(D) 7** — date in draft ASP **≤ 12 months** from BC expiry **(B)4** and **not earlier** than AP expected completion **(D)6** |

| Document pack typically required (C) | Design / AP implication |
|---|---|
| Draft S/D + draft ASP (duplicate) | Sales / unit schedule must match GA plans |
| Form 12 (approved building plans) + Form 14 (commencement) | Consent tracks approved GA package |
| AP Certificate(s) | Foundations / stage certificate |
| DDH / Master Plans / Landscaping approvals (if lease-required) | Lock lease SC approvals before consent programme |
| Approved building plans | Same as Form 12 pack |

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme pre-sale consent until foundations are done, stake >30% is achievable, DDH/MLP/landscape lease approvals (if any) are on the critical path, and the ASP completion date sits inside the BC + 12-month envelope.

---

### 2. Saleable area — ASP interpretation Cl. 1(1)(r) (design measurement lock)

| Element | How measured |
|---|---|
| Unit enclosed by walls | Floor area incl. balconies & verandahs; from **exterior** of enclosing walls; party wall between units → **middle**; includes internal partitions & columns; excludes common parts outside enclosing walls; if wall abuts common area → **whole thickness** of that wall included |
| Cockloft | From **interior** of enclosing walls |
| Bay window (not to floor) | From exterior of enclosing walls/glass; from point where bay meets drop wall; **exclude** thickness of drop wall |
| Carparking space | From **interior** of demarcating lines / enclosing walls (dimensions in Sch. 3) |
| Yard / terrace / garden / flat roof / roof | From **interior** of boundary lines; if wall boundary → **interior** of wall |
| Schedule 3 | Each of the above items in the Property must be **specified and described separately** |

**SD takeaway:** Unit area schedules, bay-window / balcony / roof marketing figures, and car-park dimensions must be measured to this ASP definition — not a casual GFA or “carpet” figure — before sales plans and Sch. 3 are frozen.

---

### 3. Building completion, BC, and purchaser rescission (ASP Cl. 4)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| ASP completion date Cl. 4(1)(c) | Must be **no later than 1 year beyond** Building Covenant Period expiry |
| Vendor must finish by BC expiry | Else apply for BC extension; notify purchaser within **30 days** of application and of grant |
| “Completed” for Cl. 4 | Date **Occupation Permit** issued |
| AP extension of Cl. 4(1)(c) date | Only for strike/lock-out, riot, force majeure, fire/accident beyond control, war, or **inclement weather** |
| Inclement weather | Rainfall **> 20 mm** in 24 h (midnight–midnight) at Royal Observatory, **or** Typhoon Signal **No. 8+** between **08:00–17:00** |
| Purchaser rescission if late | After Cl. 4(1)(c) + AP extensions; if not rescinded within **28 days**, deemed to wait (interest accrues); absolute rescission option again after **6 months** further delay |

**SD takeaway:** Tie construction programme and consent ASP date to BC expiry + ≤12 months; treat AP weather EOT rules as contractual (not BO) — Signal 8 daytime and >20 mm rain days are the only weather extensions recognised here.

---

### 4. Plan alterations affecting the Property (ASP Cl. 20)

| Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Vendor alters building plans affecting the Property | Notify purchaser when BA approves amendment |
| Saleable area change | Purchase price adjusted **pro rata** to affected parts |
| Area change **> 5%** of Sch. 3 saleable area | Purchaser may **rescind** (refund + interest); must elect within **30 days** of notice of BA approval |
| Disputes on area / price / finishes substitution | First to project AP; then another Cap. 123 AP as expert; failing agreement → President of HKIS appoints |

**SD takeaway:** Post-consent GA amendments that move unit saleable area by more than **5%** create purchaser walk-away risk — freeze unit envelopes early or budget for price adjustment / rescission exposure.

---

### 5. Undivided shares (ASP Cl. 21)

| Limit | Rule |
|---|---|
| Vendor may adjust undivided shares | Must **not** alter total undivided shares by **more than 3%** without prior written consent of Director of Buildings and Lands |
| Purchaser’s unit | Adjustment must not affect exclusive right to hold/use/occupy the Property |

**SD takeaway:** Late reallocation of common-parts or GA shares that swings the estate total by >3% needs Lands consent — keep share matrix stable once DMC/ASP drafts are lodged.

---

### 6. Warranties, finishes, communal facilities (ASP Cl. 10)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| At ASP date | Plans approved; AP certified foundations complete; BA consent to commence **superstructure**; no known impediment to Cl. 4(1)(c) date |
| Fittings & finishes | Sch. 5 must be included; schedule must conform to **LOCM No. 101**; substitution only if force majeure / beyond control, AP-certified comparable quality |
| Communal / recreational | Sch. 6 facilities must be provided on completion (where applicable) |
| Plan warranty | Subject to Cl. 20, Property as shown on attached plan; saleable area as Sch. 3 |

**SD takeaway:** Sales brochure finishes schedule and recreational facilities list are contractual under Consent Scheme ASP — coordinate Sch. 5/6 with LOCM 101 before consent, not after marketing launch.

---

### 7. DMC guidelines snapshot — Checklist B Appendix (design-facing items)

| Guideline | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| First Manager term | Initial term **≤ 2 years** |
| Manager remuneration | ≤ **20%** (≤20 units) / **15%** (21–100) / **10%** (101+) of reasonable management expenditure |
| First owners’ meeting | Within **9 months** of DMC date |
| Quorum | Owners (in person/proxy) holding **10%** of undivided shares |
| Extraordinary meeting | Manager, OC, or owners of **20%** of undivided shares |
| Management deposit | **≤ 25%** of first-year budgeted management expenses |
| Major contracts | Sums **> 20%** of annual budget → fair tendering, intervals **≤ 3 years** |
| Common parts | Defined; plan annexed **or** kept at Manager’s office for inspection |
| Structural alterations | No structural alterations interfering with other owners’ rights |
| Cap. 344 | No DMC provision may prejudice Building Management Ordinance |

**SD takeaway:** Common-parts definition and plans must be design-ready for DMC annexation/inspection; GIC/facilities and slope items are expanded in CM 1A / 4 / 4A — read those with this CM.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 1A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 October 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Immediate effect | Supplemental to LACO CM No. 1

> Scope note: Replaces **Checklist B** (DMC & MA approval) attached to CM No. 1. Adds structured DMC guideline annexes and a full **Government Accommodation (GA) / FSI** checklist for GPA. It is **not** a BO plan code; it locks what must appear in the DMC for common parts, slope maintenance, management economics, and GIC/GA rights — items that drive SD layout of common areas, slope parcels, and government premises.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM 1A applies to all **Deed of Mutual Covenant & Management Agreement** approval applications under the Consent Scheme where Checklist B is required. The revised Checklist B (and Annexes 1–2) replaces the CM No. 1 form with immediate effect; cases in hand may voluntarily use the revised checklist to speed GPA/LACO review where Government Accommodation is involved.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Checklist B — front-sheet lease / development facts (Part A)

| Item | What solicitors must declare (architect to supply) |
|---|---|
| Lot / Conditions or New Grant / term / user (SC) | Lease identity and permitted user |
| Type of development; English & Chinese name | Marketing / DMC naming |
| **Total GFA** of development | Matches approved GA GFA |
| Government Accommodation required? | **No / Yes → Annex 2** |
| Restriction on alienation (SC) | Sales / assignment constraints |
| DMC approval SC; other special DMC SCs | Lease compliance clauses |
| Principal vs Sub-DMC | Sub-DMC needs Principal DMC approval date |
| Guidelines compliance | **No / Yes → Annex 1** |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze development GFA, GA quantum, and whether the scheme is Principal or Sub-DMC before DMC drafting — Checklist B will quote them against lease SCs.

---

### 2. Annex 1 — DMC guidelines (design-facing)

| Part | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| **Common parts** | Define common parts; **either** annex plans to DMC **or** require developer-prepared plans kept at Manager’s office, free inspection by owners |
| **First Manager** | Initial term **only 2 years**; resign/terminate on **3-month** notice after initial term; no notice until initial term ends |
| **Entry for repairs** | Manager may enter units on reasonable notice (except emergency) for structure/common-parts repairs; remedy/liability for negligent/wilful acts |
| **Insurance** | Full new reinstatement (fire & other perils) on common parts + public liability |
| **Major contracts** | Sums **> 20%** of annual budget → fair tendering, intervals **≤ 3 years** |
| **Manager remuneration** | Cap **20% / 15% / 10%** by unit band (≤20 / 21–100 / 101+) |
| **Fair share** | No owner pays more than fair share; developer pays for **unsold** shares |
| **Budgets / accounts** | Annual budgets (after first, with OC); first annual accounts from **OP**; accounting year change ≤ once / 5 years unless OC approves |
| **Trust monies** | Interest-bearing licensed-bank account (except day-to-day float approved by OC) |
| **Deposit** | **≤ 25%** of first-year budgeted management expenses |
| **Capital / special fund** | Trust fund for major capital works; interest-bearing; AGM sets contribution |
| **Structural alterations** | No structural alterations interfering with other owners’ rights; owners retain right to sue |
| **Owners’ meetings** | First meeting **≤ 9 months** from DMC; 20% shares may convene; quorum **10%** shares; Cap. 344 OI displaces DMC meeting/OC machinery while in existence |
| **Chinese translation** | Developer provides Chinese translation/summary within **2 months**; English prevails on dispute |
| **Common-parts shares** | If shares allocated to common parts and held by Manager → held as **trustee** for all owners |

**SD takeaway:** Produce a clear common-parts plan set early (for DMC annex or Manager’s office) and align structural / shared-system boundaries with the “no harmful structural alteration” covenant.

---

### 3. Annex 1 Part VII — slope maintenance (pre–Geoguide 5 text)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Owners’ duty | At own expense maintain slopes, retaining walls, other structures **within or outside** lot/development as required by lease |
| Standard cited in this CM | **“Annual Maintenance Work on Slopes and Retaining Walls Within Private Property”** (GEO) — later replaced by **Geoguide 5** under CM 4A |
| Annex to DMC | That GEO document **and** plan(s) of areas to be maintained |
| Manager authority | Engage qualified personnel to inspect/maintain per government guidelines |
| Cost recovery | Manager collects costs from owners; Manager **not personally liable** if, despite reasonable endeavours, cannot collect from all owners |

**SD takeaway:** If the lease has slope/retaining-wall obligations, identify and plan-demarcate those areas at SD for DMC annexation — CM 4A later swaps the cited standard to **Geoguide 5**.

---

### 4. Annex 2 — Government Accommodation / FSI (when GA required)

| Theme | Design / DMC lock |
|---|---|
| Extra copies | **+6 copies** of draft DMC & MA to LACO(FSIS), 20/F Queensway Government Offices |
| Define FSI | Financial Secretary Incorporated (Cap. 1015) |
| Define GA | By plan attached to DMC (if feasible) + type; parts included/excluded |
| Rights reserved to GA owner | Passage over common parts; support; services (water, sewage, gas, electricity, A/C, telephone, etc.); alter/divert exclusive GA services; access for works; uninterrupted ROW; exclusive signage on GA structure; access to services on GA structure; run additional exclusive services; **(FSI only)** alter/vary user of GA |
| Owners (ex-GA) maintain “Items” | External finishes & structure of/around/above/below GA; lifts/escalators/stairs serving GA + rest of development; shared FS/building services; shared common parts; for basement under GA — structural slabs + drainage under GA |
| Neighbourhood public GA (independently run by Government) | Broad fee exemptions (common parts, estate management, Items maintenance, capital fund, debris fee) |
| Integrated GA | Prior consult on Manager appointment/termination; GA pays only for facilities that actually serve GA (GPA/nominated person opinion); FSI liability only after GPA written approval and **≤ GA GFA / total GFA** proportion; payment from Assignment or takeover (earlier); exemptions on deposits, capital fund (unless GPA-approved capex), GA insurance, debris fee; no late-payment penalty regime on GA; schedule/plan of facilities enjoyed/not enjoyed |
| Other | GA seats on Owners’ Committee; undivided & management shares allocated to GA; FSI not bound by fitting-out regs; Manager must not represent FSI vs Government |
| Submit with draft DMC | GA GFA calc; GA plans; common-parts plans (esp. GA floor); BS lists (exclusive / exclusive-but-routed-outside / shared / non-serving-but-located-in-GA); undivided & management share bases |

**SD takeaway:** Treat GIC/GA as a separate design package: exclusive vs shared services routing, structure around GA, common-parts interfaces, and fee/share logic must be drawable before DMC — GPA will check Annex 2 against plans.

## LACO Circular Memorandum No. 1B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 December 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Immediate effect | Revises Checklist A from LACO CM No. 1

> Scope note: Splits pre-sale **Checklist A** into **A1** (residential consents subject to **LACO CM No. 7**) and **A2** (consents not subject to CM No. 7). Relaxes some submission timing (approved plans; executed superstructure contract). It is **not** a BO design code; it locks consent readiness, **ASP completion dating vs BC**, **stake >30%**, and lease approval items (DDH / MLP / car park / landscape) that sit on the SD programme.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM 1B applies to **Lands Department Consent Scheme pre-sale consent** applications from the date of the circular. Mixed developments that include both CM No. 7 residential units and other consented elements must submit **both A1 and A2**. Omitting completed A1/A2 (or Checklist B for DMC) delays consent/approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which checklist? (body paras.)

| Situation | Checklist |
|---|---|
| Residential developments **other than PSPS and HK Housing Society**, subject to measures in **LACO CM No. 7** | **A1** |
| Consents where CM No. 7 measures **do not** apply | **A2** |
| Mixed: CM 7 residential **plus** other consented elements | **Both A1 and A2** |

**SD takeaway:** Classify the consent package against CM No. 7 early — the ASP completion-date rule differs sharply between A1 and A2 (below).

---

### 2. Submission relaxations (body paras.)

| Former expectation | New rule under CM 1B |
|---|---|
| Full set of **approved building plans** with every application | **No longer required** at application; LACO may call for them in individual cases and will return when consent issues |
| Executed superstructure building contract + AP certificate of superstructure construction costs at application | **Not required at application**; submit AP-certified **estimate** of total construction costs (incl. superstructure); executed contract + precise costs **when available and in any event before consent is issued** |

**SD takeaway:** Consent can start earlier on estimates, but consent will not issue until contract/precise costs arrive — keep AP cost certificates on the consent critical path.

---

### 3. Hard rejection / 28-day gates (Notes to A1 & A2)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Missing **(C) 1–7** (relevant items) | Reject; **no priority number** until complete |
| Only **(C) 8–11** outstanding | Reject unless produced within **28 days** of LACO notice |

## Regulatory Overview

It restates that alienation (or agreement to alienate) in breach of the ASP/Assignment covenants or authorised mortgage terms is **void** (s. 17B) and generally a **criminal offence** (s. 27A), and that premium assessed by the Housing Authority must be paid before free alienation after the statutory conditions are met.

---



## Critical main topics and subtopics



### 1. Void alienation — Cap. 283 s. 17B (paras. 2)


| Actor                   | Breach that makes alienation / agreement to alienate **void**                           |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HOS/PSPS flat purchaser | Breach of any term/condition of ASP or covenant in Deed of Assignment                   |
| Mortgagee               | Breach of any terms on which the mortgage was **authorised by the Director of Housing** |


**SD takeaway:** Do not assume an HOS/PSPS unit can be freely traded or redeveloped into a private scheme narrative without checking Assignment covenants and Housing authorisations.

---



### 2. Offence — Cap. 283 s. 27A (para. 3)


| Who                                           | Liability                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Any person alienating in s. 17B circumstances | Offence: fine **$200,000** and imprisonment **1 year**             |
| Exception                                     | Mortgagee under a mortgage **guaranteed by the Housing Authority** |


**SD takeaway:** Unlawful HOS/PSPS alienation is criminal, not merely a private contractual defect — flag early if the brief involves such stock.

---



### 3. When alienation (or agreement) is allowed — Schedule to Cap. 283 (para. 4)

Alienation or agreement to alienate is restricted **unless**:


| Condition                      | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Time / Authority first refusal | **(a)** **Ten years** have elapsed since assignment to the purchaser; **OR (b)** before that, the Authority (or nominee) has **declined** to accept an assignment offered by the purchaser |
| **AND**                        | **Premium** as assessed by the Authority has been **paid**                                                                                                                                 |


**SD takeaway:** Clock and premium are both mandatory — ten years alone does not unlock sale without premium; early sale still needs Authority decline + premium.

---



### 4. Limited dealings without premium paid yet (para. 5)


| Allowed without premium already paid | Conditions                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mortgage / charge                    | Prior written approval of Director of Housing on authorised terms                                                                                                                                 |
| Enter into ASP                       | Only if para. 4(a) or 4(b) already satisfied; ASP must provide premium paid within Director-specified period and **in any event before assignment**                                               |
| Premium assessment timing            | Vendor should obtain assessment **before** ASP, **or** ASP must state assessment is still outstanding; Authority **will not** commit to finish assessment to meet a purchaser-set completion date |


**SD takeaway:** Programme any HOS/PSPS resale or charge around Housing premium assessment lead time — do not lock completion dates that assume a fixed Authority turnaround.
