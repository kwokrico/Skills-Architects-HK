# LACO Circular Memoranda — Building Index (English)

**Scope:** Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office (LACO) Circular Memoranda Nos. 1–88 in this library (139 instruments; lettered variants included).
**Detail:** Each number opens the Critical Summary in `summaries/`.
**How to use:** Jump from the contents to a building topic. The sentence says what the circular changes for the architect or the authorized person. Open the linked summary for figures and citations.
**Titles:** Titles are the subject lines used in those summary files. LACO circulars are conveyancing instruments, not Buildings Ordinance design codes.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before pre-sale, assignment, or a Deed of Mutual Covenant (DMC) submission, verify the current Lands Department LACO list, the land grant, the outline zoning plan notes, and the Buildings Department practice notes.

These circulars sit beside Buildings Department control. The Building Authority approves the building: plans, gross floor area, site coverage, occupation. Lands Department, through LACO, decides whether units in an uncompleted development may be sold (the Consent Scheme), whether the DMC may be approved under the lease, and how saleable area and common parts are described to purchasers. A layout that satisfies the Buildings Ordinance can still fail consent or DMC approval if lease gates (development-design-and-disposition, master layout, landscaping, car park, government accommodation, slopes) or measurement schedules are not locked with the drawings.

**For a new private residential pre-sale,** use the **72–72F** pack (read with **80** and the current DMC pack **79–79B**). **For a new non-residential pre-sale,** use **75–75D**. Older numbers remain for a consent already issued under that regime.

## Lease approvals that gate the building

Lease conditions typically require Lands Department approval of design, disposition and height (or a similar clause), a master layout plan, landscaping, and car parks. Those are not Buildings Department approvals, but they freeze massing, podium, greenery, and parking that the general building plans must then follow.
