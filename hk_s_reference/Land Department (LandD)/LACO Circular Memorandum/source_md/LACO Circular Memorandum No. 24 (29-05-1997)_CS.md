# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 24
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
29 May 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Task Force on Land Supply & Property Prices measures

> Scope note: Adds residential Consent Scheme measures for pre-sale applications received on/after **26 March 1997**: **15-month** consent window, **6-month** offer-all-units deadline, and **≥20%** public-sale batch size. Programme locks for sales launch — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Following Government’s 26 March 1997 Task Force measures, CM No. 24 amends CM Nos. 7 and 16 for affected residential pre-sale applications. Consents may issue up to **15 months** before AP estimated lease-compliance completion; consent letters require all consented units to be offered within **6 months** or consent lapses.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent timing — 15 months (amends CM 7 / 16)

| Applications on/after 26 Mar 1997 | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Consent window | ≤ **15 months** before AP’s estimated completion by compliance with grant conditions |

**SD takeaway:** Task Force-era residential consents can start earlier (15 months) — still tied to AP lease-compliance estimate.

---

### 2. Offer-all within 6 months / consent lapse

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Offer for sale | **All** units subject to that consent letter (including private sales) within **6 months** of consent issue |
| If not | Consent **lapses**; need renewal/extension to sell later (before CoC) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not obtain consent far ahead of a real launch capability — 6-month offer clock is hard.

---

### 3. Minimum public-sale batch (20%)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Each offering occasion | Offer ≥ **20%** of the flats subject to the consent that are to be made available for **public sale** |

**SD takeaway:** Size each public release to at least 20% of consented public-sale stock — avoid drip-feeding tiny batches.

---

### 4. Early submission encouraged

| Advice |
|---|
| Submit pre-sale applications and DMCs well before the 15-month window once prerequisites are met |

**SD takeaway:** Advance DMC/consent documentation even if the 15-month issue window is later — reduces last-minute programme risk.
