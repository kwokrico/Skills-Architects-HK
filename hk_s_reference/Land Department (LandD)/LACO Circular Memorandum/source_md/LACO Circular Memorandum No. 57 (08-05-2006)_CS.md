# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 57
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 May 2006 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Fee scale from **15 May 2006**

> Scope note: Updates **LACO non-statutory fees**; large surrender cases (>100 lots, non–Small House) charged on cost-recovery. Cancels CM Nos. 43, 43C, 43D. Administrative — no design constraints beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 15.5.2006, the Appendix fee list applies to fees demanded after that date (even if application was earlier). Standard surrender fee HK$15,250 is demanded first; if lots ultimately exceed 100, balance to cost-recovery is demanded at binding basic terms. Items (E) and (F) from CM 43 Appendix deleted.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Key admin points

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Effective | Fees demanded after **15.5.2006** at revised rates |
| Large surrenders (>100 lots, not NT Small House) | Cost recovery; initial demand **HK$15,250**, balance later |
| Cancelled | **CM Nos. 43, 43C, 43D** |

**SD takeaway:** Use CM 57 Appendix (until later fee CMs) for consent/DMC fee budgets — large exchange surrenders can cost more than the standard fee.
