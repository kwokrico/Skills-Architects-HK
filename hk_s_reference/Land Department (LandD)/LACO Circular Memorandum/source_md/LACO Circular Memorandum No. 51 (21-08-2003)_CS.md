# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 51
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 August 2003 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Trial Consent Scheme measures (Working Group)

> Scope note: Announces Working Group review and **trial measures** (extra brochure disclosures; solicitor-firm substitution checks; extra purchaser notifications). Applied case-by-case until further notice; later formalised/expanded in **CM No. 54**. Architect lock: name the AP firm and related parties in brochures/ads.

---

## Regulatory Overview

A multi-stakeholder Working Group is reviewing the Consent Scheme. Meanwhile LACO introduces trial measures for all consents to sell until further notice, with flexible case-by-case application. Practitioners may approach LACO if compliance is difficult.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Extra disclosure (measure 1)

| Must disclose in sales brochures and advertisements (TV/radio exception) |
|---|
| Developer and parent company; AP and architect firm; solicitor firm(s) for the sale; mortgagee bank (if any) |
| Also to appear in LACO monthly press release, Consent Hotline fax-on-demand, and Lands website |

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate brochure/ad party lists early — AP firm name is mandatory marketing content under the trial.

---

### 2. Substitute/additional solicitors (measure 2)

| LACO will check supplementary SDs for | Focus |
|---|---|
| Resources to advise purchasers | Firm size vs unit count / prior experience |
| Stakeholder account arrangements | Preference for lead firm operating the account |
| Conflicts of interest | No conflict |

**SD takeaway:** No design lock — flag if sales conveyancing firm changes after consent.

---

### 3. Additional purchaser notifications (measure 3 — ASP Cl. 4 path)

| Direction |
|---|
| Vendor notification duties under ASP (e.g. BC extension under Cl. 4(2)) are reinforced as part of the trial package (see full CM for list) |

**SD takeaway:** Keep AP/vendor notification workflows aligned with ASP Cl. 4 when BC or completion dates move.
