# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 9
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 July 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Effective **1 August 1994**

> Scope note: Amends **LOCM No. 91 DMC Guidelines** so they apply to **all sizes** of development (with limited LACO exceptions) and updates Manager-agency and Chinese-translation rules. Design-facing mainly via universal DMC guideline coverage; not a BO code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 August 1994, LOCM 91 Guidelines apply to **all developments of whatever size**, except where LACO accepts good reasons to vary them for specific complex developments. Paragraphs 7 and 26 of the Guidelines are rewritten as set out in the CM.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Universal application of DMC Guidelines (para. 1)

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Guidelines scoped mainly to typical tower/podium cases (per LOCM 91 para. 3 practice) | Apply to **all** developments unless LACO agrees variation for complex cases |

**SD takeaway:** Assume LOCM 91 (+ later LACO CMs) DMC rules apply even to large/complex schemes unless LACO has agreed a departure.

---

### 2. Amended Guidelines paras. 7 and 26

| Para | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| 7 — Manager as agent | Manager appointed to act as agent for all owners, duly authorised under the DMC, on matters concerning the **common parts** |
| 26 — Chinese translation | Developer provides direct translation or Chinese summary of DMC within **2 months** of signing; available at management office for inspection/copies at owners’ expense; on dispute, **English prevails** |

**SD takeaway:** Common-parts definition/plans must be DMC-ready for Manager agency; bilingual DMC pack is a post-signing delivery obligation with English controlling.
