# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 79A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 June 2021 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 11 | Revised DMC Guidelines (noise / green features)

> Scope note: Amends CM 79 DMC Guidelines. Guideline 18 expands manager house-rule power for **noise abatement**, waste reduction and recycling. Guideline 34 updated for Joint Practice Notes on green/innovative features GFA exemption.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 79A revises selected DMC Guidelines under CM No. 79 to reflect latest departmental requirements and good building-management practice. Remaining CM 79 framework continues except as amended.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Guideline 18 — environmental / noise house rules

| Manager may make house rules to | Reference |
|---|---|
| Protect environment; implement **noise abatement**, waste reduction and recycling | Guidelines on property management issued from time to time by the **Director of Environmental Protection** |

**SD takeaway:** Expect DMC/manager powers covering construction/operational noise and recycling measures — brief MEP/acoustics and refuse strategy accordingly.

---

### 2. Guideline 34 — green / innovative features (JPN 1 & 2)

| Context |
|---|
| Joint Practice Notes Nos. 1 and 2 (BD/LandsD/PlanD) on GFA exemption of green and innovative features — DMC Guideline 34 revised accordingly |

**SD takeaway:** If SD relies on green-feature GFA exemptions, ensure DMC treatment matches the revised Guideline 34 / JPN package.
