# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72C
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 June 2016 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 9 | Cap. 623 ASP clause; processing service levels

> Scope note: Revises residential ASP for **Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Ordinance (Cap. 623)**. Processing targets and inactive-application rules; 24-hour consent hotline ceases 4 Jul 2016. Form/admin focus.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72C updates the Consent Scheme ASP for Cap. 623 and sets internal processing aims for residential pre-sale consent applications. Revised ASP must be used for all ongoing and new applications immediately.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cap. 623 ASP clause

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Ongoing / new consents | Adopt revised ASP with Cap. 623 clause **immediately** |
| Existing consents | May add/replace Cap. 623 clause **without reference to LACO** (deemed approved) |

**SD takeaway:** No new dimensional SD locks — ensure legal pack uses Cap. 623-compatible ASP wording.

---

### 2. Processing aims / inactive apps

| Practice |
|---|---|
| First-round comments aim: within **1 calendar month** of application |
| Subsequent rounds: reply within **2 weeks** of solicitor response |
| Inactive applications may be treated as **withdrawn** |
| 24-hour residential consent hotline ceases from **4 Jul 2016** |

**SD takeaway:** Keep consent applications active with timely responses — dormancy risks withdrawal.
