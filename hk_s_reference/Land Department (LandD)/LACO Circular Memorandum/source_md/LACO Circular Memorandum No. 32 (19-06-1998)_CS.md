# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 32
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
19 June 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Limits title-requisition enquiries to LACO

> Scope note: Directs conveyancers **away from LACO** for routine title requisitions (lease copies, CoC copies, lease interpretation, premium, old consent letters). Practice-management circular — **no** schematic design constraints; useful only for knowing where CoC/consent evidence lives.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 32 explains that LACO’s primary role is advising Lands Department and administering the Consent Scheme/DMC approvals. Routine title requisitions should go to owners, Land Registry, District Lands Officers, or original developer solicitors — not LACO.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where to direct common title queries (paras. 3–10)

| Query type | Direct to |
|---|---|
| Legible lease/grant copies | Owner / Land Registrar (inspect originals at LR); **not** LACO |
| Missing old Government leases | Advise client; Land Registrar + DLO |
| CoC existence/copies (old leases) | LOCM No. 77 (22 Jan 1985) attached — CoC only after original grantee compliance; no new CoC on later redevelopment if prior CoC issued |
| Lease interpretation / no-breach / premium / DDH approvals | DLO (or BD for building-plan/structural matters); LACO advises Government only |
| Consent/DMC approval letters | Confirm date only via LACO; obtain copies from **original developer solicitors**; pre-1990 letters may have been destroyed |

**SD takeaway:** For redevelopment title packs, source CoC/consent evidence from LR, DLO, and original solicitors — do not programme LACO as a copy bureau.
