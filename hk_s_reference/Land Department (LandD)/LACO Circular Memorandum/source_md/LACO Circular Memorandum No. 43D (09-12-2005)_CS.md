# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 43D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 December 2005 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 Pt. IX | Consent reinstatement fee revision

> Scope note: Revises CM No. 43B reinstatement fee to **HK$26,800** from **28 December 2005**. Administrative only.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 28.12.2005, the fee for reinstatement of Consent to ASP (suspended or cancelled for any reason) is revised to HK$26,800.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised fee

| Service | Revised rate (from 28.12.2005) |
|---|---|
| Reinstatement of Consent to ASP | **HK$26,800** |

**SD takeaway:** Update any consent-reinstatement budget from the CM 43B figure to HK$26,800.
