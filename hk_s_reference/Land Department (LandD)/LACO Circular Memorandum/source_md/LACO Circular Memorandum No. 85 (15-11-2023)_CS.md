# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 85
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 November 2023 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Title checking procedures in land exchange and resumption cases

> Scope note: Sets out LACO general procedures for **title checking** in land exchange and resumption (Annex). Primarily conveyancing for owners/solicitors; architects note only where exchange/resumption programme gates site availability.

---

## Regulatory Overview

LACO conducts title checking and advises Lands Department on title issues in land exchange and resumption cases. CM No. 85 publishes those general procedures in an Annex so parties can prepare submissions earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Title-checking Annex

| Audience | Use |
|---|---|
| Land owners and their solicitors in exchange/resumption cases | Refer to Annex for LACO title-checking process and early preparation |

**SD takeaway:** If the site depends on land exchange or resumption, flag title-checking lead time in the master programme — not a BO dimensional control.
