# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 2
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
6 August 1993 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | New fee scale effective **16 August 1993**

> Scope note: Revises **LACO non-statutory fees** for Consent Scheme and related land documents on a cost-recovery basis (last revision April 1989). It is a **fee schedule only** — no design geometry, occupancy, or construction standards. Use it to budget consent / DMC approval cashflow; do not treat fee amounts as still current without checking later LACO revisions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 2 sets the fees charged by LACO for Land Authority Consent Scheme services, Government lease preparation, and deeds of surrender. It applies to solicitors/applicants using those LACO services from 16 August 1993; it does not change what must be designed or submitted under CM Nos. 1 / 1A / 1B.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent Scheme fees (from 16 Aug 1993)

| Service | Previous ($) | New ($) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Consent to Agreements for Sale and Purchase | 15,000 | **32,000** |
| Consent to Assignments, Deeds of Exchange etc. | 15,000 | **16,000** |
| Consent to Tenancy Agreements etc. | 15,000 | 15,000 |
| Consent to Building Mortgages | 6,000 | 6,000 |
| Consent to Building Further Charges | 3,000 | 3,000 |
| Consent to Deeds of Variation of approved Building Mortgages and/or Further Charges | 3,000 | 3,000 |
| Consent to amendments to approved ASP, DMC etc. | 1,500 | **1,600** |
| Approval of Deeds of Mutual Covenant | 6,500 | **30,000** |
| Approval of Sub-DMC, Supplementary DMC or separate Management Agreement | 6,500 | **15,000** |
| Second and subsequent re-engrossment of land modification documents | 1,500 | 1,500 |

**SD takeaway:** DMC approval and pre-sale ASP consent are the large fee items — phase applications (principal DMC vs multiple sub-DMCs) has a real cost consequence under this schedule.

---

### 2. Government lease & surrender fees

| Service | Previous ($) | New ($) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Preparation, signature and issue of Government lease and counterpart | 2,000 | **4,000** |
| Plans attached to Government lease and counterpart | 1,400 / plan | **2,400 / plan** |
| Survey when new Government lease issued for whole/portion of lot formerly held under a Government lease | 10,000 | **16,000** |
| Preparation of Deeds of Surrender of Government lease or other tenancy from the Government | 1,000 | **2,000** |

**SD takeaway:** Lease re-issue / modification plan sets attract per-plan fees — keep lease plan revisions minimal when coordinating lot boundary or surrender drawings.
