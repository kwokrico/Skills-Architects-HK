# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 73C
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 March 2014 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 73C revises the NT Small House surrender/regrant fee.
From 24 March 2014 the fee is HK$7,330.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 24.3.2014** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Varies **CM No. 73** for this fee item |

**SD takeaway:** NT Small House conveyancing fee only — no building SD impact.

### 2. Revised rate

| Service | Rate (from 24.3.2014) |
|---|---|
| Surrender of Government lease/tenancy for new grant — N.T. Small House | **HK$7,330** |

**SD takeaway:** Fee-only item.
