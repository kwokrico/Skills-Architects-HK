# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 8
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 July 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Revised Statutory Declaration from **1 August 1994**

> Scope note: Issues a **revised Statutory Declaration** for Consent Scheme forward-sale applications. Architect-facing locks are the **single AP certificate**, **GFA-based undivided-share / management-share allocation** (the “common basis”), and DMC conformity with LOCM 91 as amended. Not a BO design code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 8 applies to consent-to-forward-sales applications submitted to district LACO offices on or after **1 August 1994** (pending cases may switch to the new form). It replaces six separate AP certificates with one composite certificate and requires the SD to state the certified basis for allocating undivided shares to common areas and management shares to units.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Single AP certificate pack (para. 2(a); SD para. 4)

| Certification (one AP certificate) | Covers |
|---|---|
| Foundations | Completed |
| Costs | Expended and remaining to complete |
| Stage | Works stage at certificate date |
| Superstructure | BA Form BD103 consent under Cap. 123 s. 14; commence date |
| Lease compliance date | Conditions of Grant can be complied with by stated date (phase wording if applicable) |
| Areas | Total GFA of Common Areas and of Development; GFA of each unit / all units (as applicable) |

**SD takeaway:** Consent SD hinges on one AP certificate covering foundations, costs, stage, BD103, compliance date, and GFAs — keep these aligned with the GA package before lodging.

---

### 2. Common basis for shares — faster DMC approval (paras. 2(c)–4)

| Basis | Rule |
|---|---|
| Common Areas undivided shares | Same proportion to total shares as **Common Areas GFA / Development GFA** (AP/Surveyor-certified) |
| Management shares per unit | Same proportion as unit **GFA** (or **value**) / total units’ GFA (or value) |
| Effect if adopted | LACO need not consult DLO for DMC approval → faster consent |
| Different method | Allowed, but DLO consultation → **delay**; exhibit alternative allocation certificate |

**SD takeaway:** Prefer GFA-proportional common-area and unit share allocation at SD — non-standard matrices slow consent.

---

### 3. Cases still needing DLO / agency advice (para. 5)

| Case | Extra consultation |
|---|---|
| HK Housing Society grant | DLO advice continues |
| HOS (PSPS) | DLO + Director of Housing |
| Government Accommodation to FSI | DLO + Government Property Agent |

**SD takeaway:** GIC / PSPS / HKHS schemes still need agency sign-off path — programme DMC consent accordingly.
