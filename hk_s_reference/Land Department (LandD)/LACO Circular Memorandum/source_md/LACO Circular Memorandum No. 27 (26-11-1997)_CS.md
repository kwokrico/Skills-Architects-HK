# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 27
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 November 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Cost-recovery fees from **1 December 1997**

> Scope note: Revises **LACO service fees** on a cost-recovery basis. Administrative fee schedule — **no** schematic design constraints beyond consent/DMC cost awareness.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 27 replaces the prior fee scale with a cost-recovery scale effective 1 December 1997. Full rates are in the Appendix.

---
