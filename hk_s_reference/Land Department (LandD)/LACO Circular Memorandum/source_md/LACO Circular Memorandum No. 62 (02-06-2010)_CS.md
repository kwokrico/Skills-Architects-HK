# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 62
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2 June 2010 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 V | Transparency measures for uncompleted residential sales

> Scope note: Adds Consent Scheme conditions on **brochure (≥7 calendar days)**, **price list (≥3 calendar days)**, and compliance of brochures, price lists, **show flats**, and printed ads with Annexes I–IV; ASP inspection info for purchasers. Strong SD locks for show-flat accuracy and brochure content. Annexes are companions (not separate CS filenames).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Following the Financial Secretary’s 21 April 2010 announcement, CM No. 62 imposes enhanced transparency conditions on consents for sale of residential units in uncompleted private developments. It amends CM No. 40 (as varied by 40C, 48, 50, 60). En bloc / public auction / tender sales have specified exemptions with Annex V undertaking where applicable. Pre-consent promotion under CM 48 remains possible but show flats/materials must meet Annexes III–IV.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Lead times before commencement of sale

| Document | Minimum lead time |
|---|---|
| Sales brochure (to public and agents) | **≥ 7 calendar days** |
| Price list | **≥ 3 calendar days** |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze brochure and price-list packages early enough for the 7+3 calendar-day gates before launch.

---

### 2. Annex compliance (brochure / price list / show flat / ads)

| Annex | Subject |
|---|---|
| I | Sales brochure requirements |
| II | Price list requirements |
| III | **Show flat** requirements |
| IV | Printed advertisements / promotional materials |
| V | Undertaking form for qualifying **en bloc** sales |

**SD takeaway:** Show flats and promotional graphics must match Annex III/IV rules — treat show-flat design as a consent-compliance deliverable, not only marketing.

---

### 3. En bloc / auction exemptions (p.2)

| Sale type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| En bloc (all house units, or not less than all units in a single block/tower, to one purchaser) | Specified rules may not apply; Annex V undertaking required |
| Public auction or public tender | Specified exemptions |

**SD takeaway:** Whole-block en bloc strategies need the Annex V undertaking path — confirm early if marketing is not unit-by-unit.
