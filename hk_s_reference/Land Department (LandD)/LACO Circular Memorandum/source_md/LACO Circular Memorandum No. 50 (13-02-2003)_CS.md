# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 50
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
13 February 2003 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Consent term variations + technical lease modifications

> Scope note: Further relaxes CM 40 sale-admin rules (attorney for PA; drop private-purchaser schedules; suspend sales reports) while retaining **20-month** consent window and **7-day** price list. Part II allows consent before completing **technical** lease modifications if disclosed and executed before first Assignment. Design-facing for DDH/MLP hold-ups caused only by technical mods.

---

## Regulatory Overview

With immediate effect (including existing consents), CM No. 50 varies Consent Scheme terms for uncompleted private residential developments and amends CM No. 40 as previously varied. Separately, where only a technical lease modification blocks grant approvals, LACO may issue consent on additional disclosure/execution terms.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Sale-term variations retained vs dropped (§I)

| Changed / dropped | Still retained |
|---|---|
| Named attorney (no substitution) may sign **preliminary agreement** in public sales | Consent effective ≤ **20 months** before AP estimated compliance |
| Private-purchaser name/ID schedules dispensed; no need ASP+deposit before public selection | Ads must specify public vs private unit numbers |
| “Exempted units” definition/provisions deleted | Private purchasers sign formal ASP and pay deposit on same basis as public |
| Audited/unaudited sales-conduct reports and monthly progress returns **suspended** | Price list **7 days** before sale; revised list **1 day** before additional public units |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the 20-month AP compliance window and 7-day price-list lead time — other private-sale paperwork is lightened.

---

### 2. Consent pending technical lease modification (§II)

| Gate | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Usual rule | No consent until all grant approvals (e.g. DDH, MLP) obtained |
| Exception | Only a **technical** modification needed (App. I examples) → consent may issue without completing the mod first |
| Extra terms | Mod executed by grantee and registered before first Assignment; brochures fully disclose nature and SCs to be modified; ASP reserves vendor right to execute mod without joining purchasers (App. II clauses = still “standard form”) |

**SD takeaway:** If DDH/MLP is blocked only by a listed technical mod (e.g. offensive trades, corner splay, I→I/G), consent can proceed with brochure disclosure — execute/register the mod before first Assignment.
