# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 87A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 March 2025 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 87A revises the NT Small House surrender/regrant fee to HK$12,100 from 31 March 2025.
Varies CM No. 87 for that fee item; revised rate also applies to earlier applications not yet demanded.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 31.3.2025** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Varies **CM No. 87** for this fee item |

**SD takeaway:** NT Small House fee only.

### 2. Revised rate

| Service | Rate (from 31.3.2025) |
|---|---|
| Surrender of Government lease/tenancy for new grant — N.T. Small House | **HK$12,100** |

**SD takeaway:** Fee-only item.
