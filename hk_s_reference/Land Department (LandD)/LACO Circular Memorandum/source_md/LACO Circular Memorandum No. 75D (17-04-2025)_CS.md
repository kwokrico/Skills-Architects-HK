# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 75D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 April 2025 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Non-res: reinstate documentary evidence of lease approvals

> Scope note: Unlike residential CM 72F (phased approvals), CM 75D **requires applicants again** to provide documentary evidence of lease approvals for non-res pre-sale consents (reversing the CM 75B dispense-and-internal-check approach for multi-department grants).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 75D revises non-residential consent requirements immediately so applicants must lodge documentary evidence of lease approvals. The change is incorporated into the Annex and Appendices attached to this CM.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Documentary evidence of lease approvals (restored)

| Under CM 75B | Under CM 75D |
|---|---|
| Applicant evidence of lease approvals dispensed; LACO relied on LandsD internal checking | Applicants **must provide documentary evidence** of lease approvals (multi-department grants need details) |

**SD takeaway:** For non-res consent after 17 Apr 2025, assemble DDH/DD and other grant-approval evidence for the LACO pack — do not rely on internal-only checking.
