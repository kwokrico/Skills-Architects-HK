# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 79B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 April 2024 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Electronic submission for DMC approval applications

> Scope note: Allows paper or electronic lodging of DMC approval applications; streamlines procedures. Certified Government Grant copy no longer required at application. Checklist companion in source_reference.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 79B introduces electronic submission as an alternative to paper for applications for approval of Deeds of Mutual Covenant. Details of both options are in Part (A) of Appendix III attached to this CM.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Paper vs electronic DMC submission

| Mode | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Paper | Original hard copies of all supporting documents at application |
| Electronic | Email copies at application; originals of specified key documents within prescribed time |

**SD takeaway:** DMC content standards remain those under CM 79/79A — only the lodging channel changes.

---

### 2. Land grant copy

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Certified Government Grant with DMC approval application | **No longer required** from developer's solicitors |

**SD takeaway:** Admin relief only — lease still drives DMC drafting.
