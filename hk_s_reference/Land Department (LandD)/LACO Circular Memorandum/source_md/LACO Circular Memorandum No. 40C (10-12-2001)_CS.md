# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 40C
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 December 2001 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | Brochure timing and ballot-as-option

> Scope note: Requires **same sales brochures for private purchasers** as public sales; sets public brochure/price-list lead time to **≥ 7 calendar days** before registration; updates App. XII; allows **optional balloting** after the 40B suspension. Consumer-info / sales-conduct — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 40C amends the CM 40 Consent Scheme (ex PSPS/SCHS/FFSS) on sales-information disclosure and restores balloting as a developer option where large public response warrants it for transparency and order.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Brochures for private and public sales (§I)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Private / internal sale | Provide prospective private purchasers sales brochures with **same information** as public-sale brochures when private sale is announced (mandatory in consents from this CM; voluntary request for earlier consents) |
| Public sale | Brochures and price list(s) available **≥ 7 calendar days** before registration starts (all future public sales, whether consent already issued or not) |
| App. XII | New Appendix replaces App. XII to CM 40A (Apps A/B unchanged); applies to consents from today; voluntary for earlier consents |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze brochure content (incl. saleable-area/finishes data per CM 40) early enough for both private and public channels with a 7-calendar-day public lead time.

---

### 2. Balloting as option (§III)

| Rule |
|---|
| After Feb 2001 ballot suspension, developers may still use balloting if desired for transparency/public order; Government has no objection |

**SD takeaway:** Choose FCFS (with 40B Appendix I measures) or ballot based on expected demand — both paths remain consent-compatible under this CM.
