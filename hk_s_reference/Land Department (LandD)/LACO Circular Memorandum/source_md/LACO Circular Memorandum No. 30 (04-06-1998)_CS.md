# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 30
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
4 June 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Immediate relaxation (excludes PSPS / SCHS / HKHS FFSS)

> Scope note: Suspends CM No. 24 Task Force measures, extends consent window to **20 months**, allows **sub-sale after formal ASP + balance deposit**, and opens case-by-case ballot/registration exemptions for higher-value schemes. Sales/consent programme instrument — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Following the Secretary for Housing’s 29 May 1998 announcement, CM No. 30 suspends CM No. 24 for private residential consents (existing and future) until further notice, and amends CM Nos. 7/16/24 so consent may be effective up to **20 months** before the AP’s estimated lease-compliance completion date. Purchasers may sub-sell after signing formal ASP and paying the balance deposit.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Suspended measures (para. 1; CM 24 off)

| Suspended |
|---|
| Prohibition of sub-sale of uncompleted flats |
| Offer-all-units within 6 months of consent |
| ≥20% of consented uncompleted flats per offering |
| Two REDA voluntary measures: company sales limited to last 15% of each batch; no change of directorship before completion — **but** other 31 Jan 1997 voluntary guidelines remain; no directorship change before formal ASP and balance deposit |

**SD takeaway:** Programme is no longer forced by the 6-month/20% Task Force clocks — still respect remaining REDA voluntary directorship rules before formal ASP.

---

### 2. Consent window — 20 months (para. 2)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Consent effective date | ≤ **20 months** before AP estimated completion + compliance with grant |

**SD takeaway:** Residential consent can start earlier (20-month window) — still anchored to AP lease-compliance estimate.

---

### 3. Sub-sale and waiver letters (paras. 3–8)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| When sub-sale allowed | After **formal ASP** signed **and** balance of deposit paid |
| Existing ASPs with sub-sale ban | Vendor must issue waiver letter on purchaser request; refusal → consent withdrawal for unsold units |
| Fees | Developer bears cost; no extra legal/admin fees to purchaser (except LR registration charges) |
| Future / unsigned ASP | Use revised Cl. 5 & 11 for Stamp Duty disclosure — no re-approval needed for existing consents |

**SD takeaway:** Sub-sale is a conveyancing pathway after formal ASP — design/sales docs must support Stamp Duty disclosure of intermediate parties.

---

### 4. Luxury ballot/registration exemption guideline (para. 4; later replaced by 30B)

| Initial guideline for case-by-case exemption |
|---|
| ≤ about **100** residential units (ex CPS) **and** average price ≥ **HK$12 million** each — apply specifically with evidence |

**SD takeaway:** Small high-value schemes may seek ballot/registration exemption — confirm current criteria under **CM No. 30B**.
