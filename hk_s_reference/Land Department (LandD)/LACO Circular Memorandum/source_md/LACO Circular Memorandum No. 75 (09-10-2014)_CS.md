# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 75
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 October 2014 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1-50/1 Pt. 2 | Non-residential Consent Scheme consolidated

> Scope note: Consolidates criteria/instructions for sale of **non-residential** units in uncompleted developments under the Consent Scheme. Consent remains at the Director's discretion; Annex + standard forms are the operative framework (companions in source_reference).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 75 is the non-residential Consent Scheme handbook parallel to the CM 72 residential consolidation. The Annex criteria are not exhaustive; the Director may impose varied conditions case by case.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Framework and discretion

| Rule |
|---|
| Essential framework for non-res pre-sale consent is the attached **Annex** + standard forms |
| Issue of consent is at the **sole discretion** of the Director of Lands |

**SD takeaway:** For non-res pre-sale after Oct 2014, use the CM 75 Annex pack (as later varied by 75A–75D) — not the older CM 1/54/67 chain alone.

---

### 2. Companion forms

| Typical appendices (in source_reference) |
|---|
| Checklist, ASP, SD, AP Certificate, FU, IAPCQSC, MFRS, QFCAP, UPR, etc. |

**SD takeaway:** AP certificates and measurement schedules in the 75 appendices are the operative non-res consent design locks.
