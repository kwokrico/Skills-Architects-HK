# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 75B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 September 2021 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 11 | Non-res Combined Method / single FU (align with 72D)

> Scope note: Amends Annex/Appendices to CM 75A to speed non-res consent and align with residential (CM 72D) where applicable. Accepts Combined Method financing; generally **one** FU only.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 75B updates non-residential Consent Scheme financing evidence and related forms. Combined Method (Building Mortgage + Finance Undertaking) is accepted for non-res consents with dedicated standard SD/FU forms.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Combined Method + single FU (non-res)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Combined Method | Accepted for non-res consent (aligns with CM 72D practice) |
| FU or Combined Method | Generally **one** FU from a licensed bank / registered DTC under Banking Ordinance s.16 |
| Multiple FUs | Generally **not** acceptable without good justifications |

**SD takeaway:** Same financing gate as residential 72D — plan one FU for the non-res consent pack.
