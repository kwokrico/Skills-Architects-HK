# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 30B
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 April 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Replaces CM No. 30 para. 4

> Scope note: Revises **luxury** criteria for case-by-case exemption from registration/balloting consent conditions. Sales-process exemption — not BO design; unit size/price thresholds guide whether ballot rules can be relaxed.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 30B replaces paragraph 4 of CM No. 30. Developers may apply to LACO for relaxation of registration and balloting requirements for luxury private residential units meeting the revised criteria; exemption is discretionary per development.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised luxury criteria (para. 2)

| Criterion | Guideline |
|---|---|
| I. Flat size | GFA about **1,500 sq ft or more** (proportion of such units in the development also considered) |
| II. Number of units | About **≤ 100** residential units (ex CPS) retained; LACO may apply flexibly |
| III. Price | About **≥ HK$7 million** (was HK$12 million average under CM 30) |

**SD takeaway:** Schemes targeting ≥~1,500 sf / ≥~$7M units in small batches may seek ballot/registration exemption — apply development-by-development with evidence.

---

### 2. Discretion (paras. 3–4)

| Rule |
|---|
| LACO may approve/disapprove on one or all criteria; solicitors apply per development with required evidence |
| CM 30 para. 4 **replaced** by this CM |

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume exemption — get written LACO decision before dropping ballot logistics from the sales programme.
