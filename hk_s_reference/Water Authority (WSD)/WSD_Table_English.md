# Water Authority Plumbing Standards Index (English)

**Issued by:** Water Supplies Department / Water Authority
**Scope:** The April 2026 technical requirements for plumbing works in buildings, the one-page April 2026 amendment list, and Part B of the September 2024 General Acceptance (GA) 2.0 product standard. Cap. 102 and Cap. 102A are indexed in the Cap. 102 folder, not here. This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The title links to the critical summary. The PDF link opens the source file.
**Titles:** The official title is the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current technical requirements, the circular letters named in the amendment list, and Part A of those requirements together with GA 2.0 Part B.
