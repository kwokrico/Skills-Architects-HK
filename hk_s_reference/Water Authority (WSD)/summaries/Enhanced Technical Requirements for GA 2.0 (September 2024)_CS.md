# Enhanced Technical Requirements for GA 2.0
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
September 2024 version | Water Supplies Department / Water Authority

> Scope note: Part B of the General Acceptance (GA) 2.0 product standard. It sets materials, tests and acceptance evidence for pipes, fittings, prescribed Water Efficiency Labelling Scheme (WELS) products and other materials used in an inside service or a fire service. It is **not** the layout code. Clause B1.2 says it is read with Part A of the current Technical Requirements for Plumbing Works in Buildings.

## Regulatory Overview
Part B tells the Water Authority which plumbing products are acceptable in a fresh-water inside service, a salt-water inside service, or a fire service, and which certificate or test report is enough to show that. At schematic design the lock is the material matrix: several common pipes and brasses are marked unsuitable for salt water, and fresh-water products must meet the leach and lead limits before they can be specified.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How a product is accepted (B1–B3)

| Rule | What the text requires |
|---|---|
| Products covered | Pipes; fittings (including pipe fittings, valves, taps and mixers); prescribed WELS products; other materials (B1.1) |
| Duty | Those products and their components, used in an inside service or a fire service, shall comply with this Part B (B2.1) |
| Deemed acceptance | Certification or test reports against the stipulated standards in B4–B8, from (a) a British Standards Institution Kitemark, (b1) the U.K. Water Regulations Advisory Scheme (WRAS), (b2) Kiwa UK Water Regulation 4 (KUKreg4), (b3) NSF REG4, or (c) a laboratory acceptable to the Water Authority (B2.2) |
| Hong Kong laboratories | Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) under Construction Materials, sub-categories: metallic materials / steels; pipes and fittings; showers; tapwares; valves (B2.2) |
| No standard listed | Ask the Water Authority. B3.2 does not let the project invent a substitute standard |

**SD takeaway:** Name the B4–B8 standard on the specification, and allow only a Kitemark, WRAS, KUKreg4, NSF REG4 or listed HOKLAS report as the acceptance path.

### 2. Pipe materials (Tables B2.3.1 and B2.3.2)

Marks are ✔ suitable when the relevant standards are met, and ✖ not suitable in general. An empty cell is not a ✔ and is not an ✖.

| Material | Fresh cold | Fresh hot | Salt-water inside service | Fresh-water fire service | Salt-water fire service |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copper | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ | ✔ | ✖ |
| Ductile iron with internal coating | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Polyethylene (PE) | ✔ | unmarked | ✔, note (2) | — | — |
| PE-X | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ | — | — |
| Plastic-lined steel, PVC-C lining | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ | — | — |
| Plastic-lined steel, PVC-U or PE lining | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ | — | — |
| PVC-C | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ | ✔ |
| PVC-U | ✔ | unmarked | ✔ | — | — |
| Stainless steel | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ | ✔ | ✖ |
| Galvanized steel | — | — | — | ✔ | ✖ |

Note (1): suitable locations for salt-water inside service may refer to the fresh-water inside service. Note (2): PE installed exposed shall be black pipe and black fittings. Fire-service rows are only those printed in Table B2.3.2.

**SD takeaway:** Do not carry copper, stainless steel, PE-X, PVC-C or plastic-lined steel across into a salt-water inside service, and do not use copper, galvanized steel or stainless steel for a salt-water fire service.

### 3. Valve and strainer materials (Table B2.3.3)

The table scores body, stem, wedge, seat, bolts, non-return cap and spring, strainer screen and drain plug, and ball-float lever, across five columns: fresh cold, fresh hot, salt-water inside service, fresh-water fire service, salt-water fire service.

| Choice that changes the spec | Printed rule |
|---|---|
| Body in salt water (inside service or fire service) | Brass, dezincification-resistant brass, grade 304 and grade 316 stainless steel are ✖. Bronze, coated cast iron, coated ductile iron and duplex stainless steel grade 1.4462 or superior are ✔ |
| Brass | Footnote **: except brass containing zinc of more than 15% by mass, because it is susceptible to dezincification |
| Coatings | Epoxy and the other coatings marked * shall comply with BS 6920:2014 for the cold-water and/or hot-water use |
| Bolts in salt water | Grade 304 is ✖ where it contacts salt water. Footnote ***: grade 304 may be used for bolts, nuts and washers that do not contact salt water |
| Strainer screen | Grade 304 is ✖ for salt water. Grade 316 and duplex 1.4462 or superior are ✔ |

**SD takeaway:** Specify bronze, coated iron or duplex 1.4462 for anything wetted by salt water, and keep ordinary brass and grade 304 off that duty.

### 4. Fresh-water health limits (B2.4–B2.8, B2.10)

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Metal leach, all metallic products in the fresh-water inside service | Table B2.4.1. Lead 0.01 mg/L, cadmium 0.003 mg/L, chromium 0.05 mg/L, nickel 0.07 mg/L, copper 2 mg/L. Test to Table B2.4.2. Table 2 of AS/NZS 4020:2018 is replaced by Table B2.4.1 |
| Scaling factors (Appendix H of AS/NZS 4020:2018) | Pipe 1.0; pipe fitting, butterfly valve and strainer 0.05; gate, globe, ball and angle valves, and expansion/settlement/flexible joints 0.01; non-return and pressure-reducing valves 0.01 at DN 100 or above and 0.05 below DN 100; tap/mixer not applicable |
| Alloy lead | Not more than 3.5% for products intended for the fresh-water inside service (B2.5) |
| Solder and braze | B2.4 does not apply. Solder: lead not more than 0.07% and antimony not more than 0.1% by mass. Braze: cadmium not more than 0.01% by mass (B2.6) |
| Polymers in fresh water | BS 6920 parts listed in B2.10, with Table 1 of BS 6920-1:2014 replaced by Table B2.4.1, or AS/NZS 4020:2018 on the same leach table and scaling factors |

**SD takeaway:** Write 3.5% maximum alloy lead, the Table B2.4.1 leach caps, and the solder and braze limits into the fresh-water specification.

### 5. Temperature, marking, and the product schedules (B2.11–B2.14, B4–B8)

| Rule | Text |
|---|---|
| Cold-water duty | Materials for cold water shall be suitable up to 35°C and tested to that temperature (B2.11) |
| Drinking water | Install only within the certified temperature (B2.12) |
| Marking | Manufacturer logo or brand, the industrial standard, and nominal size and direction of flow, where applicable (B2.13) |
| B4 | Pipes and fittings: copper, stainless steel, ductile iron, galvanized steel (lined, and unlined for fire service), PVC-U, PVC-C, PE-X, PE, and meter connectors |
| B5 | Valves, including flushing, gate, check, ball, globe, butterfly, ball-float, pressure-reducing, hydrant, PVC-U and PE |
| B6 | Taps and mixers, including thermostatic, sensor and self-closing |
| B7 | Prescribed WELS types: showers, water taps, washing machines, urinal equipment, flow controllers, water closets. Each must fully comply with the latest WSD scheme document named in that row |
| B8 | Expansion and settlement joints, strainers, instantaneous electric and gas water heaters, glass-reinforced plastic potable tanks and their linings, brazing and soft solder |

**SD takeaway:** Do not specify a fresh-water product above its certified temperature, and point each product line at its B4–B8 row plus the WELS scheme document where B7 applies.
