# List of Amendments — Technical Requirements for Plumbing Works in Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
April 2026 version | Water Supplies Department / Water Authority

> Scope note: A one-page list of twelve amendments to the Technical Requirements for Plumbing Works in Buildings, April 2026 version. It names the clause or figure and the circular letter that prompted the change. It does **not** print the replacement clause text.

## Regulatory Overview
The list updates meter-position fittings, separate meters for subdivided units, Legionnaires’ disease and water-mist controls, and backflow prevention for washing machines and dishwashers. Several other rows only align English and Chinese wording or a symbol name, and they do not add a new design rule on this page.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What this page does and does not contain

| Printed column | Use |
|---|---|
| No. of Amendment | Amendments 1 to 12 |
| Location | The clause, table, figure or appendix in the technical requirements |
| Content of Amendment | One line describing the change |
| Remarks | The Water Supplies Department circular letter being incorporated |

No row reprints the new sentence, the new dimension, or a revised diagram.

**SD takeaway:** Treat this page as a change index. Open the named circular, or the amended technical requirements, before freezing a meter room, a subdivided-unit riser, or an appliance connection.

### 3. Subdivided units (Amendment 6)

| Item | Printed text |

| Location | Clause 3.6 and Appendix 2 |

**SD takeaway:** A subdivided-unit scheme needs a separate-meter strategy under clause 3.6 and Appendix 2; this page does not say how many meters or where they sit.

### 4. Legionnaires’ disease and water mist (Amendment 7)

| Item | Printed text |
|---|---|
| Location | Clause 4.2.1.5, Table 4.2.3.7.2 and Table 4.2.5.2.1 |
| Change | New requirement related to the Code of Practice for Prevention of Legionnaires’ Disease and a water misting system |
| Circular | No. 9/2025 |

**SD takeaway:** Flag a water-mist system and any cooling or storage plant that the Legionnaires’ disease code would catch, and take the new limits from Circular No. 9/2025.
