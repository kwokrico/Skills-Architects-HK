# Waterworks Ordinance (Cap. 102)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Consolidation: contents last updated date 16.6.2024 | Cap. 102 | Water Authority means the Director of Water Supplies (section 2)

> Scope note: This Ordinance governs custody of the waterworks, approval of a consumer and of an agent, connexion or refusal of connexion, restriction, suspension and disconnexion of a supply, who may carry out specified plumbing works on a fire service or inside service, mapped gathering grounds, and waste, misuse, unlawful taking, pollution, and damage where those rules constrain site works or drainage. It does not prescribe pipe diameters, cistern geometry, flushing volumes, or meter positions; those sit in the Waterworks Regulations (Cap. 102 sub. leg. A). Fishing, deposits, charges, penalties, and prosecution time limits are omitted here except where a power stops a connexion.

## Regulatory Overview

The Water Authority has custody and control of the waterworks and of the water in them, except leased land within a gathering ground (section 3), and may approve who is responsible for a fire service or inside service before that service is connected to the main (sections 7 and 8). Written permission is required to construct, install, alter, or remove a fire service or inside service, unless the Water Authority waives it for an alteration of a minor nature, and construction, installation, or alteration must be carried out in the prescribed way with pipes and fittings of the prescribed nature, size, and quality (section 14).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. System boundaries (section 2)

| Term | Rule in section 2 | Schematic implication |
|---|---|---|
| Water Authority | The Director of Water Supplies | Submissions, permission, and connexion decisions are Water Authority decisions |
| Fire service | Pipes and fittings in the premises, and any pipes and fittings between the premises and a connexion to the main, used or intended to be used for a supply solely for fire fighting | A fire service is a dedicated system; it is not an inside service |
| Inside service | Pipes and fittings in the premises, and any pipes and fittings between the premises and a connexion to the main, other than those forming part of a fire service, used or intended for a supply | Potable, flushing, and other non-fire supplies are inside service |
| Communal service | That part of a fire service or inside service used in common by more than one consumer in the same premises | Shared risers and common pipes are a communal service and need an agent |
| Connexion to the main | The pipe between the main and the control valve nearest to the main which regulates flow from the main into a fire service or inside service, that control valve, and all fittings between that valve and the main | The first control valve and the pipe back to the main are the connexion |
| Main | Includes a connexion to the main and any pipe owned by the Government and maintained by the Water Authority for a supply | Government supply pipework is the main, including the connexion |
| Fitting | Any apparatus, cistern, cock, equipment, machinery, material, tank, tap, and valve, and any appliance or device other than a meter, installed or used in a fire service or inside service | Tanks, taps, and valves are fittings; a meter is not a fitting |
| Meter | An appliance or device owned by the Government and maintained by the Water Authority for measuring consumption | The billing meter is Government equipment |
| Domestic purpose | A purpose connected solely with occupation of a dwelling-house. It does not include a purpose connected with a garden, lawn, playground, or swimming pool appurtenant to a dwelling-house | Garden, lawn, playground, and pool use is outside domestic purpose |
| Gathering ground | Any surface of land in or by which rain or other water is collected and from which water is, or is intended to be, drawn for a supply, and which is mapped under section 23 | Only mapped land is a gathering ground |
| Premises | Any building or structure or any part of it, and any place in which there is a fire service, inside service, or any part of the waterworks, or in which a fire service or inside service is intended to be constructed or installed | The site of an intended service is already “premises” |
| Waterworks | Any property occupied, used, or maintained by the Water Authority for the purposes of the Ordinance, and any gathering ground | Gathering grounds are part of the waterworks |
| Licensed plumber | A person licensed under the Ordinance to construct, install, maintain, alter, repair, or remove fire services or inside services | Licensing is under this Ordinance; the trade list is in the Schedule |
| Supply | A supply of water provided by the Water Authority from the waterworks | Water from the waterworks is the regulated supply |

**SD takeaway:** Draw the fire service and the inside service as separate systems, and stop the consumer’s pipe at the connexion: the control valve nearest the main, and everything between that valve and the main, is part of the main.

### 2. Consumer, agent, and refusal of connexion (sections 7 and 8)

| Rule | Section | What must be in place |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer of a fire service or inside service | 7(1) | A person who occupies the premises, or is responsible for management of the premises or any part, and who undertakes to pay charges and to accept custody and maintenance of the service and custody of any meter on it |
| Agent of a communal service | 7(2) | A person who occupies the premises, or is responsible for management, and who undertakes to pay charges for the communal service and to accept its custody and maintenance |
| Cancellation | 7(3) | The Water Authority cancels the undertaking when all charges due have been paid; the person then ceases to be consumer or agent |
| Refusal of connexion or reconnexion | 8(1) | The Water Authority may refuse if the fire service or inside service, or any alteration to it, is not approved, or if there is no consumer, or, where there is a communal service, no agent |
| Notice | 8(2) | Refusal is served on the applicant and must state the reasons |

**SD takeaway:** A shared riser is a communal service and cannot be connected until there is an approved consumer and an approved agent; an unapproved service or alteration is a statutory ground for refusal of connexion (section 8).

### 3. Restriction, suspension, and disconnexion (sections 9, 10, 11, and 16)

| Power | Section | Grounds that affect the design |
|---|---|---|
| Restrict or suspend a supply | 9 | To conserve water; to prevent waste; to construct, install, inspect, test, regulate, alter, repair, or remove any part of the waterworks or any fire service or inside service; to avoid damage or breakdown from fire, pollution, waste, or otherwise; or for protection of life or property |
| Disconnect a fire service or inside service | 10 | Includes: no consumer, or no agent where there is a communal service; the service does not comply with the Ordinance; the service is constructed, installed, or altered without permission; repairs required under section 16 are not carried out; waste, misuse, or pollution of the supply has occurred or is likely |
| Notice | 11 | Except in an unforeseen emergency, prior notice stating the reasons is served on the consumer and agent, or, if there is none, on the occupier or left at the premises |
| Repair notice | 16(1) | The Water Authority may require the consumer to repair where the service is in a condition that waste or pollution has occurred or is likely, has been altered without permission, or does not comply with the Ordinance |
| Communal service | 16(2) | A section 16 notice for a communal service is served on the agent |

Section 19(2) and (3) also allow disconnexion of an existing service, or refusal to connect a new service, where a required deposit is not paid. That is a connexion gate, not a layout dimension.

**SD takeaway:** An unapproved installation or alteration, or a layout in which waste or pollution of the supply is likely, is a ground for a repair notice and for disconnexion of both the fire service and the inside service (sections 10 and 16).

### 4. Who may construct fire services and inside services (Part III, sections 13A, 13B, 14, 15, and 15A, and the Schedule)

| Rule | Section | Hard point |
|---|---|---|
| Specified plumbing works | 13A | Construction, installation, maintenance, alteration, repair, or removal of a fire service or inside service |
| Instruction and supervision | 13B | The work is at the supervisor’s direction and within the scope the supervisor decides, and the supervisor specifies the method and manner |
| Written permission | 14(1) | A person must not construct, install, alter, or remove a fire service or inside service unless the Water Authority has granted written permission |
| Waiver | 14(2) | The Water Authority may waive permission for alterations which are, in his opinion, of a minor nature |
| Who may apply | 14(2A) | Written permission may be granted on the Water Authority’s own initiative or on the application of a licensed plumber |
| Prescribed construction | 14(3) | Construction, installation, or alteration must be carried out in the prescribed way, and the pipes and fittings must be of the prescribed nature, size, and quality |
| Who may do the work | 15(1) | A person who is not a designated person must not carry out specified plumbing works |
| Designated person | 15(6) | A licensed plumber; a registered plumbing worker; a registered plumbing worker (provisional); a person working under the instruction and supervision of a licensed plumber or registered plumbing worker; or a public officer authorized by the Water Authority |
| Work without a designated person | 15(2) | Allowed only for alterations or repairs that are, in the Water Authority’s opinion, of a minor nature; construction work on a construction site carried out in accordance with section 3(2), 3A, or 4 of the Construction Workers Registration Ordinance (Cap. 583); or construction work exempted from those sections under section 4, 7, or 8 of the Construction Workers Registration (Exemption) Regulation (Cap. 583 sub. leg. C) |
| Not minor | 15A | For sections 14(2) and 15(2)(a), alterations or repairs must not be treated as minor if, in the Water Authority’s opinion, they would adversely affect the efficiency of the service in providing a reliable and adequate supply, or the quality of the water |

The Schedule (sections 2 and 38A) states who is a registered plumbing worker, by reference to registration under the Construction Workers Registration Ordinance (Cap. 583).

| Work | Part 1 — registered plumbing worker | Part 2 — registered plumbing worker (provisional) |
|---|---|---|
| Fire service | Registered skilled worker for the plumber trade division; drain and pipe layer (master) trade division; fire service mechanic (master) trade division; or fire service mechanical fitter trade division; or registered semi-skilled worker for the plumber trade division or the fire service mechanical fitter trade division | Registered skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber, drain and pipe layer (master), or fire service mechanical fitter trade division; or registered semi-skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber or fire service mechanical fitter trade division |
| Inside service | Registered skilled worker for the plumber trade division or the drain and pipe layer (master) trade division; or registered semi-skilled worker for the plumber trade division | Registered skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber or drain and pipe layer (master) trade division; or registered semi-skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber trade division |
| Installation of a meter | Same trade divisions as for a fire service in Part 1 | Registered skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber, drain and pipe layer (master), or fire service mechanical fitter trade division; or registered semi-skilled worker (provisional) for the plumber or fire service mechanical fitter trade division |

Section 15(6)(d) allows work under the instruction and supervision of a licensed plumber or a registered plumbing worker. It does not name a registered plumbing worker (provisional) as that supervisor.

**SD takeaway:** New construction and any alteration that could affect a reliable and adequate supply, or water quality, needs written Water Authority permission and a designated person; section 15A blocks the use of the “minor” waiver for that work.

### 5. Gathering grounds (sections 3(2), 23, 24, 25, and 37(3))

| Rule | Section | Site constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Custody | 3(2) | Section 3(1) custody and control of the waterworks does not apply to leased land within a gathering ground |
| Maps | 23(1)–(3) | The Water Authority prepares maps of gathering grounds, marks a new gathering ground or an extension, and alters maps when the area is reduced. Section 23(2) requires consideration of traditional rights to take water for agricultural and domestic purposes |
| Deposit and notice | 23(4)–(6) | The map is signed and dated by the Water Authority, deposited in the Land Registry, and notified in the Gazette with the Land Registry address |
| Leased land | 24(1) | The Chief Executive may by notice require a lessee of land within a gathering ground to drain, treat, or develop the leased land, in the manner specified, for any purpose connected with the waterworks, including prevention, control, or rectification of contamination or damage |
| If the lessee does not do the work | 25 | The Chief Executive may require the Water Authority to do it. Entry onto the leased land is on 14 days’ notice to the lessee (section 25(2)) |
| Gathering-ground regulations | 37(3) | Regulations under section 37(1)(o), (p), or (q) (access, burial grounds, camping, recreation, and other use of gathering grounds) do not apply to leased land within a gathering ground |

**SD takeaway:** On leased land, confirm against the section 23 map in the Land Registry whether the lot is a gathering ground before freezing site drainage; section 24 can require that land to be drained, treated, or developed to protect the waterworks.

### 6. Waste, misuse, pollution, and damage constraining site works (sections 13(2), 18, and 28–31)

| Rule | Section | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Supply measured | 18 | Except where the Ordinance otherwise provides, a supply shall be measured by meter or in such other manner as the Water Authority may determine |
| Public standpipe | 13(2) | Except with written permission, water from a public standpipe may be taken only for a domestic purpose |
| Waste or misuse | 28 | Wasting or misusing a supply, or causing or permitting that, is an offence |
| Unlawful taking | 29(1) | Except with the Water Authority’s permission, no person shall take water from the waterworks other than through a fire service, inside service, or public standpipe; take water through a fire service for any purpose other than fire fighting; take water through an inside service for any purpose other than that for which it is supplied; take unmeasured water through a fire service or inside service (subject to section 18); or divert water from the waterworks |
| Pollution by deposit | 30(1) | No person shall deposit, or cause or permit to be deposited, any solid or liquid matter so that it may fall or be washed or carried into water forming part of the waterworks |
| Pollution by entry | 30(2) | Entering, bathing, or washing in that water, washing or causing or permitting any animal to enter it, or throwing or placing any thing in it, is an offence |
| Permission | 30(3) | An act is not an offence under section 30 if done with the written permission of the Water Authority |
| Damage | 31 | Altering, interfering with, damaging, or destroying any part of the waterworks requires the written permission of the Water Authority |

**SD takeaway:** Keep site drainage and wash water from discharging solids or liquids into water forming part of the waterworks, do not divert or tap a main, and use a fire service only for fire fighting (sections 29–31).
