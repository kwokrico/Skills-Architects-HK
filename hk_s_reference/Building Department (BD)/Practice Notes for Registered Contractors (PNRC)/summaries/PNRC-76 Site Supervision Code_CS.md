# PNRC-76 Site Supervision Code
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2015; last revision December 2023; this revision August 2024 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 76 tells registered contractors which edition of the Code of Practice for Site Supervision applies, and it adds tower-crane temporary-works duties and where to find accepted TCP courses. The supervision standard is that Code. Appendices A and B are the December 2023 and August 2024 amendment schedules; their clause text is not in the text layer of this PDF.

## Regulatory Overview

PNRC 76 applies to registered general building contractors and registered specialist contractors supervising building works under a supervision plan. Circular-letter amendments made between 2015 and 2021 were incorporated into the Code of Practice for Site Supervision (2021 Edition), and Appendices A (December 2023) and B (August 2024) are incorporated in the Supervision Code (2024 Edition) on the Buildings Department website.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which Supervision Code edition applies

| Layer | What this note says |
|---|---|
| Circular letters, 2015–2021 | Incorporated into the Supervision Code (2021 Edition) |
| Appendix A | December 2023 amendments to the 2021 Edition |
| Appendix B | August 2024 amendments to the 2021 Edition |
| Supervision Code (2024 Edition) | Incorporates those amendments; published at www.bd.gov.hk |

The amendment pages in the PDF are headed “Amendments to the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2021 Edition)”, with legends “Amended” and “Deleted”. The changed wording is not in the text layer.

**SD takeaway:** Set site supervision to the Code of Practice for Site Supervision (2024 Edition) on the BD website.

### 2. Temporary works providing support to tower cranes

These duties sit in addition to paragraph 4.9 of the Supervision Code (2021 Edition). They apply to all development projects with the specified Form BA 10 submitted on or after 1 November 2024.

| Role appointed by the RGBC / RSC | Duty |
|---|---|
| Design engineer | Prepare plans, construction drawings and design justifications of the temporary works |
| Independent checking engineer | Check those documents for compliance with the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations made under it, and the relevant codes of practice |

Certification forms TW1, TW2 and TW3 are on the BD PNRC 76 page.

**SD takeaway:** A tower crane on temporary works needs a design engineer and a separate checking engineer, with TW1–TW3, once the specified Form BA 10 is submitted on or after 1 November 2024.

### 3. TCP qualifications, accepted courses, and repealed membership recognition

TCP professional and academic qualifications and experience are set out in paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24 of the Supervision Code (2021 Edition). Courses run by local and non-local institutes or universities that BD has accepted as equivalent academic qualifications are listed on the BD website for TCP registration and appointment under that Code.

Corresponding recognition of a TCP based on membership of particular professional institutions, formerly Appendix X of the Supervision Code, was repealed by circular letter on 8 May 2015. Appendix C reproduces that appendix for reference. A person previously accepted to practise as a TCP on that recognition may continue if evidence of the previous recognition is given to BD with the supervision plan.

**SD takeaway:** Accept a TCP course only from BD’s published list under paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24, and carry an old institution-based recognition forward only with evidence on the supervision plan.

A similar practice note has been issued to authorized persons, registered structural engineers and registered geotechnical engineers.
