# PNRC-34 Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 1996 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Announces the Works Branch *Code of Practice on Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes* and points authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered contractors to it for buried water-carrying services in or near a slope. Inspection frequencies, detection methods, and permanent-works details are in that Code, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview

The Code guides inspection and maintenance of buried water-carrying services affecting slopes, for government departments, professionals, contractors, and building managers. Under Buildings Ordinance section 27C, the Building Authority may require a building owner to investigate and repair buried water-carrying services laid in or near a slope.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the Code is for

The Code focuses on three aspects:

| Aspect | Content named in this note |
|---|---|
| Programme | Planning an effective inspection and maintenance programme |
| Buried services | Options for detecting, inspecting, and repairing buried services affecting a slope |
| Leakage | Recommendations for permanent measures to minimize impacts of leakage from buried services on slope safety |

The Code was published by the Works Branch and sold through the Government Publications Centre.

**SD takeaway:** Any buried water main, drain, or other water-carrying service in or near a slope is designed with a permanent leakage-control measure and an inspection route, using the Code rather than a site-only repair note.

### 2. When the design team must use it

Authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered contractors appointed for the design, construction, or maintenance of buried services are to refer to the Code so that slopes in the vicinity remain safe. Section 27C is an owner obligation to investigate and repair when the Building Authority so requires.

**SD takeaway:** Show buried services and the slope they can affect on the same schematic plan, because a section 27C investigation can be required for services laid in or near that slope.
