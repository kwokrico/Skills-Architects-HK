# PNRC-43 Corruption Prevention
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue October 1999 / This revision November 2001 (AD/Support) – paras. 4 and 5 added, para. 6 amended | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note tells registered general building contractors and registered specialist contractors how to prohibit advantages, avoid a supervision conflict, and declare a connection on proof test core-drilling. It does not set a structural or foundation design standard. Appendix I is an extract of the Cap. 201 definition of "advantage"; it is not a full reprint of the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance.

## Regulatory Overview
Registered general building contractors and registered specialist contractors appointed for building works or street works are required under the Buildings Ordinance to give continuous supervision so that the works are constructed in accordance with the Buildings Ordinance and the approved building plans. The note states that it is important they explicitly prohibit their staff from soliciting or accepting advantages from subcontractors and suppliers, or offering advantages to other people, in relation to their work, and that from 2 July 2001 ground investigation works, predrilling, interface core-drilling, post-installation drilling and proof test core-drilling must be carried out by a registered specialist contractor in the Ground Investigation Field Works category.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Prohibit advantages and keep supervision off the subcontract
| Rule | What the note requires |
|---|---|
| Supervision | Continuous supervision so the works follow the Buildings Ordinance and the approved building plans. Site staff are often employed to assist. |
| Prohibition | Staff must be explicitly prohibited from soliciting or accepting advantages from subcontractors and suppliers, or offering advantages to other people, in relation to their work. |
| House rules | Develop a company Code of Conduct and promulgate staff instructions. The ICAC Advisory Services Group assists, on Tel. No. 2526 6363. |
| If a bribe is offered | Report immediately to the ICAC on 2526 6366 (24 hours). |
| What an advantage is | Not only money. Appendix I extracts Cap. 201: any gift, loan, fee, reward or commission of money, valuable security, or property; any office, employment or contract; any payment, release, discharge or liquidation of any loan, obligation or other liability; any other service or favour (other than entertainment), including protection from penalty or from disciplinary, civil or criminal action; the exercise or forbearance from the exercise of any right, power or duty; and any offer, undertaking or promise of any of those. |
| Conflict | Do not sublet or subcontract any works to staff who are required to supervise those works. Do not sublet or subcontract to a body corporate and/or partnership whose director, manager, partner or other officer is such a member of staff. If that arrangement exists, replace the supervisory staff. |

**SD takeaway:** Do not let the same people supervise the works and hold the subcontract for those works; the approved plans remain the construction baseline the contractor must supervise.

### 2. Proof test core-drilling is a separate ground-investigation specialist, with a connection declaration
The registered specialist contractor appointed to carry out proof test core-drilling for certain foundation works, as required under Building (Construction) Regulation 30, should declare its connection with the contractor appointed to carry out the foundation works. The declaration should include whether or not that specialist is a holding, subsidiary or associated company of the foundation contractor, or has a financial relationship with it (for example cross-directorship), or has a financial interest in the foundation works.

**SD takeaway:** Where foundation works need proof test core-drilling under Building (Construction) Regulation 30, appoint a Ground Investigation Field Works registered specialist contractor who can declare that connection, and keep that contractor distinct from the foundation contractor in the procurement schedule.
