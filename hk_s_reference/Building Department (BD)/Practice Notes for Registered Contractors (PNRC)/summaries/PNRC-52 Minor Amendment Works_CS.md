# PNRC-52 Minor Amendment Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue July 2002; last revision February 2023; this revision October 2024 (AD/NB2) (paragraph 2 amended and footnote 1 added). Appendix A rev. 2/2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note is the deferral, after first consent, of approval and consent for minor amendments under a Building Authority modification of regulation 33(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations. It is not a permission to start works without first consent. Amendments listed in Appendix A still need prior approval and consent.

## Regulatory Overview
Where the Building Authority has granted a modification of regulation 33(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations under section 42(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, minor amendments to building, superstructure, drainage, foundation, pile cap, site formation, and excavation and lateral support works for which first consent has already been given do not need a further prior approval and consent. Appendix A is the list of amendments that do not qualify as minor amendments for that deferral.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What may be deferred, and the secondary-element permit
The exemption is conditional. Minor amendments must be clearly documented and deposited in the site office. The authorized person, registered structural engineer and registered geotechnical engineer must still obtain approval and consent for all these amendments before certifying completion or applying for an occupation permit or temporary occupation permit.

Footnote 1: once the permit modifying or exempting regulation 33(1) for superstructure works has been granted, that permit also covers minor amendments of related secondary structural element works for which first consent to commencement has been given. A separate modification or exemption of regulation 33(A) for those secondary elements is not required.

| Secondary structural elements named in footnote 1 |
|---|
| Curtain wall, window wall, cladding, space frame, precast elements, canopy, skylight, balustrade, external ceiling, grille/louvre, ventilation duct, maintenance platform, and similar secondary structural elements not affecting the overall stability of the building |

**SD takeaway:** Detail curtain wall, cladding, canopies and the other footnote 1 elements so they can ride the superstructure regulation 33(1) permit, and keep any change that affects overall stability off that list.

### 2. Building, superstructure and drainage — not a minor amendment

| Works | Amendment that does not qualify |
|---|---|
| Building and building (A&A) | Major revision or localised major revision under PNAP APP-55 |
| Building and building (A&A) | Material effect on the fundamental issues |
| Building and building (A&A) | Concern on stability or deformation of slopes, retaining walls, adjoining land and buildings |
| Building and building (A&A) | An application for exemption or modification of the Buildings Ordinance or its subsidiary regulations |
| Building and building (A&A) | Not in compliance with the relevant Outline Zoning Plan, or deviating from a scheme approved by the Town Planning Board |
| Building and building (A&A) | Works outside the lot boundary |
| Superstructure and superstructure (A&A) | Affecting the overall structural stability of the building |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | Revision of drainage layout as a result of a major revision of the building proposal |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | Change of disposal system or connections to public sewers |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | Concern on stability or deformation of slopes, retaining walls, adjoining land and buildings |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | An application for exemption or modification of the Buildings Ordinance or its subsidiary regulations |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | Excavations deeper than 3 m |
| Drainage and drainage (A&A) | Works outside the lot boundary |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze lot boundary, Outline Zoning Plan compliance, sewer connection and any drainage excavation deeper than 3 m at the consented scheme, because those amendments cannot be deferred.

### 3. Foundation and pile cap — not a minor amendment

| Trigger | Foundation | Pile cap |
|---|---|---|
| Change in design assumptions | Yes, together with construction method or material, safety precautionary measures, safety tolerances, monitoring measures or criteria, founding material, and allowable load carrying capacity | Yes |
| Below the approved bulk excavation limit in Area Number 1 of the scheduled areas in Schedule 5 | Excavation for pile caps or footing | Excavation for pile caps |
| Railway: Area Number 3 of the scheduled areas in Schedule 5, or other railway protection areas in PNAP APP-24 | Impact that results in not complying with the monitoring control limits on the latest approved plans, or the APP-24 guidelines | Same |
| Drainage and sewage tunnels: Area Number 5 of the scheduled areas in Schedule 5, or Drainage Services Department tunnel protection zones in PNAP APP-62 | Impact that results in not complying with the monitoring control limits on the latest approved plans, the APP-62 technical notes, or Drainage Services Department Practice Note No. 2/2017 | Same |
| Mechanical couplers | Change from Type 1 to Type 2 for steel reinforcing bars, or vice versa, or a coupler change that needs a further manufacturer quality assurance scheme before consent to the splicing assembly | Same |
| Latest approved or accepted excavation and lateral support plans | — | An amendment that results in an excavation and lateral support amendment described in Appendix A paragraph 6 |
| Steel H-piles | Driven to bedrock steeper than 25 degrees from the horizontal, if any, as shown on the latest approved plans | — |
| Piles over a slope | Piles constructed over a slope whose stability will affect or be affected by the piles | — |
| Clear distance | Not in compliance with the approved minimum clear distance between the foundation and the site boundaries or adjacent sensitive buildings or features | — |
| Pile position | Relocation of any pile to a position more than 5 m from its original location on the latest approved plans | — |
| Pile count, same type, against the latest approved plans | More than 5% except large diameter bored piles; more than 15% for large diameter bored piles only | — |

Pile-count footnote: if the calculated number involves decimals, round up to the nearest whole number. The note’s examples are 15 non-large-diameter bored piles (15 × 5% = 0.75, round up to 1, so the increase should not be more than 1) and 10 large diameter bored piles (10 × 15% = 1.5, round up to 2, so the increase should not be more than 2).

**SD takeaway:** Hold pile positions within 5 m of the approved location and pile numbers within the 5% or 15% caps after rounding up, and do not change Type 1 and Type 2 couplers or the Area Number 1, 3 or 5 limits without a fresh consent.

### 4. Excavation and lateral support, and site formation — not a minor amendment

| Trigger | Excavation and lateral support | Site formation |
|---|---|---|
| Change in design assumptions, construction method or material, safety precautionary measures, safety tolerances, or monitoring measures or criteria | Yes, and also type, size or installation method of vertical earth retaining elements, or construction sequence including pre-loading of struts | Yes |
| Excavation below the approved bulk excavation limit in Area Number 1 of the scheduled areas in Schedule 5 | Yes | Yes |
| Railway Area Number 3, or other railway protection areas in PNAP APP-24, where the impact does not comply with the approved monitoring limits or the APP-24 guidelines | Yes | Yes |
| Area Number 5 drainage and sewage tunnels, or Drainage Services Department tunnel protection zones in PNAP APP-62, where the impact does not comply with the approved monitoring limits, the APP-62 technical notes, or Drainage Services Department Practice Note No. 2/2017 | Yes | Yes |
| Strutting | Change in strutting layout (horizontal and inclined), construction material of the lateral support system, or reduction of size of structural members of the approved system | — |
| Strutting exception that can still be minor | Localised minor changes to the horizontal or inclined arrangement of structural members, without reduction of the stiffness of the affected struts, to resolve obstruction to the permanent structures | — |
| Formation level | — | Change of any site formation level on the latest approved plans by more than 1 m |
| Effect on the excavation and lateral support system | — | Change of site formation level or profile that adversely affects the overall stability of the approved excavation and lateral support system in the site, or adjoining structures, slopes or utilities |
| Permanent earth retaining structures | — | An increase in retained height, or a reduction of such for more than 1 m |
| Mechanical couplers | Not listed under excavation and lateral support | Type 1 to Type 2 or vice versa, or a coupler change needing a further manufacturer quality assurance scheme before consent to the splicing assembly |

**SD takeaway:** Lock bulk excavation in Area Number 1, strut sizes and site formation levels at consent, because a formation-level change of more than 1 m, or an excavation and lateral support change outside the localised strut exception, is not a minor amendment.
