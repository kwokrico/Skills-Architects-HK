# PNRC-72 Authorised Signatory for Minor Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2010 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 72 governs the authorized signatory who acts for a prescribed registered contractor on minor works commenced under the simplified requirements, a change of that signatory, and a temporary absence. A registered minor works contractor (Individual) is outside the duty to appoint an authorized signatory. Addition of a signatory to the registration follows PNRC 38 or PNRC 69.

## Regulatory Overview
For the purposes of section 9AA of the Buildings Ordinance, a prescribed registered contractor is appointed under section 28 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulations, and under section 9AA(6) that contractor provides continuous supervision of the minor works and complies with the Ordinance generally. Apart from a registered minor works contractor (Individual), every prescribed registered contractor appoints at least one authorized signatory, and the Building Authority accepts only one authorized signatory for the minor works under each minor works submission.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. One signatory for the submission, and the MW forms that person signs
The duties of the prescribed registered contractor are further stipulated in Part 7 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulations, the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans, and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision.

The authorized signatory who signs Form MW01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 10, 11, and 12, for minor works commenced under the simplified requirements, is the person responsible for the contractor's duties under the Buildings Ordinance for those works. The authorized signatory who signs Form MW01 or MW03 is deemed to be that person.

**SD takeaway:** Name one authorized signatory on the minor works submission and treat the signatory of Form MW01 or MW03 as the person accountable for the contractor's duties on that submission.

### 2. The same name on the specified forms and on the supervision plan
The name of the authorized signatory appointed for the submission is indicated on all specified forms relating to the submission. If the name is not shown on a specified form, the Building Authority considers the form incomplete and unacceptable.

If the name on the supervision plan differs from the name on Form MW01 of the submission, the Building Authority rejects the supervision plan or the specified forms, unless the change of authorized signatory has been approved or notified under paragraph 4.

**SD takeaway:** Use the same authorized signatory on Form MW01 and on the supervision plan, and show that name on every specified form for the submission.

### 3. Change of authorized signatory
| Situation | What the note requires |
|---|---|
| Prescribed registered contractor, other than a sole proprietorship, with only one authorized signatory | A proposal to change the authorized signatory of the submission will not be accepted unless an application to add the new authorized signatory to the contractor's registration has been made under PNRC 38 or PNRC 69 and approved by the Building Authority. |
| Contractor with more than one authorized signatory on its registration | Notify the Building Authority in the standard form at Appendix A, countersigned by the authorized person of the submission if the works involve Class I minor works, not less than 7 days before the change. Otherwise the original authorized signatory is still regarded as responsible for the submission. |

Appendix A (12/2010) is the notification of change for minor works commenced under the simplified requirements. It records the prescribed registered contractor (registered general building contractor, registered specialist contractor, or registered minor works contractor), registration number and expiry, the original and new authorized signatories, the effective date, and the minor works submission numbers. Both signatories sign. The authorized person signs where the works are Class I. If extra sheets are used, each sheet is signed and dated and carries the Certificate of Registration Number of the person concerned.

**SD takeaway:** For a Class I submission, obtain the authorized person's countersignature and give the Building Authority at least 7 days' notice before the new signatory takes the submission; a one-signatory contractor waits for an addition approved under PNRC 38 or 69.

### 4. Temporary absence
If the prescribed registered contractor becomes unable to act, it should immediately cease the minor works under its purview.

The Building Authority accepts a temporary nomination so the works can continue, on all of the following conditions:

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| Notice | Standard form at Appendix B. If the works involve Class I minor works, the authorized person of the submission countersigns. |
| Timing | Submitted at least 7 days before the absence, except sudden illness or accident. In that case the contractor or the authorized signatory provides documentary proof within 3 days that the signatory is unable to act because of illness or accident. |
| Who may be nominated | Another authorized signatory of the same contractor, currently accepted by the Building Authority to act for that contractor for the classes and types of minor works under the submission; or an authorized signatory of another contractor, currently accepted by the Building Authority to act for that contractor for those classes and types. If that other contractor is a partnership or a corporation, an authorization letter from the rest of the partners, or from the required quorum of directors, is submitted with the notification. |
| Duration | Maximum 14 days. A further extension is considered only on documentary proof of special circumstances, such as illness. The total temporary acting period permitted is a maximum of 30 days. |

If the nominee does not meet the criteria above, the Building Authority does not accept the nomination and informs the contractor or authorized signatory and, if the works involve Class I minor works, the authorized person. The minor works may have to cease if no authorized signatory is acting.

If the absence for the submission exceeds 30 days, the contractor changes to another authorized signatory of the same company, or applies to add a new authorized signatory to its registration, under paragraph 4.

During the acting period the nominated signatory fulfils the site-supervision duties and signs the inspection records on site in accordance with the accepted supervision plans, if any. That person is not permitted to sign any supervision plans or specified forms for the submission.

Appendix B (12/2010) is the nomination notice. It records the period of illness or temporary absence, the minor works submission numbers, the original signatory's signature, the authorized person's signature for Class I minor works, and the nominee's acceptance, including identity document, telephone and fax numbers, the prescribed registered contractor for whom the nominee is currently an authorized signatory, and the authorization letter where the form requires it. Extra sheets are signed, dated, and marked with the Certificate of Registration Number.

**SD takeaway:** A temporary signatory may sign site inspection records for a total of not more than 30 days, and only if already accepted for the classes and types on that submission; that person does not sign Form MW01 or the supervision plan.
