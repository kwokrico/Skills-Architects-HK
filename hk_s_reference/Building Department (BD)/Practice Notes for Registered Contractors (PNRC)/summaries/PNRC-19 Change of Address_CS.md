# PNRC-19 Change of Address
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued January 1992 / Last revision March 1997 / This revision April 2019 (AD/CS) (Para. 2 amended) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note is the registered contractor's duty to notify a change of business address. It does not cover a change of authorized signatory, a change of site address, or a change of the owner's address.

## Regulatory Overview
Regulation 45 of the Building (Administration) Regulations requires every registered contractor to notify the Building Authority, in the specified form, of any change in the address at which he carries on business, within 14 days of that change. The specified form is Form BA 24.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Form BA 24 within 14 days
| Item | Rule in the note |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Any change in the address at which the registered contractor carries on business |
| Time | Within 14 days thereof |
| Form | BA 24 (see PNRC 2) |
| Online | Electronic Forms Submission System on www.bd.gov.hk |
| Paper | Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon, Hong Kong |

**SD takeaway:** The business address used for the contractor in the schematic appointment documents is the address that must be updated with Form BA 24 within 14 days if it changes.
