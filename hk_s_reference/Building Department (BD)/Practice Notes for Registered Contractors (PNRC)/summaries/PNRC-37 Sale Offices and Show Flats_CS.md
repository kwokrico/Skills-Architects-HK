# PNRC-37 Sale Offices and Show Flats
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1997 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs sale offices and show flats open to the public on a construction site before the new building is certified for occupation. It is not a sales-and-marketing approval and it is not a waiver of occupation control under the Buildings Ordinance.

## Regulatory Overview

A sale office or show flat open to the public is treated as incompatible with construction works. No part of a new building, and no temporary building on the site, may be used that way without the permits this note names, and the Building Authority must be satisfied on structure, fire separation, escape and fire service installations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which permit is required before the public enters

| Place | Must be in hand |
|---|---|
| Any part of a new building, still under construction or substantially completed | A temporary occupation permit |
| A temporary building within the construction site | A temporary building permit and a temporary occupation permit for that purpose |

The contractor, before allowing the public in, satisfies itself that a temporary occupation permit for the relevant premises has been issued and that every condition is observed.

**SD takeaway:** A show flat or site sales office is a temporary-occupation problem from schematic stage: either a segregated part of the new building with a temporary occupation permit, or a temporary building with both a temporary building permit and a temporary occupation permit.

### 2. Tests the Building Authority applies, and conditions it may impose

The Building Authority has to be satisfied that:

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Structure | The premises are structurally suitable |
| Fire compartment | Adequately segregated from the rest of the construction works by fire-resisting construction |
| Escape | Adequate independent and protected means of access and escape, with direct connection to a street |
| Fire services | Adequate fire service installations to the satisfaction of the Director of Fire Services |

Conditions may limit the period of occupation, restrict the number of persons at any one time, and, if necessary, require partial or total suspension of building works during that occupation. Violation may lead to revocation of the temporary occupation permit and prosecution under section 40(1) and (5) of the Buildings Ordinance.

**SD takeaway:** The show-flat plan needs its own street-connected protected escape, a fire separation from live construction, and a works programme that can stop if the permit condition requires it.
