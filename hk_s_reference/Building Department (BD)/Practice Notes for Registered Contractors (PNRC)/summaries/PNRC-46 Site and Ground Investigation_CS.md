# PNRC-46 Site and Ground Investigation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue January 1990; last revision June 2021; this revision September 2025 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Acceptable standards for site investigation and for ground investigation used to design and construct building works such as foundations and site formation. Field density tests and laboratory testing of samples sit outside the registered specialist contractor (ground investigation field works) and are done by accredited laboratories under PNAP APP-64.

## Regulatory Overview

“Site investigation” in section 2 of the Building (Construction) Regulation is investigation of the physical characteristics of the site and includes documentary studies, site surveys, and ground investigation. In this note, ground investigation means surface or subsurface investigation by methods such as boreholes and test pits, including on-site and laboratory tests.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Standards for foundation design

Geotechnical Engineering Office Technical Guidance Note No. 1 lists the documents the Geotechnical Engineering Office uses as Hong Kong geotechnical standards, including Geoguide 2 (Guide to Site Investigation) and Geoguide 3 (Guide to Rock and Soil Descriptions). Site investigation for the design and construction of foundations is to follow those technical guidance documents and the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 (2024 Edition).

**SD takeaway:** Foundation and site-formation layouts are not fixed until the investigation matches Geoguide 2, Geoguide 3, and the Foundations Code 2017 (2024 Edition).

### 2. Who may do the field work, and which submissions it supports

Ground investigation field works are all site operations in ground investigation except laboratory testing of samples and field density tests. They are a category of specialized works. All such field works are carried out by a registered specialist contractor in the ground investigation field works category. Scope and registration are in PNRC 38.

That contractor is responsible for field works mainly for:

| Submission | Statutory hook in this note |
|---|---|
| Ground investigation report supporting a plan for Building Authority approval, including foundation plans, site formation plans, and excavation and lateral support plans | Building (Administration) Regulations regulations 8(1) and 9 |
| Geotechnical assessment submitted with a general building plan, including a plan showing the scope and extent of the ground investigation | Regulation 8(1)(ba) |

The contractor must ensure information in ground investigation reports submitted to the Building Authority is correct, including where some operations, such as geophysical surveys, were done by others.

**SD takeaway:** Engage the ground-investigation specialist contractor early enough for the report to support foundation, site formation, and excavation-and-lateral-support plans, and for the geotechnical assessment that accompanies the general building plan.

### 3. Cores, supervision, and digital reports

Drillhole cores and samples from the ground investigation, other than samples selected for testing, are kept in good condition for inspection by the Buildings Department and, if necessary, the Geotechnical Engineering Office, until acknowledgement of satisfactory completion of the site formation, foundation, or other related works.

Quality supervision of the field works follows the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009 and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2024 Edition).

Ground investigation and laboratory test reports are submitted digitally, in both PDF and the Association of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Specialists (AGS) data format, with the local additions for soil and rock description in Geoguide 2 and 3. Include:

| File | Rule |
|---|---|
| Each ground investigation station (borehole, trial pit, corehole, surface stripping, and similar), with photographs, in PDF | Filename unique to the project, for example SSB-BH1 |
| AGS data | ASCII text, not split into parts |

**SD takeaway:** Allow site storage of cores until foundation and site-formation completion is acknowledged, and specify a single unsplit AGS file plus one PDF per station.
