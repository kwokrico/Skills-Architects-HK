# PNRC-69 Minor Works Contractors Registration
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2009; last revision September 2020; this revision March 2023 (Appendix B amended) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 69 governs registration of minor works contractors so that only contractors competent for the relevant items may carry out minor works. Item, type, and class references in the note are construed under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. This extract covers the note's own instructions, not every page of the 74-page registration document: the body (paragraphs 1 to 39), Appendix A, Appendix B through Class III item 3.5, and the last page of the performance appendix.

## Regulatory Overview
Minor works under the Minor Works Control System are classified into three classes according to their scale, complexity, and risk to safety, and the size, location, and criteria of each item are described in Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. Under section 8A(1)(c) of the Buildings Ordinance the Building Authority keeps a register of minor works contractors qualified to carry out the class, type, and item specified in the register for which they are registered.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Individual for Class III items, company for types and classes
| Stream | How the note describes registration |
|---|---|
| Registered Minor Works Contractor (Individual) | Registered under section 10(1)(a) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation in the name of an individual self-employed worker, for items of Class III minor works. The applicant specifies the items in the application. |
| Registered Minor Works Contractor (Company) | Registered under section 10(1)(b) in the name of a company, including a corporation, sole proprietorship, or partnership, for types and classes of minor works. The applicant specifies the types and classes in the application. |

Where the applicant is found qualified for certain items, types, and classes, the Building Authority specifies in the register only those items, types, and classes. Names, the items, types, and classes, the expiry date, and the registration number are published annually in the Gazette. The Buildings Department website also posts the Authorized Signatory.

An individual may apply for additional Class III items after registration. A company may apply for an additional class or type. In either case the validity of the original registration is not affected by the result of that application. Procedures sit in Appendices E and L, which this extract does not include.

**SD takeaway:** Match the contractor to the submission: an individual only for the Class III items entered on that registration, and a company only for the types and classes entered on that registration.

### 2. What an individual must hold
Under section 11(2) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, a person who intends to register as an individual must satisfy the Building Authority on the appropriate qualifications and experience; the ability and skills to personally carry out the Class III item or items; completion of a recognised training course on Class III minor works; and suitability for registration.

Appendix A, marked Rev. 12/2018, requires a valid Construction Industry Safety Training Certificate (Green Card) at the time of application, and one of these alternatives:

| Alternative | Qualification | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registration under the Construction Workers Registration Ordinance (Cap. 583) as a registered skilled worker for a designated trade division | None stated |
| 2 | A trade test certificate issued by the Construction Industry Council Training Academy or the Vocational Training Council in a relevant trade | None stated |
| 3 | A certificate of completing an apprenticeship in the relevant trade issued under section 28 of the Apprenticeship Ordinance (Cap. 47) | None stated |
| 4 | A qualification recognized by the Construction Workers Registration Authority for registration under Cap. 583 as a registered skilled worker for a designated trade division | None stated |
| 5 | Registration under Cap. 583 as a registered semi-skilled worker for a designated trade division | 4 years in the building industry personally carrying out building works, 1 year of which gained locally |
| 6 | An intermediate trade test certificate issued by the Construction Industry Council Training Academy or the Vocational Training Council in the relevant trade | Same 4 years, of which 1 year gained locally |

A designated trade division is a trade division or an occupation set out in column 2 of Part A or B of Schedule 1 to Cap. 583. Experience is substantiated by endorsement from the employer or building owners; from the authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer, registered inspector, government architect, engineer or surveyor, registered architect, registered professional engineer, registered professional surveyor, or registered contractor of the project; by specified forms, a client's completion or payment certificate, contract documents, accepted works orders, or quotations where the person was self-employed or an employer's endorsement cannot be obtained; or by endorsement from recognized trade associations and labour unions. A statutory declaration before a Justice of the Peace or a lawyer, or a declaration under oath before a specially assigned officer of the Buildings Department, may cover not more than 2 years of experience. A relevant minor works item can include work items of similar nature or under the same trade.

Appendix B lists the trade certificates and vocational qualifications acceptable for registration as an individual:

| | Acceptable certificate or registration |
|---|---|
| (a) | Construction Industry Council trade test certificate |
| (b) | Registration under the Construction Workers Registration Ordinance as Registered Skilled Worker and Registered Skilled Worker (Provisional) |
| (c) | Certificate of completion of an apprenticeship issued under the Apprenticeship Ordinance |
| (d) | Trade certificate issued by the Vocational Training Council |
| (e) | Registration under the Electricity (Registration) Regulation as Grade A, B, C, or R Registered Electrical Worker |
| (f) | Grade I plumber's licence under the Waterworks Regulation and the Waterworks Ordinance |
| (g) | Registration under the Gas Safety (Registration of Gas Installers and Gas Contractors) Regulations as Class 2, 3, 5, or 6 Registered Gas Installer |

The certificate or qualification is in the field or trade relevant to the items of minor works. The extract's trade table starts at Class III items 3.1 to 3.5 and stops during item 3.5. Limits printed for those items:

| Item in this extract | Limit printed in Appendix B |
|---|---|
| 3.1 Removal of the whole of an internal staircase on the lowest storey, where it is not a means of escape or a means of access for firefighting and rescue | Height of the staircase not more than 1.5 m. No alteration of any other structural element, except a simply supported beam that is not of pre-stressed construction and is not used to support any column, flat slab, or ribbed beam. |
| 3.2 Removal of a supporting structure for a building services installation, or a metal casing for such an installation | Located on-grade, on a canopy other than a cantilevered slab, or on a roof other than a cantilevered slab. Height not more than 2.5 m where the structure is for an antenna or transceiver; not more than 2 m where it is for any other building services installation. |
| 3.2 Removal of a ventilation duct or associated supporting frame | Located on-grade, or on a roof other than a cantilevered slab. Height not more than 2 m. |
| 3.3 Repair or replacement of a protective barrier, other than an external reinforced concrete wall or block wall, in accordance with the original design | No additional load to any cantilevered slab. Difference in height between the barrier's level and the adjacent level not more than 2 m. |
| 3.4 Removal of a solid fence wall on-grade | Height of the wall not more than 3 m. |
| 3.5 Removal of an external mesh fence or metal railing on-grade, with or without a solid fence wall as its lower part | Height of the structure, including any feature at its top, not more than 5 m. If the lower part is a solid fence wall, the height of the wall alone not more than 2.5 m. |

A person registered as an individual may be employed by a contractor firm to act as its Authorized Signatory, and should not be the sole proprietor, partner, or technical director of that firm simultaneously.

**SD takeaway:** For a Class III item within the heights and locations printed above, use an individual who holds a Green Card, has completed a recognised Class III training course, and is registered for that item on a relevant trade certificate.

### 3. Company key personnel
Under section 12(5) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, an applicant for registration as a company must satisfy the Building Authority on: the qualifications and experience of its key personnel; access to plants and resources; if it is a corporation, an adequate management structure; the ability of the persons appointed to act for it to understand the minor works under application through relevant experience and a general knowledge of the basic statutory requirements; and suitability for registration.

| Role | Rule in the note |
|---|---|
| Authorized Signatory | Minimum of one, appointed to act for the applicant for the purposes of the Buildings Ordinance. Sole proprietorship: the sole proprietor is the only person eligible. Partnership: any partner appointed by all the other partners. Corporation: a suitable person appointed by the board of directors. |
| Technical Director | For a corporation, a minimum of one director from the board, appointed under the Companies Ordinance and appointed by the board. The director is authorised to have access to plants and resources, provide technical and financial support for the execution of minor works, and make decisions for the company and supervise the Authorized Signatory and other personnel. |
| Same person | May be both Authorized Signatory and Technical Director if he meets the requirements of both. |
| Another firm | A person accepted as Authorized Signatory or Technical Director for a company cannot act as key personnel for another contractor firm simultaneously. Key personnel here means an Authorized Signatory, a Technical Director, or an Other Officer as described in Practice Note for Registered Contractors 38. |

Qualifications and experience are in Appendix G, and academic-qualification guidelines in Appendix H. Neither appendix is in this extract. The Building Authority may refuse an application if the information or documentary proof it considers necessary is not submitted. New applications, renewal and restoration, addition and resignation of Authorized Signatory or Technical Director, and additional class or type, are in Appendices I, J, K, and L. Conviction, disciplinary, and suspension records, business registration documents, and job references are in Appendix M. Those appendices are not in this extract.

**SD takeaway:** Appoint a company only with at least one accepted Authorized Signatory for the types and classes on the submission, and, for a corporation, a Technical Director who is a board director of that company alone.

### 4. Minor Works Contractors Registration Committee
The committee is an independent body appointed by the Building Authority under section 6 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. It examines qualifications, inquires into experience, interviews applicants and their key personnel, advises the Building Authority to allow, defer, or refuse an application either in whole or in part, and reviews decisions of the Building Authority or recommendations of another such committee.

Under section 7(1) the committee consists of one person nominated by the Building Authority who in the Building Authority's opinion has knowledge and experience in minor works; one authorized person or registered structural engineer selected by the Building Authority from nominations by the Architects Registration Board, the Engineers Registration Board, and the Surveyors Registration Board; and one person selected by the Building Authority from persons nominated by such bodies as the Building Authority thinks fit. Under section 7(3) the members elect the Chairman among themselves.

| Case | Rule printed in the note |
|---|---|
| Application involving Class I minor works, including registration as a company, addition of minor works, and addition of an Authorized Signatory or Technical Director | The Building Authority refers it to the committee. The Authorized Signatory nominated in the application, or where necessary the Technical Director, attends the interview. |
| Application not involving Class I minor works | In general not referred, except where special circumstances render a referral necessary. |
| Referred application for registration, additional minor works, or additional Authorized Signatory or Technical Director | The Building Authority must not allow the application, in whole or in part, unless the committee so recommends. |
| Renewal and restoration | The Building Authority may seek the committee's advice under sections 15(1) and 19(1). Interview requirements are in Appendix O, which this extract does not include. |
| Whether an interview is required, and its scope | Dependent on the circumstances of each case and solely at the discretion of the Building Authority and the committee. Performance at the interview and the applicant's track records are taken into consideration. |

An applicant aggrieved by a decision of the Building Authority, or by a recommendation of a committee, on registration, renewal, restoration, addition of items, types, or classes, or addition of an Authorized Signatory or Technical Director, may request a review under section 26 by a specified form submitted to the Building Authority. On payment of the prescribed fees the request is presented to a committee, and where required the person attends an interview. If the Building Authority substitutes the original decision with another decision, the review fee is refunded. The reviewing committee shall not consist of a member who considered the original application.

**SD takeaway:** For a Class I submission, allow time for a committee recommendation before treating the company, the added class, or a new Authorized Signatory as registered for that work.

### 5. Performance Enhancement Course (last page of the extract)
Paragraph 39 points to Appendix P for the Contractor Performance Enhancement Scheme. This extract contains only the closing section of that appendix, marked (11/2019), paragraphs 8 to 11.

| Rule printed on the last page | |
|---|---|
| Purpose | The course enhances performance of a registered contractor in carrying out minor works and prescribed window inspections. |
| Two types | If the contractor is qualified as a Qualified Person (QP) under MWIS, the course covers MWCS and MWIS. If the contractor is not qualified as a QP, the course covers MWCS only. |
| Who attends | The Authorized Signatory represents the contractor. If there is more than one, the Authorized Signatory with the highest PP attends, or the one nominated by the contractor if no one fits that criterion. |
| Certificate | The institution issues a certificate of attendance. The contractor submits it to the Buildings Department in accordance with paragraph 3 of that appendix in order to remit the 15 PP. Paragraph 3 is not in this extract. |
| Conviction or discipline | A contractor convicted or disciplined under the Buildings Ordinance from carrying out minor works or prescribed window inspection or repair is to attend. The Buildings Department issues an NL to attend within three months from the date of the NL. The contractor nominates a responsible Authorized Signatory and submits the certificate. |
| Interview still required | Attendance does not exempt the contractor, where a conviction or disciplinary record so requires, from an interview with the Minor Works Contractors Registration Committee for renewal or restoration. |

The footnote states that, currently, the Hong Kong Institute of Vocational Education will organise the course.

**SD takeaway:** Keep a contractor with a conviction or disciplinary record for minor works or prescribed window inspection or repair on the renewal path that includes a committee interview; the course certificate remits 15 PP and leaves that interview in place.
