# PNRC-12 Overheight Vehicles
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued September 1986; this update January 1990 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs the height of a load on a goods vehicle carrying construction plant, under the Road Traffic Ordinance (Cap. 374). It is not a structural headroom standard for the permanent building and it is not a permit to strike or pass under existing structures.

## Regulatory Overview

Road Traffic (Traffic Control) Regulation 55(d) requires that the height of any load on a goods vehicle must not exceed 4.6 m above the adjacent road surface. An overheight load is an offence by the driver and can damage, or bring down, any structure the vehicle is driven under.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The 4.6 m load limit

Operators of goods vehicles must use the correct type of vehicle, and no part of the load may stand higher than **4.6 m** above the road surface. The Building Authority issued the note at the request of the Commissioner for Transport after incidents involving transport of construction plant. Damage to a structure the vehicle is driven under can collapse that structure or drop debris onto the road.

**SD takeaway:** Haul routes, temporary openings and any structure the contractor must pass under are checked against a **4.6 m** load height before plant is specified.
