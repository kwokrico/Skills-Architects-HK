# PNRC-62 Construction Site Employment Statistics
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue March 2006 (AD/NB2) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note asks registered general building contractors and registered specialist contractors on private building works to return Form GF 527A to the Census and Statistics Department. It does not set a staffing number, a site-safety standard, or a statutory penalty for a missing return. Public-works contracts already submit the same form under Environment, Transport and Works Bureau Technical Circular (Works) No. 4/2005.

## Regulatory Overview
On the last full working day of the reporting period, the return records persons employed by the contractor and his sub-contractors who worked or were engaged at the site, including the site office, for three hours or more, and it also records how many of those persons are employers or self-employed and the number of vacancies for manual workers. For private (non-government) sites the form is completed quarterly for March, June, September, and December, and sent direct to the Census and Statistics Department within the first two weeks of the following month, commencing from the first quarter of 2006.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Who fills GF 527A, how many copies, and by when
| Item | Private / non-government sites | Government sites (Appendix B) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Registered general building contractors and registered specialist contractors involved in private building works, on a site or contract basis | One GF 527A for each contract, completed with GF 527. Nominated sub-contractors complete a separate GF 527A and submit it to the principal contractor for consolidation |
| Copies | Original to the Census and Statistics Department; duplicate kept by the authorized person; triplicate filed as the site record; quadruplicate kept by the contractor | Same four copies, except the duplicate is kept by the project office or the authorized person |
| When | Within the first two weeks of the month following March, June, September, and December | To the client's most senior site representative within the first four working days of the following month. That representative checks the form, except the vacancy and employer/self-employed numbers, which the contractor must keep accurate, and completes onward submission within the first two weeks |
| From when | Quarterly, commencing the first quarter of 2006 | Every month from 1 January 2005, under Technical Circular (Works) No. 4/2005 |
| Signing | The contractor checks and signs before submission. Insert the data or "0". Do not leave a cell blank | Same |

The reporting day is the last full working day of the reporting month. Saturdays, Sundays, public holidays, and days of inclement weather (strong winds, heavy rains, or typhoons) when construction activities may be affected are excluded, and the day is advanced accordingly. Appendix B's example: if the 30th is a Saturday, the last full working day is the 29th (Friday); if the weather on the 29th is adverse, it advances to the 28th (Thursday), and so on.

**SD takeaway:** The authorized person keeps the duplicate of GF 527A, so the schematic appointment should name who on the project team receives that quarterly private-site return within the first two weeks after March, June, September, and December.

### 2. Who is counted on that day
Only persons who have worked or been engaged at the site, including in the site office, for three hours or more on the reporting day are entered. The count covers persons employed by the contractor and his sub-contractors. On a government site, persons employed by nominated sub-contractors are on that nominated sub-contractor's own GF 527A, which goes to the principal contractor for consolidation.

| Row on GF 527A | Who Appendix B describes |
|---|---|
| (i) Manual workers | All persons engaged in manual work, directly employed by the main contractor or called upon by sub-contractors or gang leaders, including skilled, semi-skilled, and general workers |
| (ii) Professional / technologist | A person applying professional skills across technical activities, normally educated and trained to the equivalent of corporate membership of a professional institution, for example architects, engineers, and surveyors |
| (iii) Technician | Between a professional/technologist and a skilled worker; applies proven techniques, normally under the guidance of a professional/technologist, for example clerk of works, site agent, and site foremen |
| (iv) Other site personnel | Persons stationed or working at the site other than (i) to (iii), for example general clerical staff, amah, and security guards |
| (A) | Total persons engaged, and of which how many are female |
| (B) | Employers / self-employed persons included in (A): a person who works for profit or fees in his own business, is not employed by anyone, and may employ others |
| (C) | Vacancies for manual workers only (excluding (ii), (iii), and (iv)). A vacancy is an unfilled job opening immediately available, for which active recruitment steps are being taken on the reporting date. Job title and number are listed |

For a government site only, a work code assigned by the project office is entered and kept for the contract period. If the contract has more than one major work type, the code for the dominant work type is used, and the same code is used on GF 527 and GF 527A.

**SD takeaway:** A person on site for under three hours on the reporting day is outside the GF 527A count, and manual-worker vacancies are recorded only where the job is immediately available and recruitment is already active.
