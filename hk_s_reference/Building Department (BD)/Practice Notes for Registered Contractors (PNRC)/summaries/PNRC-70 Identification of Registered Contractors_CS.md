# PNRC-70 Identification of Registered Contractors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2010 / This revision May 2017 (AD/CS) (General Revamp) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note tells the public, and the contractor, how to confirm registration and how registration is to be shown on publicity. It does not list which class, type, or item of minor works a contractor may carry out, and it does not authorise use of the Buildings Department logo.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority maintains separate registers of registered general building contractors, registered specialist contractors, and registered minor works contractors, which any person may inspect at the Buildings Department or at http://www.bd.gov.hk to check whether a contractor or his authorized signatory is registered. Registered contractors are asked to display their registration numbers and relevant details clearly and conspicuously on publicity materials, and not to use the Buildings Department logo.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Check the register, then show the registration number
| Ask | What the note says |
|---|---|
| Is this contractor, or this authorized signatory, registered? | Inspect the relevant register at the Buildings Department or at http://www.bd.gov.hk. |
| What should appear on advertisements, pamphlets, brochures, and business cards? | Registration numbers and relevant details, displayed clearly and conspicuously, so the public can identify an appropriate contractor for the category of building works or street works, or the class, type, or item of minor works. |
| Private-premises access | Registered Class III minor works contractors (individual) and Qualified Persons are encouraged to produce the registration card and the QP card respectively, for minor works and for inspecting windows, supervising, or carrying out repair works under the Mandatory Window Inspection Scheme. |

**SD takeaway:** Name the contractor in the schematic appointment only after the register shows that contractor, or that authorized signatory, is registered for the category, class, type, or item the works need, and require that registration number on site and publicity material.

### 2. The Buildings Department logo stays off the contractor's material
The Buildings Department logo is protected under the Copyright Ordinance (Cap. 528). The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is the owner of that copyright. Reproduction, copying, adaptation, distribution, dissemination, uploading, posting, modification, publication, or transmission of the logo, or using the logo for any commercial purpose, without the authorisation of the Buildings Department is strictly prohibited. The Department may take appropriate action against anyone using the logo without authorisation.

**SD takeaway:** Contractor drawings, hoarding boards, and appointment stationery in the schematic set carry the registration number and do not carry the Buildings Department logo.
