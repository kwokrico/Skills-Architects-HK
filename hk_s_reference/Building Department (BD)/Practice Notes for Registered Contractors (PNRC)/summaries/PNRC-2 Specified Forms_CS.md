# PNRC-2 Specified Forms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue June 1973 / Last revision June 2024 / This revision May 2026 (AD/CS) (Paragraph 4 & Appendix A amended, and paragraphs 5 & 6 added) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note tells registered general building contractors, registered specialist contractors, and registered minor works contractors which specified forms to use, the time windows printed in Appendix A, and which Appendix A column accepts each form (EFSS and e-Counter, RMWMS, or ESH). It is an extract of the contractor forms. The full list is Appendix A to APP-60. Mandatory Building and Window Inspection Scheme forms are in PNBI-3. Plan-file rules are in PNRC 42.

## Regulatory Overview
Where the Buildings Ordinance or a regulation made under it requires an application, notice, or certificate in a specified form, the form specified by the Building Authority under section 22(4) should be used, and failure to use the correct form may invalidate the application, notice, or certificate. Forms are not to be altered or amended except in accordance with the instructions stated in the forms, and the latest versions are downloaded from the Buildings Department website.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Use the current specified form, on the channel Appendix A marks for it
Electronic Forms Submission System (EFSS) and e-Counter (receipt@bd.gov.hk), the Electronic Submission Hub (ESH), and the Registration and Minor Works Management System (RMWMS, https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk, launched 29 May 2026) each take only the forms Appendix A marks for that channel. ESH Stages 1, 2, and 3 launched on 30 June 2022, 31 March 2023, and 30 June 2024, and commonly used specified forms for new building and alteration and addition works are available via ESH. A hard-copy form should still be filled in on a computer so the information is stored in the QR code. Copies can be obtained during office hours from the General Enquiry and Receipt Counter, G/F, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon.

**SD takeaway:** Lock each notice and certificate in the programme to the form number and to a channel Appendix A marks Yes for that form.

### 2. Time windows that set the construction sequence
Appendix A gives no timing remark for BA2, BA2C, BA25, BA25C, BA25D, BA26, and BA26C. Where a remark states a window, that window is the rule:

| Form | Window stated in the remarks |
|---|---|
| BA2A | Received by the Building Authority not earlier than 4 months and not later than 28 days prior to the date of expiry of the relevant registration. |
| BA2B | A contractor removed under section 8C(6) of the Buildings Ordinance may apply within 2 years of the date of expiry of the registration. |
| BA7 | Submitted within 48 hours of the accident or emergency. Works should be supervised by an authorized person. |
| BA10 | Confirm the appointment and undertaking, and notify commencement of building works or street works. |
| BA11 | Completed and delivered to the authorized person within 7 days of ceasing to be appointed. |
| BA12 | Completed and delivered to the authorized person within 7 days of completion of the new temporary building, new building, or part (temporary occupation permit). |
| BA13 | Completed and delivered to the authorized person within 7 days of completion of a new building. |
| BA14 | Completed and delivered to the authorized person or registered geotechnical engineer within 7 days of completion of building works not resulting in a new building, or of street works. |
| BA14A / BA14B | Completed and delivered to the authorized person within 7 days of completion of the demolition works (BA14B is the streamlined procedure). |
| BA14C | Completed and delivered to the authorized person or registered geotechnical engineer within 7 days of completion (streamlined procedures; works not resulting in a new building). |
| BA18 | A contractor's shed may not be erected until the permit has been obtained. |
| BA20 | Posted on site on commencement of the demolition works. |
| BA24 | Submitted within 14 days of change of address. |
| BA25A / BA26A | Submitted to the Building Authority not more than 4 months but not less than 28 days prior to the expiry of the registration. |
| BA25B / BA26B | A person removed under section 17(1) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation may apply within 2 years from the expiry of the registration. |
| BA25E / BA26D | Submitted within 28 days from the date the reasons for the decision, or the decision to which the recommendation relates, were given. |
| MW01 | Not less than 7 days before commencement of Class I and/or Class II minor works. |
| MW02 | Within 14 days after completion of Class I and/or Class II and/or Class III minor works. |
| MW03 | Not less than 7 days before commencement of Class II minor works. |
| MW04 | Within 14 days after completion of Class II and/or Class III minor works. |
| MW05 | Within 14 days after completion of Class III minor works. |
| MW06-1 / MW06-2 / MW06-3 | Within 14 days after completion of the inspection and/or alteration, rectification or reinforcement of the prescribed building or building works, with the safety inspection report, plans, photographs, and descriptions. |
| MW07 | Within 7 days after the date of appointment of Class I and/or Class II minor works. |
| MW10 | Class I: delivered to the authorized person within 7 days after the date of cessation; the authorized person then submits to the Building Authority within 7 days after the day of receipt. Class II: submitted to the Building Authority within 7 days after the date of cessation. |
| MW11 | Not less than 7 days before commencement of Class I and/or Class II minor works. |
| MW12 | Not less than 7 days before commencement of Class II minor works. |
| MW31 / MW32 | Building (Minor Works) Regulation sections 50 and 36 respectively. Appendix A states no day-count. |
| SC01 / SC02 | Within 14 days after completion of the inspection, with the safety inspection report, plans, photographs, and description, and certify that the signboard is structurally safe and comply with the Buildings Ordinance if no alteration or strengthening is required. Not less than 7 days before commencement of the alteration or strengthening works. |
| SC01C / SC02C | Within 14 days after completion of the alteration or strengthening, and certify that the signboard is structurally safe and comply with the Buildings Ordinance. |
| SC03 | Within 14 days after completion of the inspection and alteration or strengthening (if any), with the safety inspection report, plans, photographs, and description, and certify that the signboard is structurally safe and comply with the Buildings Ordinance. |

**SD takeaway:** Put the 7-day minor-works commencement notices (MW01, MW03, MW11, MW12, and the SC01/SC02 works notices) and the 7-day or 14-day completion certificates on the programme before the schematic start and finish dates are fixed, and do not show a contractor's shed until BA18 is obtained.
