# PNRC-32 Display of Site Information
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue February 1996; this revision January 2005 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs the site board that lets the public contact the registered contractor, and what development information may appear on a hoarding or covered walkway under Building (Planning) Regulation 66(4). It is not a hoarding structural note and it is not permission to advertise on the hoarding.

## Regulatory Overview

The contractor is asked to display, in a prominent position, the site identity and the names and telephone numbers of the project team so the public can report unsafe conditions. Advertisement on a hoarding or covered walkway is not permitted; basic development particulars may be shown there, and any other display stays inside the lot and clear of site operations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The contact board

Display in a prominent position, for the public:

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Site | Address and/or lot number |
| People | Names of the authorized person, the registered structural engineer and the registered contractor |
| Contact | Telephone number(s) |

**SD takeaway:** The hoarding elevation reserves a prominent panel for address or lot number, the three names, and a telephone number.

### 2. What may be shown on the hoarding

Display of advertisement is not permitted on any hoarding or covered walkway. Building (Planning) Regulation 66(4) is not objected to for basic development information: proposed works, use, height, number of blocks, and the anticipated project completion date. Information displayed anywhere other than the hoarding and covered walkway must stay within the lot boundaries and must not interfere with site activities.

**SD takeaway:** Sales graphics and advertising stay off the hoarding and covered walkway; only the basic development facts listed in the note belong there.
