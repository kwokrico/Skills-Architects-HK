# PNRC-54 Contractor's Sheds
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 2002; this revision October 2007 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs permits for contractor’s sheds used as site office, store, staff room or canteen, under Building (Planning) Regulation 53. It is not a permanent building approval and it is not a hoarding permit (a shed must not derive its support from the hoarding or covered walkway).

## Regulatory Overview

A contractor’s shed on a building site needs a permit from the Building Authority, issued on a self-certification whose author depends on size, height and geotechnical setting. A combustible shed is kept clear of adjoining buildings and the site boundary, every shed carries the fire provisions in Appendix B, and a shed must not block or rely on the hoarding.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who may certify, and when the permit is refused

Professional input depends on size and scale. The Building Authority issues the permit on receipt of a complete application and audits some applications in detail at random. The certifier remains responsible for stability and safety in use.

| Path | When | Submission |
|---|---|---|
| Registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor | Shed meets every criterion in Appendix A | Parts I and II of Form BA18, with details of the shed |
| Authorized person and registered structural engineer (they need not be the appointees for the parent site) | Shed is within the curtilage of the building site and does not meet Appendix A | Parts I and III of Form BA18, with structural documents comparable to Building (Administration) Regulations 8(1)(d) and (i) |
| Add a registered geotechnical engineer | The location raises the geotechnical tests in Appendix A item (c) | Geotechnical documents comparable to regulation 8(1)(bb), showing the shed will not affect or be affected by the nearby slope or retaining structures |

A permit is not issued for a shed sited over a hoarding or covered walkway if its support depends on that structure, or if any support obstructs the clear width of the hoarding or covered walkway. Renewal is a written application with the original Form BD 108 and written confirmation from the same certifier that the Form BA18 certification remains valid for the extended period. Form BA18 Part II still cites the criteria in Appendix A of PNAP 273; the criteria printed with this note are its own Appendix A.

**SD takeaway:** Site offices are either inside the Appendix A envelope and contractor-certified, or they are an authorized-person and registered-structural-engineer temporary structure — and they are never carried on the hoarding.

### 2. Appendix A envelope for contractor self-certification

All of the following:

| Criterion | Limit |
|---|---|
| Storeys and height | Single storey, storey height less than **3 m** |
| Floor area | Less than **230 m²** |
| Stilts | Base not more than **1.5 m** above ground |
| Gradient on the shed site | Not more than **15°** |
| Gradient in the **10 m** margin around the footprint | Overall gradient less than **15°** |
| Slopes in that **10 m** margin | None steeper than **30°** or higher than **1.5 m** |
| Retaining or terrace walls | None higher than **1.5 m** on the shed site or in that **10 m** margin |
| Support | Not on a cantilevered structure, and not above a hoarding or covered walkway |
| Location | Inside the building-site boundaries, and not readily accessible to the public |

**SD takeaway:** A shed on a slope, on a cantilever, above the walkway, or at or over 230 m² or 3 m storey height leaves the contractor self-certification path.

### 3. Fire safety (Appendix B) and combustible construction

If the shed is of combustible material, it is located at least **3 m** from any adjoining building or the site boundary, and that space is kept clear. Travel distance inside the shed complies with the Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996.

| Shed | Provisions |
|---|---|
| Certified by a registered contractor: single storey, less than **230 m²** | One **4.5 kg** CO₂ or **9-litre** water extinguisher at each entrance |
| Certified by authorized person / registered structural engineer: more than one storey and less than **230 m²** | The same extinguisher at each entrance, and at least one open staircase |
| Certified by authorized person / registered structural engineer: single storey over **230 m²** | The same extinguisher at each entrance, and fire-engine access such that travel distance from the shed is **30 m** |
| Any other design, or non-compliance with the rows above | Fire Services Department sets requirements case by case |

**SD takeaway:** A combustible site shed needs a clear 3 m strip to the boundary or the next building, and a shed over 230 m² needs a fire-engine approach within 30 m.
