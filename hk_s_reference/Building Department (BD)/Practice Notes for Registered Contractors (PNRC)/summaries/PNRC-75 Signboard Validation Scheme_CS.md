# PNRC-75 Signboard Validation Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue September 2013; this revision December 2020 (AD/CS) (paragraphs 1 and 10 and Appendix A amended). Appendix A rev. 12/2020 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Validation, from 2 September 2013, of existing unauthorised signboards that already fall within the minor-works signboard specifications. It is not a consent path for a new signboard. This summary uses pages 1–12 and the last page (Appendix D) of the 24-page note. Pages 13–23 of the safety inspection checklist were not in the extract and are not summarised.

## Regulatory Overview
The Signboard Validation Scheme applies to unauthorised signboards erected before 2 September 2013 whose type and dimensions follow the minor-works signboards in the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, and it allows continued use after safety inspection, strengthening if necessary, and certification. A validated signboard is inspected and certified again, or removed, at intervals of 5 years, and an unauthorised signboard that is ineligible or has not been validated is subject to Buildings Department enforcement.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who may certify, and the submission by class
Only a prescribed building professional and/or prescribed registered contractor registered for the specific classes and types or items of minor works relating to erection and alteration of signboards may certify the corresponding unauthorised signboard. The signboard owner appoints that person under section 39C(2) of the Buildings Ordinance. Specified construction means a construction that satisfies all the criteria in section 37(4) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. The prescribed registered contractor for repair or strengthening is a registered general building contractor or a registered minor works contractor registered for the class or item of the corresponding type C minor works.

| | Class I | Class II | Class III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspection | Authorized person, and a registered structural engineer if the signboard is not a specified construction | Prescribed building professional (authorized person, registered structural engineer or registered inspector) or prescribed registered contractor | Same as Class II |
| Repair or strengthening | Prescribed registered contractor, under the authorized person’s and registered structural engineer’s supervision as necessary | Prescribed registered contractor | Prescribed registered contractor |
| Inspection and certification form | SC01, with record photos of physical condition and fixing details, plans, and structural calculations, plus the safety inspection checklist | SC02, with record photos of physical condition and fixing details, and plans, plus the safety inspection checklist | SC03, with record photos of physical condition and fixing details, plus the safety inspection checklist |
| If alteration or strengthening is involved | SC01 to notify commencement, and SC01C to notify completion | SC02 to notify commencement, and SC02C to notify completion | Notify completion on SC03 |

Appendix B: alteration or strengthening is carried out under the simplified requirements of the Minor Works Control System by a registered general building contractor or registered minor works contractor registered for that class and type or item. For Class I and Class II, notify the Building Authority of commencement at least 7 days before the works, on SC01 or SC02. For all classes, notify completion within 14 days after completing the works, on SC01C, SC02C or SC03. If the inspected signboard is not structurally safe and repair or strengthening will not be carried out, the signboard should be removed under the Minor Works Control System. The Buildings Department issues an acknowledgement if the required information is provided, and carries out a random audit check.

Validation submissions are not referred to other government departments and are free of charge. If strengthening falls under another department’s jurisdiction, the appointed person obtains that department’s approval or consent separately. Technical guidelines are in Section 7.2 of the Technical Guidelines on Minor Works Control System. The appointed person also observes PNAP APP-126.

If the signboard is in common parts, the owner and appointed person have regard to the deed of mutual covenant and the Building Management Ordinance (Cap. 344), and are advised to obtain consent from the management office, the owners’ corporation or the owners and to arrange third-party liability insurance. Paragraph 6 states that they may have to bear the civil liability for failure to do so. Signboards that cannot be demonstrated to maintain the required lateral spacing from other approved or validated signboards will not be eligible for validation.

**SD takeaway:** Class I validation needs an authorized person and, unless the signboard is a specified construction, a registered structural engineer, and any strengthening of a Class I or Class II signboard is notified at least 7 days before it starts.

**Signboard on the roof (item 3 of Part 3), corresponding item 1.21.** Not consist of stone; no additional load to a cantilevered slab; not involve alteration of structural elements; display area ≤ 20 m²; no part projects beyond the external wall; thickness ≤ 600 mm; any part ≤ 6 m from roof level.

**SD takeaway:** Size an existing signboard to the Appendix A column for its item number, including the spread-footing cap of display area ≤ 1 m², thickness ≤ 300 mm, height ≤ 3 m and footing excavation ≤ 500 mm.

| From kerb | Minimum 1 m |

| Over a pedestrian walkway | Minimum 3.5 m |
| Over a carriageway, or over a tramway | Minimum 5.8 m, or 7 m |

The checklist’s on-grade height cap of ≤ 2 m without a spread footing, and its spread-footing class boxes of 2 m² and 20 m², are not the same figures as Appendix A items 1.23, 2.21 and 2.22. Use Appendix A for eligibility. The rest of the checklist, after Remark 1, is in the unread pages.

**SD takeaway:** Keep projecting signboards within the diagram’s 4.2 m projection, 2.4 m lateral spacing, 3 m opposite-side spacing, 3.5 m over the footway and 5.8 m over the carriageway, and do not replace Appendix A’s spread-footing limit of ≤ 1 m² with the checklist’s 2 m² and 20 m² boxes.

### 4. Display of the validation number (last page)
Appendix D recommends that the signboard owner display the Buildings Department reference number. Letters and digits are black on white, in block capitals, Arial, minimum 35 mm high. The validation submission number is displayed after validation, for example SC13122345. A later validation submission made because of physical changes uses the new number, and the previous number is removed. The number is on durable material, securely fixed, and conspicuous: nearest pavement or pedestrian way for a projecting signboard, a signboard on or hung underneath the soffit of a balcony or canopy, or a signboard fixed on-grade; nearest or opposite pavement or pedestrian way for a wall signboard; main roof if the signboard is on the roof.

**SD takeaway:** Leave a conspicuous face on the signboard for a 35 mm validation number, and replace that number when a later validation follows a physical change.
