# PNRC Series Index (English)

**Scope:** 65 practice notes in this library (PNRC 1 to PNRC 85). Twenty numbers in that run have no source file here; they are listed at the end. This library was not checked against the live Buildings Department list.
**How to use:** Jump from the contents to a topic. Each number opens the critical summary beside this file. The PDF is in `../source_reference/`.
**Titles:** Official titles are the PDF cover headings. Spacing in the PDF text layer is cleaned only where the words are unambiguous.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Check the current Buildings Department PNRC before use. PNRC 1 says the list in force is kept on the Buildings Department website.

| [PNRC-43](<./PNRC-43 Corruption Prevention_CS.md>) | Corruption Prevention | Prohibit advantages, keep supervising staff off the subcontract, report a bribe to the ICAC, and declare any connection between the proof-test ground-investigation specialist and the foundation contractor. |
| [PNRC-62](<./PNRC-62 Construction Site Employment Statistics_CS.md>) | Return on Construction Site Employment Statistics | Return Form GF 527A to the Census and Statistics Department for the last full working day, quarterly for private sites, within the first two weeks after March, June, September, and December. |

| [PNRC-74](<./PNRC-74 Submissions to the Buildings Department_CS.md>) | Submissions to the Buildings Department | Lodge paper new-building and alteration-and-addition submissions at the Taikoo Shing New Buildings counter, and other paper submissions at the Yau Ma Tei Headquarters counter. |
| [PNRC-79](<./PNRC-79 Conduct of Registered Contractors_CS.md>) | Conduct of Registered Contractors under the Buildings Ordinance | Supervise the works, notify contraventions, and avoid the Appendix A examples of negligence and misconduct, which may be referred to a disciplinary board. |

## Minor works, amendments, and validation

| No. | Official title | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PNRC-52](<./PNRC-52 Minor Amendment Works_CS.md>) | Minor Amendment Works | After first consent, minor amendments can proceed under a regulation 33(1) modification, except the Appendix A cases, including major revisions, works outside the lot, pile moves of more than 5 m, and pile-count increases of more than 5% (more than 15% for large diameter bored piles). |

## Site safety, demolition, hoarding, and temporary works

| No. | Official title | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PNRC-4](<./PNRC-4 Hoardings Covered Walkways and Gantries_CS.md>) | Hoardings, Covered Walkways and Gantries | Keep a 1.1 m clear walkway, use steel-plate hoardings for urban demolition, and allow 30-day permit and parking-suspension lead times. |
| [PNRC-6](<./PNRC-6 Demolition Works Measures for Public Safety_CS.md>) | Demolition Works: Measures for Public Safety | Demolition needs a supervision plan, Form BA20, video kept at least 14 days, certificated plant operators, and same-team minor works before demolition. |
| [PNRC-12](<./PNRC-12 Overheight Vehicles_CS.md>) | Overheight Vehicles | No load on a goods vehicle higher than 4.6 m above the road. |
| [PNRC-23](<./PNRC-23 Certification of Plant Operators_CS.md>) | Certification of Plant Operators for Lifting Appliances and Lifting Gear | Lifting appliances and gear are operated only by a person with a current Construction Industry Training Authority certificate, or one approved by the Commissioner for Labour. |
| [PNRC-24](<./PNRC-24 Metal Refuse Chutes_CS.md>) | Metal Refuse Chutes at Construction Sites | Do not rely on external metal-barrel chutes; use a safer, properly secured debris path, preferably internal. |
| [PNRC-26](<./PNRC-26 Plastic Sheet on Scaffolding_CS.md>) | Use of Plastic Sheet to cover scaffolding outside buildings | Screens, fans, or catch platforms are obligatory; any plastic sheet must be flame-retardant and must not be the wind or ventilation strategy. |
| [PNRC-29](<./PNRC-29 Lift Shaft Platforms_CS.md>) | Lift Shaft Platforms | Every shaft platform is designed by the contractor's authorized person or registered structural engineer, with T4 method and T1 supervision, including under a temporary occupation permit. |
| [PNRC-30](<./PNRC-30 Suspended Working Platforms_CS.md>) | Suspended Working Platforms | Deck at least 440 mm wide, guard-rails 900–1150 mm, toe boards at least 200 mm, and counterweight at least three times the balance load. |
| [PNRC-31](<./PNRC-31 Monitoring for Site Safety and Quality_CS.md>) | Monitoring for Site Safety and Quality | The Site Monitoring Section inspects high-risk operations more often; approved plans, sequences, and supervision plans stay on site. |
| [PNRC-32](<./PNRC-32 Display of Site Information_CS.md>) | Display of Site Information | A prominent board shows the address or lot, authorized person, registered structural engineer, contractor, and telephone; the hoarding carries no advertisement. |
| [PNRC-37](<./PNRC-37 Sale Offices and Show Flats_CS.md>) | Sale Offices and Show Flats on Construction Sites | No public sales use without a temporary occupation permit, fire separation, and a protected escape direct to a street. |
| [PNRC-49](<./PNRC-49 Site Auditing for Building Works_CS.md>) | Site Auditing for Building Works | Surprise structural audits run from one month into piling and three months into superstructure, with a shorter track for fast-track two-storey warehouses. |
| [PNRC-54](<./PNRC-54 Contractors Sheds_CS.md>) | Contractor's Sheds | A permit is required under Building (Planning) Regulation 53; contractor self-certification applies only inside the Appendix A envelope and never on the hoarding. |
| [PNRC-64](<./PNRC-64 Site Safety Enhancement_CS.md>) | Site Safety Enhancement | Coordinate the supervision plan with the safety-management tiers, including a six-monthly audit at $100 million or 100 workers. |
| [PNRC-78](<./PNRC-78 Pay for Safety Scheme_CS.md>) | Construction Site Safety — Pay for Safety Scheme | Price safety, including training, as certified items in the Site Safety section of the bills; the supervision codes still apply. |
| [PNRC-83](<./PNRC-83 Demolition of Buildings Code_CS.md>) | Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 | Follow the 2004 code as amended, including scaffold ties, catch platforms, and the stated vibration limits. |
| [PNRC-85](<./PNRC-85 Fire Retardant Scaffolding Materials_CS.md>) | Fire Retardant Performance of Protective Nets, Screens, Tarpaulins and Plastic Sheeting Installed on Scaffolding of External Walls | Test each lot to GB 5725, BS 5867-2 Type B, or NFPA 701, notify the Buildings Department within seven days, and retest at least every 12 months. |

## Materials, structure, and the envelope

| No. | Official title | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PNRC-5](<./PNRC-5 Concrete against Adjoining Walls_CS.md>) | Pouring of Concrete against Walls of Adjoining Buildings | An adjoining wall may be permanent shutter only after a physical check of thickness, condition, and capacity. |
| [PNRC-13](<./PNRC-13 Pulverised Fuel Ash in Concrete_CS.md>) | Pulverised Fuel Ash in Concrete | PFA is limited to 35% of cementitious content, with 25% the usual ceiling for normal work. |
| [PNRC-22](<./PNRC-22 Ban on Hand-dug Caissons_CS.md>) | Ban on Hand-dug Caissons | From 1 February 1996, plans with a hand-dug caisson are refused unless the shaft is not deeper than 3 m with an inscribed circle of at least 1.5 m, or no safe alternative exists. |
| [PNRC-27](<./PNRC-27 Cantilevered Reinforced Concrete Structures_CS.md>) | Design and Construction of Cantilevered Reinforced Concrete Structures | Slab thickness steps at 500 mm and 750 mm span, and an exposed span over 750 mm needs 35 MPa waterproof concrete and galvanized main bars. |
| [PNRC-33](<./PNRC-33 Testing of Reinforcement for Concrete_CS.md>) | Testing of Reinforcement for Concrete | Purchaser's tests are HOKLAS tests to CS2:2012, and prefabricated bars need an RSE list within 21 days of delivery. |
| [PNRC-41](<./PNRC-41 Fixing of Reinforcement_CS.md>) | Fixing of Reinforcement for Concrete Works | Spacers and chairs are at 1.5 m maximum, closer for 20 mm bars and smaller, and a batch is not fixed before its test is complete. |
| [PNRC-47](<./PNRC-47 Aluminium Windows_CS.md>) | Aluminium Windows | Structural aluminium is at least 2 mm thick, mullions at least 38 mm deep, fixing lugs at least 1.5 mm at 300 mm centres, side-hung width not over 700 mm, and top-hung area not over 2.5 m². |
| [PNRC-48](<./PNRC-48 Testing of Building Materials_CS.md>) | Testing of Building Materials | The Building Authority relies on HOKLAS or mutually recognised laboratories and does not issue its own product certificate. |
| [PNRC-63](<./PNRC-63 Precast Concrete Construction_CS.md>) | Quality Control and Supervision of Precast Concrete Construction | Structural precast needs an ISO 9001 factory; the note does not apply to non-structural blocks, architectural features, or planters. |
| [PNRC-67](<./PNRC-67 Wet-fixed External Tiles_CS.md>) | Building External Finishes – Wet-fixed Tiles | Render coats are 8–16 mm and not more than 20 mm in total, and pull-off sampling is at least three samples of each tile type per ten typical floors. |
| [PNRC-68](<./PNRC-68 Large Metal Gates_CS.md>) | Large Metal Gates | A gate higher than 3.2 m needs structural plans, and drilled anchors are pull-tested at five of each type and size to 1.5 times the recommended load. |
| [PNRC-81](<./PNRC-81 Product Certification_CS.md>) | Product Certification System | A certificate of conformity, usually valid for 3 years, can replace a laboratory report for fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partitions under the Issue 3 passive-fire scheme. |
| [PNRC-82](<./PNRC-82 Early-age In-situ Concrete_CS.md>) | Quality of In-situ Reinforced Concrete Works at an Early Age | Different concrete grades trigger rebound-hammer tests between day 7 and day 10, at 50% or 10% by element, with alerts at 55% or 65% of characteristic strength. |
| [PNRC-84](<./PNRC-84 Curtain Wall Window and Window Wall_CS.md>) | Curtain Wall, Window and Window Wall | Tempered glass is heat-soaked under a T3 supervisor for at least 30% of panes, and spider and locking-device proof tests are sampled at 1% or 3 and 0.1% or 5. |

## Ground, railway, drainage, and environment

| No. | Official title | In one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PNRC-11](<./PNRC-11 Testing of Drainage Works_CS.md>) | Testing of Drainage Works | Drainage tests are witnessed under PNAP APP-58, certified to the Building Authority within 7 days, and trenches stay open for four working days, to BS EN 1610:2015. |
| [PNRC-14](<./PNRC-14 Railway Protection_CS.md>) | Railway Protection under Railways Ordinance, Mass Transit Railway (Land Resumption and Related Provisions) Ordinance and Area Number 3 of the Scheduled Areas in Schedule 5 to the Buildings Ordinance | Inside the about-30 m protection zone, piles stay 3 m off underground railway structures and 3 m off a fence or 7 m off an unfenced track centreline, pressure change stays within 20 kPa, and movement is designed to the Alert triggers. |
| [PNRC-15](<./PNRC-15 Asbestos_CS.md>) | Asbestos | New use is prohibited, and in-situ asbestos-containing material needs a registered consultant, 28-day notice to the Environmental Protection Department, and abatement before other building works. |
| [PNRC-17](<./PNRC-17 Environmental Nuisance from Construction Sites_CS.md>) | Control of Environmental Nuisance from Construction Sites | Site layout has to absorb construction-noise permit hours, prior dust notification, de-silting before public drains, and the hoarding plan. |
| [PNRC-21](<./PNRC-21 Tropical Hardwood Timber_CS.md>) | Tropical Hardwood Timber | Reduce tropical hardwood with steel hoardings, steel or precast formwork, and hardwood from renewable sources only where no alternative is available. |
| [PNRC-34](<./PNRC-34 Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes_CS.md>) | Code of Practice on Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes | Buried water-carrying services in or near a slope follow the Works Branch Code, and section 27C allows the Building Authority to require investigation and repair. |
| [PNRC-46](<./PNRC-46 Site and Ground Investigation_CS.md>) | Site Investigation and Ground Investigation | Foundation investigation follows Geoguide 2, Geoguide 3, and the Foundations Code, with field works by a registered specialist contractor and reports in PDF plus one unsplit AGS file. |
| [PNRC-60](<./PNRC-60 Water Seepage_CS.md>) | Water Seepage | Regulations 34, 38, 41, and 48 protect external walls, roofs, and kitchen and bathroom walls and floors, and the note also flags windows, pipes, car park floors, and basements. |
| [PNRC-61](<./PNRC-61 Protection of Natural Streams_CS.md>) | Protection of natural streams/rivers from adverse impacts arising from construction works | Avoid works in natural streams, especially Ecologically Important Streams; a smooth concrete channel is the last option, and construction isolates the bed and keeps discharge away from the stream. |
