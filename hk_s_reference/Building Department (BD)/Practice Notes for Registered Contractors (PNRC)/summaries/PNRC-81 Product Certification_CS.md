# PNRC-81 Product Certification
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue September 2021 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: governs third-party product certification as a quality route for building products, and the Building Authority’s acceptance of certificates of conformity for two passive fire-protection products. It is not a general substitute for accredited laboratory test or assessment reports except for the fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls named in paragraph 4.

## Regulatory Overview
A certification body accredited under the Hong Kong Certification Body Accreditation Scheme, or under a multilateral or mutual recognition partner of that scheme, may issue a certificate of conformity when a product meets a specified Product Conformity Certification Scheme. On completion, authorized persons and registered contractors may submit those certificates with the Certificate and Schedule of Building Materials and Products in PNAP APP-13 and Practice Note for Registered Contractors 25.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What a certificate covers (paras 1–3)

Product certification is upstream control plus continuing surveillance of factory production. The certification body evaluates the application, including a factory audit and on-site selection of one or more samples representative of production, tested by an independent laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme or by a mutual-recognition partner. Periodic surveillance audits cover the plant and the quality-system management office, including audit compliance tests of randomly selected samples. A certificate of conformity is usually valid for 3 years. Re-certification on expiry is carried out as an initial certification audit. If the manufacturer or the product fails the scheme, including a surveillance audit, the certification body may suspend or withdraw the certificate. Authorized persons should require the supplier to provide the test reports described in paragraph 2, for record and for submission to Buildings Department if necessary. Lists of schemes are on the Hong Kong Council for Testing and Certification website.

**SD takeaway:** A product certificate is usually a 3-year document backed by factory audit and accredited laboratory samples, and it can be suspended if surveillance fails.

### 2. Where the certificate replaces a fire test report (paras 4–7)

| Acceptance | Limit |
|---|---|
| Products | Fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls |
| Scheme | Product Conformity Certification Scheme for Passive Fire Protection Products (Fire Door and Non-loadbearing Fire Partition) (Issue 3) |
| Scheme owner | The Hong Kong Institute of Steel Construction |
| What is accepted | A valid certificate of conformity from an accredited certification body, as an alternative to a test or assessment report from an accredited laboratory, to demonstrate compliance with the Buildings Ordinance |
| Completion submission | The certificates with the Schedule under PNAP APP-13 and PNRC 25; test or assessment reports for these products are not required |
| Audit | Buildings Department may randomly select materials or products that have certificates and check that the certificates are relevant; certificate information is then transferred to the Central Data Bank under PNAP ADM-20 |
| Other schemes | Buildings Department will advise scheme owners on compliance with the Buildings Ordinance and will keep suitable schemes under review; that advice is not itself an acceptance of other products |

**SD takeaway:** Specify fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls against Issue 3 of that passive-fire scheme if you intend the certificate of conformity to stand in place of a laboratory report at completion.
