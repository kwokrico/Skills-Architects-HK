# PNRC-1 Practice Notes in Force
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue January 1990 / Last revision November 2002 / This revision July 2012 (AD/CS) (General Revision) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note tells registered contractors how Practice Notes for Registered Contractors (PNRCs) are issued and where the list in force is kept. It does not set a building standard, a dimension, or a submission format.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority issues practice notes to registered contractors to promulgate how he applies and enforces the Buildings Ordinance and its subsidiary regulations, and other administrative and advisory matters in the administration of the Buildings Ordinance related to registered contractors. All PNRCs are disseminated on the Buildings Department website, issue of hard copies ceases, and the list of PNRCs in force is kept updated at http://www.bd.gov.hk.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Use the website list, not a printed index
| Rule | What the note says |
|---|---|

| List in force | Kept updated at http://www.bd.gov.hk. |

**SD takeaway:** Before the schematic package assigns a contractor duty to a PNRC, confirm that note is still on the Buildings Department list in force.
