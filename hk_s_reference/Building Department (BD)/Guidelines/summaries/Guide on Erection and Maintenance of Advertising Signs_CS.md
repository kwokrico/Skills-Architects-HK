# Guide on Erection and Maintenance of Advertising Signs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
The file does not print an edition or effective date. The PDF document title is Guide on Erection & Maintenance of Advertising Signs | Buildings Department

> Scope note: General guidance on erecting and maintaining an advertising sign, including clearances the Buildings Department states and the locks stated by other departments. The advice is not exhaustive.

## Regulatory Overview
A sign that is building works under the Buildings Ordinance needs approval and consent from the Building Authority, an authorized person to coordinate, and a registered contractor to erect it, unless an exemption applies. A sign shall not project more than 4.2 m from the main building line or beyond the centre line of a street, and it must keep the clearances in section 3.3.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Buildings Department geometry and structure (section 3)
| Rule | Lock |
| --- | --- |
| Escape, light, and air | Shall not obstruct or reduce the width of an escape route or stair, or a service lane, and shall not obstruct or reduce required natural lighting, ventilation, or open air |
| Projection | Not more than 4.2 m from the main building line, and not beyond the centre line of a street |
| Over a pavement | Minimum clearance 3.5 m, and minimum clear distance 1 m from the kerb |
| Over a street | Minimum clearance 5.8 m |
| Over a tramway | Minimum clearance 7 m |
| Adjacent signs | Minimum lateral distance 2.4 m |
| Signs from opposite sides of a street | Minimum clear distance 3 m |
| Street | Except where the Building Authority exempts it, no building or other structure shall be erected in, over, or upon any portion of any street |
| Supports | Minimum of two support points on the building. A sign shall not be fixed to, or take support from, another sign |
| Materials | Durable, fire resistant, and protected against rust and corrosion. Structural steel members shall have at least two coats of paint or other corrosion protection. Avoid electrolytic reaction, for example steel screws directly into aluminium |
| Owner | Details of the sign owner prominently displayed. Repair or renew a damaged sign. Remove it when no longer required |
| Before erection | Permission of the building owners and/or owners’ corporation, prior approval of the electricity supplier where appropriate, and a check that the land lease is not contravened |

**SD takeaway:** Cap the projection at 4.2 m and short of the street centre line, and set the underside at 3.5 m over a pavement, 5.8 m over a street, or 7 m over a tramway.

### 2. Electrical and other departments (sections 4–9)
| Source | Lock printed in the guide |
| --- | --- |
| Electrical and Mechanical Services Department | Neon and other current-using signs follow the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations and the related code. A registered electrical contractor or worker undertakes the installation. High-voltage parts are out of reach. Transformers are in an earthed metal container or a ventilated incombustible or fire-resisting enclosure |
| Fireman’s switch | Approved type, conspicuous, not more than 3 m from ground. Outside, as nearly as possible vertically below the sign, or a notice directly below the sign. Inside, near the main entrance. Push upward is OFF and push downward is ON. Board about 300 mm by 250 mm, white with a 50 mm red border, inscribed NEON SIGN-FIREMAN’S SWITCH in English and Chinese |
| Civil Aviation Department | No sign above the airport height limits under the Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301) |
| Country and Marine Parks Authority | A written permit under regulation 10(1)(a) of the Country Parks and Special Areas Regulations is required to display a sign in a country park. Permanent commercial signs are in general not allowed. The application states the purpose, a dimensioned sketch with design and colour, and a location plan, and a copy goes to the District Lands Office as well as to the Director of Agriculture and Fisheries |
| Transport Department | Not on expressways and other high-speed roads, not at junctions, crossings, or curves where visibility is already restricted, and not where colours or shape could be confused with traffic signs. Shall not obstruct the Central–Mid-Levels Escalator, traffic signs, traffic lights, or surveillance cameras. Consult the Transport Department if the sign will be visible to drivers |
| Director of Marine | A light or illuminated sign shall not obscure or interfere with a signal station or aid to navigation, shall not be likely to be mistaken for one, and shall not interfere with safe navigation. If it does, it is removed, extinguished, or screened. Occulting flashing lights may not be used on the roof of an advertising sign visible from seawards |
| Tobacco | Every tobacco advertisement shall contain the health warning required by the Smoking (Public Health) Ordinance (Cap. 371) |
| Medical | The Undesirable Medical Advertisements Ordinance (Cap. 231) restricts advertisements for the diseases and purposes in its schedules, and section 4 prohibits advertisements relating to abortion |

A sign shall not contain obscene or indecent materials.

**SD takeaway:** Put the fireman’s switch within 3 m of the ground, directly below an external neon sign, on the stated white-and-red board.
