# Guidelines on the Design and Construction of Bamboo Scaffolds
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2025 (first issue May 2006) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Good practice for double-layered, truss-out, and signboard bamboo scaffolds, including putlogs, nylon strips, drilled-in anchors, and steel brackets. If the recommended standards are not followed, or another type of bamboo scaffold is used, a professional engineer designs it to the Labour Department Code of Practice for Bamboo Scaffolding Safety. Compliance with these guidelines does not confer immunity from the law. Where workers use the scaffold, the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations and that Code also apply.

## Regulatory Overview
Double-layered bamboo scaffolds follow the putlog grid in Table 1, and a bamboo scaffold higher than 15 m is customised and designed by a professional engineer. A truss-out bamboo scaffold should not exceed 6 m in overall height and must be notified to the Labour Department before erection.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Materials and fixings (Part B, section 1)
| Component | Lock |
| --- | --- |
| Kao Jue | Nominal external diameter not less than 40 mm |
| Mao Jue | Nominal external diameter along the unlapped length not less than 75 mm, and nominal minimum thickness 10 mm |
| Nylon strip | Tensile strength not less than 0.5 kN per strip. Nominal width 5.5 to 6.0 mm. Nominal thickness 0.85 to 1.0 mm. Knot twice to give one round turn |
| Tie wire | At least 6 mm diameter mild steel, yield strength 250 N/mm², anchored to the permanent structure with an anchor bolt and a properly designed bracket |
| Drilled-in anchor | Heavy-duty, at least 12 mm diameter, tensile capacity greater than 7 kN, installed to the manufacturer’s recommendations. Test anchors are selected randomly at different locations |
| Steel brackets | Members of the brackets described for double-layered and truss-out scaffolds jointed with 5 mm fillet welds all round |
| Building profile | Projections, recesses, and free-form profiles should be designed so that bamboo scaffolds can be erected for external wall works and later maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** Detail the facade so that 12 mm anchors and steel brackets can land on structure, not on finishes.

### 2. Double-layered scaffolds and putlogs (section 2 and Table 1)
The inner layer is about 200–250 mm from the building face. The working scaffold is about 600 mm outside the inner layer. Mao Jue posts are about 650 mm apart. Ledgers are at about 600–750 mm vertically. Bracings are at 45° to 60°.

| Height above ground | Wind reference pressure | Putlog grid (horizontal × vertical) | Area per putlog |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 100 m | 2.86 kPa | 3 m × 6.3 m | 18.9 m² |
| 100 m to 500 m | 3.72 kPa | 3 m × 4.2 m | 12.6 m² |

Horizontal spacing of putlogs is not greater than 3 m. Vertical spacing is not greater than 6.3 m below 100 m, and not greater than 4.2 m at 100 m or more. Above 15 m in height, a professional engineer customises the design.

Catch-fans are at 15 m vertical intervals in general, and at 10 m for demolition, and they connect to the working platforms. The horizontal span is not less than 1.5 m from the outer layer. The outer edge is tied with 6 mm steel guy wire at not more than 2.6 m horizontally. Any other support is designed by a professional engineer. Catch-fans are covered with galvanised zinc sheets. A large catch-fan needs extra hanging poles and rakers.

**SD takeaway:** Tighten the putlog grid from 6.3 m to 4.2 m vertically once the scaffold passes 100 m, and provide a catch-fan every 15 m, or every 10 m if the work is demolition.

### 3. Truss-out scaffolds (paragraphs 2.11–2.12)
Truss-out scaffolds are for repair of external drain pipes, spalled concrete, loose rendering, and removal of external unauthorised building works. Overall height should not exceed 6 m. They are light duty, so Kao Jue generally suffices. Rakers, standards, and parallel ledgers must be supported by steel brackets fixed to the permanent structure. Follow the Labour Department code and the Guide on Construction and Work Safety of Truss-out Bamboo Scaffolds. Notify the Labour Department before erection on the Construction Industry Council notification form.

**SD takeaway:** Keep a truss-out scaffold at or under 6 m and land every raker on a steel bracket, and lodge the Labour Department notification before it is erected.
