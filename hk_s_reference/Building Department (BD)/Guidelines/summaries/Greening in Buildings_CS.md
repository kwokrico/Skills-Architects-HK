# Greening in Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
February 2017 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: The text layer does not print a separate cover title. The stated purpose is an easy reference for safe provision of greening in buildings. Building safety and structural integrity come before planting. Greening may be at ground level, on a roof, in a sky garden, on a podium, or on a facade.

## Regulatory Overview
Prior approval is required when greening is new building works, including a new structure, strengthening, or a change to the drainage system, or when it affects the structure, drainage, or fire safety, or is tied to a change in use. A roof and its greenery are given a surface fall of not less than 1:80, and greenery must not reduce or obstruct a refuge floor.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When approval is required (planning section and Table 1)
No approval is required if the proposal adds no new building works, for example no new structure, no strengthening, and no modification of the drainage system. Approval is required if it does involve those works, or if the greening is in an existing building and the assessment shows that submission is necessary.

| Situation | What the guide asks |
| --- | --- |
| New building | Appoint an authorized person and/or registered structural engineer. Include the greening in the building, structural, and drainage plans for approval and consent as appropriate. Appoint a registered contractor for the associated building works |
| Existing greening already approved | Keep installation and use consistent with the original design. If it is not, carry out an overall assessment |
| Existing building, greening not approved | Assess structural, drainage, and fire-safety effects. Submit if alteration and addition or structural strengthening is required. Remove unauthorised building works. Consult an authorized person or registered structural engineer |
| Change of plant or growing medium | May change the design load. If the original design parameters would be affected, obtain professional advice before the change |
| Loading | Depends on soil, vegetation, and use. Check record drawings and survey the structure. Inaccessible roofs may have been designed only for maintenance. Long-span roofs, podiums, and cantilevers are sensitive. Water ponds are heavy and can cause seepage |

**SD takeaway:** If the greening adds structure, strengthening, or drainage, put it on the plans for approval before the planting layout is treated as settled.

### 2. Falls, refuge, and maintenance access
| Rule | Lock |
| --- | --- |
| Fall | Surface fall of not less than 1:80 to the roof and the greenery |
| Drainage | Extra outlets if the existing system cannot take the greening. Discharge heavy rain. Inspect irrigation, drainage, and waterproofing. Repair leakage, blockage, and defects immediately |
| Waterproofing | Prove the roof or podium waterproofing before planting |
| Refuge floor | The refuge extent cannot be reduced or obstructed by greenery or any other use |
| Edge | Provide maintenance access and a protective barrier, such as a railing, at the outer edge |
| Upkeep | Irrigate, weed, and prune so that growth does not overload the structure or block outlets, weepholes, or gutters. Secure plants that could fall, especially before the wet season or a typhoon. Repair spalled concrete and failed waterproofing promptly |

**SD takeaway:** Fall the roof and the planting area at 1:80 or steeper, and keep planting off the refuge floor.
