# Addendum: General Guidelines on Validation Scheme for Unauthorised Signboards
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Revised November 2014 (printed Revised: 11/2014). The scheme was fully implemented on 2 September 2013 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Section 7.4 addendum to the general guidelines on the signboard validation scheme under the Signboard Control System. It is the procedure for retaining an existing unauthorised signboard. It is not a design guide for a new signboard.

## Regulatory Overview
An unauthorised signboard that existed or was completed before 2 September 2013, and that falls within Part 3 of Schedule 3 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, may be retained for five years after inspection, strengthening if required, and certification. A signboard that is not validated, or that misses the five-year cycle, is subject to enforcement, and a validated signboard remains an unauthorised building work because it was erected without approval.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which signboards can be validated (paragraph 7.4.3)
The signboard must have existed or been completed before 2 September 2013. It must be in the list of prescribed building or building works relating to section 39C(1A) of the Buildings Ordinance, in Part 3 of Schedule 3. Technical specifications follow the corresponding minor works items. New Territories exempted houses covered by a certificate of exemption under the Buildings Ordinance (Application to the New Territories) Ordinance are outside the scheme as stated in the background. Designated exempted works item 10 in Part 2 of Schedule 2 is also outside the scheme, because section 41(3B) already exempts those works when the prescribed conditions are met.

| Unauthorised signboard | Class I item | Class II item | Class III item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Projecting signboard | 1.20 | 2.18 | 3.16 |
| Wall signboard, including a shopfront signboard | 1.22 | 2.19 | 3.17 |
| Signboard on the roof | 1.21 | — | — |
| Outdoor signboard fixed on grade, other than construction of the spread footing | 1.23 | 2.21 | — |
| Outdoor signboard with a spread footing | — | 2.22 | — |
| Signboard on or hung underneath the soffit of a balcony or canopy, other than a cantilevered slab | — | 2.20 | — |

Strengthening alone is not accepted unless it is a prerequisite of validation and has been declared on the specified form. Alteration or strengthening of an unauthorised signboard is itself unauthorised works unless the signboard has been or is being validated.

**SD takeaway:** Date the signboard before 2 September 2013 and match it to one row of the table before offering validation as a way to keep it.

### 2. Who may certify (paragraph 7.4.3.7)
Only prescribed building professionals and prescribed registered contractors registered for the signboard class, type, or item may inspect, strengthen, and certify. A prescribed registered contractor must be appointed if strengthening is involved. Specified construction means a construction that meets all the criteria in section 37(4) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation.

| Signboard | Appointed person |
| --- | --- |
| Class I, and it is a specified construction | Authorized person |
| Class I, and it is not a specified construction | Authorized person and registered structural engineer |
| Class II | Authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered inspector, registered general building contractor, or registered minor works contractor (company) registered for Class I or II Type C |
| Class III | The Class II list, plus a registered minor works contractor (company) registered for Class I, II, or III Type C, or a registered minor works contractor (individual) registered for items 3.16 and 3.17 |

A registered inspector may validate a Class II or Class III signboard whether or not the parent building has a Mandatory Building Inspection Scheme notice.

The person who arranges the inspection or strengthening, or the signboard owner under section 24(2)(c)(i), may initiate validation. Personal particulars and contact details of the signboard owner must be on the form before the Department will process it. The owner undertakes to keep the signboard structurally safe, to remove it when the business ceases, and to notify the Department.

**SD takeaway:** Match the appointed person to the class, and add a prescribed registered contractor whenever strengthening is part of the validation.

### 3. Forms, clocks, and what validation does (paragraphs 7.4.4–7.4.6)
| Step | Lock |
| --- | --- |
| After inspection, no strengthening, or the certification | Within 14 days after the inspection, submit Form SC01, SC02, or SC03 with photographs and a description, certifying structural safety and compliance with the Buildings Ordinance |
| Strengthening of a Class I or Class II description | Form SC01 or SC02, with prescribed plans, detail drawings, or calculations if necessary, at least 7 days before strengthening starts |
| Completion of strengthening, all classes | Form SC01C, SC02C, or SC03 within 14 days after completion |
| Acknowledgement | The Department’s letter means the validation procedure is complete. The appointed person remains responsible for compliance |
| Audit | Random audit. Further calculations, details, and certificates may be required. Irregularity can lead to enforcement, prosecution, or disciplinary action |
| Effect | The Department will not serve a section 24 order or a section 24C notice solely because the signboard was built without approval and consent or outside the simplified minor-works requirements. Action is still taken if a change of circumstances creates a safety risk |
| Validity | 5 years. At the end, make a fresh validation submission or remove the signboard. A new owner may use the remainder of the current 5 years, then validate afresh or remove |
| Fee | Submissions, including strengthening under the minor works system if required, are free of charge |
| Legal status | A validated signboard remains an unauthorised building work |
| Common parts | Liaise with co-owners, the owners’ corporation, the management company, and the owners concerned, especially before strengthening, and observe the deed of mutual covenant, including third-party liability insurance |

**SD takeaway:** File the inspection certificate within 14 days, give 7 days’ notice before Class I or II strengthening, and diary the five-year renewal or removal.
