# Amendment to the Technical Guidelines on the Minor Works Control System (October 2025)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
October 2025 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: A two-page Chinese amendment to section 11.2.10 of the Technical Guidelines on the Minor Works Control System (2024 revision). The left column reprints the current scaffold paragraph. The right column adds flame-retardant coverings. It does not amend any other item.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 11.2.10 still requires the design, erection, substantial extension, alteration, maintenance, dismantling, and use of a scaffold to follow the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations and, as applicable, the Labour Department Code of Practice for Bamboo Scaffolding Safety and Code of Practice for Metal Scaffolding Safety. The October 2025 revision adds that every protective net, protective screen, tarpaulin, or plastic sheet installed to cover an external wall and the related scaffold must have suitable flame-retardant properties and meet a recognised standard.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Scaffold paragraph that the sheet leaves in place (section 11.2.10, current column)
The current text, reprinted unchanged beside the revision, defines the scaffold as the working platforms, gangways, runs, and associated ladders or step-ladders, together with the guard rails, toe-boards or other protective devices, and all fixings. Those parts are to be of sound and good material that has adequate strength for the use to which it will be put.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the scaffold itself under the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations and the Labour Department bamboo and metal scaffold codes; this sheet does not relax that paragraph.

### 2. Flame-retardant coverings added in the revision column (section 11.2.10)
The added sentence applies to every protective net, protective screen, tarpaulin, or plastic sheet used to cover the external wall and the related scaffold. The sheet gives these recognised standards as examples:

| Mark on the sheet | Standard named |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | GB 5725-2009, safety nets, or the former GB 16909-1997, dense-mesh safety vertical nets |
| (b) | BS 5867-2:2008, Type B performance requirements, fabrics for curtains, drapes and window blinds, Part 2, flammability requirements, specification |
| (c) | NFPA 701:2019, Test Method 2, standard methods of fire tests for flame propagation of textiles and films |

Another standard may be used if its flame-retardant performance is equal to the standards named above. The sheet is marked (10/2025).

**SD takeaway:** Specify the scaffold net, screen, tarpaulin, or plastic sheet to one of the three named standards, or to a standard of equal flame-retardant performance, before the covering is ordered.
