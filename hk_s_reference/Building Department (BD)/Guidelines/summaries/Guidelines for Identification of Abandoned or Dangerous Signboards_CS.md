# Guidelines for Identification of Abandoned or Dangerous Signboards
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
The file does not print an edition or effective date | Buildings Department

> Scope note: How the Buildings Department identifies an abandoned or dangerous signboard for a Dangerous Structure Removal Notice under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance (Cap. 132). It is not a sizing guide for a new signboard.

## Regulatory Overview
The Department removes abandoned or dangerous signboards that endanger the public by serving a Dangerous Structure Removal Notice on the owner under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance (Cap. 132). If the owner does not comply, the Department arranges removal and recovers the cost, and in an emergency it removes the signboard immediately.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Abandoned signboard
A signboard is treated as abandoned if its display is removed or blank, so that it shows no advertisement in words or logo for a shop or a product, or if the shop named on the signboard does not exist in any premises of the building to which the signboard is fixed. Examples printed: abandoned when the shop was removed; the shop name painted over or removed; only the metal supporting frame left.

**SD takeaway:** A blank panel or a sign for a shop that is no longer in the building is enough for the Department to treat the signboard as abandoned.

### 2. Dangerous signboard
A signboard is treated as dangerous if any one of the following is true:

| Test | What a member of the public could reasonably be concerned about |
| --- | --- |
| Framework | Supporting members, bracing members, or suspension steel wires are not fully intact, or are dilapidated or heavily rusty |
| Fixing to the parent building | Loose or heavily rusty fixing plates or anchor bolts, or cracks at the fixing areas |
| Display | The framework or the display surface is seriously damaged or dilapidated, for example torn, loosely hung, or missing, so that lighting fixtures inside are exposed |

Examples printed: display surface seriously damaged; metal frame seriously distorted; metal frame seriously corroded and the display surface tilted.

**SD takeaway:** Rust, a loose anchor, a crack at the fixing, or a torn face is a removal case, not a matter to leave until the next tenancy.
