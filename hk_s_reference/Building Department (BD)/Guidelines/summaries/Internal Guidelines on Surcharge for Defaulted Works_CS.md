# Internal Guidelines on Imposition of a Surcharge on Defaulted Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
January 2014 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: A summary of the Department’s internal guidelines on the surcharge under section 33 of the Buildings Ordinance when the Department carries out inspection, investigation, or works in default of the owners. The section 33 amendment that allows a surcharge of not more than 20% came into operation on 20 July 2012.

## Regulatory Overview
If owners do not comply with an order or notice, the Department may carry out the works through its contractor under a default-works consultant and then recover the cost of the works, the supervision charge, and a surcharge of not more than 20% of the cost. The rate actually imposed is 0%, 10%, or 20%, depending on the case.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which rate applies
| Case | Surcharge |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency works, and no statutory order or notice has been issued | None |
| The default-works consultant or contractor has been engaged, but the owner takes the work back before the contractor starts on site and then complies | None |
| The letter notifying the decision to carry out the works in default was issued before 20 July 2012 | None. If the owner was later notified of a surcharge after 20 July 2012 and before the works started, this waiver does not apply |
| Genuine practical difficulty: a tenant refuses access, someone obstructs access to common parts, or an attempt to organise works in the common parts fails. Documentary evidence is required | 10% of the total cost |
| The owner is old, infirm, or has a disability or mental illness, and also has a genuine practical difficulty as above | None |
| Defaulted works under Operation Building Bright | None, so that staff costs already funded by the operation are not charged twice |
| All other cases | 20% of the total cost |

An owner aged 70 or above on the date of service of the order or notice is regarded as old. That age follows an eligibility criterion of the Higher Old Age Allowance under the Social Welfare Department’s Social Security Allowance Scheme. A medical certificate, or a reference from the Social Welfare Department, is accepted as proof of infirmity, disability, or mental illness. The scenarios are not exhaustive. Staff consult a supervisor if other circumstances should be considered.

**SD takeaway:** A default works bill is the works cost plus supervision plus up to 20%, and the 10% or nil rate has to be proved with documents, or with age 70 or a medical or social-welfare reference.
