# Cap 344B — Building Management (Third Party Risks Insurance) Regulation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 27 July 2023 | Cap. 344 sub. leg. B

> Scope note: Cap 344B fills out Cap 344 **s.28(1)** — the mandatory third-party risks policy an owners’ corporation must keep for **common parts** and corporation property. Critical for architects advising OCs on A&A / MBIS / façade / slope packages: the statutory policy **does not cover** liability from Cap 123 contraventions (UBW, BA-order failures, material divergence from approved plans).

> Parent: Cap 344 s.28 · Companion: Cap 344A (Land Registry fees only).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 344B (made under Cap 344 s.41; in force from 1 January 2011) prescribes what liabilities the OC’s third-party policy must cover, the **minimum $10 million** sum insured per event, notice-of-insurance display, void policy restrictions, and insurer duties toward third-party judgments.

It is an **insurance compliance** regulation for incorporated owners — not a building design code. Design impact is the **exclusion** of unauthorized / Cap 123-noncompliant works from the mandatory cover.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Hook to Cap 344 — what must be insured

| Source | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Cap 344 **s.28(1)** | OC must procure and keep in force third-party risks insurance for **common parts** + property of the corporation, complying with this Regulation |
| Cap 344B **s.3(1)** | Policy must insure the assured corporation for liability for **death of, or bodily injury to, any person** (subject to s.3(2) exclusions) |
| Cap 344B **s.4** | Cover **not less than $10 million** for death and/or bodily injury arising out of **one event** |

Optional fire / reinstatement cover remains Cap 344 s.28(3) — outside this Regulation’s mandatory package.

---

### 2. Hard exclusions — architect kill-switches (s.3(2))

A policy is **not required** to cover:

| Exclusion | SD / works flag |
|---|---|
| **(c) Cap 123 breach liability** — duty imposed by law re (i) a Cap 123 **building erected in contravention**, or (ii) **building works** or **street works** carried out in contravention of Cap 123 | **UBW / unauthorized A&A / material divergence from approved plans** → outside mandatory OC TPL. Cap 344B defines “contravention” to include BA order / condition failures **and** material divergence from an **approved plan** |
| (a) Motor third-party (Cap 272 s.6(1)(b)) | Vehicle use — separate regime |
| (b) Employee death / injury arising out of employment | Employers’ liability — separate |
| (d) Nuclear / radioactivity / nuclear weapons; chemical / biological / EM weapons; war / hostilities / civil war / rebellion; **terrorist act** (Cap 575); asbestos use / detection / removal / exposure | Specialty risks |
| (e) **Contractual liability** | Pure contract claims not forced into the statutory policy |

**SD takeaway:** Do not tell an OC that Cap 344 / 344B insurance “covers” unauthorized rooftop structures, enclosed common parts, or works that diverge from approved plans. Those sit outside the mandatory policy — clear Cap 123 first.

---

### 3. Key defined terms that import Cap 123 (s.2)

| Term | Meaning for architects |
|---|---|
| **approved plan** | Cap 123 plan approved by the Building Authority |
| **building works / street works** | Cap 123 s.2 meanings |
| **contravention** (of Cap 123) | Includes failure to comply with BA order / condition, **and** material divergence / deviation from an approved plan |
| **relevant person** | AP / RSE / RGE (Cap 123) |
| **statutory instrument** | Order / notice / direction under an Ordinance requiring maintenance, improvement, alteration, repair, demolition, fire-safety installation / improvement, or appointment of a relevant person to investigate; **or** a notice / direction that will be registered in the Land Registry if such work is not done by a stated date |

---

### 4. Notice of insurance — display (s.5)

| Duty | Detail |
|---|---|
| Insurer | Must issue policy **and** a notice of insurance in the Schedule form at the same time |
| OC | Display the notice in a **prominent place** in the building **as soon as practicable**, and keep it displayed **while the policy is in effect** |
| Expired policy | Must **not** display a notice for a policy no longer in effect |
| Lost / destroyed notice | Chairman, vice-chairman (if any), or secretary of the MC must make a statutory declaration |

Breach by OC → every MC member liable (fine at level 1), subject to due-diligence defence.

---

### 5. Void policy restrictions (s.6) — old stock / notices

As respects prescribed liability, policy wording that restricts cover by reference to any of the following is **of no effect**:

| Restriction that cannot defeat prescribed liability |
|---|
| Number of claims during the policy period |
| **Age** of the building |
| **Condition or maintenance** of the building |
| **Number of flats** |
| **Use** of the building |
| Existence of a **statutory instrument** (BA / FSD / other Ordinance orders / notices) |

Insurer may still recover from the OC sums it had to pay only because of this override. Post-event “do X or cover ceases” conditions are also ineffective against prescribed liability (s.6(5)–(6)).

**SD takeaway:** Outstanding MBIS / MWIS / Cap 502 / Cap 572 / BA orders do not automatically void the mandatory TPL as against injured third parties — but Cap 123 **contravention** liability remains excluded under s.3(2)(c).

---

### 6. Other points (compact)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Cannot contract out of prescribed liability (s.7)** | Agreements qualifying future TPL toward a person are ineffective; voluntary assumption of risk does not negative prescribed liability |
| **Insurer must satisfy judgments (ss.8–9)** | Subject to notice / stay / cancellation / avoidance exceptions — mainly insurance-law detail |
| **Winding-up (s.10)** | OC winding-up / bankruptcy of related persons does not alone defeat certain third-party claims |
| **Information duty (s.11)** | OC must give prescribed insurance information when required |
