# Cap 344A — Building Management (Fees) Regulations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 28 June 2018 | Cap. 344 sub. leg. A

> Scope note: Cap 344A is a **Land Registry fee schedule** for owners’ corporation registration and related filings under Cap 344. It sets **no** design, common-parts, or works constraints. Use only for OC registration / due-diligence cost checks.

> Parent: Cap 344 (Building Management Ordinance) · Companion: Cap 344B (third-party risks insurance).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 344A is made under Cap 344 s.41 and prescribes the fees payable to the **Land Registrar** for OC certificates, document registration / filing, register inspection, and certified copies / extracts.

It does not regulate building design, common parts, DMC terms, or procurement — those remain in Cap 344 / Cap 344B.

### 2. Fee schedule (reg. 2 + Schedule)

Fees payable to the **Land Registrar**:

| Item | Matter | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Certificate of registration of a corporation | **$1,300** |
| 2 | Duplicate certificate of registration | **$79** |
| 3 | Amended certificate on change of name | **$185** |
| 4 | Duplicate amended certificate on change of name | **$79** |
| 5 | Registration or filing of any document required under Cap 344 | **$58** |
| 6 | Inspect register of corporations + related documents | **$14** |
| 7 | Copy / extract of register or document in item 6, **per folio or part** | **$14** |
| 8 | Land Registrar’s certificate on any copy / extract in item 7 | **$43** |

Reg. 1 is repealed. No other design or procedural content.
