# Cap 344 — Building Management Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 13 July 2025 | Cap. 344

> Scope note: Cap 344 is a **building-management / multi-ownership statute**, not a Cap 123 design code. It locks **flat vs common parts**, DMC overrides, owners’ corporation (OC) powers, and (from 13 Jul 2025) high-value / large-scale **maintenance procurement** gates. At SD / A&A / estate works on multi-owned stock: read the **registered DMC** first for exclusive designation → map undesignated parts to **Schedule 1** → programme OC / MC approvals and tender thresholds before freezing façade, roof, plant, recreational, or slope packages.

> Companions: **Cap 344A** (Land Registry OC fees) · **Cap 344B** (mandatory third-party risks insurance detail, incl. Cap 123 contravention exclusion).

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 344 applies to multi-owned **buildings** (premises with flats on **2 or more levels**, including basements / underground parking, plus the land and co-owned land) and governs formation of a management committee / owners’ corporation, mandatory and optional DMC terms, common-parts control, funds, accounts, and procurement of supplies / goods / services for management and maintenance.

It does **not** approve building plans or set MOE / FRC / structural standards — those remain Cap 123 / FSD. Cap 344 decides **who owns, who must maintain, who may convert, and how the OC must buy** the works that touch common parts.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. SD gate — when Cap 344 bites

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Building (s.2)** | Flats on **≥ 2 levels** (incl. basements / underground parking) + land + other land in common ownership / held for common use |
| **Flat (s.2)** | Premises in a DMC (any name / use) of which the owner has **exclusive possession** as against other owners |
| **DMC (s.2)** | Registered Land Registry document defining owners’ mutual rights / obligations |
| **Part VIA (ss.34C–34L)** | Applies where a DMC is in force (before or after 8 May 1993). On inconsistency with DMC / other agreement → **Part VIA prevails** |
| **OC / MC** | Management committee under s.3 / 3A / 4 / 40C; corporation registered under s.8 → OC exercises owners’ common-parts rights (s.16) |

**SD action before freezing massing / A&A scope:**

1. Pull registered DMC + plans / undivided-share schedule.  
2. Mark every space as **exclusive** (named in a Land Registry instrument) or **common**.  
3. If undesignated → test against **Schedule 1** (default common).  
4. Any work on common parts → OC / MC path + procurement classification (Type 1 / Type 2 / large-scale maintenance).  
5. Exclusive conversion of common parts → s.34I owners’ committee (or MC) resolution required.

---

### 2. Common parts — the definition that controls design (s.2)

**common parts** means:

| Limb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | The **whole building**, except parts **specified or designated** in a Land Registry-registered instrument as for the **exclusive** use, occupation or enjoyment of an owner |
| **(b)** | Unless so specified / designated → the parts listed in **Schedule 1** |

**Design traps:**

- Cap 123 GFA / “private open space” marketing labels ≠ Cap 344 exclusive designation.  
- Roof gardens, clubhouses, external walls, refuse rooms, plant rooms: often **common** even if one flat “uses” them daily.  
- A fixture **inside** a flat that serves another flat / the building is still common (Sch 1 item 11).

### 3. Schedule 1 — default common-parts checklist

Unless exclusively designated in a registered instrument, treat as common:

| # | Part | SD flag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | External walls, load-bearing walls, foundations, columns, beams, other structural supports | Façade / A&A / UBW on external wall = common-parts work |
| 2 | Walls enclosing passageways, corridors, staircases | Corridor shopfront / lobby carve-outs |
| 3 | Roofs, chimneys, gables, gutters, lightning conductors, satellite dishes & ancillary equipment, aerials & cables | Roof plant, signage, dishes |
| 4 | Parapet walls, fences, boundary walls | Boundary / podium edge |
| 5 | Vents serving **2 or more** flats | Shared kitchen / toilet / smoke vents |
| 6 | Water tanks, reservoirs, pumps, wells, sewers, sewage treatment plants, drains, soil / waste pipes, channels, water-courses, gutters, ducts, downpipes, cables, conduits, refuse chutes, hoppers, refuse container chambers | Building services + refuse |
| 7 | Cellars, toilets, WCs, wash houses, bathhouses, kitchens, caretakers’ flats | Shared amenity / staff flat |
| 8 | Passageways, corridors, staircases, landings, light wells, staircase window frames & glazing, hatchways, roofways, roof outlets, doors / gates giving access thereto | MOE / lobby / light well |
| 9 | Lifts, escalators, lift shafts, machinery / apparatus, housing thereof | Lift lobby / machine room |
| 10 | Lighting, air-conditioning, central heating, fire-fighting equipment / installations for owners generally + rooms housing them | Shared FSI / AC plant rooms |
| 11 | Fixtures in a flat used in connexion with enjoyment of any other flat or portion of the building | Risers / stacks / shared M&E inside units |
| 12 | Lawns, gardens, playgrounds, other recreational areas | Landscape / podium gardens |

### 4. s.34I — common-parts conversion kill-switch

No person may:

| Ban | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Convert** any common part to his own use | Unless approved by **owners’ committee** resolution (if any) |
| **Use / permit use** of common parts so as to | (i) unreasonably interfere with owners’ / occupiers’ use and enjoyment; or (ii) cause nuisance or hazard to any person lawfully in the building |

Contravention = deemed **breach of DMC** obligation.

**SD takeaway:** Enclosing corridor / roof / external wall / light well / podium garden into a flat, shop, or exclusive terrace needs the statutory / DMC approval path — plan it before GAD freeze. Once an MC is appointed, it **is** the owners’ committee for DMC purposes (s.34K).

---

### 5. s.34H — exclusive parts still must be maintained

If a person owns, or has exclusive possession / exclusive right to use, occupation or enjoyment of a part, but the DMC is **silent** on maintenance → that person **must maintain it in good repair and condition**.

Obligation is deemed owed to **all owners** under the DMC.

**SD takeaway:** Exclusive roof / balcony / garden / shopfront still carries a maintain duty — do not treat “exclusive” as “no repair obligation.”

### 6. Who authorizes and executes works (ss.16, 18, 34K, 40)

#### 6.1 OC duties / powers on common parts (s.18)

| Must (s.18(1)) | May (s.18(2) — select) |
|---|---|
| Maintain common parts + corporation property in good / serviceable repair and clean condition | Engage manager / professionals for OC duties |
| Carry out works ordered / required on common parts by any public officer / body under any Ordinance | Purchase / hire movable property for common enjoyment or to satisfy public-officer requirements |
| Enforce DMC control / management / administration obligations | Establish / maintain lawns, gardens, playgrounds on common parts |
| Have regard to / be guided by **Codes of Practice** under s.44 (s.18(2A)) | **Renovation, improvement or decoration** of common parts (s.18(2)(fa)) |

Rights of owners re common parts are exercised by the corporation; liabilities of owners re common parts are enforceable against the corporation (s.16).

#### 6.2 Entry into flats (s.40)

MC member / authorized person may, on reasonable notice, enter a flat at reasonable time to:

- Inspect / repair / maintain / renew **common parts in the flat**, or other property in the flat whose condition adversely affects common parts / other owners  
- Abate hazard / nuisance affecting common parts / other owners  

Magistrate may authorize break-in with police present. Costs for non-common adverse property / nuisance abatement recoverable from the flat owner.

**SD takeaway:** Design common risers / stacks / FSI inside flats knowing OC will need **access rights** — coordinate with DMC and unit layouts.

### 7. 2024 procurement gates — Type 1 / Type 2 / large-scale maintenance (ss.2D–2E, 28A–28F)

Applies to procurement of supplies, goods or services required to perform a function under the DMC or Cap 344 (typical OC façade / MBIS / roof / slope / plant packages). Classification drives tender + resolution requirements. Monetary amounts / percentages may be Gazette-amended by the Authority.

#### 7.1 Classification

| Class | Test | Core process (ss.28D–28F) |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1 high-value** | Value **> $200,000** **and** ≤ **20%** of **reference amount**; **not** large-scale maintenance | Invitation to tender + Sch 6A tendering + Sch 6B Part 1 declarations |
| **Type 2 high-value** | Value **> 20%** of reference amount; **not** large-scale maintenance | Tender + Sch 6A + Sch 6B Part 1 + **corporation resolution** to accept tender; vary / terminate only by corporation resolution |
| **Large-scale maintenance** | For **repairing / replacing / maintaining / improving common parts**; **and** value ÷ number of flats **> $30,000**; **and** not cleaning / security / manager’s building-management services | Tender + Sch 6A + Sch 6B Parts 1 **and** 2 + corporation resolution to accept; vary / terminate only by corporation resolution; **Sch 6C** meeting procedure for acceptance / variation / termination |

**Flat count for large-scale test (s.2E(2)):** exclude garage / carpark / carport **unless** every flat in the building is (or is part of) a garage / carpark / carport.

**Reference amount (s.2D(3)):** average of last 3 full years’ specified annual expenditure if available; else last Sch 5 (or DMC) proposed annual expenditure budget before initiation date.

#### 7.2 Same-supplier shortcut (Type 1 / Type 2 only)

Tender invitation may be skipped if target supplies are of the **same type** as those already supplied for the building **and** a **corporation resolution** directs procurement from that supplier on specified terms — **not** available to bypass large-scale maintenance rules.

#### 7.3 Architect programming flag

For OC A&A / façade / roof / slope / FSI upgrade packages, estimate contract value ÷ flat count **early**. Crossing **$30,000 / flat** on common-parts repair / improve work pulls the **large-scale** track (longer programme, voting-in-person thresholds under Sch 6C, declaration regime). Non-compliance with essential tender / acceptance requirements can make contracts **voidable** (ss.28H–28I).

### 8. Insurance hook (s.28 → Cap 344B)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Mandatory (s.28(1))** | OC must procure and keep in force third-party risks insurance for **common parts** + corporation property, complying with prescribed requirements (**Cap 344B**) |
| **Optional (s.28(3))** | May also insure common parts / corporation property to **reinstatement** value against fire and other risks |
| **Breach** | Accountable MC participants commit an offence (fine at level 5) |

See Cap 344B summary for **$10 million** minimum and the critical **Cap 123 contravention / UBW exclusion**.

---

### 9. Mandatory vs optional DMC terms (ss.34E–34F) — SD awareness only

| Layer | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Schedule 7 (s.34E)** | Impliedly incorporated into every DMC (pre- and post–8 May 1993); binds owners and manager; **prevails** over inconsistent DMC terms. Includes manager duties, accounts, procurement mirrors, etc. |
| **Schedule 8 (s.34F)** | Implied only **to the extent consistent** with the DMC; may later be amended / deleted / re-incorporated by corporation (or owners) resolution |
| **s.34J** | DMC cannot prevent OC formation or management business at meetings; impossible quorums that frustrate management business are void |

Do not redesign DMC text at SD — but do not assume private DMC wording can override Schedule 7 or Part VIA.
