# Code of Practice for Mandatory Building Inspection Scheme and Mandatory Window Inspection Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
2012 Code (2023 Edition, revision May 2023; incorporates PNBI-10 amendments of Sep 2014, Mar 2017, Apr 2018, Jul 2020, Jan 2022) | BD Code of Practice under Cap. 123 ss.30B–30C / Cap. 123P (B(I&R)R)

> Scope note: This CoP is the **technical + procedural rulebook for prescribed inspection and repair** once the Building Authority (BA) serves an MBIS (s.30B) or MWIS (s.30C) notice. It is **not** a new-build massing code. At schematic design it matters for: (1) existing-building A&A / adaptive reuse / façade packages; (2) designing envelopes, projections, windows and common parts that remain **inspectable, repairable and durable** through future MBIS/MWIS cycles; (3) knowing which fabric sits on the RI path vs the QP window path; (4) cost / programme risk from opening-up ratios, proof tests and dual MWCS vs full-consent tracks. Read with Cap. 123P, Cap. 123N (MWCS), PNAP APP-116 / ADV-31 / APP-45, PNBI series, and CoP for Site Supervision.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This Code applies when the BA has served a notice under Cap. 123 **s.30B (MBIS)** or **s.30C (MWIS)** for a target building. MBIS covers **common parts** (Cap. 344 meaning; excluding areas within individual private premises), **all external walls** (including party walls, whether or not common), **prescribed projections** under Cap. 123P / B(I&R)R, **signboards**, and building works within or projecting outside the building envelope or lot boundary — including common drains that run through non-common parts. MWIS covers **all windows, glass louvers and window walls** in both private premises and common parts (but not curtain walls, internal glass partitions, or G/F shopfront show windows).

Statutory age triggers live in Cap. 123 (typically MBIS for buildings aged **≥ 30 years** from OP; MWIS on a shorter cycle — confirm current BA selection policy). Repair must restore safety **until the next inspection cycle**, measured against the **original OP-era standards and approved A&A**, or **completed Cap. 502 / Cap. 572 FS Works standards** if already upgraded — not automatically current design codes. Most prescribed repairs run under the **Minor Works Control System**; non-minor / non-exempt works need full BA approval and consent under Cap. 123 s.14.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. MBIS vs MWIS — split the façade / fenestration package early

| Scheme | Notice | Who inspects | Who repairs | What is in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MBIS** | Cap. 123 s.30B | **Registered Inspector (RI)** on inspectors’ register (BO s.3(3B)) | **RC** — RGBC or RMWC qualified for the works — under RI supervision | Common parts, external walls, projections, signboards, exposed structure, fire safety provisions, common drainage, UBW |
| **MWIS** | Cap. 123 s.30C | **Qualified Person (QP)** — AP / RSE / RI / RGBC / RMWC registered for window or window-wall works (excluding persons under disciplinary order) | RC registered for window / window-wall works (QP may also act as RC if eligible) | Windows, glass louvers, window walls |

**Hard splits (do not mix at SD):**
- **Curtain walls → MBIS only** (§8.5) — including associated openable windows on the CW system (§3.3.1)
- **Windows on external walls → MWIS only** (§3.3.1(b), §8.3–8.5)
- **Internal glass partitions** and **G/F shopfront show windows → out of MWIS** (§8.4)
- **Skylights in common parts → MBIS** (§3.3.1(b))
- **Lower part of window / window wall acting as protective barrier** — eligible RMWC-as-QP may **inspect**; **repair** of the barrier portion needs an RMWC registered for protective-barrier works (§9.1.2)

**SD takeaway:** Specifying “curtain wall + punched windows + window wall + shopfront glass” creates **two parallel statutory regimes**, two professionals, and two repair tracks. Coordinate access, warranties, tender packages and fee proposals accordingly.

### 2. MBIS inspection map — five limbs only (§3.1)

Mandatory inspection covers **only**:

1. External elements and other physical elements  
2. Structural elements  
3. Fire safety elements  
4. Drainage system  
5. Unauthorised building works (UBW)

#### 2.1 Explicitly **out of scope** (§3.1.3) — do not budget under MBIS

Foundations; buried / embedded pile caps and ground beams; freestanding earth-retaining structures; slopes and buried water services in slopes; lifts; escalators; fire service installations; electric wiring; ventilation / AC systems; gas and water supply installations.

#### 2.2 Single-ownership buildings without DMC (§1.3)

Inspect external walls, prescribed projections, signboards, and parts **not** for exclusive use / occupation / enjoyment of any owner / occupier.

#### 2.3 Definitions that lock scope

| Term | Meaning used by this CoP |
|---|---|
| **Common parts** | Cap. 344 s.2 |
| **External wall** | Whole or any part of an outer wall, even if adjoining another building; **includes party wall** |
| **Signboard** | Cap. 123 s.2 definition |
| **Minor works** | Those designated in Cap. 123N B(MW)R |
| **Exempted building works** | Cap. 123 ss.41(3), 41(3B), 41(3C) — no prior approval/consent and no AP/RSE/RGE/RC |

#### 2.4 Common drains in non-common parts (§3.1.1)

Common drains inside private premises remain within MBIS drainage scope.

### 4. External envelope — design for inspectability and durability (§3.3 / §5.3)

#### 4.1 Elements that will be opened, tapped and reported

| Element | Typical defects RI must find | Repair / SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| External finishes (tile / render) | Loose / missing, cracks, bulging, delamination, corrosion of embeds, spalling | Prefer **remove + reinstate**; see App. IV numbers below |
| Cladding | Displacement, cracked panels, sealant failure, rust stains, corroded anchors / frames | Fixings only opened if defects show; renewed panels **non-combustible**, ≥ original thickness/strength/durability; corrosion-protected permanent dowels (§5.3.2) |
| Fins / grilles / louvers | Dilapidation, corrosion, loose fixings | Renew durable + securely fixed; permanent corrosion-protected anchors (§5.3.3) |
| Protective barriers / parapets / balustrades | Broken glass, corroded fixings, loose rails, sealant failure | Also on **structural** limb — critical; replace defective components ≥ original standard (§5.3.6) |
| Curtain wall + CW openables | Broken glass, fixings, sealants, **defective fire stop**, locking devices / hinges, water behind CW | Inspect from common parts **and** private premises if external defects found; replacement materials (glass, structural sealant, fire stop) need **certificates/reports to BA** (§5.3.4) |
| Appendages (AC frames, cooling towers, chimneys, pipes/ducts, eaves, mouldings, architectural projections, drying racks, signboards, TV-screen signboards, window canopies) | Dilapidation, corrosion, loose fixings | Removal + reinstatement often MWCS; renewed install ≥ strength/stiffness/durability; **permanent corrosion-protected anchors into structure** (§5.3.5) |
| Metal gates (manual / electric) | Defective / missing hinges; defective track / guide / stopper / anti-derail device; corroded steel posts / fixings | Steel post repair follows structural-steel rules (§5.3.6 / §5.4.2) |
| Common corridor finishes / false ceilings / stone cladding | Falling-hazard focus | Defective false ceiling → remove and/or replace; finishes may reinstate to match appearance (§5.3.6) |

#### 4.2 Inspection methods (§3.3.2(A))

Visual and/or NDT: hammer-tapping, infrared thermography, or other feasible means — from ground and available vantage points / openings. Special technologies allowed via agency, but RI remains personally responsible. Party walls: inspect exposed parts not within private premises. **Daily inspection records** (time, date, locations, items) → submit to BD on completion.

#### 4.3 Minimum opening-up ratios for concealed fabric (§3.3.2(J))

| Condition | Minimum inspection exposure |
|---|---|
| External wall with **any** concealed element | **≥ 30%** of that wall area |
| Wall concealed by signboard with display area **> 40 m²** | **≥ 30%** of concealed area (remove display temporarily or equivalent); expand if defects found |

**SD takeaway:** Large wall signage and closed rainscreens without access create statutory opening-up cost. Design inspection panels, BMU / gondola reach, and removable cladding zones.

#### 4.4 Rendering / tiling repair numbers (App. IV) — lock into façade specs

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Preferred method | Remove all debonded finish + bedding to sound substrate |
| Max total render thickness | Generally **≤ 20 mm** |
| Max per cement:sand (1:3) layer | **≤ 10 mm** |
| Bonding / tile adhesive | Follow PNAP **ADV-31** App. A |
| Integrity check after repair | All repaired areas + **~200 mm** surround (hammer-tap etc.) |
| Pull-off rate (tiling) | **≥ 1 / 25 m²** per tile type; **≥ 1 per elevation**; full retiling → **≥ 1 per storey** |
| Pull-off stress | **≥ 0.5 N/mm²** |
| Failed pull-off → further tests | `(n² − 2n + 3)` where n = accumulated unsuccessful tests |

### 5. Structural elements — high-risk geometries (§3.4 / §4 / §5.4 / App. III & V)

#### 5.1 In-scope structure (§3.4.1)

External walls + common parts of main building **and** ancillary structures within the lot (elevated driveways, bridging structures, clubhouses, guard houses, swimming pools including filtration plant rooms, etc.).

Listed elements: columns; walls; beams; slabs; staircases; **cantilevered projecting structures**; **transfer structures**; water tanks and suspended manholes; protective barriers / railings / parapets / balustrades; screen walls and basement walls; hanging structures; **exposed** pile caps; other exposed special structural elements.

#### 5.2 Defects / phenomena that must be identified (§3.4.2(A))

Dampness; rust stains / reinforcement corrosion; cracks or distress; concrete spalling; delamination; exposed reinforcement; voids / honeycombing; deformation / displacement; **abnormal separation from adjoining buildings**.

#### 5.3 Cantilevered projecting structures — SD red flag (§3.4.2(B))

| Type | Opening-up if concealed (shopfront / false ceiling etc.) |
|---|---|
| **Pure cantilevered slab** (canopy / balcony) | **100%** of concealed structure |
| **Beam-slab type** cantilever | **≥ 30%** |

**Street-projecting cantilevered slab canopy — mandatory investigation** (in addition to visual):
1. Open-up concrete cover at canopy **root** adjoining parent structure → assess main bar corrosion  
2. Coring → actual slab thickness, compressive strength, carbonation  
3. At open-ups → bar diameter, spacing, concrete cover, screed + waterproofing thickness  
4. Measure span; parapet / downturn / architectural features  
5. Sampling: **≥ 2 locations per discrete canopy**, or **1 per 6 m** of canopy length — **whichever is greater**

Also watch: water ponding, defective drains, seepage, UBW on / under soffit. If safety uncertain after open-up → consider detailed investigation.

**SD takeaway:** Avoid pure cantilevered slab canopies over streets; prefer framed / tied-back systems with accessible structure and durable waterproofing / drainage.

#### 5.4 Transfer plates / beams (§3.4.2(C))

Critical for stability. If concealed: expose **≥ 30%**. Other concealed structure (not cantilever / transfer): RI judgment for representative exposure.

#### 5.5 Detailed investigation (DI) — when and how (§4)

**Trigger:** serious defects **not** from normal wear that threaten structural stability or serious health hazard; or extent / cause cannot be ascertained; may also cover drainage integrity or FRR capability uncertainty.

**Process:** notify BA + submit DI proposal (purpose, scope/methods, justification, annotated photos / marked-up plans of defects) **before** starting. Do not commence until BA endorses. If BA refuses, RI may still proceed only if the person for whom inspection is carried out is informed and still elects to proceed (§4.2.3).

**Structural defect types that should prompt DI consideration (§4.3.1):**
- Serious flexural cracks on beam/slab sides and soffit  
- Diagonal cracks at / near beam supports  
- Helical cracks on beam faces  
- Cracks at tension zone of cantilevers  
- Cracking or crushing of columns  
- Tensile cracks at hanging structures  
- Other serious structural cracks  
- Excessive deformation  
- Global building movement  
- Extensive spalling or serious rebar / structural-steel corrosion  
- Serious main-bar corrosion at cantilever slab canopy root after open-up  

**Methods:** visual + hammer-tap, carbonation, chloride / cement content, coring, rebound hammer, crack survey, covermeter, etc. Specialists allowed under RI supervision; RI remains personally responsible for applying findings to the repair proposal.

**FSP outcome (§4.3.4):** if limit-state resistance < factored load effects → **report BA immediately + emergency remedial works**.

#### 5.6 Factor of Structural Performance (App. III) — assessment rules

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Loads | Characteristic dead / imposed / wind / water / soil per BO; may use **original** codes from approved plans; if plans missing → by OP date |
| Material properties | Original characteristic values OK if not deteriorated; must remain valid **until next inspection cycle** → protect materials in repair proposal |
| **WCS** usable when | Characteristic assessment shows element may fail full load; damage/deterioration below characteristic; or design characteristic values unretrievable |
| Rebar / tendon WCS | Extract + test specimens without reducing capacity or damaging adjacent highly stressed bars |
| Concrete WCS | Lower-bound from cores: \(WCS = (1 - 0.12/\sqrt{n}) \times (\sum f_c / n)\); **min 3 cores**; whole-structure sampling **≥ 1 core / 50 m³**; take lesser of equation result or least individual result |
| γm concrete (WCS cores) | **1.25** (flexure / axial) |
| γm reinforcement | **1.1** (characteristic / min specified); **1.05** if from WCS tests |
| Other standards | Must be used **in entirety** — do not mix with App. III modified factors |

**Modified partial safety factors for factored load effects (Table 1, ULS):**

| Combination | Dead adverse / beneficial | Imposed adverse / beneficial | Earth | Water | Wind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dead + imposed | **1.2** / **1.0** | **1.5** / **0** | **1.3** | **1.1** | — |
| Dead + wind | **1.2** / **1.0** | — | **1.3** | **1.1** | **1.4** |
| Dead + wind + imposed | **1.2** / **1.0** | **1.2** / **0** | **1.2** | **1.1** | **1.2** |

Notes: dimensions by site measurement; beneficial dead when it causes worst total effect; patterned alternate-span loading need not be considered; water from worst probable level.

#### 5.7 Reinforced concrete repair (App. V) — design / tender implications

**Patch repair**
- For localized, non-critical defects; repair depth **< 75 mm**  
- **Not** for critical elements (e.g. columns with extensive defects) where capacity depends on the repair  
- Mortar compressive strength ≥ substrate; 7-day compressive + bond tests (**≥ 2 specimens each**) unless valid Product Conformity Certification Scheme CoC for repair mortar  
- Hack to expose rebar with **10–20 mm** clear gap around bars; roughen substrate  
- If rebar section loss **> 15%** → replace with new bars (CS2, grade/size ≥ original) + adequate lap; prime after cleaning  
- Mortar layers generally **≥ 10 mm** each (unless manufacturer says otherwise)  
- Proof: hammer-tap + pull-off **≥ 1 / 25 m²** and **≥ 1 per day’s** patching; stress **≥ 0.5 N/mm²** unless fracture in substrate; failed tests → `(n² − 2n + 3)` further tests  

**Recasting** (required when extensive spalling / deterioration, chloride **> 0.8% by weight of cement**, or significant rebar corrosion)
- Formwork + adequate propping until strength gained  
- New concrete strength ≥ parent; cube testing per CS1 by HOKLAS lab; sampling / acceptance per Building (Construction) Regulation  
- New rebar CS2 ≥ original; mill certificates in Completion Report; substantial replacement → consider further tests (PNAP APP-45)  
- Proof: rebound hammer or other suitable tests  

**Crack repair options:** brush cement grout; open larger cracks + patch; pour low-viscosity polymer resin; pressurized epoxy injection. Clean / dry cracks first; epoxy compressive strength compatible with substrate.

#### 5.8 Structural steel / masonry repair (§5.4.2–5.4.3)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Corrosion prep | Remove loose corrosion (brush / grind / grit blast) to recognized surface-prep grades; paint system + coat thickness for exposure |
| Capacity significantly impaired | Consider remove + replace ≥ original grade/size; welded renewals → NDT (MPI / ultrasonic) |
| Bolts / rivets | Replace corroded friction-grip / black bolts / rivets; new bolts torque-checked; grade/size ≥ original |
| Fire protection on steel | Reinstate FRR ≥ original; ensure FR material / primer / steel compatibility; follow manufacturer |
| Masonry sulphate attack | Stop damp source; localized → rake + sulphate-resisting cement mortar; severe → partial rebuild |
| Regular maintenance flag | Metal coating / painting of steel may need interim reinstatement before next cycle |

### 6. Fire safety elements — restore approved standard (§3.5 / §5.5)

Inspect against legislations / standards / codes **at time of construction and approved A&A**, or **completed FS Works** under Cap. 502 / Cap. 572 Directions. Incomplete Directions → **highlight in Inspection Report** (MBIS does not substitute Cap. 502/572 upgrading).

#### 6.1 Inspection findings (layout killers for A&A)

| Limb | Defects / deficiencies RI must identify |
|---|---|
| **Means of escape** | Defective / missing exit signs; inadequate lighting; defective balustrades / handrails; door swing obstructing escape; blocked / obstructed routes; defective treads |
| **Means of access** | Blocked access to building (esp. rear lane); blocked access within building (e.g. fireman’s lift lobby on G/F); dilapidated EVA; defects in fireman’s lift lobby integrity |
| **Fire resisting construction** | Dilapidated materials → inadequate FRR; ventilation / services through compartments breaching FRC; broken fire-door hinges / glass; missing self-closers; broken / inadequate FR doors or FR glazing; non-emergency services in escape stairs / lobbies without protection; damaged staircase enclosure / protected lobby; obstructed fire dampers / shutters; defective / blocked staircase top vents |

#### 6.2 Unauthorised A&A that must be reported to BA (§3.5.2(E))

- Doors / gates / roller shutters obstructing escape  
- Unprotected openings in exit / compartment walls, floors, ceilings  
- Structures on **refuge floors**  
- Door openings from cocklofts to exit stairs in **single-staircase** buildings  
- Alterations degrading escape, protected lobbies or FRC  
- Roof / flat-roof structures causing inadequate MOE  
- Roof / flat-roof structures causing inadequate fire separation to refuge roof  
- Change in fireman’s lift or firefighting & rescue stairway lobby provision  
- Removal of fire doors / shutters / other FRC  

#### 6.3 Unsuitable change in use (§3.5.2(F)) — report to BA

Examples: storage of hazardous materials; change exceeding approved MOE capacity and/or imposed load of room / storey.

#### 6.4 Private flat entrance doors (§3.5.3)

If defective entrance door of individual premises adversely affects building fire safety or has inadequate FRR → record in Inspection Report for BD attention (even though private interiors are otherwise out of MBIS).

#### 6.5 Rectification methods (§5.5.1–5.5.3)

| Area | Typical rectification |
|---|---|
| MOE | Replace/reinstate exit signs; fix lighting; repair balustrades/handrails/treads; rectify door swing; remove obstruction; rectify non-complying locking devices |
| MOA | Remove access obstructions; repair EVA; reinstate fireman’s lift lobby integrity |
| FRC | Repair/replace inadequate FR materials; provide dampers where ducts cross compartments; repair/replace fire doors (self-closers, hinges, wire-mesh glass); replace inadequate FR door/glazing; fire-protect non-emergency services in stairs/lobbies; reinstate staircase / protected-lobby enclosure ≥ original; replace/reinstate fire stops / sealing at penetrations; repair staircase top vents |

#### 6.6 Owner maintenance advice RI must give (§5.5.4)

Do not wedge fire doors open; keep self-closers working; keep escape routes clear; disallow UBW that hurts fire safety.

**SD takeaway:** Façade / roof / lobby A&A that eats into MOE, refuge floors, fireman’s lift lobbies, or compartmentation will fail MBIS and trigger BA reporting — stay within the approved fire strategy.

### 7. Drainage — common systems, access and proof tests (§3.6 / §5.6)

#### 7.1 Scope

External-wall drains; common-parts drains; drains in common pipe ducts; underground and above-ground common drains; any common drainage whose failure affects sanitary / hygienic condition. Includes anti-syphonage and ventilating pipes.

#### 7.2 Access rule for common stacks in private flats (§3.6.2)

Inspect from private premises **at least every 3 floors** and at pipe-duct outlets — **or** use CCTV / equivalent so overall condition is still assessed.

**SD takeaway:** Prefer **common accessible pipe ducts** with inspection doors / platforms over stacks reachable only via private units. Design CCTV-friendly bends.

#### 7.3 Defects to identify

Corrosion / leakage / deformation / displacement / damage / blockage of pipes and traps (incl. underground and misconnected common drains); corroded / loose / broken metal brackets; damaged / blocked manholes / septic tanks; broken / blocked surface channels, gullies, catch pits; missing drain pipes or grating at top of ventilating pipes; deteriorated / missing mica flap in fresh-air inlet.

#### 7.4 Misconnections / UBW drainage reported to BA

Foul or waste → surface water; foul → waste-only pipe; foul/waste to external / open areas; surface water → foul/waste; untreated trade effluent (incl. **hot water**) to drains or open areas.

**Urgent public health:** underground drain blockage / defects in private streets / lanes accessible to public → owners arrange urgent remedy; if not possible → report BA / BD immediately.

#### 7.5 Repair standards (§5.6)

Comply with Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations **as at time of construction**, plus BD / DSD / EPD guidelines.

| Component | Key rules |
|---|---|
| Common drains | Replace defective sections; rectify misconnections; plastic = durable, non-flammable, UV-proof, resistant to corrosion and hot/acid/alkaline liquids; cast iron coated inside/out; corrosion-resistant brackets/screws/bolts; vertical stacks fixed **immediately above and below** every horizontal branch; sleeves at wall/floor penetrations waterproof and/or fireproof |
| Underground | Replace or resin liner; chemical-resistant materials; flexible joints for rigid pipes (precast concrete / clay); jetting / rodding for minor blockage; or full replacement |
| Manholes / channels / gullies / catch pits | Repair concrete ≥ substrate strength; serious defects → recast; realign / re-level if settlement |

#### 7.6 Proof tests (§5.6.5)

| Test | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Ball test | Clearance + adequacy of fall |
| Air (above ground) | **38 mm** water gauge held **3 min** |
| Air (underground) | **100 mm** water gauge; drop ≤ **25 mm** in **5 min** |
| Water (above ground) | To flood level of lowest appliance or max **5 m** static head; no visual leak **15 min** |
| Water (underground) | Min **1.5 m** head above invert; measure every 5 min for **30 min**; leakage ≤ `d × l × t / 60` litres (d, l in m; t in min) |
| Smoke | Pipes **> 300 mm** dia. |
| CCTV | Colour camera, 100% RH capable, rotating mirror, full circumferential record |
| Exposed vertical drains | Proof tests may be omitted if RI inspects performance ≥ required standard |

### 8. UBW — identification and s.39C validated appendages (§3.7)

#### 8.1 Where UBW are hunted

Common parts (incl. external ground, open space, gardens, playgrounds, private streets, access roads); exterior other than common parts (external wall, roof, podium, adjoining yard / slope); street the building fronts or abuts.

#### 8.2 Categories flagged (§3.7.1(b))

1. Structures in common parts and on rooftop, flat roof, yard, lane or street  
2. Projections and signboards on the exterior  
3. Alterations / additions affecting fire safety (§3.5.2(E))  
4. Alterations / additions of structural elements  
5. Alterations / additions of drainage (§3.6.2(B))  
6. Alterations of external walls or parapets  
7. Removal or alteration of **barrier-free access** provisions  
8. Alterations / additions to balconies, canopies or other **approved** projections  

Also record signs of **flat subdivision** (many door openings, doorbells, electricity meters, drain connections) in the Inspection Report.

#### 8.3 Immediate BA report (§3.7.2(B))

UBW posing obvious hazard / imminent danger to life or property; overloaded parent structure with serious distress; serious environmental nuisance / health hazard; UBW **under construction**.

#### 8.4 Cap. 123 s.39C validated frames (§3.7.2(C))

Supporting structures / frames for **AC units, drying racks, window canopies** validated under s.39C are still inspected for dilapidation, corrosion, loose fixings — validation is not “forever exempt from inspection.”

#### 8.5 Follow-up

Record all UBW; flag any that obstruct inspection / repair; advise owners to **remove all identified UBW**; propose repair for defective s.39C frames.

**SD takeaway:** Design lawful, durable AC / drying / canopy supports from day one (approved or MWCS). Informal owner frames become recurring MBIS findings and programme risk.

### 9. Repair philosophy and dual consent tracks (§5 / §1.4 / §7)

| Rule | Practical meaning |
|---|---|
| Performance **not inferior** to OP-era / approved A&A / completed FS Works | Restoration of approved safety — not a free upgrade to current CoP |
| Follow latest approved plans + MWCS submissions + completed FS Works | Repair to the legal as-built |
| Selection criteria (§5.2) | Design life, exposure severity, condition, defect cause, occupant/public impact, environmental effect, durability, material/substrate compatibility, safety/health |
| Most repairs → **Minor Works (Cap. 123N)** | Simplified MWCS; RI acts as **AP for Class I** prescribed repair / associated demolition (B(MW)R s.27) |
| Non-minor / non-exempt → full BA approval + consent | Owners engage AP (+ RSE / RGE / RGBC / RSC as needed); RI still coordinates MBIS compliance |
| Durability to **next inspection cycle** | Highlight interim-maintenance items (fixings, coatings, hinges, locks, rivets, screws) |
| Concurrent Cap. 502/572 / other upgrades | **Clearly separate** MBIS-required repair from voluntary improvement in the proposal (§7.2.6) |

**Exempted works** (ss.41(3)/41(3B)/41(3C)): no prior approval/consent and no AP/RSE/RGE/RC appointment — still must meet safety outcome if used within MBIS context.

### 10. MWIS — window / window-wall constraints in full (§8–12)

#### 10.1 Scope checklist

| In MWIS | Out of MWIS |
|---|---|
| All windows & glass louvers (private + common) | Internal glass partitions |
| Window walls | G/F shopfront show windows |
| Protective-barrier portion of window / window wall (inspect / repair rules as §9.1.2) | Curtain walls (→ MBIS) |

#### 10.2 Inspection practice (§10)

- QP (natural person) inspects **personally**; company RGBC/RMWC → Authorized Signatory personally  
- Cover **all** elements of openable sash **and** fixed panel  
- Remove decoration / furniture obstruction to enable full component inspection  
- Inspect from interior as far as practicable; temporarily secure windows (e.g. safety rope) to prevent fall during inspection  
- Emergency → advise owners/occupants for immediate action + notify BA  

#### 10.3 Safety vs weather items (§10.5)

**Prescribed:** components affecting **window safety**.  
**Not prescribed (advisory):** weather sealant, weather gasket/strip, etc. — may recommend concurrent repair but must label which items are MWIS-mandatory vs recommended.

#### 10.4 Component defect focus by material (§10.6)

**Aluminium framed:** frames/sashes hard to open / jerky / deformed / cannot secure; hinges deformed / missing / seriously corroded; rivets/screws loose / missing / greyish-white powder or rust; sliding tracks / shoes / stays deformed / missing / corroded; lock set loose / missing / broken strap / jammed; glass broken/cracked; glazing bead/gasket missing/defective.

**Steel / iron framed:** as above plus rusty frames, flaking paint / exposed steel; cracked or seriously corroded welds; putty missing/cracked.

**Timber framed:** as aluminium safety items plus rotten / missing / damaged timber; flaking paint / exposed timber; putty missing/cracked.

**Window walls:** broken/cracked glass panes/fins; mullions/transoms seriously corroded / deformed / defective; brackets / spider fixings loose / missing / deformed; anchors/bolts/nuts/screws loose / missing / corroded; glazing bead/gasket missing/defective; structural sealant missing / defective / colour change / wrinkle / split — specialist may inspect/repair structural sealant under QP oversight.

#### 10.5 Mandatory repair numbers (§11.1) — lock into window specs / APP-116

| Requirement | Number / rule |
|---|---|
| Outcome | All windows **rendered safe**; defective safety components repaired/replaced |
| Screws / rivets / cracked glass | Replace; materials/size **≥ original** |
| Whole-window replacement | **Not** normally required if suitable repair achieves safety |
| Fire resistance | Maintain specified FRR; Cap. 502/572 premises follow those FRR rules |
| Hinge fastening | **≥ 3** screws or rivets to frame **and** to openable sash |
| SS hinge / SS hinge fasteners | Use **stainless steel** rivets or screws only |
| SS screw | Diameter **≥ 5 mm** + sufficient anchorage |
| SS rivet (open-end / multi-grip / rivet nut etc.) | Diameter **≥ 4.8 mm** + adequate head clamp |
| Manufacturer specs | Install fasteners per manufacturer |
| Enlarged corroded holes | Add SS fasteners at proper new positions (in addition to originals) |
| Corrosion protection | Sealant in holes before install + sealant on fastener heads (**PNAP APP-116**) |

#### 10.6 Best practice (§11.2) — specify where possible

- Prefer **4-bar stainless steel hinges** with adjustable friction shoes  
- SS hinge bars preferably **≥ 2.5 mm** thick  
- Prefer SS rivets/screws for all replacements  
- May advise upgrade to **current** design standards beyond original (advisory, beyond minimum prescribed)  
- Other aluminium installation → PNAP APP-116  

#### 10.7 Regular maintenance (§11.3)

Light lubrication of hinges; clear dust/dirt at hinges and tracks — QP must advise owners.

#### 10.8 QP / RC supervision (§12)

QP supervises RC repair to required standards (incl. FRR). RC provides continuous supervision of workers and scaffolds. If same person is QP + RC → still responsible for safety until next cycle. Bamboo scaffold / typhoon / security rules mirror MBIS §6.

### 11. Roles, clocks, forms and independence (§2 / §7 / §9 / App. I & X)

#### 11.1 RI main duties (snapshot)

Carry out building inspection personally (limited representative use only after nature/cause established, to ascertain **extent**); supervise repair (or coordinate if another AP supervises non-MW works); ensure materials comply with BO and are properly mixed/applied/fixed; ensure building rendered safe; notify BA of building-safety emergencies; notify BA of contravening building works found in common parts / exterior / abutting street; comply generally with BO.

#### 11.2 Key MBIS procedural clocks

| Action | Clock / form |
|---|---|
| Notify BA of RI appointment | **MBI 1** within **7 days** |
| Nominate another RI temporarily for repair supervision | **MBI 2** within **7 days** of nomination |
| Cease temporary nomination / cease to act as RI | Written notice within **7 days** |
| Notify intended appointment of supervision representative | **≥ 7 days before** appointment |
| Notify intention to conduct DI + submit proposal | Before DI starts |
| Inspection Report + certificate to BA | **MBI 3** or **MBI 3a** (if DI); also deliver to person for whom inspection done within **7 days** after inspection completion |
| If different RI for repair | Pass documents to supervising RI within **2 months** of BA submission **or** before repair start — whichever earlier |
| Revised repair proposal during works | To BA within **7 days**; same day to person for whom repair done |
| Completion Report + certificate | **MBI 4** within **14 days** after repair completion |
| Independence certificate (inspecting RI ≠ partner/director/AS of repairing RC) | **MBI 5** when supervising RI ≠ inspecting RI |

Also comply with MWCS or full approval/consent procedures in parallel when applicable.

#### 11.3 Key MWIS procedural clocks

| Scenario | Clock / form |
|---|---|
| No repair needed | **WI 1** to BA within **14 days** after inspection; same day to person inspected |
| Same QP inspects + supervises repair | **WI 1** within **14 days** after repair completion |
| QP inspects only | Inspection Report + **WI 2** within **7 days** after inspection; pass to repair QP within **1 month** of BA submission or before repair start |
| QP supervises repair only | Revised proposal within **7 days** if needed; **WI 3** within **14 days** after repair; deliver Inspection Report to RC before start if QP ≠ RC |
| Cease to act as QP | Written notice within **7 days** |

#### 11.4 MBIS process flow (App. I — condensed)

Owner appoints RI → MBI 1 → RI inspects → (optional DI proposal → BA endorse → DI) → Inspection Report + MBI 3/3a → if repair needed, owner appoints RC → repair under RI supervision (MWCS or full consent) → Completion Report + MBI 4.

#### 11.5 Supervision organisation (App. VI)

| Role | Minimum qualification | Min site frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **RI** | — | First inspection of Level-2-specified repair / proof tests |
| **Level 2 representative** | TCP T3 for Building Works / Class I MW, **or** passed specified top-up + **≥ 5 years** relevant experience | **Fortnightly** |
| **Level 1 representative** | TCP T1 for Building Works / Class I MW | **Weekly** |

- Level 2 may cover Level 1 duties **if** Level 1 frequency still met  
- Scale factor per CoP for Site Supervision  
- Same RI for inspect + supervise → may put supervision proposal in Inspection Report repair proposal; different supervising RI → notify BA + submit supervision proposal within **7 days** of appointment  
- Change of supervision team → notify BA within **7 days**  
- Class I MW → also Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans + CoP for Site Supervision as necessary  
- RI + team inspect **entire** completed works before completion certificate  
- RC: **continuous** supervision of workers + scaffolds; give RI advance notice for examinations  

Checklist themes (Table 3): scaffolding / catch fans / mats; temporary works vs method statement; substrate prep before tiling; anchor-bolt location/size; cladding / CW dimensions and sealant; hammer-tap and pull-off witnessing; concrete hacking / crack opening; bond coat / mortar / recasting; material delivery vs approved requirements; non-conformity escalation.

#### 11.6 Site safety / security during repair (§6 / §12)

- Protect workers, occupants, public, adjacent buildings; maintain safe passage; remove debris regularly (avoid overload / obstruction)  
- Bamboo scaffolds per BD *Guidelines on Design and Construction of Bamboo Scaffolds* + Labour Department bamboo scaffolding safety CoP; sufficient lighting  
- More frequent scaffold integrity checks under tropical cyclone / strong monsoon / severe gusts; dismantle immediately after works  
- RC liaise with management / OC / owners; post security notices (PNRC)

### 12. Reporting deliverables architects should expect in tenders (§7 / App. VII–VIII)

#### 12.1 Inspection Report must support

Findings of inspection / assessment / DI; repair proposal with plans, method statements, details; clear demarcation of **MWCS minor works** vs **full BA approval/consent works**; supervision proposal (if same RI); list of components needing regular maintenance before next cycle; UBW schedule; FS Directions status; daily inspection records.

#### 12.2 Completion Report must support

What was repaired; proof-test results (pull-off, air/water, NDT, cubes, etc.); mill certificates / CoCs; statement confirming prior MWCS or s.14 record submissions with case references (no need to resubmit duplicates); certificate **MBI 4**.

### 13. Schematic design checklist (before freezing envelope / A&A)

1. **Confirm Cap. 123 s.30B / s.30C status** — active notice, impending cycle, or none.  
2. **Split façade** into curtain wall (MBIS) vs windows / window walls (MWIS) vs shopfront show glass (out).  
3. **Map every appendage** — AC, drying rack, canopy, architectural projection, signboard — as inspectable, demountable, corrosion-protected, preferably MWCS-classifiable.  
4. **Avoid pure cantilevered slab canopies over streets**; if unavoidable, design for 100% future open-up, root access, waterproofing and drainage.  
5. **Expose or provide access** to transfer structures (≥30% if concealed), CW fire stops, common drainage stacks (every 3 floors or CCTV strategy).  
6. **Budget inspection access** for tall façades and wall signage **> 40 m²** (≥30% concealed-area exposure).  
7. **Do not degrade MOE / MOA / FRC / BFA / refuge floors / fireman’s lift lobbies** — these are MBIS report triggers.  
8. **Specify windows to APP-116 + §11.1** (3 fixings per hinge side, SS ≥5 mm screws / ≥4.8 mm rivets, hole+head sealant, prefer 4-bar SS hinges ≥2.5 mm bars).  
9. **Façade finishes:** prefer systems that can meet App. IV/V proof tests (0.5 N/mm² pull-off; render ≤20 mm; non-combustible cladding replacements).  
10. **Assume dual track:** most repair = MWCS; flag non-minor works needing full BA approval early in cost plan.  
11. **Separate** statutory MBIS/MWIS safety repair from voluntary upgrades (FS Works, aesthetic re-cladding) in programme and fees.  
12. **Retrieve OP / approved plans / s.39C / FS Directions / outstanding orders** before committing to A&A scope that intersects MBIS limbs.  
13. **Check RI ↔ RC independence** before proposing single-entity inspect+repair delivery for building (non-window) works.  
14. **Design common pipe ducts and BMU / gondola reach** as maintainability infrastructure, not afterthoughts.
