# Planning and Construction Codes Index (English)

**Issued by:** Buildings Department
**Scope:** The 2024 Edition of the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance, the February 2021 issue sitting beside it, and the Design Manual on Universal Accessibility 2026 (3 documents). This table does not index the other codes already in this folder. This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The short title links to the critical summary in this folder. The PDF is in `source_reference/` under the same filename stem.
**Titles:** The official title is the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current Buildings Department code or manual, the Building (Construction) Regulation or the Building (Planning) Regulations as applicable, and the lease.
