# Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
June 1996 revision (first issue December 1959) | Building Authority / Buildings Department

> Scope note: Guidance on compliance with **B(P)R 41(1)** — means of escape geometry, population, exits, travel distance, stair discharge, basements, refuge floors, and places of public entertainment. Fire-resisting construction, means of access, and active FSI are in other Codes (FRC 1996 / MOA / FSI CoP). **Superseded for new work by FS Code 2011 Part B** (unified MOE + FRC + MOA); keep this summary for legacy A&A, historic approvals, and understanding the tables Part B inherited. Read with APP-82 / APP-92 / APP-121 and FSD CL **1/97** (refuge-floor FSI).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This Code sets the **escape envelope** for buildings: how many people each use generates (Table 1), how many exit doors/routes and widths are needed (Table 2), travel/direct-distance limits, stair discharge capacity (Tables 5–6), single-stair concessions, basements, and refuge floors for tall buildings, plus a dedicated Part III for places of public entertainment.

It applies to **all buildings** except single-family domestic buildings of not more than **three main domestic storeys** with highest floor not more than **13 m** above ground. Compliance is by **prescriptive provisions** or an **alternative / fire engineering approach** for special size, height, use, design, construction or location.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Definitions that lock schematic decisions (¶4)

| Term | Meaning — why it matters at SD |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | Design population of a room or storey. Where a maisonette entrance sits on a storey, that storey includes **all floors of the maisonette**. |
| **Usable floor area (UFA)** | Aggregate floor area excluding staircases, public circulation, lift landings, lavatories, WCs, kitchens in self-contained flats, and lift/A/C machinery space — **unless Table 1 notes say otherwise**. |
| **Direct distance** | Straight-line notional path **inside a room** from any point to the centre of an exit door. Caps room depth / open plan. |
| **Travel distance** | Horizontal distance **outside the room** along exit-route centreline from exit-door centre to stair FR door centre / first tread / street or open-area discharge (¶8.2). |
| **Discharge value** | Persons a stair of given width may discharge from a given number of storeys (Tables 5–6). |
| **Exit route** | Route to a place of safety outside the building — may include rooms, corridors, stairs, refuge areas. **Not** a revolving door, lift, or escalator. |
| **Basement** | Storey below the lower/lowest ground storey from which any required exit route goes **upward**. |
| **Ground storey** | Storey with a street entrance. Multi-street / split-level sites can have **more than one** ground storey. |
| **Maisonette** | Flat of **more than one but not more than three** storeys (not a tenement floor). |
| **Protected lobby** | Intercepted approach to a stair/exit route acting as fire/smoke check — walls/doors per **FRC Code**. |
| **Balcony approach** | External approach to a common stair serving **≥2 occupancies**. |
| **Refuge floor** | Protected floor for occupants to assemble in a fire. |
| **Place of public entertainment** | As defined in Places of Public Entertainment Ordinance (Cap. 172) — triggers Part III. |
| **Factory / industrial undertaking / notifiable workplace** | As in Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance — map early for door-swing and special-hazard rules. |

**SD takeaway:** Wrong “basement”, “ground storey”, or UFA definition cascades into exit count, travel distance, and refuge triggers.

### 2. Two compliance paths (¶3)

#### 2.1 Prescriptive path (¶3.1)
Follow Parts II–III + Tables 1–6 + Diagrams 1–7. Deemed reliable for BA.

#### 2.2 Alternative / fire engineering path (¶3.2–3.6)
Use when size, height, use, design, construction or location make prescription impractical (large atria, stadia ≥10,000 persons, complex links).

BA assesses against **all** of:
- Means of escape · means of access · fire service installations · fire resisting construction
- Size · height · use · location · management

May enhance / trade off among prevention, early detection/warning, MOE standard, smoke control, fire-spread control, structural resistance, containment, separation, active extinguishment, firefighting/rescue facilities, management, trained personnel, maintenance.

Quantitative risk methods allowed if assumptions are documented from competent authorities.

**SD takeaway:** Oversized travel, single-stair stretch, or ≥10,000-person venues usually force fire engineering — budget BA/FSD interface at concept, not DD.

### 3. Application / exclusions (¶5) + special hazard (¶6)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Exempt** | Single-family domestic ≤ **3** main domestic storeys **and** highest floor ≤ **13 m** above ground |
| **Does not overrule** | Bedspace Apartments Ord.; Child Care Centres Ord.; Education Ord.; FIUO; Hotel & Guesthouse Ord.; Residential Care Homes (Elderly Persons) Ord. — and regs made under them |
| **Uncovered cases** | BA determines MOE case-by-case (¶5.3) |

**Special hazard occupancy (¶6):** Where special hazard (e.g. combustible store, restaurant kitchen, boiler room) opens onto an exit route → **protected lobby**. Escape from normal hazard **must not** pass through special hazard. Kitchen attached to restaurant = industrial undertaking under FIUO. FRC covers construction FRP for special-hazard areas.

### 4. Lock population first — Table 1 (¶7)

Every exit count, stair width, and travel check hinges on capacity. Use **UFA ÷ factor** unless notes say otherwise. BA accepts **actual counting**. Unlisted uses → BA sets factor.

| Ref | Intended use of storey | Factor (m²/person) |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Assembly halls, auditoria, stadia — no seating or movable seating | **0.5** |
| **(b)** | Public areas in viewing galleries, banking halls, betting centres, public service counters | **0.5** |
| **(c)** | Dance halls (dancing area), disco, restaurant reception | **0.75** |
| **(d)** | Restaurants (dining area), lounges, committee / conference / meeting / common / function / waiting rooms | **1** |
| **(e)** | Kitchens attached to restaurants | **4.5** |
| **(f)** | Museums, exhibition halls, trademarts, display areas | **2** |
| **(g)** | Supermarkets, showrooms, jewellery/goldsmith, pawn shops, money changers | **2** |
| **(h)** | Shopping arcades, department stores, shopping areas — basement, G/F, 1/F & 2/F | **3** |
| **(h)** | Same — 3/F & above | **4.5** |
| **(i)** | Offices | **9** |
| **(j)** | Tenement houses, barracks, dormitories; single-room flats or living area subdivided by rooms | **3** |
| **(k)** | Self-contained flats, corridor/balcony access, **≥5 flats** per floor per staircase | **4.5** |
| **(l)** | Flats not covered by (j) or (k) | **9** |
| **(m)** | Flatted factories | **4.5** |
| **(n)** | Warehouses, godowns, storage | **30** |
| **(o)** | Classrooms (not under Education Ordinance), lecture rooms, library, study rooms | **2** |

**Notes that bite at SD:**
- **(g) & (h):** include public circulation in UFA, **except** shopping arcades — use notional arcade width **2.5 m** (single-loaded) / **3.0 m** (double-loaded).
- Hospitals, hotels, hostels, PPE → BA on detailed layout plans.
- **Carpark:** worst-case capacity; **not less than 1.5 carparking spaces per person**; travel measured along aisle assuming carpark full.
- Single-user specialised industrial workplace → Commissioner for Labour.
- Unlisted uses → BA; actual counting accepted.

**SD takeaway:** Size cores from the **densest** floors (lower retail, F&B, banking), not the average office plate.

### 5. General exit-route geometry (¶8)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Ultimate safety** | Every exit route leads to a **street** or open area at ground level with unobstructed access ≥ total required exit width discharging into it; doors/gates on that access open from inside without key, in exit direction |
| **Podium / upper open area (¶8.3)** | Stair may discharge to podium open area **only if** route is permanently defined (handrail/railing), continues to street/¶8.2 open area, and is designed as part of the exit stair (MOE + FRC). Roof of that route = external wall; slab of open area = floor for FRC |
| **Lighting** | Normal ≥ **30 lux** at floor; emergency ≥ **2 lux** (FSI CoP) |
| **Private lane** | Only if paved, permanently unobstructed, and BA satisfied on integrity as MOE |
| **Adjoining discharges (¶8.6)** | Enclosing walls returned/projected ≥ **450 mm** along frontage — return must not cut effective width |
| **Clear height** | ≥ **2000 mm**; side-wall sprinklers may project ≤ **90 mm** and cut clear height by ≤ **105 mm** |
| **Drop at final discharge (¶8.8)** | Door/gate set back ≥ width of exit route from drop line; single step clearly marked |

**SD takeaway:** Podium “escape” that is just an open deck without a protected, defined continuation to street fails ¶8.3 — lock this in massing (see Diagram 7).

### 6. Single-staircase buildings (¶9) — rarely usable

Only if **all** apply:

| Criterion | Limit |
|---|---|
| Height | ≤ **6 storeys** and uppermost floor ≤ **17 m** above ground at stair discharge |
| Use above G/F | Domestic or offices only (G/F may be shop/carpark with FRC stair enclosure) |
| Rescue access at G/F | Reach ≥1 window of every occupancy above G/F: access strip **≥3 m** (highest floor ≤10 m) or **≥4.5 m** (highest floor >10 m) |
| Highest floor ≤13 m | UFA any storey above G/F ≤ **250 m²** |
| Highest floor >13 m | UFA any storey above G/F ≤ **150 m²** + protected lobby (common, integral to stair) + stair continued to **roof** + roof refuge ≥ **0.5 m²/person** for all above-G/F population (Table 1) + roof per FRC |

Otherwise every storey needs **≥2** exit routes (¶11), or more per Table 2.

### 7. Exits from rooms / storeys / ground (¶10–12)

#### 7.1 Table 2 — exit count and clear widths

Applies to room exit doors **or** storey exit routes. Maisonette: applies to **one** of its storeys only (¶11.1(a)). PPE → Part III overrides.

| Capacity | Min no. exits | Min total width doors / routes (mm) | Min each door / route (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤30 | 1 | 750 / 1050 | 750 / 1050 |
| 31–200 | 2 | 1750 / 2100 | 850 / 1050 |
| 201–300 | 2 | 2500 / 2500 | 1050 / 1050 |
| 301–500 | 2 | 3000 / 3000 | 1050 / 1050 |
| 501–750 | 3 | 4500 / 4500 | 1200 / 1200 |
| 751–1000 | 4 | 6000 / 6000 | 1200 / 1200 |
| 1001–1250 | 5 | 7500 / 7500 | 1350 / 1350 |
| 1251–1500 | 6 | 9000 / 9000 | 1350 / 1350 |
| >1500 | As BA may require | **300 mm per 50 persons** | 1500 / 1500 |

**Critical sizing rules:**
- Door width = least clear width between vertical frame members.
- Stair / landing / corridor width = between finished walls or inner balustrade; only handrails may project (≤ **90 mm** each).
- Uneven exit group: discard any door/route width **>50% above the narrowest** when summing totals (¶10.3 / ¶11.1(b)).
- Capacity ≤200: exits may share one corridor/balcony approach **if** escape is possible in more than one direction (¶10.2).
- Shopping arcade / department store / shopping at basement–2/F over 500 persons: BA may allow **fewer** exits than Table 2 **if** total width still met (Note v).

#### 7.2 Inter-stair access (¶11.2)

Where ≥2 exit stairs required: users of one stair must reach another **without entering private premises**, either at **each floor** or, where refuge floors exist, at the **refuge floor(s) and the roof**.

**Does not apply** to domestic or composite buildings ≤ **15 storeys** above lowest ground storey.

#### 7.3 Ground-storey discharge (¶12)

- Stair enclosure continued at G/F to separate the passage to the exit door from the rest of the building.
- **≥2 stairs:** cloakroom, lavatory, WC, caretaker office/counter, fire control room may open off that passage.
- **≥3 stairs:** one in every three may discharge through FR doors into a hall/arcade — that part of hall/arcade forming the exit route still meets ¶8.2 and ¶8.4.
- Shared G/F exit (stair + G/F + basement): width ≥ **½ G/F exit** + upper-stair width + basement-stair width (¶12.2).

### 8. Access to staircases within building (¶13)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Separation | Every stair separated per **FRC Code** (Diagram 1) |
| Approach directions | Each stair approached from a different direction; dead-ends only per ¶14.3(b) |
| Stair-to-stair separation | Doors (or landing perimeter points if no door) ≥ **6 m** apart measured in a straight line **along the wall** |
| No stair-through-stair | Escape must not require passing through one stair enclosure/landing to reach another |
| Door swing on corridor | Must not reduce required corridor width at any point of swing |
| **Protected lobby** to each stair unless | (i) ≥**50%** of stair perimeter open to external air (balustrade top to soffit of flight above); **or** (ii) single-stair building, highest floor ≤**13 m**; **or** (iii) ≥2 stairs and highest floor ≤**20 m** |
| Lobby status | Common area, integral to stair — not absorbable into adjacent units |
| Balcony approach | Per FRC; landing **not** part of balcony; door swing must not cut width; windows cannot fix open into clear width; sill ≥ **1000 mm**; 180° fix-open OK; fanlights ≥ **2000 mm** OK |

### 9. Direct distance and travel distance (¶14)

#### 9.1 Inside rooms (¶14.1, 14.4, 14.6–14.7)

| Condition | Max direct distance |
|---|---|
| Alternative exit door to alternative stair / street discharge | **18 m** (except exhibition hall, concert hall, transport terminal and similar — case basis) |
| No alternative exit door | **15 m** |
| Open plan / unknown partitions — secondary exit provided | **30 m** |
| Open plan — no secondary exit | **18 m** |

**30° rule:** Where ≥2 exit doors required (or to count as “secondary exit”), lines of direct distance from any point to two exits must form ≥ **30°**.

#### 9.2 Single-staircase buildings — Table 3

| Exit-route type | Max travel | Max direct + travel |
|---|---|---|
| **(A)** Balcony approach or ventilated internal corridor (FRC) | **18 m** | **24 m** |
| **(B)** Internal corridor without ventilation (FRC) | **12 m** | **24 m** |
| **(C)** Partitioned storey, exit not along (A)/(B) FRC corridor | **18 m** | **18 m** |

#### 9.3 Two or more stairs — Table 4

| Use | Type | Max travel | Max direct + travel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offices, schools, shops | **(A)** | **36 m** | **45 m** |
| Offices, schools, shops | **(B)** | **24 m** | **36 m** |
| Offices, schools, shops | **(C)** | **30 m** | **30 m** |
| All other | **(A)** | **30 m** | **36 m** |
| All other | **(B)** | **24 m** | **36 m** |
| All other | **(C)** | **30 m** | **30 m** |

| Extra limit | Value |
|---|---|
| **Dead-end** (travel possible in one direction only) | Direct + travel ≤ **18 m** |
| Corridor distance between any two stairs / discharge points | ≤ **48 m** (centreline; measure door-to-door / landing-to-landing / open-podium first tread) |

#### 9.4 Inner rooms (¶14.5) + APP-121

Allowed only if **all** apply:
- Capacity ≤ **30**
- Escape through **not more than one** other room
- Escape distance in inner room ≤ ¶14.1 direct-distance limit
- Access room not special hazard; same occupant control
- Vision panel in door/walls (**except toilet**) — **APP-121** allows substitute (e.g. audible fire alarm in the inner room); ¶14.5 does **not** apply to habitable rooms escaping through living/dining/kitchen in domestic flats

#### 9.5 “Ventilated” corridor (¶14.8)

Either:
- Cross-ventilated permanent openings (ventilators OK if smoke-detector triggered + manual override): total free area ≥ **6.25%** of corridor floor; each opening ≥ **1.5 m²**; **or**
- Static/dynamic smoke extract to FSD satisfaction

**SD takeaway:** Place stairs **6–48 m** apart, kill dead-ends >18 m early, and decide balcony vs enclosed corridor before locking the core.

| Modifier | Factor |
|---|---|
| Exit direction **upward** | × **0.8** |
| **Scissors stair** with no intermediate landing between consecutive floors | × **0.7** |

**Large occupancy (≥10,000 persons)** — stadia, arenas, convention centres, passenger terminals (¶15.8–15.9):
- Total stair width ≥ **1.2 ×** Table 2 exit-route width for the area; if some exits go direct to ultimate safety, apply 1.2× only to the remainder that goes to stairs.
- Fire engineering often required.

**Commentary basis (not design input, but explains the numbers):** total evacuation; 2.5 min (non-sprinkler) / 5 min (sprinkler) to protected zone; ~80 persons/m width/min descending; no one-stair discount because lobbies/ventilation + inter-stair links are mandated.

**SD takeaway:** Decide **whole-building sprinklers** early — Table 6 roughly doubles stair capacity and can shrink core width.

### 11. Doors / stairs / ramps / lift lobbies (¶16–19)

#### 11.1 Doors (¶16)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Swing | Across exit, or into exit from room/storey >**30** persons → open in exit direction; two-way doors need transparent upper view panel |
| Locks | Openable from inside without key; electrical locks OK if auto-release on smoke/alarm/central override **and** on power failure; stair/lobby locks must still allow ¶11.2 re-entry logic |
| Landing swing | Must not cut landing effective radius below stair width |
| Min door (>3 persons) | ≥ **750 mm** clear; double leaf each ≥ **600 mm**; rebated meeting stiles need closing-sequence device |
| Stair / protected-lobby doors | Transparent upper view panel of required FR; self-closer **without** hold-open check; keep-closed notices both sides |
| Other self-closing exit doors | May use approved hold-open released manually + automatically on smoke/alarm (FSD) |
| **Notifiable workplaces** | Exit doors from workplace open outward; room doors where ≥10 employed open outward; workplace exit doors effective self-closing |

#### 11.2 Stair construction (¶17)

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Geometry | Straight flights, **no winders**; **2–16** risers per flight |
| Tread / rise (general) | Tread ≥ **225 mm** clear of nosings; rise ≤ **175 mm** |
| Schools | Tread ≥ **250 mm**; rise **75–150 mm** |
| PPE | Tread ≥ **280 mm**; rise ≤ **150 mm** |
| Landings | Width and length ≥ stair width; door swing must not cut effective width/radius |
| Clear height | ≥ **2000 mm** |
| Max undivided width | **1800 mm** — wider → central handrail into sections each ≥ **1050 mm** |
| Handrails | Both sides; height **850–1100 mm**; project ≤ **90 mm** each into clear width; continuous per flight (PPE: carry round landings) |

#### 11.3 Ramps (¶18)
Exit-route ramp gradient ≤ **1:12**.

#### 11.4 Lift lobbies (¶19)
Every lift lobby needs unobstructed, unlockable access to an exit route at all times. Alternative: direct intercom to management office if building has good management.

### 12. Basements (¶20)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Min exits | ≥ **2**, except basement ≤ **3000 mm** below discharge ground, area ≤ **150 m²**, **and** used solely as lavatory or plantroom |
| No through-stair | Stair serving upper floors **must not** continue direct into basement |
| Signs | Bilingual illuminated EXIT signs (white on green or green on white/black); letters ≥ **50 mm**; on central side wall of each flight at **1500 mm** from nosing line |
| Independent street exit | Genuine basement (below lowest ground storey, all exits upward), except lavatory-only: ≥**1** exit discharges independently to street / ¶8.2 open area |

**SD takeaway:** Separate basement stairs from tower stairs at G/F — shared shafts fail ¶20.2.

### 13. Refuge floors (¶21)

#### 13.1 When required

| Building type | Trigger | Max interval |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial | > **25 storeys** above lowest ground storey | Not more than **20 storeys** from another refuge floor / street / ¶8.2 open area |
| Non-industrial (not domestic/composite concession) | > **25 storeys** | Not more than **25 storeys** |
| Domestic / composite ≤40 storeys | Refuge-floor ¶21.1 **does not apply**; if >25 and ≤40 storeys → **main roof must be a refuge floor** (¶21.3–21.4) |
| Domestic / composite >40 storeys | Full ¶21.1 applies | As non-industrial interval |

Storeys containing solely mechanical plant may be excluded from the storey count (¶21.1).

#### 13.2 Refuge floor package (¶21.2) — Diagram 6

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Occupancy at refuge level | No occupied accommodation / accessible plant except **FS water tanks + associated FSI plant** |
| Net refuge area | ≥ **50%** of gross floor area of refuge floor; clear height ≥ **2300 mm** |
| Min dimension | ≥ **1.5 ×** width of widest stair passing through |
| Separation / open sides | Per FRC; open-sided above safe parapet on ≥ **two opposite sides** for cross-ventilation |
| Stair discontinuity | Stairs **discontinued** at refuge — exit diverted across refuge before continuing down |
| Lighting | ≥30 lux normal / ≥2 lux emergency (FSI CoP) |
| FSI | As required by Director of Fire Services — see **FSD CL 1/97** |
| Fireman’s lift | Serves refuge floor; doors locked in normal operation; release on fireman’s switch |

#### 13.3 Main roof as refuge (¶21.3) — Diagram 7

Flat roof per FRC; net refuge ≥ **50%** of typical floor below; stairs continue to roof unobstructed; min dimension ≥ **1.5 ×** widest serving stair; 30/2 lux lighting.

#### 13.4 Signage (¶21.4)
Bilingual illuminated signs at 1500 mm: stair number + entrance before entering refuge; “REFUGE FLOOR — For Temporary Rest During Emergency Escape” + exits to street/stair numbers after entering; directional signs; main-entrance notice (≥25 mm letters) stating where refuge floors are.

**SD takeaway:** Lock refuge level(s), open-sided façades, stair breaks, and fireman’s-lift door strategy before freezing the tower section. FSI (drencher etc.) is an FSD package, not in this Code’s geometry.

### 14. Part III — Places of public entertainment (¶22–34)

In addition to Part II.

#### 14.1 Site / thoroughfares (¶22)

Site abuts **≥2 thoroughfares**. Frontages ≥ **½** of site boundary (subject to ¶22.7) and allow exits from each tier/floor to ≥2 thoroughfares.

| Capacity | Thoroughfare widths |
|---|---|
| ≤500 | BA determines |
| >500–2000 | One thoroughfare ≥ **12 m** |
| >2000–3000 | One ≥ **12 m** + other ≥ **9 m** (carriageway) or **6 m** (footway) |
| >3000–5000 | One ≥ **15 m** + other ≥ **9 m** |
| >5000 | Further frontage as BA requires |

#### 14.2 Cinemas in multi-storey non-domestic / composite (¶23)

- Aggregate ≤ **2000** persons in one building; any storey(s) of non-domestic / non-domestic part of composite.
- Shared exits sized on **total** cinema capacity; exits relatively remote; majority discharge to streets ≥ **4.5 m** accessible to fire appliances (FSD).
- **First basement only**, and only if: short direct exits; all cinema stairs/exits discharge independently to street; other basement stairs sealed at cinema level; ≥1 exit is a **firefighting & rescue stairway** (MOA Code); widths per table:

| Persons in each PPE | Min exit routes | Min width each |
|---|---|---|
| <300 | 2 | **1.5 m** |
| 301–500 | 2 | **2 m** |
| 501–750 | 3 | **2 m** |
| >750 | BA decides | — |

#### 14.3 Exit count / width from tiers (¶25)

| Capacity of tier/floor | Exit routes | Min width each |
|---|---|---|
| ≤500 | **2** (+1 per each additional 250 or part above 500) | ≤300 persons: **1.2 m**; >300: **1.5 m** |
| ≥12 m above pavement | Total exit width **+25%** over ¶25.3 | — |

Two exits from each tier/floor open to **different** thoroughfares. Include standing/waiting/tea room/lounge/restaurant/foyer in capacity. Exits remote from stage where stage is principal fire risk.

#### 14.4 PPE stairs / seating / stage (¶26–32)

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Public stair width | ≤300 persons: ≥ **1.2 m**; >300: ≥ **1.5 m** |
| Flights | No winders; **3–16** steps; ≤2 successive flights without a turn; if >12 steps in a flight → not more than one flight without a turn |
| Gangways | ≥ **1.05 m**; lead to exits; no seat > **3 m** from gangway along seating line |
| Seatway clear depth | ≥ **300 mm** back-of-seat to front-of-seat behind |
| Stage | Exit each side; one direct to street; stage basement exit direct to street |
| EXIT notices | English + Chinese, letters ≥ **175 mm**, ≥2 m above floor; dual lighting (¶33); never extinguish while public present; no “NO EXIT” notices |
| Lighting | Same 30 / 2 lux package as ¶8.4 |
| Barriers / locks | Exit-line barriers open on pressure; no chains/padlocks; openable from inside without key |

#### 14.5 Temporary PPE buildings (¶34) — key geometry

Seatway ≥300 mm; chairs battened in lengths ≥4; gangways ≥ **1.2 m**, no seat >3 m from gangway; cross-gangways divide seating into blocks ≤ **9 m** deep; exit ways ≥ **2.4 m** per prescribed pattern; doors open in exit direction on light pressure.

### 16. Explicitly out of scope (commentaries)

- Means of escape for the **disabled** — not covered; commentary points to BS 5588 Part 8:1988 and suggests a call panel adjacent to the fireman’s lift as a minimum.
- Passive FRP / enclosure details deleted from 1986 edition → use **FRC 1996**.
- Active FSI schedules → **FSI CoP** / FSD.

**SD checklist before freezing cores:**
