# Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance 2021 (2024 Edition)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue February 2021; this revision September 2024 | Buildings Department (BD)

> Scope note: Deemed-to-satisfy guidance for adequate maintenance and repair (M&R) access to the outer surface of external walls, claddings, curtain walls, roofs, and projections from them, under the Building (Construction) Regulation (Cap. 123Q) (B(C)R) sections 27(2), 28(5), 31(3) and 34(3). The 2024 Edition incorporates the September 2024 amendments by Practice Note for Authorized Persons, Registered Structural Engineers and Registered Geotechnical Engineers (PNAP) APP-163, and it supersedes the advisory Code of Practice on Design for Safety – External Maintenance 2019. It does not override work-at-height duties under the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance (Cap. 509) (OSHO) or the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59) (FIUO). Drawn dimensions in Figures B1 to B3 are not transcribed in the text extract and are not stated below.

## Regulatory Overview
The Code sets guidelines for means of access for maintenance to the external wall, curtain wall, external cladding, roof and projection of a building, and compliance with the Code is deemed to satisfy B(C)R sections 27(2), 28(5), 31(3) and 34(3). It was first issued in February 2021; this revision is September 2024 and incorporates the amendments made in September 2024 by PNAP APP-163.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What M&R access means, and which elements are in scope (Part 1, sections 1–2)
| Term | Meaning printed in the Code |
|---|---|
| External building element | External wall, curtain wall, external cladding or roof, and the projection from any of those |
| External wall | The whole, or any part, of the external wall |
| Maintenance | Inspection, routine cleansing, repair and replacement of defective external building elements and their components |
| Means of access for maintenance | Any means providing safe access for M&R of the external building elements. Common types are in Part 3 |
| Projection | Projections under Part II of the Building (Planning) Regulations (Cap. 123F) (B(P)R); projections under PNAP APP-19; signboards under section 2 of the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) (BO); and other architectural projections. Examples: eave, cornice, moulding, overhang, fin, canopy, sun-shade, reflector, projecting window, balcony, utility platform (UP), projecting planter box, drying rack, air-conditioner (AC) platform, external drainage pipe, supporting frame of vertical greenery, retractable awning |
| Projection from a roof | Any structure erected or installed on a roof, such as an architectural projection, water tank, gantry, lightning rod, signboard, supporting structure of a solar energy system |
| Roof | Flat roof, main roof, upper roof, pitched roof, profiled roof, skylight, membrane roof, and the roof of a covered walkway or a footbridge |
| Direct staircase | A staircase providing access to an accessible roof |
| Maintenance access window / door | An openable window, or a door, for maintenance access |
| Fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway | A metal cat ladder or maintenance walkway |
| Maintenance access ladder and gantry system | Gantries travelling on tracks fixed to the building, for an inclined facade, dome-shaped roof, skylights or other features |
| Power-operated elevating work platform | Also named cherry pickers and aerial platforms; vehicle-mounted, self-propelled, towed or manually moved |
| Suspended working platform (gondola) | Same meaning as in the Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Suspended Working Platforms) Regulation (Cap. 59AC): a scaffold (not a slung scaffold) or working platform suspended by lifting gear and capable of being raised or lowered. It does not include a boatswain’s chair. It includes lifting appliances and gear, counterweights, ballast, outriggers, other supports, and the mechanical and electrical apparatus |

**SD takeaway:** Treat every external wall, curtain wall, cladding, roof (including a walkway or footbridge roof) and listed projection as needing a named M&R method before the elevation and roof plan are frozen.

### 2. External wall, curtain wall and external cladding (Part 2, sections 1–2)
| Rule | Text |
|---|---|
| General duty | Suitable, adequate and safe M&R access to external walls, curtain walls, external claddings, roofs and building projections, so workers can reach those elements safely |
| Not exhaustive | One type, or a combination, may be required |
| Scaffold geometry | Design of building projection, building recess and free-form profile shall enable erection of temporary scaffold in accordance with the Guidelines on the Design and Construction of Bamboo Scaffolds |
| M&R works | Inspection, cleansing, repair and replacement of wall finishes, windows, window walls, glass panes, cladding panels, and similar components |
| Allowed access | Maintenance access window, maintenance door, power-operated elevating work platform, or suspended working platform |

No height or storey trigger for these elements is printed in Part 2 section 2.

**SD takeaway:** Every facade zone needs one of those four access types, and recesses or free-form profiles must still be scaffoldable.

### 3. Projections from an external wall, curtain wall or cladding (Part 2, section 3)
No height, depth or area trigger is printed for these projections except where a dimension is shown in the table. Outer-surface access is a separate requirement from access onto the element.

| Element | M&R works | Access | Extra rule printed as text |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC platform (3.1) | Inspection, cleansing, repair and replacement of the AC, and maintenance of the platform | Onto the platform: maintenance access window, or balcony/UP combined with an AC platform designed to Appendix B. Outer surface: elevating work platform or suspended working platform | May also serve other utilities if AC working space is not jeopardised. All Appendix C design and safety provisions apply |
| Balcony and UP (3.2) | Inspection, cleansing and repair | Onto the balcony/UP: maintenance access door. Outer surface: elevating work platform or suspended working platform | At least one anchor for a personal fall-arrest system at every balcony and UP, readily accessible from the adjoining floor, at a height of not less than 1.5 m and not more than 1.8 m above the finished floor of the adjoining floor, and complying with Appendix D paragraph 7. Structural/fixing detail submitted per Appendix F |
| Canopy (3.3) | Inspection, cleansing, removal of debris, repair and replacement of defective parts and the drainage outlet | Elevating work platform, suspended working platform, or maintenance access window | No size trigger printed |
| Drying rack (3.4) | Cleansing, repair and replacement | Maintenance access window, maintenance access door, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform | No size trigger printed |
| External drainage pipe (3.5) | Inspection, repair and replacement | Cat ladder with grating platform in a pipe well complying with PNAP APP-93; a projecting platform such as an AC platform; maintenance access window; elevating work platform; or suspended working platform | Planning and design of M&R access to drainage works for a new building shall comply with PNAP APP-93 |
| External vertical greenery (3.6) | Inspection, general gardening, and repair of the drainage point, irrigation/drainage pipework and supporting frame | Elevating work platform, suspended working platform, or a fixed walkway accessible from the interior through a maintenance access window or maintenance door | No size trigger printed |
| Planter box (3.7) | Cleansing, irrigation and replacement of flower pots | Outer surface: maintenance access window, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform | No size trigger printed |
| Projecting window (3.8) | Inspection, cleansing of glass, repair and replacement of panes, casements and sealant | Outer surface: maintenance access window, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform | No size trigger printed |
| Signboard (3.9) | Inspection, repair and replacement of the signboard and its components | Other than the inspection panel: fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway; maintenance staircase; ladder-and-gantry system accessible from the interior through a maintenance access window or door; elevating work platform; or suspended working platform | Inspection panel for all concealed fixings and structural members unless they meet designated exempted works in Part 2 of Schedule 2 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (Cap. 123N). Side or diameter of the panel not less than 200 mm; readily openable without special tools or destructive means. No panel where a banner/vinyl display on supporting frames is readily removable to expose the concealed fixing |
| Sun-shade, reflector, eave, cornice, moulding, overhang, fin and other architectural projection (3.10) | Inspection, repair and replacement | Maintenance access window, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform | No size trigger printed |

**SD takeaway:** Balconies and UPs need a maintenance door plus an anchor at 1.5–1.8 m on the adjoining floor; AC platforms need a separate outer-surface method (elevating platform or gondola) as well as access onto the platform.

### 4. Accessible roof, inaccessible roof, and projections from a roof (Part 2, sections 4–5)
| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Accessible roof (4.1) | Protective barrier at the outer edge in accordance with B(C)R sections 37 and 38. M&R covers roof finishes, features, facilities (including solar energy systems and building services) and signboards. Access by direct staircase or maintenance door |
| Signboard on an accessible roof | Fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway if any part of the signboard is more than 5 m high measured from the roof finishes |
| Inaccessible roof (4.2) | Not intended for occupation; accessible to personnel for M&R only (inspection, cleansing, repair and replacement of finishes, features, facilities such as water tank, solar energy system and building services, and signboards). Access by fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway, ladder-and-gantry system, maintenance door, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform |
| Stepping onto an inaccessible roof | Guard-rails with toe-boards at the edge, to Appendix D paragraph 4. If permanent guard-rails around the whole perimeter are impracticable, subject to 4.2.4: (i) a proprietary collapsible guard-rail system designed for imposed and wind loads not inferior to B(C)R, the Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads (Loading Code) and the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong; and/or (ii) temporary edge protection with a suitable platform and access ladder, designed from a prior risk assessment |
| Anchorage where 4.2.3 alternatives are used (4.2.4) | Suitable anchorage points or a horizontal lifeline for a personal fall-arrest system and/or travel-restraint system, complying with OSHO and FIUO, at (i) the roof access, or (ii) other locations not less than 2 m from the roof edge / fragile area, so the lanyard can be attached before the user is at risk of a fall. If access is within 2 m of the roof edge, any fragile area, or opens onto a pitched roof, permanent guard-rails should be provided at least around the roof access point or landing. Prescribed general notes for the general building plans are printed in 4.2.4 |
| Signboard on an inaccessible roof | Fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway if any part is more than 5 m high measured from the roof finishes |
| Projection from a roof (5) | Inspection, cleansing, repair and replacement. Access by fixed maintenance ladder or external walkway, ladder-and-gantry system, maintenance staircase, elevating work platform, or suspended working platform |

**SD takeaway:** An occupied or barrier-protected roof needs a direct staircase or maintenance door; a non-occupied roof still needs an access method, and any signboard more than 5 m above the roof finishes needs a fixed ladder or external walkway.

### 5. Design sizes for the access devices (Part 3)
| Device | Hard dimensions and rules in the text |
|---|---|
| Reach (1.2) | Workers shall reach the intended element within a hand-reachable range of not more than 500 mm in front of the element. Recesses that hinder access must be considered |
| Material delivery and premises (1.1) | Access shall cater for operational need including material delivery. If access is from individual premises, the Deed of Mutual Covenant (DMC) shall give a reasonable right of access for maintenance personnel. If no DMC, put the right in the sale and purchase agreement, assignment or tenancy agreement |
| Parallel compliance (1.3) | Also comply with Parts B and C of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings (FS Code); protective barriers under B(C)R sections 36, 37 and 38; B(P)R regulation 3A; PNAP APP-110; and the Loading Code, as appropriate |
| Fall of 2 m or more (1.4) | Guard-rails and toe-boards to Appendix D paragraph 4(a) and (b), unless a protective barrier under B(C)R sections 37 and 38 is provided. Guard-rails resist imposed loads for “Area where congregation of people is not expected” in Table 3.13 of the Loading Code. If subject to wind, use the more stringent of that imposed load or the design wind loads in the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong |
| Suspended working platform (2.1) | Building designed for the platform. Supporting frames, members and fixings, including any screen, designed for all load combinations including wind, and approved under the BO. Corrosion resistant or protected. When not in operation, tied to the building. Must not obstruct means of escape under FS Code Part B. Restraints at a wind-exposed facade against tipping, tilting, swaying or horizontal movement. If a worker entering the platform at height risks a fall of 2 m or more, one or more permanent cast-in anchors on the roof for each worker. A temporary platform needs mechanical booms or roof anchorages, corrosion resistant or protected |
| Maintenance access window (2.2) | Clearance not less than 460 mm wide by 1 100 mm high. Sill not less than 1.1 m above the internal finished floor of the adjoining floor. One or more permanent cast-in anchors on the external wall, close to the window, readily accessible, at not less than 1.5 m and not more than 1.8 m above the finished floor of the adjoining floor. The open window shall not obstruct use of the anchors |
| Maintenance staircase (2.3) | Width not less than 900 mm, with guard-rails on both sides to Appendix D paragraph 4 |
| Maintenance door (2.4) | Not less than 600 mm wide by 2 000 mm high. Locked. Warning notice on the outside face, English and Chinese, letters and characters not less than 25 mm high: “DANGER / UNAUTHORISED ACCESS PROHIBITED / CLOSE AND LOCK THIS DOOR” and the Chinese text printed in the Code |
| Fixed ladder (2.5.1) | Width not less than 400 mm and not more than 600 mm. Safety hoops not less than 650 mm and not more than 800 mm wide. Comply with Appendix D paragraph 8. A slab opening for M&R access: side or diameter not less than 500 mm |
| External walkway (2.5.2) | Comply with Appendix D paragraph 4. Shall not obstruct M&R of external elements. Where erected over a roof or other projections, working space underneath not less than 1.2 m high |
| Ladder and gantry (2.6) | Designed to fit the building profile and the M&R works; Appendix D paragraph 8 |
| Elevating work platform (2.7) | External walls accessed shall not exceed 1.5 m above the platform level at its maximum operating height. A levelled and firm designated parking area in the development |
| Ladder over 3 m (Appendix D paragraph 8) | If the distance between the highest point and the ground or floor is more than 3 m: anti-climbing device at the lower level, suitable safety hoops, and a landing/platform as a rest place at an interval of not greater than 9 m, with guard-rails and toe-board to Part 3 paragraph 1.4 |

**SD takeaway:** Lock a 500 mm hand-reach in front of every element, and size windows (460 mm × 1 100 mm, sill ≥ 1.1 m), doors (600 mm × 2 000 mm), stairs (≥ 900 mm) and ladders (400–600 mm) to the Part 3 minima before the wall module is fixed.

### 6. AC platform with a balcony or UP, screens, and gross floor area (Appendices B and C)
Appendix B applies where an AC platform is combined with a balcony and/or UP. Disposition should follow Figures B1, B2 or B3. Those figures are not dimensioned in the extracted text.

| Item | Printed rule |
|---|---|
| Level change (App. B(b)) | Minimum 50 mm rise from the finished floor of the balcony/UP to the AC platform |
| Working space (App. B(d); App. C para. 2(b)) | In front or at the back of the AC, with direct access from the balcony/UP. Length not less than the length of the AC platform (Appendix B) or not less than the length of the AC (Appendix C), and depth 400 mm. Length of working space on one side of the AC not less than 400 mm, which may be increased to a maximum of 500 mm if it also serves as the worker’s landing through windows |
| Barriers (App. B(e)) | Outer edges: protective barriers under B(C)R sections 37 and 38 |
| Screens between platform and balcony/UP (App. B(f)) | Not higher than 1.6 m; permeability not less than 70%. Permeability means visual and physical porosity |
| Screens at the platform edge (App. B(g)) | Not higher than the parapet of the balcony and/or UP (1.6 m for a platform with stacked ACs); permeability not less than 70% |
| Gross floor area exclusion (App. B(h)) | Connected balcony/UP subject to Joint Practice Notes No. 1 and No. 2 (JPN 1 and JPN 2). Area of each AC platform in Figures B1 to B3 not more than 0.8 m², so the outer edges of the AC platform are not included in the perimeter of the balcony/UP or the covered area under the lowest balcony/UP. The JPN letter of undertaking shall designate the AC platforms as “areas for air-conditioning” in the DMC (or, if no DMC, in the sale and purchase agreement, assignment or tenancy agreement), and individual AC platforms must not be erected at the external walls |
| Open-air test (App. B footnote 2) | For the JPN requirement that a balcony/UP faces into the open air, a balcony/UP combined with an AC platform uses the perimeter of the merged floor to assess the 40% requirement. A screen under paragraph (g) at that perimeter is not regarded as facing into the open air if it is higher than the parapet of the balcony/UP |
| Access opening to the platform (App. C para. 1) | Normally not less than 460 mm by 1 100 mm, and in no case less than the size of the AC. Bottom of the opening not higher than 1 300 mm from the finished floor of the adjoining AC platform |
| Projection and width (App. C para. 2(a)) | Maximum projection of the AC platform may be up to 900 mm, disregarding the thickness of the protective barrier, guard-rail or screen including supporting members. Width limited to housing the AC and the working space; not a catwalk. Show platform size and the AC on general building plans |
| Ventilation gap (App. C para. 2(c)) | Minimum 100 mm between the AC and the outer edge of the platform (or inner side of a screen, if any) or the external wall |
| Screen height (App. C para. 2(d)) | Platforms with access in or from the units may stack two ACs; screen, if any, not more than 1.6 m. One layer of AC: screen not less than 0.9 m and not more than 1.1 m |
| Screen construction (App. C para. 4) | Maximum 50 mm thick; not less than 70% permeability from elevation; panels a trade worker can reasonably operate; able to withstand the impact load, being the minimum imposed loads for “area where congregation of people is not expected” in Table 3.13 of the Loading Code |
| Other services (3.1.4 and App. C para. 3) | Services not serving the AC may pass through if working space and safety are not jeopardised |
| Anchors and guard-rails at AC platforms (App. C para. 6) | Where access to an external wall is required, cast-in anchors and guard-rails in accordance with the Construction Industry Council (CIC) Guidelines on the Design, Installation and Maintenance of Cast-in Anchors at External Walls of New Buildings (Version 2). Show design and location on general building plans and structural plans. Prescribed general note is printed in Appendix C |

**SD takeaway:** A combined AC platform is a 50 mm step, a 400 mm deep working zone, a projection of up to 900 mm, and screens of at least 70% permeability; gross floor area exclusion of each platform (not more than 0.8 m²) is tied to JPN 1 and JPN 2 and the merged-floor 40% open-air test.

### 7. Guard-rails, safety belts and cast-in anchors (Part 3 section 1.5; Appendices D, F and G)
| Item | Printed rule |
|---|---|
| When personal fall protection is used | Appendix D paragraph 6: a safe working platform and access/egress is considered first. Personal fall protection, including cast-in anchors, is only a supplementary measure or the last resort |
| Anchor standard | Appendix D paragraph 7: anchorages comply with the Labour Department “Guidance Notes on Classification and Use of Safety Belts and their Anchorage Systems” and the CIC cast-in anchor guidelines (Version 2). An example is Appendix G. Cast-in anchor structural details are submitted for approval under the BO (Part 3, 1.5) |
| Where they are cast in (App. F para. 3; App. G) | Pre-determined locations at external reinforced concrete walls with a minimum thickness of 125 mm, or at external structural elements. Safely accessible and easily visible. Workers must not be exposed to a fall while securing or detaching the anchorage. Design life the same as the building. Installed before casting. Anchor bars of stainless steel in Grade B500 complying with BS 6744:2016 and designation number 1.4436, or equivalent, with a minimum diameter of 16 mm, or designed for the load test required for a Type A anchor device in BS EN 795:2012 |
| Guard-rails where fall risk is 2 m or more (App. D para. 4, Third Schedule of the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 59I)) | Top guard-rail not less than 900 mm and not more than 1 150 mm; intermediate guard-rail not less than 450 mm and not more than 600 mm; toe-board not less than 200 mm. Gangways and runs not less than 400 mm wide, and not less than 650 mm wide for movement of materials, so far as reasonably practicable |
| Platform fencing (App. D para. 5) | Where the platform could be a danger, Occupational Safety and Health Regulation (Cap. 509A) section 6: securely fenced to a height of 900 mm, or an effective personal fall-protection system if fencing is not reasonably practicable |
| Alteration and addition drill-in anchors (App. F) | If drill-in anchors are proposed for alteration and addition works, submit a structural-viability assessment with the structural plans. PNAP APP-117 is the stated reference |

**SD takeaway:** Cast-in anchors go into external reinforced concrete at least 125 mm thick, with a 16 mm stainless bar (or a tested Type A device), and they do not replace guard-rails where the fall risk is 2 m or more.

### 8. What must be shown, and when (Part 3 section 3; Appendix F)
| Item | Printed rule |
|---|---|
| M&R access plans | Plans, elevations and sections at a legible ratio, preferably not less than 1:300, showing location and design at each floor/zone. Technical specification of adequacy (for example cradle length and tie-back restraints). A summary against the Appendix E checklist |
| First general building plan | M&R access plans need not be in the first submission. The plan shall carry the note that, before applying for consent to commence the superstructure works, the M&R access provisions required under the Code will be submitted to and approved by BD |
| Structural submission | Separate approval for cast-in anchors, restraint sockets, mechanical booms or anchorages for a temporary suspended working platform, with the details listed in Appendix F paragraph 4 |
| DMC | For new buildings, the developer or owner undertakes to designate the M&R access as “common areas” in the DMC. If access is from individual premises, the DMC (or, if none, the sale and purchase agreement, assignment or tenancy agreement) gives maintenance personnel a reasonable right of access |
| Record plans | In addition to PNAP APP-13, an additional set of M&R access record plans on application for an occupation permit, or with the certificate of completion for alteration and addition works (Form BA14), for referral to the Labour Department |

Appendix E of the Code is a checklist of which access type serves which element. It does not add dimensions.

**SD takeaway:** The first general building plan can defer the M&R drawings, but it must carry the consent-stage undertaking, and anchor and gondola structure is a separate BO submission.
