# Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue December 2010 | 2024 Edition (Aug 2024 via PNAP APP-157) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Operational companion to the **Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009** (TM) under BO Cap. 123 s.39A. Governs **quality supervision** + **site safety supervision** via Supervision Plans, TCP grades T1–T5 across four streams (AP / RSE / RGE / RC), temporary-works Cases 1–3, critical-stage uplift, Smart Site Safety System (4S), Non-conformity Form B, and BA reporting clocks. Read with **TMSS2009**, PNAP APP-157 / APP-158, PNRC 76 / 77, Geoguide 2, and Steel Code 2011 (2023) for crane-support welding. This Code does **not** set architectural planning standards (GFA, MOE, OTTV) — it sets the **site-control envelope** that locks programme, consultant/RC staffing cost, specialist contractor types, and which massing/sequence choices become “critical stages”.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This Code applies to **building works and street works** under the Buildings Ordinance — major works under consent and **minor works** under simplified requirements — wherever a Supervision Plan is required by the TM. It covers both (a) ensuring works match approved/submitted plans, BO and BA conditions (**quality**), and (b) controlling hazards to workers, the public, and adjoining buildings/structures/land (**site safety**).

At schematic design, treat it as a **delivery-risk and consultant-deployment code**: scale factor (capped at **2**), geotechnical sensitivity / Scheduled Areas, tower-crane strategy, heritage loadbearing A&A, street cantilevers / podium over street, and deep excavation near railways/mains drive TCP manpower, critical-stage full-time attendance, 4S alert systems (typically estimated cost **> $30 M**), and the 21-day Case 3 temporary-works lead-in that must sit in the master programme.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two supervision types, four streams, TCP ladder (§1, §3–4, Fig. 4.1)

#### 1.1 What “supervision” means

| Type | Statutory meaning | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality supervision** | Works carried out in general accordance with BO/Regs, approved plans (or MW submitted plans), and BA orders/conditions | GI must be by RSC(GIFW); foundation/soil-nail stages have % / full-time gates; BA can impose extra % at approval/consent |
| **Site safety supervision** | Control hazards to (i) workers, (ii) persons around site, (iii) adjoining buildings/structures/land | Hoarding, scaffold/catch fans, monitoring, ELS sequence, TW integrity, 4S alerts |

**Assessment principles (§3.2–3.5):** requirements follow **type of works** + **scale**; **additional** supervision at **critical stages**; BA may adjust requirements with technology/methods and may upgrade TCP quals/frequencies based on market supply.

#### 1.2 Safety management structure

| Stream head | Representative | Typical TCP grades | Core ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AP** | Highest-grade TCP in AP stream (or AP self) | T4, T3 | Coordinates & **submits** whole plan; oversees RC stream; BA reporting |
| **RSE** | Highest in RSE stream (or RSE self) | T5, T3 | Structural compliance; **Case 3 TW written permission**; design-assumption validation |
| **RGE** | Highest in RGE stream (or RGE self) | T5, T2/T3 | Geotech compliance; Case 3 TW permission (geotech effects); **DSS** on sensitive sites |
| **AS of RC** | Highest in RC stream (or AS self) | T4/T5, T2/T3, T1 | **Continuous** supervision (B(A)R 41); Case 2/3 TW docs; 4S implementation plan |

**TCP ladder:** T1–T3 = routine safety; T4–T5 = engineering safety. Higher grade may absorb lower. Duties of TCPs may be **combined** (§8.12–8.17 + Form C). AP/RSE/RGE are deemed to meet TCP quals in their own stream and may personally cover grades if their inspection frequency ≥ required (§4.6).

**Interstream rule (§4.15, §10.1):** within-stream = engineering ↔ routine; interstream = counterparts between streams. TCPs must notify counterparts of safety/quality concerns and of BA-imposed task-specific conditions.

**SD takeaway:** Name AP / RSE / RGE / likely RSC specialties (GIFW, Foundation, Site Formation, Demolition) before SD freeze — Supervision Plan is not a late procurement item.

### 4. Scale factor — staffing cost & frequency uplift (§8.4–8.11, Tables 8.1–8.3)

**Scale factor** = (measurable item) ÷ (basic value), **capped at 2**. AP assesses scale per works type in the plan.

| Works type | Measurable item | Basic value |
|---|---|---|
| Demolition | Max floor area demolished **per storey** | **750 m²** |
| GIFW | No. of drilling rigs | **6** |
| GIFW (no drillholes) | Concurrent trial pits / coreholes / slope strippings | **20** |
| Site formation | Total cost# | **$40 M** |
| Slope / RW / buried services repair | Total cost# | **$12 M** |
| Excavation & lateral support | Avg cost / month# | **$8 M** |
| Pile wall | Avg cost / month# | **$8 M** |
| Tunnel works | Total cost# | **$47 M** |
| Large-diameter bored / barrette piles | Avg cost / month# | **$19 M** |
| Other piles | Avg cost / month# | **$9.5 M** |
| Cap / footing / basement | Total cost# | **$47 M** |
| Superstructure | Total **construction floor area** | **20 000 m²** |
| Curtain wall / cladding | Total aggregated surface area | **10 000 m²** |
| A&A | Total cost# | **$17.5 M** |
| Minor works | Total cost# | **$11 M** |
| Street works | Total cost# | **$12 M** |

# Based on CIC Estimated Total Value of Construction Operations (levy under Cap. 587). May deduct: (a) contingency & CPF — **revise Supervision Plan** if revised sum changes scale/resources; (b) E&M not under BO; (c) BO-exempt fit-out.

| Scale | Effect |
|---|---|
| **< 1** | Visit duration may be &lt; full day; **frequency must not drop** below TM Table 1 |
| **= 1** | Full-day inspection assumed per occasion |
| **> 1** | Upgrade frequency of heads + T2–T5; **and/or** increase number of full-time T1 (sometimes T2/T3) |

**Notional supervision input (scale = 1), Table 8.2:**

| Level | Description | Man-days / month |
|---|---|---|
| L5 | Full time | **25** |
| L4 | Weekly | **4** |
| L3 | Fortnightly | **2** |
| L2 | Monthly | **1** |
| L1 | As and when required | **0.5** (when head also acts as TCP) |

**Sub-levels when more than weekly (Table 8.3):** L4.1 = 8; L4.2 = 12; L4.3 = 16; L4.4 = 20 man-days/mo.

**Continuous supervision (§8.8 + note 16):** Full-time TCPs stationed on site. RC must keep continuous cover while works are underway so that safety measures run, works match plans, problems are solved **before cover-up / next stage**, and NCs/incidents are handled. Intermittent absence must not allow improper performance or serious consequence — manner varies with task complexity and detectability of defects.

**Combination rules (§8.12–8.17):**
- Higher grade may take lower-grade duties in same stream if quals met
- Combination **only** for works types **concurrent** on site
- Use Form C; append to Supervision Plan
- After per-stream calc, AP/RSE/RGE may further combine across the three consultant streams on same principles
- Derived frequency after combination **≥** TM Table 1 minimum

**SD takeaway:** Tower CFA ≫ 20 000 m², large CW area, or heavy ELS/piling monthly spend → scale toward **2** → budget TCP headcount, site office desks, and consultant fee contingency at SD.

### 5. Temporary works Cases 1 / 2 / 3 (§4.8–4.13, Fig. 4.2)

All parties share duty for integrity of the temporary structure **and** fixing methods (§4.8). RC keeps on site a register + set of TW drawings, calcs, method statements, precautionary measures for TCPs/BA (§4.13).

| Case | When | Who owns integrity | Process |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | TW + sequence/method **shown on prescribed plans** | AP/RSE/RGE **and** RC | Upon BA approval & consent → RC builds; all streams supervise to plans |
| **2** | TW **not** on prescribed plans; **no** overstress/overload of permanent / adjoining | **RC alone** | RC prepares construction drawings¹ + design justifications²; builds |
| **3** | TW not on prescribed plans **but may** overstress/overload permanent / adjoining / geotech | **RC** integrity; **RSE/RGE** written permission | See timeline below |

¹ Construction drawings = details, specs, sequence, method statements, precautionary/protective measures.  
² Design justifications = TW calcs + assessment of effects on permanent structures / adjoining buildings & lands.

#### 5.1 Case 2 — works that almost always need drawings + calcs (§4.10)

**Falsework for concrete casting of:**
- Transfer plate; vehicular ramps
- Cantilevered slab **> 1.5 m**
- Beam span **> 12 m**
- Deep beams depth **> 3 m**
- Elevated water tank; space frame
- Vehicular bridge / footbridge; prestressed structure

**Formwork and shoring for:**
- Columns / walls height **> 6 m**
- Retaining wall higher than **4 m**

**Also:** temporary working platforms for plant/machinery; lifting operations of plant/machinery.

#### 5.2 Case 3 — programme-critical timeline (§4.11)

1. RC prepares drawings + calcs; appoints **T5 Person** (quals ≥ TCP T5) to **certify** documents  
2. Submit to RSE/RGE **≥ 21 days before** commencement  
3. RSE/RGE check effects on permanent / adjoining / geotech; may demand more info; then give **written permission**  
4. RC builds to certified docs  
5. On completion, T5 Person **personally inspects** and signs completion certificate → to RSE for acknowledgement **within 7 days**

**Doubt rule:** If unsure whether TW affects permanent works → consult RSE/RGE and follow **Case 3**.

**Heritage A&A note (Table 9.1 Note 5):** Cases **1 and 3** temporary works for heritage loadbearing A&A are themselves treated as critical-stage triggers.

**SD takeaway:** Transfer plates, long spans, deep cantilevers, tall walls, prestressed elements, and top-down decks are structural **and** TW-governance decisions — they consume programme float via the 21-day gate.

### 6. Tower-crane support temporary works (§4.12, Fig. 4.3, Forms TW1–TW3)

Applies **in addition** to Case 2 or 3 whenever TW supports a tower crane (including connections crane ↔ TW ↔ permanent structure).

| Role | Minimum | Duty |
|---|---|---|
| **Design Engineer (DE)** | RPE (civil/structural) + **≥ 5 years** relevant | Design + sign drawings/calcs; Form **TW1** |
| **Independent Checking Engineer (ICE)** | Same quals; **no** holding/subsidiary/employer-employee or other relationship with DE/firms | Check + Form **TW2** |
| Completion certifiers | RC’s **TCP T4** + **T5 Person** (may be DE or ICE) | Joint Form **TW3** after personal inspection |

| Step | Case 2 (no permanent overload) | Case 3 (may overload) |
|---|---|---|
| Before start | TW1+TW2 + certified package → **AP for record** + **RSE for acknowledgement** | Same package → RSE acknowledgement + **RSE/RGE review ≥ 21 days** before start → written permission → then copy to **AP for record** |
| During | Continuous RC supervision; AP/RSE/RGE periodic checks per plan | Same |
| Welding | Qualified welders per Steel Code 2011 (2023) cl. **14.3**; HOKLAS (or MRA) NDT on **on-site and off-site** welds (cl. 14.3.6); **TCP T4 (RC)** reviews results with joint locations clear | Same |
| After completion | TW3 → AP record + RSE acknowledgement **within 7 days** | Same |
| Crane load test / exam (FIU Lifting Regs) | **Not** before RSE acknowledges TW3 | Same |
| **Free-standing** crane (base support only) | RSE submits all docs to **BD within 14 days** of TW completion, with signed statement on completeness + no adverse effect on permanent/adjoining | Same for both Case 2 & 3 |

Keep on site for BA: certified drawings/calcs, TW1/TW2, test reports, TCP supervision records, RSE-acknowledged TW3.

**SD takeaway:** Decide tied-in vs free-standing crane, tie-floor levels, and whether supports need structural steel welding **with RSE at SD** — free-standing adds a BD record submission path; tied-in loads permanent members.

### 7. Smart Site Safety System / 4S (§4.14)

**Trigger:** Building works with estimated cost **> $30 M**, where BA imposes BO s.17(1) conditions for superstructure works involving **mobile plants** and/or **tower cranes**.

| System | What it must do | General danger-zone minima (Safety Officer sets by risk assessment) |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile plant alert** | 360° sensors on chassis + movable superstructures; alert operator **and** personnel entering zone | **≥ 2 m** from any part of plant |
| **Tower crane alert** | Sensors covering all loading/unloading areas at **all floor levels** involved; alert operator + personnel | **≥ 7 m** radius from crane hook; clearance activating warning generally **≥ 3 m** |

**“Mobile plant” includes:** bulldozer, compactor, crawler crane, dumper, excavator, gantry crane, grader, loader, scraper, truck-mounted crane, wheeled telescopic mobile crane.  
**Excluded:** fork-lift, lorry, MEWP, truck; plants stationed **&lt; 1 day** (e.g. concrete mixer, shotcrete truck, water pump truck).

**RC duties:** AS (or under AS supervision) prepares **implementation plan** — plant/crane types, alert systems, danger-zone definitions, operation brief, **fallback on system failure**. AS + Representative ensure execution. TCP **T1 and T3** check systems installed/operating and report malfunctions per plan.

**SD takeaway:** Put 4S (and danger-zone space at loading bays / plant routes) into preliminaries and site logistics diagrams for mid-size+ projects.

### 9. Quality supervision — GIFW, soil nails, foundations (§6)

**Principles (§6.3–6.6):** AP/RSE/RGE give periodic supervision via TCP teams at specified frequency/% (B(A)R 37); they remain accountable under BO for quality in their roles; heads should personally inspect as they judge necessary. RC/AS give **continuous** supervision (B(A)R 41) even when works are sub-contracted. Contemporaneous records mandatory.

Further guidance for **superstructure / ELS / site formation** quality: **PNAP APP-158** and **PNRC 77** (not fully duplicated in this Code).

#### 9.1 GIFW (Table 6.1) — blocks plan approval if wrong

| Stage | Check |
|---|---|
| **I1** Drilling/coring | Methods suit ground; in Scheduled Areas also match approved plans |
| **I2** Sampling | Technique, seal/store, no contamination, moisture preserved, depths/locations recorded |
| **I3** Instrumentation | Good practice / manufacturer; Scheduled Areas also per approved plans |
| **I4** Field testing | Good practice (field density → HOKLAS lab) |

**Hard gates:**
- All GIFW (scheduled **and** non-scheduled) by **RSC(GIFW)** under proper supervision
- Follow **Geoguide 2**; experienced drillers; logging by **competent person** (App. VII)
- Keep cores/samples until BA acknowledges satisfactory SF/foundation/related works
- GI report supporting plan approval needs certificate by **RGE + AS of RSC(GIFW)** (App. VIII)
- **GBP / foundation / SF / ELS plans accompanied by geotech assessment are approved only when BA is satisfied GIFW was by RSC(GIFW) and results satisfy design assumptions** (§6.13)
- Admin procedures: App. VIII; Supervision Plan still required for GIFW

#### 9.2 Soil nailing (Table 6.2)

| Who | Attendance |
|---|---|
| RGE | T5 + T3 |
| RSC(SF) | T4 + T1 |
| Full-time resident | **≥1 RGE T3** + **≥1 RSC T1** during **every stage for each nail** |
| Notice | RSC notifies RGE’s T3 **before** each stage starts |

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| N1 | Pull-out / site trial per approved plans + acceptance criteria |
| N2 | Setting-out vs plans |
| N3 | Drillhole Ø, length, inclination, bearing; report ground/GW anomalies |
| N4 | Assembly — length, type, corrosion protection, couplers, centralisers, grout pipes |
| N5 | Insertion — hole clean, correct rigid assembly |
| N6–N7 | Grout mix/tests; same-day grouting after insertion; sustained clean return; report excessive time/volume/settlement |
| N8 | Nail heads + structural supports between heads |

**Personal minimums (RGE T5 and RSC T4 each):** ≥1 nail pull-out + ≥1 site trial; ≥2 working nails early for insertion; ≥2 early for grouting; ≥1 nail head.

**Records:** App. IX key records certified by RGE T3; full set on site. On completion RGE submits to BA. If BA concerned → NDT of **≥ 1%** of nails, **min 2 per slope/wall** (GEO Report 133 or BA-agreed method).

Actual %/numbers set case-by-case at approval; RGE must size team for concurrent nail construction and review adequacy during construction.

#### 9.3 Foundations (Table 6.3) — stage map

| Who | Grades |
|---|---|
| RSE | T5 + T3 |
| RSC(Foundation) | T4 + T2 |

BA sets actual full-time vs % at approval/consent. Pre-drilling, interface proof drilling, post-installation drilling, proof core-drilling **must** be by **RSC(GIFW)** under the foundation quality supervisors — **no separate Supervision Plan** for those drillings (§6.27).

| Type | Critical stages (summary) |
|---|---|
| **Driven piles** | Setting-out → driving test → driving → splicing → final sets → proof load test (APP-18) |

| **Raft / spread** | Bearing stratum inspection; plate loading test where required |

### 10. Significant geotechnical content + DSS + Scheduled Areas (§7, §8.2–8.3)

#### 10.1 What counts as “significant geotechnical content” (§7.1)

| Category | Triggers (common; list not exhaustive) |
|---|---|
| **Site formation** | Blasting; prestressed ground anchors (incl. sensitive sites*); soil nails; cut slopes **> 3 m** (rock/soil); rock-slope stabilisation; fill slopes **> 5 m**, or &lt;5 m with CTL Cat. **1 or 2** (APP-109); reinforced fill; natural terrain hazard mitigation |
| **ELS / temp retaining** | Depth **> 4.5 m** ( **> 7.5 m** on sensitive sites*) |
| **Permanent retaining** | Diaphragm walls; bored-pile walls; reinforced fill structures; cantilever/gravity RW **> 5 m**; screen/basement walls **> 7.5 m** |
| **Ground treatment** | Vertical / horizontal/raking drains; grouting & dewatering for cut-cover / tunnel/shaft/cavern; GW drainage works in **Scheduled Area No. 1** |
| **Demolition affecting slopes/RWs** | Ground stabilisation by soil nails or anchors |
| **Foundation** | Buildings in SA **No. 1**; deep foundations in SA **Nos. 2 & 4** and **Designated Area of Northshore Lantau**; foundations that could affect or be affected by tunnel/cavern/tunnel works |
| **Water wells** | Well yield test + effect of proposed extraction |
| **Tunnel works*** | Cut-cover; drill & blast; soft ground; TBM / micro-tunnelling (incl. directional drilling) / pipe jacking; associated support, ground treatment, GW control |

#### 10.2 Staffing consequences

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Normal | RGE provides TCP **T3 + T5** |
| Structural-only overlay | Where works have significant geotech content, RSE team only required if there is also **structural** works (§8.2) |
| Scheduled Areas 1, 2, 4 + Northshore Lantau foundations | **Extra RGE stream** on top of TM Table 1 foundation requirements (§8.3 / TM Note 5) |
| **DSS** | May be required for items marked * or other sites GEO/BA consider appropriate |

**Sensitive sites (§7.4):** works that could adversely affect life/property — e.g. old buildings on shallow foundations, old tunnels/caverns, major roads, railways, water/gas mains, slopes/RWs, sites with instability history.

**DSS (§7.5–7.6):** Imposed in approval/consent letters; name in Supervision Plan. Must be RPE (geotechnical) who is partner/director of the firm that prepared the geotech submission — **or** the project RGE.

### 12. Non-conformity triage + site reports (§5, §10)

#### 12.1 Three NC types (Fig. 5.1)

| Type | Form B? | Still record? |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1** — very minor; rectified same inspection (e.g. misplaced spacers, debris in formwork) | No | No (neither Form B nor site report) |
| **Type 2** — no material safety concern; confirmed OK by re-inspection **before cover-up** | No | Yes — in TCP **site supervision reports** (§5.7) |
| **All other NCs** | **Yes — Form B** | Yes |

#### 12.2 Site supervision reports (§10.2–10.4)

Required whenever any TCP does safety or quality supervision. Each TCP keeps own copy (name, post, signature). Filed at site office. AP/RSE/RGE/AS keep notes/photos of items inspected — especially at critical stages. Must be **contemporaneous**.

Minimum content: items inspected; results; Form B notes / §5.7 notes; **monthly NC summary** by each stream Representative for lesson-learning across streams.

#### 12.3 Form B procedure clocks (§10.5, Fig. 10.1)

1. TCP informs own Representative → informs AP/RSE/RGE/AS; completes Form B Part 1  
2. **Imminent danger** → AP reports BA **within 48 hrs** of discovery; other streams → AP within **12 hrs** (AS may report BA direct + copy AP within 48 hrs)  
3. AP/RSE/RGE issue rectification instruction to RC  
4. AS/Representative rectify promptly; consult AP/RSE/RGE if doubt  
5. Consultant Representative confirms completion  
6. If material safety concern + RC fails instruction → AP reports BA within **48 hrs** of knowing failure  
7. Else AP/RSE/RGE certify completion → Form B Part 2  
8. If was imminent danger → AP reports BA within **48 hrs after** rectification complete (AS → AP within **12 hrs** of completion)  
9. If also major/serious incident → follow §11 in parallel  

**Discovery** = earliest discovery by AP/RSE/RGE/AS or any Representative/TCP in their streams.

**Urgent emergency works:** Form **BA7** under BO s.19 — before owner authorises **or within 48 hrs** of start, whichever earlier.

### 13. Major / serious site incidents (§11–12)

#### 13.1 Safety-related (§11.2–11.5) — AP → BA within **48 hrs**

Criteria: may/has caused loss of life, **serious bodily injury** (limb loss/amputation or critical in hospital), or property damage; imminent danger to buildings/structures/land; or material concern for safety of building/street works.

**Examples (non-exhaustive):**
- Collapse of permanent structure (or part)
- Collapse of temp steel platform / falsework / formwork / propping / ELS
- Collapse of plant/equipment (tower crane, material hoist, passenger hoist)
- Slope distress / notable landslide
- Monitoring reading at/above **Action Level**
- Undue settlement, distress or damage to adjacent structures/ground/services
- Railway facilities/operation affected so works must suspend
- Sign of distress/instability in permanent structure
- Incidents that may become the above if RC fails to rectify

Other streams → AP within **12 hrs**. AS may report BA direct + copy AP within 48 hrs. Immediate assessment / urgent works / rectification still required. AP also reports BA within **48 hrs after** rectification complete. Does **not** replace calling Police for emergency.

#### 13.2 Quality-related (§11.6–11.7) — AP → BA within **14 days**

Other streams → AP within **5 days**. AS may report BA direct + copy AP within 14 days.

**Examples:**
- Material deviation from approved plan / material quality concern
- Substandard works needing rectification/demolition that affect sequence, permanent structure, or need prior approval/consent before restart
- Extensive concrete defects (deformation, honeycomb, exposed rebar) after striking formwork + RC fails rectification instruction

#### 13.3 How to report (§12.1)

Email: **siteincident@bd.gov.hk**

Include: site address; location/date/time of discovery and of happening; contact (name/phone/email); works type; injury/death/property damage; remedial actions underway; plans/photos/tests/docs if available.

### 16. SD decision checklist (use before massing / programme freeze)

1. **Works-type map** — demolition / SF / ELS / foundation / basement / superstructure / CW / A&A / MW / street — each has own scale factor + TM Table 1 TCP matrix.  
2. **CFA vs 20 000 m²** and **CW area vs 10 000 m²** — scale uplift toward 2?  
3. **Scheduled Area / Northshore Lantau / sensitive neighbours** (railway, water &gt;200 mm, gas, shallow-footed or &gt;40-yr buildings, slopes) — RGE + possible DSS + critical-stage 2×/week or full time.  
4. **Transfer plate / long span / street cantilever / podium over street / prestressed / walls &gt;6 m / RW &gt;4 m** — Case 2/3 TW + critical full-time attendance.  
5. **Top-down?** — highest deck before dig; enclosing-wall integrity; deflection checks (E15, C30–C31).  
6. **Tower crane** — tied vs free-standing; tie floors; welding NDT path; TW3 before load test; 14-day BD record if free-standing.  
7. **Project cost &gt; $30 M** — budget 4S; keep loading-bay danger zones (≥7 m hook / ≥2 m plant) in site logistics.  
8. **Heritage loadbearing A&A** — critical-stage project from day one; confirm heritage status with AMO if needed.  
9. **GI by RSC(GIFW)** + RGE/AS certificate **before** relying on geotech for GBP/foundation/SF/ELS approval.  
10. **Soil nails / slopes** — full-time RGE T3 + RSC T1 per nail stage; App. IX records; NDT contingency.  
11. **Hoarding / scaffold / catch fans / monitoring** — design extents that A2–A4 / C2–C9 can actually police.  
12. **Programme float** — ≥21 days Case 3 TW lead-in; TW3 before crane test; critical-stage TCP booking; 48-hr / 14-day BA reporting culture in AP terms.  
13. **Fee contingency** — scale factor &gt;1, concurrent works (limited combination), critical-stage full-time T4/T5, DSS, 4S, DE+ICE for crane TW.  
14. **Cross-refs** — keep TMSS2009 Table 1 open beside this Code; APP-158/PNRC 77 for superstructure/ELS/SF quality % not fully spelled here.
