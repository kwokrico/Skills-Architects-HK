# Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Issued under BO Cap. 123 s.39A | Effective 31 Dec 2010 | Secretary for Development  
Companion: Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (as amended) | PNAP APP-157 / APP-158 | PNRC 76 / 77

> Scope note: This TM is the **parent statutory instrument** for Supervision Plans. It defines *what* must exist (principles, streams, TCP grades, Table 1 minima, lodging clocks, exemptions). The **CoP** supplies *how* (scale factor capped at 2, critical-stage uplift, Form C combining, temporary-works Cases 1–3, tower-crane TW1–TW3, 4S, Form B NC workflow). This TM does **not** set GFA, MOE, OTTV or planning standards — it locks the **site-control envelope** that gates consent, consultant/RC staffing cost, and which massing / sequence / adjacency choices force full SP overhead.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This Technical Memorandum supplements the Buildings Ordinance on **supervision of building works and street works**. It applies wherever a Supervision Plan is required — major works under BA consent, and **minor works** under simplified requirements (Class I when SP is required; Class II/III are exempt under §11).

At schematic design, treat it as a **consent-gating and delivery-cost code**: non-exempt packages need a coordinated multi-author Supervision Plan (AP Preamble + Part I; RSE Part II; RGE Part III; AS Part IV) lodged **before or with first consent** (or ≥7 days before Class I MW start). Table 1 baseline frequencies — typically **RC T1 full-time (Level 5)** — become hard fee and programme assumptions before schematic freeze.

### 2. Key definitions architects must internalise (§3.1)

| Term | Meaning that changes design / delivery |
|---|---|
| **Author** | AP, RSE, RGE or **AS** responsible for preparing *their part* of the Supervision Plan |
| **Authorized signatory (AS)** | Person appointed by the RC to act for BO purposes and authorised to exercise **overall control** of the contractor’s safety-management structure for the project. Definition covers RGBC/RSC body corporate (s.8B(2)(d) appointee), partnership partner, sole proprietor, and RMWC AS under B(MW)R register / provisional register entries |
| **Code of Practice** | BA’s code of practice for site supervision of building works and street works |
| **Designated area** | Area with complex geological or groundwater conditions — specified in PNAP / PNRC (not listed inside this TM). Drives “significant geotechnical content” foundation rows in Table 1 |
| **Method statement** | Document setting out working procedures and sequences, including those necessary for safety management |
| **Minor works** | Works designated as MW in the Building (Minor Works) Regulation |
| **Non-conformity** | Site conditions, methods/measures, or completed works that do **not** conform to this TM, the CoP, the Supervision Plan, or supplementary docs (approved plans, method statements, precautionary/protective statements) |
| **Permanent works** | Construction activities or completed works forming the complete permanent form of the building/street works |
| **Quality supervision** | Supervision ensuring works are in general accordance with BO/Regs, **approved plans** (or MW plans submitted under simplified requirements), and any BA order/condition |
| **Registered contractor** | Person on s.8A registers appointed to carry out the works on site |
| **Scale** | Assessment of size/extent of works falling within the Supervision Plan under consideration (numeric method → CoP scale factor) |
| **Simplified requirements** | Requirements prescribed as such in B(MW)R |
| **Site safety supervision** | Supervision checking that carrying out of works — including **construction sequence**, **temporary works** at all stages, and **working environment** — is safe, so hazards are controlled and risks to workers, persons around the site, and adjoining properties/land are mitigated |
| **Technically competent person (TCP)** | Person whose academic/professional quals or experience satisfy this TM + CoP for a particular supervision/management task. **Five grades** (T1–T5) |
| **Temporary works** | Activity or construction that enables, supports or protects permanent works and will be removed or become redundant on completion. **May include a particular sequence of activity within a method statement** |
| **Works** | Whole or part of works (other than MW under simplified requirements) that is the subject of a **consent** application and of a Supervision Plan; or, for MW under simplified requirements, the subject of a Supervision Plan |

**SD takeaway:** “Temporary works” in this TM expressly includes **sequence**. Top-down decks, ELS strike sequence, and demolition order are Supervision Plan content, not just structural detailing.

### 3. What a Supervision Plan actually controls (§4)

#### 3.1 Definition and authors (§4.1)

A Supervision Plan is defined under BO **s.2(1)**: a plan setting out the **plan of safety management** of building works or street works.

It must be drawn up by:
- **AP**
- **RSE**
- **RGE**
- **AS** of the RC appointed for the works
- **and any other person as necessary**

…to address **quality supervision** and to characterise the particular **site-safety features and hazards** of the whole works, including grade and number of TCPs required.

#### 3.2 Two pillars of “safety management” (§4.2)

| Pillar | Statutory meaning | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Quality supervision** | Works in general accordance with BO/Regs, approved plans (or MW submitted plans), and BA orders/conditions | Design assumptions must be field-verifiable; GI / foundation / ELS cannot be “design-only” afterthoughts |
| **Site safety supervision** | Control hazards to (i) workers on site, (ii) all persons around the site, (iii) adjoining buildings, structures and land | Hoarding, plant support, construction sequence, TW and intermediate stages are all inside the plan |

#### 3.3 Mandatory management-structure outcomes (§4.3)

The plan’s management structure must provide for:

| (a)–(d) | Requirement | Design/logistics hook |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Adequate safety measures → safe physical working environment | Site logistics, access, catch fans, exclusion zones |
| (b) | Adequate measures → proper structural support for plant/machinery + safe operation | Crane bases, elevated platforms, piling platforms |
| (c) | Adequate control of **construction sequence** and **temporary works** → all intermediate stages safe | Top-down, ELS, transfer falsework, demolition sequence |
| (d) | Adequate measures → works match BO/Regs, approved or MW-submitted plans, BA orders/conditions | Quality gates; hold points before cover-up |

#### 3.4 Consent gate and lodging clocks (§4.4, §8.1–8.3)

| Works path | Rule |
|---|---|
| Major building / street works | Lodge SP with BA **prior to or at the same time as** first **consent** application. SP is a **pre-requisite** to issue of consent for the works referred to in the plan |
| Urgent works | SP may be required as a result of carrying out urgent works → prepare/revise plan |
| Minor works (simplified) | If SP required, submit **≥ 7 days** before commencement |
| Subsequent stages change / new info invalidates plan | Lodge **revised** SP (§8.2) |
| TCP change (except §9 retrospect cases) | AP/RSE/RGE/AS submit revised **relevant part only** within **7 days** (§8.3) |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze works-type packaging early. One SP may cover multiple approved (or MW-submitted) types (§6.8). Late package splits = late revised plans and consent risk.

### 4. Four streams — safety-management structure (§5)

#### 4.1 Organisation (§5.1, §5.2–5.4)

- TCPs are organised in **four streams** led by **AS (RC), RSE, RGE, AP**.
- All persons adopt current practice in the **CoP**, and where practicable best practice of their profession.
- Within each stream, TCPs of appropriate grades are appointed **full-time or part-time** depending on works type; they must exercise all reasonable skill, care and diligence.
- Each of AP / RSE / RGE / RC appoints the **highest-grade TCP** in the stream as **representative**, **or** the AP / RSE / RGE / AS may act personally — **only if** their inspection frequency is **not less than** that required of the highest-grade TCP in that stream (§5.3, §5.11–5.14).
- Stream heads remain responsible for operation of their parts of the plan and must inspect as necessary to satisfy themselves that the plan is implemented and that representatives/TCPs perform (§5.4).

#### 4.2 RC / AS stream functions (§5.1.1)

| Duty | Content |
|---|---|
| Execute | Follow the part of the plan prepared by the AS; carry out safety-management measures/actions to meet §4.3 objectives |
| Quality | Check works match BO/Regs, approved or MW-submitted plans, BA orders/conditions |
| Supervise | At the frequency in the plan, that works (incl. method statements and TW) shown on **prescribed plans** are adhered to |

**SD implication:** Continuous site presence culture; Table 1 almost always puts **T1 at Level 5 (full-time)** on this stream.

#### 4.3 RSE stream functions (§5.1.2)

| Duty | Content |
|---|---|
| Oversight of RC | Check and be satisfied, at plan frequency, that RC’s safety-management system complies with the AS’s part of the plan |
| Design-assumption gate | **Verify site conditions are consistent with assumptions** in design of permanent works **and** in design of method statements / TW shown on prescribed plans |
| Quality | Works in general accordance with BO/Regs, approved/MW plans, BA conditions |
| Supervise | Adherence to works (incl. method statements and TW) on prescribed plans at stated frequency |

**SD implication:** Design-assumption validation is a **site duty**, not only a desk duty. Unexpected ground, GW, or loading → RSE stream must escalate to designer.

#### 4.4 RGE stream functions (§5.1.3)

Same four-function pattern as RSE, focused on geotechnical permanent works, method statements and TW on prescribed plans.

**SD implication:** Triggered whenever works have significant geotechnical content or designated-area foundations (list → CoP Notes to Table 1). Massing that forces deep basement, slope cutting, or Scheduled/designated-area piling pulls this stream into fee and programme.

#### 4.5 AP stream functions (§5.1.4)

| Duty | Content |
|---|---|
| Oversight of RC | Check RC safety-management system vs AS’s part of plan |
| Quality | BO/Regs + approved/MW plans + BA conditions |
| **BA reporting** | Compile and prepare **non-conformity and rectification reports** to BA under §5.21 |
| Supervise | Adherence to prescribed plans / method statements / TW at stated frequency |

**SD implication:** AP appointment letters must expressly cover SP coordination/lodging, scale assessment (CoP), and BA NC reporting.

#### 4.6 When RSE and/or RGE are not appointed (§5.1.5)

For works types that do **not** require appointment of an RSE and/or RGE, the **AP must appoint TCPs** who meet the requirements of the RSE and/or RGE streams and carry out the duties in §§5.1.2, 5.1.3, 5.12 and 5.13.

**SD implication:** “Simple” jobs without named RSE/RGE still need those **TCP grade bars** on the AP’s roster — do not under-price.

#### 4.7 Common responsibilities of all TCPs (§5.5)

In addition to CoP-specified responsibilities, every TCP must:
1. **Keep records** and report on their own work; and
2. **Co-operate and communicate** with TCPs in other streams.

#### 4.8 Interstream communication (§5.15–5.16)

- Includes all communication arising from the duty to co-operate with other streams.
- TCPs are deemed to have a duty to communicate **directly** with other streams at a priority **no less than** within-stream communication.

**SD takeaway:** Do not design a siloed consultant org chart. AP/RSE/RGE/RC counterparts must be named and reachable for safety/quality concerns and BA-imposed task-specific conditions.

### 5. Engineering vs routine safety supervision vs quality (§5.6–5.10)

#### 5.1 Engineering safety supervision — grades T4 and/or T5 (§5.7)

Requires judgement. Includes but is not confined to:

| Item | Task |
|---|---|
| (a) | Consider principles of working methods on site and suitability for conditions encountered |
| (b) | Check specified aspects of work comply with **design requirements** where related to site safety |
| (c) | Check site works comply with Supervision Plan incl. method statements and precautionary/protective measures |
| (d) | Verify that provisions of method statements and precautionary/protective methods remain **valid for conditions as encountered** |
| (e) | **Notify the designer** of method statements and precautionary/protective measures of variations in site conditions inconsistent with design assumptions |
| (f) | Ensure systems are in place and followed to record that safety supervision has been properly executed |

#### 5.2 Routine safety supervision — grades T1 to T3 (§5.8)

Includes but is not confined to:

| Item | Task |
|---|---|
| (a) | Monitor that site operations/working methods meet safety standards in BO and BA codes; that unsafe practices are not followed |
| (b) | Check general and minor safety aspects of the works |
| (c) | Check that work on site complies with approved, accepted or submitted method statements and precautionary/protective measures |

#### 5.3 Site-safety task headings (§5.9)

Specific tasks (tables in CoP) fall under:
1. **General safety measures** and minor safety aspects
2. **Structural support for and operation of plant and machinery**
3. **Temporary works and intermediate stages** of the works

#### 5.4 Quality supervision tasks (§5.10)

Includes but is not confined to:
1. Checking works in general accordance with BO and regulations
2. Checking works match approved plans (or MW submitted plans) and BA orders/conditions
3. Checking that **design assumptions agree with actual site conditions**

**SD takeaway:** Schemes with complex TW, deep ELS, temporary plant platforms, heritage loadbearing A&A, or sensitive adjacency pull **T4/T5 engineering** attendance. Price into consultant appointments at SD, not at tender. Detailed checklists (hoarding, monitoring, top-down, 4S, etc.) live in CoP Tables 5.1–5.4.

### 6. Works types requiring a Supervision Plan (§6.4)

Subject to §11 exemptions, SP required for:

#### 6.1 Building works

| # | Type | Typical SD trigger |
|---|---|---|
| (i) | Demolition | Any strip-out beyond §11 small-building thresholds |
| (ii) | Ground investigation field works | Boreholes, trial pits, instrumentation — RSC(GIFW) path in CoP |
| (iii) | Foundation | Piles, footings, rafts; designated-area uplift |
| (iv) | Site formation | Cut/fill, slopes, RW beyond §11 |
| (v) | Excavation and lateral support | Basements, cut-and-cover, ELS |
| (vi) | Cap / footing / basement | Substructure packages |
| (vii) | Superstructure | Tower / podium framing |
| (viii) | Curtain wall / cladding | Envelope packages (often separate consent) |
| (ix) | Addition and alteration | Structural A&A; heritage loadbearing |
| (x) | Slope / retaining wall / buried services repairs | Slope/RW maintenance + repair |
| (xi) | Tunnel works | Cavern / tunnel / associated |

#### 6.2 Street works

Street works require SP unless an exemption path applies (drainage-only works are separately exempt under §11; excavation depth/adjacency may still catch ELS rules).

#### 6.3 Complexity uplift (§6.1, §6.3)

- **Classes of supervision** = number + grades of TCPs + **frequency level** of inspection.
- **Minimum** grades and frequencies → **Table 1**.
- Number of TCPs and frequency **increase with complexity**.
- Complexity assessed on: types of works; construction and **span** of structure; **locality**; effect on adjacent buildings, structures, lands, streets and utilities.
- Method of assessment and extent of uplift → **CoP** (scale factor, critical stages).
- BA may review CoP details over time to reflect good practice (§6.3).

### 8. TCP grade qualifications — Table 2 (§6.2)

| Grade | Minimum qualifications and experience |
|---|---|
| **T1** | Certificate or diploma + **≥ 2 years** total relevant working experience (to BA satisfaction) |
| **T2** | Higher certificate or higher diploma + **≥ 3 years** |
| **T3** | Higher certificate/diploma + **≥ 5 years**, **or** degree + **≥ 2 years** |
| **T4** | Degree + **≥ 4 years**, **or** a **registered professional** |
| **T5** | **Registered professional** + **≥ 5 years** |

**Table 2 notes architects must know:**

| Note | Rule |
|---|---|
| (1) | “Relevant working experience” = experience BA recognises as relevant to that TCP grade under each stream. “Total relevant working experience” = aggregated by a BA-recognised method |
| (2) | A person who satisfies BA by training, experience and skill in **safety management** that they are fit and proper may be **deemed qualified** for a particular TCP grade for safety-management employment. Detail → CoP |
| (3) | For some grades, **absence of required quals may be compensated** by extra relevant experience per BA guidelines. Detail → CoP / PNAP / PNRC |
| (4) | Full detail of minimum quals/experience per grade → **CoP** |

**SD takeaway:** T5 = registered professional + 5 years — scarce resource for Case 3 TW certification path in CoP. Flag long-span / transfer / deep ELS schemes early.

### 9. Flexible deployment — combining TCP roles (§6.5–6.9)

#### 9.1 Within one stream (§6.5)

If more than one TCP is required under any stream, AP / RSE / RGE / RC may assign one or more TCPs to cover more than one role **provided**:
1. The appointed TCP satisfies the requirements of the **highest** grade specified; **and**
2. Inspections are undertaken at a frequency **no less than** that derived from the CoP and Table 1.

#### 9.2 Across AP / RSE / RGE streams (§6.6)

AP / RSE / RGE may appoint the **same TCP** to cover roles in two or three of those streams **provided**:
1. TCP satisfies grade requirements under **all** related streams;
2. TCP satisfies the **highest** grade specified; **and**
3. Combined inspection frequency ≥ CoP + Table 1 derived frequency.

#### 9.3 Hard firewall with RC stream (§6.7)

| Forbidden | |
|---|---|
| AP, RSE, RGE or their TCPs | **Cannot** combine supervision duties with TCPs within the **RC** stream |
| AS and RC TCPs | **Cannot** act as TCPs within AP, RSE or RGE streams |

#### 9.4 Combined Supervision Plan for multiple works types (§6.8)

- One SP may cover more than one type of building/street works **if** the respective plans have been approved by BA (or MW plans submitted under simplified requirements).
- Separate TCP roles may then be combined into one or more persons if quals/experience are satisfied and **combined inspection frequency ≥** that under the separate plans.

#### 9.5 Amended plan for additional same-type works (§6.9)

When an existing SP covers a type of works, and additional/new work of the **same type** is to be carried out under the **same** AP, RSE, RGE and AS, an **amended** SP may be submitted incorporating work currently supervised under one or more existing plans.

**SD takeaway:** Fee savings from “one person, many hats” only work **above** the RC line. Contractor full-time T1 is **not** absorbable by the AP’s T3/T4. Operational combining maths → CoP Form C.

**SD takeaway:** At SD you are not filling forms yet — but you **are** deciding works-type split, whether RGE is in play, scale order-of-magnitude (CoP basic values), and whether critical stages (transfer, top-down, heritage, deep foundation) will force full-time uplift. Those decisions determine Part I–IV headcount.

**CoP form map (operational handoff):** App. I Supervision Plan + TCP annex; Form A task records; Form B NC; Form C TCP combination; TW1–TW3 tower-crane support.

### 11. Deviations, emergencies, and retrospect notification (§9–10)

#### 11.1 Minor deviations notifiable in retrospect — TCP absence (§9)

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| TCP absent or likely absent due to **accident, illness or emergency** (§9.1) | Post replacement TCP of **equal or better** quals/experience within **48 hours**. Enter name + ID card no. in **site register** until further appointment or original returns. AP/RSE/RGE/AS notify BA within **7 days** |
| TCP absent for **other reasons** (§9.2) | Stream representative checks that site register entry explains circumstances and action to be taken |

#### 11.2 Proposed/actual deviations including emergency (§10)

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Urgent work, accident, emergency or other special circumstances make compliance with SP **impracticable** (§10.1) | May deviate for **maximum 48 hours**, then make arrangements **equivalent to or better than** original SP requirements. **Notify BA within 48 hours** after such work has commenced. AP/RSE/RGE/AS prepare **revised** SP as a result of the urgent works |
| Increase in number of TCPs (§10.2) | **Not** a deviation from a Supervision Plan |

**SD takeaway:** Programme risk contingency must assume 48-hour emergency windows and 7-day TCP-change amendments — not open-ended informal cover.

### 12. When NO Supervision Plan is required — §11 exemption thresholds

**Screening rule:** All bullets under a works type must be satisfied. Fail any one → SP required (subject to works type being in §6.4).

#### 12.1 Demolition — exempt only if **all** apply

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Scheduled areas | Building **not** in **Scheduled Area No. 1** (Fifth Schedule, BO) |
| Total height | ≤ **10 m** |
| Storey height | ≤ **5 m** |
| Structural span | Any structural element ≤ **6 m** |
| Cantilevers | No cantilever structures exceeding **1 m** span |
| Prestressed | No prestressed concrete elements |
| Retaining | Building not retaining ground level difference > **1.5 m** |
| Adjacency | No other buildings or streets within **5 m** |

**SD flips that kill exemption:** Mid-block party-wall sites (neighbours &lt; 5 m), any prestress, cantilever balconies &gt; 1 m, buildings in Area 1, storeys &gt; 5 m (common in older industrial).

#### 12.2 Site formation — exempt only if **all** apply

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Lot gradient | Max gradient across lot boundary-to-boundary ≤ **15°** |
| Wider gradient | Overall gradient of area bounded by lines **10 m** outside lot &lt; **15°** |
| Nearby slopes | No slope within 10 m outside lot steeper than **30°** or higher than **1.5 m** |
| Existing walls | No retaining/terrace wall on lot or within 10 m outside higher than **1.5 m** |
| New walls | No RW/terrace wall higher than **1.5 m** to be constructed |
| New slopes | No slope steeper than **30°** nor higher than **1.5 m** to be constructed |
| Combined | Combined height of RW + slope constructed ≤ **1.5 m** |
| RW proportion | Retaining height : breadth ≤ **2** |
| RW material | Retaining walls of **masonry or concrete** |

**SD flips:** Stepped hillside lots, podium cut into slope, any RW &gt; 1.5 m, gabion/other non-masonry/concrete walls.

#### 12.3 Excavation — exempt only if **both** apply

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Depth | Excavation depth **&lt; 2.5 m** |
| Influence zone | No roads, buildings, other structures, slopes steeper than **30°**, or water mains **≥ 75 mm** diameter within the profile described by a line drawn at **45°** to the horizontal from the perimeter of the **base** of excavation to the ground surface |

**SD flips:** Any basement, lift pit, or utilities trench ≥ 2.5 m; urban sites with roads/mains inside the 45° cone — almost always fails.

#### 12.4 Superstructure — exempt only if **all** apply

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Height | Total building height ≤ **10 m** |
| Span | Any structural element span ≤ **6 m** |
| Material | Structural elements constructed of **reinforced concrete** |
| Area | Construction floor area **&lt; 500 m²** |

**SD flips:** Steel/composite frames, span &gt; 6 m, CFA ≥ 500 m², height &gt; 10 m — even a small pavilion can fail on span or material alone.

#### 12.5 Categories always exempt under this TM §11

| Category | Rule | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **Drainage** | All drainage works | Excavation of significant depth still caught by excavation / ELS rules (TM note) |
| **Alteration and addition** | Work **not** requiring demolition or **structural** alteration | Non-structural fit-out only; touch structure → SP path |
| **Building repairs** | All building repairs | Distinct from structural A&A |
| **Minor works** | All **Class II** and all **Class III** MW | **Class I** still needs SP when required (Table 1 MW row) |

**SD takeaway:** A low-rise RC pavilion under 10 m / &lt;500 m² CFA with short spans can avoid SP. The same footprint with steel span &gt; 6 m, excavation ≥ 2.5 m near a road/main, or A&A that touches structure, flips back into Table 1 staffing. Screen adjacency (**5 m** / **45°** / **≥75 mm** mains / **10 m** slope-RW buffer) before locking basement, party-wall, or hillside strategies.

### 13. Reports — site supervision and non-conformity (§5.17–5.22)

Two report types are mandatory:

#### 13.1 Site supervision reports (§5.19–5.20)

| Who | When | Where filed | Must cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| **All TCPs** | Whenever they supervise on site; **daily** for full-time TCPs | **Site office** | (a) items inspected; (b) results; (c) notes of non-conformity |

#### 13.2 Non-conformity and rectification reports to BA (§5.21–5.22)

| Who | Trigger (either) |
|---|---|
| **AP** compiles, prepares and **submits to BA** when AP notes or is made aware of NC | **(a)** NC poses an **imminent danger**; **or** **(b)** NC causes a **material concern** for safety or quality **and** the RC **fails to comply** with the rectification instruction |

How NC is dealt with at TCP level → **CoP** (Form B workflow).

**SD takeaway:** AP appointment and PI risk allocation must expressly cover BA NC reporting. Site office filing systems are a contractual prelim item, not an afterthought.

### 14. What this TM deliberately leaves to the CoP — do not ignore at SD

These are **not** detailed in the TM text but are the operational consequences of §§4–6 principles. Budget and programme them at schematic design; full numbers live in the CoP summary:

| Topic | Why SD cares |
|---|---|
| **Scale factor** (measurable item ÷ basic value, **cap 2**) | Tower CFA ≫ 20 000 m², large CW area, heavy ELS/piling spend → more TCP headcount / higher frequency |

### 15. Schematic-design checklist (TM-driven)

1. **Classify every package** against §6.4 works types and §11 exemptions — never assume “small job = no SP”.
2. **Run adjacency screens:** Scheduled Area 1; demolition 5 m neighbours/streets; excavation 45° influence + ≥75 mm water mains; site formation 10 m / 15° / 1.5 m / 30° slope-RW buffers.
3. **Decide geotech intensity early:** significant geotech content / designated-area foundations pull RGE stream + heavier Table 1 rows (list → CoP).
4. **Budget baseline manpower from Table 1:** RC **T1 Level 5** default; foundation/geotech often **T1+T2 Level 5** + RSE/RGE stacks; AP typically T3 weekly + T4 monthly on major works.
5. **Appointments before SD freeze:** Name AP / RSE / RGE / likely RSC specialties (GIFW, Foundation, Site Formation, Demolition). If no RSE/RGE appointed, AP still staffs those TCP grades (§5.1.5).
6. **Respect the RC firewall (§6.7):** consultant streams cannot absorb contractor TCP duties.
7. **Programme gates:** SP with first consent (or Class I MW −7 days); revised plan on package change; TCP change in 7 days; emergency deviate max 48 hours + BA notify in 48 hours; TCP illness replacement in 48 hours.
8. **Sequence is TW (§3.1, §4.3):** top-down, ELS strike, demolition order, plant platforms = Supervision Plan content.
9. **AP BA reporting duty (§5.21):** imminent danger **or** material concern + RC fails rectification — write into AP scope.
10. **Handoff to CoP for numbers:** scale factor, critical stages, TW Cases, tower-crane TW, 4S, Form C, quality % gates — use the companion Architect Critical Summary for Site Supervision CoP.
