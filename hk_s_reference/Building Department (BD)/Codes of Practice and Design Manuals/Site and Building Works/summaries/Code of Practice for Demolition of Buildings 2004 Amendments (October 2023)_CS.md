# Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 — Amendments (October 2023)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
October 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This is the October 2023 amendment sheet to the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004. It adds interface protection between adjoining sites, guarding of openings, and a tighter exterior-column pull-down sequence. It is **not** the 2004 code and it does **not** repeat the September 2016 sheet.

## Regulatory Overview
The October 2023 changes are site-safety design constraints for demolition next to another active site, for floor openings, and for dropping an exterior column. Table 3.4 gains a propping case for a light mechanical plant, but the prop sizes on that table did not extract as text.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Adjoining sites (clause 3.8.8)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who designs | The Authorized Person and Registered Structural Engineer, to suit the site, in particular where the adjoining building is also under demolition or construction |
| Coordination | With the Authorized Person and Registered Structural Engineer of the adjoining site, to protect the public and site personnel |
| When the site changes | Review the measures. Submit amendment plans to the Buildings Department if necessary |
| Contractors | The Registered Specialist Contractor (Demolition) coordinates with the contractors of the adjacent sites so the measures match the stage of work |

**SD takeaway:** A demolition beside another live site needs a designed interface protection, agreed with the adjoining AP/RSE, and a plan amendment if that interface changes.

### 2. Openings, edges and debris shafts (clauses 3.8.9 and 3.8.10)

Railings follow the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations and must include:

| Element | Dimension |
|---|---|
| Top railing | **900 mm to 1150 mm** high |
| Intermediate railing | **450 mm to 600 mm** high |
| Toe board | **200 mm** high where there is no permanent upstand |
| Mesh | Brightly coloured safety mesh from the top railing down to the toe board |
| Opening covers | Solid, strong enough, fixed, and marked. Openings not in use are barricaded, with warning signs |
| If a cover is impracticable | Safety nets of suitable size and strength, kept clear of debris |
| If railings or covers are impracticable | Fall-arrest system, using the Labour Department guidance notes on safety belts and anchorages |

Debris through floors:

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Path | Only through openings with suitable chutes, full enclosures or shafts |
| Stockpile | Remove regularly so it does not affect structural integrity, access, fire risk, or health and safety |
| Designated area | Defined by the Registered Specialist Contractor (Demolition) and accepted by the AP/RSE. Enclosed so falling or rebounding debris cannot strike people. Fenced. Overhead tipping stops while debris is being cleared inside, and everyone is told |
| Chute | Adequate strength, fixed, fully enclosed at every entry, secured for the weight of the chute plus accumulated load, dust-tight, and able to minimise noise |
| Alternative | If any of those cannot be met, an alternative proposal accepted by the AP/RSE before debris is conveyed |

**SD takeaway:** Floor openings get a marked solid cover or a 900–1150 mm railing with a 200 mm toe board, and debris leaves the floor only inside a chute or shaft accepted by the AP/RSE.

### 3. Plant propping, exterior columns, and cantilever platforms (Table 3.4, clause 4.2.4(C), Appendices F and G)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Table 3.4 | Adds a propping requirement for light-weight mechanical plant of maximum **5 800 kg**. The prop sections and spacing are on the table graphic and did not extract as text |
| Exterior column | Tie **top and bottom** to a structural member with wires and winches (previously the top only), then pre-weaken and pull toward the interior |
| Figure 4.5 | Tie the column to the existing structure or an excavator arm; break out concrete and cut the exterior-half bars immediately before pulling; pull in a controlled motion |
| Cantilever temporary platforms | Required on the listed Appendix F and G figures unless the cantilever is demolished by cut-and-lift or another technique in clause 3.5.1(B) |
| Form BA20 | May be posted with the Hong Kong identity card number partly redacted: alphabet(s) and the first three digits only |

**SD takeaway:** An exterior column is tied at both ends before it is weakened, and a machine up to 5 800 kg needs the new Table 3.4 propping case read from the sheet, not assumed from the older table.
