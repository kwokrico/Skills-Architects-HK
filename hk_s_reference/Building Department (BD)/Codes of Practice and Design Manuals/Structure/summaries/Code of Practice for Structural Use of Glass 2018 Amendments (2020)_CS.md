# Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018 — Amendments (Annex)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Annex amendment (no month printed on the sheet) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This annex amends the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018 on structural-sealant retention, gaskets, and the glass-fin torsion symbols. It is **not** the 2018 code, and it is **not** the February 2024 sheet. The file name is the 2020 amendment; the sheet itself does not print a month.

## Regulatory Overview
Four-side structural-sealant glazing at 5 m or more above an accessible floor must be restrained at two opposing edges if the sealant bond fails. The retaining device and the glass are checked for 37% of the unadjusted wind reference pressure.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Structural sealant retention (clause 7.1.1)

The rule applies where the façade or glass element is fixed by structural sealant on **four sides**, and any point of the pane is **5 m or more** above the finished floor of the accessible area on either side.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bond failure | Design so the pane cannot fall |
| Retaining device | Feature capping, angle, bracket or insert, at **any two opposing edges** |
| Strength | Retaining device and the associated panes must resist **37%** of the design wind pressure on the pane, times a partial load factor of **1.0** |
| Design wind pressure | Wind reference pressure from the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, **without** adjustment factors |
| Self-weight | Still carried mechanically by setting blocks |

**SD takeaway:** Four-side structural silicone at or above 5 m needs retaining devices on two opposite edges, sized for 37% of the 2019 code reference pressure with no adjustment factors.

### 2. Gaskets and glass-fin torsion (clauses 7.5.1 and Annex C2)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gasket materials | Extruded silicone rubber, EPDM, or another material compatible with silicone sealant (neoprene, TPE) |
| Both sides | Gaskets on both sides of the glass pane unless it is structurally glazed |
| Engagement | Continuous mechanical engagement to the framing members |
| Glass fin G and E | **28,700 N/mm²** and **70,000 N/mm²** |
| Torsion J | For a solid rectangular fin, J uses depth d and thickness t. The text layer did not preserve a reliable transcription of the formula; use the printed equation |

**SD takeaway:** A non-structural glass pane still needs gaskets on both sides, and a glass fin uses G = 28,700 N/mm² and E = 70,000 N/mm².
