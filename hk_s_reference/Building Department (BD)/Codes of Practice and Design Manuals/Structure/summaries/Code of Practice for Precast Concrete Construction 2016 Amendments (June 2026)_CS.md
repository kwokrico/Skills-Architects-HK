# Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016 — Amendments (June 2026)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
June 2026 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This is the June 2026 amendment sheet to the Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016. It replaces the bearing-stress factors with a confinement-modified strength, and it sets a field water test for panel and window joints. It is **not** the 2016 code.

## Regulatory Overview
Bearing at a precast support is now a design ultimate strength fcb that depends on a confinement factor, not the old fixed fractions of fcu. Completed panel joints and window joints are water-tested to AAMA 501.2 on every joint.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Bearing strength (clause 2.7.9.4, Figure 2.5a)

fcb is the design ultimate bearing strength on the weaker of the two surfaces.

| Case | fcb |
|---|---|
| Dry bearing on concrete | **0.27 m fcu** |
| Bedded bearing on concrete | **0.40 m fcu** |
| Steel bearing plate cast in | **0.80 m fcu**, if each plate dimension does not exceed **40%** of the corresponding concrete dimension |
| Flexible bedding | An intermediate value between dry and bedded may be used |
| Confinement factor m | A2/A1, not greater than **2**. A1 is the loaded area; A2 is the load-distribution area (Figure 2.5a) |

The previous caps (0.4 fcu dry, 0.6 fcu bedded, 0.8 fcu for the plate) are replaced. Symbol fcb is added in clause 1.3.

**SD takeaway:** A precast bearing must use 0.27 m, 0.40 m or 0.80 m times fcu, with m not above 2, instead of the old 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8 fcu caps.

### 2. Water test of completed joints (clause 2.8.2.8, Table 2.7a)

On-site testing of completed joints between precast panels and in-situ elements, and of completed panels and installed windows, follows **AAMA 501.2** as Table 2.7a.

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Samples | **100%** |
| Nozzle pressure | **205 to 240 kPa** |
| Hose diameter | **19 mm** |
| Duration | **5 minutes** in every **1.5 m** of joint, nozzle moved slowly |
| Distance | **305 mm ± 25 mm** from the test section |
| Failure | Dampness or leakage on interior surfaces during the test or within **two hours** after |
| After a failure | Remedy the joint and retest every remedied joint until all meet the criteria |

**SD takeaway:** Every completed precast panel joint and window joint is hose-tested at 205–240 kPa, and any damp patch within two hours fails that joint.
