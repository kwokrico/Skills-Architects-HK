# Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 — Amendments (June 2023)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
June 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This is the June 2023 amendment sheet to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition). It changes two detailing rules only. It is **not** a restatement of the 2020 Edition, and it does **not** replace the February 2022 or April 2024 amendment sheets.

## Regulatory Overview
The June 2023 sheet adds a referenced test method for type 1 mechanical couplers and an alternative hook arrangement for beam links and ties. All other clauses of the 2020 Edition stay as previously amended.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Type 1 mechanical couplers (clause 3.2.8.3, Annex A)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use | A type 1 mechanical coupler may replace a tension or compression lap if the listed criteria are met |
| Elongation test | On a representative gauge-length assembly of the bar diameter, grade and profile, and the precise coupler type, permanent elongation after loading to 0.6fy must not exceed **0.1 mm** |
| Test method | Footnote added: reference may be made to clause 5.4 of **ISO 15835-2**, excluding sub-clause 5.4.4 |
| Annex A | Adds **ISO 15835-2:2018** (steels for the reinforcement of concrete — reinforcement couplers for mechanical splices of bars — Part 2: Test methods) |

**SD takeaway:** Specify type 1 couplers against ISO 15835-2 clause 5.4 (excluding 5.4.4) and keep the 0.1 mm permanent-elongation cap at 0.6fy.

### 2. Alternative beam links and ties (clause 9.9.1.3(b), Figure 9.6a)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Default anchorage | Hooks with a bend of not less than **135°**, in accordance with clause 8.5 |
| Alternative | Hooks of not less than **135°** at one end and **90°** at the other, alternated end for end along the longitudinal bars (Figure 9.6a) |
| Figure 9.6a | Shows the alternated links/ties and a single link/tie |
| Still forbidden | Anchorage by welded cross bars |

**SD takeaway:** If 135° hooks at both ends will not fit, use the Figure 9.6a alternated 135°/90° pair; do not close links with welded cross bars.
