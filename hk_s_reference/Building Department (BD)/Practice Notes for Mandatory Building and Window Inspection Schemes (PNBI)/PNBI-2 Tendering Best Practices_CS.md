# PNBI-2 Best Practices on Tendering Procedures for Engagement of RI QP and RC under MBIS and MWIS
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue December 2012 | Buildings Department (ICAC / HKHS / URA Maintenance Guidelines aligned)

> Scope note: Anti-tender-rigging and ethics rules for engaging **RI / QP / RC** under MBIS and MWIS. Not a design code — but it locks **fee, appointment and independence** constraints that affect A&A / façade-repair packages and Cap. 123P independence of RI from RC.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Applies when owners engage consultants and contractors for prescribed building/window inspection and repair under MBIS/MWIS. Engagement should normally go through **invitation to tender**, following HKHS/URA Maintenance Guidelines (「維修工程指引」, Chinese) developed with ICAC from Operation Building Bright experience. Read with **PNAP ADV-18 / PNRC 43** (Corruption Prevention). Conviction for corruption/fraud in building works may trigger **BO disciplinary action**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Default procurement mode

| Rule | Practice |
|---|---|
| Engagement of RI / QP / RC | Normally by **invitation to tender** |
| Repair tendering by RI/QP | Prefer **open tendering**; invite RCs with good performance records |
| Tender documents | Issued **on behalf of owners**; **do not disclose** RI/QP or company details on tender docs |
| Pre-assessment | RI/QP may declare in writing no connection with bidders |

---

### 2. RI / QP tender declarations (submit even if not asked)

At tender, RI/QP (and company) should declare they did/will **not**:

- Disclose tender price except to owner  
- Adjust price by arrangement with others  
- Agree on tendering with others  
- Commit fraud in the tender  
- Be involved in corruption / fraud offences on any projects  

On appointment, consider:

- Letter declaring conviction status (corruption / fraud)  
- Ethical commitment declaration: no advantages under **Prevention of Bribery Ordinance Cap. 201 s.2**; written conflict-of-interest disclosure + mitigation; no conflicting side work; protect confidential owner data  

Declarations may form part of the employment contract.

---

### 3. RC side — ethics clauses in the repair contract

| Action | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Tender | Integrity / anti-tender-rigging declaration (per Guidelines) |
| Contract | Incorporate ethical commitment clauses |
| On appointment | Declaration of compliance + conviction confirmation letter |

---

### 4. Hard statutory independence (do not design the team around a conflict)

Under **Building (Inspection and Repair) Regulation s.13(2)(c)**: within **14 days** after completion of prescribed building repair, the supervising RI must submit a certificate (specified form) confirming the RI is **not** a partner, director or authorized signatory of the RC who carried out the repair.

**SD / fee takeaway:** Same practice cannot be both RI-of-record for supervision and AS/partner of the repair contractor. Structure appointments and warranties accordingly when packaging façade / MBIS repair tenders with A&A.
