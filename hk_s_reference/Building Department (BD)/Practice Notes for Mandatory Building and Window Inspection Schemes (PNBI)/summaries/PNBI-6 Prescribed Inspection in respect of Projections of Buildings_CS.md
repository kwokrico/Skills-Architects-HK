# PNBI-6 Prescribed Inspection in respect of Projections of Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue May 2013 | Last revision April 2018 | This revision December 2019 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: How Cap. 123 **s.30B(3)/(4)/(5)** notices attach to **projections**, and what “unenclosed balcony / verandah” still means after illegal enclosure. **Highest SD value in the PNBI series** for massing, AC platforms, canopies, fins, balconies and ownership of repair liability.

---

## Regulatory Overview

BA may serve MBIS notices requiring prescribed inspection (and repair if needed) of building projections under s.30B. Notice recipient and inspection scope depend on whether the parent external wall is **common parts**, whether the projection is treated as part of an **external wall**, or whether it is an **unenclosed balcony/verandah** (or owner-built projection) under s.30B(5) / Cap. 123P B(I&R)R s.9(1).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Three statutory hooks — pick the right one at SD

| Hook | When it applies | Notice served on | Typical elements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **s.30B(3)** | Projection from an EW that is in **common parts** | All owners / IO | Canopies, planters, drying racks, window canopies, BSI support frames + pipes/ducts, architectural features, fins, eaves, cornices, mouldings, etc. |
| **s.30B(4)** | Projection from an EW that is **not** common parts | Owner of that **external wall** | Same element types as above |
| **s.30B(5)** | Unenclosed balcony / verandah connected to and used with a premises; also projections built by individual owners under the BO | Owner of the **individual premises** | Unenclosed balcony / verandah (as defined) |

**SD takeaway:** Same physical typology (e.g. canopy vs balcony) can land on different notice parties. Freeze DMC / ownership of EW and projections early.

---

### 2. Unenclosed balcony / verandah — definition that survives enclosure

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Statutory meaning | B(I&R)R **s.9(1)** “balcony” / “verandah” = those shown as **unenclosed** on the **original approved plans** |
| Later enclosure | Unenclosed balconies/verandahs **later enclosed** after occupation are **still treated as unenclosed** for s.30B(5) |
| Why | Weather-exposed; higher public-safety risk |

**SD takeaway:** Enclosing a balcony for kitchen/living expansion does **not** remove it from s.30B(5) MBIS projection inspection. Do not design illegal enclosure as a way out of projection liability.

---

### 3. What gets inspected on a s.30B(5) balcony / verandah

| In scope under s.30B(5) notice | Out of that notice (falls to EW inspection) |
|---|---|
| Floor | — |
| Ceiling | — |
| Metal railings / glass balustrades | — |
| **Internal** surface of solid parapets | **External** surface of solid parapets → s.30B(3) or (4) with the external wall |

---

### 4. Railings / balustrades-only notices

If **only** metal railings / glass balustrades of unenclosed balconies/verandahs need prescribed inspection/repair, BA may serve under **s.30B(4)** on the owners of those balconies/verandahs (not necessarily the full s.30B(5) package).

---

### 5. Owner-constructed projections

Projections constructed by individual owners in accordance with the BO may attract **s.30B(5)** notices on those owners — design AC platforms, drying racks, window canopies and similar for clear ownership + future RI access.

---

### 6. SD checklist for projections package

- Tag every projection: **common EW / private EW / balcony-verandah / owner-built**  
- Keep balconies/verandahs **structurally inspectable** (soffit, support, drainage, barriers) — see PNBI-7 SC8/SC9  
- Do not hide cantilever roots behind irremovable finishes if street-overhang canopies are proposed (PNBI-10 / CoP open-up rules)  
- Align protective-barrier detailing with APP-110 and CoP §5.3.6
