# PNBI-3 Specified Forms under MBIS and MWIS
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue December 2012 | Last revision April 2018 | This revision March 2019 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Mandatory **specified forms** under Cap. 123 s.22(4) for MBIS/MWIS notifications and certificates. Wrong or altered forms can **invalidate** the submission. At SD this is a programme / appointment checklist for existing-building and A&A packages that may trigger or overlap MBIS/MWIS.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Where the Buildings Ordinance or its regulations require an application, notice or certificate in a specified form for MBIS or MWIS, use **only** the forms in Appendix I — **do not alter or amend**. Download current versions from **bd.gov.hk**; prefer online submission or computer fill-in so the QR code stores the data. Hard copies also available at BD HQ, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. MBIS forms — map to stage

| Stage | Purpose | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Prescribed **inspection** | Notify appointment of RI for inspection and/or supervision of repair | **MBI 1** |
| Inspection | Certificate of building inspection — **repair required** | **MBI 3** |
| Inspection | Certificate of building inspection — **no repair required** | **MBI 3a** |
| Prescribed **repair** | Notify appointment of RI (inspection and/or repair supervision) | **MBI 1** |
| Repair | Nominate another RI to **temporarily** supervise repair | **MBI 2** |
| Repair | Certificate of building repair | **MBI 4** |
| Repair | Certificate of **no business connection** (RI who inspected did **not** supervise the completed repair) | **MBI 5** |

**SD takeaway:** If inspection RI ≠ repair-supervising RI, budget **MBI 5** and the split appointments early (links to PNBI-2 independence + Cap. 123P).

---

### 2. MWIS forms — same QP vs different QPs

#### (I) Same QP for inspection **and** repair supervision

| When | Purpose | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Within **14 days** after completion of inspection **or** repair | Notification of appointment of QP **and** certificate of prescribed inspection / repair of windows | **WI 1** |

#### (II) Different QPs for inspection vs repair supervision

| Stage | Deadline | Purpose | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspection | Within **7 days** after completion of inspection | Notify appointment + certificate of inspection | **WI 2** |
| Repair | Within **14 days** after completion of repair | Notify appointment + certificate of repair | **WI 3** |

**SD takeaway:** Split QP appointments compress the inspection certificate to a **7-day** clock — programme consultants and access windows accordingly.

---

### 3. Naming note vs older PNBIs

Older notes (PNBI-4 / PNBI-5 / early CoP text) refer to **Form MWI 1**. Current PNBI-3 (Mar 2019) uses **WI 1 / WI 2 / WI 3**. Always take the **live BD website** form number; do not freeze old labels into project QA checklists.

---

### 4. Practice rules that kill submissions

- Use the form listed for the exact purpose — no “adapted” house templates  
- Prefer QR-enabled computer fill / online submission  
- Keep forms under regular BD revision — re-download before each filing
