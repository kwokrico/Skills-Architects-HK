# PNBI-5 Representative of Qualified Person
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue December 2012 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: When the MWIS Qualified Person is a **company** (RGBC / RMWC), who must personally inspect the windows. Design implication is thin but appointment and Form fields are non-negotiable.

---

## Regulatory Overview

For Cap. 123 **s.30E(4)**: if the QP appointed for window inspection is **not a natural person**, a representative of the QP must carry out the prescribed inspection **personally** and comply with the BO. Under **Building (Inspection and Repair) Regulation s.10**, where the QP is an **RGBC** or **RMWC**, the prescribed representative is an **Authorized Signatory (AS)** of that contractor.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who may inspect when QP is a company

| QP entity | Who must inspect personally |
|---|---|
| Natural person (AP / RSE / RI / etc.) | The QP personally (see CoP / PNBI-10) |
| **RGBC** or **RMWC** (company) | An **Authorized Signatory** of that contractor (B(I&R)R s.10) |

---

### 2. Notification to BA

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| Initial appointment | Provide AS particulars on specified form (**Form MWI 1** in this PN; live form may be **WI** series — check bd.gov.hk / PNBI-3) |
| Change of AS | Submit a **new** Form MWI 1 (or current equivalent) reporting the change |

---

### 3. SD / fee proposal hooks

- If owners appoint a **contractor company** as QP, name the **AS** who will physically inspect — not a generic site agent  
- Align AS availability with unit-by-unit access programme (MWIS covers private premises windows)  
- Keep AS change control on the same form track as appointment
