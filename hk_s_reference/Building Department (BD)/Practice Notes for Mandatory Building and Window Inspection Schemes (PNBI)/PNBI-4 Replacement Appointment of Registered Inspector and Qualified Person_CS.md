# PNBI-4 Replacement Appointment of Registered Inspector and Qualified Person
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue December 2012 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Rules when an RI (MBIS) or QP (MWIS) **cannot continue**. Critical for programme continuity on long façade-repair jobs and for knowing who may / may not “hand over” mid-inspection.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Under Cap. 123 **ss.30D(8)–(10)** and **ss.30E(8)–(9)**, if the appointed RI or QP becomes unwilling or unable to act, the person for whom the prescribed inspection or repair is carried out **must appoint another** RI/QP. Temporary nomination is tightly limited: allowed only for RI **supervising repair**, not for inspection RI or for any QP role.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Full replacement (unwilling / unable)

| Action | Deadline / form |
|---|---|
| Appoint replacement RI / QP | Mandatory when original unwilling or unable |
| Notify BA | New appointment notification in specified form — **Form MBI 1** (RI) / **Form MWI 1** (QP) — within **7 days** after appointment date |

*(Current MWIS form labels may be WI series per PNBI-3 — confirm on bd.gov.hk.)*

---

### 2. What is **forbidden** — no temporary stand-in

| Role | Illness / absence from HK | Can nominate another? |
|---|---|---|
| RI appointed for **prescribed inspection** (s.30D(9)) | Temporarily unable | **No** |
| QP for **window inspection** or **supervision of window repair** (s.30E(9)) | Temporarily unable | **No** |

**SD takeaway:** Inspection-stage RI/QP absence = owners must **replace**, not cover with a nominee. Avoid single-person-dependent programmes for long inspections.

---

### 3. Only exception — RI supervising **building repair** (s.30D(10))

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who may nominate | RI appointed to **supervise prescribed repair** of buildings (nominator) |
| When | Temporarily unable due to **illness** or **absence from Hong Kong** |
| Nominee | Another RI — acts only for the period of illness/absence |
| Notify BA of nomination | **Form MBI 2** within **7 days** after nomination |
| If nomination ceases | Notify BA **in writing** within **7 days** after cessation |
| Chain nomination | Nominee **cannot** further nominate another RI |

---

### 4. Programme / contract hooks

- Put replacement and temporary-nomination clauses in RI/QP appointments aligned with ss.30D–30E  
- Keep MBI 1 / MBI 2 (and current WI forms) in the project document control matrix  
- Do not assume QP can “cover” for another QP during leave — statute blocks it
