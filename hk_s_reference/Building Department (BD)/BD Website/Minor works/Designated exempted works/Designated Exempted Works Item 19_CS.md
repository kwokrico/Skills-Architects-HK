## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **19**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any pole on a roof of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 19 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any pole on a roof of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| The height of the pole, including any feature at its top | **Not more than 1.1 m** |
| If there is no protective barrier with a height of **not less than 1.1 m** at any edge of the roof, the distance between any part of the pole and that edge | **More than 1.5 m** |
| For erection or alteration works—if there is **more than 1** pole on the roof, the distance between the poles | **At least 2.5 m** away from each other |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
