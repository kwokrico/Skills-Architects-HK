## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **15**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Removal of any drying rack projecting from the external wall of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 15 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Removal of any drying rack projecting from the external wall of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 750mm** |
| The distance between the highest point of the rack and the roof or ground | **Not more than 3m** |
| The rack projects over any street or common part of the building | No |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
