## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **12**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Removal of any supporting structure or metal casing for a building services installation (BSI), ventilation ducts or their associated supporting frames**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 12 |
| Nature of works | Removal of any supporting structure or metal casing for a building services installation (BSI), ventilation ducts or their associated supporting frames |
| Location of the supporting structure or metal casing for a building service installation (supporting structure) or any metal casing (casing) for such an installation | Located on-grade, on a canopy (other than a cantilevered slab) or on a roof (other than a cantilevered slab) of a building |
| Location of the ventilation duct (duct) or associated supporting frame (frame) | Located on-grade or on a roof (other than a cantilevered slab) of a building |
| The height of the supporting structure, duct or frame | **Not more than 1m** |
| If the supporting structure, casing, duct or frame is located on a roof of a building | the distance between any part of the structure, casing, duct or frame and that edge of the roof is **more than 1.5m**; or there is a protective barrier with a height of **not less than 1.1m** at the edge of the roof |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
