## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **26**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal supporting frame for the growing of plants that is fixed to an external wall of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 26 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal supporting frame for the growing of plants that is fixed to an external wall of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The maximum loading that the frame, together with any plants growing on it, adds to the wall | **Not more than 100** kg per m² of the wall area |
| The frame projects over any street or common part of the building | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 300 mm** |
| The distance between the highest point of the frame and the roof or ground | **Not more than 3 m** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
