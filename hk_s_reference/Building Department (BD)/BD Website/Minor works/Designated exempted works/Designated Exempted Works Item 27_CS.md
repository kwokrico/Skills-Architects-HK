## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **27**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal window security grille, or metal wind guard, for an opening on an external wall of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 27 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal window security grille, or metal wind guard, for an opening on an external wall of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The grille or guard projects over any street or a common part of the building | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 300mm** |
| The distance between the highest point of the grille or guard and the roof or ground | **Not more than 3m** |
| If the grille/guard is larger than the opening, the edge distance between the projection image of the grille or guard and the opening (not include the corners) | **Not more than 300mm** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
