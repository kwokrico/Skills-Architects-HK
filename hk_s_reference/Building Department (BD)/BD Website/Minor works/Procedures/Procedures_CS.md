In general, acknowledgement letters will be issued within **four working days**. For minor works submissions delivered by hand, BD will endeavour to issue the acknowledgement letters on the same day over the counter. For a batch of more than five submissions delivered after 4:00 p.m., acknowledgement letters will be issued on the next working day.
