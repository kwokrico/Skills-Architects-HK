## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.50**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, E**. BD files it under: Supporting structures for an air-conditioning unit, light fitting, antenna or transceiver located on-grade, on a canopy or on a roof; Supporting structures or metal casings for a building services installation (BSI) located on-grade, on a canopy or on a roof. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.50 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A E |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration |
| Location of the supporting structure or metal casing for a building services installation | Located on-grade or on a roof (other than a cantilevered slab and an inaccessible roof) of a building |
| The works involve alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| In the case of a supporting structure | the weight of the building service installation is **not more than 200**kg and **not more than 100**kg per m² of the ground or slab area the height of the structure for an antenna or transceiver is **not more than 2.5m** for other building service installation is **not more than 1.5m** |
| In the case of a metal casing | the weight of the casing is **not more than 10**% of the weight of the installation; and the inner surface of the casing and the installation are **not more than 200mm** apart in all directions |
