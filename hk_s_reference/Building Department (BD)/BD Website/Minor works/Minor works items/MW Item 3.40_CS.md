## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.40**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Partition walls inside flats. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.40 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A |
| Nature of works | Erection |
| Location of the block wall | In a non-domestic flat |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve the alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| Density of wall | **Not more than 650**kg per m 3 |
| Height of wall | **Not more than 3.5m** |
| Thickness of wall | **Not more than 100mm** |
| The thickness of the floor screeding of the floor slab supporting the wall | **Not more than 25mm** (measured from the structural floor level) |
| The aggregate length of any additional wall per m² of the floor area of the flat | **More than 0.2m** but **not more than 0.4m** |
