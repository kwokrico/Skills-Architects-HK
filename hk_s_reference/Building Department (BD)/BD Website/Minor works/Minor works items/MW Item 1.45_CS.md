## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class I** item **1.45**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Trellises. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 1.45 |
| Item class/type | Class I Type A |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration |
| Location of the trellis | Located on a roof of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve the alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| Height of the trellis | **Not more than 2.5m** |
| The length and width of every opening of the overhead framework of the trellis | **Not less than 200mm** |
| The horizontal clearance of the trellis from any other structure | **Not less than 500mm** |
| For works carried out in a common part of the building and at the completion of the works | Each trellis located in that common part covers an area of **not more than 20m²** ; and The aggregate of the area covered by each trellis located in that common part is **not more than 5**% of the total area of that part |
| For works carried out in a part that is not a common part of the building (non-common part) and at the completion of the works | The aggregate of the area covered by each trellis is: **not more than 20m²** ; and **not more than 5**% of the total area of that part |
| Other requirement | Not involve Minor Works item².45 |
