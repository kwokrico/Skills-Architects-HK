## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class I** item **1.28**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, E**. BD files it under: Supporting frames for an air-conditioning unit, light fitting, antenna or transceiver projecting from wall. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Removal**.
