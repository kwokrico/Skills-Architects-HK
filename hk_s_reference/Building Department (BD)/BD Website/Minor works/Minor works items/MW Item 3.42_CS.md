## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.42**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Thickening of floor slabs. Nature of works stated on the page: **Thickening of any floor slab in a flat by laying solid screeding**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.42 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A |
| Nature of works | Thickening of any floor slab in a flat by laying solid screeding |
| Location of the solid screeding | In a non-domestic flat |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| If any non-load bearing block wall is erected in the flat, aggregate length of any additional wall per m² of the floor area of flat | **Less than 0.2m**, and complied with the conditions in minor works item 3.40(a), (b), (c), (d) and (e) |
| Density of the screeding | **Not more than 1200**kg per m 3 |
| If the density of the screeding is **not more than 650**kg per m 3 | in the case that the aggregate area of the screeding is **not more than 2.5m²** within a floor area of **10m²** of the flat and the nearest horizontal distance between each of the area of screeding is **not less than 2m**, the thickness of the screeding is **more than 25mm** but **not more than 200mm** (measured from the structural floor level) in any other case, the thickness of the screeding is **more than 25mm** but **not more than 125mm** (measured from the structural floor level) |
| If the density of the screeding is **more than 650** kg per m 3 | in the case that the aggregate area of the screeding is **not more than 2m²** within a floor area of **10 m²** of the flat and the nearest horizontal distance between each of the area of screeding is **not less than 2m**, the thickness of the screeding is **more than 25mm** but **not more than 150mm** (as measured from the structural floor level) in any other case, the thickness of the screeding is **more than 25mm** but **not more than 75mm** (as measured from the structural floor level) |
