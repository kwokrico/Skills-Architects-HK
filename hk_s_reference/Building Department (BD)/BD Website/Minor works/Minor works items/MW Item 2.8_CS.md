## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class II** item **2.8**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Windows, window walls or curtain walls. Nature of works stated on the page: **Construction/Alteration/Repair/Replacement**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 2.8 |
| Item class/type | Class II Type A |
| Nature of works | Construction/Alteration/Repair/Replacement |
| Scope of works | The works involve windows or window walls |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| Span of structural element of the window or window wall | **Not more than 6m** |
| The works involve the alteration of any other structural elements (except a simply supported beam that - is not of pre-stressed construction; and is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam.) | No |
| For Construction or Alteration |  |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **more than 3.5m** but **not more than 100m** | The works involve the main frame of the window or window wall; or The works involve the sub-frame of the window or window wall, and the length of the sub-frame is **more than 1.2m** |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **more than 100m** | The area of the external wall opening for the window or window wall is **not more than 6m²** ; and The length or width (whichever is shorter) of the opening is **not more than 1.8m** |
| For Repair or Replacement |  |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **more than 3.5m** but **not more than 100m** | The works involve the main frame of the window or window wall; or The works involve the sub-frame of the window or window wall, and the length of the sub-frame is **more than 1.2m** |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **more than 100m** | the works are not carried out in accordance with the original design the area of the external wall opening for the window or window wall is **not more than 6m²** ; and the length or width (whichever is shorter) of the opening is **not more than 1.8m**; or the works are carried out in accordance with the original design |
