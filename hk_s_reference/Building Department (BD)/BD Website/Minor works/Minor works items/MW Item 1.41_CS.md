## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class I** item **1.41**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Subdivided flats. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection of any non-load bearing block wall, laying of any solid floor screeding, or erection or alteration of any aboveground drain in a domestic flat**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

| Item class/type | Class I Type A |
