## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.31**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, F**. BD files it under: External renderings, external wall tiles, roof finishes or external claddings. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Repair/Removal**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.31 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A F |
| Nature of works | Erection/Repair/Removal |
| Scope of works | The works involve any external cladding |
| The distance between any part of the cladding and the adjoining ground or adjoining roof | **Not more than 6m** |
| For an external cladding that is not fixed to an external wall of a building | Metal cladding only It is not fixed to any cantilevered slab If the cladding is above a roof The setback distance of the cladding from the edge of the roof is **more than 600mm** The roof is **not more than 20m** above ground |
