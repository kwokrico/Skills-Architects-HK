## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class I** item **1.62**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, F**. BD files it under: External renderings, external wall tiles, roof finishes or external claddings. Nature of works stated on the page: **Laying/Repair**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 1.62 |
| Item class/type | Class I Type A F |
| Nature of works | Laying/Repair |
| Scope of works | The works involve roof finishes of a building |
| The works involve alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| In the case of the laying or repair of roof finishes | The thickness of roof finishes is more than that in the original design |
| Other requirement | Not involve Designated Exempted Works item 7 or Minor Works item².34 |
