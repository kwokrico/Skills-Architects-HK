## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.26**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, E, G**. BD files it under: Removal of other structures (chimneys/architectural projections/racks); Canopies or retractable awnings; Supporting frames for an air- conditioning unit, light fitting, antenna or transceiver projecting from wall. Nature of works stated on the page: **Removal**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.26 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A E G |
| Nature of works | Removal |
| Scope of works | The works involve any architectural projection, canopy, supporting frame for an air-conditioning unit, light fitting or antenna or transceiver for public telecommunications services, or rack (other than a drying rack) |
| Location of the projection, canopy, frame or rack | Projecting from an external wall of a building or a fence wall |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 750mm** |
| Constructed of concrete | No |
| Other requirement | Not involve Designated Exempted Works item 13 or 14 |
