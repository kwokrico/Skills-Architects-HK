## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.38**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A, E**. BD files it under: Canopies or retractable awnings. Nature of works stated on the page: **Alteration**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.38 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A E |
| Nature of works | Alteration |
| Scope of works | Any unauthorised canopy projecting from the external wall of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| Projection from the wall | Immediately before the alteration, the canopy projects **more than 500mm** from the wall, but no part of the canopy projects **more than 750 mm** from the wall; Immediately after the alteration, no part of the canopy projects **more than 500mm** from the wall |
| Constructed of concrete | No |
| If the distance between the highest point of the canopy and the roof or ground is **not more than 3m** | The canopy cannot project over any street or common part of the building. |
