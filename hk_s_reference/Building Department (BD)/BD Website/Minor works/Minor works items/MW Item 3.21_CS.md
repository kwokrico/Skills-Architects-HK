## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.21**. Registered contractor types on the page: **C, G**. BD files it under: Signboard on or hung underneath balcony or canopy. Nature of works stated on the page: **Removal**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.21 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type C G |
| Nature of works | Removal |
| Scope of works | Located on or hung underneath the soffit of a balcony (other than a cantilevered slab) or canopy (other than a cantilevered slab) |
| If located on a balcony or canopy, the display area of the signboard | **Not more than 5m²** |
| If hung underneath the soffit of a balcony or canopy, the display area of the signboard | **Not more than 2m²** |
| The height of the signboard | **Not more than 1m** |
