### 2. Common Minor Works

- Windows, window walls or curtain walls
- Supporting structures/frames for building service installation
- Ventilation duct works or associated supporting frames
- Drainage
- Repair of structural elements
- External renderings/tiles, roof finishes or claddings
- Signboards

### 3. Structural Alteration or Removal works

- Removal of unauthorised structures
- Removal of other structures
- Protective barriers
- Floor openings
- Spread footings

**SD takeaway:** Removal of unauthorised structures Removal of other structures Protective barriers Floor openings Spread footings

### 4. Roof or External Works

- Canopies or retractable awnings
- Drying racks
- Non-load bearing external walls
- Solid fence walls
- Mesh fences or metal railings
- Poles
- Metal gates at building entrances/fence walls
- Water tanks
- Wind guards
- Accesses for maintenance
- Planters, ponds or fountains
- Trellises
- Routine maintenance of slopes, retaining walls or surface drains
- Excavation works

### 5. Subdivided Flat or Interior Works

- Subdivided flats
- Internal staircases
- Openings to staircases/lobbies
- Partition walls inside flats
- Thickening of floor slabs
- Panels fixed with metal dowels
- Lifts or lifting platforms
- Supporting frames for suspending air-conditioning plant or mechanical ventilation plant inside a building
- Ventilation duct works or associated supporting frames inside a building
- Fire dampers in ventilation system
