## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class III** item **3.6**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Windows, window walls or curtain walls. Nature of works stated on the page: **Construction/Alteration/Repair/Replacement**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.6 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A |
| Nature of works | Construction/Alteration/Repair/Replacement |
| Scope of works | The works involve windows or window walls |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground | **Not more than 100m** |
| The works involve the alteration of any other structural elements (except a simply supported beam that - is not of pre-stressed construction; and is not used to support any column, flat slab or ribbed beam.) | No |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **not more than 3.5m** | No structural element of the window or window wall has a span of **more than 6m** |
| If the distance between the highest point of the window or window wall and the ground is **more than 3.5m** but **not more than 100m** | The works involve the sub-frame of the window or window wall only; and The length of the sub-frame is **not more than 1.2m** |
