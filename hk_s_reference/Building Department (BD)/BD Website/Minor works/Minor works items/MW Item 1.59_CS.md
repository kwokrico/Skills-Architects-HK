## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class I** item **1.59**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Mesh fences or metal railings (on top of a solid fence wall and on-grade); Poles (on top of a solid fence wall and on-grade). Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 1.59 |
| Item class/type | Class I Type A |
| Nature of works | Erection |
| Scope of Works | The works involve any mesh fence,metal railing or pole |
| Location of the mesh fence, metal railing or pole | Located on top of a solid fence wall (other than an unauthorised solid fence wall) (wall) on-grade |
| The height of the wall alone | **Not more than 2.5m** |
| The aggregate height of the wall and the fence, railing or pole, including any feature at its top | **Not more than 10m** |
| Other requirement | Not involve Designated Exempted Works item 21 or Minor Works item².57 |
