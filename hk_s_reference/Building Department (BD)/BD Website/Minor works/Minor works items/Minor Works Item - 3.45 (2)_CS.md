### 2. Item details

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 3.45 |
| Item class/type | Class III Type A B D E |
| Nature of works | Formation of openings on an external non-load bearing block wall |
| Material of the wall | Block wall |
| The distance between the 2 points that are farthest away from each other within the area of the opening | **Not more than 150mm** |
| If the wall has 2 or more openings (whether before or after the formation works), the distance between each of the openings | **Not less than 150mm** |
| At the completion of the formation works, the number of openings within a wall area of **1m²** | **Not more than 3** openings |
