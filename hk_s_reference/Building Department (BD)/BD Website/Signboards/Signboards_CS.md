## Regulatory Overview

Taking into consideration that many of the existing unauthorised signboards are in active use in business operations and their existence carries notable value for sustaining local commercial activities and contributing to Hong Kong’s prosperity, the BD has introduced the Signboard Control System (SBCS) to control existing unauthorised signboards, including the introduction of Signboard Validation Scheme .
