## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What this page decides

- signboard that is fixed to the external wall of a building; and
- no part of which projects **more than 600 mm** from the wall.

If the wall signboard projects over a footpath, it shall have a clear space of **not less than 2.5m** beneath every part of it.

**SD takeaway:** signboard that is fixed to the external wall of a building; and no part of which projects **more than 600 mm** from the wall.

### 3. Erection/Alteration

- Not involve alteration of structural elements
- **≤ 600mm**
- **5m²** – **20m²**
- **10m²** – **40m²**
- Not consist of stone if any part of signboard **> 6m** from ground
