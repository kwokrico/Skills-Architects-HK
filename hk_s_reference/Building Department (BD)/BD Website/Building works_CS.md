## Regulatory Overview

The Buildings Department works with building professionals on plan approval and on audit checks during construction so that private building works comply with building standards. The menu splits that work into new building works, alterations and additions, minor works, signboards, and site monitoring.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. SD gate — which summary to open

| If schematic design is about | Open |
|---|---|
| New building, demolition, site formation, excavation and lateral support, foundations, consent, or occupation | [New building works](New%20building%20works/New%20building%20works_CS.md) |
| Extension, floor opening, unit combination, facade cladding, roof structure, balcony enclosure, open kitchen, common parts | [Alterations and additions](Alterations%20and%20additions/Alterations%20and%20additions_CS.md) |
| A scheduled small work, its class, or a designated exempted work | [Minor works](Minor%20works/Minor%20works_CS.md) and the matching item sheet |
| A new signboard or an existing unauthorised signboard | [Signboards](Signboards/Signboards_CS.md) |
| What BD inspects on a live site under approval and consent, or Class I minor works | [Site Monitoring](Site%20monitoring/Site%20Monitoring_CS.md) |
| Which specified form | [Minor works forms](Specified%20forms/Minor%20Works_CS.md), [signboard validation forms](Specified%20forms/Signboards%20Validation_CS.md), [new building / A&A forms](Specified%20forms/New%20Building%20Works%20Alterations%20and%20Additions%20Works_CS.md) |

**SD takeaway:** Lock the track before the works are drawn. This menu page does not state a size limit; the child summary does.
