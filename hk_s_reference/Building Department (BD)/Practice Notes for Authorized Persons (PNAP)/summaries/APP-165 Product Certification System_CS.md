# APP-165 Product Certification System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue September 2021 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: When a certificate of conformity under a product certification scheme may stand in place of a laboratory test or assessment report for two passive fire-protection products. It is not a general materials-acceptance note. A similar practice note has been issued to registered contractors. Completion paperwork remains PNAP APP-13, and audit records go to the Central Data Bank under PNAP ADM-20.

## Regulatory Overview
Product certification is a third-party certificate that a supplier’s product meets a specified Product Conformity Certification Scheme, with factory audit and testing by an independent accredited laboratory. The Building Authority accepts valid certificates for fire rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls under one named scheme as an alternative way to demonstrate compliance with the Buildings Ordinance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What a certificate is (paragraphs 1–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who issues it | A certification body accredited under the Hong Kong Certification Body Accreditation Scheme, or a Multilateral Recognition Arrangement or Mutual Recognition Agreement partner |
| Evaluation | Factory audit of production, and on-site selection of one or more samples representative of production, tested by an independent laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme or a mutual-recognition partner |
| Surveillance | Periodic surveillance audit of the plant and the Quality System Management Office, including audit compliance tests of randomly selected samples |
| Validity | A certificate is usually valid for 3 years. Re-certification is carried out as an initial audit on expiry. The certificate may be suspended or withdrawn if the manufacturer or product fails the scheme |
| Records | The authorized person should require the supplier to provide the relevant test reports of the products in paragraph 2, for record and for submission to the Buildings Department if necessary |

**SD takeaway:** Treat a product certificate as time-limited, usually 3 years, and keep the underlying test reports.

### 2. Doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partitions (paragraphs 4–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted certificates | Valid certificates of fire rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls under the Product Conformity Certification Scheme for Passive Fire Protection Products (Fire Door and Non-loadbearing Fire Partition) (Issue 3), issued by accredited certification bodies. The Hong Kong Institute of Steel Construction is the scheme owner |
| At completion | Submit the certificates with the Certificate and Schedule of Building Materials and Products under PNAP APP-13. Test or assessment reports for these products will not be required |
| Audit | The Buildings Department will randomly select materials and products with certificates from the Schedule for audit, then transfer certificate information to the Central Data Bank under PNAP ADM-20 |
| Other schemes | The Department is prepared to advise scheme owners on compliance with the Buildings Ordinance and is keeping other schemes under view |

**SD takeaway:** A current Issue 3 certificate can replace the laboratory report for a fire doorset or a non-loadbearing fire partition on the materials schedule.
