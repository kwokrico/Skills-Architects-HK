# APP-164

**Official title:** Enhanced Design Standards of Aboveground Drainage System

**In plain words:** This note sets design rules for floor drains, anti-syphonage pipes, branch discharge pipes, ventilating pipes, and rainwater pipes, issued under Buildings Ordinance section 28(1) until the Drainage Regulations are amended. It does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue April 2021. Last revision April 2023. This revision November 2023. Paragraph 9 was added and Appendix A was amended.
- In kitchens of domestic units, toilets, and pantries, divert used water from a lavatory basin, bath, or shower to refill the floor-drain trap seal, with no backflow. The trap must comply with Drainage Regulation 25.
- Pipes connecting the main anti-syphonage pipe to the drainage stack must slope upwards at 67.5° or less from the stack. Their internal diameter must be at least that of the main anti-syphonage pipe.
- Buildings of more than 5 storeys and up to 20 storeys: connections at intervals of 5 storeys or less. Buildings of more than 20 storeys: intervals of 3 storeys or less. Start from the lowest storey that has a branch discharge into the stack. For a podium or basement stack, count the podium or basement storeys.
- A connection is required below the lowest branch and above the bend of an offset. It is not required between offsets that have no branches.
- Restricted zone at every offset: no drainage connection within up to 600 mm above the bend invert for a building of 5 storeys or less, or within 1 000 mm for a building of more than 5 storeys, and within 2 500 mm horizontally from the bend.
- Sanitary fitments and the stack that discharges to a manhole. More than 3 storeys and up to 20 storeys: fitments on the discharge floor must not connect to that stack. Use separate pipework to the manhole. More than 20 storeys: the same rule covers the discharge floor and the floor above.
- Open ends of ventilating pipes at roof level must be at least 3 m from the common boundary, and at least 3 m from any window, opening, or fresh-air intake. Authorized persons (APs) are also advised to carry vents at least 3 m above accessible roofs.
- Advisory, aligned with the Drainage Services Department, for rainwater pipes of 100 mm internal diameter or less. Maximum horizontal roofed-over area: 40 mm pipe, 9 m²; 65 mm, 33 m²; 80 mm, 58 m²; 100 mm, 106 m².
- The Building Authority is prepared to consider favourably a modification of Drainage Regulation 30(2)(b)(ii) so the anti-syphonage connection to a branch soil or waste pipe is 750 mm or less from the trap outlet (Appendix A Figure 8).
- A new drainage plan, or a major revision, for a development or for alterations and additions (A&A), submitted for approval after 31 August 2021, must comply.
- Where superstructure consent is after 31 August 2021, paragraph 2(b), 2(c), and 2(f) must be met.
- Aboveground drainage under the Minor Works Control System started after 31 August 2021 must also comply.

**What to submit**
- Submit complying drainage plans for approvals sought after 31 August 2021.
- Where superstructure consent is after 31 August 2021, amend the drainage plans. Deferred minor amendments may follow PNAP ADM-19 paragraphs 22 to 24.
- Appendix B maps which paragraph 2 requirements apply to A&A and minor-works examples: a new floor drain, a new stack, a new offset, a new connection, a new fitment on the lowest or second-lowest floor, and a new or altered roof vent.

**Watch-outs**
- A new stack with the wrong anti-syphonage interval (every 5 storeys, or every 3 storeys), or with a connection inside an offset restricted zone.
- Connecting fitments on the lowest floor, or on the second-lowest floor of a building over 20 storeys, into the stack that discharges to a manhole.
- A roof vent less than 3 m from a boundary or an opening.
- Open hopper heads. Debris and vegetation block them. On a repair, consider a closed trapped system.
