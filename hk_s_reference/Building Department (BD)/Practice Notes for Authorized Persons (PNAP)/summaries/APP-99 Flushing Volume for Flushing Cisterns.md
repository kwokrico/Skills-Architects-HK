# APP-99

**Official title:** Flushing Volume for Flushing Cisterns

**In plain words:** A smaller flush than the default range can be accepted if one flush still clears the bowl and the Water Authority accepts the fitting. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 220. First issued May 2000. This revision February 2012.
- Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations regulation 19 sets the default water-closet cistern discharge at 9 to 14 litres.
- The Building Authority (BA) and the Water Authority do not object to a smaller design volume if it suits the bowl, clears the waste in a single flush, and the apparatus meets Water Authority requirements.
- Under regulation 91, regulations 19 to 23 do not apply to a soil fitment flushed with waterworks water. A reduced flush on waterworks supply therefore needs no modification of regulation 19. The Authorized Person (AP) must still comply with the Waterworks Ordinance, the Waterworks Regulations, and Water Authority circulars.
- If the flush water is not from the waterworks, the BA will consider a modification of regulation 19 for an individual water closet that meets those performance requirements.

**What to submit**
- Waterworks-fed low-flush: no Buildings Department modification. Comply with the Water Authority.
- Non-waterworks low-flush: apply to modify regulation 19, and show that the cistern and bowl perform and that the Water Authority’s requirements are met.

**Watch-outs**
- A regulation 19 modification is not required for a waterworks supply. It is required when the flush water is not from the waterworks.
- A low-flush cistern that does not match the bowl will not clear the waste and may be rejected by the Water Authority.
- A non-waterworks system without the Buildings Department modification is not accepted.
