# APP-143

**Official title:** Quality Control and Supervision of Precast Concrete Construction

**In plain words:** This note sets quality assurance, supervision, and audit for key precast concrete elements. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage, and modular integrated construction links to Joint Practice Note 8 are a separate APP-151 item (list item 38).

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 299. The note uses the Precast Concrete Code 2016 and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2021 Edition). APP-158 is the quality-control supervision frequency baseline for in-situ reinforced concrete. Minor non-structural blocks, architectural features, and planters are excluded.
- The factory must have ISO 9001 quality-assurance certification. That is a condition under Buildings Ordinance section 17(1) item 6.
- The quality assurance scheme covers material quality-control tests; laboratory equipment calibration; casting-yard equipment; the production process; testing procedures; inspection frequency and extent (in-house and independent HKAS certification or inspection bodies); and audit frequency and extent.
- The registered structural engineer’s quality control supervisor is at least a grade T3 technically competent person in the registered structural engineer stream, at least once a week.
- The registered contractor’s quality control coordinator is at least a grade T1 technically competent person in the registered contractor stream, with continuous supervision at the factory.
- Quality audits: the registered structural engineer and the authorised signatory visit in person for the first batch. After that, at least monthly. In-person visits at least quarterly. In the months between, videotelephony at 480p colour or better, recorded on a non-rewritable DVD-ROM, may be used.
- Alternative: the registered structural engineer visits the factory first, then audits delivered elements on site. Give the Building Authority 1 month’s prior written notice.
- A separate registered structural engineer is allowed for precast that does not affect parent-structure stability (façade or stair). Forms BA4 and BA5. The project registered structural engineer gives written acknowledgment. The separate registered structural engineer certifies under Building (Administration) Regulation 25(3).
- Submit the quality assurance scheme and the registered structural engineer’s confirmation at least 14 days before factory production starts. Submit the Precast Concrete Supervision Plan at least 14 days before production. Send each quality audit report to the Buildings Department within 14 days of the audit, including the first visit, and the DVD if videotelephony was used.

**What to submit**
- Quality assurance scheme and registered structural engineer confirmation at least 14 days before production.
- Precast Concrete Supervision Plan at least 14 days before production.
- Quality audit reports within 14 days of each audit, with the DVD where videotelephony was used.
- If there is a separate registered structural engineer: an assessment report on effects on the parent structure, and a statement by the project registered structural engineer on the connections. Forms BA4 and BA5.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not start production before the quality assurance scheme and supervision plan are submitted, and before the factory’s ISO 9001 status is confirmed.
- The authorised signatory and registered structural engineer must not skip audits or keep inadequate videotelephony records.
- Do not leave a gap between the project registered structural engineer and the separate registered structural engineer on connections that are pre-installed in the parent structure.
