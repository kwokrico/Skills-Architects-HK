# APP-35

**Official title:** Refuse Storage and Collection — Building (Refuse Storage and Material Recovery Chambers and Refuse Chutes) Regulations

**In plain words:** Refuse storage and material recovery rooms, chutes, and the path for the refuse collection vehicle (RCV) must meet the sizes below, and those areas may be left out of gross floor area (GFA) under Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) regulation 23(3)(b) when the design complies. Where a recovery room is not provided on every floor, each domestic floor needs a refuse storage room and the building needs a recycling corner, and the vehicle must still reach the chamber directly from the street.

**Numbers that matter**
- Worst-case refuse collection vehicle used by the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD): length 11 m; width 3 m from mirror to mirror; height 4 m; bin-lift headroom 4.5 m; ground clearance 0.24 m; departure angle 10 degrees; wall-to-wall turning circle 20 m; gross vehicle weight 26 tonnes; maximum gradient 1:10; access width preferably 4 m and at least 3.5 m; parking space 5 m by 12 m.
- If the vehicle cannot leave in one continuous forward movement: a hammer-head 27 m by 9.5 m, with an approach 10.9 m wide, or another layout based on those vehicle sizes (Appendix A).
- A refuse storage and material recovery chamber (RS&MRC) that the vehicle visits is on the ground floor (G/F) or a podium, with direct vehicular access from the street and no floors in between. Show the access and exit routes on the building plans.
- Submit calculations of the usable floor space and the aggregate, the minimum chamber floor space, and the proposed chamber size, using the Schedule to regulation 3.
- Fire protection of a refuse storage and material recovery room (RS&MRR): an alarm and sprinklers; walls with a fire resistance rating (FRR) of at least -/120/120; a door with an FRR of at least -/60/60; and a self-closer that cannot hold the door open. Layouts and dimensions are in Appendix B.
- Ventilation may be a central system with a single purifier before discharge, or a fan and a particulate filter in each chamber where there is no particular odour. The main exhaust is preferably at the upper roof, away from neighbours, or at the main chamber if taller buildings surround it. Follow the Noise Control Ordinance (Cap. 400) Technical Memorandum. Provide fire dampers where ducts or grilles serve each floor. Air-purifying devices are required in the refuse storage and material recovery chamber.
- Alternative to a recovery room on every floor, under regulation 3A, by modification if Appendix C is met and total usable floor space (TUFS) is at least 1320 m²: a refuse storage room (RSR) on each domestic floor, with a minimum dimension of 1 m and a floor area of 1.5 m², plus one central recycling corner (RC).
- Recycling corner area by the tower’s total usable floor space: Low, 1320 to 7260 m², gives 10 to 14 m²; Medium, 7260 to 13200 m², gives 14 to 16 m²; High, above 13200 m², gives 16 to 21 m².
- The recycling corner is at the main entrance and exit level, in the common parts or covered by a deed of mutual covenant (DMC) undertaking. Minimum dimension 1.5 m; manoeuvring space 1.5 m by 1.5 m; mechanical ventilation of at least 3 air changes per hour (ACH), plus purifying; lighting of at least 120 lux; the same fire resistance rating as a refuse storage and material recovery room; a water supply and a sink; a floor drain of at least 100 mm leading to a back-inlet trapped gully outside.
- Buildings under 1320 m² may share a recycling corner if the combined total usable floor space is at least 1320 m², the horizontal distance is at most 75 m, and the size follows Table 1.
- Under B(P)R regulation 23(3)(b), subject to satisfactory planning and to Appendix C where the alternative is used, gross floor area may exclude the material recovery chamber (MRC), the refuse storage and material recovery chamber, the refuse storage and material recovery room, refuse chutes, hopper rooms, and, on the alternative path, the refuse storage room and the recycling corner. The vehicle access geometry drives the ground-floor and podium layout.

**What to submit**
- Show the vehicle routes, the chamber-size calculations, and the fire and ventilation details.
- For the refuse storage room plus recycling corner, apply to modify regulation 3A, show compliance with Appendix C, and provide a common-parts or deed of mutual covenant undertaking where one is needed.

**Watch-outs**
- Vehicle manoeuvring space that is too small, or access that passes through intervening floors.
- Missing fire resistance, or a self-closer that can hold the door open.
- Claiming the gross floor area exclusion without a complying chamber design.
- A recycling corner that is too small for its total-usable-floor-space band, or that is not in the common parts.
- A shared recycling corner more than 75 m away.
