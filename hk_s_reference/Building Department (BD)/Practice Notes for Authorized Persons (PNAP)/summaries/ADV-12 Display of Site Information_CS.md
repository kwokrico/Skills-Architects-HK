# ADV-12 Display of Site Information
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision January 2005 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issue February 1996)

> Scope note: What to display on a building site so the public can contact the authorized person and the registered structural engineer. It is not a signage design code for the finished building.

## Regulatory Overview
The note asks for site identity and contact details in a prominent position so the public can report undesirable or unsafe conditions to the authorized person or registered structural engineer. Advertisement is not permitted on a hoarding or covered walkway, but Building (Planning) Regulation 66(4) does not object to basic development information on that hoarding or walkway.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What to display, and where (paragraphs 1–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Prominent position | Address and/or lot number; names of the authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered contractor; contact telephone numbers |
| On hoarding or covered walkway | No advertisement. Basic development information is acceptable: proposed works, use, height, number of blocks, and anticipated completion date |
| Elsewhere | Display within lot boundaries and do not interfere with site activities |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve a prominent board inside the lot, or on the hoarding within regulation 66(4), for the lot identity, the names of the authorized person, structural engineer, and contractor, and a telephone number.
