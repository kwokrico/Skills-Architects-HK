# APP-58

**Official title:** Testing of Drainage Works

**In plain words:** Drain tests must be done, recorded and endorsed on the programme even if the Building Authority is not on site, and the authorized person (AP) must send the certificate within 7 days. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but the tests often matter at the occupation-permit stage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations regulation 73. A matching contractor note is PNRC 11.
- Tests are certified as carried out in compliance with BS 8301:1985.
- Within 7 days of attending the test, the AP submits the “certificate on completion of drain test” (Annex I), with the endorsed results and record plans. Building (Administration) Regulations regulation 44 is also cited.
- On a large project, more than 100 tests is common. The AP must still keep endorsed records for every test.

**What to submit**
- Every drain-test application under regulation 73(1) must be copied to the AP at the same time.
- The AP, or the AP’s representative, attends and endorses the records and results.
- Send Annex I to the Building Authority within 7 days. Copy the registered contractor.

**Watch-outs**
- Attendance by the Building Authority does not reduce the AP’s or the contractor’s duty to meet the prescribed standard.
- Do not reach the occupation-permit stage and find the drains were never duly tested.
- If site changes to pipe runs, alignment or invert are so extensive that an amended plan is needed, the AP may not know unless someone from the AP attends.
- Do not let sectional testing on a large job leave gaps in the endorsed records.
