# ADV-18

**Official title:** Corruption Prevention

**In plain words:** Corruption cases have been rising, and they put compliance with the Buildings Ordinance at risk. This note asks authorized persons (APs) and registered structural engineers (RSEs) to forbid site staff from taking or offering advantages, and to report a bribe to the ICAC at once. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Corrupt supervision can still produce a building that does not match the approved plans.

**Numbers that matter**
- Under the Buildings Ordinance, the AP and the RSE give periodic supervision and the inspections needed so that the works match the Ordinance and the approved plans. Site staff often help with that.
- Expressly prohibit site staff from soliciting or accepting an advantage from a contractor or supplier, and from offering an advantage to anyone else in relation to the work. Write a company code of conduct and staff instructions.
- If you, the RSE, or a member of staff is offered a bribe, report it immediately to the ICAC on 2526 6366, which is open 24 hours.
- “Advantage” in the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) includes, as Appendix I sets out: a gift, loan, fee, reward, or commission of money or property; an office, employment, or contract; payment, release, or discharge of a liability; any other service or favour, other than entertainment, including protection from a penalty or from disciplinary, civil, or criminal action; the exercise or forbearance of a right, power, or duty; and any offer, undertaking, or promise of those things.
- The ICAC Advisory Services Group gives free confidential advice and helps with codes of conduct. The hotline is 2526 6363.

**What to submit**
- No Buildings Department form. Keep records of site inspections and of the staff instructions. A report to the ICAC is outside the plan-approval system.

**Watch-outs**
- A site staff member who accepts a favour from a contractor, and defective work that does not match the approved plans is then concealed, is both a Cap. 201 offence and a failure of supervision under the Buildings Ordinance.
- Having no written code, and no written prohibition, is a weak position if an advantage is accepted under your watch.
- Cash is not the only bribe. Cap. 201 also covers employment, loans, favours, and forbearance.
