# APP-168

**Official title:** Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011

**In plain words:** Design structural steelwork to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel, 2023 Edition, as amended in April 2026, which takes effect immediately. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue March 2023. This revision April 2026. Appendix A is the April 2026 amendment set.
- Earlier amendments of November 2016, May 2021, and March 2023 are summarised in the Buildings Department old-versions package.
- The April 2026 subjects below are among those amended. The full text is in Appendix A and in the Steel Code 2023 Edition as amended.
- Clause 3.1.3: yield strength (Ys) and ultimate strength (Us) for ultra-high-strength steel above 690 N/mm², by reference to clause 3.1.2.
- Clause 3.1.5: a duplicated paragraph is deleted.
- Clause 3.4: welding of different steel grades is clarified.
- Clause 5.3.4: building acceleration limits are revised.
- Equations 9.9 and 9.10: a lap-joint typographical error, and a clarification of net cross-sectional area.
- Clause 10.1.2 and Table 10.1: the short-term elastic modulus of concrete is added.
- Figure 10.17: composite column classification.
- Clause 10.5.1(3): allowable concrete and steel grades in composite columns are revised.
- Clause 10.5.2(5): quality control for concrete-filled steel tube (CFST) composite columns.
- Table 10.11: geometric ratios.
- Clause 10.5.3.2: design strengths.
- Table 10.13: buckling curves and imperfections.
- Clause 10.5.3.3: buckling strength checks.
- Clause 13.6.4: footbridge vibration response.
- Clauses 14.3.2 and 14.3.3, Table 14.2b, and Annex A1.4.2.2: mechanised and automatic welding, and the definition of carbon equivalent.
- Annex A2.5: a vibration reference for steel-framed systems.

**What to submit**
- Design to the Steel Code 2023 Edition as amended in April 2026.
- The composite-column and welding quality-control updates may change the supervision records and documents expected at consent and at completion.

**Watch-outs**
- Designing a CFST or other composite column, or ultra-high-strength steel, to the clauses from before April 2026, without checking the revised strengths, geometric limits, buckling curves, and concrete quality control.
- Ignoring the new acceptance standards for mechanised and automatic welding, and the footbridge vibration criteria.
