# APP-123

**Official title:** Alternative Designs — Paragraph 12.3 of the Code of Practice for Fire Resistance Construction 1996 (FRC Code)

**In plain words:** Paragraph 12.3 of the 1996 Code of Practice for Fire Resistance Construction normally separates an external wall from the floor below by a spandrel at least 900 mm high, and where that vertical separation is not practicable a horizontal apron 900 mm wide may be used, with the Building Authority (BA) prepared to waive paragraph 12.3(a) for a balcony open on at least two sides. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), though the apron does affect the building’s bulk.

**Numbers that matter**
- The spandrel must be at least 900 mm high, or the apron must project 900 mm.
- The waiver is for balconies open on at least two sides, described as ventilated balconies with a lower fire risk. The rest of the unit must still fully meet the paragraph 12.3 separation.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued October 2001.
- File reference: BD GP/BREG/C/19/L (VII).

**What to submit**
- Show either the 900 mm spandrel or the 900 mm apron.
- For an open balcony, show that it is open on at least two sides, and show that the remainder of the unit still complies.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not claim the balcony waiver for a balcony that is not open on at least two sides.
- Do not ignore the separation for the part of the unit that is not the balcony.
