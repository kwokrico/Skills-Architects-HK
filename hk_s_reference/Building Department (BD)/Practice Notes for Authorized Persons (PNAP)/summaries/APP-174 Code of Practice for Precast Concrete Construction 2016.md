# APP-174

**Official title:** Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016

**In plain words:** Precast concrete design, connections, and site water tests must follow the Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016 as amended by Appendix A (November 2020) and Appendix B (June 2026). This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue of this practice note: June 2026. The amendments are on the Buildings Department website.
- November 2020 (Appendix A):
  - Clause 2.4.3: corrosion cover follows the Concrete Code 2013, not the Building (Construction) Regulations alone.
  - Clause 2.4.4.1: the connection fill must also stop water seepage.
  - Clause 2.6.1: materials must follow the Building (Construction) Regulations and the Concrete Code 2013.
  - Clause 2.6.2.1, alkali-silica reaction (ASR): take expert advice before using alkali-reactive aggregates; use non-reactive aggregate under CS1; reduce moisture getting in; keep equivalent sodium oxide (Na₂O) at or below 3.0 kg/m³, with the supplier’s mix design and Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) certificates.
- June 2026 (Appendix B):
  - New Table 2.7a: on-site water-tightness tests of completed panel joints and window joints under AAMA 501.2. Test 100% of the samples. Nozzle pressure 205 to 240 kPa. Hose diameter 19 mm. 5 minutes per 1.5 m of joint. Nozzle distance 305 mm, plus or minus 25 mm. Failure is dampness or leakage on the interior during the test, or within 2 hours after it. Remedy the failed joints and retest them.
  - Clause 2.7.9.4, ultimate bearing stress f_cb, where f_cu is the concrete cube strength and m is the confinement factor: f_cb = 0.27 × m × f_cu (dry); 0.40 × m × f_cu (bedded); 0.80 × m × f_cu where a steel plate is not more than 40% of the corresponding concrete dimension. m = √(A₂/A₁), and m is not more than 2 (new Figure 2.5a). The symbol f_cb is added.
  - Clause 2.8.2.8 is rewritten so completed joints must be tested to AAMA 501.2.
  - Annex A references are updated to AAMA 501.1, 501.2, and 501.3; ASTM C719, C794, C920, C1087, E283, E330, E331, and E547; ANSI Z97.1; and BS 6213.

**What to submit**
- Design precast bearing and connections to amended clause 2.7.9.4.
- Plan and demonstrate the AAMA 501.2 field test on 100% of completed panel joints and window joints, including the failure rule, the remedy, and the retest.
- Keep the HOKLAS ASR package where alkali control applies.

**Watch-outs**
- Using the old bearing stresses of 0.4, 0.6, and 0.8 times f_cu, without the confinement factor m.
- Describing a hose spray without the AAMA 501.2 pressure, hose size, time, distance, 100% joint coverage, and the 2-hour observation after the test.
- ASR control with no documentation of the 3.0 kg/m³ reactive-alkali limit.
