# APP-19

**Official title:** Projections in relation to Site Coverage and Plot Ratio — Building (Planning) Regulations 20 & 21

**In plain words:** This note sets which projecting features can be left out of site coverage (SC), plot ratio (PR), and gross floor area (GFA), including projecting windows, tall overhangs, and common greenery. Planters can be exempt without PNAP APP-151 (the practice note on GFA concession preconditions and the overall cap); a maintenance walkway concession is subject to those APP-151 preconditions and the cap.

**Numbers that matter**
- SC includes every building component, including projections, because it controls bulk. A projection that is not at floor level, and that does not dominate the facade, need not be GFA.
- The following may be disregarded for SC and PR if they are not exaggerated. Pitched eaves and flat-roof overhangs under Building (Planning) Regulation 7(1), not a parapet used as an accessible roof. An individual air-conditioning box or platform not more than 750 mm, with built-in condensate disposal. Air-conditioning platforms that follow Appendices B and C of the Code of Practice for Access for External Maintenance. Window hoods and porches projecting not more than 2 m and complying with the Building (Planning) Regulations. Window cills and surrounds not more than 100 mm. String courses, fins, and mouldings under Regulation 7(1), and not structural beams or columns. Window flower boxes not more than 500 mm (Diagram 1). Drainage pipes and gutters under Regulation 7(2). Sunshades for energy conservation not more than 1.5 m (APP-67 and APP-156). Reflectors not more than 1.5 m, with a quantitative environmental assessment if the projection is more than 0.5 m. Entrance canopies not more than 3.5 m. Drying racks, light fittings, and telecom frames under Regulation 7(3). Retractable awnings under Regulation 7(4) and minor-works item 2.43. Plant support frames not more than 300 mm and within the lot (Diagram 2).
- A projecting window is not GFA, SC, or PR if every condition is met: it opens from a living room, dining room, or bedroom only; one per room and on one wall only; its area on the elevation is not more than 50% of that external wall; it projects not more than 100 mm; its base is at least 500 mm above finished floor level; it has a protective barrier under Regulation 3A (1,100 mm from the floor, and the window is fixed or protected within that height); it is not a piecemeal addition to an existing building.
- The covered area under an overhang is not GFA if the clear height is more than 7.5 m, it gives no weather-protected usable shelter, access is impossible or abuse is unlikely, the ratio of covered width to clear height is at least 1:8, and no department objects. Leave out shopfront overheads and signboards. A special form that misses these tests can be justified on design merits (see APP-2).
- Projections over a street are normally refused, except under Part II of the Building (Planning) Regulations or where there is a public interest. An individual air-conditioning unit not more than 750 mm, or a window hood not more than 500 mm, may be permitted if it is at least 2.5 m above the ground (APP-42). In a wholesale conversion of an industrial building, an air-conditioning unit of more than 750 mm, or another street projection, may be looked on favourably where the layout is constrained, with Grade 1 room air-conditioning under the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department energy-labelling code imposed as a condition.
- Common greenery inside the lot: a planter not more than 600 mm (Diagram 3), with the external-maintenance code’s safety provisions, automatic irrigation, and drainage, may be exempted from GFA and SC without the APP-151 preconditions or cap. A maintenance walkway 400 to 600 mm wide, with a drop of at least 150 mm and the code’s safety provisions, may get a GFA concession that is subject to the APP-151 preconditions and the overall cap. A combined walkway and planter projecting more than 750 mm obstructs prescribed windows. A deed of mutual covenant, or a sale and purchase agreement, undertaking is registered at the Land Registry before the occupation permit (OP).

**What to submit**
- Show drainage for cantilevered reinforced-concrete structures to APP-68 on the general building plans and on the drainage plans.
- For greenery, the developer undertakes that the features are common parts, and that undertaking is registered at the Land Registry before the OP.
- A street air-conditioning exception needs the justification, and compliance with the Grade 1 energy-label condition.

**Watch-outs**
- A “flower box” that is really a structural beam is counted in SC and PR.
- A projecting window that misses the 50% area limit, the 100 mm projection, or the 500 mm height above the floor is counted.
- A maintenance walkway is not an APP-151-free concession.
- A combined greenery projection of more than 750 mm affects prescribed windows.
