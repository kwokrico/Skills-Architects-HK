# APP-110 Protective Barriers
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision January 2026 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 235; first issue September 2000)

> Scope note: Design and plan submission for protective barriers required by sections 36 and 37 of the Building (Construction) Regulation, regulation 3A of the Building (Planning) Regulations, and the design rules in sections 9 and 38 of the Building (Construction) Regulation. Inaccessible roofs and maintenance-only areas are outside these barrier rules, but the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance 2021 and section 6 of the Occupational Safety and Health Regulation still apply. Open stair-wells are cross-referred to PNAP APP-119.

## Regulatory Overview
Barriers are required at the edge of a balcony, verandah, floor, roof, staircase, landing or projection, and at every opening in an external wall above ground floor, to stop persons or objects passing through or toppling to a lower level. The note fixes height, gap, curb, and public-entertainment geometry, and what must appear on structural or superstructure plans.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Height, solid base, and gaps (paragraphs 2–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Function | Prevent a person or object from falling, rolling, sliding or slipping through a gap, and prevent climbing over |
| Height | Not less than 1.1 m, measured from the finished floor level of the surface adjoining the barrier where people could step on |
| Solid base | Lowest 150 mm built solid, except staircases enclosed with walls and without an open stair-well |
| Openings | Inhibit passage of particles more than 100 mm in their smallest dimension |
| Curb not treated as floor | The curb is higher than 500 mm; or, for a non-railing barrier, the protruding width is less than 75 mm; or, for a railing barrier with a curb less than 500 mm high, the top of the lowest horizontal rail is not more than 250 mm above the adjoining floor, or, if there are only vertical balustrades and no bottom rail, the curb is not more than 250 mm high with protruding width less than 75 mm |
| Protruding width | Measured horizontally from the inner finished surface of the barrier to the inner side of the finished curb, excluding a splay steeper than 45 degrees to the horizontal |

**SD takeaway:** Set every barrier at not less than 1.1 m from the step-on surface, with a 150 mm solid toe and no opening that passes a 100 mm particle.

### 2. Places of public entertainment, structure, and glass (paragraphs 4–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Upper tier | Any horizontal surface inclined towards the seating at 30 degrees and concave in section, or otherwise so that only small articles can be placed on it and articles are not liable to fall to a tier or area below |
| Loads | Resist imposed loads prescribed in section 9 of the Building (Construction) Regulation, and comply with the relevant codes |
| Glass | Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018, including typical clamping for free-standing glass barriers. Tempered glass, structural sealant, and spider fixing also follow PNAP APP-37 |
| Inaccessible roof or maintenance-only area | The barrier rules above do not apply. Still observe the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance 2021 and section 6 of the Occupational Safety and Health Regulation |

**SD takeaway:** Slope the outer edge of an upper tier in a place of public entertainment at 30 degrees towards the seats, and design glass barriers to the 2018 glass code.

### 3. What to show, and minor works (paragraphs 8–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Structural plans | Framing and element details; anchors and supports; design loads, standards and codes; material specifications; workmanship for connections and protection; calculations covering the parent structure, adequacy and stability, element design, and deflection of major members if applicable |
| Streamlined route | Typical details, layout, and general notes may sit in the superstructure plans. Location, layout, and elevation still go on the general building plans. Works must match the approved general building plans. Checklist at Appendix B5 and samples at Appendix B8 of PNAP ADV-33 |
| Supervision | A suitably qualified person supervises construction to the approved plans |
| Minor works | Certain erection, alteration, repair, replacement, or removal of a barrier may be minor works under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation and PNAP APP-147 |

**SD takeaway:** Put barrier location and elevation on the general building plans, and either submit structural barrier plans or embed the paragraph 8 details in the superstructure plans.
