# APP-23 Hoardings, Covered Walkways and Gantries
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2025 | Buildings Department (first issue February 1981)

> Scope note: Hoardings, covered walkways, gantries, and the catch platforms on them, under Part IX of the Building (Planning) Regulations, including temporary construction-vehicle access. A permit under regulation 64 is obtained before they are erected, and regulation 66 requires them to be erected before building works start and kept in repair for the life of the permit. Demolition follows the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 and Appendix C. Other building works follow Appendix D and the design requirements in Appendix C. One set of plans is enough if the application is made through the Electronic Submission Hub. Otherwise six sets are required.

## Regulatory Overview
The plans show the hoarding, any temporary vehicular access and post holes, existing traffic signs and traffic provisions, planters, and trees, and they declare any sign or street light that will be obscured or resited and any effect on public transport, pedestrian crossings, or carriageway width. A traffic lane kept open is at least 3 m wide for one-way traffic and a single carriageway for two-way traffic is at least 5.5 m, and if those widths cannot be met on a traffic-sensitive road, or within 70 m of a signalised junction with such a road, a traffic impact assessment is normally required at least two months before the permit application.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Pedestrian width, lighting, and the carriageway edge (paragraphs 5 and 9–15 and Appendix A)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Existing pavement 2.5 m or less | Clear walkway normally at least 1.5 m. A narrower width may be considered if pedestrian level of service C is maintained, and in any case not less than 1.1 m |
| Existing pavement over 2.5 m to 3 m | Clear width equal to the pavement width minus 1 m |
| Existing pavement over 3 m | Clear width not less than 2 m |
| On a carriageway | Clear pedestrian walkway at least 1.1 m. Clear width of at least 500 mm between the hoarding edge and the carriageway. Drainage channels and gullies are not obstructed |
| Inside the covered walkway | Vertical clearance at least 2.3 m. The clear width is not obstructed by trees, planters, signs, scaffold poles, formwork supports, or gantry gates |
| Lighting | Average illuminance on the floor between 35 lux and 50 lux. A recommended layout for a walkway 2 m wide and 2.5 m high is 18 W or 20 W tubular fluorescent lamps, 600 mm long, at 3 m spacing |
| Utility pits | The undertaking is told before construction. Footings do not obstruct opening the cover |
| Temporary vehicle access | Position agreed by the Transport Department. Formed to the Highways Department standard run-in, PNAP APP-144, or Annex 1 to Appendix A, under an excavation permit, and reinstated by the owner. No reversing of construction traffic except into a small or narrow site |
| Trees on government land | No felling, pruning, or interference without approval. Preservation, protection, and monitoring go to the tree maintenance department before the permit application. Damage is reported the same day. A gap left for a tree is itself protected against falling objects |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a covered walkway at least 2.3 m high and at least 1.1 m clear, widen that clear width with the pavement as the table requires, and leave 500 mm between a hoarding and the carriageway.

### 2. Structure of the hoarding and catch platform (Appendix C)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Catch platform next to a street | Width not less than 2 m, reducible by the building's setback. If it enters the 500 mm zone at the carriageway edge, raise it to keep 5,500 mm headroom over the carriageway |
| Service lane 3.5 m wide or less | A single deck that also acts as the catch platform is acceptable. Structural justification is not required if the standard-design parameters are followed strictly |
| Wind | 37% of the wind reference pressure in clause 3.2 of the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, under clause 2.5, without directionality or sheltering adjustments |
| Deck loads | Covered-walkway roof: uniformly distributed load of 5 kPa. Catch-platform deck: 5 kPa, or a point load of 20 kN on an effective area of 300 mm by 300 mm |
| Reused steel | Checked to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011 (2023 Edition) |
| Standard designs | Annex 1, including precast-concrete footings and demountable steel connections. A variation may be accepted for the site. No contractor shed rests on the hoarding, walkway, gantry, or catch platform |
| Demolition on a narrow pavement | May have to stand on the carriageway, and then a raised floor is required. Other building works are normally entirely on the footpath, without a raised floor |

**SD takeaway:** Design the catch platform at least 2 m wide for 5 kPa or a 20 kN point load, and hold 5.5 m headroom if it overhangs the carriageway edge zone.
