# ADM-19 Building Approval Process
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2025 | Buildings Department (first issue July 2002)

> Scope note: The curtailed-check approval process, staged general building plans, and concurrent consent for defined classes of work. Dimensional fire, barrier-free, and structural rules sit in the codes and the APP notes, not in this process note.

## Regulatory Overview
The general building plan is the layout that every other plan must follow, and approval of a structural, drainage, or site-formation plan is not approval of a different layout shown on that plan. For general building plans, superstructure plans, and drainage plans the Buildings Department checks fundamental issues only, and the authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered geotechnical engineer remain responsible for everything the curtailed check does not examine.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. One layout, and what is actually checked (paragraphs 2–13)
| Plan | What the Department checks |
| --- | --- |
| General building plan | Density, safety, health, environment, and major issues under allied legislation (Appendix A). The layout on this plan prevails |
| Superstructure, including secondary elements that do not affect overall stability (curtain wall, window wall, cladding, space frame, precast element, canopy, skylight, balustrade, external ceiling, grille or louvre, ventilation duct, maintenance platform, and similar) | Master framing plans, notes, design loads, design methods, material standards, design codes, and fire-resistance rating (Appendix B) |
| Drainage | Disposal system, underground drain layout, and access for maintenance. Only a percentage of the drainage is randomly selected (Appendix C) |
| Demolition, site formation, foundation, excavation and lateral support | Appendix D |

Non-fundamental issues are not disapproval items. They must still comply before works start, and any contravention found after commencement must be rectified before completion is certified. Some submissions are audited in detail at random. Checklists are in PNAP ADV-33.

**SD takeaway:** Lock the general building plan layout first; later structural and drainage plans must match it, and items outside the curtailed check are still the designer's duty before consent.

### 2. Three stages for a new building (paragraphs 14–20)
Applies to new building developments, not alteration and addition, whose first general building plan or major revision is submitted after 31 March 2023. Deferred items are listed in Appendix A1 of PNAP ADV-33.

| Stage | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | First approval of the general building plan | Only Stage I items are checked, even if later-stage information is drawn. Approval covers Stage I only |
| II | Before consent to commence superstructure | A full set incorporating Stages I and II is required by a regulation 10 condition. Mark each plan at the lower right: "This set of plans is submitted for Stage II Curtailed Check" |
| III | Before occupation permit or temporary occupation permit | Obtain approval of the Stage III amendment before that application |

Regulation 30(3)(c) of the Building (Administration) Regulations applies to these amendment plans unless they are a major revision under regulation 30(3)(b). Consent may be refused under section 16(3)(a), and an occupation permit under section 21(6)(a), if prescribed plans under regulation 8(1) are not yet approved. Early Stage II checking may be requested for a simple building (single-storey plant room, or a two-storey warehouse on the fast track), a building that must finish quickly, or a project already close to superstructure consent.

**SD takeaway:** For a new building submitted after 31 March 2023, obtain Stage II approval of the general building plan before superstructure consent, and Stage III approval before the occupation permit.

### 3. Early advice, concept drawings, and area files (paragraphs 4–5 and 21–27)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Letter of assurance | A written pre-submission enquiry is normally answered within 45 days. Use it for a new structural theory, material, system, sophisticated design, or unconventional method |
| Enquiry during the job | An answer on interpretation of regulations and codes is normally given within four weeks |
| Concept drawings | Coloured key plans of site coverage, plot ratio, accountable gross floor area, fire strategy, and barrier-free strategy, for a pre-submission conference (Appendix E). Samples are on www.bd.gov.hk |
| Area calculation file | If a computer calculates floor areas, submit a digital file verifiable in AutoCAD, MicroStation, Revit, ArchiCAD, or other software accepted by the Department, with the paper general building plans |
| BIM | Follow the Guidelines for Using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plan Submission and PNAP ADV-34. Submitting the model permits Lands and Planning to extract the features listed in paragraphs 26 and 27 |
| Mismatch with a minor amendment | Plans may be approved if the authorized person puts the revision into the digital file at the next amendment that changes area calculations. A consolidated file is required with the occupation permit, temporary occupation permit, or alteration-and-addition completion certificate |

**SD takeaway:** Clear a novel structure or a concession strategy with a concept drawing before detailed design, and keep the area-calculation file in step with every amendment that changes area.

### 4. Minor amendments after the first consent (paragraphs 28–31)
If a modification of regulation 33(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations is granted under section 42(1), prior approval and consent are not required for minor amendments of building, superstructure, drainage, foundation, pile cap, site formation, and excavation and lateral support works for which first consent has been given, except the amendments in Appendix G. Apply with or after the first consent for that type of works. Document the amendments and keep them in the site office. Obtain approval and consent for all of them before certifying completion or applying for an occupation permit. A permit for superstructure also covers minor amendments of related secondary structural elements; a separate regulation 33(1) application for those elements is not required.

**SD takeaway:** After first consent, minor amendments listed as eligible can proceed without a fresh consent only if the regulation 33(1) modification is in place and the record is in the site office, and they still need approval before the occupation permit.

### 5. Concurrent approval and consent (paragraphs 33–39 and Appendix I)
| Case | Time and conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Alteration and addition, drainage, and secondary structural elements (paragraph 34) | Concurrent application if there is no foundation other than spread footing excavated not more than 4.5 m, no significant geotechnical content, no precautionary works that must be finished before commencement, and all regulation 8 plans plus consent documents are submitted. Curtain-wall certificates under PNAP APP-37 may follow before the occupation permit |
| Simple alteration and addition, including simple drainage (paragraph 35) | Processed within 30 days if the works do not involve the structure and do not affect fundamental issues, or only minor changes that comply are confirmed, and no referral is needed except to the Fire Services Department |
| Two-storey warehouse (Appendix I) | Not more than two storeys, no basement, general storage, Class A, B, or C site, emergency vehicular access complying with regulation 41D and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings, no compartment over 28,000 m³ or 10,500 m². Concurrent approval and consent within 45 days, and bearing-stratum inspection within one working day of notification. Submit via the Efficiency Office One Stop Centre or direct to the Buildings Department, including general building, structural, drainage, demolition, site supervision, and hoarding plans and the consent application |
| Repair of curtain wall, window wall, or cladding where the structural system is not altered (paragraphs 37–38) | Within 30 days. Minor-works items 1.48, 1.60, 1.61, 2.8, 2.48, 3.6, and 3.31 may instead use the simplified requirements. Like-for-like replacement of glass panes, sealants, setting blocks, and cladding panels does not need structural analysis of the replacement piece or a check of the parent structure |
| Form BA8A | The consent application takes effect on the date of approval, or of deemed approval. It is not processed if the plans are not approved. If approval is given but not with consent, the 28 days under regulation 32(3) run from the date of approval |

**SD takeaway:** A two-storey general-storage warehouse with no basement, compartments within 10,500 m² and 28,000 m³, and a complying emergency vehicular access can be lodged for approval and consent together and is undertaken in 45 days.
