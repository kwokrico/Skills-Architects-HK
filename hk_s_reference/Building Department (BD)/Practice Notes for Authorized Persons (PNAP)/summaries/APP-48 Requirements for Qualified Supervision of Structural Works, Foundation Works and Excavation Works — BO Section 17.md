# APP-48

**Official title:** Requirements for Qualified Supervision of Structural Works, Foundation Works and Excavation Works — BO Section 17

**In plain words:** The Building Authority (BA) may impose Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 17 conditions requiring qualified supervision of structural works, foundation works, and excavation at stated stages. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- The conditions may be imposed at plan approval or at consent, and they may cover major excavation and foundation construction; concreting; compliance testing; prestressing, including pre-tensioning, post-tensioning, grouting, and lock-off; fabrication, erection, and examination of key structural steel elements on site; and specialist building works.
- Supervisors need adequate technical knowledge and experience, and they need to be fully conversant with the basis of the engineering design.
- The authorized person (AP), the registered structural engineer (RSE), the registered geotechnical engineer (RGE), and the registered contractor (RC) must ensure that qualified supervision is provided, under BO sections 4(3) and 9(3) and Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulations 37 and 41.
- Qualified geotechnical supervision is set out in practice note APP-28, formerly PNAP 83.

**What to submit**
- Anticipate the section 17 supervision conditions, and comply with them.
- Assign personnel who are qualified for the operation and who understand the design basis.
- Where the works overlap with geotechnical supervision, coordinate with practice note APP-28.

**Watch-outs**
- Assigning a supervisor who is not familiar with the design basis.
- Missing supervision at prestressing, at erection of key structural steel, at major foundation or excavation work, or at a compliance-testing hold point.
