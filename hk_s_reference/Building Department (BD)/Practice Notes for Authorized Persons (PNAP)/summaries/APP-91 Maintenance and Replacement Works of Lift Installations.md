# APP-91

**Official title:** Maintenance and Replacement Works of Lift Installations

**In plain words:** Landing doors must keep the lift shaft’s fire resistance while the lift is maintained or replaced. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issued July 1997. Re-issued August 2009. A similar note went to registered contractors. The rules were to be written into the Fire Resisting Construction Code.
- Fire Resisting Construction Code 1996 paragraph 11.2: landing doors need a fire resistance period (FRP) of at least 1 hour integrity, and stay closed unless the car is at that landing.
- Subject to the next rule, all landing doors stay closed during the works.
- If a door must be open, normally no more than one door is open in a shaft. Any opening where the car is not levelled is attended by lift workers. If more than one door is open, the maximum is three, and there must be no hot works or welding.
- Avoid removing more than one landing door in a shaft at the same time.
- If more than one door is removed, protect the openings with temporary hoarding of at least 1 hour FRP.
- Openings in that hoarding are limited to small vents and an access door. Each vent is at most 5,500 mm², in the upper portion. At most 2 vents per liftway, and at most 4 vents in the hoarding.
- The access door has the same FRP as the hoarding, is self-closing, locks from the outside, and opens from the inside without a key.
- The hoarding must not obstruct or reduce an escape route as far as reasonably practicable.
- Scaffold, formwork, planking, and strutting inside the shaft must be non-combustible.
- Before the shaft is left unattended, including at lunch and at the end of the day, close open doors or enclose the openings with fire-resisting hoarding.

**What to submit**
- The Authorized Person (AP) or Registered Structural Engineer (RSE) must follow these rules and brief everyone they supervise.
- These requirements were intended to be incorporated into the Fire Resisting Construction Code.

**Watch-outs**
- Several open or removed landing doors without FRP hoarding create a vertical path for fire.
- Hot works with more than one door open are not allowed.
- Combustible temporary works inside the shaft are not allowed.
- Leaving openings unprotected overnight, or over any unattended break, is not allowed.
- Temporary hoarding must not block the escape route.
