# ADM-5

**Official title:** Submissions to the Buildings Department

**In plain words:** This note says which counter takes which paper submission, the opening hours, and how to fold plans so they can be time-stamped. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- New building developments, and alteration and addition (A&A) work, go to the Receipt and Dispatch Counter, New Buildings Divisions, 7/F, 14 Taikoo Wan Road, Taikoo Shing, Hong Kong. That counter does not take work done in response to a statutory order, notice, direction, or advisory letter about building or slope safety.
- Other submissions go to the General Enquiry and Receipt Counter, Buildings Department Headquarters, G/F, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon.
- Counters are open Mondays to Fridays, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm.
- Fold plans as shown in Appendix A so they fit the time-stamp machine. The preferred folds follow the dotted lines in Steps 1 and 2.
- Electronic submissions follow ADM-17 and APP-60.

**What to submit**
- Paper packages at the correct counter, folded as Appendix A shows. Electronic files go through the routes in ADM-17 and APP-60.

**Watch-outs**
- The wrong counter, especially New Buildings Divisions versus Headquarters, can delay the statutory processing clock.
- Plans that are not folded, or folded the wrong way, are hard to time-stamp and can lead to a dispute about when they were received.
