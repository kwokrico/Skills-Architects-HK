# APP-151

**Official title:** Building Design to Foster a Quality and Sustainable Built Environment

**In plain words:** This note is the main control on gross floor area (GFA) concessions for a quality and sustainable building, tied to sustainable building design (SBD) and energy efficiency. Capped concessions together cannot exceed 10% of the development GFA, worked out separately at 10% for the domestic part and 10% for the non-domestic part of a composite building.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue January 2011. This revision July 2025 (Appendices A and C amended). An enhanced BEAM Plus (Hong Kong green-building assessment) and specific-standards mechanism applies.
- Pre-requisites in paragraphs 6 to 9 apply to new general building plans, major revisions, and resubmitted disapproved general building plans on or after 30 June 2024. Earlier approvals use the August 2022 version (Appendix D), subject to Buildings Ordinance section 16(3)(d).
- Three pillars: SBD Guidelines (APP-152); GFA concessions; energy efficiency.
- Overall cap: total GFA concessions for capped features are at most 10% of the total GFA of the development. In a composite building, the domestic part and the non-domestic part are each capped at 10% of that part’s GFA.
- Not subject to the overall 10% cap (paragraph 5), though they may still have their own criteria: (a) mandatory or essential plant rooms, for example a refuse storage room, or a telecommunications and broadcasting (TBE) room; (b) communal podium or sky gardens that improve permeability; (c) parking and loading/unloading floor space, which is controlled separately; (d) voids for a cinema, arcade, or similar with an operational need, in a non-domestic building; (e) bonus GFA, dedication or surrender, and setback exemptions under the SBD Guidelines; (f) hotel concessions under Building (Planning) Regulation 23A.
- Pre-requisites for GFA concessions (paragraph 6) for Appendix A green and amenity features and non-mandatory plant:
  1. APP-152 SBD Guidelines, where they apply.
  2. For a domestic or composite building, APP-156 energy efficiency requirements, where they apply.
  3. An official Hong Kong Green Building Council letter acknowledging satisfactory project registration for BEAM Plus New Buildings Version 2.0 or above.
  4. State on the general building plans the target compliance assessment (CA) rating under BEAM Plus New Buildings, and any required specific standards. Anticipated Gold: no specific standard. Anticipated Silver: one specific standard. Anticipated Bronze: two specific standards.
  5. Document cascade: BEAM Society Limited provisional assessment credit summary, aligned with the target, before superstructure consent; Appendix B energy performance form before consent; update at occupation permit; BEAM Society Limited compliance assessment result at occupation permit achieving the target on the approved general building plans; provisional and final energy efficiency reports under APP-156 where they apply.
  6. The overall 10% cap, where it applies.
  7. Acceptance criteria for the individual feature.
- Specific standards (Appendix C): (a) enhanced greenery; (b) health and well-being; (c) enhanced natural ventilation; (d) BIM (building information modelling) in the plan submission; (e) multi-trade integrated mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MiMEP); (f) elderly-friendly building. The selection may change before the occupation permit application.
- Enhanced greenery (Appendix C1) is additional to the APP-152 site coverage of greenery:

| Site area | Minimum additional greenery | Minimum number of feature types (skyrise / green buffer / tree cluster) |
|-----------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Under 1,000 m² | 5% of the site | 1 |
| 1,000 m² or more and under 20,000 m² | 5% of the site | 2 |
| 20,000 m² or more | 7.5% of the site | 2 |

- A small site of 1,000 m² or less that meets the Appendix E setback alternative may have the additional 5% waived if a green buffer or a tree cluster is provided.
- Green buffer: a tree-pit strip at least 2 m wide along the full street frontage. Each pit at least 1.5 m × 1.5 m × 1.2 m. At least 6 m² of greenery per pit. At least 1 pit. Zero irrigation. Greenery area uses a ×2 multiplier.
- Tree cluster: at least 2 adjoining pits, minimum 12 m², zero irrigation.
- MiMEP (Appendix C5): coverage at least 50% of the qualifying plant and services area. The calculation method is in Appendix C5.
- Paragraph 7 conditions: submit the pre-consent items before consent, and the occupation permit items at occupation permit. The modification is revoked if consent is sought before the pre-consent submissions.
- The authorized person must consult a registered professional engineer in the relevant discipline for the energy form (paragraph 8).
- After the occupation permit, disclose energy data, the BEAM Plus compliance assessment result, and the finalised residential thermal transfer value (RTTV) or overall thermal transfer value (OTTV) for residential recreational facilities.

**What to submit**
- General building plans must state the target BEAM Plus rating and the selected specific standard or standards (sample drawing C042 / ADV-33).
- Before superstructure consent: Hong Kong Green Building Council registration letter; BEAM Society Limited provisional credit summary; Appendix B energy form; APP-156 provisional energy report if it applies; and proof of SBD compliance.
- At occupation permit: updated energy form; BEAM Society Limited compliance assessment result matching the approved target; APP-156 final energy report; and Land Registry undertakings for the specific standards that apply, for example greenery.
- If consent is sought before the pre-consent package, the modification is revoked.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not claim capped concessions (balconies, utility platforms, prefabricated walls, recreational facilities, non-mandatory plant, and similar) without the SBD path, BEAM Plus, the rating and specific-standards path, and the 10% calculation.
- Bronze or Silver without enough specific standards documented on the general building plans is not acceptable.
- Do not change the target rating without amending the general building plans before the occupation permit.
- Do not omit the energy engineer’s input on Appendix B.
- Car park, mandatory plant, podium and sky gardens, bonus GFA, hotel concessions, and a public transport terminus escape the overall 10% cap, but they still have their own practice notes and transport policies.
- Do not use the pre-30 June 2024 mechanism after the general building plan cut-off without Appendix D grandfathering.
