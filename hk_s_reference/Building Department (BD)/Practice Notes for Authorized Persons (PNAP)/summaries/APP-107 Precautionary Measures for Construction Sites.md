# APP-107

**Official title:** Precautionary Measures for Construction Sites

**In plain words:** This reminder tells the team to protect neighbouring property and the public while the site is under construction, covering demolition, dewatering, lateral support, concrete poured against a neighbour’s wall, and structural works. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Follow the related practice notes and codes for each kind of work, including the practice notes then numbered 71, 74, 81, 148, and 224, the then Draft Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings, and environmental nuisance control under the practice note then numbered 144.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued December 1998.
- File reference: BD GR/ACT/20.

**What to submit**
- Plan and carry out the protective measures that fit the site, following the practice note or code for that type of work.

**Watch-outs**
- If the measures are inadequate, adjoining property can be damaged and the public can be affected. The authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and contractor may be liable under the Buildings Ordinance (BO) and at common law.
