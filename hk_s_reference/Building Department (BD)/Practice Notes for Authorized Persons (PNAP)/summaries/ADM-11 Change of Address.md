# ADM-11

**Official title:** Change of Address

**In plain words:** This note is the rule for telling the Building Authority when your business address changes. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Administration) Regulation 45 requires every authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) to notify the Building Authority, on the specified form, of any change in the address at which they carry on business, within 14 days of the change.
- The specified form is Form BA 24. See also APP-60.

**What to submit**
- Form BA 24 online through the Buildings Department Electronic Forms Submission System (www.bd.gov.hk), or by post to the Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon.

**Watch-outs**
- Missing the 14-day notice is a breach of Building (Administration) Regulation 45. Letters about consent, the occupation permit, and emergencies can then go to the old address.
