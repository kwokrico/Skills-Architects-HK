# APP-26

**Official title:** Pouring of Concrete against Walls of Adjoining Buildings

**In plain words:** Do not use the wall of an adjoining building as permanent formwork for a concrete pour unless a physical check confirms its thickness, its condition, and its strength for the load of wet concrete and tamping. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 40: if that wall fails in whole or in part, the failure is taken to show negligence in the checks or the precautions, and it is an offence, unless the evidence clearly shows otherwise.

**Watch-outs**
- Treating a party wall or adjoining wall as adequate formwork without a verified thickness, condition, and load capacity.
- Any failure of such a wall brings the Buildings Ordinance section 40 presumption of negligence.
- Where the check is incomplete, use independent formwork so the adjoining wall carries no load, or support the wall and keep the load on it as small as possible.
