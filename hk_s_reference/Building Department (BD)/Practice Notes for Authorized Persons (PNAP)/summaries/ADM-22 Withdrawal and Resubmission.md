# ADM-22

**Official title:** Withdrawal and Resubmission

**In plain words:** This note lets you restart the statutory processing clock without physically taking the plans back. You write to the Building Authority, withdraw the application, and resubmit it in the same letter. It does not change site coverage (SC) or plot ratio.

**Numbers that matter**
- Withdrawal and resubmission applies to an application for plan approval, an occupation permit (OP), or consent.
- It must be in writing, and it must give reasons.
- The same submission may generally be withdrawn and resubmitted not more than two times.

**What to submit**
- A written letter to the Building Authority that withdraws and resubmits the application and states the reasons. You do not take the plans or documents away and bring them back.

**Watch-outs**
- An oral request, or a letter with no reasons, does not count.
- A third withdrawal and resubmission of the same submission is generally not accepted. The underlying problem is still there.
- Using the letter only to buy time, without fixing a fundamental or density defect, leads to refusal again.
