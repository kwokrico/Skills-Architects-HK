# APP-7

**Official title:** Application for Registration as Authorized Persons, Registered Structural Engineers, Registered Geotechnical Engineers and Registered Inspectors

**In plain words:** This note sets who may join the registers of authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), registered geotechnical engineer (RGE), and registered inspector (RI), and how registration is kept or restored. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Inclusion needs the prescribed qualifications and a recommendation from the Registration Committee (RC). Exception: an AP or RSE with the prescribed experience may join the inspectors’ register without a recommendation from the Inspectors Registration Committee (IRC), under Buildings Ordinance section 3(7AA)(a).
- AP: a registered architect, a registered professional engineer (structural or civil), or a registered professional surveyor, plus 1 continuous year of Hong Kong practical experience within the preceding 3 years.
- RSE: a registered professional engineer in structural engineering, or a civil registered professional engineer with structural experience approved by the RC, plus the same 1 year of Hong Kong experience within 3 years.
- RGE: a registered professional engineer in geotechnical engineering, plus the same 1 year within 3 years.
- RI: an AP, an RSE, a registered architect, a registered professional engineer in building, structural, civil, building-services (building), or materials (building), or a registered professional surveyor in building surveying or quantity surveying. Experience is counted within 7 years for an AP or RSE route, or as an aggregate of 1 year in 3 years, or as 3 years with at least 1 year in the last 3 years, depending on the discipline.
- Registration lasts 5 years. To retain it, submit Form BA1A not earlier than 4 months, and not later than 28 days, before expiry. Restoration within 2 years of expiry is on Form BA1B.

**What to submit**
- Form BA1, with the fee, for first inclusion. Use BA1A to retain registration and BA1B to restore it. The professional interview follows Appendix A (Buildings Ordinance and regulations, codes, roles, and local knowledge).

**Watch-outs**
- A retention application outside the statutory window is not accepted.
- If the Hong Kong experience window is not documented, the RC can reject the application at interview.
