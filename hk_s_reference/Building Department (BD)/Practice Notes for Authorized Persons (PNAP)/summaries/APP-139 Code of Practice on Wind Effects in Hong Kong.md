# APP-139

**Official title:** Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong

**In plain words:** This note brings in the 2019 Code of Practice on Wind Effects, including the December 2023 amendments. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage, but wind load drives the structural form and cladding pressures.

**Numbers that matter**
- Implements the Wind Effects Code 2019, plus December 2023 amendments to the Code and Explanatory Notes. PNAP revision December 2023. The grace period for the 2004 Code ended on 31 March 2021.
- After 31 March 2021, only the 2019 Code is accepted for new foundation or superstructure submissions, unless an earlier approval used the same Code for later related submissions, including loading allowance for future structures.
- Alteration and addition after the grace period: use APP-117 for new elements and for checking the existing structure.
- Wind tunnel tests follow Section 6 of the 2019 Code. If the test is not fully to the Code, send a methodology proposal to the Buildings Department. The Department normally decides within 45 days. Pre-submission enquiry is ADM-19. Appendices C and D list what the methodology and quality-assurance plan must contain.
- The witness is the registered structural engineer, or an independent engineer who is an HKIE Corporate Member or equivalent with at least 5 years of relevant experience. The witness must not be staff of the tunnel laboratory.
- The December 2023 amendments include, among other items, wind-tunnel trigger figures, the size factor, and Explanatory Notes clause 2.2.3 on fundamental-frequency assumptions for multi-tower-over-podium buildings. Hoarding and covered-walkway design net pressures: height 2.5 m or less → 0.63 kPa; 5 m → 0.70 kPa; 10 m → 0.77 kPa. Shelter benefit is limited to 80% of the Standard Method base moments if there is no building-removal investigation.
- Buildings Ordinance section 16(3)(d): consent may be refused if more than 2 years have passed since plan approval.

**What to submit**
- Foundation and superstructure submissions to the 2019 Code after the grace period has ended.
- Wind-tunnel methodology and quality-assurance plan before or with the structural submissions, as applicable. A tunnel report certified by the registered structural engineer for use in the structural design.
- The report must include procedures, full readings, structural and dynamic properties, and a statement by the registered structural engineer on supervision and that the raw data and results meet the 2019 Code.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not use the 2004 Code after the grace period unless the earlier approved package is grandfathered.
- Do not run a tunnel test without an accepted departure from the method, with a weak quality-assurance plan, or without supervision of the laboratory run.
- An independent witness who is laboratory staff is not acceptable.
