# APP-126

**Official title:** Erection of Signboards

**In plain words:** A signboard is building works under the Buildings Ordinance (BO), and this note sets where it may project, how big it may be, and when an oversized board is treated as a new building. A board outside the stated limits, and enclosed floor space under a tall board, counts in gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Projection over a street under BO section 31(1)(aa) is allowed only if there is no structural danger, no traffic or fire hazard, no obstruction of traffic signs, signals, markings, or monitoring, no obstruction or reduction of means of escape (MOE) or means of access (MOA), no obstruction that reduces the required lighting and ventilation (L&V), and no public danger.
- Headroom in Appendix B: at least 5.8 m if the board is within 1 m of the outer edge of the footpath or is over the carriageway. Over a footpath, at least 3.5 m, or at least 2.5 m if the board projects not more than 600 mm. Over a tramway, at least 7 m.
- Keep at least 1.5 m from the vertical line at the centre of the street, and at least 3 m between signboards that face each other. If a board projects more than 600 mm from the wall, keep at least 2.4 m laterally from adjacent projecting signboards, for the full height of the building. Keep at least 1.2 m from a common site boundary.
- Under the sustainable building design setbacks in PNAP APP-152, no signboard may project more than 600 mm inside the setback area, up to 15 m above street level, where the building is set back for those guidelines.
- A wall signboard may project not more than 600 mm. A shopfront head signboard must be independent of roller shutters and air-conditioning units. An enclosed space behind a signboard is not for storage.
- A projecting signboard may project not more than 4.2 m from the external wall. It must sit in a virtual prism whose vertical planar surface is not more than 40 m² and whose horizontal width is not more than 600 mm. Several boards in the same vertical plane, including existing boards, must have a combined planar area of not more than 40 m² and a combined prism height of not more than 20 m.
- A roof signboard must be at least 1.5 m from the inside face of the parapet or curb, and the setback area must be accessible.
- Design the supporting frame for full wind pressure. Check the overall stability of the parent building if the projecting signboard area is 10% or more of the building’s wind-exposure area.
- A signboard more than 3 m high, on the ground or on a roof, must not create enclosed or partly enclosed floor space unless that space is included in GFA and SC, or is occupied by machinery or ductwork.
- A wall or projecting signboard that exceeds the Appendix B size or projection is treated as a new building under the BO, including GFA and SC.
- The fast track uses at least 11 sets of plans, the Appendix I certificate, and the Appendix F checklist. Processing is 30 days if the Appendix G information is complete. An unconventional or non-conforming case is excluded, and the authorized person (AP) is told within 28 days.
- From 1 September 2021, when Form BA12, BA13, or BA14 is submitted on completion, provide the signboard information in Appendix N for the GeoInfo database.

**What to submit**
- Unless the work is minor works under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (B(MW)R) and PNAP APP-147, or is exempted under Schedule 2, obtain approval and consent first. A registered general building contractor (RGBC) carries out the work.
- For the fast track, submit at least 11 sets of plans, the Appendix I certificate, and the Appendix F checklist. Concurrent consent follows PNAP ADM-19.
- A supervision plan is generally not required, unless paragraph 4 treats the signboard as a new building. The AP, registered structural engineer (RSE), and RGBC must still protect the public, including against falls, with shoring or a working platform as needed.
- On completion, from 1 September 2021, give the Appendix N information with Form BA12, BA13, or BA14.
- Displaying the Buildings Department (BD) reference number is recommended, in line with PNAP APP-147 and PNAP APP-155.
- Inspect every year and after a typhoon, and issue the maintenance manual in Appendix M.
- Also consider the Lands Department lease, especially projection over government land (Appendix J), Electrical and Mechanical Services Department lighting requirements (Appendices K and L), and the Environment Bureau’s external lighting guidelines.
- Construction must meet Appendix A. Position and size must meet Appendix B. Fire Services Department, Transport Department, and Highways Department requirements are in Appendices C to E. Airport height controls, the outline zoning plan, railway controls, harbour-facing illuminated signs, and monument controls also apply.
- Do not fix a signboard to an unauthorized structure or to another signboard. Do not support it from a cantilevered slab. A projecting structure or balcony is generally not a supporting member, apart from the limited exception in Appendix G.

**Watch-outs**
- Misrepresentation or misconduct is viewed seriously. AP and RSE duties are under BO section 4. Sanctions are under sections 7 and 40.
- Fixing to a common part without consent under the deed of mutual covenant (DMC), the Building Management Ordinance (BMO), or the owners’ corporation (OC) can bring civil liability.
- Support from a cantilever or an unauthorized building, blocking MOE, L&V, or emergency vehicular access (EVA), or projecting into a sustainable-building-design setback, does not comply.
- Exceeding the 40 m² virtual prism, or the 4.2 m projection, without treating the board as a new building, misses the GFA and SC consequences.
