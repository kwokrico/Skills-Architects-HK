# APP-75

**Official title:** Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue in Buildings (B(P)R 41A, 41B, 41C)

**In plain words:** Fireman’s lifts must give firefighters a safe, unobstructed way in during a fire, and a firefighting and rescue stairway (FRS) may contain only fireman’s lifts, with its stair serving every floor. This note sets that mandatory access arrangement and does not itself change gross floor area or site coverage; how the core is counted still depends on the fire-safety code and other practice notes.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Planning) (Amendment) Regulation 1995. The Code of Practice for Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue was revised to match. Application is through Buildings Ordinance section 39.
- Section 39(2): the amendment does not apply to works carried out, or consented, on or before 22 May 1995.
- Regulation 41B, as revised: fireman’s lifts are designed and located so that, in a fire, firefighters have safe and unobstructed access to the lifts and to the floors they serve.
- Regulation 41C, as revised: only fireman’s lifts are installed in a firefighting and rescue stairway. The access stairway in that stairway serves all floors of the building, so firefighters can use all of its facilities.

**What to submit**
- On the general building plans, show compliance with Building (Planning) Regulations 41A, 41B and 41C, and with the revised means-of-access code, for a development that is subject to the amendment (works or consent after 22 May 1995).
- The revised code was available from the Government Publications Sales Centre, as stated in the note.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not put an ordinary lift inside a firefighting and rescue stairway. Only a fireman’s lift is permitted.
- Do not stop the stairway in a firefighting and rescue stairway short of some floors. It must serve all floors.
- Do not provide a fireman’s lift without an unobstructed access path at the time of a fire.
- Do not apply the pre-22 May 1995 grandfathering to a later scheme.
