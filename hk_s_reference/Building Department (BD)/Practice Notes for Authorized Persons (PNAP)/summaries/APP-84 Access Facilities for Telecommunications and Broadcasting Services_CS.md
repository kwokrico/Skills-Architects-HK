# APP-84 Access Facilities for Telecommunications and Broadcasting Services
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 201; first issue November 1997)

> Scope note: Access facilities required by regulation 28A of the Building (Planning) Regulations in every commercial building, industrial building, residential building other than a single-family residence, and hotel. Fixed-network sizes are in Appendix A and apply to a new or major revision of general building plans approved on or after 1 November 2006. Mobile access facilities apply to a specified building whose earliest general building plan approval for erection, rebuilding, or alteration resulting in a new building is on or after 1 April 2025. A single-family house is not required to provide these facilities and is not a specified building.

## Regulatory Overview
Lead-in ducts are 100 mm inside diameter and are sealed against water and gas with a seal the operator can remove, and a telecommunications and broadcasting equipment room is kept out of flood, has its own earth electrode, and contains no water pipe, sewage pipe, drain, sprinkler, transformer, or supply exceeding 600 V between phase and earth for three-phase, 1,000 V for single-phase, or 1,500 V direct current. Separate towers on a commercial podium each have their own room, and the podium has its own.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fixed facilities (Appendix A)
| Building | Bands and the room |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial or industrial, by usable floor space | Up to 2,000 m2: no minimum room, but up to 10 m2 may be exempted; 2 lead-in ducts; 2 risers of 100 mm by 100 mm. Then, in bands to 4,000, 12,000, 24,000, 48,000, 72,000, 96,000, 120,000, and 144,000 m2: minimum room 22, 42, 51, 61, 79, 88, 107, and 113 m2, and maximum exemption 28, 53, 64, 77, 99, 111, 134, and 142 m2. Ducts 3, then 3, 4, 4, 8, 8, 8, and 8. Risers 300 by 200 mm, then 300 by 200, 400 by 200, 400 by 200, 600 by 250, 650 by 250, 750 by 250, and 900 by 250 mm. Always 2 risers and 3 m clear height. Above 144,000 m2, add the largest band to the remainder. The note's example for 180,000 m2 is a minimum room of 174 m2 and a maximum exemption of 219 m2 |
| Residential, by number of flats in the block | Up to 5: no minimum room, exemption up to 4 m2, 2 ducts, 1 riser of 75 mm by 75 mm, clear height 2.8 m. Then, in bands to 50, 100, 250, 500, 750, 1,000, and 1,500 flats: minimum room 7, 21, 24, 29, 39, 43, and 48 m2; maximum exemption 9, 27, 31, 37, 49, 54, and 60 m2. Ducts 3, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4, and 4. Risers: one of 200 by 100, 200 by 100, 200 by 200, or 300 by 200 mm through 500 flats, then two of 300 by 200, 300 by 200, and 400 by 200 mm. Clear height 2.8 m up to 1,000 flats and 3 m above that |
| Hotel, by number of rooms | Up to 200 and up to 400: minimum room 24 m2, exemption 30 m2, 1 riser. Then to 600, 800, 1,000, and 1,200 rooms: minimum 26, 28, 35, and 37 m2; exemption 33, 36, 44, and 47 m2; 2 risers from 400 rooms upward. Always 3 ducts and 3 m clear height. Riser size 200 by 200 mm, then 300 by 200 mm through 800 rooms, then 400 by 200 mm |
| Gross floor area | Regulation 23(3)(b) may disregard space used solely for these regulation 28A facilities, up to the maximum in the table |

**SD takeaway:** Size the fixed equipment room, the 100 mm lead-in ducts, and the risers from Appendix A for the usable floor space, the flat count, or the room count, and keep the room out of a flood path.

### 2. Mobile access facilities in a specified building (paragraphs 12–19 and Appendices C and D)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Which buildings must provide them | A commercial or industrial building with more than 3,000 m2 usable floor space, a residential building with more than 50 flats, or a hotel with more than 75 rooms. Where several buildings meet that test, the number required is the lowest integer not less than 25% of those buildings. If more than five specified buildings exist but none meets the test, one building provides them. Each tower and a commercial podium count separately. A mixed-use building counts once if any use meets the test, and then follows the use that needs the largest facility. Select the tallest buildings unless the Office of the Communications Authority supports another |
| What is provided | A mobile equipment room, a rooftop equipment room, an intermediate equipment room if the building exceeds 175 m measured under regulation 23(1), and a vertical riser. The rooftop room is not combined with the intermediate room. If the roof cannot take it, the rooftop room may be not lower than 90% of the building height. Rooms are common parts, with an access path and a power connection point. Windows of a rooftop or intermediate room are fixed and opaque to sight but allow radio signals, for example glass or fibre-glass |
| Areas in Appendix D | For commercial, industrial, residential, and hotel bands, the minimum area of each of the mobile equipment room, the rooftop room, and the intermediate room is 10, 15, or 20 m2, and the maximum that may be exempted is 20, 25, or 30 m2, stepping up with usable floor space, flats, or rooms as Appendix D sets out. The sample residential schedule shows a required clear height of 2.8 m |
| Exemption | Not granted if the mobile equipment room, rooftop room, or intermediate room is less than 10 m2. Mobile facilities in a building that is not a specified building are included in gross floor area. Voluntary mobile facilities under paragraphs 13 and 14 may be disregarded only in a specified building, and a rooftop or intermediate room not otherwise required needs the support of the Office of the Communications Authority |
| Plans | Show the mobile facilities on the general building plans in the form of the Annex to Appendix C |

**SD takeaway:** From 1 April 2025, give at least a quarter of the qualifying towers a mobile room of at least 10 m2, a rooftop room, and, above 175 m, a separate intermediate room.
