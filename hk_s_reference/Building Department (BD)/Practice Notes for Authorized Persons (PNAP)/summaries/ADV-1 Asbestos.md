# ADV-1

**Official title:** Asbestos

**In plain words:** Do not use asbestos-containing material in new work. If an existing building has it, or might have it, investigate and remove it through registered people before other building work starts. This note does not change how gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) is measured. It does change the programme for demolition and for clearing unauthorised building works.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Asbestos) Regulation (Cap. 59AD) prohibits asbestos and asbestos-containing material in any industrial or manufacturing process, including building operations.
- From 4 April 2014, the Air Pollution Control Ordinance (Cap. 311) bans the import, transhipment, use, and supply of asbestos and asbestos-containing material. Material already in an existing building is not affected by that ban.
- Where such material is present or suspected: appoint a registered asbestos consultant early; have samples tested by a registered asbestos laboratory where needed; if the material is confirmed, submit to the Environmental Protection Department an asbestos abatement plan for planned removal, and/or an operation and maintenance plan for deferred treatment such as encapsulation or enclosure; use a registered asbestos contractor under the consultant’s supervision; give the Department 28 days’ notice before the work starts; protect workers as the Labour Department requires; and dispose of contaminated waste as the Environmental Protection Department requires.
- Finish the investigation and the abatement before other building activities. Keep unauthorised people and scavengers out until the material in place has been inventoried, photographed, labelled, and segregated. The consultant certifies completion against the investigation report and the abatement plan, then tells the Environmental Protection Department immediately.
- Some low-risk, readily identifiable, non-friable material may be exempt from the consultant and/or contractor requirement. See Gazette Notice 25/1997 (GN 3021 and GN 3022, 6 June 1997). The list is on the Environmental Protection Department website.
- Codes of practice cover handling, transport, and disposal of asbestos waste; investigation reports, management plans, and abatement plans; full and mini containment; safe handling of low-risk material; and the glove-bag method. Also see the Labour Department code on safety and health at work with asbestos, the Buildings Department code on demolition, and ProPECC Practice Note 2/97.
- Registers of consultants, contractors, supervisors, and laboratories are held by the Asbestos Management and Control Section. The Labour Department Occupational Safety and Health Branch advises on worker safety. A similar note goes to registered contractors.

**What to submit**
- The abatement plan or the operation and maintenance plan, and the 28-day notice, go to the Environmental Protection Department. They do not replace a Buildings Department plan submission. After abatement, the consultant’s completion certificate and an immediate notice to the Environmental Protection Department.

**Watch-outs**
- Specifying or allowing asbestos-containing material after the Cap. 311 ban, or in breach of Cap. 59AD.
- Starting demolition, repair, or clearance of unauthorised building works before the investigation and abatement are finished.
- Letting scavengers or unauthorised people in before the material is inventoried, labelled, and segregated.
- Missing the 28-day notice, or disposing of the waste outside the Environmental Protection Department’s requirements.
- Using people who are not registered, except where the Gazette exemption applies.
