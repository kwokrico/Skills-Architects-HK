# APP-129

**Official title:** Use of Recycled Aggregates in Concrete for Minor Structures and Non-structural Works

**In plain words:** This note allows C20 concrete made with 100% recycled coarse aggregate, and C25 to C35 concrete made with at most 20% recycled coarse aggregate, only for the minor and non-structural uses it lists. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Appendix A, C20 with 100% recycled coarse aggregate, per 100 kg of ordinary Portland cement (OPC): 180 kg fine aggregate, 180 kg of 20 mm aggregate, and 90 kg of 10 mm aggregate. Slump 75 mm. Cube strength 14 MPa at 7 days and 20 MPa at 28 days. In laboratory trials, each of 3 cubes must reach at least 26 MPa at 28 days. Fine aggregate is grading M of Construction Standard CS3. Recycled fine aggregate is not allowed.
- Recycled aggregate limits in Table 1: dry density at least 2,000 kg/m³; water absorption not more than 10%; wood and other light material not more than 0.5%; other foreign materials not more than 1%; fines not more than 4%; sand under 4 mm not more than 5%; sulphate not more than 1%; flakiness index not more than 40%; 10% fines value 100 kN; chloride not more than 0.05% under CS3 clause 5.2.3. Wet the aggregate thoroughly before use.
- Appendix B, C25 to C35 with at most 20% recycled coarse aggregate and 80% natural coarse aggregate. Cement is OPC only, to BS EN 197-1. Test the source weekly. Store the recycled aggregate separately, in its own compartment. Minimum slump 75 mm. Carry out laboratory and plant trials to the Annex criteria. The same Table 1 limits apply.
- Quality control generally follows the Concrete Code 2013.
- Previously PNAP 275. First issued February 2003. This revision February 2022.
- File reference: BD GP/BREG/RC/4(V).

**What to submit**
- State the use of recycled aggregate on the structural general notes and in Part I of the structural calculations, under PNAP ADM-8. Affirm compliance with Appendix A or Appendix B, and show the extent on the relevant structural plans. The other rules for concrete structures still apply.
- Appendix A is only for minor or non-structural work: on-grade slabs, blinding, U-channels, pipe bedding and haunching, fence-post footings, and mass fill that does not carry an appreciable load.
- Appendix B covers landscape reinforced-concrete features, walls and footings for landscape fences and mass walls, stormwater manholes and sand traps, footways, cycle tracks, reinforced-concrete infill walls, and mass concrete under footings or rafts.
- Precast paving units follow Highways Department guidance notes and the particular specification.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not use 100% recycled C20 in a structural member.
- Do not use recycled fines. Do not use the 20% mix in a liquid-retaining, prestressed, transfer, or hanger structure. Those structure types are not permitted.
- Omitting the statements on the plans, or the trial-compliance records, does not comply.
