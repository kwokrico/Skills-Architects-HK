# ADM-16

**Official title:** Ground Investigation Works in Scheduled Areas — Approval and Consent

**In plain words:** Ground investigation in the Scheduled Areas is building works. It needs approval and consent under the Buildings Ordinance. This note offers a faster path that handles approval and consent together. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- The concurrent path is available for new ground investigation in the Scheduled Areas if you do both of the following.
- Submit a site supervision plan with the ground investigation plans.
- Say in the covering letter that you want the concurrent option, and apply for consent after the 32nd day from the day the plans were submitted for approval.

**What to submit**
- For the concurrent path: the ground investigation plans, the site supervision plan, a covering letter that chooses the concurrent option, and the consent application after day 32.
- If you do not choose that path, approval and consent stay separate, in the normal order.

**Watch-outs**
- Starting Scheduled Area ground investigation without approval and consent is not allowed.
- Choosing the concurrent path without a supervision plan, or applying for consent before day 32, loses the concurrent advantage or fails the process.
- Weak supervision papers for a Scheduled Area raise enforcement risk under the ground-investigation rules.
