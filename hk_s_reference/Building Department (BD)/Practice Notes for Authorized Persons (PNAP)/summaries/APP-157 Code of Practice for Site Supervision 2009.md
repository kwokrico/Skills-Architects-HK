# APP-157

**Official title:** Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009

**In plain words:** This note directs the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) to the Code of Practice for Site Supervision, 2024 Edition, and adds extra checks for temporary works that support a tower crane. It does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue May 2015. Last revision December 2023. This revision August 2024.
- The 2024 Edition on the Buildings Department website includes the 2021 Edition plus Appendix A (December 2023) and Appendix B (August 2024).
- Tower-crane support works are in addition to paragraph 4.9 of the Supervision Code (2021 Edition).
- The extra tower-crane rule applies to every development project whose specified Form BA 10 is submitted on or after 1 November 2024.
- Certification forms are TW1, TW2, and TW3. Soft copies are on the Buildings Department APP-157 page.
- Technically competent person (TCP) academic qualifications are in paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24 of the Supervision Code (2021 Edition). The Buildings Department publishes an accepted course list, local and non-local, on its website.
- A circular letter of 8 May 2015 repealed TCP recognition based on membership of particular professional institutions (“corresponding recognition”). Appendix C of this note is the reference copy. A person accepted before the repeal may continue as a TCP if evidence of that earlier recognition is supplied with the supervision plan.

**What to submit**
- Use the Supervision Code 2024 Edition as the working code.
- For temporary works supporting a tower crane, the registered general building contractor (RGBC) or registered specialist contractor (RSC) must appoint a design engineer to prepare the plans, construction drawings, and design justifications. An independent checking engineer must check those documents against the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations, and the relevant codes of practice. Certify them on TW1, TW2, and TW3.
- If the supervision plan relies on pre-repeal institutional TCP recognition, attach evidence of the earlier acceptance.

**Watch-outs**
- A Form BA 10 dated on or after 1 November 2024, without a design engineer and an independent checking engineer for tower-crane support works, does not meet the enhanced code.
- Appointing a TCP on the repealed “corresponding recognition” route, without documentary evidence, can get the supervision plan rejected.
- Take the December 2023 and August 2024 amendments from the Buildings Department 2024 Edition. A similar practice note was issued to registered contractors.
