# ADV-28

**Official title:** Provision of Sanitary Fitments in Offices, Shopping Arcades, Department Stores, Places of Public Entertainment, Cinemas and Other Public Places

**In plain words:** Until the regulations are amended, the Building Authority will consider a modification so you may use the male-to-female ratios and the fitment numbers in Appendix A, where those numbers are lower than the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations. This note does not grant a gross floor area (GFA) concession. Larger toilet cores do use floor area.

**Numbers that matter**
- Population ratios, male to female. Offices and sports stadiums: 1 to 1. Shopping arcades, department stores, places of public entertainment, cinemas, religious institutions, and funeral parlours: 1 to 1.5.
- How to assess the population, in square metres of usable floor area per person. Shopping, basement to 2/F: 3. Shopping, 3/F and above: 4.5. Usable floor area here includes the arcade and the public circulation. An assembly hall, auditorium, or stadium without fixed seating: 0.5. A dance hall or disco: 0.75. A museum, exhibition hall, trade mart, or display area: 2. A religious or funeral worship area: 0.5. A cinema or place of public entertainment with fixed seating: the seats, plus employees. Offices: regulation 5(5)(a).
- Shopping and department stores. Male water closets: for 1 to 500 persons, 1 per 125 or part; above 500, 4 plus 1 per additional 250. Urinals: 1 per 250 or part. Female water closets: for 1 to 50, 1 per 25; for 51 to 125, 3; for 126 to 1,800, 3 plus 1 per additional 80 over 125; above 1,800, 24 plus 1 per additional 90 over 1,800. Basins, male and female: for 1 to 500, 1 per 125; above 500, 4 plus 1 per additional 250.
- Places of public entertainment. Male water closets: for 1 to 400, 1 per 100; above 400, 4 plus 1 per additional 250. Urinals: 1 per 50. Female water closets: for 1 to 250, 2 per 50; above 250, 10 plus 1 per additional 40. Basins, male and female: 1 per 100.
- Cinemas. Male water closets: 1 for 1 to 200 persons; 2 for 201 to 500; 3 for 501 to 1,000; above 1,000, 3 plus 1 per additional 500. Urinals: 1 per 100. Female water closets: for 1 to 120, 1 per 40; for 121 to 250, 3 plus 1 per additional 65 over 120; for 251 to 420, 5 plus 1 per additional 85 over 250; above 420, 7 plus 1 per additional 100 over 420. Basins, the same for male and female: stepped 1, then 2, then 3, then 1 more per 500 over 1,000.
- Sports stadiums. Male water closets 1 per 100. Urinals 1 per 100. Female water closets 1 per 50. Basins, male and female, 1 per 100.
- Religious institutions and funeral parlours. Male water closets 1 per 150. Urinals 1 per 150. Female water closets 1 per 75. Basins, male and female, 1 per 300.
- Offices: numbers follow regulations 5(1), 5(3), and 5(4). The male-to-female ratio may be modified under regulation 5(5)(c) as above.
- Design some urinals, basins, and water closets for children. Do not count a children’s water closet toward the numbers prescribed in regulations 4 to 8.
- Unisex toilets are encouraged, for a caregiver or a parent with a child of the opposite sex. On the plans, say whether a unisex toilet is additional or is counted toward the male or female provision, and give the justification.
- Minimum space in a public place that is not a toilet for persons with a disability. A water-closet cubicle: internal size at least 700 mm by 1,200 mm; clear floor in front of the pan at least 700 mm by 600 mm; circulation outside the cubicle at least 700 mm by 600 mm.
- Urinals: clear floor at least 500 mm by 500 mm; centreline at least 250 mm from a side wall; centres at least 500 mm apart. A cubicle is at least 1,000 mm by 500 mm internally, plus the 500 mm by 500 mm clear floor, and circulation of at least 500 mm by 600 mm. On a trough, every 500 mm counts as one urinal.
- Basins: clear floor at least 600 mm by 600 mm; centreline at least 400 mm from a side wall; centres at least 600 mm apart; circulation at least 600 mm by 600 mm. Clear floor spaces of different fitments must not overlap. Circulation spaces may overlap. Spaces for persons with a disability follow the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access 2008.

**What to submit**
- Form BA16, to modify the male-to-female ratio under regulations 5(5)(c), 6(3)(b), and 7(3)(b), and/or the fitment numbers, where Appendix A produces fewer fitments than the regulations.
- Mark unisex toilets clearly on the plans, with the counting justification.

**Watch-outs**
- Counting a children’s water closet toward the adult statutory number.
- A unisex toilet whose counting is ambiguous, so it is either counted twice or not provided at all.
- Public-toilet space below the Appendix B minima, outside the scope of the barrier-free-access manual.
