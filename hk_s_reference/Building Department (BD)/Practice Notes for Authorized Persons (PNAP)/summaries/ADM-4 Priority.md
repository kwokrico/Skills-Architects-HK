# ADM-4

**Official title:** Priority

**In plain words:** This note says which new-building submissions can be processed ahead of the queue. Priority is a Buildings Department processing favour only. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), and it does not speed up land transactions.

**Numbers that matter**
- Priority for a new building may be granted if you apply, and the project is one of the following:
  1. An urgently needed public utility or institutional building, such as a school or an electricity sub-station, where the public interest and the urgency are shown.
  2. A Hong Kong Housing Society project.
  3. A project on land owned by the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (formerly the Hong Kong Industrial Estates Corporation).
  4. A project submitted for or on behalf of the Urban Renewal Authority by a wholly owned subsidiary, or by a joint-venture consortium in which the Authority is a partner.
  5. A project likely to add significantly to Hong Kong’s economic growth. This one needs positive support from the relevant government departments.
- A project outside that list needs exceptional circumstances and consultation with the departments concerned.
- Ask for priority with the first submission of plans.
- Alteration and addition (A&A) work to an existing building is normally not given priority. The exception is a project designed to fight air, noise, or water pollution, or to cut or stop existing polluting waste and noxious or toxic effluent.
- Priority is reviewed all the time. It can be withdrawn if the project does not move forward promptly.
- Priority applies only to Buildings Department matters. It has no effect on land transactions.

**What to submit**
- The priority request with the first plan submission. Mark it on the ADM-2 Appendix A checklist where that checklist asks. For the economic-growth case, include evidence of urgency or public interest and the departmental support.

**Watch-outs**
- Ordinary A&A work, or a private commercial scheme that does not fit the list, should not be expected to get priority.
- The economic-growth case without departmental support is refused.
- Slow progress after priority is granted can lead to withdrawal.
- Lands Department and lease dealings are not covered by this priority.
