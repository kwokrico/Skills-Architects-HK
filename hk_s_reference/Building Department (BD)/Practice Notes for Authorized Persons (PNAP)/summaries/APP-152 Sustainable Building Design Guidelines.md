# APP-152

**Official title:** Sustainable Building Design Guidelines

**In plain words:** Sustainable building design (SBD) rules on building separation, building setback, and site coverage of greenery are a pre-requisite for many gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage (SC) concessions under APP-151. A covered setback under the footprint or a canopy may be left out of GFA if it is common area with no commercial activity.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue January 2011. This version September 2023 (Appendices A and D amended). Three elements: building separation, building setback, and site coverage of greenery.
- Building separation applies if the site is 20,000 m² or more, or if the site is under 20,000 m² and the continuous projected façade length Lp is 60 m or more.
- Assessment zones measured from Level Zero (the mean of the lowest abutting street): Low zone 20 m or less; Middle zone 20 m to 60 m; High zone over 60 m.
- Design requirement (1), on Lp: for buildings within 30 m of the street centreline, maximum Lp = 5 × U, where U is the mean street canyon width. Portions in the low zone that are 6.67 m high or less may be disregarded for Lp. This requirement does not apply if the site has no street, or if no building in that zone is within 30 m of the street centreline.
- Design requirement (2), on separating distance S and permeability P: use orthogonal projection planes at 75° to 105°. One low-zone plane is parallel to a street. Provide P by at least two-thirds intervening space and at most one-third permeable element.
- Minimum P on each assessment plane:

| Height of the tallest building | Site under 20,000 m² and Lp 60 m or more (each plane) | Site 20,000 m² or more, Plane 1 | Plane 2 |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|---------|
| H 60 m or less | 20% | 20% | 25% |
| H over 60 m | 20% | 20% | 33.3% |

- Intervening-space width S is at least 7.5 m to the boundary or the street centreline (mean at least 7.5 m; no part within 3 m of the boundary if S varies). Additional intervening space between buildings is at least 15 m. Intervening-space height is at least two-thirds of the zone, or the space is open to the sky. A permeable element has clear width and height of at least 3 m.
- Lp is the continuous projected façade where separations are under 15 m. A standalone residential building 15 m high or less is exempt, and is disregarded when assessing other buildings.
- Building setback, if the building fronts a street under 15 m wide: either (a) keep a 15 m × 15 m ventilation corridor, with no part of the building at 15 m or less above the street within 7.5 m of the street centreline; or (b) provide a cross-ventilated communal podium garden with clear height at least 4.5 m, and keep the building below a 45° inclined plane drawn from the opposite street boundary.
- Structure more than 15 m above street level may build over the setback. Canopies follow Regulation 10. Minor features and signboards may project at most 600 mm, at a clear height of at least 2.5 m. Footbridges are allowed. Columns follow Figure C2. The setback is a landscaped or paved open common area.
- Setback is not required if the building height is less than 2 times the mean street width.
- Covered setback under the footprint or a canopy may be exempt from GFA if it is common area with no commercial activity.
- Site coverage of greenery, Table 2. The primary zone is the lowest 15 m vertical zone.

| Site area | Primary zone (15 m vertical) | Overall |
|-----------|------------------------------|---------|
| 1,000 m² to 20,000 m² | 10% | 20% |
| 20,000 m² or more | 15% | 30% |

- Not applicable to a site that contains only a single-family house.
- Greening features may contribute at most 30% of the required overall greenery, after reduction factors: covered greenery in the primary zone at 50%; water features in the primary zone or on an uncovered communal roof at 50%; grass paving at 50%, excluding car park and loading/unloading; inaccessible-roof planters in the primary zone at 50%; vertical greening with no reduction; landscape-treated slopes or retaining walls steeper than 45° with no reduction.
- A green buffer under APP-151 Appendix C1 also uses the ×2 multiplier for this site coverage of greenery.
- Removable pots are not counted. Covered greenery above the primary zone is generally not counted, with limited exceptions. On vertical climbing frames, parts more than 7.5 m high are not accountable. All greenery is common parts, with irrigation and drainage, and the roof must deal with imperviousness and structural load.
- Alternatives in Appendix E: less bulk (site coverage 65% or less) plus a street setback (frontage at least 50% of the boundary and at least 10 m, or the full frontage if it is under 10 m; setback area at least 15% of the site) may waive low-zone separation. A performance air ventilation assessment by wind tunnel or computational fluid dynamics may be used if the minimum P in Table 1 is still met and equivalence to a compliant baseline is shown. The Building Committee may review.
- Compliance is an APP-151 pre-requisite for the listed GFA and SC concessions. Covered setback GFA exemption, and APP-151 item 36 covered setback, sit alongside bonus and dedication paths which remain under APP-108. A greenery letter of undertaking is registered at the Land Registry before the occupation permit when GFA modifications are granted on those conditions.

**What to submit**
- Separation, setback, and greenery information as required by Appendix F. Greenery plans are uploaded to the Buildings Department website after the occupation permit.
- Section 42 modification conditions may lock greenery as common parts and may restrict the deed of mutual covenant.
- Land Registry registration of the greenery undertaking before the occupation permit, when approval conditions require it.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not claim GFA concessions on a large site or a long façade without meeting Lp, P, and S in every assessment zone.
- Do not count permeable elements as more than one-third of the required P, use intervening spaces that are too narrow, or count removable pots or covered gardens above the primary zone.
- A street under 15 m wide still needs the setback or the 45° option, unless the height-based exemption applies.
- Greenery that is not designated as common parts, or a missing Land Registry undertaking when the approval conditions require one, is not acceptable.
