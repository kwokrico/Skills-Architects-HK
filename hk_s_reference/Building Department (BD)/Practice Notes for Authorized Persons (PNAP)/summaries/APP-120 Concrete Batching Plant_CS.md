# APP-120 Concrete Batching Plant
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue March 2002 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 255)

> Scope note: When a concrete batching plant is a building requiring approval and consent, and the processing arrangements for the common type shown in Appendix A. A longer-term self-certification idea for plants under 50 tonnes total silo capacity and under 15 m overall height is stated as something to be explored, not as a current system. Appendix A is a typical drawing.

## Regulatory Overview
A concrete batching plant fixed to and founded on the ground, connected to a utility supply, and with a control room is a building under the Buildings Ordinance, so approval and consent are required before erection. New plants and associated structures erected after 31 March 2002 without prior approval and consent are subject to enforcement as unauthorized building works.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New plants (paragraphs 3–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| What is a building | Fixed to and founded on the ground, connected to a utility supply, and with a control room |
| Submission | Building plans by an authorized person and a registered structural engineer, complying with the Buildings Ordinance and regulations, including the town plan, before erection. Associated structures include cement, sand, and aggregate handling facilities |
| Common type in Appendix A | The Building Authority is prepared to process approval and consent simultaneously on request, as outlined in Appendix B |
| Completion | Apply for an occupation permit or a temporary occupation permit, depending on the operator or the nature of the site |
| After 31 March 2002 | Enforcement against new plants and associated structures erected after that date without prior approval and consent |
| Existing plants before 1 May 2002 | No action if structural safety is shown by a satisfactory appraisal report by a registered structural engineer by 30 October 2002 and the plant is properly maintained. Other departments may still enforce other ordinances or land-use requirements |

**SD takeaway:** Submit a grounded, utility-connected batching plant with a control room for approval before it is erected.

### 2. Waivers for the Appendix A type (Appendix B)
For the type in Appendix A, the number of production lines may vary. The following are accepted:

| Waiver | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Fire resisting construction | Waived if no part of the plant is within 1800 mm of the site boundary other than a sea wall, and control-room gross floor area does not exceed 30 m2 per production line. Water storage tanks and settlement pits will normally be allowed up to the site boundary without fire resisting construction |
| Means of escape | Waived if control-room gross floor area does not exceed 30 m2 per production line and personnel in it do not exceed 5 per production line. Stair access to the control room not less than 750 mm clear, with a handrail each side, and a going not exceeding 45 degrees. Building (Planning) Regulation 72 is waived for the identified control room if these criteria are met |
| Both waivers | Number of production lines does not exceed 4, and total gross floor area of all control rooms does not exceed 100 m2 |
| Drainage | Off-site drainage connections or discharges must meet Drainage Services Department and Environmental Protection Department requirements. Drainage details may be on the general building plan |
| Plot ratio and site coverage | May exclude conveyor belts, but not their supporting structures. Plot ratio based on the site-coverage area may be accepted subject to extra office floors and the like at other levels being included |
| Minor layout change after an occupation permit | A minor alteration involving a conveyor-belt support and associated hopper will be processed within 30 days |
| Non-typical plant | If features or accommodation are not those of a typical batching plant, or the waiver requirements cannot be met, the whole plant is processed as an ordinary building |

**SD takeaway:** A waived plant stays at not more than 4 production lines, 30 m2 of control room per line, and 100 m2 of control rooms in total, and keeps the plant 1800 mm off the site boundary other than a sea wall.

### 3. Relocation and existing unapproved plants (Appendix B and Appendix C)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Relocation of an approved plant | New 60-day submission. Original structural calculations may be used subject to reassessment for wind load and ground conditions. Structural submission processed within 30 days if the site is similar in all respects and Appendix C is satisfied. General building plan also within 30 days if referral to other departments, except the Fire Services Department, is not required |
| Appendix C, 30-day structural processing | The registered structural engineer certifies that superstructure framing, members, and connections, and the calculations (method, wind, dead, live, material stresses, and standards), match the approved plans and remain equally applicable. Foundation system, allowable bearing pressure, type, layout, and details, and the foundation calculations reviewed with the new site investigation, are equally applicable. The engineer also certifies that referrals are not required for the scheduled areas, railway protection, slopes, culverts, streets, highways, sea walls, reclamation, public drainage, and water mains listed in Appendix C |
| Existing unapproved plant | Structural justification restricted to basic details of the main structural components and at least one trial pit at the existing foundations, plus an appraisal and inspection report and certification of fitness for use |
| Relocation of an existing unapproved plant | New 60-day submission. Above-ground justification by laboratory tests and similar, with an appraisal and inspection report and certification of fitness for re-use |

**SD takeaway:** A relocated approved plant can reuse the old calculations only if wind and ground are reassessed and Appendix C is certified.
