# ADV-1 Asbestos
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision January 2015 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 114; first issue September 1985)

> Scope note: Bans new use of asbestos-containing material and sets the investigation and abatement sequence where it is already in a building. It does not specify removal methods; those are in the Environmental Protection Department codes.

## Regulatory Overview
Persons involved in design, construction, and maintenance must avoid asbestos-containing material, and removal from an existing building is to be carried out only by registered asbestos personnel. From 4 April 2014 the Air Pollution Control Ordinance (Cap. 311) bans the import, transhipment, use, and supply of asbestos and asbestos-containing material, while material already present in existing buildings is not affected by that ban.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When works may disturb material already in the building (paragraphs 2–4)
| Step | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Prohibition on new use | Use in any industrial or manufacturing process, including any building operation, is prohibited under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Asbestos) Regulation (Cap. 59AD) |
| Investigation | Appoint a registered asbestos consultant as early as possible. Samples of suspected material may be tested by a registered asbestos laboratory |
| If confirmed | Submit to the Environmental Protection Department an Asbestos Abatement Plan for planned removal and/or an Operation and Maintenance Plan for deferred treatment such as encapsulation or enclosure |
| Removal | A registered asbestos contractor, supervised by an asbestos consultant |
| Notice | 28 days' advance notice to the Environmental Protection Department before commencement |
| Sequence | Complete investigation and abatement before other building activities. Keep unauthorised persons, scavengers in particular, out until in-situ material is inventoried, photographed, conspicuously labelled, and segregated |
| Completion | The supervising consultant certifies completion against the investigation report and the abatement plan, and notifies the Environmental Protection Department immediately |
| Waste and workers | Dispose of contaminated waste as the Environmental Protection Department requires, and protect workers as the Labour Department requires |

**SD takeaway:** On a demolition, repair, or clearance of unauthorised works, finish the asbestos investigation and abatement, with 28 days' notice, before other building activities start.

### 2. Low-risk exemption (paragraph 7)
For certain abatement works involving only low-risk and readily identifiable non-friable asbestos-containing material, the services of an asbestos consultant and/or contractor may be exempted. The list is Gazette Notice 25/1997, GN 3021 and GN 3022, of 6 June 1997, on the Environmental Protection Department website.

**SD takeaway:** Do not assume an exemption; use it only for material on the 6 June 1997 gazette list of low-risk non-friable work.
