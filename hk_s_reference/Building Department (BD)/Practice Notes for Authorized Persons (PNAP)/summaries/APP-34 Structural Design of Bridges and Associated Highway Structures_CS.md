# APP-34 Structural Design of Bridges and Associated Highway Structures
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 1995 | Buildings Department (first issued February 1983; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: A bridge or associated highway structure required in connection with a private development, whether inside the lot, outside it, or partly outside it. Design follows the Structures Design Manual for Highways and Railways, and plans are examined by the Advisory Committee on the Appearance of Bridges and Associated Structures. PNAP 107 covers that referral.

## Regulatory Overview
A bridge that is wholly or partly within a private lot, or that gains support from or is connected to a building on a private lot, is processed under the Buildings Ordinance and must also satisfy the Building (Construction) Regulations. A free-standing bridge built entirely on government land or over public highways is exempted from the Buildings Ordinance, and the Director of Highways is the competent authority.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which regime applies (paragraphs 3–6)
| Location | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Bridge between two private buildings, gaining support from either or both | Buildings Ordinance |
| Bridge supported on a private building at one end and on government land at the other, whether or not there is separate access from a public street | Buildings Ordinance |
| Free-standing, entirely on government land or over public highways | Exempted. Highways Department vets plans, permits the works, imposes foundation testing, and examines materials quality control |

**SD takeaway:** If the bridge touches a private building or stands inside the lot, submit it under the Buildings Ordinance as well as to the Highways design manual; if it stands wholly on government land, Highways Department is the approving authority.

### 2. Standards for a bridge wholly outside the lot (paragraphs 7–8)
| Item | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Geometry | Transport Planning and Design Manual Volume 2 |
| Materials and workmanship | General Specification for Civil Engineering Works, 1992 Edition |
| Retaining walls | Geoguide 1, second edition |
| Lighting | Lighting Division of the Highways Department and the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department |
| Geotechnical | Geotechnical Manual for Slopes |
| Landscape | Minimise adverse impact by treatment and planting, as part of the design. Prior consultation with the Landscape Unit of the Highways Department is welcomed |
| First submission to the Buildings Department | 3 sets of plans, 2 sets of calculations, and 2 sets of site investigation documents, including resubmissions, for transmission to the Highways Department. Completion is certified in writing by the Highways Department |

**SD takeaway:** Even for a bridge outside the lot, lodge three sets of structural details with the Buildings Department for transmission, and design retaining walls to Geoguide 1 and slopes to the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes.
