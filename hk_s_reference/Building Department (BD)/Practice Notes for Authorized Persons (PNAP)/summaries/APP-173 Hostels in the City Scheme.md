# APP-173

**Official title:** Hostels in the City Scheme

**In plain words:** For a student hostel that the Education Bureau (EDB) has confirmed under the Hostels in the City Scheme, the Building Authority may treat the hostel as non-domestic for site coverage and plot ratio, relax open space, service lane, and kitchens in the units, and exempt listed hostel support facilities from gross floor area (GFA). The note does not override the Lands Department, the Planning Department, the Fire Services Department, or the Transport Department.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue July 2025. This revision September 2025. Paragraph 3 was amended. Paragraphs 13 to 15 were added, covering newly built hostels under the 2025 Policy Address. Scheme information is at www.studenthostel.gov.hk.
- Conversion of an existing commercial building or hotel, for an eligible hostel. The Building Authority is prepared to:
  - Treat the hostel as non-domestic, or as a non-domestic part, for site coverage and plot ratio under Building (Planning) Regulations 19 to 22.
  - Allow no open space under regulation 25, and no service lane under regulation 28, on the merits of the case. An example is no existing lane, plus acceptable servicing.
  - Allow no kitchen in the individual units under regulation 45, if there is a communal canteen, kitchen, or pantry.
- A converted hostel must still meet the other domestic requirements, including natural lighting and ventilation, and hostel or dormitory fire safety.
- Hostel facilities that may be exempted from GFA (Appendix): linen store; laundry; study, collaboration, or leisure room; gymnasium or sports facilities; communal kitchen, pantry, or canteen; staff rest room or toilet; and other support facilities accepted by the EDB and the Building Authority.
- Redundant GFA that was previously exempted or disregarded may be converted, favourably, to mandatory features, plant rooms, green or amenity features, or hostel facilities. If the alterations and additions (A&A) create a new building, the APP-151 prerequisites apply.
- Former hotel pick-up and set-down areas, and back-of-house (BOH) areas, disregarded under regulation 23A(3) and Cap. 349, may be converted to hostel facilities with a modification of regulation 23(3)(a).
- If that redundant area is under 5% of hostel GFA, hostel facilities may be exempted up to 5%. If the redundant area is over 5%, all of the redundant area may be converted to hostel facilities. Exemption of hostel facilities above 5% is considered case by case.
- Partial conversion is allowed if the Scheme Application Guidelines are met (EDB and the Development Bureau DPFO). “Total GFA of the student hostel” means the GFA of the hostel portion only.
- Newly built hostels, from issuance of this practice note: the same non-domestic site coverage and plot ratio treatment, the same open space and lane treatment, and the same non-provision of unit kitchens. Hostel facilities are exempt up to 5% of total hostel GFA. APP-151 prerequisites apply. The overall APP-151 GFA concession cap does not apply. The hostel is treated as non-residential for Joint Practice Notes (JPN) 1 and 2, APP-42, and APP-104.
- The new-build facilitation applies to a new general building plan, or a major revision, submitted on or after issuance of this practice note. Paragraphs 13 to 15 are the September 2025 revision.

**What to submit**
- Obtain EDB confirmation of Scheme eligibility before relying on the facilitation.
- Apply for the specific Building (Planning) Regulations modifications and exemptions.
- If there is no kitchen in the units, show the communal canteen, kitchen, or pantry.
- List the hostel facilities for which GFA exemption is sought.
- Coordinate with the Lands Department, Planning Department, Fire Services Department, and Transport Department.
- If the premises cease to be a student hostel, the exemptions are revoked. The owner must appoint an authorized person (AP) to submit a change of use that shows Buildings Ordinance compliance, or face Buildings Department enforcement.

**Watch-outs**
- Claiming the facilitation without EDB Scheme eligibility.
- Seeking an exemption from kitchens in the units while omitting the communal canteen, kitchen, or pantry.
- Exceeding the 5% hostel-facility GFA exemption without case approval, or miscounting the redundant area on a conversion.
- Continuing to rely on the exemptions after the premises stop being a student hostel under the Scheme.
