# APP-8

**Official title:** Chimneys and Flues

**In plain words:** This note sets fire, structural, and corrosion rules for chimneys and flues, and the referral of those plans to the Environmental Protection Department. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Any GFA modification for a chimney shaft is in APP-2, not here.

**Numbers that matter**
- Comply with Building (Construction) Regulation 44: non-combustible materials; fire safety of the design, construction, and termination under section 44(2) to (4); insulation justified by calculation (fuel type and rate, nearby materials, and use), with BS 5854:1980 as guidance.
- Design a steel chimney to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011, especially clause 13.2. Check wind-excited oscillation by aerodynamic methods. A circular chimney may use the simplified method in clause 13.2.8.
- Protect against corrosion and avoid bimetallic action (clause 13.2.7, Annex A of the Steel Code, and the manufacturer’s instructions). At penetrations, use flashing or a weather hood that allows differential expansion and avoids bimetallic action. BS EN 1856-1:2009 and BS 5854:1980 also apply.

**What to submit**
- General building plans that include chimneys or flues are referred to the Environmental Protection Department under the centralised processing in ADM-2. Prior approval under the Air Pollution Control Ordinance may be required. A food business or an offensive trade may also have licensing requirements.

**Watch-outs**
- Missing insulation or fire justification, or a missing oscillation analysis, is a refusal risk.
- If Environmental Protection Department comments or approvals are not obtained, Air Pollution Control Ordinance and licensing delays follow.
