# ADM-7

**Official title:** Geotechnical Information Unit

**In plain words:** This note describes the Geotechnical Information Unit, the public archive of ground investigation and slope records run by the Geotechnical Engineering Office. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Use it before you design on a slope, in the Mid-levels, or in a scheduled area.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Unit is in the Civil Engineering Library, First Lower Ground Floor, Civil Engineering Building, Homantin. Opening hours are 09:00 to 12:30 and 14:00 to 16:15, Mondays to Fridays, except public holidays.
- Holdings include ground investigation reports and geophysical surveys; laboratory tests of soils and rocks; a card index of cut slopes, fill slopes, and retaining walls; landslip record cards; piezometer readings; and rain-gauge records. The Mid-levels Study (1979 to 1981) geology, hydrology, and soil-property data are included.
- Records are found with the Unit’s cell referencing system on 1:5 000 survey sheets in the urban area, and 1:20 000 sheets for the rest of the territory.
- The library also holds textbooks, journals, standards, and published papers on Hong Kong geology and geotechnical engineering. A computer catalogue can be searched by keyword.
- You may read the material in the reading area. You may not take it out. Photocopying is charged at a nominal rate.
- Catalogue of Slopes plans, data sheets, and photographs are available. General enquiries go to the librarian on 2762 5147. Application forms for catalogue information are at District Offices and at the Unit. Preliminary information is sent in ten days.

**What to submit**
- Nothing to the Buildings Department under this note. Ask the Unit for catalogue information on the application form. Expect a preliminary reply in about ten days.

**Watch-outs**
- Designing without checking the Unit or the Catalogue, where the site needs it, is weak geotechnical homework for the Mid-levels, scheduled areas, and slope-affected sites.
- Removing Unit material from the library is not allowed.
