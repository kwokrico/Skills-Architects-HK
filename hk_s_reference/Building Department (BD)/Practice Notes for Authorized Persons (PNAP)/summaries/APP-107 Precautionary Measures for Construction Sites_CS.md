# APP-107 Precautionary Measures for Construction Sites
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 1998; re-issued August 2009 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 232)

> Scope note: A reminder to provide protective and precautionary measures so construction does not damage adjoining properties or cause nuisance. Detailed methods sit in the older practice notes and the Draft Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings named in paragraph 4. It is not itself a demolition or excavation design code.

## Regulatory Overview
Construction activities can damage adjoining properties and cause nuisance to the public, and the measures required depend on the site. The note names the work types that need particular attention before those works are planned.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Works that need precautionary measures (paragraphs 2–3)
| Work type | Attention required |
| --- | --- |
| Demolition works | Protective and precautionary measures to limit adverse effect on adjoining buildings and nuisance to the public |
| Dewatering | In foundation and basement excavation |
| Lateral support | For excavation |
| Concrete against adjoining walls | Pouring of concrete against walls of adjoining buildings |
| Structural works | Same protective purpose |

**SD takeaway:** Flag demolition, dewatering, lateral support, concrete cast against a neighbour’s wall, and structural works as items that need site-specific protection of adjoining buildings.

### 2. Where the detailed advice sits (paragraphs 4–5)
General advice on those works is in Practice Notes for Authorized Persons and Registered Structural Engineers 71, 74, 81, 148 and 224, and in the Draft Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings. PNAP 144 covers control of environmental nuisance.

**SD takeaway:** Pair this reminder with PNAP 71, 74, 81, 148, 224 and 144, and the Draft Demolition code, when those works are on the programme.
