# ADV-11

**Official title:** Suspended Working Platforms

**In plain words:** A suspended working platform, often called a gondola, that carries people is regulated by the Labour Department. This note sets the minimum width, guard rails, ropes, and tests. It does not itself change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Permanent roof tracks and counterweights are building works and are covered with ADV-14.

**Numbers that matter**
- The definition covers a scaffold or working platform suspended from lifting gear and raised or lowered by lifting appliances, including the appliances, gear, counterweights, ballast, outriggers, supports, and the mechanical and electrical apparatus. The regulation applies to an industrial undertaking that uses such a platform to carry persons. It was generally in force from 1 July 1995. Section 17 was in force from 1 January 1996.
- Construction must be sound, adequately supported, and properly maintained. Electrical parts, motors, and cables must be protected against accidental damage and the weather.
- The platform must be long enough for the work. Width is at least 440 mm. Guard rails are 900 mm to 1,150 mm high. Toe boards are at least 200 mm high. The gap between the toe board and the lowest guard rail is not more than 700 mm. Provide safe access and a safe way off.
- Use wire ropes only for raising, lowering, and suspension. Suspension points must be far enough, horizontally, from the face of the building that the platform does not hit the facade. Counterweights on outriggers must be at least 3 times the weight needed to balance the load on the projecting part when the platform is fully loaded. At each suspension point, provide a safety rope with an automatic safety device, in addition to the two suspension ropes. Erection, dismantling, and alteration are only under the supervision of a competent person.
- Workers must be at least 18, and must hold a certificate after training recognised by the Commissioner for Labour, or training provided by the manufacturer or the local agent. On the platform they wear a safety harness, with the lanyard attached to an independent lifeline or to an anchorage with fittings.
- A competent person inspects the platform before use. A competent examiner carries out a load test and a thorough examination before use, and after a substantial repair, re-erection, adjustment, failure, collapse, or weather likely to affect stability, and at intervals after that. Display the most recent certificate or report prominently on the platform. Mark the safe working load and the maximum number of persons, and give the platform a unique identification mark.
- Labour Department publications: a guide to the regulation, the code of practice on safe use and operation, guidance notes on inspection, thorough examination, and testing, and safety hints, on labour.gov.hk.

**What to submit**
- The day-to-day regime is the Labour Department’s. Structural supports that are permanent building works still need Building Authority approval. See ADV-14.

**Watch-outs**
- A platform narrower than 440 mm, guard rails or toe boards outside the sizes above, counterweight below 3 times the balance requirement, or a missing safety rope and automatic device.
- An untrained worker, or no independent lifeline and harness.
- Use without a current competent-examiner certificate on display.
- The note cites fatal accidents from rope and mechanism failure. A permanent gondola designed under ADV-14 must be capable of being used in the way this note requires.
