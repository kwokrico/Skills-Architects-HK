# APP-21 Demolition Works — Measures for Public Safety
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 71; first issue May 1980)

> Scope note: Approval, consent, supervision, and completion of demolition under the Buildings Ordinance, the Building (Administration) Regulations, and the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations. Design of the works follows the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004. Hoardings, covered walkways, gantries, and catch platforms follow Chapter 3 of that Code and PNAP APP-23. This note does not itself set hoarding dimensions.

## Regulatory Overview
Demolition plans are submitted under regulations 8(3) and (4), and consent may be refused under section 16(3)(a) and (b) if those plans are not approved. A supervision plan following the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2024 Edition) is lodged with the consent application unless the works meet every criterion in paragraph 11 of the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans, and consent may be refused under section 16(3)(bc) if it is not lodged.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Measures that must be in place, and the record of the works (paragraphs 5–16 and 20–26, Appendix B)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| May be erected without consent, if they do not affect the structure | Protective screens and safety nets; dust screens of plastic or tarpaulin; scaffolds and intermediate catchfans; hoarding, covered walkway, gantry, and catch platforms; shoring and propping to stabilise the building under demolition or an adjoining building |
| Before consent | Those measures, and the documents in Appendix B, are in place. Consent may be refused under section 16(3)(b), (ba), or (bc) if documents are missing, or under section 16(3)(bb) if the protective measures are inadequate |
| Video | At least one camera per site, at locations agreed by the authorized person and the engineers and shown on the demolition plan, recording the whole process including debris movement and sequence. The contractor keeps the records for at least 14 days |
| Plant operators | Meet regulation 9(3) of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations. For regulation 9(3)(b), the Hong Kong Institute of Construction course "Demolition of Building Course for Plant Operators", or an equivalent certificate. Particulars accompany the consent application under regulation 31, or consent may be refused under section 16(3)(b). A change of operator is notified within 7 days |
| Fire and explosion | Remove flammable goods. Regulation 6 of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations |
| Slopes and retaining walls | The structural engineer incorporates the geotechnical engineer's recommendations. Geotechnical reports are prepared and signed by the geotechnical engineer |
| Facade covering | Protective net, screen, and tarpaulin or plastic sheeting have fire-retarding properties to a recognised standard, with certificates available on site |
| Completion | Form BA14A within 14 days, or Form BA14B where the fast-track criteria are met |
| Drains and street names | Tell the Drainage Services Department when drains are sealed (regulation 3(2)(b)). Tell the Director of Highways if street name plates are fixed to the building (section 32) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not apply for demolition consent until hoarding, catch platforms, propping, sealed drains, and at least one video camera are in place, and name the plant operators in the application.

### 2. Fast-track acceptance, dangerous buildings, and minor works first (paragraphs 17 and 28–32, Appendix C)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fast-track completion, all of the following | The building is structurally independent; no basement or below-ground structure is involved, except that filling a basement with debris only to counteract buoyancy still qualifies, while filling to retain earth does not; nothing above ground remains except the ground-floor slab and beams; no party wall or adjoining external wall needs structural support; slopes and retaining structures are not affected and no sealing, extra drainage, or stabilisation of a sloping site is required |
| How to use it | State compliance on the demolition plans. Submit Form BA14B with colour photos of the site, adjacent land or streets, and all elevations of adjoining buildings, and a mark-up of photo directions. A reply is generally within 14 days if not audited, or notice of audit within 14 days and a reply generally within 28 days |
| Dangerous-building shores | Preferably structural steel of Grade 250 or above, to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011 (2023 Edition) or equivalent. Agree dead-shore positions with the Existing Buildings Division before erection. Temporary shores are removed after demolition and replaced by permanent support where necessary. Adjacent buildings to be demolished together should start together |
| Party walls that remain | Usually need raking or flying shores from the demolished site (Appendix A). Inspect them as demolition proceeds and repair concurrently |
| Minor works before demolition, same project team | Form MW01 not less than 7 days before those works, submitted with the demolition-plan application. The plans show the minor works and state that protective measures follow the Technical Guidelines on the Minor Works Control System. Form MW02 goes with Form BA14A or BA14B |

**SD takeaway:** Use fast-track completion only where the building stands alone, has no basement to retain, and leaves no party wall that still needs shoring.
