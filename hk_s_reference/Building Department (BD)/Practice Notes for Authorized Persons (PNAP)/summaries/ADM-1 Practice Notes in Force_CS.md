# ADM-1 Practice Notes in Force
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision August 2009 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 1; first issue November 1983)

> Scope note: Explains how the Building Authority issues and groups practice notes. It does not set dimensional design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority issues practice notes to authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered geotechnical engineers to show how the Buildings Ordinance and its regulations are applied, and to cover other administrative and advisory matters. From August 2009 the notes are regrouped as ADM (administration), APP (application of the Ordinance and regulations), and ADV (advisory), and the live list is the one kept on the Buildings Department website.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Three-part numbering (paragraphs 2–4)
| Part | Prefix | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Administration | ADM | General administration of the Buildings Department, plan approval, consent, occupation permits, and related matters |
| B — Application | APP | How the Building Authority applies the Buildings Ordinance, its regulations, and other ordinances that affect plan approval and building control |
| C — Advisory | ADV | Advisory notes and promotion of good practice |

A note filed under one part may still contain material that belongs in another. Contents of the notes re-issued in August 2009 were unchanged; only the grouping, serial numbers, and the name (now including registered geotechnical engineers) were updated. The abbreviation remains PNAP.

**SD takeaway:** Cite the current ADM, APP, or ADV number from the website list, and read across parts when a design topic is split.

### 2. Where the current list lives (paragraphs 5–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| List in force | Appendix A of this note, kept updated at www.bd.gov.hk |
| Hard copies | Issue of hard copies ceased; notes are disseminated on the website |
| Footnote indexes | Become redundant and are deleted when a note is next revised |
| Language | Chinese versions were published with the re-issue; later new and revised notes are bilingual |

**SD takeaway:** Do not lock a submission to a printed index; confirm the note still in force on the Buildings Department website before design freeze.
