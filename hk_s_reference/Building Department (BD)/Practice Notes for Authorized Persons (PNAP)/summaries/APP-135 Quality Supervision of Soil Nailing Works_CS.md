# APP-135 Quality Supervision of Soil Nailing Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2006 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 284; first issue October 2003)

> Scope note: Buildability and the particulars to include in a site-formation submission for soil nails. Supervision qualifications, testing, and acceptance criteria are in the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision, not restated here. CEDD particular specifications and GEO Report No. 133 may be used as a guide.

## Regulatory Overview
Soil nails used to upgrade existing slopes or form new slopes are buried, so buildability has to be designed in, especially for long nails. The registered geotechnical engineer must put site-trial, pull-out, and non-destructive-test particulars in the site-formation submission where they are required.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Long nails and difficult ground (paragraphs 4–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Length over 20 m | Higher chance of loose or permeable ground or a buried stream course: drillhole collapse that can obstruct the reinforcement assembly, high grout loss, and grout quality harder to ensure |
| When an assessment is required | Long nails, or ground or groundwater likely to be adverse. The registered geotechnical engineer may be required to assess buildability and the effects of installation on existing ground and groundwater, based on a site trial, before the soil nailing works |
| Trial timing | The site trial may be incorporated in, and should include, pull-out tests where possible. Trial and pull-out nails are installed and tested before working nails |
| Difficult ground | Loose fill or colluvium, rockfill, soil pipes, buried stream courses, rock mass with open discontinuities and fractures, alternating soil and rock. Groundwater flow makes collapse and grout quality worse. Groundwater drainage may have to be incorporated |

**SD takeaway:** Treat nails longer than 20 m, and nails in loose ground or groundwater, as needing a site trial and pull-out tests before working nails.

### 2. Particulars for plan approval (paragraph 6)
The registered geotechnical engineer includes in the site-formation submission:

| Particular | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Site trial | Details, if required, to confirm buildability, including locations |
| Pull-out | Procedure adopted for the pull-out tests |
| Non-destructive test | Methodology and details for verifying the length of installed nails, with provisions so the test can be carried out |

Quality supervision, minimum qualification and experience of site supervisors, and non-destructive testing details are in the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision.

**SD takeaway:** Show trial locations, the pull-out procedure, and how nail length will be verified, on the site-formation submission.
