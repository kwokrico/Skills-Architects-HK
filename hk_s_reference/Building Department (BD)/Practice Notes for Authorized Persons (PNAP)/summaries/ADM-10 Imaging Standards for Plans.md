# ADM-10

**Official title:** Imaging Standards for Plans

**In plain words:** Approved plans are stored as electronic images. This note asks for drawing standards so those images stay readable. Failing the guidelines is not, by itself, a reason to disapprove the plans. Approval is still judged under the Buildings Ordinance. The note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- “Plans” here means building, drainage, site formation, and structural plans, and the calculations referred to in ADM-8 paragraphs 8 and 9.
- Outside edges of sheets: A0 is 1189 × 841 mm; A1 is 841 × 594 mm; A2 is 594 × 420 mm; A3 is 420 × 297 mm; A4 is 297 × 210 mm. Avoid anything larger than A0. The border normally sits inside those dimensions.
- Leave unused borders of at least 20 mm on A0 and A1, and at least 10 mm on A2, A3, and A4, on all four sides.
- Put a centre mark on each side of the drawing frame. Use a matt surface with good contrast. Give each drawing a unique number. Mark revisions as (Rev.A), (Rev.B), and so on. The title should state the location and the type of work. Notes should be readable from the same direction (dimensions are excepted). Show a numbered or graphic scale. Put the legend of markings and symbols, preferably, on the right.
- Colours do not show on the image. Materials and construction must also be identifiable by hatching or notes, for example “rc”, “br”, or “ms”. Do not cover lettering or numbers with heavy hatching, dark shading, or a dark colour.
- Leave an unused space of 90 mm wide by 150 mm high at the lower right corner of every plan, for the Building Authority approval stamps and for certified-copy certification.
- With the occupation permit (OP) application, submit a Plan Index of all approved plans. The index itself is imaged, so use A sizes. Use a separate Plan Index for each category (building, structural, drainage, and so on). Columns are: BD drawing number (for Department use), AP or RSE drawing number, description, and approval date. Mark the latest as-completed approved plans with an asterisk. This index is in addition to the record-plan system.

**What to submit**
- Approval plans drawn to Appendix A. Keep the 90 mm × 150 mm stamp space clear. With the occupation permit application, submit a Plan Index for each plan category, with an asterisk on the current as-built approved sheets.

**Watch-outs**
- A sheet larger than A0, borders that are too small, or a missing stamp space causes archive problems. It may also get in the way of stamping, even though it is not by itself a ground for disapproval.
- Colour-only differences, with no hatch or note, are unreadable on the archive image.
- A wrong or incomplete Plan Index at occupation permit records the wrong approved sheets as the as-built set.
- Text or numbers buried under a dark colour or hatch will not read on the image.
