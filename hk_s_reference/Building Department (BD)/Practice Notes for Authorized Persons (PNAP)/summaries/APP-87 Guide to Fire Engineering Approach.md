# APP-87

**Official title:** Guide to Fire Engineering Approach

**In plain words:** Fire engineering may replace the means-of-escape, means-of-access, and fire-resisting-construction codes only if overall safety is equal to or better than full code compliance under Building (Construction) Regulations regulation 90 and Building (Planning) Regulations regulations 41, 41A, 41B, and 41C. This note does not give an automatic gross floor area or site coverage concession.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 204. First issued March 1998. Re-issued August 2009.
- Paragraph 3 of the three codes recognises the fire engineering approach.
- The case must cover no unacceptable fire growth for the use, escape without dangerous heat or smoke, no spread to neighbouring property, fire and smoke kept to the compartment of origin, firefighter access without undue risk, and stability, insulation, and integrity for the stated fire intensity and duration.
- Hong Kong does not have local statistics for a full quantitative risk assessment, so that method is limited. Quantitative risk assessment and large-scale tests are for special buildings only.

**What to submit**
- Consult the Buildings Department and the Fire Services Department early, before the plan submission, and get agreement in principle.
- The Authorized Person (AP) leads the work. There was no fire-engineer accreditation when this note was issued. Other disciplines add specialist input.
- Submit a Fire Safety Strategy Report covering, as relevant: introduction, the development, sources, means of escape, design fires, smoke control, fire-spread control, structural performance, sprinklers, alarm, first-aid firefighting, firefighter facilities, emergency lighting and signs, and the role of management, with calculations and sketches for smoke filling, the design fire, and egress.
- Follow the appendix flow chart. Practical tests may be needed to show like-for-like equivalence.

**Watch-outs**
- A fire engineering submission without early Buildings Department and Fire Services Department consultation is not the agreed path.
- A claim of fire engineering without proof of equivalence to the codes fails.
- Leaving out scenarios, the management role, or maintenance of fire-safety systems leaves the case incomplete.
- Overseas tools or statistics need a local justification.
- The AP remains responsible for coordinating the specialist inputs.
