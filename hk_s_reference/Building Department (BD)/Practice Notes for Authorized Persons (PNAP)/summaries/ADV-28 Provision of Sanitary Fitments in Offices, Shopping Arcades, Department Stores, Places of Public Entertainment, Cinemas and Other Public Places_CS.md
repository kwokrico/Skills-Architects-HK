# ADV-28 Provision of Sanitary Fitments in Offices, Shopping Arcades, Department Stores, Places of Public Entertainment, Cinemas and Other Public Places
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision November 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 297; first issue May 2005)

> Scope note: Voluntary recommendations, pending amendment of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations, for the number and the minimum space of sanitary fitments in new buildings. Where the recommended number is below the Regulations, the Building Authority is prepared to consider a modification. Accessible toilets remain as in the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access 2008.

## Regulatory Overview
For offices the recommended male-to-female ratio is 1:1, and the Building Authority will favourably consider a modification of section 5(5)(c) of the Regulations; office populations and fitment numbers themselves stay on sections 5(5)(a) and 5(1), (3), and (4). For shopping arcades, department stores, places of public entertainment, cinemas, religious institutions, and funeral parlours the recommended ratio is 1:1.5, with a modification of sections 6(3)(b) and 7(3)(b), while sports stadiums use 1:1.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Population (paragraphs 6–8 and Appendix A, Tables 1–3)
| Use | Factor |
| --- | --- |
| Shopping arcade or department store: basement, G/F, 1/F, and 2/F | 3 m² of usable floor area per person. Usable floor area includes the arcade and its public circulation |
| Shopping arcade or department store: 3/F or above | 4.5 m² per person |
| Assembly halls, auditoria, and stadia without seating or with movable seating | 0.5 m² per person on the areas intended for spectators |
| Dance halls, on the dancing area, disco, and venues for a dancing party | 0.75 m² per person |
| Museums, exhibition halls, trademarts, and display areas | 2 m² per person |
| Religious institutions and funeral parlours | 0.5 m² per person on the worship places |
| Cinema or place of public entertainment with fixed seating | The number of seats plus the estimated number of employees |
| Office | Section 5(5)(a) of the Regulations |

**SD takeaway:** For a shopping floor at basement, ground, first, or second floor, assess population at 3 m² of usable floor area per person, then split that population 1 male to 1.5 female.

### 2. Numbers for shopping arcades and department stores (Tables 4–6)
Male water closets: 1 for every 125 persons, or part, up to 500; above 500, 4 plus 1 for every additional 250 persons, or part, over 500. Male urinals: 1 for every 250 persons, or part, including above 500.

| Female persons | Water closets |
| --- | --- |
| 1–50 | 1 for every 25 persons, or part |
| 51–125 | 3 |
| 126–1,800 | 3 plus 1 for every additional 80 persons, or part, over 125 |
| More than 1,800 | 24 plus 1 for every additional 90 persons, or part, over 1,800 |

Lavatory basins, male and female separately: 1 for every 125 persons, or part, up to 500; above 500, 4 plus 1 for every additional 250 persons, or part, over 500.

**SD takeaway:** Size the female water closets on the 1-per-25 then 1-per-80 scale, which is much tighter than the male water-closet scale of 1 per 125.

### 3. Places of public entertainment, cinemas, stadiums, and worship (Tables 7–18)
| Use | Male | Female |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place of public entertainment | Water closets: 1 per 100 up to 400; above 400, 4 plus 1 per additional 250 over 400. Urinals: 1 per 50. Basins: 1 per 100 | Water closets: 2 per 50 up to 250; above 250, 10 plus 1 per additional 40 over 250. Basins: 1 per 100 |
| Cinema | Water closets: 1 up to 200; 2 for 201–500; 3 for 501–1,000; above 1,000, 3 plus 1 per additional 500 over 1,000. Urinals: 1 per 100. Basins follow the same bands as the male water closets | Water closets: 1 per 40 up to 120; 121–250, 3 plus 1 per additional 65 over 120; 251–420, 5 plus 1 per additional 85 over 250; above 420, 7 plus 1 per additional 100 over 420. Basins follow the male basin bands |
| Sports stadium | Water closets 1 per 100; urinals 1 per 100; basins 1 per 100 | Water closets 1 per 50; basins 1 per 100 |
| Religious institution or funeral parlour | Water closets 1 per 150; urinals 1 per 150; basins 1 per 300 | Water closets 1 per 75; basins 1 per 300 |

**SD takeaway:** A cinema and a place of public entertainment do not share a table: entertainment uses 1 male water closet per 100 and 2 female water closets per 50, while a cinema uses stepped male numbers and 1 female water closet per 40 at the low end.

### 4. Children, unisex toilets, and minimum space (paragraphs 11–17)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Children | Design some urinals, basins, and water closets for children’s size and height in shopping arcades, department stores, and other public places. A child’s water closet is not counted toward the prescribed numbers under sections 4 to 8 of the Regulations |
| Unisex | Consider them for a caregiver of the opposite sex, a parent with a child of the opposite sex, or a man with a baby. State on the plans whether each unisex toilet is additional or is counted as male or female, with justification |
| Water-closet cubicle, including a squatting toilet | Internal dimensions not less than 700 mm by 1,200 mm. Clear floor space in front of the water closet, inside the cubicle, not less than 700 mm by 600 mm. Circulation space outside the cubicle not less than 700 mm by 600 mm |
| Urinal | Clear floor space not less than 500 mm by 500 mm. Centreline at least 250 mm from a side wall. Where more than one, at least 500 mm centre to centre. A urinal in a cubicle: internal dimensions not less than 1,000 mm by 500 mm, with 500 mm by 500 mm clear in front, and circulation outside of not less than 500 mm by 600 mm. Every 500 mm of a trough urinal counts as one urinal |
| Lavatory basin | Clear floor space not less than 600 mm by 600 mm. Centreline at least 400 mm from a side wall. Basins at least 600 mm centre to centre. Circulation space in front of the clear floor space not less than 600 mm by 600 mm |
| Overlap | The clear floor space of one fitment must not overlap the clear floor space of another. Circulation spaces may overlap |
| Persons with a disability | Space and fitments follow the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access 2008. The dimensions above are for other users. Designs above these minima are encouraged |

**SD takeaway:** Draw each water-closet cubicle at not less than 700 mm by 1,200 mm with 700 mm by 600 mm clear in front, and do not count a child’s water closet toward the prescribed number.
