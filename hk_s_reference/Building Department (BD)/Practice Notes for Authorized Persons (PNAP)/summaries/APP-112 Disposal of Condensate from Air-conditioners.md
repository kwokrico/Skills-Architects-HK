# APP-112

**Official title:** Disposal of Condensate from Air-conditioners

**In plain words:** Condensate from air-conditioners must be collected and discharged as part of the building’s surface-water drainage. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Plans that omit the system may be disapproved under Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 28(1).
- Previously PNAP 238. First issued March 2000. This revision March 2019.
- File references: BD GP/BREG/VS/2(II); GP/BORD/92.

**What to submit**
- Show the condensate disposal system on the drainage submission.
- For an existing building that has no system, a communal system is strongly recommended when major maintenance is carried out.

**Watch-outs**
- Leaving condensate drainage off the drainage plans can lead to disapproval under section 28(1).
- Uncontrolled discharge is a nuisance and can lead to enforcement against owners and the authorized person (AP) advising them.
