# APP-114

**Official title:** Waste Minimization — Provision of Fitments and Fittings in New Buildings

**In plain words:** Sanitary fitments required by the building regulations should normally be installed before the occupation permit (OP). The Building Authority (BA) may agree to defer them where a restaurant or hotel will be heavily renovated after the OP and the fitments would only be ripped out, or where the developer lets purchasers choose the fittings. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- The modification is of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations.
- The Water Authority (WA) is notified on form WW046.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued July 2000. Revision December 2000.
- File references: BD GP/BREG/RC/2; GP/BREG/SF/1 (II).

**What to submit**
- Apply for the modification and describe which fitments are deferred.
- Give an undertaking that the outstanding fitments will be installed before that part of the building is actually occupied, that they will comply with the sanitary-fitment regulations and WA requirements, and that they will be installed under the authorized person’s (AP) supervision by a registered general building contractor (RGBC) employing a licensed plumber.
- The WA must be notified on form WW046.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not defer fitments without the modification and the undertaking.
- Occupation before the outstanding fitments are installed breaches the undertaking.
- Plumbing without a licensed plumber, or without notifying the WA, does not comply.
