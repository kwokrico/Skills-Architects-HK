# APP-40

**Official title:** Hotel Development

**In plain words:** A genuine hotel may be treated as non-domestic for site coverage (SC) and plot ratio, and certain staff-only back-of-house (BOH) rooms and the pick-up and set-down area may be disregarded from gross floor area (GFA), under Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) regulation 23A. The treatment does not apply to service apartments; plant rooms for central air-conditioning and centralised hot water are a separate disregard under regulation 23(3)(b) and practice note APP-151, inside the overall 10% GFA concession cap.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Building Authority (BA) considers regulation 23A(2) and 23A(3) where (a) the site is suitable, meaning Column 1 of the outline zoning plan (OZP) or planning permission under section 16, an intensity compatible with the surroundings, adequate streets, and parking and loading acceptable to the Transport Department, (b) basic facilities are provided, namely reception, linen stores, an admin office, and staff toilets, plus ancillary dining, entertainment, or shopping commensurate with patronage, (c) the whole hotel, or the hotel portion, has central air-conditioning and centralised hot water, and (d) there is evidence it will operate as a hotel licensed under Cap. 349. Confirmation that an operator has been appointed may be enough.
- A hotel of 100 or more guest rooms is normally expected to meet the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines (HKPSG) for parking and loading. The Transport Department assessment still applies.
- Unique supporting facilities disregarded under regulation 23A(3)(b) are a reasonable size, have staff-only access, and are unlikely to be abused. Their GFA is expected to be at most 5% of total hotel GFA unless there is a strong justification. An admin or management office is not a unique supporting facility under regulation 23A(3)(b)(iv).
- Back-of-house rooms that may be disregarded as unique: linen store, required in every hotel; laundry; carpentry and mechanical and electrical workshops; dry goods store, required where there are more than 10 rooms; stores for food, beverages, and furniture; staff canteen, changing rooms, and rest rooms; and staff toilet, required in every hotel, which may be disregarded when it is not part of the admin office. An admin office is also required where there are more than 10 rooms, and it stays in GFA.
- Other back-of-house rooms that stay in GFA: the admin and management office, purchasing and receiving, and security.
- Front-of-house (FOH) rooms that stay in GFA: reception and lobby, which are required; shopping; dining; business centre; and function rooms.
- The minimum total of required back-of-house and front-of-house provision is 3% of total hotel GFA.
- Plant rooms of a reasonable size, serving the central air-conditioning and the centralised hot water, may be disregarded under B(P)R regulation 23(3)(b), subject to the pre-requisites in practice note APP-151 and the overall 10% GFA concession cap.
- The note covers bona fide hotels only, including a new hotel and an extension. An alteration or addition that adds GFA or bulk is reassessed under the current regulation 23A. A change of use to a hotel is assessed on the same criteria. An unauthorised change of use can be sanctioned under regulations 23A(8) and 23A(9).

**What to submit**
- Justify site suitability, the facilities, central air-conditioning and centralised hot water, and the Cap. 349 evidence.
- Schedule the back-of-house and front-of-house areas against Appendix A.
- Coordinate with the Transport Department on traffic and parking.
- Include the practice note APP-151 package for the plant-room disregard and for any other GFA concession.

**Watch-outs**
- Claiming regulation 23A for service apartments.
- Counting the admin office as disregarded unique back-of-house area.
- Disregarding back-of-house area well above 5% of hotel GFA without justification.
- Missing central air-conditioning or centralised hot water.
- No evidence that the premises will be operated under a hotel licence.
- A later change from hotel to residential use, which can trigger the sanctions in regulations 23A(8) and 23A(9).
