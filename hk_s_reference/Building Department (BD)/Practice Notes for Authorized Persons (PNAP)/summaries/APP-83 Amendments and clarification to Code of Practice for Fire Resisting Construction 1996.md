# APP-83

**Official title:** Amendments and clarification to Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996

**In plain words:** This note corrects the 1996 Fire Resisting Construction (FRC) Code, including compartment-wall openings and raised floors. It does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but the opening-width limits and the sprinkler trade-off still affect compartment design and fire-service coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 199. First issued August 1996. Last revision August 2011. This revision November 2012 (paragraph 2(i) amended).
- January 1996 edition, as corrected in later reprints: on page 9, paragraph 7.2, delete “but in no case less than 2 hours”.
- At the end of paragraph 17.2 on page 18, add the “to be kept closed” fire-door diagram in English and Chinese.
- Table C on page 24: concrete cover for a 2-hour fire resistance period (FRP) to continuous floors and landings of solid reinforced concrete is 25 mm, not 40 mm.
- Revised paragraph 10.1, for openings that let people pass between adjoining compartments (not openings that combine them), protected by a door or fire shutter:
  - Total opening width of 25% or less of the compartment-wall length: FRP matching the wall for integrity only.
  - Total opening width above 25%: FRP matching the wall for integrity and insulation.
  - That insulation FRP may drop to 30 minutes if extra sprinkler heads on each side are part of the building sprinkler system, follow the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, cover each side in full, and are spaced to the Loss Prevention Council (LPC) Rules incorporating BS EN 12845:2003.
- The paragraph 10.1 revision applies to consent on or after 18 August 2011. For a new building, that means consent to start foundation works. For alteration and addition, it means consent to start those works.
- On page 14, fourth line, add “with an FRP as described in (iii) above” after “located”.
- On page 32, replace “fixed light;” with “fixed light with FRP ≥ ½ FRPe;” (at least half the fire resistance period of the element).
- A raised floor 600 mm or less above the structural floor is not an element of construction and needs no FRP. Compartment and fire-separation walls must start from the structural floor.

**What to submit**
- Use the updated FRC Code on www.bd.gov.hk.
- Where openings exceed 25% of the wall and insulation is reduced, show the extra sprinklers on the plans and in the fire-service installation submission.
- Check the consent date against 18 August 2011 before using revised paragraph 10.1.

**Watch-outs**
- A compartment wall must not rest on a raised floor of 600 mm or less.
- An opening wider than 25% of the wall needs integrity and insulation, unless the extra sprinklers comply.
- Using 40 mm cover instead of the corrected 25 mm for a 2-hour continuous floor or landing is wrong.
- Applying revised paragraph 10.1 to a foundation consent before 18 August 2011, or failing to apply it on or after that date, is wrong.
