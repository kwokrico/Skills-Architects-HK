# ADV-4

**Official title:** Control of Environmental Nuisance from Construction Sites

**In plain words:** Appendix A is a checklist of how to limit noise, dust, dirty water, pests, and road obstruction from a construction site, which department enforces each item, and what the penalties are. This note does not change permanent gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Wheel-wash bays, silt traps, waste sorting, and the hoarding do affect the temporary site layout. Hoardings are also covered by APP-23. A similar note goes to registered contractors.

**Numbers that matter**
- Noise, Noise Control Ordinance. Percussive piling between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. on a day that is not a public holiday needs a construction noise permit, and is generally prohibited at other times. Restricted hours are 7:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m., and any time on a general holiday including Sunday. In those hours, powered mechanical equipment for general construction, and prescribed construction work in a designated area that is predominantly built up, also need a permit. Prescribed work includes erecting or dismantling formwork or scaffolding; loading or unloading rubble, boards, bars, wood, or scaffolding; and hammering. The maximum fine is level 6 on a first conviction, and $200,000 on a subsequent conviction.
- Air and construction dust. Cover or dampen stockpiles. Hard-pave haul roads and water them. Cover loads. Wash wheels. Water the site. Erect a boundary hoarding. A concrete batching plant needs a specified-process licence if the law requires one. Notify the Environmental Protection Department beforehand for site formation, reclamation, demolition, tunnel works except the part more than 100 m from the exit to the open air, building foundation, building superstructure, and road construction. No notice: maximum $25,000 first conviction, $50,000 subsequently. No dust-reduction measures: $50,000 first, and $100,000 plus 3 months’ imprisonment subsequently. A specified process without a licence: $200,000 plus 6 months, and $20,000 a day. Open burning under the Air Pollution Control (Open Burning) Regulation: $50,000 plus $500 for every 15 minutes it continues, on a first conviction. Demolition without fans, catch platforms, or dust screens, under the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations: maximum $25,000 (level 4).
- Drains and water. Provide temporary drainage with de-silting before you connect to a main, sand and grease traps, perimeter channels or sandbags, and a pH or petrol interceptor where needed. Wash wheels. Seal sewer and drainage connections before demolition or foundation work. Do not connect surface water illicitly into a foul sewer. A water-pollution control licence is required for construction discharges. Discharging polluting matter: maximum $200,000 plus 6 months on a first offence. An unsealed demolition connection: $25,000 (level 4) under the demolition regulations. Foreign matter in a public drain: maximum $50,000 (level 5) under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance.
- Health. Drain or pump stagnant water that breeds mosquitoes. If you cannot, spray diesel weekly, cover containers, level the ground, and remove tyres and cans. For flies, rodents, and refuse: cover food and refuse, remove it daily, provide proper latrines, and seal holes. For chemical waste: register as a chemical waste producer, package, label, and store it, and use a licensed collector to a licensed disposal site. Under the Waste Disposal Ordinance and the Chemical Waste (General) Regulation, the maximum is $200,000 plus 6 months.
- Roads, footpaths, and hoardings. Secure loads. Wash wheels and the vehicle body. Follow the Excavation Permit Processing Manual. Keep the minimum footpath width. Light, sign, and guard the works as the Highways Department code requires. Agree temporary traffic signs with the Transport Department. Hoarding width follows the approved hoarding plan. See APP-23. An unsound, inadequate, or hazardous hoarding is dealt with under the Building (Planning) Regulations.
- If the project is a Schedule 2 designated project under the Environmental Impact Assessment Ordinance, obtain an environmental permit before construction or operation, and comply with every permit condition.

**What to submit**
- To the Buildings Department side: demolition dust screens, fans, and catch platforms; sealed drainage connections; the footpath repair interface; and hoarding-plan compliance under the Building (Planning) Regulations, see APP-23.
- Separately: a construction noise permit, a water-pollution licence, construction-dust notification, and chemical-waste registration to the Environmental Protection Department; an excavation permit to the Highways Department; and traffic management with the Transport Department and the Police.

**Watch-outs**
- Restricted-hours work, or percussive piling, without a construction noise permit.
- Superstructure, foundation, or demolition without the dust notification, or without dust measures.
- Demolition without sealed drains, or without fans, catch platforms, and dust screens.
- Temporary toilets or kitchens discharging to storm water, which is a water-pollution offence.
- A hoarding that is unsound, too narrow, or obstructing, compared with the approved plan.
- Starting a Schedule 2 designated project without an environmental permit.
