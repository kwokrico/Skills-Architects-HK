# APP-101

**Official title:** Podium Height Restriction under Building (Planning) Regulation 20(3)

**In plain words:** This note explains how the Building Authority (BA) looks at a request to relax the rule that a non-domestic podium may cover the whole site only up to 15 m high, which changes site coverage (SC). The BA weighs function, site limits, the environment, public interest, and the chance of abuse, and cases that often help include a public transport terminus or similar use, a taller podium that looks better or is kinder to the surroundings, a building of special design or use, and a lease that needs special floor heights.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Planning) Regulation (B(P)R) 20(3) limits full site coverage of a non-domestic podium to 15 m high. The index also cites B(P)R 23(3).
- If a modification is granted, the podium is still limited to not more than 4 storeys and not more than 20 m from ground level.
- First issued April 1998. Re-issued August 2009.
- File reference: GP/BREG/P/2.

**What to submit**
- Apply for a relaxation or modification of the B(P)R 20(3) podium height limit. The BA decides under this practice note.

**Watch-outs**
- Going above 15 m without an approved modification breaches B(P)R.
- A favourable example can still be refused if there is a risk of abuse, harm to the environment, or a weak functional reason.
- Do not treat a modification as an open-ended podium height. The cap remains 4 storeys and 20 m.
- The authorized person (AP) must still fit the outline zoning plan (OZP), the lease, and other site coverage and plot ratio (PR) limits.
