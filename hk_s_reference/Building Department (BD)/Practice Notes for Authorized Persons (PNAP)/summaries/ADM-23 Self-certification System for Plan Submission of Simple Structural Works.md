# ADM-23

**Official title:** Self-certification System for Plan Submission of Simple Structural Works

**In plain words:** For a short list of simple structural works, the registered structural engineer (RSE) can certify the plans, and the Buildings Department then checks mainly that the papers are complete and that a few fundamental items are right. Categories C, D, and E also need an independent checking engineer. This speeds processing. It does not create a gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) concession. The parent building’s density still follows the approved general building plans.

**Numbers that matter**
- Every category uses only conventional materials: reinforced concrete, precast reinforced concrete, structural glass, steel, stainless steel, aluminium, stone, and other materials in the Central Data Bank. The work must not affect the overall structural stability of the parent building. There is no prestressed structure and no retaining structure.
- Category A, RSE only: new development works of the same nature, scale, and complexity as a minor-works Schedule 1 item, or minor works in an existing building that are submitted for approval and consent instead of the minor-works system.
- Category B, RSE only, and not Category A and not exempted or designated exempted works. Erection, with anchors and supports, of:
  - Metal or glass cladding, a wind-loaded suspended false ceiling, or an exterior or suspended louvre or grille: highest point not above 100 m above ground; the least side of the panel’s rectangle not more than 2 m; panel area not more than 6 m².
  - Vertical-greening proprietary modular units: height and width not more than 0.75 m, projection not more than 0.2 m, and the same 100 m height limit.
  - A metal or glass canopy: the same panel limits, and projection not more than 2 m from the column, wall, or beam.
  - Internal frames that suspend building services: imposed load not more than 2 kPa, and each item not more than 2,000 kg.
  - Glass-reinforced plastic tank frames, or building-services frames, on grade or on a slab: imposed load not more than 20 kPa, and each item not more than 9,000 kg.
  - Internal maintenance-platform frames: design imposed load not more than 2 kPa.
- Category C, RSE plus an independent checking engineer: stone cladding on a new development, not Category A, with the same 100 m, 2 m, and 6 m² limits.
- Category D, RSE plus an independent checking engineer: a single-storey refuse room, plant room, or covered walkway with span not more than 8 m, height not more than 5 m, and no basement; or a detached or semi-detached single-family house of not more than 3 storeys, span not more than 8 m, storey height not more than 4.5 m, and no basement. These may sit on a podium, a shallow foundation, or a pile cap on piles. Associated works (curtain wall, skylight, precast elements, an external steel maintenance platform, a metal stair, a cat-ladder other than designated exempted works item 28, and similar) are allowed if the general criteria are met.
- Category E, RSE plus an independent checking engineer: a shallow foundation (spread, pad, strip, raft, or similar) that supports Category D. Allowable pressure not more than 300 kPa, or not more than 150 kPa if below the groundwater table. Not on soft clay or mud. Founding not more than 3 m below existing ground level. Not in Schedule 5 Areas 1, 3, or 5. Overall gradient within 10 m downhill not steeper than 15 degrees, and no slope steeper than 15 degrees in that zone. No retaining wall or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m in that zone, and none that rises above a 45-degree line drawn from the base of the footing.
- The RSE submits Form BA5, or BA6 for alteration and addition, the Appendix B1 checklist, and the Appendix C certificate. Plans and calculations follow ADM-8. Appendix D standard statements go on the plans and calculations.
- For Categories C to E, the independent checking engineer must be an RSE on the register, appointed by the owner, with no conflict of interest. Appendix E (appointment and declaration) is submitted on or before the plan submission. The engineer completes the Appendix B2 checklist, Appendix C Part B, and the Appendix D statement.
- The Department’s curtailed check looks at whether the documents are complete, whether the people are registered, and whether these items are correct: compatibility with the supporting structure, fire resistance rating, design loads, codes, materials and workmanship specifications, testing criteria, and corrosion resistance.
- Processing times:

| Submission | Through the Electronic Submission Hub | Other than the Hub |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First submission, resubmission, or major revision | 20 days | 40 days |
| Amendment, including Type II works seeking fast-track consent | 10 days | 20 days |

- Once you adopt self-certification, later resubmissions and amendments should stay on it. If you drop it, the submission may be treated under the normal Building (Administration) Regulation 30 time. You may still introduce self-certification on a later submission even if the first one was normal.
- Approval and consent can run together for Categories A to E if the corresponding building plans are already approved. Follow ADM-19 and submit every consent supporting document.
- The Department audits fundamental issues at random. Misrepresentation or misconduct leads to action under the Buildings Ordinance.
- Footing factors of safety on checklist B1 include, as examples: sliding and uplift at least 1.5; overturning at least 1.5 for wind or groundwater and at least 2 for other cases; anchorage at least 3. An optional factor of 1.1 may be used for the highest groundwater level, as the Foundation Code clause 2.5.4 allows.

**What to submit**
- Checklist B1 for every category, and B2 for Categories C to E. Appendix C in duplicate, one for the owner and one for the authorized person. Appendix D statements on the plans. Appendix E for Categories C to E.
- Form BA8A if you also want consent at the same time.

**Watch-outs**
- Certifying work outside the list (prestress, a retaining structure, a foundation in Area 1, 3, or 5, an oversize panel, cladding above 100 m, and similar) is a false certificate. The forms say that is a criminal offence and can lead to disciplinary action.
- An independent checking engineer who has a conflict of interest, or who was not appointed by the owner, is not acceptable.
- Dropping self-certification part way through puts you back on the normal processing time.
- Concurrent consent without approved building plans, or without the full ADM-19 consent package, is not available.
- A misrepresentation found on audit leads to sanctions against the RSE and the independent checking engineer.
