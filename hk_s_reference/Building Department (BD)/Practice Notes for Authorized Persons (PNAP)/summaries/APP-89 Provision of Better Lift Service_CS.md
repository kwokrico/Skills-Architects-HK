# APP-89 Provision of Better Lift Service
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This version June 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 207; first issue August 1997)

> Scope note: When lift-shaft area above a stated percentage of gross floor area may be excluded from gross floor area and site coverage, for domestic, hotel, and office buildings. Fireman's lifts and accessible lifts remain required separately. The concession is also subject to the prerequisites in PNAP APP-151. Buildings that already have an occupation permit are not considered. Completed phases of a phased development are not considered, and gross floor area and site coverage are not transferred from them.

## Regulatory Overview
Lift and lift-shaft areas are accountable under regulations 20 and 23(3)(a) of the Building (Planning) Regulations. The Building Authority may favourably exclude shaft area that exceeds the average standard, up to the caps in paragraph 5, if each car and shaft meets the minimum internal areas and a registered lift engineer or a building-services engineer certifies that handling capacity and waiting time are above the acceptance level in international codes and that maintenance space is adequate.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How much shaft area may be disregarded (paragraphs 5–6)
| Building | Exempt area |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic or composite building, including a hotel | Shaft area over and above 2.5% of total domestic or hotel gross floor area, but the exempted area is in no case more than 3.5% of that gross floor area. If the shafts exceed 6%, the maximum exemption is 3.5% |
| Office, or office/commercial, with office-floor gross floor area less than 10,000 m2 | Area over and above 5% of office gross floor area, capped at 3%. If the shafts exceed 8%, the maximum exemption is 3% |
| Office, or office/commercial, with office-floor gross floor area of 10,000 m2 or more | Area over and above 3.5%, capped at 2.5%. If the shafts exceed 6%, the maximum exemption is 2.5%. Between 10,000 m2 and 12,000 m2, a maximum exempted area of 300 m2 may be allowed |
| What is included | Shafts at podium floors whose lobbies serve only the domestic, hotel, or office accommodation. Shafts serving non-accountable storeys such as a refuge floor are excluded. Office gross floor area includes ancillary office use and facilities, and excludes other commercial use |
| Site coverage | The gross-floor-area exemption divided by the number of storeys occupied by those shafts |

**SD takeaway:** Size domestic or hotel lift shafts so that the area above 2.5% of that gross floor area, and not more than 3.5%, can be disregarded, and do not count refuge-floor shafts in the concession.

### 2. Minimum car and shaft sizes (paragraph 8)
| Use | Minimum internal area |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic or composite building, each lift car | 1.82 m2 |
| Domestic or composite building, each lift shaft | 4.12 m2 |
| Office accommodation, each lift car | 2.1 m2 |
| Office accommodation, each lift shaft | 4.4 m2 |
| Certificate | A registered lift engineer under the Lifts and Escalators Ordinance, Cap. 618, or a registered professional engineer in the building-services discipline under the Engineers Registration Ordinance, Cap. 409 |

**SD takeaway:** Do not claim the concession unless every domestic car is at least 1.82 m2 inside a 4.12 m2 shaft, or every office car is at least 2.1 m2 inside a 4.4 m2 shaft, with the engineer's certificate.
