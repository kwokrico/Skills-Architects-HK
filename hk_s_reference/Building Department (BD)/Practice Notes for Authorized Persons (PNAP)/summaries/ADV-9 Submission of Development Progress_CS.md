# ADV-9 Submission of Development Progress
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 1998 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issue December 1994)

> Scope note: A twice-yearly statistical return of private projects in hand. The area definitions are for that return and are not the gross-floor-area rules in the Building (Planning) Regulations.

## Regulatory Overview
Authorized persons are asked to complete Appendix A in early March and early September each year for every private building project in hand, including projects where the client has instructed preparation of building proposals. The return goes to the Director of Buildings, attention Chief Officer/Management, by fax on 2523 9380 or 2840 0451 or by post.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the return measures (Appendix A notes)
| Column | Definition in the note |
| --- | --- |
| Occupation permit | Expected date of a temporary or full occupation permit |
| Progress | Comments such as building plans approved, foundation completed, constructed to 4th floor, external finishing completed |
| Domestic unit | A self-contained dwelling with separate cooking facilities and a bathroom and/or lavatory |
| Saleable area | Floor area exclusively allocated to the unit, including balconies and verandahs, excluding common areas such as stairs, lift shafts, pipe ducts, lobbies, and communal toilets. Measured from the outside of the exterior enclosing walls and the middle of the party walls. For this return, exclude bay windows, yards, gardens, terraces, flat roofs, carports, and the like |
| Non-domestic units | If a floor is not yet partitioned, count the floor as one unit. The note's example: 5 units per floor on 1/F to 3/F and unpartitioned floors from 4/F to 10/F gives 22 units |
| Internal floor area | All enclosed space of a unit, measured to the internal face of enclosing external and/or party walls |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a March and September schedule of occupation-permit dates and unit counts, and do not use this saleable-area definition as the gross-floor-area calculation.
