# APP-98

**Official title:** Lighting and Ventilation for Bathrooms and Lavatories in Domestic Buildings

**In plain words:** An internal bathroom or lavatory in a home can be accepted without the usual window if the Building Authority (BA) modifies Building (Planning) Regulations regulation 36 and the conditions below are met. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 219. First issued December 1997. Last revision February 2010. This revision June 2013.
- The modification is for a room that is part of a domestic unit, of a reasonable size, and unlikely to harm public health or safety. It covers new and existing domestic buildings.
- When granted, the room needs artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation giving 5 air changes an hour whenever it is in use, changing air with the outside. A ventilation duct with cleaning access is acceptable.
- It also needs permanent ventilation to “open air” as defined in regulation 2, or to “external air” as defined in regulation 31, or to another room whose window is sized for both rooms together. That may be a duct with cleaning access, or a wall or door opening that stays open or is louvred, at least 1/20 of the floor area.
- Unless the duct is demountable or replaceable, provide an access door or panel at least 150 × 225 mm where the duct is 150 × 150 mm or larger, or at least 100 × 225 mm for a smaller duct, every 4 m, at every change of direction, and next to turning vanes or dampers, where it can be reached.
- Comply with regulation 35A and APP-27 on water heating where they apply.
- Each unit has its own lighting, mechanical ventilation, and ducting, or a communal system with proper management. For a new building, or for completed alteration and addition, a property management company is accepted as evidence of proper management.
- Annual inspection and cleaning of the ducts is preferred.

**What to submit**
- Apply to modify regulation 36 and show that the room meets the three criteria. Expect the conditions above on the permit.
- If ventilation is communal, show the management arrangements.

**Watch-outs**
- An internal bathroom without the modification, or without 5 air changes an hour plus the permanent vent, is not acceptable.
- Shared mechanical ventilation without management arrangements fails the condition.
- A duct without access panels every 4 m and at bends cannot be cleaned.
- A water heater in an internal bathroom must still meet APP-27 and regulation 35A.
