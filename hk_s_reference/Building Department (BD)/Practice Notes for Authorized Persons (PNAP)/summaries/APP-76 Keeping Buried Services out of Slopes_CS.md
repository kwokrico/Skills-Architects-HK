# APP-76 Keeping Buried Services out of Slopes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued June 1995 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Routing of buried services so that leakage does not threaten slopes and retaining walls. Water-carrying services and dry conduits such as telephone ducts, cable ducts, and disused pipes can all transmit water. The guidelines are not exhaustive. Read with Section 9.7 and Chapter 5 of the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes, and with PNAP 168 for record plans.

## Regulatory Overview
As a general rule, buried services should not be placed in a slope nearer to the crest than a distance equal to the vertical height of the slope. Where siting outside that crest area is impractical, design the slope to the factors of safety in Chapter 5, taking leakage into account, or use the measures in paragraph 6.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Crest area, fill slopes, and records (paragraphs 5–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Existing services | Consider re-routing when a slope in or next to the lot is upgraded, or when services are relaid or repaired |
| If services must stay in the crest area | Lay them on or above the ground, with protection against corrosion or damage and an acceptable appearance; or house them in a duct, sealed trench, or sleeve drained to a surface-water system or natural stream, with the discharge monitored and inspection chambers for access; or, for water mains, install stop valves at each side of the slope |
| Fill slope | Allow for differential settlement of the services and their ancillary facilities, especially after leakage |
| Sewers and storm drains in fill where settlement may be large | Avoid rigid pipelines. Use flexible joints and short pipe pieces. Consider non-brittle materials so the pipe does not shear |
| As-built record | Update building, drainage, site formation, and water-services plans, incorporate them in the slope and retaining-wall record plans in PNAP 168, and give copies to the developer and to the owners or managers who will maintain the services |

**SD takeaway:** Keep buried services at least one slope-height back from the crest; if they must cross the crest of a fill slope, use short flexible pipes in a drained sleeve and show them on the PNAP 168 record plans.
