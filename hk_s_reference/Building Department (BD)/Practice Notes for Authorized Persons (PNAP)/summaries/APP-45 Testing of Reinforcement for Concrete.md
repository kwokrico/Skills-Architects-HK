# APP-45

**Official title:** Testing of Reinforcement for Concrete

**In plain words:** The Building Authority (BA) may require purchaser’s tests on the reinforcing steel delivered to the site or to the prefabrication factory, in addition to the mill tests, under Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 17(1)6. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- The acceptable standard is Construction Standard CS2:2012. Specimens are taken at random from every batch. The condition is imposed at structural approval or at consent, to verify the steel actually delivered.
- CS2:2012 covers straight bars, coil, and decoiled products, except cold-worked bars. The grades are 250, for bars of 12 mm or less, 500B, and 500C. The tests cover mass per metre, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and bond. The scheme includes quality-assured stockists, quality-assured coil processors, and the purchaser’s tests.
- The purchaser’s tests are carried out by a laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS). The report includes the Class 1 or Class 2 quality-assured stockist classification; the manufacturer, country of origin, grade, and size; the deformation pattern and the mill marks; the batch identity and the date of delivery to the site or the factory; the measured mass per metre; the product chemistry; the tensile and bend results; and the bond result where it is required.
- For coil and decoiled bar: Amendment No. 2/2018 and Technical Note No. 5. Every coil processor is a quality-assured coil processor. A yard approved under Development Bureau Technical Circular (Works) (DEVB TC(W)) 10/2018 is recognised.
- Prefabricated reinforcement from an Approved Yard, within that yard’s approved scope, is treated as conforming to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition) where the qualified supervision required by the Buildings Ordinance is in place.
- Approval of reinforced-concrete plans typically imposes section 17(1)6 qualified supervision by the registered structural engineer (RSE) and by the registered general building contractor (RGBC) or registered specialist contractor (RSC), for sampling and testing.
- On the streamlined Approved Yard path, batch sampling under a quality management system (QMS), witnessed by the independent audit team (IAT), is accepted. The RSE and the contractor check the sampling and witness it. The yard keeps the HOKLAS reports, and copies are kept on site after delivery. Within 21 days of delivery, submit to the Buildings Department a list of the products and a statement by the RSE that the reinforcement complies. The alternative is the contractor’s own quality control supervisor (QCS) and quality control coordinator (QCC) arrangements for sampling and testing under CS2.

**What to submit**
- Carry out the testing imposed under section 17, and keep the HOKLAS reports.
- For products from an Approved Yard: keep the records on site, and submit the RSE’s compliance statement within 21 days of delivery.

**Watch-outs**
- Sampling that is not random, or that does not cover every batch.
- A laboratory that is not accredited by HOKLAS.
- No delivery confirmation to the Buildings Department within 21 days for prefabricated reinforcement.
- Relying on mill certificates alone, as if they were the purchaser’s tests.
