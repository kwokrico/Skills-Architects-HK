# APP-13

**Official title:** Submission of Certificate of Completion of Building Works, Application for Occupation Permit and Submission of Record Plans and Information

**In plain words:** This note sets the paperwork for completion, the occupation permit (OP), record plans, and the soft copies that must go with them. It does not change how gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) is measured, but an incomplete APP-151 package (the practice note on GFA concession preconditions and the overall cap) can still block the OP.

**Numbers that matter**
- Completion is certified on Forms BA14, BA14A, BA14B, and BA14C, and/or MW02 or MW04 where the works do not create a new building, or on BA12 or BA13 for a new building, under Building (Administration) Regulation 25. Typical reply times: BA14 and BA14A, 28 days; BA14B and BA14C, 14 days if not audited and 28 days if audited; BA12 or BA13 together with the OP, within 14 days (Buildings Ordinance section 21).
- If the same contractor builds a spread footing (excavation not deeper than 4.5 m) and the superstructure, one BA12/BA13 or one BA14 can cover both. A separate footing BA14 is not required.
- Amend the plans and get approval before the OP. A minor shift in position that does not breach the Buildings Ordinance may be acceptable.
- Record plans (regulation 46 “new plans” where the alterations are major): an amalgamated set of general building plans as completed, carrying the Building Authority approval chop or an authorized person (AP) certificate that they match the approved plans. Soft copy on DVD-ROM, or a photo-reduction at 1:200 or larger. Show no unapproved amendment. An AutoCAD or Microstation DVD is encouraged for the Rating and Valuation Department.
- A materials schedule under Building (Administration) Regulation 44 goes with the OP. The AP certifies compliance and the registered contractor confirms the materials were used. Cover structural-integrity and fire-safety products that are not on the approved plans, and cast-iron pipes (APP-133). Fire-rated doorsets and partitions may use product-conformity certificates under APP-165. A registered specialist contractor in ventilation inspects and certifies fire dampers in safe working order (Fire Safety Code clause E8.3).
- The registered structural engineer submits structural-document summary tables half-yearly, and again at foundation completion and at the OP (Appendices C to E).
- OP soft copies go on DVD for the items uploadable under ADM-2, APP-126, APP-151, APP-152, and APP-156.
- A fast-track BA14C is available for simple alteration and addition under ADM-19 paragraph 29; curtain-wall, window-wall, or cladding repair and replacement under ADM-19 paragraph 32; and signboards under APP-126 paragraphs 8 to 10. Those cases need record plans or documents, plus colour photos or mark-ups.

**What to submit**
- The BA12, BA13, and BA14 series, as the case requires. Materials schedules in Appendices A and B. The ventilation specialist’s certificate for dampers. Checklists in Appendices F and G. Use a DVD for the paperless set, except the hard-copy certificates the note still requires. Index the discs as in Appendix H, and put the AP’s declaration on the disc cover.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not apply for the OP at the same time as you submit amendment plans that are not yet approved.
- Record plans must not show unapproved deviations. A false materials certificate carries the criminal risk printed on the forms.
- Missing APP-151 or BEAM-related soft copies at OP can stop the permit.
- Late structural documents from the registered structural engineer delay BA14 and the OP.
