# APP-138

**Official title:** Appointment of Authorised Signatory to Act for Registered Contractor and Temporary absence of Authorised Signatories

**In plain words:** This note says which authorised signatory (AS) acts for the registered contractor, and how a short-term stand-in is appointed. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 290. First issue May 2004. See also PNRC 38 and PNRC 59.
- Only one AS is accepted per project for the registered contractor. The AS who signs Form BA10 is the responsible AS.
- On Forms BA 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 14A, 18, and 20, the signing AS is responsible for the contractor’s Buildings Ordinance duties.
- From 1 July 2004, the AS name must appear on the specified forms, or the form is incomplete and unacceptable.
- Change of AS where the contractor has more than one AS: notify the Building Authority on PNRC 59 Appendix A, with the authorized person countersigning, at least 7 days before the change.
- Temporary AS: PNRC 59 Appendix B. Give at least 7 days’ notice, or 3 days’ documentary proof for sudden illness or accident. The nominee is another AS of the same contractor on the same register or sub-register, or an AS of another contractor with authorisation. Maximum 14 days acting, and 30 days in total with special proof.
- A temporary AS may sign inspection records only. Not supervision plans or the specified forms.
- Absence of more than 30 days needs a formal AS change, or a new AS added to the registration.
- A firm with only one AS (and that is not a sole proprietor) cannot change the project AS until a new AS is added under PNRC 38.
- Works with no AS acting must stop under Building (Administration) Regulation 22(2).

**What to submit**
- Put the AS name on all the forms above, matching the supervision plan.
- Lodge the change or temporary nomination notices as set out above. The authorized person countersigns a change of AS.

**Watch-outs**
- An AS on the supervision plan who does not match Forms BA10, BA13, or BA14 leads to rejection.
- A temporary AS who signs forms or plans makes those documents invalid.
- If no AS is acting, works must cease under Building (Administration) Regulation 22(2).
