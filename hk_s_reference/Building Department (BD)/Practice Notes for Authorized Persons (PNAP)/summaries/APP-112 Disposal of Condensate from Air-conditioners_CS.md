# APP-112 Disposal of Condensate from Air-conditioners
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 2019 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 238; first issue March 2000)

> Scope note: Condensate from air-conditioners is to be drained as part of the surface water drainage system. It is not an air-conditioning plant or energy code.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority requires a proper disposal system for air-conditioner condensate as part of the building’s surface water drainage. Drainage plans that do not show the system will be disapproved under section 28(1) of the Buildings Ordinance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New buildings (paragraph 1)
Indicate the condensate disposal system in the drainage submission. Plans that omit it will be disapproved under section 28(1) of the Buildings Ordinance.

**SD takeaway:** Draw a condensate drain into the surface-water system on the drainage plans, or the submission will be disapproved.

### 2. Existing buildings (paragraph 2)
Where an existing building has no disposal system, clients should be advised to install a communal system when comprehensive maintenance works are carried out.

**SD takeaway:** On a comprehensive maintenance job, add a communal condensate drain if the building does not already have one.
