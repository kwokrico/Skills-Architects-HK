# ADM-17

**Official title:** Submission of Plans and Documents in Electronic Format

**In plain words:** The Electronic Transactions Ordinance (Cap. 553) lets you submit plans and forms electronically. This note sets the file rules, the systems, and when the statutory clock starts. It does not set a gross floor area (GFA) concession. Computer checks of floor area sit in ADM-19.

**Numbers that matter**
- Section 5 of the Ordinance treats an electronic record that can be used later as writing. Section 6 treats a digital signature, made with a recognised certificate that is still valid, as a signature. Those provisions have been in force since 7 April 2000.
- The Electronic Submission Hub (https://esh.bd.gov.hk) takes plan submissions 24 hours a day, except during maintenance. Sign in with a personal digital certificate or iAM Smart.
- e-Counter (receipt@bd.gov.hk) accepts email of 25 MB or less in total.
- The Electronic Forms Submission System takes certain specified forms, 25 MB or less in total, with digital signatures.
- The Registered Minor Works Management System (https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk) takes certain specified forms and supporting documents. It launched on 29 May 2026. Each form is 25 MB or less. Hongkong Post, Digi-Sign, or iAM Smart may be used.
- Hub stages in Appendix A: Stage 1 from 30 June 2022 covered above-ground structural plans with no cross-department referral, excluding alteration and addition (A&A). Stage 2 from 31 March 2023 added hoarding, covered walkway, gantry, demolition, drainage, excavation and lateral support, ground investigation, foundation, site formation, and all structural plans, still excluding A&A. Stage 3 from 30 June 2024 covers all plan types, including general building plans and A&A, and the related applications.
- An electronic submission is one set. The Building Authority may still ask for extra hard copies under Building (Administration) Regulation 11(2), and may reply on paper.
- Hub plan files (Appendix B): PDF with no security; one drawing per file; filename of 50 alphanumeric characters or fewer; file of 25 MB or less; signed with the Hub tool; paper size as in ADM-10, landscape; unused stamp space of 90 mm by 150 mm at the lower right. Prefer a PDF from the native CAD or BIM file. A scan must be at least 300 dpi and 24-bit colour. Do not use multiple layers or post-edited mark-up.
- Other documents: single-layer PDF, 500 MB or less, with a stamp space of 100 mm by 65 mm on the front page. Extra CAD or BIM files are 500 MB or less each. See ADM-19 and ADV-34.
- The electronic plan index (Appendix C) is online. Drawing title, number, and revision are each 50 characters or fewer. The index compiles itself if the title block has the captions “Drawing Title:”, “Drawing Number:”, and “Revision Number:” as machine-readable text, on separate rows.
- e-Counter: specified forms as PDF, digitally signed in the specified places. Other records as PDF (each page no larger than A3) or as TIFF or JPEG at 200 dpi, with adjacent sides no larger than 3300 by 2340 pixels, in 24-bit colour. Each document is digitally signed. The whole email is 25 MB or less.
- For the Hub, the statutory period starts the next day after the day of receipt, even if that next day is a public holiday, a gale warning, or a black rainstorm warning. Example: receipt at 23:59 on Friday or Saturday starts the clock on Sunday. Receipt at 00:00 on Sunday starts the clock on Monday.
- A digitally signed Form BA10 through the Hub: the Department will look favourably on a modification of Building (Administration) Regulation 20(1) so that notice of commencement may be not less than 1 day before the start, instead of the longer usual notice. You may apply for that with the consent application. The condition is that the BA10 itself goes through the Hub. Building (Administration) Regulation 20(2) and (3) still apply: technically competent person appointment details and the supervision plan at least 7 days before commencement.
- After the first Hub submission, later amendments should stay on the Hub.
- Identity is checked by a digital certificate of the Postmaster General or another recognised certification authority. A submission that does not follow the gazetted format, manner, or procedure is invalid.
- Using the Hub means you accept it as the designated system for receiving the Department’s electronic records, under section 19(2)(a)(i) of the Ordinance.
- You must be able to keep electronic records on site and produce them to the Building Authority under Building (Administration) Regulations 36 and 40.
- Further detail on the forms systems is in APP-60.

**What to submit**
- Plans and forms through the Hub once your plan type is in scope, in the file format above. Keep equipment on site so electronic records can be produced.

**Watch-outs**
- The wrong format, an oversized file, a missing digital signature, or a multi-layer PDF makes the submission invalid, and the clock may not run as you expect.
- The 1-day BA10 modification does not waive the 7-day rule for the technically competent person and the supervision plan.
- Switching to paper amendments after you started on the Hub breaks the tracking. Stay on the Hub.
- Being unable to produce the electronic records on site breaches regulations 36 and 40.
