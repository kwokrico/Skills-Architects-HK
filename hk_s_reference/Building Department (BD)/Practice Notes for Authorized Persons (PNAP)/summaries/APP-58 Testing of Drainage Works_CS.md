# APP-58 Testing of Drainage Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued April 1993 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: The authorized person's role when drains are tested under Regulation 73 of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations. The Building Authority's test is a monitoring role; compliance remains with the registered contractor and the authorized person.

## Regulatory Overview
Applications for drain testing under Regulation 73(1) must be copied to the authorized person, who or whose representative attends, and the test proceeds and is recorded even if the Building Authority is not present. Within 7 days of attending, the authorized person submits a certificate on completion of drain test in the Annex I format, with the endorsed results.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Deviations and the certificate (paragraphs 2–3 and Annex I)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Untested drains at occupation-permit stage | Identified as a concern. Testing must be duly carried out, recorded, and endorsed |
| Changes to pipe runs, alignment, or invert levels | If deviations are so extensive as to warrant it, submit an amended plan |
| Projects with many tests | Developments requiring more than 100 drain tests are not uncommon; fragmented testing is to be managed by the authorized person attending and certifying |
| Certificate | Confirms the test was witnessed, encloses endorsed test results and record plans under Building (Administration) Regulation 44, and certifies compliance with BS 8301:1985 and satisfaction with the results. Copy the registered contractor |

**SD takeaway:** Keep drainage on the approved alignment and inverts, or amend the plans before the test, and file the Annex I certificate within 7 days certifying BS 8301:1985.
