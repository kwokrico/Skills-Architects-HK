# APP-163

**Official title:** Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance 2021

**In plain words:** External walls, curtain walls, cladding, roofs, and projections need a means of access for maintenance and repair, under the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance, 2024 Edition, which consolidates the TC amendments and replaces the Code of Practice on Design for Safety – External Maintenance 2019. This note sets no gross floor area concession, and those features are still counted under the Buildings Ordinance and the Building (Planning) Regulations unless another note exempts them.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue February 2021. This revision September 2024. Paragraphs 3 to 5 were amended. Paragraph 6 and Appendix A were added.
- Statutory basis: Building (Construction) Regulations sections 27(2), 28(5), 31(3), and 34(3).
- Buildings Ordinance section 39(2) grandfathers works that were under way, or already consented, as at 31 January 2021. Foundation works of the proposed development generally count.
- New paragraph 3.2.4, Appendix A item 1: at least one anchor for a personal fall-arrest system at every balcony and every utility platform (UP). The anchor must be easy to reach from the adjoining floor. Its height must be at least 1.5 m and not more than 1.8 m above the finished floor of that adjoining floor. Comply with Appendix D paragraph 7. Structural and fixing details follow Appendix F.
- The outer face is still reached by a power-operated elevating work platform or a suspended working platform (paragraph 3.2.3).
- Amended clause 4.2.3 and new clause 4.2.4: where a permanent guard-rail around the whole roof perimeter is not practicable, use a proprietary collapsible guard-rail, temporary edge protection, or both. The collapsible rail’s loads and wind resistance must be no worse than the Building (Construction) Regulations, the Loading Code, and the Wind Code. Temporary edge protection, with a platform or ladder, follows the Labour Department “Five Steps to Risk Assessment”. Provide anchorage points or a horizontal lifeline at least 2 m from the edge or fragile area, or at the roof access, and attach it before anyone enters the fall-risk zone. If access is within 2 m of the edge, or onto a pitched roof, provide permanent rails at least around the access and landing.
- Other Appendix A items update Labour Department references. They add “Overview of Work-at-Height Safety” and “Five Steps to Risk Assessment”, and renumber the items that follow.
- Every Appendix A item except new paragraph 3.2.4 takes effect immediately. Paragraph 3.2.4 applies to a new general building plan (GBP), a major revision, or a previously disapproved GBP resubmitted for approval, on or after 1 December 2024.

**What to submit**
- Submit maintenance and repair access plans under the Code, with the details, supporting documents, and the developer or owner’s letter of undertaking.
- Put the specified general notes on the GBP for the roof-edge alternatives.
- Authorized persons (APs) are advised to provide the balcony and UP anchors on ongoing projects as well.

**Watch-outs**
- A new GBP submitted for approval on or after 1 December 2024 with no balcony or UP fall-arrest anchor at 1.5 m to 1.8 m.
- Relying only on a collapsible or temporary roof-edge guard, with no lifeline or anchor before anyone enters the fall-risk zone, or with no Labour Department risk-assessment notes on the plans.
- Alterations and additions to an existing building that ignore the Code and do not look for a workable maintenance route. Labour Department occupational safety and health references still apply.
