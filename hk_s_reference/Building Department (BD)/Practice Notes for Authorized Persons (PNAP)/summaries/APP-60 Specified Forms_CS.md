# APP-60 Specified Forms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2026 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 159; first issue August 1993)

> Scope note: Forms specified under section 22(4) of the Buildings Ordinance. Where the Ordinance or a regulation requires an application, notice, or certificate in a specified form, the form in Appendix A is used and is not altered. An altered form may invalidate the submission. This note does not set design standards. Electronic plan format is in PNAP ADM-17. Mandatory building and window inspection forms are in PNBI-3.

## Regulatory Overview
Common specified forms for new building works and for alteration and addition are submitted online through the Electronic Submission Hub, launched in stages on 30 June 2022, 31 March 2023, and 30 June 2024, and the Registration and Minor Works Management System, launched on 29 May 2026, receives certain specified forms and their supporting documents. Other forms use the Electronic Forms Submission System or the e-Counter at receipt@bd.gov.hk, as Appendix A marks for each form.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Forms that carry a design submission (Appendix A)
| Form | Use | Online route marked in Appendix A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BA4 | Notice of appointment of the authorized person, registered structural engineer, and/or registered geotechnical engineer. Section 4 and regulation 23 | Electronic Forms Submission System and e-Counter, and the Electronic Submission Hub. Not the Registration and Minor Works Management System |
| BA5 | Application for approval of plans of building works and/or street works, and certificate of preparation. Section 14(1)(a) and regulations 18A and 29 | Electronic Submission Hub only |
| BA6 | Stability certificate. Regulation 18 | Electronic Submission Hub only |
| BA8 | Consent to commence building works or street works. Section 14(1)(b) and regulation 31 | Electronic Forms Submission System and e-Counter, within office hours, and the Electronic Submission Hub |
| BA8A | Concurrent consent. Section 14(1)(b) and regulation 31 | Electronic Submission Hub only |
| BA10 | Appointment of the registered contractor, notice of commencement, and the contractor's undertaking. Section 9 and regulation 20 | Electronic Forms Submission System and e-Counter, and the Electronic Submission Hub |
| BA12 | Completion certificate and application for a temporary occupation permit. Section 21 and regulation 25 | Electronic Submission Hub only |
| BA14 and BA14A or BA14B | Completion of works. Listed in the same appendix for the relevant section | Use the unaltered form for that purpose |

A hard-copy form should still be filled on a computer so the information is stored in the two-dimensional barcode. The current form is the one on the Buildings Department website.

**SD takeaway:** Submit plan approval on an unaltered Form BA5 and consent on an unaltered Form BA8 or BA8A, using the online route Appendix A marks for that form.
