# ADV-16

**Official title:** Street Name and Building Number

**In plain words:** The Commissioner of Rating and Valuation allocates the building number. This note says how large the digits must be, and when to apply. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). The entrance design has to leave a clear place for the number plate. Floor numbering inside the building is a separate matter, under ADV-3.

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Ordinance section 32(2): the Commissioner allocates building numbers and may prescribe how they are marked. The Commissioner or the Building Authority may order the owner to mark the allocated numbers in the prescribed manner.
- Follow Rating and Valuation Department Practice Note 1/2007, which is on rvd.gov.hk.
- Display the number clearly above or at the side of the entrance. The colour or the number plate must contrast with the background. Each digit is at least 50 mm high and 40 mm wide.
- For a new building, the developer or the authorized person (AP) should consider the numbering principles at design stage. Apply in writing for a provisional number after the Building Authority consents to commencement. The Commissioner notifies the official number on completion. Advise the developer to display it as the guidelines require once it is allocated.
- On an existing building, if alteration, addition, repair, or renovation hides the street name or the building number, the AP makes sure it is repaired or replaced as soon as possible.
- Enquiries: 15/F, Cheung Sha Wan Government Offices, 303 Cheung Sha Wan Road; enquiries@rvd.gov.hk; telephone 2150 8986 or 2150 8902; fax 2152 0138.
- The Commissioner adopts the floor numbers on the approved building plans under ADV-3. That is separate from the street building number under this note.

**What to submit**
- A written application to the Commissioner for a provisional number after consent to commence. Obtain the official number on completion, and display it before occupation so emergency services and the post can find the building.

**Watch-outs**
- Completing or occupying without a displayed official number that meets the 50 mm by 40 mm digit size and the contrast rule risks an order, and delays emergency access.
- Alteration or addition that hides the street name or number, without putting it back promptly.
- Ignoring the numbering principles at design, for example where there are several entrances or street frontages, leads to a confused allocation.
