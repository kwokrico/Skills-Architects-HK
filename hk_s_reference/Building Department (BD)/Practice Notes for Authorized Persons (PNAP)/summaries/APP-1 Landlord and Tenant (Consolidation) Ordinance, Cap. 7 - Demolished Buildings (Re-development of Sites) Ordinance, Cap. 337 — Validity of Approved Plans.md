# APP-1

**Official title:** Landlord and Tenant (Consolidation) Ordinance, Cap. 7 / Demolished Buildings (Re-development of Sites) Ordinance, Cap. 337 — Validity of Approved Plans

**In plain words:** This note explains when approved plans that support an Exclusion Order under Cap. 7 stay usable, and when a design change is large enough to need an amended order. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Ordinance section 16(3A) normally keeps section 16(3)(d) from applying to approved plans that form the basis of a Cap. 7 Exclusion Order.
- Consent applied for more than 2 years after approval will still be given if the work can still be finished within the Re-development Order period.
- A change is a material variation if floor space is reduced by more than 10%, or the use of any part of the proposed building changes, or other premises that are also under Cap. 7 are brought in (for example, the project is extended).
- The Building Authority may grant 1 extra month to start, with no penalty, if you ask before the commencement period in the Exclusion Order expires. Examples that can justify the request: you cannot get vacant possession even though proceedings were started and pursued with due diligence; you cannot enter the adjoining property for shoring or precautionary works; or you cannot get demolition consent for reasons outside your control.

**What to submit**
- If there is a material variation, the authorized person (AP), or the client’s solicitors, must apply to the Lands Tribunal (Registrar, Lands Tribunal, Wanchai Law Courts) for an amending Exclusion Order.
- Ask the Building Authority for the penalty-free commencement extension before the commencement period expires.

**Watch-outs**
- Approval under the Buildings Ordinance does not mean the scheme is accepted under Cap. 7. Both tracks still apply.
- Consent after more than 2 years still depends on whether the work can practically be finished within the Re-development Order period.
- A material variation without an amending Exclusion Order can breach Cap. 7 even if the Buildings Department plans are otherwise acceptable.
