# ADM-2

**Official title:** Centralised Processing of Building Plans

**In plain words:** The Building Authority asks the other government departments for comments and collects them within set times. The Buildings Department is the contact point for private-development plan issues. This note also says how many paper copies to send, how exemptions are recorded, and how gross floor area (GFA) concessions are shown so they can be published after the occupation permit (OP). It does not create new concession amounts. Those sit in the APP notes and the joint practice notes. An incomplete paper trail can still block or reverse a concession at occupation permit.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Electronic Submission Hub is the preferred route. It sends the submission to the Department and the relevant departments at the same time. On paper, too few sets delays the referral and can delay the comments that come back to the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE).
- Complete Appendix A1 before you submit general building plans. Structural set numbers are in ADM-8. Fire service installation proposals and lease matters use Appendices B and C.
- Plans for a proposal inside the Tung Chung Cable Car Route Protection Area are referred to Ngong Ping 360 Limited, and to others listed in Appendix A and Appendix I, to check cable-car safety.
- Department comments that are available go into the approval or disapproval letter. An adverse comment that is inside the Buildings Ordinance and is a fundamental issue under ADM-19 is a disapproval item. Other comments are written in separate paragraphs and are not disapproval items. Late comments go out by a separate letter or a Hub notification.
- The Building Authority decides matters governed by the Buildings Ordinance. For a comment outside the Ordinance, go to that department directly. If departments disagree with each other, the Buildings Department arranges a meeting.
- Discuss a specific uncertainty with the Department before the formal submission. On a complex site, a presentation, with a three-dimensional model if that helps, may be held after submission, at the request of or with the agreement of the relevant Chief Professional Officer.
- On the 45th day of a new building-plan submission, the AP, RSE, or RGE may ask whether the scheme is fundamentally acceptable, and may ask to discuss non-conformities before a disapproval letter. If the scheme is fundamentally unacceptable and the disapproval items are notified within the statutory 60 days, new disapproval items are not normally added when the same proposal is resubmitted.
- Each exemption or modification goes on Form BA16, with the special circumstances and the justification. Appendix E guides the applications that come up often. Conditions are recorded on Form BD 106 under Buildings Ordinance section 42. Two conditions are mandatory. Put the relevant modification and exemption conditions on later amendment plans before the Building (Administration) Regulation 25 completion certificate. Sample tables are in Appendix A3 of ADV-33. Submit a checklist of the valid Forms BD 106 with that certificate. A sample is Appendix F.
- Approval does not give you title and does not waive the lease (Buildings Ordinance section 14(2)). Work on an adjoining lot or building needs the owners’ consent before it starts. Consult the other authorities.
- After the occupation permit, the Department publishes GFA concession information on its website. Plans must show a detailed breakdown of the concession areas (Appendix G). A summary of concessions and car-park numbers (Appendix H is a sample) goes with the occupation permit application and is uploaded after the permit.
- From 2 July 2024, result letters for structural approval and consent are issued electronically even if you submitted on paper. A hard copy is no longer posted. Structural works here means excavation and lateral support, ground investigation in scheduled areas, foundations, and all structural works. It does not include alteration and addition structural submissions, or railway-consultation structural submissions.
- Standard paper general building plan quantities, Appendix A Part 2: 2 signed coloured sets for the Building Authority; 2 sets for the Director of Fire Services, or 3 if the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance, the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance, or the Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance applies; 1 set for the District Planning Officer; for Lands, 1 set to the urban District Lands Office, or 2 if the lease has a design, disposition, and height clause, and 2 sets in the New Territories.
- Appendix A asks, among other site-control questions, whether the site abuts a street at least 4.5 m wide.
- Appendix E figures that often support a BA16 application:
  - Filtration plant: justify spacing of more than 1.5 m from the plants or the walls, for a communal pool or a recreational plant room.
  - A cockloft void in a single-staircase shop: at least 1.5 m clear across the front; one cockloft per shop; storage only; no sanitary fitments; not at staircase landing level; access only from the ground-floor shop; openings protected by barriers.
  - A horizontal screen or covered walkway wider than 2 m needs justification.
  - Fresh air, Annex 2: an ancillary office of not more than 30% of the host premises’ GFA needs 5 air changes per hour; a kitchen in licensed premises needs 20 air changes per hour; a non-domestic toilet needs 10 air changes per hour; a purpose-designed office with air-conditioning needs 1.1 litres per second per square metre, or 10 litres per second per person; a hotel bathroom needs 18 litres per second per room. Toilet and hotel-bathroom exhaust may be drawn from adjacent air. Do not recirculate exhaust air.
  - Fresh-air intake, Annex 3: at least 5 m from a contamination source (exhausts, traffic, car parks, refuse, cooling towers, emergency generators, plumbing vents, kitchens, toilets). Not below ground and not close to cooling towers. Face it away from pollution. Provide rain protection and a screen.
  - Openable windows in a curtain-wall non-domestic commercial building: the openable area is at least 1% of the floor area, evenly distributed, readily openable, and able to give natural ventilation if the mechanical system fails.
  - If natural lighting to a staircase is omitted: permanent artificial lighting of at least 30 lux, plus emergency lighting of at least 2 lux horizontal illuminance at floor level (Fire Services Installation Code of Practice, Director of Fire Services, BS 5266 Part 1:1988).
  - A reduced drain fall under Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulation 48: show a minimum velocity of 750 mm per second.
- Appendix G and the APP-151 10% cap: for new building plans submitted for approval on or after 1 April 2011, items 2.3, 5, 6, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17B to 20, 22, 25, 26, and 29 are subject to the 10% overall cap in APP-151. Item 17A (a trellis, or a horizontal screen or covered walkway with greenery to the Building Authority’s satisfaction under APP-42) is not under the 10% cap. Item 17B is under the cap. The car-park loading and unloading summary excludes public transport terminus, item 32.

**What to submit**
- Appendix A, covering site special-control questions, documents, and how many referral sets. The Hub is encouraged.
- Forms BA4, BA5, BA6 where alteration and addition affects stability, BA16 with justifications, and BA17 for a temporary building. Fees follow APP-55. Include a development schedule. Where APP-25 requires it, two sets of the geotechnical assessment.
- Fire Services Department, Appendix B: on paper, copy the covering letter to the Department. On every plan that shows fire service installations under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(b)(ii), put a bold note at the lower right identifying the Fire Services referral set. That note is for paper only. The Hub uses the designated Fire Services folder. Collect the endorsed plans and pay the fee under the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations (Cap. 95C) regulation 3. Paper plans not collected within 3 months of the Fire Services notification are treated as unwanted and disposed of. Fire Services comments are headed: reasons for refusal of a certificate under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(b); strongly recommended practice; optional matters.
- Lease, Appendix C: on paper, general building plans with a development schedule, coloured building plans, and CAD files under Lands Administration Office Practice Note 3/2018, or BIM files on DVD-ROM when a computer was used for floor areas. Print the AP’s name and telephone number on the plans. On the Hub, upload the general building plans and the CAD or BIM files for direct referral to the District Lands Office or Building Plan Unit (see ADM-17 and APP-60). Amendments use the Lands Department streamlined reply slip. Lease enquiries and lease modifications do not go through the Hub. Go to the District Lands Office, the Building Plan Unit, or the Railway Development Section. A lease modification should come before the formal plan submission.
- At occupation permit: Appendix H (GFA concessions and car-park numbers); the checklist of valid Forms BD 106 (Appendix F) with the Building (Administration) Regulation 25 certificate; and amendment plans that have already built in the BD 106 conditions.

**Watch-outs**
- Too few paper sets delays referral and produces late comments.
- Treating a non-Ordinance departmental comment as a Building Authority disapproval, or ignoring an Ordinance fundamental item, both go wrong.
- A BA16 with no special-circumstances justification, or without the Appendix E supporting papers, delays or refuses the modification.
- Leaving BD 106 conditions off the amendment plans, or leaving the valid BD 106 checklist off the regulation 25 certificate, puts the occupation permit and the concessions at risk.
- Missing the Appendix G breakdown on the plans, or the Appendix H summary at occupation permit, leads to a wrong public concession figure and pushback from the Authority.
- Approval does not waive the lease or adjoining-owner consent.
- Fire Services paper plans not collected within 3 months are disposed of.
- A lease modification that starts after the formal plan submission is the wrong order.
- Capped APP-151 items that are not tracked can breach the 10% overall concession cap.
- A false certificate or declaration on a checklist is a criminal matter. Appendix A says so under Matters to Note.
