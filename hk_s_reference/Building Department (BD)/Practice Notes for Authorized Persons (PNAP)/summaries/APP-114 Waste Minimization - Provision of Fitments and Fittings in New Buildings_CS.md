# APP-114 Waste Minimization — Provision of Fitments and Fittings in New Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2000 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 245; first issued July 2000)

> Scope note: When basic sanitary fitments required under the building regulations must be installed before an occupation permit, and when a modification may defer some of them. It is not a sanitary-fitment count or a drainage design code.

## Regulatory Overview
Basic sanitary fitments required under the building regulations should be installed before an occupation permit is issued. The Building Authority will favourably consider a modification of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations so that certain fitments are not installed at that time in two stated cases.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When fitments may be deferred (paragraph 3)
| Case | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurants and hotels | Extensive renovation and fitting out by the operator will be carried out only after the occupation permit, and any sanitary fitting installed before then would be dismantled |
| Purchaser choice | A developer has offered to provide fittings to individual purchaser’s choices |

**SD takeaway:** Defer sanitary fitments past the occupation permit only for a restaurant or hotel that will strip them out, or where the developer is fitting them to purchaser choice.

### 2. Undertaking if a modification is sought (paragraph 4)
The application must show the extent of fitments and fittings to be installed after the occupation permit, with an undertaking that:

| Item | Undertaking |
| --- | --- |
| Timing | Outstanding fitments and fittings installed before actual occupation of the relevant part |
| Standards | Installed in accordance with the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations and the requirements of the Water Authority |
| Who installs | Under the authorized person’s supervision, by the registered general building contractor, who will employ a licensed plumber for the plumbing works |
| Water Authority | Notified by standard form WW046 for installation of the outstanding plumbing fittings |

**SD takeaway:** If fitments are left out at occupation-permit stage, bind their later installation to the sanitary regulations, a licensed plumber, and form WW046.

### 3. Finishes that owners often replace (paragraph 5)
The survey noted that some doors, floor finishes, and partitions are taken out and replaced by owners of new flats. More choices of colour schemes, door types, and floor-finish patterns may be offered to purchasers.

**SD takeaway:** Offer door, floor-finish, and partition choices at sale if those items are likely to be stripped out after occupation.
