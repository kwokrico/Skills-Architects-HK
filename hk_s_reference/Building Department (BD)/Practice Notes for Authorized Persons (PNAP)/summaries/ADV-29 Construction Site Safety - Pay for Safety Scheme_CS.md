# ADV-29 Construction Site Safety — Pay for Safety Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision August 2017 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 298; first issue March 2006)

> Scope note: Encourages clients to adopt the Pay for Safety Scheme so safety items are priced and paid separately. It does not replace the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans or the Code of Practice for Site Supervision.

## Regulatory Overview
Authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered geotechnical engineers must ensure that building works under their supervision are carried out safely, and contractors should already have priced continuous supervision and their safety duties in the tender. If those costs are not identified as safety items for pricing, valuation, and payment, the resources for the measures may be cut.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How the scheme pays (paragraphs 3–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Since | The Pay for Safety Scheme was introduced in 1996 |
| Payment | The employer pays the contractor on satisfactory completion of the safety-related items, including training, vetted and certified by the employer's representative, at the pre-priced rates in the Site Safety section of the bills of quantities or schedule of rates |
| Recommendation | Authorized persons are encouraged to recommend that clients adopt the scheme |
| Still required | Site-safety monitoring under the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision |

**SD takeaway:** Ask the client to put a Site Safety section in the bills, with pre-priced items including training, so safety is paid only when those items are certified complete.
