# APP-32

**Official title:** Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301) — Airport Height Restrictions

**In plain words:** Cap. 301 limits building height for aviation safety, and the limit depends on the Airport Height Restriction Plan for that place. This note does not give a gross floor area (GFA) concession; the restrictions cap the absolute height of the building and of construction plant.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Airport Height Restriction Plans are at the Land Registry, the Lands Department Map Publication Centres, and on the Civil Aviation Department website.
- A temporary exemption is given by the Director-General of Civil Aviation (DGCA). It is normally for 2 months or less, and it may be extended by further periods of 2 months, subject to conditions.
- Where the site is under these restrictions, the authorized person (AP) and the registered structural engineer (RSE) must ensure the registered contractor (RC) obtains prior DGCA approval for any temporary plant or equipment that may project above the prescribed height. See practice note PNRC 7.
- Third Runway System (3RS) Amendment Order 2021: the new airport height restrictions operate from 31 May 2022. They are stricter near the sea and the mountains around Hong Kong International Airport, and less strict over most of Hong Kong.
- The new restrictions do not apply to a building erected in accordance with plans the Building Authority (BA) approved before 31 May 2022, nor to minor works or designated exempted works completed before that date.

**What to submit**
- Check the applicable Airport Height Restriction Plan at the start.
- Apply to the Director-General of Civil Aviation for a temporary exemption for plant.
- Keep the plans within the airport height restriction unless they are covered by the pre-31 May 2022 approval.

**Watch-outs**
- Cranes or plant above the airport height restriction without a temporary DGCA exemption.
- Using the old height envelope for a new approval after 31 May 2022.
