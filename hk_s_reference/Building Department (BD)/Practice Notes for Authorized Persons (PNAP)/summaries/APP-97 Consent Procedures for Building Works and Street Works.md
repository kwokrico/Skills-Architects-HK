# APP-97

**Official title:** Consent Procedures for Building Works and Street Works

**In plain words:** The first consent for a set of works uses approval then consent, while a later amendment that is not a major revision or a material deviation may use a fast track that approves and consents together. This note does not itself change gross floor area or site coverage, but first consent often still depends on the concession papers.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issued November 1997. Last revision December 2005. This revision May 2023 (paragraph 9 and the Appendix A first-consent checklist added).
- Consent is under Buildings Ordinance section 14(1)(b) and Building (Administration) Regulations regulations 31 and 33. Sanctions for starting without consent are sections 7 and 40.
- Type I works have not yet had a first Building Authority (BA) consent. First consent means the first time for those works. It is not consent for an amendment plan, but it does include a major revision or a material deviation approved later. Type I uses the normal procedure. Fast track is not available.
- Type II works already have approval and first consent, and the proposal amends the approved plans. Major revisions and material deviations are excluded. Type II may use fast track or the normal path.
- The fast-track statement goes at the lower right of the plan: the works are Type II (for example a curtain wall) and fast-track consent is sought under regulation 33. That statement is accepted instead of Form BA8 for Type II consent.
- A wrong classification may be refused under section 16(3)(a) or 16(3)(b).
- Fast-track consent covers the approved plans only. If the structure also needs amendment, do not build until those structural plans are approved and consented.
- There is no statutory expiry of approved plans. On consent, the BA may use section 16(3)(d) if the plans were approved more than 2 years earlier and the works do not meet current Buildings Ordinance standards. That power is not used to enforce outline zoning plan changes made after the building plans were approved.
- Under section 39(2), if works were already under way or already consented before a new regulation, the previous law applies. If works that the new regulation covers are not shown on any plans approved within 2 years at the time of first consent, the BA may refuse under section 16(3)(a). If the BA does not refuse, the Authorized Person (AP), Registered Structural Engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) must still bring the works up to the new regulation before the occupation permit, with amendment plans if needed. Failure can mean refusal of the occupation permit under section 21(6).

**What to submit**
- Normal path: apply for approval, then apply for consent. This is required for all Type I works and optional for Type II.
- Fast-track Type II: add the endorsement. The outcome may be approval and consent together, disapproval and refusal together, or approval with consent refused. If consent is refused, use the normal consent path afterwards.
- Name the work type: demolition; ground investigation; site formation; slope or retaining wall; excavation and lateral support; foundation; cap, footing, or basement; superstructure; curtain wall or cladding; alteration and addition; street or access road; drainage; tunnel; or general building works. A phase may be added in brackets.
- Before the first superstructure or drainage consent, use the Appendix A checklist. The checklist itself is not submitted. It covers party-wall safety and shoring, the ADV-12 site board, Form BA8 or BA8A, the supervision plan, gross-floor-area concession documents (a BEAM Plus provisional assessment under the building environmental assessment method, energy performance, and practice notes APP-151 and APP-156), proof of ownership, lease-modification documents, ADM-19 and ADV-33 general building plan Stage II, APP-67 overall thermal transfer value, ADM-21 site parameters, section 17(1) conditions, the matching structural and drainage approvals, APP-12 anchors, Geotechnical Engineering Office conditions, new codes and practice notes, the Antiquities and Monuments Office if the site is heritage, lease-modification validity, APP-23 hoarding, section 113 party-wall consent, Form BA 14 for foundation completion, and ADM-19 deferral of minor amendments.
- Obtain every consent before work starts.

**Watch-outs**
- Calling a major or material amendment Type II, in order to use the fast track, can be refused.
- Do not start Type II building-plan amendments before the matching structural consent.
- First consent on plans more than 2 years old must be checked against current standards under section 16(3)(d).
- A first-consent package missing APP-151, ownership proof, or the supervision plan will stall.
- Starting without consent is an offence under section 40 and can lead to disciplinary action under section 7.
