# APP-100 Structural Plans of Glass Reinforced Polyester Water Tanks
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2010 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 222; first issue March 1998)

> Scope note: Structural submissions and tests for glass-reinforced polyester water tanks, which are building works. Certain works to such tanks in an existing building may be minor works under Schedule 1 and PNAP APP-147.

## Regulatory Overview
Glass-reinforced polyester is high in strength and low in stiffness, so design is influenced more strongly by stiffness than by strength, and an allowance is required for creep and for the service environment. Structural plans and calculations are required except for the small tanks in paragraphs 7 and 8.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Plans, consent tests, and performance tests (paragraphs 3–6)
| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Structural plans | Design standards; material types, compliance standards, and mechanical properties; general arrangement with maximum designed capacity, principal dimensions, and thicknesses; fabrication of major components; fixing and tank supports. Calculations for the tank and the base support |
| Before consent | Recognised-laboratory reports on the mechanical properties of the fibre-glass panel and its ability to withstand 6 times the design hydrostatic pressure; manufacturer's quality-control details for panel production and tank fabrication |
| Leakage test | Filled to overflow at ambient temperature. No visible leakage after standing for at least 48 hours |
| Deflection test | After the full water load is maintained for at least 2 hours, deflection of the sides not exceeding 1.0% of the depth of the tank, and deflection of the bottom not exceeding 10 mm |
| Report | Submitted with a statement signed by the authorized person or registered structural engineer who prepared the plans |

**SD takeaway:** Submit calculations and a panel test to 6 times the design hydrostatic pressure before consent, and prove no leak after 48 hours and side deflection within 1% of tank depth.

### 2. Small tanks (paragraphs 7–8)
| Tank | What the structural plans must show |
| --- | --- |
| Volume not exceeding 9 m3 and water head not exceeding 2 m | Paragraphs 3 to 5 are normally not required. Show location, principal dimensions, capacity, and weight; design standards and material specification; fixing details with calculations for the supports |
| Indoor, volume not exceeding 4 m3 and water head not exceeding 1.2 m | Only location, principal dimensions, capacity, and weight, plus calculations for the floor structure supporting the tank |

**SD takeaway:** A tank over 9 m3 or over 2 m water head needs the full structural submission and the 48-hour leak test; a smaller tank still needs its support, or the floor, justified by calculation.
