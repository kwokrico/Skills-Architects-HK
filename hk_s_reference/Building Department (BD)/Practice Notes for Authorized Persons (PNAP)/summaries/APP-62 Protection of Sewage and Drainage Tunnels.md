# APP-62

**Official title:** Protection of Sewage and Drainage Tunnels

**In plain words:** Works near a sewage tunnel, and some other Drainage Services Department tunnels, must stay inside limits on pressure, movement and vibration, and those limits can force a foundation redesign that reduces podium or basement floor area. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage rules.

**Numbers that matter**
- Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance. Buildings Ordinance section 17A. Scheduled Area No. 5. Other Drainage Services Department tunnels follow Practice Note No. 2/2017.
- Sewage tunnels typically have at least 30 m of rock cover. The ends between Ap Lei Chau and Aberdeen are noted as exceptions.
- Building works within 100 m of the centreline of a gazetted sewage-tunnel route, or ground investigation inside Scheduled Area No. 5, are circulated to the Drainage Services Department and the Geotechnical Engineering Office. Works in a tunnel protection zone for other Drainage Services Department tunnels are circulated in the same way.
- Ground investigation in Scheduled Area No. 5: building works are under Buildings Ordinance section 2 and Part II control.
- For site formation, foundations, and tunnel or cavern works, soil structures: the change in vertical or horizontal pressure is not more than 20 kPa, or not more than 5 percent of the total overburden where the depth is more than 20 m. Rock structures, treated as hydrostatic: the change is not more than 50 kPa.
- Diametric distortion not more than 0.1 percent of the internal diameter. Total movement in any plane not more than 20 mm.
- Peak particle velocity not more than 25 mm/s for blasting, and not more than 15 mm/s for other prolonged vibration such as driving or withdrawing piles.
- No hole or excavation within 3 m of the tunnel structure unless the Building Authority has first approved the works and the method. No pile, foundation, well, soil nail, horizontal drain, rock bolt or dowel within 3 m in any plane. No part of a ground anchor within 3 m of any sewage tunnel structure.
- Works within 10 m need a detailed method statement and monitoring of alignment and depth. Send monitoring proposals to the Building Authority before you start, and keep sending data and interpretation.
- Dewatering or wells within 100 m: assess sewage leakage and gas migration, and agree a monitoring programme.
- Rock means Geoguide 3 Grades I to III. Soil means Grades IV to VI, plus reclamation, marine deposits and alluvium.
- Other Drainage Services Department tunnels, and their associated structures, follow the tunnel protection zones in Practice Note No. 2/2017: Hong Kong West, Kai Tak Transfer, Lai Chi Kok, Tsuen Wan, North West New Territories sewage, Tolo Harbour effluent, and Tseung Kwan O sewage.

**What to submit**
- The Building Authority may refuse approval, or impose conditions, under Buildings Ordinance section 17A if the works do not sit with the gazetted sewage-tunnel works.
- Submit monitoring, and, if the works are within 10 m, method statements, before and during the works.
- Compensation questions under the Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance go to the Director of Lands.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not put foundations, anchors or ground investigation within 3 m of a tunnel structure.
- Do not exceed the pressure, movement or peak-particle-velocity limits.
- Do not dewater near a tunnel without assessing gas and sewage migration.
- Do not ignore tunnel protection zones for Drainage Services Department tunnels that are not gazetted under the sewage-tunnel ordinance. Use Practice Note No. 2/2017.
