# APP-54

**Official title:** Retaining Wall

**In plain words:** A retaining wall here is a permanent structure on land that holds back earth or fill, and anchor plates that support a rock or earth face are not retaining walls. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but the wall and backfill rules can limit how much of the site you can build on.

**Numbers that matter**
- Design and construction must comply with Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) section 23. Also use PNAP APP-63 and Geoguide 1.
- Drainage and filters, other than a minor retaining wall under B(C)R section 22, follow B(C)R section 24. The filter is clean, sound, durable material, free from clay, organics and impurities. A geotextile of resistant synthetic polymers is acceptable where the site is not aggressive (Geoguide 1 section 8.5; GEO Publication 1/93).
- Crushed-rock backfill (Appendix A Table 1): maximum size 200 mm; percent passing 63 µm is 0; coefficient of uniformity (Cu) at least 5; liquid limit and plasticity index do not apply.
- Soil backfill: maximum size 75 mm. Up to 5 percent rock fragments of 200 mm or less are allowed if they do not interfere with compaction or cause damage. Percent passing 63 µm is 0 to 45. Coefficient of uniformity (Cu) at least 5 for soil of weathered-rock origin. Note 4: alluvial sands and gravels also need Cu at least 5, and must not be gap-graded. Liquid limit (LL) not more than 45 percent. Plasticity index (PI) not more than 20 percent. You do not need the LL and PI check if less than 30 percent by weight is finer than 63 µm. Measure the particle size distribution without dispersants. The maximum particle size must not exceed two-thirds of the compacted layer thickness.
- Remedial or preventive work to existing walls: section 7.3.3 of the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes is accepted.
- Demolition: plans and supporting documents under Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulations 8(3) and 8(4).
- Some routine slope and retaining-wall maintenance is minor works under the Minor Works Control System, Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, and PNAP APP-147.

**What to submit**
- Show structural and geotechnical compliance with B(C)R sections 22 to 24 and Geoguide 1.
- Demolish an existing retaining wall only through the plan-approval route in B(A)R 8(3) and 8(4).
- Minor-works items may use the simplified requirements instead of prior approval and consent.
- During construction, measure how the wall performs and its effects on groundwater, the site, buildings, structures, land, streets and services.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not treat anchor-plate support to a rock face as a retaining wall.
- Do not treat a rock face as “earth” when calculating earth pressure.
- Do not use backfill that contains peat, vegetation, timber, organic or degradable material, dangerous or toxic material, combustible material, metal, rubber, plastic or other synthetics, material that can change volume a lot (marine mud, swelling clays, collapsible soils), or soluble material. The fill must not be chemically aggressive, for example with excessive sulphate.
- Do not demolish an existing retaining wall without the prescribed plan submission.
- Do not skip construction monitoring where the effects on adjoining property or services are material.
