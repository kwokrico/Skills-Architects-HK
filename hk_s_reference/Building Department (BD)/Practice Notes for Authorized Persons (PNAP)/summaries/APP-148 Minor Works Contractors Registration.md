# APP-148

**Official title:** Minor Works Contractors Registration

**In plain words:** This note covers registration of individual and company minor works contractors. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Registration of a registered minor works contractor (individual) and a registered minor works contractor (company) under Buildings Ordinance section 8A(1)(c) and the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. First issue December 2010. Detailed procedures are in PNRC 69.
- An individual registers for specified Class III items. The Building Authority looks at qualifications and experience, personal ability and skills, a recognised Class III training course, and suitability.
- A company must show qualifications of key personnel, plant and resources, adequate corporate management, that the authorised signatory understands the minor works applied for, and suitability. Minimum 1 authorised signatory. A corporation also needs minimum 1 technical director, who has access to plant and resources, gives technical and financial support, and supervises the authorised signatory.
- One person may be both authorised signatory and technical director if that person meets both sets of criteria. Key personnel cannot at the same time be authorised signatory, technical director, or other officer for another contractor firm.
- Class I registration, adding minor works items, or adding an authorised signatory or technical director requires a Minor Works Contractors Registration Committee interview and recommendation before the Building Authority can allow the application in whole or in part.
- An aggrieved applicant may request a review under section 26 on the specified form, with a fee. The fee is refunded if the Building Authority substitutes the decision.

**What to submit**
- Specify the items (individual) or the types and classes (company). The Building Authority registers only those found qualified.
- The Committee examines qualifications, inquires into experience, interviews the applicant, and advises allow, defer, or refuse, and it reviews decisions.
- A section 26 review, if sought, on the specified form with the fee.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not carry out minor works outside the registered class, type, or item.
- Do not hold key personnel roles in two firms at once.
- Incomplete documentary proof can lead the Building Authority to refuse registration.
