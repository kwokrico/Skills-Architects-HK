# APP-84

**Official title:** Access Facilities for Telecommunications and Broadcasting Services

**In plain words:** Commercial, industrial, hotel, and multi-family residential buildings need fixed telecom access, and specified buildings approved on or after 1 April 2025 also need mobile access facilities (MAF). Space used only for the required access can be left out of gross floor area (GFA) within the caps below, while MAF in a building that is not specified still counts in GFA, and a mobile-access room under 10 m² is not exempt.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 201. First issued November 1997. Last revision June 2006. This revision September 2024. Appendices A and B are amended. Appendices C, D, and E are added.
- Building (Planning) Regulations regulation 28A requires the access. The Building Authority (BA) may disregard floor space used only for regulation 28A access facilities under regulation 23(3)(b).
- Fixed network operators (FNOs) are covered by Telecommunications Ordinance section 14. Mobile network operators (MNOs) have access rights in a specified building: a regulation 28A building whose earliest general building plan (GBP) approval for erection, rebuilding, or alteration that creates a new building is on or after 1 April 2025.
- Lead-in ducts must be sealable, and the fixed operator must be able to remove the seal easily. Internal diameter is typically 100 mm. The count rises with size. For example, a commercial building of up to 2 × 1,000 m² usable floor space (UFS) needs 2 ducts, and a large commercial building needs up to 8.
- Telecommunications and broadcasting (TBE) room minimum areas, and the maximum area that can be left out of GFA, are in Appendix A. Clear height is 3 m for commercial, industrial, and hotel buildings, and 2.8 m to 3 m for residential buildings depending on the number of flats.
- Provide 1 or 2 vertical risers, sized from 75 × 75 mm to 900 × 250 mm, as Appendix A requires.
- A TBE room must not be where it can flood. A podium with towers needs a separate TBE room for each tower and for the podium, linked to the vertical block wiring. Do not put water, sewage, drainage, sprinklers, transformers, or high-voltage cables inside. High voltage here means above 600 V three-phase to earth, 1000 V single-phase, or 1500 V direct current. Provide lighting, power, ventilation, and a separate telecom earth. Minimum clear height is as Appendix A.
- If the building is larger than the biggest Appendix A band, add the largest-band requirement to the requirement for the remainder. The note gives a commercial example of 180,000 m² UFS.
- MAF is required for a specified building that is commercial or industrial above 3,000 m² UFS, residential above 50 flats, or a hotel above 75 rooms. If there are more than 5 specified buildings and none of them meets those sizes, one building gets the smallest MAF.
- Where several buildings meet the size test, the number that get MAF is 25% of those buildings, rounded up. Count a podium and its towers separately. In mixed use, if any use qualifies, the building counts as one, and if it is selected, use the largest MAF of the qualifying uses. Choose the tallest building or buildings. Another choice needs support from the Office of the Communications Authority (OFCA).
- MAF includes a TBE room for MAF (TBE_MAF), a rooftop telecommunications equipment (RTE) room, an intermediate telecommunications equipment (ITE) room if the building is taller than 175 m under regulation 23(1), and a vertical riser for MAF. Do not combine the RTE room with the ITE room. Provide them in common parts, with power connection points. RTE and ITE windows must be opaque but must still let radio signals through. Keep out water, sewage, drainage, sprinklers, high-voltage cables, and transformers. Provide lighting, power, ventilation, and maintenance access.
- If the roof cannot take the RTE room, it may go elsewhere, including where regulation 28A does not apply or height is limited under the Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap. 301), but not lower than 90% of the building height.
- A combined TBE and TBE_MAF room needs a floor demarcation, and the Appendix A TBE rules apply. If combining them is not practical, use separate rooms.
- Appendix D examples: minimum areas of 10 m², 15 m², and 20 m² for TBE_MAF, RTE, and ITE, with maximum exempt areas of 20 m², 25 m², and 30 m². Clear height is 3 m, or 2.8 m to 3 m in residential buildings. The MAF vertical riser is at least 1 slot of 200 × 200 mm.
- There is no GFA exemption if a TBE_MAF, RTE, or ITE room is smaller than 10 m².
- MAF GFA exemption applies only to MAF in a specified building under paragraphs 12 to 13, and paragraph 14 where OFCA is involved. MAF in a building that is not specified counts in GFA.
- The fixed-access rules apply to a new GBP, or a major GBP revision, approved on or after 1 November 2006. An earlier approval may follow the September 2000 practice note.
- The MAF rules apply to a new or major GBP of a specified building approved on or after 1 April 2025, and to alteration and addition that creates a new specified building. That is at least half the volume rebuilt, or at least half the superficial area of the main walls reconstructed, under Telecommunications Ordinance section 14(10A).
- A specified building that does not have to provide MAF may still provide a TBE_MAF room and riser. RTE and ITE rooms on that optional path need OFCA support and the Authorized Person’s (AP) justification, including a developer–MNO agreement.

**What to submit**
- Settle fixed-operator and mobile-operator needs at planning stage. Appointing more than one fixed operator for block wiring is advisable. Operator lists are in the Communications Authority codes of practice.
- Show the MAF schedule on the GBP in the form in the annex to Appendix C: buildings that meet the criteria, the 25% count, the tallest-building choice, and areas and heights provided against what is required and what is exempt.
- Use Appendix B for fixed-access diagrams and Appendix E for MAF diagrams.

**Watch-outs**
- Multi-family residential, commercial, industrial, and hotel buildings need the regulation 28A facilities.
- Do not claim a MAF GFA exemption for a building that is not specified, or for a room under 10 m².
- A wrong 25% count, skipping the tallest building, combining RTE with ITE, or putting the RTE room below 90% of the height without justification, will fail.
- Water or high-voltage services inside a TBE, RTE, or ITE room, and a TBE room that can flood, are not acceptable.
- Do not apply the MAF rules to a GBP approved before 1 April 2025, and do not miss them for an approval on or after that date.
- Lead-in seals must be proper and removable by the operator.
