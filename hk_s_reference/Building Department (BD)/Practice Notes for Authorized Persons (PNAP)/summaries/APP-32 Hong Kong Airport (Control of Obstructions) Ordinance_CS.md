# APP-32 Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2022 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 88; first issue August 1982)

> Scope note: Airport height restrictions under the Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance, Cap. 301, including temporary exemptions and the restrictions that operate from 31 May 2022. The height limits themselves are on the Airport Height Restriction Plans, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
Cap. 301 restricts building heights in the interest of aviation safety, and the limits vary by location on Airport Height Restriction Plans deposited in the Land Registry. Temporary plant or equipment that would project beyond those limits during construction needs the prior approval of the Director-General of Civil Aviation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Temporary exemption and construction plant (paragraphs 2–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Temporary exemption | Applied for from the Director-General of Civil Aviation, on such terms and conditions as are imposed. Normally valid for a period not exceeding two months, and may be extended for further periods of two months |
| During construction | The registered contractor obtains prior approval for any item of temporary plant or equipment likely to project beyond the prescribed height limits at any time. Guidelines are in Practice Note for Registered Contractors 7 |

**SD takeaway:** Check the Airport Height Restriction Plan for the site, and do not allow cranes or other temporary plant above that limit without a Director-General of Civil Aviation approval, normally in two-month blocks.

### 2. Restrictions operating from 31 May 2022 (paragraph 4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument | Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) (No. 2) Order 1997 (Amendment) Order 2021, in operation on 31 May 2022 |
| Near the airport | Certain areas in the vicinity of the Hong Kong International Airport, mainly the surrounding sea or mountainous areas, are subject to more stringent restrictions |
| Elsewhere | The remaining areas, constituting the majority of Hong Kong, are subject to less stringent restrictions |
| Saved approvals | The new restrictions do not apply to a building erected in accordance with plans approved by the Building Authority before 31 May 2022, or to minor works and designated exempted works completed before 31 May 2022 |

**SD takeaway:** For a scheme not saved by a pre-31 May 2022 approval, design to the Airport Height Restriction Plan that has applied since that date, which is tighter mainly over sea and mountains near the airport.
