# ADM-14

**Official title:** Minor Amendments to Plans and Specified Forms

**In plain words:** If plans are approvable under the Buildings Ordinance once small changes are made, the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) may amend the plans and the related specified forms, but only with the Buildings Department’s prior agreement. This note does not redefine gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). A change that affects the lease, including a GFA exclusion or a conversion of accountable floor space into hotel back-of-house, still has to be shown to the Lands Department.

**Numbers that matter**
- There are four ways to amend.
- Amend in the Department. This is the usual interview with the design team. The AP, RSE, or RGE may amend the plans or forms in the Department and sign them. For specialist or proprietary design (glazing, cladding, curtain wall, foundation, hoarding), the AP, RSE, or RGE, or a competent representative who understands the whole project, should attend with the specialist so interface questions can be settled.
- Substitute sheets or forms. Hand in substitute sheets with every amendment clearly noted and signed individually. Agree this with the case officer first. The substitute sheets must reach the officer on the agreed date, not more than 2 working days from the approach to the case officer. Specified forms may be replaced the same way, with prior agreement.
- Take part of the submission away. Ask in writing, naming the plan or form to be removed, and undertake to return the amended plans or forms in not more than 2 working days. Agree the return date beforehand. Returned items must be clearly noted and signed individually.
- Electronic Submission Hub “Revised Submission”. Submit amended plans, forms, and/or documents under that route to replace or add to the electronic submission. Agree the amendments and the deadline with the case officer first, as for paper.
- If routes 2, 3, or 4 miss the agreed date, the submission may be disapproved under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(i) and/or section 16(1)(c), because the prescribed plans or the required specified forms were not submitted.
- If the amendment has lease implications, paper submissions need an extra copy of the plans with the amendments noted or coloured, plus a development schedule showing the alterations and how far the lease is complied with, for the Lands Department. Examples include a GFA exclusion, and conversion of GFA-accountable floor space into hotel back-of-house. Electronic submissions send the amendments and the development schedule through the Hub for direct referral to the District Lands Officer.
- If the facility is abused, or it causes operational difficulty, the Department may change or cancel it.

**What to submit**
- Prior agreement with the case officer for substitute sheets, take-away, and Hub revisions. In-Department amendments are authenticated by the AP, RSE, or RGE signature.
- For a lease-sensitive change: the extra annotated or coloured copy and the development schedule, or the Hub equivalent.

**Watch-outs**
- Missing the agreed return date risks disapproval under section 16(1)(i) or 16(1)(c).
- A lease or GFA change without the extra copy and the development schedule leaves a gap in the Lands Department referral.
- A specialist-works interview without someone who understands the whole project leaves interface queries unresolved.
