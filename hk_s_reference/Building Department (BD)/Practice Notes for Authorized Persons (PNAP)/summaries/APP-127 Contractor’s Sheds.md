# APP-127

**Official title:** Contractor’s Sheds

**In plain words:** A contractor’s shed needs a permit under Building (Planning) Regulation (B(P)R) 53, and a simple shed can use the self-certification route in Appendix A. These are temporary works and do not count as the development’s gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), and they must not be left behind as unauthorized building works (UBW).

**Numbers that matter**
- Self-certification by a registered general building contractor (RGBC) or registered specialist contractor (RSC), under Appendix A, is only for a shed that is single storey, under 3 m storey height, and under 230 m² floor area. If it stands on stilts, the base is not more than 1.5 m above ground. It must raise no geotechnical concern: the gradient across the site is not more than 15°; the overall gradient within 10 m outside the footprint is under 15°; no slope within 10 m is steeper than 30° or higher than 1.5 m; and no retaining wall or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m is within the site or within 10 m outside it. It must not sit on a cantilever or above a hoarding or covered walkway. It must be within the site and not readily accessible to the public.
- Any other shed needs an authorized person (AP) and registered structural engineer (RSE) certification on Form BA18, Parts I and III, with structural documents comparable to Building (Administration) Regulation (B(A)R) 8(1)(d) and (i). If there is a geotechnical concern, a registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) submits geotechnical documents to the B(A)R 8(1)(bb) standard.
- A combustible shed must be at least 3 m clear of adjoining buildings or the site boundary.
- No permit is issued if the shed depends on a hoarding or walkway for support, or if it obstructs the clear width of the walkway.
- Fire safety in Appendix B: a 4.5 kg carbon-dioxide extinguisher, or a 9 litre water extinguisher, at each entrance. A multi-storey shed under 230 m² also needs at least one open stair. A single-storey shed over 230 m² also needs fire-engine access within 30 m travel. Other designs are decided case by case by the Fire Services Department (FSD). Travel distance inside the shed follows the 1996 means-of-escape code.
- Renewal uses the original BD 108 plus written confirmation that the certification is still valid.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued November 2002. Revision October 2007, amending paragraph 5 and Form BA18.
- File reference: BD GP/BREG/P/16 (IV).

**What to submit**
- Submit Form BA18 and plans. The Building Authority (BA) issues the permit and audits cases at random.
- For renewal, submit the original BD 108 and written confirmation that the certification is still valid.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not certify Appendix A eligibility if the geotechnical or slope tests fail.
- A combustible shed without the 3 m separation does not comply.
- Do not rely on the hoarding structure to hold the shed up.
- The RGBC, RSC, AP, and RSE remain responsible for the structural-safety certification even though the BA may audit it.
