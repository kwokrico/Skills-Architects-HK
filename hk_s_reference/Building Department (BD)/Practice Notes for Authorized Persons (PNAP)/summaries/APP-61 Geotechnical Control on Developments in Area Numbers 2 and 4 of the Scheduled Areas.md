# APP-61

**Official title:** Geotechnical Control on Developments in Area Numbers 2 and 4 of the Scheduled Areas

**In plain words:** Scheduled Area No. 2 (north-west New Territories / Yuen Long) and Scheduled Area No. 4 (Ma On Shan) have marble cavities, so ground investigation and foundations are tightly controlled, and finding cavities early can force the building to move. This note does not grant a gross floor area or site coverage concession.

**Numbers that matter**
- This note complements the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 and PNAP APP-18.
- Ground investigation needs approval under Buildings Ordinance section 41(3). Plans are under Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulation 8(1)(l). Consent is required before you start. Staged investigation is preferred.
- Drill deep holes to find marble cavities. If marble is found, penetrate at least 20 m into sound marble. Use inclined holes where the main cavity trend is near vertical. Use geophysics between holes. Grout every hole on completion. Control flushing water, because it can start a sinkhole. Use a triple-tube core barrel and air foam where you need high-quality cores of the cavity filling.
- Give the district Chief Geotechnical Engineer at least 2 weeks’ prior notice before ground investigation starts (B(A)R regulation 20). Keep cores and samples on site until substantial completion so the Buildings Department and the Geotechnical Engineering Office can inspect them.
- Supervision and logging: Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009, a Competent Person (Logging), and Geoguide 3. Log cavity location and size, the cavity wall, the infill and the discontinuities. Put total core recovery, solid core recovery, rock quality designation and fracture index on the logs.
- Prefer driven piles or machine-bored piles for a heavily loaded structure on marble. Hand-dug caissons are discouraged because of dewatering and sinkholes (PNAP APP-59). For driven piles, use a heavy section and a modified or strengthened tip. Use more piles than the minimum so load can be shared, and consider that in the pile cap. For bored piles and barrettes, check cavities under and around each pile in the stress zone.
- Record every blow count: per 500 mm in easy driving and per 100 mm in hard driving. A suggested split between easy and hard is 10 blows per 100 mm. Plot blows per 100 mm against depth. Survey how far each pile wanders from its designed position. Assess the records at close intervals. Use trial piling, instrumentation and non-destructive testing as needed.
- Agree the monitoring scheme with the Building Authority before the foundations are completed. Submit results and an assessment every month. If settlement is unusual, notify the Buildings Department immediately. The Geotechnical Engineering Office may keep monitoring after the occupation permit.
- Flushing water is normally supplied in these areas (see PNAP APP-4; Yuen Long Town and Ma On Shan).
- Foundation plans must be accompanied by the supporting documents under B(A)R 8(1)(d)(ii), or they may be refused under Buildings Ordinance sections 16(1)(a) and 16(1)(i).
- Consent for the superstructure may be refused under section 16(3)(ba) if the performance review in clause 7.8 of the Foundation Code does not show adequate inspection and record assessment, or the necessary design changes and approved amendments.
- Further reading: GEO Technical Guidance Note 26 (foundations on marble), Technical Guidance Note 5 (ground-investigation sources), and GEO Publication 1/2006 (piles).

**What to submit**
- Ground-investigation plans, consent, and the notices and logs above, before and during investigation.
- The registered geotechnical engineer must submit an assessment that taking groundwater from wells will not harm the site or its surroundings.
- Foundation plans with the B(A)R 8(1)(d)(ii) documents, pile records, and the monthly monitoring assessment.
- The clause 7.8 performance review before superstructure consent.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not freeze the general building plans before the cavity investigation is adequate.
- Do not stop short of the 20 m of sound marble, leave holes ungrouted, or use too much flush water.
- Do not omit the registered geotechnical engineer’s well assessment.
- Do not go for superstructure consent with weak driven-pile blow-count or deviation records, or without the performance review.
