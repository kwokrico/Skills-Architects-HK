# APP-97 Consent Procedures for Building Works and Street Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2023 | Buildings Department (first issue November 1997)

> Scope note: Normal and fast-track consent under section 14(1)(b) of the Buildings Ordinance and regulations 31 and 33 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. It does not change the design standards that the plans must meet. The first-consent checklist in Appendix A is for the authorized person's own use and is not submitted.

## Regulatory Overview
Type I works are works that have not yet received a first consent, and they also include a major revision or an amendment that contains material deviations. Type II works are amendments to plans that already have approval and a first consent, excluding those major revisions and material deviations, and fast-track processing, in which approval and consent are given together, applies only to Type II works.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fast-track consent and the two-year review (paragraphs 5–8 and 11–16)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Marking | On the lower right-hand corner: the works are Type II works, naming them, and consent is applied for under regulation 33 for fast-track processing |
| Wrong classification | The Building Authority may refuse consent under section 16(3)(a) or (b) |
| Limit of the consent | Only what is shown on the plans approved at the same time. If a corresponding structural amendment is still required, do not carry out the works until that amendment is also approved and consented |
| Form | The paragraph 5 statement is accepted in lieu of Form BA8 for Type II consent |
| Plans approved more than 2 years earlier | Section 16(3)(d) will be used to refuse consent if the works do not comply with the current standards in the Ordinance and allied regulations. It will not be used to enforce an approved or draft outline zoning plan introduced after the building plans were approved |
| New regulation | Under section 39(2), works already being carried out, or already consented, keep the previous law. If the works a new regulation requires are not on plans approved within the 2 years before the first consent application, consent may be refused under section 16(3)(a). If consent is not refused on that ground, amended plans are submitted as necessary before the occupation permit, which may be refused under section 21(6) if the new regulation is not met |
| Sanctions | Sections 7 and 40 if works start without the necessary consents |

**SD takeaway:** Apply for first consent within 2 years of approval if the design is to stay on the standards then in force, and use fast-track consent only for amendments that are not a major revision.

### 2. Items to clear before the first superstructure consent (paragraph 9 and Appendix A, Parts 1 and 2)
| Item | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Adjoining structure | Existing party wall or adjoining building is structurally safe, and required shoring or temporary support is installed |
| Site board | Names and telephone numbers of the authorized person, engineers, and contractor displayed. PNAP ADV-12 |
| Forms | Form BA8, or Form BA8A for concurrent consent, and the supervision plan |
| Concession conditions, if imposed | Provisional BEAM Plus assessment (PNAP APP-2, APP-130, APP-150, and APP-151); estimated energy performance of common parts for a domestic development, or of the whole building for a non-domestic development; provisional energy efficiency report for a domestic or composite development (PNAP APP-151 and APP-156) |

**SD takeaway:** Do not apply for the first superstructure consent until shoring is in place and any BEAM Plus or energy-report condition attached to a concession has been met.
