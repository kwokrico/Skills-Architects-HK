# ADV-24 Floor Drains in Kitchens and Bathrooms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue June 2003 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: A design suggestion so the water seal of a kitchen or bathroom floor drain does not dry out. Sanitary-fitment numbers and drainage standards remain in the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations.

## Regulatory Overview
Water-seal traps are required for all sanitary fitments, including floor drains if they are provided, before connection to a common drainage stack, and the seal can dry out by evaporation unless it is replenished. Authorized persons may divert some waste water from a waste fitment into the U-trap of a kitchen or bathroom floor drain so that manual topping-up is not required.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Diversion into the floor-drain trap (paragraphs 2–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Aim | Replenish the trap from waste-water discharge |
| Compliance | The design must comply with the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations and ensure a smooth discharge |
| Backflow | Prevent back-flowing at the floor drain |
| Modification | Any provision that needs a modification of those regulations may be considered on the merits of the case, on application |

**SD takeaway:** If a kitchen or bathroom has a floor drain, route some waste discharge into its U-trap and detail the connection so the trap cannot back-flow onto the floor.
