# APP-3

**Official title:** Nomination of an Authorized Person, Registered Structural Engineer or Registered Geotechnical Engineer to act in stead

**In plain words:** This note lets an authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) name a short-term stand-in during illness or absence from Hong Kong. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Nominate the stand-in on Form BA21 (building or street works) or Form MW09 (Class I minor works) within 7 days of the incapacity or absence. The statutory hooks are Buildings Ordinance sections 4(2) and 4A(5), Building (Administration) Regulation 23(2), and Building (Minor Works) Regulation 49.
- One BA21 can cover all active building and street works, with the project list in two copies. If an RSE or RGE works under more than one AP, use one BA21 for each AP, signed by that AP. For Class I minor works, one MW09 covers all active minor-works projects.
- The nominee must quote the BA21 or MW09 when writing to the Buildings Department, and cannot nominate a further AP, RSE, or RGE.
- If the original person recovers or returns before the nominated period ends, tell the Buildings Department by letter within 7 days. If a Class I minor-works nomination stops, the nominee notifies the Department on Form MW31 within 7 days (Building (Minor Works) Regulation 50).
- The arrangement is temporary, and only for illness or absence from Hong Kong. A period of more than 3 months, or frequent repeated absences, is generally not treated as temporary. The Building Authority may refuse, and the owner is advised to appoint someone else.

**What to submit**
- Submit BA21 or MW09. The Buildings Department acknowledges it, with a copy to the owner or the person arranging the minor works, and states the period and the reason.

**Watch-outs**
- Using a temporary nomination for a long absence, or for frequent absences, is likely to be refused, and the owner will be pressed to make a real new appointment.
- If several APs or projects are involved, split the BA21 forms by AP.
- The nominee must not pass the nomination on to someone else.
