# ADM-14 Minor Amendments to Plans and Specified Forms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 190; first issue December 1995)

> Scope note: Four ways to make agreed minor amendments to plans that the Buildings Department already treats as approvable. It is not a route for a redesign.

## Regulatory Overview
Where plans are approvable under the Buildings Ordinance subject only to minor amendments, the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer may make those amendments, and amend the related specified forms, with the Buildings Department's prior agreement. If agreed amendments are not returned by the agreed date, the submission may be disapproved under sections 16(1)(i) and/or 16(1)(c).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Four routes (paragraph 2)
| Route | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Amend in the Department | At the usual pre-amendment interview, amend the plans and specified forms in the Department and authenticate by signature. For specialist work (glazing, cladding, curtain wall, foundation, hoarding), the AP, RSE, or RGE, or a competent representative who understands the whole project, should attend with the specialist |
| (b) Substitute sheets | Hand in substitute sheets with amendments clearly annotated and individually authenticated. Agree first with the case officer. Sheets must reach the case officer on an agreed date, in any case not more than two working days from the approach. Specified forms may be substituted the same way |
| (c) Take part away | Written request identifying the plans or forms to be removed, with an undertaking to return them in not more than two working days. Agree the return date first. Annotate and authenticate |
| (d) Electronic Submission Hub | Submit amended plans, forms, and/or documents under "Revised Submission" to supersede or supplement the electronic submission. Agree the amendments and the deadline with the case officer first |

**SD takeaway:** Agree the minor amendment with the case officer first, and return substitute sheets or the taken-away set within two working days, or the submission may be disapproved.

### 2. Late return and lease effects (paragraphs 3–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Missed date | If plans or forms for routes (b), (c), or (d) are not returned or not submitted as a Revised Submission by the agreed date, disapproval may follow under BO sections 16(1)(i) and/or 16(1)(c) because prescribed plans and/or required specified forms have not been submitted |
| Lease implication | Paper: an extra copy of the amended plans, clearly annotated or coloured, plus a Development Schedule showing the alterations and the extent of lease compliance, for onward transmission to the Lands Department |
| Examples | Exclusion of floor space from gross floor area, or conversion of gross-floor-area-accountable space into back-of-house facilities for a hotel |
| Electronic | Submit those amendments and the Development Schedule via the Electronic Submission Hub for direct referral to the District Lands Officer |

**SD takeaway:** If a minor amendment changes gross floor area or a hotel back-of-house exemption, send the extra annotated set and a Development Schedule to Lands at the same time.
