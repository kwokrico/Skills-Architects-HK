# ADV-16 Street Name and Building Number
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2007 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issue July 1998)

> Scope note: How a building number is allocated and the minimum size for displaying it. The allocation principles are in the Rating and Valuation Department practice note, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 32(2) of the Buildings Ordinance empowers the Commissioner of Rating and Valuation to allocate building numbers and prescribe how they are marked, and the Commissioner or the Building Authority may order the owner to mark the building in that manner. For a new building, the numbering principles in Rating and Valuation Department Practice Note 1/2007 are to be taken into account at design stage.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Size, place, and when the number is issued (paragraphs 4–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Place | Clearly displayed above or at the side of the entrance, under Rating and Valuation Department Practice Note 1/2007 |
| Contrast | The number or number plate contrasts clearly with its background |
| Digit size | Minimum height 50 mm and minimum width 40 mm |
| Provisional number | Allocated on a written application from the developer or authorized person after consent to commence the building works |
| Official number | Notified by the Commissioner on completion. Display it under the same practice note once allocated |
| Alteration or repair | If the works obscure, displace, or damage a street name or building number, repair or replace it as soon as possible |
| Enquiries | 15/F, Cheung Sha Wan Government Offices, 303 Cheung Sha Wan Road; enquiries@rvd.gov.hk; 2150 8986 or 2150 8902; fax 2152 0138 |

**SD takeaway:** Leave a contrasting number plate at the entrance, with each digit at least 50 mm high and 40 mm wide, and apply for the provisional number only after consent to commence.
