# APP-117

**Official title:** Structural Requirements for Alteration and Addition Works in Existing Buildings

**In plain words:** New structural members in alteration and addition (A&A) work are designed to the current Building Regulations and codes, while existing structure may be checked against the regulations and codes in force when the building was designed, except for the wind, storage-load, and flat-slab cases set out below. This note does not directly change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), though the structure still has to work before an A&A scheme, including one that changes GFA, can proceed.

**Numbers that matter**
- If major wind-resisting walls or frames lose 5% or more of their stiffness, or if the area exposed to wind grows by 10% or more, check the building to the current wind code.
- A floor used for storage must be checked to the current Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) minimum imposed loads.
- A flat slab originally designed by working stress, if it is altered or given more load, must have its shear checked to the 1987 Concrete Code.
- If approved plans are missing, compare the new loading, bending moment, and shear with the original. Open up the structure for reinforcement and concrete strength if needed. Where reinforcement details exist, show them. The practice note then numbered 121 is relevant.
- For fire resistance, see the practice notes then numbered 202 and 231.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued December 2000.
- File reference: BD SED/T/48.

**What to submit**
- Submit structural plans with an assessment of whether the existing structure can take the work.
- The authorized person (AP) and registered structural engineer (RSE) must inspect the existing members that are affected. Submit site photos where possible, and include test results and the remedial works proposed.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not keep using the old wind code after a significant loss of stiffness or a significant increase in wind exposure.
- Do not convert a floor to storage without updating the imposed loads.
- Do not proceed without knowing the existing reinforcement and capacity.
