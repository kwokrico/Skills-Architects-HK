# ADV-6

**Official title:** Lightning Protection for Buildings

**In plain words:** Consider a lightning protection system on a new building. Decide what is needed by a risk assessment, not by a fixed Buildings Department form. This note does not state a gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) rule. Roof terminals, down conductors, and bonding still have to be coordinated with roof plant and the facade.

**Numbers that matter**
- Assess the risk, and design, build, and maintain the system, to a standard such as BS EN 62305, AS/NZS 1768, or IEC 62305.
- As a general rule, provide a system where the building is very tall or isolated; serves an exhibition or assembly use; is used for care or education, including a school, hospital, child-care centre, or residential care home for the elderly or for persons with a disability; is a public utility or a public service, which includes an exposed support for electric lines (Electricity Supply Regulations, Cap. 406A, regulation 11); or stands in an open area such as an outdoor swimming pool or a sports field.

**What to submit**
- No form is mandated by this note. Fixings and supports that are building works follow the normal structural submission. The risk assessment and the system details follow the standards cited above.

**Watch-outs**
- Omitting the risk assessment for a tall or isolated building, an assembly building, a care or education building, a public utility or public service, or an open swimming pool or sports field.
- Ignoring Cap. 406A regulation 11 for an exposed support to an electric line.
