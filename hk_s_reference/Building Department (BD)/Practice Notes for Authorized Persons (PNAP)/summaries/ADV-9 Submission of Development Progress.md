# ADV-9

**Official title:** Submission of Development Progress

**In plain words:** Twice a year, tell the Building Authority how each private building project in your hands is progressing, including a project where the client has only instructed you to prepare a proposal. The floor-area figures on this return are for government statistics. They are not the gross floor area (GFA) used under the Building (Planning) Regulations, and they are not lease site coverage or plot ratio.

**Numbers that matter**
- Return Appendix A in early March and early September each year.
- The form asks for the address, the lot number, the anticipated occupation permit date (temporary or full), a progress note, and, for domestic use, the type, the number of self-contained units, and the saleable area of each unit. For non-domestic use it asks for the type, the number of units, and the total internal floor area.
- A self-contained domestic unit is an independent dwelling with its own cooking facilities and a bathroom and/or lavatory.
- Saleable area, for this return only, is the floor area allocated exclusively to the unit, including balconies and verandahs, excluding common areas such as stairs, lift shafts, pipe ducts, lobbies, and communal toilets. Measure from the outside of the exterior enclosing walls and the middle of party walls. Exclude bay windows, yards, gardens, terraces, flat roofs, carports, and the like.
- Where a non-domestic floor is not yet partitioned, treat the whole floor as one unit. Example: 5 units a floor on 1/F to 3/F, plus unpartitioned 4/F to 10/F, is 22 units.
- Internal floor area is all enclosed space, measured to the internal face of the enclosing external walls and/or party walls.
- Send the return to the Director of Buildings, attention Chief Officer/Management, by fax to 2523 9380 or 2840 0451, or by post.

**What to submit**
- Appendix A only. It is not a plan-approval document and not an occupation permit document.

**Watch-outs**
- Leaving out a project that is still at the instruction-to-prepare-proposals stage, counting an unpartitioned floor as many units, or using gross floor area instead of saleable area or internal floor area, spoils the forecast this return is for.
