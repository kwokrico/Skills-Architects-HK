# APP-22 Dewatering in Foundation and Basement Excavation Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 1994 | Buildings Department (first issued February 1981; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Information the Building Authority expects when dewatering is caused by, or used for, foundation and basement excavation. It is not a groundwater design code; the General Specification for Civil Engineering Works and BS 8004:1986 are the companion references.

## Regulatory Overview
Procedures for dewatering, and precautions against impairing stability or causing undue settlement of adjoining buildings, streets, and land, must be to the satisfaction of the Building Authority. The plans and the reports listed below are submitted together.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the plans and reports must contain (paragraphs 2–3)
| Submission | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Plans | Foundation details of adjoining buildings; nearby underground utilities (fresh water pipe, gas main, sewer, and the like); dewatering proposals including ground treatment such as grouting and recharging; method and sequence of construction |
| Instrumentation on the plans | Location and details for monitoring effects on adjoining buildings, streets, and land, including change in groundwater in the adjoining ground; criteria for limiting movements and groundwater pressures and the action if those values are reached; intervals between readings; availability of monitoring records |
| Site investigation report | Groundwater conditions, permeability, compressibility and consolidation characteristics of the soil strata, particle-size analyses, and other results relevant to dewatering and settlement prevention |
| Adjoining buildings | Structure and condition of all adjoining buildings that may be adversely affected |
| Shoring or underpinning | Details, if any adjoining building needs them |
| Effects assessment | Assessment of excavation and dewatering effects on adjoining buildings, streets, and land, including the limiting-movement criteria |

**SD takeaway:** Do not submit a basement excavation that dewaters without adjoining foundations, utilities, recharge or grouting, limiting criteria, and a condition survey of every building that could move.

### 2. Supervision and records during the works (paragraphs 4–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Supervision | The appointed authorized person and registered structural engineer supervise and inspect so that the works follow the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations, and the approved plans, and so that design assumptions are verified during construction. Employ a suitably experienced full-time supervisor where necessary |
| Records | Piezometric and settlement monitoring records available at all times, with copies submitted to the Building Authority periodically |
| Companion documents | Relevant groundwater and drawdown sections of the General Specification for Civil Engineering Works, and BS 8004:1986 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep piezometers and settlement points live for the whole excavation, with limits agreed on the approved plans and records copied to the Building Authority.
