# ADM-21 Site Parameters — Documentary Proof
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue January 2017 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Which documents prove site area, boundaries, and site class for site coverage and plot ratio. It does not set the numerical limits in the Building (Planning) Regulations.

## Regulatory Overview
Maximum site coverage and plot ratio depend on the area of the site, which must be under the applicant's title or realistic prospect of control, and on boundaries and setting-out that support the claimed area and site classification. A lease plan from the District Survey Office or a land survey plan by an authorized land surveyor is the proof the Buildings Department accepts for processing; other documents are provisional until superstructure consent.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Acceptable proof and the superstructure hold point (paragraphs 2–5 and 8)
| Document | Status |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Lease plan by the District Survey Office, with lot areas, boundaries, and setting-out coordinates | Acceptable for processing general building plans, and required before superstructure consent |
| (b) Land survey plan by an authorized land surveyor, with the same details | Same as (a) |
| (c) Lease documents with approximate areas and boundaries but no setting-out coordinates | Provisional reference only, to facilitate plan approval |
| (d) Deed polls of subdivided lots, with or without coordinates | Provisional reference only |
| (e) Approved general building plans of existing buildings showing site area in the site-coverage and plot-ratio calculation | Provisional reference only |

Regulation 8(1)(h)(i) of the Building (Administration) Regulations requires a diagrammatic plan with dimensions and calculations of height, site coverage, plot ratio, and disposition under regulations 19, 20, and 21 of the Building (Planning) Regulations. Item 3(a)(1.1) Part C in Appendix A1 to PNAP ADV-33 requires a detailed lease plan or land survey plan with the general building plans. A condition under regulation 10 is imposed so that (a) or (b) is submitted before consent to commence superstructure. If the later plan changes the approved layout, site coverage, or plot ratio, amended general building plans are required before that consent is processed. The authorized person must update the parameters again before certifying completion.

**SD takeaway:** General building plans may proceed on a provisional lease or an old approved plan, but superstructure consent waits for a District Survey Office lease plan or an authorized land surveyor's plan.

### 2. Conflicts and rounding (paragraphs 6–7 and 9–10)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Conflict with a weaker document | A lease plan or land survey plan is generally preferred. See Buildings Ordinance section 14(2) |
| Conflict between lease plan and land survey plan | Give the rationale for adopting one of them, and obtain acceptance before superstructure consent |
| Coordinates and dimensions on the survey plan | To 0.001 m, under the Code of Practice issued under the Land Survey Ordinance (Cap. 473) |
| Area rounding | 5 up, 4 down: nearest 0.1 m² if the area is under 2,000 m²; nearest 1 m² if the area is 2,000 m² or above |
| Copies | Two sets of a land survey plan. One is referred to the District Lands Office for the District Survey Office, with form SMF-0043, a survey report, and a survey record plan (Lands Department Practice Note 2/2013). Lands Administration Office Practice Note 4/2008 requires the authorized person to ensure the works do not encroach on government land |

**SD takeaway:** Round site area to 0.1 m² below 2,000 m² and to 1 m² at or above 2,000 m², and resolve any clash between the lease plan and the land survey plan before superstructure consent.
