# APP-149

**Official title:** Appointment of Authorized Signatory to Act for Prescribed Registered Contractor and Temporary absence of Authorized Signatories in respect of Minor Works commenced under the Simplified Requirements

**In plain words:** This note applies the authorised-signatory rules to prescribed registered contractors under the minor works control system. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Companion to APP-138. First issue December 2010. Forms are in the appendices via PNRC 72.
- One authorised signatory per minor works submission. The authorised signatory who signs MW01 or MW03 is the responsible one.
- The authorised signatory’s name must appear on all related forms and must match the supervision plan.
- Change of authorised signatory where the contractor has more than one: PNRC 72 Appendix A. The authorized person countersigns for Class I. Give at least 7 days’ notice.
- Temporary authorised signatory: PNRC 72 Appendix B. The authorized person countersigns for Class I. Give 7 days’ notice, or 3 days’ proof for sudden incapacity. The nominee must be an authorised signatory the Building Authority has accepted for the same classes and types of minor works as the submission. Maximum 14 days acting, and 30 days in total.
- A temporary authorised signatory may sign site inspection records only. Not plans or the specified forms.
- Absence of more than 30 days means change the authorised signatory, or add an authorised signatory to the registration.

**What to submit**
- Notifications to the Building Authority. Class I needs the authorized person’s countersignature.
- Use PNRC 72 Appendix A for a change and Appendix B for a temporary nomination.

**Watch-outs**
- A temporary authorised signatory who signs minor works forms makes those forms invalid.
- If no authorised signatory is acting, the minor works must cease.
- A Class I temporary nomination without the authorized person’s countersignature is not acceptable.
