# APP-101 Podium Height Restriction under Building (Planning) Regulation 20(3)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Re-issued August 2009 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 223; first issued April 1998)

> Scope note: How the Building Authority (BA) may treat applications to relax the podium height limit in Building (Planning) Regulation 20(3). It is not a general podium design code.

## Regulatory Overview
Building (Planning) Regulation 20(3) restricts full site coverage of a non-domestic podium to a height of 15 metres. The note sets the factors and the general limits the Building Authority applies when considering a relaxation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When a relaxation may be looked at favourably (paragraphs 2–3)
| Factor or example | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Factors | Functional requirements, site constraints, impact on the environment, public interest, and the likelihood of abuse |
| Public transport | A building that provides a public transport terminus or a facility of a similar nature |
| Appearance | An increase in podium height that would give a better aesthetic or environmental effect |
| Special building | Buildings of special design or use |
| Lease | Buildings subject to special floor-height requirements under lease conditions |

**SD takeaway:** Treat 15 m as the full-site-coverage podium limit, and only test a taller podium where one of the listed examples is real.

### 2. Limits if a modification is given (paragraph 4)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Storeys in the podium | Not more than 4 |
| Height | Not more than 20 metres from ground level |

**SD takeaway:** A modified podium stays within 4 storeys and 20 m from ground level.
