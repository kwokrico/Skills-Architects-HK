# APP-130

**Official title:** Lighting and Ventilation Requirements – Performance-based Approach

**In plain words:** This note accepts performance standards in place of Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) 30, 31, and 32 for natural lighting and ventilation of habitable rooms and domestic kitchens, and it gives no gross floor area (GFA) concession and does not change site coverage (SC). It is a different note from PNAP APP-152, which holds the sustainable building design rules on building separation, street setbacks, and greenery.

**Numbers that matter**
- Natural lighting, as vertical daylight factor (VDF) at the centre of the window pane: 8% for a habitable room and 4% for a kitchen.
- Natural ventilation: 1.5 air changes per hour by natural means for a habitable room. A kitchen needs 1.5 air changes per hour by natural means plus 5 air changes per hour by mechanical means.
- The simplified method in Appendix A is deemed to meet the standards if the windows pass the unobstructed vision area (UVA) lighting tests in Part II and the ventilation rules in Part III.
- The UVA cone is 100°, centred on the line normal to the glazing, so 50° each side. The maximum cone length is the height of the facade, measured from the window head of the lowest storey that has a window up to the top of the main-roof parapet.
- Measure UVA to the lot boundary, or include the full street width if the site abuts a street. A sector beyond the common boundary or the street centreline may use a multiplying factor of 4, capped so that it does not exceed the UVA that could be measured out to the facade height.
- For an obstruction on the same site, if the vertical angle of obstruction is 30° or less, the area above that structure and inside the cone may be counted.
- A window counts only if it faces an uncovered space that is not closed on the opposite side by a building obstruction, the top of the window is at least 2 m above the floor, and the glass area, meaning the effective glazing, is at least 10% of the room floor area.
- Table 1 is for habitable rooms at 8% VDF, and Table 2 is for kitchens at 4% VDF. Both give the minimum UVA in square metres against facade height, for glazing equal to 10%, 15%, or 20% of the floor area. Stated examples: a habitable room with a 10 m facade needs 50, 30, or 20 m² of UVA at those three glazing ratios. A habitable room with a 100 m facade needs 2,400, 1,800, or 1,300 m². A kitchen with a 10 m facade needs 20, 15, or 10 m². Interpolate between the 10%, 15%, and 20% columns, and between facade heights. If glazing is more than 20%, still use the 20% column.
- Under the simple ventilation rule, primary openings must be at least 1/16 of the floor area and must face a clear area that complies as open air. A kitchen also needs 5 air changes per hour by mechanical means.
- The cross-ventilation relaxation: primary openings at least 2% of the floor area, and secondary openings at least 2% of the floor area, placed in the rear half of the room, on a different plane, and facing open air. Room depth measured from the primary opening may extend to a maximum of 12 m. That relaxes the depth limit in regulation 32.
- For B(P)R 31(2), the base may be measured at an angle of 15° or less from the external wall.
- A project may mix the prescriptive regulations and these performance standards.
- An authorized person (AP) may use a validated lighting simulation, or computational fluid dynamics (CFD), instead of the simplified method. Validation notes are in Appendices B and C.
- Wholesale conversion to office use: the Building Authority (BA) will look favourably on a modification of regulations 30 and 31 if there is adequate artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation, and an energy-efficient design that achieves 40% in the energy use (EU) category and 40% in the indoor environmental quality (IEQ) category of BEAM Plus, under the Hong Kong Green Building Council (HKGBC).
- On a plan-approval application for that wholesale conversion path, submit an HKGBC letter acknowledging registration of the project, and an undertaking to submit a BEAM Plus Provisional Assessment showing 40% in EU and IEQ before consent is given, and a Final Assessment showing 40% in EU and IEQ within 6 months of the BA’s acknowledgement of Form BA14.
- First issued December 2003. Last revision February 2015. This revision December 2016, amending Appendix A paragraph 6.
- File references: BD GP/BREG/P/42; P/18/1(E)(VII); P/18/1(G)(II).

**What to submit**
- Apply to modify B(P)R 30, 31, and 32 when the performance path is used. Prove the result with the UVA tables or with a validated simulation or CFD study.
- For wholesale conversion of an industrial building to offices, submit the HKGBC registration acknowledgement and the undertaking on the two BEAM Plus milestones: provisional assessment before consent, and final assessment within 6 months of acknowledgement of Form BA14.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not count a window that fails the 2 m head height, the 10% glass area, or the open-air facing test.
- Facade height runs to the main-roof parapet. An intermediate roof is the wrong measuring point.
- The factor of 4 on a sector beyond the boundary or street centreline is capped by the cone measured to the facade height.
- The cross-ventilation relaxation needs a real secondary opening, on a different plane, in the rear half of the room.
- A wholesale conversion without BEAM Plus registration and the undertaking milestones puts the modification and the consent at risk.
- Building separation, the 15 m setback, and greenery percentages belong to PNAP APP-152. They are not in this note.
