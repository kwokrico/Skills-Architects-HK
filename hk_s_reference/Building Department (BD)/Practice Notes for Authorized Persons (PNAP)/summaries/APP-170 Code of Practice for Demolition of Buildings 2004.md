# APP-170

**Official title:** Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004

**In plain words:** Demolition design and method statements must follow the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 as amended by Appendix A (September 2016) and Appendix B (October 2023), and a similar practice note went to registered contractors. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue of this practice note: October 2023. The amendments are on the Buildings Department website.
- September 2016 (Appendix A) metrics from the amendment table: bamboo scaffold ties at most 4 m horizontally and vertically; a scaffold more than 15 m high needs steel brackets at intervals of 15 m or less; after demolition, an unbraced scaffold must stand no more than 2 m above the nearest anchor; an optional extra heavy-duty nylon net of at least 3.5 mm diameter, with mesh not more than 50 mm; tarpaulin fire performance Type B under BS 5867.
- September 2016 themes, besides those figures: the registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) statutory role; Minor Works Control System (MWCS) Type G demolition; scaffold catch-fan and typhoon stability; an extra heavy-duty nylon net layer; guidance for machinery and for demolition of aged LCC buildings; updated waste-disposal facilities and debris procedures.
- October 2023 (Appendix B):
  - Table 3.4: propping for lightweight mechanical plant, maximum 5,800 kg.
  - Clause 3.8.1: Hong Kong Institute of Construction (formerly the Construction Industry Training Authority, CITA).
  - New clause 3.8.8: precautions at the interface of adjacent demolition and construction sites.
  - New clause 3.8.9: floor openings and free edges.
  - New clause 3.8.10: control of debris conveyed through floor openings.
  - Clause 3.10.7: clarification of construction and demolition (C&D) material disposal.
  - Revisions to the exterior column demolition method and Figure 4.5.
  - Form BA20: post personal information near the site front entrance.
  - Appendices F and G: temporary platforms are required unless the cantilevered structure is demolished by cut-and-lift or a similar method (clause 3.5.1(B)).

**What to submit**
- Demolition design, method statements, and plans must reflect the amended Code.
- Involve an RGE where there is a geotechnical aspect.
- MWCS Type G demolition follows the Technical Guidelines. It does not follow this Code’s prior-approval path.
- Continuous supervision is by the registered specialist contractor (Demolition). Safety features are designed by the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or RGE, as applicable.

**Watch-outs**
- Demolition beside slopes or retaining walls with no RGE input.
- Unsecured cantilevered bamboo above the top floor, or above the floor being demolished, in a typhoon.
- Missing temporary platforms for a cantilever that is not demolished by cut-and-lift.
- Weak precautions at the interface with an adjoining site, or uncontrolled debris through floor openings.
