# APP-132

**Official title:** Site Coverage and Open Space Provision

**In plain words:** The Building Authority may relax site coverage (SC, how much of the site the building covers) under Building (Planning) Regulations 20 and 25 when the design sets the building back or leaves enough unbuilt open space. Those SC concessions depend on the same pre-conditions as the gross floor area (GFA, floor area counted for the development) concessions in APP-151, and town-plan SC limits and any lease change still apply.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 280. This revision is December 2012 (paragraphs 2–4 and 7 amended; paragraphs 5–6 added). Lands Department Practice Note 1/2004 still applies to lease modification.
- The Building Authority may modify Building (Planning) Regulations 20 and 25 case by case.
- Setback approach: criteria (a) to (f) must all be met. (a) Setback area at least 8% of the site for non-domestic use, and at least 18% for domestic use. On a composite building, apply 8% and 18% to the matching portions.
- (b) Setback length at least half the frontage and at least 10 m, or the full frontage if the frontage is under 10 m.
- (c) Satisfactory control and maintenance of the setback, flat roofs, and covered areas. (d) The setback is landscaped and/or paved, open and uncovered, with no permanent building structures other than landscaped features and perforated boundary walls. (e) It improves the street environment. (f) Do not count land dedicated or surrendered for public passage under Regulation 22.
- If those criteria are met, Appendices A (domestic) and B (non-domestic) set SC by height. Total covered area (the footprint, including Joint Practice Note 1 and 2 features that are exempt from SC, and excluding APP-19 projections) must not exceed 63% for domestic use. For non-domestic use it must not exceed 80% of the site area or the Appendix B percentage, whichever is greater.
- Left out of the SC check at a level: (a) unexcavated or backfilled ground under the footprint on a sloping site; (b) open-sided covered areas of non-domestic buildings that are green features under Joint Practice Notes 1 and 2, used as common areas, open to all occupants, with no commercial activity.
- Open-space approach: unbuilt open-space volume at least that of a viable notional scheme, and GFA not above that scheme. Maximum SC at any level is 63% for the domestic building or domestic part. For the non-domestic building or part it is 92% at 24 m or less from the street, and 80% more than 24 m from the street. Full maximum SC on every floor may not qualify as innovative design.
- Unbuilt volume is measured from the mean level of the lowest abutting street to the mean roof height over the highest usable floor of the notional scheme.
- Appendix A domestic SC from street level: 24 m or less → 63%; the 25 m step → 62%, then decreasing, to over 61 m → 40%.
- Appendix B non-domestic SC: 24 m or less → 92%, decreasing to over 61 m → 65%.
- SC concessions under paragraphs 3 to 5 use the same pre-requisites as the GFA concessions in APP-151. Outline zoning plan SC still applies.

**What to submit**
- A case-by-case modification showing the setback criteria, or the open-space criteria plus a viable notional scheme that meets the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations, and the outline zoning plan.
- A lease modification through Lands Department procedures if the lease must change. A premium may be payable.

**Watch-outs**
- Missing the APP-151 pre-requisites means the SC concessions are not available.
- Counting a dedicated public passage under Regulation 22 as setback fails criterion (f).
- Maximum SC on every floor is not, by itself, innovative design.
- Outline zoning plan SC controls, and lease modification or premium, still apply.
