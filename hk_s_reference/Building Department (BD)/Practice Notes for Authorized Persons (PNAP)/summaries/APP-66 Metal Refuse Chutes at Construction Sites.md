# APP-66

**Official title:** Metal Refuse Chutes at Construction Sites

**In plain words:** Temporary chutes made from old metal barrels are a poor way to drop site rubbish, because they are noisy and can collapse, and checking them is a site-safety duty of the authorized person (AP) and the registered structural engineer (RSE). This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Watch-outs**
- An external chute of old metal barrels is unsatisfactory. Noise is a nuisance. Weather and the impact of debris affect stability. Collapse can injure workers and people passing by.
- Prefer a safer way to lower or remove rubbish. If a chute is used, assemble, secure and install it properly, use materials that are strong enough, and prefer a location inside the building so noise is reduced.
- An external, makeshift metal-barrel chute that is not properly assembled and secured is a collapse and public-injury risk. The authorized person and the registered structural engineer have a role in the site safety measures.
