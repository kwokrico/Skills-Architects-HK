# APP-62 Protection of Sewage and Drainage Tunnels
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 2018 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 165; first issue April 1994)

> Scope note: Limits on building works near sewage tunnels gazetted under the Sewage Tunnels (Statutory Easements) Ordinance, and near other Drainage Services Department tunnels. Scheduled Area No. 5 is the sewage tunnel protection area. Plans within 100 m of a gazetted sewage-tunnel centreline, and ground investigation in Area No. 5, are circulated to the Drainage Services Department and the Geotechnical Engineering Office. Other tunnels follow Drainage Services Department Practice Note No. 2/2017.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 17A of the Buildings Ordinance allows the Building Authority to refuse or condition proposals incompatible with gazetted sewage tunnel works. Sewage tunnels are normally built with a minimum cover of 30 m of bedrock, but foundation, shaft, borehole, and dewatering works can still affect them, and rock is Grades I to III while soil is Grades IV to VI plus reclamation, marine, and alluvial deposits, as classified in Geoguide 3.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Limits beside a sewage tunnel (Appendix, paragraphs 8–12)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Pressure change on a structure in soil | Not more than 20 kPa, or 5% of total overburden pressure where the structure is deeper than 20 m, from filling, dewatering, and foundation loads including construction loads |
| Pressure change on a structure in rock, where ground-pressure change cannot be assessed | Hydrostatic pressure not increased or decreased by more than 50 kPa |
| Movement | Calculated final diametric distortion not exceeding 0.1% of the internal diameter, and calculated total movement in any plane not exceeding 20 mm |
| Vibration | Peak particle velocity not exceeding 25 mm/s from blasting, and 15 mm/s from pile driving or withdrawal or any prolonged vibration. The same limits apply to ground investigation shocks and percussion or hammer drilling |
| Exclusion | No hole or excavation, and no pile, foundation, well, soil nail, horizontal drain, or rock bolt or dowel, within 3 m of any point of a sewage tunnel structure, and no ground anchor within 3 m, without prior approval of the works and the method |
| Within 10 m | Detailed method statements for construction and for monitoring alignment and depth, before works commence. Monitoring data and interpretation are submitted for the duration of the works |
| Dewatering and wells within 100 m | Assess leakage of sewage and migration of gas, and submit a monitoring programme |
| Shaft extent | External diameter in soil; internal diameter plus 5 m in rock. The Ap Lei Chau to Aberdeen section is under soil or shallow rock at both ends; elsewhere cover is generally at least 30 m of rock |
| Ground investigation in Area No. 5 | Submit hole locations relative to the tunnels, depths, and method statements. If a hole is less than 10 m from a tunnel, also a method for checking alignment and depth. No hole within 3 m |

**SD takeaway:** Keep piles, anchors, wells, and excavations more than 3 m from a sewage tunnel, hold pressure change to 20 kPa in soil or 50 kPa hydrostatic in rock, and do not let calculated movement exceed 20 mm.
