# APP-162 Conditions and Requirements Imposed under the Buildings Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: The kinds of conditions the Building Authority may impose under section 17 when approving plans or consenting to work, and the smart-site alert systems required from 1 July 2025 on projects over $30 million. Common conditions are summarised in Appendix A and are not exhaustive. Quality-supervision detail is in PNAP APP-158 and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009. It is not a structural design code.

## Regulatory Overview
Conditions may cover quality supervision, qualified supervision, quality assurance, material testing, instrumentation, and records, and they are complied with before consent, during the works, or at certification of completion. From 1 July 2025, a project whose estimated building-works cost exceeds $30 million is conditioned to use two smart alert systems where mobile plant or tower cranes are used.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Supervision and the $30 million threshold (paragraphs 2–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who supervises | The authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered geotechnical engineer give periodic supervision. The registered contractor gives continuous supervision, commensurate with the task. Connecting steel reinforcing bars to type 2 mechanical couplers is an example of full-time technically competent persons of the contractor. Laying and fixing bars may be inspected at hold points |
| Quality supervision | Part of the supervision plan under the 2009 code and the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009. Further guidance in PNAP APP-158 |
| Qualified supervision | Item 6 of section 17(1) may impose it for certain stages or operations |
| From 1 July 2025 | If estimated total cost of building works exceeds $30 million, conditions under item 6(e) require the Mobile Plant Alert System and the Tower Crane Alert System under the Smart Site Safety System, on the first approval or major revision, and/or the first consent, for demolition, site formation, excavation and lateral support, foundation, pile cap, and superstructure works that use mobile plant and/or tower cranes. The same applies to an alteration and addition project that involves structural works and meets the cost threshold |
| Superstructure already announced | Circular letter of 28 March 2024 made the two systems mandatory for superstructure using mobile plant or tower cranes from the first approval or major revision on or after 1 July 2024, and the subsequent consent |
| Declaration | Under regulation 10 of the Building (Administration) Regulations, with the first consent application, whichever works type is submitted earlier. Later consents do not need another declaration. Failure to declare may refuse consent under section 16(3)(b). If the declaration exceeds $30 million, the conditions are imposed on the first consent for all the work types in paragraph 4 |

**SD takeaway:** On a job declared over $30 million, plan for the mobile-plant and tower-crane alert systems from the first consent that uses those machines.

### 2. Materials, monitoring, and records (paragraphs 6–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Section 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulation. Adequately tested by recognised tests. Follow the manufacturer’s installation, operation, and maintenance recommendations. Do not use a material until the tests are passed, unless full traceability allows a failed batch to be removed |
| Effect of the works | Monitor vibration, tilting, and movement of adjacent buildings, structures, land, streets, or services, and, if appropriate, the groundwater table |
| Records | Regulation 10 may require record plans and reports for inspection and for certification of completion |
| On site | Keep the supervision team aware of the conditions, and keep a copy of the approval and consent letters on site. Appendix A lists common conditions and is not exhaustive |

**SD takeaway:** Monitor movement of neighbours and the groundwater table during the works, and keep the consent conditions on site.
