# APP-135

**Official title:** Quality Supervision of Soil Nailing Works

**In plain words:** This note sets supervision, testing, and certification rules for soil nails, including when a site trial is needed before working nails. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 284. First issue October 2003. Revision February 2006 (registered geotechnical engineer; non-destructive testing; paragraphs 7–18 and appendices deleted; new paragraph 7).
- Nails longer than 20 m raise the risk of collapse, high grout loss, and poor grout quality. Adverse ground may need a registered geotechnical engineer buildability assessment and a site trial, including a pull-out test where possible, before working nails.
- Difficult ground includes loose fill or colluvium, rockfill, soil pipes, buried streams, open discontinuities, alternating soil and rock, and groundwater flow.
- References: CEDD landslip preventive measures specifications and drawings, and GEO Report 133 on non-destructive testing of steel soil nail lengths.
- Quality supervision, technically competent person qualifications, and non-destructive testing details follow the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision.

**What to submit**
- The site formation submission must include site trial details and locations if a trial is required, pull-out test procedures, and a non-destructive testing method to check installed nail length, with provisions that allow the testing.
- The registered geotechnical engineer includes the trial, pull-out, and non-destructive testing particulars in the site formation approval package.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not install working nails before the trial and pull-out test where nails are long or the ground is adverse.
- The design must include enough non-destructive testing provisions to verify nail length.
