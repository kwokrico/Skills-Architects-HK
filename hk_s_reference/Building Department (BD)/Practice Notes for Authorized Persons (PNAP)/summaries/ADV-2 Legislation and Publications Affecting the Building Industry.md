# ADV-2

**Official title:** Legislation and Publications Affecting the Building Industry

**In plain words:** Appendix A is a reading list of the main ordinances, technical memoranda, codes, design manuals, and practice notes that affect building work. It is not exhaustive, and it is general information only. It does not itself set a gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) rule. Many of the instruments it points to do govern floor area, sanitary fitments, energy, lifts, town planning, and the lease.

**Numbers that matter**
- The building group includes the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) and its subsidiary legislation Cap. 123A to Cap. 123P: administration, construction, demolition, planning, private streets and access roads, refuse storage and material recovery chambers and refuse chutes, standards of sanitary fitments, plumbing, drainage and latrines, ventilating systems, oil storage, appeal, energy efficiency, minor works, minor-works fees, and inspection and repair.
- Other statutes the appendix excerpts include Cap. 7 (Landlord and Tenant), Cap. 51 (Gas Safety), Cap. 53 (Antiquities), Cap. 59 (Factories and Industrial Undertakings), Cap. 95 (Fire Services), Cap. 102 (Waterworks), Cap. 121 (Buildings Ordinance applied to the New Territories), Cap. 131 (Town Planning), Cap. 172 (Places of Public Entertainment), Cap. 295 (Dangerous Goods), Cap. 311 (Air Pollution Control), Cap. 337 (Demolished Buildings, Re-development of Sites), Cap. 344 (Building Management), Cap. 349 (Hotel and Guesthouse), Cap. 354 (Waste Disposal), Cap. 358 (Water Pollution Control), Cap. 370 (Roads), Cap. 400 (Noise Control), Cap. 406 (Electricity), Cap. 408, 409, and 417 (registration ordinances), Cap. 487 (Disability Discrimination), Cap. 499 (Environmental Impact Assessment), Cap. 502 and Cap. 572 (fire safety of premises and of buildings), Cap. 509 (Occupational Safety and Health), Cap. 610 (Buildings Energy Efficiency), Cap. 618 (Lifts and Escalators), and Cap. 585 (Land Titles, noted as not yet in operation at the time of the practice note).
- Technical memoranda include supervision plans under Buildings Ordinance section 39A, the environmental impact assessment process, and effluent standards under the Water Pollution Control Ordinance section 21.
- Buildings Department codes listed as examples include lifts and escalators building works 2011, dead and imposed loads 2011, demolition 2004, fire resisting construction 1996 and the Fire Safety Code 2011, foundations, mandatory building and window inspection 2012, oil storage 1992, overall thermal transfer value 1995, precast concrete 2003, means of access for firefighting 1995, means of escape 1996, site supervision 2009, structural concrete 2013, structural steel 2011, wind effects 2004, and the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access 2008.
- The appendix also points to codes of the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department, Fire Services Department, Labour Department, Lands Department, Transport Department, Home Affairs Department, and Environmental Protection Department; Geoguides, Geospecs, and CS1 from the Civil Engineering and Development Department; the Highways Department Structures Design Manual; the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines; joint practice notes; PNAP, practice notes for registered contractors, and mandatory building inspection practice notes; ProPECC, Lands Administration Office, Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office, Survey and Mapping Office, and Planning Department practice notes; minor-works guidelines; environmental impact assessment and water-pollution guides; and Town Planning Board guidelines.
- Buy or view publications through the Information Services Department Publications Sales Unit (2537 1910), the Government Bookstore, the Department’s order form or fax 2523 7195, puborder@isd.gov.hk, or the department websites.

**What to submit**
- Nothing under this note. Use Appendix A to find the instrument that governs a particular submission.

**Watch-outs**
- Treating the list as complete, or as current, without checking the latest chapter or code on the Department of Justice website or the Buildings Department website.
- Missing an interface statute, such as an environmental permit for a Schedule 2 designated project, a water-pollution licence, a noise-control technical memorandum, or the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance (Cap. 572), can delay or undermine an approval or an occupation permit.
