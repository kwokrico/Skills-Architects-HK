# ADM-19

**Official title:** Building Approval Process

**In plain words:** This is the main note on how the Buildings Department approves plans. The general building plan (GBP) is the layout that every other plan must follow. The Department checks fundamental issues only. You remain responsible for full compliance. The note also covers staged plans, concept drawings, computer area calculations, minor amendments after the first consent, and several fast tracks. It is the density gate: site area, site class, gross floor area (GFA), site coverage (SC), and plot ratio are checked here. The fast tracks speed the process. They do not create a GFA concession.

**Numbers that matter**

### Plan hierarchy

- The general building plan layout is the basis for the other plan types. The authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) must make the other plans match the latest approved general building plan. Approval of another plan type is not approval of the building layout shown on it.
- Before an occupation permit, a temporary occupation permit, or an alteration and addition (A&A) completion, every amendment must be approved and consented. The latest amended general building plan, structural plan, drainage plan, and site formation plan, where they apply, must tally with each other.
- For general building plans, superstructure, and drainage, the Department checks fundamental issues only. Non-fundamental items are not raised as disapproval items. You must still comply with the Buildings Ordinance and the regulations in full before work starts, and you must put right any contravention before you certify completion. The Department audits submissions in detail at random. Blatant or repeated contraventions lead to action by the Building Authority.
- A written pre-submission enquiry normally produces a letter of assurance within 45 days. Pre-submission conferences with departments are available. Use them early for a new structural theory, material, or system, a sophisticated design, or an unconventional method.
- Questions about compliance or interpretation of regulations and codes, during the development, are normally answered within 4 weeks.

### What the general building plan check covers

Density, site parameters: the claimed site area; whether site classification under Building (Planning) Regulation 19(2) has been determined; and any required private street or service lane, which is deducted from the site area and not built over.

Density, site coverage and plot ratio: GFA diagrams split into accountable, non-accountable, and exempted; the same split for site coverage; exempted and non-accountable areas; dedication or surrender for bonus area; and the overall plot ratio and site coverage checked against what is permitted, including any bonus granted, together with mean street level and podium and building height.

Safety, means of access for firefighting: number and position of fireman’s lifts and firefighting and rescue stairways; initial access; emergency vehicular access.

Safety, means of escape: single-staircase rules; the means-of-escape table (occupancy, exit doors, and route widths); discharge value; ground-floor discharge; how staircases relate to each other; direct distance and travel distance; an independent staircase for a basement, a kindergarten, or a child-care centre; refuge floors; premises under the Places of Public Entertainment Ordinance.

Safety, fire resisting construction: the fire resisting construction table against compartment volume and use; protection of adjoining buildings; external walls of staircases and lobbies; smoke vents; a bridge or tunnel, including the fire shutter and the bypass lobby.

Health and environment: windows to a habitable room, kitchen, or office, and where they face; open space required and provided, and where it is.

Allied legislation, major issues only: barrier-free access (entrance, accessible route, accessible toilet); the fire certificate; the outline zoning plan; vehicular run-in and run-out (Transport Department and Highways Department); geotechnical assessment under APP-25; airport height; railway protection; road works under the Roads (Works, Use and Compensation) Ordinance (Cap. 370) section 22; sewage tunnels under the Buildings Ordinance section 17A; monuments under the Antiquities and Monuments Ordinance (Cap. 53) section 6.

### Superstructure, drainage, and other fundamentals

- Superstructure, Appendices B and B1: the master framing and the detailed plans are checked for stability and framing, fire resistance rating, loading, and materials. The checklist covers the structural system; wind modelling and whether the general building plan loads match the foundation loads; stability under Building (Construction) Regulation 15, including maximum lateral deflection as a fraction of height; curtain-wall anchorage; robustness, especially of precast work; fire resistance rating against the general building plan and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011; floor loads under Building (Construction) Regulation 17(1) and (2); the Code of Practice on Wind Effects 2004 and impact under regulation 17(3) and (4); dynamic effects under regulation 17(5); the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 and the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011; concrete grade, which is flagged if it is above C45; reinforcement to CS2:2010; and prestress, steel, aluminium, and glass specifications.
- Drainage, Appendix C: a disposal method acceptable to the Drainage Services Department and the Environmental Protection Department; an underground layout with no cross-connection of storm water and foul water; on reclaimed land, clause 2.4 of the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017; drains kept out of slopes under APP-76; terminal manhole, invert, and sizes to the Drainage Services Department’s satisfaction; access, with common drains and stacks in common areas under APP-93 and no water-borne pipe inside the structure under APP-105; wells under APP-4; gathering grounds and the Water Supplies Department; a fish-culture zone within 200 m and the Environmental Protection Department; railway protection for deep excavation or a bored well, with the MTR Corporation; condensate under APP-112. Only a percentage of the drainage is checked at random.
- Demolition, site formation, excavation and lateral support, and foundation, Appendix D: codes and standards; geotechnical parameters; margins of safety; structural adequacy of the major elements; effects on adjoining buildings, lands, and services, including settlement and groundwater; whether the sequence can be built; precautionary measures. Demolition also covers hoarding and propping, the appraisal and method, stability during and after the work, and supports and protection.

### Staged general building plans

- For a new development whose first general building plan, or a major revision, is submitted after 31 March 2023, essential information may be deferred across three stages, following ADV-33 Appendix A1. Stage I is the first approval. Stage II is before superstructure consent. Stage III is before the occupation permit or temporary occupation permit.
- Building (Administration) Regulation 30(3)(c) applies to amendment plans for the deferred items, unless the change is a major revision under regulation 30(3)(b). The Building Authority may refuse consent under Buildings Ordinance section 16(3)(a), or refuse an occupation permit or temporary occupation permit under section 21(6)(a), if the plans and details required by Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1) are not all approved at the time that matters.
- Stage I approval covers Stage I items only. Under Building (Administration) Regulation 10, a full set of Stage I and Stage II general building plans is required for Stage II approval before superstructure consent. Mark the Stage II set: “This set of plans is submitted for Stage II Curtailed Check”.
- An early Stage II check on the first general building plan is considered case by case for a simple building (a single-storey plant room, or the two-storey warehouse in paragraph 36), a government, institution, or community use or a short building covenant, and an advanced project that is close to superstructure consent.
- Staging does not apply to alteration and addition.

### Concept drawings

- Coloured key plans are recommended for a pre-submission conference, an enquiry, or the formal general building plans. They summarise, in broad terms, site coverage (accountable, non-accountable, and exempted), GFA (accountable, non-accountable, and exempted, split into domestic and non-domestic), the fire strategy (means of escape, means of access, and fire resisting construction), and basic wheelchair access and facilities.
- Use A2, A3, or A4. Follow the sample colour schemes and the means-of-escape, means-of-access, and fire resisting construction signs on the Buildings Department website.

### Computer and BIM area calculation

- If floor areas are calculated by computer, submit a digital file that can be checked in AutoCAD, MicroStation, Revit, ArchiCAD, or another program the Building Authority accepts, together with the paper general building plans for centralised referral.
- AutoCAD and MicroStation follow Appendix F. Revit and ArchiCAD follow the BIM general building plan guidelines and the sample models on bd.gov.hk. See also ADV-34.
- If the CAD requirements are met and the area layers are clear, diagrammatic breakdowns of GFA, usable floor area, site coverage, plot ratio, refuge-floor area, and green-feature area need not be drawn on the plans. Annotations and dimensions of those areas are still required on the plans.
- The soft copy is the floor plans with the area-diagram layers. The hard copy is the layouts, area diagrams, and calculations without those breakdowns. The soft copy must match the hard copy, or the submission is rejected. The AP certifies each finalised DVD-ROM with a permanent marker.
- Use a non-rewritable DVD-ROM. AutoCAD Release 14 or later, file type .dwg, or MicroStation SE, J, or V8 or later, file type .dgn. One hard-copy drawing per file. Layer names follow the CAD Standard for Works Projects, for example ARC08240, ARC08244, and ARC08246 for non-domestic GFA; ARC08250, ARC08254, and ARC08256 for domestic GFA; ARC08210 to ARC08226 for site coverage; ARC08200 and ARC08204 for open space; ARC08290 and ARC08294 for usable floor area; and ARC082_8 for dimensions that the program generates. Use closed polylines. True dimensions are generated by the program. Areas and volumes go to 3 decimal places, in square metres or cubic metres. True scale is 1 unit = 1 mm, or 1 unit = 1 m.
- The completed-project BIM model is referred to the Lands Department and the Planning Department for three-dimensional spatial and planning updates. Submitting it is treated as consent to extract walls, floors, stairs, lifts, routes, uses, heights, bulk, and development parameters.
- A minor paper revision under ADM-14 that does not yet match the digital file may still be approved, if the digital file is updated at the next area-calculation amendment. At occupation permit, temporary occupation permit, or the alteration and addition completion certificate, submit a consolidated digital file.

### Minor amendments after the first consent

- With a Buildings Ordinance section 42(1) modification of Building (Administration) Regulation 33(1), prior approval and consent are not required for a minor amendment to building work, superstructure including secondary structure once the superstructure permit is granted, drainage, foundation, pile cap, site formation, or excavation and lateral support, except the exclusions in Appendix G. Document the amendments and keep them in the site office. Obtain approval and consent for all of them before the completion certificate, occupation permit, or temporary occupation permit. The work must comply with the regulations, department requirements, and the lease before it is carried out.
- Apply for the modification with, or after, the first consent for that type of work.
- These are not minor, and must not be deferred. A major or localised major revision under APP-55. A change with a material effect on a fundamental issue. A slope, retaining wall, or adjoining-stability concern. An exemption or modification application. Non-compliance with an outline zoning plan or a Town Planning Board decision. Work outside the lot.
- Superstructure that is not minor: a change to overall stability, a fundamental issue, or an exemption.
- Drainage that is not minor: a layout change driven by a major building revision; a change to the disposal system or the public-sewer connection; excavation deeper than 3 m; work outside the lot.
- Foundation that is not minor: a change to design assumptions, method, materials, precautions, tolerances, monitoring, founding, or capacity; non-compliance with railway Area 3, APP-24 monitoring; non-compliance with sewage-tunnel Area 5, APP-62, or Drainage Services Department Practice Note 2/2017; a bulk-excavation overrun in Area 1; H-piles on bedrock steeper than 25 degrees; piles over interacting slopes; a breach of a boundary or sensitive clear distance; a change between Type 1 and Type 2 couplers, or a new quality-assurance scheme; a pile moved more than 5 m; a pile-count increase of more than 5% for piles other than large-diameter bored piles, or more than 15% for large-diameter bored piles. Round the percentage up.
- Pile cap, excavation and lateral support, and site formation follow the same idea: assumptions, monitoring, and the Area 1, 3, and 5 limits. A site formation level change of more than 1 m, or a permanent earth-retaining-structure retained-height change of more than 1 m, is not minor. A localised strut tweak that does not reduce stiffness may remain minor.
- Secondary structural elements include curtain wall, window wall, cladding, space frame, precast elements, canopy, skylight, balustrade, external ceiling, grille or louvre, ventilation duct, and maintenance platform. They do not include overall stability.

### Concurrent approval and consent, and fast tracks

- Alteration and addition, drainage, and secondary structural elements may be approved and consented together if there is no foundation, except a spread footing with excavation not deeper than 4.5 m, and no significant geotechnical content; no precautionary or safety work that the Building Authority must be satisfied with before the start; all the plans and documents under Building (Administration) Regulation 8; and all the consent supports, including supervision, hoarding, and APP-117 reports. Curtain-wall compliance certificates and tests under APP-37 may wait until the occupation permit or temporary occupation permit.
- Simple alteration and addition, fast track, 30 days: the AP and RSE confirm there is no structural involvement and no change to a fundamental issue, or that any change is minor and complies with the Ordinance; there is no referral except to the Fire Services Department; use the Appendix H certification and Form BA8A.
- Two-storey warehouse, Appendix I: concurrent processing if the warehouse is for general storage, not more than 2 storeys, and has no basement; the site is Class A, B, or C with emergency vehicular access under Building (Planning) Regulation 41D and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings; no compartment exceeds 28,000 m³ or 10,500 m²; and the other ADM-19 criteria are met. The Department processes this concurrent fast track within 45 days. The foundation bearing stratum is inspected within 1 working day of notification. You may go through the Efficiency Office One-Stop Centre, or direct to the Buildings Department with the full plan suite and the consent application.
- Curtain wall, window wall, and cladding repairs, Appendix J: prefer the minor-works system where the work is scheduled (items 1.48, 1.60, 1.61, 2.8, 2.48, 3.6, and 3.31). Otherwise a concurrent fast track of 30 days is available if the structural system is not altered. Submit a supervision plan. Tempered glass needs a heat-soak quality supervision plan and the manufacturer’s quality assurance scheme under APP-37. If the replacement has a worse thermal transmittance, submit a provisional overall thermal transfer value under APP-67 and, where it applies, an APP-156 energy report. A material upgrade needs structural, non-combustibility, and fire-resistance justifications. Like-for-like replacement of a component may waive structural analysis of the replacement and of the parent member.
- Form BA8A: the covering letter notes that approval and consent are concurrent. The consent effective date is the plan approval date, or the deemed approval date. Consent is not processed if the plans are not approved. If approval is given without concurrent consent, the 28-day period in Building (Administration) Regulation 32(3) runs from the approval date.
- Fire dampers, air-conditioning plant frames, and large metal ducts, paragraphs 40 to 43: minor-works items 1.46, 1.51, 2.42, and 2.46 are available. For a project whose superstructure consent, or alteration and addition consent, is after 31 August 2021, include these in the plans before the occupation permit, temporary occupation permit, or alteration and addition completion certificate, unless the alteration and addition plans state the minor-works route. The streamlined path is an amended general building plan for the locations (ADV-33 Appendix A4) and a concurrent 30-day structural approval and consent for the frames and ducts. Alternatively, put the layouts and the ADV-33 Appendix B11 details in the first general building plan and superstructure submission, and use the minor-amendment deferral for later tweaks.

**What to submit**

- Prepare with the ADV-33 checklists, sample tables, and sample drawings. Structural Part I follows ADM-8.
- Concurrent tracks: Form BA8A and a covering letter. The warehouse may go through the Efficiency Office or direct to the Department. Use Appendix H, I, or J as it applies.
- For post-consent minor amendments: the modification of Building (Administration) Regulation 33(1), site records, and approval and consent of every amendment before occupation permit or temporary occupation permit.

**Watch-outs**

- Treating foundation, drainage, or structural approval as layout approval is wrong. The general building plan layout prevails. A mismatch at occupation permit or temporary occupation permit is fatal.
- A curtailed check is not a check of everything. Non-fundamental items remain your duty. An audit can lead to disciplinary action for a blatant or repeated breach.
- Carrying out an amendment that Appendix G says is not minor, for example a pile increase above 5% or 15%, a pile moved more than 5 m, a formation change of more than 1 m, work outside the lot, or a clash with the outline zoning plan, without prior approval and consent.
- Treating a Stage I-only general building plan as complete for superstructure consent or occupation permit risks refusal under section 16(3)(a) or section 21(6)(a).
- A soft copy that does not match the hard copy, or area layers that do not follow the CAD standard, is rejected. An occupation permit without the consolidated digital area file is incomplete.
- An Appendix H fast-track certificate that falsely says there is no change to density, fire, health, or allied matters is a false certificate.
- Leaving fire dampers or large ducts until late, on a project consented after 31 August 2021, delays the occupation permit.
