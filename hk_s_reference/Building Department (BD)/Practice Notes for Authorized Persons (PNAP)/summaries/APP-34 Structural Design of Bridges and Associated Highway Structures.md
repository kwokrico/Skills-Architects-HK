# APP-34

**Official title:** Structural Design of Bridges and Associated Highway Structures

**In plain words:** Design these bridges to the Highways Department structures manual; the Buildings Ordinance (BO) applies when the bridge is inside a private lot or is supported by a private building, and the Director of Highways is the authority when it stands wholly on government land. Commercial use on a bridge is generally counted in gross floor area (GFA) under the outline zoning plan (OZP) where that use is allowed, while the structure itself is often outside the lot’s GFA envelope, depending on the lease and which authority is in control.

**Numbers that matter**
- Plans are examined by the Advisory Committee on the Appearance of Bridges and Associated Structures (ACABAS). The Building Authority (BA) arranges that through centralised processing. See also practice note APP-38, formerly PNAP 107.
- A bridge wholly or partly inside a private lot, or supported by a private building, is processed under the Buildings Ordinance. That includes a bridge between private buildings, and a bridge supported by a private building at one end and by government land at the other. It must also satisfy the Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) and the Buildings Department codes of practice and practice notes.
- A bridge wholly outside a private lot, free-standing on government land or a public highway, is exempt from the Buildings Ordinance. The Highways Department also requires the Transport Planning and Design Manual Volume 2 for geometry; the General Specification for Civil Engineering Works 1992 for materials and workmanship; GEOGUIDE 1 for retaining walls; lighting through the Highways Department Lighting Division and the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department (EMSD); the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes; and landscape treatment, in consultation with the Highways Department Landscape Unit.
- For a structure outside the lot, the first submission is to the Buildings Department, for transmission to the Highways Department: 3 sets of plans, 2 sets of calculations, and 2 sets of site investigation (SI) documents. The Highways Department certifies completion in writing.
- A bridge over a street may need an exemption under Buildings Ordinance section 31(1), under practice note APP-38.

**What to submit**
- For a bridge inside the lot, or partly supported by a private building: submit under the Buildings Ordinance, including the structural submission and ACABAS.
- For a bridge wholly outside the lot: submit through the Buildings Department for the Highways Department. The Highways Department permits the works, tests the foundations, checks quality control, and corresponds with the authorized person (AP) and the registered structural engineer (RSE).

**Watch-outs**
- Misreading the support or the connection, so the wrong authority is used.
- Leaving out ACABAS.
- A private-lot bridge that follows the Highways Department manual and ignores the Building (Construction) Regulations.
- An incomplete set of plans, calculations, or site investigation documents for transmission to the Highways Department.
