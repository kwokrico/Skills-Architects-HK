# APP-116

**Official title:** Aluminium Windows

**In plain words:** Meeting paragraphs 4 to 9 of this note is accepted as compliance with section 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) for aluminium windows, and a departure needs its own proof. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- A large window that meets the PNAP APP-37 criteria needs a structural submission. Other windows must still meet B(C)R safety and performance. The standards are in Appendix A of APP-37 and in PNAP APP-53.
- Fixing lugs must be stainless steel or hot-dip galvanised, at least 1.5 mm thick, and at not more than 300 mm centres. Wider spacing needs the authorized person (AP) or registered structural engineer (RSE) to show that stability and waterproofing are not compromised.
- Aluminium structural members must be at least 2 mm thick. A mullion must be at least 38 mm deep.
- A side-hung sash must not be wider than 700 mm. A top-hung sash must not be larger than 2.5 m².
- A 4-bar hinge in Appendix A must be stainless steel, with an adjustable friction shoe. Bars must be at least 2.5 mm thick. Each of the top and bottom bars needs at least 3 stainless-steel rivets of at least 4.8 mm diameter, or stainless-steel screws of at least 5 mm diameter. Anchorage plates, bars, or angles must be at least 3 mm thick, or the member must be thickened locally to at least 5 mm. The hinge must be at least 60% of the sash width. Keep dissimilar metals from touching.
- Appendix B recommends a projecting drip-nose fin on the top frame member.
- Locking devices follow APP-37. A corrosive cleaning agent may be used only if the window is washed at once with clean water.
- Field water-penetration tests are advised, using the methods in Annex A of the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018.
- Previously PNAP 248. First issued July 2001. Last revision March 2006. This revision February 2021.
- File references: BD GP/BORD/105 (II); GP/BREG/P/38 (II).

**What to submit**
- Submit a structural submission where the APP-37 size thresholds are met. For other windows, the AP, RSE, and registered contractor (RC) still owe a duty of care on design and installation quality.
- Repair or replacement of a window may be minor works under the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (B(MW)R) and PNAP APP-147.
- A similar practice note is issued to registered contractors.

**Watch-outs**
- Undersized aluminium sections, an openable sash that is too big, or lugs spaced too far apart can lead to a window falling out or leaking, and to public-safety liability.
- Do not fix a stainless-steel hinge with aluminium rivets. The two metals corrode.
- Lug fixing needs proper site supervision.
