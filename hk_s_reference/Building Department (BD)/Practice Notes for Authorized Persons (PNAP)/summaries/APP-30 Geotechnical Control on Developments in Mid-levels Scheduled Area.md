# APP-30

**Official title:** Geotechnical Control on Developments in Mid-levels Scheduled Area

**In plain words:** In Scheduled Area No. 1, the Mid-levels, ground investigation, demolition, bulk excavation, and foundations need early input from a registered geotechnical engineer (RGE), and a bulk excavation limit must be agreed before the design is fixed. This note does not give a gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) concession, but the limit and the Mid-levels rules can restrict basement depth, the volume of cutting, the foundation type, and the development envelope.

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 41(3): ground investigation needs Building Authority (BA) approval. Submit ground-investigation plans, including laboratory testing, under Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulation 8(1)(l), and obtain both approval and consent before starting. Follow Geoguide 2 and Geoguide 3. The Building Authority typically also requires adequate supervision under BO section 17(1)(6)(e) by an experienced engineer or engineering geologist, and the cores must be examined and logged. See also practice note APP-49.
- Demolition plans under B(A)R regulations 8(3) and 8(4) must be submitted for approval.
- Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) section 25 limits bulk excavation so that the cumulative effect on the hillside is kept down. Ask the Geotechnical Engineering Office (GEO) for a provisional bulk excavation limit early, normally within 10 working days.
- The authorized person (AP) and the RGE, and the registered structural engineer (RSE) if needed, submit the bulk-excavation-limit assessment to the Buildings Department for agreement before site formation, foundation, excavation and lateral support (ELS), or structural plans that the limit would constrain. Follow GEO Technical Guidance Note (TGN) 50. A limit fixed that way is treated as satisfying B(C)R section 25. Agreement is normally given within one month, and that figure is the final bulk excavation limit.
- Show the excavation envelope and the bulk excavation limit on the site formation plans (B(A)R regulation 8(1)(bb)(vii)).
- B(A)R regulation 11A: the foundation plan is submitted with the site formation plan. If it is not, the plans may be refused under BO section 16(1)(a), 16(1)(e), or 16(1)(i).
- A performance review may be imposed under BO section 2(1). If it is required and is not satisfied, an occupation permit (OP) or a temporary occupation permit (TOP) may be refused under section 21(6)(f).
- Further controls cover resistance to landslip debris, the sequence of works, and groundwater drainage, under BO section 16(1)(q), section 17(1)(6)(f), and sections 28A to 28D.

**What to submit**
- Appoint an RGE. Obtain ground-investigation approval and consent before those works start. Agree the bulk excavation limit before lodging submissions that the limit constrains. Lodge the foundation plans and the site formation plans together. Carry out a performance review when one is imposed.
- Provide one extra set of the key plans: general building plans; site formation plans with reports and calculations; foundation plans, including pile caps, with reports and calculations; and excavation and lateral support plans.
- The scheduled-area boundary is at the Building Information Centre (BIC) and on the Buildings Department scheduled-areas webpage. Use the Mid-levels Study and Ground Investigation Unit data under practice note ADM-7.

**Watch-outs**
- Starting ground investigation without approval and consent.
- Submitting foundation or excavation and lateral support plans before the bulk excavation limit is agreed.
- Foundation plans without the site formation plans at the same time, which can be refused under B(A)R regulation 11A.
- An occupation permit application when a required performance review has not been satisfied.
