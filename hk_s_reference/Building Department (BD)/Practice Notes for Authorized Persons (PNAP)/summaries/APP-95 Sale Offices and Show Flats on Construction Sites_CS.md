# APP-95 Sale Offices and Show Flats on Construction Sites
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1997 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Conditions for occupying a sale office or show flat on a construction site before the new building is certified for occupation. Public access is treated as incompatible with construction unless these conditions are met.

## Regulatory Overview
No part of a new building, whether still under construction or substantially completed, may be used as a sale office or show flat without a temporary occupation permit. No temporary building on a construction site may be so used without a temporary building permit and a temporary occupation permit for that purpose.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the Building Authority must be satisfied of (paragraphs 2–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Structure | The premises are structurally suitable |
| Fire separation | Adequately segregated from the rest of the construction works by fire-resisting construction |
| Access | Adequate independent and protected means of access and escape, with direct connection to a street |
| Fire service installations | Adequate, to the satisfaction of the Director of Fire Services |
| Conditions that may be imposed | A limit on the period of use; a restriction on the number of persons at any one time; partial or total suspension of building works during that use, if necessary |
| Application | Submit enough information to justify paragraph 2, and state the period and the expected number of persons |
| Breach | The temporary occupation permit may be revoked, and prosecution under Buildings Ordinance section 40(1) and (5) may follow |

**SD takeaway:** Isolate the show flat with fire-resisting construction, give it its own protected escape straight to a street, and obtain a temporary occupation permit before the public is admitted.
