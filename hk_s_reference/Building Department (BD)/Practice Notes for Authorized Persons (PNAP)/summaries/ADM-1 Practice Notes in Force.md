# ADM-1

**Official title:** Practice Notes in Force

**In plain words:** This note explains how Buildings Department practice notes are grouped and where to find the current set. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- From August 2009, practice notes fall into three parts. Part A, Administration, uses the prefix ADM and covers how the Buildings Department handles plan approval, consent, the occupation permit (OP), and related office matters. Part B, Application of the Buildings Ordinance and regulations, uses the prefix APP and explains how the Building Authority applies the Buildings Ordinance and other laws that affect plan approval and building control. Part C, Advisory, uses the prefix ADV and sets out good practice.
- Existing notes were re-issued with the new prefixes. The wording did not change at that re-issue. A note in one part can still contain information that belongs with another part.
- After the Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 2004, the notes are also sent to registered geotechnical engineers (RGEs). The full name is Practice Note for Authorized Persons, Registered Structural Engineers and Registered Geotechnical Engineers. The short name remains PNAP.
- Chinese versions were published with the re-issue. New and revised notes are bilingual.
- Printed copies stopped. Current notes are on the Buildings Department website (www.bd.gov.hk). Footnote indexes on older notes are deleted when a note is revised. Appendix A, the printed list of notes in force, is not kept up to date. Use the website list.

**What to submit**
- Nothing under this note. Use the current notes on the Buildings Department website, not an old printed Appendix A.

**Watch-outs**
- An old printed list or an old hard copy can miss a revision or a new serial number.
- Do not treat the three parts as sealed boxes. An ADM note can contain APP technical rules, and the other way round.
