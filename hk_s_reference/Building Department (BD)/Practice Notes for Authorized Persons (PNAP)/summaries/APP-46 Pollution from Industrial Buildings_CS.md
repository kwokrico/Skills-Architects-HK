# APP-46 Pollution from Industrial Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This update March 1992 | Buildings Department (first issued May 1987; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Drainage provisions so that untreated noxious effluent from industrial buildings does not enter drains, sewers, or watercourses, under Buildings Ordinance sections 24 and 28 and Regulation 90 of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations. A petrol interceptor is required where vehicles are serviced or parked; the note attaches standard details rather than stating dimensions in the text.

## Regulatory Overview
State the building uses at drainage-plan stage and make adequate provision to treat noxious effluent, or the plans may be rejected under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(i). For a flatted factory, failure to provide for future effluent discharge may lead to disapproval under Regulation 90.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Single-occupancy factories and flatted factories (paragraphs 3–7)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Single-occupancy specialized industrial building, processes known | The Environmental Protection Department is consulted so that specific facilities can be allowed for |
| Flatted factory | Assume that undertakings will discharge effluent of a volume or chemical composition calling for drainage facilities other than those in Regulation 5(1), unless specific facilities are provided |
| Outlet per industrial unit, unless specific facilities are provided | Individual outlet, adequately trapped, receiving treated trade effluent, connected to the foul-water system and not to any storm-water system. Less than 1,000 m2 usable floor area: 100 mm diameter. More than 1,000 m2: 150 mm diameter minimum. May be temporarily sealed at floor level until the occupant needs it. Effluent identified in Regulation 90 is treated by the occupant before discharge |
| Vertical foul-water downpipe | Generally 150 mm to 300 mm diameter, depending on the number of floors served |
| Vehicle servicing and parking | Provide a petrol interceptor |

**SD takeaway:** Give every flatted-factory unit a trapped foul outlet of 100 mm, or 150 mm if the unit exceeds 1,000 m2, and do not connect it to the storm drain.
