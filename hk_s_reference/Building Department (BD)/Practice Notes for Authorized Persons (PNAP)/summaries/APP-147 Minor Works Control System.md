# APP-147

**Official title:** Minor Works Control System

**In plain words:** This note explains the minor works control system, which allows listed minor works without prior Building Authority approval and consent. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage, though certified safe amenity features can have enforcement deferred, except under Buildings Ordinance section 14(1) and Building (Administration) Regulation 25.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue December 2010. This revision September 2021. There are 187 minor works items: Class I 58, Class II 68, Class III 61 (Building (Minor Works) Regulation Schedule 1). Submissions are free of charge. No referral to other departments, except Area No. 3 railway-adjacent footing, excavation, or drainage, which the Buildings Department forwards to MTRCL.
- Class I: a prescribed building professional (authorized person, plus registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer, or registered inspector as required) designs and supervises, and a prescribed registered contractor carries out the work. Notice of commencement and a completion certificate.
- Class II: the prescribed registered contractor prepares, submits, plans, supervises, and carries out the work. Notice and completion certificate.
- Class III: the prescribed registered contractor submits a combined notice and certificate of completion.
- Plan scales: plan and section at 1:100 or larger (ratio at least 1:100). Block plan at 1:500 or larger.
- Signboards: display the minor works submission numbers (Appendix D), black on white, letters and digits at least 35 mm in Arial. Use a yellow background when altering a previously unauthorised signboard.
- Validation schemes: Household Minor Works Validation Scheme for prescribed building works before 31 December 2010 in Schedule 3 Part 2. Minor Amenity Feature Validation Scheme from 1 September 2021, covering 21 amenity feature types designated before 1 September 2020, Schedule 3 Part 4. Signboard validation is in APP-155.
- Forms MW01 to MW12, and standards MW31 to MW33 and PR1 to PR6 (Appendix C).
- Minor deviations that do not change design assumptions, and that do not increase quantity, are shown on revised plans with the completion submission. Otherwise give a new notice at least 7 days ahead, or use MW11 or MW12 for additional items.
- Prescribed building works certification on Forms MW06-1, MW06-2, and MW06-3. Alter or strengthen under the simplified minor works path before certifying again.
- Cessation notices within 7 days.
- Radio base stations and antennas: minor works items in Appendix E, plus the OFTA declaration and guidance note.
- Enforcement on a validated amenity feature is deferred if it is certified safe and compliant, except Buildings Ordinance section 14(1) and Building (Administration) Regulation 25.

**What to submit**
- Class I and Class II commencement: the form, photos, coloured plans and details, a supervision plan for Class I, and other required documents.
- Completion certificates and, where used, revised plans, MW11 or MW12, and MW06-1, MW06-2, or MW06-3.
- A cessation notice within 7 days if the works stop.
- For radio base stations and antennas, the Appendix E items and the OFTA declaration.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not start works before acknowledgement, and do not assume a Buildings Department referral covers Lands Department, Planning Department, or other departments.
- Deed of mutual covenant and common-parts liabilities still apply.
- Area No. 3 works without MTRCL agreement carry Railways Ordinance and Cap. 276 risks.
- Wrong class, or the wrong prescribed building professional, and missing supervision-plan requirements under the Technical Memorandum and the Supervision Code, are not acceptable.
