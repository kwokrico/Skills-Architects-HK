# ADV-33 Essential Information in Plan Submissions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2024 | Buildings Department (first issue February 2016)

> Scope note: Checklists and sample drawings so that general building plans, structural plans, and drainage plans contain the information needed for processing. The checklists are not submitted to the Buildings Department, and they are not exhaustive. What the Department checks under the curtailed system is in the appendices to PNAP ADM-19.

## Regulatory Overview
Column I information is shown on the first general building plans for Stage I approval, column II on a later amendment before superstructure consent, and column III on a later amendment before an occupation permit or temporary occupation permit. An authorized person who defers column II or III information that is prescribed under regulation 8(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations includes, on the first plans, a statement that the deferred information will be approved before the relevant consent or occupation-permit application.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the first general building plans must carry (Appendix A1, Part C)
| Topic | Stage I content |
| --- | --- |
| Location | Whether the site is in a special control area, including a Scheduled Area, the Tung Chung Cable Car Route Protection Area, the Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance area, or a country park. Permitted use under the outline zoning plan, or compliance with a planning approval. Works in, over, under, or upon a street, with the extent and depth or height of the projection |
| Plans | Floor and roof plans, elevations with street information, sections with the site and street profile, a block plan, a key plan, and diagrams for plot ratio, site coverage, and open space. Foundations on these plans are indicative only. Gross-floor-area, site-coverage, usable-floor-area, usable-floor-space, and compartmentation diagrams use the colour coding in Table 1 of the Guideline for Using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plans Submission 2019, drawn over the corresponding floor plans |
| Site | Dimensions and area, including service lanes, streets, surrender, dedication, lease special areas, and non-building areas. For a Class B or C site, the percentage of the boundary abutting specified streets not less than 4.5 m wide, and the width of the narrowest part of those streets |
| Density | Accountable, disregarded, and exempted areas, areas under the 10% gross-floor-area cap, and areas subject to pre-requisites but not that cap. Overall plot ratio and site coverage, the mean street level of the lowest specified street, the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space, and the 15 m level for full site coverage on the elevation and section. A summary of gross-floor-area concessions. Details for PNAP APP-152. Car parking, including electric-vehicle charging locations and sizes, and any above-ground car park to be regarded as underground |
| Sanitary fitments | A schedule with usable-floor-area figures |
| Fire service access | Fireman’s lifts and firefighting and rescue stairways, with passage measurements at critical locations more than 44 m (open-plan direct line) and 59 m (actual passage). Distance from the ground-floor fire-service access point to the fireman’s lift or stairway where it is more than 17 m. An emergency vehicular access plan and the major-façade length |
| Means of escape | An occupant-capacity table for every room, compartment, and storey, including the ground floor, with usable floor area. A table of required and provided numbers and widths of exit doors and exit routes, and discharge-value calculations. Where staircases are less than 7 m apart, the 6 m separation at critical locations. The 48 m horizontal distance between staircases at a critical location for a situation over 47 m. Travel distance measured where it is more than 47 m horizontal. The 30° rule shown where two or more exit doors form an angle of less than 33°. For a place of public entertainment, at least one half of the perimeter of the building having those premises, with exit routes from each tier direct to two or more thoroughfares |
| Fire resisting construction | Compartment diagram and area and volume calculations. A table of fire resistance ratings. The 6 m separation for an unprotected external wall and opening of a required staircase and its protected lobby. Fixed-light percentage where it exceeds 24% under clause C9.7 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011. Smoke-outlet spacing where it exceeds 29 m, with the total area against the required area |
| Refuge | Number of storeys between refuge floors, the refuge roof, and the lowest ground storey, disregarding floors used solely for mechanical plant, and the net refuge area against the minimum |
| Light, air, and open space | Prescribed-window calculations are column II, for a critical case where the surplus window area is less than 10% of the required provision. Open-space area, disposition, diagram, and calculations are column I, under regulation 25 and the Second Schedule |
| Access for persons with a disability | Column I includes the access route to an accessible entrance, ramps and landings with handrails, manoeuvring space in corridors, lobbies, and paths including a dead end, and the lift. Many other facilities, including the accessible water-closet cubicle, are column II |

**SD takeaway:** The first general building plans carry the site class against streets of not less than 4.5 m, the plot ratio and site coverage, the exit widths, and the compartment diagram; deferral of prescribed information needs a statement on those plans.

### 2. Later general-building-plan information, and typical modifications (Appendix A1 columns II and III, and Appendix A2)
| When | Examples in the checklist |
| --- | --- |
| Before superstructure consent (column II) | Standard details such as gas flue, utility platform, balcony, air-conditioning platform, curtain wall, non-structural prefabricated external wall, projecting window, cladding, sunken slab, acoustic window, acoustic fin, vertical greening, protective barrier, top-hung window, accessible toilet, and tactile warning strips. For a sunken slab, only a typical section showing the minimum storey height under Building (Planning) Regulation 24 is required. Usable-floor-area diagrams for sanitary fitments on podium and non-typical floors, and for means of escape on those floors. Designed imposed loads on the floor plans. Prescribed-window area calculations for critical situations, that is a surplus window area of less than 10% |
| Before occupation permit (column III) | Locations of minor building works, including a fire damper in a ventilation system and frames for suspended air-conditioning or mechanical ventilation plant and large metal ducts. Signage for refuge floors. Usable-floor-area diagrams for typical tower floors |
| Appendix A2 | A checklist of typical modifications and exemptions, including projections over a street under section 31(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, exclusion of projections from plot ratio and site coverage under regulations 20 and 21, and the gross-floor-area exemptions the checklist ties to regulation 23(3)(a) and the related practice notes. Calculations for an area to be exempted are shown on the plans. Appendix A3 is the sample table of modifications granted or applied for, attached to Form BA16 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep curtain-wall, balcony, and cladding details, and the critical prescribed-window sums, for the amendment before superstructure consent, and say so on the first submission if they are deferred.

### 3. Structural and drainage checklists (paragraph 2 and Appendices B and C)
| Submission | Checklist and samples |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | Appendix B1. The synopsis states the foundation type, design codes and edition, material grades, geotechnical parameters, groundwater, and the load path into the founding stratum, plus a summary of effects on adjoining buildings, land, streets, utilities, slopes, and retaining walls. Extra sets when the works involve Scheduled Areas, a railway protection area, the Designated Area of Northshore Lantau, slopes or retaining structures or deep excavation or a disused tunnel, a culvert, nullah, or stream course (3 sets of plans), Chek Lap Kok Airport, a structure in, over, under, or upon a street, a highway structure, a seawall, reclamation or a pier, or a public drain or water main |
| Excavation and lateral support | Appendix B2 |
| Superstructure | Appendix B3 |
| Curtain wall | Appendix B4, with sample embed details in Appendix B12 |
| Glass balustrade | Appendix B5, with sample drawings in Appendix B8 |
| Metal cladding | Appendix B6, with sample drawings in Appendix B9 |
| Metal ceiling, grille, or louvre | Appendix B7, with sample drawings in Appendix B10 |
| Suspended duct and fan frames | Sample drawings in Appendix B11 for supporting frames of a suspended horizontal air duct, axial fan, cabinet fan, and air handling unit inside a building |
| Drainage | Appendix C1, typical modifications and exemptions in Appendix C2, and sample drawings in Appendix C3 |

**SD takeaway:** A foundation submission names the system, the code edition, and the effect on adjoining buildings and any stream or railway, and a site in a Scheduled Area or over a nullah needs the extra sets listed in Appendix B1.
