# APP-149 Authorized Signatory for a Prescribed Registered Contractor on Minor Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2010 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Who signs the minor-works forms, and how a temporary substitute is limited, for minor works commenced under the simplified requirements. It is not a design note. Appointment of the prescribed registered contractor is under section 28 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. Continuous supervision is under section 9AA(6) of the Buildings Ordinance, Part 7 of that regulation, the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans, and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision. A registered minor works contractor (individual) is outside the duty to appoint a separate authorized signatory.

## Regulatory Overview
Apart from an individual minor-works contractor, every prescribed registered contractor appoints at least one authorized signatory, and the Building Authority accepts only one for each minor-works submission, being the person who signs Form MW01 or MW03. If that person cannot act and no acceptable substitute is in place, the minor works cease.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. One signatory per submission (paragraphs 2–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Forms | The signatory of Forms MW01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 10, 11, and 12 is responsible for the contractor’s duties. The signatory of Form MW01 or 03 is that person |
| Name missing | The form is incomplete and unacceptable |
| Name differs from the supervision plan | The plan or the forms are rejected unless the change has been approved or notified |
| Only one signatory on the registration | A change needs an application to add the new signatory under PNRC 38 or 69, and approval |
| More than one | Notify on Appendix A to PNRC 72, countersigned by the authorized person if the works are Class I, not less than 7 days before the change |

**SD takeaway:** Name one authorized signatory on Form MW01 or MW03, and give 7 days’ notice before changing to another signatory of the same contractor.

### 2. Temporary absence (paragraphs 5–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Notice | Appendix B to PNRC 72, countersigned by the authorized person for Class I, at least 7 days before the absence. Sudden illness or accident: documentary proof within 3 days |
| Who | Another authorized signatory of the same contractor, currently accepted for the classes and types in the submission, or an authorized signatory of another contractor currently accepted for those classes and types. A partnership or corporation needs an authorisation from the other partners or the required quorum of directors |
| Period | Maximum 14 days, extendable only with proof such as illness, and 30 days in total |
| Beyond 30 days | Change to another signatory of the same company, or add a new one |
| What may be signed | Inspection records under an accepted supervision plan. Not supervision plans or specified forms |
| If the nominee is not accepted | The minor works may have to cease |

**SD takeaway:** A stand-in can supervise minor works for at most 14 days, and 30 days in total, and cannot sign the specified forms.
