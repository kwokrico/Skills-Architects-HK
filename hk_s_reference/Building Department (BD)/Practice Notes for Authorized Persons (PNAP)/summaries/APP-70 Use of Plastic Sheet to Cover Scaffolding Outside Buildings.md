# APP-70

**Official title:** Use of Plastic Sheet to Cover Scaffolding Outside Buildings

**In plain words:** Screens, fans and catch platforms must stop falling objects and dust, under the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations (Cap. 59) regulation 49(1), the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations regulation 3(2)(a), and the Summary Offences Ordinance (Cap. 228) section 4B(2). Covering a scaffold with plastic sheet does not change gross floor area or site coverage, and it is not a general-building-plan or thermal-transfer design item.

**Watch-outs**
- Wrapping the whole scaffold in PVC worries the Fire Services Department because fire can spread, and it worries the Labour Department because a poorly fixed bamboo scaffold can become unstable in strong wind. The sheet can also block natural ventilation and light to an occupied building under renovation.
- If plastic sheeting is used, it must be flame retardant, the scaffold must be reinforced for strong winds, and natural ventilation and lighting of occupied buildings should not be obstructed as far as practicable.
- Do not use a non-flame-retardant PVC wrap over the whole scaffold of an occupied building.
- Do not let the sheeting weaken the scaffold, and do not block lighting and ventilation of occupied units while screening renovation or demolition.
