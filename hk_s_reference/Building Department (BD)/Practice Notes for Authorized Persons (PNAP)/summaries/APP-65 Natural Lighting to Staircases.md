# APP-65

**Official title:** Natural Lighting to Staircases

**In plain words:** A staircase used in common by more than two tenants needs adequate natural light under Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) regulation 40, or a waiver based on permanent artificial light and emergency light, and this affects the core, light wells, lanes and scissor stairs. This note does not state a gross floor area bonus, and it does not change site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Use clear glass. The total glass area on each storey is at least one-tenth of the plan area inside the staircase enclosure.
- In a building of 12 storeys or fewer, the windows face an uncovered space that meets the “open air” definition in B(P)R regulation 2(1).
- In a building of more than 12 storeys, the windows face an uncovered and unenclosed space whose base is at least 2.3 m wide, and at least 4.5 m long measured at right angles to that width. The full width of an adjoining service lane may be included.
- Scissor stairs need even light. Acceptable examples: a window on the end wall at the landing, placed centrally, at least 1.3 m wide; or windows on the side wall, each at least 650 mm wide, at least two windows per storey, with the separating wall reduced so the back flight receives light.
- The Building Authority may waive natural light if permanent artificial lighting gives at least 30 lux at floor level, and automatic emergency backup meets the fire-service-installation code of practice with horizontal illuminance of at least 2 lux in the emergency mode.
- Management is normally accepted if a management company maintains the system after occupation, and the deed of mutual covenant requires regular maintenance.

**What to submit**
- Show B(P)R 40 natural lighting on the general building plans, or ask the Building Authority for a waiver and provide the artificial-light and emergency-light design plus evidence of management and the deed of mutual covenant.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not use opaque glass, or glass area below one-tenth of the stair enclosure.
- On a building over 12 storeys, do not place staircase windows onto an outside space smaller than 2.3 m by 4.5 m.
- Do not seek an artificial-light waiver without emergency backup, the 30 lux and 2 lux levels, or a deed of mutual covenant and management arrangement that can actually be enforced.
