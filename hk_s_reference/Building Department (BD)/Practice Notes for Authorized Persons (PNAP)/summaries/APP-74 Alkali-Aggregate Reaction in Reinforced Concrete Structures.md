# APP-74

**Official title:** Alkali-Aggregate Reaction in Reinforced Concrete Structures

**In plain words:** Alkali-aggregate reaction (AAR) cracks concrete when reactive aggregate, enough alkali and enough moisture are all present, and the recommended control is a limit on reactive alkali that should be stated on the plans if you adopt it. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- The reaction needs reactive minerals in the aggregate, sufficient alkali and sufficient moisture. The gel expands, the concrete cracks, reinforcement corrodes faster, and concrete strength falls.
- Control measures include limiting the alkali in the cement, using low-alkali cement, and replacing part of the cement with pulverised fuel ash.
- Recommended limit: reactive alkali, as equivalent sodium oxide (Na₂O), not more than 3.0 kg per cubic metre of concrete, calculated by the Appendix A method. The exception is where the authorized person or the registered structural engineer is of the opinion that the element will not be subject to moisture ingress throughout its design life.
- Equivalent Na₂O = A + B + C. A is the acid-soluble alkalis of the cement, admixtures and water. B is one-sixth of the total alkalis of the pulverised fuel ash. C is 0.76 times the chloride content of the aggregate.
- Test standards: BS 4550 Part 2:1970, excluding amendment AMD 7285; BS 1881 Part 124:1988; American Public Health Association, 17th edition, methods 3500-K and 3500-Na; BS 812 Part 4:1976, as specified.
- The supplier gives the authorized person or registered structural engineer Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) certificates not more than 6 months old, and the mix alkali calculation. New certificates and a recalculation are required every quarter.

**What to submit**
- If you adopt the clauses, state on the plans that reactive alkali is not more than 3.0 kg per cubic metre, calculated to Appendix A.
- The authorized person or registered structural engineer approves the contractor’s control measures, or requires the clause to be followed, except for elements that will stay dry for their design life.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not leave a member that will get wet without the alkali limit, or without pulverised fuel ash or low-alkali measures.
- Do not use the “not subject to moisture” opinion for an outdoor element, a retaining element, or a wet-process element without justification.
- Do not submit without HOKLAS certificates, and do not use alkali test data older than 6 months.
