# APP-69 Conservation of Historic Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision January 2016 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 175; first issue January 1995)

> Scope note: Adaptive re-use, alteration and addition, and preservation-cum-development of a declared or proposed monument under the Antiquities and Monuments Ordinance, Cap. 53, or a building graded or proposed to be graded by the Antiquities Advisory Board. Graded buildings have no statutory protection under Cap. 53, but plans are referred to the Commissioner for Heritage's Office and the Antiquities and Monuments Office. Alternative designs are in the Practice Guidebook for Adaptive Re-use of and Alteration and Addition Works to Heritage Buildings 2012. Modifications are under section 42.

## Regulatory Overview
Works in a declared monument or a proposed monument need a permit from the Antiquities Authority, the Secretary for Development, under section 6 of Cap. 53, and without it the plans may be disapproved under section 16(1)(d). Performance-based alternatives may be accepted where they maintain equivalent safety and health, including imposed loads determined under section 4.2 of the Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011 where strengthening would damage heritage value.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Existing fabric that may be retained with a modification (Appendix A, Table A)
| Item | Threshold the note will consider |
| --- | --- |
| Verandah or balcony over a street, section 31(1) | Show clear height, projection, and widths of pavement and carriageway. The retained area is included in gross floor area. A supporting letter from the Antiquities and Monuments Office is required |
| Existing protective barrier, Building (Construction) Regulation 8 | Height less than 1,100 mm but not less than 900 mm, gaps only marginally wider than 100 mm, or a solid curb less than 150 mm, and the premises are not used primarily by children or the elderly. Compensatory measures for verandahs and balconies follow sections 5.30(d) and 5.30(e) of the Practice Guidebook, and for staircases section 5.30(f), plus a management plan and an owner's undertaking |
| Window sill or bottom of opening, regulation 3A | Existing opening not less than 900 mm above the floor, with compensatory measures under section 5.30(e) of the Practice Guidebook |
| Floors below the minimum imposed load, regulation 17(1) | Show fixtures that set the load, evaluate the greatest load likely from the intended use, assess the existing structure, and where occupant numbers are controlled submit a management plan. Actual counting is recognised under Note 3 of Table B1 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 |
| Barriers below the horizontal imposed load, regulation 17(3) | A performance-based category from Table 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulation may be used if management shows that congregation is not expected |
| Natural lighting and ventilation | Follow PNAP APP-130 |
| No emergency vehicular access, or a deficient one, regulation 41D | Details to PNAP APP-136, a fire safety assessment to the 2011 fire safety code, and enhanced fire safety measures |
| No gutters or rainwater pipes, sanitary regulation 39 | Rainwater from the existing roof discharges to a drainage system within the site, hardship is shown, and no nuisance is created |

**SD takeaway:** Keep an existing barrier or window opening only if it is still at least 900 mm high, the use is not primarily for children or the elderly, and a management plan plus an Antiquities and Monuments Office letter supports the modification.

### 2. Stairs, timber, and lifts (Appendix A, Table B)
| Item | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Existing required staircase of timber, or a timber roof | Compensatory measures under sections 5.20 and 5.22 of the Practice Guidebook, with a management plan |
| Existing required staircase narrower than 1,050 mm | Width not less than 860 mm, and if more than one exit staircase is required at least one complies with the 2011 fire safety code. Compensatory measures under section 5.13(b), with occupant control in the management plan |
| Winders, or a flight of more than 16 and not more than 18 risers | Compensatory measures under sections 5.14(b) and 5.15 |
| Required staircase continuing directly into a basement | Only where the premises are not used primarily by children or the elderly, with measures under section 5.17(b) |
| Lift pit, machine room, well, or car off the 2011 lift code | A letter from a registered lift contractor or registered lift engineer that the installation fits, can be maintained and tested safely, and complies with the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department code |
| Early advice | Contact the Antiquities and Monuments Office to identify character-defining elements, and use the pre-submission enquiry in PNAP ADM-19. Heritage Units in the New Buildings Divisions process these proposals |

**SD takeaway:** An existing required stair may stay at 860 mm wide only if another required stair, where more than one is needed, fully meets the fire code, and a management plan caps the number of occupants.
