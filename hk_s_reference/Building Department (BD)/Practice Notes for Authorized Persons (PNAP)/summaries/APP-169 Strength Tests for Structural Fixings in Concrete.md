# APP-169

**Official title:** Strength Tests for Structural Fixings in Concrete

**In plain words:** Proof-load tests check the manufacturer’s recommended tensile load, shear load, or both, for drilled-in anchors and for cementitious or polymer grouted bolts, dowels, reinforcing bars, and steel T-bolts with cast-in channels, and the Building Authority may impose the tests under Buildings Ordinance section 17(1) item 6. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue October 2023.
- The test load must be at least 1.5 times the manufacturer’s recommended load.
- Appendix A, drilled-in anchors for a cantilevered structure, a hanger, or curtain-wall remedial works:
  - Sample at least 5%, or 5 numbers, whichever is more, of each type and size.
  - Apparatus: frame supports at least 8A; displacement gauge at least 12A for tension, accuracy 0.02 mm; shear steel block of 5d diameter; PTFE (polytetrafluoroethylene) sheet not thicker than 2 mm; load accuracy 2%.
  - Procedure: visual check; preload not more than 1%; load continuously at 9 to 11 N/(mm²·s), or in at least 10 equal increments; hold at least 1 hour at the maximum load; unload in at least 5 decrements; record the recovery.
  - Acceptance: no separation, plastic deformation, or harmful effect. Recovery of deformation must be at least 80% of the total deformation at the maximum load. Plot force against displacement. Record the failure modes.
- Appendix B, drilled-in anchors for other works:
  - Sample at least 1% or 5 numbers.
  - Load at a constant rate, hold at least 2 minutes, then unload.
  - Acceptance is no adverse sign. There is no 80% recovery criterion.
  - The apparatus is similar, but an independent tension displacement gauge is not required in the way Appendix A requires it.
- Appendix C, cementitious or polymer grouted bolts, dowels, or reinforcing bars, or steel T-bolts with cast-in channels, for curtain wall or cladding:
  - Sample at least 1%, or 5, of each type, size, and embedment, or of the assemblies.
  - Supports at least 8A, or 2hef for cast-in channels (hef is the effective embedment depth).
  - Hold the load for at least 2 minutes.
  - Acceptance is no adverse sign. Record the extended failure modes, including failure of a cast-in channel leg.

**What to submit**
- If the tests follow Appendix A, B, or C, the registered structural engineer (RSE) may quote that appendix on the submitted plans. A separate method statement is then not required.
- Any other method statement must be submitted to the Buildings Department for agreement.

**Watch-outs**
- Cantilever, hanger, and curtain-wall remedial anchors must use Appendix A: at least 5% or 5 numbers, a hold of at least 1 hour, and at least 80% recovery. Appendix B sampling and the 2-minute hold do not apply to those anchors.
- Reaction distances or measurement accuracy that fall short of the appendix.
- Failing to report the load and the mode when a fixing fails before the proof load.
