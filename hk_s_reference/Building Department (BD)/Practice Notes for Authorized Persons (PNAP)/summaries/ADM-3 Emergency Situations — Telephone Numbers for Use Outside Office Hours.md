# ADM-3

**Official title:** Emergency Situations — Telephone Numbers for Use Outside Office Hours

**In plain words:** This note asks the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) to give the Buildings Department a telephone number that works outside office hours. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Department staff are on call 24 hours for emergencies, including sites where work is in progress.
- The AP, RSE, and RGE must make sure the works they are responsible for do not put the public in danger. A quick call can avoid a long closure of a main road, for example after scaffolding or a hoarding collapses, and can avoid the Government Contractor doing the clearance at the owner’s cost.
- Give the Department your telephone, fax, and mobile numbers for emergencies. If a number changes, send the Appendix A letter to the Registration Unit (fax 2625 0437).
- Staff use those numbers only for emergencies outside office hours, and only for sites where you are the AP, RSE, or RGE.

**What to submit**
- The Appendix A form to the Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon.

**Watch-outs**
- If the Department cannot reach the AP, RSE, or RGE, the public is at risk, the site takes longer to clear, and the owner may have to pay for the Government Contractor.
- A changed number that was never updated means staff cannot call the right person out of hours.
