# APP-165

**Official title:** Product Certification System

**In plain words:** A valid certificate of conformity (CoC) from an accredited certification body can replace laboratory test or assessment reports for fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue September 2021.
- A CoC is typically valid for 3 years. It can be suspended or withdrawn if the product does not comply.
- The scheme now accepted under the Buildings Ordinance, as an alternative to test or assessment reports, is the Product Conformity Certification Scheme (PCCS) for Passive Fire Protection Products (Fire Door and Non-loadbearing Fire Partition), Issue 3. The scheme owner is the Hong Kong Institute of Steel Construction.
- The certification body must be accredited by the Hong Kong Certification Body Accreditation Scheme (HKCAS), or under a mutual recognition arrangement (MRA). Factory audit is combined with sample testing by an independent laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) or an MRA, plus periodic surveillance.
- Lists of schemes are on the Hong Kong Council for Testing and Certification website. Accredited bodies are in the Hong Kong Accreditation Service HKCAS directory.

**What to submit**
- On completion, the authorized person (AP) or registered contractor (RC) may submit the CoCs with the Certificate and Schedule of Building Materials and Products under PNAP APP-13. Test or assessment reports for those products are then not required.
- The Buildings Department audits CoCs from the Schedule at random. The data go to the Central Data Bank under PNAP ADM-20.
- The AP should require the supplier to provide the test reports used in the paragraph 2 evaluation, for the record and for submission if the Buildings Department asks.
- The Buildings Department is prepared to advise other PCCS scheme owners on Buildings Ordinance compliance.

**Watch-outs**
- An expired, suspended, or wrong-scheme CoC.
- Using the CoC route for a product outside the Issue 3 fire-door and non-loadbearing partition acceptance, without Buildings Department confirmation.
- Failing to keep the supplier’s test reports for audit.
