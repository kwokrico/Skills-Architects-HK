# APP-9

**Official title:** Country Parks Ordinance, Cap 208 — Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(d)

**In plain words:** After a notice under section 9(1) of the Country Parks Ordinance, new development in a proposed country park shown on a draft map needs prior approval from the Country Parks Authority under section 10(1), and the Building Authority may refuse the plans under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(d) until that approval, and its conditions, are in place. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**What to submit**
- Consult the Country Parks Authority as early as possible on an affected site. Buy the boundary plans from the Agriculture and Fisheries Department (the department named in the note).

**Watch-outs**
- General building plans for a site in a designated or proposed country park, or in a special area, can be refused under section 16(1)(d) if Authority approval is missing.
- Failure to meet conditions attached to that approval can also lead to rejection.
