# ADV-37

**Official title:** Conduct of Registered Building Professionals under the Buildings Ordinance

**In plain words:** A registered building professional supervises the works, tells the Buildings Department about contraventions, and complies with the Buildings Ordinance. The Department’s curtailed check and its audits do not take that duty away. This note gives examples of conduct that can go to a disciplinary board. It does not change a floor-area figure. False floor-area certificates and unauthorised works are common ways people get into trouble under it.

**Numbers that matter**
- Registered building professionals here means authorized persons, registered structural engineers, registered geotechnical engineers, registered inspectors, and qualified persons.
- The Building Authority may refer negligence or professional misconduct to a disciplinary board under Buildings Ordinance section 7(1A)(b), after considering whether the matter is serious enough for censure under section 7(1).
- Appendix A examples:
  - Failing to give material supervision, or a material deviation from the approved plans or the minor-works plans; knowingly misrepresenting facts to the Building Authority; undertaking work you are not qualified to do.
  - Abetting unauthorised building works, or disregarding the minor-works control system; falsifying documents; advertising skill in unauthorised building works; an undeclared financial or personal interest.
  - Repeatedly withholding information or reports that are required; obstructing a Department investigation; ignoring a written warning.
  - Repeatedly failing to remind the contractor of site-safety duties under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59); misusing the Buildings Department logo.
- Report an irregularity to the Chief Officer/Technical Services. Reports are treated in the strictest confidence.

**What to submit**
- No special form. Exercise due diligence on every submission and certificate. You may report an irregularity, in confidence, to Technical Services.

**Watch-outs**
- Knowing misrepresentation, helping unauthorised building works, or chronically ignoring the Department, is disciplinary exposure.
- Repeatedly omitting the Cap. 59 reminder to the contractor.
- Putting the Buildings Department logo on stationery.
