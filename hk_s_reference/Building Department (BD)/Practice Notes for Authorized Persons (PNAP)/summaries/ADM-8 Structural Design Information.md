# ADM-8

**Official title:** Structural Design Information

**In plain words:** This note is the presentation standard for structural plans and calculations. Part I is the synopsis the Buildings Department keeps. Part II is the detailed printout, which is returned after processing. The note does not measure gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). The design imposed loads on the structural plans must match the loads shown on the general building plans.

**Numbers that matter**
- Index the structural plans properly. Title and number every drawing.
- The first drawing carries the general notes and the material specification: design codes and standards; concrete materials (cement, aggregates, pulverised fuel ash, ground granulated blast-furnace slag); reinforcement and other structural materials; grade and characteristic strength; cover, corrosion protection, and fire resistance; and construction method, workmanship, and testing.
- Drawings include key plans, floor and layout plans, sections, and elevations, with layout, dimensions, levels, and an identity for every frame and member.
- Notes of design imposed loads, including partitions, screeds, and dynamic effects, go on the matching floor or layout plans. Where the load changes across the floor, show it on a diagram. Show the same design imposed loads on the general building plans.
- For alteration and addition, show the reinforcement or steelwork of the affected existing members, taken from the approved plans of those members.
- Foundation proposals, and proposals with geotechnical content (retaining walls, excavation and lateral support, site formation), show the site and its surroundings: ground-investigation boreholes, adjacent buildings, structures and their foundations, lands, streets, utilities, slopes, nullahs, retaining walls, and the precautionary and protective measures, monitoring, and contingency plans. See the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017, and APP-18, APP-54, APP-57, and ADM-15.
- Calculations are in two parts.
  - Part I, which the Department keeps, is a synopsis. It covers how the foundation or structural system is stable; design methods, assumptions, codes, and stress and deformation limits; and the computer design record (program name, version, developer, Buildings Department reference, and validity; the accepted scope and limits; model assumptions, parameters, and boundary conditions; input framing graphics with nodes, elements, and fixity; a summary of the critical moment, shear, and displacement envelopes plus a force-compatibility check; and how to read the output). It also covers lateral-load design (the system, the model, member locations, section properties, a load summary and how load is shared by floor, the principal reactions, and an equilibrium check at foundation level and at levels where the configuration changes a lot); dead and imposed principal reactions at the foundation and at every floor; major transfer members; cantilevered canopies, balconies, corbels, and major appendages. For works with geotechnical content, it covers ground conditions from the site or ground investigation, parameters, founding stratum, allowable bearing pressure, and a summary of effects on nearby buildings, structures, slopes, retaining walls, land, streets, and services.
  - Part II, which is returned after processing, is the detailed element analysis and design, usually from a program the Department has already accepted.
- A novel or sophisticated design should go through a pre-submission enquiry or conference under ADM-19. If you did not enquire first, the proposal is examined in parallel, and you submit one extra set of plans and supporting documents for the Department’s Technical Services Unit.
- Appendix A paper quantities: 2 signed sets of plans for approval; Part I volumes as you declare; Part II volumes and/or a CD or DVD; the ground investigation report as 2 sets or on CD or DVD; the adjacent or existing building appraisal as 1 set or on CD or DVD; Forms BA4, BA5, BA6, and BA16 as they apply.
- Extra referral sets depend on the trigger. Examples: Scheduled Areas 1, 2, and 4, one plan set and one set of documents to the Geotechnical Engineering Office; Area 3 or railway protection, 2 plan sets to the MTR Corporation and the Highways Department Railway Division; Area 5, 2 plan sets and 1 set of documents to the Geotechnical Engineering Office and the Drainage Services Department; the Northshore Lantau Designated Area, 1 plan set and 1 document set to the Geotechnical Engineering Office; slopes, retaining walls, deep excavation, or a disused tunnel, the same 1 and 1 to that Office; a culvert, nullah, or stream, 3 plan sets to the Geotechnical Engineering Office, Drainage Services Department, and Highways Department; a proposed railway, 1 set to the Railway Development Office of Highways Department; Chek Lap Kok Airport, 1 set to the Civil Aviation Department; a structure in, over, under, or upon a street, 2 sets to the Lands Department and Highways Department; a highway structure, 1 plan set and 1 document set to Highways Department; a seawall, 1 and 1 to Port Works of the Civil Engineering and Development Department; reclamation or a pier, 2 and 2 to the Marine Department and Port Works; public drainage, 1 set to Drainage Services Department; water mains, 1 set to the Water Supplies Department; a monument, graded building, or archaeological site, 1 set to the Antiquities and Monuments Office; a novel structure, 1 plan set and 1 document set to the Technical Services Unit. The checklist is not exhaustive.
- Until full electronic submission, Part II and Appendix B documents on disc use a non-rewriteable CD-ROM (ISO 9660) or DVD-ROM (ISO/IEC 13346:1995). Print the cover with the Department reference, the submission title, the RSE or RGE name, and the disc number. The RSE or RGE signs each cover. Files are PDF. Each page is no larger than A3. The disc is indexed. Text is searchable. Images are at least 300 dpi. Once submitted, the disc is not replaced and not returned. Additions go on a supplementary disc.
- Appendix B’s list of documents that may go on disc does not include the hard-copy statements the RSE or RGE must sign: materials and testing; quality-assurance documents; quality-assurance supervision and audit reports; RGE technically competent person grade T5 or detailed site supervision reports; ground investigation and laboratory reports; method statements; pre-construction surveys; adjacent-building appraisals; monitoring readings; and site and works management documents.
- The Electronic Submission Hub is preferred for plans, forms, and documents. See ADM-17.

**What to submit**
- Plans and calculations split into Part I and Part II, with a completed and signed Appendix A checklist.
- Imposed loads on both the structural plans and the general building plans.
- An ADM-6 accepted program, with the Part I computer record and ADM-6 Appendices B and C.
- Prefer the Hub. Otherwise follow the disc rules for Part II and the Appendix B document types, and keep signed compliance statements on paper.
- For a novel method: an ADM-19 pre-submission enquiry, or the extra Technical Services Unit set.

**Watch-outs**
- A missing first-drawing specification, missing load diagrams, or alteration-and-addition work that does not show the existing reinforcement from the approved plans, delays processing or leads to disapproval.
- A thin Part I, especially without lateral equilibrium checks, transfer members, cantilever appendages, or the geotechnical assessment, is not saved by a thick Part II printout.
- A novel structure with no ADM-19 enquiry and no extra Technical Services Unit set is incomplete.
- Too few referral sets for a scheduled area, a railway, the waterfront, or a monument holds the referral up.
- An unsigned disc, the wrong disc format, a page larger than A3, an image below 300 dpi, or an attempt to swap the disc after submission, is not accepted.
- Imposed loads on the structural plans that do not match the general building plans are a processing problem.
