# ADV-12

**Official title:** Display of Site Information

**In plain words:** Put basic contact details on the site so the public can reach the people responsible. Building (Planning) Regulation 66(4) forbids advertising on a hoarding or covered walkway, but it does not forbid a short description of the development. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Display, in a prominent position: the address and/or lot number; the names of the authorized person (AP), the registered structural engineer (RSE), and the registered contractor; and the contact telephone numbers.
- An advertisement is not permitted on any hoarding or covered walkway. There is no objection to basic development information, such as the proposed works, the use, the height, the number of blocks, and the anticipated completion date.
- Information displayed somewhere other than the hoarding or covered walkway must stay inside the lot boundaries and must not interfere with site activities.
- A similar note goes to registered contractors.

**What to submit**
- No separate submission. Keep the display consistent with the approved hoarding or covered walkway.

**Watch-outs**
- Commercial advertising on a hoarding or covered walkway is contrary to Building (Planning) Regulation 66(4).
- Missing names and telephone numbers means the public cannot report an unsafe condition to a named professional.
- A sign outside the lot, or a sign that obstructs site operations, is not acceptable.
