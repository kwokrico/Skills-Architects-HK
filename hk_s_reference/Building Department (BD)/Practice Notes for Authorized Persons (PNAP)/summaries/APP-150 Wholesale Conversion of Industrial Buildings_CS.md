# APP-150 Wholesale Conversion of Industrial Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2013 | Buildings Department (first issue September 2010)

> Scope note: How the Building Authority will treat modification and exemption applications for wholesale conversion of industrial buildings where green or energy-efficient features are provided, and one route for redundant car parks in a conversion that needs extra plant. Lands Department practice notes set the land policy. It is not itself a green-building code.

## Regulatory Overview
Wholesale conversion is treated as a sustainable alternative to redevelopment, and green or energy-efficient features are a relevant factor in granting modification or exemption from specific regulations, given the constraints of the original industrial building. Partial as well as wholesale conversion is addressed for places of public entertainment and for essential plant rooms.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Modifications supported by green features (paragraph 3)
| Case | Favourable consideration |
| --- | --- |
| Refuge floor required by the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011, but the technical requirements are difficult because of the site | A refuge floor with greenery and enhanced fire service installations, subject to no adverse comments from the Director of Fire Services. PNAP APP-122 is relevant |
| Office conversion that cannot provide the natural lighting and ventilation required by regulations 30 and 31 of the Building (Planning) Regulations | Adequate artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation, and an energy-efficient design that could achieve 40% in Energy Use and Indoor Environmental Quality under BEAM Plus Certification conferred by the Hong Kong Green Building Council. PNAP APP-130 is relevant |
| Individual air-conditioning boxes or platforms on external walls, larger than the usually accepted size and/or projecting over a street | Incorporated use of energy-efficient or environmentally friendly air-conditioning units. PNAP APP-19 is relevant |
| Curtain wall on an existing facade, exemption from section 31(1) of the Buildings Ordinance to project over a street | Low-energy absorbent glazing or energy-efficient materials, with an energy-efficient curtain-wall design that could achieve 40% in Energy Use and Indoor Environmental Quality under the same BEAM Plus Certification. PNAP APP-2 is relevant |

**SD takeaway:** An office conversion that cannot meet natural lighting and ventilation should target 40% in Energy Use and Indoor Environmental Quality under BEAM Plus before seeking a modification of regulations 30 and 31.

### 2. Places of public entertainment and redundant car parks (paragraphs 4–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Places of public entertainment in non-domestic buildings | Revised requirements are in Clauses B19.2 and B20.9 of the fire safety code |
| Redundant covered car parks | If conversion to facilities needing special electrical and mechanical installations, such as a data centre, makes covered car-parking spaces shown on the approved plans redundant, and those spaces were disregarded from gross floor area under regulation 23(3)(b), an application to modify regulation 23(3)(a) so they become additional transformer rooms or other mandatory features or essential plant rooms will be favourably considered on the special circumstances and subject to favourable comments from relevant departments |
| Plant types | Appendix A to PNAP APP-151 for mandatory features and essential plant rooms |
| Undertaking | At building-plan stage, a letter by the registered owner and the operator that, when the facilities wholly or partly cease and the premises revert to industrial use, the additional plant rooms will be demolished and the floor space reinstated as car-parking spaces in accordance with the approved plans of the original industrial building |

**SD takeaway:** Redundant industrial car parks can be tested as plant rooms only with an undertaking to reinstate them if the special use ends.
