# APP-156 Design and Construction Requirements for Energy Efficiency of Residential Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Residential thermal transfer value, overall thermal transfer value of residents’ recreational facilities, glass transmittance and reflectance, and the natural-ventilation submission, as a prerequisite for gross floor area concessions under PNAP APP-151. Calculation methods are in the Guidelines on Design and Construction Requirements for Energy Efficiency of Residential Buildings and the Code of Practice for Overall Thermal Transfer Value in Buildings 1995. A residential building here is a domestic building under section 2(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, excluding a hotel, guesthouse, or residential care home whose air-conditioning profile is not that of a normal household. The note does not apply to alteration and addition or a change in use that does not result in a new residential building.

## Regulatory Overview
Gross floor area concessions for green and amenity features and for non-mandatory or non-essential plant rooms in a residential building depend on meeting the envelope limits in paragraph 6. The first building-plan submission may carry a compliance statement instead of the calculations; the summary sheets come before consent, and the energy efficiency report comes with the occupation permit.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Envelope limits (paragraphs 4–6)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Wall residential thermal transfer value | Not exceeding 12.5 W/m2 (previously 14 W/m2) |
| Roof residential thermal transfer value | Not exceeding 3.5 W/m2 (previously 4 W/m2) |
| Overall thermal transfer value of residents’ recreational facilities in a tower | Not exceeding 20 W/m2 (previously 21 W/m2), calculated to the 1995 code |
| Same, in a podium | Not exceeding 40 W/m2 (previously 50 W/m2) |
| Visible light transmittance of envelope glass | Not less than 50%. This applies only to glass in the prescribed windows under regulations 30 and 31 of the Building (Planning) Regulations |
| External reflectance of that glass | Not more than 20% |
| Natural ventilation for thermal comfort | The percentage of habitable-space area complying with the Guidelines is submitted. It is collected for research and is not put on the Buildings Department website |
| Podium and tower dates | The revised overall-thermal-transfer limits in paragraph 6(b) apply to new building plans or a major revision submitted on or after 31 December 2025 |

**SD takeaway:** Hold the residential wall at or below 12.5 W/m2 and the roof at or below 3.5 W/m2, with glass transmittance at least 50% and external reflectance at most 20%.

### 2. When the numbers are submitted (paragraphs 7–10 and 15)
| Stage | Submission |
| --- | --- |
| First building plans | A statement that the development will comply. Calculations need not accompany the first submission |
| After general building plan approval, before consent | Summary sheets in Appendices A and B for paragraphs 6(a), 6(b), and 6(d) |
| Before occupation permit | Final values and glass shading coefficients on the general building plans for record |
| With the occupation permit | An energy efficiency report: residential-thermal-transfer calculations on Appendix III of the Guidelines; overall-thermal-transfer forms OTTV 1 to 4 if applicable; record plans; test certificates or published specifications; final summary sheets; and the natural-ventilation extent in Appendix VI of the Guidelines |
| After the occupation permit | Wall and roof residential thermal transfer values, and the recreational-facility overall thermal transfer value, are uploaded to the Buildings Department website |

**SD takeaway:** Put the compliance statement on the first general building plans, the summary sheets before consent, and the energy report with the occupation permit.

### 3. Sunshades (paragraphs 12–14)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable area | Genuine sunshades that reduce the thermal transfer values are not accountable for gross floor area and are not included in site coverage |
| Over 750 mm from the external wall | Quantitative assessment for the Building Authority. Projection over 1.5 m from the external walls is not envisaged |
| Prescribed windows | A sunshade projecting not more than 750 mm is regarded as not obstructing a prescribed window |
| Over a street | Not allowed under section 31(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, unless an exemption is justified |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a sunshade within 750 mm of the wall if it is not to be assessed, and do not project it over a street without an exemption.
