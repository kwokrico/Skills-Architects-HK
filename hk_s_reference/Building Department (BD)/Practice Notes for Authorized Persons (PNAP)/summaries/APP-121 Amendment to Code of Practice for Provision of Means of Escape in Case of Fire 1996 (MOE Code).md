# APP-121

**Official title:** Amendment to Code of Practice for Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996 (MOE Code)

**In plain words:** Paragraph 14.5(e) of the Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996 normally requires a vision panel in a room, other than a toilet, whose only exit is through another room, and this note allows another way of warning the people inside, such as a fire alarm that can be heard in the inner room. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), and it does not apply to a habitable room in a domestic flat that escapes through the living room, dining room, or kitchen.

**Numbers that matter**
- The rule being softened is paragraph 14.5(e) of the 1996 means-of-escape code.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued June 2001.
- File reference: BD GP/LEG/6/A (VII).

**What to submit**
- If the vision panel is omitted, document the alternative warning that alerts occupants to fire or smoke.

**Watch-outs**
- Omitting both the vision panel and an equivalent warning, for an inner room that is not covered by the domestic-flat exception, does not comply.
- Do not apply paragraph 14.5(e) to an ordinary domestic flat where a bedroom escapes through the living room.
