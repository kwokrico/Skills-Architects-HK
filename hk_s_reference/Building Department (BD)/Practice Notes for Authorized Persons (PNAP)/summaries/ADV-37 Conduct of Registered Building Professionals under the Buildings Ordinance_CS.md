# ADV-37 Conduct of Registered Building Professionals under the Buildings Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2018 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Examples of negligence and misconduct that the Building Authority may refer to a disciplinary board. In this note, registered building professionals are authorized persons, registered structural engineers, registered geotechnical engineers, registered inspectors, and qualified persons. It does not set a design standard.

## Regulatory Overview
Building owners who wish to carry out building works or street works appoint a registered building professional, who has statutory duties to supervise the works, to notify the Buildings Department of any contravention of the regulations, and to comply generally with the Buildings Ordinance. Curtailed checks on submissions and audit checks on completed works do not shift that duty: the professional ensures full compliance with the Ordinance, its subsidiary legislation, and the relevant laws, codes of practice, and practice notes.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When conduct is referred (paragraph 3 and Appendix A)
| Ground | Examples in Appendix A |
| --- | --- |
| Section 7(1A)(b) | If a registered building professional has been negligent or has misconducted himself in a professional way, the Building Authority may bring the matter to a disciplinary board appointed under section 5. Before referral, the Authority considers the seriousness of the case, including whether the person deserves censure under section 7(1) |
| Compliance | Fails to give the required supervision and the work diverges or deviates in any material way from the plan approved by the Building Authority, or from a plan relating to minor works. Knowingly misrepresents a material fact in a document given to the Authority. Undertakes professional work he or she is not qualified to discharge |
| Integrity | Abets an owner or another person in unauthorised building works, or in disregarding the Minor Works Control System. Falsifies information, documents, or records. Advertises skill in carrying out unauthorised building works. Has a financial or personal interest in a business related to the works and does not declare it to the client |
| Co-operation | Repeatedly fails, without reasonable explanation, to provide factual information, inspection reports, or site records that the Department requires. Obstructs an investigation with a malicious intent to cover up. Repeatedly fails, without reasonable explanation, to respond to a written warning about deficiencies or non-compliance |
| Other laws | Repeatedly fails to remind the appointed contractor to observe site safety under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Chapter 59). Uses the Buildings Department logo on a name card, letterhead, or publicity material |

**SD takeaway:** Supervise so that the built work does not deviate in any material way from the approved plan, and declare any financial interest in the works to the client.

### 2. Reporting (paragraph 5)
Registered building professionals are encouraged to report irregularities observed in the course of their work to the Chief Officer/Technical Services of the Buildings Department. Reports are treated in the strictest confidence.

**SD takeaway:** Report an irregularity found during the appointment to the Chief Officer/Technical Services, and treat that report as confidential.
