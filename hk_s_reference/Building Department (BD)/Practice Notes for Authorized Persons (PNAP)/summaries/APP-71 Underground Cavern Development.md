# APP-71

**Official title:** Underground Cavern Development

**In plain words:** A rock cavern can be a real alternative for some uses, but it must be planned and designed to the underground guides named here, not as an ordinary building. This note does not say how cavern floor space counts in gross floor area or site coverage; check Chapter 11 of the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines, and the relevant Buildings Ordinance practice, separately.

**Numbers that matter**
- Uses studied as viable include a container freight station (CFS) / trailer park, oil or gas storage, sewage treatment, a refuse transfer station (RTS), warehousing, and commercial or government, institution and community (GIC) space. An environmental benefit is possible.
- Geoguide 4 (March 1992): civil engineering of rock caverns.
- Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines, Chapter 11: planning standards and guidelines for underground rock cavern development.
- Guide to Fire Safety Design for Caverns (1994): fire-safety design guidelines.
- The background study is the 1988 study on potential uses of underground space (SPUN).

**What to submit**
- Design submissions for cavern works should follow Geoguide 4 and the 1994 fire-safety guide. Planning compliance is through Chapter 11 of the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not propose a cavern without fire-safety design to the 1994 guide, or without geotechnical design to Geoguide 4.
- Do not treat a cavern as a conventional above-ground building and skip the underground precautions.
