# APP-125

**Official title:** External Area and Floor Adjoining External Ground or Roof

**In plain words:** This note gives deemed-to-comply ways to stop water entering from external ground or a roof, under Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) sections 33(2), 34(2), and 33(3). It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), though steps and kerbs still need to work with barrier-free access (BFA).

**Numbers that matter**
- Either the internal floor is at least 150 mm above the adjoining external ground or roof, or extra drainage channels are provided, each with at least 2 outlets, and the external surface falls at least 1:80 away from the internal floor.
- If neither is practicable, a waterproof kerb with a total height of 150 mm above the external level may be considered case by case.
- External paving, under section 33(3), other than a landscaped area, must fall at least 1:80 to a gully trap or to drainage channels that lead to a surface-water drain.
- Previously PNAP 266. First issued December 2001. Last revision January 2005. This revision February 2021.
- File reference: BD GP/BREG/C/23 (VIII).

**What to submit**
- Show levels, falls, channels and outlets, or the kerb details, on the general building plans (GBP) and the drainage plans as appropriate.

**Watch-outs**
- A flush threshold, without drainage that performs as well as the deemed-to-comply options, does not keep water out.
- External paving without a fall of at least 1:80 to surface-water drainage does not comply.
