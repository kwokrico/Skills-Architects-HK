# APP-108

**Official title:** Dedication of Land/Area for Use as Public Passage

**In plain words:** This note sets when land or floor given to the public as a passage can be left out of gross floor area (GFA) and can earn extra plot ratio (PR) and site coverage (SC). Leaving the dedicated area out of GFA is the more readily available concession once the dedication is accepted as desirable, while bonus PR and SC are available only if the Government considers the dedication essential, including whether another passage nearby already exists.

**Numbers that matter**
- Category (i) is a street-level setback for public passage. Permitted SC and PR may be exceeded under the Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R). The maximum extra PR is 5 times the dedicated area, or 20% of the permissible PR, whichever is less. The Building Authority (BA) may also exempt the dedicated area from GFA.
- Category (ii) is a public passage within or through the building at ground floor or at another floor. The concession is by modification, on the merits, and can range from exempting the dedicated area from GFA up to bonus PR and SC. Bonus SC is normally under B(P)R 22(1). The maximum bonus PR, as additional GFA, is generally 5 times the dedicated area at ground level and 2 times at other floors. Total bonus must not exceed 20% of the permissible PR, and the dedicated area is exempt from accountable GFA.
- B(P)R 22(2) applies where land is surrendered for street widening shown on a town plan or a street improvement plan.
- The resulting intensity must not exceed outline zoning plan (OZP) limits unless the plan says otherwise.
- Where the public and building users share the space, the exempted or bonus GFA is assessed in proportion to the traffic each generates.
- First issued November 1999. Revised December 2002. This revision June 2003.
- File reference: BD GP/BREG/P/3(III).

**What to submit**
- Apply with a proper case that there is a real public need and that the dedicated area is reasonable.
- A dedication may be accepted if it eases vehicle or pedestrian congestion, links to a public footbridge or subway and improves safety or convenience, improves road safety or public convenience, or provides suitable greenery that fits the passage.
- The temporary occupation permit (TOP) or occupation permit (OP) will not be issued until the Deed of Dedication is executed and all of its terms are met. Tell the client early.
- Display, in a conspicuous place, notices of the dedication, the opening hours, and who is responsible. The owner provides, maintains, and repairs the dedicated area.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not claim bonus PR or SC unless the dedication is accepted as essential.
- The bonus formula does not override an OZP intensity limit.
- A late Deed of Dedication delays the OP.
- Failing to display the notices, or failing to maintain the dedicated area, breaches the dedication conditions.
