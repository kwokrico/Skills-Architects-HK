# APP-7 Application for Registration as Authorized Persons, Registered Structural Engineers, Registered Geotechnical Engineers and Registered Inspectors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2011 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 43; first issue August 1977)

> Scope note: Who may be included in the four registers under section 3 of the Buildings Ordinance, and how long a registration lasts. It does not set building design standards. The scope of the professional interview is in Appendix A.

## Regulatory Overview
A person is not included in a register unless he has the qualifications prescribed in Building (Administration) Regulation 3 and is recommended by the relevant Registration Committee, except that an authorized person or registered structural engineer with the experience in paragraph 4(b) may be included in the inspectors' register without an Inspectors Registration Committee recommendation. Registration is valid for 5 years.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Qualifications and experience (paragraphs 3–4)
| Register | Qualification and recent experience |
| --- | --- |
| Authorized persons | Registered architect, registered professional engineer in structural or civil engineering, or registered professional surveyor. Plus, for a continuous period of 1 year within the 3 years before the application, practical experience in Hong Kong that the committee considers appropriate |
| Structural engineers | Registered professional engineer in structural engineering, or in civil engineering with practical structural experience approved by the committee. Same 1-year-in-3 Hong Kong experience |
| Geotechnical engineers | Registered professional engineer in the geotechnical discipline. Same 1-year-in-3 Hong Kong experience |
| Inspectors | Authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered architect, registered professional engineer in building, structural, civil, building services (building), or materials (building), or registered professional surveyor in building surveying or quantity surveying |
| Inspector who is already an authorized person or registered structural engineer | Within the 7 years before the application, Hong Kong experience as an authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered architect, registered professional engineer, or registered professional surveyor in any building repair and maintenance project |
| Inspector who is a registered architect, a building or structural engineer, or a building surveyor | Not less than 1 year in aggregate, within the 3 years before the application, of Hong Kong practical experience in building construction, repair, and maintenance that the committee considers appropriate |
| Inspector who is a civil, building-services, or materials engineer, or a quantity surveyor | Not less than 3 years in aggregate, of which at least 1 year falls within the 3 years before the application, of that same Hong Kong experience |

**SD takeaway:** Appoint an authorized person, structural engineer, or geotechnical engineer whose Hong Kong practical experience includes a continuous year inside the last three years, and treat a registration as lasting five years.

### 2. Renewal and restoration (paragraphs 8–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| First application | Form BA 1 and the prescribed fee |
| Retention | Form BA 1A. Not earlier than 4 months and not later than 28 days before expiry. Applications outside that window are not accepted |
| Restoration after removal under sections 3(11A), 3(11B), or 3(11C) | Within 2 years beginning on the date the registration expires, on Form BA 1B |

**SD takeaway:** Lodge renewal on Form BA 1A inside the window from 4 months to 28 days before expiry, or the name drops off the register.
