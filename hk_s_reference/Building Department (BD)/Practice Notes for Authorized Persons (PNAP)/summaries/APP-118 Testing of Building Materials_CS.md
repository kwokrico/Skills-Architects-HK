# APP-118 Testing of Building Materials
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 251; first issue October 2001)

> Scope note: Which building materials and products the Building Authority expects to be tested by an accredited laboratory, and how fire-rated products are certified. The Buildings Ordinance has no provision for the Building Authority to issue a certificate or test report for materials. Completion certification of materials is in PNAP APP-13. It is not a materials specification.

## Regulatory Overview
Authorized persons, registered structural engineers, registered geotechnical engineers, and registered contractors supervise selection and application of materials and certify compliance. The Building Authority relies on them and on independent laboratories, and recognises laboratories accredited by the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme or by bodies with a mutual recognition arrangement with that scheme, for tests within the laboratory’s scope of accreditation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Materials normally tested by an accredited laboratory (paragraphs 3–4)
The list is not exhaustive:

| Product | Testing |
| --- | --- |
| Concrete cube and core | Accredited laboratory as in paragraph 3 |
| Steel reinforcement | Same |
| Reinforcement splices and couplers | Same |
| Curtain wall systems | Same |
| Fire rated products, materials, and construction components | Same, and paragraphs 5–7 |

**SD takeaway:** Specify concrete, reinforcement, couplers, curtain wall, and fire-rated products for test by a laboratory accredited for that test.

### 2. Fire-rated products (paragraphs 5–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Performance | Compartment walls, protective cover to structural steel, fire rated doors, and other materials for fire resisting construction comply with the performance standards in Building (Construction) Regulation 90, by reference to the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 |
| Test or assessment | Tested to the standards in that code, or assessed against those standards from relevant test reports |
| Reports | Prepared in accordance with Clauses E16.1 and E16.2 of the code. Reports whose validity period has expired will not be accepted |
| Certification bodies | Those recognised by the Building Authority under Clause E16.1, listed under the Hong Kong Certification Body Accreditation Scheme or its multilateral recognition partners |

**SD takeaway:** Accept a fire-rated product only with a current test or assessment report that meets Clauses E16.1 and E16.2 of the 2011 fire safety code.
