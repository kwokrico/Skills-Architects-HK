# APP-79 Geoguide 5 Guide to Slope Maintenance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2020 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 189; first issue January 1996)

> Scope note: Maintenance standards for man-made slopes, retaining walls, and man-made features on natural hillsides, to be specified in site formation submissions under regulation 8(1)(bb)(E) of the Building (Administration) Regulations. The inspection standards themselves are in Geoguide 5.

## Regulatory Overview
Geoguide 5, published by the Geotechnical Engineering Office, is the standard of good practice to adopt when maintenance requirements are specified in a site formation submission. The designer prepares a Maintenance Manual, including the information listed in Chapter 2 of Geoguide 5, and the submission requires that manual at the Form BA 14 stage.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Maintenance Manual and later works (paragraphs 2–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Submission | In duplicate with Form BA 14. After acknowledgement, one copy is returned for transmission to the owner or the parties who will maintain the development |
| Content of maintenance | Includes a review of the previous Stability Assessment. Engineer Inspections for Maintenance are clarified in Geoguide 5 |
| Routine maintenance under the minor works system | Certain routine maintenance to slopes and retaining walls may use the simplified requirements. See Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation and PNAP APP-147 |

**SD takeaway:** Write a Geoguide 5 Maintenance Manual into the site formation submission and lodge it in duplicate with Form BA 14.
