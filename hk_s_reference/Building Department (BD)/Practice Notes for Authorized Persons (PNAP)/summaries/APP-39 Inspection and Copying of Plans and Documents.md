# APP-39

**Official title:** Inspection and Copying of Plans and Documents

**In plain words:** Plans and documents lodged with the Building Authority (BA) are public records, and inspection or copies may be allowed under Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 36G on request and payment of the prescribed fee, but not before the building or the works are completed. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Release is also subject to the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, the Copyright Ordinance, the Code on Access to Information, and duties of confidence.
- Records that can be released, for completed projects only: the latest approved plans, calculations, and related documents; the latest Class I and Class II minor-works plans; and, for completed minor works under the simplified requirements, the latest Class III minor-works plans, descriptions, photographs, and related documents.
- At the Building Information Centre (BIC), in person: Form BIC1 to inspect, with an electronic pledge within 3 working days; Form BIC2 for copies after inspection; Form BIC-3 for one-stop copies, with the result and the fees advised within 10 days; Form BIC4 for copies of an occupation permit (OP).
- Online through BRAVO: register on Form BIC5 or on the website. The login is sent by registered mail.
- Fees are charged under Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulation 42. Delivery is free to most addresses in Hong Kong. It does not cover a post-office box, the Closed Area, Lantau, or the Outlying Islands.
- A purpose under paragraph 4(c) needs a special justification, or authority from the copyright owner together with a positive statement of ownership.

**What to submit**
- Use the Building Information Centre forms, BRAVO, or the electronic form submission system (EFSS). Declare the purpose and pay the fee.
- The declared purpose may be a construction matter, compliance with the Buildings Ordinance or related enactments, or another public-interest purpose the Building Authority considers appropriate.
- Copyright in the plans, after a change of authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE), has to be obtained by the new professional. Release of a copy by the Buildings Department does not remove the risk of infringing copyright.

**Watch-outs**
- Asking for records of a project that is not yet complete.
- Reusing plans after a change of authorized person, structural engineer, or geotechnical engineer, without copyright consent.
- A wrong lot number or address on a request for an occupation-permit copy (Form BIC4).
