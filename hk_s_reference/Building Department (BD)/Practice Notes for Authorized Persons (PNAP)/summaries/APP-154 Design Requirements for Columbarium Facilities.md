# APP-154

**Official title:** Design Requirements for Columbarium Facilities

**In plain words:** This note sets Buildings Ordinance design rules for a columbarium, including escape, fire resistance, access, sanitary fitments, and barrier-free access. It does not itself change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage, though the occupancy load affects how much space escape routes and toilets take.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue August 2012.
- Means of escape: Fire Safety Code Part B. Table B1 item 5d occupancy is 2 m² of usable floor area per person. Include corridors of a balcony approach, circulation, and lift lobbies in that usable floor area. Exclude niche accommodation areas and staircases from that calculation.
- Fire resisting construction: Fire Safety Code Part C. Use classification “Other Assembly Premises” (Table C1). Burner and offering facilities are a special hazard (subsection C13 separation).
- Means of access and emergency vehicular access: Building (Planning) Regulations 41A to 41D and Fire Safety Code Part D. A non-standard emergency vehicular access needs Form BA16 and APP-136.
- Sanitary fitments (Table 1): population at 2 m² per person. Male to female ratio 1:1.5. Male: 1 water closet plus 1 urinal per 150 persons, and 1 basin per 300 persons. Female: 1 water closet per 75 persons, and 1 basin per 300 persons. Provide separate rooms.
- Barrier-free access: Building (Planning) Regulation section 72 and the Design Manual 2008. Provide aisle space for worship in addition to the minimum passageway and means-of-escape clearances.
- Loads: Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011.
- A conversion is a material change of use under Buildings Ordinance section 25. Columbarium use is in non-domestic buildings only. In an industrial building, convert the entire building only, for Fire Services Department fire safety. Partial conversion is undesirable.

**What to submit**
- Plans showing means of escape, fire resisting construction, means of access, sanitary fitments, barrier-free access, and loads.
- Other departments are separate: planning, hygiene, traffic, Fire Services Department, and Lands Department on the lease.
- Change-of-use notifications where the project is a conversion.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not under-count usable floor area for means of escape by leaving out circulation.
- Do not convert only part of an industrial building to a columbarium.
- Buildings Ordinance compliance alone does not satisfy the lease, the outline zoning plan, or licensing.
