# ADM-6

**Official title:** Computer Programs for Use in Structural and Geotechnical Design

**In plain words:** You may use a computer program for structural or geotechnical design, but the Building Authority must have accepted that program before you submit the plans. Acceptance of the program does not move design responsibility off the registered structural engineer (RSE) or the registered geotechnical engineer (RGE). This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- The RSE applies for prior acceptance of a structural program. The RGE applies for a geotechnical program. An accepted program gets a Buildings Department reference: prefix S for structural, prefix G for geotechnical. Quote that reference in the submission.
- The application (Appendix A) and the user manual must cover mathematical modelling; flow charts of the analysis and design procedure; the Building Regulations, codes, or other requirements the program follows; the theory, algorithm, and references; limitations and where the program applies; how data go in and results come out; how to read the results; and checked examples that cover every part of the program at typical input values. More test examples may be asked for.
- Structural applications: one copy of the manual. Geotechnical applications: two copies.
- In the design submission, put the computer-calculation information in Part I of the structural calculations, as ADM-8 paragraph 9(b) describes. The RSE is responsible for running a structural program. The RGE is responsible for running a geotechnical program. Complete Appendix B (structural) and/or Appendix C (geotechnical).
- A program with no valid Department reference may have to be documented in full, as paragraph 3 requires, inside the submission itself.
- Prior acceptance lasts up to three years from the last acceptance date. It is treated as expired three years after that date. Renewal is not earlier than four months before expiry. If the program changes, submit the amendment details and an updated manual, and get acceptance, before you use the revised program.
- Lists of accepted programs and their limits are at the New Buildings Divisions Receipt and Dispatch Counter, 7/F, Cityplaza 3, 14 Taikoo Wan Road, and on the Buildings Department website.

**What to submit**
- For prior acceptance or renewal: Appendix A, with manuals and flow charts.
- With the design: Appendices B and/or C, plus the ADM-8 Part I computer record (name, version, developer, Department reference, validity, accepted scope and limits, model assumptions, input graphics, critical output envelopes, and how to read them).
- The RSE or RGE confirmation that the program is adequate and suitable, and, for geotechnical work, that they are responsible for running it.

**Watch-outs**
- Software without a valid S or G reference can force a full re-documentation in the middle of a submission, or delay approval.
- An acceptance older than three years, or a revised version that was never accepted, is not valid.
- Missing Appendices B or C, or an incomplete Part I computer record under ADM-8, holds the submission up.
- Prior acceptance of a program is not a transfer of design liability.
