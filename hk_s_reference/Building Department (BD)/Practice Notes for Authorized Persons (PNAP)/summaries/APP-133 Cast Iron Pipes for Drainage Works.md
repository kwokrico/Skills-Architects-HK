# APP-133

**Official title:** Cast Iron Pipes for Drainage Works

**In plain words:** This note sets quality rules for cast iron drainage pipes and fittings. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 282. First issue November 2003. Mandatory for drainage plan approvals after 1 November 2004.
- Pipes and fittings to the relevant British Standard or equivalent. Examples: BS 416:Part 1 or BS EN 877 above ground; BS 437 or BS 4622 underground, or above ground where the size is over 150 mm.
- Internal and external anti-corrosion coating. Cold-applied bitumen-based coatings to BS 3416 or equivalent. External coating to include anti-corrosion inhibitors.
- Pipes must allow further external finishing coatings. Fixings must resist corrosion (for example stainless steel brackets) and be anchored into a solid wall.
- The Building Authority requires compliance under Buildings Ordinance section 28(1) for drainage plan approvals after 1 November 2004.

**What to submit**
- Include cast iron pipes in the Schedule of Building Materials and Products under PNAP 53 (the APP materials schedule) at the occupation permit application.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not specify uncoated or poorly coated cast iron, or fixings that are not corrosion-resistant.
- Do not leave cast iron pipes off the materials schedule at the occupation permit stage.
