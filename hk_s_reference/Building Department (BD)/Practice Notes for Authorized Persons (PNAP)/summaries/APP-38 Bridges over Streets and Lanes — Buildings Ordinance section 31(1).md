# APP-38

**Official title:** Bridges over Streets and Lanes — Buildings Ordinance section 31(1)

**In plain words:** A bridge over a street or lane that lies wholly or partly in a private lot, or is supported by or connected to a private building, needs a Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 31(1) exemption and an acceptable appearance review. A link used only for circulation is typically outside commercial gross floor area (GFA); if commercial activity is permitted, that area is accountable for GFA under the outline zoning plan (OZP), and the width, storeys, and air path also affect the street and the gap between buildings.

**Numbers that matter**
- The exemption may be given where (a) the bridge is required by the lease or a planning permission, or is consistent with the outline zoning plan, outline development plan, or layout plan; or it is functionally necessary for efficient pedestrian movement between buildings, to relieve congestion at street level, and is acceptable to the Lands Department, Planning Department, Transport Department, and Highways Department; or it provides an identified public benefit, such as separating pedestrians from vehicles or linking to public transport or the public footbridge network, acceptable to those departments; or another special circumstance is justified.
- The bridge is for a pedestrian or vehicular link only. Commercial activity is generally not allowed unless there is planning permission and a justified public benefit, in which case the commercial area is accountable for GFA under the outline zoning plan.
- The width and the number of storeys must be fully justified by pedestrian flow. A bridge of more than 1 storey, or with a clear width of more than 6 m, may be required to include visual and air-ventilation impact studies.
- The Advisory Committee on the Appearance of Bridges and Associated Structures (ACABAS) must find the aesthetic design acceptable for a structure over, under, on, or next to a public road.
- The ACABAS submission is 10 sets of stapled A3 drawings plus a CD or DVD of PDF or JPG files, sent direct to the ACABAS secretary. The contents follow Environment, Transport and Works Bureau Technical Circular (Works) (ETWB TC(W)) 36/2004: a general arrangement; elevations and a perspective or axonometric drawing showing form, texture, and colour; a comprehensive set of site photographs; and, for a footbridge, the anticipated pedestrian flows marked on the plans.
- A free-standing bridge wholly outside a private lot is exempt from the Buildings Ordinance and is submitted to the Highways Department. Agree maintenance responsibilities with the Highways Department before construction. Read this note with practice note APP-34 and with ETWB TC(W) 10/2005 on planting.

**What to submit**
- The section 31(1) exemption application, with the purpose justified.
- The ACABAS package, in parallel.
- Confirmation that the relevant departments accept the proposal.
- The Highways Department route where the bridge is wholly outside the lot.

**Watch-outs**
- Commercial use on the bridge without planning permission and without counting that area in gross floor area.
- A bridge of more than 1 storey, or wider than 6 m in the clear, without the air-ventilation and visual studies.
- A rejection by ACABAS, which blocks the exemption.
- Maintenance responsibility left unresolved.
