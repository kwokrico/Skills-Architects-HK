# ADM-15

**Official title:** Submission of Site Formation Proposals

**In plain words:** A site formation submission may be split into two parts so the layout and the structural design of retaining structures can move in parallel. This note does not set gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) figures. The slope, retaining-structure, and drainage layout still shapes the platform you can build on, and that platform is what later density checks use.

**Numbers that matter**
- Part A is the site formation details: layout and details of the slope works and the associated drainage, and the general layout and size of any earth retaining structures, as Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1)(bb) requires.
- Part B is the structural details of the earth retaining structures.
- The authorized person (AP) or the registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) coordinates and signs Part A. The registered structural engineer (RSE) signs Part B. Responsibilities follow Building (Administration) Regulation 12 and Task 4 of Appendix B in APP-141.
- You may submit Parts A and B together. You may submit Part A first and Part B when the soil-structure interaction can be prescribed. Or you may submit enough complete sets, with one set clearly marked Part A and another marked Part B.
- A split may not be needed for a simple earth retaining structure, small-scale works such as New Territories exempted houses, or a slope drainage system of box culverts, sand traps, catchpits, and stormwater manholes, if there is no major earth retaining structure.
- If the site formation proposal includes piling or similar operations, also follow the monitoring requirements in APP-18 and APP-137.

**What to submit**
- Enough sets, with the information needed for consultation with government departments. See ADM-2, ADM-8, and APP-141. The aim is to process Parts A and B in parallel and to keep the responsibilities clear.

**Watch-outs**
- Submitting Part B before the soil-structure interaction can be prescribed risks an abortive submission or disapproval.
- Leaving out APP-18 and APP-137 monitoring, when piling is in the site formation package, is a gap.
- Unclear Part A and Part B marking, or the wrong person signing, exposes the team under Building (Administration) Regulation 12.
