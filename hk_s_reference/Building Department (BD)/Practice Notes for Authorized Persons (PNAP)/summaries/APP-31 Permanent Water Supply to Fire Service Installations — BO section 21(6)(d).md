# APP-31

**Official title:** Permanent Water Supply to Fire Service Installations — BO section 21(6)(d)

**In plain words:** Where the Fire Services Department code on minimum fire service installations requires a permanent water supply, that connection must be made before anyone occupies the building. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 21(6)(d) is the fire services certificate step before an occupation permit (OP).
- If no fire-service water supply is required, or the permanent connection is already made, the fire services certificate is issued in the normal way.
- If the installations are satisfactory to the Director of Fire Services (DFS) but the permanent water is not yet connected, the certificate is issued with a rider that is valid only when the permanent supply is connected.
- The occupation permit will not be issued until the terms of that rider are fulfilled.

**What to submit**
- Time the Water Supplies Department connection and the Fire Services Department (FSD) clearance so that they match the occupation-permit application.
- The fire services certificate must be free of any rider, or the rider must already have been fulfilled, before the occupation permit.

**Watch-outs**
- Applying for an occupation permit while the fire services certificate still carries a permanent water-supply rider that has not been met.
