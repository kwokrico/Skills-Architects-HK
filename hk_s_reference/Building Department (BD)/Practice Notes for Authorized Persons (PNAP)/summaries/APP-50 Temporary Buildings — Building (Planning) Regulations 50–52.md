# APP-50

**Official title:** Temporary Buildings — Building (Planning) Regulations 50–52

**In plain words:** A temporary building, defined by a temporary purpose or by short-lived materials, is still subject to the whole of the Buildings Ordinance (BO), including approval, consent, supervision, a registered contractor, and a temporary occupation permit (TOP). There is no lasting gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC) concession, and planning and lease controls still apply for the period authorised.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) regulations 50 to 52 apply. Regulation 50(1)(a)(i) and (ii) define a temporary building by its temporary purpose or its short-lived materials. The authorized person (AP) and the registered structural engineer (RSE) supervise, and a completion certificate is required.
- Plans are submitted with Form BA17, which replaces Form BA5 as the application for approval and Form BA8 as the application for consent, in one package. Form BA5 is still submitted as the certificate that the AP and the RSE prepared the plans. Form BA4 is the notice of appointment.
- If approval is given and consent is also granted, Form BD107 is issued. It replaces the approval letter S.L.9 and Form BD103, and it states the permitted duration.
- Under BO section 20, the works must start within 3 months of Form BD107, failing which consent is renewed on Form BA9. The building may remain until Form BD107 expires.
- The time limit for processing Form BA17 is 60 days for a first submission and 30 days for a resubmission. The approval period and the consent period run concurrently.
- Occupation requires a temporary occupation permit on Form BD105, applied for on Form BA12. Conditions on Form BD107, including the maximum number of persons, removal on expiry or cancellation, and the expiry date, become conditions of the temporary occupation permit.
- On expiry or cancellation, the building is removed forthwith, or the Building Authority (BA) may order removal under BO section 24(1). Occupation after the temporary occupation permit expires or is revoked may be prosecuted under BO section 40(1).
- An extension is applied for in writing, with the original Form BD107 and Form BD105, and with a certificate by the AP and the RSE that the building can remain for the extended term in a safe condition, and in a sanitary condition where that applies, for the stated purposes. The extended term is at most 5 years.

**What to submit**
- Follow the Form BA17, Form BA4, Form BA5, and Form BD107 path.
- Commence the works within 3 months, or renew consent on Form BA9.
- Obtain the temporary occupation permit, Form BD105, through Form BA12, before occupation.
- Apply for an extension only with the original permits and with the AP and RSE certificate of the safe duration, for a term of 5 years or less.

**Watch-outs**
- Using Form BA5 and Form BA8 as the approval-and-consent applications, instead of Form BA17.
- Failing to commence within 3 months.
- Occupying the building without a temporary occupation permit.
- Allowing occupation after Form BD105 has expired.
- An extension with no AP and RSE certificate that the building can safely last the extended period.
