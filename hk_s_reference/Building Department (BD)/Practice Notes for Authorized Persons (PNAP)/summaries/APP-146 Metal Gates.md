# APP-146

**Official title:** Metal Gates

**In plain words:** This note sets design, installation, testing, and maintenance rules for metal gates in fence walls and at entrances. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 304. Latest revision March 2025 (welding non-destructive testing, anchors, Appendices A and B).
- New buildings: show the gate on plans for approval and consent. If the height is over 3.2 m, submit structural details and calculations for approval. Existing buildings: if the height is over 3.2 m, submit building and structural plans before installation. The minor works control system may apply to some existing installations (APP-147 and Building (Minor Works) Regulation Schedule 1).
- Performance: Building (Construction) Regulation section 3; recognised standards; solid construction for the intended life.
- Structural submission covers the wind Code; frame, posts, and foundation; connection to the supporting structure; swing hinges; sliding end stoppers and multi-pass connections; and guides against lift-off and derailment.
- Swing gates: at least 3 hinges per post. If the gate is over 3.2 m, use stainless hinges at 1000 mm centres or less, and within 300 mm of the top and the bottom. Orient the hinges so the gate cannot lift off.
- Sliding gates: at least 2 pairs of guiding wheels, spaced at least 1000 mm apart. At least 1 leaf stopper, plus further stopper(s) on the ground, a column, or a wall. Provide a rubber cushion and derailment safeguards.
- Drilled-in anchors: pull-out test at least 5 of each type and size to APP-169 Appendix B. Capacity at least 1.5 times the manufacturer’s recommended tensile capacity. A pass means no separation, plastic movement, or other harmful effect.
- Non-destructive testing of all welded joints at supports and at mechanical end stoppers, by a HOKLAS laboratory or a laboratory under a mutual-recognition agreement. Direction of the test: the authorized person or registered structural engineer where the works are approved; a prescribed building professional for Class I minor works; or a registered general building contractor or registered minor works contractor for Class II or III minor works where there is no prescribed building professional.
- Electrically operated gates: follow the EMSD Code of Practice for Sliding Gates, Glass Doors, and Rolling Shutters.
- Maintenance: the owner or incorporated owners should document a routine check every 3 months.

**What to submit**
- Prior approval and consent, or the minor works simplified path where it applies.
- Qualified supervision by a registered general building contractor or registered minor works contractor. Inspect and trial-operate the gate on completion.
- Anchor and weld test records, directed as above.

**Watch-outs**
- A large gate without structural approval, or a gate missing end stoppers or anti-derailment measures, is not acceptable.
- Do not skip HOKLAS pull-out or weld tests. Do not cover hinge welds in a way that prevents inspection.
- Defects in hinges, wheels, track, or steel should be picked up in the recommended 3-month check, and recorded by the owner or incorporated owners.
