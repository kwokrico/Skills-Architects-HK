# APP-134

**Official title:** Development in the Designated Area of Northshore Lantau

**In plain words:** This note sets extra ground investigation and foundation submission rules for the Designated Area of Northshore Lantau, where the ground is complex. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage, although the ground may force a layout change or stop a tower and so affect the bulk that can be built.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 283. First issue January 2004. Revision February 2006 (registered geotechnical engineer added).
- The boundary plan is in the Civil Engineering Library GIU and Appendix A. See also GEO Technical Guidance Note No. 12.
- Ground investigation is preferably staged, enough to judge effects on the development, and some deep drillholes are required. Early investigation before general building plans are fixed is advised, because changing the layout may be the cheaper option.
- Guidance: PNAP 66, Code of Practice for Foundations, GEO Publication 1/96, and the Guide to Site Investigation.

**What to submit**
- Foundation plans in the Designated Area must include (i) an explanatory guide, (ii) a ground investigation report with equipment and procedures, (iii) a critical examination and interpretation, a schedule of design assumptions, anticipated problems, and geotechnical requirements for design, construction, testing, inspection, and maintenance, and (iv) foundation design calculations.
- Supervise ground investigation under the Code of Practice for Site Supervision. The authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered geotechnical engineer supervise the foundations under that Code.
- Seek advice from the Chief Geotechnical Engineer / Mainland West (GEO) on the investigation proposal before work starts.

**Watch-outs**
- Inadequate staged deep investigation before the layout is locked has led to costly or abandoned towers.
- An incomplete foundation package for the Designated Area is not acceptable.
