# APP-20

**Official title:** Building Proposals affected by Street Widening — Building (Planning) Regulation 22(2)

**In plain words:** If land is given up for street widening, compensation may be bonus plot ratio (PR) and site coverage (SC) under Building (Planning) Regulation 22(2), or cash or a land exchange from the Lands Department. Where the bonus route is used, the surrendered land may also count as site area for PR and SC, but only for the first building put up on the reduced site.

**Numbers that matter**
- Subject to paragraph 4 of the note and to Regulation 23(2)(a), the Building Authority will accept the surrendered street-widening land as site area for the permitted PR and SC, only for the first building afterwards erected on the reduced site. Extra PR under Regulation 22(2)(b) is available only if the density is not excessive. Each case is decided on its own.
- Under Regulation 25, the same idea applies to a setback: it may count as open space at the side or rear.
- A temporary occupation permit or an occupation permit (OP) will not be issued before the Agreement to Surrender, containing the relevant terms, has been executed.

**What to submit**
- A separate block plan, in four copies, showing the surrender area. Building plans endorsed to show the surrender areas. A formal Agreement to Surrender to the satisfaction of the Lands Department, registered at the Land Registry.
- When street widening affects the lot, copy the plans at the same time to the Chief Estate Surveyor/Acquisition at the Lands Department.

**Watch-outs**
- Claiming the bonus without an executed Agreement to Surrender blocks the temporary occupation permit and the OP.
- The bonus site area does not apply to a building after the first one on the reduced site.
- A missing block plan, a missing endorsement, or a missing density justification undermines the claim.
