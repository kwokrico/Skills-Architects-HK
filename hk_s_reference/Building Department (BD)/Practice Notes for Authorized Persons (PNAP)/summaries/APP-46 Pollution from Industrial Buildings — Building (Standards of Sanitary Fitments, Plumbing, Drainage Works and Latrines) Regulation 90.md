# APP-46

**Official title:** Pollution from Industrial Buildings — Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulation 90

**In plain words:** Industrial buildings must allow for treatment and drainage of trade effluent, so that it does not damage drains, overload sewers, or put workers at risk, under regulation 90 and Buildings Ordinance (BO) sections 24 and 28. This note does not give a gross floor area (GFA) concession; the risers, treatment plant, and petrol interceptors affect the basement, the services, and the floor layout.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Building Authority (BA) may use BO sections 24 and 28, and regulation 90, against water pollution from industrial effluent.
- For a single-occupancy specialised industrial building with known processes, consult the Environmental Protection Department (EPD) and allow the specific treatment facilities.
- For a flatted factory, assume the effluent may need facilities beyond regulation 5(1). Failure to provide for a future discharge may lead to disapproval under regulation 90. State the use of the building clearly, and show adequate treatment of noxious effluent at drainage-plan stage. Failure to do so may lead to refusal under BO section 16(1)(i).
- Unless specific facilities are provided, each industrial unit has its own adequately trapped drainage outlet for treated trade effluent, connected to the foul system and not to the storm system. Usable floor area under 1000 m²: a 100 mm diameter outlet. Usable floor area over 1000 m²: an outlet of at least 150 mm diameter. Vertical foul downpipes are generally 150 mm to 300 mm in diameter, depending on the number of floors they serve.
- An outlet may be sealed temporarily at floor level until it is needed. Assume the occupant treats the effluent to the standard in regulation 90 before discharging it.
- Provide petrol interceptors where vehicles are serviced or parked. Other industrial treatment may need specialist advice. Standard petrol-interceptor details are attached to the note.

**What to submit**
- State the industrial processes and uses on the drainage submission.
- Provide an outlet for each unit, sized as above.
- Consult the Environmental Protection Department where the process is specialised.
- Include petrol interceptors where vehicles are serviced or parked.

**Watch-outs**
- Connecting a trade outlet to the storm-water system.
- A flatted factory with no foul outlet for each unit.
- A vague description of the industrial use on the drainage plans, which can lead to refusal under BO section 16(1)(i).
- An outlet that is too small for the unit’s usable floor area.
