# APP-49 Site Investigation and Ground Investigation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 132; first issue January 1990)

> Scope note: Acceptable standards for site investigation and ground investigation used to design foundations and site formation. Site investigation, as defined in section 2 of the Building (Construction) Regulation, includes documentary studies, site surveys, and ground investigation. Ground investigation is the surface or subsurface work, such as boreholes and test pits, and the associated tests.

## Regulatory Overview
Site investigation for the design and construction of foundations follows the technical guidance documents listed in Geotechnical Engineering Office Technical Guidance Note No. 1, including Geoguide 2 and Geoguide 3, and the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 (2024 Edition). Ground investigation field works are specialized works and must be carried out by a registered specialist contractor in that category.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who does the work, and what is submitted (paragraphs 4–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Field works | All site operations in ground investigation, excluding laboratory testing of samples and field density tests. Carried out by a registered specialist contractor (ground investigation field works). Scope and registration are in PNAP APP-96 |
| Laboratory and field density tests | Accredited laboratories for the tests concerned. See PNAP APP-64 |
| Reports the contractor compiles | Ground investigation report supporting foundation, site formation, and excavation and lateral support plans under regulations 8(1) and 9; and the geotechnical assessment under regulation 8(1)(ba), including a plan of the scope and extent of the investigation |
| Cores and samples | Kept, other than samples selected for testing, for inspection until acknowledgement of satisfactory completion of the site formation, foundation, or related works |
| Supervision | Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009 and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2024 Edition) |
| Digital submission | PDF and AGS data format, with the local additions for Geoguide 2 and 3. One PDF per station, with photographs, unique filename such as SSB-BH1. AGS data in ASCII, not split into parts |

**SD takeaway:** Commission ground investigation through a registered specialist contractor in that category, to Geoguide 2 and the Foundations Code, and keep the cores until the foundation or formation works are acknowledged complete.
