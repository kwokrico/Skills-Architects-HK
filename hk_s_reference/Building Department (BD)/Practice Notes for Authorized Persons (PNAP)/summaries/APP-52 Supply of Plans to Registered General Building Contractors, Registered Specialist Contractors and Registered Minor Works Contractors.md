# APP-52

**Official title:** Supply of Plans to Registered General Building Contractors, Registered Specialist Contractors and Registered Minor Works Contractors

**In plain words:** The authorized person (AP) must give the registered contractors exact copies of the approved plans, structural details and any supervision plan so they can keep supervising the work. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulation 36: supply a copy of plans approved by the Building Authority (BA) under B(A)R 30, a copy of structural details by the registered structural engineer (RSE) approved by the BA, and a copy of any supervision plan. Give these to the registered general building contractor (RGBC), registered specialist contractor (RSC) and registered minor works contractor (RMWC).
- The regulation sets no time limit. The AP must still supply the latest plans promptly so supervision can continue without a break.
- The copy of the approved plans must be an exact reproduction of the plans as approved, stamped, signed and dated by the BA.
- Class I minor works, Building (Minor Works) Regulation (B(MW)R) section 55: deliver to the prescribed registered contractor a copy of the prescribed plans and details under section 30(c). If the technical memorandum requires it, also deliver a copy of the supervision plan under section 30(d) or 48(2)(b) or 48(3)(b).

**What to submit**
- This is a duty of the appointed AP to the contractors. It is not itself a BA plan-approval checklist item. Failure still breaks the lawful supervision chain under the Buildings Ordinance, the B(A)R and the B(MW)R.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not supply unstamped, unapproved or outdated plans.
- Do not delay supply so the RGBC, RSC or RMWC cannot supervise continuously.
- For Class I minor works, do not omit the prescribed plans and details, or the supervision plan where it is required.
