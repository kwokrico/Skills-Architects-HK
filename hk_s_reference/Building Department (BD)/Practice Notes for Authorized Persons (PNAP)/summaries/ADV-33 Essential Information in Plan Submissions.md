# ADV-33

**Official title:** Essential Information in Plan Submissions

**In plain words:** This note is the checklist of what must be on general building plans, structural plans, and drainage plans so the curtailed check in ADM-19 can be done. The checklists are for you. You do not submit them to the Buildings Department. The note does not create a new gross floor area (GFA) concession. It is the place where the APP-151 overall 10% concession cap, the items that have prerequisites but are not capped, car-park concessions, and modular integrated construction floor area have to be drawn and labelled. If that drawing is missing, the concession is refused or deferred.

**Numbers that matter**
- General building plans: Appendix A1 is the checklist, A2 is a typical list of modifications and exemptions, A3 is sample tables for those modifications and exemptions, and A4 is sample drawings. Structural plans: Appendices B1 to B12 cover foundation, excavation and lateral support, superstructure, curtain wall, glass balustrade, metal cladding, metal ceiling, grille, and louvre, with samples and embedment details. Drainage: Appendices C1 to C3.
- Staged information on general building plans, Appendix A1. Stage I is the first approval. Stage II is before superstructure consent. Stage III is before the occupation permit or temporary occupation permit. If you defer Stage II or Stage III information, the first general building plan must say that the deferred items prescribed by Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1) will be submitted and approved before superstructure consent, or before the occupation permit, as appropriate.
- Administration pack: Forms BA4, BA5, BA6, BA8A, BA16, and BA17; 2 signed coloured sets for the Building Authority, the Fire Services sets, and the District Lands Office sets (1 in the urban area, 2 in the New Territories, and 2 where the lease has a design, disposition, and height clause); the fee and cheque on BD24; proof of ownership; supporting documents for BA16; any priority or fast-track application; and a geotechnical assessment under APP-25 where it is required.
- GFA and site coverage must be drawn as diagrams of accountable area, disregarded or exempted area, area subject to the 10% GFA cap, and area that has prerequisites but is not under the 10% cap. See Building (Planning) Regulation 23 and APP-151. Also show the overall plot ratio and site coverage calculations, mean street level, the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space, the level of full site coverage up to a maximum of 15 m on the elevation or section, a summary of GFA concessions, APP-152 compliance, the locations and dimensions of electric-vehicle charging facilities, and, where an above-ground car park is treated as underground, the details of that treatment. A green balcony larger than 2 m² needs usable-floor-space figures for the exemption calculation.
- Fire and access lengths to flag on the plans, because they are at the limit: the passage to a fireman’s lift or a firefighting and rescue stairway is critical above 44 m in an open plan, or above 59 m of actual passage; ground-floor fire-service access to that lift or stairway is critical above 17 m; staircase separation of 6 m is critical if the staircases are less than 7 m apart; a horizontal distance of 48 m is critical when the distance is more than 47 m; travel distance is critical above 47 m; the angle of an exit door is critical if it is less than 33 degrees where 30 degrees is required; a smoke outlet is critical above 29 m; a fire resisting construction external wall or opening is critical above 24% fixed light; a prescribed-window surplus is critical if it is less than 10% of the required area; a place-of-public-entertainment tier has a gradient not steeper than 35 degrees and a headroom of at least 3 m.
- Appendix A2 examples of typical modifications and exemptions: balcony and utility platform; wider corridors and lobbies; acoustic fins; non-structural prefabricated walls; voids; residential recreational facilities; a covered walkway or trellis, where a width above 2 m needs justification, and where greenery detail is needed if the item is to be kept out of the overall GFA concession cap; lift shafts; a cockloft clearance of 1.5 m; chimney and plant spacing, noting 1.5 m; filtration plant; pipe ducts and air ducts; sky gardens and podium gardens under Joint Practice Notes 1 and 2; modular integrated construction GFA exemption, item 27, which needs a note on the plans and a diagram of the module types and the modular floor-area calculations, citing APP-161; openable windows of at least 1% of usable floor area where curtain-wall commercial space seeks a reduction under Building (Planning) Regulation 30(2)(a)(ii); and omission of natural lighting and ventilation to an ancillary office, limited to not more than 30% of the GFA of the host premises. Many of these items need an APP-151 undertaking letter, and designation as common parts in the deed of mutual covenant and the sale and purchase agreement.
- Where you use building information modelling, colour GFA, site coverage, usable floor area, usable floor space, and compartment diagrams, with the floor plan underneath, to Table 1 of the 2019 Guideline for Using BIM in General Building Plan Submission.
- A land survey plan by an authorized land surveyor, to the Code of Practice under the Land Survey Ordinance (Cap. 473), in duplicate, substantiates site area and boundaries. That is typically a Stage II item.
- Electric-vehicle charging: design information and certification by a registered professional engineer, at the stage the checklist states. Transport Department justifications for the car-park layout, and for any constraint that stops the car park being underground, as the checklist lists them.
- Appendix A3 is the sample table of modifications granted or applied for. Appendix A4 is sample general building plan details for a gas flue, a utility platform, a balcony, air-conditioning, curtain wall, prefabricated wall, vertical greening, protective barriers, an accessible water closet, and similar items.

**What to submit**
- The plans themselves, with the information in the checklists. You complete the checklists internally. Curtailed-check items are also in the ADM-19 appendices.

**Watch-outs**
- A Stage I general building plan that does not separate area under the 10% cap from area that is not capped.
- Deferred items with no statement that Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1) will still be met, which breaks the first-approval path.
- An APP-151 concession claimed without the undertaking, or without evidence that the area will be common parts in the deed of mutual covenant.
- A modular integrated construction exemption claimed without the plan note and the module and floor-area diagram (item 27 and APP-161).
- Fire travel distances and firefighting distances that are not measured at the worst location.
- An incomplete site-classification case for a Class B or Class C site: the percentage of the boundary that abuts a street, and whether that street is at least 4.5 m wide.
