# APP-140

**Official title:** Registration of Authorized Persons and Registered Structural Engineers as Registered Geotechnical Engineers

**In plain words:** This note was a short-term route for an authorized person on the engineers list, or a registered structural engineer, to register as a registered geotechnical engineer. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Transitional path under the Buildings Ordinance Amendment Ordinance 2004. First issue December 2004. Read with PNAP 43. The window closed on 30 December 2005.
- Apply within 12 months from the start of the registered geotechnical engineer register on 31 December 2004.
- Conditions, all within the 7 years before the application: the person engaged in or took part in site formation works completed under the Buildings Ordinance; was the authorized person or registered structural engineer for those works; and satisfies the Building Authority of appropriate geotechnical experience and competence. That means detailed engagement from plan preparation through completion. The authorized person certifies, or the registered structural engineer reports to the certifying authorized person. No record of contravening the Buildings Ordinance.
- Registration is valid for 5 years, and can be renewed while the person remains on the authorized person or registered structural engineer register.
- Application under Buildings Ordinance section 53I transitional provisions, with whatever information and documents the Building Authority requires.

**What to submit**
- An application with the justifying information and documents the Building Authority requires under Buildings Ordinance section 53I.

**Watch-outs**
- This is a historical transitional note only. Current registered geotechnical engineer registration follows the Buildings Ordinance routes now in force, not this window.
