# ADV-2 Legislation and Publications Affecting the Building Industry
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision November 2014 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 115; first issue October 1985)

> Scope note: A non-exhaustive pointer list of ordinances, technical memoranda, codes, manuals, practice notes, and guidelines. It does not itself set a design standard, and the editions named in Appendix A are those current when the appendix was revised in November 2014.

## Regulatory Overview
Appendix A groups the instruments an authorized person is expected to know, starting with the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) and its subsidiary legislation, then other ordinances, technical memoranda, codes of practice, design manuals, practice notes, and guidelines. The note states that the list is not exhaustive and is offered for general information only, and that the publications are viewed on departmental websites or bought through the Information Services Department.
