# APP-42

**Official title:** Amenity Features

**In plain words:** Amenity features may be exempted or modified, case by case, to improve management and daily use, to reduce the temptation to carry out unauthorised building works (UBW), and to fit the neighbourhood. Most of the gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage (SC) concessions depend on practice note APP-151, including its pre-requisites and, often, a BEAM Plus assessment and the overall GFA concession cap.

**Numbers that matter**
- For a large scheme, discuss the proposal with the Buildings Department beforehand, and with the Lands Department and the Planning Department where they are affected.
- Air-conditioning. On a residential building, a permanent wall box or platform may be excluded from site coverage under practice note APP-19. On a commercial or industrial building, use central air-conditioning or set aside internal area, with no projection over the street. Floor space for air-conditioning plant may be disregarded from GFA under Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R) regulation 23(3)(b), subject to APP-151. As a reasonable default, air-conditioning plant rooms are at most 1% of total GFA, and air-handling units are at most 4% of the GFA of the floor they serve. A plant room inside a flat is preferably reached from outside the flat or from the common area. If it is inside the flat, provide a difference in floor level of at least 500 mm, at least one side open to the air (a railing, grille, or louvre may run up to the parapet, and further enclosure is allowed for intake, exhaust, or clause C11.1 of the Fire Safety Code), and a justified size.
- Security gates must not obstruct a means of escape, and the lock must be openable from the inside without a key. They may be installed before the occupation permit (OP) or Form BA14, or their positions may be shown, at unit entrances, at main staircase exits, and at the separation between domestic and non-domestic parts.
- A watchman’s counter, management counter, or similar room, subject to APP-151: not excessive, and in the common parts or under a deed of mutual covenant (DMC) undertaking. Domestic: the smaller of 0.2% of domestic GFA or 5 m² per 50 flats. Non-domestic: the smaller of 0.2% of non-domestic GFA or 120 m².
- An owners’ corporation office or caretakers’ quarters may receive a favourable GFA exemption, based on the size in the lease or on other justification, subject to APP-151.
- Clothes-drying racks, anti-burglar bars, and small window hoods for an individual domestic unit have no site-coverage or plot-ratio implication.
- A horizontal screen or covered walkway, subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites: open-sided and communal, on the ground floor or a podium; linking the main access at the lot boundary to the building’s main entrance, and/or linking building to building; not obstructing a prescribed window; in the common parts and not a commercial premises; maximum width 3.5 m and height 4.5 m, which may be relaxed where the walkway spans an emergency vehicular access; and acceptable to other departments. GFA and site coverage may then be exempted.
- A trellis, subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites. On a private roof or garden: the lesser of 5% of that roof or garden, or 20 m². In the common parts: at most 5% of the roof, or at most 10% of the garden, and each trellis at most 20 m². Open-sided and no higher than one storey; bars at least 200 mm apart; not obstructing a prescribed window; no trellis on a refuge roof.
- A shelter over outdoor fixed seats, subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites: at most 10% of the communal outdoor space, and each shelter at most 20 m²; open-sided, and at most 3.5 m in both width and height; horizontal clearance at least 3.5 m; in the common parts; not on a refuge roof.
- A prestige entrance follows practice note APP-2, and it must protect the means of escape and the fireman’s-lift lobbies. It is subject to the APP-151 cap and pre-requisites.
- A communal podium garden, or a covered landscaped and play area under a domestic tower: B(P)R regulation 23(3)(a) may be modified where the area is open and not encumbered by structure. The exempted total is at most 5% of domestic GFA, subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites.
- Covered area in a public open space required by the lease or by planning: resting facilities are shown; the area is not commercial; it is in the common parts, with an exception where the Government manages the public open space; and the Lands Department and the Planning Department have no objection. Subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites.
- Recreational facilities, such as a squash court, gym, indoor pool, sauna, or function room: regulation 23(3)(a) may be modified case by case, subject to the limits in practice note APP-104 and to APP-151.
- Satellite master antenna television (SMATV): a cabinet of at most 0.6 m by 0.6 m by 2.2 m is not controlled under the Buildings Ordinance (BO) if it involves no building works. A room in the common parts of at most 1.2 m by 1.2 m may be exempted from GFA under practice note APP-2.
- A radio base station: a cabinet of at most 1.5 m by 1.0 m by 2.3 m is plant and is not controlled under the Buildings Ordinance. A larger cabinet, or a room, is accountable for GFA and may need an occupation permit as a new building. The supporting structure may be Class I minor works.
- A covered pick-up and set-down area: not excessive; open-sided; in the common parts; and acceptable to the Lands Department, Planning Department, Transport Department, and Highways Department. Subject to the APP-151 pre-requisites.
- Extension of an accessible lift in an existing building, other than a house of 3 storeys or fewer: not excessive; designed to the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access; in the common parts; with an undertaking not to remove the existing lifts without agreement of the Building Authority (BA). GFA and site coverage are exempted.
- An outdoor prefabricated structure of at most 3 m³ and at most 2 m in height is not a building. The radio base-station rule above is a separate case. Anything larger is a building and needs approval, consent, and an occupation permit.
- Paragraph 46: the amenity features in paragraphs 20, 24, 30, and 42, which are the elderly-friendly items, apply to residential buildings, composite buildings, and commercial buildings patronised by elderly people. A GFA exemption cannot be applied afterwards so as to turn a feature that is already counted in GFA into area that can be transferred.

**What to submit**
- The modification or exemption application, with the justification, the deed of mutual covenant or common-parts undertaking, and the departmental clearances.
- An unusual air-conditioning system is shown in detail and is in operation before the occupation permit or Form BA14.
- The practice note APP-151 package is required for each concession that note lists.
- Consult the Buildings Department before lodging a large or innovative scheme.

**Watch-outs**
- Claiming a concession without the APP-151 pre-requisites, or outside the concession cap.
- An in-flat air-conditioning plant room without the difference in floor level and an open side.
- A management room inside a private unit.
- A covered walkway that functions as commercial premises.
- A trellis or a shelter on a refuge roof.
- Converting an amenity that is already counted in gross floor area into transferable area.
