# APP-86

**Official title:** Non-loadbearing Partition Walls

**In plain words:** A non-loadbearing partition still has to be designed and demolished safely, especially in alteration and addition work. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but the partition layout can still affect fire compartments, means of escape, and licensing.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 203. First issued October 1996. This revision December 2020.
- Design to the relevant standards. APP-53 covers slenderness, supports, and restraints that affect stiffness and stability.
- Demolition follows APP-21.
- Some partition work is minor works under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. See APP-147.

**What to submit**
- Use normal approval and consent, or the minor-works path where Schedule 1 applies.
- Show that the design has considered the APP-53 stability factors even though the wall is not structural.

**Watch-outs**
- Calling a non-loadbearing wall “outside the Buildings Ordinance” does not remove the duty to protect the public.
- Demolition needs precautions suited to the site, experienced workers, and supervision throughout.
- Ignoring slenderness or restraint can make the wall unstable.
- Use minor works only when that path truly applies. Use full approval when it does not.
