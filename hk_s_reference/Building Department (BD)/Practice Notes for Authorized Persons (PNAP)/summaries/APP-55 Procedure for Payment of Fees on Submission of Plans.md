# APP-55

**Official title:** Procedure for Payment of Fees on Submission of Plans

**In plain words:** Fees are charged on the first plan submission and on a major revision, worked out from gross floor area (GFA) or from the number of plans, and a change that looks small can still be a new fee. This note does not change GFA or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulations 29, 33 and 42. Resubmissions are free. Fees are not refundable if the submission is withdrawn.
- New buildings, including temporary buildings: the fee is based on the GFA of the new building. That fee also covers the associated site formation, structural and drainage plans.
- Where there is no accountable GFA, or the work is alteration and addition, or the work does not create a new building: charge by the total number of submitted plans of A1 size or smaller. A plan larger than A1 counts as more than one plan. This covers transformer rooms, petrol filling stations, oil storage, jetties and similar cases. Minor ancillary accommodation that would otherwise count as GFA may be ignored for this fee.
- Pay by crossed cheque to “The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region” or “The Government of the HKSAR”. Cash and post-dated cheques are not accepted. Enclose Form BD 24 (Appendix A) with every first submission and major revision.
- Pay any supplementary fee within 14 days of notification. If you do not, the plans may be disapproved under Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 16(1)(f). The same risk applies if the fee is missing or the cheque is dishonoured.
- BD 24 rates, as printed in Appendix A. Industrial building, GFA of 20,000 m² or less: $2,740 per 100 m², minimum $10,400. Industrial building over 20,000 m²: $2,200 per 100 m², minimum $547,100. Non-industrial building, GFA of 10,000 m² or less: $4,340 per 100 m², minimum $10,350. Non-industrial building over 10,000 m²: $3,480 per 100 m², minimum $434,400. No-GFA or alteration-and-addition work: $14,200 times the number of plans of A1 size or smaller.
- A localised major revision may be charged on the localised GFA, for example per podium or the affected podium floors. Other variations are case by case under BO section 42.
- Electronic Submission Hub (ESH): pay by Faster Payment System (FPS) or cheque. If you pay by cheque, send the cheque with a copy of the acknowledgement receipt to the Receipt and Dispatch Counter within 14 days of completing the electronic submission.
- If you are unsure whether a change is a major revision, ask the relevant Chief Building Surveyor (CBS) of New Buildings Division 1. You should get an answer within seven days.

**What to submit**
- The authorized person (AP) calculates the fee on Form BD 24 and encloses the cheque with the application for approval.
- Every major revision needs a new Form BA5.
- A non-profit school, hospital or similar community building can ask for a fee exemption on Form BA16, with documentary support. In that case, do not send a cheque with the plans. Exemption is generally considered favourably.
- No fee is charged for proposals that respond to a statutory order or notice, or to an advisory letter on building, fire or slope safety.
- Keep the payment receipt in case an overpayment can be refunded.

**Watch-outs**
- Under-declaring GFA or the plan count leads to a supplementary fee and a risk of disapproval under section 16(1)(f).
- Do not treat a major revision as a free resubmission, and do not omit Form BA5. Examples of a major revision, not a full list, are: a change in the layout or number of blocks; a change in the number of storeys, especially podium or basement storeys; a major floor-plan change that affects plot ratio, site coverage, lighting and ventilation, or means of escape; a change of principal use, such as office to residential or hotel to office; a substantial change in site area or site shape; a serious change in access; substantial modifications, exemptions or bonuses that need a fundamental review; wrong ground conditions that force the site formation to be reassessed; and changes that must be examined under new legislation or a new draft or approved outline zoning plan.
- Do not miss the 14-day cheque deadline on an ESH submission.
- Do not assume a fee exemption without Form BA16 and the supporting documents.
