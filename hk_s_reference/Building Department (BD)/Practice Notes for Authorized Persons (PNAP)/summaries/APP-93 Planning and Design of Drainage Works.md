# APP-93

**Official title:** Planning and Design of Drainage Works

**In plain words:** New-building drainage must be planned so it can be maintained later, under Buildings Ordinance section 28(1). Pipe ducts and pipe wells may be left out of gross floor area (GFA), and open pipe wells may also be left out of site coverage, but if they also carry non-mandatory or non-essential plant pipes the exemption is subject to practice note APP-151.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 211. First issued August 1997. Last revision September 2012. This revision May 2014 (paragraph 7 and Appendices B and C added). See also APP-151, ADV-14, and Fire Safety Code 2011 Part C.
- Common underground drains, except under car-park floors, run in sterilised land or common parts.
- In a domestic building that is not a single household, common soil and waste stacks are in common parts. A pipe duct is reached from common parts, with at least 700 mm clear working space in front of the pipes, and doors or panels at least 600 × 2,000 mm that meet Fire Safety Code 2011 Part C.
- A pipe well is at least 1,200 × 1,500 mm. Openings are only for inspection and maintenance from common parts. Access is at most 21 storeys apart. Provide a full-height cat ladder with guard rings, and grating platforms at most 4 storeys apart. Access is at least 600 × 2,000 mm and meets Part C fire resistance. Vent openings at the top and at the bottom are each at least 1/10 of the pipe well’s horizontal area.
- Pipe ducts and pipe wells may be left out of gross floor area. An open pipe well may also be left out of site coverage. Paragraph 4: if the space houses rainwater, soil, or waste pipes and also pipes to plant that is not mandatory or essential, APP-151 pre-requisites and the overall GFA concession cap apply.
- The lowest level of a re-entrant or light well that has external stacks is common parts, with adequate access, including a cat ladder if needed.
- In a multi-unit domestic building, one unit’s pipes must not project into a lower unit in separate occupation. Paragraph 3(d) is not mandatory for non-domestic buildings, but it is encouraged.
- In a building of more than 3 storeys, sanitary fitments at the same floor as the bottom of a stack have their own pipework to a manhole.
- The nominal diameter of a soil pipe is at least the outlet diameter of the water closets or slop sinks it serves.
- Use bends only where unavoidable. A bend at the bottom of a stack should have a centreline radius of at least 4 times the stack radius where practical, and never less than 200 mm on the centreline.
- Under Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations regulation 25(1), the Building Authority (BA) may allow a bathtub or shower tray trap up to 750 mm long at a minimum gradient of 1:40. Do not backfill a sunken-slab void with concrete or similar material. If that cannot be avoided, provide access panels or cleaning eyes, including for closed-circuit television (CCTV), and warn owners that floors may have to be opened for repairs.
- Appendix C partial screen, which is not treated as enclosed: at least one side exposed; permeable features at least 70% permeable; an unscreened vertical zone at least 800 mm high on every floor for pipes and branches; projection at most 500 mm.
- Appendix B enclosed pipes, under a CCTV regime: feature projection at most 500 mm (also the depth noted for GFA exemption on the Appendix B diagrams); vents at least 50% of the horizontal area at top and bottom; access openings at least 300 × 300 mm, with more openings based on cable length; a clear vertical camera path of at least 120 mm; demountable panels with a safety chain. The Authorized Person (AP) inspects by CCTV before the occupation permit.

**What to submit**
- Show maintenance access on the drainage plans and the building plans, under section 28(1).
- For pipes inside architectural features, show how they can be seen from common parts (Appendix B).
- For enclosed pipes, submit the owner or developer letter covering deed of mutual covenant undertakings, or sale-and-purchase or tenancy provisions, for the CCTV device, common parts, and regular inspections.
- Submit the “Certificate on Completion of Inspection…” (Annex 3) with Form BA 13 after the pre-occupation-permit CCTV inspection.
- Apply for a modification of regulation 25(1) where the longer trap is needed.
- Submit structural plans for demountable architectural features.
- Use ADV-14 for external drainage maintenance, and consider separate pipes by zone on a high-rise.

**Watch-outs**
- Common drains under shops, restaurants, or private units cannot be repaired later.
- A pipe duct without 700 mm clear space, or with undersized access, fails this note.
- Domestic pipes projecting into the flat below breach paragraph 3(d).
- A sunken slab backfilled with concrete, with no access eye, hides the drains.
- An architectural enclosure without the CCTV design, the deed of mutual covenant undertakings, or the occupation-permit inspection certificate is not acceptable.
- A screen is not an Appendix C partial screen if permeability is under 70% or the 800 mm exposed zone is missing.
