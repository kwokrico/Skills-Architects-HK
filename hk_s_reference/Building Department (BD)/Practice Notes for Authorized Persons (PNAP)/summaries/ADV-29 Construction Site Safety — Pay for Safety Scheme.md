# ADV-29

**Official title:** Construction Site Safety — Pay for Safety Scheme

**In plain words:** The authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) must see that building works under their supervision are carried out safely. This note asks you to recommend that the client pay the contractor separately for priced safety items, so safety is not squeezed out of the general rates. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), and it does not replace the supervision rules.

**Numbers that matter**
- The duty sits alongside the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision.
- The Pay for Safety Scheme has existed since 1996. The employer pays the contractor at the pre-priced rates in a Site Safety section of the bills of quantities or the schedule of rates, when the safety items, including training, are satisfactorily completed and certified by the employer’s representative.
- References: the Development Bureau Construction Site Safety Manual; the Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Safety Management) Regulation (Cap. 59AF); the REDA and HKCA Safety Partnering Programme; the Construction Industry Council Pay for Safety Scheme guidelines and reference materials, chapter 7 (March 2017); and Labour Department and Occupational Safety and Health Council training courses.

**What to submit**
- No statutory submission to the Buildings Department. Recommend that the client put the scheme in the construction contract.

**Watch-outs**
- If safety is not priced on its own, the resources for it get undermined.
- The scheme does not replace the Technical Memorandum, the Code of Practice for Site Supervision, or the duties under Cap. 59.
