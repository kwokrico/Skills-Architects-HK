# APP-31 Permanent Water Supply to Fire Service Installations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 1994 | Buildings Department (first issued July 1982; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Buildings Ordinance section 21(6)(d) and the timing of a permanent water connection to a fire service installation before an occupation permit. It does not design the installation; that is in the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment.

## Regulatory Overview
Where a permanent supply of water for a fire service installation is required by the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, a permanent connection must be made before the building is occupied. An occupation permit is withheld until any rider on the Fire Services certificate about that connection has been fulfilled.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fire Services certificate and occupation permit (paragraph 2)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Supply not required, or already permanently connected | The certificate under Buildings Ordinance section 21(6)(d) is issued in the normal way |
| Installation satisfactory to the Director of Fire Services, but permanent connection not yet made | The certificate is issued with a rider that it is valid only when the permanent water supply has been connected |
| Rider outstanding | An occupation permit will not be issued until the terms of the rider have been fulfilled |

**SD takeaway:** Programme the permanent water connection to the fire service installation before occupation, because a rider on the Fire Services certificate blocks the occupation permit.
