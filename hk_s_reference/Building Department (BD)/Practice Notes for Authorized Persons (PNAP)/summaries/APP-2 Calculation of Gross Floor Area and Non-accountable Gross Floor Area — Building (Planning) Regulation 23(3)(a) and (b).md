# APP-2

**Official title:** Calculation of Gross Floor Area and Non-accountable Gross Floor Area — Building (Planning) Regulation 23(3)(a) and (b)

**In plain words:** This note is the main rule for measuring gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage (SC), and for which voids, plant, curtain walls, cladding, car parks, and bicycle parking can be left out of the account. Several concessions also depend on PNAP APP-151 (the practice note that sets preconditions and an overall cap on GFA concessions) and, for some street-facing curtain walls, on a BEAM Plus score.

**Numbers that matter**
- Under Building (Planning) Regulation 23(3)(a), GFA is the area within the outer face of the external walls at each floor, and unfloored portions count as well.
- A modification for a void may be considered for: (a) cinema or theatre balconies; (b) banking halls; (c) a shopping arcade whose total void is not more than 10% of the shopping arcade GFA; (d) a single-staircase building with ground-storey cockloft storage; (e) an auditorium, sporting hall (including a squash court), school hall, or religious institution; (f) the main common entrance lobby of a non-domestic building; (g) a duplex flat or house that meets Appendix A. Items (a) to (g) must meet APP-151 preconditions (a) to (g). The overall GFA concession cap applies only to (f) and (g).
- A split level of less than 1 m may be treated as one level. A split of more than 1 m needs clarification from the Buildings Department first.
- Duplex void (Appendix A): usable floor space (UFS) at least 150 m²; void not more than 10% of that UFS; headroom not more than 6.5 m; void only in the living or dining area or the entrance foyer; not more than two void levels in a tower, and those voids not more than 0.5% of total domestic GFA (with flexibility for low-rise, low-density buildings).
- House void (Appendix A): UFS at least 250 m²; void not more than 5% of that UFS; headroom not more than 7.0 m. Under a sloping pitched roof, measure to the mean underside above the void.
- Duplex and house voids also need abuse controls (upstand beams, large exterior glazing, and no adjoining structural wall or beam), must not breach the town plan or lease after the void is exempted, and need a deed of mutual covenant (DMC), or a sale and purchase agreement (SPA) or tenancy, that designates the voids.
- Favourable consideration may be given to these features (paragraph 4). APP-151 preconditions apply to all of them except (e). The APP-151 overall cap applies only to (a) to (d): (a) chimney shafts; (b) filtration plant rooms for a hotel swimming pool or a communal water feature; (c) a satellite master antenna television (SMATV) room not larger than 1.2 m wide by 1.2 m deep (APP-42 paragraph 33); (d) pipe or air ducts for plant that is not mandatory and not essential; (e) pipe or air ducts for mandatory or essential plant, which have neither the APP-151 precondition nor the cap. E&F plant rooms for rainwater or greywater recycling, and solar battery rooms, are also on this favourable list.
- A refuge floor may be modified if it complies with the Fire Safety Code (FS Code).
- A curtain wall is a non-loadbearing enclosure on a load-bearing structure (Building (Construction) Regulation 26). The outer face of the structural elements can be used for GFA and SC if all of these are met: (a) the curtain wall is not part of the parent structural system; (b) there is a reinforced-concrete dwarf wall around the perimeter at least 300 mm high from the floor, and it adds no floor area; (c) the projection is not more than 200 mm in a domestic building, or 250 mm in a non-domestic building (a hotel is treated as non-domestic); (d) the external reflectance of the glass is not more than 20%. This acceptance does not apply to an industrial building with small workshop units.
- For a wholesale conversion, a curtain wall that projects over a street may get a favourable exemption under Buildings Ordinance section 31(1) if low-energy glazing or an energy-efficient design reaches 40% in BEAM Plus Energy Use (EU) and Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ), as run by the Hong Kong Green Building Council (HKGBC). You need the HKGBC registration acknowledgement, an undertaking to obtain the Provisional Assessment before consent, and the Final Assessment within 6 months of the Building Authority’s acknowledgement of Form BA14 (the certificate of completion). A localised facade, or a curtain wall on an existing balcony over a street, is not accepted. If a street projection is permitted, it is not accountable for GFA or SC.
- Cladding of nominal thickness, or cladding not more than 90 mm overall (not more than 75 mm where the cladding is on a non-structural prefabricated external wall), may be disregarded in the Regulation 23(3) measurement. It must not project beyond the site boundary. A projection over a street needs a section 31(1) exemption.
- Under Regulation 23(3)(b), floor area for machinery or equipment for a lift, air-conditioning, heating, or a similar service can be non-accountable. That reading includes a fire control centre, water tanks, electrical switch rooms, meter rooms, transformer rooms, generator rooms, pump rooms, telephone equipment, a cable riser duct, CO rooms, hose reel closets, sewage treatment plant rooms, and smoke extraction. In a single-family house, do not duplicate services whose areas are already excluded elsewhere; count only the minimum GFA; a multipurpose or centralised room may be required. Walls and a protected lobby that only serve a disregarded room may also be disregarded, and the wall is normally not thicker than 100 mm. An air-conditioning or heating plant room concession is subject to the APP-151 preconditions and the overall cap. Staircases, lift shafts, and vertical ducts are measured with the floors they pass, except at a refuge floor, and they may be discounted if they only serve floors that are themselves non-accountable.
- Car park disregard follows paragraphs 15 to 19 and Appendix C, with reference to the Commissioner for Transport, statutory town plans, and the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines (HKPSG). An open podium roof is generally not GFA. Electric-vehicle (EV) charging-enabling is required, except for automated parking. Underground private or public parking is 100% disregarded, and so is underground loading and unloading. Aboveground parking on not more than 2 floors is 100% disregarded, and other floors are 50%. For loading and unloading, the ground level is 100% and floors above ground level are 50%. Aboveground parking can be 100% where an underground car park is technically infeasible, or where there is no adverse environmental or visual impact. Government accommodation (GA) required under the lease is 100%. Hong Kong Housing Society (HKHS) or Urban Renewal Authority (URA) subsidised flats under the lease are 100%.
- Effective dates: underground public car parks from 1 Mar 2017; paragraph 18(b), and aboveground public parking under paragraph 18(c), from 1 Nov 2025; paragraphs 18(d) and 18(e) from 1 Aug 2023. EV requirements in Appendix C paragraph 4 apply to a new general building plan, or a major revision, on or after 1 Nov 2025. Approvals from before that revision follow the December 2023 version of this practice note.
- “Underground” means the soffit is at or below mean street level (one street), or at or below the average of the abutting streets, and not more than 5 m above the mean level of the lowest street. It can also mean a protrusion of not more than half a storey, or not more than 2.5 m, for natural light and ventilation, or a semi-sunken car park forced by a special site constraint.
- Examples of paragraph 18(c)(i) technical infeasibility: major underground utilities; Buildings Ordinance Schedule 5 Areas 1 to 4; complex geotechnical conditions. An example of 18(c)(ii): plot ratio not more than 1, and not more than 6 storeys, of which 5 are above a 1-storey car park.
- Aboveground parking on more than 2 floors may still be 100% disregarded if the total aboveground car park floor area is not more than 2× the site area because of site constraints (slope, old and valuable trees, government accommodation, public open space, a non-building area, a wind corridor, a railway reserve, and similar limits).
- Ancillary car park area is disregarded at 100%, 50%, or 0%, depending on whether it serves only a 100% park, only a 50% park, or neither. A loading or unloading ramp that runs directly to warehouse floors is fully GFA. A mixed run-in that serves both 100% and 50% floors is split pro-rata. Parking for a refuse vehicle next to a refuse storage and material recovery chamber (RSMRC) may be disregarded if the Refuse Storage regulations require vehicular access.
- A public transport terminus (PTT) is generally GFA unless the town plan or a planning approval says otherwise.
- Covered bicycle parking that the lease requires, and that is designated as common area, can be exempted from Regulation 23(3)(a) GFA on application.
- The APP-151 overall cap cited in the red flags of this note is 10%. Missing that cap, or the preconditions, on a capped item means the concession is refused.

**What to submit**
- Apply for modification for voids and for the features above. For a duplex void, the owner gives a DMC, SPA, or tenancy undertaking that designates the voids.
- For a wholesale-conversion curtain wall over a street: an HKGBC registration letter, plus an undertaking to complete the Provisional Assessment before consent and the Final Assessment within 6 months of the BA14 acknowledgement.
- For a car park: a car-parking schedule on the general building plans, and an EV charging facility report by a registered professional engineer (RPE) in electrical or building services. The EV report may be deferred to the consent stage if the Building Authority imposes a condition before superstructure consent. The Environmental Protection Department (EPD) has 22 calendar days to comment. For the occupation permit (OP), the RPE completion certificate is followed by an EPD no-objection within 10 days. EV meter rooms are essential plant under Regulation 23(3)(b).
- For HKHS or URA subsidised flats: their letter, and provisions agreed with the Transport Department, before the plans are submitted.

**Watch-outs**
- If a capped item misses the APP-151 10% overall cap or the preconditions, the concession is refused.
- If the curtain wall projection is too large, glass reflectance is over 20%, or the 300 mm dwarf wall is missing, GFA and SC are measured to the outer face of the curtain wall.
- Do not abuse plant rooms in a house or single-family building, and do not oversize areas you want disregarded.
- A car park without EV charging-enabling, from the date that applies to it, loses the disregard.
- Treat a PTT as accountable unless the town plan provides otherwise.
- A claim of site constraint, or of “no adverse impact”, for 100% aboveground parking needs evidence.
