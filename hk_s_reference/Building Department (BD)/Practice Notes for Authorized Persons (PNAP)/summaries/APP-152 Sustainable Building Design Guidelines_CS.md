# APP-152 Sustainable Building Design Guidelines
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This version September 2023 | Buildings Department (first issue January 2011)

> Scope note: Building separation, building setback, and site coverage of greenery, which PNAP APP-151 treats as a prerequisite for gross floor area concessions where they apply. Measurement methods are in the appendices. Special buildings and adaptive reuse of historic buildings may use the alternative approaches in Appendix E. It is not a plot-ratio code.

## Regulatory Overview
Separation applies to a site of 20,000 m2 or more, or to a smaller site whose continuous projected facade length is 60 m or more, and setback applies where the building fronts a street less than 15 m wide. Greenery applies to a site of 1,000 m2 or more, except a site with a single-family house only.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Building separation (paragraphs 3–6 and Table 1)
Maximum continuous projected facade length along a street is 5 times the mean width of the street canyon. Permeability is at least two-thirds intervening space and at most one-third permeable element, on two projection planes.

| Height of the tallest building | Site under 20,000 m2 with projected facade 60 m or more, each plane | Site of 20,000 m2 or more, plane 1 | Plane 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 m or less | 20% | 20% | 25% |
| Over 60 m | 20% | 20% | 33.3% |

Intervening space between a projected facade and the site boundary, or the centreline of an adjoining street or lane, is not less than 7.5 m wide. If that is not enough for two-thirds of the permeability, further intervening space not less than 15 m wide may be provided between two projected facades. A standalone residential block not exceeding 15 m high is exempt and is disregarded when assessing other buildings.

**SD takeaway:** On a site of 20,000 m2 or more with a tower over 60 m, provide 20% and 33.3% permeability on the two planes, with intervening spaces at least 7.5 m, or 15 m between facades.

### 2. Setback from a street under 15 m (paragraphs 7–9)
One of the following, up to 15 m above street level:

| Option | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Corridor | No part of the building within 7.5 m of the street centreline, so the ventilation section is at least 15 m by 15 m. Where the street level varies, keep that section along the full frontage |
| Podium garden | A cross-ventilated communal podium garden with clear height not less than 4.5 m, and no part of the building, up to 15 m above street level, protruding above a 45-degree plane whose base is at street level on the opposite boundary |
| Above 15 m | Structure may build over the setback. An uncovered setback may have a canopy complying with regulation 10 of the Building (Planning) Regulations. The ground-level setback under such structure or canopy may be exempt from gross floor area if it is common parts, accessible to occupants, and without commercial activities |
| Minor projections | Not more than 600 mm from the external wall, clear height not less than 2.5 m. Single-storey footbridges may cross the setback. Columns may stand in the setback as in Figure C2 |
| Surface | Landscaped and paved, open, without permanent structures other than landscaped features, perforated balustrades, perforated boundary walls, and structural columns |
| Low building | Exempt from the whole or part of the setback to a street if building height is less than 2 times the mean width of that street. Height is from the mean street level to the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor |

**SD takeaway:** On a street under 15 m, keep the building 7.5 m off the centreline up to 15 m above the street, or use a 4.5 m podium garden and the 45-degree line.

### 3. Greenery (paragraphs 10–11 and Table 2)
| Site area | Primary zone | Overall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 m2 to 20,000 m2 | 10% | 20% |
| 20,000 m2 or more | 15% | 30% |

Not applicable to a site with a single-family house only. Greenery plans are uploaded after the occupation permit. Information to demonstrate compliance is in Appendix F.

**SD takeaway:** A site of 1,000 m2 or more needs 20% greenery overall, of which 10% is in the primary zone, rising to 30% and 15% at 20,000 m2.
