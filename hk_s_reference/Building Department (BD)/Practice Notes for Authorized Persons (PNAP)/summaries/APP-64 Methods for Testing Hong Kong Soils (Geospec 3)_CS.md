# APP-64 Methods for Testing Hong Kong Soils (Geospec 3)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2005 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 167; first issue September 1994; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Geospec 3 is the soil-testing standard to specify for investigation, design, and construction. It contains 32 procedures for expanded Phase I tests (classification and compaction) and 7 procedures for Phase II tests (shear strength and compressibility). It amends BS 1377:1990 for Hong Kong. The test methods themselves are in Geospec 3.

## Regulatory Overview
A test report supporting a plan, or complying with a condition under Buildings Ordinance section 17(1) item 6(b), may be refused if the soil tests were not carried out to Geospec 3, and the same applies to ground investigation plans in the Scheduled Areas, site formation plans, and other plans with geotechnical content. A certificate of completion for filling works may not be accepted if in-situ density tests and laboratory compaction tests on fill were not done to Geospec 3, as PNAP 55 describes.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Laboratories and the date from which Geospec 3 accreditation is required (paragraphs 4–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who may test | Only a laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme for the relevant tests. Results are accepted only on endorsed certificates or reports. Clause 2.3 of Geospec 3 gives guidance on selecting a laboratory |
| From 1 July 2004 | Only endorsed certificates from laboratories accredited for the relevant tests in accordance with Geospec 3 |
| Credibility | Schedule tests to the geology and give the soil type required by Geospec 3. Supervise sampling, storage, transport, and preparation so samples are not disturbed. Do not reproduce an endorsed report except in full |
| Sanction | Knowingly misrepresenting material facts in a submission is treated seriously, both as a ground for disapproval and for sanctions |

**SD takeaway:** Specify Geospec 3 for every soil classification, compaction, strength, and compressibility test, and accept only a Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme report from a laboratory accredited to that standard.
