# APP-89

**Official title:** Provision of Better Lift Service

**In plain words:** Lift shaft area above a stated standard may be left out of gross floor area (GFA) and site coverage (SC) for domestic, hotel, and office lifts, up to the caps in this note, if APP-151 is also met. A building that already has an occupation permit (OP) cannot use this exemption.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 207. First issued August 1997. Last revision January 2013. This version June 2025.
- Lift and shaft areas normally count for site coverage under regulation 20 and for GFA under regulation 23(3)(a). Only a fireman’s lift and an accessible lift have a size set by the Buildings Ordinance.
- Domestic or composite buildings, including hotels, GFA: exempt the shaft area over and above 2.5% of total domestic or hotel GFA. The total exempted area is capped at 3.5% of that GFA. If the shaft area is more than 6% of domestic or hotel GFA, the maximum exemption is still 3.5%.
- Office with office GFA under 10,000 m²: exempt the shaft area over and above 5% of office GFA. The cap is 3% of office GFA. If the shaft is more than 8% of office GFA, the maximum exemption is 3%.
- Office with office GFA of 10,000 m² or more: exempt the shaft area over and above 3.5% of office GFA. The cap is 2.5% of office GFA. If the shaft is more than 6% of office GFA, the maximum exemption is 2.5%.
- Where office GFA is from 10,000 m² to 12,000 m², the maximum exempted area is 300 m².
- The shaft includes podium floors whose lobbies serve only the domestic, hotel, or office use. It excludes shafts that serve storeys which do not count in GFA, such as a refuge floor. Office GFA includes ancillary office facilities and excludes other commercial uses in an office or commercial building.
- Site coverage exemption equals the GFA-exempt shaft area divided by the number of storeys that shaft occupies.
- Paragraph 8 minimums: domestic or composite, each car at least 1.82 m² and each shaft at least 4.12 m² internally. Office, each car at least 2.1 m² and each shaft at least 4.4 m².
- A registered lift engineer under Cap. 618, or a registered professional engineer (RPE) in building services under Cap. 409, must certify that handling capacity and waiting time are above the acceptance level in the international codes, and that there is enough room to maintain the lift.
- APP-151 pre-requisites and the APP-151 concession cap also apply.
- Appendix A example: an office of 24,000 m² with shafts at 6% gives 600 m² of GFA exemption. Site coverage exemption is 600 divided by 20, which is 30 m².
- In a phased development, a completed phase gets no exemption. GFA and site coverage exemption cannot move between a completed phase and a later phase. See the Appendix B examples.

**What to submit**
- Apply for a modification to exempt the shaft from GFA and site coverage.
- Include the lift engineer’s or RPE’s assessment, the car and shaft areas, and evidence that APP-151 is met.
- For a phased project, calculate the exemption on the incomplete phase only.

**Watch-outs**
- An exemption without the lift engineer’s certificate, or with a car or shaft below the minimum area, is not available.
- APP-151’s overall GFA concession cap and pre-requisites still bind this exemption.
- Do not transfer an exemption from a phase that already has an OP to a later phase.
- Do not treat retail floor area as office GFA in a composite building.
- Do not apply this note to a building that already has an OP.
