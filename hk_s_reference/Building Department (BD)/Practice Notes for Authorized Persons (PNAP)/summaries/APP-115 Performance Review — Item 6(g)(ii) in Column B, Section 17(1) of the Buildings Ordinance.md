# APP-115

**Official title:** Performance Review — Item 6(g)(ii) in Column B, Section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance

**In plain words:** This note explains the performance review the Building Authority (BA) may require under section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance (BO), Column B item 6(g)(ii), for certain works outside the scheduled areas. It does not directly change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC), but an unfinished or unsatisfactory review can stop the occupation permit (OP) or temporary occupation permit (TOP).

**Numbers that matter**
- The review must show that inspection and monitoring during construction were adequate, that the geotechnical design assumptions remain valid, that the ground conditions actually met were reviewed, and that any design changes that followed were made and the plans amended and approved.
- Besides Scheduled Area Nos. 1, 2, and 4, a review is likely where the geology must be verified, where the groundwater regime is adversely affected, or where the design is unconventional.
- Geology cases include a slope with a history of significant continuous ground movement, and abnormal weakness such as persistent weak kaolinitic clay in weathered rock.
- Groundwater cases include damming by congested foundations below groundwater level, or a cutoff formed by bored piles or diaphragm walls. Examples given are land downslope of Mid-levels Area No. 1, hilly parts of Hong Kong Island, and the foothills of Kowloon.
- The review must satisfy the BA before the OP or TOP. Otherwise the application may be refused under BO section 21(6)(f).
- First issued September 2000. This revision December 2005.
- File references: BD GP/BORD/A/41; GP/BORD/75.

**What to submit**
- The BA may impose the review as a condition of approval or of consent.
- Submit the performance review, and obtain the BA’s satisfaction, before applying for the OP or TOP.

**Watch-outs**
- The OP or TOP may be refused if the review is outstanding or unsatisfactory.
- Leaving geotechnical assumptions untested on a complex or unconventional site is a public-safety liability.
