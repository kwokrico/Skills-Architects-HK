# APP-137

**Official title:** Ground-borne Vibration and Ground Settlement arising from Pile Foundation and Excavation and Lateral Support Works

**In plain words:** This note sets monitoring and control of vibration and settlement from piling and from excavation and lateral support. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly PNAP 289. Latest revision November 2024. See Foundation Code 2017 clause 7.2, APP-18, and the APP-24 railway and settlement approach.
- Plan submissions should address Foundation Code monitoring and assessment. The Building Authority may require a pre-construction condition survey with photos; a preliminary vibration and settlement assessment; control limits and a monitoring proposal; a detailed assessment if limits exceed Appendix A (references BS ISO 4866 and CIRIA TN142); and trial piles near vulnerable receptors.
- Percussive or vibratory piles near vibration-sensitive receptors (Foundation Code 7.2.6) are normally not accepted without trial piles. Precautionary shoring may be needed before trials. Excavation and lateral support with temporary pile walls uses the same monitoring items 3(a) to 3(d), and trial piles if vulnerable receptors are nearby.
- On test and trial piles, record maximum peak particle velocity (ppv) every metre of penetration, at final set, and at obstructions, on three orthogonal axes at ground level of the receptors.
- Enhanced 5A trigger levels in Appendix A (empirical, if there are no sensitive receptors):

| Criterion | Alert | Alarm | Action L1 | Action L2 | Action L3 |
|-----------|-------|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Ground settlement | 12 mm | 18 mm | 20 mm | 22 mm | 25 mm |
| Services settlement / angular distortion | 12 mm or 1:600 | 18 mm or 1:450 | 20 mm or 1:400 | 22 mm or 1:350 | 25 mm or 1:300 |
| Building tilting (angular distortion) | 1:1000 | 1:750 | 1:600 | 1:550 | 1:500 |

- Peak particle velocity guide, in mm/s, for robust buildings: transient Alert / Alarm / Action 1 / Action 2 / Action 3 = 9 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15. Continuous = 4.5 / 6 / 6.5 / 7 / 7.5. For vibration-sensitive or dilapidated buildings, use half of those (transient 4.5 to 7.5; continuous 1.8 to 3).
- Action Level 3 also applies if settlement rises 5 mm between consecutive daily readings, or if distress or damage is seen.
- Site-specific engineering limits under APP-24 Appendix C need acceptance by the owner or authority before the Building Authority approves the plans.
- A performance review plan is advised (Foundation Code 7.2.7).

**What to submit**
- Include the assessment, monitoring, and trial-pile proposals with the foundation, excavation and lateral support, or site formation plans.
- Before works, the registered structural engineer confirms the method, how many piles run at once, and the plant, and re-assesses if that changes.
- Trial-pile reports: 2 sets to the Building Authority before consent for working piles, or 1 set if using the electronic submission hub (ESH). Also a post-trial condition survey.
- If a limit is exceeded, stop the works, review precautionary measures, and resume only after a review the Building Authority accepts.

**Watch-outs**
- Buildings Ordinance duty: the works must not impair stability or damage buildings, structures, land, or services.
- Do not adopt vibration limits above Appendix A without a rigorous assessment, including the effect of many piles together.
- Do not continue works after a peak particle velocity or settlement exceedance, or after damage is seen.
- In a railway protection area, follow APP-24 Appendix B vibration controls.
