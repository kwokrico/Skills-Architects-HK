# APP-56

**Official title:** Exemption Criteria for Site Formation Works associated with Exempted Building Works in the New Territories

**In plain words:** Site formation tied to exempted New Territories building works can be exempted under Cap. 121 only if the ground is gentle and nearby walls and slopes are low; if the tests fail, submit site formation plans to the Buildings Department in the normal way. This note does not change Buildings Ordinance gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- The Director of Lands, through the District Lands Officer, issues the site-formation exemption under the Buildings Ordinance (Application to the New Territories) Ordinance (Cap. 121) section 6(a). Exemption is considered only if all of the following are met.
- The maximum gradient across the lot, boundary to boundary, is not more than 15 degrees.
- The overall gradient of the area bounded by lines 10 m outside the lot boundary, in any direction, is less than 15 degrees.
- There is no slope within 10 m outside the lot steeper than 30 degrees or higher than 1.5 m.
- There is no retaining wall or terrace wall inside the lot, or within 10 m outside the lot, higher than 1.5 m.
- Conditions on a Certificate of Exemption for site formation: no retaining wall or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m; the combined height of retaining wall and slope is not more than 1.5 m; the retaining height-to-breadth ratio is not more than 2; the wall is masonry or concrete; no slope is steeper than 30 degrees or higher than 1.5 m.
- Conditions on a Certificate of Exemption for building works (Appendix A): all retaining walls are excluded from the building-works exemption and are classed as site formation works. Any structure that retains earth or fill is a retaining wall. Investigation of existing slopes or retaining walls may be required. Delete the items that do not apply.

**What to submit**
- If the criteria or conditions are not met, submit the prescribed site formation plans to the Buildings Department in the normal way. The authorized person must be engaged, as shown on the flow chart.
- Appendix B flow: the District Lands Officer consults the Geotechnical Engineering Office (GEO). Investigation may be required. A Certificate of Exemption is issued with conditions. If the works would adversely affect existing slopes or retaining walls, or if landslip preventive (LP) or remedial works are needed, use the Buildings Department approval route.

**Watch-outs**
- A Cap. 121 building exemption does not automatically cover site formation or retaining walls.
- Do not build walls or slopes that exceed the height, steepness or height-to-breadth conditions.
- Do not skip a stability investigation when the Geotechnical Engineering Office requires one.
