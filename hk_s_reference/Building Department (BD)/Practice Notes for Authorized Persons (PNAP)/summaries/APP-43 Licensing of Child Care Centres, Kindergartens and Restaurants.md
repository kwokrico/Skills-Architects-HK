# APP-43

**Official title:** Licensing of Child Care Centres, Kindergartens and Restaurants

**In plain words:** When a child care centre, a kindergarten, or a restaurant is licensed, the Buildings Department comments on structural safety and means of escape, and an upper-floor centre or kindergarten in an ordinary building is allowed only where the location rules are met. This note does not itself change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC); the new use can consume staircase discharge capacity and floor loading, and that can constrain the tenancy layout.

**Numbers that matter**
- A child care centre or kindergarten above the ground floor, in a building not purpose-built for that use, is acceptable only where the location and condition tests are met, including compliance with the Means of Escape Code of Practice.
- Child care centre: maximum height 12 m or 24 m under regulation 19 of the Child Care Centre Regulations.
- Kindergarten: maximum height 24 m under regulation 7 of the Education Regulations.
- Certain cases in a wholly commercial building, on more than one commercial floor, or with a single staircase, need two exclusive means of escape, or two extra staircases with the original single staircase sealed off.
- A single-family domestic building of 3 storeys or fewer, and a New Territories exempted building, may be used without those extra conditions.
- A change in use that does not comply will be prohibited under Buildings Ordinance (BO) section 25. These uses are not accepted in an industrial building, because they are incompatible with fire-fighting and with planning.
- The authorized person (AP) or the registered structural engineer (RSE) may certify building-safety items imposed by the Buildings Department directly to the licensing authority. The Buildings Department audits those certificates, and negligence or misconduct can lead to action.
- Licensing authorities are advised to reject an application while unauthorised building works (UBW) remain. Works that are not exempt need prior approval and consent from the Building Authority (BA). See former PNAP 125, now practice note APP-47.
- When shops or offices change to restaurants, include the discharge capacity already used by the existing restaurants. Any surplus may already have been taken up.
- Check the floors for self-weight, finishes, partitions, plant, and the minimum imposed loads in the Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R). Appendix A asks for particulars of raised screeds, with a core test if the authorized person or registered structural engineer did not supervise them, and of brick walls, fish tanks, large kitchen equipment, walk-in freezers, and large central air-conditioning units, including sizes, densities, and catalogues.

**What to submit**
- Advise the client against a child care centre or kindergarten in a prohibited upper-floor location.
- For a restaurant: a structural justification pack; a discharge-value analysis covering every existing restaurant in the building; the direct certificate to the licensing authority; and confirmation that no unauthorised building works remain.

**Watch-outs**
- Licensing a child care centre or kindergarten above the height limit, or inside an industrial building.
- Certifying a restaurant without checking the discharge capacity of the whole building, including the existing restaurants.
- A load check that omits fish tanks, screeds, or plant.
- Certifying while unauthorised building works are still present.
