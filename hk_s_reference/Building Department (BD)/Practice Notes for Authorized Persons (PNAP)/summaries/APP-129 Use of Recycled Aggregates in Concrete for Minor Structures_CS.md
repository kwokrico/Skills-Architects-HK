# APP-129 Use of Recycled Aggregates in Concrete for Minor Structures
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2022 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 275; first issue February 2003)

> Scope note: Where concrete with recycled coarse aggregate of grade C20 to C35 may be used, and the mix and test limits in Appendices A and B. It is not a structural-concrete code. Design, construction, and quality control still follow the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013, and other concrete requirements still apply. Paving units follow the Highways Department guidance notes.

## Regulatory Overview
C20 concrete with 100% recycled coarse aggregate is limited to minor or non-structural works. C25 to C35 concrete with at most 20% recycled coarse aggregate may be used only for the listed works that do not have a major structural concern, and neither mix may be used in liquid-retaining, prestressed, transfer, or hanger structures.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where it may be used (paragraphs 5–8)
| Mix | Allowed use |
| --- | --- |
| C20, 100% recycled coarse aggregate | Minor structures or non-structural works, for example on-grade slabs, blinding, U-channels and stepped channels, bedding and haunching for pipes, footings for posts and fences, and mass concrete fill that does not sustain appreciable loading |
| C25 to C35, maximum 20% recycled aggregate | Landscape features such as planters and planter walls, fence walls, mass concrete walls and footings for landscape features; storm-water manholes and sand traps; footways, cycle tracks, reinforced concrete infill walls, and mass concrete under footings or rafts |
| Not allowed | Liquid-retaining structures, prestressed concrete, transfer structures, or hanger structures |
| Precast paving units | Highways Department requirements, including Guidance Notes on Design and Construction of Pavements with Paving Units |

**SD takeaway:** Do not put recycled-aggregate concrete in a water tank, a transfer structure, a hanger, or prestressed work.

### 2. C20 with 100% recycled coarse aggregate (Appendix A)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fine aggregate | Grading M of CS3:2013. Fine aggregate derived from recycled concrete shall not be used |
| Coarse grading | Single-sized 20 mm and 10 mm limits in CS3 |
| Mix per stated batch | Ordinary portland cement 100 kg; fine aggregate 180 kg; 20 mm coarse 180 kg; 10 mm coarse 90 kg |
| Slump | 75 mm when ready to compact. Recycled coarse aggregate thoroughly wetted first |
| Cube strength | Minimum 14 MPa at 7 days and 20 MPa at 28 days, tested to CS1:2010 |
| Trial | Three cubes, each not less than 26 MPa at 28 days, before the mix is used |
| Aggregate limits | Dry particle density at least 2000 kg/m3; water absorption at most 10%; wood and material less dense than water at most 0.5%; other foreign materials at most 1%; fines at most 4%; sand under 4 mm at most 5%; sulphate at most 1%; flakiness index 40%; 10% fines test 100 kN; chloride 0.05% by mass of chloride ion of combined aggregate under clause 5.2.3 of CS3 |

**SD takeaway:** A 100% recycled C20 mix uses the stated 100:180:180:90 proportions and a 26 MPa trial before it goes into minor work.

### 3. C25 to C35 with 20% recycled coarse aggregate (Appendix B and its Annex)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Cement | Ordinary portland cement to BS EN 197-1 only |
| Coarse aggregate | 80% natural mineral aggregate as defined in CS3. Recycled fraction from crushed old concrete, tested weekly against the same Table 1 limits as Appendix A |
| Fine aggregate | CS3. Recycled fine aggregate from old concrete shall not be used |
| Batching | Separate stockpiles or silos, and a separate compartment in the plant |
| Slump | Minimum 75 mm when ready to compact |
| Strength | Tested to CS1 for the specified grade under the 2013 concrete code |
| Plans | State the use on the plan that contains the general structural specifications and in the Part I structural calculations (PNAP ADM-8), state compliance with Appendix A and/or B, and show the scope on the structural plans |

Laboratory trials: three batches, two slump tests and six cubes each. If plant standard deviation exceeds 5.5 MPa (100 mm cubes) or 5 MPa (150 mm cubes), or there are no acceptable data, the average of 18 cubes at 28 days must exceed grade strength by at least 14 MPa (100 mm) or 12 MPa (150 mm), and each cube by at least 7 MPa or 6 MPa. If the plant standard deviation does not exceed those values, the margins are 10 MPa or 8 MPa on the average and 3 MPa or 2 MPa on each cube. Average of six slumps within 20 mm or 25% of the design slump, whichever is the greater. Plant trials: one batch of at least 60% of mixer capacity on each of three days; samples at about 1/6, 1/2, and 5/6 of discharge. Average of nine slumps within 20 mm or 25%; range of three slumps in a batch not over 20% of their average; average of 18 cubes exceeds grade by at least 12 MPa (100 mm) or 10 MPa (150 mm), each cube by at least 5 MPa or 4 MPa; range of six cubes from a batch not over 20% of their average.

**SD takeaway:** Keep recycled coarse aggregate at 20% or less for C25 to C35, store it apart from natural aggregate, and pass the laboratory and plant trial margins before use.
