# ADV-34 Building Information Modelling (BIM)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2023 | Buildings Department (first issue September 2016)

> Scope note: How to use a building information model when preparing statutory plans, so that the Buildings Department can check the submission. Approval is given on the prescribed plans. The note does not require BIM, and it does not make the model the approved document.

## Regulatory Overview
The Buildings Department encourages authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered geotechnical engineers to use BIM for new buildings and for alteration and addition works. When a model is used, the information in the BIM files must be identical to the information on the prescribed plans, and post-manual editing of drawings generated from the model should be minimised.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which file is approved (paragraphs 6–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| What is approved | Plans in paper or in two-dimensional portable document format. The Department processes approval under the Buildings Ordinance on that information |
| What the model is for | Checking the plans against the Buildings Ordinance |
| If they differ | The plans prevail |
| Drawing edits | Keep manual edits of model-generated drawings to a minimum |

**SD takeaway:** The approved document is the prescribed plan; if the model and the sheet disagree, the sheet governs, so generate the sheet from the model and do not redraw it by hand.

### 2. Guidelines by plan type (paragraphs 3–5)
| Submission | Follow |
| --- | --- |
| General building plans | PNAP ADM-19, the “Guidelines for using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plans Submission”, and the sample drawings and BIM models on the Buildings Department website |
| Other statutory plans | The “Guidelines for using Building Information Modelling in Statutory Plan Submissions (other than General Building Plan)”, and the sample drawings, BIM objects, models, and templates issued jointly by the Construction Industry Council and the Buildings Department |
| Council standards | The user guides and BIM standards published by the Construction Industry Council, at the website named in the note |

**SD takeaway:** A general building plan model follows the general-building-plan guideline and ADM-19; a foundation, drainage, or superstructure model follows the separate statutory-plan guideline.

### 3. What the model should show (Appendix A)
| Plans | Information to illustrate | Optional real-time simulation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General building plans | Irregular or twisted form; projections; site profile and street levels against the building; area demarcation and calculations; means of escape and compartmentation; compatibility between plan types; spatial arrangement; existing building against proposed alteration and addition | Sequence and phasing of demolition, foundation, excavation and lateral support, site formation, substructure, and superstructure, as applicable, and of alteration and addition works |
| Drainage | Complex systems and connections; underground drainage against foundations or site formation; compatibility between plan types | The same phasing |
| Superstructure | Material and grade; fire resistance rating and concrete cover; complex steelwork and connections; transfer structures and load path; basement structures supporting adjoining ground or geotechnical features; façade, glass wall, curtain wall, and cladding assembly and connections; compatibility between plan types; existing structure against alteration and addition; working space, temporary supports, and strengthening | The same phasing |
| Foundation | Type and arrangement, top level and toe level, bearing-stratum level, and allowable bearing capacity; relationship to substructures, excavation and lateral support, geological profiles, adjoining foundations, geotechnical features, and sensitive structures | Phasing, including top-down construction |
| Excavation and lateral support, and site formation | Site profiles, geological profiles, the proposed works, adjoining foundations, geotechnical features, and sensitive structures | Phasing of the stages |
| Demolition | A complicated sequence, and the final stage of a partially demolished structure | Sequence, method, and temporary precautionary measures |
| Video | A supplementary file in Windows Media Video (wmv) or Audio Video Interleave (avi), playable in Windows Media Player 11 or above | |

**SD takeaway:** Use the model to show area sums, escape and compartments, and the load path, and add a phasing video only as a supplement to the plans.
