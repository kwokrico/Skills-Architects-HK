# APP-120

**Official title:** Concrete Batching Plant

**In plain words:** A concrete batching plant (CBP) that is fixed to the ground, connected to a utility supply, and has a control room is a building under the Buildings Ordinance (BO) and needs approval and consent before it is erected. The total floor area of the control rooms counts toward the gross floor area (GFA) limit for the fast track, the plant must also comply with the town plan and the land use, and this note does not set a site coverage (SC) concession.

**Numbers that matter**
- The fast track, with approval and consent handled together, is for the common type in Appendix A where there are not more than 4 production lines and the total GFA of all control rooms is not more than 100 m².
- A possible later self-certification route, for a silo under 50 tonnes and an overall height under 15 m, is only a feasibility statement in this note. It is not a rule you can use.
- Appendix C gives 30-day structural processing when an already approved plant is relocated, if the registered structural engineer (RSE) certifies that the frame and foundations are identical and the non-referral conditions are met.
- New plants and associated structures erected after 31 March 2002 without approval and consent are enforced against.
- A plant existing before 1 May 2002 draws no Building Authority (BA) action if an RSE structural appraisal was made by 30 October 2002 and the plant is properly maintained. Other departments may still enforce.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued March 2002.
- File reference: GP/BORD/73 (II).

**What to submit**
- For a new plant, the authorized person (AP) and RSE submit building plans under the BO before erection. An occupation permit (OP) or temporary occupation permit (TOP) is required on completion.
- For relocation under Appendix C, submit the RSE certifications described above.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not treat a fixed, utility-connected plant with a control room as mere machinery in order to stay outside BO control.
- An unapproved plant built after the cutoff is an unauthorized building work.
- Lands and lease controls, and other departments’ requirements, still apply even if the BA has accepted an appraisal of an old plant.
- Do not use the under-50-tonne and under-15 m idea as if self-certification were already in force.
