# APP-25 Geotechnical Assessment at General Building Plan Stage
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2015 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 78; first issue April 1981)

> Scope note: When Regulation 8(1)(ba) of the Building (Administration) Regulations requires a geotechnical assessment with the general building plans. It is an early feasibility check, not the site-formation design.

## Regulatory Overview
A general building plan that meets the criteria in paragraph 2 must be accompanied by a geotechnical assessment, so that fundamental geotechnical constraints are identified before the building form is fixed. A submission that meets those criteria and has no assessment may be rejected under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(a).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When an assessment is required (paragraph 2)
| Case | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| New building, or alteration and addition that results in a new building | Maximum gradient across the site, or across any 50 m strip on a large site, greater than 15° |
| Slope | A slope steeper than 30° and higher than 7.5 m, including any retaining wall at its toe or crest, on the site or within 7.5 m of the site |
| Excavation | A face longer than 10 m, and either deeper than 7.5 m at any point at any stage, or below a plane projecting down at 45° from the toe of any retaining wall, or below Hong Kong Principal Datum minus 1 m on reclaimed coastal land |
| Retaining wall | Retained height greater than 6 m on the site, or a 6 m or longer portion of such a wall within 6 m of the site |
| Special geology | Previous landslides, debris flows, threatening boulders, or potential rockfalls |
| Other alteration and addition | Works that may significantly affect existing slopes, retaining walls, or adjacent foundations, or excavation under paragraph 2(a)(iii) |

**SD takeaway:** If the site is steeper than 15°, has a slope over 30° and 7.5 m within 7.5 m, or needs an excavation deeper than 7.5 m on a face longer than 10 m, submit a geotechnical assessment with the first general building plans.

### 2. What the assessment contains (paragraph 3)
| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | Outline of topography, geology, and presumed groundwater |
| Effect | How existing slopes, retaining walls, and adjacent foundations will affect, or be affected by, the works |
| Feasibility | Construction methods and sequence |
| Drawings | Schematic plans and sections of site formation and foundations |
| Ground investigation | A plan of scope and extent, and the level of independent site supervision by reference to section 15.3 of Geoguide 2 |
| Existing features | Intended investigation of the stability of existing slopes and retaining walls, and any preventive or remedial works |

**SD takeaway:** The general building plans must show a buildable formation and foundation scheme, not only a building footprint, wherever paragraph 2 applies.
