# APP-60

**Official title:** Specified Forms

**In plain words:** Where the Buildings Ordinance or its regulations require a specified form, use the Appendix A form unaltered, because a wrong or altered form can invalidate the application, notice or certificate. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Forms are specified under Buildings Ordinance section 22(4).
- Appendix A maps Forms BA1 to BA26*, MW01 to MW32, and SC01 to SC03 to the Electronic Forms Submission System (EFSS) or e-Counter, the minor-works system RMWMS, and the Electronic Submission Hub (ESH). Availability is shown as a tick matrix.
- ESH stages: Stage 1 on 30 June 2022, Stage 2 on 31 March 2023, Stage 3 on 30 June 2024.
- RMWMS launched on 29 May 2026 at https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk.
- e-Counter address: receipt@bd.gov.hk.
- Forms that matter to design and concessions include BA5 (approval of plans), BA16 (modification or exemption under section 42), BA8 and BA8A (consent), and BA12 and BA13 (occupation permit or temporary occupation permit).
- BA8 is accepted through EFSS only within office hours, as noted in the source. The same applies to BA15.

**What to submit**
- Match the form number to the Ordinance or regulation section, and use the online channel shown for that form in Appendix A.
- Prefer a computer-filled hard copy so the QR code or barcode stores the data. Take the latest form from the Buildings Department website. Hard copies are also at the ground-floor counters of Buildings Department headquarters.
- Mandatory Building Inspection and Window Inspection schemes: see PNBI-3. Electronic plan format: PNAP ADM-17.

**Watch-outs**
- An altered or obsolete form makes the submission invalid.
- Do not use the wrong channel. For example, do not send a minor-works form only through ESH when RMWMS or EFSS is required.
- Incomplete appointment forms (BA4 and BA10) or completion certificates (BA12 to BA14C) delay consent or the occupation permit.
