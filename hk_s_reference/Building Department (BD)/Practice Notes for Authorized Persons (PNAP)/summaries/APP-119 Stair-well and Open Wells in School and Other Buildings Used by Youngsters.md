# APP-119

**Official title:** Stair-well and Open Wells in School and Other Buildings Used by Youngsters

**In plain words:** After a fatal fall through a school stair-well, this note asks designers to avoid open wells and stair-wells in new schools where that is practicable, and to judge the hazard, including misuse, wherever children or youngsters use the building. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Where a hazard remains, add further protection such as safety nets or a higher railing.
- Handrails follow paragraph 17.6 of the Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996, and paragraph 8 of the Third Schedule to the Building (Planning) Regulations (B(P)R).
- Balustrades must be at least 1,100 mm high under Building (Construction) Regulation (B(C)R) 8.
- Provide a protective barrier where the difference in level is more than 600 mm.
- Re-issued August 2009. First issued May 2001.
- File reference: BD GP/LEG/26 (III).

**What to submit**
- On the general building plans (GBP), show the protective barriers and handrails for open stair-wells and open wells.

**Watch-outs**
- An open stair-well in a school, without extra child-safety measures, conflicts with the Coroner’s recommendation and with the authorized person’s (AP) duty of care.
- A barrier lower than 1.1 m, or an unprotected drop of more than 600 mm, does not comply.
