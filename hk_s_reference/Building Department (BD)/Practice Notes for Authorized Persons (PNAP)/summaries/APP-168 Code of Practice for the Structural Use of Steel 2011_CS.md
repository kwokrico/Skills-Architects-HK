# APP-168 Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Amendments to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011. Amendments of November 2016, May 2021, and March 2023 are already in the Code (2023 Edition), promulgated in March 2023. Appendix A (April 2026) amends that edition with immediate effect. It is not a substitute for the steel code.

## Regulatory Overview
Composite-column design, building acceleration, welding of different grades, and footbridge vibration are among the April 2026 changes. The design strengths, grades, and geometric limits sit in the amended clauses and tables of the 2023 Edition, not as standalone numbers in the amendment list.
