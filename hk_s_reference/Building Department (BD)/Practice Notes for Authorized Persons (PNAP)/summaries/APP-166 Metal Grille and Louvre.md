# APP-166

**Official title:** Metal Grille and Louvre

**In plain words:** Structural plans are required for a metal grille or louvre when it is high external cladding, a large or highly loaded external wall, or a protective barrier, while interior facing or decoration on interior structure needs no structural submission and concrete or glass follows the relevant code of practice. This note sets no gross floor area concession.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue December 2021. This revision January 2026. Paragraphs 4 and 5 were added. Paragraph 7 was amended.
- Type 1, external cladding: a structural submission is required if any part is installed more than 6 m above the adjoining ground or floor (APP-16 paragraph 4). Design reference: ADV-33 Appendices B6 and B9.
- Type 2, wholly or partly an external wall: a submission is required if the structural span is more than 6 m, or if the design wind pressure is 2.86 kPa or more and the structural opening area is more than 6 m² or the least dimension is more than 1.8 m (APP-37 paragraphs 4 to 7). Design reference: ADV-33 Appendices B7 and B10.
- Type 3, protective barrier: a structural submission is always required, except where the grille or louvre is also Type 2 and meets the Type 2 exemption. Design reference: APP-110 paragraph 5.
- Designated exempted works: Building (Minor Works) Regulation Schedule 2 Part 2 item 27, a metal window security grille or metal wind guard for an external wall opening that meets that description.
- There is no specific minor-works item for a grille or louvre. It may fall under minor works item 3.31 (cladding), 2.8 or 3.6 (window or window wall), or 1.6 (protective barrier). See APP-147 and Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation.

**What to submit**
- Where a submission is required, the structural plans must show the framing and elements, anchors and supports, design loads, standards and codes, material specifications, workmanship for connections and protection, and calculations. The calculations cover the parent structure, system stability, element design, and deflection where that applies.
- As a shorter route, the registered structural engineer (RSE) may put typical details, layout, and general notes in the superstructure plans instead of separate grille or louvre structural plans. Location, layout, and elevation still go on the general building plans (GBP). The authorized person (AP) and the RSE must keep them compatible with the approved GBP and with the Buildings Ordinance and regulations. Use the ADV-33 Appendix B7 checklist and Appendix B10 samples.
- Where no submission is required, the AP and the RSE remain responsible for safe design, fabrication, and installation.

**Watch-outs**
- Omitting a structural submission for Type 1 above 6 m, for a Type 2 span or wind-and-opening trigger, or for a Type 3 barrier.
- Using the superstructure-note route while the GBP location and elevation do not match.
- Classing a protective-barrier louvre as decoration to avoid a submission.
