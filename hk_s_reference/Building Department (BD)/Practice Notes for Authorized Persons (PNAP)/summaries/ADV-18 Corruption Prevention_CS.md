# ADV-18 Corruption Prevention
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue July 1999 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Reminds authorized persons and registered structural engineers to prohibit bribes in site supervision and to report offers to the ICAC. It does not set design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
The Buildings Department's concern is that corruption in construction may lead to works that do not comply with the Buildings Ordinance, under which authorized persons and registered structural engineers must give periodic supervision and make necessary inspections so that works follow the Ordinance and the approved plans. They should expressly prohibit site staff from soliciting or accepting advantages from contractors and suppliers, or offering advantages, and should report a bribe at once.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Reporting and what counts as an advantage (paragraphs 2–4 and Appendix I)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Report a bribe | Immediately to the ICAC, 2526 6366, 24 hours |
| Not only money | A loan, gift, favour, offer of employment, or any other advantage. The definition is in the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance, Cap. 201 |
| Appendix I extract | Gift, loan, fee, reward, or commission; office, employment, or contract; payment or release of a liability; any service or favour other than entertainment, including protection from a penalty or proceedings; exercise or forbearance of a right, power, or duty; and any offer or promise of those |
| Code of conduct | Advisable to issue a company code and staff instructions. The ICAC Advisory Services Group gives free confidential advice on 2526 6363 |

**SD takeaway:** Instruct site staff, before works start, that any advantage from a contractor or supplier is refused and reported to the ICAC on 2526 6366.
