# APP-118

**Official title:** Testing of Building Materials

**In plain words:** The Building Authority (BA) does not certify building materials, so the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), and registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) rely on tests from independent accredited laboratories and certify compliance themselves. This note does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Tests should normally be done by a Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) laboratory, or a laboratory accredited by a mutual-recognition partner, and only within that laboratory’s scope. The usual tests are concrete cubes and cores, steel reinforcement, splices and couplers, curtain-wall systems, and fire-rated products, materials, and components.
- A fire-rated product must meet the performance in Building (Construction) Regulation (B(C)R) 90 through the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings (FS Code) 2011. Tests and assessments follow the FS Code. Reports follow clauses E16.1 and E16.2. A report whose validity has expired is not accepted. Certification bodies are those accredited by the Hong Kong Certification Body Accreditation Scheme (HKCAS) or by a multilateral partner.
- Compliance of materials and products is certified on completion under PNAP APP-13.
- Previously PNAP 251. First issued October 2001. This revision May 2012.
- File references: BD GR/BM/5(2)(II) and GR/1-10/461.

**What to submit**
- Certify material and product compliance on completion, as PNAP APP-13 requires.
- The AP, RSE, RGE, and registered contractor (RC) supervise selection and use, and certify compliance with the Buildings Ordinance (BO).

**Watch-outs**
- There is no BA “approval” of a material to rely on. The BA has no such power.
- A non-accredited laboratory, or a test outside the laboratory’s scope, is not acceptable.
- An expired fire test or assessment report is not acceptable.
