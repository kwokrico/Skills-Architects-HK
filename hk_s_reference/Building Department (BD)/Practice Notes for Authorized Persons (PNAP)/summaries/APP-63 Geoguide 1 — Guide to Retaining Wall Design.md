# APP-63

**Official title:** Geoguide 1 — Guide to Retaining Wall Design

**In plain words:** A permanent retaining wall may be designed entirely to either edition of Geoguide 1, the submission must say which edition was used, and this is the design basis APP-54 relies on. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- The 1st edition (1982) uses limit-state design with global factors of safety.
- The 2nd edition (1993, and kept up to date) uses limit-state design with partial factors. It gives detailed guidance on geotechnical parameters and groundwater pressure, and on how design and construction depend on each other. The 2nd edition is strongly recommended.
- A design that follows one edition in full is acceptable. A mixture of the 1st and 2nd editions is not acceptable.

**What to submit**
- State which edition of Geoguide 1 the design uses. The updated 2nd edition is on the Civil Engineering and Development Department website.
- The note accepts Geoguide 1 for permanent retaining walls, including reinforced-concrete walls, gravity, crib, gabion and mass-concrete walls, and cantilevered retaining walls.

**Watch-outs**
- A design that mixes the 1st and 2nd editions is not acceptable.
- Do not omit the statement of which edition was used.
