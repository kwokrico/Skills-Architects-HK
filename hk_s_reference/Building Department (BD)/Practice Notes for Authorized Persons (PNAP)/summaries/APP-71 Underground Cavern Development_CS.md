# APP-71 Underground Cavern Development
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued April 1995 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Points to the three guidance documents for underground caverns. It does not itself set cavern dimensions or uses. The 1988 study (SPUN) treated caverns as a viable alternative to above-ground construction for uses including container freight, oil and gas storage, sewage treatment, refuse transfer, warehousing, and commercial, government, institution, and community space.

## Regulatory Overview
Initial guidance on cavern construction draws on local deep basements, tunnels, and caverns, and on that study. Three published documents are the references for engineering, planning, and fire safety.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Documents to design to (paragraph 2)
| Document | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Geoguide 4, "Guide to Cavern Engineering", March 1992 | Recommended standard of good practice for the civil engineering aspects of rock caverns in Hong Kong |
| Chapter 11 of the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines | Planning standards and guidelines for underground rock cavern development |
| "Guide to Fire Safety Design for Caverns 1994" | Fire safety in underground caverns |

**SD takeaway:** Design a rock cavern to Geoguide 4, Chapter 11 of the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines, and the 1994 fire-safety guide together.
