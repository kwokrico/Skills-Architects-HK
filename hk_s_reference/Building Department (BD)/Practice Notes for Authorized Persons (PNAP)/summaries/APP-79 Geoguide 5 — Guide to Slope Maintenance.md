# APP-79

**Official title:** Geoguide 5 — Guide to Slope Maintenance

**In plain words:** Use Geoguide 5 when you specify maintenance of man-made slopes, retaining walls, and man-made features on natural hillsides. This note does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but maintenance access, drainage, and a Maintenance Manual still have to be designed in.

**Numbers that matter**
- Formerly practice note (PNAP) 189. First issued January 1996. Last revision April 1999. This revision September 2020.
- Geoguide 5 must be followed when maintenance is specified in a site formation submission under Building (Administration) Regulations regulation 8(1)(bb)(E).
- The Maintenance Manual follows Geoguide 5 Chapter 2. The site formation submission must require it at Form BA 14 stage.
- Some routine slope and retaining-wall maintenance is minor works under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. See APP-147.
- Geoguide 5 is on www.cedd.gov.hk. A shorter layman’s guide is also available.

**What to submit**
- State in the site formation submission that a Maintenance Manual will be submitted.
- Submit the Maintenance Manual in duplicate with Form BA 14.
- After the Building Authority (BA) acknowledges it, one copy is returned to the Authorized Person (AP) or Registered Structural Engineer (RSE) to pass to the owners or the people who will maintain the slope.

**Watch-outs**
- Leaving the Manual out of the site formation submission, or failing to submit it with Form BA 14, breaks the requirement.
- Failing to pass the Manual to the owners leaves ongoing slope-failure liability.
- Maintenance specified below Geoguide 5 is not acceptable.
- Routine work that qualifies as minor works should use that path where it applies.
