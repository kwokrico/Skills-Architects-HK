# APP-9 Country Parks Ordinance Cap 208
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This update January 1990 | Buildings Department (first issued March 1978; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(d) may be used to reject a proposal inside a designated or proposed country park or special area until the Country Parks Authority has approved it. It does not map the parks; those plans are bought from the Agriculture and Fisheries Department.

## Regulatory Overview
After a notice under section 9(1) of the Country Parks Ordinance, Cap. 208, section 10(1) provides that no new development shall be carried out within the area of the proposed country park shown in the draft map without the prior approval of the Authority. The Building Authority may reject such a proposal under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(d) until that approval is obtained, and may likewise reject it if conditions attached to the approval are not complied with.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Approval before a building submission (paragraphs 2–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Land in a designated or proposed country park or special area to which section 10(1) applies |
| Sequence | Consult the Country Parks Authority as soon as possible on an application for development of such a site |
| Plans | Copies of plans showing locations and boundaries may be purchased from the Agriculture and Fisheries Department |

**SD takeaway:** Obtain Country Parks Authority approval, including any conditions, before relying on a building-plan approval for a site inside a designated or proposed country park or special area.
