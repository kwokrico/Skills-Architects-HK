# APP-53

**Official title:** Building (Construction) Regulation — Accepted Standards / Technical Criteria

**In plain words:** The Building Authority (BA) treats these standards as meeting the Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R), and they are the technical baseline for structural, fire and foundation design. This note does not itself change gross floor area or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Editions below include amendments effective as at December 2023. They may not be the latest edition of each standard.
- Concrete: Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition).
- Fire safety: Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 (June 2023 Edition).
- Foundations: Code of Practice for Foundations 2017.
- Precast concrete: Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016.
- Steel: Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011 (2023 Edition), plus explanatory materials.
- Wind: Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, plus explanatory notes.
- Dead and imposed loads: Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011 (2021 Edition).
- Glass: Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Glass 2018.
- Aluminium: BS 8118 Part 1:1991. BS EN 1999 has replaced it, but BS 8118 is still accepted locally. From 1 January 2017, use a partial load factor of 1.4 for wind instead of the factor 1.2 in Table 3.1. The exception is an amendment submission already approved at 1.2.
- Stainless steel: Steel Construction Institute publication P291.
- Timber: BS 5268 Parts 2, 3 and 4, in the years stated in the accepted list.
- Glassfibre-reinforced concrete (GRC): Practical Design Guide by the GRC Association Technical Committee.
- Elastomeric and mechanical bearings: Civil Engineering and Development Department (CEDD) General Specification for Civil Engineering Works 2020.
- Masonry, earthworks and stone cladding: BS 5628 Part 1:2005; BS 6031:2009; BS 8298 Parts 1 to 4:2010.
- Materials, workmanship and testing are in the appendices, covering concrete, steel, aluminium, timber, glass, stainless steel, masonry, GRC, bearings, sealants, gypsum, natural stone, fire testing and anchors (BS 5080). Use the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS) BS EN, ISO and ASTM standards listed in Appendix A of the source note.

**What to submit**
- Plans that use other versions, standards or criteria may still be approved if the performance requirements are met. To help processing, set out the full background and show they suit local conditions.
- Approach the Buildings Department (BD) early if you want agreement in principle to an alternative standard or criterion.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not mix standards without BA agreement.
- On new aluminium designs after 1 January 2017, do not keep the BS 8118 wind partial factor of 1.2. Use 1.4.
- Do not assume the latest overseas edition is accepted. Check the BA list of accepted editions, as at December 2023.
