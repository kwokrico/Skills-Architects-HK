# APP-88

**Official title:** Code of Practice on Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes

**In plain words:** This note points an Authorized Person (AP), a Registered Structural Engineer (RSE), and registered contractors to the government code on inspecting and maintaining buried water-carrying services that can affect slopes, including orders under Buildings Ordinance section 27C. It does not change gross floor area or site coverage, and it complements practice note APP-76 on keeping those services out of slopes.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issued November 1996. Re-issued August 2009. Read it with practice note (PNAP) 168.
- Under section 27C, the Building Authority (BA) may order an owner to investigate and repair buried water-carrying services in or near a slope.
- The code is sold at the Government Publications Centre. A similar note went to registered contractors.

**What to submit**
- Use the code when designing or maintaining buried services near slopes, and when responding to a section 27C order.
- Coordinate with the slope-record duties in PNAP 168.

**Watch-outs**
- Ignoring the code when you are appointed for services near a slope, or for section 27C work, is a breach of this guidance.
- Design measures under APP-76 and later inspection are both needed. Leakage remains critical to the slope.
- PNAP 168 record and maintenance handover still applies.
