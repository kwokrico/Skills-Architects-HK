# ADM-20

**Official title:** Central Data Bank

**In plain words:** The Central Data Bank is a website list of building materials, components, and construction systems that some government departments, mainly the Buildings Department, have accepted before. It is historical information, not a current product approval, and it does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). Quoting a listing can save you from attaching the same test report again.

**Numbers that matter**
- A Buildings Department listing is generally based on tests or assessments by an accredited laboratory, or a certificate of conformity from an accredited certification body. At the time of a project submission, the authorized person (AP), registered structural engineer (RSE), or registered geotechnical engineer (RGE) still assesses and certifies the product against the standards then in force, under APP-118 and APP-165.
- A structural product is listed if it was used in a particular completed project that the Department accepted and the AP, RSE, and registered contractor certified, or if it is currently on the Department’s recognised pile-types list.
- Non-structural products come mainly from the Schedule of Building Materials and Products under APP-13. The Department audits those schedules at random and transfers relevant entries to the Bank.
- Design details, test and assessment reports, and certificates of conformity are not shown, for copyright reasons. The Bank shows reference data only. Appendix A is a sample for a fire door: fire resistance rating, laboratory, report number, validity, and conformity scheme.
- Suppliers cannot apply directly for a listing.
- Quote the Bank reference in the certification, or in the “Remarks/Comments” column of the APP-13 schedule, so you do not resubmit identical hard copies of a listed report or certificate. Any extra test that is not the listed one must still be submitted.
- The AP, RSE, and RGE remain fully responsible for the application and for performance. They must check that the latest standards and any product conformity certification scheme are met.

**What to submit**
- The Bank reference number on the APP-13 schedule when the reports are identical to the listed entries. Submit any extra or non-identical report. Appendix B shows a sample schedule format for fire-resisting products, covering both the test path and the certificate-of-conformity path.

**Watch-outs**
- A listing is not a current approval and not a design waiver. Responsibility stays with the AP, RSE, and RGE.
- Quoting the Bank while using an expired, superseded, or non-identical report or certificate, without submitting the difference, is not acceptable.
- Using a listing without checking the latest standard or conformity scheme leaves the certification exposed.
