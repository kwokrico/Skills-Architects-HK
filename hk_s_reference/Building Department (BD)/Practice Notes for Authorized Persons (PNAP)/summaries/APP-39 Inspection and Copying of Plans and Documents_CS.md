# APP-39 Inspection and Copying of Plans and Documents
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision July 2016 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 110; first issue February 1985)

> Scope note: When the public may inspect or copy building records under Buildings Ordinance section 36G, and the copyright consequence of changing the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer. Fees are in Regulation 42 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. It does not set design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
Plans and documents submitted for the purposes of the Buildings Ordinance are public records, but under the obligation of confidence they are not open for inspection or copying until the building or works are completed, and only if the copyright owner has not specifically denied access. The records that may then be inspected or copied are the latest approved plans, structural calculations, and related documents of completed projects, and, for completed minor works under the simplified requirements, the latest submitted plans and details of Class I and Class II minor works and the latest submitted plans or description, photos, and related documents of Class III minor works.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Purpose, timing, and a change of appointment (paragraphs 2–5 and 15)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Permitted purposes | Construction of a building or carrying out of building works, including minor works, or street works; whether those works comply with the Ordinance or any other enactment; or another matter the Building Authority considers appropriate in the public interest. The applicant declares which applies |
| Public-interest purpose | Considered only with relevant special circumstances, or with an authorization from the copyright owner stating that he is the owner |
| Before completion | Inspection and copies are not permitted |
| New appointee using the resigned appointee's plans | The new appointee must have the copyright owner's consent to use the approved plans, or the submitted minor-works plans and details, to build or to amend. Otherwise copyright may be infringed |
| Electronic records at the Building Information Centre | Pledged within 3 working days of an application on Form BIC1. One-stop copies without prior inspection, on Form BIC-3, are answered in writing within 10 days. Hard-copy delivery is free to a Hong Kong address except a post office box, the Closed Area, Lantau Island, and the Outlying Islands |

**SD takeaway:** Do not expect to inspect another project's approved plans until that building is complete, and obtain the copyright owner's consent before a new authorized person amends those plans.
