# APP-141

**Official title:** Division of Responsibilities between Authorized Person, Registered Structural Engineer and Registered Geotechnical Engineer

**In plain words:** This note splits geotechnical duties between the authorized person, the registered structural engineer, and the registered geotechnical engineer after the registered geotechnical engineer appointment rule started. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Registered geotechnical engineer appointment from 31 December 2005 under Buildings Ordinance section 4(1)(c). Formerly PNAP 294. This revision February 2009.
- Cross-references the geotechnical series APP/PNAP 50, 75, 77, 83, 85, 132, 148, 161, 200, 283, and 284. Sanctions under Buildings Ordinance sections 40 and 7.
- Executive role (prepare and sign geotechnical plans and reports; certify that geotechnical works are complete to the approved plans and are geotechnically safe): Task 2A ground investigation in Scheduled Areas; Task 2B ground investigation outside Scheduled Areas; Task 4 site formation; Task 7A groundwater drainage in Scheduled Area 1; Task 7B water supply and wells; Task 10 remedial works to dangerous hillsides; Task 11 long-term monitoring after the occupation permit.
- Advisory role (sign geotechnical reports and documents; the authorized person or registered structural engineer signs non-geotechnical plans): Task 1 demolition affecting slopes or retaining walls; Task 3 geotechnical assessment for general building plans; Task 5A foundations in Scheduled Areas 1, 2, and 4 and the Northshore Lantau Designated Area; Task 5B foundations affecting slopes or retaining walls; Task 6 excavation and lateral support; Task 8 geotechnical design parameters for the superstructure; Task 12 hoardings, covered walkways, and gantries affecting nearby slopes or retaining walls.
- Appendix B sign-off examples: Demolition — authorized person and registered structural engineer sign plans, registered geotechnical engineer signs geotechnical reports and geotechnical supervision, authorized person and registered structural engineer certify. Site formation — authorized person signs the general layout, registered geotechnical engineer signs site formation (non-structural), registered structural engineer signs structural details, and authorized person plus registered geotechnical engineer certify (and the registered structural engineer if the work is structural). Foundations in geotechnically sensitive areas — registered structural engineer signs plans, registered geotechnical engineer signs geotechnical reports and the performance review, supervision is shared. Excavation and lateral support — registered structural engineer signs plans that incorporate the registered geotechnical engineer’s recommendations, and the registered geotechnical engineer signs the geotechnical reports.
- Prescribed plans and documents follow Building (Administration) Regulation 12.

**What to submit**
- Prescribed plans and documents under Building (Administration) Regulation 12. Resolve differences between the registered structural engineer and the registered geotechnical engineer before submission to the Buildings Department.
- Performance reviews and monitoring reports for the specified works, prepared and signed by the registered geotechnical engineer.

**Watch-outs**
- It is wrong to treat the authorized person as still statutorily responsible for the geotechnical elements after 31 December 2005.
- Do not submit plans with unresolved conflicts between the registered structural engineer and the registered geotechnical engineer, or geotechnical documents missing the registered geotechnical engineer’s signature.
- Prosecution and disciplinary action can reach the registered geotechnical engineer in the same way as the authorized person and the registered structural engineer.
