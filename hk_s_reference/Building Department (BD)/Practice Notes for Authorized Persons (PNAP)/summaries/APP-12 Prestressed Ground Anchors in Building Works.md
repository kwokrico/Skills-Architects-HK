# APP-12

**Official title:** Prestressed Ground Anchors in Building Works

**In plain words:** The Buildings Department treats permanent prestressed ground anchors that need long-term monitoring as short-lived temporary works, and will normally refuse them as part of a permanent building. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC).

**Numbers that matter**
- Exceptional acceptance uses GEOSPEC 1 and the Geotechnical Engineering Office Prior Approval System for Permanent Prestressed Ground Anchors (PASA), for approved anchor systems and types.
- An anchor that leaves the site and enters a public or private street, government land, or adjoining private land needs exceptional approval. Government land needs early permission from the Lands Department. For adjoining private land, Form BA5 for the site formation should include the adjoining lot number, as evidence of acquiescence.
- Grease monitoring follows GEOSPEC 1 Corrigenda 1/98 and 2/98: hexane extraction, a blank determination, and ion contents in parts per million by mass of the final aqueous extract over the mass of deionized water.

**What to submit**
- At approval, for PASA anchors: plans of location, inclination, and length; anchor class, design working load, and free length, with geotechnical and design calculations; a schedule of suitability tests and extended acceptance tests; the allowance for compression movement; anchor-pad details; a long-term monitoring schedule, access, and a maintenance-party agreement.
- Plans and sections showing boundaries and geotechnical information, with your own design calculations, and utility and structure plans showing that nothing will be damaged.
- Before consent: the names of the approved system and type; specifications of replaceable materials; the monitoring schedule and interpretation guidelines; the name and experience of the nominated anchor contractor. Conditions on workmanship and supervision may be imposed under Buildings Ordinance section 17(1).
- Keep the certified prior-approval documents on site. After installation, submit as-built drawings of the full length, the system and type, loads, as-built lengths, strata, grout and pressure, dates, stressing and test records, monitoring results, and the future monitoring schedule and guidelines.

**Watch-outs**
- Putting forward permanent monitored anchors as a routine solution is likely to be refused.
- A missing maintenance-party undertaking, or an anchor outside the lot without Lands Department or neighbour consent, is a refusal risk.
- PASA acceptance does not remove the authorized person’s or registered structural engineer’s duties under the Buildings Ordinance.
