# APP-85

**Official title:** Application of the Revised Fire Safety Codes

**In plain words:** This note explains how to apply the revised fire codes — Means of Access 1995, Fire Resisting Construction 1996, and Means of Escape 1996 — where the ordinance and regulations are silent. It does not change gross floor area or site coverage, but the way you count people can still change exit sizes.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issued October 1996. Re-issued August 2009.
- For plans approved before a revised code took effect, the Building Authority (BA) generally does not object to consent if the consent application is within 2 years of the approval date and is otherwise in order.
- Fire engineering is recognised in all three codes as an alternative. The administrative practice note came later (see APP-87).

**What to submit**
- For alteration and addition, show compliance for the areas affected, including shared exits. Unaffected parts need not meet the revised codes.
- For a restaurant licence, apply the revised codes only to the area in the new licence application.
- If you use a head count, show that the comparison premises match in location, management, patronage, class, type, and age. For a new building, a prescriptive population may leave more room for later alteration.
- Fire engineering submissions follow APP-87.

**Watch-outs**
- Putting a whole existing building onto the new codes for a small alteration over-scopes the work. Leaving out shared exits under-scopes it.
- Consent more than 2 years after approval needs a fresh check against the revised codes.
- A weak comparison will not support a head-count population.
- Fire engineering is not accepted until the administrative process is followed.
