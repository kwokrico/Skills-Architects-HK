# APP-155

**Official title:** Signboard Validation Scheme

**In plain words:** This note allows validation of certain unauthorised signboards that were already up before the signboard control system started, on a five-year cycle. It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage.

**Numbers that matter**
- Validation of existing unauthorised signboards under the Signboard Control System from 2 September 2013. Revision December 2020. Re-validation every 5 years. Submissions are free. There is no departmental referral.
- Eligible signboards were erected before 2 September 2013 and match Building (Minor Works) Regulation Schedule 3 Part 3, or the corresponding Schedule 1 minor works items: projecting, wall or shopfront, roof, on-grade, spread footing, and balcony or canopy soffit.
- Projecting sign, Class I: display area more than 10 m² and up to 20 m², projection 4.2 m or less, thickness 600 mm or less. Class II: display area 10 m² or less, projection 1 m or less, thickness 300 mm or less. Class III: display area 1 m² or less, any part 6 m or less from the ground. No stone. No extra load on a cantilevered slab. No alteration of a structural element.
- Wall sign: LED display area more than 5 m² up to 20 m², or up to 5 m², by class. Non-LED display area more than 10 m² up to 40 m², up to 10 m², or up to 5 m², by class. Stone is restricted if the sign is more than 6 m from the ground.
- Roof sign: display area 20 m² or less, thickness 600 mm or less, 6 m or less above the roof, and not projecting beyond the external wall.
- On-grade sign: display area up to 20 m² or up to 10 m², and 6 m or 2 m or less from the ground, by class.
- Spread footing: display area 1 m² or less, thickness 300 mm or less, height 3 m or less, excavation 500 mm or less.
- Balcony or canopy soffit: display area 2 m² or less, height 600 mm or less, thickness 100 mm or less, and no projection beyond the balcony or canopy.
- Position: follow APP-126 spacings, including minimum lateral spacing to opposite signboards and clearances over a walkway, carriageway, or tramway as diagrammed. If the required lateral spacing cannot be shown, the signboard is ineligible.
- Class I: an authorized person, plus a registered structural engineer if the construction is not the specified construction under Building (Minor Works) Regulation section 37(4). Class II or III: a prescribed building professional, or a prescribed registered contractor registered for the corresponding type C minor works. Strengthening is by a prescribed registered contractor, and for Class I under authorized person or registered structural engineer supervision as necessary.
- Re-validate or remove at 5-year intervals. Common parts: deed of mutual covenant or Cap. 344 consent, and third-party insurance, are advised.

**What to submit**
- Forms SC01 or SC01C for Class I, SC02 or SC02C for Class II, and SC03 for Class III, with photos, fixing details, plans, calculations for Class I, and safety inspection checklists (Appendix C).
- Notify alteration or strengthening, and certify completion, on the related forms.
- Display of the validation submission numbers is recommended (Appendix D).

**Watch-outs**
- Signboards put up without approval on or after 2 September 2013 cannot be validated under this scheme.
- A spacing conflict with an approved or validated neighbour makes the signboard ineligible.
- Do not use the wrong class of professional, and do not assume a Buildings Department submission notifies other departments.
- A validated signboard that passes the 5-year point without re-certification or removal is at risk of enforcement.
