# APP-25

**Official title:** Requirements for a Geotechnical Assessment at General Building Plan Submission Stage — Regulation 8(1)(ba) of Building (Administration) Regulations

**In plain words:** The Building Authority may require a geotechnical assessment of whether the site is adequate, at the general building plan stage, under Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1)(ba). It does not change gross floor area (GFA) or site coverage (SC). It is a feasibility check, not a measurement rule.

**Numbers that matter**
- Submit the assessment with the general building plans for a new building, or for alteration and addition that results in a new building, if any of these apply. (i) The maximum gradient from boundary to boundary, or across any 50 m strip on a large site, is steeper than 15°. (ii) A slope steeper than 30° and higher than 7.5 m, including a retaining wall at the toe or crest, is on the site or within 7.5 m of it. (iii) An excavation face longer than 10 m is, at any stage, deeper than 7.5 m, or it goes below a 45° plane drawn from the toe of any retaining wall, or it goes below Hong Kong Principal Datum minus 1 m on reclaimed coastal land. (iv) A retaining wall on the site retains more than 6 m, or at least 6 m length of such a wall lies within 6 m of the site. (v) The geology is special: previous landslides, debris flows, threatening boulders, or potential rockfalls.
- For alteration and addition that does not result in a new building, submit the assessment if the works significantly affect existing geotechnical features or adjacent foundations, or if the excavation falls under item 2(a)(iii) above.
- The assessment describes topography, geology, and the presumed groundwater; how features and foundations affect the works and are affected by them; feasibility, including methods and sequence; schematic site-formation and foundation plans and sections; a ground-investigation scope plan with the supervision level in Geoguide 2 section 15.3; and the stability investigations and preventive or remedial works that are intended.

**What to submit**
- Submit the geotechnical assessment with the general building plans when any criterion above is met. If it is missing, the plans may be rejected under Buildings Ordinance section 16(1)(a).

**Watch-outs**
- General building plans on a sloping site, a deep excavation, or a tall retaining wall, submitted without the assessment, can be refused.
- A thin assessment that misses construction sequence, feasibility, or the ground-investigation supervision level will not identify the constraints early enough.
