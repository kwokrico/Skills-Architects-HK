# APP-45 Testing of Reinforcement for Concrete
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2023 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 122; first issued December 1986)

> Scope note: Purchaser's verification tests of steel reinforcing bars under section 17(1) item 6 of the Buildings Ordinance, in addition to the manufacturer's mill tests. The Buildings Department adopts Construction Standard CS2:2012 as an acceptable standard. Cold-worked bars are outside CS2. Grades covered are 250, for bars up to 12 mm diameter, and 500B and 500C.

## Regulatory Overview
Specimens are selected at random from every batch delivered to site or to the prefabrication factory, and the purchaser's tests in CS2 are carried out by a laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme. Approval of structural plans for reinforced concrete is conditioned on qualified supervision by the registered structural engineer and the registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor, including sampling and testing.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the test report contains, and off-site yards (paragraphs 2–10)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| CS2 scope | Straight bars, coil, and decoiled products, except cold-worked bars. Mass per metre, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and bond property. Quality Assured Stockists and Quality Assured Coil Processors |
| Report | Classification and certification by a Quality Assured Stockist for Class 1 and Class 2 bars; manufacturer, country of origin, grade, and size; sketch of the deformation pattern and mill marks; batch identification and delivery date; measured mass per metre; chemical composition, tensile properties, bend performance, and bond tests if required |
| Coils | Amendment No. 2/2018 and Technical Note No. 5, issued 26 September 2018. All coil processors are Quality Assured Coil Processors. Yards approved under Technical Circular (Works) No. 10/2018 are recognised as such |
| Approved yards | Prefabricated products within the approved scope, such as cutting and bending, cages, and threading or coupling, are considered to conform to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition) if qualified supervision follows the Ordinance |
| Streamlined sampling | Batch sampling under the yard's quality system, witnessed by the Independent Audit Team, satisfies the qualified-supervision requirement for sampling under Section 5 of CS2. Keep the sampling records, witness records, and test reports on site. Submit the list of products and a statement signed by the registered structural engineer, confirming compliance, within 21 days of delivery to the site |
| Alternative | The registered structural engineer and the contractor arrange their own supervision of sampling, delivery to their appointed accredited laboratory, and testing to CS2 |

**SD takeaway:** Specify CS2 grades and require a random purchaser's test of every batch, or, for an approved prefabrication yard, file the witnessed test reports and the structural engineer's statement within 21 days of delivery.
