# APP-142 Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 296; first issue June 2007)

> Scope note: Amendments to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition), which already included the circular letters of 13 June 2017 and 24 November 2020. The amendments are Appendices A (February 2022), B (June 2023), and C (April 2024). It is not a substitute for the concrete code.

## Regulatory Overview
Design follows the 2020 Edition of the 2013 concrete code as amended by this note. The three appendices add plain-concrete lining design, cube-size rules, the maturity method, coupler testing, an alternative beam link, strut-and-tie provisions, and a mix for concrete not exceeding 20 N/mm2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. February 2022 (Appendix A)
| Clause | Amendment |
| --- | --- |
| 6.2.3 and Figure 6.18b | Design requirements for plain concrete linings |
| 10.3.4.2 and Table 10.2 | Requirements on the use of 100 mm and 150 mm concrete cubes |
| 11.7.5.4 and Table 11.2 | Guidelines on monitoring early compressive strength of in-situ concrete by the maturity method |
| Annex A | ASTM C1074-19ε1 for that clause |

**SD takeaway:** Early in-situ strength may be monitored by the maturity method in clause 11.7.5.4, which PNAP APP-167 also allows as an alternative to rebound hammer tests.

### 2. June 2023 (Appendix B)
| Clause | Amendment |
| --- | --- |
| 3.2.8.3 and Annex A | ISO 15835-2 as the test method for a mechanical coupler |
| 9.9.1.3(b) and Figure 9.6a | An alternative arrangement of links or ties for a beam |

**SD takeaway:** Coupler tests are to ISO 15835-2, and a beam may use the alternative link arrangement in Figure 9.6a.
