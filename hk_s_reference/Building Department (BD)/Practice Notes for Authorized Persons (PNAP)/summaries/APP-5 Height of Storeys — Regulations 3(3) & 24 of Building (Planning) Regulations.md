# APP-5

**Official title:** Height of Storeys — Regulations 3(3) & 24 of Building (Planning) Regulations

**In plain words:** Storey height and balcony clear height are measured to the structure, ignoring finishes, and a domestic storey or a transfer plate counts in gross floor area (GFA) once it exceeds the caps below. The caps are applied under Building (Planning) Regulation 23(3)(a).

**Numbers that matter**
- Building (Planning) Regulation 24 measures storey height between structural members and disregards finishes (false ceilings, screeding, plaster, flooring). Balcony clear height under Regulation 3(3) is measured the same way.
- A peripheral beam that projects inward from an external wall may be 2 m to its underside where the hard minimum is 2.3 m. A beam over a doorway may be at door height. Staircase clear height is at least 2 m. A beam across a narrow corridor may be at the height of a notional doorway.
- Rooms for habitation or offices need a clear height of at least 2.5 m, for health.
- Maximum domestic storey heights for Regulation 23(3)(a): a typical flat storey 3.5 m; the topmost floor 4 m (in a duplex or triplex, that 4 m maximum applies to only one storey of the stack); a house 4.5 m.
- A transfer plate is not GFA if: (a) in a domestic building of not more than 40 storeys, its thickness is not more than the typical floor storey height; (b) in a domestic building of more than 40 storeys, and in a non-domestic building, its thickness is not more than 4.5 m; (c) the total transfer-plate area is not more than 2× the total GFA of the lowest floor of the blocks sitting on it. Where several domestic blocks share one plate, use the lowest domestic block. A building-services zone inside the plate is left out of the thickness measurement, but it needs safe access for maintenance.

**What to submit**
- Show the storey heights and the transfer-plate thickness and area on the plans. There is no extra form beyond the normal plan submission.

**Watch-outs**
- Measure clear height to the structure, not to the finishes.
- A transfer plate that is too thick, or too large in area, becomes GFA.
- Do not apply the 4 m top-floor duplex height to more than one storey of the stack.
