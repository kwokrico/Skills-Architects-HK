# APP-150

**Official title:** Wholesale Conversion of Industrial Buildings

**In plain words:** This note gives pragmatic Building Authority modifications for a wholesale alteration-and-addition conversion of an industrial building, in support of the Government revitalisation measures. Greenery and energy features can support site coverage and gross floor area (GFA) modifications, and a disregarded car park may be swapped for plant rooms only with a promise to reinstate it.

**Numbers that matter**
- Supports Lands Department industrial-building revitalisation measures. First issue September 2010. Revision June 2013.
- Where a refuge floor is difficult, a favourable factor is greenery design plus enhanced fire service installations (APP-122), with no adverse comment from the Director of Fire Services.
- Office conversion lighting and ventilation: Building (Planning) Regulations 30 and 31 may be modified if artificial lighting and ventilation, and an energy design, achieve 40% in energy use (EU) and indoor environmental quality (IEQ) under BEAM Plus (the Hong Kong green-building assessment), following APP-130.
- Oversized air-conditioning boxes or platforms, or boxes over a street: energy-efficient or environmentally friendly units under APP-19.
- A curtain wall projecting over a street may be exempted under Buildings Ordinance section 31(1) if it uses low-energy glazing or materials with 40% in energy use and indoor environmental quality under BEAM Plus, following APP-2.
- Places of public entertainment (PPE) in the non-domestic portion: revised requirements in Fire Safety Code clauses B19.2 and B20.9.
- Redundant car parks disregarded under Regulation 23(3)(b) may be converted to transformer rooms, or to mandatory or essential plant rooms of the types in APP-151 Appendix A, under a modification of Regulation 23(3)(a), with stakeholder support. The owner and operator must undertake that, when the facilities cease, the plant rooms will be demolished and the car parks reinstated to the original approved plans.

**What to submit**
- Alteration-and-addition modification applications, with BEAM Plus, fire service installation, or energy justifications as applicable.
- For a car-park-to-plant conversion: an undertaking letter from the owner and the operator, plus justifications, at plan submission.

**Watch-outs**
- Do not claim Regulation 23(3)(a) plant-room GFA without the reversion undertaking and department support.
- Partial conversion of an industrial building to a columbarium, or places of public entertainment, raises fire-risk issues. See also APP-154 for a columbarium.
