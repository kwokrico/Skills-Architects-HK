# ADM-3 Emergency Situations — Telephone Numbers for Use Outside Office Hours
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2019 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 34; first issue December 1976)

> Scope note: Requires out-of-hours contact numbers for the professionals responsible for a site. It does not set building design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
Buildings Department staff are on 24-hour call for emergencies at sites where building works are in progress, and contacting the responsible authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer in time can avoid clearance of major thoroughfares or use of the Government Contractor at the owner's expense. Those professionals must lodge telephone, fax, and mobile numbers for that purpose and keep the Department informed of any change.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Numbers to lodge (paragraphs 2–4 and Appendix A)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Authorized person, registered structural engineer, and/or registered geotechnical engineer responsible for the development |
| Duty stated | Ensure that no danger is caused to the public by the building works for which they are responsible |
| What to give | Telephone number, fax number, and mobile telephone number if any |
| How to update | Letter in Appendix A to the Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei (fax 2625 0437) |
| Use | Buildings Department staff only, for emergencies outside office hours concerning sites for which the person is the AP, RSE, or RGE |
| Personal data | Provision is voluntary; access and correction via the Registration Unit at 2626 1616 |

**SD takeaway:** Before works start, lodge a working out-of-hours number and update it when it changes so an emergency on site can reach the responsible professional.
