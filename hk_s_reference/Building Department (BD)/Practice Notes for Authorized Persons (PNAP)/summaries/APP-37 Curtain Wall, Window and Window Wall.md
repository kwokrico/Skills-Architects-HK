# APP-37

**Official title:** Curtain Wall, Window and Window Wall

**In plain words:** A curtain wall, and a window or window wall that crosses the size or wind triggers, needs a structural submission, fire-separation details, and quality control of glass, sealant, and fixings. This note does not give a gross floor area (GFA) concession; the facade affects prescribed windows, protective barriers, and fire separation at floors and stair lobbies, and it is read with the Glass Code, practice note APP-53, and practice note APP-116 on aluminium windows.

**Numbers that matter**
- Curtain-wall performance: Building (Construction) Regulations (B(C)R) sections 29 to 31; wind loads; horizontal imposed loads under section 9 and Schedule Table 3 where there is no protective barrier; resistance to corrosion and suitable materials; and limits on fire and smoke spread under B(C)R section 35 and the Fire Safety Code 2011, between floors, between compartments, and between accommodation and a staircase or lobby.
- A structural submission for a window or window wall is triggered when it forms part of the external wall and the least structural opening dimension is more than 1.8 m, or the area is more than 6 m², and the opening is at least 100 m above adjoining ground or the design wind pressure Q₀,z is at least 2.86 kPa (or q_z where the Wind Code 2004 is used). It is also triggered when a structural element, such as a mullion or a glass fin, has a design span of more than 6 m.
- Deflection limits are those in the Glass Code 2018.
- Some repair, replacement, and alteration falls under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (B(MW)R) and practice note APP-147.
- Tempered glass comes from an ISO 9001 factory. The quality assurance scheme (QAS) is submitted at least 14 days before production, with the registered structural engineer’s (RSE) declaration under Glass Code clause 9.1.3. Heat soaking follows BS EN 14179-1:2016. Compliance reports are submitted before the occupation permit (OP) or Form BA14. The quality supervision plan is submitted at least 14 days before production. Quality control supervision (QCS, about grade T3) covers at least 30% of the panes. A videotelephony option is allowed at a resolution of at least 480p, recorded on a non-rewritable DVD. A quality control coordinator (QCC, about grade T1) is present full-time, with an independent data logger at 1-minute intervals. The quality supervision report is submitted before the occupation permit.
- Structural sealant: compatibility, adhesion, and print-review certificates, and the deglazing certificates, before the occupation permit or Form BA14.
- Spider fixings: a proof-load test under Appendix C on a sample of at least 1% or 3 numbers, with residual deformation of at most 5%. The test may be waived if the fixing is in the Central Data Bank under practice note ADM-20. Show the proprietary model on the plans.
- Locking devices on plans on or after 1 August 2020: durable and non-combustible; ultimate design strength equal to the characteristic strength divided by a factor of safety of 1.8; a proof test on a sample of at least 0.1% or 5 numbers under Appendix D; at most 8 locking points on each handle bar; a top-hung sash of at most 2.5 m²; a side-hung width of at most 700 mm.
- The safety test is by a laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS), with an RSE statement, before the occupation permit or Form BA14. It is waived for fast-track repair or replacement under practice note ADM-19, or for minor works item 1.61.

**What to submit**
- Structural plans and calculations, compatible with the approved general building plans (GBPs). Concurrent approval and consent for a curtain wall is available under practice note ADM-19.
- Curtain-wall plan contents, from paragraph 2 of the note: framing, key details, and installation sequence; calculations covering the parent structure, stability, aluminium, fixings, glazing, and deflection; workmanship for welding, galvanising, bi-metallic contact, and corrosion; elevations and the pane layout; typical and non-typical sections and connections; support tolerance and the remedial work; the support mode, cast-in or welded, or a modification of B(C)R section 31 where the fixings are drilled and bolted; the codes used; material specifications; and the fire sections.
- Where a separate RSE is appointed: Forms BA4 and BA5; the completion certificate under Building (Administration) Regulations (B(A)R) regulation 25(3); an assessment report; and a statement by the project RSE on the effects on the parent structure. The separate RSE supervises the work, including cast-in anchors, unless the project RSE has already installed those anchors.
- Tempered-glass quality assurance scheme, supervision plan, and reports; sealant certificates; spider-fixing and locking-device proof tests, or Central Data Bank references; and the HOKLAS safety tests.
- Spare-glass Schedules A and B (Appendix E) for future repair. Form BA14 and the occupation permit are the milestones for the endorsed test packs.

**Watch-outs**
- Curtain-wall drawings with no fire-compartment section across the floors.
- A window wall above the triggers, submitted without a structural submission.
- Drilled or bolted fixings without a modification of B(C)R section 31.
- Heat-soak supervision below 30% of the panes, or no data logger with the quality control coordinator.
- A factor of safety or a locking layout that does not comply.
- An occupation permit without the endorsed test packs.
- Spare glass used with no recorded trail on Schedule A and Schedule B.
