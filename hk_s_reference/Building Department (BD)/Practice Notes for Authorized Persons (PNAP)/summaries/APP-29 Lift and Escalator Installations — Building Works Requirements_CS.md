# APP-29 Lift and Escalator Installations — Building Works Requirements
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2019 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 84; first issue May 1982)

> Scope note: Building works that accommodate lifts and escalators, under regulation 9A of the Building (Construction) Regulations. The deemed-to-satisfy standard is the Code of Practice for the Design and Construction of Buildings and Building Works for the Installation and Safe Use of Lifts and Escalators 2011. Electrical, mechanical, and operational requirements are in the Electrical and Mechanical Services Department codes under the Lifts and Escalators Ordinance, Cap. 618. Appendix B applies to new building plans, or a major revision, submitted for approval on or after 1 December 2019. Appendix A (August 2015) applies to alteration of existing liftwell inspection and emergency doors.

## Regulatory Overview
The structure, including guide-rail brackets, the lift machine, and deflector-sheave brackets, must be strong enough for safe operation, maintenance, and inspection, with the layout, materials, anchors, and load distribution shown. Other standards may be accepted if they achieve equivalent performance and their suitability for local conditions is explained.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Well ventilation, separation, and pit access (Appendix B, amendments to paragraphs 3.3.2, 3.7, and 3.8)
| Item | Rule, for plans on or after 1 December 2019 |
| --- | --- |
| Vent at the top of a well | At least 1% of the horizontal cross-sectional area of the well, to the open air directly or through ducting or the machine or pulley room. Not less than 0.3 m2 net free area for each well. A common well with four, five, or six lifts: not less than 0.4 m2, 0.5 m2, or 0.6 m2 respectively. Ferrules over openings above the well project at least 50 mm above the slab or finished floor |
| Partition between moving parts of different lifts | Adequate strength, for example a solid block wall or wire mesh, from the pit floor through the full height of the well and across its whole depth. If each lift is separated by a wall, each well is ventilated to paragraph 3.3.2. The earlier rule that the partition need only rise 2.5 m, and need be full height only if the car-roof edge is within 300 mm of an adjacent moving part, is replaced by this full-height partition |
| Pit access door | If the pit is deeper than 2 m, an imperforate door leading to a stair, not opening into the well, with a key-operated lock that can be reclosed without a key and opened from inside without a key. If the pit is deeper than 1.6 m but not more than 2 m, the same door is provided if the layout permits |
| Fall-arrest anchor | At the lowest landing, adjacent to the landing door, between 1.5 m and 1.8 m above finished floor level, unless a pit access door meeting paragraph 3.8.3 is provided |
| Inspection and emergency doors (Appendix A) | Dual key-operated locks needing two designated keys together to open from outside, openable from inside without a key, and a figure-type warning not less than 100 mm high. A single designated key is acceptable for landing doors used as those doors, or where the sill is 1 m or more above the adjoining floor |

**SD takeaway:** Vent each well at not less than 1% of its plan area and 0.3 m2, separate adjacent lifts for the full height of the well, and give a pit deeper than 2 m its own access door to a stair.

### 2. Stairs to machine rooms and hoisting points (Appendix B, paragraphs 3.12 and 3.17)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Level difference greater than 0.6 m | Access and egress by stairs, not a ladder |
| Those stairs | Width not less than 900 mm. Treads not less than 225 mm, with an anti-slip surface, and risers not exceeding 175 mm. Not more than 16 steps in a flight without a landing. Handrails on both sides. Protective barriers at the outer edges not less than 1.1 m high |
| Ladder, only if the difference does not exceed 0.6 m | Permanently fixed, with a platform, railings, and handholds at the top |
| Hoisting | Metal supports or hooks, with a safe-working-load notice, in the machine-room ceiling or on beams, and also at the ceiling soffit of the liftwell for the car or the suspension |
| Notices | Incised or embossed, in English and Chinese, in durable material: not less than 25 mm high on well doors and machine-room doors, and not less than 15 mm high at landings |
| Before the lift is put into service | Complete the associated building works listed in Appendices C and D before applying to the Director of Electrical and Mechanical Services |

**SD takeaway:** Reach a machine or pulley room by a stair at least 900 mm wide once the level change exceeds 0.6 m, and provide a rated lifting point in both the machine room and the well soffit.
