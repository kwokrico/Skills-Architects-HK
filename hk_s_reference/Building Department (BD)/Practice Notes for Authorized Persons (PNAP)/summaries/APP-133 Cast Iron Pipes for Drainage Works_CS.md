# APP-133 Cast Iron Pipes for Drainage Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 2003 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 282)

> Scope note: Quality and fixings for cast iron drainage pipes. For drainage plans approved after 1 November 2004, the Building Authority will require compliance under section 28(1) of the Buildings Ordinance. The pipes are also to be listed in the Schedule of Building Materials and Products under PNAP 53 when applying for an occupation permit. It is not a drainage layout code.

## Regulatory Overview
Premature rusting, leakage, and corroded fixings of cast iron pipes are treated as a public safety, health, and hygiene problem. The note sets coating, standards, and fixing rules for specifying those pipes.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Pipe quality and fixings (paragraph 1)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Standards | Relevant British Standards or equivalent. Examples: BS 416:Part 1 or BS EN 877 for above-ground drains; BS 437 or BS 4622 for underground systems and above-ground drains larger than 150 mm diameter |
| Coating | Suitably coated internally and externally. Cold-applied bitumen-based coating to comply with BS 3416 or equivalent. External coating should contain anti-corrosion inhibitors normally used on metallic structures |
| Further finish | Pipes in a state that permits additional external finishing coatings |
| Fixings | Corrosion-resistant fixings such as stainless steel brackets and connections, or similar, properly anchored into solid wall |

**SD takeaway:** Specify coated cast iron to the stated British Standards, and anchor stainless-steel or equivalent brackets into solid wall.

### 2. Approval after 1 November 2004 (paragraph 2)
Drainage plans approved after 1 November 2004 must comply under section 28(1) of the Buildings Ordinance. The pipes are included in the Schedule of Building Materials and Products under PNAP 53 at occupation-permit stage.

**SD takeaway:** List the cast iron drainage pipes on the materials schedule when applying for the occupation permit.
