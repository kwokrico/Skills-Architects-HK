# APP-3 Nomination of an Authorized Person, Registered Structural Engineer or Registered Geotechnical Engineer to Act in Stead
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2010 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 14; first issue December 1974)

> Scope note: Temporary nomination of another authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer when the appointed person is ill or absent from Hong Kong. It is not a procedure for a permanent change of appointment.

## Regulatory Overview
Sections 4(2) and 4A(5) of the Buildings Ordinance allow a temporary nomination only when the appointed person is unable to act because of illness or absence from Hong Kong. The nomination is made on Form BA 21, or Form MW09 for class I minor works, within 7 days.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How the nomination is lodged (paragraphs 1–2 and 5)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Building works or street works | One Form BA 21 for all active projects, with a list of those projects, submitted in duplicate |
| Registered structural engineer or registered geotechnical engineer under more than one authorized person | One Form BA 21 for all active projects under each authorized person, signed by that authorized person |
| Class I minor works | One Form MW09 for all active minor works projects |
| Nominee's correspondence | On every occasion, refer briefly to the earlier Form BA 21 or Form MW09 |
| Further nomination | The nominee cannot nominate another person to act in his stead |
| Acknowledgement | The Buildings Department copies the owner, or the person who arranged the minor works, with the period and reason of acting |

**SD takeaway:** Lodge one Form BA 21, or one Form MW09 for class I minor works, within 7 days of illness or absence, and do not allow the nominee to pass the appointment on again.

### 2. What counts as temporary, and how it ends (paragraphs 2(f), 2(g), and 4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Not temporary | An acting period exceeding 3 months, or nominations at frequent intervals, may not be accepted. The owner may be advised to appoint another person |
| Early return or recovery, building or street works | Advise the Buildings Department by letter within 7 days that the Form BA 21 arrangements have ceased |
| Class I minor works | The nominee notifies the Buildings Department on Form MW31 within 7 days after cessation. Section 50 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation refers |

**SD takeaway:** Keep any acting appointment under 3 months and not repeated at frequent intervals, and notify cessation within 7 days.
