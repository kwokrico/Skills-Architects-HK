# APP-158

**Official title:** Quality Supervision of Building Works

**In plain words:** Quality supervision is part of the supervision plan under the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009, so the works must follow the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations, the approved plans, and the approval and consent conditions. The note grants no new gross floor area (GFA) concession, but the fitting-out checks include features already given a GFA exemption, and a failed check can put that exemption at risk at audit or at the occupation permit.

**Numbers that matter**
- First issue November 2016. Last revision February 2021. This revision September 2024. Paragraph 3 and Appendix A were amended.
- Appendix A is a non-exhaustive set of inspection items: superstructure in-situ reinforced concrete QC1 to QC11; general building works QB1 to QB8; excavation and lateral support QE1 to QE9; site formation monitoring QSF1.
- Steel bar delivery and sampling follow CS2 (steel reinforcing bars). Bar fixing and cover follow PNAP ADV-15. Concrete delivery and cubes follow CS1 (concrete), including high-strength coring conditions where they apply.
- Formwork and falsework stability follows the split of registered structural engineer (RSE) and registered contractor (RC) duties in Supervision Code clauses 4.7 to 4.9.
- Windows: prescribed areas and openable areas, PNAP APP-116, and any modification conditions.
- Barrier-free access (BFA) dimensions follow the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access.
- Curtain wall mock-up and on-site water penetration tests follow Glass Code 2018 Annex A. Precast façade water penetration tests follow the Precast Code of Practice 2016 clauses 2.8.2.8 and 4.3.
- Site formation cross-references: cut and fill APP-15; rock slopes APP-17; retaining walls, including regulation 48 domestic cases for waterproofing and insulation; reinforced fill drainage and filter APP-54, and backfill APP-15.
- Use Form B where a non-conformity has a material safety concern, or the work cannot be checked because it has been covered up.

**What to submit**
- Keep every checklist and inspection record on site for the Site Monitoring Section and the Building Authority.
- The authorized person (AP), RSE, registered geotechnical engineer (RGE), and authorized signatory (AS) build the technically competent person (TCP) checklists from the typical items plus anything the project needs, including quality or qualified supervision conditions imposed by the Building Authority.
- Appendix B is a sample record only. It shows TCP name and grade, frequency, item numbers, location, satisfactory / not satisfactory / not applicable, remedial remarks, and photos. Make a project-specific record.
- Reinforced concrete items cover location and size; steel delivery and sampling; bar fixing and cover; concrete delivery and cubes; placing, compaction, and curing; transfer-structure sequence and temporary works; formwork and falsework stability; concrete condition after stripping; couplers; cast-in embeds and anchors; and in-situ strength by coring or rebound.
- General building items cover setting-out of basements, ground floor, and upper floors (lanes, emergency vehicular access, setbacks, dedicated areas, storey height, fire-service staircases, smoke vents, refuge, and barriers). They also cover fitting-out: exit routes, fireman’s lift lobbies, temporary refuge spaces, GFA-concession features (balconies, utility platforms, wider corridors, plant rooms, and residents’ recreational facilities), fire barriers and fire resistance rating (FRR) products, and roof waterproofing. They also cover windows, barrier-free dimensions, waterproofing tests for roofs, balconies, utility platforms, wet areas, and basements, curtain wall water tests, and precast façade water tests.
- Excavation and lateral support items cover temporary planking location and installation, grout curtain, pumping and ground-treatment tests, strut erection and removal sequence, preloading, staged excavation with no over-excavation or unsafe stockpile, backfilling and compaction, and instrumentation for movement, groundwater drawdown, and ingress. The RGE stream applies only when geotechnical qualified supervision is an approval condition. The AP stream often has the coordinating role marked (*).
- Site formation items cover monitoring, drainage and rainstorm protection, cut and fill, rock slopes, retaining walls, and reinforced fill.
- Where the plan is marked (*), the AP makes sure the RSE, RGE, or RC does the check. Where it is marked (#), the AP checks that the layout matches the approved plans.

**Watch-outs**
- An incomplete checklist that skips curtain wall, glass, prestress, steel, or waterproofing where those works exist.
- Missing site records can fail a Buildings Department audit of supervision duties under the Buildings Ordinance.
- A “not satisfactory” result with no Form B, when there is a material safety concern or the works are covered up.
- Missing GFA-concession sizes, fire-door FRR, or prescribed window areas during interior fitting-out.
