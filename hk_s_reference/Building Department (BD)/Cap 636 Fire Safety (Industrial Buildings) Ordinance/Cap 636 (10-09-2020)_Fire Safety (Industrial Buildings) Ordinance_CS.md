# Cap 636 — Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 10 September 2020 | Cap. 636 | Commenced 19 June 2020

> Scope note: Cap 636 is a **retrofit / upgrade statute** for the **whole** of a **pre–1 March 1987 industrial building**. It is **not** the Cap. 123 / FS Code path for new BO submissions. It **does not apply** if Cap. 502 or Cap. 572 already applies to the **whole** building. At SD on old IBs / IB revitalisation / stratified industrial A&A: age + industrial construction use → Cap 502/572 whole-building exclusion → owner (Sch. 1) vs occupier (Sch. 2) → FSI integration across units → design to **FSD CoP 2012** + **FS Code 2011 (Oct 2015)** → flag prohibition-order / Land Registry risk early.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 636 applies to the **whole of a building** that is an **industrial building**: all or part was constructed for use as a factory, industrial undertaking, godown, warehouse, place of bulk storage, or similar industrial premises, **and** that building/part was constructed to Cap. 123 plans first submitted to the BA **on or before 1 March 1987** (and approved), or constructed on/before that date with **no** Cap. 123 plans submitted by then.

BD (planning / design / construction) and FSD (fire service installations) may issue fire safety directions forcing owners and occupiers to provide or improve sprinklers, FH/HR, alarms, emergency lighting, exit signs, secondary power, fireman’s lifts, MOE/FRC works and related FSI — with magistrate compliance orders, District Court **occupation prohibition**, Land Registry registration, and public publication if upgrades are not done.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 0. Definitions that lock schematic decisions (s. 2)

| Term | Statutory meaning — why it matters at SD |
|---|---|
| **Fire safety requirement** | Owner → **all or any** of Schedule 1; Occupier → **all or any** of Schedule 2. Direction need not demand the entire Schedule at once. |
| **Fire safety direction** | Written direction under s. 5, 6 or 7 (incl. amended under s. 9). |
| **Fire safety compliance order** | Magistrate order under s. 13 (incl. varied under s. 14) — replaces the direction. |
| **Prohibition order** | District Court order under s. 18 — **bans occupation** of building/part. |
| **Certificate of compliance** | Certificate under s. 27 that direction/order measures have been complied with — key exit from escalation. |
| **Enforcement authority** | **FSD** for fire service installation/equipment; **BD** for planning, design and construction. |
| **Fire service installation or equipment** | Anything for extinguishing/attacking/preventing/limiting fire; warning; firefighter access; evacuation; or stand-by power to any of the above. |
| **Mechanical ventilating system** | **Includes** an air-conditioning system — triggers auto cut-off rules. |
| **Owner** | Cap. 123 s. 2(1) meaning (broader than “title holder only”). |
| **Occupier** | Person occupying building/part as owner or under lease/licence. |
| **Owners’ corporation** | Cap. 344 registered OC — Land Registry registration of orders against OC binds **each owner individually** (s. 31). |
| **Authorized officer** | DFS, DB, police officer, or public officer appointed in writing under s. 33. |

### 1. Does Cap 636 apply? — three-gate test (s. 3)

| Gate | Pass = Cap 636 in play |
|---|---|
| **1. Use** | All **or part** of the building was **constructed for use** as factory / industrial undertaking / godown / warehouse / bulk storage / **similar industrial premises** |
| **2. Age** | That building **or part** was constructed (i) to plans first submitted to BA **on or before 1 Mar 1987** and approved under Cap. 123; **or** (ii) on/before 1 Mar 1987 with **no** Cap. 123 plans submitted by then |
| **3. Exclusion** | Neither Cap. **502** nor Cap. **572** applies to the **whole** of the building |

**SD kill switches:**
- Hits the **whole building**, not a Cap 502-style “prescribed premises” slice
- Gate 1 only needs **part** of the building to have been industrial-constructed — Cap 636 then still applies to the **whole**
- Gate 2 age is measured on the industrial-constructed building **or part**, not “first occupation of current tenant”
- Cap 502 or 572 must apply to the **entire** building to switch Cap 636 off (s. 3(3))
- Partial Cap 502 PCP on one shop floor does **not** by itself kill Cap 636 for the IB
- Post–1 Mar 1987 industrial stock → Cap 636 out; use Cap. 123 + current FS Code / FSD CoP

#### 1.1 “Industrial undertaking” — broad construction use (s. 3(4))

Includes premises where articles are manufactured, altered, cleansed, repaired, ornamented, finished, adapted for sale, broken up or demolished, **or** where materials are **transformed**.

**SD takeaway:** Workshop / light industry / repair / processing / finishing IBs often fall in even if the current OP wording is fuzzy — filter on **original industrial construction intent + age**, not current café fit-out.

### 2. Unoccupied stock — owner carries occupier liability (s. 4)

If building/part is unoccupied:
1. Owner is **regarded as occupier**
2. Enforcement powers aimed at an occupier may be exercised against the owner
3. Owner’s liability from that exercise **survives** later occupation

**SD takeaway:** Vacant IB floors pending revitalisation still attract Sch. 2-style occupier directions against the owner — do not assume “empty = no Cap 636 risk.”

### 3. Who enforces what — and who can walk the site (ss. 2, 33–40)

| Subject | Authority |
|---|---|
| Fire service installation or equipment | **Director of Fire Services** |
| Planning, design and construction | **Director of Buildings** |

#### 3.1 Entry without warrant (s. 36)
Authorized officer who knows/reasonably believes the building is Cap 636 stock (or that an offence is/was being committed) may **enter and inspect** during **reasonable hours without warrant**.

#### 3.2 Entry with warrant (ss. 37–39)
Magistrate may authorize entry (**by force if necessary**) where Cap 636 applies (or good reason to believe so), there is good reason to enter, **and** one of:
- unoccupied; or
- owner/occupier cannot be contacted after reasonable efforts; or
- admission refused / refusal reasonably expected; or
- admission unreasonably delayed; or
- urgency  

Warrant lasts until purpose fulfilled or **1 month** from issue (whichever earlier). After entering unoccupied premises on warrant, officer must leave them **as securely against trespassers** as before entry.

#### 3.3 Ownership / occupation intel (ss. 40–41)
Authorized officer may require a person to identify owner/occupier if officer reasonably believes they have the info **and** it is not readily available from public records. Refusal / false or misleading answer without reasonable excuse → Level 4 fine.

**SD takeaway:** Expect BD/FSD inspections of cores, plant rooms, basements and unit MVAC during due diligence — design surveys should assume regulatory access.

### 4. Fire safety directions — owner vs occupier mechanics (ss. 5–12)

#### 4.1 Directing the owner (s. 5)

| Ownership pattern | Direction may cover |
|---|---|
| **Only 1 owner** | Fire safety requirement for the **building** (Sch. 1) |
| **Multiple owners**, each with exclusive occupation of a part | That owner’s **exclusive part**; **and/or** parts **none** of the owners exclusively occupies (common parts) |

#### 4.2 FSI integration — stratified IB killer item (s. 5(3)–(4))
Direction may require any owner to provide **connections or other forms of integration** of FSI:
- among different exclusively occupied parts; **and/or**
- between an exclusive part and common parts

**SD freeze:** At IB A&A / revitalisation, design **building-wide** sprinkler / alarm / FH-HR networking — not isolated unit-only islands. Coordinate OC / multi-owner interfaces early.

#### 4.3 Directing the occupier (s. 6)
Authority may direct the occupier of a building **or part** to comply with a fire safety requirement for that building/part (Sch. 2).

#### 4.4 Escape hatch — “other appropriate measures” (s. 7)
If requiring a Schedule item would be **unreasonable** having regard to:
- structural integrity of the building/part; **or**
- technology available to comply; **or**
- any other relevant factors  

→ authority may direct **other appropriate measures instead** of that Schedule requirement.

Use early when fireman’s lift shafts, stair/exit upgrades, full sprinklers, or secondary-power plant threaten structure or are physically impracticable.

#### 4.5 Advisory committee on alternatives (s. 12)
Authority **may** set up an advisory committee of persons with relevant expertise to advise:
- whether to use s. 7 alternatives; and
- if so, what measures are appropriate  

Only the authority may refer a case. Committee may invite **written representations** from owner/occupier and must consider them before advising. Authority **must consider** the advice if given.

**SD takeaway:** For constrained old IB cores, prepare a structured alternative package (with RSE / fire engineer) for possible advisory-committee route — do not invent “equivalents” unilaterally.

#### 4.6 Direction formalities & life cycle (ss. 8–10)
- Must be **in writing**
- Must state a **compliance time limit** that allows **reasonable** time
- May be amended or withdrawn by written notice (s. 9)
- Ceases when: certificate of compliance issued; **or** withdrawn; **or** replaced by compliance order

#### 4.7 Non-compliance with direction (s. 11)
Without reasonable excuse → Level 4 fine + **$2,500 per day** (or part-day) after time limit.

**Reasonable excuse includes** (not limited to) it not being reasonable to expect compliance in time because:
- compliance would risk **prejudicially affecting structural integrity**; or
- required technology is **not reasonably available**

### 5. Schedule 1 — owner package (AP / RSE / building-wide core)

**Locked design standards** (until Sec for Security + LegCo update under s. 55):
- FSI detail → *Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment **2012*** (“2012 Code”)
- Passive / construction detail → *Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings **2011** (October **2015** version)*

#### 5.1 Fire service installations — provide or improve (Sch. 1 s. 1)

| Item | Design implication at SD |
|---|---|
| **(a) Automatic sprinkler system** | With or without direct FSD link — control spread + sound alarm. Space-proof tank, pump, inlet, risers, drainage. |
| **(b) Fire hydrant + hose reel** | Water supply for firefighting — FH inlet, risers, HR coverage on floors/common areas. |
| **(c) Manual fire alarm** | Alert persons in the building — break-glass / call-point strategy, integration across units. |
| **(d) Emergency lighting** | Within **common areas** — power-failure evacuation of the building. |
| **(e) Exit signs** (incl. directional) | Indicate exit routes — common MOE wayfinding. |
| **(f) Secondary electrical power** | Emergency generator **or otherwise** — stand-by power to FSI **or a fireman’s lift** on loss of normal supply. Generator room / fuel / acoustic / exhaust are early massing items. |
| **(g) MVAC auto cut-off** | Only if MVAC (incl. AC) is provided **and** forms an **integral part** of the building/part — limit smoke spread through the system. |
| **(h) Other FSI per 2012 Code** | Catch-all — do not assume (a)–(g) is exhaustive. |

Detailed specs for (a)–(g) are in the **2012 Code**.

#### 5.2 Fire safety construction — provide / improve (Sch. 1 s. 2)

| Theme | Directed works | SD freeze |
|---|---|---|
| **MOE** | Improve exit arrangements on **storeys** and at **ground storeys** | Travel / exit width / ground discharge / dead-end clean-up on typical floors and G/F |
| **Means of access** | Provide **fireman’s lifts** | Shaft, lobby, door FR, secondary power, firefighter controls — often the hardest old-IB insert |
| **FRC — exits/stairs** | Protect exit routes and staircases with FR separating walls; provide **fire doors** | Core enclosure upgrades; replace non-FR lobby doors |
| **FRC — external** | Improve FR of external walls; protect openings to inhibit spread to **adjoining buildings** | Party-wall / opening / spandrel strategy vs neighbours |
| **FRC — internal** | FR separation between **different parts** of the building | Unit-to-unit / use-to-use compartmentation |
| **Basements** | Provide **smoke vents** to basements | Vent area / path / street discharge — early basement planning |

Detail → **FS Code 2011 (Oct 2015 version)**.

#### 5.3 Owner SD plant / core checklist
- Sprinkler tank + pump room + street inlet
- FH/HR risers and coverage
- MFA / emergency lighting / exit signs in common parts
- Secondary power sized for FSI **and** fireman’s lift
- Fireman’s lift (new or upgraded) + FR lobby
- Stair/exit FR enclosure + fire doors
- External-wall FR / opening protection
- Inter-unit / inter-use FR separation
- Basement smoke vents
- Building-wide FSI integration across exclusive + common parts

### 6. Schedule 2 — occupier package (unit / tenancy fit-out)

#### 6.1 Fire compartment definition (Sch. 2 s. 1)
Space enclosed on **all sides** by fire barriers (or appropriate construction) meeting FRR in **FS Code 2011 (Oct 2015)**.

#### 6.2 FSI — provide or improve (Sch. 2 s. 2)

| Item | Gate / content |
|---|---|
| **Emergency lighting** | Within the **occupied area** — evacuate that area on power failure |
| **MVAC auto cut-off** | System provided in occupied area **and serves only that area**; **and** either capacity **> 1 m³/s** **or** serves **more than one fire compartment** within the occupied area — limit smoke via the system |

Specs → **FSD CoP 2012**.

**SD takeaway for tenants / fit-out:**
- Demised-area emergency lighting is an occupier (or vacant-owner) item even if common-area lighting is owner Sch. 1
- Local AC / MVAC serving only one unit can still trigger cut-off if > 1 m³/s or multi-compartment
- Unit subdivision that creates multiple fire compartments can pull in cut-off where a single compartment would not

### 7. Escalation ladder — full architect / client risk map (ss. 13–32)

#### 7.1 Magistrate fire safety compliance order (ss. 13–16)
- Available after conviction for failing to comply with a direction (s. 11)
- On enforcement authority’s application; owner/occupier entitled to be heard
- Must set reasonable compliance time limit
- **Replaces** the fire safety direction
- May be varied or revoked on application of authority or owner/occupier (both entitled to be heard)
- Ceases when certificate of compliance issued **or** order revoked; if certificate issued, authority must notify magistrate’s clerk and serve copy of notification on owner/occupier
- Non-compliance → Level 5 fine + **$5,000 per day** (or part-day) after time limit (**no** “reasonable excuse” limb in s. 16 — stricter than direction stage)

#### 7.2 Prohibition order — when and how (ss. 17–18)
Authority may apply to District Court for prohibition of occupation of building/part if direction **or** compliance-order measures not complied with.

| Process rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Timing | Only **after** compliance time limit has expired |
| Pre-notice | ≥ **7 days’** notice to owner/occupier |
| Posting | Copy of notice posted conspicuously inside building/part **or** at/near each entrance |
| Court must be satisfied of **all** | (a) failure to comply; (b) reasonable to expect compliance; (c) time allowed was reasonable; (d) order reasonable **and necessary**; (e) **substantial fire risks** if occupied |

#### 7.3 Effect while prohibition order is in force (s. 19)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Occupation ban | **No person** may occupy except (i) person with written permission under s. 19(2), or (ii) authorized officer on duty |
| Works permission | Authority may permit occupation **subject to conditions** solely to implement measures needed for discharge/revocation — cancellable anytime |
| Secure against entry | Owner **and** occupier must take **all practicable measures** to secure against entry by anyone else |

#### 7.4 When prohibition order bites / dies (s. 22)

| Event | Timing |
|---|---|
| Comes into force (no appeal) | **29th day** after service of copy on owner **and** occupier |
| Comes into force (appeal) | Day after appeal finally rejected or withdrawn |
| Court acceleration | Court may order force from **date of service** or any date within **28 days** of service |
| Service date | If owner and occupier served on different dates → **last** of those dates; posting under s. 24(1)(a) does **not** count as the service that starts the clock |
| Ceases | Earliest of: discharge (s. 20); revocation (s. 21); or building/part **ceases to exist** |

#### 7.5 Getting out of a prohibition order (ss. 20–21, 26–28)

| Path | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Discharge** | Authority issues certificate of compliance → must ASAP apply to District Court to discharge; court **must** discharge unless special grounds not to |
| **Revocation by owner/occupier** | If authority rejects certificate request **or** fails to issue certificate within **28 days** of request notice → apply to District Court; notify authority within **7 days** of application (personal delivery or post); court may make any order it considers appropriate |
| **Certificate request** | While direction or compliance order in force, owner/occupier may request certificate by written notice (personal delivery or post) |
| **Certificate issue** | If satisfied of compliance, authority must issue ASAP — on request **or** own initiative |
| **Rejection** | Must give written reasons ASAP |

#### 7.6 Criminal exposure on prohibition (ss. 23–25)

| Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Occupy in breach of s. 19(1) without reasonable excuse | **$250,000** + **3 years** + **$25,000/day** |
| Fail to secure against entry (s. 19(4)) without reasonable excuse | Level 4 + **6 months** |
| Remove / deface / interfere with posted order copy | Level 2 |
| — | Police inspector+ may **remove** persons apparently contravening / about to contravene and **prevent re-entry** |

#### 7.7 Land Registry registration — transaction killer (ss. 29–31)
Authority **may** register by memorial against the land register:
- fire safety compliance order; and/or
- prohibition order  

If registered, authority **must** also register within **1 month** of the instrument date:
- variations / revocations of compliance orders; certificates of compliance; discharge orders; subsequent s. 21(4) orders  

**Against an OC:** compliance order / variation treated as made against **each owner individually**; certificate treated as issued to **each owner individually**.

**SD / acquisition takeaway:** Search Land Registry + BD/FSD published lists before IB purchase or major A&A commitment.

#### 7.8 Public publication (s. 32)
Authority may publish (website or otherwise) direction/order info including serial number, address, date, and **compliance status**.

| Trigger | Liability snapshot |
|---|---|
| Fail direction (s. 11) | Level 4 + **$2,500/day** |
| Fail compliance order (s. 16) | Level 5 + **$5,000/day** |
| Occupy under prohibition (s. 23) | **$250k** + **3 yrs** + **$25k/day** |
| Fail to secure prohibited premises (s. 23) | Level 4 + 6 months |
| Interfere with posted order (s. 24) | Level 2 |
| False/refuse owner ID info (s. 41) | Level 4 |
| Unlawful disclosure of official info (s. 42) | Level 5 + 6 months |
| Obstruct authorized officer (s. 43) | Level 4 + 6 months |

**Corporate / partnership / OC management** (s. 46): directors, persons concerned in management, or partners can also commit the offence if consent/connivance or neglect/omission.

**Prosecution deadline** (s. 47): within **12 months** from discovery / notice to enforcement authority (replaces Cap. 227 s. 26 default).

### 9. Cap 636 vs Cap 502 / Cap 572 — pick the right statute

| Ordinance | Typical stock | Distinctive gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Cap 636** | Pre–**1 Mar 1987** **industrial** buildings | Whole building; industrial construction use + age; **no** 230 m² PCP list |
| **Cap 502** | Prescribed commercial premises (Schedule 1 uses + **> 230 m²**, any age) **and/or** pre-1987 **commercial** buildings | Banking, betting, jewelry+security area, supermarket/dept store, arcade; or commercial-building definition |
| **Cap 572** | Composite / domestic-related fire safety improvement buildings | Separate composite regime |

| Cap 636 codes | Cap 502 codes (for contrast) |
|---|---|
| FSD CoP **2012**; FS Code **2011 (Oct 2015)** | FSD CoP **1994**; MOE **1996**; FRC **1996**; MOA **1995** |

**Whole-building Cap 502 or 572 → Cap 636 off.** Confirm which statute BD/FSD are actually running before pricing the upgrade package.

| Discipline | Schedule reference |
|---|---|
| FSI | FSD CoP **2012** |
| Passive / MOE / FRC / access construction detail | FS Code **2011 (October 2015 version)** |

Secretary for Security may Gazette-amend Schedule code references **subject to LegCo approval**; notice cannot take effect before Gazette date.

**SD takeaway:** Cap 636 tracks **2011/2012/2015** standards — closer to current practice than Cap 502’s older suite — but still **Schedule-locked**, not “latest FS Code PDF on BD website today.”

### 11. Service / evidence notes that affect programme (ss. 48–53)

Directions and orders can be served personally, by registered post, left with adult occupier, posted conspicuously inside, by fax, or by email (with special rules for HK / non-HK companies). Deemed service dates:
- personal / left / posted → **day after**
- post → **second working day** after posting
- fax / email → **day after** sending  

Building plans showing Cap 636 applies, and authority correspondence, are among documents that can be certified as true copies for court use (s. 52) — age/use classification disputes will lean on approved plans.

### 12. Schematic design checklist

1. **Age the IB** against **1 Mar 1987** (first BA submission / construction without plans).  
2. **Confirm original construction use** as factory / industrial undertaking / godown / warehouse / bulk storage / similar.  
3. **Check Cap 502 / Cap 572** — if either applies to the **whole** building, Cap 636 is out.  
4. **Search Land Registry + published BD/FSD lists** for existing directions, compliance orders, prohibition orders, certificates.  
5. **Map ownership:** single owner vs stratified units / OC — budget FSI **integration** across exclusive + common parts.  
6. **Owner works (Sch. 1):** sprinklers; FH/HR; MFA; common emergency lighting; exit signs; secondary power (FSI + fireman’s lift); integral-MVAC cut-off; fireman’s lifts; exit FR + fire doors; external-wall FR/openings; inter-part FR separation; basement smoke vents; other 2012 Code FSI.  
7. **Occupier works (Sch. 2):** emergency lighting in demised area; local MVAC cut-off if > 1 m³/s or multi-compartment.  
8. **Space-proof plant early:** sprinkler tank/pump, generator, FH inlet, fireman’s lift shaft/lobby/overrun, basement smoke vents.  
9. **If upgrades look structurally / technically impossible** → engage BD/FSD early on s. 7 alternatives + possible s. 12 advisory committee; keep RSE/fire-engineer evidence ready for “reasonable excuse” / unreasonableness arguments.  
10. **Programme risk:** direction → compliance order → prohibition (occupation kill) → Land Registry stain; certificate of compliance is the clean exit.  
11. **Design to FSD CoP 2012 + FS Code 2011 (Oct 2015)** as cited — not Cap 502’s 1994–96 suite, and not an unapproved later FS Code edition.  
12. **Vacant floors:** owner still wears occupier liability (s. 4) — include Sch. 2 in vacant-stock upgrade scopes.

### 13. What this Ordinance does **not** replace

| Still govern (as applicable) | Role |
|---|---|
| Cap. 123 + current FS Code for **new** works / BA submissions | New building / major A&A approval path |
| Cap. 59 Factories & Industrial Undertakings / licensing | Operational industrial safety — separate from Cap 636 |
| Cap. 502 / Cap. 572 | Displace Cap 636 if whole-building application |
| Town planning / lease / IB revitalisation policy | Use conversion still needs PlanD / LandsD / BD change-of-use logic |
| Cap. 344 building management | OC formation/management — but Cap 636 registration against OC binds each owner |
