# Cap 502 — Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 13 December 2024 | Cap. 502

> Scope note: Cap 502 is a **retrofit / upgrade statute** for (1) **prescribed commercial premises** of named high-risk uses over **230 m²**, and (2) **pre–1 March 1987 specified commercial buildings**. It is **not** the FS Code for new BO submissions, and **not** Cap. 572 (composite buildings) or Cap. 502A regs. At SD on old commercial stock or PCP change-of-use: classify PCP vs SCB → freeze owner vs occupier upgrade package → size sprinklers, FH/HR, MOE/FRC/MOA upgrades against the **locked 1994–96** codes cited in the Schedules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 502 applies to **prescribed commercial premises** (Schedule 1 uses with total floor area **> 230 m²**, in buildings of any age — including post–2 May 1997) and to **specified commercial buildings** listed by definition in Schedule 4 (commercial buildings whose BA plans were first submitted, or which were constructed without plans, **on or before 1 March 1987**).

It lets BD (planning / design / construction) and FSD (fire service installations) issue directions forcing owners and occupiers to upgrade MOE, FRC, firefighting access, sprinklers, alarms, emergency lighting, and related FSI — with District Court power to **ban the prescribed use** or **prohibit occupation** if upgrades are not done.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 0. Two regimes — lock which one applies before SD (ss. 3–4)

| Regime | What it hits | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Prescribed commercial premises (PCP)** | A building **or part** used / proposed for a Schedule 1 activity | Schedule 1 use **and** total floor area **> 230 m²** |
| **Specified commercial building (SCB)** | Whole **commercial building** (and any unit / part) | Schedule 4 age cut-off (**≤ 1 Mar 1987**) |

**SD kill switches:**
- PCP and SCB can **stack** on the same building (s. 4(2)–(3)) — do not assume one disapplies the other
- Cap 502 applies to PCP in **existing** buildings built **on or before or after** 2 May 1997 (s. 4(1)(a))
- New BO / FS Code compliance ≠ automatic Cap 502 clearance for old stock or PCP change-of-use

---

### 1. What counts as a “commercial building” (s. 3)

**In:** whole **non-domestic** building of one or more levels (incl. basements / underground parking), constructed or used for **office, business, trade or entertainment**.

**Out (exclusive use of whole building):**
| Excluded exclusive use |
|---|
| Hotel / serviced apartment / guest-house / similar |
| Kindergarten / school / college / university / similar |
| Hospital / clinic / medical / rehab centre / similar |
| Carpark |
| Elderly home / PWD home / child care / nursery / social services centre |
| Factory / industrial undertaking |
| Godown / warehouse / bulk storage |
| Utilities / power station / sub-station |
| Cinema / theatre |

**Also out:** building **partly** constructed or used for **domestic or industrial** purposes.

**Age gate (same as Schedule 4):** plans first submitted under Cap. 123A reg. 29 **on or before 1 Mar 1987**, **or** constructed on/before that date with **no** such plans submitted.

**Domestic** (for this exclusion) = human habitation / family dwelling — **excludes** hotel, serviced apartment, guest-house, dormitory, elderly/PWD homes, child care, nursery or similar.

---

### 2. Prescribed commercial activities — Schedule 1 (the PCP use list)

Only these uses can be PCP (Secretary for Security may amend by Gazette — s. 24):

| # | Activity | Design flag |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | **Banking** (not merchant banking) | Retail banking halls / branches |
| (b) | **Off-course betting** | HKJC betting centres |
| (c) | **Jewelry / goldsmith** with a **security area** | Security partition (e.g. bullet-proof glass) segregating staff/secure zone from public |
| (d) | **Supermarket / hypermarket / department store** | Dept store = wide variety of goods in separate departments |
| (e) | **Shopping arcade** | Includes passageway between shops **and** ceiling above that passageway |

**SD takeaway:** Ordinary office / restaurant / small shop alone is **not** PCP unless it is one of the five above **and** clears the area test.

---

### 3. The 230 m² gate — how floor area is measured (s. 3(2)–(5))

PCP only if total floor area of the building **or part** **exceeds 230 m²**.

**Include (GFA-style):**
- Area within external walls at **each** floor level (**including below ground**)
- Balcony area
- Thickness of external walls and balconies

**Disregard:**
- Floor used **only** for parking or loading/unloading motor vehicles
- Floor occupied **only** by lift / escalator / AC / heating / cooling (or similar) machinery serving the building or part

**Shopping arcade special rule (s. 3(3)):** sum of **all shops + any passageway between shops** — if that sum **> 230 m²**, the arcade (building or part) is PCP.

**Private / invite-only carve-out (s. 3(6)) — not PCP if all three:**
1. Public access only by **express invitation**; **and**
2. Physically separated by **fire-resistant** wall / floor / ceiling from parts with general public access, **with separate means of egress**; **and**
3. That separate egress **does not pass through** the public parts

---

### 4. Who enforces what (s. 3 “enforcement authority”)

| Subject | Authority |
|---|---|
| Planning, design, construction of PCP or SCB | **Director of Buildings** |
| Fire service installation or equipment | **Director of Fire Services** |

Directions may be issued **jointly**; joint directions may only be amended/withdrawn jointly (s. 5(6)).

---

### 5. Owner vs occupier — who must design for what

| Target | Instrument | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| PCP **owner** | Fire safety direction (s. 5(1)) | **Schedule 2** |
| PCP **occupier** | Fire safety direction (s. 5(2)) | **Schedule 3** |
| SCB **owner** | Fire safety **improvement** direction (s. 5(1A)) | **Schedule 5** (or other appropriate measures in place of any listed requirement) |
| SCB **occupier** | Fire safety improvement direction (s. 5(2A)) | **Schedule 6** |

**Owner** = Cap. 123 meaning. **Occupier** = person occupying (owner or under lease/licence); for PCP also includes person carrying on/managing the prescribed activity or managing as agent. For vacant SCB, occupier defaults to **owner**.

---

### 6. Schedule 2 — PCP owner package (the AP / RSE core)

#### 6.1 Fire service installations (owner)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Sprinklers** | Install automatic sprinkler system on the premises |
| **MVAC auto cut-off** | Only if the mechanical ventilating system is an **integral part of the building** **and** also serves **other premises** in that building |
| **Emergency lighting (arcade)** | In **common parts** of the arcade (power-failure evacuation) |
| **Manual fire alarm** | One or more alarms to alert occupants / visitors |
| **FSD CoP 1994** | Also the requirements in *Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment **1994*** |

#### 6.2 Construction (owner) — locked to 1995/96 BD codes

Owner can be directed to provide:
- Adequate **means of escape**
- Adequate **means of access** for firefighting and rescue
- Measures to **inhibit fire spread** and protect **structural integrity** of the host building

**Cited codes (Schedules — not automatically updated to FS Code 2011):**
- *Means of Escape in Case of Fire **1996***
- *Fire Resisting Construction **1996***
- *Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue **1995***

> s. 25: a substituted/amended code applies to Cap 502 **only** after LegCo resolution + Gazette amendment of the Schedule reference. For Cap 502 works, design to the **cited edition**, not today’s FS Code, unless Schedule/resolution says otherwise.

---

### 7. Schedule 3 — PCP occupier package (fit-out / tenant)

| Item | When / what |
|---|---|
| **MVAC auto cut-off** | System does **not** serve other premises; **and** capacity **> 1 m³/s** **or** serves **> 1 fire compartment** within the premises |
| **Emergency lighting** | Arcade: each **separately occupied** area. Non-arcade: within the premises |
| **Portable extinguishers** | ≥ **1 per 100 m²** floor area (or part) — **waived** if premises have a **hose reel system** |

**Fire compartment** (Sch. 3): part separated by walls + floor + ceiling meeting FRC **1996** fire resistance.

---

### 8. Schedule 4 / 5 / 6 — pre-1987 commercial buildings (SCB)

#### 8.1 Schedule 4 definition
SCB = any **commercial building** (s. 3 definition) whose plans were first submitted to BA **on or before 1 Mar 1987**, or constructed on/before that date without such plans. Secretary for Security may amend Schedule 4 by Gazette (s. 24A).

#### 8.2 Schedule 5 — SCB **owner** (building-wide upgrades)

**FSI / equipment (to FSD CoP 1994 detail):**
| System | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Emergency lighting in **common parts** | Evacuation on power failure |
| MVAC auto cut-off | Limit smoke spread — only if system is integral to building **and** serves other separately occupied areas/parts |
| Manual fire alarm | Alert building occupants |
| **Fire hydrant + hose reel** | Water supply for firefighting |
| **Automatic sprinkler** (with or without direct FSD link) | Control spread + alarm |
| Other FSI per **FSD CoP 1994** | As directed |

Unit owners may be required to **integrate** unit FSI with building FSI.

**Construction upgrades (to MOE 1996 / FRC 1996 / MOA 1995):**

| Theme | Typical directed works |
|---|---|
| **MOE** | Widen / add stairs; FR separating walls to exits/stairs; fix exit arrangement (rooms / storeys / ground storey / stair access / direct or travel distance); provide fire doors |
| **Means of access** | Upgrade **≥ 1 existing lift** **or** install a **new** lift to **fireman’s lift** standard |
| **FRC** | Improve FR of external walls + protect openings (adjoining buildings); FR separation between parts; **smoke vents to basements** |

#### 8.3 Schedule 6 — SCB **occupier** (unit)

| Item | Gate |
|---|---|
| Emergency lighting | Each separately occupied area |
| MVAC auto cut-off | System does **not** serve other separately occupied areas; **and** capacity **> 1 m³/s** **or** serves **> 1 fire compartment** |

Specs → FSD CoP **1994**.

### 9. Escalation path architects must warn clients about (ss. 5–13)

| Step | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Direction** (s. 5) | Written; reasonable compliance period; non-compliance → Level 4 fine + **$2,500/day** |
| **Magistrate compliance order** (s. 6) | Replaces direction; Level 5 + **$5,000/day** |
| **Use restriction order** (s. 7–8) — PCP only | District Court may **prohibit prescribed commercial activity** on the premises |
| **Prohibition order** (s. 7A–8A) — SCB | District Court may **prohibit occupation** of unit/part if substantial fire risk |
| Default effect | Usually **28 days** after service (court may accelerate); appeal suspends until finally rejected/withdrawn |
| Contravention of restriction / prohibition | Fine **$250,000** + **3 years** + **$25,000/day** |
| Exit | Certificate of compliance (s. 12) → District Court discharge; or apply for revocation (s. 13) |

**Reasonable excuse** for missing a direction can include structural integrity risk or technology not reasonably available (s. 5(8)) — flag early if upgrades threaten structure.

Enforcement authorities may **publish** direction/order address, date, and compliance status (s. 13A — added 2024).

---

### 10. Schematic design checklist

1. **Age the building:** plans / OP / construction vs **1 Mar 1987** → SCB candidate?  
2. **Map uses:** any Schedule 1 activity proposed or existing? Jewelry with security glass? Arcade?  
3. **Run the 230 m² test** (include basements/balconies/wall thickness; exclude pure parking/plant; for arcades sum shops + passages).  
4. **Test the invite-only carve-out** before assuming VIP / private banking floors are outside PCP.  
5. **Split owner vs occupier scopes** (building common FSI / MOE / fireman’s lift vs tenant MVAC cut-off / emergency lighting / extinguishers).  
6. **Budget Cap 502 packages against 1994 FSD + 1995/96 BD codes** cited in Schedules — do not silently substitute FS Code 2011 unless Cap 502 Schedules have been updated via s. 25.  
7. **Arcade:** owner emergency lighting in **common parts**; occupier lighting in each **demised** area; sprinklers on owner side.  
8. **Pre-1987 commercial:** expect FH/HR, sprinklers, fireman’s lift upgrade, stair/exit/FR works, basement smoke vents.  
9. **Dual exposure:** PCP shop inside pre-1987 commercial block → Schedules 2/3 **and** 5/6 may both apply.  
10. **Change of use to supermarket / bank / arcade / betting / secured jewelry over 230 m²** in any age building → treat Cap 502 as a live SD constraint, not only a “pre-87” issue.

| Still govern (as applicable) | Role |
