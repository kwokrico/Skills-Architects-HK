# Cap 121 — Buildings Ordinance (Application to the New Territories) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 2 August 2012 | Cap. 121

> Scope note: Cap 121 does **not** invent a separate design code. It (1) applies **Cap 123 to the New Territories** (excluding New Kowloon), then (2) creates a **LandsD Certificate of Exemption** track that switches off named Cap 123 controls — **only if** the finished building stays inside the Schedule dimensional envelopes **and** certificate conditions are met. At SD on NT village / small-house / exempt-house / agri / replacement work: decide **Cap 123 full track vs Cap 121 exempt track** before locking storeys, height, roofed-over area, wall build-up, balconies, stairhoods, tanks, party-wall subdivision, SF and drainage packages.

> Companion distinction: **Cap 121** = statutory exemption envelopes (this note). **NTEH / Small House Policy** = LandsD administrative policy (often quoted as 3 storeys / 65.03 m² / 8.23 m / 66.7% site coverage). Policy site-coverage and “per floor” marketing figures are **not** Cap 121 text — verify both layers separately.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 121 applies the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) to the **New Territories excluding New Kowloon**, and provides for **certificates of exemption** from specified Cap 123 provisions for building works, site formation works and drainage works for certain NT buildings: new non-industrial housing, community buildings, agricultural buildings on agricultural land, replacement of temporary structures, plus a discretionary general power for other NT building works.

Exemption effect is **conditional and personal**: the finished building must not exceed the Schedule Part matching the certificate type; works must be by or on behalf of the **person named** on the certificate; and any Director conditions under s.9 must be complied with. Exceed any of those → Cap 123 controls remain (or snap back) fully on.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. SD gate — Cap 123 full track vs Cap 121 exempt track (ss.2–3)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Geography (s.2)** | “New Territories” **does not include New Kowloon**. New Kowloon sites → Cap 121 exemption track **unavailable**; full Cap 123 |
| **Default application (s.3)** | Subject to Part 3, **Cap 123 applies to the NT** — exemption is an opt-in certificate regime, not an automatic village free-pass |
| **No certificate** | Design and submit under **full Cap 123** (AP/RSE, plans, consent, OP, regs, MBIS/MWIS where applicable) |
| **Exempt track** | Valid certificate + inside Schedule dimensions + s.9 conditions complied with + works by/for named person → named Cap 123 sections/regs **do not apply** to the certified works/building |
| **Issuing authority** | **Director of Lands** (not Building Authority). District Lands Officer may exercise Director powers **except** the general s.4 discretionary power (s.11) |

**SD action before freezing massing:**

1. Confirm site is NT proper (not New Kowloon).  
2. Map intended use to s.5(a)/(b)/(c)/(d) or s.4 discretionary.  
3. Choose Schedule Part 1 / 2 / 3 envelope and measure with Cap 121 definitions (not Cap 123 GFA habits).  
4. Decide whether SF and drainage need separate s.6 certificates or stay on Cap 123.  
5. If any Schedule limit will be exceeded → commit to **full Cap 123** from day one (do not design “almost exempt”).

### 2. Defined terms that control the design (s.2) — do not skim

Cap 121 measurement language is **not** the same as Cap 123 GFA / SC / BHR practice. Wrong definition = silent loss of exemption.

#### 2.1 Core defined terms

| Term | Cap 121 meaning | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **balcony / canopy** | Same meaning as Building (Planning) Regulations (Cap. **123F**) reg. 2 | Use Cap 123F definitions when testing the roofed-over-area exclusion (s.2(5)) |
| **building works** | Cap 123 s.2 meaning **minus** site formation works, drainage works, and construction of **seawalls** | Building-works certificate alone does **not** cover SF, drainage, or seawalls |
| **certificate of exemption** | Certificate issued under this Ordinance | Must exist in writing (s.8); keep certified copy for title / due diligence (s.10) |
| **… in respect of new housing** | Issued under **s.5(a)** | Schedule **Part 1** envelope |
| **… in respect of community use** | Issued under **s.5(b)** | Schedule **Part 1** envelope |
| **… in respect of agricultural use** | Issued under **s.5(c)** | Schedule **Part 2** envelope |
| **… in respect of replacement housing** | Issued under **s.5(d)** | Schedule **Part 3** envelope |
| **Director** | Director of Lands + persons authorized under s.11 | LandsD / DLO interface — not BD for the certificate itself |
| **District** | As in District Councils Ordinance (Cap. 547) | Relevant to which DLO can act |
| **height** | Perpendicular height from **lowest point at ground level** to highest point of roof (subject to s.2(4) exclusions) | Slope / stepped sites: measure from the **lowest** ground touching the building — not average grade |
| **New Territories** | **Excludes New Kowloon** | Geography kill-switch |
| **party wall** | Load-bearing separating wall meeting continuity + no-opening rules in s.2(1) | Required for “separate building” treatment of subdivided domestic blocks (s.2(3)) |
| **roofed-over area** | Area within external faces of main structural walls (incl. party walls) **plus** projections listed in s.2(1), subject to s.2(5) exclusions | Closer to a **covered footprint + projections** measure than Cap 123 GFA |

#### 2.2 Words borrowed from Cap 123 (s.2(2))

Any word/phrase not defined in Cap 121 s.2(1) that is defined in Cap 123 s.2 takes the Cap 123 meaning (e.g. site formation works, drainage works, building — unless Cap 121 overrides).

#### 2.3 Height exclusions — what you may ignore (s.2(4))

In finding the “highest point of roof”, **ignore only**:

| Feature | Hard caps (all must be met) | If you breach any cap… |
|---|---|---|
| **One stairhood** | Roof area ≤ **7.44 m²** (~80 ft²); height ≤ **2.14 m** (~7 ft); erected and used **solely** to protect a stairway used to gain roof access from rain/sun | Counts fully toward building height |
| **Parapet on the roof** | Height ≤ **1.22 m** (~4 ft) | Excess / whole parapet may count — stay ≤1.22 m |
| **One water storage tank** | Roof area ≤ **2 m²**; height ≤ **1.22 m**; installed on the roof **other than on a stairhood** | Counts toward height; tank **on** stairhood always counts |

**Design traps:**

- **Second** stairhood or tank → not ignored.  
- Stairhood used as plant room / store / covered roof room → fails “solely” protection test → counts.  
- Oversize stairhood (area or height) → counts.  
- Decorative / screening structures, AC platforms, roof features beyond the three carve-outs → count.

#### 2.4 Roofed-over area — what is in / out (s.2(1) + s.2(5))

**In (counts):**

- Area enclosed by external faces of **main structural walls** (includes party walls).  
- Plus area of any **balcony, stairway, verandah, porch, canopy or any other projection** from the building — unless carved out by s.2(5).

**Out (may exclude under s.2(5)) — all conditions mandatory:**

| May exclude | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Area of **not more than 2 balconies** and **one canopy** | (a) they **all** project from the **same side** of the building; (b) project ≤ **1.22 m** (~4 ft); (c) they are **not enclosed** |

**Design traps:**

| Move | Effect on roofed-over area |
|---|---|
| Enclosed balcony / “winter garden” | **Include** — exclusion lost |
| Balconies on two different façades | **Include** — “same side” fails |
| Projection > 1.22 m | **Include** that projection |
| Third balcony, or second canopy | Extra items **include** |
| Side stair projection / porch / verandah not qualifying as the allowed balconies/canopy | **Include** |
| Eaves / other projections | **Include** unless they fit the balcony/canopy exclusion |

**SD habit:** Draw the main-wall footprint first; add every projection; subtract only the qualifying same-side ≤1.22 m unenclosed 2-balcony + 1-canopy set; compare to Schedule limit.

### 3. What a certificate actually switches off (s.7) — Cap 123 map

#### 3.1 Preconditions for s.7(1) protection

All must hold:

1. Certificate issued under Cap 121.  
2. Building dimensions **not greater** than the matching Schedule Part (s.7(2) kill switch).  
3. Compliance with any conditions imposed under **s.9**.  
4. Works carried out **by or on behalf of the person named** in the certificate.

#### 3.2 Disapplied Cap 123 provisions

| Works covered by the certificate | Cap 123 provisions that **do not apply** |
|---|---|
| **Building works** or **site formation works** for the certified building | ss.**4, 9, 9AA, 14, 21, 30, 30A, 30B, 30C, 30D, 30E, 30F** **and** the **regulations** made under Cap 123 |
| **Drainage works** for the certified building | Everything above **plus s.28** |

#### 3.3 Practical meaning of each switched-off Cap 123 theme

| Cap 123 | Theme | If exempt — SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **s.4** | Appointment / duties of AP, RSE, RGBC, etc. | No Cap 123 statutory AP/RSE appointment for the certified works |
| **s.9 / s.9AA** | Registered contractors; supervision / prescribed building professionals stream | Outside normal BD registered-contractor / supervision track for those works |
| **s.14** | Consent to commence building works | No BA consent under Cap 123 for certified works |
| **s.21** | Occupation permit | No Cap 123 OP for the certified building |
| **ss.30, 30A–30F** | Prescribed inspection / repair / window regimes (MBIS/MWIS family) | Those Cap 123 regimes do not apply to the certified building |
| **Cap 123 regulations** | B(P)R, construction, drainage regs, etc. under Cap 123 | Do **not** apply to certified works/building under s.7 |
| **s.28** | Drainage | Only if a **drainage** certificate of exemption also covers those works |

#### 3.4 What is **not** switched off by Cap 121

| Still live | Why it matters at SD |
|---|---|
| Cap 123 sections **other than** those listed in s.7 | Do not assume “whole Cap 123 is off” |
| Lease / Small House Policy / indigenous villager entitlement | LandsD policy layer — separate from Cap 121 |
| OZP / planning permission / s.16 | PlanD |
| Other ordinances (e.g. fire safety statutes that carve Cap 121 in or out) | Cap **572** does **not** apply to buildings constructed under Cap 121 — confirm construction path on existing stock |
| Certificate **conditions** (s.9) | Often the real safety/health design brief once Cap 123 regs are off |
| Works for a person **not** named on the certificate | No s.7 protection |
| Seawalls | Outside Cap 121 “building works”; not covered by a building-works certificate alone |

#### 3.5 Kill switch — oversize building (s.7(2))

| Certificate type | Must not exceed |
|---|---|
| New housing (s.5(a)), community use (s.5(b)), or discretionary s.4 | Schedule **Part 1** |
| Agricultural use (s.5(c)) | Schedule **Part 2** |
| Replacement housing (s.5(d)) | Schedule **Part 3** |

If the building is of **greater dimensions** than described in the matching Part → s.7(1) **does not apply** → Cap 123 (including the listed sections and regs) applies to that building as if there were no effective exemption.

**SD rule:** Design to the Schedule with contingency margin. “Slightly over” is not a soft LandsD negotiation under Cap 121 — it is a statutory loss of exemption effect.

### 4. Certificate types — who gets what (ss.4–6, 8–11)

#### 4.1 Mandatory issue — Director **shall** issue (s.5)

| Certificate | Statutory trigger | Schedule envelope | Typical SD use | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New housing** s.5(a) | Building by any person; used for **non-industrial** purposes | Part 1 | Classic NT exempt / village / small-house track | “Non-industrial” is the use test — industrial / workshop / godown use kills this path |
| **Community use** s.5(b) | Built by a **community organization** for community use | Part 1 | Village office, ancestral hall, community facility | Confirm the client qualifies as a community organization **before** SD |
| **Agricultural use** s.5(c) | On **agricultural land**; used **solely** for agricultural purposes | Part 2 | Farm store, agri shed, livestock building | Dual use (agri + domestic / retail) fails “solely”; land must be agricultural |
| **Replacement housing** s.5(d) | Replacement of a **temporary structure** in the NT | Part 3 | Replacing recognized temporary housing | Very small envelope; confirm the temporary structure status with LandsD |

#### 4.2 Discretionary issue — Director **may** issue (s.4)

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scope | **Any** building works in the NT |
| vs s.5 | Broader eligibility, but **not** a “shall issue” right |
| Envelope for exemption effect | Still Schedule **Part 1** (s.7(2)(a)(iii)) |
| Who can issue | **Director of Lands only** — DLO **cannot** exercise s.4 power (s.11) |

**SD implication:** Non-standard NT building works that do not fit s.5 still need Director-level buy-in; do not assume DLO can grant a general s.4 certificate.

#### 4.3 Site formation & drainage add-ons (s.6)

Where a building-works certificate already exists under s.4 or s.5, Director **may also** issue:

| Add-on certificate | Covers | Without it… |
|---|---|---|
| Site formation works | SF package for that project | SF stays under Cap 123 (and Cap 121’s own definition already excludes SF from “building works”) |
| Drainage works | Drainage package | Drainage stays under Cap 123; s.28 remains live |

**SD takeaway:** Three certificates may be needed (building + SF + drainage). Building exemption ≠ automatic SF/drainage exemption. On sloping NT sites, budget SF early.

#### 4.4 Form, conditions, proof, delegation (ss.8–11)

| Section | Rule | Architect / client action |
|---|---|---|
| **s.8** | Certificate must be **in writing**, form determined by Director | Do not rely on verbal / email “in principle” alone for statutory protection |
| **s.9** | Director may impose conditions relating to **safety and health** or other reasonable conditions; conditions recorded on the certificate | Read conditions as the **design brief** once Cap 123 regs are off; non-compliance loses s.7 protection |
| **s.10** | Certified true copy by Director / DLO is receivable as evidence; prima facie proof of contents | On acquisition / A&A of existing NT stock, obtain and file the certificate copy |
| **s.11** | DLO may exercise Director powers **except s.4** | Route discretionary s.4 cases to Director; routine s.5 / s.6 / conditions may be DLO |

#### 4.5 Transitional stock (s.13)

Works that were under the repealed Cap **322** Ordinance/Regulations and were **in course** at Cap 121 commencement continue under that old Cap 322 regime. Rare on new SD — relevant when auditing very old incomplete / historic NT files.

### 5. Hard dimensional envelopes — Schedule (the SD massing brief)

Imperial figures in parentheses are the historic feet/sq ft equivalents commonly used in practice; Cap 121 text is metric.

#### 5.1 Part 1 — New housing / community use / s.4 certificate

**Universal Part 1 hard cap:** when constructed, **not more than 3 storeys**, **and** one of the options below.

| Option | Height | Roofed-over area | Load-bearing wall minimums | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — taller box** | **> 7.62 m** (~25 ft) and **≤ 8.23 m** (~27 ft) | **≤ 65.03 m²** (~700 ft²) | Each LB wall: RC ≥ **175 mm**; **or** brick lowest storey ≥ **340 mm** (~13.5") **and** higher storeys ≥ **225 mm** (~9") | Need extra floor-to-floor / roof build-up within 27 ft |
| **B — larger footprint on approved plans** | **≤ 7.62 m** (~25 ft) | **≤ 92.90 m²** (~1,000 ft²) | No extra wall-thickness rules in Part 1 for this option | Only if building complies with Director **approved plans** (or consented modification) |
| **C — default lower box** | **≤ 7.62 m** (~25 ft) | **≤ 65.03 m²** (~700 ft²) | No extra wall-thickness rules in Part 1 for this option | Default village-house envelope without approved-plan bonus |

**“Approved plans” (Part 1 para. 1(2)):** plans **prepared by the Director**, or so prepared and **modified with his consent**. This is a LandsD plan path — not BA-approved Cap 123 plans.

**Part 1 SD rules of thumb:**

| Goal | Safe Cap 121 reading |
|---|---|
| Default exempt house without special LandsD plans | ≤ **3** storeys + ≤ **7.62 m** + ≤ **65.03 m²** roofed-over (Option C) |
| Need ~1,000 ft² covered footprint | Must secure Director approved plans → Option B; height still ≤ **7.62 m** |
| Need up to **8.23 m** height | Option A only; area still capped at **65.03 m²**; satisfy RC/brick thickness schedule for **every** load-bearing wall |
| 4th storey | **Kills** Part 1 regardless of height/area |

**Wall schedule note:** Part 1 wall thickness rules are written into **Option A** only. Party-wall thickness rules in s.2(3) are a **separate** test used when claiming multiple “buildings” in one block.

#### 5.2 Part 2 — Agricultural use

| Parameter | Limit |
|---|---|
| Storeys | **Single storey** only |
| Height | **≤ 4.57 m** (~15 ft) |

No separate roofed-over area figure in Part 2. Height still uses Cap 121 s.2 measurement (stairhood / parapet / tank exclusions). Multi-storey or loft marketed as a second storey → exemption effect fails.

**Use trap:** Building must be on agricultural land and used **solely** for agricultural purposes (s.5(c)). Domestic rooms, farm-stay, retail, workshop → wrong certificate path and/or lose “solely”.

#### 5.3 Part 3 — Replacement housing

| Parameter | Limit |
|---|---|
| Roofed-over area | **≤ 37.16 m²** (~400 ft²) |
| Height | **≤ 5.18 m** (~17 ft) |

No express storey count in Part 3 — height and roofed-over area are the hard caps. Still apply s.2 measurement rules. This is a **replacement of temporary structure** path (s.5(d)), not a general small-house envelope.

#### 5.4 Quick conversion board (practice memory aid)

| Metric (Cap 121) | Approx. imperial |
|---|---|
| 7.62 m | 25 ft |
| 8.23 m | 27 ft |
| 4.57 m | 15 ft |
| 5.18 m | 17 ft |
| 1.22 m | 4 ft |

### 6. Party walls — when one block = multiple Cap 121 “buildings” (s.2(3))

#### 6.1 Why this matters

Roofed-over area and Schedule limits apply **per building**. If a terrace / duplex / linked pair qualifies as **separate buildings**, each can have its own envelope. If not, the whole block is one building for Cap 121.

#### 6.2 Tests — all must pass

A domestic building divided into units by party wall(s) → each unit is a separate building **if** each such party wall:

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Load-bearing** | Must be load-bearing |
| **Function** | Forms part of a building used/constructed to separate adjoining buildings |
| **Vertical continuity** | Extends continuously from floor level of the **lowest floor** up to the **underside of the roof** |
| **Horizontal continuity** | Extends from an **external wall** to the **opposite external wall** |
| **No openings** | **No** door, doorway, arch, archway, window or other opening in it |
| **Thickness** | Meets the height-banded table below |

| Building height | Party wall minimum |
|---|---|
| **≤ 7.62 m** | Load-bearing brick ≥ **225 mm** |
| **> 7.62 m and ≤ 8.23 m** | Lowest storey brick ≥ **340 mm**; subsequent floors brick ≥ **225 mm** |
| Alternative (any height in scope) | Load-bearing RC ≥ **175 mm** |

#### 6.3 SD failure modes (collapse “separate building” claim)

| Design move | Result |
|---|---|
| Door / opening between units in the separating wall | Units merge for Cap 121 |
| Party wall stops at ceiling of top floor (not to underside of roof) | Fails continuity |
| Party wall does not run wall-to-wall | Fails continuity |
| Non-load-bearing partition only | Not a Cap 121 party wall |
| Under-thickness brick/RC | Fails thickness test |
| Sharing one roofed-over calculation across linked units without qualifying party walls | Risk of treating whole terrace as one oversize building |

### 7. Cap 121 vs NTEH / Small House Policy vs Cap 123 — do not conflate

| Layer | Controls | Typical figures | If you exceed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cap 121 Schedule** | Whether exemption effect exists | Part 1/2/3 envelopes above; **roofed-over area** of the building | Cap 123 snaps on (s.7(2)) |
| **NTEH / Small House Policy** (LandsD admin) | Whether LandsD will grant / process small-house / exempt-house entitlement | Often quoted: 3 storeys, ~65.03 m², 8.23 m, **66.7% site coverage** | Policy refusal / lease breach — separate from Cap 121 text |
| **Full Cap 123** | Default NT control when no effective exemption | GFA, SC, BHR, B(P)R, FS Code path, OP, etc. | Normal BA refusal / UBW risk |

**Critical nuance:** Cap 121 Part 1 measures **roofed-over area of the building**, not “GFA” and not expressly “per storey floor area.” Practice often aligns a 65.03 m² footprint across 3 identical floors with the Part 1 area cap — but projections, enclosed balconies, and same-side exclusion rules still decide compliance. **Site coverage 66.7% is not in Cap 121** — treat it as policy/lease, not this Ordinance.

### 8. Worked SD massing scenarios

#### 8.1 Standard 3-storey village house (Part 1 Option C)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Storeys | 3 |
| Height | ≤ 7.62 m (use stairhood ≤7.44 m² / ≤2.14 m; parapet ≤1.22 m; one tank ≤2 m² / ≤1.22 m off stairhood) |
| Roofed-over | ≤ 65.03 m² after including projections and applying same-side 2-balcony + 1-canopy exclusion only if fully qualified |
| Balconies | Prefer ≤2 unenclosed, same façade, ≤1.22 m projection |
| Certificates | Building (s.5(a)); ask for SF / drainage (s.6) if needed |

#### 8.2 Taller 27 ft house (Part 1 Option A)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Storeys | ≤ 3 |
| Height | ≤ 8.23 m but > 7.62 m |
| Roofed-over | Still ≤ 65.03 m² (no area bonus for height) |
| Walls | Every LB wall: RC ≥175 mm **or** brick 340 mm (lowest) / 225 mm (upper) |
| Risk | Extra height consumed by thick roof / deep beams / high parapet / oversized stairhood |

#### 8.3 Larger ~1,000 ft² footprint (Part 1 Option B)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Height | ≤ 7.62 m |
| Roofed-over | ≤ 92.90 m² |
| Prerequisite | Comply with Director **approved plans** (or consented modification) — lock with LandsD before SD area claims |
| Risk | Designing 92.90 m² without approved-plan status → only 65.03 m² is safe |

#### 8.4 Linked pair / terrace claiming two envelopes

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Each unit | Own Part 1 envelope |
| Separating wall | True Cap 121 party wall: LB, full height to roof soffit, wall-to-wall, **no openings**, correct thickness |
| Risk | Internal door between units → one building → combined roofed-over area likely exceeds 65.03 / 92.90 m² |

#### 8.5 Agricultural shed (Part 2)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Land / use | Agricultural land; solely agricultural |
| Form | Single storey; height ≤ 4.57 m |
| Risk | Mezzanine / domestic annex / retail counter |

#### 8.6 Replacement of temporary structure (Part 3)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Roofed-over | ≤ 37.16 m² |
| Height | ≤ 5.18 m |
| Risk | Designing a “mini village house” on Part 3 certificate |

#### 8.7 Exceeds any Schedule limit

| Action | Path |
|---|---|
| Stop exempt-track assumptions | Full **Cap 123** AP/RSE, plans, consent, OP, regs |
| Do not rely on “certificate already issued” | s.7(2) kills exemption effect for oversize buildings |

### 10. Existing NT stock — due diligence notes

| Situation | Cap 121 angle |
|---|---|
| Buying / altering an “exempt” village house | Obtain written certificate (s.8/s.10); confirm as-built still within Schedule; check s.9 conditions |
| As-built oversize vs Schedule | Treat as Cap 123 / UBW risk — exemption effect may never have attached or may be lost |
| Openings cut through party wall after completion | May collapse separate-building treatment for linked houses |
| Cap 572 upgrade pressure on old domestic stock | Cap 572 **excludes** buildings constructed under Cap 121 — verify the actual construction / exemption path before assuming Cap 572 duties |
| DMBA / lift / BFA expectations | Cap 123 regs may be off under exemption; do not assume DMBA applies via Cap 123 regs — check certificate conditions and any other applicable controls |
| Incomplete historic works started under Cap 322 | s.13 transitional — old regime may still govern those works |
