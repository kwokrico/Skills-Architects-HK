# Cap 572 — Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 13 December 2024 | Cap. 572  
(Includes Fire Safety (Buildings) (Amendment) Ordinance 2024 — Ord. 32 of 2024, in force 13 December 2024)

> Scope note: Cap 572 is an **existing-stock fire-upgrade statute**, not the new-build fire-safety design code. It forces owners (and some occupiers) of **pre–1 March 1987 composite and domestic buildings** to retrofit FSI and passive construction to Schedules 1–3. For **new buildings / new A&A under Cap 123**, design to **FS Code 2011** + current FSD Minimum FSI CoP — Cap 572 does not replace those. Sister regimes: **Cap 502** (commercial premises), **Cap 636** (industrial buildings). At SD on old stock: date-stamp the building → classify composite vs domestic → map Sch 1 / 2 / 3 → search for open directions / orders → budget cores, FH/HR, sprinklers (non-dom), fireman’s lift, FR separation, and common-part emergency lighting before locking the A&A / acquisition brief.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap 572 applies only to **composite buildings** and **domestic buildings** whose building-works plans were first submitted to BA under Cap 123A reg. 29 (or which were constructed with no such plans) **on or before 1 March 1987**. It does **not** apply to buildings constructed under Cap **121** (NT exempt houses). It also excludes from the “composite building” definition any building whose non-domestic part consists **wholly** of a factory, industrial undertaking, godown, warehouse or place of bulk storage.

Its purpose (s.2) is better fire protection for occupants, users and visitors of that pre-1987 stock — via **fire safety directions** (BD for construction; FSD for FSI), escalating to magistrate **compliance orders**, District Court **prohibition of occupation**, Land Registry registration, and (from Dec 2024) EA power to **enter, close and carry out** improvement works then recover costs + surcharges as a **legal charge** on the land.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How Cap 572 sits in the fire-control stack (do not confuse the layers)

| Layer | Instrument | What it decides | Cap 572 role |
|---|---|---|---|
| New-build / Cap 123 plan approval | **FS Code 2011** + Cap 123 s.16(1)(b) FSI certificate | MOE, FRC, means of access, Use Class for **new** works | Cap 572 does **not** set new-build DTC |
| Active FSI for new submissions | Current **FSD Minimum FSI CoP** | Sprinklers, FH/HR, MFA, emergency lighting specs for new FSI | Cap 572 Schedules still cite **1994** CoP until s.25 updates them |
| Existing commercial premises upgrade | **Cap 502** | Prescribed commercial premises improvement | Different stock — not Cap 572 |
| Existing industrial upgrade | **Cap 636** | Industrial buildings improvement | Different stock — not Cap 572 |
| Existing composite / domestic upgrade | **Cap 572** | Pre–1 Mar 1987 composite + domestic retrofit | **This Cap** |
| MBIS inspection baseline | Cap **123P** s.5(2)(c) | Cap 502 / Cap 572 completed improvement works **raise** the MBIS comparison bar | Finished Cap 572 works become the “latest” fabric standard for prescribed inspection |
| Common parts / OC | Cap **344** | OC formation, common parts, contribution | Cap 572 s.18A criminalises obstructing OC works / refusing contribution after notice |

**SD takeaway:** Cap 572 alone never “approves” a new building. On old stock it is an **upgrade / enforcement** regime that can freeze occupation, charge the title, and force spatial retrofit — often in parallel with Cap 123 A&A.

### 2. Does Cap 572 hit this building? — SD filter (ss.3–4)

| Gate | Rule — fail any = Cap 572 out |
|---|---|
| **Building type** | Must be *composite* **or** *domestic* as defined below |
| **Age / submission cut-off** | Plans first submitted under Cap 123A reg. 29 **on or before 1 March 1987**, **or** constructed on/before that date with **no** such submission |
| **NT exclusion (s.4)** | Constructed in accordance with Cap **121** → Ordinance **does not apply** |
| **Industrial carve-out** | Composite whose non-domestic part is **wholly** factory / industrial undertaking / godown / warehouse / bulk storage → **not** a Cap 572 “composite building” |

| Defined type | Exact statutory test | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Composite building** | Constructed / intended **partly for domestic purposes and partly for non-domestic purposes**, meeting the 1 Mar 1987 cut-off | Split the brief: **Sch 1** on non-dom parts, **Sch 2** on domestic parts, **Sch 3** on non-dom occupiers |
| **Domestic building** | Constructed / intended for domestic purposes, with **more than 3 storeys** used principally for such purposes, meeting the cut-off | **Sch 2** only (owner). Includes ancillary **clubhouse, carpark and recreation facilities** provided for the **exclusive use** of residents and persons invited by residents |
| **Domestic purposes** | Use for human habitation — **but does not include** a building or part used as hotel, guesthouse, home for elderly persons (Cap 459), home for persons with disabilities (Cap 613), child care centre or nursery | Those excluded uses are **non-domestic** for Cap 572 |
| **Non-domestic purposes** | Use for a purpose other than domestic purposes | Retail / office / F&B / hotel / RCHE / nursery etc. in a composite = Sch **1** owner + Sch **3** occupier track |
| **s.3(3) clarification** | Non-domestic purposes **includes** hotel, guesthouse, home for elderly, home for PWDs, child care centre, nursery | Do **not** treat hotelisation / RCHE conversion of old domestic as “still Sch 2 only” |

**Unoccupied parts (s.3(2)):** where a building or part is unoccupied, the **owner is deemed the occupier** — EA may exercise occupier powers against the owner, and the owner remains liable even if the part is later occupied.

**Owner / occupier meanings:**
- **Owner** — same meaning as Cap **123** (lessee, mortgagee in possession, rent recipient, etc. — not limited to the Land Registry nameplate)
- **Occupier** — person occupying (as owner or under any lease / licence)
- **Common parts** — Cap **344** s.2 meaning (relevant to OC duties and s.18A)
- **Owners’ corporation** — registered under Cap 344 s.8

**SD action:** On any A&A / adaptive reuse / change-of-use / OC-led upgrade / acquisition of pre-1987 stock:
1. Pull approved-plan first-submission date (or construction evidence if no plans)
2. Confirm Cap 572 applicability vs Cap 121 / industrial carve-out
3. Search Land Registry + BD / FSD published lists for direction / compliance order / prohibition / closure / recovery certificate
4. Freeze programme contingency before massing or tenancy commitments

### 3. Key defined terms that control the design brief (s.3)

| Term | Meaning — why it matters at SD |
|---|---|
| **Fire safety direction** | Written direction under s.5(1)/(2)/(3) (incl. as amended under s.5(5)) — the primary retrofit brief |
| **Fire safety compliance order** | Magistrate order under s.6(1) (incl. as varied under s.6(4)) — replaces the direction; higher penalties |
| **Prohibition order** | District Court order under s.7 — can ban occupation of the building / part |
| **Certificate of compliance** | Instrument under s.12(2) or (3) — needed to discharge a prohibition order |
| **Closure order** | District Court order under s.19N — empties the building so EA can execute improvement works (distinct from prohibition order) |
| **Fire safety improvement works** | Works to ensure a direction or compliance order is complied with — **includes** inspection, investigation, other works, and services required for those works |
| **Fire service installation or equipment** | Anything manufactured / used / designed to: extinguish / attack / prevent / limit fire; warn of fire; provide access for firefighting; facilitate evacuation; **or** provide stand-by power to any of the above |
| **Mechanical ventilating system** | Includes an **air conditioning system** — triggers Sch 1 / Sch 3 auto cut-off rules |
| **Enforcement authority** | **DB** for planning / design / construction; **DFS** for any FSI — split brief, often joint directions |
| **Function** | Includes a power and a duty |

### 4. Who enforces what — and entry powers (ss.3, 15–17)

#### 4.1 Split enforcement authority

| Subject matter | Authority |
|---|---|
| Planning, design and **construction** of the composite / domestic building (passive Schedule works) | **Director of Buildings** |
| Any **fire service installation or equipment** | **Director of Fire Services** |

Directions may be given by **both EAs jointly**; a joint direction may be amended or withdrawn **only jointly** (s.5(7)). Joint applications also available for compliance orders and prohibition orders (ss.6(7), 7(3)).

#### 4.2 Authorized officers (s.15)

| Who | Status |
|---|---|
| Public officer authorized in writing by an EA | Authorized officer for specified functions |
| **Director of Fire Services**, **Director of Buildings**, **all police officers** | Deemed authorized officers |
| Assistants | Officer may be assisted by persons reasonably required |
| ID | Must produce identity card (+ authorization, except the deemed officers) if required |

#### 4.3 Power to enter and inspect (s.16)

| Power | Condition |
|---|---|
| Enter + inspect **without warrant** | Officer reasonably believes it is / may be a Cap 572 composite or domestic building (or part); **or** an offence is being / has been committed; **or** to check whether a direction / compliance order has been complied with |
| Domestic exclusive-use parts | Must **not** enter a part intended for domestic purposes that the occupier exclusively uses / enjoys, unless **≥ 24 hours’ written notice** to that occupier (s.16(3)) |
| Magistrate warrant | If admission refused / refusal expected / unoccupied / urgency, **and** good reason to enter → warrant authorizing entry with such force as necessary |
| Warrant life | 1 month from issue, or until purpose fulfilled — whichever first |
| Leaving unoccupied premises | Must leave as effectively secured against trespassers as found |

#### 4.4 Ownership / occupation information (s.17)
Authorized officer may ask a person for information identifying an owner or occupier if the officer reasonably believes the person has it **and** it is not readily available from public records. Refuse / false or misleading answer without reasonable excuse → fine **level 4**.

#### 4.5 General obstruction (s.18)
Resist / obstruct / delay a person exercising Cap 572 functions without reasonable excuse → fine **level 4** + imprisonment **6 months**.

### 5. Enforcement ladder — programme risk on live buildings (Part 2)

```
Direction (s.5)
    ↓ non-compliance (+ conviction)
Compliance order (s.6)  — replaces direction
    ↓ still non-compliant after period expires
Prohibition order (s.7)  — can ban occupation
    ↓ and/or
EA default works (ss.19A–19W) — enter / close / execute / charge land
```

#### 5.1 Fire safety direction (s.5) — the primary design brief

| Serve on | Building / part | Schedule that can be directed |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** — one person owns all of it (s.5(1)) | Whole composite / domestic building | Composite non-dom → Sch **1**; composite domestic → Sch **2**; domestic building → Sch **2** |
| **Owner** — multi-owner, each with exclusive right to occupy a specified part (s.5(2)) | The part exclusively occupied **and/or** the part **not** exclusively occupied (common parts) | Same Sch 1 / 2 split by domestic vs non-domestic intended purpose |
| **Occupier** (s.5(3)) | Composite building, part intended for **non-domestic** purposes only | Sch **3** |

**Alternative measures (s.5(1)–(2)) — critical SD escape hatch:**  
EA may direct **other measures in place of** any Sch 1 or Sch 2 requirement if EA is of the opinion it would not be reasonable for the owner to comply, having regard to:
1. **Structural integrity** of the building; and  
2. **Technology available** to comply.

Advisory committee (ss.14D–14E) advises EA on whether alternatives are appropriate and what they should be — invite owner representations before advising.

**FSI integration across exclusive parts (s.5(2)):** for integration of FSI between parts exclusively occupied by different owners, a direction may require an owner to provide **relevant connections or other forms of integration**. Budget cross-owner riser / alarm / sprinkler / FH interfacing early on strata composites.

| Direction rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Form (s.5(4)) | Must be in **writing**; must specify a compliance period that is **reasonable** and allows sufficient time |
| Amend / withdraw (s.5(5)) | EA may amend or withdraw by similar notice from time to time |
| Remains in force until (s.5(6)) | (a) complied with to EA satisfaction; **or** (b) withdrawn; **or** (c) replaced by a fire safety compliance order |
| Compliance instrument (s.5(6A)) | If owner direction under s.5(1)/(2) is complied with → EA **must** issue instrument certifying compliance |
| Withdrawal instrument (s.5(6B)) | If withdrawn → EA **must** issue instrument notifying withdrawal |
| Offence (s.5(8)) | Fail without reasonable excuse → fine **level 6** + further fine **$10,000** for each day or part-day after the specified period |
| Reasonable excuse (s.5(9)) | Includes (not limited to): risk of **prejudicially affecting structural integrity**; or required **technology not reasonably available** |

**SD takeaway:** Treat every open direction as a **binding design brief** with a clock. Structural / technology excuses are real but must be engineered and argued — not assumed.

#### 5.2 Fire safety compliance order (s.6)

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| When | Magistrate finds owner (composite or domestic) or occupier (composite) guilty under s.5(8); EA applies |
| Content | Direct compliance with all or any requirements in the related fire safety direction |
| Period | Must specify a reasonable compliance period |
| Effect | **Replaces** the relevant fire safety direction (s.6(3)) |
| Vary / revoke | Magistrate may revoke or vary on application of EA or the applicable owner / occupier; owner/occupier entitled to be heard on EA’s application |
| Cessation | When revoked under s.6(4), **or** when EA by written notice informs magistrate’s clerk that order complied with (copy served on owner/occupier) |
| Joint application | Both EAs may apply jointly |
| Offence (s.6(8)) | Fail to comply → fine **$200,000** + **$20,000** per day or part-day after the period |

#### 5.3 Prohibition order (ss.7–13) — occupation kill switch

**Application gate (s.7):**
- Ground: a requirement of a direction **or** compliance order is not being / has not been complied with
- Timing: only **after** the compliance period has expired — not before
- Notice: EA must give ≥ **7 days’** notice to applicable owner/occupier before applying; as soon as practicable after giving notice, post a copy conspicuously **inside** the building/part **or** at / in the immediate vicinity of **each entrance**
- Joint application by both EAs allowed

**Court may make the order only if satisfied of all of (s.7(7)):**
1. Owner/occupier failed to comply with a requirement in the direction or compliance order  
2. It is **reasonable to expect** compliance  
3. Time allowed was **reasonable**  
4. Order is **reasonable and necessary** in the circumstances  
5. There could be **substantial fire risks** if the building/part is occupied  

**Effect while in force (s.8(1)):**
- No person (other than an authorized officer on duty) may occupy without EA written permission
- EA may permit occupation subject to conditions for implementing measures needed to discharge / revoke the order; permission cancellable anytime
- Applicable owner (and occupier if different) must take all practicable steps to **secure against entry** by anyone other than authorized officers / permitted persons

| Timing | Rule |
|---|---|
| Default start (s.8(2)) | **28 days** after service on owner/occupier; **or** if appealed, when appeal finally rejected / withdrawn |
| Accelerated start (s.8(3)) | District Court may direct earlier start from service date or any later date before end of the 28-day period |
| Remains in force until (s.8(4)) | Discharged under s.12(5); **or** revoked under s.13; **or** building/part **ceases to exist** — whichever first |

**Posting (s.10):** EA must post copy at conspicuous place inside / at each entrance as soon as practicable after order made (failure to post does **not** invalidate). Interfering with posted copy → fine level 2. EA must remove copies when order ceases.

**Police removal (s.11):** inspector-rank or above may remove persons apparently contravening / about to contravene s.8(1), and prevent re-entry while order in force.

**Certificate of compliance path (s.12):**
1. While prohibition order in force, owner/occupier may request in writing that EA certify the underlying direction / compliance-order requirements have been met  
2. EA must issue certificate if satisfied, else reject with reasons  
3. EA may also issue certificate **without** request if satisfied  
4. As soon as practicable after issuing certificate, EA **must** apply to District Court to discharge the order (application accompanied by certificate copy)  
5. Court **must** discharge unless of opinion there are **special grounds** not to  

**Owner revocation application (s.13):** if EA rejects the certificate request, **or** fails to issue within **28 days** of request → owner/occupier may apply to District Court to revoke; must notify EA within **7 days** of making the application; Court may make any order it thinks fit.

| Offence | Exposure |
|---|---|
| Occupy contrary to s.8(1)(a)(i) without reasonable excuse (s.9(1)) | Fine **$250,000** + imprisonment **3 years** + **$25,000**/day |
| Fail to secure against entry under s.8(1)(b) (s.9(2)) | Fine **level 4** + imprisonment **6 months** |

**SD takeaway:** An open Cap 572 direction / order on a target acquisition, A&A or leasing site is a **title, programme, occupation and financing risk** — not soft guidance.

### 6. Schedules — the actual retrofit design constraints

Technical detail is pinned to **legacy CoPs named in the Schedules**. Under **s.25**, a substituted or amended CoP applies for Cap 572 purposes **only** when:
1. LegCo passes a resolution declaring it applies; **and**  
2. Secretary for Security amends the Schedule reference by Gazette order; **and**  
3. That amendment has taken effect.

| Schedule CoP references (as at 13.12.2024) | Role |
|---|---|
| FSD **Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment 1994** | Detailed specs for Sch 1 paras (a)–(e) and Sch 2 FSI items; also Sch 1 “other FSI” catch-all |
| BD **Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996** | Sch 1 / 2 construction — MOE geometry |
| BD **Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996** | Sch 1 / 2 construction — FR; also defines Sch 3 “fire compartment” |
| BD **Code of Practice for Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue 1995** | Sch 1 / 2 construction — fireman’s lift / access |

**Critical practice split:** Cap 572 Schedule CoPs = **improvement benchmark for directed upgrades**. Concurrent Cap **123** A&A / new works still need **current** FS Code 2011 + current FSD CoP. Do not assume one package satisfies both.

#### 6.1 Schedule 1 — Owner duties: composite **non-domestic** parts

**A. Fire service installation / equipment (Sch 1 s.1)**

| Item | What EA can require | SD / spatial implication |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Automatic sprinkler system** | Provide or improve, with or without direct link to FSD — control spread + sound alarm | Tank / pump room, risers, ceiling void coordination, street inlet, possible FSD telemetry — major GFA / plant / structural load |
| **(b) Fire hydrant + hose reel** | Provide or improve as firefighting water source | Street FH coverage, intermediate tanks, HR cabinets on every floor, riser shafts |
| **(c) Manual fire alarm** | Provide or improve — alert occupants | Call points, sounders, zoning, interface with other FSI |
| **(d) Emergency lighting (common parts)** | Provide or improve in **common parts** for evacuation on power failure | Exit routes, stairs, lobbies, corridors — battery / generator strategy |
| **(e) MV auto cut-off** | Provide or improve automatic cut-off to limit smoke spread through the ventilating system, **where one is provided**, but **only if** the system (i) forms an **integral part** of the non-dom portion **and** (ii) also **serves other separately occupied** non-dom areas | Shared podium / mall / office AC = classic trigger; local single-shop systems may fall to Sch 3 instead |
| **(f) Other FSI** | Provide or improve other FSI per FSD Minimum FSI CoP **1994** | Catch-all — can pull additional 1994 CoP items beyond (a)–(e) |

Detailed specs for (a)–(e) are in FSD Minimum FSI CoP **1994**.

**B. Fire safety construction (Sch 1 s.2)**

| Theme | What EA can require | SD spatial traps |
|---|---|---|
| **MOE — staircases** | Improve staircases in terms of **width and number** | Core enlargement; possible second stair; GFA / efficiency hit |
| **MOE — protection** | Protect exit routes and staircases with separating walls of **fire resisting construction** | Lobby enclosures; close open stairs; FR door sets |
| **MOE — exit arrangements** | Improve exits from rooms, storeys and ground storeys; access to staircases; **direct distance or travel distance** | Re-plan tenancy layouts; shorten dead-ends; add cross-corridor doors |
| **MOE — fire doors** | Provide fire doors | Door schedules, self-closers, hold-open + detection interface |
| **Means of access** | Improve **at least one** existing lift to **fireman’s lift** standard; **or** install a **new** fireman’s lift | Shaft, lobby, lobby ventilation, secondary power, recall — often the hardest Sch 1 item in old towers |
| **FRC — external walls** | Improve FR of external walls and protect openings to inhibit spread to **adjoining buildings** | Façade FR upgrades; protected openings on party / close boundaries |
| **FRC — separation** | Provide FR separation between **different parts** in a building | Domestic ↔ non-domestic podium separation; vertical / horizontal compartmentation |
| **FRC — basements** | Provide **smoke vents to basements** | Façade / pavement openings; LandsD / HyD interface for pavement vents |

Detailed design / construction / installation requirements for (a)–(c) are in **MOE 1996**, **FRC 1996** and **MOA 1995**.

#### 6.2 Schedule 2 — Owner duties: composite **domestic** parts + **domestic buildings**

**A. FSI (Sch 2 s.1) — lighter than Sch 1**

| Item | Requirement | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Fire hydrant + hose reel** | Provide or improve | Same plant / riser logic as Sch 1, without Schedule-listed sprinkler |
| **(b) Manual fire alarm** | Provide or improve | — |
| **(c) Emergency lighting (common parts)** | Provide or improve in **common parts** | Not inside flats under Sch 2 |

**No** Schedule-listed sprinkler mandate and **no** Schedule-listed fireman’s-lift mandate for Sch 2 — but:
- EA may still direct **other measures** under s.5 if Sch items are unreasonable / inadequate in context
- Concurrent Cap 123 A&A may independently trigger **current** FS Code / FSI CoP (including sprinklers / fireman’s lifts where those codes require them)

**B. Construction (Sch 2 s.2) — narrower than Sch 1**

| Item | Requirement | SD implication |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** Protect staircases with FR separating walls | Enclose open stairs; FR lobby strategy | Classic old domestic / tenement stair upgrade |
| **(b)** Improve exit arrangements from domestic parts **to the street** | Clear path from flat → stair → final exit at street | Ground-floor shops / locked gates / obstructed final exits are frequent failures |
| **(c)** Provide fire doors | Flat entrance doors / stair doors as directed | Coordinate with Cap 344 common parts and unit deeds |

Same legacy CoP trio: MOE 1996, FRC 1996, MOA 1995.

#### 6.3 Schedule 3 — Occupier duties: composite **non-domestic** parts only

| Item | When / what | SD / tenancy implication |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Emergency lighting** | Within the **area the occupier occupies** — facilitate evacuation on power failure | Shop / restaurant / office fit-out item — often on tenant |
| **(b) MV auto cut-off** | Only if the system **does not** serve other separately occupied non-dom areas, **and** either (i) capacity **> 1 m³/s**, **or** (ii) serves **more than one fire compartment** within that non-dom part | Local large AC / kitchen extract / multi-room shop systems |

**Fire compartment (Sch 3 s.2):** part of the building physically separated from adjoining parts by walls **and** by a floor and ceiling that meet the FR standard in **FRC 1996**.

**Landlord / tenant coordination:**
- Sch **1** = owner (often common parts + building-wide FSI / construction)
- Sch **3** = occupier (unit emergency lighting + local MV cut-off)
- Fit-out programmes must not assume landlord works cover Sch 3 — and vice versa

#### 6.4 Schedule comparison (quick scan)

| Measure | Sch 1 (owner, non-dom) | Sch 2 (owner, domestic) | Sch 3 (occupier, non-dom) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprinklers | Yes | No (Schedule) | No |
| FH / HR | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manual fire alarm | Yes | Yes | No |
| Emergency lighting | Common parts | Common parts | Occupied area |
| MV auto cut-off | Shared / integral system | No | Local system if >1 m³/s or multi-compartment |
| Other FSI (1994 CoP) | Yes | No | No |
| Stair width / number | Yes | No | No |
| Stair FR protection | Yes (routes + stairs) | Yes (stairs) | No |
| Travel / exit geometry | Yes (full) | Exit to street only | No |
| Fire doors | Yes | Yes | No |
| Fireman’s lift | Yes (≥1 upgrade or new) | No (Schedule) | No |
| External wall FR / openings | Yes | No | No |
| FR separation between parts | Yes | No | No |

### 7. Owner vs occupier vs OC — who is directed, who can block (ss.5, 14A, 18A)

| Party | Cap 572 exposure | Architect / PM action |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** (Cap 123 meaning) | Sch 1 / 2 directions; multi-owner: exclusive part **and** non-exclusive (common) parts | Identify all owners early — not just OC chairman |
| **Occupier** | Sch 3 on composite non-dom; if unoccupied, owner deemed occupier | Align tenancy break clauses / fit-out scopes with Sch 3 |
| **Owners’ corporation** | For Land Registry purposes (s.14A): direction / order / s.5(6A)/(6B) instruments / certificate of compliance served on OC are treated as served on **each owner individually** | OC service still hits every owner’s title risk |
| **Person notified by OC** that a direction / compliance order exists for **common parts** (s.18A) | Must **not** (a) obstruct persons employed/engaged by OC carrying out improvement works; (b) refuse reasonably necessary access / use of any part of the building for those works; (c) refuse to **contribute to the costs** of those works | Criminal offence — programme can proceed over hold-out owners once notice given |

| s.18A offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Obstruct works / refuse access (s.18A(1)) | Fine **level 3** + imprisonment **6 months** |
| Refuse cost contribution (s.18A(2)) | Fine **level 4** |

**SD / procurement:** On OC-led Cap 572 upgrades, sequence common-part FH/HR risers, staircase FR lining, fire doors, and emergency lighting first; run unit-level Sch 3 in parallel with tenancy programmes; document OC notices for s.18A leverage.

### 8. Land Registry, ownership transfer, public listing (ss.13A–14C)

#### 8.1 Registration of fire safety direction (s.13A)

| Event | EA duty / power |
|---|---|
| Direction served on owner | EA **may** register by memorial against land register of building / part |
| Direction ceases by compliance (s.5(6)(a)) | Must register s.5(6A) instrument ASAP, in any event **≤ 2 months** after instrument date |
| Direction ceases by withdrawal (s.5(6)(b)) | Must register s.5(6B) instrument ASAP, **≤ 2 months** |
| Direction ceases by replacement with compliance order (s.5(6)(c)) | Must register the compliance order ASAP, **≤ 2 months** after order date |

#### 8.2 Registration of compliance order / prohibition order (s.14)

| Event | Rule |
|---|---|
| Order made | EA **may** register against land register |
| Compliance order revoked / complied with (s.6 notice) | Must register revocation / notice ASAP, **≤ 1 month** |
| Prohibition order: certificate issued / discharged / revoked | Must register certificate / discharge / revocation ASAP, **≤ 1 month** |

#### 8.3 OC registration fiction (s.14A)
For ss.13A and 14 only: service / making / issue of direction, compliance order, s.5(6A)/(6B) instruments, or certificate of compliance **on/to an OC** is treated as on/to **each owner individually**.

#### 8.4 New owner notification duty (s.14B)

If a fire safety direction **or** compliance order is in force **and** there is a transfer of interests → **new owner must notify** the relevant EA in writing within **3 months** after the transfer is completed. Failure → fine **level 3**.

**Acquisition checklist:** diary the 3-month clock from completion; notify both DB and/or DFS as applicable to the outstanding instruments.

#### 8.5 Publishing information (s.14C)
EA may upload / otherwise publish: serial number; address; date of direction/order; compliance status — for directions, compliance orders, prohibition orders and **closure orders**.

**Due diligence:** title search + BD/FSD websites before pricing Cap 572 contingency.

### 9. Advisory committee — alternative-measures and EA works priority (ss.14D–14F)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Establishment | Each EA **must** establish a committee of members with relevant expertise |
| Functions (only on matters EA refers) | (a) advise whether **other measures** under s.5(1)/(2) are appropriate and what they are; (b) advise **prioritisation factors** for EA exercising Part 3 Div 2 default-works powers; (c) advise on **technical matters** of improvement-works proposals |
| Owner voice | Before advising on (a) or (c), committee may invite written representations from the related owner |
| Binding process | If advice given, EA **must consider** it before performing any function concerning that matter |
| Continuity (s.14F) | Pre-2024 s.5(10) committees / members continue under the new ss.14D–14E framework |

**SD use:** where Sch 1 fireman’s lift, stair widening, or full sprinklers are structurally impractical, prepare an engineered alternative package (e.g. enhanced detection / evacuation / compartmentation / water mist / etc. as appropriate) for EA referral to the advisory committee — do not invent informal “waivers”.

### 10. EA can do the works — 2024 default-execution regime (Part 3 Div 2, ss.19A–19W)

#### 10.1 Trigger and cast

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Auxiliary person** | Person (not an authorized officer) authorized in writing under s.19C when EA knows / reasonably believes a direction or compliance order in force is not complied with |
| **Specified person** | Authorized officer **or** auxiliary person |
| Scope of works | Any **fire safety improvement works** the authorized officer considers necessary for the building / part |

This Division does **not** limit the general Part 3 Div 1 inspection powers (s.19B).

#### 10.2 Entry to carry out works (ss.19D–19L)

| Actor | Entry basis | What they may do |
|---|---|---|
| **Authorized officer** (s.19D) | (1) Knows / reasonably believes non-compliance; **and** (2) owner/occupier/controller **permits** — **or** warrant under s.19G — **or** urgency makes warrant impracticable | At any reasonable time: enter any premises/land; if necessary **break in** in presence of a police officer; carry out necessary improvement works |
| **Auxiliary person** (s.19E) | Directed by authorized officer; needs permission **or** warrant (no urgency break-in limb of their own) | Enter at reasonable time and carry out works the authorized officer considers necessary |
| Assistance (s.19F) | Specified person may use any assistants considered necessary | — |

**Warrant (s.19G):** magistrate may issue if satisfied by oath that:
1. Reasonable grounds to suspect non-compliance; **and**  
2. Entry was refused **or** could not be gained despite visits on ≥ **2 different days**, and/or carrying out of works was refused / unsuccessful; **and**  
3. Notice of intention to apply for warrant has been served on each owner or occupier of the premises/land  

Warrant must specify premises/land, purpose, name/capacity of authorized entrant, and date of issue. Remains in force until the specified purpose is fulfilled (s.19I). Specified person relying on warrant must produce it for inspection (s.19H).

**After entry:** leave unoccupied premises secured (s.19J); may dispose of unused materials and wastes (s.19K). Obstruct specified person / assistant → fine level 4 + 6 months (s.19L).

#### 10.3 Closure orders to enable EA works (ss.19M–19S) — distinct from prohibition orders

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| When EA may apply | EA of opinion building/part should be closed so a specified person can perform Subdivision 2 functions **without danger** to occupiers or the public |
| Pre-application notice | ≥ **7 days** before application: post bilingual notice of intention conspicuously inside / at each entrance; notice must state effect of s.19S(1)–(2) clearly in English **and** Chinese |
| Court order (s.19N) | District Court may order building/part closed under direction of a police officer if notice was duly given |
| Remove persons (s.19O) | Inspector-rank+ police may remove persons present; may require assistance |
| Seal (s.19P) | EA may seal any/all entrances or exits |
| Permission despite closure (s.19Q) | EA may permit entry/presence subject to conditions; cancellable |
| Offence (s.19R) | Enter / be present without excuse (except specified person, assistant, or s.19Q permission), or interfere with seals/locks → fine **level 2** + **6 months** |
| Cessation (s.19S) | Remains until EA-issued **expiry date** (posted, served on each owner, and published on website and/or Gazette); **or** building/part completely demolished / ceases to exist |

**SD distinction:**
- **Prohibition order (s.7)** = punishment / risk control for non-compliance — bans occupation because of fire risk  
- **Closure order (s.19N)** = works-enabling tool — empties the building so EA can execute improvements  

Either can wipe out occupation / rental income during works.

#### 10.4 Cost recovery, surcharge, interest, land charge (ss.19T–19W)

**Who pays (s.19T(1)):**
- If the relevant direction is registered under s.13A **or** compliance order registered under s.14 → recoverable from whoever is **owner on the date works are completed**  
- Otherwise → from the owner on whom the direction was served / against whom the compliance order was made  

**Recoverable costs (s.19T(2)) include:**
- Carrying out the improvement works  
- Materials supplied  
- Supervision charges  
- Disposal of unused materials / wastes (s.19K)  
- **Abortive visits** (entry denied / unsuccessful)  
- Costs of sealing entrances/exits under s.19P  
- Any other incidental costs  

| Add-on | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Surcharge** | ≤ **20%** of costs — also recoverable as civil debt |
| **Interest** | If certificate copy served under s.19U and unpaid / partly unpaid after **30 days** → simple interest **10% p.a.** on unpaid amount from the next day |
| Contribution rights | Paying person may still recover from another person who is liable |

**Recovery certificate (s.19U):** EA may issue certificate specifying payment due, works, each liable person, and (if more than one) an **apportionment** EA considers appropriate. Copy must be served on each liable person. Certificate is admissible and proves facts stated unless contrary evidence.

**Land Registry charge (s.19V) — acquisition critical:**

| Effect | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | EA may register recovery certificate while payment not fully recovered — treated as instrument affecting land under Cap 128 |
| After registration | Specified payment recoverable by court action from anyone who appears on the land register as owner **on or after** registration date |
| Legal charge | Constitutes a **legal charge** giving EA the same powers/remedies as a **mortgagee under a mortgage by deed** |
| Priority | Despite Cap 128 ss.3(1) & 5 — priority from beginning of the day **after** registration |
| Cap on recovery from a person | Not recoverable beyond the **value of that person’s interest** |
| Charge void when | Payment recovered to the extent of the value of the building/part |
| Bona fide purchaser / mortgagee protection | If after works complete but **before** certificate registration a person acquires and registers an interest as bona fide purchaser/mortgagee for valuable consideration → charge void against that person; no s.19V(3) liability accrues |
| Full recovery | EA must register instrument certifying recovery |

**Writ service shortcut (s.19W):** for recovering s.19T debts, writ may be served by leaving at defendant’s residence/business; if unknown, at the building/part (or outside/adjacent to an entrance if entry denied) — displaces ordinary ss.23 / 23A service rules for that writ.

**SD implication:** Non-compliance is no longer “only a fine”. The building can be **closed**, works executed by government agents, costs + ≤20% surcharge + 10% interest charged, and a **mortgage-like charge** registered against title. Price voluntary compliance ahead of EA default works.

### 11. Miscellaneous that still bites practice (Part 4)

#### 11.1 Liability shields (ss.20–21)
Government not liable for certain matters arising from Cap 572 functions; authorized officers not personally liable for certain acts/omissions in good faith. Do not rely on government works quality as a substitute for AP/RSE due diligence on concurrent A&A.

#### 11.2 Unlawful disclosure (s.22)
Offence to disclose information obtained officially under Cap 572 (subject to statutory exceptions in the section).

#### 11.3 Corporate / OC / partnership offences (s.22A)
If offence by body corporate / OC / partnership with consent, connivance, or attributable to neglect/omission of a director / management person / partner → that person also commits the offence.

#### 11.4 Prosecution deadline (s.22B)
Prosecution may only start within **12 months** beginning on the date the offence is discovered by, or comes to the notice of, the EA (displaces Cap 227 s.26 time limit). Does **not** apply to offences committed before 13 December 2024.

#### 11.5 Service of documents (ss.23–23B)
Broad service methods on individuals and body corporates (including registered non-Hong Kong companies via authorized representative): personal delivery, registered post, leave with adult occupier, post conspicuously inside building/part, fax, email. Deemed service timings: next day for personal/left/posted/fax/email; **second working day** for post.

#### 11.6 Evidence (ss.23C–23E)
EA certificates of service admissible; EA/authorized officers may certify true copies of directions, orders, certificates, **building plans showing Cap 572 applies**, and related correspondence — certified copies admissible without further proof.

#### 11.7 Regulations and CoP updates (ss.24–25)
CE in Council may make regulations. CoP substitutions/amendments in Schedules only apply after LegCo resolution + Secretary for Security Gazette order (see §6).

### 12. What Cap 572 is *not* — avoid the common mix-ups

| Do not confuse with | Difference |
|---|---|
| **FS Code 2011** | New-build / Cap 123 plan-approval DTC for MOE + FRC + means of access |
| **Current FSD Minimum FSI CoP** | New FSI submissions; Cap 572 Schedules still cite **1994** until s.25 process updates them |
| **Cap 502** Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) | Commercial premises improvement — different stock |
| **Cap 636** Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) | Industrial buildings improvement — different stock |
| **Cap 123 s.21 OP** | Cap 572 does not grant OP or approve new buildings |
| **MBIS / Cap 123P** | Structural/safety inspection cycle; Cap 572 is fire-upgrade specific — but completed Cap 572 works raise Cap 123P inspection baseline |
| **Prohibition order vs closure order** | Prohibition (s.7) = occupation ban for fire risk after non-compliance; Closure (s.19N) = empty building so EA can execute works |
| **Sch 1 vs Sch 2 vs Sch 3** | Owner non-dom / owner domestic / occupier non-dom — different FSI and construction lists |

### 13. Schematic design checklist (pre–1 Mar 1987 composite / domestic)

1. Confirm **first-submission / construction date** vs 1 March 1987; exclude Cap **121** NT stock and wholly industrial composites.  
2. Classify **composite vs domestic**; map hotel / guesthouse / RCHE / RCHD / child care / nursery as **non-domestic** under Cap 572.  
3. Include exclusive-use resident **clubhouse / carpark / recreation** inside domestic-building scope where applicable.  
4. Search Land Registry + BD/FSD published lists for **direction / compliance order / prohibition / closure / recovery certificate**.  
5. Split scope: **Sch 1** non-dom owner | **Sch 2** domestic owner | **Sch 3** non-dom occupier.  
6. Non-dom owner: budget **sprinklers**, FH/HR, MFA, common-part emergency lighting, possible **fireman’s lift**, stair width/number, travel distance, FR route protection, FR separation, external-wall FR / openings, basement smoke vents.  
7. Domestic owner: budget FH/HR, MFA, common-part emergency lighting, **stair FR enclosure**, exits to street, fire doors.  
8. Occupier non-dom: budget unit emergency lighting + local MV cut-off if >**1 m³/s** or multi-compartment.  
9. Check MV against Sch 1 (**integral + shared**) vs Sch 3 (**local**) tests — same AC system can flip which party is directed.  
10. On multi-owner buildings, plan **FSI interconnection** between exclusive parts (s.5(2)).  
11. If full Sch 1 construction is structurally impractical → prepare **alternative-measures** case for advisory committee (s.5 + ss.14D–14E).  
12. Coordinate Cap 572 retrofit with concurrent Cap **123** A&A (current FS Code / FSI CoP may apply to the new works portion independently).  
13. Engage **OC** early — after notice, obstructing works / refusing access / refusing cost contribution is criminal (s.18A).  
14. On acquisition: diary **s.14B 3-month** EA notification; price registered recovery certificates / mortgage-like charges (s.19V).  
15. Do **not** assume Cap 572 Schedule CoPs = FS Code 2011; track s.25 Gazette updates separately.  
16. Distinguish **prohibition** (occupation ban) from **closure** (EA works enabling) in programme risk registers.  
17. Remember Cap 123P may treat completed Cap 572 works as the **raised** MBIS inspection baseline going forward.
