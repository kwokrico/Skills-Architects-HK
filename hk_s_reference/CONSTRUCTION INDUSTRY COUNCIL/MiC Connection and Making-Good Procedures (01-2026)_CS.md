# MiC Connection and Making-Good Procedures (01/2026)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Version 01/2026 | Hong Kong Construction Industry Trade Testing Centre, Construction Industry Council

> Scope note: Trade-test demonstration of how existing trade skills close **selected** joints on a MiC mock-up (factory assembly, then site installation). It is **not** a Building Authority code, not exhaustive, and not a project specification. Other joint types are allowed. Design, manufacturer requirements and the project specification govern. Every content sheet repeats that limit.

## Regulatory Overview

The leaflet walks site making-good of MiC joints for external-wall render, ceiling and wall finishes, floor vinyl, indoor supply and drainage, external-wall drainage, sunken-slab toilets, and plastic conduits.

At schematic design, set joint flatness, sleeve positions, falls and module-top access so these sequences can be built; do not treat the mock-up method as the only acceptable detail.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the leaflet is (引言)

| Item | Rule in this leaflet |
|---|---|
| MiC split | (1) Factory assembly (2) Site installation |
| Purpose cited | Shorter site time, factory quality control, fewer site operations, less waste, dust and noise |
| Status | Shows trade skills applied to **some** MiC joints only |

**SD takeaway:** Lock joint strategy in the module design and specification; this leaflet only illustrates one mock-up method.

### 2. External wall joint (外牆接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Joint flatness | Proceed only within a reasonable tolerance of **10–15 mm** |
| Joint edges | Confirm they are already keyed (打花) so render can bond |
| Materials | Must meet the relevant rules and still be within the use-by date |
| Weather | Do not render if rain that day could peel the coat |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clean concrete dust |
| 2 | At the render joint, apply waterproof adhesive slurry (materials approved for the project) |
| 3 | Fix render mesh (chicken wire, 雞仔網) with **stainless** screws at **not more than 150 mm** centres |
| 4 | Mix render to the approved waterproof-slurry ratio and open time |
| 5 | Apply within the stated open time |
| 6 | Straightedge so the coat is full and flat, then sieve-finish |

External-wall work: platform in a safe condition, full-body harness tied to a compliant anchorage. Do not stack debris on the platform. Protect finished work.

**SD takeaway:** Module-to-module external joints must land inside **10–15 mm**, with edges keyed for render and mesh fixings at **≤150 mm**.

### 3. Ceiling and wall joint (天花及牆身接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Flatness | Within a reasonable tolerance before starting. No millimetre figure is given on this sheet |
| Materials | Higher crack resistance: paper-tape filler and paint, with paper tape |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare and clean the joint; fill with paper-tape compound |
| 2 | Cover the filled joint with paper tape |
| 3 | Fill again over the tape with paper-tape compound |
| 4 | Sand the filler |
| 5 | Final emulsion: wall flat, smooth, colour consistent |

Work area needs good ventilation and fire safety. Off-ground work uses a suitable, inspected light platform.

**SD takeaway:** Internal MiC joints are a tape-and-filler detail, not the external render build-up. Specify crack-resistant joint materials; this sheet gives no numeric tolerance.

### 4. Floor joint (地板接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Flatness | Within a reasonable tolerance. No millimetre figure on this sheet |
| Hollows / high spots | Prepare mixed filler |
| Vinyl | Check sheet size and colour for deviation |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare and clean; fill hollows and high spots with mixed filler |
| 2 | Vacuum |
| 3 | Water-based floor adhesive, spread with a notched trowel |
| 4 | Lay vinyl by the specified method |
| 5 | Before completion, check flatness at joints and remove excess adhesive |

Protect floor and adjacent walls; strip protection and clean on completion.

**SD takeaway:** Floor joints are a filled, vacuumed, notched-trowel vinyl joint. Coordinate module lip heights so filler can take out the step.

### 5. Indoor supply and drainage (室內來去水接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Sleeves through the MiC wall | Must suit the joint pipe layout |
| Supply and waste materials | Comply with the relevant rules. Solder must be **lead-free and Water Supplies Department approved** |
| Waste joints | Falls as the drawing requires, and the reserved sleeve must actually be through |
| Pipe ends | Clean; no foreign matter left in the bore |

| Who may do designated water-pipe works | Waterworks Ordinance s.15, as listed on this sheet |
|---|---|
| Examples given | Licensed plumber; registered plumbing worker; a person doing designated water-pipe works under the instruction and supervision of a licensed plumber or registered plumbing worker |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clean the supply joint; confirm the bore is clear |
| 2 | Drill the wall and install the pipe clip if required |
| 3 | Cut copper to length |
| 4 | Braze the copper joint |
| 5 | Pressure-test before completion; supply must not leak |
| 6 | Wrap and finish the welded joint (蝦篩 finish) |

Control drill speed so tiles are not broken. Welding and other hot work: hot-work permit system, and a valid permit before starting. Protect surroundings so the wall is not blackened. Wrap and protect the finished pipe joint.

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate MiC sleeves with the plumbing layout and drawing falls, and specify WSD-approved lead-free solder. Water-pipe works sit under Waterworks Ordinance s.15 designated persons.

### 6. External-wall drainage (外牆來去水接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Materials and fittings | Comply with the relevant rules |
| Geometry | Stack plumb; lateral branches to the required fall |
| Pipe ends | Clean, undamaged, bore clear |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clean the external MiC drain joint |
| 2 | Cut and arrange the pipes from the approved pipe drawing |
| 3 | Connect the larger stack first, watching lateral falls; then branch pipes by size, keeping the stack vertical |
| 4 | Approved adhesive on the pipe outside and inside the fitting |
| 5 | Connect branch drains and traps |
| 6 | Before completion, tighten every pipe, waste fitting and trap joint |

Same external-work platform and harness rules as the external wall sheet. Do not stack debris on the platform.

**SD takeaway:** External stacks and branches are set out from the approved pipe drawing: stack first, then branches, with falls and plumb checked before adhesive.

### 7. Sunken-slab toilet (外牆來去水接駁收口 — 下沉式樓板設計廁所)

Same pre-checks as the external drainage sheet: compliant materials, plumb stack, branches to fall, clean undamaged bores.

| Sunken-slab point on this sheet | Effect |
|---|---|
| Most pipes concealed in the toilet base and above the false ceiling | Fewer external-wall supply/waste joints than a conventional toilet |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Drill and install rawlbolts at the fish-wire marks; watch drill depth if required |
| 2 | Install anchor bolts at the fish-wire marks; lock the nut after positioning if required |
| 3 | Install the stack and the flush pipe; adjust branch height and fall |
| 4 | Approved adhesive on the pipe outside and inside the fitting |
| 5 | Connect branch drains and traps |
| 6 | Tighten all pipes, fittings and trap joints before completion |

**SD takeaway:** A sunken-slab toilet moves most pipework off the external wall. Still design fish-wire fixing points, stack, flush pipe, and branch height and fall.

### 8. Conduit joints (燈喉接駁收口)

| Check before work | Hard point |
|---|---|
| Conduit | Plastic conduit that meets the applicable standard |
| Cuts | Proper cutter; ends square and perpendicular |
| Ends before jointing | Clean; no dust or debris in the joint |
| Solvent cement | Correct cement, applied on the pipe end by the manufacturer’s method; insert promptly and accurately and hold until the joint is firm |

| Sequence | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clear concrete and sand from the sleeve joint; bore must be unobstructed |
| 2 | Join with a socket/coupler (疏結) and a proper amount of cement — direct connection |
| 3 | Measure the conduit length to be jointed |
| 4 | Cut to length |
| 5 | Cement the joint; it must be tight, firm, with no play |
| 6 | Confirm the joint is fully bonded with no gap; clear tools and surplus material |

Work on top of a MiC module: enough suitable guardrail around the edge. External-wall conduit work uses the same platform and harness rules as the external wall sheet.

**SD takeaway:** Conduit sleeves through modules must be clear of concrete, and joints are solvent-welded couplers held to the manufacturer’s method. Provide edge protection where work is on the module top.
