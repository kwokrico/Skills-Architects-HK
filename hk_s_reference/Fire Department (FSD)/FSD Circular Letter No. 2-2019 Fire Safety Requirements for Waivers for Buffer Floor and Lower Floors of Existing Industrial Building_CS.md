# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2019 — Fire Safety Requirements for Waivers for Buffer Floor and Lower Floors of Existing Industrial Building
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 March 2019 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Supports Lands Department Practice Note Issue No. 3/2019 and TPB industrial-zone buffer-floor policy.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular sets FSD fire safety requirements for converting the lowest three floors of an existing industrial building to non-industrial uses using a non-hazardous buffer floor to separate upper industrial portions, including extended use for telecoms exchange and computer/data processing centres.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Separation and independence (§3.1–3.2)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Buffer floor + industrial portion above vs non-industrial below | Completely separated; prescribed FRR for industrial building; **no ingress/egress** between portions |
| Independent services | Separate MOE, MOA, entrance lobbies, staircases, passenger/cargo lifts, loading/unloading |
| Buffer floor construction | Independent floor with prescribed industrial FRR + own MOE/MOA |
| Voids | False ceiling voids and raised floors are **not** a buffer floor |

### 2. Basement and occupancy limits

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Basements | Cannot convert to non-industrial; only non-hazardous use (carpark, E&M plant); no industrial activity/loading in basement |
| Telecoms / data centre occupancy factor | ≥ **30 m² usable floor area per person** (e.g. 300 m² max 10 persons) |
| A&A works | Formal BD centralised processing approval required |
| FSI | Per prevailing FSI Code for alteration works |

### 3. Applicability

| Scope | Detail |
|---|---|
| §3.4 occupancy factor | Specific to telecoms/data centre buffer floors |
| §3.1–3.3, 3.5–3.7 | Also apply to other non-hazardous buffer floors (carpark, E&M plant, refuge floor) |

**SD takeaway:** Buffer-floor conversions are three-dimensional separation problems — no shared stairs/lifts between industrial upper and converted lower; size data/telecom floors to occupancy factor 30 m²/person at SD.
