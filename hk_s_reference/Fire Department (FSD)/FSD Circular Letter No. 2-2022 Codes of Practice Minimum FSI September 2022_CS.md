# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2022 — Codes of Practice for Minimum FSI (September 2022)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 July 2022 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Promulgates **September 2022** edition of Minimum FSI CoP + Inspection/Testing CoP. Effective **1 September 2022** for qualifying plan submissions. Read with CL **4/2024** corrigendum for English text fixes.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular announces the September 2022 revision of the Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment, incorporating numerous FSD circular letters and international standard updates from a multi-stakeholder review. The September 2022 CoP becomes the governing FSI standard for new GBP submissions received on or after **1 September 2022** (and major A&A triggering new OP), with voluntary early compliance encouraged.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Effective date & plan submission triggers

| Applies September 2022 CoP | Does not auto-apply |
|---|---|
| **New** GBP for **new building** received ≥ **1 Sep 2022** | Plans approved under earlier CoP edition (requirements frozen at approval) |
| **New** GBP for existing building: **≥50% by volume** rebuilt **or** OP-altering A&A | Minor A&A below threshold |
| Voluntary early compliance | Licensing regimes — requirements at time of approval |

**SD takeaway:** Fix CoP edition at **first FSD/BD submission date** after 1 Sep 2022; do not mix 2005/2012/2022 provisions on one project.
