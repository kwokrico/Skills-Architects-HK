FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2012
Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and
Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment
(April 2012) (English Version)

This Circular Letter announces that the 2005 edition of the Codes of Practice for
Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection, Testing and Maintenance
of Installations and Equipment has been revised. The new edition (April 2012) has
incorporated, inter alia, various provisions of fire services requirements promulgated in
existing FSD Circular Letters. A table showing those existing Circular Letters which have
been incorporated in the new edition of the Codes is attached at the Appendix for reference.

The above new Codes will be implemented with effect from 1 April 2012. The
new Codes are applicable to all new submissions of building plans received by the Buildings
Department and Fire Services Department on and after the implementation date.

In respect of projects with building plans submitted to the Buildings Department
before the implementation date, the fire services requirements prevailing at the time of
submission are still applicable.

Regarding fire services requirements imposed under various licensing regimes, the
fire services requirements prevailing at the time of approval are still applicable.

For building plans submitted after the implementation date involving major
alterations and additions in existing buildings in excess of 50% by volume or where new
Occupation Permit is required, the fire services requirements as stipulated in the new Codes
may be imposed by this Department.

Hard copies of the Codes are under printing and will soon be available for sale at

the Publications Sale Unit of the Information Services Department. Details are available on
the ISD website: http://www.isd.gov.hk/eng/bookorder.htm.
