# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2012 — Codes of Practice for Minimum FSI (April 2012)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 March 2012 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Promulgates **April 2012** edition replacing **July 2005** CoP. Superseded for new submissions by **September 2022** CoP (CL **2/2022**) from **1 Sep 2022**. Retain for projects approved under 2012–2021 regime.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular announces revision of the Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment CoP and the Inspection, Testing and Maintenance CoP, incorporating fire service requirements from prior FSD circular letters into a single April 2012 publication. The April 2012 CoP applies to all new building plan submissions received by BD and FSD on or after **1 April 2012**, with earlier submissions governed by the requirements prevailing at submission date.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Effective date & applicability matrix

| Scenario | Governing standard |
|---|---|
| New GBP received ≥ **1 Apr 2012** | **April 2012 CoP** |
| GBP submitted before 1 Apr 2012 | Requirements at **submission date** |
| Major A&A after 1 Apr 2012: **>50% volume** or **new OP** | **2012 CoP may be imposed** |
| FS(Cap.502) / FS(Cap.572) licensing | **1994 CoP** continues for those regimes |

**SD takeaway:** Record **CoP edition = 2012** on drawing registers for projects between Apr 2012 and Aug 2022.
