# Cap. 95B Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Last updated date on the consolidated PDF: 1 November 2025 | Cap. 95 sub. leg. B

> Scope note: Cap. 95B controls sale and supply of portable equipment, who may install or maintain a fire service installation, the owner’s duty to keep it in working order, and the certificate a registered contractor must issue. It is **not** the list of systems a building must have. That list is the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, which regulation 10 uses as the test of efficient working order.

## Regulatory Overview

These regulations require a registered fire service installation contractor for installation, maintenance, inspection and repair of a fire service installation or equipment in premises, and they require the owner to keep that installation in efficient working order and to have it inspected at least once in every 12 months.

Portable equipment and stand-alone fire detectors that the law does not require are outside parts of that regime, and nothing in these regulations states sprinkler, hydrant, detector or tank provisions.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Portable equipment that may be specified (regs. 2, 3, 4, 5)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Portable equipment | Fire service equipment manufactured, used or designed to be used as an independent unit to extinguish, attack, prevent or limit a fire |
| Stand-alone fire detector | A self-contained battery-operated installation manufactured, used or designed to be used to detect fire and give warning, whether or not that is its sole purpose |
| Registered contractor | A fire service installation contractor registered under the Fire Service (Installation Contractors) Regulations (Cap. 95 sub. leg. A) |
| Approval | The Director may approve any type of portable equipment for sale or supply (reg. 3(1)) |
| Equipment list | The Director keeps the list and publishes it in the Gazette at least once every year, and otherwise as the Director considers appropriate (reg. 4) |
| Sale or supply | A person must not sell or supply portable equipment that is not on the list, or that is on the list marked as approval expired (reg. 5) |

**SD takeaway:** Specify only portable extinguishers, blankets or similar units that are on the Director’s current equipment list; an unlisted or expired type cannot be sold or supplied.

### 2. Who may install, maintain, inspect or repair (regs. 6, 7)

| Rule | Who may do the work |
|---|---|
| reg. 6(1) | No fire service installation or equipment shall be installed in any premises by any person other than a registered contractor |
| reg. 6(2) | That prohibition does not apply to portable equipment or a stand-alone fire detector that is not required by or pursuant to law to be installed |
| reg. 7(1) | No person other than a registered contractor shall maintain, inspect or repair any fire service installation or equipment installed in any premises |
| reg. 7(2) | The same exemption as reg. 6(2) for portable equipment or a stand-alone fire detector not required by law |
| reg. 7(3) | A registered fire engineer (fire service installation) may inspect and test any fire service installation or equipment installed in, or for, premises prescribed by regulations under section 25 of the Ordinance |
| reg. 7(4) | The Director, or a person authorized by the Director in writing, may inspect and test any such installation |
| reg. 11 | The Director may authorize the Deputy Director or a Chief Fire Officer to exercise the Director’s powers or duties under these regulations |

**SD takeaway:** Every system the Code of Practice or another law requires must be installed by a registered contractor; a voluntary domestic smoke alarm or a voluntary extinguisher is the exception, not the building’s certified installations.

### 3. Owner’s duty, and when a domestic portable unit drops out (reg. 8)

| Duty | Interval |
|---|---|
| reg. 8(1)(a) | Keep the fire service installation or equipment in efficient working order at all times |
| reg. 8(1)(b) | Have it inspected by a registered contractor at least once in every 12 months |
| reg. 8(2) | Paragraph (1) does not apply to a stand-alone fire detector that is not required by or pursuant to law |
| reg. 8(3) | Paragraph (1) does not apply to listed portable equipment installed in domestic premises and not required by or pursuant to law |

Domestic premises means premises intended to be used solely or principally for residential purposes and constructed as a separate unit. The definition excludes:

| Not domestic premises for reg. 8 |
|---|
| A child care centre, or a mutual help child care centre, under the Child Care Services Ordinance (Cap. 243) |
| A hotel or guesthouse within the Hotel and Guesthouse Accommodation Ordinance (Cap. 349) |
| A bedspace apartment under the Bedspace Apartments Ordinance (Cap. 447) |
| A residential care home under the Residential Care Homes (Elderly Persons) Ordinance (Cap. 459) |
| A treatment centre under the Drug Dependent Persons Treatment and Rehabilitation Centres (Licensing) Ordinance (Cap. 566) |
| A residential care home for persons with disabilities under the Residential Care Homes (Persons with Disabilities) Ordinance (Cap. 613) |
| Specified premises: premises used for an operation that provides accommodation and is regulated through a licensing or registration regime established by an Ordinance |

**SD takeaway:** Budget a registered-contractor inspection at least every 12 months for every required installation; a hotel, guesthouse, bedspace apartment or care home is not “domestic premises”, so the portable-equipment exemption in reg. 8(3) does not apply there.

### 4. Certificate after installation or inspection (reg. 9)

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| When | Whenever a registered contractor installs, maintains, repairs or inspects any fire service installation or equipment |
| Time | Within 14 days after completion, issue a certificate to the person on whose instructions the work was undertaken, and forward a copy to the Director |
| Must state | Address of the premises; description of the installation or equipment; date of completion of the work; nature of the work; whether or not it is in efficient working order |
| Signature | Signed by the person authorized under regulation 3A of the Fire Service (Installation Contractors) Regulations (Cap. 95 sub. leg. A) |

**SD takeaway:** The schematic package is not complete at occupation until a registered contractor can certify the installation in efficient working order and copy that certificate to the Director within 14 days.

### 5. The Code of Practice is the working-order test (reg. 10)

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| reg. 10(1) | The Director may, by notice in the Gazette, prescribe a Code of Practice governing inspection and testing of fire service installation or equipment |
| reg. 10(2) | An installation or equipment is deemed in efficient working order if it complies with the requirements the Director specifies in that Code |
| reg. 10(3) | That Code is published in the Gazette at least once every year, and is available for inspection free of charge at all reasonable times at Fire Services Department offices and at each fire station |
| reg. 12 | Contravention of regulation 5, 6, 7 or 8 is an offence liable to a fine at level 5 |

**SD takeaway:** Design to the gazetted Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment; regulation 10 deems compliance with that Code to be efficient working order, and these regulations add no separate hydraulic or coverage numbers.
