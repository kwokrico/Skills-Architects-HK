# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2013 — Fire Hydrant / Hose Reel System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 August 2013 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Amends **FSI Code (April 2012)** hydrant/hose reel extent via Amendment Sheet 2/2013. Effective initial submissions **≥ 1 October 2013** (voluntary earlier OK; recommended for A&A).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular decouples fire hydrant and hose reel siting logic, allowing hydrants in **all approach lobbies to required staircases/staircase enclosures** while hose reels remain on the **30 m reach** rule. The change eliminates the previous coupled layout that forced elongated approach lobbies and aligns hydrant provision with FS Code maximum travel distances.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised extent wording (FSI CoP ¶4.x / ¶5.14)

| System | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Fire hydrants** | In **all approach lobbies** to **required staircases** or **required staircase enclosures** |
| Buildings **without required staircase** | Hydrants **to satisfaction of DFS** (case-by-case) |
| **Hose reels** | Sufficient on each floor so every part reachable within **≤ 30 m hose reel tubing** |
| **Coupling** | Hydrant and hose reel spacing assessed **separately** if fire safety/operations not compromised |

### 2. Prohibited hydrant location

| Location | Status |
|---|---|
| **Central core of staircase enclosure** | **Prohibited** — hose would cross MOE when connected |

### 3. Fire-engineering / non-standard buildings

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Travel distance **> FS Code** limits (fire-engineered) | Hydrant layout **case-by-case** |
| No required staircase | Hydrants **case-by-case** |

**SD takeaway:** Lobby planning can be **compact** — place hydrants in **every required stair approach lobby**, hose reels on **30 m coverage** in corridors/units; never locate hydrants at **stair core centre**.
