# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2000 — Exit and Directional Signs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 April 2000 | Fire Services Department — Fire Protection Command

> Amends FSI CoP §5.10. Later supplemented by CL **5/2008** (graphical symbol exit signs). Green/black colour combo deleted after grace to **1.10.2000**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular updates acceptance of **exit and directional signs** under the Minimum FSI CoP: when self-luminous signs are allowed, how radioactive signs must be labelled, and which colour contrasts remain acceptable. It applies from the letter date with no retrospective effect on existing compliant signs (except green/black after the grace period).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Self-luminous signs — ownership / management gate

Allowed as **both** exit and directional signs **only** where premises are under **single ownership or central management**, e.g.:

- Premises needing A/V advisory systems (§5.1)  
- Government buildings; gymnasiums / stadiums  
- Hotels / service apartments; institutional buildings (§3.1)  
- Museums, exhibition halls, libraries, places of worship  
- Offices; parking garages; passenger/cargo terminals; tunnels; temporary show flats  
- Other premises approved by DFS from time to time  

**SD takeaway:** Multi-owner strata without central management → plan for **internally illuminated** signs (mains + emergency / secondary battery), not tritium self-luminous.

### 2. Internally illuminated signs

- To **BS 5499 Part 3**  
- Connected to **mains + emergency power**; if no emergency generator → secondary battery per **BS 5266 Part 1**

### 3. Radioactive (tritium) self-luminous labelling

- Indelible mark at lower corner: radiation hazard + **expiry (Month/Year)**  
- Back label: disposal method per Radiation Board  

### 4. Colours (post this CL)

| Allowed contrast | Deleted |
|---|---|
| Green / White; White / Green | **Green / Black** (not accepted after **1.10.2000**) |

Prefer white figure/lettering on internally illuminated and self-luminous signs; keep contrast **consistent** throughout the same building.

### 5. Size / language / directional need

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Wording | English + Chinese, characters **≥ 125 mm** high; Helvetica / Marigold / Modified Garamond; Chinese stroke widths ≥15 mm vertical / ≥10 mm horizontal |
| Directional signs | Required if sign not immediately above exit **or** exit not clearly visible from normally occupied parts |

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate exit/directional locations with MOE routes at SD; CL **5/2008** later adds graphic-only options — pick **one type per new development**.
