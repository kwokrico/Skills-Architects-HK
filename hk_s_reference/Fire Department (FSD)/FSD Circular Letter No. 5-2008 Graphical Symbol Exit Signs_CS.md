# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2008 — Graphical Symbol Exit Signs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
13 May 2008 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective for new exit-sign installations from **1 September 2008**

> Read with FSI CoP §5.10 and CL **2/2000**. Other §5.10 specs (power, contrast, placement) still apply.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular accepts three exit-sign designs in Hong Kong: traditional bilingual text, combined graphical symbol + text, and graphical-symbol-only. It applies to new installations from **1.9.2008**; new buildings must use a **single type** within the same development.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Three accepted designs

| Design | Content | Key size |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Current exit sign** | Existing bilingual text design (EXIT / 出口) | Per CoP §5.10 |
| **(b) Combined symbol + characters** | Graphical symbol **between** Chinese and English | Symbol **≥ 125 mm × 125 mm** (scale per Fig. 4) |
| **(c) Graphical symbol only** | Symbol alone | Symbol **≥ 125 mm × 125 mm**; Fig. 4: **a ≥ 125 mm**, **b ≥ 10 mm** |

### 2. Uniformity rule (SD / fit-out)

| Building status | Rule |
|---|---|
| **New buildings / same development** | **Single type only** throughout |
| **Existing buildings** | Mixed types acceptable; encourage single type on major replacement |

**SD takeaway:** Spec one exit-sign family in the SD FSI strategy; do not mix text-only and graphic-only on a new tower.
