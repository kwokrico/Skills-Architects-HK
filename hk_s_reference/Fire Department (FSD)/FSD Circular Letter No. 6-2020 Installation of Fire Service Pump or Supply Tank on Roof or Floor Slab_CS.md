# FSD Circular Letter No. 6/2020 — Installation of Fire Service Pump or Supply Tank on Roof or Floor Slab
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 September 2020 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Supersedes **CL 1/2012**. Aligns FS pump/tank works with **BD Minor Works Control System (MWCS)** from **1 September 2020**. Appendix B route retained for pumps **directly seated** on slab without structural works.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular routes qualifying FS pump/tank installation and supporting-structure works through **BD MWCS** (Building (Minor Works) (Amendment) Regulation 2020), superseding CL 1/2012. FSD's **structural certificate** process (Appendix B) is preserved where a pump sits directly on an existing roof/floor slab without construction of plinths, spreaders or other building works.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New MWCS minor works items (Class I / III examples)

| MW item | Typical FS application | Key limits (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| **1.49** GRP water tank on slab | On-grade/roof FS tanks | Capacity **≤ 4.5 m³**; no other structural alteration; Class **I** |
| **1.50** Supporting structure/casing on roof/slab | FS pump support/casing | Structure height **≤ 1.5 m**; casing weight **≤ 10%** of installation; Class **I** |
| **3.50** Larger/heavier supports | FS pump **≤ 200 kg** with limits on weight/area | Class **III** |

### 2. Direct slab-seated pump — no building works (Appendix B)

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Applicability | Pump **directly on** existing roof/floor slab — **no** plinth/block/spreader/structural system |
| Structural check | **AP or RSE** confirms adequacy; keeps calculations available |
| Completion | RFSIC installs → AP/RSE submits **structural certificate + plan + photos** to FSD |
| FSD action | **100% audit** referral to BD SCU (30-day response target) |
| Non-compliance | BD may act on **uncertified slab-seated pumps** |

**SD takeaway:** At SD choose **plant weight path** — light direct-mounted pumps may use **Appendix B certificate**; tanks/frames triggering MWCS need **MW contractor + BD MW submission**, not full GBP for minor supports.
