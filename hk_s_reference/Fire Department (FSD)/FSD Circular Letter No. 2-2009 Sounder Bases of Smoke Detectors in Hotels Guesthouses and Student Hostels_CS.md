# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2009 — Sounder Bases of Smoke Detectors in Hotels, Guesthouses and Student Hostels
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 July 2009 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 September 2009** for initial building plan submissions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular requires smoke detectors in all guestrooms of hotels/guesthouses and bedrooms of student hostels to use proprietary integrated sounder bases, enhancing local alarm audibility in sleeping areas in addition to the general building fire alarm.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Detector and sounder specification

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Smoke detector installation | BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008 |
| Sounder base | BS EN 54-3:2001 (or FSD-approved equivalent) |
| Exemption | Detectors in concealed spaces and ceiling voids above false ceiling |

### 2. Alarm behaviour and sound level

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| On detector actuation | Integrated sounder **and** general building fire alarm both sound |
| Measurement condition | 1 m above floor, directly below sounder; all guestroom/bedroom windows fully open, doors closed |
| Minimum sound level | Per FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2009, List 2, Item 2.35 (when general alarm absent) |

**SD takeaway:** Hospitality and student hostel room layouts must schedule sounder-base detectors (not plain detectors) in every guest/bedroom — allow ceiling/device coordination with MEP and confirm dB compliance at layout stage.
