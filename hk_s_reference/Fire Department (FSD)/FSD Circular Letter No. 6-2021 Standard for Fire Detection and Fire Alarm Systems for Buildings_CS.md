# FSD Circular Letter No. 6/2021 — Standard for Fire Detection and Fire Alarm Systems for Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 June 2021 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Adopts **BS 5839-1:2017 (+ Corrigendum 1)** with Technical Guidance local schedule + revised T&C Checklist. Supersedes BS 5839-1:2002 regime (CL 1/2009 / 1/2015 checklist basis). Effective initial FSD submissions **≥ 1 January 2022**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular adopts **BS 5839-1:2017 (+ Corrigendum 1)** subject to the enclosed **Technical Guidance** and a **revised Testing and Commissioning Checklist**, replacing the BS 5839-1:2002 regime under CL 1/2009. Hong Kong does **not** adopt BS L/P system categories; FSI extent follows **FSI CoP and approved plans**, with local modifications effective for initial FSD submissions from **1 January 2022**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Key HK deltas vs raw BS 5839-1:2017 (SD-relevant)

| Topic | Local modification |
|---|---|
| System categories (L/P) | **Clause 8.2 not applied** — follow FSI CoP / approved plans |
| Variations from standard | **Clause 7.2 reference only** — FSD/FSCoP governs |
| Definitions | Added **fire service access point**, **ultimate place of safety**, **emergency generator**; revised **ARC**, **fire-resisting construction**, **maximum alarm load**, **primary power supply** |
| A&A applicability | Extensions/alterations excluded unless **> 50% by volume** major A&A |
| Explosive atmospheres | Revise to **BS EN 60079-14 / 60079-17** or FSD-accepted equivalent |

### 2. Circuit integrity — addressable systems (massing / riser planning)

| Fault scenario | Maximum disabled area |
|---|---|
| Single open/short on detection/MCP circuit | **≤ 2,000 m²** aggregate floor (detector-covered areas only) **and** not more than **one floor** |
| Two simultaneous faults | **≤ 10,000 m²** gross floor area |
| Non-addressable | Separate rules for MCP vs detection circuits (see Guidance Figure A) |

**SD takeaway:** Large open floors need **loop/zone segmentation** at SD — do not leave single-loop coverage across multiple floors or > 2,000 m² detector areas.

### 3. Detector placement — revised HK limits

| Item | Local rule |
|---|---|
| Ceiling height "H" | **Structural floor to structural ceiling** |
| Horizontal voids | **≥ 800 mm** height → detection required in void (with stated exceptions) |
| Beam obstructions | Beams **> 10% of H** subdivide void like walls |
| Mounting below ceiling | Smoke: **25–600 mm**; heat: **25–150 mm** |
| Intermediate surfaces | **> 3.5 m wide** and underside **> 800 mm** above floor → protection underneath |

### 4. Revised ceiling height limits (Table 3 — selected)

| Detector | Max ceiling (general) | Up to 10% of ceiling area |
|---|---|---|
| Heat Class A1 | **9.0 m** | **10.5 m** |
| Other heat classes | **7.5 m** | **10.5 m** |
| Point smoke | **10.5 m** | **12.5 m** |
| CO | **10.5 m** | **12.5 m** |
| Optical beam (normal) | **25.0 m** | **28.0 m** |
| Aspirating Class C (≥15 holes) | **25.0 m** | **28.0 m** |

### 5. Checklist deltas architects must design to

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Open-kitchen residential flat** | Smoke detector with **sounder base inside flat** **and** smoke detector in **common area outside flat** |
| Cables | **CL 2/2017** minimum fire-resisting cable schedule |
| Linear heat detection | **System integrity** requirements added to checklist |
| MCP at wide exits | Exit opening **≥ 12 m** → **two MCPs** (one each end) |
| Detector removal | Must trigger **audio + visual fault** at CIE |

**SD takeaway:** High/atrium ceilings, open kitchens and deep ceiling voids are **detector-type selection** issues at SD — not detail-stage surprises. Download full Technical Guidance + Checklist from [FSD notices](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/fire_protection/notices/).
