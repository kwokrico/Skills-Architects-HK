# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-2017 Chinese Version of the Codes of Practice for Minimum FSI April 2012
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
6 December 2017 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: Announces the Chinese version of the April 2012 Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and for Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment. This letter does not add design dimensions, ratings, or installation rules.

## Regulatory Overview
The circular releases the Chinese text of those two April 2012 Codes and requires that text to be read with FSD Circular Letter Nos. 2/2013, 1/2015, 1/2016, 1/2017 and 2/2017. Schematic FSI criteria remain the Codes and those circulars.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

**SD takeaway:** Lock schematic FSI to the April 2012 Codes read with Circular Letters 2/2013, 1/2015, 1/2016, 1/2017 and 2/2017; this letter only supplies the Chinese text.
