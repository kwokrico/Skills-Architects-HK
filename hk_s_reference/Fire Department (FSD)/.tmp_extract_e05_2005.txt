FSD Circular Letter No. 5 /2005 
 
Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment 
and Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment 
(July 2005) 
 
 
 
This Circular Letter announces that the 1998 edition of Codes of Practice 
for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection, Testing and 
Maintenance of Installations and Equipment has been revised.  The new edition (July 
2005) has incorporated with, inter alia, various provisions of fire service requirements 
promulgated in existing Circular Letters.  In this connection, a summary table 
showing those existing Circular Letters which have been incorporated in the new 
edition of Codes is attached at Appendix I for reference. 
 
The above new edition of Codes will be implemented with effect from 
16.12.2005.  They are fully applicable to all new submissions of building plans 
received by Buildings Department from the implementation date inclusive and 
subsequently endorsed by this Department. 
 
In respect of building projects with building plans approved by this 
Department before the implementation date, the fire service requirements prevailing at 
the time of approval are still applicable. 
 
In respect of fire service requirements imposed under various licensing 
regimes, the fire service requirements prevailing at the time of approval are still 
applicable.

For building plan submissions after the implementation date involving 
major alterations and additions in existing buildings in excess of 50% by volume or 
where new Occupation Permit is required, the fire service requirements as stipulated in 
the new edition of Codes may be imposed. 
 
Printed copies of the English version of the new edition of Codes are now 
available for sale at the Publications Sales Unit, Room 402, 4/F., Murray Building, 
Garden Road, Central, Hong Kong at HK $28 per copy.  The Chinese version is under 
printing and will be available for sale before end of this year. 
 
Both English and Chinese versions of the new edition of Codes are 
available on the FSD website : www.hkfsd.gov.hk. 
 
For the purpose of the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance and 
the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance, the 1994 edition of the Codes will continue to 
be applicable. 
 
Opportunity is taken to remind all Class 3 registered FSI contractors that 
the revised version of maintenance label for portable hand-operated approved 
appliance as promulgated in paragraph 2.19 of the Special Supplement No. 4 to 
Gazette No. 31/2004 issued on 30.7.2004 has taken effect on 1.2.2005.  The old label 
as depicted in Appendix D of Part VI in Circular Letter No. 4/96 has become obsolete 
and no longer valid since 1.2.2005.
