# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2016 — Improvised Hose Reel System (Cap. 572)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
6 September 2016 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Read with FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2007.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular accepts an improvised hose reel system with direct water supply from Government mains (direct-feed type) for the **non-domestic portion of target composite buildings of 3 storeys or below** under Cap. 572, eliminating supply tank and fixed fire pumps after successful pilot trial.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Scope and eligibility

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Building type | Target composite buildings under Cap. 572 |
| Storeys | Non-domestic portion **≤ 3 storeys** |
| Replaces | Conventional HR with supply tank + fixed pumps |

### 2. Additional components (beyond 1994 FSI CoP §5.11/5.14)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Backflow preventer | BS EN 12729:2002 Family B Type A (or WA-approved equivalent); **Water Authority approval** before construction |
| Flow switch + alarm bell/sounder | Visual indication on fire alarm panel; independent from MCP alarm |
| Gate valve | Secured and locked open |
| Secondary electricity | Required (72 hr battery capacity for FA system; 30 min device operation, or power company cut-out) |
| Hydraulic calculation | Required in FSI drawing submission |

### 3. Maintenance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual RFSIC inspection | Same as conventional HR |
| Backflow preventer test | Manufacturer's procedure at acceptance and annual inspection |
| FS251 | Record in Parts 1–3 as appropriate |

**SD takeaway:** Low-rise composite podium HR upgrades without tank space can propose direct-feed HR — coordinate WSD backflow preventer approval and hydraulic calc with RFSIC at SD for Cap. 572 compliance paths.
