# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2017 — Minimum Fire Resisting Cable Requirements for Fire Service Installations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 September 2017 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Supersedes **CL 1/2003** and scattered cable rules in FSI CoP / CL 4/96 / CL 1/2009 / CL 1/2015. Effective initial BD and/or FSD submissions **≥ 12 June 2018**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular consolidates **minimum fire-survival cable standards** for all FSI circuits into Appendix I, superseding CL 1/2003 and scattered cable rules in FSI CoP and earlier circular letters. Cables must meet the listed BS/EN categories unless a stated **exemption condition** applies, effective for initial BD/FSD submissions from **12 June 2018**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Standard vs enhanced cables — fire alarm / detection (Items 19–20)

| Category | Acceptable standards (any one path) |
|---|---|
| **Standard** (BS 5839-1) | BS 5839-1; or BS EN 50200 **PH30** + Annex E; or BS EN 60702; or BS 7629-1 Cat. Standard 30; or BS 7846 Cat. F2/F30; or BS 6387 CWZ |
| **Enhanced** | BS EN 50200 **PH120** + BS 8434-2; or BS EN 60702; or BS 7629-1 Cat. Enhanced 120; or BS 7846 Cat. F2/F120; or BS 6387 CWZ |

### 2. Emergency lighting + exit signs (Item 17)

| Category | Key survival time |
|---|---|
| Standard | PH**60** / Cat. Standard 60 / CWZ paths (see Appendix I) |
| Enhanced | PH**120** / Cat. Enhanced 120 paths |

### 3. Power circuits to FSI plant (Items 2–16, 21–22, emergency generator)

| Requirement | **120-minute** fire survival (BS 8491, PH120, Cat. Enhanced 120, CWZ, etc.) |
|---|---|
| Applies to | Sprinkler, deluge, drencher, FH/HR, foam, pressurization, smoke extract, water mist/spray, ring main, FS water supply, **generator to main essential switchboard**, **switchboard to fireman's lift**, etc. |

### 4. Common exemptions (route planning at SD)

| Exemption | Condition (summary) |
|---|---|
| (a) | Cables entirely within **switch/plant room or FSCC** serving equipment in same room |
| (b) | Between **two adjoining** switch/plant rooms through common wall |
| (c–d) | In concealed conduit embedded **≥ 12 mm** in concrete/plaster (plaster route needs sprinkler/water-based or clean-agent protection) |
| (e–f) | Underground ducts/trenches; soil **≥ 300 mm** |
| (g) | Dedicated **fire-resisting cable ducts/enclosures** ≥ compartment rating |
| (h) | Flexible/surface metal conduit **≤ 2 m** in sprinkler/protected area |
| (i) | Surface metal conduit/trunking **≤ 3 m** within same compartment to FA panel/repeater |

**SD takeaway:** Riser and cross-floor **FSI power/control routing** must be planned for **enhanced fire cables** unless an exemption applies — affects **conduit space, riser size and EL/FA circuit segregation** at SD.
