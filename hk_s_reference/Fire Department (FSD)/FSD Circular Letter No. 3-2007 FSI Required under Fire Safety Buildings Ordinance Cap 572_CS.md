# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2007 — Fire Service Installations and Equipment required under the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 August 2007 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Cap. 572 in force from 1 July 2007; sets pragmatic relaxations for pre-1987 composite and domestic buildings.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular explains FSD's flexible enforcement approach under Cap. 572 for old buildings with structural or spatial constraints, listing example relaxations that may be accepted where a reasonable fire safety level is still achieved.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Non-domestic portion — ≤ 6 storeys

| System | Possible relaxation |
|---|---|
| Sprinkler | Not required if aggregate non-domestic floor area ≤ **230 m²** |
| Sprinkler (constraints) | Improvised sprinkler: town's main direct feed (height ≤ **15 m**), connection from existing FH/HR, or reduced tank — **FSCC direct line required** for improvised sprinkler alarm |
| Fire hydrant | Not required if direct vehicular access to major facade |
| Hose reel | Reduced tubing length, smaller drums, or high-level drums; extinguishers instead if MOE still seriously obstructed |
| Emergency lighting | Not required for separate rooms < **8 m²** adjacent to lit exit routes |

### 2. Non-domestic portion — > 6 storeys

| System | Possible relaxation |
|---|---|
| Sprinkler | Same ≤ 230 m² exemption and improvised options as above |
| Hose reel | Reduced tubing / high-level drums (no extinguisher substitution stated) |
| Emergency lighting | Same < 8 m² room exemption |

### 3. Domestic portion / domestic building

| Height | Relaxation examples |
|---|---|
| ≤ 6 storeys | FH waived with direct vehicular access; HR improvisations; extinguishers if improvised HR still obstructs MOE |
| > 6 storeys | HR reduced tubing / high-level drums only |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 572 upgrade surveys should document structural/spatial constraints early — relaxations are case-by-case merits, not automatic; every improvised sprinkler needs FSCC alarm linkage.
