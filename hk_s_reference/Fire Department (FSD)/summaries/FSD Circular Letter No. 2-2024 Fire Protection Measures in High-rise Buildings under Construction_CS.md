# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2024 — Fire Protection Measures in High-rise Buildings under Construction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 March 2024 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 July 2024** for initial submissions; supersedes CL 2/2008 for later projects (voluntary early compliance encouraged).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular replaces CL 2/2008 with enhanced requirements for buildings under construction with designed height **≥ 30 m**, mandating closed-circuit water relaying and additional site fire protection measures when construction reaches 30 m above ground.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Scope and trigger

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Applies when | Designed construction height **≥ 30 m** from ground/street level |
| Activate at | Construction reaches **30 m** height |
| Exemption | Blocks where staircase not required under Buildings Ordinance — no closed-circuit relay |

### 2. Closed-circuit water relay — revised key values

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| F.S. inlet | Prominent exterior ground location near staircase; coupling centre **600–1,000 mm** AFF; ≥ 100 mm clearance in enclosure; bilingual **TEMPORARY F.S. INLET** signage |
| Permanent FH not commissioned | Blue tape + yellow notice: not ready until works complete |
| Riser | ≥ **80 mm** at designated access staircase; pump bypass with non-return valve if pumps interposed |
| Fire pump | Electrical; ≥ **900 L/min** total (2 × 450 L/min at 350–850 kPa) with 800 kPa inlet boost test; manual run until stopped; start/stop at inlet + pump location |
| Hydrant outlets | Every stabilized floor; BS 5041 Pt 1 / BS 336; **800–1,200 mm** AFF; topmost ≤ **3 storeys or 30 m** below top working floor |
| Site hoses | ≥ **2 × 70 mm × 20 m** BS 336 hoses + nozzle every **5 storeys** at outlets |
| Secondary power | Auto switchover; external source (e.g. diesel) ≥ **6 hr** full pump load, available ≤ **15 s** |
| Reuse | Temporary relay components may be reused in permanent FH/HR under BO s.16(1)(b)(ii) |

### 3. RGBC/AP ongoing duties

| Duty | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maintenance | Regular check — system efficient at all times |
| Fire station notification | Written notice at key stages (start, 30 m reached, pump install, FSI acceptance) |
| Defects/shutdown | Inform local station immediately; alternative measures without delay |
| Site information | A3 bilingual plans at site entrance and each building entrance (construction status, pump location, schematic power, layout) |
| Enforcement | Fire Hazard Abatement Notices under Cap. 95F for non-provision or poor maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** CL 2/2024 tightens temporary FH performance (3-storey top outlet rule, 6 hr secondary power, site hoses every 5 floors) — integrate temporary/permanent riser design and RGBC notification milestones into construction programme from SD.
