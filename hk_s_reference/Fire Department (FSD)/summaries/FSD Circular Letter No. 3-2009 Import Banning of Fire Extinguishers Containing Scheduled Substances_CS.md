# FSD Circular Letter No. 3-2009 Import Banning of Fire Extinguishers Containing Scheduled Substances
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
11 December 2009 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular stops new approval to sell or supply fire extinguishers that contain scheduled ozone-depleting substances, from 1 January 2010. It is not a selection table for replacement agents, and it does not require removal of units already in use.

## Regulatory Overview
The import ban on fire extinguishers containing a scheduled substance listed in Part 1, 2, 3, 8 or 9 of the Schedule to the Ozone Layer Protection Ordinance, Cap. 403, comes into operation on 1 January 2010. From that date the Department gives no new approval for the sale or supply of those extinguishers in Hong Kong.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Substances and dates (letter paras (a) and (b); footnote 1)

| Schedule part | Substance named in the circular | Effect in this circular |
|---|---|---|
| Parts 1 and 3 | Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) | Within the 1 January 2010 import ban and the stop on new sale or supply approval |
| Part 2 | Halons | Already banned since 1994 |
| Part 8 | Hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs) | Within the 1 January 2010 ban |
| Part 9 | Bromochloromethane (BCM) | Newly added to the Schedule; within the 1 January 2010 ban |
| Previously type-approved units listed in the Appendix | May still be sold or supplied until delisted from the Gazette, stated as probably in two years | Not a new-approval route |
| Units already in use, including sprayer units | May continue in use if in efficient working order and properly maintained | Early replacement is advised |

**SD takeaway:** Do not specify a new CFC, halon, HCFC or BCM extinguisher or sprayer unit for sale or supply on a project after 1 January 2010.

### 2. What the Appendix records (Appendix)

The Appendix lists previously type-approved models still allowed onto the market only until Gazette delisting. Families named include HCFC / chlorotetrafluoroethane (FE-241) automatic and manual-automatic units, NAF-SIII, NAFS-III, Hartindo AF 11E (HCFC / dichlorotrifluoroethane), halogenated hydrocarbon compound, and Halotron I. Where a maximum protected volume is printed, it is a property of that listed model (for example Fireboy CG2 / MA2 volumes, Sea-Fire FE-241 volumes, and Walter Kidde FE-241 volumes). It is not a design allowance for a new scheduled-substance installation.

**SD takeaway:** Size any replacement from an extinguisher that is still approvable; do not reuse an Appendix model's protected volume as the basis for a new scheduled-substance specification.
