# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2019 — Guidance Notes on FSI in Modular Integrated Construction Building Projects
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 March 2019 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular provides guidance on submission, approval and acceptance inspection of fire service installations (FSI) in Modular Integrated Construction (MiC) projects where modules are prefabricated off-site with FSI or associated assembly installed during factory production.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Submission requirements (design stage)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| General Building Plans | AP must declare MiC method in F.S. Notes and covering letters |
| Early engagement | AP + RFSIC at design stage for issues not encountered in conventional construction |
| Covered-up FSI | Adequate access points, inspection pits or recesses for install/test/maintenance |
| Flexible pipe joints | Permitted between modules for services connection where required |
| Cabling between modules | Allow on-site power/control cable installation; **no cable joints** on fire-resistant FSI cables |
| Equipment & materials | Product listing / FSD acceptance per Circular Letter No. 1/2007 |

### 2. Off-site quality assurance

| Responsibility | Detail |
|---|---|
| RFSIC supervision | Regular supervision during module prefabrication; inspection log book with RFSIC names/FSD-RC numbers |
| Site delivery | Inspect FSI in modules after delivery, before on-site assembly |
| Assembly monitoring | RFSIC to monitor proper fixing of FSI elements during module connection |

### 3. Acceptance inspection

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Procedure | Same as conventional buildings |
| Pre-requisite | Strictly adhere to FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2015 procedures |
| Ultimate responsibility | RFSIC ensures FSI compliance regardless of construction method |

**SD takeaway:** MiC FSI needs accessible inspection openings, joint-free fire-rated cabling between modules and a documented off-site QA log — flag covered sprinkler heads, HR points and FA devices on module junction drawings at SD.
