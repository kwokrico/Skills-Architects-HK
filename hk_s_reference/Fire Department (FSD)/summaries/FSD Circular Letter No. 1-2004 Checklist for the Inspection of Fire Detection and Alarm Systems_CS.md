# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-2004 Checklist for the Inspection of Fire Detection and Alarm Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
23 March 2004 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular replaces Appendix 4 of the Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and for Inspection, Testing and Maintenance, for projects completed after FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2002 (1 January 2003). It is the compliance-inspection checklist against BS 5839: Part 1: 1988, the LPC detection rules and cited FSD circulars. It is not a new detector-spacing code.

## Regulatory Overview
The attached checklist is the acceptance test for fire detection and alarm systems designed to the LPC Rules and BS 5839: Part 1: 1988 adopted by Circular Letter No. 1/2002. Zoning limits, device positions, sound levels, panel location, standby duration and cable fire performance below are the schematic locks that inspection will measure.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Detection coverage and zoning (checklist 4.2; BS 7.2; LPC 6(d); CL 1/2002 paras 2.4)

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| One detection loop | Aggregate floor area of detector-protected portions ≦ 10,000 m² (4.2.7) |
| One detection zone | Aggregate floor area of detector-protected portions ≦ 2,000 m² (4.2.8, BS 7.2(a)) |
| Two simultaneous faults | Must not remove protection from an aggregate detector area greater than 10,000 m² (5.6.4, LPC 6.6.2) |
| Building total floor area ≦ 300 m², more than one storey | Single detection zone is accepted (4.2.11, BS 7.2(c)) |
| Building total floor area > 300 m² | Every zone is one storey, except the two cases below (4.2.12, BS 7.2(d)) |
| Multi-storey fire compartment ≦ 300 m² that meets other compartments only at the lowest level | That compartment may be one zone |
| Stairwell, lightwell, liftwell or other flue-like shaft in one fire compartment | The shaft volume is one or more separate zones |
| Search distance > 30 m | Remote indicator lamp showing the detector position (4.2.9, BS 7.2(b)) |
| Detector in a room whose door is likely to be locked | Remote indicator outside, near the door exit (4.2.10) |
| Sleeping risk (hotel, hospital and similar) | Heat detector in the kitchen; smoke detectors in other areas, except sprinkler-protected toilets, bathrooms and staircases (4.2.4) |
| Basement | Detectors throughout, except car parking, strong room and safe-deposit vault (4.2.5) |
| Explosive or flammable gas may be present | Intrinsically safe detector (4.2.6) |

**SD takeaway:** Cap each detection zone at 2,000 m² and each loop at 10,000 m² of detector-covered floor, and keep zones to one storey once the building exceeds 300 m².

### 2. Manual call points (checklist 4.4, 5.3; BS 7.2(f), 10.1, 10.2; CL 1/2002 paras 2.12, 2.23, 2.24)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Zoning | At least one manual-call-point zone per floor or storey (4.4.1) |
| Locations | One call point at each hose-reel point, adjacent to every storey exit, and adjacent to every exit to open air (4.4.2) |
| Travel | ≦ 30 m to a call point (4.4.3) |
| Mounting | About 1.2 m above finished floor (4.4.4) |
| Alarm delay | Operation of a call point to the general alarm ≦ 3 seconds (5.3.4) |
| Pump | Any call point starts the fixed fire pump, regardless of call-point zoning (5.3.2, Code 5.14(b)) |

**SD takeaway:** Place a call point at every hose reel and every storey exit and open-air exit so the walking distance is within 30 m, at about 1.2 m above floor.

### 3. Alarm sounders (checklist 4.3, 5.2; LPC 9.1, 9.2, 9.4; CL 1/2002 paras 2.16, 2.19, 2.20)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| External sounder | Weatherproof sounder outside, near the main entrance (4.3.1) |
| Panel | One sounder near the control and indicating equipment (4.3.2) |
| Hose reels | One sounder at each hose-reel point (4.3.3) |
| Case | Red, marked "FIRE ALARM" in English and Chinese, white lettering; English wording ≧ 10 mm, Chinese wording ≧ 15 mm (4.3.4) |
| Domestic | Minimum level measured 3 m inside the main entrance door, all flat doors shut: ≧ 60 dB(A) and ≧ 5 dB(A) above background noise likely to persist longer than 30 seconds (5.2.2, 5.2.3) |
| Other than domestic | Same measurement point, all room doors shut: ≧ 65 dB(A) and ≧ 5 dB(A) above that background (5.2.4) |
| Noisy machines | Primary sounders meet the level when machine noise has ceased and secondary sounders are out of service (5.2.5) |
| Sequence | Sounding sequence per Circular Letter No. 4/96, Part VIII, Item 4 (5.2.8) |

**SD takeaway:** Design alarm sound to 60 dB(A) in domestic buildings and 65 dB(A) elsewhere, each also 5 dB(A) above persistent background, measured 3 m inside the main entrance with doors shut.

### 4. Visual fire alarm (checklist 4.5, 5.4; CL 4/2001 Part I para. 4)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Signal | Red flashing light (4.5.2) |
| Coverage | At least one lamp per compartment, and areas as on the approved building plans (4.5.3, 4.5.4) |
| Legend | "FIRE ALARM" in English and Chinese; English ≧ 10 mm, Chinese ≧ 15 mm (4.5.1) |
| Design basis | Section 4-4 of NFPA 72: 1999, or clause 9.7 of BS 5839: Part 1: 1988 (4.5.5) |
| Power | DC with battery backup; or AC with emergency generator; or AC taken from before the consumer main switch (5.4.1) |
| Distinction | Clearly distinguishable from non-fire visual signals (5.4.3, BS 9.7) |

**SD takeaway:** Provide a red flashing visual alarm in every compartment that the approved plans mark for visual fire alarm, on one of the three listed supplies.

### 5. Panel, zone diagram and transmission delay (checklist 4.1, 4.6, 4.7, 5.5; BS 15.3.2, 14.6, 14.7; CL 4/2001 Part II; CL 1/2002 paras 2.33, 2.34, 2.38)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Main panel | Ground floor, immediate vicinity of the building entrance used by FSD, or in the fire control centre / room (4.7.1) |
| Repeaters | At other FSD entry points shown on the approved building plans (4.7.2) |
| Beside the panel | Operating instructions, a diagram of entrances, circulation, escape routes and zone divisions, the as-fitted zoning schedule, and the log book (4.1.3 to 4.1.6) |
| Indications | Alarm origin in Chinese and English (4.7.4) |
| Weather | Severe-weather locations need a weatherproof ventilated enclosure (4.7.3) |
| Multiple occupancy | Power-supply equipment sited in common access (4.8.8, BS 16.7.3) |
| Time-related system | FSD approval required; indicator of working or silent hours; manual override only de-activates the system and cannot re-activate it, and no automatic function may cancel that override (4.6, 5.5.7, 5.5.8) |
| Sensitivity changeover | Repeated switch operations must not extend the delay more than 6 minutes after the last operation (5.5.1) |
| Transmission | Automatic transmission to FSCC after a fixed delay ≦ 1 minute unless manually overridden; a further delay ≦ 5 minutes is allowed after manual operation at the control equipment (5.5.2 to 5.5.4) |
| No delay | During any delay, a manual operation at the panel or a manual call point sounds and transmits immediately. A manual call point or a sprinkler flow switch is never delayed (5.5.5, 5.5.6) |
| Restart | After re-initialisation, fault repair or power restoration, a program-controlled system sounds a general alarm within 30 seconds and reaches normal operation within a further 10 minutes (5.6.11) |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve a ground-floor panel at the FSD entrance, or a fire control room, and do not let any time delay hold a manual call point or a sprinkler flow switch.

### 6. Standby power (checklist 4.8, 5.7; BS 16.2, 16.3.2.1, 16.5)

| Condition | Battery duty |
|---|---|
| Supervised at intervals ≦ 12 hours, or a link warns a remote manned centre of normal-supply failure | 24 hours operation, then an evacuation alarm in all zones for at least 30 minutes (5.7.3, BS 16.5.1.2) |
| Not supervised | Not less than 24 hours after a normal-supply fault is detected and remedial action starts (5.7.4) |
| Unoccupied and not supervised for periods > 24 hours | At least 24 hours after reoccupation, then 30 minutes evacuation alarm in all zones (5.7.5) |
| Standby generator backup | Not less than 6 hours, then 30 minutes evacuation alarm in all zones (5.7.6) |
| Charger | Fully discharged batteries recharged to fully charged within 24 hours (4.8.4) |
| Mains connection | Isolating protective device reserved solely for the fire service installations (4.8.5) |
| Each supply | Normal and standby battery supplies each carry the maximum alarm load irrespective of the other (4.8.7, 5.7.1) |
| Labels | White engraving on red: "FIRE ALARM"; "FIRE ALARM DO NOT SWITCH OFF"; or "WARNING: THIS SWITCH ALSO CONTROLS THE SUPPLY TO THE FIRE ALARM SYSTEM", with Chinese wording. English ≧ 10 mm, Chinese ≧ 15 mm (4.8.6) |

**SD takeaway:** Size the alarm battery for 24 hours plus 30 minutes of all-zone evacuation, or 6 hours plus 30 minutes where a standby generator backs the system.

### 7. Fire-alarm cables (checklist 4.9; BS 17.2, 17.4.2, 17.5.2, 17.10; CL 3/2002)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Circuits between visual alarms, sounders, control equipment and power supplies | Suitable for prolonged operation during a fire, or able to resist fire for at least 30 minutes (4.9.1) |
| Prolonged-operation methods | MICS to BS 6207; or BS 6387 Category AWX or SWX; or embedded with the equivalent of at least 12 mm of plaster; or separated from significant fire risk by a wall, partition or floor of at least 30 minutes fire resistance; or another means in an FSD circular (4.9.2) |
| Mechanical protection | Required, other than for MICS to BS 6207 or steel-wire-armoured cable to BS 6346 or BS 5467, where the cable is not monitored, is less than 2.25 m above the floor, or damage or rodent attack is likely (4.9.3) |
| Separation from other systems | Dedicated conduit, duct, trunking or channel; or a rigid continuous non-combustible partition; or at least 300 mm clearance; or BS 7629 cable; or sheathed MICS (4.9.5) |
| Segregation labels | Where not in reserved ducting, trunking or channel, mark at intervals not exceeding 2 m (4.9.6) |
| Joints | Accessible junction box labelled "FIRE ALARM" in English and Chinese, white on red; English ≧ 10 mm, Chinese ≧ 15 mm (4.9.9) |

**SD takeaway:** Route alarm, sounder and visual-alarm cabling as a 30-minute fire-resisting system, separated from other services by a barrier, BS 7629 or MICS, or at least 300 mm.
