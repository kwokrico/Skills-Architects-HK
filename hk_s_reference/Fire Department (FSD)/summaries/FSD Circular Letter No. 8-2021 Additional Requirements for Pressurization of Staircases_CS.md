# FSD Circular Letter No. 8/2021 — Additional Requirements for the Pressurization of Staircases
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 September 2021 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Supplements **FSI CoP §5.21** and **CL 2/2006**. Adds bilingual **Notice Plate** requirement. Effective initial FSD submissions **≥ 1 January 2022** (voluntary earlier; recommended for SPS A&A).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular requires bilingual **Notice Plates** at staircases protected by pressurization so firefighters can immediately see system class and supply-fan operation status. The requirement augments existing indicator-light provisions under FSI CoP §5.21 and CL 2/2006, applying to initial FSD submissions from **1 January 2022**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Notice Plate specification

| Feature | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Wording | Bilingual **"Staircase Pressurization Class A/B"** / **「樓梯加壓 Class A/B」** — specify installed class |
| Letter height | **≥ 50 mm**, **red** on **white or stainless steel** background |
| Indicator | **Red light ON** while **supply fan** of protecting SPS is operating |
| Illumination | EL-backed or internally illuminated, with **emergency power** |

### 2. Locations (per staircase protected by SPS)

| Location | Plate required? |
|---|---|
| **Outside final exit** | **Yes** |
| **Inside and outside exit on top floor** (roof or top exit) | **Yes** |
| **Refuge floor(s)** — inside and outside staircase exit/entrance | **Yes** (if building has refuge floors + SPS) |

**SD takeaway:** Allow **wall area at final exits, roof/refuge stair doors** for notice plates in elevation and signage schedules — coordinate with **CL 2/2006** indicator panel graphics at FS access/FSCC.
