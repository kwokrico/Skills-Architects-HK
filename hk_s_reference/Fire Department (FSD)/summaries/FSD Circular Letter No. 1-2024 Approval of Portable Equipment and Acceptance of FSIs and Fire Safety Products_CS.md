# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-2024 Approval of Portable Equipment and Acceptance of FSIs and Fire Safety Products
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
19 February 2024 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular replaces FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2020 from 1 April 2024 and sets how portable equipment is approved for sale or supply, and how other fire service installations and fire safety products are accepted for use in buildings. The guidance bound into this PDF is marked June 2026. It is not a layout, spacing or hydraulic design code. Products still have to meet the Codes of Practice and the local requirements those codes and circulars call up.

## Regulatory Overview
Under the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations (Cap. 95B), portable equipment must be approved by the Director of Fire Services before it is sold or supplied in Hong Kong, and other fire service installations required for the protection of buildings, together with listed fire safety products, must be of an accepted type. Approval or acceptance is for a maximum of 5 years, and an installation or product that is listed and published by a product certification body is deemed accepted without a separate acceptance application if the listing is still valid and the installation conforms with all relevant local requirements.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What must be an approved or accepted type (Guidance paras 1.1, 6.1–6.3; Appendices I and II)
Portable equipment requiring approval before sale or supply (Appendix I; the list is not exhaustive):

| Item | Types named |
|---|---|
| Fire blanket | — |
| Fire extinguisher | Carbon dioxide; clean agent; dry powder; foam; water; wheeled type; miscellaneous |
| Fixed automatically operated approved appliance (wall or ceiling mounted) | Fixed sprayer unit |

Fire service installations (except portable equipment) and fire safety products requiring acceptance before installation or use (Appendix II; not exhaustive):

| System | Components named |
|---|---|
| Automatic actuating device | Emergency exit device; electromagnetic door releasing device used with a fire detection system |
| Automatic fixed installation other than water | Fixed carbon dioxide; fixed clean agent; fixed dry chemical |
| Automatic fixed installation using water | Deluge (deluge valve, sprinkler head, alarm valve); drencher (nozzle, valve); water spray (nozzle, deluge valve); sprinkler (head, alarm valve, accelerator, motorised butterfly valve, flow switch, piping and fittings such as CPVC, vortex inhibitor, sprinkler tank other than concrete, control and indicator panel, pressure switch, pressure reducing valve); water mist |
| Fire alarm | Manual call point; alarm bell |
| Fire detection | Fire alarm control panel; heat, smoke, beam, integrated smoke, and flame detectors; intrinsically safe or explosion-proof detector; alarm or sounder integrated with strobe used with a fire detection system |
| Fire hydrant / hose reel | Hose reel unit (tubing, nozzle, drum); hydrant inlet and outlet valve; FS inlet and fireboat inlet; pressure reducing valve |
| Fire resisting cable | Cables for fire service installations, except optical fibre cables, which require an application for use in the individual project |
| Fire retardant solution | Wall and ceiling linings to Class 1 or Class 2 of BS 476: Part 7, or another acceptable standard; curtains and drapes to BS 5867: Part 2, or another acceptable standard |
| Fixed foam | Foam monitor; proportioner or maker; bladder tank; ratio flow controller; foam nozzle; foam-water nozzle |
| Petrol filling station | Flammable liquid dispensing device; flammable liquid storage tank |
| Gas detection | Ammonia; carbon monoxide; carbon dioxide; chlorine; hydrogen; methane; special gases used in the micro-electronics industry |
| Smoke control | Smoke extraction fans and air-release fans for staircase pressurisation; fire and smoke dampers and actuators in a smoke extraction system; fire-rated ductwork and smoke barriers; static smoke ventilator |
| Ventilating system | Electrostatic filter or precipitator; fusible link. Under the Building (Ventilating Systems) Regulations (Cap. 123J) and the Ventilation of Scheduled Premises Regulation (Cap. 132CE), a duct through a floor, wall or ceiling is fitted with a damper operated by a fusible link of a type approved by the Director, and every electrostatic filter or precipitator is of a type approved by the Director |

**SD takeaway:** Specify accepted types for the components in Appendix II, and treat optical-fibre fire cables as a project-specific acceptance, not a catalogue type.

### 2. Products that do not need prior acceptance (application-form Appendix II)
Prior acceptance is not required. Conformity and a test certificate or report are still required at acceptance inspection (for example a general restaurant licence or a Fire Services Certificate for a new building). The list is not exhaustive.

| Product | Conformity named in the guidance |
|---|---|
| Emergency lighting | Latest FSD CoP; PPA 104 (central power supply); PPA 104(A) (self-contained luminaires); Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011; Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations |
| Exit sign, including directional sign | Latest FSD CoP; Buildings Department fire safety code; wiring code |
| Strobe light not integrated with an alarm or sounder and not used with an automatic fire alarm system | Latest FSD CoP; FSD circular letter; wiring code. Self-luminous signs must also observe the Radiation Board of Hong Kong |
| Module for an automatic fire alarm system monitor | Latest FSD CoP; wiring code |
| Smoke stop door; door lock; fire or smoke seal for a smoke stop door | Buildings Department fire safety code |
| Door releasing device not used with an automatic fire alarm system | Buildings Department fire safety code; wiring code |
| Piping and associated fittings for sprinkler and fire hydrant / hose reel systems | Latest FSD CoP; FSD circular letter; Water Supplies Department Handbook on Plumbing Installation for Buildings |
| Sand buckets | Latest FSD CoP |
| Equipment in a dangerous-goods store or on a dangerous-goods vehicle, other than a flammable-liquid dispensing device and a flammable-liquid storage tank | Documents submitted with the formal dangerous-goods licence application, not a prior product acceptance |

**SD takeaway:** Emergency lighting, exit signs, smoke-stop doors and ordinary sprinkler or hose-reel pipework are specified to the codes above; they are not held for a separate FSD product-acceptance letter.

### 3. Listing, validity and standards (paras 1.3–1.5, 3.3–3.5, 6.4–6.8, 11.1–11.2, 12.1)
| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Approval of portable equipment | Maximum 5 years. For a product listed by a product certification body, validity runs to the certificate expiry, still capped at 5 years |
| Acceptance of other installations and fire safety products | Maximum 5 years |
| New portable equipment not listed by a certification body | Test report to the current standard, issued within the past 5 years |
| Deemed acceptance | A non-portable installation or fire safety product listed and published by a product certification body needs no acceptance application while the certification is valid and local requirements are met. Delisting invalidates the deemed acceptance |
| Change of conditions | Change of compliance standard, design, manufacturer, or an updated, repealed or obsolete test standard, or a change in certification status, invalidates the approval or acceptance. A fresh application is required. Portable equipment already sold, and installations already installed, may continue in use with proper maintenance by a registered contractor |
| Stock made before a certification-body delisting | Acceptance of stock held by a registered contractor remains valid if, at building-plan submission, the product meets the current minimum-FSI and inspection codes |
| Laboratories | Test reports from laboratories accredited by HKAS under HOKLAS, by CNAS where a mutual recognition arrangement with HKAS exists, or by an overseas body with such an arrangement |
| Standards | Codes of Practice and FSD circular letters, plus national, international or equivalent standards. For portable equipment, mainland Industrial Standards are accepted as equivalent to national and international standards |
| Processing | Acceptance applications for installations other than portable equipment, and for fire safety products, are to be answered within 15 working days of receipt |

**SD takeaway:** A product certification listing can clear acceptance for non-portable equipment, but the specification must still match current local codes at plan submission, and the listing must still be valid.
