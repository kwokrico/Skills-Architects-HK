# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-2022 Calibration Requirements for Test Instruments Used for Acceptance Inspection of FSI
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
12 December 2022 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular sets calibration rules for instruments used at acceptance inspection of fire service installations and equipment. It revises named paragraphs of the Code of Practice for Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment (September 2022). It does not set sprinkler, detection, or means-of-escape design criteria.

## Regulatory Overview
Test instruments used for acceptance inspection must be calibrated by a recognised laboratory, carry a valid certificate and a tamper seal, and, where an appropriate standard exists, be manufactured to a British Standard or a recognised equivalent international standard. The circular takes immediate effect and repeals and replaces FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2016.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Instruments that must be calibrated (paras (a)–(d); Note 1)
| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Instruments | Multi-meter; pressure gauge; test instruments used for pressurisation of staircase and dynamic smoke extraction |
| Laboratory | Recognised testing or calibration laboratory: HKAS accreditation under HOKLAS, or an overseas body that has signed a mutual recognition agreement with HKAS |
| At the test | Valid calibration certificate for each calibrated instrument |
| Seal | Calibration label or serial tape sealed by that laboratory, so the instrument is protected from tampering |
| Manufacture | Appropriate British Standard, or recognised equivalent international standard, where appropriate and available |

**SD takeaway:** Acceptance testing of staircase pressurisation and dynamic smoke extraction cannot be specified around uncalibrated or unsealed instruments; the commissioning set must be a recognised-laboratory calibration.

### 2. Accuracy and validity (paras (e) and (f))
| Instrument | Measuring parameter | Accuracy | Calibration validity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-meter | Electrical characteristics | ±5% | 1 year |
| Pressure gauge | Water pressure | ±5% | 3 years |
| Instruments used for acceptance tests of staircase pressurisation and dynamic smoke extraction | Quantity: provided in duplicate. Exception: simple devices such as pitot-static tubes, inclined manometers, U gauges and the like | ±5% | 1 year |

**SD takeaway:** Pressure gauges used for water-pressure acceptance may be up to 3 years from calibration; staircase-pressurisation and dynamic-smoke-extraction meters are a duplicate set at ±5% and 1 year, excluding those simple devices.

### 3. Amendments to the September 2022 ITM Code (amendment sheet)
| Location replaced | New text |
|---|---|
| Part II, Section 2.21(i), paragraph 6(d) | Calibrated by a recognised testing or calibration laboratory not more than 1 year prior to the date of test. The laboratory calibration certificate shall be available during the test |
| Part II, Section 2.23A.(i), paragraph 5(d) | Same 1-year calibration and certificate-at-test rule |
| Part III, Appendix 6, Section I, paragraph 1.1 | Measuring instrument used for testing shall be provided in duplicate and calibrated in the past 1 year |

**SD takeaway:** Lock the acceptance-test specification to the September 2022 ITM Code as amended by this sheet, not to Circular Letter No. 1/2016.
