# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2021 — Fire Safety Requirements for Emergency Lighting Systems in Licensed/Registered Premises
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 May 2021 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Supersedes **CL 1/2006**. Revised **PPA/104 (5th Revision)** — central supply; **PPA/104(A) (5th Revision)** — self-contained. Effective licensing/FSD applications **≥ 1 June 2022**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular updates FSD's **licensed/registered premises** emergency lighting specifications via revised **PPA/104** (central supply) and **PPA/104(A)** (self-contained) 5th Revisions, superseding CL 1/2006. Requirements align with BS 5266-1:2016 / BS EN 1838:2013 and **CL 2/2017** cables, applying to licensing/FSD applications from **1 June 2022**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Two parallel specification tracks

| Document | System type |
|---|---|
| **PPA/104 (5th Revision)** | Emergency lighting with **central power supply** |
| **PPA/104(A) (5th Revision)** | **Self-contained luminaire** emergency lighting |

Both require compliance with **BS 5266-1:2016** and **BS EN 1838:2013** unless overridden in the PPA schedules.

### 2. Main revisions (SD / fit-out stage)

| Revision area | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cables | **CL 2/2017** minimum fire-resisting cable requirements |
| Maintenance | Routine inspection/testing per **BS EN 50172:2004** |
| Central supply only — escape lighting | Additional escape-lighting provisions for facilities forming part of an escape route |
| Central supply only — performance | Performance verification requirements added |
| Self-contained only — duration | Minimum **2-hour rated duration** for self-contained luminaires |
| Batteries (central) | Heavy-duty rechargeable only; location rules + capacity/type limits (e.g. enclosed types ≤ **400 Ah** per cited BS EN standards) |

### 3. Cinemas / theatres / entertainment venues (central supply extract)

| Occupancy | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **≥ 500 persons** | Enhanced central supply/battery provisions per PPA/104 Section B (review full appendix for auditorium sizing) |

**SD takeaway:** Licensed F&B, entertainment and childcare fit-outs need **early EL system selection** (central vs self-contained) — **2-hour self-contained** vs battery room/plant for central supply. Confirm which PPA schedule applies with licensing authority at concept stage.
