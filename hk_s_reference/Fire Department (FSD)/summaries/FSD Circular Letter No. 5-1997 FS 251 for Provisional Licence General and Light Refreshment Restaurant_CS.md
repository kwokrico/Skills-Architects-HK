# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-1997 FS 251 for Provisional Licence General and Light Refreshment Restaurant
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
13 March 1997 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular sets the submission route for the Certificate of Fire Service Installations and Equipment (F.S.251) and Certificate C when applying for a provisional licence for a general or light refreshment restaurant. It does not set restaurant fire-service design standards. A list of Fire Protection Regional Office addresses is stated to be attached; that list is not in this two-page PDF.

## Regulatory Overview
After the premises meet the fire service requirements, the FSI contractor, the authorized person, and the applicant each have a set document to lodge before the Urban or Regional Council can receive the provisional-licence application. The same works are also reported to the Fire Services Department, and the route depends on whether the work amends the approved building plans.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Documents for the provisional licence (items (i) to (iv))

| Step | Who | Document |
|---|---|---|
| (i) | Responsible FSI contractor | Complete F.S.251 and give a certified true photocopy to the provisional-licence applicant |
| (ii) | Responsible FSI contractor or contractors, and the responsible authorized person | Sign Certificate C (Certificate of Compliance — Fire Services Requirements), certifying that all fire services requirements have been fully complied with |
| (iii) | Applicant | Submit to the Urban or Regional Council: certified true copy of F.S.251, Certificate C, provisional-licence application form, and Certificates A, B, and D |
| (iv) | Responsible FSI contractor | At the same time, forward the yellow copy of F.S.251 to the respective Fire Protection Regional Office under regulation 9 of the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations, Chapter 95 |

**SD takeaway:** The authorized person signs Certificate C for the provisional restaurant licence; the yellow F.S.251 goes to the Fire Protection Regional Office under Cap. 95 regulation 9, not only to the licensing council.

### 2. Layout changes: FSI plan route (item (v))

| Nature of the FSI work | Submission |
|---|---|
| Does not involve building works. Example given: change in position or spacing of sprinkler heads | Responsible FSI contractor submits the amended FSI layout plan or plans with FS 314A to New Projects Division. A certified true copy of FS 314A also goes to the respective Fire Protection Regional Office for record |
| Involves amendments to the approved building plans. Examples given: change in position of fire hydrant, hose reel, or manual alarm | Follow FSD Circular Letter Part 1, paragraph 3 |

**SD takeaway:** Moving sprinkler heads can stay on an FS 314A layout. Moving a hydrant, hose reel, or manual call point is an approved-building-plan amendment under Circular Letter Part 1, paragraph 3.
