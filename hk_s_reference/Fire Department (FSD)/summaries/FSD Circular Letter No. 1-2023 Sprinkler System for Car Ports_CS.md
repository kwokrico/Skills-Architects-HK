# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2023 — Sprinkler System for Car Ports
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
1 March 2023 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Inserts sprinkler requirement into **FSI Code (September 2022)** via Amendment Sheet 1/2023. Effective **1 April 2023** for qualifying new FSD plan submissions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular imposes **automatic sprinkler protection for car ports with total floor area exceeding 230 m²**, driven by intensified vehicle fire behaviour (plastics, EV batteries). The requirement is inserted into FSI Code ¶4.9 and applies to new building plans and major rebuild/alteration submissions received by FSD on or after the effective date.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Trigger and extent

| Trigger | FSI consequence |
|---|---|
| Car port **total floor area > 230 m²** | Sprinkler system required |
| Extent | **All parts of car port**, including **staircases leading to the car port** |

### 2. When it bites (plan submissions)

Applies to FSD submissions **on or after 1.4.2023** for:

| Submission type | Covered? |
|---|---|
| New building — initial plan | **Yes** |
| Existing building — **≥ 50% by volume** rebuilt | **Yes** |
| Alteration reconstructing **≥ 50%** of main wall superficial area | **Yes** |
| New occupation permit expected | **Yes** |

**SD takeaway:** Size car parks early — **230 m²** is a hard sprinkler + tank/pump driver. Include access stairs within sprinkler extent. Voluntary early compliance encouraged.
