# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2021 — Specification for Emergency Lighting
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 May 2021 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Adopts **BS 5266-1:2016** + **BS EN 1838:2013** with Technical Guidance local schedule. Effective initial FSD submissions **≥ 1 January 2022**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular replaces the prior BS 5266 / EN 1838 regime with the 2016/2013 editions, subject to local modifications in the enclosed **Technical Guidance**. Clauses marked not applied, revised or reference only in Parts 2–3 of the Guidance override the Standards; other Standard requirements apply directly under FSI CoP.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Concepts **not** adopted locally

| BS concept | HK status |
|---|---|
| Emergency safety lighting (5.3) | **Not applied** |
| Standby lighting (5.4) | **Not applied** |
| Safety signs within EL scope (5.2.9) | **Not applied** — exit signs separate FSI |
| Risk-based design / normal lighting regulation (6.2) | **Not applied** — FSD does not regulate normal lighting |
| BS EN 1838 Clauses 3.5–3.7 | **Not applied** |

### 2. HK-specific design revisions (SD-relevant)

| Topic | Local rule |
|---|---|
| Plant/switch rooms | EL for **all plant/switch rooms** and **adjacent to main FSI control equipment** |
| Car parks | **Pedestrian escape routes** from covered/multi-storey car parks require EL (no risk-assessment opt-out) |
| Protected exits — luminaire count | **≥ 2 luminaires** per protected exit on exit route, except small areas **< 2.5 m²** with borrowed light or illuminated exit/directional sign |
| Classifications / duration labelling (6.6 etc.) | Largely **not applied** — follow FSI CoP durations |

### 3. Effective date and use

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Applies to | Initial FSD building plan submissions **≥ 1.1.2022** |
| Reference | Technical Guidance downloadable from FSD website |

**SD takeaway:** EL layout follows **FS Code protected exits/exit routes + FSD Guidance** — not full BS risk-assessment routes. Coordinate **dual luminaires at protected exits** and **FSCC/plant room** coverage at SD.
