# FSD Circular Letter No. 7-2016 Testing Standards for Static Tanks Category 5 Class 3 Dangerous Goods
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
22 December 2016 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular sets the leakage test and the design particulars required before a static tank for Category 5 Class 3 dangerous goods is commissioned, for initial licence or approval applications on or after 1 April 2017. It is not the general fire-safety requirements for that storage, which remain on the FSD website.

## Regulatory Overview
Aboveground and underground static storage tanks for Category 5 Class 3 dangerous goods must be leak-tested by a Competent Person before they are put into commission. The standard applies to initial applications for licence or approval made on or after 1 April 2017, and it does not apply to two classes of tank named in the circular.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Tanks inside and outside the test (letter paras 1 and 3)

| Tank | This test standard |
|---|---|
| Aboveground or underground static storage tank for Category 5 Class 3 dangerous goods, on an initial licence or approval application on or after 1 April 2017 | Applies |
| A tank classified as an "oil storage installation" within section 2 of the Buildings Ordinance, Cap. 123 | Does not apply |
| A fuel tank of 500 litres or below storing Category 5 Class 3 dangerous goods to supply an emergency generator for a fire service installation or equipment, or an emergency power backup facility | Does not apply |

**SD takeaway:** A Category 5 Class 3 static tank above 500 litres, other than a Cap. 123 oil storage installation, is inside this test on applications from 1 April 2017. A generator or emergency-power fuel tank of 500 litres or below is outside it.

### 2. Test and particulars to be submitted (letter para. 2; footnote 1)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Test | Leakage test, hydraulic or pneumatic |
| Pressure | Minimum 70 kPa |
| Duration | Not less than one hour |
| When | Before the tank is put into commission |
| Tester | A Competent Person: a Registered Professional Engineer in the Marine and Naval Architecture discipline or the Mechanical discipline; or an Authorized Surveyor under the Merchant Shipping (Local Vessels) Ordinance, Cap. 548; or a Boiler Inspector under the Boilers and Pressure Vessels Ordinance, Cap. 56 |
| Submission to the Director | A full set of designs and specifications, including wall thickness, dimensions, volume and material, with the application for any licence or tank approval |

**SD takeaway:** Issue tank thickness, dimensions, volume and material with the licence set, and allow a 70 kPa leak test held for at least one hour before commissioning.
