# FSD Circular Letter No. 7/2020 — Certification of FSI under Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance (Cap. 636)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
25 November 2020 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Cap. 636 in force from **19 June 2020**; form effective **25 November 2020**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular establishes the certification procedure for fire service installation plans under Cap. 636 for industrial buildings constructed or with plans submitted on or before 1 March 1987, using a new form FSI/314D submitted to Building Improvement Division 3.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Submission procedure

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Certification route | Same as FSD Circular Letters 1996 Part VI §4 for FSI contractors |
| Form | **FSI/314D** (downloadable from FSD website) |
| Submit to | Building Improvement Division 3, Fire Safety Command |
| Certifier | FSI contractor/consultant signs that plans comply with Cap. 636 FSD requirements and applicable CoP |

### 2. FSI/314D scope (tick applicable)

| System | Listed on form |
|---|---|
| Fire alarm system | Yes |
| Fire detection system | Yes |
| Fire hydrant and hose reel system | Yes |
| Secondary power supply | Yes |
| Automatic cut-off device for mechanical ventilating system | Yes |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 636 industrial building upgrades use **FSI/314D** (not FSI/314B/C) — align FSI improvement scope with Fire Safety Direction and route submissions through BID3 from SD/planning stage.
