# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2005 — Codes of Practice for Minimum FSI (July 2005)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 November 2005 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Promulgates **July 2005** edition replacing **1998** CoP. Superseded for new submissions by **April 2012** CoP (CL **4/2012**) from **1 Apr 2012**. Historical baseline for 2005–2011 projects.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular announces revision of the Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment CoP and the Inspection, Testing and Maintenance CoP, consolidating provisions from existing FSD circular letters into the July 2005 edition effective **16 December 2005**. The July 2005 CoP applies to all new building plan submissions received by BD from the implementation date and subsequently endorsed by FSD, with projects approved earlier remaining on prior requirements.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Effective date & transition rules

| Applies July 2005 CoP | Frozen on earlier CoP |
|---|---|
| New BD submissions received ≥ **16 Dec 2005** (FSD-endorsed) | Plans **approved by FSD before** 16 Dec 2005 |
| Major A&A after date: **>50% volume** or **new OP** — 2005 CoP **may be imposed** | Licensing regimes — requirements at approval |
| FS(Cap.502) / FS(Cap.572) | **1994 CoP** still applies |

**SD takeaway:** 2005 CoP bridges pre-2012 projects — verify FS Notes edition if refurbishing 2006–2011 buildings.
