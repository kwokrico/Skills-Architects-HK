# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2023 — Fire Safety Measures under Cap. 572: Fresh Water and Improvised Hose Reel Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
5 July 2023 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Immediate effect; read with FSD Circular Letters Nos. 3/2007, 2/2016, 5/2016 and 3/2017.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Part I formalises acceptance of fresh-water incorporation into FH/HR systems after the 3/2017 pilot. Part II extends improvised HR and FH/HR direct-pumping designs (fixed pumps on government mains, no FS tank) to target buildings of **4–12 storeys** where tank installation is unfeasible.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Part I — FW incorporation (now standard)

| Scope | Target buildings **> 20 m or ≥ 7 storeys** |
|---|---|
| Acceptance | Five Examples 1–5 from CL 3/2017 (Appendices I–V) |
| Submission | WSD approval for FW alterations; water consumption calc if combined storage; BD plans if building works |

### 2. Part II — direct pumping improvised systems

| System | Scope |
|---|---|
| Improvised HR (direct pumping) | 4–12 storeys where FS tank unfeasible |
| Improvised FH/HR (direct pumping) | Same |
| No FS tank | Fixed fire pumps connected to government mains permitted |

### 3. Additional components (Appendix VI)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Backflow preventer | BS EN 12729 + BS EN 1717 (or WSD-approved); WSD approval before construction |
| Pressure reducing valves | Pilot-operated; FSD- **and** WSD-approved; per CL 9/2020 for installation |
| Flow switch + alarm | Independent fire alarm panel indication from MCP |
| Min pipe to mains | **40 mm** (HR); **80 mm** (FH/HR) |
| Pump operating range | Within WSD-advised min/max government mains residual pressure |
| Gate valve | Locked open |
| Primary + secondary power | Required |

**SD takeaway:** Mid-rise Cap. 572 projects now have a formal no-tank pump-from-mains path — budget WSD backflow preventer, PRV sets, hydraulic calcs and duplicated power; FW incorporation remains the preferred tall-building alternative to 9,000 L / 4,500 L tanks.
