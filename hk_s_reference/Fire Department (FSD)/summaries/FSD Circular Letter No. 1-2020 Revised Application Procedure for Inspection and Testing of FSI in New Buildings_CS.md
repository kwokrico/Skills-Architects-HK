# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2020 — Revised Application Procedure for Inspection and Testing of FSI in New Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 February 2020 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Effective **1 May 2020**. Splits **FSI/501** into **FSI/501 (Rev. 2020)** (AP ± RPE) + **FSI/501a** (RFSIC per Reg. 9 Cap. 95B). **New building** = BO definition (≥50% volume rebuild or main-wall reconstruction). Portable equipment: **FSI/501a by Class 3 RFSIC** — **no F.S.251** in new buildings.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular revises the fire service installation acceptance application by separating contractor completion certification (FSI/501a) from the AP-led inspection request (FSI/501 Rev. 2020), clarifying AP, RPE, and RFSIC responsibilities for new building projects. The split forms take effect 1 May 2020 and require APs to ensure all FSIs are covered by duly signed FSI/501a certificates before submitting FSI/501 for statutory FS 172 or acceptance memo/letter.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Form split & workflow

| Form | Signatory | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **FSI/501a** | **RFSIC** (Class 3 for portable/fixed approved appliances) | Reg. 9 certificate — installed, tested, efficient working order per CoP |
| **FSI/501 (Rev. 2020) Part A** | **RPE** (if applicable) | Staircase pressurization, **dynamic SES**, water mist, other RPE-scope FSI — operating per approved design |
| **FSI/501 (Rev. 2020) Part B** | **AP** | All listed FSI + attached FSI/501a installed per **FSD-stamped GBP**; ready for inspection |

| Timing | Rule |
|---|---|
| FSI/501a to client | Within **14 days** of completion |
| FSI/501a copy to FSD | **Mandatory** copy to Director |
| FSI/501 submission | AP tenders with **all** relevant FSI/501a attached |

**SD takeaway:** Programme **multiple FSI/501a** (by trade package) before AP signs FSI/501 — gaps block FS 172.

### 2. RPE-certification triggers (Part A)

| System | RPE cert required at Part A |
|---|---|
| Staircase pressurization | Yes |
| Dynamic smoke extraction | Yes |
| Water mist | Yes |
| Other RPE-purview FSI | Yes — specify on form |

| Attachment | RPE to attach |
|---|---|
| Dynamic SES / SPS test reports | **RPE-endorsed** T&C reports on FSI/501a |

**SD takeaway:** Engage RPE early for **SES/SPS/water mist** — AP Part B alone insufficient.

### 3. Portable / fixed approved appliances

| Item | Change |
|---|---|
| Fire extinguishers, blankets, sand buckets, fixed sprayer units | Separate **FSI/501a** by **Class 3 RFSIC** |
| F.S.251 in new buildings | **No longer required** |
| Maintenance labels | Still required per Cap. 95B + CoP |

**SD takeaway:** Include **Class 3 portable-equipment FSI/501a** in handover document list — not FS 172 blocker if missing from AP application.

### 4. AP pre-inspection coordination (process — SD programme)

| Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| AP | Site readiness; all FSI/501a collected; WSD completion advice; owner 24 hr attendant undertaking |
| RPE | System T&C sign-off; endorsed reports |
| RFSIC | Checklists, listing certificates, FSD approval letters, catalogues attached to FSI/501a |
| Owner | Annual inspection from **FS 172 / acceptance letter date** (not OP date) |

### 5. FSI/501 (Rev. 2020) — information required (Part B)

| Field | SD / handover relevance |
|---|---|
| FSD-stamped GBP date | Must match approved design baseline |
| WSD Fire Service Completion Advice | Applied for/issued — attach if issued |
| 24 hr attendant undertaking | Pending direct telephone link |
| GFA + domestic/non-domestic | BO definition on form |
| RFSIC ref. nos. of attached FSI/501a | Cross-reference every package |

**SD takeaway:** Build **acceptance dossier tracker** (501a refs ↔ systems ↔ RPE reports) at SD — mirrors FSD Practical Guide Part III irregularities list.
