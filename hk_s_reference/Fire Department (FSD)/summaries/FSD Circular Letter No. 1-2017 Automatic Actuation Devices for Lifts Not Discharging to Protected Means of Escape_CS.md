# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2017 — Automatic Actuation Devices for Lifts Not Discharging to Protected Means of Escape
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
27 February 2017 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective for **initial building plan submissions from 1 April 2017** (voluntary earlier OK; recommended for A&A)

> Inserts special risk **§4.35** into FSI CoP April 2012. Read with EMSD Lift Code (home landing / fireman’s switch).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular requires **smoke-detector automatic actuation devices (AAD)** outside lift doors that open directly into accommodation without first discharging to a protected lobby/corridor with access to protected MOE. It applies to buildings that are **not** sprinkler-protected and are **not** domestic of **3 storeys or below**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When AAD is required (SD gate)

| Condition | AAD required? |
|---|---|
| Lift opens **direct to accommodation** (no protected lobby/corridor to protected MOE) | **Yes**, unless exempt |
| Building is **sprinkler-protected** | **Exempt** |
| **Domestic ≤ 3 storeys** | **Exempt** |

**SD takeaway:** Direct-landing residential / office lifts without lobby = either **sprinkler the building** or provide AAD + management annunciation — or redesign to protected discharge.

### 2. Detector placement & logic

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Location | Smoke detector(s) within **1.5 m** outside **every** such lift door opening; BS 5839-1 as far as practicable |
| Effect of any detector on any floor | Initiate **Home Landing Operation** of that lift car (EMSD Lift Code) |
| Must **not** override | Fireman’s lift switch **or** manual lift-homing switch under Lift Code |

### 3. Alarm routing (do not tie into building FA / FSCC)

| Signal goes to | Must **not** |
|---|---|
| Standalone annunciation panel at management office / caretaker / Fire Control Centre | Actuate building fire alarm system |
| | Link to Fire Services Communication Centre |

### 4. CoP insertions (for FSI schedule)

- New special risk: **Lifts not Discharging to Protected Means of Escape** → FSI: Automatic Actuating Devices  
- Extent mirrors the rules above  

**SD takeaway:** At SD, schedule every lift discharge condition floor-by-floor. Lobby-free lift landings in non-sprinkler towers are a hard FSI + EMSD coordination item.
