# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2016 — Revised Supply Tank Requirement for Hose Reel System (Cap. 572)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 October 2016 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **12 October 2016**; read with FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2007.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular reduces the effective hose reel supply tank capacity for Cap. 572 target buildings of six storeys or below (or < 20 m height) with EVA-accessible major facade, where only hose reel (not full FH/HR) is required, based on FSD emergency response times.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. General rule vs revised capacity

| Rule | Capacity |
|---|---|
| General HR supply tank (pre-revision) | **2,000 litres** effective |
| Built-up areas (FSD response pledge ≤ **6 minutes**) | [verify in PDF] |
| Dispersed risks and isolated developments (response **9–23 minutes**) | **500 litres** effective |

### 2. Eligibility context

| Condition | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target buildings | Composite and domestic under Cap. 572 |
| Height | ≤ 6 storeys or < 20 m with major facade reachable via EVA |
| System | Hose reel only (FH/HR relaxed to HR per CL 3/2007) |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 572 low-rise HR tank sizing is now response-time-based — confirm built-up vs dispersed risk classification with FSD before allocating plant room volume; 500 L minimum confirmed for dispersed/isolated zones.
