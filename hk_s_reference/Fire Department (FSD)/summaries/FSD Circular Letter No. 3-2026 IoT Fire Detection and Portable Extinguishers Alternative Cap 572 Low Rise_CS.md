# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2026 — IoT Fire Detection and Portable Extinguishers Alternative (Cap. 572 Low Rise)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 April 2026 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
**Immediate effect** for Cap. 572 target buildings **≤ 6 storeys**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular accepts an Internet of Things (IoT) fire detection system plus portable fire extinguishers as an **alternative to hose reel and manual fire alarm systems** in composite and domestic target buildings of six storeys or below, following a successful 2025–2026 pilot in 10 buildings.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What IoT FDS replaces (only)

| Replaced FSI | Not replaced |
|---|---|
| Hose reel system | Sprinkler, ACD for MV, other Cap. 572 requirements |
| Manual fire alarm system | |

### 2. IoT FDS major components

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Multi-sensor detectors | Smoke + CO; in each unit (near entrance) and common areas |
| Gateways (common areas) | Manual call points + alarm sounders + wireless receiver; **dual SIM** different networks |
| Monitoring platform | CFATS link to FSCC; API feed to FSD server |
| Mobile app | Provided to FSD for fireground access/control |
| Portable extinguishers | ≥ **2 × 5 kg powder** per floor |

### 3. Statutory responsibilities

| Party | Role |
|---|---|
| RFSIC | Install/maintain/inspect **FSI components** (detectors, gateways) |
| CFATS service provider | Maintain communication to FSCC |
| IoT FDS service provider | Overall coordination; monitoring platform |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 572 low-rise rehab without HR/FA space can pursue IoT FDS + extinguishers — still need sprinklers/ACD where applicable; confirm FSD published service-provider list and dual-SIM gateway locations at SD.
