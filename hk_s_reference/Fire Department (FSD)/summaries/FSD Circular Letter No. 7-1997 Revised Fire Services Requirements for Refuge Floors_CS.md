# FSD Circular Letter No. 7/97 — Revised Fire Services Requirements for Refuge Floors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 June 1997 | Fire Services Department — Fire Protection Command

> Supersedes the **external sprinkler in lieu of drencher** option in CL **1/97**. Read with CL 1/97 for the rest of the refuge FSI package.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular standardises drencher protection for refuge-floor external openings in high-rise buildings regardless of other FSI mixes. It applies to all projects whose **initial building plan submission** is received by FSD **on or after 1.9.1997** (earlier voluntary adoption encouraged).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Design rule change (the only SD-critical delta)

| Was (CL 1/97) | Now (CL 7/97) |
|---|---|
| External sprinkler heads allowed in lieu of drencher where building is sprinklered | **Not accepted** — sprinklers ≠ drencher function |
| — | **All** external wall openings of refuge floors shall be protected by an **external drencher system** |

### 2. Effective date for submissions

- **Mandatory** for initial GBP received by FSD **≥ 1.9.1997**
- Strongly advised for earlier submissions

**SD takeaway:** Budget independent drencher water supply and façade coverage on every refuge floor; do not propose external sprinklers as a substitute.
