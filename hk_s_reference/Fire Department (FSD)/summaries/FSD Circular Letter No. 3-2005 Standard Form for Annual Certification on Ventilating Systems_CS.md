# FSD Circular Letter No. 3-2005 Standard Form for Annual Certification on Ventilating Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
3 June 2005 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular standardises the annual inspection certificate for ventilating systems under the Building (Ventilating Systems) Regulations, Cap. 123J, and the Ventilation of Scheduled Premises Regulation, Cap. 132CE. It is not a duct, damper or fan design code.

## Regulatory Overview
Ventilating systems governed by Cap. 123J or Cap. 132CE must be inspected and certified by a registered specialist contractor in the ventilation works category at intervals not exceeding 12 months, with a certificate issued to the owner or representative and to the Fire Services Department. From 1 July 2005 the Department accepts only the standard templates in Appendices A and B.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the annual certificate covers (letter; Appendix A Reg. 5A; Appendix B Reg. 6)

| Instrument | Premises | Apparatus that must be inspected |
|---|---|---|
| Cap. 123J, Regulation 5A (Appendix A) | The building, or the type stated on a licence | Every damper, filter and precipitator in the ventilating system |
| Cap. 132CE, Regulation 6 (Appendix B) | Scheduled premises that use ducting or trunking: restaurant, dancing establishment, theatre, cinema, funeral parlour, factory canteen | Every damper, filter and precipitator in those ducted or trunked systems |

The certificate states that those items, other than defects listed on the form, are in safe and efficient working order. Any apparatus or location left out must be described as an exclusion.

**SD takeaway:** Provide access to every fire damper, filter and precipitator; the annual certificate cannot be completed for plant that cannot be inspected.

### 2. Certificate boundary (Appendix A footnotes i–iv; Appendix B footnotes; Appendices C and D)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Building | One certificate for each building block or complex. A development name or lot number alone is not the address |
| Licensed premises | Use the address, and for Cap. 132CE the premises type, shown on the licence |
| Defects | State apparatus type, location and a brief account. A supplementary sheet is allowed |
| Exclusions | State the location and apparatus type not covered |
| Effective date | 1 July 2005. Certificates that do not use the standard template are not accepted after that date |

The sample certificates treat shop-tenant installations, a system crossing the premises to serve an adjacent shop, and a landlord central fresh-air supply as exclusions only where they are described.

**SD takeaway:** Draw a clear boundary between the landlord ventilating system and each tenant system so the annual certificate can name what it covers and what it excludes.
