# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2024 — Application Procedure for Inspection and Testing of FSI under Cap. 502, 572 and 636
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 December 2024 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 January 2025** for the Three Ordinances (Cap. 502, 572, 636).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular revises the FSI acceptance inspection application procedure for premises under the Three Ordinances, replacing BI/RC Form with BI/RC (Rev.2024) and introducing BI/RC/a as the Regulation 9 certificate for installed/tested FSI.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised forms

| Form | Purpose |
|---|---|
| BI/RC (Rev.2024) | Application for inspection/testing; Part B signed by owner/occupier confirming site readiness |
| BI/RC/a | RFSIC certificate under Cap. 95B Reg. 9 that listed FSI installed, tested and in efficient working order |

### 2. RFSIC responsibilities before inspection

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Site readiness | All FSI on BI/RC/a adequately covered and ready |
| Documentation | Valid supporting documents for verification |
| Testing | Comprehensive T&C complete; defects rectified |
| BI/RC/a issuance | Within **14 days** of work completion to client + copy to FSD Director |
| Portable equipment | Class **3** RFSIC certifies via separate BI/RC/a; FS251 **not required** under Three Ordinances (maintenance labels still required) |

### 3. Post-compliance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual inspection | All FSI inspected by RFSIC at least every **12 months** after compliance letter |
| Downloads | https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/enquiry/download/download_572_502/index.html |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 502/572/636 completion now hinges on BI/RC/a not FS251 — programme owner sign-off (Part B) and RFSIC 14-day certification into handover critical path.
