# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2023 — Fire Safety Requirements for Mechanical Ventilating Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 June 2023 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command  
Effective **1 September 2023**; supersedes Part XI of FSD Circular Letter No. 4/96.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular promulgates revised fire safety requirements for mechanical ventilating systems under Cap. 123J, Cap. 132CE and aligned Dangerous Goods and FSI regulations. Initial building plans received on or after 1 September 2023 must comply with Appendix 1.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Combustibles and penetrations (§1)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Combustible materials in ductwork/equipment | **Not permitted** unless otherwise specified |
| Wiring/tubes/controls in ducts | Encapsulated in metallic conduits or castings |

### 2. Air filters (§2)

| Item | Standard / requirement |
|---|---|
| Filter cells & media | BS 476 Pt 4; or BS 476 Pt 6 (I ≤ 12, i₁ ≤ 6); or UL 900 Class 1/2; or DIN 53438 Class F1 |
| Support framework | Non-combustible (BS 476 Pt 4); strength ≥ 1 mm galvanised steel equivalent |
| Wholly metallic media | Exempt from §2.1 unless coated with combustible substance |
| Special filters (e.g. activated carbon) | Prior FSD relaxation submission required |
| Type-test certificate | Required from HKCAS-accredited or equivalent certification body |

### 3. Fire dampers (§3 — selected)

| Location | Blade/casing steel thickness |
|---|---|
| Through floor/ceiling | 6 mm mild steel |
| Through wall FRR < 2 hr | 3 mm |
| Through wall FRR ≥ 2 hr | 6 mm |
| Max single blade length | 600 mm (extensions need proof) |
| Closing mechanism | **Gravity only** — no springs/dead weights |
| Blade-to-casing clearance | ≤ 1.5 mm; overlap ≥ 5 mm |

### 4. Insulation near fire dampers (§5 — updated)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| External insulation at damper | Must **not pass through** fire-rated structure |
| Internal insulation within 1 m of electric heating element | **Not permitted** |
| Lining near damper waiver | Only if insulation securely encased in non-combustible material |

### 5. Flexible ducts & plenum use (§7–8)

| Prohibition | Detail |
|---|---|
| Flexible ducts as main distribution | Not permitted |
| Flexible ducts through FRR walls/floors/ceilings | Not permitted |
| False ceiling / raised floor as duct (non-computer room) | Generally not permitted; special non-combustible designs need FSD approval |

**SD takeaway:** Schedule filter type-test certificates, damper FRR matching, and insulation stopping points at every fire-rated duct penetration — Cap. 123J submissions will be checked against Appendix 1 checklist.
