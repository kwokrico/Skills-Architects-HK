# FSD Circular Letter No. 2-2026 Reporting Chain Effects of Shutdown of FSIs and Revised Measures for Building Notices
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
8 January 2026 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular covers chain-effect reporting when a fire service installation (FSI) stipulated in Circular Letter No. 1/2021 is shut down, and it replaces the building notice in paragraph 7(b) of that letter. It does not itself list which FSIs require a shutdown notification.

## Regulatory Overview
Before any FSI stipulated in Circular Letter No. 1/2021 is shut down, the contractor must assess effects on other FSIs and report every affected installation together. Building notices for that shutdown must follow the A3 format in this circular, which takes effect immediately and replaces the notice in paragraph 7(b) of Circular Letter No. 1/2021.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Chain effects ((A))

The letter states that it does not add a new chain-effect duty. It restates the existing duty and gives examples, which it says are not exhaustive.

| Action | If the assessment shows this | What must be reported together |
|---|---|---|
| Shutdown of the fire hydrant/hose reel (FH/HR) system | Failure of the fire alarm system | Scope and extent of impact on both systems |
| Shutdown of the fire alarm system | Failure of the FH/HR system, for example the fire pump does not activate properly | Scope and extent of impact on both systems |

The assessment is required whenever an FSI stipulated in Circular Letter No. 1/2021 is shut down, and it must be done beforehand so that the shutdown does not affect normal operation of other FSIs. If chain effects are expected, all affected FSIs are reported to FSD together.

**SD takeaway:** Treat fire-alarm and FH/HR pump-start supplies as coupled: isolating one can take the other off, and both then have to be reported.

### 2. Building notices ((B), Appendix I)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| When | Before the relevant works start, with the consent of the relevant parties, including the property management company |
| Where | Prominent locations at all building entrances/exits, the lift lobby, affected floors, and outside the fire control room |
| Size | Not smaller than A3 (42 cm × 29 cm) |
| Content | Commencement date of the failure; reason for the shutdown; anticipated end date if applicable; type of FSI and the scope; contractor name and emergency telephone; management-office telephone |
| While shut down | Intact and clearly legible until the FSIs have resumed normal operation |
| Extension | Notice updated within 24 hours after the decision to extend |
| Replaces | The existing notice in paragraph 7(b) of Circular Letter No. 1/2021 |
| If posting or keeping the notice is refused | Contractor notifies the respective Fire Station and the Fire Protection Facilities Supervision Division by email as soon as practicable |

Appendix I names, each with floor(s)/area(s) involved: Fire Alarm System; Fire Hydrant/Hose Reel System or Dry Riser System; Sprinkler System; other major system. The suspension reason on the template is defect, maintenance, inspection, or repair, until a date or further notice.

**SD takeaway:** Reserve a prominent A3 face at every entrance and exit, at the lift lobby, on affected floors, and outside the fire control room.

### 3. Commencement ((D))

| Item | Timing |
|---|---|
| Revised building-notice measures | Immediate effect |
| FSI works started before the letter and not yet complete | Adopt these arrangements where practicable |

**SD takeaway:** New shutdown notices use this A3 notice, not the notice in paragraph 7(b) of Circular Letter No. 1/2021.
