# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2014 — Use of Pre-insulated Panels for Construction of Air Ducts in Mechanical Ventilating Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 October 2014 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command  
Immediate effect (initial **2-year trial** under Cap. 123J only); **not** applicable to Cap. 132CE scheduled premises.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Cap. 123J normally requires air ducts of non-combustible material with strength not less than galvanised sheet steel. This circular permits approved pre-insulated panels as an alternative within strict scope limits for single-compartment duct runs.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Panel performance (Appendix §2)

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BS 476 Pt 6 (fire propagation) | I ≤ 12 and i₁ ≤ 6 |
| BS 476 Pt 7 (surface spread of flame) | Class 1 |
| Flexural rigidity | Class R3 per BS EN 13403 |
| Construction | PU / polyisocyanurate / phenolic foam with aluminium or stainless foil both sides |

### 2. Application limits (Appendix §3)

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| Compartment floor area > 230 m² | **Not permitted** without sprinkler protection |
| Protected areas, fireman's lift lobby, plant rooms, DG stores, boiler rooms | **Not permitted** |
| Toxic/corrosive/grease air (fume cupboards, kitchen hoods) | **Not permitted** |
| Max duct cross-section | **0.35 m²** (0.7 m² only for FCU blower access duct — not walkable) |
| Duct routing | Entire duct, supports and connected fan/FCU within **one fire compartment** — no penetration of FRR walls/floors/ceilings/partitions |
| FCU final distribution ducts | Max FCU flow **1,000 l/s**; diffusers/grilles directly attached |
| Aspect ratio | Must prevent person entry (except §3.5 access duct) |

### 3. Exclusions

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Galvanised sheet steel ducts | Still fully acceptable |
| Cap. 132CE scheduled premises | Circular **does not apply** |

**SD takeaway:** Pre-insulated duct is a niche single-compartment FCU distribution solution — flag early if duct must cross compartments or serve kitchen/grease extract; default to metal ductwork.
