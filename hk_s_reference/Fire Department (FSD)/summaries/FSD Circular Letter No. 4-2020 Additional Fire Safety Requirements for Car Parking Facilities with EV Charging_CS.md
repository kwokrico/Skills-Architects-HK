# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2020 — Additional Fire Safety Requirements for Car Parking Facilities with EV Charging
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 July 2020 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 September 2020** for initial building plan submissions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular adds FSI Code requirements for car parking facilities with electric vehicle (EV) charging, addressing fire risks during battery charging. Exempt: single-family domestic car park ≤ 3 storeys (except basement car parks).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fire detection (where not sprinkler-protected)

| Floor area | Detection coverage |
|---|---|
| ≤ 230 m² | **Entire** car parking facility |
| > 230 m² | Areas with **EV charging facilities** only |
| Detector type | Heat or multi-sensor per BS 5839-1 (or FSD-approved equivalent) |
| Linkage | To building fire alarm system |
| FSCC direct line | Not required in domestic buildings where FSCC link not otherwise mandatory |

### 2. Additional equipment

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fire extinguisher | **Dry powder or CO₂** at each hose reel point |
| Fireman's emergency switch | Cut all EV charging power; at vehicle entrance(s), FCC or FSD-approved location |

### 3. Fireman's emergency switch (Appendix)

| Detail | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Standard | EMSD CoP compliant |
| Height at vehicle entrance | **2.5–3.0 m** above ground; out of public reach |
| Multiple switches | Clearly marked to distinguish |
| Board | ~300 × 250 mm white board, 50 mm red border; bilingual inscription |
| Switch convention | Push up = OFF; push down = ON |
| Remote location | Notice plate at vehicle entrance showing switch location |

**SD takeaway:** EV car parks need detection zoning, extra extinguishers and prominently located fireman's switches — coordinate with EMSD EV charging layout and car-park hose reel positions at SD.
