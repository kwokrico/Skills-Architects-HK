# FSD Circular Letter No. 9/2020 — Pressure Reducing Valves for Fire Hydrant/Hose Reel Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 December 2020 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Maintenance/enforcement reminder for **PRVs on FH/HR systems**. Read with **CL 4/2019** annual inspection checklists. Not a new design standard — governs **ongoing compliance and A&A restrictions**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular reminds that FH/HR systems and their **pressure reducing valves (PRVs)** shown on approved FSI record drawings must remain in **good order** and conform to submitted plans at all times. **Removal of PRVs** is treated as a fire hazard unless hydraulically justified and formally applied for — FSD will **not accept** casual PRV removal via FSI/314A A&A route.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. PRV design principles (why they stay on drawings)

| Function | Detail |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Maintain FH/HR performance per FSI CoP; protect pipework from **excessive pressure** |
| Bypass | **Required** for isolating defective PRV for repair — **never** permanent supply bypass |
| Record drawings | PRV locations/ settings part of approved FSI — **cannot be removed ad hoc** |

### 2. PRV removal — only if hydraulically necessary

| Action | FSD position |
|---|---|
| Remove PRV without hydraulic justification | **Deemed fire hazard** |
| Apply to remove via FSI/314A A&A | **Not accepted** unless compliance-driven hydraulic case |

### 3. Annual inspection (RFSIC duties — affects landlords/A&A)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Testing | PRV testing per **CL 4/2019** FH/HR checklists |
| Maintenance | Inspect/clean/test/recalibrate/lubricate **moving parts** (disc/stem, piston/spring) especially on aged valves |
| Certification | RFSIC certifies entire FSI in efficient working order |

**SD takeaway:** High-rise FH/HR hydraulics often **require PRVs at intermediate levels** — do not "value engineer" them out post-occupation; allow **bypass valve space** in riser cupboards for maintenance access.
