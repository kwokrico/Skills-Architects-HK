# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-1998 Miscellaneous Topics - Transformers
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
16 December 1998 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular amends page X.2 of Part X of F.S.D. Circular Letter No. 4/96 after R-Temp fluid was approved for transformers in Hong Kong. It replaces that page only. It does not restate the rest of Part X. The replacement page opens with items (e) and (f) of the preceding clause; that clause heading is not on this page.

## Regulatory Overview
Page X.2 of Part X of Circular Letter No. 4/96 is withdrawn and replaced by the page attached to this letter, signed 22 October 1996 and marked revised on 16 December 1998. Clause 1.2 of the replacement page covers Midel 7131 (pentaerythritol ester), silicone fluid, R-Temp (mixtures of paraffinic hydrocarbons and additives), and dry-type transformers, above ground and in basements.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Items printed at the top of replacement page X.2

These two items appear before clause 1.2. The parent clause is not printed on this page.

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| (e) | Gas-leakage alarm, visual and audible, with trip circuit |
| (f) | Standard FSD requirements for a transformer room, as stipulated |

**SD takeaway:** Where the preceding transformer clause applies, provide a visual and audible gas-leakage alarm that trips the circuit, and meet the standard transformer-room requirements.

### 2. Midel 7131, silicone, R-Temp, and dry type above ground (¶1.2.1)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| (a) | Transformer room at the periphery of the building. Windows on the external wall, openable when required to supplement ventilation |
| (b) | Mechanical ventilation in the form of an individual air-conditioning system for the transformer room |
| (c) | A lobby outside the transformer room |
| (d) | A 4.5 kg CO2 fire extinguisher, or an appropriate equivalent alternative type |
| (e) | Standard FSD requirements for a transformer room, as stipulated |

**SD takeaway:** Place these transformer rooms on an external wall with openable windows, give them their own air-conditioning system and an outside lobby, and provide a 4.5 kg CO2 extinguisher or an accepted equivalent.

### 3. Same transformer types in a basement (¶1.2.2)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| (a) | An independent and separate staircase, leading to open air at ground level, solely for the transformer room |
| (b) | Mechanical ventilation in the form of an individual air-conditioning system |
| (c) | A lobby outside the transformer room |
| (d) | A 4.5 kg CO2 fire extinguisher, or an appropriate equivalent alternative type |
| (e) | Standard FSD requirements for a transformer room, as stipulated |

**SD takeaway:** A basement room for these fluids or for a dry-type transformer needs its own stair to open air at ground, plus the same lobby, dedicated air-conditioning, and 4.5 kg CO2 (or equivalent) as the above-ground room.
