# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2010 — Emergency Exit Devices
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
4 February 2010 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command

> Rescinds the frozen EED product list in FSD CL **4/96** Part III pp. III.18–III.21 from **1 March 2010**. Use the live FSD approved FSI list instead.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular withdraws acceptance of new installations of Emergency Exit Devices (panic hardware / magnetic holders, etc.) that were listed only in the 1996 circular, because those products no longer meet current safety standards or are off-market. It applies to **new EED installations from 1.3.2010**; pre-installed listed devices remain acceptable.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What changes on 1 March 2010

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rescind CL 4/96 Part III EED list (pp. III.18–III.21) | List no longer a valid acceptance source |
| **New** installation of those listed EEDs | **Not acceptable** |
| EEDs from that list installed **before** 1.3.2010 | Still acceptable to FSD |

### 2. Where architects / APs get the live list

Use the current FSD approved FSI / equipment list on the FSD website (not the 1996 appendix).

**SD takeaway:** On MOE doors specify EED from the **current FSD accepted list** + relevant product standards; never cite CL 4/96 Part III product models for new work. Coordinate panic hardware with MOE Code door furniture rules and fire-resisting door schedules.
