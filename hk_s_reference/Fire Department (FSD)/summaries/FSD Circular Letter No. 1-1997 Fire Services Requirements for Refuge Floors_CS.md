# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/97 — Fire Services Requirements for Refuge Floors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
13 February 1997 | Fire Services Department — Fire Protection Command

> Read with: MOE Code 1996 ¶21.2(h); FSI CoP; CL **7/97** (revises drencher vs external sprinkler). For new work also check current FS Code / FSI CoP refuge-floor clauses.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular sets the **active FSI package on refuge floors** once MOE Code 1996 made refuge floors mandatory for buildings **exceeding 25 storeys**. It replaces the old standalone “Standard Fire Services Requirements for the Refuge Floor (NP/107)” with extension of the building’s FSI plus protection of external wall openings.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Trigger

| Trigger | FSI consequence |
|---|---|
| Building **> 25 storeys** (MOE 1996) | Refuge floor(s) mandatory → apply this FSI package |

### 2. FSI that must extend onto the refuge floor

Extend onto refuge floor(s) **all FSI required for the building** under the Minimum FSI CoP, including typically:

- Sprinkler system  
- Fire hydrant / hose reel  
- Emergency lighting  
- Exit signs  
- Manual fire alarm  

### 3. External wall openings — drencher (baseline in 1/97)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **External drencher** | Protect **all external wall openings** on the refuge floor |
| Water supply | **Independent** water supply |
| Actuation | Automatic via quick-opening / deluge valve operated by approved **heat detectors or sprinklers** in the same areas + **manual control** |
| 1/97 alternative | Where building has sprinklers: **external sprinkler heads** in lieu of drencher — **withdrawn by CL 7/97** |

### 4. Roof used as refuge floor

| System | Required? |
|---|---|
| Sprinkler or drencher on roof refuge | **No** |
| Other FSIs (FH/HR, EL, exit signs, MFA, etc.) | **Yes** |

**SD takeaway:** Lock refuge floor level(s) and façade openings early — drencher coverage + independent tank/pump are massing and plant-room drivers. Do not rely on the 1/97 “external sprinkler in lieu” option; see **7/97**.
