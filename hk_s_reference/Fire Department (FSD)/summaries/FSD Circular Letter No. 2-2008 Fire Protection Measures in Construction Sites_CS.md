# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2008 — Fire Protection Measures in Construction Sites
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 March 2008 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 July 2008** for initial submissions; supersedes CL 4/96 Part X §7 for later projects.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular revises temporary fire protection for buildings under construction, requiring closed-circuit water relaying facilities for high-rise sites and progressive commissioning of permanent fire hydrant systems under Buildings Ordinance s.16(1)(b)(ii).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Trigger heights

| Designed height | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **30–80 m** | Closed-circuit water relay (Annex I) **or** electrical/portable fire pumps on tested floors (900 L/min, 65 mm BS 336 couplings) — closed-circuit recommended |
| **> 80 m** | Closed-circuit water relay **mandatory** from **30 m** construction height per block |

### 2. Closed-circuit system — key parameters (Annex I)

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Flow / pressure | ≥ **900 L/min** at **350–850 kPa** running pressure |
| Riser | ≥ **80 mm**; at/adjacent designated access staircase; inter-connected if multiple |
| F.S. inlet | Ground level; BS 336; non-return valve; bilingual 'Site F.S. Inlet' signage ≥ 50 mm |
| Hydrant outlets | Every stabilized floor except ground; topmost outlet ≤ **7 storeys or 30 m** below top working floor |
| Outlet height | **800–1,200 mm** above floor |
| Pumps | Fixed/portable relay above 30 m at contractor discretion if performance met |
| Power | Sufficient for all pumps simultaneous; duplicated cables recommended; secondary auto changeover |
| Site info | A1 bilingual plans at site entrance + local fire station; responsible person 24 hr contact |

### 3. Enforcement

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| FSD site visits | Advice under FSO s.7(c); practical tests after reasonable period |
| Defects/shutdown | Inform local fire station; Fire Hazard Abatement Notices under Cap. 95F if unresolved |

**SD takeaway:** Construction-stage fire protection is a design deliverable from 30 m height — coordinate temporary riser/pump routes with permanent FH shaft and access staircase positions before superstructure passes 30 m.
