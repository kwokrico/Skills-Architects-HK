# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2016 — Guidelines on Fire Safety Requirements for New Railway Infrastructures
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 October 2016 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
3rd issue (bilingual); electronic version on FSD website.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular announces the 3rd issue of Guidelines on Formulation of Fire Safety Requirements for New Railway Infrastructures, incorporating new Appendix VII design requirements for smoke control systems in railway projects.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Guideline scope (read with)

| Document | Relevance |
|---|---|
| FSI Code + IT&M CoP | Primary FSI requirements |
| FSD Circular Letters | Supplementary requirements |
| BD Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 | Building fire safety interface |

### 2. New Appendix VII — smoke control systems

| Annex | System |
|---|---|
| (a) | Smoke extraction system |
| (b) | Pressurization of staircase |
| (c) | Tunnel ventilation system |

### 3. SD coordination

| Discipline | Action |
|---|---|
| Railway / tunnel design | Apply Appendix VII at concept for station boxes, tunnels, depots |
| FSD engagement | Railway Development Strategy Division for project-specific formulation |

**SD takeaway:** Railway FSI is not standard building CoP — download the 3rd-issue Guidelines and map Appendix VII smoke-control annexes to every underground/open station and tunnel segment at masterplan stage.
