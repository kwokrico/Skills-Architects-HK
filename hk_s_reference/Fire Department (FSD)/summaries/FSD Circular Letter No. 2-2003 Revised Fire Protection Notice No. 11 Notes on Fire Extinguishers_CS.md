# FSD Circular Letter No. 2-2003 Revised Fire Protection Notice No. 11 Notes on Fire Extinguishers
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
1 June 2003 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular announces the April 2003 revision of Fire Protection Notice No. 11, Notes on Fire Extinguishers (Suitability and Maintenance), and lists the points that changed. The Notice itself is not printed in this PDF, so suitability tables, agent ratings, and maintenance steps that are not summarised here are not stated.

## Regulatory Overview
Fire Protection Notice No. 11, issued in Chinese and English in April 2003, is written primarily for fire service installation contractors and is also for the interested public. The maintenance procedures in the Notice take effect on the date of this circular, 1 June 2003.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Changes that affect specification (letter, items (b) to (e))

| Topic | Rule in this circular |
|---|---|
| Hydraulic pressure test | Portable fire extinguishers: test interval set at 5 years |
| Clean agent extinguishers | The BCF section is removed and replaced by a section on clean agent fire extinguishers |
| Foam (chemical) | Inverted-type foam extinguishers have ceased production and are no longer permitted for sale in Hong Kong. Units already sold may continue to be used |
| Fire blankets | For licensing or certification requirements imposed by FSD, only FSD-approved heavy-duty fire blankets are acceptable |

Item (a) states that portable-equipment maintenance procedures were updated, with emphasis on inspection of the extinguisher body and on safety precautions during maintenance. Those procedures are in the Notice, not in this letter.

**SD takeaway:** Specify clean-agent portables in place of BCF, do not allow new inverted chemical-foam extinguishers, and where a licence or FSD certificate requires a fire blanket, use an FSD-approved heavy-duty blanket.
