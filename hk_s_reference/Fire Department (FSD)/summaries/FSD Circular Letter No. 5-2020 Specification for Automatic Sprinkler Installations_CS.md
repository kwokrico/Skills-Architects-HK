# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2020 — Specification for Automatic Sprinkler Installations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 September 2020 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> Supersedes LPC/BS 5268-era sprinkler specs for new work. Read with **Technical Guidance** (enclosed + FSD website) and **CL 3/2024** (flexible connectors). Effective for initial building plan submissions to FSD **on or after 1 January 2021**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular adopts the **LPC Rules for Automatic Sprinkler Installations incorporating BS EN 12845:2015**, subject to local modifications set out in the enclosed **Technical Guidance**. Clauses/parts marked in the Guidance as **not to be applied locally**, **to be revised**, or **for reference only** override the Rules; all other Rules clauses (including Technical Bulletins, Notes, Commentary and Recommendations) apply directly.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What changed at SD stage

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Base standard | LPC Rules + BS EN 12845:2015 |
| Local filter | Technical Guidance Parts 2 & 3 — mandatory local schedule |
| FSI CoP link | Recommended standard for sprinkler extent/hazard classification under Minimum FSI CoP |
| Effective date | Initial FSD building plan submissions **≥ 1.1.2021** |

**SD takeaway:** Schedule sprinkler from **LPC Rules + FSD Technical Guidance**, not legacy LPC 2009 / pre-2020 practice. Download current Guidance from [FSD fire protection notices](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/fire_protection/notices/guidance.html).

### 2. How to use the Technical Guidance

| Symbol in Guidance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not to be applied locally | Clause **excluded** in HK |
| To be revised | Follow **revised HK wording** in Guidance |
| For reference only | Non-mandatory background |

**SD takeaway:** Hazard classification, extent and special-risk sprinkler triggers still come from **FSI CoP**; hydraulic/design detail from **LPC + Guidance**. Cross-check both at concept design.
