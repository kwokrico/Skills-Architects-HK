# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-2023 Revised Annual Inspection Checklist for Sprinkler Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
31 August 2023 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular replaces the annual inspection checklist for sprinkler systems and states the minimum items that must be inspected or tested. It is not a sprinkler design code. Design conformity is judged against the edition of the Sprinkler Installation Rules applicable to the system and the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment relevant to the building.

## Regulatory Overview
The revised checklist takes effect on 1 November 2023 and supersedes FSD Circular Letters No. 8/2020 (15 December 2020) and No. 3/2021 (7 April 2021, Chinese version). Registered Fire Service Installation Contractors must also follow the checklist-completion and duty principles in FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2019, and certify against the minimum-FSI code and the Code of Practice for Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Water supply and where the checklist splits (checklist ss. 1–3; Appendices I–IV)
| Supply type (s. 1) | Where the water-supply portion is inspected |
|---|---|
| Direct town main connection without any pump | Appendix I |
| Sprinkler tank refilled directly from town main | Appendix I where applicable; supply tanks use the Checklist for Supply Tanks |
| Sprinkler tank refilled from town main via a transfer pumping installation | Checklist for Supply Tanks when a tank is involved |
| Internal fire main serving more than one building and/or system | Appendix I |

Appendix I applies only where the system is fed directly from the town main without a sprinkler tank, the tank is refilled directly from the town main without a transfer pump, or the system is fed from an internal fire main serving more than one building and/or system. Sprinkler pumps are Appendix II. Diesel engine driven pump sets add Appendix III. Intermediate booster pumps are Appendix IV.

**SD takeaway:** At schematic design, name the supply type (direct town main, tank, transfer pump, or shared internal fire main) so the pump room, tank, and inlet are on the correct checklist path.

### 2. Sprinkler inlet (checklist s. 4)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Built-in stop valve installed, or last detailed-inspected, for 4 years or more | Stop valve with bonnet, stem, disc and disc seat taken out for detailed inspection. Installation or last detailed-inspection date displayed conspicuously on the inlet |
| Cabinet label | "SPRINKLER INLET 花灑入水掣" in lettering at least 50 mm high |
| Identification plate | Metal plate, raised or engraved, English and Chinese characters at least 50 mm high |
| More than one inlet, not interconnected | Each inlet labelled for the block, floor or area served |
| Clearance | Inlets clear of obstructions and usable freely |
| Intermediate booster pumps | Start/stop buttons and audio and/or visual alarm adjacent to the corresponding inlet |
| Improvised system | Pump status indicators and pump starting button adjacent to the sprinkler inlet |
| Drain | Inlet equipped with a drain cock for pressure relief |

**SD takeaway:** Detail the inlet cabinet, 50 mm bilingual labels, and keep the approach clear; booster and improvised systems need their start controls and alarms at the inlet, not only in the pump room.

### 3. Control valves, subsidiary valves and sprinkler layout (ss. 6–10)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Control-valve room or enclosure, where provided | Labelled for usage. As-built framed schematic displayed conspicuously at the pump room, enclosure or space (Appendix II, A2.7) |
| Subsidiary stop valve | Labelled for the floor or area served. If concealed in a false ceiling, pipe duct or other enclosure, a duplicate label at a prominent position on the ceiling or outside the duct or enclosure. Padlocked fully open and labelled "Normally Open" |
| Flow-switch test and drain valves, where provided | Padlocked fully closed and labelled "Normally Closed" |
| Spacing | Other than in-rack sprinklers and sprinklers for a ceiling opening formed by an escalator, stair or the like, sprinklers are not closer than 2 m apart unless a baffle plate or intervening constructional feature is present |
| Shields and finish | Metal water shield of appropriate size where water from a higher sprinkler may wet a sprinkler in a rack or under a perforated shelf, platform, grating or similar location. No ornamentation or coating except as recommended by the manufacturer. No foreign covering |
| Coverage | Other than exempted areas, sprinkler protection throughout the building or premises. Coverage, spacing and clearance take account of installation conditions and obstructions |
| Dry, pre-action or recycling | Sprinklers installed upright, except where dry pendent sprinklers are used |
| Flexible drop | Installed to its listing, including the maximum allowable number of turns and the minimum radius of each turn. This circular does not state those numbers |
| Air vent, where provided | Vent opening unobstructed and not capped closed |
| Spares | Sufficient spare sprinklers, including the types, temperature ratings and K-factors installed |

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate ceiling voids so subsidiary valves that are concealed still have a duplicate label outside the void, and keep sprinkler spacing at not less than 2 m unless the exception (in-rack, escalator or stair opening, or a baffle) applies.

### 4. Pumps, pressure and diesel sets (Table II notes; Appendix II A2.8–A2.9; Appendix III)
| Test | Rule |
|---|---|
| Jockey pump | Zero-flow (churning) pressure only, measured at the sprinkler control valve |
| Pumps other than jockey | Two rated flow characteristics, measured at the sprinkler control valve: "flow" and "maximum demand flow" as per EN 12845 (known as "low flow" and "high flow" as per BS 5306-2, and the two "characteristics not less than" as per FOC) |
| Pumps designed to deliver up to nominal data | Also test at nominal data, measured at the pump room, enclosure or space |
| High-rise standing pressure | At each control valve, not less than 125% of the static head difference between the highest sprinkler in that installation and the corresponding control valve (jockey cut-in pressure is the example given) |
| Jockey priming tank, where applicable | Jockey starts when the water level drops to two-thirds of the normal level |
| Run time | Jockey not less than 10 minutes. Sprinkler pump no. 1 not less than 10 minutes, or 30 minutes if it is the diesel pump. Pump no. 2 the same |
| Diesel assignment | Where a diesel pump is provided, it is assigned as pump no. 2 |
| Diesel start | By closing the fuel supply valve, the starting sequence and battery supply must start the engine for 6 attempts, then give audible and visual fault alarms |
| Diesel cranking voltage | With a.c. supply to the charger disconnected, battery cranking voltage during all 6 start attempts exceeds 9 volts for a 12-volt system and 18 volts for a 24-volt system |
| Governor | Engine speed varies within ±10% when discharge flow varies from zero flow to nominal data |
| Thermal overload | Fault indication while not stopping pump operation |

**SD takeaway:** Size the high-rise installation so standing pressure at each control valve is at least 125% of the static head to the highest sprinkler, and if a diesel pump is used treat it as pump no. 2 with a 6-attempt start and the 9 V / 18 V cranking limits.

### 5. Batteries, zone integrity and what is not an FS 251 defect (Appendices XIII–XV; checklist s. 11 and notes)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Secondary battery | Installation date displayed. A battery past its nominal design life is replaced. If the design life is unknown it is deemed 4 years. Replacement batteries have a nominal design life of not less than 4 years |
| Charger, maximum alarm load, battery disconnected | Output voltage not less than 95% of nominal voltage. A dummy load may be used instead of a full alarm load |
| BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008 zone or loop short circuit | Loss of protection limited to not more than one floor plus a maximum of five devices (initiation and/or alarm devices) on the floor immediately above and five devices on the floor immediately below |
| BS 5839-1:2017 zone or loop short circuit | Loss of protection limited to not more than one floor |
| Sounder survival | For systems on BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008 or BS 5839-1:2017, the audio fire alarm device at the sprinkler annunciator or control panel, or on the external wall as applicable, still operates if a short-circuit or open-circuit fault affects other audio devices |
| Part 11 observations | Occupancy and goods storage within system design limits; pump-room entrance doors kept locked; direct access to a pump space kept available. These items are not reflected in FS 251. Owners must still rectify them |
| Residential flats (Appendix XII) | May be recorded separately if the building annual inspection was completed with one or more flats excluded |

**SD takeaway:** Zone the sprinkler alarm and actuating circuits to the BS 5839 edition the system will follow (one floor, or one floor plus five devices above and below), and keep pump-room access and storage limits as owner obligations even though they do not appear on FS 251.
