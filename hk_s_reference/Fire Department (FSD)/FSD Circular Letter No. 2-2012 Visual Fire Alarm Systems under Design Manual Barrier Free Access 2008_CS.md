# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2012 — Visual Fire Alarm Systems (DM-BFA 2008) & FDA Checklist
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 February 2012 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Part I (VFA extent) effective for initial plans to FSD/BD/HD from **1 April 2012**

> Supersedes extent confusion under CLs 10/97, 5/99, 4/2001 Part I for DM-BFA **2008**. Part II is an acceptance-inspection checklist (BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008) — process, not SD geometry.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Part I aligns **visual fire alarm (VFA)** coverage with Design Manual: Barrier Free Access **2008** Table 2 / §5.2.3 for new buildings and substantial A&A. Part II standardises compliance inspection of fire detection/alarm systems to BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When VFA applies (FSD’s reading of the Manual)

| Applies to | Does **not** broadly apply to |
|---|---|
| **New buildings** | Minor works below FSD’s volume/use threshold |
| A&A involving **> 50% by volume** **or** **change in use** | Pure maintenance / small A&A without those triggers |

Also honour Manual exemptions in ¶2.2 and ¶5.2.3(b).

### 2. Design / acceptance standards

| Path | Notes |
|---|---|
| **NFPA 72:2010** §18.5 | Red flashing lights |
| **BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008** Clause 17 | Red flashing lights; List-Item **2.40** of CL 1/2009 **deleted** |
| Plus | Minimum FSI CoP |

Acceptance labels: **“FIRE ALARM / 火警”** — English ≥ **10 mm**, Chinese ≥ **15 mm**. Power: DC + battery **or** AC with secondary from emergency generator / supply before main switch.

### 3. Where VFA is required — Annex highlights (SD)

| Occupancy | VFA |
|---|---|
| Club house (incl. ancillary to domestic / SA / student hostel) | **Yes** |
| Carpark (incl. ancillary to domestic / SA / student hostel) | **Yes** |
| Hotel / guesthouse / hostel — non-guestroom | **Yes** |
| Hotel etc. — guestrooms | Only **accessible guestrooms** under Manual ¶7(2) **and the access thereof** |
| Manual exempted areas (¶2.2 / ¶5.2.3(b)) | **No** |
| All other types | Per **Table 2, Chapter 2** of DM-BFA 2008 |

**Note vs CL 4/2001:** Clubhouses and carparks ancillary to domestic / SA / student hostels are **no longer waived** under this 2012 reading.

### 4. What goes on GBP vs FSI drawings

| Drawing | Show |
|---|---|
| Building plans | **Areas to be covered** only |
| FSI drawings + FSI/314 or 314A | Detailed VFA positions |

**SD takeaway:** Pull DM-BFA Table 2 early; do not omit VFA in ancillary club/carpark of residential towers. Guestroom VFA is accessibility-driven, not “every room.”
