# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-1998 Certification of FSI under Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) (Amendment) Ordinance 1998
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2 June 1998 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular requires revised form FSI/314B for fire service installation (FSI) plan submissions on prescribed commercial premises and specified commercial buildings, and it encloses a June 1998 leaflet on the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance. The leaflet says it is guidance only and is not a detailed analysis of the requirements. This file is not the Ordinance.

## Regulatory Overview
From 1 June 1998, FSI plan submissions for prescribed commercial premises and specified commercial buildings use revised form FSI/314B and follow the existing procedure in paragraph 4 of Part VI, Inspection of Fire Service Installations, in “FSD Circular Letters 1996”. The enclosed leaflet gives the use, area, and age tests for those premises and buildings, and lists the FSI and construction measures an owner or occupier may be directed to provide.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which premises and buildings (enclosed leaflet, sections 3, 4 and 6)

The Amendment Ordinance came into operation on 1 June 1998. The leaflet, revised June 1998, states the following tests.

| Class | Test in the leaflet |
|---|---|
| Prescribed commercial premises | The building or part is used, or proposed to be used, for any of: banking other than merchant banking; off-course betting; a jewellery or goldsmith business on premises that have a security area; a supermarket, hypermarket or department store; a shopping arcade. And the total floor area of the building or part exceeds 230 m² |
| Specified commercial building | Constructed to be used, or being used, for office, business, trade, or any entertainment. And either constructed on or before 23 March 1973, or the plans were first submitted to the Building Authority for approval on or before 23 March 1973 |

| Implementation | Date and phasing in the leaflet |
|---|---|
| Ordinance on prescribed commercial premises | 2 May 1997, in four phases: first 3 years, about 500 prescribed commercial premises already identified as without sprinklers (mostly built before 1973); subsequent 4 years, occupation permits issued before 1980; subsequent 4 years, occupation permits issued between 1980 and 1990; final phase, occupation permits issued after 1990 |
| High fire hazard found without protection | Enforcement action taken immediately, without reference to the occupation-permit date |
| Ordinance on specified commercial buildings | 1 June 1998 |

**SD takeaway:** Test a commercial unit against both the use-plus-230 m² prescribed-premises test and the pre-23 March 1973 specified-commercial-building test before locking the FSI and construction scope.

### 2. Measures a direction may require (enclosed leaflet, sections 2 and 5)

The Director of Fire Services enforces provision of or improvement on FSIs. The Director of Buildings enforces fire safety measures in the planning, design, and construction of the commercial premises or commercial buildings. An owner or occupier may be required to comply with all or any of the following.

| Group | Measures |
|---|---|
| FSI | Automatic sprinkler system; automatic cut-off devices for mechanical ventilating systems; emergency lighting; fire hydrant and hose reel system; manual fire alarms; portable fire extinguishers |
| Construction | Adequate means of escape from the premises or building in the event of fire; adequate means of access to the premises or building to facilitate access for firefighting and rescue; measures to inhibit the spread of fire and to ensure the integrity of the structure of the building |

FSI works are carried out by a registered FSI contractor. Construction requirements involving building works are advised on and supervised by an authorized person, namely an architect, engineer, or surveyor registered under the Buildings Ordinance.

**SD takeaway:** For a premises or building inside either test, allow at schematic design for sprinklers, mechanical-ventilation cut-off, emergency lighting, hydrant and hose reel, manual fire alarms, portable extinguishers, means of escape, firefighting access, and fire and structural integrity, because a direction may require all or any of them.

### 3. Plan certification (letter and form FSI/314B (Revised 5/98))

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Procedure | Alteration and addition certification follows paragraph 4 of Part VI, Inspection of Fire Service Installations, in “FSD Circular Letters 1996” |
| Form | FSI/314B (Revised 5/98), for prescribed commercial premises and specified commercial buildings |
| Submit to | Legislation and Control Division, Fire Protection Command |
| In force | 1 June 1998 |
| Supersedes | Circular Letter No. 6/97 dated 10 June 1997 |
| What the form certifies | Details and specifications of all installations on the attached FSI plans are as prescribed by FSD under the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance and in accordance with the relevant rules and codes of practice, as applicable |
| Examples named on the form | Rules of the Loss Prevention Council for automatic sprinkler installation; Fire Offices’ Committee for automatic sprinkler installation (29th edition); Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, Fire Services Department |

**SD takeaway:** Issue FSI plans for these premises and buildings for certification on FSI/314B (Revised 5/98), against the Ordinance and the sprinkler rules and minimum-FSI code named on that form.
