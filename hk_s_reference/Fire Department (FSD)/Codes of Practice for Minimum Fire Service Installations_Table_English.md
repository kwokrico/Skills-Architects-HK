# Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations Index (English)

**Issued by:** Fire Services Department
**Scope:** The March 1994 and September 2022 codes in this library (2 volumes). This table does not index Fire Services Department circular letters. This library was not checked against the live departmental website.
**How to use:** The edition date links to the critical summary in `summaries/`. The PDF is in `source_reference/` under the same filename stem.
**Titles:** The official title is the summary heading.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, check the current Fire Services Department code, the Buildings Department fire-safety code, and the lease.
