# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2017 — Reduced Supply Tank Capacity and Fresh Water Incorporation (Cap. 572)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
27 September 2017 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Read with FSD Circular Letters Nos. 3/2007, 2/2016 and 5/2016.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Part I further reduces FH/HR supply tank capacity for tall target buildings with EVA and nearby street hydrant. Part II launches a pilot scheme incorporating fresh water supply into FH/HR systems as an alternative to conventional dedicated fire tanks.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Part I — reduced tank (7 storeys+ / > 20 m)

| Previous requirement | Revised |
|---|---|
| FH/HR supply tank **9,000 L** effective | **4,500 L** effective |
| Conditions | Direct vehicular access to one major facade **and** street fire hydrant within **50 m** |

### 2. Part II — pilot fresh-water incorporation (5 examples)

| Example | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| 1 | FS pumps direct from FW roof tank + backflow preventer downstream (no extra FS tank if capacity adequate) |
| 2 | FW roof tank feeds additional FS water tank |
| 3 | Branch from FW upfeed (sump-and-pump FW system) to additional FS tank |
| 4 | Partition existing FW tank into domestic + FS sections |
| 5 | Example 4 plus additional FS tank connected to FS partition |

### 3. WSD conditions

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Contamination prevention | Backflow preventer; WSD approval of FW system alterations |
| Domestic draw-off | Size FW + any top-up FS tank for simultaneous domestic consumption |
| BD approval | Structural/building works need AP submission where applicable |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 572 tall-building upgrades may halve FS tank volume to 4,500 L with EVA + nearby hydrant — or pursue FW incorporation pilot; involve WSD early for roof-tank capacity and backflow preventer routing.
