# FSD Circular Letter No. 5-2025 Revised Certificate of Fire Service Installations and Equipment FS 251
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
5 September 2025 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular brings in F.S. 251 (Rev. 1/2025), the certificate issued after inspection, installation, maintenance or repair of fire service installations and equipment. It does not change installation design standards. Major-defect classification is by FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2021, or any later circular on shutdown of fire service installations for maintenance, inspection, modification or repair.

## Regulatory Overview
F.S. 251 (Rev. 1/2025) comes into general use on 1 October 2025 and repeals FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2001 on that date. From 1 October 2025 to 31 March 2026 both Rev. 1/2025 and Rev. 1/2016 are accepted; from 1 April 2026 Rev. 1/2016 is obsolete and will not be accepted.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How the certificate is split (Appendix I; Appendix III Parts III and IV)
| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| One certificate | A separate F.S. 251 for each building block, and for each licensed or registered premises (the explanatory note gives food premises, guesthouses and dangerous-goods stores as examples). One certificate must not cover more than one block or more than one independent licensed or registered premises |
| Part 1 | Annual inspection only. Regulation 8(1)(b) of the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations: the owner has the installation inspected by a registered contractor at least once in every 12 months |
| Part 2 | Installation, maintenance, repair or inspection work |
| Part 3 | Defects. Major defects in major systems are marked with an asterisk. Classification follows Circular Letter No. 1/2021 or a later shutdown circular |
| Due date | Normally one year after the completion date of the works, unless FSD specifies otherwise |
| Shutdown reference | If the Notification of FSI Shutdown / Major Defect / Resumption has an FSD serial number from the Fire Service Communication Centre, that number is entered. If submitted and not yet answered, enter "Pending" |
| Display | The certificate is displayed at a conspicuous place in the building or premises for FSD inspection |
| Copy to the Director | Issued to the person who instructed the work, and a copy forwarded to the Director within 14 days after completion (Appendix III, Important Notes) |

**SD takeaway:** Plan fire-service certification by block and by each licensed premises; a single F.S. 251 cannot cover two blocks or two independent licensed units.
