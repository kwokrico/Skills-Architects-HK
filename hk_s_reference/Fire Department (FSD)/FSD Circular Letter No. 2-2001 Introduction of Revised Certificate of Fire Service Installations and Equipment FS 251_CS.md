# FSD Circular Letter No. 2-2001 Introduction of Revised Certificate of Fire Service Installations and Equipment FS 251
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
30 May 2001 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular brings in the revised Certificate of Fire Service Installations and Equipment, F.S.251 (Rev. 7/2000), and the notes for completing it. It does not set installation design standards, pipe sizes, or hazard classifications.

## Regulatory Overview
The revised F.S.251 records building particulars and the annual maintenance, installation, modification, repair, and inspection of fire service installations. It is in general use from 1 June 2001; the previous form ceases to be accepted on 31 December 2001, and both forms are acceptable between those dates.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Changeover dates (letter)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Revised F.S.251 in general use | 1 June 2001 |
| Old F.S.251 obsolete | 31 December 2001 |
| Interim, both forms acceptable | 1 June 2001 to 31 December 2001 |

**SD takeaway:** Certificate format does not change the FSI package; lock the design to the applicable installation code, then use F.S.251 (Rev. 7/2000) for completion records from 1 June 2001.

### 2. What the certificate must identify (Appendix III)

| Field | What is entered |
|---|---|
| Building particulars | FSD file reference, client, building name, street number / town lot, street / road / estate, block / district, area, type of building |
| Each installation | Code and type from the overleaf list (Appendix IV); exact location; completion date; whether it conforms with FSD requirements |
| Where work is maintenance, alteration, addition, or repair | Nature of work; next due date, normally one year after completion; outstanding defects (defective, missing, or malfunctioning); remedial comment (replacement, repair, and similar) |
| Contractor | Signature of a person whose specimen signature is held by FSD; name; FSD registered-contractor number; company; telephone; date of signing |
| Inappropriate lines | Cross out or insert “N.A.” |

Blank and completed form samples are attached as Appendix I and Appendix II. Those sample sheets in this PDF did not yield field-by-field text beyond the explanatory notes.

**SD takeaway:** Every system on the schematic FSI schedule needs a location and a type that matches the Appendix IV list, because that is what the completion certificate records.

### 4. Display after annual maintenance (Appendix III, Remarks 1)

Where annual maintenance is involved, the registered contractor is to tell the client to display the F.S.251 at a prominent location in the building for FSD inspection.

**SD takeaway:** Allow a prominent, inspectable display position for the annual F.S.251 in the building (management office, fire control room, or main entrance), separate from the plant rooms.
