# FSD Circular Letter No. 8-1997 Specification for Portable Fire Extinguishers
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
5 August 1997 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular states which test standards FSD accepts for portable fire extinguishers after BS 5423:1987 was withdrawn. It does not set extinguisher ratings, distribution, or which agent to use for a given risk.

## Regulatory Overview
BS 5423:1987, Specification for Portable Fire Extinguishers, was withdrawn and replaced by BS EN 3: Parts 1 to 6 on 1 January 1997. From this circular, FSD accepts extinguisher test reports to BS EN 3: Parts 1 to 6, and still accepts BS 5423:1987 reports that are not more than 10 years old.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Accepted test reports (letter)

| Test standard | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| BS EN 3: Parts 1 to 6 | Accepted. This is the replacement for BS 5423:1987 from 1 January 1997 |
| BS 5423:1987 | Still accepted, provided the test report is within 10 years from its date of issue |

**SD takeaway:** Specify portable extinguishers with a BS EN 3: Parts 1 to 6 test report, or a BS 5423:1987 report whose issue date is still inside the 10-year window.
