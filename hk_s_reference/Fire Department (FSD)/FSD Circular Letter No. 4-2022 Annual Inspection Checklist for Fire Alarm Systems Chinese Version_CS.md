# FSD Circular Letter No. 4-2022 Annual Inspection Checklist for Fire Alarm Systems Chinese Version
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
10 November 2022 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: Issues the Chinese text of the Annual Inspection Checklist for Fire Alarm Systems, further to FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2022 of 26 September 2022. The English cover letter adds no installation dimensions, cable ratings, or a new commencement date.

## Regulatory Overview
This circular encloses the Chinese Annual Inspection Checklist for Fire Alarm Systems for retention. Design and inspection numbers remain those of Circular Letter No. 3/2022, which this letter identifies as the circular issued on 26 September 2022.
