# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2010 — Multi-sensor Detectors in Fire Detection Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 February 2010 | Fire Services Department

> **SUPERSEDED / absorbed for new SD** into later BS 5839 adoptions — use **CL 6/2021** for current detection standards and product acceptance. Do not use this letter alone for new schematic design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular addressed acceptance of multi-sensor detectors within fire detection systems under the then BS 5839 ruleset. Current detector selection and spacing for new work follow CL 6/2021 and the Minimum FSI CoP.
