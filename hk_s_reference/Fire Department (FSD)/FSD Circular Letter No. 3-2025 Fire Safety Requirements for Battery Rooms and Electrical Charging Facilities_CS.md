# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2025 — Fire Safety Requirements for Battery Rooms and Electrical Charging Facilities
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 June 2025 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 August 2025** for new submissions and major A&A (≥ half volume rebuild or half main-wall reconstruction / new OP).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular adds **automatic fixed installation using water** as a mandatory FSI for battery rooms and electrical charging facilities (BRECF) where Li-ion battery capacity exceeds 20 kWh per fire compartment, reflecting enhanced fire risk from high-energy-density batteries.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised §4.5 FSI requirements

| System | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Automatic fixed installation (non-water) | To compartment **not** protected by water-based fixed installation |
| **Automatic fixed installation using water** | **Mandatory** where Li-ion capacity **> 20 kWh** per fire compartment |
| Exit signs | All exit routes per staircase configuration |
| Fire alarm | Actuating point + audio at all exit doorways; visual per BFA |
| Fire detection | Areas not covered by automatic fixed installations |
| Gas extraction | Approved type where flammable vapours generated; reduce below LEL |
| Portable extinguishers | As required by occupancy |
| V/AC control | Stop mechanically induced air in designated fire compartment |

### 2. Li-ion capacity thresholds

| Capacity | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ≤ 20 kWh Li-ion (or other battery types any capacity) | Water-based fixed installation **strongly encouraged**, not mandatory |
| > 20 kWh Li-ion per compartment | Water-based fixed installation **mandatory** |
| > 600 kWh Li-ion per compartment | **Individual FSD consideration** required |

**SD takeaway:** Size battery/charging rooms by fire compartment and kWh tally at SD — EV charging hubs, BESS and UPS rooms above 20 kWh Li-ion need water-based suppression in the FSI schedule; pair with gas extraction per CL 3/2023.
