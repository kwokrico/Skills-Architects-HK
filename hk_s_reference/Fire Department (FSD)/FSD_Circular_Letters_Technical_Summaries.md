# FSD Circular Letters — Building Index (English)

**Scope:** 72 circular letters that change the building, the general building plans, or an improvement to an existing building. Letters in this library that cover only maintenance, certificates, or product recalls are not listed.
**Detail:** Each number opens the Critical Summary in `summaries/`.
**How to use:** Jump from the contents to a building topic. The sentence says what the letter changes for the architect or the authorized person. Open the linked summary for figures and citations.
**Titles:** Titles are the headings in those summary files.
**Caveat:** Desk reference only. Before a submission, verify the current Fire Services Department circular letter list, the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings.

These letters sit beside Buildings Department control. The Building Authority approves the building: means of escape, fire-resisting construction, emergency vehicular access, and occupation. The Fire Services Department sets the installations to be shown on the plans and accepted before occupation, and it advises on improvements under the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance (Cap. 502), the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance (Cap. 572), and the Fire Safety (Industrial Buildings) Ordinance (Cap. 636).
