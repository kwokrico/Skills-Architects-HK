# Cap 408 — Architects Registration Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
Consolidated version: 30 September 2020 | Cap. 408

> Scope note: Cap 408 provides for the **registration of architects** and **disciplinary control** of the professional activities of registered architects. It does **not** approve building plans or set design, fire, or structural standards — those sit in Cap 123 and the codes.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This Ordinance applies to any person who is involved in the design, construction or fitting out of buildings and who describes himself as an architect (s.3).

It controls who may be entered on the register, how long that entry lasts, and who may use the description “architect”, “registered architect”, or the initials “R.A.”, including how a firm or company must supervise the business of architecture.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who may be registered (s.13)

The Board shall not register a person unless **all** of the following are met:

| Gate | Rule (s.13(1)) |
|---|---|
| **Qualification — one of** | (i) member of the Institute (HKIA Member or Fellow — s.2); **or** (ii) member of an architectural body the Board accepts as of a standard **not less than** a member of the Institute; **or** (iii) passed such examinations and received such training and experience as the Board accepts, generally or in a particular case, as of a standard **not less than** a member of the Institute |
| **Hong Kong experience** | **1 year’s** relevant professional experience **in Hong Kong** before the date of the application |
| **Residence** | Ordinarily resident in Hong Kong |
| **Clean of a barring order** | Not the subject of an inquiry committee or a Part IV disciplinary order which precludes registration under this Ordinance |
| **Competence** | Satisfies the Board by **declaration in writing** that he is competent to practise as an architect |
| **Character** | A fit and proper person to be registered |

The Board may, in its discretion, require a written examination of knowledge of architecture and professional matters under the s.13(1)(a)(iii) route (s.14(3)).

Without limiting “fit and proper”, the Board **may refuse** registration where the person (s.13(2)):

| Ground | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Conviction** | Convicted in Hong Kong or elsewhere of an offence which may bring the profession into disrepute **and** sentenced to imprisonment, whether suspended or not |
| **Conduct** | Has committed misconduct or neglect in a professional respect |

If the Board was satisfied of competence and is later satisfied the person is **not** competent to practise architecture, it may refer the matter to an inquiry committee as if it were a complaint under s.22(1) (s.13(3)).

A registration committee of not less than 3 Institute members may examine qualifications and recommend on s.13(1)(a)(ii) or (iii). The Board is **not bound** by that recommendation (s.17). Rejection of an application or renewal must be given with reasons (s.15(3)).

**SD takeaway:** Freeze the named registered architect only if the qualification route, the **1 year in Hong Kong**, ordinary residence, and the written competence declaration are all in hand — a foreign membership alone does not put the name on the register.

### 2. Currency of registration (ss.12, 16, 19, 20)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Duration (s.16(1))** | Entry remains in force for **12 months** from the date of registration; renewable annually |
| **Renewal window (s.16(2))** | Apply to the Registrar **not earlier than 3 months** and **not later than 28 days** before expiry of the current registration |
| **Lapse (s.16(4))** | If renewal is not applied for before expiry, the Registrar notes that registration has not been renewed, and the person is **deemed not to be currently on the register** from the date of expiry |
| **Extension (s.16(6)–(7))** | The Board may extend time for renewal on payment of the extension fee. Any such extension **does not affect** an offence under any other Ordinance committed because renewal was late |
| **Expired entry (s.16(8))** | If registration has expired, the Board may require a **fresh application** instead of renewal |
| **Renewal refusal (s.16(5))** | The Board may reject renewal if the applicant does not continue to comply with s.13 |
| **Register contents (s.12(1))** | Name and address; qualification on which registration is based; any other details the Board directs |
| **Change of particulars (s.12(3))** | Notify the Registrar of any change in those particulars within **28 days**. No fee to amend the register (s.12(4)) |
| **Inspection (s.12(2))** | Register available for inspection **free of charge** at the office of the Institute at reasonable times the Board directs |
| **Long absence (s.19)** | Notify the Board if likely to be absent from Hong Kong for a continuous period of **more than 6 months** |
| **Not ordinarily resident (s.20(2))** | Failure to reside in Hong Kong for **2 years or more** → the Board shall not consider the person ordinarily resident in Hong Kong |

The Registrar may remove a name if the registered architect has died, applied to discontinue, ceased (in the Board’s opinion) to be ordinarily resident, failed to renew, ceased to hold the qualifying qualification, or failed to notify a s.12(3) change (s.20(1)).

For residence, non-renewal, loss of qualification, or failure to notify a change, the Registrar must send notice by **prepaid registered post** to the registered address and must not remove the name until **28 days** after posting (s.20(4)). If the defect is cured before removal, the name stays (s.20(5)).

Removal **cancels** registration. Any certificate must be returned to the Registrar. Fees are not refunded (s.20(6)–(7)).

**SD takeaway:** Diary the **28-day-before-expiry** renewal cutoff. From the expiry date with no application in, the person is not currently on the register — do not describe that person, or a practice relying on that person, as architect until the entry is live again.

### 3. Use of title and how a practice may be described (s.30)

| Who | What is locked |
|---|---|
| **Person not on the register (s.30(1))** | Not entitled to describe himself as “architect” or “registered architect”, or to use the initials “R.A.” after his name |
| **Injunction (s.30(2))** | The Board may apply to a judge to restrain that description or those initials |

**Exceptions (s.30(3))** — a person whose name is not on the register may still describe himself as an architect if:

| Exception | Limit |
|---|---|
| **(a) Unrelated discipline** | The description is by reference to an architectural discipline **not** related to the design, construction or fitting out of buildings |
| **(b) Overseas body** | The description is by reference to membership of a body or institute of architects formed **outside Hong Kong**, and the description does **not** imply a right to practise architecture in Hong Kong under the description of architect |

**Firm, company, or any person** shall not use “architects”, “registered architects”, or “R.A.” unless (s.30(4), subject to s.30(3)):

| Limb | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **(a) Every place of business** | At **each place** where the business of architecture is carried on, the business is conducted under the **supervision** of a registered architect |
| **Exclusivity (both limbs)** | That registered architect does **not** at the same time act in a similar capacity for any other person, other than a firm or company with **substantially the same beneficial ownership and management** |
| **(b) Multidisciplinary practice** | So far as the business relates to architecture, it is under the **full-time control and management** of a registered architect who meets the same exclusivity test |

**SD takeaway:** One registered architect cannot be the supervising architect at two unrelated practices at once. At schematic design, name the registered architect who supervises **each** office, and, if the practice is multidisciplinary, the registered architect in **full-time** control of the architecture work.

### 4. False title and related offences (s.31)

Any person who does any of the following commits an offence and is liable to a **fine at level 5** and to **imprisonment for 1 year** (s.31(1)):

| Para. | Offence |
|---|---|
| **(a)–(b)** | Without reasonable / lawful excuse, fails to attend an inquiry when summoned, or attends and refuses or fails to answer |
| **(c)–(d)** | Fraudulently obtains registration, or obtains it by misleading, false or fraudulent representation or statement, for himself or any other person |
| **(e)** | Makes or causes any falsification in the register |
| **(f)** | Impersonates or falsely represents himself as the person named in a certificate or document presented to the Board or an inquiry committee |
| **(g)** | Falsely takes or uses any name, initials, title, addition or description implying his name is on the register |
| **(h)** | Not being a registered architect, knowingly permits or uses in connection with his business or profession: “architect”; “registered architect”; “R.A.”; or any initials or abbreviations intended to cause, or which may reasonably cause, a person to believe the user is on the register |
| **(i)** | Not being on the register, advertises or represents himself as a registered architect, or knowingly permits that |
| **(j)** | Holds himself out as ordinarily resident in Hong Kong when applying for registration when he is not |

**Overseas-body defence (s.31(2)):** s.31(1)(h) and (i) do not apply to a member of a body or institute of architects formed outside Hong Kong who is not a registered architect, using a description or initials he is entitled to use under that body’s constitution, **if** the use does not represent that his name is entered on the register.

A certificate purporting to be signed by the Registrar — that a name has or has not been entered, or has been removed or ordered to be removed — is evidence of those facts without further proof (s.32).

**SD takeaway:** Drawing titles, letterheads, and consultant schedules must match the live register. “Architect” on a building project is not available via an overseas affix that implies a Hong Kong right to practise.

### 5. Disciplinary offences that can remove the name (ss.21, 22(7), 24)

A registered architect commits a disciplinary offence if he (s.21(1)):

| Para. | Offence |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | Misconduct or neglect in any professional respect |
| **(b)** | Convicted of an offence under this Ordinance |
| **(c)** | Obtained registration by fraud or misrepresentation |
| **(d)** | Was not entitled to be registered at the time of registration |
| **(e)** | Without reasonable excuse, fails to attend an inquiry committee when summoned as a witness or as the person whose conduct is under inquiry |
| **(f)** | Convicted in Hong Kong or elsewhere of an offence which may bring the profession into disrepute **and** sentenced to imprisonment, whether suspended or not |

**Disclosure safe harbour (s.21(2)):** If the person informs the Board of misconduct or neglect in a professional respect, a conviction under this Ordinance, or a conviction likely to bring the profession into disrepute with a sentence of imprisonment, **when applying** for registration or renewal, and the Board **then accepts** the application, that disclosed matter is not later treated as a disciplinary offence for the purposes of registration or renewal.

In deciding whether a disciplinary offence was committed, an inquiry committee may have regard to any code of professional conduct or practice **promulgated by the Board** or **currently in use by the Institute** (s.22(7)). This Ordinance does not set out that code.

Where a disciplinary offence is found, and after review-committee confirmation or variation, the inquiry committee may make one or more of these orders (s.24(1)(a)–(f)):

| Order | Effect |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | Registrar to **remove** the name from the register |
| **(b)** | Remove the name for such period as the inquiry committee thinks fit |
| **(c)** | **Reprimand in writing** and record the reprimand on the register |
| **(d)** | Suspend an order under s.24, subject to conditions, for a period **not exceeding 2 years** |
| **(e)** | Board shall not accept a registration application for a fixed period, or until the architect satisfies the Board that he should be registered |
| **(f)** | Chairman to admonish the registered architect orally |

**SD takeaway:** A name removed or barred under s.24 cannot carry the title or the s.30 supervision role. Disclose prior misconduct or imprisonment convictions **on the application or renewal** if the practice needs the Board’s acceptance to keep the entry usable.
