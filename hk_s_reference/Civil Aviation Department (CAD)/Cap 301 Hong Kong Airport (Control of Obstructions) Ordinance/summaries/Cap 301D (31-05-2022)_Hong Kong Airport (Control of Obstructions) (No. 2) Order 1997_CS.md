## Regulatory Overview

The Order prescribes the areas delineated in red on the named plans as areas within which no building shall exceed the restricted height above Hong Kong Principal Datum specified on those plans for that area. Section 5 disapplies the Order from the buildings and works it lists, including buildings erected in accordance with plans approved by the Building Authority before 31 May 2022 and stated minor works and designated exempted works completed before 31 May 2022.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Prescribed areas and restricted height (s.3(1))

Section 3(1), as amended by L.N. 167 of 2021, prints this rule:

> Each of the areas delineated in red on the plans specified in subsection (2) is an area within which no building shall exceed the height above the principal datum (referred to in the plans as “the restricted height”) specified in the plans in relation to that area, and the principal datum is also known as Hong Kong Principal Datum.

| Point | What section 3(1) prints |
|---|---|
| **Where the limit applies** | Each area delineated in red on the plans in section 3(2) |
| **What must not be exceeded** | The height above the principal datum specified in the plans for that area |
| **Name on the plans** | “the restricted height” |
| **Datum** | The principal datum, also known as Hong Kong Principal Datum |
| **Numeric heights** | None are printed in this Order |

“Building” is not redefined in this Order. Cap. 301 section 2(1) defines building to include the whole or any part of a building and any mast, pole, pile driver, scaffolding, hoist, crane, or other structure projecting skywards. “Secretary” is not redefined in this Order. Cap. 301 section 2(1) states that Secretary means the Secretary for Development.

**SD takeaway:** Set the airport height limit for the site from the red-delineated restricted height on the deposited AHRP 2021 sheet, measured above Hong Kong Principal Datum. Do not take a metre figure from this Order. There is none in the text. Roof plant, masts, and other structures projecting skywards count as buildings under the parent definition unless section 5 disapplies the Order.

### 3. Disapplication (s.5)

Section 5(1) states that this Order shall not apply to the cases below. L.N. 167 of 2021 is printed on this section.

| Paragraph | The Order does not apply to |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | Any building erected in accordance with plans approved by the Building Authority before 31 May 2022 |
| **(b)** | Any building that, at the time of erection, was exempted from the provisions of the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) by virtue of section 41(1)(a) of that Ordinance. The printed paragraph (b) does not itself state the date 31 May 2022 |
| **(c)** | Any building that, at the time of erection, was exempted from the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) by virtue of section 41(1)(aa), and is erected by or on behalf of the Housing Authority in accordance with plans endorsed by the Housing Authority or the Office of the Permanent Secretary for Transport and Housing (Housing) before 31 May 2022 |
| **(d)** | Any building erected before 31 May 2022 that, at the time of erection, was exempted from the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) by virtue of section 41(1)(b) |
| **(e)** | Part of a building referred to in paragraph (a) or (c) that comprises a structure the construction of which is minor works or designated exempted works, carried out in relation to the external part of the building, and completed before 31 May 2022 |
| **(f)** | Any building that is a structure projecting skywards the construction of which is minor works or designated exempted works completed before 31 May 2022 |

Section 5(2) prints these meanings:

| Term | Meaning printed in section 5(2) |
|---|---|
| **Building Authority** | The Director of Buildings |
| **designated exempted works** | The meaning given by section 2(1) of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (Cap. 123 sub. leg. N) |
| **minor works** | The meaning given by section 2(1) of the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) |
| **plans approved by the Building Authority** | Plans approved by the Building Authority under the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123), or under the Buildings Ordinance 1935 (18 of 1935) |

Paragraph (e) is a part of a building that is already within paragraph (a) or (c). Both the external-part test and completion before 31 May 2022 are printed. Paragraph (f) is a separate case: the building itself is a structure projecting skywards, and the minor works or designated exempted works were completed before 31 May 2022.

**SD takeaway:** A new building, and a new structure projecting skywards, is outside these paragraphs unless the approval, endorsement, erection, or completion date printed for that paragraph is met. Plans approved on or after 31 May 2022 are outside paragraph (a). Minor works or designated exempted works completed on or after 31 May 2022 are outside paragraphs (e) and (f). For anything the Order still applies to, the cap remains the restricted height on the deposited plan. This Order does not print that height.
