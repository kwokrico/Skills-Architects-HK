## ARB Code Of Professional Conduct

Under the Architects Registration Ordinance (“ARO”) section 9, the Architects Registration Board, Hong Kong Special Administrative Region may make rules for the conduct and discipline of a registered architect (“RA”).
You can download a copy here (become effective on 14 May 2024).
