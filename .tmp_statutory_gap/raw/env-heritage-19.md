      <span property="schema:name" content="Industrial activity which involves any installation or alteration of fuel-burning equipment and chimney" class="rdf-meta hidden"></span>


  
  <div class="node__content">
    
            <div property="schema:text" class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p>The <a href="http://www.justice.gov.hk/home.htm" target="_blank">Air Pollution Control (Furnaces, Oven &amp; Chimneys) (Installation &amp; Alteration) Regulations</a> require the person to obtain prior approval from EPD before he installs or alters any chimney or furnace with total fuel consumption rate exceeding the statutory limits.</p>

<p>These regulations apply to the installation with total fuel consumption rate more than:</p>

<p>(a) 25 litres of liquid fuel per hour; or</p>

<p>(b) 35 kilograms of solid fuel per hour; or</p>

<p>(c) 1150 Megajoules of gaseous fuel per hour,</p>

<h3><strong><span class="content_subtitle">How to apply: </span></strong></h3>

<p>i. Application for approval is free of charge but must be made not less than 28 days before the commencement of the work with supporting documents.</p>

<p>ii. Applicant should first consult a qualified engineer / Authorised Person for the preparation of the application documents.</p>

<h3><strong><span class="content_subtitle">Documents required: </span></strong></h3>

<p>iii. Application should be submitted with the elevations and plan views of the subject furnace, oven, chimney or flue, prepared by an Authorised Person or a qualified engineer;</p>

<p>iv. The applicant should also submit the specifications of the subject furnace, oven, chimney or flue. The specifications should include:</p>

<p>(a) Type, grade and quantity of fuel to be used;</p>

<p>(b) The expected operating hours in each period of twenty-four hours of these furnace, oven, chimney or flue;</p>

<p>(c) Details of any automatic or semi-automatic control connected;</p>

<p>(d) Details of any control on the quantity of fuel consumed and air admitted;</p>

<p>(e) If the furnace or oven is attached to a boiler, the manufacturer's published rating of that boiler;</p>

<p>(f) Structural details including the height, cross-sectional area and material of construction of any chimney, whether to be installed or existing on the premises;</p>

<p>(g) For firing with solid fuel, the details of any mechanical stoking device;</p>

<p>(h) For firing with liquid or gaseous fuel, the details of any burner appliance used.</p>

<p>All these items should be prepared by a qualified engineer, except item (f) which should be made by an Authorised Person.</p>

<p>v. Application should be submitted to any of the <a href="/epd/english/application_for_licences/sub_applic/application_maincontent38.html">Customer Service Counters</a> of EPD.</p>

<p>For detailed information, please refer to "<a href="/epd/sites/default/files/epd/gn_pdf/GN2014P008-e.pdf" target="_blank">Guidance Notes for Application under Air Pollution Control (Furnaces, Ovens and Chimneys) (Installation and Alteration) Regulations</a>".</p>
