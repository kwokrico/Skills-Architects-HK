## Legislative Amendments

The Development Bureau (DEVB) has launched a consultation on the proposals to amend the Buildings Ordinance in early 2025. Taking into account the public views collected, the DEVB has commenced drafting of the amendment bill for introducing it into the Legislative Council by end of 2026.
