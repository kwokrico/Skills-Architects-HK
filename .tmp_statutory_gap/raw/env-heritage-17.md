    <div id="content"><h2>Report No. : Geoguide 5</h2>
<p>Report Title : Guide to Slope Maintenance</p>
<p>Author : Geotechnical Engineering Office</p>
<h2>Abstract</h2>
<p style="text-align: left;">This Geoguide presents a recommended standard of good practice for the maintenance of man-made slopes and retaining walls, as well as natural terrain hazard mitigation measures, in Hong Kong. It is aimed primarily at the engineering profession, but it will be of use to other parties concerned with the maintenance of slopes, retaining walls and natural terrain hazard mitigation measures.</p>
