5. Secretary to establish quality objectives
 (1) The Secretary shall, after consultation with the Advisory

the quality which, in the opinion of the Secretary, should be 
achieved and maintained in order to promote the conservation 
and best use of those waters in the public interest.

In any proceedings for an offence under section 8(1), 8(1A), 
8(2), 9(1) or 9(2) in which it is alleged that the defendant caused 
matter to enter the waters of Hong Kong or inland waters or 
a communal sewer or communal drain or caused matter to be 
deposited as provided in section 2(3) it shall not be necessary for 
the prosecution to prove that the acts or omissions in question were 
accompanied by any intention, knowledge or negligence on the 
part of the defendant as to any element of the offence.
