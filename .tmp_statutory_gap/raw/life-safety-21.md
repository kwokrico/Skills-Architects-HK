(Cap. 95, section 25)

[1 June 1972]

L.N. 56 of 1972

Regulation 1. Citation. These regulations may be cited as the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations.

Regulation 6. Installation of fire service installation or equipment. No fire service installation or equipment shall be installed in any premises by any person other than a registered contractor. Paragraph (1) does not apply to any portable equipment or stand-alone fire detector that is not required by or pursuant to law to be installed in any premises.
