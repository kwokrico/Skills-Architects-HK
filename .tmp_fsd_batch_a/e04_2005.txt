                                         FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2005
                                          Emergency Vehicular Access


                   The Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance came into operation on 31.12.2004 and one of
    the amendments is the introduction of the new Regulation 41D of the Building (Planning) Regulations
    (B(P)R). According to this new regulation, an emergency vehicular access (EVA) shall be provided
    for each new building in virgin and redevelopment sites. Under the new B(P)R 41D(2), the design
    and construction standards of an EVA shall accord with the requirements specified in Part VI of the
    Code of Practice for Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue (MoA Code). Any proposed
    EVA that meets the requirements of the MoA Code is deemed to have complied with the provisions
    of B(P)R 41D.


                    Since the implementation of the new ordinance on 31.12.2004, the authority on the
    provision of EVA has been vested upon the Building Authority (BA), and this Department is
    responsible for giving advice from a user’s point of view. As all requirements on EVA have been
    specified in the MoA Code, the requirements on access for fire appliances as stipulated in Paragraph 5,
    Part X of FSD Circular Letter 4/1996 are no longer applicable. Accordingly, Paragraph 5 and
    Appendices A to E of Part X of the said Circular Letter are to be deleted.
