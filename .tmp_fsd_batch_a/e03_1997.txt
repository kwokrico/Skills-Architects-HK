                                             FSD Circular Letter No. 3/97
                                         Checklist for Fire Shutters Installation


           The revised “Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996” (FRC Code 1996) was
effective on 1 August 1996. Under paragraph 10.7 of this revised Code, the Building Authority has taken
over the authority to accept the construction, installation and assembly of fire shutters in private building
works.

          With immediate effect, the “Checklist for Fire Shutters Constructed and Installed in accordance
with the Rules of the Fire Offices’ Committee” as per Appendix 2 of the “Code of Practice for Minimum
Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection and Testing of Installation and Equipment” shall
become obsolete.

           This department will continue to carry out the inspection and testing of all activating devices as well
as the operation of the fire shutters in accordance with the attached checklist.
