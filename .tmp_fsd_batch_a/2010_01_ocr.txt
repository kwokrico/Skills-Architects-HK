Dear Sirs/Madams,

FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2010
Emergency Exit Devices

This Circular Letter announces the changes to the acceptance of use of Emergency Exit
Devices (EED). A recent review of those EED listed on Page No. III.18 to IIf.21 of Part ITT of
FSD Circular Letter No. 4/96 indicated they are either not in production or no longer available on
the market. Those EED were manufactured to the then product standards and cannot meet
current safety standards.

In view of the above, the following changes will take effect:

(a) The list of EED (the List) on Page No. III.18 to III.21 of Part III of FSD Circular
Letter No. 4/96 will be rescinded with effect from 1 March 2010.

(b) New installation of EED listed in FSD Circular Letter No. 4/96 will not be
acceptable with effect from 1 March 2010.

(c) Those EED on the List which were installed before 1 March 2010 will be
considered acceptable to the FSD.

A list of EED currently acceptable to the FSD which are not affected by the above
changes can be found on the FSD internet website via the following hyperlink:

hutp:/Avww.hkfsd.gov.hk/home/eng/source/licensing/approved_fsi.pdf
