# FSD Circular Letter No. 4-2025 Annual Inspection Checklist for Fire Detection Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
30 June 2025 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular introduces the annual inspection checklist for fire detection systems and states which related systems are inspected on this form and which stay on the sprinkler or fire-alarm checklists. It is not a detector-spacing design code. Provision is still governed by the applicable Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, the inspection and maintenance code, accepted standards, and relevant circular letters.

## Regulatory Overview
The checklist takes effect on 1 September 2025, is to be reviewed after 12 months, and remains applicable after that review unless FSD announces otherwise. The same checklist-completion, retention and registered-contractor duty principles are also applied to the earlier annual inspection checklists for water supplies (Circular Letter No. 7/2021), fire hydrant/hose reel systems and supply tanks (No. 9/2021), fire alarm systems (No. 3/2022) and sprinkler systems (No. 5/2023).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What this checklist covers (Annex s. 1)
| Included | Recorded elsewhere |
|---|---|
| Fire detection systems | — |
| Fire alarm systems incorporated with the fire detection system | An individual fire alarm system: Annual Inspection Checklist for Fire Alarm Systems |
| Flow switches, alarm pressure switches, other fire-alarm initiation devices, and status indications in sprinkler systems that are incorporated with the fire detection system | Annunciator panel serving only the sprinkler system, and detectors that only actuate a pre-action, deluge, recycling or multiple-jet-control valve: Annual Inspection Checklist for Sprinkler Systems |
| Fire-alarm initiation devices and status indications in a fire-service installation incorporated with the fire detection system | — |

Repeater panels, mimic panels, and an external charger and battery are Appendices I, II and III where provided. The completed checklist is kept by the contractor, scanned or in hard copy, for at least 7 years. A certificate (FS 251) is also issued under regulation 9 of the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations (Cap. 95B), with a copy to the Director within 14 days after the annual inspection.

**SD takeaway:** Decide at schematic design whether the sprinkler alarm devices sit on the detection panel or on a sprinkler-only annunciator; the annual inspection form follows that split.

### 2. Visual fire alarms and remote indicators (ss. 3 and 4.2)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Remote indicator, where provided | Clearly labelled with the words "FIRE 火警" and a graphic symbol of the location served |
| Visual fire alarm mounting | Fixed to the manufacturer’s ceiling or wall position and orientation, mounting height no less than 2.1 m |
| Visibility | Free from obstruction; visible by direct view of a flashing red light or by illumination of the surrounding area |
| Provision | As required by the appropriate Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, the inspection and maintenance code, and FSD circular letters |
| Number and spacing | At least 1 visual fire alarm unit for each compartment where required. Maximum distance between two visual fire alarm units does not exceed 60 m |

**SD takeaway:** Place visual fire alarms at not less than 2.1 m and not more than 60 m apart, with at least one in each compartment where the codes require them.

### 3. Panels, batteries and circuit integrity (ss. 5, 7.7 and 8; Appendix III)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| As-built schematic | Displayed adjacent to the fire-service control and indicating panel, where documentation is provided |
| As-built zoning schedule | Provided adjacent to that panel |
| Log book | Inside the fire control centre or fire-service control room, or, if neither exists, near a status panel at the main entrance or caretaker’s counter |
| Secondary battery | Past its nominal design life, replaced. Unknown design life is deemed 4 years. Replacement life not less than 4 years |
| Charger, battery disconnected, maximum alarm load | Output voltage no less than 95% of nominal voltage |
| BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008 short circuit on a zone or loop | Loss of protection limited to no more than one floor plus a maximum of five devices (initiation and/or alarm devices) on the floor immediately above and five on the floor immediately below |
| BS 5839-1:2017 short circuit on a zone or loop | Loss of protection limited to no more than one floor |
| Sounder at the panel or external wall | For systems on BS 5839-1:2002+A2:2008 or BS 5839-1:2017, that audio device still operates if a short-circuit or open-circuit fault affects other audio devices |

**SD takeaway:** Zone detection loops to the BS 5839 edition being used, keep the schematic and zone schedule at the indicating panel, and locate the log book at the fire control centre or, failing that, at the main-entrance status panel.
