# FSD Circular Letter No. 6-2025 Obsolete FSD Circular Letters
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
10 October 2025 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular withdraws a list of earlier FSD circular letters and states the successor, or that the letter is no longer valid. It does not itself set new installation dimensions or system selection rules.

## Regulatory Overview
The listed circulars are no longer valid and have been removed from the FSD website. Where a successor is named, that circular is the one to use; where the remarks say only that a letter is no longer valid, this circular does not name a replacement.
