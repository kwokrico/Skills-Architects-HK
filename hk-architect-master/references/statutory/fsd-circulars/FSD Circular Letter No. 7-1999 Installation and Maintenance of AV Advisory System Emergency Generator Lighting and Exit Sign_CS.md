# FSD Circular Letter No. 7/99 — Installation and Maintenance of Audio/Visual Advisory System, Emergency Generator, Emergency Lighting and Exit Sign
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 December 1999 | Fire Services Department — Fire Protection Command

> Short enforcement reminder under **Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations (Cap. 95B)**. All captioned works are **FSI** requiring **Registered Fire Service Installation Contractors (RFSIC)**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular confirms that **audio/visual advisory systems, emergency generators, emergency lighting and exit signs** are classified as fire service installations and equipment under Cap. 95B. They must be installed, maintained, inspected and repaired **only by RFSICs** — contravention is an offence (fine up to **$5,000** on conviction).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Contract/procurement implication

| Work type | Who may perform |
|---|---|
| AV advisory system | **RFSIC only** |
| Emergency generator (FSI scope) | **RFSIC only** |
| Emergency lighting | **RFSIC only** |
| Exit signs | **RFSIC only** |
| Maintenance / inspection / repair | **RFSIC only** |

### 2. SD / contract drafting

| Action | Detail |
|---|---|
| Construction contracts | Must **not** assign installation or maintenance of these FSIs to non-registered contractors |
| Maintenance contracts | Must appoint **RFSIC** for ongoing statutory inspection/certification |

**SD takeaway:** Treat AV advisory, generator, EL and exit signs as a **single FSI procurement package** under RFSIC — separate them from general electrical or signage contractors in specs and tender splits.
