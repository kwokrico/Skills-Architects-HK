# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2018 — Guidelines on Formulation of Fire Safety Improvement Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 December 2018 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command  
Applies to Cap. 502 and Cap. 572 fire safety improvement works.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular advises work procedures for FSI shutdown during Cap. 502/572 improvement works, requiring advance planning so FSIs are not left inoperative for extended periods and uncommissioned equipment is clearly marked.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Work sequencing (Annex I)

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | **No works before FSD approval** of FSI plans (FSI/314B or FSI/314C); other dept approvals in advance |
| 2 | Install major components first (FS/sprinkler **tanks and pumps**) for early system readiness |
| 3 | Mark uncommissioned FSI with **blue label tape** + laminated notice (samples in Annex II) |
| 4 | Convert existing FSI only at conversion stage — minimise prolonged shutdown; maintain annual inspection during works |

### 2. Shutdown rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scope | **Only FSIs in affected zone** — per Appendix 9 IT&M CoP 2012 and CL 3/2008 |
| Dry riser → FH/HR conversion | Convert at **final stage** to avoid months without dry riser protection |
| Misleading partial install | Do not install FH inlets/HR/MCP before water supply and electrics complete |

### 3. Blue-label notice content

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Warning | Installation part of FSI improvement works — **not ready for use** until complete |
| Contacts | RFSIC name + emergency contact |
| Fire action | Call 999 and notify management |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 502/572 phasing must show tank/pump-first sequence and zone-limited shutdown windows — programme blue-label periods on drawings so occupants and firefighters are not misled by partial FSI.
