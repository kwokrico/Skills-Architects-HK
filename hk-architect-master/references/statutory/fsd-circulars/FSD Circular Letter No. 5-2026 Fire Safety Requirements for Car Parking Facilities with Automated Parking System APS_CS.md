# FSD Circular Letter No. 5/2026 — Fire Safety Requirements for Car Parking Facilities with Automated Parking System (APS)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
29 May 2026 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command  
Effective **1 July 2026** for new submissions and major A&A (≥ half volume rebuild or half main-wall reconstruction / new OP).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular revises FSI Code §4.9 Car ports and §4.27 Garages for automated parking systems (AGV and mechanized systems under Cap. 618), consolidating EV charging and APS fire safety into the special-risk schedules.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. APS definitions & scope

| Term | Includes |
|---|---|
| Automated Parking System | Automated Guided Vehicle System; Mechanized Vehicle Parking System (Cap. 618) |
| Applies to | Car ports (§4.9) and Garages (§4.27) with APS and/or EV charging |

### 2. Additional FSI for APS car ports / garages

| System | APS-specific extent |
|---|---|
| CCTV | Entire floor including MOE/MOA (APS car ports) |
| Emergency generator | Mechanized vehicle parking systems |
| Fire detection | Heat/multi-sensor: entire car port ≤ 230 m² with EV charging (no sprinkler); or EV areas only if > 230 m² |
| Fireman's emergency switch | APS and/or EV charging facilities |
| Sprinkler | Required if total floor area > 230 m²; **fast response heads** in mechanized APS areas |
| Extra extinguishers | Dry powder or CO₂ at each hose reel on EV floors |

### 3. Mechanized APS — additional building requirements

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Access staircase | Per FS in Buildings CoP; clear width ≥ **1,050 mm**; headroom ≥ **2,000 mm**; FRR ≥ elements of construction; ≥ **1 staircase per 60 m** of car port/garage |
| Firefighter access walkways | ≥ **800 mm** wide; ≥ **2,000 mm** high; guard rail; reach ≥ one side of each vehicle (prefer front); connect to protected lobby + access staircase |
| Deck separation | Non-perforated non-combustible between upper/lower decks; stability FRR ≥ **60 min** if non-sprinkler |
| AGV system | Auto-halt all machinery movement on fire alarm actuation |

### 4. Garage smoke extraction (mechanized APS)

| Trigger | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Compartment > **7,000 m³** AND openable window area ≤ **6.25%** of floor area | Static/dynamic smoke extraction required |
| Headroom ≥ 12 m or irregular/large compartments | Hot smoke test may be stipulated |

**SD takeaway:** APS car parks are a distinct FSI + spatial package — allow firefighter walkways, deck fire separation, AGV halt interlocks and fast-response sprinklers in SD massing; read with CL 4/2020 for EV elements.
