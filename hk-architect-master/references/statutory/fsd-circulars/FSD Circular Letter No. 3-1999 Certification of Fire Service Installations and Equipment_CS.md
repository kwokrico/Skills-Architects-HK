# FSD Circular Letter No. 3-1999 Certification of Fire Service Installations and Equipment
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
5 March 1999 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular replaces forms FSI/314 and FSI/314A used to certify fire service installations and equipment for new buildings and for minor addition and alteration projects. It does not itself set spacing, water supply, or occupancy rules. On this copy, the enclosed FSI/314 is over-stamped “Deleted. See FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2005”; the enclosed FSI/314A is not stamped that way.

## Regulatory Overview
Forms FSI/314 and FSI/314A were revised for changes in technology, local design codes, and international fire standards, and both new forms take effect on 5 March 1999. Authorized persons and registered FSI contractors submit the completed form with the required documents to Fire Protection Command, New Projects Division, for certification of the installations and equipment.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who certifies which form (FSI/314 and FSI/314A)

| Form | Certification on the form |
|---|---|
| FSI/314 Part I | Authorized person certifies that the attached FSI plans are identical to the building plans approved by FSD, and states the approval date and file reference FP 8/… |
| FSI/314 Part II | FSI contractor or consultant certifies that the details and specifications on those plans are as prescribed by FSD and in accordance with the rules and codes marked on the form |
| FSI/314A | FSI contractor or consultant makes the same details-and-specifications certification. There is no authorized-person “identical to approved building plans” part on this form |

The letter describes both forms as those used for new buildings and for minor addition and alteration projects. It does not add a further split between the two forms.

**SD takeaway:** FSI plans that amend the approved building plans need the authorized person’s Part I certificate on FSI/314; keep the FSI drawing identical to the approved building plans that FSD holds.

### 2. Rules and codes the certificate can invoke (FSI/314 Part II and FSI/314A)

Mark the applicable line. The two forms use the same list except for the Fire Offices’ Committee automatic fire alarm edition.

| Instrument | FSI/314 | FSI/314A |
|---|---|---|
| Fire Offices’ Committee, Automatic Sprinkler Installations | 29th Edition | 29th Edition |
| Fire Offices’ Committee, Automatic Fire Alarm Installations | 12th Edition | 11th/12th Edition |
| Fire Offices’ Committee, Installation of External Drenchers | 4th Edition | 4th Edition |
| Loss Prevention Council, Automatic Sprinkler Installations | Listed | Listed |
| NFPA 12, Carbon dioxide extinguishing systems | Listed | Listed |
| NFPA 2001, Clean agent fire extinguishing systems | Listed | Listed |
| NFPA 15, Water spray fixed systems for fire protection | Listed | Listed |
| FSD Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment | Listed | Listed |
| Fire alarm systems | Listed | Listed |
| Fire hydrant/hose reel systems | Listed | Listed |
| Others | Blank line | Blank line |

**SD takeaway:** Schematic specifications for sprinklers, alarms, external drenchers, carbon dioxide, clean agent, and water spray must be written to the edition named on the form that will certify them.
