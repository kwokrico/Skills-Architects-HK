# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-2000 Flammability Standards for PU Foam Mattresses and Upholstered Furniture in Licensed Premises and Public Areas
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
20 January 2000 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular adopts flammability standards for polyurethane (PU) foam filled mattresses and upholstered furniture, fixed and movable, in licensed premises and public areas. It is not a standard for other furniture fillings, and it does not set building fire-resistance ratings.

## Regulatory Overview
For new applications, and for existing licensed premises applying for major alterations, every PU foam filled furniture item used in the premises must meet the medium-hazard standards named in the letter. Existing licensed premises must replace items that do not meet those standards through a phased programme, and the letter does not give the phase dates.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What must comply, and from when (letter)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Products | PU foam filled mattresses, and upholstered furniture, both fixed and movable |
| Places | Licensed premises and public areas |
| New applications, and existing licensed premises applying for major alterations | All such items to be used in the premises shall meet the specified standards |
| Other existing licensed premises | Items that do not meet the standards shall be replaced through a phased programme. No phase dates are stated |
| In force | 20 January 2000 |
| Equivalent | “Or its international equivalent” is stated for both British Standards below |

| Product | Standard adopted in the letter |
|---|---|
| Mattresses, divans and bed bases | BS 7177:1996, for use in medium-hazard premises/building. The letter says the extract is at Appendix I |
| Upholstered furniture (non-domestic seating, composites) | BS 7176:1995, for use in medium-hazard premises/building. The letter says the extract is at Appendix II |

The attached sheets are labelled the other way: the sheet headed Appendix I is BS 7176:1995, and the sheet headed Appendix II is BS 7177:1995. Specify by the British Standard number in the letter. The medium-hazard labels (Appendices III and IV) match the letter: BS 7177:1996 for mattresses and BS 7176:1995 for furniture.

**SD takeaway:** In licensed premises and public areas, specify PU foam mattresses to BS 7177:1996 medium hazard and PU foam upholstered furniture to BS 7176:1995 medium hazard, including loose items.

### 2. Medium-hazard tests on the attached extracts (Appendices I and II as printed)

The letter adopts the medium-hazard application. The sheets also reprint the low, high, and very high columns of the British Standard tables.

| Product | Medium-hazard resistance on the attached table |
|---|---|
| Upholstered furniture (sheet headed BS 7176:1995, Appendix I) | Smouldering cigarette of BS EN 1021-1:1994, and match-flame equivalent of BS EN 1021-2:1994 |
| Mattresses (sheet headed BS 7177:1995, Appendix II) | Smouldering cigarette of BS EN 597-1:1995, match-flame equivalent of BS EN 597-2:1995, and ignition source 5 of BS 6807:1996, Section 2 |

Higher columns printed on the same sheets, which the letter does not adopt as the local standard:

| Sheet | High hazard | Very high hazard |
|---|---|---|
| BS 7176:1995 furniture | Medium-hazard sources, plus ignition source 5 in section 4 of BS 5852:1990 | Smouldering cigarette of BS EN 1021-1:1994; match-flame equivalent of BS EN 1021-2:1994; section 4 or section 5 of BS 5852:1990, at the discretion of the specifier but at least high-hazard requirements |
| BS 7177:1995 mattresses | Cigarette and match sources above, plus ignition source 7 of BS 6807:1996, Section 2 | Those high-hazard sources, plus additional tests specified by the user with greater than high-hazard requirements |

Typical examples printed on the furniture sheet (BS 7176:1995): low hazard — offices, schools, colleges, universities, museums, exhibitions, day centres; medium hazard — hotel bedrooms, public buildings, restaurants, services’ messes, places of public entertainment, public halls, public houses and bars, casinos, hospitals, hostels; high hazard — sleeping accommodation in certain hospital wards and in certain hostels, offshore installations; very high hazard — prison cells. Note 1 on that sheet: if a low-hazard premises is also used for sleeping, consideration should be given to a higher performance level. Note 2: upholstered furniture ordinarily intended for private use in a dwelling is subject to Government Regulations.

Typical examples printed on the mattress sheet (BS 7177:1995): domestic (low hazard) — domestic dwellings, including non-motorized caravans; medium hazard — hospitals, services’ messes, day centres, hotels, hostels, old peoples’ homes, holiday camp chalets, boarding schools, residential schools, halls of residence at universities and colleges; high hazard — certain hospital wards, off-shore installations, hotels, hostels, old peoples’ homes; very high hazard — prison cells, locked psychiatric accommodation. Note 3: fillings of domestic (low-hazard) mattresses, divans and bed bases are also subject to Government Regulations. Note 4: meeting medium, high, or very high hazard does not automatically mean the fillings meet the government requirements for fillings.

**SD takeaway:** The circular’s specification line is the medium-hazard column; do not substitute the domestic or low-hazard column for licensed premises or public areas.

### 3. Labels and documents (Appendices III and IV)

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Mattress label (Appendix III) | Appropriate label. The example reads “Complies with BS 7177:1996 for medium hazard”, with a “RESISTANT” mark showing a match and a flame crib marked 5 |
| Furniture label (Appendix IV) | Appropriate label. The example reads “COMPLIES WITH BS 7176:1995”, then “DIRECT TEST/PREDICTIVE TEST” with whichever is inappropriate deleted, then “FOR MEDIUM HAZARD”, with the same “RESISTANT” mark |
| Invoice | From the manufacturer or supplier, indicating that the items meet the relevant flammability standards |
| Test certificate | Copy showing the material complies with the relevant standards. Issued by a laboratory accredited to conduct tests to the specified standards. Must bear the supplier’s or manufacturer’s company seal |

**SD takeaway:** Loose and fixed PU foam mattresses and upholstered furniture in the fit-out need the medium-hazard label, the invoice, and a sealed accredited test certificate, not a catalogue claim alone.
