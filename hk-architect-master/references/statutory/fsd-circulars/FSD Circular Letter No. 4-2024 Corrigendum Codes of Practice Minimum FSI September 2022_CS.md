# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2024 — Corrigendum to Minimum FSI CoP (September 2022)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
26 April 2024 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> Part I: **English corrigendum** to September 2022 CoP. Part II: release of **Chinese version** (incorporating same fixes). Read with CL **2/2022**, **5/2022**, **1/2023**, **3/2023**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular publishes textual corrections and clarifications to the English September 2022 Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and the companion Inspection, Testing and Maintenance CoP, and announces the Chinese edition on the FSD website. Architects should treat the corrigendum as the authoritative English wording for affected paragraphs when specifying FS Notes, premises extent, and system specifications at schematic design.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Minimum FSI CoP — design-relevant corrections

| Item | Paragraph | Correction (SD impact) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3.2** Group I special hazards | Adds **cold storage areas** and **lifts not discharging to protected means of escape** to sprinkler/detection hazard list |
| 2 | **4.1, 4.2, 4.7, 4.8, 4.13, 4.17, 4.27, 4.28, 4.40, 4.44** FH/HR extent | Hose reach: **every part of the premises** (not “building”) within **30 m** hose reel tubing |
| 3 | **4.3, 4.4** basement FH/HR | Basement without required staircase: hydrants **to FSD satisfaction**; hose reach as above |
| 4 | **5.21 A.6(a)** pressurized lobby/duct | “Fire resisting period” → **fire resistance rating** (aligns with FRC terminology) |
| 5 | **5.23(i) B.16** enclosure | Same **fire resistance rating** wording fix |
| 6 | **5.23(i) B.24** VAC control | Remote **on/off override** at fire control panel (punctuation/clarity) |
| 7 | **5.23(i) L.4(a)** main switchboard | BS EN **61439** (was 60439); 2 hr room, type-tested cubicle — unchanged intent |

**SD takeaway:** Re-check **Group I hazard table** and **FH/HR 30 m coverage diagrams** against corrected Part IV text; flag **non-discharge lifts** explicitly in FS Notes.

### 2. Inspection / Testing CoP — corrections affecting specification cross-ref

| Item | Paragraph | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **2.20(iii)** | “Portable equipment” → **portable hand-operated approved appliances**; label rules unchanged |
| 2 | **Appendix 6 ¶2.3(b.2, d.4)** | Fire **resistance rating** wording; mechanical air release cross-ref **d.10–d.11** |
| 3 | **Appendix 7 ¶4.2.2.4** | Starting instructions in **pump room/enclosure** |
| 4 | **Appendix 8 Item 1(i)** | BS 7629-1 **Cat. Enhanced 120** (was Cat. Standard 120) |
| 5 | **Appendix 8 Item 2-15(c)** | BS 8491 — **duration of survival time** 120 minutes |
| 6 | **Appendix 10 Remarks 3** | Approved persons list — refer to FSD webpage (URL format updated) |
| 7 | **Appendix 11 title** | Adds **Fixed Automatically Operated Approved Appliances** to maintenance notes scope |

**SD takeaway:** Update FS Notes referencing **fire-resisting cables** to **Enhanced 120** category where applicable.
