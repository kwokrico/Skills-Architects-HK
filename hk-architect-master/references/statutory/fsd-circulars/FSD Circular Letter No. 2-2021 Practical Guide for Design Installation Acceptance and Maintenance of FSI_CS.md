# FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2021 — Practical Guide for Design, Installation, Acceptance and Maintenance of FSI
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 February 2021 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> **Announcement circular only** — no new statutory requirements. The Guide is **general reference** (not exhaustive); consult CoP and individual CLs for compliance. Download: [FSD Practical Guide PDF](https://www.hkfsd.gov.hk/eng/source/licensing/Practical_Guide.pdf). Enquiries: Divisional Officer (FSITF) **3961 5002**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular announces publication of the FSD Practical Guide, compiling FSD Connects seminar material into a trade reference for APs, RPEs, RFSICs, and property managers on FSI lifecycle compliance. For schematic design, the Guide's value is procedural — GBP workflow, FSAR/FSMP/SCS submission checklists, and common irregularities — rather than numeric code provisions.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the Guide covers (four parts — SD-relevant index)

| Part | Chapters | SD use |
|---|---|---|
| **I — Design** | 1.1 GBP workflow (PNAP ADM-2); 1.2 GBP DOs/DON'Ts + common irregularities; 1.3 **FSAR** submission; 1.4 **FSMP** submission; 1.5 **Smoke control system** submission | **Primary SD reference** — plan-stage gates |
| **II — Installation** | Proper installation; FSI provisions; specs; T&C; DOs/DON'Ts | Brief RFSIC coordination |
| **III — Acceptance** | FSI/501 application; pre-inspection meeting; on-site inspection; document checking; FS 172 issuance; common irregularities | Handover / OP readiness |
| **IV — Maintenance** | AI checklists; FCP/FH/HR/FAS inspection; moving parts; shutdown; FSI/314A; fire safety improvement plans; extinguisher disposal | FM briefing — not SD geometry |

### 2. Part I — design-stage SD takeaways

| Topic | Guide content (for SD) |
|---|---|
| GBP workflow | Simplified private-project flow cross-ref **PNAP ADM-2** |
| GBP DOs | Cross-check FS Notes typos; incorporate prior amendments; early written enquiry for non-compliant items; track latest FSD CLs |
| GBP DON'Ts | No piecemeal amendments; FS Notes must match layouts; no FSI in notes without layout; no unrelated items (e.g. electromagnetic locks) in FS Notes |
| Common irregularities | Missing detector standards; FH/HR coverage gaps; sprinkler tank/pump errors; FCC omissions; VAC not shown [see Guide ¶1.2.2 detail] |
| FSAR | Tips + general guidance for fire engineering submissions |
| FSMP | Tips + general guidance where FSMP required |
| SCS | Useful tips for smoke extraction / pressurization / VAC drawing packages |

**SD takeaway:** Use **Appendix I** (new/amended plan checklist) and **Appendix IV** (SES/SPS/VAC checklist) as internal QA before GBP issue — mirrors CL 4/1996 ¶3.7 but in checklist form.
