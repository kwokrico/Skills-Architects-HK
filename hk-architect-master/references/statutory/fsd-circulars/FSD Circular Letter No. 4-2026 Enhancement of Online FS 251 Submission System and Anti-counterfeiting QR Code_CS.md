# FSD Circular Letter No. 4-2026 Enhancement of Online FS 251 Submission System and Anti-counterfeiting QR Code
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
15 May 2026 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular changes how a Certificate of Fire Service Installation and Equipment (F.S. 251) is prepared and submitted, and which form versions FSD will accept. It does not set installation design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
A new F.S. 251 electronic certificate carries an anti-counterfeiting QR code at the bottom left corner, and from 18 May 2026 contractors prepare and submit it through the Online Preparation and Submission System. From 1 July 2026 paper certificates are obsolete, and FSD will not accept certificates sent by fax or by any non-designated electronic means.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Submission channel ((A), (B), (C))

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| New certificate | F.S. 251 with an anti-counterfeiting QR code at the bottom left corner. Scanning the code verifies the certificate and its authenticity |
| System in service | 18 May 2026. Certificates prepared and submitted through the Online Submission System |
| After 1 July 2026 | All paper-certificate versions obsolete. Fax and any non-designated electronic means no longer accepted. Submission is through the Online Submission System |
| Statutory submission | Regulation 9(1) of the Fire Service (Installations and Equipment) Regulations, Cap. 95B |
| Electronic signature | iAM Smart+ or Hong Kong Post e-Cert (Personal), set up before use of the signature function |
| Until 30 June 2026 | Contractors not yet set up for electronic signature, or not yet familiar with the system, may still use the versions in items 2 and 3 of section (D) |

**SD takeaway:** Programme FSI completion certification as an Online Submission System F.S. 251 with the QR code; do not plan on paper or fax after 30 June 2026.

### 2. F.S. 251 versions ((D), (E))

| Version | Status in this circular |
|---|---|
| F.S. 251 (Rev. 1/2016) and earlier | Obsolete from 1 April 2026 under Circular Letter No. 5/2025. Not accepted from that date |
| Physical copy of F.S. 251 (Rev. 1/2025) | Sale ceased. Copies already purchased may be used on or before 30 June 2026 and submitted under the existing procedures |
| Electronic forms F.S. 251 (Rev. 1/2012) and (Rev. 1/2025) | Obsolete from 1 July 2026. Not accepted from that date |
| Certificate issued on or after 1 July 2026 without the anti-counterfeiting QR code | The letter says the public may report it to FSD |

**SD takeaway:** An inspection submitted on or after 1 July 2026 needs the QR-coded F.S. 251 from the Online Submission System, not Rev. 1/2016 or earlier and not the Rev. 1/2012 or Rev. 1/2025 electronic forms.
