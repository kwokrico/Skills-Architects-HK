# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2008 — Arrangements for Submissions of FSI Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
6 May 2008 | Fire Services Department — Fire Safety Command

> **Repeals Stage 1 design-stage schematic vetting** under CL **1/2005** (optional until **31 Oct 2008**). Introduces **FSI/314 (Rev. 08)** + **FSI/501 (Rev. 08)** and optional **Help Desk**. Superseded in part by CL **1/2020** (FSI/501 split). Smoke control / VAC / improvised sprinkler submissions **still fully vetted**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular streamlines FSI plan submission for initial compliance inspection by eliminating mandatory Stage 1 schematic vetting and requiring instead a single submission of schematic plus as-fitted plans with revised FSI/314 and FSI/501 forms. FSD stamps plans as certified records but explicitly does not examine or approve them, shifting design responsibility to AP and RFSIC while targeting initial inspection within 15 working days of FSI/501 receipt.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. New default submission package (New Projects Division)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Plans | **Two sets** — full schematic + as-fitted; **one set coloured** |
| FSI/314 (Rev. 08) | AP certifies identical to FSD-held GBP; FSIC certifies CoP-compliant specs |
| FSI/501 (Rev. 08) | AP + FSIC — application for inspection & testing |
| FSD stamp | **“AS-FITTED FIRE SERVICE INSTALLATION PLANS… (FOR COMPLIANCE INSPECTION)”** — **no FSD signature** |
| FSD action | **No vetting / no endorsement** — receipt for inspection only |
| Inspection target | **15 working days** from FSI/501 (Rev. 08) receipt |
| Error handling | Major errors → withdraw FSI/501; liability on **AP and/or FSIC** |
| FS 172 risk | Defects at inspection → refusal/delay; responsibility AP/FSIC |

**SD takeaway:** Budget **coloured as-fitted set** at tender; do not treat FSD chop as design approval — coordinate T&C before FSI/501.

### 2. Transition from CL 1/2005

| Period | Option |
|---|---|
| Immediate – **31 Oct 2008** | **New arrangements (Rev. 08)** **or** old CL 1/2005 practice |
| From **1 Nov 2008** | Rev. 08 forms mandatory; CL 1/2005 forms obsolete |

### 3. Help Desk (optional — not approval)

| Prerequisite | Detail |
|---|---|
| Trigger | FSIC doubt on FSI design vs approved FS Notes |
| Required docs | FSD-stamped **approved GBP + FS Notes**; **schematic drawings** |
| Scope | Specific spec queries on **approved** design only — not general design service |
| Output | Written advice; meeting notes by FSIC, confirmed by FSD |
| Guarantee | **No deadline guarantee** |

**SD takeaway:** Use Help Desk for **edge-case spec interpretation** before site works — not a substitute for RPE smoke-engineering sign-off.

### 4. Exemptions — full vetting still required

| Division / submission type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Railway Development Strategy Division | Unchanged — full check |
| Commercial Buildings and Premises Division | Unchanged — full check |
| Building Improvement & Support Division | Unchanged — full check |
| **VAC control systems** | **Full detailed checking** |
| **Smoke control** (dynamic **and** static) | **Full detailed checking** |
| **Improvised sprinkler systems** | **Full detailed checking** |

**SD takeaway:** SES/SPS/VAC packages still need **early dedicated FSD submission** — CL 4/2008 streamlining does **not** apply.

### 5. FSI/314 (Rev. 08) — systems tick-box (Appendix A)

Covers: sprinklers, fire alarm, FH/HR, exit signs, emergency lighting, emergency generator, VAC, smoke extraction, staircase pressurization, gaseous systems, LPC/NFPA rules, Minimum FSI CoP.

**SD takeaway:** Ensure FSI/314 tick-boxes match **FS Notes schedule** exactly before inspection application.
