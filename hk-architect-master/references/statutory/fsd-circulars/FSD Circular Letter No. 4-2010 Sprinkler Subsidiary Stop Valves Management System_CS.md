# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2010 — Sprinkler Subsidiary Stop Valves Management System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
27 September 2010 | Fire Services Department

> Operational / management circular for sprinkler subsidiary stop valves. Limited SD spatial impact — mainly FSI layout labelling, zoning, and management procedures. Read with current sprinkler rules (**CL 5/2020**) and CoP Sep 2022.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular adopts a management system for sprinkler subsidiary stop valves so isolation for maintenance does not leave unprotected zones unmanaged. It applies where sprinkler systems use subsidiary stop valves on range/distribution piping.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. SD relevance

| Topic | Implication |
|---|---|
| Zoning / valve locations | Coordinate accessible valve positions with FSI engineer; avoid valves buried in inaccessible ceilings without management access |
| Signage / schedules | Expect valve schedules and management procedures at acceptance |
| Design standard | Sprinkler design itself → **CL 5/2020**, not this letter |

**SD takeaway:** Not a massing driver; ensure plant/access strategy allows managed subsidiary valves. For sprinkler design rules use CL 5/2020.
