# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/97 — Checklist for Fire Shutter Installation
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 March 1997 | Fire Services Department — Fire Protection Command

> Construction/acceptance of fire shutters in private works sits with the **Building Authority** under FRC Code 1996 ¶10.7. FSD inspects **activating devices and operation** only.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular makes the old FOC-rules shutter checklist in the Minimum FSI CoP **obsolete** for construction acceptance, and publishes FSD’s remaining inspection checklist for shutter actuation and descent. It applies wherever fire shutters are used as fire separation / compartmentation devices with automatic or manual actuation.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Split of authority (SD coordination)

| Party | Owns |
|---|---|
| **Building Authority** | Construction, installation, assembly of fire shutters (FRC ¶10.7) |
| **FSD** | Inspection/testing of **activating devices** and **operation** |

Do not put FOC shutter construction checklist from old FSI CoP Appendix 2 on the critical path — it is obsolete under this CL.

### 2. Actuation layout rules (from FSD checklist)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Auto self-closing devices | Must **not** interfere with manual open/close |
| Smoke detectors for actuation | Fitted to **both sides** of the wall opening |
| Detector installation | As far as practicable per FOC AFA rules (era standard) |
| Manual controls | Provided on **both sides** of the opening |
| Secondary electricity supply | Required |
| Nameplate | Permanent, with adequate information |

### 3. Descending speed (hard numbers)

| Opening height | Acceptable descending time |
|---|---|
| Opening **> 2.5 m** high | **15–60 seconds** total |
| Opening height **≤ 2.5 m** | Not faster than **8 seconds**; bottom rail reaches **mid-height in ≥ half** of total descending time |

**SD takeaway:** Fire shutters on shop fronts / atria / vehicle openings need **dual-side detection + dual-side manual controls**, secondary power, and descent timed to the table — coordinate early with FRC FRP of the wall and MOE clearances when shutter is across an exit path.
