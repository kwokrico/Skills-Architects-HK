# FSD Circular Letter No. 2-2007 Certification of FSI under Fire Safety Buildings Ordinance Cap 572
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
15 June 2007 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular sets the certification route and form FSI/314C for fire service installation alterations and additions required under the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance, Cap. 572. It is not the list of which installations Cap. 572 requires.

## Regulatory Overview
Cap. 572 comes into operation on 1 July 2007 and applies to certain composite buildings and domestic buildings that were constructed, or whose building plans were submitted, on or before 1 March 1987. Certification of the required fire service installation alterations and additions follows paragraph 4 of Part VI of the 1996 FSD Circular Letters, using form FSI/314C from 1 July 2007.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Buildings within the ordinance (letter paras 1–2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Operative date | 1 July 2007 |
| Building types named | Composite buildings and domestic buildings |
| Age trigger | Constructed, or building plans submitted, on or before 1 March 1987 |
| Purpose stated | Better fire protection for occupants, users and visitors of those buildings |

**SD takeaway:** Treat a composite or domestic building with plans on or before 1 March 1987 as inside the Cap. 572 improvement regime when scoping alteration work.

### 2. Plan certification (letter para. 3; form FSI/314C)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Form | FSI/314C for every submission on a composite or domestic building within the ordinance |
| From | 1 July 2007, returned to the Building Improvement and Support Division, Fire Safety Command |
| Certificate | The FSI contractor or consultant certifies that the details and specifications on the attached fire service installation plans are as prescribed by the Department under Cap. 572 and in accordance with the applicable rules and codes |
| Examples named on the form | Rules of the Loss Prevention Council for automatic sprinkler installations; Fire Offices' Committee Rules for Automatic Sprinkler Installations (29th Edition); Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment |
| Procedure | Paragraph 4 of Part VI (Inspections of Fire Service Installations) in the 1996 FSD Circular Letters |

**SD takeaway:** Cap. 572 fire service drawings for these buildings must be certifiable on FSI/314C to the sprinkler rules and the Minimum Fire Service Installations code that the form names.
