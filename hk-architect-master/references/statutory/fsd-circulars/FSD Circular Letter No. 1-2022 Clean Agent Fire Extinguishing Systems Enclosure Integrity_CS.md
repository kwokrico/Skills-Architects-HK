# FSD Circular Letter No. 1/2022 — Clean Agent Fire Extinguishing Systems — Review and Annual Inspection of Enclosure Integrity
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
30 June 2022 | Fire Services Department — Licensing and Certification Command

> For systems to **NFPA 2001:2018+**. Links to **CL 1/2020** (new buildings) and **CL 2/2021** (occupied premises). Trial period from **1 August 2022** (2-year review).

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular requires **quantitative enclosure integrity test reports** for clean-agent systems complying with **NFPA 2001:2018+**, demonstrating that design agent concentration can be held for the specified duration. Annual **owner/occupier declarations** (Annex I) must confirm no A&A has compromised room integrity when RFSIC performs inspection under Cap. 95B Reg. 8(1), effective **1 August 2022** for a two-year trial.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When integrity test report is required

| Scenario | Submission |
|---|---|
| **New building** installation | RFSIC-signed test report with **acceptance inspection** documents per **CL 1/2020** |
| **New system in occupied premises** | RFSIC-signed report with **FS 251** + **Form FSI/314A** and plans per **CL 2/2021** |

### 2. Annual inspection — ongoing design liability

| Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| RFSIC | Verify **no A&A affecting enclosure** (walls, slabs, penetrations, services) before certifying annual inspection |
| Owner/occupier | Complete **Annex I declaration** confirming no integrity-affecting works in inspection period |
| Submission | Declaration copy to FSD **with FS 251** |

### 3. Factors affecting integrity (design + tenancy)

| Factor | Examples |
|---|---|
| Enclosure changes | Walls, ceiling/floor slabs altered |
| Penetrations | Cables/services added/removed through enclosure |

**SD takeaway:** Treat protected rooms as **sealed controlled volumes** — coordinate **cable penetrations, raised floor, demountable partitions** with hold-time testing; tenants cannot add openings without re-test and FSD notification path.
