# FSD Circular Letter No. 6/2016 — Stop Valves Management System for Fire Hydrant/Hose Reel Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 November 2016 | Fire Services Department

> Operational / management circular for FH/HR stop valves. Limited SD spatial impact — coordinate valve access with FSI. Design geometry of FH/HR remains under CoP / CL **2/2013** lineage and current CoP Sep 2022.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular sets management arrangements for stop valves on fire hydrant/hose reel systems so isolation does not leave floors unprotected without control. It applies to buildings with FH/HR systems using stop valves on the distribution network.
