# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-2026 Enhanced Measures on Transparency of Defect and Power Supply Management of FSIs
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
8 January 2026 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular sets posting standards for defective fire service installation (FSI) notices and for seals on the FSI main power switch. It does not change which FSIs a building must have, and shutdown notification remains Circular Letter No. 1/2021.

## Regulatory Overview
Registered Fire Service Installation Contractors must post a defective-FSI notice at installations that are not in effective operating condition, and must seal the FSI main power switch green when the supply is normal or red when it is cut off. The notice and seal standards take effect with the letter, while other notice and seal formats remain acceptable through 28 February 2026.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Defective FSI notices ((A)(1)–(3), Appendix I)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Where | Prominent position near an FSI that is not in effective operating condition: fire hydrant, hose reel, manual call point, F.S. inlet, sprinkler inlet, or street fire hydrant |
| Size | Not smaller than 15 cm × 8 cm; unobstructed and clearly visible |
| Concealed hose reel | Notice on both the hose reel and its cabinet door |
| Damp or outdoor location | Waterproof material |
| While defective | Kept intact and clearly legible until normal operation is restored |
| On restoration | All notices removed immediately when the works are complete |
| Appendix I wording | “Fire Hydrant/Hose Reel System”, “Fire Alarm System”, “Dry Riser System”, or “Sprinkler System” “is not in efficient working order” |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a clear 15 cm × 8 cm face at each of those FSI positions, and on both the reel and the door where the hose reel is concealed.

### 2. FSI main power supply and status seals ((B), Appendix II)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Default | Main FSI power supply shall not be cut off or switched off, except for an actual need from electrical safety or system maintenance |
| Green seal | Affixed firmly to the power switch when the supply is confirmed in normal operating condition. Appendix II: “The power supply is currently in normal status.” |
| Red seal | Used if the supply is switched off overnight, or for more than 24 hours, because of works or equipment failure. Appendix II: “Work in progress. The power supply is currently in a cut-off status.” |
| Seal size and tape | Not smaller than 30 cm × 8 cm. High-adhesion tape, not easily detachable, leaving no residual mark. Operation or interference leaves an irreversible “VOID” watermark |
| Label beside the switch | After each inspection: contractor name, registration number, and affixation date |
| After each inspection | Existing seal replaced with a new one |
| Shutdown | Notification of FSIs Shutdown to FSD under Circular Letter No. 1/2021, and the owner, occupier, and property management company informed |
| Colours | Green = power supply normal. Red = power supply cut off |

**SD takeaway:** The FSI main switch needs a clear face for a 30 cm × 8 cm seal, and that supply is not a general isolator switched off in normal operation.

### 3. Commencement ((D))

| Item | Timing |
|---|---|
| Notice and seal standards in this circular | Immediate effect |
| Other notice and seal formats still accepted | Transitional period until 28 February 2026 |
| Works started before the letter, or started during the transitional period, and not yet complete | Prescribed notice and seal formats recommended where practicable |

**SD takeaway:** Draw the Appendix I notice and Appendix II seal into the FSI and switch locations; other formats are accepted only until 28 February 2026.
