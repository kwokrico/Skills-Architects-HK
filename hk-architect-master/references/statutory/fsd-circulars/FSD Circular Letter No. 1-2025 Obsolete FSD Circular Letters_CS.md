# FSD Circular Letter No. 1-2025 Obsolete FSD Circular Letters
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
13 March 2025 | Fire Services Department

> Scope note: This circular withdraws six earlier FSD circular letters from use and names the instrument that replaces each one, or states that the 1998 codes are no longer for sale. It does not itself set fire-service design criteria.

## Regulatory Overview
The listed circulars are no longer valid and have been removed from the FSD website. Design and plan submission that previously relied on them follows the successor named in the remarks column.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Circulars withdrawn (table in the letter)
| FSD CL No. | Subject | Remarks |
|---|---|---|
| 2/99 | Processing of Fire Service Installations (FSI) Plans | Superseded by FSD CL No. 1/2005 |
| 6/99 | Chinese version of the Codes of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment and Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of Installations and Equipment 1998 | No longer available for sale |
| 2/2004 | Standard Spreader for Supporting Fire Service Pumps at Flat Roof | Superseded by FSD CL No. 6/2020 |
| 1/2012 | Installation of Fire Service Pump on Roof or Floor Slab | Superseded by FSD CL No. 6/2020 |
| 1/2013 | Guidelines on Formulation of Fire Safety Requirements for New Railway Infrastructures (English Version) | Superseded by FSD CL No. 3/2016 |
| 3/2013 | Guidelines on Formulation of Fire Safety Requirements for New Railway Infrastructures (2nd Edition) | Superseded by FSD CL No. 3/2016 |

The remarks cell for Nos. 2/2004 and 1/2012 is one cell pointing to No. 6/2020. The remarks cell for Nos. 1/2013 and 3/2013 is one cell pointing to No. 3/2016.

**SD takeaway:** Do not detail a roof fire-service pump to the flat-roof spreader in Circular Letter No. 2/2004 or to No. 1/2012; use No. 6/2020. New railway fire-safety guidelines are No. 3/2016, not Nos. 1/2013 or 3/2013.
