# FSD Circular Letter No. 3/2023 — Specification of Gas Extraction System for Battery Room and Electrical Charging Facilities
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
7 June 2023 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command  
Effective **1 September 2023** for initial building plan submissions; supersedes Part XI §8 of FSD Circular Letter No. 4/96.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular revises the gas extraction system specification for dedicated battery rooms and electrical charging facilities, supplementary to the FSI Code (Sep 2022). The system reduces flammable vapour (e.g. hydrogen from charging) below the lower explosive limit.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When required (§1.3)

| Trigger | Detail |
|---|---|
| Dedicated battery room with total output ≥ **400 Ah** | Required for lead-acid, nickel-cadmium, or other batteries evolving flammable vapour on recharge |
| Open-type batteries | **All sizes** must be in dedicated battery room |

### 2. Design extraction rate (§3)

| Method | Rate |
|---|---|
| Hydrogen limit | Max **1% by volume** during worst-case simultaneous boost charging of all batteries |
| Alternative minimum | **5.1 L/s per m²** of room floor area |
| Design standard | BS EN IEC 62485-1/62485-2, NFPA 1/111 or FSD-agreed equivalent; design calculations may be required |

### 3. Technical installation (§4)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fan | Non-combustible; TEFC motor |
| System independence | Independent of all other building systems; exhaust direct to open air |
| Extraction/inlet layout | Extract at high level; inlet at low level; cross-flow all areas |
| Operation | Continuous ventilation **or** electrically interlocked with battery charger (charger cannot run if fan off) |
| Power | Independent circuit from charger; interlock relay separately protected |
| Duct through compartments | Fire-resisting enclosure BS 476 Pt 20 — FRR of higher of served/containing compartment |
| High-pressure duct (> 750 Pa) | Pressure test to B&ES DW 143; no pressure-sensitive tapes |
| Monitoring | Local panel: power on / running / failed; audio-visual at fire control panel |

### 4. Maintenance

| Item | Frequency |
|---|---|
| RFSIC inspection | At least **every 12 months** |
| Performance test | Flow rate, actuation, audio-visual monitoring |

**SD takeaway:** Battery rooms are a combined FSI package — gas extraction, monitoring to FCC, and (from 2025) water-based fixed installation for Li-ion > 20 kWh; allow dedicated plant space and independent power at SD.
