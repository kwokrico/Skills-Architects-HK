# FSD Circular Letter No. 4/2006 — FSI Guidelines for New Territories Exempted Houses (Small Houses)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 August 2006 | Fire Services Department — Licensing & Certification Command  
Effective **1 July 2006**; read with Lands Department Guide to Fire Safety Requirements for Small Houses.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This circular promulgates three FSI guideline volumes for Small Houses (NTEH) where emergency vehicular access (EVA) is impracticable, providing alternative fire safety measures in clusters of nine or more houses within a 30 m radius.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. EVA vs alternatives (cluster rule)

| Cluster size (30 m radius) | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ≤ 9 houses (incl. proposed) | EVA **not required** |
| ≥ 10 houses | EVA required first; if impracticable → fire safety alternative |

### 2. EVA requirements (when applicable)

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Vehicular access | Emergency vehicles within **30 m** of NTEH |
| Access width | ≥ **4.5 m** (or lesser if FSD accepts); headroom **4.5 m**; 16-tonne fire appliance loading |
| Street fire hydrant | Within **100 m** of proposed house site |

### 3. Fire safety alternatives (if EVA impracticable)

| Option | Applicability |
|---|---|
| (i) Automatic sprinkler system | Any |
| (ii) Fire detection + hose reel | No fire separation between floors of 3-storey house |
| (iii) Fire detection + extinguisher each floor | Fire separation **between** floors of 3-storey house |
| Training | Options (ii)/(iii): owner/representative must attend FSD fire safety course before Certificate of Compliance |

### 4. FSI guideline volumes (Appendix II)

| Volume | System |
|---|---|
| I | Automatic sprinkler system |
| II | Fire detection + hose reel |
| III | Fire detection + extinguisher |

**SD takeaway:** Small House SD must resolve cluster count early — ≥10 houses triggers EVA or alternative FSI with dedicated FSD guideline submissions (FS251) and possible owner training obligation.
