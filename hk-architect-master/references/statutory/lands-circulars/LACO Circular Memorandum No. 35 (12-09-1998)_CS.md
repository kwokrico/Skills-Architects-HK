# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 35
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 September 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Further residential consent relaxations

> Scope note: Further suspends/relaxes private residential Consent Scheme deposit and private-sale measures (excludes PSPS / SCHS / FFSS). Amends CM 30A ASP Cl. 3(1)/Sch. 4. Sales terms — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

With immediate effect until further notice, CM No. 35 suspends or relaxes specified consent measures for future private residential consents and for existing consents’ unsold units (no preliminary agreement yet). Revised ASP Cl. 3(1) and Schedule 4 (Appendix) may be adopted without fresh LACO approval (except as noted for 2(a)).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Deposit and instalment relaxations (para. 2(a)–(b))

| Former | New (suspended/reduced) |
|---|---|
| Preliminary deposit ≈ **10%** of average price of units in that sale | ≈ **5%** |
| Further instalment after formal ASP to bring total to **20%** | **Suspended** |
| Fail to execute formal ASP | Forfeit **5%** preliminary deposit or amount actually paid, whichever less |
| Cancel after formal ASP | Forfeiture remains **5%** of purchase price |

**SD takeaway:** Price lists and ASP Sch. 4 must reflect ~5% preliminary deposit and no mandatory 20% post-ASP top-up while this CM applies.

---

### 2. Private sale quota and forfeiture (para. 2(c)–(d))

| Measure | New rule |
|---|---|
| Private purchasers quota | Increased to **20%** of total units under the consent (other private-sale requirements still apply) |
| Forfeiture on failed formal ASP | As above — 5% or actual paid, whichever less |

**SD takeaway:** Private-sale reservation can be up to 20% of consented units — still subject to remaining private-sale identity/deposit rules.

---

### 3. Deemed variation of existing consents (paras. 3–4)

| Rule |
|---|
| Appendix Cl. 3(1)/Sch. 4 may be used for residential sales (ex PSPS/SCHS/FFSS) including consented but unsigned formal ASPs (with stated exception for 2(a)) without LACO variation approval |
| CM terms deemed incorporated into existing consent letters for units unsold (no preliminary agreement before this CM’s date) |

**SD takeaway:** Treat unsold stock as automatically on the relaxed deposit terms — coordinate sales launch paperwork with the Appendix schedule.
