# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 41A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 August 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | DMC submission checklist for CM No. 41

> Scope note: Issues a **Checklist** (with Annex for full BMO 7th/8th Schedule text) that must accompany DMC approval applications. Replaces CM 1A Part (A) and Annex 1. Process lock: incomplete checklist delays approval; common-area plans may follow but are required before approval.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 9 August 1999, all DMC approval applications must include the attached Checklist duly completed. Pending cases may submit it to facilitate processing. The Checklist tracks compliance with CM No. 41 guidelines topic-by-topic.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory submission pack (Checklist Parts A–B)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Draft DMC | In **duplicate** |
| Grant + owners record | Certified by LR or solicitor (waived if already with concurrent consent application) |
| General info | Lot, grant, term, user SC, development type/name, total GFA, GIC flag, alienation/DMC SCs, principal vs sub-DMC |

**SD takeaway:** Complete the checklist data (especially total GFA and GIC flag) from the GA/lease package before lodging.

---

### 2. Common-area plans timing (Part C I)

| Rule |
|---|
| Plans showing common areas to be annexed; AP-certified accuracy; keep certified copy at Manager’s office |
| Plans need **not** be submitted at application if not yet available — but **must** be produced to LACO **before** DMC approval |

**SD takeaway:** Common-area plan production is on the critical path to DMC approval even if not in the first lodging.
