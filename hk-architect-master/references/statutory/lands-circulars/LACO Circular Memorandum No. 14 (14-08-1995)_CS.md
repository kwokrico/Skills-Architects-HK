# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 14
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
14 August 1995 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | New fee scale from **29 August 1995**

> Scope note: Further update of **LACO service fees** (superseding the CM No. 10 scale). Administrative only — **no** schematic design constraints beyond consent/DMC cost awareness.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 14 revises LACO Consent Scheme and related non-statutory fees with effect from 29 August 1995. The Appendix replaces the previous scale (CM No. 10).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Key Consent Scheme fees (Appendix A — selected)

| Service | New rate (HK$) |
|---|---|
| Consent to ASP | **38,500** |
| Consent to assignment / deed of exchange etc. | **19,240** |
| Consent to building mortgage/charge | **7,200** |
| Consent to ASP/DMC form amendments | **1,920** |
| Approval of DMC | **36,070** |
| Approval of sub-DMC / supplementary DMC / separate MA | **18,030** |

**SD takeaway:** Update consent/DMC fee budgets from the CM No. 10 figures to this 29 Aug 1995 scale when programming applications from that date.
