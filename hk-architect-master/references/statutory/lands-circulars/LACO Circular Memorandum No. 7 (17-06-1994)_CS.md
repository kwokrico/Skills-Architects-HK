# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 7
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 June 1994 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Effective for residential consents from **8 June 1994**

> Scope note: Introduces **anti-speculation residential pre-sale consent measures** and a revised residential **ASP**. Applies to **residential** Consent Scheme sales (not PSPS / HKHS restricted-resale / Sandwich Class). For architects it locks **consent timing vs AP estimated lease compliance**, **BC + 3 months** ASP completion envelope, **car-park bundling**, and saleable-area / plan-change rescission rules carried in the ASP.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 7 binds Land Authority Consent Scheme applications for **residential units** from 8 June 1994 (and amendments to earlier residential consents). It pairs Annex I sales measures with a revised ASP; consents will not issue until immediately before the **9-month** window from the AP’s estimated date of **lease-condition compliance** for the lot.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Consent timing and AP certificate (body; Annex I item 4)

| Gate | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Consent window | Presale consent **≤ 9 months** before AP’s estimated **completion of the development by compliance with the grant** |
| AP certificate | Must state estimated date of completion of **whole development of the lot in compliance with all lease conditions** (not merely expected CoC letter date) |
| ASP Cl. 4(1)(c) date | That AP compliance estimate; **not later than 3 months after** Building Covenant period expiry |
| Priority | Residential consents get higher LACO processing priority |

**SD takeaway:** Programme pre-sale off AP **lease-compliance** date (fact of compliance), not OP alone — keep ASP date inside BC expiry + **3 months**.

---

### 2. Sales / parking design locks (Annex I)

| Measure | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Private sale quota | Reduced **50% → 10%** of total units; private buyers pay **10%** deposit before public sale; formal ASP within **5 working days** |
| Private buyers | Individuals with HKID/passport only — **no companies** |
| Resale / transfer | No subsale or transfer of ASP benefit before Assignment |
| Deposit / forfeiture | Initial deposit **10%**; preliminary-agreement forfeiture **5%** (was 3%) |
| Brochures / price lists | Available **≥ 7 working days** before first ballot registration day |
| Residential car parks | Sold **only with** a residential unit; **max 2** spaces per residential unit |
| Cancelled units | Re-offer only via waiting-list priority or later public ballot |

**SD takeaway:** Design and sales packaging must keep residential CPS **bundled** (≤2 per flat) and brochure-ready ≥7 working days before ballot registration.

---

### 3. ASP completion, weather EOT, plan change, shares (revised ASP)

| Topic | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| “Completed” for Cl. 4 | Vendor in position validly to assign = **CoC** or **consent to assign**, whichever first |
| Weather EOT | Rainfall **> 20 mm**/24 h (RO) **or** Signal **No. 8+** between **08:00–17:00** |
| Plan alteration > **5%** saleable area (Sch. 3) | Purchaser may rescind within **30 days** of BA-approval notice |
| Undivided shares | Total change **> 3%** needs prior Director of Lands written consent |
| Warranties at ASP date | Approved plans; foundations certified complete; BA consent to commence **superstructure** |
| Saleable area | Same exterior-wall / bay / CPS / roof measurement rules as CM 1 ASP Cl. 1(1)(r); items listed separately in Sch. 3 |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze unit envelopes early (5% rescission), keep share matrix within 3%, and treat Signal 8 daytime / >20 mm rain as the only weather EOTs in this ASP.
