# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 26A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
11 May 1998 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Hotline enhancements

> Scope note: Enhances the CM No. 26 consent hotline (Putonghua; private vs SCHS/FFSS/PSPS categories; fax-on-demand). Still **no** design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 26A upgrades the “Consents to sell Residential Units” hotline (2147 5475) with Putonghua, separate private vs subsidised-scheme categories, and fax-on-demand reports for issued/pending consents.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Service upgrades (paras. (a)–(c))

| Upgrade | Detail |
|---|---|
| Language | Putonghua added (plus Cantonese/English) |
| Categories | (a) private developments; (b) Sandwich Class / Flat-for-Sale / PSPS |
| Fax-on-demand | Issued (last 6 months); monthly issued; pending; plus User Guide |

**SD takeaway:** No design lock — confirm consent status via categorised hotline/fax reports if needed for programme intelligence.
