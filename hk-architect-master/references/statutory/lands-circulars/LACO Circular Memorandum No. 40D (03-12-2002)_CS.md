# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 40D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 December 2002 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Removes private-sale quota and one-unit/2-CPS cap

> Scope note: Implements 13 Nov 2002 market measures by abolishing the **30% internal/private-sale quota** and the **one unit (≤2 CPS) per purchaser** restriction for all sale categories. Retrospective for existing effective consents. Sales packaging lock — not BO design.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 40D further amends CM No. 40 (as previously varied) for uncompleted private residential developments (ex PSPS/SCHS/FFSS). Removals apply to future consents and retrospectively to existing consents still in effect. Other Consent Scheme terms remain in force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Private/internal sale quota removed (para. 2(a))

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Max **30%** (under CM 40B) reserved for private/internal sale | **No maximum** — any number may be reserved for internal sale if usual private-sale rules (deposits, PAs, brochures, etc.) are met |

**SD takeaway:** Internal/private reservation is no longer percentage-capped — still meet private-sale process and brochure duties.

---

### 2. One-unit / max-2-CPS per purchaser abolished (para. 2(b))

| Scope | Effect |
|---|---|
| Public sale; leftovers after selection; private sale; exempted units | Restriction on **one unit (with ≤2 carparking spaces) per purchaser** is **abolished** |

**SD takeaway:** Multi-unit and multi-CPS purchases are no longer barred by this Consent Scheme cap — confirm any remaining lease/consent letter limits separately.
