# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 41
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
29 June 1999 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 III | Revised DMC Guidelines (replaces LOCM 91 + CM 4/4A/9 selected paras)

> Scope note: Issues comprehensive **DMC Guidelines** for Director’s approval under lease conditions. Strong SD locks: **AP-certified common-area plans**, carpark commoning, **GFA-based shares**, **slope structures + ≥1:500 AP plan**, retained areas, manager caps. **CM No. 34 GIC guidelines unchanged**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 41 replaces LOCM 91 and LACO CM Nos. 4 (para. 3(a)), 4A (para. 3) and 9. Guidelines apply to pending and future DMC approvals for developments of any user/size. Non-adherence must be explained in writing on submission. Director may vary guidelines case-by-case for fairness.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Common areas, carpark, AP plans (Guidelines 3, 24, 29–30)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Common areas | Defined; plans annexed; AP-certified accuracy; certified copy at Manager’s office for inspection |
| Common undivided shares | Allocated to commons; assigned to manager as trustee free of cost; no voting / no fee liability / not in quorum |
| Carpark | Whole carpark (except BA-approved designated spaces) = common **or** if developer retains whole carpark, manage it; on sale of spaces, circulation/lifts/landings become carpark commons via sub-DMC |
| Share basis | Normally **GFA**; other bases may need justification (avoid disproportionate voting / OC hindrance) |
| Conversion | No converting commons↔private without Owners’ Committee/OC approval; no redesignation of commons by developer/manager |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze AP-certified common-area plans and GFA share matrix before DMC approval — carpark strategy (common vs retained) must be explicit.

---

### 2. Slopes (Guideline 28)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Duty | Owners maintain slope structures per Government Grant and Geoguide 5 |
| Plan | If slope structures exist: annex plan **≥ 1:500**, AP-certified as including all such structures (also for phase sub-DMC) |
| Manager | Authority to engage qualified personnel; costs payable by owners; manager not personally liable if unable to collect after reasonable endeavours |

**SD takeaway:** Prepare Geoguide-aligned slope inventory plan for the DMC at consent stage — later reinforced by **CM No. 41B** Slope Maintenance Manual annexation.

---

### 3. Manager, deposits, structural alterations (Guidelines 8–9, 15, 27)

| Topic | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| First manager term | ≤ **2 years** |
| Remuneration (residential) | ≤ **20% / 15% / 10%** of reasonable management expenditure for ≤20 / 21–100 / 101+ units; non-res ≤15%; composite counts non-res as residential units |
| First owners’ meeting | ≤ **9 months** after DMC |
| Deposits (selected) | Management deposit ≤ **3/12** first-year budget; advance ≤2/12; debris ≤1/12 res / 3/12 commercial; special fund ≤2/12; aggregate caps in Guideline 15 |
| Structural alterations | No structural alteration interfering with other owners’ rights |

**SD takeaway:** DMC management economics and common-parts definition must be design-ready; structural alteration covenant protects shared structure assumptions.

---

### 4. Retained areas & Chinese translation (Guidelines 6, 33)

| Topic | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Retained Areas | Clearly defined; fair shares; developer pays pro rata; any purchaser use rights stated in brochure/advertising |
| Chinese translation | Developer provides direct translation or summary within **3 months** of DMC; available at management office; state which language prevails |

**SD takeaway:** Identify retained areas on plans early and disclose purchaser-use rights in sales materials.
