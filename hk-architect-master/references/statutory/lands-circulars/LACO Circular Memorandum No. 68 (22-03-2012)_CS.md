# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 68
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 March 2012 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ/127 VI | Brochure cross-section plans

> Scope note: Adds Annex I para. **B15** requiring sales brochures to include a **cross-section plan of every building**. Immediate effect; consent conditional on revised Annex compliance. Direct SD graphic deliverable.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 68 revises Annex I to CM No. 62 (as varied by CM 63) by adding B15. It amends CM No. 40 (as varied by 62/63). Other Consent Scheme terms remain in force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cross-section plans in sales brochure (new B15)

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Brochure content | Must include a **cross-section plan** in relation to **every building** in the development |
| Effect | Consent to sell conditioned on brochure complying with revised Annex |

**SD takeaway:** Produce building cross-sections as a consent brochure deliverable for each building — not optional marketing graphics.
