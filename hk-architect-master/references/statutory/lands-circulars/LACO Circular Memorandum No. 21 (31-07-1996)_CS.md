# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 21
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 July 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | GPA Legal Advisory Division from **1 July 1996**

> Scope note: Announces **GPA Legal Advisory Division** taking over GIC conveyancing/DMC commenting duties from Senior Solicitor/FSI. Routing change for GIC DMC packs — design still follows **CM No. 11** GIC DMC provisions.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 July 1996, GPA’s Legal Advisory Division handles GPA conveyancing, sales/purchases, leasing, commenting on DMCs with GIC accommodation, and commenting on GIC conditions in land grants. CM Nos. 1A and 3 are amended for document routing to that Division.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Document routing for GIC DMCs (paras. 2–3; Annex 2 Note)

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Senior Solicitor/FSI at QGO | **Senior Solicitor, Legal Advisory Division, GPA** (40/F Revenue Tower, 5 Gloucester Road, Wan Chai) |
| CM 1A Checklist B Note 2 | Replacement page: extra **6 copies** of draft DMC & MA to GPA Legal Advisory Division |
| CM 3 para. 4 | Extra document set sent direct to that Division to settle draft DMC |

**SD takeaway:** When GIC is in the scheme, send the extra DMC/MA copies to **GPA Legal Advisory Division** — wrong routing delays DMC/consent.
