# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 75C
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
12 April 2024 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Electronic submission for non-residential pre-sale consent

> Scope note: Non-residential parallel to CM 72E — paper or electronic lodging; certified Government Grant copy no longer required at application.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 75C introduces electronic submission as an alternative to paper for consent to sell non-residential units in uncompleted developments. Procedures are in the attached Annex; form pack is updated.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Paper vs electronic submission (non-res)

| Mode | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Paper | Original hard copies of all supporting documents at application |
| Electronic | Email copies at application; originals of specified key documents within prescribed time |

**SD takeaway:** Content of AP certificates/plans unchanged — only lodging channel.

---

### 2. Land grant copy

| Former | New |
|---|---|
| Certified Government Grant with application | **No longer required** from developer's solicitors |

**SD takeaway:** Lease still governs design; grant copy need not be lodged at consent filing under this CM.
