# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 17
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
3 April 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Undertakings / bank undertakings for pre-compliance sales

> Scope note: Standardises **solicitor undertakings** and **bank undertakings** evidencing finance ability for Consent Scheme sales before lease compliance. Financing/consent paperwork — architect relevance is ensuring construction-cost cover exists for consent; not a plan-design code.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 17 guides forms of undertaking/bank undertaking for applications to enter ASPs of undivided shares prior to compliance with New Grant GCs/SCs. Existing already-executed undertakings are unaffected.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Solicitor firm undertaking (guideline (i))

| Acceptable form | Role |
|---|---|
| Letter from solicitor’s firm undertaking to observe consent letter conditions **(o)(i)–(iii)** | Satisfies undertaking required by condition **(o)(iv)** |

**SD takeaway:** Consent condition (o) package can be closed with a firm letter covering (o)(i)–(iii) — coordinate with solicitors early in the consent pack.

---

### 2. Bank undertaking (guideline (ii) + attached form)

| Requirement | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| Form | Use attached **standard bank undertaking** if bank finances construction costs |
| Execution proof | Solicitor certificate that undertaking was duly executed per bank’s M&A and authorised by properly convened meeting/resolution |

**SD takeaway:** If consent finance evidence is a bank undertaking, use the LACO standard form with solicitor execution certificate — non-standard forms risk delay.
