# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 64
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
28 June 2011 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 VI | Revised DMC Guidelines (supersedes CM No. 56)

> Scope note: Issues revised **DMC Guidelines and Checklist** aligning with **Building Management (Amendment) Ordinance 2007** and revised **JPN Nos. 1 & 2** (Jan 2011). Applies to ongoing and new DMC/Sub-DMC applications. **CM No. 34** GIC guidelines unchanged.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 64 supersedes the Guidelines and Checklist attached to CM No. 56. New applications must use the revised Checklist. Guidelines continue to govern LACO approval of DMCs under land grants, covering common areas, shares, manager, slopes, green features, telecom, etc. (see attached index themes).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Application gate

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| New DMC/Sub-DMC apps | Must attach duly completed **revised Checklist** |
| Scope | All ongoing and new approval applications |
| Not replaced | **CM No. 34** Government Accommodation guidelines |

**SD takeaway:** Lodge DMCs with the CM 64 checklist; keep GIC packs on the CM 34/34A track in parallel.

---

### 2. Design-facing continuity (guideline index)

| Themes carried/updated |
|---|
| Common areas & parking commons; GFA share allocation; green/innovative features; slope maintenance; telecom network areas; retained areas; no structural alterations; works & installations maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** Use CM 64 (not 56/41) as the current DMC design checklist baseline for post-Jun 2011 submissions — align JPN green features and Cap. 344 amendments in drafting.
