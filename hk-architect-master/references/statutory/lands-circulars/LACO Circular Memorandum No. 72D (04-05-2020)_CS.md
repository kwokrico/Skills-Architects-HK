# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 72D
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
4 May 2020 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt. 10 | Combined Building Mortgage + FU; single FU rule

> Scope note: Amends Annex/Appendices to CM 72C to speed residential consent. Accepts **Combined Method** (Building Mortgage + Finance Undertaking) with new SD/FU forms; generally **one** FU only from an authorized bank/DTC.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 72D updates residential Consent Scheme financing evidence and related forms. Combined Method financing is acceptable with the new standard SD and Finance Undertaking forms attached.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Combined Method financing

| Method | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Building Mortgage alone | Accepted (existing) |
| Finance Undertaking alone | Accepted (existing) |
| **Combined Method** (BM + FU) | Now accepted with dedicated standard SD / FU forms |

**SD takeaway:** Financing structure is solicitor/bank territory — AP certificates still track the Annex pack for the chosen method.

---

### 2. Single Finance Undertaking

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| FU or Combined Method | LACO accepts **only one** FU from a licensed bank or registered DTC under Banking Ordinance s.16 |
| Multiple FUs | Generally **not** acceptable without good justifications |

**SD takeaway:** Plan completion funding so only one FU is lodged with the consent pack.
