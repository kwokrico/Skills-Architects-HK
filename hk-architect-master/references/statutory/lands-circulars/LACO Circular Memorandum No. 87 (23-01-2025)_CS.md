# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 87
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
23 January 2025 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Fees charged for services delivered by LACO

> Scope note: LACO non-statutory fee circular only. No schematic-design dimensional or Consent Scheme form rules beyond fee budgeting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 87 adjusts 28 LACO fee items from 1 February 2025 and publishes a full fee list.
Adjusted rates also apply to earlier applications for which fees have not yet been demanded.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fee update

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Fee demand date | Rates apply to fees **demanded from 1.2.2025** (even if application was earlier) |
| Status of prior CMs | Supersedes **CM No. 86** from 1.2.2025 |

**SD takeaway:** Budget LACO fees to CM 87 Appendix (as varied by 87A).
