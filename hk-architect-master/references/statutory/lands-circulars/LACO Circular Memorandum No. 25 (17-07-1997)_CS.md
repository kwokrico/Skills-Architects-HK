# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 25
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
17 July 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Cap. 219 s.34A costs clauses from **30 June 1997**

> Scope note: Aligns Consent Scheme ASP solicitor-costs clauses with **Legal Services Legislation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Ordinance 1997** amending Cap. 219 s.34A(3)–(4). Conveyancing costs allocation — **no** schematic design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Section 14 of the 1997 Ordinance voids ASP provisions requiring the purchaser (when separately legally represented) to pay the vendor’s costs of the agreement/assignment. Standard residential ASP Cl. 11(2)(b) (and analogous PSPS / non-residential clauses) must be amended accordingly for ASPs on/after 30 June 1997.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory ASP costs wording (body)

| Form | Amendment |
|---|---|
| Standard residential ASP Cl. 11(2)(b) | If purchaser instructs other solicitors, **each party pays its own** solicitors’ costs of ASP and Assignment (incl. preparation, completion, stamping, registration) |
| PSPS ASP | Similar amendment to Cl. 11(a) |
| Non-residential ASP | Similar amendment to Cl. 11(c) |
| Already-consented ASPs entered 30 Jun 1997–before this CM | May incorporate new clauses **without** specific LACO approval each case |
| Preliminary agreements | Also covered by the legislation (s.14(3)(c)); LACO does not approve preliminary agreement forms |

**SD takeaway:** No design lock — ensure sales ASP pack uses the post-30 Jun 1997 costs clause when purchaser has separate solicitors.
