# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 47
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 August 2002 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Saleable area — utility platforms vs A/C platforms

> Scope note: Adds **utility platform** to Consent Scheme residential ASP saleable-area definition (separate Sch. 3 / brochure item). **A/C platforms are not saleable area** but may be assigned as Property with separate Sch. 3 listing. Direct SD measurement lock for green-feature platforms.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 47 responds to Joint Practice Note green features: utility platforms may be sold as part of a unit under a standard ASP definition; A/C platforms must not be counted in saleable area. Immediate effect for all residential consent applications (including those being processed). Appendices A/B may be used without separate LACO approval if the ASP is otherwise standard.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Utility platforms (para. 3(a))

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| ASP | Standard definition of “utility platform” as Cl. **1(1)(s)(vi)** (App. A) |
| Disclosure | Separately specified/described as part of unit saleable area in **Sch. 3** (App. B format) **and** sales brochures (CM 40 as varied) |

**SD takeaway:** Design utility platforms as discrete saleable-area items with clear Sch. 3/brochure figures — do not bury them inside the flat figure.

---

### 2. A/C platforms (para. 3(b))

| Rule | Hard limit |
|---|---|
| Saleable area | A/C platform area **must not** be included in saleable area; not allowed as a Cl. 1(1)(s) variation |
| Assignment | May still assign A/C platform with the unit; owners maintain; list in Sch. 3 as separate Property item **without** counting area in saleable area |
| Brochure | Separately specify and label as **not** forming part of saleable area |

**SD takeaway:** Keep A/C hoods/platforms out of saleable-area schedules; label them clearly as non-SA Property if sold with the unit.

---

### 3. No “additional area” labelling (para. 5)

| Rule |
|---|
| All applicable Cl. 1(1)(s) items go in App. B “A.” and must **not** be described as “additional” areas or similar |

**SD takeaway:** Brochure/ASP schedules must use Consent Scheme saleable-area categories — no marketing “additional area” workaround.
