# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 18
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 May 1996 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 | Practice change from **1 June 1996**

> Scope note: Stops LACO (HK/Kln) requesting Land Registry **endorsement/attachment** of modification and related documents onto lease conditions. Title-search practice only — **no** schematic design constraints.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 1 June 1996, LACO ceases asking the Land Registry to endorse/attach modification and listed related documents to lease conditions in Hong Kong and Kowloon (aligning with NT practice). Documents remain registered by memorial; searches must check the computer/deed register, not rely on lease endorsements alone.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Documents no longer endorsed onto lease conditions (Note)

| Document types listed |
|---|
| Modification Letter; No-objection Letter; Waiver Letter; Deed of Release; Deed of Rectification; Conditions of Extension of Lease Term; Extension Letter; Deed of Grant of Easement |

**SD takeaway:** When verifying lease modifications for design/user, search the memorial register — do not assume the lease counterpart shows every modification physically attached.
