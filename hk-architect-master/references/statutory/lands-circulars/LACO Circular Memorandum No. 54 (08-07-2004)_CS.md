# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 54
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
8 July 2004 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 IV | Consent Scheme review measures (from apps after **1 July 2004**)

> Scope note: Formalises/expands CM 51 trial measures for **all** uncompleted-unit consents (residential and non-residential) received after 1 July 2004. Strong SD locks: expanded **brochure/ad party disclosure**, and building-mortgage-specific Annex Section II controls. Pre-1.7.2004 apps stay on CM 51.

---

## Regulatory Overview

After industry consultation, CM No. 54’s Annex measures apply to consent applications received after 1 July 2004 (res and non-res). CM 51 continues for earlier applications. Previous consent CMs are further amended as appropriate; other Consent Scheme terms remain in force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory disclosure in brochures and ads (Annex §I.1)

| Must name clearly |
|---|
| Developer (registered owner) and parent/holding company |
| AP and the professional firm to which AP belongs |
| Main superstructure contractor registered with BA |
| Solicitors firm for the sale |
| Mortgagee bank under building mortgage (if any) |
| Bank giving Undertaking to finance completion (if any) |
| Financier already lending and joining ASP (if any) |
| Exception | Certain media (TV/radio/public transport/billboards etc.) where full list is impractical |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze the project party list (especially AP firm and main contractor) for brochure/ad compliance before marketing.

---

### 2. Scope split (Annex)

| Section I | Section II |
|---|---|
| Measures for **all** consents to sell uncompleted units | Additional measures only where development is financed by **building mortgage** |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm financing structure early — building-mortgage schemes trigger extra Annex II consent conditions (see full Annex/Appendices in source).
