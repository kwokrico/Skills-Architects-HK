# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 70A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 January 2013 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Communication-network lease fee from **28 January 2013**

> Scope note: Revises fee for **Approval of Lease for Communication Network Areas** to **HK$9,610**. Varies CM No. 69 for that item.

---

## Regulatory Overview

From 28.1.2013, the communication-network lease approval fee is HK$9,610. Fees demanded from that date use the revised rate even if application was earlier.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised fee

| Service | Rate (from 28.1.2013) |
|---|---|
| Approval of Lease for Communication Network Areas | **HK$9,610** |

**SD takeaway:** Relevant only for communication-network lease approvals — otherwise no SD impact.
