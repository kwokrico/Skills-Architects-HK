# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 34A
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 November 2018 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD/LACO/HQ 1/410/1993 Pt 10 | Revises GPA checklist under CM No. 34

> Scope note: Updates GPA’s **Guidelines and Checklist** supporting-document list for DMCs with Government Accommodation. CM No. 34 remains in force except as varied. Architect-facing change: mandatory **AP-certified DMC plans** showing common areas and GA (extra copy if public transport terminus).

---

## Regulatory Overview

CM No. 34A replaces the checklist attachment to CM No. 34 for all new DMC approval applications involving Government Accommodation. A copy of the completed checklist need **no longer** be sent to LACO for record. Except as varied here, CM No. 34 continues in full force.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Revised mandatory documents before GPA scrutiny (Checklist §I)

| Item | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| DMC | Three copies (revised drafts/pages in triplicate) |
| DMC Plans | **Five** AP-certified copies showing **common areas and GA**; **+1** if GA includes a public transport terminus (more if GPA requires) |
| Land Grant | One LR- or solicitor-certified copy including modifications |
| LR search | One updated historical and current search |
| Other items 1–5 | DMC will **not** be scrutinised until items 1–5 submitted to GPA’s satisfaction with completed checklist |

**SD takeaway:** Produce AP-certified common-area + GA plans as a DMC deliverable (extra set for PTT) — without them GPA will not start.

---

### 2. Process change vs CM 34

| Former (CM 34) | New (CM 34A) |
|---|---|
| Completed checklist copy also to LACO for record | **No longer** necessary to send checklist copy to LACO |

**SD takeaway:** Route the completed checklist to GPA; LACO record copy is dropped.
