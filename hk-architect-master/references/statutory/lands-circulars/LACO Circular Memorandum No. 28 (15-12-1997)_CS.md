# LACO Circular Memorandum No. 28
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 December 1997 | Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LACO/HQ/127 II | Immediate effect

> Scope note: Stops districts withholding **DMC approval** until all lease approvals (MLP, landscape, DDH, etc.) are obtained. Process lock: DMC can proceed earlier, but lease approvals remain mandatory and post-approval plan changes may force DMC amendments.

---

## Regulatory Overview

With immediate effect, LACO districts will approve DMCs without requiring all lease-condition approvals to be in place first. Those lease approvals are still required to comply with the lease; if plans/proposals later change, consequential DMC amendments must be submitted and approved.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Decoupled DMC vs lease approvals (paras. 1–3)

| Former practice | New practice |
|---|---|
| Some districts waited for MLP / landscaping / DDH etc. before DMC approval | DMC may be approved **without** all lease approvals first |
| Lease compliance | Still required — CM does **not** waive lease SCs |
| Later plan changes | If DMC drifts from amended plans/proposals → submit consequential DMC amendments for approval |

**SD takeaway:** Advance DMC drafting/approval in parallel with MLP/landscape/DDH — but keep DMC text aligned when lease-approved plans change.
