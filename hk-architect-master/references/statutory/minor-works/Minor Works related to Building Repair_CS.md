- flat slab, cantilevered slab, ribbed slab, waffle slab, pre-stressed beam, post-tensioned beam, cantilevered beam, transfer plate, transfer beam or earth retaining structure

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Repair of Drainage

- Excavation of depth **> 1.5m** & **≤ 3m**
- Excavation of depth **≤ 1.5m**
- Not involved MW item 3.23
- Not involve main pipes, other than the replacement of components at existing junctions

Item².30 and 3.23: Please note that the works do not involve any repair or replacement of an internal branch pipe or sanitary fitment.

### 2. Repair of Structural Elements or Concrete Projection

- flat slab, cantilevered slab, ribbed slab, waffle slab, pre-stressed beam, post-tensioned beam, cantilevered beam, transfer plate, transfer beam or earth retaining structure
- any column, load bearing wall, slab or beam
- thickness **≤ 125mm**; and
- projecting from structural member **≤ 150mm**

Where defects of a structure are quite extensive or severe, recasting of structural elements may be required and can be carried out under minors works item 1.17.

**SD takeaway:** flat slab, cantilevered slab, ribbed slab, waffle slab, pre-stressed beam, post-tensioned beam, cantilevered beam, transfer plate, transfer beam or earth retaining structure any column, load bearing wall, slab or beam thickness **≤ 125mm**; and projecting from structural member **≤ 150mm** Where defects of a structure.

### 3. Repair of Non-load bearing External Wall of a building

- Height of the wall **≤ 3.5m**

### 4. Repair of External renderings, external wall tiles or roof finishes

- Thickness of the roof finishes > the original design of the finishes
- Thickness of the roof finishes ≤ the original design of the finishes

### 5. Repair of External claddings

- In accordance with the original design
- Cladding made of any material
- Not MW item².48 or 3.31
- In accordance with the original design
- Metal cladding only
- Not MW item 3.31
- Cladding made of any material; and
- Distance from the adjoining ground or roof **≤ 6m**
- Metal cladding only;
- Distance from the adjoining ground or roof **≤ 6m**;
- Not fixed to any cantilevered slab; and
- setback distance from the edge of roof **> 600mm**; and
- the roof **≤ 20m** above the ground
- If the cladding is above a roof:

For minor works carried out at common parts of a building (such as external wall of a building), the person who arranged the works to be carried out or his appointed agent (including the PBP/PRC) should seek consent from all owners and the management company of the building and observe all duties as stipulated in the deed of mutual convenant.
