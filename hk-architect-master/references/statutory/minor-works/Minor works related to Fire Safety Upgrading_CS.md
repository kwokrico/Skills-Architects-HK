## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Water Tanks for Fire Services

- material of the water tank: Glass reinforced polyester
- located on grade or on a slab (other than a cantilevered slab)
- capacity of the tank ≤ 4.5m3
- material of the water tank: Glass reinforced polyester
- located on a roof
- capacity of the tank ≤ 9m3
- water head of the tank **≤ 2m**
- distance from the edge of the roof **≤ 1.5m**
- in accordance with the original design

### 4. External Non-Load Bearing Walls Openings for Fixed Lights, Top Vents or F.S. Pipes

- height of the wall **1.1m** - **3.5m**
- height of the wall **≤ 1.1m**
- height of the wall **1.1m** - **3.5m**
- height of the wall **≤ 1.1m**
- distance between the 2 farthest points within the area of opening **≤ 150mm** ;
