## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **7**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Laying/ Repair/Removal of any external rendering, external wall tile or roof finishes of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 7 |
| Nature of works | Laying/ Repair/Removal of any external rendering, external wall tile or roof finishes of a building |
| For external rendering and external wall tile—the distance between the highest point of the rendering or tile and the adjoining ground or adjoining roof | **Not more than 3m** |
| The gradient of the roof | **Not more than 1** in 4 |
| The use of the roof | Not an inaccessible roof |
| In the case of the laying or repair of roof finishes—at the completion of the works, the thickness of the finishes | Not more than that in the original design |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
