## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **24**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any outdoor planter, pond or fountain on-grade**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 24 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any outdoor planter, pond or fountain on-grade |
| Excavation depth | **Not more than 300mm** |
| For erection or alteration works—the distance between the highest point of the planter, pond or fountain and the adjoining ground | **Not more than 600mm** |
| For repair or removal works—the distance between the highest point of the planter, pond or fountain and the adjoining ground | **Not more than 1.1 m** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
