## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **1**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Formation of an opening in a slab**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 1 |
| Nature of works | Formation of an opening in a slab |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| The distance between the 2 points that are farthest away from each other within the area of the opening | **Not more than 150 mm** |
| The distance between openings | The distance between the centre of the opening to be formed and any other existing opening in the slab is **not less than 450mm** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
