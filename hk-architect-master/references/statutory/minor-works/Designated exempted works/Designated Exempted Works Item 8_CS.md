## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **8**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal gate at a fence wall or at an entrance to a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 8 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal of any metal gate at a fence wall or at an entrance to a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| The weight of each leaf of the gate | **Not more than 100**kg |
| The width of each leaf of the gate | **Not more than 1.2m** |
| The height of the gate | **Not more than 2.2m** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
