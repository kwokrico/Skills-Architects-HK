## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **11**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Removal of any signboard fixed to the external wall of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 11 |
| Nature of works | Removal of any signboard fixed to the external wall of a building |
| The display area of the signboard | **Not more than 1m²** |
| Comprise any display system consisting of light emitting diodes | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 600mm** |
| The distance between any part of the signboard and the ground | **Not more than 3m** |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
