## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **21**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection of any mesh fence, metal railing or pole on top of a solid fence wall (other than an unauthorised solid fence wall) on-grade**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 21 |
| Nature of works | Erection of any mesh fence, metal railing or pole on top of a solid fence wall (other than an unauthorised solid fence wall) on-grade |
| The height of the wall alone | **Not more than 2.5m** |
| The aggregate height of the wall and the fence, railing or pole, including any feature at its top | **Not more than 3m** |
| The mesh fence or metal railing is used as a protective barrier | No |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
