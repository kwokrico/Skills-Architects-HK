## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Designated exempted works** item **13**. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Removal of any supporting frame projecting from an external wall of a building**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Designated Exempted Works item no. | 13 |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Removal of any supporting frame projecting from an external wall of a building |
| The frame is for supporting | Air-conditioning unit or light fitting |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 600mm** |
| The distance between the highest point of the frame and the roof or ground | **Not more than 3m** |
| The frame is designed for | Weight of an air-conditioning unit or light fitting of **not more than 100**kg |
| The frame projects over any street or common part of the building | No |

**SD takeaway:** If any row of the gate is not met, this item does not apply.
