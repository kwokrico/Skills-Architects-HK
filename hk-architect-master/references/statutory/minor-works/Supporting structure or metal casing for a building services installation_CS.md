## Regulatory Overview

The technical specifications of the supporting structure or metal casing for a building services installation for validation and corresponding minor works items are listed in Part 4 of Schedule 3 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation (B(MW)R). The works must be in existence or completed before 1 September 2020.
