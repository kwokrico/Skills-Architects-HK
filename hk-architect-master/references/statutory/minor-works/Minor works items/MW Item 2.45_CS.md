## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class II** item **2.45**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Trellises. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Removal**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 2.45 |
| Item class/type | Class II Type A |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Removal |
| Location of the trellis | Located on a roof of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| The works involve the alteration of any other structural elements | No |
| Height of the trellis | **Not more than 2.5m** |
| For erection or alteration |  |
| The length and width of every opening of the overhead framework of the trellis | **Not less than 200mm** |
| The horizontal clearance of the trellis from any other structure | **Not less than 500 mm** |
| For works carried out in a common part of the building and at the completion of the works | Each trellis located in that common part covers an area of **not more than 5m²** ; and The aggregate of the area covered by each trellis located in that common part is **not more than 5**% of the total area of that part |
| For works carried out in a part that is not a common part of the building (non-common part) and at the completion of the works | Each trellis located in that non-common part covers an area of **not more than 5m²** ; and The aggregate of the area covered by each trellis located in that non-common part is **not more than 20m²** and **not more than 5**% of the total area of that part |
| For removal |  |
| The area covered by the trellis | **Not more than 20m²** |
