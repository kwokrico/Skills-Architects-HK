## Regulatory Overview

This page covers **Class II** item **2.41**. Registered contractor types on the page: **A**. BD files it under: Wind guards. Nature of works stated on the page: **Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Item gate

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Item no. | 2.41 |
| Item class/type | Class II Type A |
| Nature of works | Erection/Alteration/Repair/Removal |
| Scope of works | The works involve any metal wind guard for an opening on an external wall of a building |
| Result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab | No |
| Projection from the wall | **Not more than 300mm** |
| The distance between the highest point of the guard and the roof or ground | **More than 3m** |
| If the wind guard is larger than the opening, the edge distance between the projection image of the guard and the opening (not including the corners) | **Not more than 300 mm** |
