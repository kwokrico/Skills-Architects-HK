### 2. Class I item 1.50 Erection/Alteration

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| The supporting structure or casing is located on-grade/on a canopy (other than a cantilevered slab) or on a roof (other than a cantilevered slab) |  |
| For the supporting structure : | Height of the supporting structure for an antenna or transceiver **≤ 2.5m**; or for BSI **≤ 1.5m**; and |
| For the casing : | Weight ≤10% of the weight of the BSI; Distance between inner surface of casing and installation **≤ 200mm** |

If any gate fails, item 1.50 on this card does not apply.

**SD takeaway:** If any gate fails, item 1.50 on this card does not apply.
