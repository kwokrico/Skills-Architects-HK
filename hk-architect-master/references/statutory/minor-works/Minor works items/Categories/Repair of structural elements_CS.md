### 2. Class I item 1.17 Repair

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Any structural elements (including any flat slab, cantilevered slab, ribbed slab, waffle slab, pre-stressed beam, post-tensioned beam, cantilevered beam, transfer plate, transfer beam or earth retaining structure), or of any concrete projection from the structural elements |  |

If any gate fails, item 1.17 on this card does not apply.

**SD takeaway:** If any gate fails, item 1.17 on this card does not apply.

### 3. Class II item 2.17 Repair

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Structural member (including any column, load bearing wall, slab or beam), or of any concrete projection from the structural member | If involve a concrete projection thickness **≤ 125mm** ; and projecting from structural member **≤ 150mm**; |
| Not involve any recasting or replacement of structural element; |  |
| Not involve the dismantling of the whole of the floor or roof; and |  |
| Not involve the removal of core from the structural members or concrete projections by drilling |  |

If any gate fails, item 2.17 on this card does not apply.

**SD takeaway:** If any gate fails, item 2.17 on this card does not apply.
