### 2. Class I item 1.45 Erection/Alteration

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Any trellis on a roof |  |
| Height of the trellis **≤ 2.5m** |  |
| If located in a common part of the building, | Aggregate area covered by trellises in the common part ≤ 5% of the total area of the common part; and; Area covered by each trellis **≤ 20m²** |
| If located in a non-common part of the building, | Aggregate area covered by trellises in the non-common part **≤ 20m²** and ≤ 5% of the total area of the non-common part |

If any gate fails, item 1.45 on this card does not apply.
