Some considerations for construction or alteration of windows or window walls

- Do not construct or alter windows or window walls to enclose green balconies and utility platforms which were granted with exemption from gross floor area calculation.
- Construction or alteration of windows or window walls shall comply with the statutory requirements of natural lighting, ventilation and fire resisting construction.
- Other considerations are given in section 3.1 of the "Technical Guidelines on MWCS".
- Please note that every opening formed on an external wall above the ground floor of any building shall be protected by a barrier which shall be **not less than 1.1m** high (hereafter referred to as "protective barrier").

If the window or window wall works involve the removal or alteration of the original approved window/window wall/protective railing/parapet walls of balconies and verandahs or that part of the external wall with function as protective barrier, the minor works items 1.6, 1.15, 2.13, 2.14 and 3.11 may be involved.

If the window or window wall works involve repair or replacement of protective barrier (other than an external reinforced concrete wall or block wall) in accordance with the original design (i.e. the design as shown on plans approved under the Buildings Ordinance), Class II minor works item².5 may be involved.

**SD takeaway:** Construction or alteration of windows or window walls shall comply with the statutory requirements of natural lighting, ventilation and fire resisting construction.
