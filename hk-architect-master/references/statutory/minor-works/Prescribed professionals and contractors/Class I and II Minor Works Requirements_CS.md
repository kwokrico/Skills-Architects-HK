## Regulatory Overview

Class I minor works are of higher degree of complexity and risk, whereas Class II minor works demand for medium degree of complexity and risk.

### 2. Before commencement of works

**At least 7 days** before the commencement of works, please submit the following documents:

- Notification of commencement of works (Specified Form MW01 or MW03 )
- Prescribed plans and details of the works
- Photographs
- Other documents to the Buildings Department
- View sample form of MW01
- View sample form of MW03

### 3. After completion of works

**Within 14 days** after completion of works, please submit the following documents:

- Certification of completion of works (Specified Form MW02 or MW04 )
- Record plans
- Photographs
- Other documents to the Buildings Department
- View sample form of MW02
- View sample form of MW04
