# LAO Practice Note No. APSRSE 2/96 — Processing of Master Layout Plan Submissions under Leases — Amendment Submissions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
September 1996 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Director of Lands (Atg.) (C.M. Mo)

> Scope note: Streamlines **when** a revised MLP must be lodged after building-plan amendments. It does **not** change the MLP information list or the 12-week clock in APSRSE 2/94.

---

## Regulatory Overview

APSRSE 2/94 of March 1994 already sets the MLP information pack and DLO reply time. This note adds that **minor** GBP-driven changes need no immediate MLP resubmission; a **substantial** departure from the approved MLP still requires a revised MLP **before** building plans are processed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Minor vs substantial MLP amendments (paras 2–3)

| Situation | MLP action |
|---|---|
| Amendments **minor** in the DLO’s view (e.g. slight variations in building layout and amenity features; variations that **do not affect plot ratio and site coverage**) | **No immediate** amendment submission to the approved MLP |
| Building plans **finalised and approved** after a run of minor changes | Submit **one** revised MLP incorporating **all** approved GBP changes to the DLO for approval |
| Building plans **differ substantially** from the approved MLP | Submit a revised MLP showing **all** changes to DLO **before** processing of the building plans |

**SD takeaway:** Do not let GBP massing, PR or SC drift off the approved MLP — a substantial departure stops GBP processing until a revised MLP is in.
