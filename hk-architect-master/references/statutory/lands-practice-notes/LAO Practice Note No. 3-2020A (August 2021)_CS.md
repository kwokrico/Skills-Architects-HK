# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2020A — DDH clause (site coverage) and underground structures
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
August 2021 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Varies para 7(a)(i) of LAO PN No. 3/2020 | Aligns with **JPN No. 7** from **23 August 2021**

> Scope note: Streamlines **site coverage (SC)** (and de-facto **roofed-over area (ROA)**) accountability of **underground** structures under the lease DDH/SC clause. It is **not** a BO GFA code and **not** a grant of extra intensity. **Does not apply** to Cap. 121-exempted NT buildings.

---

## Regulatory Overview

JPN 7’s streamlined SC arrangements among BD, LandsD and PlanD take effect from 23 August 2021. This PN varies PN 3/2020 para 7(a)(i) only insofar as underground structures in SC calculation are concerned; other lease parameters stay unchanged.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Leases with max GFA and/or plot ratio **plus** SC (paras 2–3)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Objective | Flexibility in **underground space** within the lot — **not** an increase in intensity beyond existing lease |
| LandsD SC accountability | Generally adopt **BD’s** treatment of facilities/features for SC, **unless** explicit lease conditions prohibit |

**SD takeaway:** On GFA/PR + SC leases, design underground plant/parking to BD SC practice unless the lease text itself blocks that treatment.

---

### 2. Leases with SC (and height) but **no** max GFA **nor** max PR (para 4)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Default | SC + height have been the intensity control; underground buildings/structures **not** already exempted from SC |
| Flexibility | Director of Lands **may**, unless the statutory town plan says otherwise, **disregard underground structures** when checking SC for development, redevelopment or A&A |
| Condition | Owner must show to the Director’s satisfaction that intensity would **not exceed** what is achievable under **existing** lease conditions |
| If flexibility would **increase** intensity | **Lease modification** is required |
| “Underground structures” | Director’s decision is **final and binding** |
| ROA leases (fn 1) | Same approach as SC |

**SD takeaway:** On SC/height-only leases, underground bulk is not a free SC gift — prove no extra intensity, or budget a modification.

---

### 3. Carve-outs and companions (paras 5–6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Cap. 121 NTEH | PN **not applicable** |
| Flexibility into the SC clause | See **LAO PN No. 1/2004** |

**SD takeaway:** Do not apply this underground-SC stream to Cap. 121 houses; for rewriting the SC clause itself use PN 1/2004.
