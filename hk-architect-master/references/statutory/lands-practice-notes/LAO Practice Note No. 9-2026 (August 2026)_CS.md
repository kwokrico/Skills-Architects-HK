# LAO Practice Note No. 9/2026 — Standard rates for the bonus plot ratio pilot
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Issue No. 9/2026, signed 19 August 2026; rates from 1 September 2026 | Lands Department, Lands Administration Office

> Scope note: Sets the standard rates for the land-premium value of the bonus plot ratio on an eligible redevelopment site under Joint Practice Note No. 9. It does not itself decide who is eligible or how much bonus plot ratio is granted. Those sit in Joint Practice Note No. 9.

## Regulatory Overview
From 1 September 2026 the land-premium value of bonus plot ratio on an eligible redevelopment site is calculated at a standard rate for the designated area in which the site sits. The rates are per gross floor area (GFA) and are subject to annual review from the effective date of Joint Practice Note No. 9 and this note.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The seven designated areas (para 2 and footnote 1)

The areas are those in the Schedule to the Land (Compulsory Sale for Redevelopment) (Specification of Lower Percentage) Notice (Cap. 545A), read as the areas of these outline zoning plans.

| No. | Designated area | Standard rate |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Sai Ying Pun & Sheung Wan | $84,000/m² |
| 2 | Wan Chai | $84,000/m² |
| 3 | Ma Tau Kok (Kowloon City and To Kwa Wan) | $66,000/m² |
| 4 | Cheung Sha Wan (Sham Shui Po) | $63,000/m² |
| 5 | Mong Kok | $70,000/m² |
| 6 | Yau Ma Tei | $70,000/m² |
| 7 | Tsuen Wan | $46,000/m² |

**SD takeaway:** Price the bonus GFA at the area rate above from 1 September 2026, and expect the rate to be reviewed annually rather than treated as a fixed project allowance.
