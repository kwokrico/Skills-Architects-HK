# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2016 — Consent to disposal under entire-IB conversion special waiver
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
1 June 2016 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 2/2016

> Scope note: Streamlines **Director’s prior written consent to sell/assign/dispose** required throughout the validity of a **special waiver for conversion of an entire existing industrial building** issued under **LAO PN 1/2010, 1/2010A and 1/2010B**. It is **not** a design/GFA PN and does not itself grant conversion rights.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The special waiver requires the Assignor, before disposal, to obtain the Director’s prior written consent and to procure a sealed **undertaking** from the Assignee (in favour of Government and the Director) to observe the waiver, and if the lot is in multiple ownership to be jointly and/or severally liable with other owners. This PN deems that consent given if the specified application and undertaking path is followed, and drops Assignee chargee/mortgagee endorsement of the undertaking.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Deemed consent and undertaking (paras 1–4, Appendices I–II)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Deemed consent | Director’s consent to sell/assign/dispose is **deemed given** if, **before** sale, conveyance or any agreement so to do, Assignor files Appendix I application **and** complies with special-waiver conditions on procuring and delivering the Appendix II undertaking |
| Appendix I trigger | On LandsD receipt of the form, consent required under **Condition 10(a)(i)** of the waiver letter is deemed given **provided** Assignor complies with **Conditions 10(a)(ii) and 10(b)** |
| Assignee chargee | Undertaking **no longer** needs endorsement by Assignee’s chargee/mortgagee |
| In-hand letters | Waiver letters **not yet issued** will reflect paras 2–3 |
| Already granted waivers | LandsD issues a **Relaxation Letter** (no fees/charges) before or upon any consent-to-disposal application |
| Chain of title | Assignee may only further dispose on (i) Director’s consent and (ii) procuring a **similar** undertaking from the next purchaser; undertaking registered **contemporaneously** with the assignment |
| Incomplete conversion works | Undertaking may include completing all waiver-permitted works by the stipulated date (and any extension letter date) |

**SD takeaway:** Conversion special-waiver units remain **covenant-bound on every assignment** — programme the AP/legal pack so the Appendix II undertaking (and any outstanding conversion-works date) is ready **before** ASP, not after.
