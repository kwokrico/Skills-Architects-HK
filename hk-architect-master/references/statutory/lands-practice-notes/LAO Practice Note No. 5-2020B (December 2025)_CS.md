# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2020B — Payment of Professional Fees in Compensation Claims
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
15 December 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Supplements **LAO PN 5/2020**; **supersedes** **LAO PN 5/2020A**

> Scope note: **Claim-form and guideline update** for reimbursement of professional fees reasonably incurred in **statutory compensation** claims. It is **not** a lease GFA/SC/BH instrument and does not set design parameters.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN issues a new **Application Form for Claim of Professional Fees** (Appendix I) and **guidelines** (Appendix II) for claimants and the firms acting for them. It does not change how compensation itself is assessed; it only structures the fee-reimbursement claim.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the claim must cover (App I)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Submission | Mail or in person to the **DLO or LandsD section**; Government not bound to accept |
| Settlement | Claim is **full and final** for professional costs under the stated Ordinance(s) for that land-interest claim — **no further fee claim** |
| Claim types (tick) | Resumption; easement & other permanent rights; temporary occupation; business disturbance; severance (marine access or resumption); removal of graves/urns; other |
| Related projects | Disclose other compensation claims in the **same and/or related project(s)** of one development (e.g. Cap. **124** resumption plus Cap. **370** roads and/or Cap. **127** reclamation for one public-housing scheme) |
| Mandatory attachments (#) | Claimant **authorisation**; disbursement breakdown **with receipts**; valuation report(s) if not already provided; **separate Claim Form (Annex) per professional and technician/paralegal** |
| Without # docs | LandsD **may not** process, validate, or quantify the claim |

**SD takeaway:** If the office is claiming fees on a resumption/roads/reclamation package, file **one consolidated** professional-fee claim and flag **related projects** — missing authorisation, receipts, or per-person timesheets can stall payment.

---

### 2. Time recording and reasonableness (Annex + App II)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Non-solicitor professionals | Work codes 1–8: inspection; research/analysis; assessment reports; team coordination; paper/letters; Government meetings; claimant/team meetings; others (e.g. Lands Tribunal) |
| Solicitors | Codes 1–6: rights/procedure advice; Government letters; correspondence (other than title requisitions); assessment/monitoring; meetings; **title requisitions** and document perusal |
| Proof of time | Meetings need minutes/notes/summaries/correspondence with attendees, **start/end time**, location, agenda; endorse by signature or email where practicable |
| Multi-disciplinary team | **One consolidated** fee claim for all professionals; optional **team coordinator** (mark “(Coordinator)”) |
| Reasonableness | LandsD weighs nature, type, extent, complexity, and **qualifications / post-qualification experience** |

**SD takeaway:** Keep dated, attendee-endorsed meeting records if an AP/RLA/surveyor is on the compensation team — LandsD will not take hours on trust.
