# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2009 — Temporary Occupation of Government Land for Building Maintenance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 June 2009 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department

> Scope note: **Short-term tenancy (STT)** of adjoining vacant Government land to support **building maintenance** (scaffolding / materials storage). It is **not** a permanent land grant, not a BO hoarding permit, and not for sites needed for Government development or other temporary use.

## Regulatory Overview

Where vacant Government land next to the building is not required for permanent development or another temporary use, LandsD may grant an STT to facilitate owner-led maintenance (including BD repair-order works). Typical caps: **≤ 6 months** and **≤ 180 m²**. Apply to the DLO **≥ 1 calendar month** in advance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who applies and what to lodge (paras. 1–2)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Applicant | **Owners’ corporation** if formed; else owners’ committee or estate manager |
| Timing | To DLO **at least one calendar month** before need |
| Support | (a) evidence maintenance contract awarded **or** about to be tendered; (b) estimated works start and date land is needed; (c) duration of occupation; (d) OC resolution **or** owners’ meeting authority to apply and execute the STT |

**SD takeaway:** For maintenance next to a Government strip, programme the STT **a month before** scaffolding — OC/owners’ resolution is a hold point.

### 2. Terms if offered (paras. 3–4)

| Term | Typical |
|---|---|
| Duration | As DLO considers reasonable for the works (body: normally **≤ 6 months**) |
| Area | Normally **≤ 180 m²** |
| Money | Admin fee + deposit **not less than $20,000**; may be **nominal rent $1** if demanded |
| Insurance | Applicant must take out **third-party insurance** covering the occupation |

**SD takeaway:** Treat the strip as a **time-boxed STT** (area, deposit, insurance) — not a site extension for design bulk.
