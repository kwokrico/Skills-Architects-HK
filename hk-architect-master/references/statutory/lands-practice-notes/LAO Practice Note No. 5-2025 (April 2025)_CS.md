# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2025 — Standard Rates for Industrial Building Redevelopment
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 April 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 12/2023**; **supersedes** **LAO PN 12/2023A**  
Rates from **1 April 2025**

> Scope note: **Rate and Option Form update** plus a restated **“B” (after-use rate)** rule for standard-rate premium on lease modification / in-situ exchange for **redevelopment of industrial buildings** on industrial lots. Parent processing in **PN 12/2023** otherwise remains valid. Five regions as delineated in **Appendix II to PN 12/2023**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Paragraph 5(ii) of PN 12/2023 (factor **B**) is rewritten so that **residential** rates apply to applications that do **not prohibit private residential**, except on **CDA** or **OU (commercial-cum-residential)** OZP/planning-approval sites, where residential rate applies only to GFA not prohibiting private residential and **commercial** (or **modern industrial**, if so specified in the modification) applies to the remainder. **Hotel and hotel-ancillary GFA = commercial**; standard rates from **1 April 2025** are tabulated below.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. After-use rate “B” (varied para 5(ii) of PN 12/2023)

| Site / GFA | Rate to use |
|---|---|
| Application **not prohibiting private residential** (general case) | **Residential** rate |
| OZP and/or planning approval is **CDA** or **OU for commercial-cum-residential** | **Residential** rate on GFA **not prohibiting private residential**; **commercial** rate on remainder, **except** GFA specified as **modern industrial** in the modification → **modern industrial** rate |
| Hotel + hotel ancillary GFA | **Commercial** |

LandsD’s decision on use type and which rate attaches to any GFA is **final**.

**SD takeaway:** On a **CDA / OU C/R** IB redevelopment, split GFA into private-residential vs commercial vs modern-industrial in the modification documents — hotel counts as **commercial**, not residential.

### 3. Option Form (replaces App I to PN 12/2023)

| Requirement | Lock |
|---|---|
| Pre-1987 IB proof | **OP(s)** for the pre-1987 IB(s); **or** BA approval letter(s) showing building plans were **first submitted on or before 1 March 1987** |
| GFA of existing building (#) | **AP or RPS** certificate of GFA of the pre-1987 IB existing on the lot(s) or existing immediately before vacancy, per **latest BA-approved plans**, **excluding** parking, L/UL, plant rooms and similar **not normally GFA-accountable** under the BO, and any GFA on those plans that **does not comply with lease**; attach the approved plans |
| Alternative “C” | Applicant may skip the Certificate and adopt GFA as if the site were redeveloped for **industrial and/or godown** (the case-by-case basis in PN 12/2023 para 5(iii)); that choice is **irreversible**; LandsD’s figure is **final** |
| If opt accepted | **No BBTO premium appeal**; fees **forfeited** if the applicant then drops; completed standard-rate deals **published** on LandsD’s website |

**SD takeaway:** Either certify **existing IB GFA** (net of typical non-accountable areas and lease-breaching GFA) or **irreversibly** take the industrial-redevelopment “C” — have the OP / 1 March 1987 first-submission proof in the option pack.
