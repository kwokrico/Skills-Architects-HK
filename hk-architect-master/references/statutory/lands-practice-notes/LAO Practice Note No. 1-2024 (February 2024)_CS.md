# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2024 — HSK/HT NDA land exchange under ECNTA
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
February 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Read with **LAO PN No. 13/2023** (Enhanced Conventional New Town Approach)

> Scope note: Lease modification / in-situ land exchange for **designated development sites** in Hung Shui Kiu/Ha Tsuen NDA. It is **not** an OZP; **PN 12/2023** IB standard rates **do not apply**. Standard-rate premium for this NDA is “to be announced”.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Sites now open are those coloured yellow hatched red or green hatched red on the LandsD Reference Plan (Second Phase and Remaining Phase private residential, mixed R/C, commercial, or OU “Port Back-up, Storage and Workshop Uses”). Follow approved OZP **S/HSK/2** (26 October 2018 or later amendments) and the adopted HSK/HT ODP. Second Phase applications have priority; Remaining Phase is lower priority pending infrastructure/access.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is (and is not) open (para 3 and fn 1)

| Open | Closed (this stage) |
|---|---|
| Designated sites on the Reference Plan (illustration only; Government may amalgamate adjoining designated sites) | Sites with adjoining/intervening GL capable of **reasonable separate alienation**; Remaining Phase sites **under planning review**; sites affected by Government works; **R+P** railway sites; other policy exclusions |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm the lot sits inside a hatched designated site on the **current** LandsD Reference Plan (updated again by PN 1/2024A) before drawing a re-grant boundary.

---

### 2. Urban design and public facilities (paras 5–6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Urban / green design | CEDD **Urban and Green Design Requirement Brief** and PlanD **Urban Design Guidelines** for Future Town Centre & District Commercial Node (hskhtnda.hk); may be written into the land grant |
| Public facilities | May be required (PTI, open space, welfare/recreation, etc.). **Relaxed 90% ownership** applicants **shall** construct public facilities; others **may** be invited. Specs and possible **premium-deductible** cost set per site |
| Planning Area **32A** (ODP) | Potential **strategic Transport Interchange Hub** facilities |

**SD takeaway:** Budget podium/site for Government-specified public facilities (and Area 32A hub) as a lease condition — they can be premium-deductible but will lock layout.

---

### 3. OU Port Back-up / Storage / Workshop sites marked “#” (para 7)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| On completion | Hand over **no less than 30% of floor space** as **Government Accommodation** for displaced brownfield operators |
| Remainder | ~70% private; GA uses “generally compatible” but **not necessarily** the same as the 70% |
| Letting | Government takes over and leases to displaced operators on Government terms |

**SD takeaway:** On hashed OU workshop sites, freeze **≥ 30% GFA as GA** with independent access/services — GPA-style GA package, not a normal industrial floor.

### 5. Specific criteria — Appendix III (must-submit design facts)

| Criterion | Lock |
|---|---|
| Confinement | Surrender **only** lots inside the designated site; outside lots not normally accepted |
| Commercial / OU C&R / OU Port Back-up sites | Surrender **all** private land in the designated site (minor boundary tweaks that do not hurt layout OK). Planning intention = **one development per designated site**. **90%+** ownership → may use PN 13/2023 relaxed criterion. Re-grant to **conform to designated site** (GL not promised) |
| **Residential** sites | Surrender ≥ **4 000 m²**. 90% + 4 000 m² → relaxed criterion available. Site **contiguous**, reasonably regular, no intervening lots except residual lots under the relaxed criterion. If using **4 000 m² only** without full-site assembly: **layout plans** proving decent full-site utilisation |
| Access | Proper **vehicular access** feasible |
| OZP | Use and parameters **comply** |
| No adverse implications | Must not block adjacent lots’ potential or access |

**SD takeaway:** Commercial/OU designated sites are **all-or-90%** assemblies; residential can be 4 000 m² but then prove the leftover of the designated site still works.

---

### 6. Submission pack (App I) — design-facing

Must: application letter with proposed user, **max GFA, SC, storeys/BH**; 1:1000 site plan; TPB approval if any; ownership printout.

Facilitate: OZP extract; assignment/Deed Poll plans for portions; **sketch plans**; approved MLP / EIA / TIA / DIA; occupant schedules (existing **and** occupants at **13 April 2017** or after who later left); development programme; ALS plan-area/boundary survey to prove the **90%** private-land threshold.

**SD takeaway:** Put GFA/SC/BH, 90% survey, 13 April 2017 occupant trail, and (for partial residential sites) full-site layouts in the first pack.
