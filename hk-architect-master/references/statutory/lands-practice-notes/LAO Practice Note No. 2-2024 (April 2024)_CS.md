# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2024 — RBS waiver fees in existing buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
April 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Effective **1 April 2024** | Varies Appendix III of LAO PN No. 5/2019

> Scope note: Updates **standard annual waiver fees** for radio base stations for mobile services in **existing buildings**. It is **not** a new waiver procedure and does **not** change PN 5/2019 eligibility or antenna/area design rules.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Except for the Appendix III rates varied here, all other information and provisions in LAO PN No. 5/2019 remain valid. Rates are subject to Government review at absolute discretion.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Standard annual waiver fee from 1 April 2024 (para 2)

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Station area **not exceeding 5 m²** with **five** antennae | **$83,400** p.a. |
| Each **additional** antenna | **$16,020** p.a. |
| Additional area | **$1,410 / m²** p.a. |

**SD takeaway:** Keep rooftop/plant RBS within **≤ 5 m² and five antennae** if the base fee is the design target; extra antennae and extra area are charged at the table rates on top of PN 5/2019’s existing-building waiver path.
