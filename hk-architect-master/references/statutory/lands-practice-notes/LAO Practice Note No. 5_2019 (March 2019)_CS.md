# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2019 — Waivers for radio base stations for mobile services
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 March 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 5/2019 | From **1 April 2019** | Target issue **≤ 2 months** from valid receipt | Together with **PN 4/2019**, supersedes **PN 9/2002, 3/2013 and 1/2015** from 1 April 2019

> Scope note: Temporary waiver for **radio base stations and associated equipment/facilities** in **existing buildings** where installation would breach lease **user** (installations treated as **commercial**). Waiver may also cover consequential breaches such as **GFA and height**. **Inside industrial buildings**, fees follow **PN 4/2019 Appendix IV** (IT & telecom rates), not Appendix III of this PN. Not a CA licence and not planning permission.

---

## Regulatory Overview

CA-licensed mobile network operators may need to install RBS in existing buildings. On lots held for **private residential** or other **non-commercial** purposes, RBS is a user breach. Applications go to the **relevant DLO**. Admin fee on demand is non-refundable. Waiver is temporary user variation only; TPB, FSD and BA remain applicable.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When a waiver is needed and what it covers (paras 2–5)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | RBS + associated equipment/facilities in buildings on **private residential** or other **non-commercial** leases (commercial nature) |
| Scope of waiver | User **and**, as the case may be, other consequential breaches (**GFA, height**, etc.) |
| IB interior | Fee = **PN 4/2019 Appendix IV** standard rates (subject to review) |
| Other existing buildings | Fee = **this PN Appendix III** |
| Term in sample form | Initially a stated number of **years** |
| Drawings required | Size/dimensions of equipment room/shelter; area covered by equipment/installations (whether housed or not); area of **base** supporting them; **number of antennae**. Premises include **rooftops and external walls** |

**SD takeaway:** Treat rooftop/external-wall RBS as a **lease GFA/height** issue as well as user — survey antenna count and equipment-base area for the waiver drawings, not only the equipment room.

---

### 2. Standard annual fees from 1 April 2019 (Appendix III) — non-IB

| Component | Fee |
|---|---|
| Station area **not exceeding 5 m²** with **5 antennae** | **$79,800 p.a.** |
| Each **additional antenna** | **$15,300 p.a.** |
| **Additional area** | **$1,350 / m² p.a.** |

Rates subject to review. IB interiors use PN 4/2019 App IV instead.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the station **≤ 5 m² and ≤ 5 antennae** for the base rate; extra dishes and extra area are priced **per antenna / per m²**.
