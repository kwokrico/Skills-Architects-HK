# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2008 — Measures to Avoid Encroachment onto Government Land
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
9 June 2008 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 4/2008 | Ref. LD 7/5010/93 VII

> Scope note: AP / grantee duties to keep the development **inside the lot** so Certificate of Compliance is not delayed. Architectural or amenity **projections on or over Government land** may still be allowed if **BD and LandsD** approve. It is **not** a survey standard.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Developments contiguous with the lot boundary risk unintentional encroachment onto adjoining Government land. Government expects grantees, through the AP, to prevent encroachment throughout the development period, or to rectify it at the earliest, or C of C applications will be delayed.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. GBP, setting-out, and C of C confirmations (paras 3–6)

| Stage | Hard rule |
|---|---|
| **GBP** (BD and LandsD) | AP must ensure the proposed development will not encroach onto Government land. Provide a **block plan** plus a **table of lot-boundary co-ordinates** in line with the co-ordinates on the **sale/grant plan** provided or agreed by the **District Survey Office** |
| **Tender / setting-out** | Tender documents should require the contractor’s setting-out to be checked by an **independent competent person** **before** construction **in close proximity to the lot boundary**. AP then confirms to DLO that, having examined that report, the AP believes the works will **not** encroach (sample Appendix I) |
| **C of C** | AP submits a further confirmation that the development is built **in accordance with approved building plans** and **does not encroach**. AP may jointly sign with the contractor and the independent competent person (sample Appendix II) |
| If encroachment is found | Director may require **reinstatement**, including **demolition**. On application, Director **may** (discretionary — may elect **not** to) grant the encroached land as an **extension** under the General Condition, including **additional premium** |
| Footnote (1) | AP or employer to consider appointing qualified professionals including a **land surveyor** as needed |
| Footnote (2) | Subject to BD and LandsD approval, installation or projection such as **architectural or amenity features** may be allowed **on or over** Government land |

**SD takeaway:** Pull DSO sale/grant-plan **co-ordinates onto the GBP block plan** at SD, keep structure off the lot line unless BD/LandsD have approved a projection, and budget independent setting-out checks — encroachment can stop C of C or force demolition / a discretionary paid extension.
