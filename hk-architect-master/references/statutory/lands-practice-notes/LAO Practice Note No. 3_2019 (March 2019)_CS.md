# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2019 — Waivers for buffer floor and lower floors of an existing IB
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
21 March 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 3/2019 | Submit to **IBRU**, Units 07–11, 27/F CDW Building, Tsuen Wan

> Scope note: Waiver for **temporary** change of use of a **buffer floor** (existing or to be converted) to **telecommunications exchange centre** and **computer or data processing centre**, together with **all floors immediately below** (max **three** lower floors) **en bloc** for **non-industrial** use — to meet **FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2019**. It is **not** a lifetime waiver under this PN. Not a BO GFA bonus route. Director of Fire Services decides what constitutes the buffer floor and lower three floors.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Buffer floors may be required to separate non-industrial uses (which themselves need waiver/modification) on lower floors from industrial uses above. Prevailing FSD practice allowed buffer floors for parking, L/UL, E&M plant, or vacant refuge. This PN widens buffer-floor user. Application only if OZP permits (or TPB permission exists), FSD CL 2/2019 is met, and the Premises are under **single ownership** (same registered owner for buffer **and** the lower floors in the application).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligible uses and package (paras 1–3, fn 3–6)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Buffer-floor user | **Telecommunications exchange centre** and **computer or data processing centre** only (Director of Lands’ determination final) |
| Lower floors | Waiver for buffer **must include** conversion of **all** floors immediately below, **subject to a maximum of three lower floors**, for **non-industrial** uses (FSD qualifying requirements; D of FS **final**) |
| Ownership | Same person/entity owns buffer **and** those lower floors |
| Not included on buffer | **No** customer services or sale of goods (whether public attends or not); **no** general commercial/office merely because they use computers/telecom equipment |
| Telecom exchange | Place where exchange equipment is installed for **public** telecom (fixed/mobile telephone and data). LandsD determination final |
| Computer / data processing centre | Computing/data processing with a **functional relationship with business activities**; housing computers/servers/telecom + support E&M in a secure controlled environment. LandsD determination final |

**SD takeaway:** Convert **buffer + up to three floors below as one ownership package**; put **data/telecom plant on the buffer** — not a shop, counter, or generic office.

---

### 2. GFA/PR, term, fees, and fire (paras 4–7)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Not lifetime | Waiver **will not** be granted for the lifetime of the existing building **under this PN** |
| Personal | **Personal to the owner**; **deemed terminated** on change of ownership of the Premises or any part |
| User during waiver | No part of Premises for uses other than those specified |
| Plot ratio | After conversion, IB PR must **not exceed** max intensity under the **BO** |
| GFA / SC | Total GFA or SC of the Premises **after** conversion (BO or lease, as the case may be) **shall not exceed** total GFA or SC of the Premises **before** conversion on the **latest BA- and LandsD-approved** plans. **No additional or bonus GFA** from BO (or otherwise) due to conversion |
| Works / FSD | Conversion and Premises must comply with **FSD Circular Letter No. 2/2019** (or amending FSD documents) |
| Fees | Admin fee on demand (**non-refundable**); waiver fee **and deposit** as imposed. Telecom-exchange fee: **PN 5/2001 & 5/2001A** as varied by **PN 1/2015**, **except** standard lump-sum lifetime rates. Computer/data centre fee: **PN 3/2012** (or amending PNs). Other uses: established practice |
| Other licences | FSD and BA still required; waiver is **not** a feasibility guarantee |

**SD takeaway:** Keep converted GFA/SC **≤ pre-conversion approved totals** with **zero bonus GFA**, and design to **FSD CL 2/2019** — this waiver is **temporary and personal**, not a building-life conversion.

---

### 3. Application pack highlights (Appendix I checklist)

| Must | Detail |
|---|---|
| Latest approved plans | 2 copies + schedule of **total GFA and total IFA** of the Premises (AP-certified) |
| OPs | 2 copies, AP-certified |
| Floor plans | **4 copies**, ≤ A3, Premises edged **pink**, IFA with use breakdown |
| Data-centre concept | If computer/data processing on buffer — conceptual design per fn 4; **energy saving plan** (OGCIO Green Data Centre Practices) |
| Works proposal | Certify any change in GFA, SC, storeys, height; **FSD CL 2/2019** compliance; no other lease non-compliance |
| Existing waiver | Replaced on execution; **no** fee refund |

**SD takeaway:** Issue pink-edged IFA plans and an FSD CL 2/2019 / GFA-no-increase certificate with the waiver — bonus PR from conversion is off the table.
