# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2023A — RCHE Incentive Enhancements Continued
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 June 2026 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Effective **20 June 2026** | Varies/supplements **LAO PN 5/2023**

> Scope note: **Continues** the PN 5/2023 enhancement measures after the three-year pilot ended **19 June 2026**. It does **not** change the design/GFA/cap rules in PN 5/2023. **LAO PN 4/2003** remains **not applicable** while these enhancements continue.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The 2003 Incentive Scheme (PN 4/2003) was enhanced for a pilot from **20 June 2023 to 19 June 2026** under PN 5/2023. Policy approval has been given for those enhancements to **continue**; from **20 June 2026**, LandsD applications under the Incentive Scheme are processed under **PN 5/2023 until further notice**, with existing arrangements and requirements **unchanged**.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What this PN changes (paras 1–6)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Effective | **20 June 2026** |
| Parent | **PN 5/2023** — all enhancement arrangements/requirements **unchanged** |
| Pilot end | **19 June 2026** (does **not** lapse the enhancements) |

**SD takeaway:** After 19 June 2026, still design to **PN 5/2023** (premium-free RCHE GFA cap, SWD location, whole-unit alienation) — do **not** fall back to PN 4/2003 unless the Government later says so.
