# LAO Practice Note No. 2/2023 — Processing lease modifications, exchanges, PTGs and other land transactions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Supersedes LAO PN Nos. **3/1999** (APSS 2/99), **9/2000**, **4/2001**, **1/2006**, **5/2006**, **9/2006**, **4/2007**, **5/2007**, **3/2008**, **3/2009** and **4/2009**

> Scope note: Territory-wide **procedure** for lease modification, land exchange, private treaty grant and other land transactions. **Does not cover** Small House Policy or **temporary** transactions. NDA cases (e.g. PN 1/2022) may use modified procedures.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Applications are accepted only from the registered owner, a prospective purchaser under a **registered ASP** with the owner’s written consent, or their authorised representative. For exchanges, the applicant (including wholly-owned subsidiaries) must already own **all** lots to be surrendered or be such a prospective purchaser. LandsD acts as landlord and may approve or reject on the merits.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who applies and first pack (para 2(i), App II)

| Must submit | Facilitate |
|---|---|
| Letter identifying property and sought variations (**user, max GFA, SC, storeys/BH**, etc.) | OZP extract + notes |
| 1:1000 location/site plan | Assignment / Deed Poll plans for portions |
| TPB approval letter if applicable | Sketch plans if available |
| Ownership printout certified by LR or solicitor, **≤ 1 month** old (hard or soft copy) | Approved MLP / EIA / TIA / DIA etc. |
| Agent authorisation / purchaser’s owner consent | |

**SD takeaway:** The opening letter must already state the **GFA / SC / BH / user** ask — that is what DLC will see.

---

### 2. Fees, title, DLC (para 2(ii)–(iv))

| Stage | Lock |
|---|---|
| Valid for processing | Demand notes for **initial administrative fee** and, if surrender is involved, **initial LACO fee**; unpaid by the stated date → **deemed withdrawn** |
| Exchange title | Submit title deeds; rectify defects, clear encumbrances (e.g. outstanding premium instalments), prove good title within the specified period (App IV) |
| DLC | Applicant may attend (confirm **≥ 2 working days** ahead) or write in; papers confidential and **not binding** |

**SD takeaway:** Budget initial fees immediately after validation; for exchanges, title unification is on the same critical path as design.

---

### 3. PBTO → premium → BBTO → execution (para 2(v)–(x), App I time targets)

| Step | Clock / money |
|---|---|
| Preliminary reply (pledge) | **3 weeks** — can the application be processed? |
| Reject / PBTO / in-principle (pledge) | **22 weeks** from preliminary reply |
| Pay initial fees / accept PBTO | Generally **2 weeks** (date in the letter) |
| PBTO missed | Deemed withdrawal; fees **forfeited** |
| Nil or nominal premium (e.g. $1,000) | Usually a **single** offer; two-stage optional (para 3) |
| BBTO accept | Generally **4 weeks**: execute Acceptance Letter; pay **balance** admin + LACO fees; pay **10% premium deposit**; mortgagee no-objection letter |
| Contract Date | Date LandsD **receives** acceptance |
| Execute documents | On or before a date **within 12 weeks of Contract Date**; pay balance premium + Land Registry fees. Failure = breach; fees **and deposit forfeited** |
| Appeal instead of accept BBTO | Signed Appeal Letter (basic terms other than premium accepted) + settle fee balances by BBTO date (App V) |
| Same-amount revised premium then refused | Deemed withdrawal; fees forfeited |
| Due date on Sat/PH or Black Rain / T8+ / “extreme conditions” | Next working day (para 4) |

**SD takeaway:** From PBTO acceptance you are on a **premium + 12-week execution** rail — freeze design before PBTO; 10% deposit is due at BBTO, not at execution.

---

### 4. Exchange title after Contract Date (App IV)

Within **8 weeks of Contract Date** (as applicable): discharge mortgages (draft release to LACO); **unify title** and register; rectify remaining title defects; complete ASP assignment and register.

**SD takeaway:** Multi-owner or mortgaged lots must be cleared inside 8 weeks of BBTO acceptance or the 12-week execution date is at risk.

---

### 5. Premium assessment and appeal (App V) — design-facing bits only

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| After PBTO accept | Applicant may send valuation-useful info within **2 weeks**; **no negotiation** on the first assessment |
| Appeal | Returning Appeal Letter **lapses** the offered premium; re-assessment is at **then** market (can go up or down) |
| Counter-offer | With supporting info within **3 months** of Appeal Letter |
| Fast track (2nd and 3rd appeals only) | Appeal Letter + justified counter-offer within **12 working days** of revised BBTO, copied to Valuation Section; if qualified, revised BBTO within **24 working days**. No justifications / recycled justifications / policy-legal follow-up / **basic-term amendments** → not qualified. **4th+ appeals**: fast track **unavailable** |

**SD takeaway:** Changing GFA/user after BBTO is a **basic-terms** change and kills fast-track premium appeal — lock the scheme before appealing numbers.
