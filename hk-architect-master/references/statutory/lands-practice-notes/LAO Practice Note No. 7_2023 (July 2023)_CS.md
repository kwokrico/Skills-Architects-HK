# LAO Practice Note No. 7/2023 — Access to Computerized "A" Rent Rolls Record
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
10 July 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Effective 10 July 2023 | Supersedes LAO PN Nos. 6/2006 and 6/2006A

> Scope note: Computerises New Territories **"A" Rent Rolls** ("A Book") at NT District Lands Offices and sets who may inspect them. It is **not** a BO/OZP development-control PN. Use it at SD only when you need NT lot identity, site area, user/class, or Government rent from the A Book — then verify at the Land Registry.

---

## Regulatory Overview

NTDLOs (Islands, North, Sai Kung, Sha Tin, Tai Po, Tsuen Wan and Kwai Tsing, Tuen Mun, Yuen Long) maintain the A Book for Government rent collection; all A Books are now electronic. From 10 July 2023, inspection of the original paper A Book ceases; electronic access is limited by the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who may inspect / what you get (paras 2–3)

| Who | Inspect electronic A Book | Printed report |
|---|---|---|
| Property owner or authorised person | **Free** — only properties under that owner's name, at the relevant NTDLO | Chargeable |
| Members of the public | **Free** — only lots **solely** owned by a deceased owner, a Tso/Tong, or a company, **or jointly owned by any of such parties** | Chargeable |

| Printed-report fields | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lot number; New Grant / Building Licence number | Identity |
| Site area; annual Government rent; class/user of the lot | SD lot facts |
| Land Registry memorial information | If available |
| Rent concession status | **Owner / authorised person only** |

Charge at issue: **$325 per property** (subject to revision). LandsD makes **no warranty** of accuracy; verify by Land Registry search / legal advice. A Book is internal LandsD use, not a public record.

**SD takeaway:** For an NT lot, treat A-Book site area / class / user as a lead only — freeze lease identity from the Land Grant and Land Registry, not from the A Book.

---

### 2. How to apply (para 4, Annexes A–D)

Submit the relevant form (Annex A, B or C) plus consent form Annex D where appropriate, to the **respective NTDLO**. Original paper A Book inspection by owners/authorised persons **stops** with this electronic service.

**SD takeaway:** Budget a NTDLO application (and $325/lot printout) if SD needs A-Book user/class; do not assume walk-in paper A Book access.
