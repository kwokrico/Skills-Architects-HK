# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2002 — Enquiries from Prospective Purchasers relating to lots offered for sale by public auction or tender
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
22 February 2002 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Ref. LD 2/1020/86 (VII) | Director of Lands (R. D. Pope)

> Scope note: How LandsD handles **enquiries on Conditions of Sale** for lots on public auction/tender, including the **Application List**. It is **not** a design code and LandsD will **not** interpret Conditions of Sale, the Buildings Ordinance, or B(P)Reg for bidders.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The note clarifies Government’s (and LandsD’s) role on Conditions of Sale for lots offered by public auction or tender, including Application List sites. Enquiries go **only** to LandsD and only **questions of a general nature** will be considered — interpretation of Conditions of Sale, BO, B(P)Reg, other legislation, or site-specific matters requires independent AP / legal / other professional advice.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Enquiry channel and sale-document amendments (paras 2–5)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Channel | Address **all** enquiries to LandsD; **avoid direct contact** with other government departments |
| Scope | **General-nature** questions only. Interpretation of Conditions of Sale, BO, B(P)Reg, other legislation, or other **site-specific** matters → independent AP / legal / professional advice |
| Contact (this PN) | Chief Estate Surveyor/Headquarters, Lands Department, Tel. **2231 3802** |
| Fundamental ambiguity | After Application List or Conditions of Sale are published, but **before** auction date / tender close: LandsD will publicise necessary amendments in the **Gazette** and/or selected newspapers, or by circulation to the departmental mailing list — in any event **before** auction / tender close |
| Auction-day amendments | Also distributed in writing to persons registering and obtaining a **Bidding Paddle**; announced and exhibited in writing in the auction room **before** the auction starts |

**SD takeaway:** Do not treat LandsD Q&A as a design ruling on Conditions of Sale — lock SD on the gazetted/tender Conditions (plus any pre-sale Gazette/newspaper/paddle amendments) and independent AP advice.
