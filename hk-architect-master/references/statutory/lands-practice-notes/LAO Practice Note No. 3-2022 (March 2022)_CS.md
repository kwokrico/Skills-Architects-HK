# LAO Practice Note No. 3/2022 — Standard-rate premium for KTN/FLN Remaining Phase exchanges
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 2022 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Option for applications under **LAO PN No. 1/2022**

> Scope note: Optional **standard-rate** land premium (instead of case-by-case) for lease modification / land exchange in the **Remaining Phase** of Kwu Tung North and Fanling North NDAs. It is **not** an intensity code. Rates and BBTO date were later varied by **PN 3/2022A**.

---

## Regulatory Overview

The Option must be exercised **not later than PBTO acceptance** via the Appendix I Option Form and is **irreversible** thereafter (no premium appeal; fees forfeited if the applicant walks; re-application after the PN 1/2022 application deadline of **15 August 2022** will not be considered). This extract’s rates are valid through the then PN 1/2022 processing period to **30 June 2023**.

### 2. Premium formula (para 4) — **premium = A × B − C × D − E** (not negotiable; negative → nil)

| Factor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Maximum **total GFA after** exchange as stipulated in the exchange document(s), determined by LandsD |
| **B** | After-exchange rate(s) by NDA. **Residential rate** on all GFA **not prohibiting private residential**; remainder at non-residential rate |
| **C** | Site area of **Agricultural land** surrendered and/or **permissible GFA** of **Building land** surrendered |
| **D** | Before-exchange rate(s). On Building land: residential-building rate on GFA not prohibiting residential; remainder at non-res. building rate |
| **E** | Deductible **construction cost of Government Accommodation** required to be built and assigned/handed back or OMM’d for public use — only if the proponent department conveys the figure **before** the Option deadline; else **E = 0**. GA eligibility at LandsD discretion |

| GFA rules for A (fn 3) | Lock |
|---|---|
| GA floor space | **May be disregarded** in max total GFA |
| NOFA stated for GA | Convert **NOFA : GFA = 1 : 1.8** (e.g. 100 m² NOFA = 180 m² GFA) |
| Other floor space | Disregarded **only** if **expressly GFA-exempted** in the exchange document |
| No max total GFA in the document | **This PN does not apply** |
| Pre-1987 IBs (fn 1) | May opt **only** these NDA rates, **not** PN 1/2021 IB standard rates |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze **lease GFA (A)**, residential vs non-residential split (drives B), surrender Ag vs Building land (C/D), and whether GA is in the deal (E and 1:1.8 NOFA) **before** ticking the Option — there is no later appeal.

### 3. Agricultural vs Building land for C (fn 4–5)

| Class | How assessed |
|---|---|
| **Agricultural land** | Old schedule / new grant ag lots, and ag portion of mixed lots — **site area** × ag rate |
| **Building land** | Land where building is allowed, and building portion of mixed lots. Old schedule descriptive terms that count as Building land include house, building, mat shed, boat hut, temple, nunnery, latrine, shop, cow shed, cattle shed, duck shed, cow byre, pigsty, school, or other uses LandsD may determine — treated as **residential building use** (residential not prohibited) |
| Permissible GFA missing | Adopt **plot ratio 1.33** |
| Untraceable lease | Use and site area at LandsD discretion |

Applicant should submit a Cap. 473 **Authorised Land Surveyor** Survey Submission (Record Plan, Land Boundary Plan, Survey Report) to support C (para 5; page 4 of this extract is blank).

**SD takeaway:** Survey and classify every surrender lot as Ag (site area) or Building (GFA or PR 1.33) at SD — that split is the credit side of premium.
