# LAO Practice Note No. 6/2023 — Tree Preservation and Removal Proposals under Lease
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
20 June 2023 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Generally revamped | **Supersedes** LAO PN **2/2020** and **2/2020A**  
SCC of TPRP (paras 10–11) effective **20 June 2023** for new TPRPs

> Scope note: Streamlined **Tree Preservation Clause** consent for building developments — TPRP inside/outside lot, deemed approval where TPB/PlanD already approved the landscape, and **self-certification**. It is **not** a GEO slope code. Read with LandsD **Guidance Notes** (flow charts and Specified Forms) on landsd.gov.hk. Standard clause: no tree on the lot or adjacent thereto shall be removed or interfered with without the Director’s prior written consent; consent may require transplanting, compensatory landscaping or replanting.

---

## Regulatory Overview

A **tree** is a plant with trunk diameter **≥ 95 mm at 1.3 m** above ground (AFCD Nature Conservation Practice Note No. 2 for DBH). AP and RLA must assess tree impact at planning/early design **in parallel with GBP** to BD (and treat existing trees as part of any Landscape Submission); unauthorised felling can block **presale consent** and **CC** and attract lease enforcement (BD site monitoring reports felling to LandsD).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. TPRP within lot — three approval tracks (paras 4–12)

| Track | When | LandsD action |
|---|---|---|
| **Deemed approval** (paras 4–5) | TPRP is **part of** a Landscape Submission (LMP/LP) **already approved** by TPB/PlanD | Specified Form + entire TPRP in the approved LMP/LP + **RLA certificate** that it is that approved landscape. Acknowledgement within **10 WD** = deemed Prior Written Consent. After PlanD approves **completion** of landscape works: Deemed Compliance Submission → acknowledgement within **10 WD** |
| Deemed tracks **do not** apply | Lot boundary **substantially different** from the planning site; **or** B/D consultation needed — trees to be felled/interfered/replanted on land to be **handed back** under lease, or on a **Coloured Area** | Full vetting |
| **Vetted approval** (para 8) | TPRP **not** part of a planning Landscape Submission | Based on latest GBP, Foundation Plans, Site Formation (incl. ELS if required). Needs: no adverse LAT landscape advice; **no** removal/interference with **trees of particular value** designated under lease (OVT, potential OVT, rare species, stonewall trees, trees of particular interest, etc.); plus Guidance Notes para 2 criteria. Processing normally **≤ 8 weeks**. Conditions typically: implement Approved TPRP to LandsD’s satisfaction; **no tree removal before BD approval of Foundation and Site Formation (incl. ELS)** |
| **During a land transaction** (para 9) | Exchange / grant / modification **containing a Tree Preservation Clause** is in progress | After **BBTO accepted and deposit paid**, submit TPRP; formal approval **after** the transaction completes |
| **SCC of TPRP** (paras 10–13) | RLA certifies: no trees of particular value affected; no unnecessary removal/excessive pruning; best endeavours to compensate and greenery; **1:1** compensatory replanting **by number within the lot** (compensatory trees ≥ trees removed including dead, **excluding undesirable species**) | Random check. Not selected: acknowledgement within **10 WD** = deemed consent. Selected: result within **8 weeks**; fail → resubmit. **Does not apply** if comments needed from a future maintenance department (e.g. compensatory trees on land to be carved out/surrendered) or para 10 cannot be met |

**SD takeaway:** If TPB/PlanD already approved the LMP/LP, do **not** redesign the tree package for LandsD — file deemed approval. If using SCC, lock **1:1 replanting inside the lot** and keep **OVTs/rare/stonewall trees**. Do not fell before **foundation/site-formation** approval on the vetted track.

### 2. Completion, minor changes, outside-lot trees (paras 14–21)

| Stage | Lock |
|---|---|
| SCC of completion | RLA inspects and certifies approved tree works complete; random **on-site** check; not selected → deemed compliance (acknowledgement in **10 WD**); selected → result in **8 weeks**; rectification needs a consolidated completion resubmission |
| Minor changes to Approved TPRP | More trees preserved/planted; minor location/size/species changes **within lot** — **no** re-approval if LandsD agrees they are minor (LandsD’s call is **final**) |
| Supervision | Approved tree works by **Qualified Professionals** (DEVB Handbook on Tree Management App I) |
| Trees **outside lot** or on Coloured Area to be handed to another B/D (e.g. LCSD, HyD) | Submit TPRP to that **maintenance B/D first**; then Specified Form to DLO with the B/D’s agreement letter; LandsD acknowledgement within **10 WD** = Prior Written Consent; that B/D checks completion |
| More than one B/D | LandsD **coordinates**; **demarcate** hand-back areas on the TPRP |
| Coloured Area **retained** by the owner, or GL that LandsD will take over | Submit TPRP to LandsD; SCC of completion (paras 14–17) applies |
| Workshop | **LAO PN 4/2018** workshop may include TPRP vs building layout; LAT may attend |

**SD takeaway:** Off-lot and coloured-area trees are a **B/D** problem first (agreement letter, then 10 WD LandsD stamp) — draw a **demarcation** where two departments take over.

### 3. Emergency felling and enforcement (paras 23–27)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Owner duty | Manage/maintain trees on the lot (and Coloured Area if the lease so requires), including horticultural maintenance and emergency felling/pruning for natural hazard/safety |
| Record | Full **colour, date-stamped** photos of ground, stem/branch/canopy/root-collar/root-zone/root-ball **before and after**; same-occasion photos acceptable for speed |
| Report | In writing to LandsD within **21 calendar days**, with reasons and photos; rely on qualified advice including RLA |
| If planning conditions exist | Also **resubmit updated LMP/LP to PlanD** |
| If no planning conditions | Cover in RLA’s TPRP re-submission/amendment with compensatory replanting |
| Breach | LandsD may **withhold presale consent or CC** until Director’s terms are met (including **premium, admin fee, compensatory replanting**). TPRP approval is **only** under the Tree Preservation Clause and does **not** prove other lease compliance; GBP can still be rejected |

**SD takeaway:** After typhoon/emergency works, diary the **21-day** LandsD report with before/after photos — and if there is a planning landscape condition, update **PlanD** as well, or CC/presale can be held.
