# LAO Practice Note No. 8/2024 — Application for Special Waiver for Conversion of an Entire Existing Industrial Building
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
31 October 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies/supplements LAO PN Nos. 6/2019 and 6/2019A

> Scope note: Extends the **entire-IB conversion special-waiver** window and **drops the 10% Designated Portion** for **pre-1987 IBs** only. Other IBs aged 15+ still carry the 10% Specified Uses lock in PN 6/2019 para 3(a). Parent PNs otherwise remain valid.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN, together with LAO PN Nos. 6/2019 and 6/2019A (collectively "the PNs"), governs special waivers to convert an entire existing industrial building. LandsD considers each application on its merits as landlord; the PNs create no expectation of approval.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Application deadline (para 2)

| Item | Lock |
|---|---|
| Former deadline (PN 6/2019A para 2) | On or before **31 October 2024** |
| New deadline | On or before **31 December 2027** |

**SD takeaway:** Entire-IB conversion special-waiver applications under the PNs must be **received** by LandsD on or before **31 December 2027**.

---

### 2. Pre-1987 IB — 10% Designated Portion dropped (paras 3–4)

A **pre-1987 IB** is a building on an industrial lot (as defined in footnote 2 of **LAO PN 2/2019**) that was wholly or partly constructed **on or before 1 March 1987**, or constructed to building plans **first submitted** to BA on or before that date.

| Building | If special waiver approved |
|---|---|
| **Pre-1987 IB**, nil waiver fee, under PN 6/2019 & 6/2019A | Condition to designate, reserve and restrict **not less than 10%** of total accountable GFA (BO) of the existing IB after conversion ("Designated Portion") for Specified Uses — **dispensed with**, including associated provisions in PN 6/2019 para 3(a) |
| Entire existing IB **other than** a pre-1987 IB, aged **15 years or more** | **Still** must designate **≥ 10%** of total accountable GFA for Specified Uses after conversion (PN 6/2019 para 3(a)) |

On-going pre-1987 applications received **before** this PN, where the special waiver is **not yet approved/granted**: applicant may submit **revised conversion-works proposals** and revised schematic parking-floor plans (if needed) deleting the Designated Portion — **not** treated as a new application. If the IB is the subject of a TPB approval, obtain the necessary **Town Planning Ordinance** approvals and lodge the TPB approval letter for the revised (no-Designated-Portion) scheme.

**SD takeaway:** For a pre-1987 entire-IB conversion, do **not** bank 10% GFA as Specified Uses — reclaim it into the conversion brief; non-pre-1987 IBs ≥15 years still need that 10% lock.

---

### 3. Ineligible stock and form (paras 5–6)

| Lock | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sample form | Appendix I to this PN **supersedes** Appendix I to PN 6/2019 |
| Not eligible | IBs that have **completed redevelopment** under IB revitalisation in **PN 1/2010, 1/2010A, 1/2010B** or **PN 2/2019, 2/2019A** |

**SD takeaway:** A lot already redeveloped under the 2010 or 2019 IB revitalisation schemes cannot take a PN 6/2019 entire-building conversion waiver.
