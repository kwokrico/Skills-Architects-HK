# LAO Practice Note No. 7/2019 — Transitional housing in converted existing industrial buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
18 April 2019 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Issue No. 7/2019 | Supplements **LAO PN 1/2010, 1/2010A, 1/2010B** (as supplemented by **PN 2/2016**) and **LAO PN 6/2019**

> Scope note: Overlay waiver for **transitional housing** in a converted existing IB (as part of, in addition to, or otherwise than the **Special Waiver** for entire-IB non-industrial conversion). **New** Special Waiver applications under the **1/2010** series are **not** accepted. Not a BO domestic conversion code and not a planning permission.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Owners may apply for a Transitional Housing Waiver for conversion and use of the converted IB **or part thereof** for transitional housing, for a term of **not more than five (5) years**, if the building is in **“Commercial”**, **“Comprehensive Development Area”**, **“OU(B)”**, or **“Residential”** on statutory plans, the use is permitted as temporary use under those plans, and the project is **NGO-run** and **supported by THB** (Task Force on Transitional Housing as one-stop facilitation).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Eligibility, term, and fees (paras 2–3)

| Lock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Operator | **Non-profit making organisation** or **social enterprise** (definitions in fn 1–2: s.88 charity; guarantee company without profit distribution; or other non-profit body under HK legislation; SE expected on HAD “Hot spots for consumptions at SEs” or SEBC Social Enterprise Directory) |
| Support | Waiver application **supported by THB**; Task Force provides one-stop facilitation |
| Zoning | **C / CDA / OU(B) / R** on TPO statutory plans; temporary transitional housing permitted (owners pointed to TPB press release **26 November 2018**) |
| Term | **Not more than 5 years** |
| Fees | If approved: **nil waiver fee**, **nil deposit**, and **nil or no additional** admin fee (as the case may be) |
| Other terms | As LandsD imposes (including Task Force recommendations), e.g. restriction on letting the wholesale-converted IB or part **to NGOs agreed by THB** |
| 1/2010 pipeline | No **new** Special Waiver under 1/2010 PNs. Owners **may** file a **supplementary** Transitional Housing Waiver where a 1/2010 Special Waiver is **under processing**, or a **new** Transitional Housing Waiver for a building **already** covered by a 1/2010 Special Waiver |
| Form | If not part of the original Special Waiver application, use **PN 6/2019 Appendices I and II** |

**SD takeaway:** Design transitional housing as a **≤ 5-year NGO operation** on **C / CDA / OU(B) / R** with **THB Task Force** backing — LandsD will not open a new 1/2010 wholesale-conversion track for it.

---

### 2. Other authorities (para 4)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Still required | TPB, FSD, BA and other licences whether or not works are involved |
| Not a guarantee | Issue of the waiver is not LandsD feasibility or cost acceptance |

**SD takeaway:** Run **FSD/BA/TPB** in parallel with the waiver — nil-fee LandsD consent does not clear fire or building conversion.
