# LAO Practice Note No. 5/2024 — Standard Rates for HSK/HT NDA Land Exchange
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
16 August 2024 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Rates valid through processing to **31 March 2025** | Option under **LAO PN 1/2024**

> Scope note: Optional **standard-rate premium** for lease modification / land exchange of sites in **Hung Shui Kiu / Ha Tsuen NDA**. It is **not** a GB/OZP design code. Processing priority and application deadlines remain those in **PN 1/2024**. Option is **irreversible** at PBTO.

---

## Regulatory Overview

An applicant may opt for standard-rate premium **not later than acceptance of the PBTO** by lodging Appendix I. Second Phase sites must have BBTO (with premium) accepted by **31 March 2025** or processing ceases (Remaining Phase is lower priority; re-application for Second Phase after **30 April 2024** will **not** be considered).

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Option window and consequences (paras 2, 6–7)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Opt by | **Not later than PBTO acceptance** (Option Form App I) |
| After opt accepted | **No appeal** against BBTO standard-rate premium; application fees **forfeited** if the applicant then drops the exchange |
| No Option Form by PBTO | Premium assessed **case-by-case**; dropping the BBTO still **forfeits** fees; **no** Second Phase re-application after **30 April 2024** |
| LandsD discretion | Whether the exchange is eligible for standard rates is **final** |
| Pre-1987 IBs (fn 1) | If the exchange involves eligible pre-1987 IBs under **LAO PN 12/2023**, applicant may opt **only** the rates **in this PN**, not other PNs’ rates |

**SD takeaway:** Decide the standard-rate option **before PBTO** — after that the premium is locked and Second Phase cannot be re-filed after **30 April 2024**.

### 2. HSK/HT standard rates (para 3) — valid to 31 March 2025

Agricultural = **per site m²**; other columns = **per GFA m²**.

| NDA | Ag | NR before^ | Res before | NR after# | Res after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hung Shui Kiu / Ha Tsuen | $4,500 | $13,500 | $26,500 | $24,000 | $34,000 |

^ Before NR includes commercial and industrial; LandsD’s type call is final.  
# After NR includes commercial and **modern industrial**; **excludes** columbarium, PFS, DG storage, offensive trades; **hotel and ancillary = non-residential**; LandsD’s type call is final.

**SD takeaway:** After-exchange residential GFA prices at **$34,000/m²**; hotel is **non-residential ($24,000/m²)** — split the GFA schedule to match.

### 3. Premium formula (para 4): A×B − C×D − E

| Factor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Maximum **total GFA after** exchange in the land documents; LandsD’s figure at absolute discretion |
| **B** | After-use rate(s) in para 3; **residential** rate on all GFA **not prohibiting private residential**; **non-residential** rate on the remainder |
| **C** | **Site area of Agricultural land** surrendered and/or **permissible GFA of Building land** surrendered |
| **D** | Before-use rate(s); on Building land, residential rate where residential is **not prohibited** |
| **E** | Deductible **construction cost of Government Accommodation** the applicant must build/hand back/operate for Government; assessed by the proponent department, **non-negotiable**; if not conveyed before the Option deadline, **E = 0** (also on case-by-case assessment) |
| Negative result | **No premium** charged |
| No max total GFA in the exchange document | **This PN does not apply** |

**GFA for A:** floor space **solely as Government Accommodation** may be disregarded. If GA is stated as **NOFA**, convert **NOFA:GFA = 1:1.8** (e.g. 100 m² NOFA = 180 m² GFA). Other floor space is disregarded **only** if **expressly** GFA-exempted in the exchange document.

**Agricultural land (C):** old-schedule and new-grant lots demised for agriculture, plus agricultural portion of mixed lots; assessed on **site area**.

**Building land (C):** land on which building is allowed, plus building portion of mixed lots. Old-schedule lots count as Building land if the Block Government Lease schedule term is one of: house, building, mat shed, boat hut, temple, nunnery, latrine, shop, cow shed, cattle shed, duck shed, cow byre, pigsty, school, or other uses LandsD determines; those old-schedule lots are treated as **residential** Building land (residential not prohibited). Permissible GFA is as specified in the lease; if **none**, plot ratio **1.33** may be adopted. Untraceable leases: use and site area by LandsD.

**SD takeaway:** You need a **stated maximum total GFA** in the exchange, a clean **ag vs building** surrender split, and a **GA NOFA→GFA at 1.8** if Government Accommodation is in the scheme — missing max GFA knocks the site off this PN.

---

### 4. Survey pack for “C” (para 5)

Provide, **not later than settlement of the initial administrative fee**: (i) Survey Submission by an **Authorised Land Surveyor** (Cap. 473) — Survey Record Plan, Land Boundary Plan, Survey Report per the Code of Practice — showing wholly surrendered lots (surveyed and registered areas), partial surrenders, and the regrant site; (ii) **title deeds** of all lots to be surrendered. LandsD then states “C” in a **separate letter at PBTO**. If the pack is not provided and the applicant still opts standard rates, LandsD fixes “C” from information it has and issues BBTO. LandsD’s “C” is **final** and **may differ** from the Block Government Lease.

**SD takeaway:** Commission the **ALS** boundary survey early enough to hit the **initial admin-fee** date — that is what sets the agricultural site area / building GFA credit.
