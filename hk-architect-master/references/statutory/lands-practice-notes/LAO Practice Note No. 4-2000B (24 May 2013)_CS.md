# LAO Practice Note No. 4/2000(B) — Recreational Facilities in Residential Developments
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
24 May 2013 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Supersedes LAO PN No. 4/2000(A)

> Scope note: Lease-administration guidelines for **indoor clubhouse / recreational facilities** that may be **GFA-exempt** under modern residential (or residential portion of composite) leases. It is **not** a BO GFA code — BA ruling is usually followed, but the Director of Lands reserves discretion. Single-residence lots get **no** exemption.

## Regulatory Overview

The PN lists commonly accepted active / passive / ancillary clubhouse uses and a **sliding-scale cap** on exempt clubhouse GFA as a percentage of total **domestic GFA** (or a maximum area, whichever is greater). Indoor facilities of appropriate scale and composition may be exempted; leases without a recreational-facilities condition need a **modification** before exemption.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When exemption is available (paras. 1–2, 4–5)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| Typical lease | Permits recreational facilities + ancillary for **common use** by residents and bona fide visitors |
| Indoor clubhouse | May be GFA-exempt at appropriate scale/composition |
| Active/passive/ancillary **area ratio** | Former rule (**≥50% usable area as active**) **no longer applies** |
| BA | BA ruling on facility types **usually followed** |
| Common-area condition | Exemption is because facilities are **common areas enjoyed by all residents** |
| Single residence | Lots intended as a **single residence** — **no** GFA exemption |
| Old leases | No recreational-facilities condition → **modification required** before GFA exemption |

**SD takeaway:** Put the clubhouse in **common parts** (not a private house amenity) and check the lease has a recreational-facilities SC before counting on GFA exemption.

### 2. Sliding-scale GFA cap (para. 3 + Table 1)

Clubhouse GFA including **voids, plant rooms** (incl. swimming-pool filtration), **covered walkways and covered courts** serving solely the clubhouse **must not exceed** Table 1 (max **% of total domestic GFA** or max area, **whichever is greater**). Scale is based on BA; for lease admin the % applies to **total domestic GFA of the lot**.

OCR of Table 1 (verify against PDF):

| Total domestic GFA | Max % / max exempt area (whichever greater) |
|---|---|
| Up to 25,000 m² | 5% (first band; confirm PDF) |
| 25,000–50,000 m² | 4.5% / 1,250 m² |
| 50,000–75,000 m² | 4.0% / 2,250 m² |
| 75,000–100,000 m² | 3.5% / 3,000 m² |
| 100,000–125,000 m² | 3.0% / 3,500 m² |
| Over 125,000 m² | 2.5% / 3,750 m² |

**SD takeaway:** Size the clubhouse (including voids and dedicated plant/walkways) **inside Table 1** against lot domestic GFA — oversizing kills exemption.

### 3. Appendix — typical accepted uses

| Class | Commonly accepted |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Swimming pool; multi-purpose / squash / basketball / football / tennis / badminton courts; children’s play; games room; indoor golf; table tennis; fitness / gymnasium / weights / aerobics; bowling; skating; volleyball; rock climbing; dance / yoga |
| **Passive** | Sitting / lounge; billiard; sauna / jacuzzi / spa; reading / study / library; function room; computer / video games; music / karaoke |
| **Ancillary (size-dependent)** | Clubhouse management office; reception; staff room; snack bar / mini-bar / pantry (**≤10 m²**); first-aid; kitchen (**≤15 m²**, sites **without commercial GFA**); changing / showers; lavatories; store rooms |

**SD takeaway:** Keep F&B ancillary tiny (pantry ≤10 m²; kitchen ≤15 m² and only if no commercial GFA) — commercial-scale F&B is not a GFA-free clubhouse.
