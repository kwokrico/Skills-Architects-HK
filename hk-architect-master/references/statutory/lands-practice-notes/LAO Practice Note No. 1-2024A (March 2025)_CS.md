# LAO Practice Note No. 1/2024A — HSK/HT NDA Second Phase BBTO deadline
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
March 2025 | Lands Administration Office — Lands Department  
Immediate effect | Varies and supplements LAO PN No. 1/2024

> Scope note: Extends the **BBTO (with premium)** processing deadline for **some** Hung Shui Kiu/Ha Tsuen Second Phase sites planned for **commercial** use. It is **not** a reopening of the 30 April 2024 application window.

---

## Regulatory Overview

This PN varies paragraph 9 of PN 1/2024 for accepted Second Phase applications. A new Reference Plan (superseding the plan in PN 1/2024 para 3) is uploaded on LandsD’s website.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Varied processing deadlines (paras 2–3)

| Site class | Cease-processing if BBTO (with premium) not issued **and** accepted by |
|---|---|
| Second Phase sites **except** designated development sites **edged red** on the updated Reference Plan | **31 March 2025** |
| Designated development sites **edged red** on the updated Reference Plan | **31 December 2025** |

| Qualification | Lock |
|---|---|
| Commercial Second Phase sites with **no** application by **30 April 2024** | Extension **does not apply** |

**SD takeaway:** Confirm whether the lot is edged red on the **updated** LandsD Reference Plan — only those commercial Second Phase sites get the 31 December 2025 BBTO window; others remain 31 March 2025, and missed 30 April 2024 applications stay dead.
