# The Rules of the Institute
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Revision 17 June 2021 (also printed: copyright 17th June 2021; earlier revisions from 3 December 1981 through 20 September 2018) | The Hong Kong Institute of Architects

> Scope note: These Rules are the internal membership, Council, meeting, and disciplinary rules of The Hong Kong Institute of Architects (HKIA), a body corporate under The Hong Kong Institute of Architects Incorporation Ordinance, Chapter 1147. They are not Cap. 408 (Architects Registration Ordinance), they do not approve building plans, and they are not themselves the Code of Professional Conduct — though Rule 63 states that any reference to “Rules” in these presents includes the Code of Professional Conduct and the Conditions of Engagement.

## Regulatory Overview

The Rules define who may be Fellows, Members, Corporate Members, Associates, Affiliates, Graduates, Students, Honorary Fellows, Retired Members, and Non-Resident Members; who may use HKIA / FHKIA titles; who may vote; how the Council and general meetings operate; and how the Institute disciplines members. Every candidate for admission must declare willingness to be bound by the Rules and the Code of Professional Conduct, and membership rights do not begin until fees are paid and a signed declaration of that binding is returned.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Membership classes (Rules 5A–9B, 21A)

| Rule | Class | Hard lock |
|---|---|---|
| 5A | Members | Qualified architects nominated and elected by Council: either (a) members of an architects’ association, institution, or register recognised by Council for admission, or (b) holders of a Council-recognised degree/diploma in architecture, with two years’ post-graduate architectural experience (or Council-defined equivalent), who have passed the HKIA/Architects Registration Board, HKSAR Professional Assessment or its approved equivalent |
| 5B | Fellows | Members with minimum 15 years’ standing in HKIA and 15 years in practice (academic/public-sector experience recognised), or who have made a significant contribution to the Institute or architecture; elected as Fellows subject to Council approval. Unless expressly otherwise provided, “Member” / “Members” includes “Fellow” / “Fellows” |
| 5C–5E | Corporate Members | Corporations carrying on architectural practice in Hong Kong meeting the director / voting-rights thresholds in Rule 5C (see Topic 4) |
| 6A | Associates | Persons who have completed a course of study in architecture and meet one of: (a) membership of a professional institute recognised by the national government of the respective country at application; (b) adequate professional experience as prescribed by Council; or (c) full-time staff of a recognised architecture course with Head of Department certification |
| 6B | Affiliates | Persons or categories considered by Council to have a connection with the architectural profession and suitable for admission |
| 7A | Graduate Members | Degree/diploma in architectural studies from a Council-recognised University or School, or passed any professional examination in architecture recognised by Council |
| 7B | Student Members | Enrolled in courses of study in architecture in a Council-recognised University or School |
| 8A | Honorary Fellows | Persons of eminence the Institute wishes to honour, or whose membership/contribution further the objects of the Institute |
| 9A | Retired Members | Any Member retired from practice and ceased to draw remuneration from architectural work, subject to Council approval |
| 9B | Non-Resident Members | Any Member, Associate, or Graduate Member not ordinarily resident in Hong Kong may apply for Non-Resident status, subject to Council approval |
| 21A | Transfers | Transfer among Fellows, Members, Associates, Affiliates, Graduates, Students, Retired, and Non-Resident is permissible subject to Council approval. Corporate Membership may not be transferred to another category |

**SD takeaway:** Only a person who meets Rule 5A (or 5B as Fellow) is an HKIA Member for professional-membership purposes; Graduate, Associate, Affiliate, Student, and Honorary Fellow are different classes with different rights.

### 2. Voting rights and attendance (Rules 17, 5E, 61)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| 17 | All categories of membership have the right to attend meetings, but only Fellows and Members have the right to vote |
| 5E | A Corporate Member shall not be entitled to vote at general meetings or to hold any office of the Institute (including Council, Boards, or Committee), but may by resolution of its governing body authorise any person to attend a general meeting as an observer |
| 61(a) | At General Meetings, AGMs, and Special General Meetings: Members are entitled to attend, speak, and vote |
| 61(b) | Corporate Members, Associates, Affiliates, Graduate Members, Student Members, Honorary Fellows, and Retired Members are entitled to attend and speak (no vote under this Rule) |

**SD takeaway:** Firm or practice “HKIA Corporate Member” status does not confer an Institute vote; only Fellows and individual Members vote.

### 3. Titles — HKIA / FHKIA and related designations (Rule 10)

| Who | May use |
|---|---|
| Fellows | “Fellow of the Hong Kong Institute of Architects” or the letters **FHKIA** after their names |
| Members | “Member of the Hong Kong Institute of Architects” or the letters **HKIA** after their names |
| Associates | “Associate of the Hong Kong Institute of Architects” or the title **Associate HKIA** after their names |
| Affiliates | “Affiliate of the Hong Kong Institute of Architects” or **Affiliate HKIA** after their names |

**SD takeaway:** Do not put “HKIA” or “FHKIA” after a name unless the person is a Member or Fellow under these Rules; Associates and Affiliates must use their own prescribed titles, not bare HKIA/FHKIA.

### 4. Corporate Members (Rules 5C–5E, 21A)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| 5C(1) | At least one director of the corporation must be a Foundation Member (as referred to in the Deed of Constitution), a Member, or a Fellow |
| 5C(2)(a) or (2)(b) | Either at least 30% of the directors are Foundation Members, Members and/or Fellows, **or** at least 30% of the voting rights of the corporation are held by Foundation Members, Members, and/or Fellows |
| 5C | If a Corporate Member ceases to fulfill the requirement, Council may terminate membership and may reinstate when the requirement is again fulfilled |
| 5D | A Corporate Member shall abide by the Rules, but its obligations are separate and distinct from the individual obligations of directors, members, or employees who may also be Institute members |
| 5E | No vote; no Institute office (Council/Boards/Committee); observer only if authorised by its governing body |
| 21A | Corporate Membership may not be transferred to another membership category |

**SD takeaway:** An architectural practice seeking Corporate Membership must keep at least one HKIA Foundation Member/Member/Fellow director and meet the 30% director or 30% voting-rights threshold — loss of that structure risks termination of Corporate Membership.

### 5. Binding to the Rules and Code of Professional Conduct (Rules 11, 16, 63)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| 11 | Every candidate for admission shall sign a statement on the standard application form that he is eligible under the Rules and willing to be bound by the Rules and Code of Professional Conduct if elected |
| 16 | On election, Council sends the Deed of Constitution, the Rules, and the Code of Professional Conduct; no elected candidate is entitled to the rights and privileges of membership until entrance fee (if any) and first annual subscription are paid **and** a signed declaration that he shall be bound by the Rules and Code is returned |
| 63 | Any reference to “Rules” in these presents shall include the Code of Professional Conduct and the Conditions of Engagement of the Hong Kong Institute of Architects. New Rules, amendments, or rescindments (including Constitution matters) need at least seven days’ notice and an affirmative two-thirds vote of Members having a right to vote and voting (or poll under Rule 62) |

**SD takeaway:** HKIA membership is conditional on being bound by both these Rules and the Code of Professional Conduct; for Institute purposes the Code and Conditions of Engagement sit inside “Rules.”

### 6. Disciplinary powers (Rules 24A–25C, 22B, 23B)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| 24A | Council may reprimand, suspend, or expel; notify the Architects Registration Board; and/or award costs against a Member, Corporate Member, Associate, Affiliate, Graduate, Student, Retired, or Non-Resident Member who: (a) wilfully breaks a rule of the Institute; (b) by conduct professionally or otherwise is unfit to be such a member; (c) in Council’s opinion brings the Institute’s name into disrepute; or (d) is convicted of an offence that breaches (a), (b), or (c) |
| 24B | No reprimand/suspension/expulsion/ARB notification unless: ≥14 calendar days’ written notice of meeting date/time/place, nature of allegations, and right to be present, heard, and legally represented at own cost (with limited absences allowed); opportunity to attend and be heard / put in evidence; written notification of Council decision and appeal right; and any appeal rejected with written notice |
| 24C | Complaint → Preliminary Committee (two Council members) → if grounds may exist, Disciplinary Board (any Council member + minimum two other Members) enquires; decisions may be published at Institute premises, in circular, and/or in newspapers/journals |
| 24D–24E | Appeal in writing to Honorary Secretary within 28 days; Council hears appeal; Disciplinary Board members whose decision is appealed shall not sit; Council decision is final |
| 24F | Majority vote; Chairman has a casting second vote in favour of the respondent if tied |
| 25B | No resignation while subject to disciplinary proceedings, or if Council considers the member is likely to be or become subject to them |
| 25C | Institute may enquire into past members for conduct during the period of membership as though still a member |
| 22B / 23B | Council may set Continuing Professional Development (CPD) requirements for Members, Non-Resident Members, Affiliates, and Graduate Members; failure after reminder may lead to termination (with possible reinstatement after CPD in the preceding 12 months) |

**SD takeaway:** Wilful breach of Institute rules, unfitness, disrepute, or relevant conviction can lead to reprimand, suspension, expulsion, and notification to the Architects Registration Board — after a defined hearing and appeal process.

### 7. Council and general meetings (Rules 26, 46–47, 55–62, 65–66)

| Rule | Lock |
|---|---|
| 26 | Council: President, two Vice Presidents, Board Chairmen, Honorary Secretary, Honorary Treasurer, eight Ordinary Members, and Immediate Past President (ex-officio) |
| 46 | Council meets at least once every quarter; quorum is half of Council membership; President or any five Council members may require an Extraordinary Council Meeting within fourteen days |
| 47 | Council decisions by vote of two-thirds of those present having the right to vote and voting |
| 55 | In January each year Council fixes dates for at least one General Meeting every quarter |
| 56–57 | Council may call a Special General Meeting; Council must summon one upon requisition stating the business and signed by not less than forty Members |
| 58 | Notice of place, date, hour, and business at least seven days previously to every Member, Corporate Member, Associate, Affiliate, Graduate, Student, Honorary Fellow, Retired, and Non-Resident Member |
| 60 | Quorum: twenty Members present; if not within half an hour, postpone 14 days with new notices; second meeting may transact business even without quorum |
| 62 | Resolutions need affirmative vote of two-thirds of those voting (or poll as provided); voting by show of hands or poll as Council sets out in the Notice |
| 65–66 | AGM is the last General Meeting of the year (time/place fixed by Council in December); Special General Meetings discuss only business stated in the notice |

Eligibility for President, Vice President, Honorary Secretary, Honorary Treasurer, and Ordinary Council Member requires ordinary residence in Hong Kong (Rules 28–32A); President additionally requires prior Council service of at least two years (Rule 28). Term and consecutive-year limits apply under Rules 28–32B.

**SD takeaway:** Institute policy and Rule changes that bind practice go through Council (two-thirds) and general meetings (two-thirds of voting Members); Corporate Members and non-voting classes attend and speak but do not carry those votes.
