# PNRC-38 Registration of Building Contractors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 1997; last revision November 2019; this revision September 2020 (Paragraph 6 amended) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 38 covers solely the registration of general building contractors and specialist contractors. Minor works contractors registration is Practice Note for Registered Contractors 69. This extract covers the note's own instructions, not every page of the 57-page registration document: the body (paragraphs 1 to 28), Appendix A, the opening of Appendix B, and the last page of the performance appendix.

## Regulatory Overview
Sections 8, 8A, 8B, 8C, 8D, 8E and 8F of the Buildings Ordinance govern this contractors registration system, so that only contractors able to perform their duties and conversant with the statutory requirements are registered and allowed to carry out building works and street works. Under section 8A the Building Authority keeps a general building contractors' register, a specialist contractors' register with sub-registers of specialized works, and a minor works contractors' register.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Appoint from the matching register
The Building Authority designates categories of specialized works by notice in the Gazette under section 8A(2). The note states there are five categories: demolition works, foundation works, ground investigation field works, site formation works, and ventilation works.

| Contractor | Works the note allows |
|---|---|
| Registered general building contractor (RGBC) | General building works and street works which do not include any specialized works in the designated categories. Also all types of minor works stipulated in Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. |
| Registered specialist contractor (RSC) | Specialized works only in the corresponding categories of the sub-registers in which the contractor is entered. Minor works only as specified in section 28(1)(b) to (ea) of that Regulation, according to those categories. |

A building owner appoints contractors from the appropriate register for the category of building works. Scope of RGBC works, and of RSC works in the five categories, is detailed in Appendices A to F. Appendix A states that an RGBC is qualified to carry out any building works and street works which are not designated as a category of specialized works. Certain general building works may be carried out by more than one category of contractors; the guidelines are in Appendix G, which this extract does not include. Names are published annually in the Gazette. The Buildings Department website posts the contractor's name, the Authorized Signatory, the registration number, and the expiry date.

**SD takeaway:** Lock the package to an RGBC only where the works are outside the five specialized categories, and name an RSC entered in the matching sub-register where the works are specialized.

### 2. Demolition is a specialist category unless every exception exists
Appendix B states that all demolition works to which the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations apply are specialized works of the demolition works category unless all of the following circumstances exist:

| | Circumstance that must exist |
|---|---|
| (a) | The building to be demolished is not in Scheduled Area Number 1. |
| (b) | No part of the building to be demolished exceeds 10 m above adjacent ground. |
| (c) | No structural element to be demolished has a clear span exceeding 6 m or a cantilever span exceeding 1 m. |
| (d) | No part of the building to be demolished is in pre-stressed concrete construction. |
| (e) | No part of the building to be demolished is an earth-retaining structure with retaining height exceeding 1.5 m. |
| (f) | There are no other buildings within 5 m from the building to be demolished. |

Works which may be carried out by more than one category are again referred to Appendix G. The extract's qualification table for a specialist contractor in the demolition category stops during the alternatives and does not include the later alternatives. In the rows that are printed, each alternative includes completion of the Demolition of Building Course for Supervisors/Foremen organised by CICTA or the Module Certificate in Building Demolition Course organised by IVE. Scope thresholds for foundation, ground investigation field works, site formation, and ventilation are in Appendices C to F, which this extract does not include.

**SD takeaway:** If any one of the six circumstances is absent, treat the demolition as specialized works and appoint a specialist contractor in the demolition sub-register.

### 3. Authorized Signatory, Technical Director, and Other Officer
Under section 8B(2) an applicant for registration as an RGBC or RSC must satisfy the Building Authority on: if it is a corporation, the adequacy of its management structure; the appropriate experience and qualifications of its personnel; its ability to have access to plant and resources; and the ability of the person appointed to act for it to understand building works and street works through relevant experience and a general knowledge of the basic statutory requirements.

| Role | Rule in the note |
|---|---|
| Authorized Signatory (AS) | Minimum of one, appointed to act for the applicant for the purposes of the Buildings Ordinance. An individual applicant is the only person eligible to act as the AS. In a partnership, any partner appointed by all the other partners is eligible. In a corporation, a suitable person appointed by the board of directors is eligible. |
| Technical Director (TD) | For a corporation, a minimum of one director from the board, appointed under the Companies Ordinance and appointed by the board. The TD is authorised to have access to plant and resources, provide technical and financial support for the execution of building works and street works, and make decisions for the company and supervise the AS and other personnel. |
| Other Officer (OO) | For a corporation which appoints a director who does not possess the required qualification or experience as TD: an OO, or an AS, authorised by the board to assist the TD. If an OO is required, that OO assists one TD only, and the AS is not permitted to take up the role of an OO. |
| Same person | A person may be both the AS and the TD of a corporation if he meets the requirements of both. |
| Another contractor | A person accepted as the AS, TD, or OO for a registered contractor will not be accepted as the AS, TD, or OO for another contractor simultaneously, except sharing among a holding company and its subsidiary companies under paragraph 27. Those requirements are in Appendix L, which this extract does not include. |

The applicant must also show that it has employed appropriate qualified staff to assist the applicant and the key personnel to execute, manage, and supervise the building works and street works. Qualifications and experience of the key personnel are in Appendices A to F. This extract includes the RGBC table in Appendix A and the start of the demolition table in Appendix B. Appendix A is marked Rev. 12/2004. Its notes state that, for alternative requirements 1 to 3, aggregation of the durations of experience under different projects of the same period is not permitted, and that alternative requirements 4 and 5 are only for applicants who had applied for registration under those alternatives on or before 6 November 2000. Academic qualifications are to be in construction technology (the note gives architecture, building studies, building surveying, civil engineering, and structural engineering as examples); other fields are assessed by the Contractors Registration Committee. General guidelines are in Appendix H, which this extract does not include.

**SD takeaway:** Place the contract with a contractor whose accepted AS is appointed for that registration, and, for a corporation, whose Technical Director is a board director authorised to commit plant, money, and supervision.

### 4. Contractors Registration Committee gate
Contractors Registration Committees are appointed by the Building Authority under section 8. Their function is to examine qualifications, inquire into experience, interview the applicants and their key personnel, and advise the Building Authority to accept, defer, or reject applications for inclusion.

| Application | Rule printed in the note |
|---|---|
| Inclusion in the general building contractors' or specialist contractors' register | Under section 8B(10) the Building Authority must not include the name unless the relevant committee so recommends. |
| Addition of AS, TD, or OO | The Building Authority may seek the committee's advice. |
| Renewal | The Building Authority may seek advice under section 8C(4). |
| Restoration of a name | The Building Authority may seek advice under section 8D(3). |

New-application procedure and interview scope are in Appendix I. Renewal, restoration, and fast-track processing of renewal are in Appendix J. Addition and resignation of AS, TD, or OO, and fast-track addition of an AS under special circumstances, are in Appendix K. Those appendices are not in this extract. Job-reference guidelines, and consideration of conviction records and of suspension from tendering for public projects, are in Appendix M, which this extract does not include.

**SD takeaway:** Hold the tender award until the contractor, and any new Authorized Signatory, is on the register with a committee recommendation where section 8B(10) or an addition application requires one.

### 5. Performance Enhancement Course (last page of the extract)
Paragraph 28 points to Appendix N for the Contractor Performance Enhancement Scheme. This extract contains only the closing section of that appendix, marked (11/2019), paragraphs 8 to 11.

| Rule printed on the last page | |
|---|---|
| Purpose | The course enhances performance of a registered contractor in carrying out minor works and prescribed window inspections. |
| Two types | If the contractor is qualified as a Qualified Person (QP) under MWIS, the course covers MWCS and MWIS. If the contractor is not qualified as a QP, the course covers MWCS only. |
| Who attends | The Authorized Signatory represents the contractor. If there is more than one AS, the AS with the highest PP attends, or the AS nominated by the contractor if no one AS fits that criterion. |
| Certificate | After the course, the institution issues a certificate of attendance. The contractor submits it to the Buildings Department in accordance with paragraph 3 of that appendix in order to remit the 15 PP. Paragraph 3 is not in this extract. |
| Conviction or discipline | A contractor convicted or disciplined under the Buildings Ordinance from carrying out minor works or prescribed window inspection or repair is to attend. The Buildings Department issues an NL to attend within three months from the date of the NL. The contractor nominates a responsible AS and submits the certificate. |
| Interview still required | Attendance does not exempt the contractor, where a conviction or disciplinary record so requires, from an interview with the Contractors Registration Committee for renewal or restoration. |

The footnote states that, currently, the Hong Kong Institute of Vocational Education will organise the course.

**SD takeaway:** Treat a conviction or disciplinary record on minor works or prescribed window inspection or repair as still requiring a committee interview on renewal or restoration; the course certificate remits 15 PP and leaves that interview in place.
