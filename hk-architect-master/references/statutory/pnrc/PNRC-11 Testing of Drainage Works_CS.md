# PNRC-11 Testing of Drainage Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 1985; last revision April 1993; this revision January 2024 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Registered-contractor hold point for testing drainage works under regulation 73 of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations (B(SSFPDW&L)R), witnessed under PNAP APP-58. The test method is BS EN 1610:2015. This note does not set pipe sizes.

## Regulatory Overview

Regulation 73(1) requires the registered contractor to apply to the Building Authority for testing of drainage works. The authorized person witnesses and certifies the test, and trenches stay open until the regulation 73(6) inspection window has elapsed.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Witness, certificate, and the 7-day submission

| Step | Rule in this note |
|---|---|
| Before the BA application | RC arranges with the AP, then submits the standard form in the Appendix, with a copy to the AP |
| Witness | AP or the AP’s representative witnesses the test and endorses the results and records (PNAP APP-58) |
| Certificate | AP prepares a completion certificate for the drainage test and submits the certificate, results, and records to the BA within **7 days** of the test, whether or not the BA has inspected or tested the drains |

**SD takeaway:** Drainage that will be buried must be sequenced so the AP can witness the test and file the certificate within 7 days.

### 2. Four working days before backfill

Under regulation 73(6), **four working days** are allowed for the BA to inspect and test the drains. Trenches are not filled until that period has elapsed.

The note’s footnote on time: where the period for an act required under an ordinance does not exceed six days, the Interpretation and General Clauses Ordinance excludes public holidays, Sundays, and periods when tropical cyclone warning signal No. 8 or above is in force.

**SD takeaway:** Leave drainage trenches open until the four working days allowed for the Building Authority to inspect and test have elapsed.

### 3. Condition of the line and the test standard

Testing equipment must be in good working order. The drainage to be tested must be laid to the requisite fall, suitably bedded, and adequately supported. Testing follows BS EN 1610:2015, Construction and testing of drains and sewers.

**SD takeaway:** Specify bedding, support, and fall so the line can be tested to BS EN 1610:2015 before cover.

### 4. Extra tests near water control zones and gathering grounds

For sites in the vicinity of a water control zone (Water Pollution Control Ordinance, Cap. 358, s.2) or a gathering ground (Waterworks Ordinance, Cap. 102, s.2), or on the advice of the Environmental Protection Department, the BA may require testing of manholes, petrol interceptors, septic tanks, and the like.

**SD takeaway:** On sites near a water control zone or gathering ground, allow BA testing of manholes, petrol interceptors, and septic tanks as well as the drains.
