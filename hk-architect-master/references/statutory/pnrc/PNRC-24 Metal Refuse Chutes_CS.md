# PNRC-24 Metal Refuse Chutes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1994 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs temporary refuse chutes assembled from old metal barrels on building construction sites. It is not a debris-chute design code and it is not a statutory ban schedule with dimensions.

## Regulatory Overview

Metal-barrel chutes fixed to the external parts of a building under construction are treated as unsatisfactory because of noise to neighbours and because weather and repeated debris impact can affect their stability. Collapse endangers workers on the site and passers-by.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Safer debris removal

The contractor is requested to adopt other safer means of lowering and removing building debris. Where a refuse chute is still used, it must be properly assembled, secured and installed. Chutes of other materials of sufficient strength, installed in internal areas of the site, should be considered so that the noise level is reduced. A similar note was issued to authorized persons and registered structural engineers.

**SD takeaway:** Debris paths at schematic stage should be internal, or otherwise clear of the public edge, rather than a chain of metal barrels on the external wall.
