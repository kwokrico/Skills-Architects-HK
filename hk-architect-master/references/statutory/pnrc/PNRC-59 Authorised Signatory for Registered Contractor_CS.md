# PNRC-59 Authorised Signatory for Registered Contractor
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2004 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 59 governs appointment of the authorised signatory who acts for a registered contractor on building works or street works, a change of that signatory, and a temporary absence. Addition of a signatory to the contractor's registration follows PNRC 38. Minor works commenced under the simplified requirements follow PNRC 72.

## Regulatory Overview
Every registered contractor should appoint at least one authorised signatory to act for it for the purposes of the Buildings Ordinance, and under sections 9(5) and (6) a registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor provides continuous supervision in accordance with the supervision plan and complies with the Ordinance generally. The Building Authority accepts only one authorised signatory for each project, and the authorised signatory who signs Form BA 10 is deemed to be the person responsible for the contractor's duties on that project.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. One signatory, and the forms that person signs
The duties of the registered contractor are further stipulated in Part V of the Building (Administration) Regulations, the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans, and the Code of Practice for Site Safety Supervision.

The authorised signatory who signs Form BA 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 14A, 18, and 20 for the building works or street works is the person responsible for the contractor's duties under the Buildings Ordinance for those works.

**SD takeaway:** Name a single authorised signatory for the project and treat the signatory of Form BA 10 as the person accountable for the contractor's statutory duties.

### 2. The same name on every specified form and on the supervision plan
The name of the authorised signatory appointed for the project is indicated on all specified forms relating to the project. With effect from 1 July 2004, if the name is not shown on a specified form, the Building Authority considers the form incomplete and unacceptable.

If the name on the supervision plan differs from the name on any specified form, such as Form BA 10, 13, or 14, the Building Authority rejects the supervision plan or the specified forms, unless the change of authorised signatory has been approved or notified under paragraph 4.

**SD takeaway:** Put one authorised signatory's name on the supervision plan and on Forms BA 10, 13, and 14 before submission, so the plan and the forms name the same person.

### 3. Change of authorised signatory
| Situation | What the note requires |
|---|---|
| Registered contractor, other than a sole proprietorship, with only one authorised signatory | A proposal to change the authorised signatory of a project will not be accepted unless an application to add the new authorised signatory to the contractor's registration has been made under PNRC 38 and approved by the Building Authority. |
| Registered contractor with more than one authorised signatory on its registration | Notify the Building Authority in the standard form at Appendix A, countersigned by the authorized person of the project, not less than 7 days before the change. Otherwise the original authorised signatory is still regarded as responsible for the project. |

Appendix A (5/2004) is the notification of change. It records the contractor (general building contractor or specialist contractor), registration number and expiry, the original and new authorised signatories, the effective date, the site address, lot number, and the Building Authority's reference, with signatures of both signatories and the full name and signature of the authorized person.

**SD takeaway:** Programme a change of signatory at least 7 days ahead with the authorized person's countersignature, and where the contractor has only one signatory wait for a PNRC 38 addition approved by the Building Authority.

### 4. Temporary absence
Under Building (Administration) Regulation 22(2), if the registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor becomes unable to act, it should immediately cease the building works or street works under its purview until another such contractor is appointed.

The Building Authority accepts a temporary nomination so the works can continue, on all of the following conditions:

| Condition | Limit |
|---|---|
| Notice | Standard form at Appendix B, countersigned by the authorized person. |
| Timing | Submitted at least 7 days before the absence, except sudden illness or accident. In that case the contractor or the authorised signatory provides documentary proof within 3 days that the signatory is unable to act because of illness or accident. |
| Who may be nominated | Another authorised signatory of the same contractor on the same register or sub-register; or an authorised signatory currently acting for another contractor on the same register or sub-register. If that other contractor is a partnership or a corporation, an authorization letter from the rest of the partners, or from the required quorum of directors, is submitted with the notification. |
| Duration | Maximum 14 days. A further extension is considered only on documentary proof of special circumstances, such as illness. The total temporary acting period permitted is a maximum of 30 days. |

If the nominee does not meet the criteria above, the Building Authority does not accept the nomination and informs the contractor or authorised signatory and the authorized person. The works may have to cease if no authorised signatory is acting.

If the absence exceeds 30 days, the contractor arranges a change to another authorised signatory of the same company, or applies to add a new authorised signatory to its registration, under paragraph 4.

During the acting period the nominated signatory fulfils the site-supervision duties and signs the inspection records on site in accordance with the accepted supervision plans. That person is not permitted to sign any supervision plans or specified forms for the project.

Appendix B (5/2004) is the nomination notice. It records the period of illness or temporary absence, the site, both signatories, the authorized person's signature, and the nominee's acceptance, including identity document, telephone and fax numbers, the contractor for whom the nominee is currently an authorised signatory, and the authorization letter where the form requires it.

**SD takeaway:** A temporary signatory may sign site inspection records for a total of not more than 30 days and may not sign the supervision plan or the specified forms; beyond 30 days appoint another signatory of the company or add one to the registration.
