# PNRC-77 Quality Supervision of Building Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 2016; this revision February 2021 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: PNRC 77 gives common quality-supervision items for superstructure, excavation and lateral support, and site formation, inside the supervision-plan system of the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009. Foundation works, ground investigation field works and soil nailing stay in that Code. Appendix A states that its lists are not exhaustive.

## Regulatory Overview

The authorized person, registered structural engineer and registered geotechnical engineer supervise building works, including street works, and the registered contractor provides continuous supervision, all in accordance with the supervision plan, so that the works follow the Buildings Ordinance and its regulations, the plans approved by the Building Authority, and any approval or consent conditions. Quality supervision is part of that system: the Code guides foundation, ground-investigation and soil-nailing inspections and critical stages, and this note’s Appendix A adds reference items for superstructure, excavation and lateral support, and site formation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Checklists, frequencies, and site records

The AP, RSE, RGE and authorized signatory devise checklists from the Code’s typical items, plus any other item essential to the project, including quality supervision and conditions imposed at approval or consent, for their TCPs of all grades. Heads of the project management structure agree the checklists, inspection frequencies and critical stages with the other streams. Appendix A tells them to add, for superstructure, items such as curtain walls, glass walls, prestressed concrete or structural steel where the project needs them.

The AP, RSE, RGE and AS operate their parts of the supervision plan, inspect the site (including personally), and see that their representatives and TCPs discharge their duties. Checklists and inspection records are kept on site for the Site Monitoring Section or other representatives of the Building Authority. Appendix B is a sample record the streams may adapt. Results are Satisfactory (S), Not Satisfactory (NS), or Not Applicable (NA). A non-conformity that is very minor and is rectified to the TCP’s satisfaction at the same inspection is recorded as satisfactory. An NS result is recorded in the site supervision report and, where applicable, Form B. Form B is required where the non-conformity has material concern for safety, or where it does not have material concern for safety but the item or the rectification cannot be verified because the area was covered up at inspection or re-inspection.

**SD takeaway:** Keep couplers, starter bars, cast-in anchors, fire barriers, waterproofing and backfill open to inspection until the relevant stream has signed them, because a covered non-conformity requires Form B.

### 2. Superstructure — in-situ reinforced concrete

Quality supervision of superstructure is by the AP/RSE and their TCPs as appropriate, and by the authorized signatory of the registered general building contractor and his TCPs. For QC1–QC11 the stream marks are RSE and RC. The RSE/RC split for formwork and falsework (QC7) is in clauses 4.7 to 4.9 of the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009.

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| QC1 Essential elements | Location, layout and sizes of reinforced concrete elements |
| QC2 Reinforcing bars | Delivery records and batch size; sampling and testing to the applicable CS2 |
| QC3 Fixing | Sizes, location and amount; cover and starter bars; workmanship to PNAP ADV-15 |
| QC4 Concrete quality | Delivery records; sampling, compression tests and cube statistics to the applicable CS1; high-strength concrete conditions, including coring |
| QC5 Placing | Quality and workmanship of placing, compaction and curing |
| QC6 Transfer structure | QC5, plus sequence, temporary work, concreting method and curing method |
| QC7 Formwork and falsework | Stability before concreting; no early stripping |
| QC8 After stripping | Quality of concrete after formwork is stripped |
| QC9 Couplers | Delivery records and batch size; sampling and testing to imposed conditions and design criteria; tightening records |
| QC10 Cast-in embeds or anchors | Location, layout and types; sampling and testing to imposed conditions |
| QC11 In-situ strength | Coring or rebound hammer, as used |
| QCn | Any other essential superstructure item |

**SD takeaway:** Transfer structures, couplers, cast-in anchors and high-strength concrete need their consent conditions and test holds on the RSE and RC checklists before those details are covered.

### 3. Superstructure — general building works

Stream marks for QB1–QB5 are AP and RC. Each item includes conformity with the general layout on the approved plans.

| Item | Critical checks named in Appendix A |
|---|---|
| QB1 Basements and G/F | Lanes and streets within the site, emergency vehicular access, building setback, dedicated areas for public passage, areas surrendered for road widening, storey height, firefighting and rescue staircases, required staircases, smoke vents |
| QB2 Upper floors and roofs | Internal dedicated areas, storey height, projections, balconies, utility platforms, protective barriers, required staircases, refuge floor and/or roof |
| QB3 Interior and fitting-out | Internal dedicated areas, exit routes, fireman’s lift lobbies, temporary refuge spaces; gross-floor-area exemption or modification features (balconies, utility platforms, wider common corridors and lift lobbies, plant rooms, pipe ducts, pipe wells, residents’ recreational facilities); fire barriers and proprietary fire-resisting products, including FRR of fire doors and smoke seal; suitable waterproofing for roofs |
| QB4 Windows | Minimum glazing area and openable area of prescribed windows, and of windows to rooms containing soil or waste fitments; PNAP APP-116 (design, installation and water-tightness test); provisions required under a modification or exemption |
| QB5 Barrier-free access, if applicable | Critical dimensions of tactile guide paths, tactile warning strips, car parking spaces, dropped kerbs, service counters, accessible lifts, ramps, landings, bathrooms, corridors, lobbies, paths, dead-end manoeuvring spaces, handrail extensions, clear door width and the unobstructed area beside the door handle, accessible toilets and urinals; the applicable Design Manual: Barrier Free Access, including tactile and braille map, automatic main entrance doors, braille and tactile information on handrails, and signage |

The note’s examples of critical exit-route checks are the continuous path from any part of a building to the ultimate place of safety; clear staircase height and width, treads and risers, and general configuration (for example not more than 16 risers); and adequate signage and lighting.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze emergency vehicular access, setbacks, surrendered areas, refuge, gross-floor-area exemptions, prescribed windows and barrier-free dimensions on the general building plans, because those are the AP and RC quality checks.

### 4. Excavation and lateral support

Quality supervision is by the AP/RSE and their TCPs as appropriate, and by the authorized signatory of the registered general building contractor and his TCPs. The RGE stream is included only when qualified supervision of the geotechnical aspects is imposed as a condition of approval.

Where a row is marked *, the AP and his TCPs see that the appropriate building professionals (RSE and/or RGE) and the RC check and supervise the works, and they coordinate follow-up on non-conformity.

| Item | Streams | Check |
|---|---|---|
| QE1 Temporary retaining planking — location | AP*, RSE, RC | Location and layout |
| QE2 Planking installation | RSE, RC | Material, sizes and installation procedure |
| QE3 Grout curtain | RGE, RC | Grout mix, pressure and volume, and compliance with the method statement |
| QE4 Pumping test or other ground-treatment test | RGE, RC | Testing procedures |
| QE5 Struts and lateral support | AP*, RSE, RC | Method statement and sequence for erection and removal of walings and struts, excavation in stages |
| QE6 Preloading of struts | RSE, RC | Preloading procedures |
| QE7 Excavation | AP*, RSE, RGE, RC | Stage-by-stage excavation; no over-excavation, excessive temporary cutting slopes, or stockpiling that adversely affects adjoining ground, structure or building |
| QE8 Backfilling | AP*, RSE, RGE, RC | Compaction, sequence, plant, materials, final levels and tests agree with the approved plans or accepted proposal |
| QE9 Instrumentation and monitoring | AP*, RSE, RGE, RC | Monitoring points in working order and read in time; no excessive ground or building movement, groundwater drawdown, or water ingress |
| QEn | as appropriate | Any other essential excavation and lateral support item |

**SD takeaway:** Put the staged excavation sequence and adjoining-building monitoring on the approved proposal, and include the RGE stream when geotechnical qualified supervision is a consent condition.

### 5. Site formation, rock slopes, retaining walls and reinforced fill

Quality supervision is by the AP, RSE and RGE and their TCPs, and by the authorized signatory of the registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor and his TCPs, as appropriate.

- \* — AP and TCPs see that the appropriate professionals and the RC check and supervise, and they coordinate non-conformity.
- # — AP and TCPs check that the works comply with the general layouts on the approved plans.
- ^ — Waterproofing and insulation (QRW8) for retaining walls forming part of a domestic building, as required under regulation 48 of the Building (Planning) Regulations.

| Item | Streams | Check |
|---|---|---|
| QSF1 Monitoring | AP*, RGE, RC | Ground or building movement, or groundwater drawdown: points working, readings correct and timely |
| QSF2 Site drainage | AP#, RGE, RC | Site drainage is carried out |
| QSF3 Rainstorm protection | AP#, RGE, RC | Protection of earthworks against heavy rainstorm |
| QSF4 Cut / fill profile | AP#, RGE, RC | Location, extent and gradient |
| QSF5 Excavation | AP*, RGE, RC | Stage-by-stage; no over-excavation, excessive temporary slopes, or harmful stockpiling |
| QSF6 Backfilling | AP*, RGE, RC | Compaction, sequence, plant, materials, levels and tests agree with approved plans or accepted proposal, and with PNAP APP-15 |
| QSF7 Backfill records | RGE, RC | Backfilling works and test results are recorded |
| QRS1 Rock cut setting-out | AP#, RGE, RC | Location, extent and gradient of the rock cut slope |
| QRS2 Rock excavation | AP*, RGE, RC | Sequence, method and standard of construction to PNAP APP-17 |
| QRS3 Scaling and trimming | RGE, RC | Done without affecting stability and integrity of the slope |
| QRS4 Treatment design | RGE, RC | Design assumptions and joint sets agree with the design and the approved plans |
| QRS5 Treatment setting-out | RGE, RC | Location, extent and layout of rock-slope treatment |
| QRS6 Treatment quality | RGE, RC | Material, sampling and testing |
| QRW1 Retaining wall location | AP#, RSE, RGE, RC | Location, extent and layout |
| QRW2 Excavation | AP*, RGE, RC | Stage-by-stage; no over-excavation, excessive temporary slopes, or harmful stockpiling |
| QRW3 Bearing stratum | RGE, RC | Ground bearing stratum agrees with approved plans, site investigation reports and design assumptions |
| QRW4 Reinforcement | RSE, RC | Sizes, amount, cover and workmanship to PNAP ADV-15 |
| QRW5 Concrete quality | RSE, RC | Delivery records, cube tests to the applicable CS1; high-strength concrete conditions, including coring |
| QRW6 Concrete placing | RSE, RC | Placing, compaction and curing |
| QRW7 Drainage and filter | RGE, RC | Drainage and filter material to PNAP APP-54 |
| QRW8 Waterproofing and insulation ^ | AP, RC | Materials and workmanship |
| QRW9 Backfilling | AP*, RGE, RC | Compaction and records agree with approved plans and with PNAP APP-15 and APP-54 |
| QRF1 Reinforced fill setting-out | AP#, RGE, RC | Location, extent and layout |
| QRF2 Drainage and filter | RGE, RC | To PNAP APP-54 |
| QRF3 Backfilling | AP*, RGE, RC | Agrees with approved plans or accepted proposal, and with PNAP APP-15 |
| QRF4 Backfill records | RGE, RC | Works and test results are recorded |

**SD takeaway:** Match cut, fill and rock profiles to the approved general layout the AP stream checks, and keep retaining-wall waterproofing (Building (Planning) Regulations regulation 48), backfill (PNAP APP-15) and filters (PNAP APP-54) open to the stream marked on that row.

A similar practice note has been issued to AP, RSE and RGE.
