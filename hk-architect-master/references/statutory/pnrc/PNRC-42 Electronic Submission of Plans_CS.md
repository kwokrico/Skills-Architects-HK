# PNRC-42 Electronic Submission of Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2000 / Last revision March 2025 / This revision May 2026 (AD/CS) (Paragraphs 3, 4, 5 & 6 amended, and paragraph 17 added) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note sets the electronic format, manner, and procedure for plans, documents, and specified forms under the Buildings Ordinance. Failure to observe that format, manner, and procedure, and the requirements of the system used, invalidates the submission. It does not replace the specified-form list in PNRC 2, and it does not fix paper counter addresses.

## Regulatory Overview
The Electronic Transactions Ordinance (Cap. 553) gives an electronic record the same standing as writing where the information is accessible for subsequent reference (section 5), and a digital signature supported by a recognised certificate and generated within that certificate's validity the same standing as a signature (section 6); those provisions have operated since 7 April 2000. The Buildings Department has set up the Electronic Submission Hub, e-Counter, the Electronic Forms Submission System, and the Registration and Minor Works Management System to receive electronic submissions under the Buildings Ordinance, and a submission made under paragraphs 8 to 17 is treated as submitted to the corresponding designated system for section 19(2) of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Sheet setup for a plan submitted on the Electronic Submission Hub
The Hub is at https://esh.bd.gov.hk and operates 24 hours a day and 7 days a week except scheduled maintenance and unexpected downtime. Authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered geotechnical engineers may submit with a personal digital certificate or "iAM Smart". The e-Workspace may be used to prepare plans for digital signature by the project owner, the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer, or the registered contractor; plans prepared there are deleted after the submission is made. Only one set is required. Under regulation 11(2) of the Building (Administration) Regulations the Building Authority may still require additional hard copies, and may reply in hard copy. After a submission is made via the Hub, subsequent amendment submissions and associated documents should continue via the Hub. By using the Hub, the user accepts it as the designated information system under section 19(2)(a)(i) of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance for receiving electronic records from the Buildings Department. Regulations 36 and 40 of the Building (Administration) Regulations still require equipment and provisions so electronic records can be supplied to the concerned parties, kept on site, and produced to the Building Authority.

Appendix A records what each stage added. Stage 3, launched 30 June 2024, covers all types of plans, including general building plans and alteration and addition plans, and related applications.

| Plan file (Appendix B) | Rule |
|---|---|
| Format | PDF, no security restrictions. Native PDF from CAD or BIM is recommended. A scan needs a minimum resolution of 300 dpi and 24-bit colour depth. |
| Contents | One drawing per file. No multiple layers, and no post-edited comments, mark-up, or annotation. |
| Name and size | File name not exceeding 50 alphanumeric characters. Each file not larger than 25 MB. |
| Signature | Digital signature using the signing tool in the Hub. |
| Sheet | Paper size as specified in PNAP ADM-10, landscape. |
| Stamp void | Minimum unused space 90 mm wide by 150 mm high at the lower right corner of every plan, for the system stamps that the plan has been digitally signed by the authorized person, registered structural engineer, and/or registered geotechnical engineer, and for the Building Authority's stamps of approval and certification of copies, in accordance with PNAP ADM-10. |
| Plan index (Appendix C) | Completed online with each submission. Each entry needs a unique description of drawing title, drawing number, and revision number, each not exceeding 50 alphanumeric characters. |
| Title block the Hub can read | Case sensitive, in separate rows, in machine-readable text: "Drawing Title:", "Drawing Number:", "Revision Number:". |
| Sample borders on Diagram 1 | Unused minimum border on four sides: A0 20 mm, A1 20 mm, A2 10 mm, A3 10 mm, A4 10 mm. |

Other prescribed documents: single-layer PDF, no security restrictions, file name not exceeding 50 alphanumeric characters, each file not larger than 500 MB, signed with the Hub signing tool, and a minimum unused space of 100 mm wide by 65 mm high at the lower right corner of the front page. CAD and BIM files not larger than 500 MB each may be submitted as supplementary information; PNAP ADM-19 and ADV-34 set the further requirements.

**SD takeaway:** Fix every schematic sheet in landscape to the PNAP ADM-10 size, with the 90 mm by 150 mm lower-right stamp void, the three title-block captions, one drawing per file, and no layers or mark-up, before the drawing set is treated as Hub-ready.

### 2. e-Counter, Electronic Forms Submission System, and Registration and Minor Works Management System
The identity of the person making an electronic submission is authenticated by a digital certificate issued by the Postmaster General or another certification authority recognised by the Commissioner for Digital Policy. e-Counter submissions go to receipt@bd.gov.hk as attachments in a digitally signed email, and should not involve voluminous documents, a customised programme, or large-size drawings.

| Channel | Limit stated in the note |
|---|---|
| e-Counter, specified forms | PDF, digital signatures at the specified locations on the form. |
| e-Counter, other records | PDF, each page not larger than A3, each document digitally signed; or TIFF or JPEG at 200 dpi, the two adjacent sides not greater than 3300 pixels by 2340 pixels, 24-bit colour for a colour document, each document digitally signed. |
| e-Counter, whole email | Total size, including attachments and digital signatures, not exceeding 25 MB. |
| Electronic Forms Submission System | Forms and attachments as detailed in PNRC 2, signed with digital signatures. Total size, including attachments and digital signatures, not exceeding 25 MB. Typing the form stores the information in the QR code. |
| Registration and Minor Works Management System | Forms and supporting documents as detailed in PNRC 2. Digital certificate of Hongkong Post or Digi-Sign, or "iAM Smart". Each form not exceeding 25 MB. Launched 29 May 2026 at https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk. |

**SD takeaway:** Keep an e-Counter email to 25 MB, with each PDF page not larger than A3; drawings that exceed those limits use the Electronic Submission Hub sheet rules in topic 1.

### 3. When the statutory clock starts, and the Form BA10 modification
For a statutory time period under the Buildings Ordinance and the relevant regulations, paper submissions and electronic submissions via the Electronic Submission Hub are computed the same way. Time starts on the next day after receipt, whether or not that next day is a public holiday, a gale warning day, or a black rainstorm warning day. A submission at 23:59 on a Saturday starts on Sunday. A submission at 00:00 on a Sunday starts on Monday.

Form BA10 signed with a digital signature and submitted via the Hub is checked automatically. The Buildings Department is prepared to favourably consider a modification of regulation 20(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations so that notification of appointment of the registered contractor and commencement of building works or street works may be not less than 1 day before commencement, if Form BA10 is submitted via the Hub. The authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer may apply for that modification together with the consent application. The modification will be conditioned on submission of Form BA10 via the Hub. Regulation 20(2) and (3) remain: notification of technically competent persons, the related information, and a supervision plan, not less than 7 days before commencement.

**SD takeaway:** A Hub BA10 can be programmed at not less than 1 day before commencement only if the modification of regulation 20(1) is granted; the technically competent person notice, information, and supervision plan stay at not less than 7 days under regulation 20(2) and (3).
