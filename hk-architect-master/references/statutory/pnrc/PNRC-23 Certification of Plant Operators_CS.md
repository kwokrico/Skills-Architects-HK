# PNRC-23 Certification of Plant Operators
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued February 1994; legislation effective 24 July 1994 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs who may operate lifting appliances and lifting gear on the contractor’s site, under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59). It is not a structural design note for the crane, gondola or lifting point, and the attached certificate forms are image pages with no readable fields in this extract.

## Regulatory Overview

From 24 July 1994, every lifting appliance and every item of lifting gear may be operated only by a person who has been appropriately trained and who holds a relevant and current certificate. The certificate is in the format attached to the note and is issued by the Construction Industry Training Authority, or by another authority approved by the Commissioner for Labour.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Current operator certificate before the plant is used

Failure to comply is an offence under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59). The contractor must see that every plant operator under the contractor’s control is trained and certificated. Training left until the last moment risks overloading the training authorities immediately before the effective date. Pages 2 to 7 of the note are the certificate forms; this extract contains no readable form fields, so no certificate layout is stated here.

**SD takeaway:** Any scheme that depends on a site crane, hoist or other lifting appliance needs certificated operators in the programme, with training booked before the plant is required.
