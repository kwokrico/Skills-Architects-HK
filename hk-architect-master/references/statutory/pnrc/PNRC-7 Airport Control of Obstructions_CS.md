# PNRC-7 Airport Control of Obstructions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue August 1982; last revision November 1998; this revision November 2015 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Contractor note that Cap 301 height control covers temporary plant projecting skywards. It does **not** publish prescribed heights or obstacle-limitation surfaces.

## Regulatory Overview
The Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance (Cap 301) restricts the height of any building to the height that Ordinance prescribes, and a building includes any mast, pole, pile driver, scaffolding, hoist, crane or other structure projecting skywards. Tower cranes and any temporary plant or equipment on a construction project must stay within those prescribed limits unless the Director-General of Civil Aviation grants a temporary exemption under closely monitored conditions.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What Cap 301 treats as a building
| Item | Rule in this note |
|---|---|
| Height | Restricted to the height prescribed under Cap 301 |
| “Building” | Includes any mast or pole, and any pile driver, scaffolding, hoist, crane or other structure projecting skywards |
| Construction projects | Tower cranes and temporary plant or equipment must not exceed the prescribed height restrictions |

**SD takeaway:** Crane height, scaffold height and any skyward temporary structure are airport-obstruction items from the start of site planning, on the same footing as the permanent building.

### 2. Temporary exemption
Under closely monitored conditions, the Director-General of Civil Aviation may grant a temporary exemption so that construction equipment can be erected above the statutory height limits. Apply on the standard form from the Airport Standards Division of the Civil Aviation Department (CAD Headquarters, 1 Tung Fai Road, Hong Kong International Airport), by fax on 2795 8469, or from the CAD website.

**SD takeaway:** A crane or plant strategy above the prescribed limit is a CAD exemption, arranged before that plant is erected.
