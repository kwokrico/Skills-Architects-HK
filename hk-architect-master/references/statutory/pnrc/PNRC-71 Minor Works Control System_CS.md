# PNRC-71 Minor Works Control System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2010; last revision December 2020; this revision September 2021 (AD/CS) (paragraphs 15 to 17 and Appendices A and C amended) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Practice note for the simplified Minor Works Control System and for inspection and certification of prescribed building or building works. Item-by-item size and location criteria sit in Schedule 1 to the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, which this note does not reproduce, except the radio-base-station item numbers and the Appendix F sample.

## Regulatory Overview
The Minor Works Control System allows building owners and occupants to carry out designated small-scale building works through simplified statutory procedures, without prior approval of plans and consent to commencement from the Building Authority. A total of 187 minor-works items are classified into three classes, and the size, location and criteria of each item are set out in Schedule 1 to the Building (Minor Works) Regulation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Three classes, and who is appointed

| | Class I | Class II | Class III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Count in the note | 58 items | 68 items | 61 items |
| Example printed in paragraph 2 | Internal staircases connecting two floors | Repair of external walls | Supporting frames for air-conditioners |
| Appointment printed in the paragraph 4 table | Prescribed building professional to design and supervise, and prescribed registered contractor to carry out the works | Prescribed registered contractor to prepare and submit the plans and documents, and to supervise and carry out the works | The table does not print a prescribed-building-professional duty in this column |
| Who submits | Authorized person / registered inspector / prescribed registered contractor | Prescribed registered contractor | Prescribed registered contractor |
| Submission | Notice of commencement of works, and certificate of completion of works | Same pair of submissions | Notice and certificate of completion of works |

Prescribed building professional means an authorized person and, if required, a registered structural engineer, a registered geotechnical engineer, or a registered inspector. Under section 27 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, a registered inspector assumes the role of an authorized person for the prescribed repair, or any associated demolition, that is Class I minor works. Prescribed registered contractor means a registered general building contractor, a registered specialist contractor, or a registered minor works contractor.

Where a supervision plan is required, the prescribed building professional and prescribed registered contractor must comply with the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans and the Code of Practice for Site Supervision. Under section 57, the prescribed registered contractor for Class I or Class II works keeps on site copies of all prescribed plans, details and the supervision plan as required, and the Buildings Department acknowledgement of the notice of commencement.

If the prescribed registered contractor ceases to be appointed, the specified form goes to the Building Authority within 7 days for Class II, and to the authorized person within 7 days for Class I so that the authorized person can submit it to the Building Authority.

**SD takeaway:** Match the works to a Schedule 1 class before schematic freeze, because Class I needs a prescribed building professional to design and supervise while Class II and Class III are carried through the prescribed registered contractor.

### 2. Documents, minor deviations, and the 7-day rule
Appendix A: the prescribed building professional or prescribed registered contractor keeps records of site supervision, structural calculations, test reports and certificates, and provides them to the Building Authority when required (section 56 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation). The acknowledgement letter assigns one submission number for the whole submission, for example MW101200001.

| | Notice of commencement (Classes I and II) | Certificate of completion (Classes I and II) | Class III notice and certificate of completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specified form | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Photographs | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Plans | Coloured prescribed plans and details | Revised coloured prescribed plans and details showing minor deviations, if any | Coloured plans or a description of the works |
| Supervision plan | As required by the Technical Memorandum, Class I only | — | — |
| Other documents named | For example geotechnical assessment, ground investigation report, structural calculations, test reports and certificates | For example schedule of building materials and products, test reports and certificates | For example schedule of building materials and products, test reports and certificates |

After the acknowledgement of the notice of commencement, a slight amendment to suit the site may be shown on revised plans with the certificate of completion if the minor deviations do not affect the original design assumptions and still tally with the descriptions of the submitted items, including a reduction in quantity. A new notice of commencement is required, not less than 7 days before commencement, if the change affects the original design assumptions, does not tally with the descriptions including an increase in quantity, or involves additional minor works items. Additional items may be incorporated with the specified form for additional minor works; all proposed minor works are coloured on the revised plans, and the additional items are delineated.

PNAP ADM-14 does not apply to minor-works submissions or to inspection and certification of prescribed building or building works. Minor amendments to submitted forms, plans and documents may be arranged at the Buildings Department case by case.

If alteration or strengthening under the validation schemes is Class I or Class II, Form MW01 or MW03 notifies commencement at least 7 days before the works.

**SD takeaway:** A change that increases quantity, adds a minor-works item, or alters the design assumptions needs a new notice of commencement at least 7 days before the work, not a note on the completion certificate.

### 3. Validation cut-off dates
Certification under section 39C of the Buildings Ordinance and sections 62 and 62A of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation is that the prescribed building or building works are structurally safe and comply with the Buildings Ordinance, except section 14(1) of the Buildings Ordinance and regulation 25 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. After certification, no enforcement action is taken unless the safety conditions have changed. Alteration or strengthening may be carried out before certification. The Household Minor Works Validation Scheme and Minor Amenity Feature Validation Scheme submission is Form MW06-1, MW06-2 or MW06-3, within 14 days after the inspection and, if alteration or strengthening is involved, within 14 days after completion of those minor works, with photographs and descriptions and, if any, colour plans or a description of the minor works.

The Household Minor Works Validation Scheme and Minor Amenity Feature Validation Scheme flowchart prints the question “Is the structure non-concrete canopy projecting ≤ 750 mm?” and also prints advice that the scheme is not applicable. The text extract does not set out which answer leads to that advice.

**SD takeaway:** Existing air-conditioner frames, drying racks and small canopies are on the Household Minor Works Validation Scheme only if erected or carried out before 31 December 2010; the further amenity features in footnote 1 use 1 September 2020.

### 4. Other departments, Area Number 3, and fees
Documents for minor works under the simplified requirements, and for inspection and certification of prescribed building or building works, are not referred to other government departments. If the works fall under another department’s jurisdiction, the prescribed building professional or prescribed registered contractor consults that department and obtains any approval or consent required. All submissions under the simplified requirements and for inspection and certification of prescribed building or building works are free of charge.

Exception printed in paragraph 19: on a notice of commencement for Class I or Class II minor works involving spread footing, excavation or underground drainage within Area Number 3 of the scheduled areas in Schedule 5 to the Buildings Ordinance, the Buildings Department forwards a copy to the MTR Corporation Limited. The MTR Corporation Limited conveys comments to the prescribed building professional or prescribed registered contractor directly. That person informs the MTR Corporation Limited and obtains its agreement before commencement. Section 27 of the Railways Ordinance (Cap. 519) or section 15 of the Mass Transit Railway (Land Resumption and Related Provisions) Ordinance (Cap. 276) will be invoked if the minor works would be incompatible with railway construction, maintenance, improvement or operation.

**SD takeaway:** In Area Number 3, do not start Class I or Class II spread footing, excavation or underground drainage until the MTR Corporation Limited has agreed, even though the minor-works file itself is not circulated to other departments.

### 5. Plans, photographs, and signboard numbers
Appendix B, citing section 38 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation: every plan and section of minor works must be drawn to a ratio of not less than 1:100, and every block plan to a ratio of not less than 1:500. Photographs must show the physical conditions immediately before commencement and after completion (sections 30(b), 31(b), 32(b), 33(b), 34(b), 35(b) and 36(c)). Before photographs are to show that the works had not commenced before the submission. Before and after photographs are taken from the same angle, and the angle and photo numbers are marked on the plans. Generally, 4R-size photographs are acceptable. For repair of an entire building, wide-angled photographs of elevations, drainage and premises are acceptable, and close-ups of every spot are not required where the prescribed plans already show the locations. Coloured plans not larger than A3 are recommended. The address on the Rating and Valuation Department demand for rates and/or government rent is recommended. Supplementary documents go with standard form MW33.

Appendix F is a sample plan for Class III item 3.27, erection of a metal supporting frame for an air-conditioning unit. The sample’s notes, not a reproduction of Schedule 1, state: the works do not result in any additional load to any cantilevered slab; no part of the frame projects more than 600 mm from the wall; the distance between the highest point of the frame and the ground is more than 3 m; the frame is designed for an air-conditioning unit of not more than 100 kg. The sample annotation is a frame 500 mm wide with 600 mm projection for a unit of approximate 30 kg. If the location is at the lowest ground storey, that ground distance is described on the plan. The sample recommends A3 paper with a minimum 10 mm border, new works coloured, and a block plan at not less than 1:500.

Appendix D standards for the recommended display of the minor-works submission number, or the Buildings Department reference number on a signboard erected or altered with prior approval and consent: block capitals, minimum 35 mm high, Arial. Black on white for a signboard under the simplified requirements (example MW101212345), for a consented signboard (examples BD 2/9876/10 and BD 2-3/9876/10(6)), and for new alteration works other than solely replacing the display surface, in which case the existing number is removed. Black on yellow where minor works alter or replace the display surface of an existing signboard that is unauthorised or was not erected under the simplified requirements. The number is on durable material at a conspicuous position: nearest pavement for a projecting signboard, a signboard on or hung under the soffit of a balcony or canopy, or a signboard fixed on-grade; nearest or opposite pavement for a wall signboard; main roof if the signboard is on the roof. Before Class III signboard works, the prescribed registered contractor is recommended to request a submission number on Form MW32. Display of numbers on signboards erected or altered with prior approval and consent is also covered by PNAP APP-126.

**SD takeaway:** Draw minor-works plans at not less than 1:100 and block plans at not less than 1:500, and treat the Appendix F 600 mm, 3 m and 100 kg notes as the sample for item 3.27 rather than as the Schedule 1 text.

### 6. Radio base station, antenna or transceiver
Appendix E: items that may be carried out under the simplified requirements are 1.14, 1.28, 1.50, 2.12, 2.31, 2.49, 3.2, 3.8, 3.26, 3.27 and 3.50. Detailed descriptions are in Schedule 1. The works covered by that list are a radio base station solely for telecommunication services in the form of an equipment cabinet on the roof, or an antenna or transceiver projecting from an external wall or on the roof.

Public telecommunication operators obtain approvals, where necessary, from the Office of the Telecommunications Authority, the Town Planning Board, the Buildings Department and the Lands Department before installing radio base stations on rooftops or mounting antennas on external walls. For erection or alteration of the supporting structure, the appointed authorized person submits to the Office of the Telecommunications Authority a declaration that the proposed radio base station complies with Buildings Department requirements, using that office’s guidance note. Removal of the supporting structures or frames, and of the equipment cabinet, may also be minor works under the simplified requirements.

**SD takeaway:** A rooftop equipment cabinet or an external antenna is a minor-works item only where it is one of the Appendix E item numbers, and the operator’s Office of the Telecommunications Authority, planning and lands approvals remain outside the minor-works submission.
