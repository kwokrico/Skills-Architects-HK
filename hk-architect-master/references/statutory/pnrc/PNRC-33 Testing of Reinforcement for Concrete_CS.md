# PNRC-33 Testing of Reinforcement for Concrete
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1996; last revision April 2019; this revision May 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: governs Building Authority purchaser’s tests on steel reinforcing bars delivered to site or to a prefabrication factory, under Buildings Ordinance section 17(1)6. It is not the manufacturer’s routine testing at the rolling mill.

## Regulatory Overview
When structural plans are approved or consent is granted, the Building Authority may require testing to verify the specified properties of steel reinforcing bars delivered to site and/or the prefabrication factory, in addition to the manufacturer’s routine tests at the rolling mill. Buildings Department adopts Construction Standard CS2:2012 as an acceptable standard, and the AP and RSE should ensure specimens are selected at random from every batch delivered to site and/or the prefabrication factory.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What CS2 covers (para 2)

Under CS2, rebars include straight bars, coil and decoiled products, except cold-worked rebars. CS2 gives material specifications for grade 250 (for rebars up to 12 mm diameter), grade 500B and grade 500C, including mass per metre, chemical composition, mechanical properties and bond property, and it specifies certification of Quality Assured (QA) Stockists and QA Coil Processors and the purchaser’s testing.

**SD takeaway:** Specify grade 250 only up to 12 mm diameter, or grade 500B or 500C, and exclude cold-worked bars from a CS2 compliance route.

### 2. HOKLAS purchaser’s test and the report (paras 3–4)

Verification tests imposed under section 17(1)6 are the purchaser’s tests in CS2 and are performed by a laboratory accredited by the Hong Kong Accreditation Service under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (HOKLAS). The test report includes classification and certification by QA Stockists for Class 1 and Class 2 rebars; a full description of the specimens (steel manufacturer, country of origin, grade and size as stated by the person submitting them); a sketch of the deformation pattern and mill marks; batch identification and the date of delivery to the site or prefabrication factory; measured mass per metre; and, if required, chemical composition (product analysis), tensile properties, bend performance and bond property.

**SD takeaway:** Rebar compliance on a private project is a HOKLAS purchaser’s test report tied to the delivery batch, not a mill certificate alone.

### 3. Coils, approved prefabrication yards, and the 21-day submission (paras 5–10)

| Route | What the note requires |
|---|---|
| Coils and decoiled products | Amendment No. 2/2018 to CS2 and Technical Note No. 5, both issued on 26 September 2018: technical, inspection and testing requirements; all coil processors to be QA Coil Processors; recognition of off-site yards approved under Technical Circular (Works) No. 10/2018 as QA Coil Processors; transitional delivery documentation |
| Approved Yards | CEDD List of Approved Steel Reinforcing Bar Prefabrication Yards; CEDD Independent Audit Team monitors the yard quality management system and, for public works and housing, centralised batch sampling and testing to CS2 |
| Products within the yard’s approved scope | Cutting and bending, reinforcement cages, and threading or coupling with reinforcement connectors are considered to conform to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 (2020 Edition) if qualified supervision follows the Buildings Ordinance and its subsidiary regulations |
| Plan approval | A section 17(1) item 6 condition requires qualified site supervision by the RSE and the registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor, including sampling and testing of rebars |
| Streamlined sampling | Batch sampling under the Approved Yard’s quality management system, witnessed by the Independent Audit Team, is accepted as qualified site supervision for sampling under Section 5 of CS2; the RSE and contractor check the yard’s sampling records, witness records and HOKLAS test reports for that project |
| On delivery to site | Keep a copy of those records and reports on site for Buildings Department inspection; submit to Buildings Department, within 21 days of delivery, the list of prefabricated rebar products used and an RSE statement that the rebars comply with the Buildings Ordinance and its subsidiary regulations |
| Alternative | The RSE and contractor may arrange their own quality control supervisor and quality control coordinator to supervise sampling, delivery to their HOKLAS laboratory, and testing to CS2 |

**SD takeaway:** Off-site cages and threaded bars are acceptable from a CEDD Approved Yard only with qualified supervision, and the product list plus the RSE’s compliance statement must reach Buildings Department within 21 days of delivery to site.
