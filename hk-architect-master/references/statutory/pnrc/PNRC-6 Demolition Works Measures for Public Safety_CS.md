# PNRC-6 Demolition Works Measures for Public Safety
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 1982; last revision January 2021; this revision September 2022 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs how a registered specialist contractor supervises demolition, records it, and protects the public, including a streamlined path for minor works carried out by the same project team before the parent building comes down. It is not the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 (that code is applied through this note and PNRC 83) and it is not an asbestos-abatement procedure.

## Regulatory Overview

Demolition by a registered specialist contractor runs under a supervision plan, personal supervision with Form BA20 on site, the Demolition Code, and a video record of the whole process. Minor works that prepare the building for demolition can be notified with the demolition plans when the same authorized person, registered structural engineer and registered specialist contractor carry both packages.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Supervision plan, personal supervision and the site engineer

| Duty | Rule |
|---|---|
| Continuous supervision | Registered specialist contractor, under Buildings Ordinance section 9(6) and Building (Administration) Regulations regulation 41, in line with the supervision plan and the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans 2009 (Buildings Ordinance section 39A) |
| Lodging the plan | Authorized person lodges it before or with the consent application, except works that meet every criterion in paragraph 11 of that Technical Memorandum |
| Contractor’s part of the plan | Prepared to the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 (2021 Edition) |
| Technically competent persons | Qualifications, competence and experience relevant to demolition; Hong Kong Institute of Construction runs a “Demolition of Building Course for Supervisors/Foremen” |
| Personal supervision | Building (Demolition Works) Regulations regulation 8; Form BA20 posted in a prominent position naming the appointed technically competent persons |
| Demolition Code | Full-time site engineer for complex structures, plus the debris disposal and management system and the other execution rules in the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 |
| Change of site engineer | Report immediately to the authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer and the Building Authority; the engineer is directly responsible to the registered specialist contractor |

**SD takeaway:** Demolition consent is not ready until the supervision plan, Form BA20 posting and, for a complex structure, a full-time site engineer are in the brief.

### 2. Video record and powered plant operators

Video cameras, provided by the registered specialist contractor, record the entire demolition, including debris movement and the overall sequence. Locations are agreed with the authorized person, registered structural engineer and registered geotechnical engineer, shown on the demolition plan, and protected from tampering. The number is fixed by those professionals; there is at least one camera on every site. The contractor keeps the records for at least **14 days**.

Operators of powered mechanical plant or equipment must meet Building (Demolition Works) Regulations regulation 9(3). For regulation 9(3)(b), the operator completes the Hong Kong Institute of Construction “Demolition of Building Course for Plant Operators” and holds the certificate; an equivalent certificate from a recognised examining body may be accepted. A crane and hammer in a densely populated area is given as an example of plant that may be unsafe and should not be allowed. Consent is accompanied, under Building (Administration) Regulations regulation 31, by each operator’s name in Chinese and English, Hong Kong identity card number, date of birth, address, telephone number, and documents of qualifications and experience. The Building Authority vets suitability. A change of operator is notified within **7 days** under regulation 22(7), with the new particulars.

**SD takeaway:** The demolition plan shows camera positions, and the consent set includes named, certificated plant operators — a crane-and-hammer method is a public-safety decision, not a contractor detail added later.

### 3. Fire, drains, public passage and completion

| Hazard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fire or explosion | Remove all inflammable goods; store any other flammable goods properly; Building (Demolition Works) Regulations regulation 6; seek departmental help if suspected hazardous materials are found |
| Drainage | Seal every sewer and drainage connection before demolition starts (regulation 3(2)(b) of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations) |
| Third parties | Hoardings, covered walkways, gantries and catch platforms maintained for safe passage at all times; minimise dust, noise and vibration |
| Completion | Within **7 days**, the contractor certifies on Form BA14A or Form BA14B; consult the authorized person and registered structural engineer on the fast-track acceptance route before submitting Form BA14B |

**SD takeaway:** Seal drainage and set the hoarding line before demolition starts, and hold seven days after completion for the BA14A / BA14B certificate.

### 4. Minor works before the parent building is demolished (Appendix A)

Certain demolition-related minor works may use the Minor Works Control System (Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation and PNRC 71) instead of prior approval and consent. Separately, Appendix A streamlines minor works done before demolition — removal of unauthorised building works, projecting structures and windows, precautionary measures, and openings in slabs to convey debris — when the authorized person, registered structural engineer and registered specialist contractor for the demolition are also the team for those minor works.

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Commencement | Form MW01 not less than **7 days** before the minor works, submitted with the demolition-plan approval application by the same team |
| Drawings | Demolition plans show the minor works and state that protective and precautionary measures follow the Technical Guidelines on the Minor Works Control System |
| Photographs at notification | Not required if the locations are already in the building appraisal report submitted with the demolition plans |
| Completion | Form MW02 with Form BA14A / BA14B; photographs of the completed minor works are not required if the whole parent building is then demolished |
| Minor works finished before Form BA10 | Submit Form MW02 separately, with the Minor Works Control System documents, to New Buildings Division 1 |

The minor works are carried out by the registered specialist contractor (demolition) who is also a registered general building contractor or a registered minor works contractor for the relevant classes and types. The authorized signatory supervising the parent demolition is the same authorized signatory for those minor works.

**SD takeaway:** Pre-demolition strip-out, projection removal and debris openings can ride with the demolition submission on Forms MW01 and MW02 when one project team holds both, which should be decided before the demolition plans are frozen.
