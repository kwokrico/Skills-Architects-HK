# PNRC-74 Submissions to the Buildings Department
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue June 2012 / Last revision March 2019 / This revision May 2022 (AD/CS) (Paragraph 1 amended and paragraph 4 added) | Buildings Department

> Scope note: This note fixes the paper receipt counters and their hours for submissions that are subject to statutory time limits. It does not state those time limits, and it does not set the electronic-file rules (those are in PNRC 42 and PNRC 2).

## Regulatory Overview
Many submissions received by the Buildings Department are subject to statutory time limits, and this note sets the paper receipt point according to the type of submission. Counters operate Mondays to Fridays, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, and plans should be folded as shown in Appendix A so the time-stamp machine can work.

## Critical main topics and subtopics
### 1. Paper goes to the counter that matches the submission
| Submission | Receipt point |
|---|---|
| New building developments and alteration and addition works, except works in response to a statutory order, notice, direction, or an advisory letter related to building or slope safety | Receipt and Dispatch Counter, office of New Buildings Divisions, 7/F, 14 Taikoo Wan Road, Taikoo Shing, Hong Kong |
| Other submissions | General Enquiry and Receipt Counter, Buildings Department Headquarters, G/F, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon |
| Electronic format | PNRC 42 and PNRC 2 |

Plans are folded in the manner indicated in Appendix A (preferred way along the dotted line, Step 1 then Step 2, front side) so the time-stamp machine can work.

**SD takeaway:** A paper general building plan or alteration-and-addition submission is lodged at the New Buildings Divisions counter in Taikoo Shing; a paper response to an order, notice, direction, or advisory letter on building or slope safety is an "other submission" and is lodged at the Headquarters counter in Yau Ma Tei.
