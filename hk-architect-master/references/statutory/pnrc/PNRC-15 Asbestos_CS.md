# PNRC-15 Asbestos
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue September 1985; last revision November 2012; this revision January 2015 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Avoidance of asbestos-containing material in design, construction, and maintenance, and control of in-situ asbestos-containing material during clearance of unauthorised building works, repair, and demolition. Asbestos-containing material already present in existing buildings is outside the 4 April 2014 import, transhipment, use, and supply ban. Certain low-risk, readily identifiable non-friable abatement may be exempt from an asbestos consultant and/or contractor under Gazette Notice 25/1997 (GN 3021 and GN 3022, 6 June 1997); the exempt items are on the Environmental Protection Department website, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview

Use of asbestos and asbestos-containing material in any industrial or manufacturing process, including any building operation, is prohibited under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Asbestos) Regulation (Cap. 59AD). From 4 April 2014, import, transhipment, use, and supply of asbestos and asbestos-containing material are banned under the Air Pollution Control Ordinance (Cap. 311).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. In-situ material — investigate, plan, and notify before other works

Where asbestos-containing material is present or suspected, this note requires:

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Investigation | Appoint a registered asbestos consultant as early as possible. Suspected material may be sampled and tested by a registered asbestos laboratory |
| If presence is confirmed | Submit to the Environmental Protection Department an Asbestos Abatement Plan for planned removal, and/or an Operation and Maintenance Plan for deferred treatment such as encapsulation or enclosure |
| Removal | Registered asbestos contractor, supervised by an asbestos consultant |
| Notice | **28-day** advance notice to the Environmental Protection Department before commencement of the works |
| People and waste | Protect workers to Labour Department requirements. Dispose of asbestos-contaminated waste to Environmental Protection Department requirements |

Authorized persons and registered structural engineers are advised to finish the asbestos investigation and the abatement before other building activities start. Until the investigation is complete and in-situ material is inventoried, photographed, conspicuously labelled, and segregated, unauthorised people, scavengers in particular, are kept off the site. The supervising consultant certifies completion against the investigation report and the abatement plan, and notifies the Environmental Protection Department immediately.

**SD takeaway:** On alteration or demolition, freeze the strip-out sequence on a registered-asbestos investigation and complete abatement, with the 28-day Environmental Protection Department notice, before other building works.

### 2. Where to confirm registers and method

The Environmental Protection Department’s Asbestos Management and Control Section advises on management and abatement and keeps registers of asbestos consultants, contractors, supervisors, and laboratories. The Labour Department’s Occupational Safety and Health Branch advises on worker safety. Method sits in the Labour Department codes on work with asbestos, the Environmental Protection Department’s five codes of practice on asbestos control (waste; investigation, management, and abatement plans; full or mini containment; low-risk material; glove bag), and the Buildings Department Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings.

**SD takeaway:** Name a registered asbestos consultant in the early consultant team, and specify removal only by a registered asbestos contractor unless the work is on the 1997 low-risk non-friable exemption list.
