# PNRC-25 Certificate of Completion and Materials Schedule
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 1994; last revision September 2021; this revision February 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: How the registered contractor certifies completion and confirms materials and products, including fire dampers. It does **not** contain the sample schedules (those are PNAP APP-13 Appendices A and B) or the product list itself.

## Regulatory Overview
Certificates of completion are submitted under regulation 25 of the Building (Administration) Regulations: Forms BA14, BA14A, BA14B or BA14C and/or Forms MW02 or MW04 where the works do not result in a new building, and Forms BA12/13 where they do. A schedule of building materials and products under regulation 44 of those Regulations is required with an occupation-permit application, and fire-damper installations need an inspection certificate under Clause E8.3 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Certificate of completion and BD reply times
There is no statutory time limit under the Buildings Ordinance for processing these certificates. BD’s stated practice is below.

| Submission | BD reply / processing |
|---|---|
| Form BA14 / BA14A | Generally a reply within **28 days**, accepting the works or listing outstanding issues |
| Form BA14B / BA14C | **14 days** (non-audit) or **28 days** (audit) under streamlined procedures |
| Form BA12 / BA13 (new building, usually with the occupation permit) | Processed together within **14 days** under section 21 of the Buildings Ordinance |
| Form MW02 / MW04 | Submitted, where relevant, for works not resulting in a new building |

Before Form BA14B (completion of demolition) or Form BA14C (A&A), the registered contractor should consult the authorized person and the registered structural engineer on the streamlined fast-track acceptance procedures.

**SD takeaway:** Completion and occupation-permit programme should assume these reply windows, and demolition or A&A fast-track (BA14B / BA14C) needs AP and RSE agreement before the form goes in.

### 2. One certificate for spread footing and superstructure
Where the same contractor carries out both the foundation and the superstructure of a new building, or A&A to an existing building, and a spread footing with excavation depth not exceeding **4.5 m** is used, completion of the footing and the superstructure may be certified together on Form BA12/13 or on a single Form BA14. A separate Form BA14 for the spread footing is not required.

**SD takeaway:** A shallow spread footing (excavation not over 4.5 m) under one contractor can close on the same completion certificate as the superstructure.

### 3. Schedule of building materials and products
The authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer and registered contractor supervise selection and application of materials and certify compliance on completion. The Building Authority requires the regulation 44 schedule so that an occupation permit can be processed faster.

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| When | With the occupation-permit application. Where appropriate, also on completion of A&A that does not result in a new building, or of minor works |
| Who signs | AP confirms and certifies that application and performance comply with the Buildings Ordinance and subsidiary regulations; the RC confirms those materials and products were used |
| What to list | Except for regularly used building materials: all materials and products not specified on the approved plans whose application would involve structural integrity and fire safety, and the cast iron pipes and fittings in PNAP APP-133 |
| Samples | Appendix A (building works) and Appendix B (minor works) of PNAP APP-13 |
| Alternative to test reports | Valid certificates of conformity for fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing fire partition walls, issued by the accredited certification body under the Product Conformity Certification Scheme in paragraph 4 of PNRC 81 |

**SD takeaway:** Materials that affect structure or fire safety and are not already on the approved plans, plus cast-iron drainage in APP-133, must be specifiable early enough to appear on the completion schedule or on a PNRC 81 conformity certificate.

### 4. Fire dampers
| Path | Rule |
|---|---|
| Installation | The project RGBC may install them, and an RSC(V) may be appointed to install them |
| Existing building | Erection or alteration of a fire damper in a ventilation system is minor works (Building (Minor Works) Regulation Schedule 1 and PNRC 71), as an alternative to approval and consent |
| Installed by RGBC or RMWC | An RSC(V) inspects and certifies safe and efficient working order under Clause E8.3 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 |
| Installed by RSC(V) | That same RSC(V) may inspect and certify |
| Submission | The AP or RMWC includes the RSC(V) inspection certificate with the fire-damper certification |

**SD takeaway:** Damper locations need an RSC(V) inspection certificate on the completion path, whether the RGBC, an RMWC or the RSC(V) installed them.
