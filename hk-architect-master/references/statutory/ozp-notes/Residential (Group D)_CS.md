## Regulatory Overview

Residential (Group D) is for upgrading existing temporary structures in rural areas into permanent buildings, and for low-rise, low-density residential development that needs planning permission. New flat is Column 2, and an existing house may be redeveloped, added to, or altered as Column 1, inside the Remarks.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for improvement and upgrading of existing temporary structures within the rural areas through redevelopment into permanent buildings. It is also intended for low-rise, low-density residential developments subject to planning permission from the Town Planning Board.

**SD takeaway:** Do not draw a new flat as of right. The as-of-right house is redevelopment or alteration of an existing house, inside 37.2 square metres and 2 storeys unless the existing building is larger.

### 5. Remarks printed on this master

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| (a) Addition, alteration, modification, or in-situ redevelopment of an existing temporary structure or existing building, except to a New Territories Exempted House or to uses annotated # | Maximum building area 37.2 m² and 2 storeys (6 m), or the area and height of the building existing on the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan, whichever is greater |
| (b) Flat and House, except a New Territories Exempted House, where (a) does not apply | Maximum plot ratio 0.4 and 3 storeys (9 m) |
| (c) Minor relaxation of the (b) plot ratio and building height | Section 16 application |

The 6 m and 9 m figures are printed in italics as remarks to be added where appropriate. The New Territories Exempted House exception in (b) is added when the zone falls partly or wholly within village environs. Uses annotated # are outside the (a) cap.

**SD takeaway:** Lock 37.2 m² and 2 storeys for rebuilding an existing structure. A new flat is plot ratio 0.4 and 3 storeys, and still needs Column 2 permission.
