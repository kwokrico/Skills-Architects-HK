## Regulatory Overview

Recreation is for public recreation and tourism or eco-tourism. Holiday camp and place of recreation are Column 1, and flat, hotel, and house are Column 2, with residential development capped in the Remarks.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for recreational developments for the use of the general public. It encourages active and/or passive recreation and tourism or eco-tourism. Uses in support of the recreational developments may be permitted subject to planning permission.

**SD takeaway:** Draw the camp or recreation use as of right. A flat or hotel is an application, and residential floor area is plot ratio 0.2 and 2 storeys.

The 6 m figure is printed in italics as a remark to be added where appropriate. The New Territories Exempted House exception is added when the zone falls partly or wholly within village environs.

**SD takeaway:** If housing is allowed at all, lock plot ratio 0.2 and 2 storeys before the application.
