## Regulatory Overview

Green Belt has a general presumption against development. Column 1 is agriculture and passive recreation, house and flat are Column 2, and filling, excavation, and stream diversion need permission even for a Column 1 use.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

Urban: conserve the existing natural environment amid the built-up area or at the urban fringe, safeguard it from urban-type development, and provide passive recreation. There is a general presumption against development. Rural area or New Town: define the limits of urban and sub-urban development by natural features, contain urban sprawl, and provide passive recreation. There is a general presumption against development.

**SD takeaway:** Do not mass a house on Green Belt as of right. Assume a presumption against development, and a section 16 case for filling or excavation.

### 4. Remarks printed on this master

Any diversion of stream, filling of land or pond, or excavation of land, including filling or excavation to effect a change of use to a Column 1 or Column 2 use or to a use always permitted under the covering notes, shall not be undertaken or continued on or after the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan without permission under section 16. The exception printed is public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government, and maintenance, repair, or rebuilding works. The stream, filling, pond, and rebuilding phrases are in italics to be added where appropriate.

**SD takeaway:** Site formation is not covered by Column 1. Filling and excavation need their own section 16 permission.
