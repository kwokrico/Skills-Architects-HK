## Regulatory Overview

Industrial (Group D) is for industrial uses that need extensive land or high headroom, and for rebuilding informal rural workshops as permanent industrial buildings. Open storage not elsewhere specified is Column 1, and a container yard and a dangerous goods godown are Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for industrial uses that cannot be accommodated in conventional flatted factories because of extensive land and/or high ceiling requirements. It is also intended for redevelopment of existing informal industrial uses, operated in workshop premises in a rural area, into properly designed permanent industrial buildings.

**SD takeaway:** Plan for a high-bay or extensive-land building, not a flatted factory. Container storage is an application.
