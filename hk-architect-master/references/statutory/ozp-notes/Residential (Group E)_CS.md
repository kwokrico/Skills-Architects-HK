## Regulatory Overview

Residential (Group E) is the schedule for phasing out industry, with a separate urban and New Town set and a rural set. New residential use is Column 2, and an existing industrial building keeps a Column 1 industrial schedule so that existing industry can continue.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

Urban and New Town: primarily for phasing out existing industrial uses through redevelopment or conversion for residential use on application. Existing industrial uses will be tolerated. New industrial developments are not permitted, to avoid perpetuating the industrial and residential interface. Rural: the same intention, through redevelopment for residential use on application, with existing industrial uses tolerated and new industrial developments not permitted.

**SD takeaway:** Do not draw a new factory. Residential use is an s.16 case. Keep the existing industrial building on the Schedule II list until it is redeveloped.

### 4. Lowest floors of an urban or New Town building

On an urban or New Town plan, for a building other than an industrial or industrial-office building, these uses are always permitted (a) on the lowest three floors, including basements, or (b) in the purpose-designed non-residential portion of an existing building. Both exclude floors used wholly or mainly for car parking, loading or unloading, or plant.

On an existing industrial or industrial-office building, a further set is always permitted in the purpose-designed non-industrial portion on the lower floors, except basements and floors used wholly or mainly for car parking, loading or unloading, or plant, if a buffer floor separates that portion from industry above and no industry sits inside it: Eating Place, Educational Institution, Exhibition or Convention Hall, Institutional Use (not elsewhere specified), Library, Off-course Betting Centre, Office, Place of Entertainment, Place of Recreation, Sports or Culture, Private Club, Public Clinic, Religious Institution, School (excluding kindergarten), Shop and Services, and Training Centre. Social Welfare Facility, excluding residential care, may be permitted there on application.

**SD takeaway:** On a non-industrial building, shop and office on the lowest three floors do not need s.16. Residential use still does.
