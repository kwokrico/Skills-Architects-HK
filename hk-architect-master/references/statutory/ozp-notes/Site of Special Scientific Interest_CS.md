## Regulatory Overview

Site of Special Scientific Interest conserves designated scientific features, is meant to deter human activities, and carries a general presumption against development. Column 1 is country park, where added, and wild animals protection area, and agricultural use is Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is to conserve and protect features of special scientific interest such as rare or particular species of fauna and flora and their habitats, corals, woodlands, marshes, or areas of geological, ecological, or botanical or biological interest which are designated as Site of Special Scientific Interest. It intends to deter human activities or developments within the site. There is a general presumption against development. No developments are permitted unless they are needed to support conservation of the features, to maintain and protect the existing character, or for educational and research purposes.

**SD takeaway:** Do not place a new use on the site. Even agriculture is Column 2, and filling or excavation needs section 16 permission.

### 4. Remarks printed on this master

Any diversion of stream, filling of land or pond, or excavation of land, including to effect a change of use to a Column 1 or Column 2 use or a use always permitted under the covering notes, shall not be undertaken or continued on or after the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan without permission under section 16. Exception: public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government, and maintenance, repair, or rebuilding works. Italic phrases are added where appropriate.

**SD takeaway:** Access tracks and fill are not implied by Column 1. They need section 16 permission.
