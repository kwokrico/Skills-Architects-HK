## Regulatory Overview

Residential (Group C) is the low-rise, low-density residential schedule. Column 1 is flat, house, a limited government use, and a private utility installation, and shop, school, and eating place are Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for low-rise, low-density residential developments where commercial uses serving the residential neighbourhood may be permitted on application to the Town Planning Board.

**SD takeaway:** Mass a house or flat. Neighbourhood retail is an application, not a podium right.
