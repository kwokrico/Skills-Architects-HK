## Regulatory Overview

Residential (Group A) is the high-density residential schedule, and flat and house are always permitted. A stated set of commercial uses is always permitted on the lowest three floors, including basements, or in the purpose-designed non-residential portion of an existing building.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for high-density residential developments. Commercial uses are always permitted on the lowest three floors of a building or in the purpose-designed non-residential portion of an existing building.

**SD takeaway:** Set the podium as the commercial layer. Above that, the always-permitted use is residential, not office or shop.

### 4. Always permitted on the lowest three floors

Always permitted (a) on the lowest three floors, taken to include basements, or (b) in the purpose-designed non-residential portion of an existing building. Both exclude floors containing wholly or mainly car parking, loading or unloading bays, and plant room.
