## Regulatory Overview

Other Specified Uses annotated Rural Use preserves rural character. Agriculture and passive recreation are Column 1, and flat and house are Column 2, capped at plot ratio 0.4 and 3 storeys except a New Territories Exempted House or a use annotated #.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for the preservation of the character of the rural area. Uses compatible with the rural landscape, such as passive recreation and a selected range of rural uses, may be allowed on application, to upgrade or improve the area or to support local communities.

**SD takeaway:** Passive recreation can be Column 1. A new house or flat is an application inside plot ratio 0.4 and 3 storeys (9 m).
