> Scope note: This is the master template for the Notes of this zone. It is not the Notes of any one Outline Zoning Plan. A use marked * is added only where that OZP includes it. It is not automatic. A figure for plot ratio, gross floor area, or building height applies only where the Remarks on this master print one, or where the adopted plan adds it. Every use on this master is marked $ . Uses are added or deleted according to the planning intention of the individual site, and only where appropriate. Column 1 is empty. On a rural OZP the house entry adds the bracket excluding rebuilding or replacement of a New Territories Exempted House under the covering notes.

## Regulatory Overview

Comprehensive Development Area has no always-permitted use on this master. Development needs planning permission and, unless the Town Planning Board says otherwise, a Master Layout Plan under section 4A(2), and the use list is a menu for the individual site.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended for comprehensive development or redevelopment of the area for residential and/or commercial uses with open space and other supporting facilities. The zoning is to facilitate planning control over the development mix, scale, design, and layout, taking account of environmental, traffic, infrastructure, and other constraints.

**SD takeaway:** Do not start a general building plan from Column 1. There is no Column 1. Freeze the brief only after the Master Layout Plan is the application.

### 3. Remarks printed on this master

Under section 4A(2), except where the Town Planning Board expressly provides that it is not required, an applicant for permission shall prepare a Master Layout Plan including: (i) area of proposed land uses, and the nature, position, dimensions, and heights of all buildings; (ii) total site area and gross floor area for various uses, total number of flats and flat size where applicable; (iii) details and extent of Government, institution or community and recreational facilities, public transport and parking, and open space; (iv) alignment, widths, and levels of any roads; (v) landscape and urban design proposals; (vi) programmes of development in detail; (vii) an environmental assessment report and mitigation; (viii) a drainage and sewerage impact assessment and mitigation; (ix) a traffic impact assessment and mitigation; (x) such other information as the Town Planning Board may require. The plan is supported by an explanatory statement covering land tenure, lease conditions, existing conditions, character, layout principles, major development parameters, design population, and types of facilities. The assessment reports should reflect the specific requirements of the individual site.

**SD takeaway:** The section 16 package is a Master Layout Plan with the ten items. Massing without that package is not the application.
