## Regulatory Overview

Coastal Protection Area conserves natural coastlines and sensitive coastal environment, with a general presumption against development and a minimum of built development. Column 1 is agriculture other than a plant nursery and passive coastal recreation, and house is redevelopment only in Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zoning is intended to conserve, protect, and retain the natural coastlines and the sensitive coastal natural environment, including attractive geological features, physical landform, or areas of high landscape, scenic, or ecological value, with a minimum of built development. It may also cover areas which shelter nearby developments against coastal erosion. There is a general presumption against development. In general, only development needed to support conservation of the existing natural landscape or scenic quality, or an essential infrastructure project with overriding public interest, may be permitted.

**SD takeaway:** Keep built development to a minimum. A new house is not in Column 1, and filling or excavation needs section 16 permission.
