## Regulatory Overview

Open Storage is for open storage that cannot sit in a conventional godown, and for regularising scattered open storage. Open storage not elsewhere specified is Column 1, and storage of cement, sand, chemical products, or dangerous goods is Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily to provide land for appropriate open storage and to regularise the haphazard proliferation of open storage uses. It provides for orderly development of land for open storage that cannot be accommodated in conventional godown premises.

**SD takeaway:** A general open store can proceed as Column 1. Storage of cement, chemicals, or dangerous goods cannot.
