# Commercial

> Scope note: This is the master template for the Notes of this zone. It is not the Notes of any one Outline Zoning Plan. A use marked * is added only where that OZP includes it. It is not automatic. A figure for plot ratio, gross floor area, or building height applies only where the Remarks on this master print one, or where the adopted plan adds it. On a rural OZP the bracket excluding rebuilding or replacement of a New Territories Exempted House under the covering notes is added.

## Regulatory Overview

The Commercial master is the default schedule for a commercial zone, with a CBD wording and a local-centre wording. Column 1 is the commercial brief, and flat, house, hospital, and petrol filling station sit in Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The PDF prints two intentions. CBD or major commercial areas are for office, shop, services, place of entertainment, eating place, and hotel, as territorial or district centres and major employment nodes. Local commercial areas are for shop, services, place of entertainment, and eating place serving the immediate neighbourhood.

**SD takeaway:** Pick the CBD wording or the local-centre wording before massing. Hotel, office, shop, and eating place are Column 1 either way.
