## Regulatory Overview

The Village Type Development master is for Small Houses and village expansion, and Column 1 house is a New Territories Exempted House only. The PDF prints three planning-intention wordings, for recognized villages, other villages, and both.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

Recognized villages: designate existing recognized villages and land suitable for village expansion, primarily for Small Houses by indigenous villagers, and concentrate village-type development for orderly layout and services. Other villages: retain and expand existing villages and reserve land to reprovision village houses affected by Government projects. Both: reflect recognized and other villages, provide expansion land, and reprovision houses affected by Government projects. In each wording, selected commercial and community uses are always permitted on the ground floor of a New Territories Exempted House, and other commercial, community, and recreational uses may be permitted on application.

**SD takeaway:** The as-of-right house is a New Territories Exempted House, capped at 3 storeys (8.23 m) unless the existing building is taller.

For an urban or New Town plan the comparator is the existing building. For a rural plan it is the building in existence on the first gazette of the interim or draft development permission area plan. The % note says to include the relevant part and delete the other.

**SD takeaway:** Lock the village house at 3 storeys and 8.23 m before drawing a taller block.
