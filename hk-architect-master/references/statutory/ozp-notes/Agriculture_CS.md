## Regulatory Overview

Agriculture is for retaining good agricultural land, farms, and fish ponds, and fallow arable land that can return to cultivation. Agricultural use is Column 1, and a New Territories Exempted House other than a covering-notes rebuild is Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily to retain and safeguard good quality agricultural land, farm, and fish ponds for agricultural purposes. It is also intended to retain fallow arable land with good potential for rehabilitation for cultivation and other agricultural purposes.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the land in cultivation. A house is not Column 1, and filling needs permission except for the two cultivation exceptions.

### 4. Remarks printed on this master

Any diversion of stream or filling of land or pond, including filling to effect a change of use to a Column 1 or Column 2 use or a use always permitted under the covering notes, shall not be undertaken or continued on or after the first gazette of the plan named in the Note without permission under section 16. Exception: public works co-ordinated or implemented by Government, and maintenance, repair, or rebuilding works. The restriction also does not apply to (i) laying of soil not exceeding 1.2 m in thickness for cultivation, or (ii) construction of any agricultural structure with prior written approval from the Lands Department. Stream and filling-of-pond phrases are in italics to be added where appropriate.

**SD takeaway:** Cultivation fill is limited to 1.2 m of soil. Any other fill is a section 16 case.
