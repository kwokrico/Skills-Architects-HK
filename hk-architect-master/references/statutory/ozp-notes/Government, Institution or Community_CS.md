## Regulatory Overview

The Government, Institution or Community master is the schedule for facilities serving local residents or a wider district, region, or the territory, and for land used by Government, social-service organisations, and other institutions. School, hospital, and market are Column 1, and flat, hotel, and office are Column 2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Planning intention

The zone is intended primarily for Government, institution, or community facilities serving local residents and/or a wider district, region, or the territory. It is also intended to provide land for uses directly related to or in support of the work of the Government, organisations providing social services, and other institutional establishments.

**SD takeaway:** A school, clinic, or social welfare facility can proceed as Column 1. A flat or hotel on the same zoning cannot.
