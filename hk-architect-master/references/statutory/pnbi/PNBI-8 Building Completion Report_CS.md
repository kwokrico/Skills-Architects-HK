# PNBI-8 Building Completion Report
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**  
First issue April 2018 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Evidence standard for the MBIS **completion report** after prescribed repair (Cap. 123P B(I&R)R **s.13(2)(a)** / **s.30(2)(b)**). At SD / tender docs, use it as the **close-out checklist** for façade, structure, drainage, FRC and UBW removal packages.

---

## Regulatory Overview

Within **14 days** after completing prescribed building repair, the RI appointed under Cap. 123 s.30D(1)(b) must submit a completion report to the BA and deliver a copy to the person for whom the repair was carried out. Content follows the CoP; PNBI-8 gives samples for projections (App. A) and common parts/EW (App. B). Hard copy + **non-rewritable DVD-ROM** PDF at **200 dpi**. BA audits; blatant/repeated breaches risk prosecution and/or discipline.

---

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Completion report spine — what must close out

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Repair summary | All deficiencies in the inspection report / DI + repair proposal (as acknowledged by BD) made good — annotated photos + mark-ups |
| Work classification | Split completed works into: **MWCS minor works** (no., item, description, start/finish) · **exempted works** · **works with prior approval + consent** (BD ref, BA14 acknowledgement) |
| Demarcation | Mark-ups for any works that needed approval/consent |
| After photos | Each elevation of building / projection after repair |
| Proof tests | Method statement + results (typically pull-off for rendering/tiling and patch concrete per CoP App. IV & V) |
| Materials | Certificates/reports: glass, structural sealant, rebar, cubes, repair mortar, structural steel, drainage pipes, fire doors, etc. |
| Supervision record | RI’s Level 1 / Level 2 representative works (from CoP App. VI); Class I MW also per Technical Memorandum + CoP for Site Supervision |
| Revisions | Account of any changes vs inspection-stage repair proposal |
| UBW | Voluntary removal record + related MWCS items |

### 3. Supervision split that must be recorded (mirrors CoP App. VI)

**Level 1 (examples):** hammer-tapping; hacking verification; crack open-up; rebar spacing/rust removal/replacement; bond coat & mortar; resin injection; rebound/cube/bond/rebar strength tests; FRC repair supervision; UG drain alignment/fixings/connections; ball/air/water/smoke tests.

**Level 2 (examples):** first trial bedding mortar; pull-off tests; examine rebar + sound concrete before bond coat; examine rebar before concreting; substrate before tiling (common-parts sample).

**Class I minor works (e.g. UG drainage):** also supervise per Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans + CoP for Site Supervision.

---

### 4. SD / documentation takeaways

- Write repair specs so close-out can produce **proof-test packs** and material certificates without redesign mid-contract  
- Keep MBIS-mandatory repair **visually and contractually separate** from voluntary Cap. 502/572 upgrading and other improvements  
- Plan UBW removal as numbered MWCS items with before/after photo mark-ups  
- Same delivery format as inspection reports: hard copy + 200 dpi DVD PDF
