# APP-78 Occupation of New Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1996 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: How an occupation-permit application under Buildings Ordinance section 21 is processed. Amendment plans, record plans, and schedules are in PNAP 53 and PNRC 25.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority must process an occupation-permit application within 14 days. The formal application is Form BA 13, submitted with the requisite certificates, record plans, and schedules, and only when the works are complete in accordance with the approved plans and the Buildings Ordinance and Regulations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Inspection, refusal, and reinspection (paragraphs 2–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection | The building surveyor arranges an early appointment. All parts of the building works must be available. The authorized person arranges this with the registered general building contractor |
| Blatantly incomplete works | For example sanitary fitments or fire doors not installed: the application is summarily refused and a fresh Form BA 13 is required. Premature certification may lead to disciplinary proceedings |
| Plant required to be operational | Such as an extensive air-conditioning installation required under the Ordinance, must be available for inspection. User plant and fixtures may be installed beforehand if structurally safe, but must not unduly hamper inspection |
| Minor rectification | Written confirmation, and photographic proof when appropriate, before the 14-day period expires, in time for the permit to be prepared |
| After refusal, other than a premature application | A fresh Form BA 13 is not required. On written notification that the items are rectified, the Building Authority will within 14 days reinspect if required and either issue the permit or refuse again |
| Notification | Refusal is notified by fax first; a formal letter follows. Comments beyond the statutory grounds may be included for information |

**SD takeaway:** Do not lodge Form BA 13 until fire doors, sanitary fitments, and plant required by the Ordinance are in place, because a blatantly incomplete inspection is refused outright and the 14-day clock starts again only on a new application.
