# APP-115 Performance Review under Section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2005 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 246; first issue September 2000)

> Scope note: When the Building Authority may impose a geotechnical performance review under item 6(g) in Column B of section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance for certain works outside the scheduled areas, and what that review must show before an occupation permit. It is not a slope or foundation design code. Paragraph 4 of an earlier version was deleted in this revision.

## Regulatory Overview
A performance review may be imposed as a condition of approval or consent where subsurface conditions or an unconventional scheme need to be checked against the design assumptions. If it is required, the review must be submitted to the satisfaction of the Building Authority before an occupation permit or temporary occupation permit, or the application may be refused under section 21(6)(f) of the Buildings Ordinance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the review must contain (paragraph 2)
| Content | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection and monitoring | Sufficient demonstration that the works were adequately inspected and monitored during construction |
| Assumptions | Geotechnical design assumptions on which the works were based are valid |
| Ground revealed | A review of the ground conditions revealed during construction |
| Design changes | Evidence that consequential design changes have been undertaken and plans suitably amended and approved |
| Timing | To the satisfaction of the Building Authority before the occupation-permit or temporary occupation-permit application |

**SD takeaway:** If a performance-review condition is imposed, do not apply for an occupation permit until the review, including any amended plans, is accepted.

### 2. Sites where a review is likely (paragraph 3)
In addition to sites in Area Numbers 1, 2, and 4 of the Scheduled Areas:

| Category | Where it applies |
| --- | --- |
| Geological conditions to be verified during construction | Developments on or adjacent to slopes with a history of significant continuous ground movements before or during site formation; sites where abnormal geological weakness has been identified, such as an unfavourably oriented persistent layer of weak and relatively impermeable kaolinitic clay in altered or weathered rocks |
| Groundwater regime adversely affected | Developments on sloping ground where the groundwater regime is susceptible to damming by congested foundations below the groundwater level, or cutoff by bored pile walls or diaphragm walls. Examples: developments downslope of Scheduled Area No. 1 (the Mid-levels scheduled area), and developments in the hilly terrain of Hong Kong Island and the foothills of Kowloon |
| Unconventional design | Sites with retaining walls of retained height exceeding 15 m or excavations in reclaimed land; sites that rely on horizontal drains for a large groundwater drawdown to improve hillside stability, or on a permanent underground drainage system to draw down groundwater and prevent buoyancy of a deep basement; sites using technologies developed locally or overseas and not tried in Hong Kong before, which need careful assessment and monitoring |

**SD takeaway:** Expect a performance review for movement-prone slopes, groundwater cutoff on a hillside, retaining walls over 15 m, reclamation excavations, and untried drainage or basement schemes.
