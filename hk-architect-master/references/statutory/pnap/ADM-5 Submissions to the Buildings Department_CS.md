# ADM-5 Submissions to the Buildings Department
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision May 2022 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP-58; first issue April 1979)

> Scope note: States where paper submissions are received and the counter hours. It does not set design standards; electronic submission is in ADM-17 and APP-60.

## Regulatory Overview
Many submissions are subject to statutory time limits, so the note fixes the paper receipt point by type of submission and the hours those counters are open. Electronic submissions are not received at these counters; they follow PNAP ADM-17 and PNAP APP-60.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Paper receipt points and hours (paragraphs 1–3)
| Submission | Receipt point |
| --- | --- |
| New building developments and alteration and addition works, except works in response to a statutory order, notice, direction, or an advisory letter related to building or slope safety | Receipt and Dispatch Counter, office of New Buildings Divisions, 7/F, 14 Taikoo Wan Road, Taikoo Shing, Hong Kong |
| Other submissions | General Enquiry and Receipt Counter, Buildings Department Headquarters, G/F, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon |

Operating hours: Mondays to Fridays, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. Fold plans in the manner shown in Appendix A so the time-stamp machine can work.

**SD takeaway:** Lodge paper plans at the counter that matches the submission type, within Monday-to-Friday 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, folded as in Appendix A so the statutory clock can be time-stamped.

### 2. Electronic format (paragraph 4)
Submissions in electronic format are covered by PNAP ADM-17 and PNAP APP-60, not by the paper counters in paragraph 1.

**SD takeaway:** Do not take an electronic pack to these counters; follow ADM-17 and APP-60 for when the processing clock starts.
