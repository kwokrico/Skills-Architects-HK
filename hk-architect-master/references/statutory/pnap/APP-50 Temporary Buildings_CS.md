# APP-50 Temporary Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 1994 | Buildings Department (first issued June 1990; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Procedure for temporary buildings under Building (Planning) Regulations 50 to 52, whether required for a temporary purpose or constructed of short-lived materials. All provisions of the Buildings Ordinance and subsidiary legislation still apply.

## Regulatory Overview
A temporary building needs approval and consent, supervision by an authorized person and/or a registered structural engineer, construction by a registered contractor, certification of completion, and a temporary occupation permit. The two procedural points in this note take precedence over similar provisions elsewhere: the forms used, and the combined approval-and-consent document.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Forms, time limits, and how long the building may stand (paragraphs 2–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Submission | Form BA17, which replaces Form BA5 for approval and Form BA8; a Form BA5 certificate of preparation of plans; and a Form BA4 notice of appointment |
| Consent document | On approval, Form BD107 is issued if consent may also be granted. It replaces the approval letter S.L. 9 and Form BD103, and states the duration for which the temporary building may remain |
| Start of work | Buildings Ordinance section 20. Commence within three months of Form BD107, or renew consent on Form BA9 |
| Processing time | Form BA17 covers both approval and consent, so the limits run together: 60 days for a first submission or 30 days for a resubmission |
| Occupation | Temporary occupation permit Form BD105, applied for on Form BA12, before occupation. Maximum number of persons, removal on expiry or cancellation, and the expiry date on Form BD107 become conditions of the permit |
| After expiry or cancellation | Remove the building forthwith, or a section 24(1) order may require removal. Occupation after expiry or revocation of the temporary occupation permit may be prosecuted under section 40(1) |

**SD takeaway:** Submit a temporary building on Form BA17, start within three months of Form BD107, and occupy only under Form BD105 for the duration stated on Form BD107.

### 2. Extension (paragraph 7)
| Document | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Application | In writing, with the original Form BD107 and Form BD105 |
| Certificate | From an authorized person and/or registered structural engineer that the building can last the extended term, maximum five years, in safe and, if applicable, sanitary conditions for the purposes specified |

**SD takeaway:** An extension needs a certificate that the building can last the extra term, and that term cannot exceed five years.
