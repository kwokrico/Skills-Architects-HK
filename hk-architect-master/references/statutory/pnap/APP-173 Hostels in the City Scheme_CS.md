# APP-173 Hostels in the City Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2025 | Buildings Department (first issue July 2025)

> Scope note: Building-regime modifications for student hostels eligible under the Hostels in the City Scheme, confirmed by the Education Bureau. Scheme details are at www.studenthostel.gov.hk. Lands, planning, fire, and transport controls still apply. Loss of hostel operation revokes the exemptions. It is not a general domestic-building code.

## Regulatory Overview
Eligible student hostels are for habitation and are therefore domestic, but the Building Authority is prepared to treat a converted or newly built hostel as non-domestic for site coverage and plot ratio, and to relax open space, service lane, and kitchens. Other domestic requirements, including natural lighting and ventilation, and the fire safety requirements for hostels and dormitories, still apply.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Non-domestic treatment and kitchens (paragraphs 3–4 and 13–15)
For an eligible conversion of a commercial building or hotel, and for a newly built hostel on plans submitted on or after the issue of this note (September 2025 revision):

| Modification | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Site coverage and plot ratio | Treated as a non-domestic building, or a non-domestic part, under regulations 19, 20, 21, and 22 of the Building (Planning) Regulations |
| Open space and service lane | Non-provision under regulations 25 and 28. A service-lane modification is on the merits, for example no existing lane and an acceptable servicing strategy |
| Kitchens | Non-provision in individual units under regulation 45, subject to a communal canteen, kitchen, or pantry |
| Still required | Natural lighting and ventilation, and hostel or dormitory fire safety |
| Joint practice notes | Because of the non-domestic treatment, the hostel is a non-residential development for Joint Practice Notes Nos. 1 and 2 and for PNAP APP-42 and APP-104 |
| Partial conversion | Allowed if the Scheme’s Application Guidelines are met. The Education Bureau, assisted by the Development Projects Facilitation Office, determines that |

**SD takeaway:** An eligible hostel can be counted as non-domestic for plot ratio and can omit kitchens in the rooms if there is a communal canteen, kitchen, or pantry.

### 2. Gross floor area of hostel facilities (paragraphs 5–8, 13(d), and the Appendix)
Facilities that may be exempted, for exclusive hostel use: linen store; laundry room; study room, collaboration room, or leisure room; gymnasium or sports room; communal kitchen, pantry, or canteen; staff rest room; staff toilet; and other facilities accepted by the Education Bureau and the Building Authority.

| Case | Concession |
| --- | --- |
| Previously exempted or disregarded facilities in the commercial building or hotel | May be retained after conversion, so the hostel need not lose existing bulk |
| Redundant exempted facilities | Modification of regulation 23(3)(a) to convert them to mandatory features, essential or non-essential plant rooms, green or amenity features, and hostel facilities, on the special circumstances. Alteration and addition that results in a new building must meet the prerequisites in PNAP APP-151 |
| Former licensed hotel, licence no longer valid under Cap. 349 | Picking-up and setting-down areas and back-of-house facilities previously disregarded under regulation 23A(3) may become hostel facilities, exempted on a modification of regulation 23(3)(a) |
| Redundant facilities under 5% of hostel gross floor area | Hostel facilities up to 5% of that gross floor area |
| Redundant facilities over 5% | All of those redundant facilities may be converted to hostel facilities. Exemption above 5% is case by case |
| Partial conversion | The 5% is of the hostel portion only |
| Newly built | Hostel facilities in the Appendix exempted up to 5% of total hostel gross floor area. Prerequisites in PNAP APP-151 apply. The concession is not subject to the overall cap on gross floor area concessions |

**SD takeaway:** Cap new-build hostel facilities at 5% of hostel gross floor area for exemption, and keep a communal kitchen if rooms have none.

### 3. If the hostel use stops (paragraph 16)
Exemptions are revoked if the premises are no longer operated as a student hostel under the Scheme. The owner must appoint an authorized person and submit the change of use for approval. Otherwise the Buildings Department may take enforcement action.

**SD takeaway:** The non-domestic plot ratio and the gross-floor-area exemptions last only while the Scheme operation continues.
