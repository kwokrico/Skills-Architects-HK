# ADM-15 Submission of Site Formation Proposals
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 200; first issue October 1996)

> Scope note: Splits a site formation proposal into geotechnical layout (Part A) and structural details of retaining structures (Part B) so the two can be processed in parallel. Design of slopes and retaining walls is in the geotechnical APP notes and the foundation code.

## Regulatory Overview
The Buildings Department processes site formation details and structural details of a site formation proposal in parallel, with responsibilities split between the authorized person or registered geotechnical engineer and the registered structural engineer. A simple proposal may stay as one submission.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Part A and Part B (paragraphs 2–4)
| Part | Content | Who signs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Layout and details of slope works and associated drainage, and the general layout and size of earth retaining structures, if any, as per Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1)(bb) | Authorized person or registered geotechnical engineer coordinates preparation and signing |
| B | Structural details of earth retaining structures | Registered structural engineer |

Parts A and B may be submitted together, or Part A first and Part B after the soil-structure interaction can be prescribed. A sufficient number of complete sets is also acceptable if one set is clearly marked Part A and another Part B. Responsibilities follow Building (Administration) Regulation 12 and Task 4 of Appendix B in PNAP APP-141.

**SD takeaway:** Freeze slope layout, associated drainage, and the size of retaining structures as Part A before, or with, the structural details in Part B, and mark the sets clearly.

### 2. When a single submission is enough (paragraph 5)
| Example of a simple proposal |
| --- |
| Site formation works involving simple earth retaining structures |
| Small-scale site formation works such as those for New Territories exempted houses |
| Drainage systems for slopes involving box culverts and/or other drainage structures such as sand traps, catchpits, and stormwater manholes, if no major earth retaining structures are involved |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a small retaining structure, an exempted-house platform, or slope drainage without a major retaining structure as one submission.

### 3. Copies and piling (paragraphs 6–7)
Submit enough sets, with essential information, for consultation with the departments and authorities concerned (PNAP ADM-2, ADM-8, and APP-141). Where piling and similar operations are included in the site formation proposal, follow the monitoring requirements in PNAP APP-18 and PNAP APP-137.

**SD takeaway:** If the formation includes piling, add the APP-18 and APP-137 monitoring package to the submission.
