# ADM-7 Geotechnical Information Unit
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This update May 1995 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issued May 1984)

> Scope note: Points to the Geotechnical Information Unit as the public collection of site-specific ground data. It does not set design parameters; those come from the investigation and the geotechnical codes.

## Regulatory Overview
Since 1983 the Geotechnical Information Unit, operated by the Geotechnical Engineering Office, has held the largest collection of site-specific geotechnical data in the Territory, including most of the Mid-levels Study data collected between 1979 and 1981. It is a reading-room source for ground investigation, slopes, and landslips before design on a slope or in an area with existing records.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is held and how it is found (paragraphs 1–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Where | Civil Engineering Library, First Lower Ground Floor, Civil Engineering Building, Homantin |
| Hours | 9:00–12:30 and 14:00–16:15, Mondays to Fridays, except public holidays |
| Holdings | Ground investigation reports including geophysical surveys; laboratory testing of soils and rocks; card index of cut slopes, fill slopes, and retaining walls; landslip record cards; piezometric monitoring data; rain gauge records |
| Mid-levels | Most geology, hydrology, and soil-property data from the 1979–1981 Mid-levels Study |
| Retrieval | GIU Cell Referencing System: numbered cells on 1:5 000 survey sheets for urban areas and 1:20 000 sheets for the rest of the Territory |
| Literature | Textbooks, reports, journals, standards, and published papers on Hong Kong geology, searched by a computerized catalogue |

**SD takeaway:** Before schematic design on a slope or in the Mid-levels, search the GIU cell for the site rather than designing only from a new borehole campaign.

### 2. Use of the records (paragraphs 5–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Taking records out | Not allowed; examine them in the reading area; photocopying is available at a nominal charge |
| Slope catalogue | Plans of registerable slopes and retaining walls from the 1977/78 Catalogue of Slopes, later updated, with data sheets and photographs |
| Enquiries | Librarian, telephone 2762 5147 |
| Catalogue information | Application form from District Offices or the GIU; preliminary information is sent within ten days |

**SD takeaway:** Budget a library visit or a ten-day catalogue enquiry; GIU records stay in the reading room and are not issued as a design deliverable.
