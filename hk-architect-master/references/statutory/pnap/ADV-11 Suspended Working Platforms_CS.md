# ADV-11 Suspended Working Platforms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2010 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 185; first issue October 1995)

> Scope note: Essential safety dimensions and suspension rules for a platform that carries persons, under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings (Suspended Working Platforms) Regulation. Permanent roof track design for a gondola is in PNAP ADV-14.

## Regulatory Overview
A suspended working platform is a scaffold or working platform hung from a building by lifting gear and able to be raised or lowered, including the ropes, counterweights, outriggers, and the mechanical and electrical gear. The Labour Department regulation applies to an industrial undertaking where such a platform carries persons; it came into operation on 1 July 1995, except section 17 which took effect on 1 January 1996.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Deck, rails, and suspension (paragraphs 5–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Construction | Sound construction, adequately supported, properly maintained. Protect electrical parts, motors, and cable against accidental or environmental damage |
| Width | Sufficient length and at least 440 mm wide |
| Guard-rails | Suitable guard-rails to a height between 900 mm and 1,150 mm |
| Toe boards | Not less than 200 mm high |
| Gap | Space between any toe board and the lowest guard-rail above it not exceeding 700 mm |
| Access | Sufficient safe means of access and egress |
| Ropes | Only wire ropes for raising, lowering, and suspension. Suspension points at an adequate horizontal distance from the face so the platform does not touch it |
| Counterweights | On any outrigger, not less than 3 times the weight necessary to balance the load on the projecting part when the platform is fully loaded |
| Safety rope | At each suspension point, in addition to the two suspension ropes, a safety rope with an automatic safety device that supports the platform if the suspension ropes or the raising or lowering mechanism fail |
| Erection | Erection, dismantling, and alteration only under the supervision of a competent person |

**SD takeaway:** Detail the roof suspension so the platform stays off the facade, with wire ropes, a safety rope at each point, and outrigger counterweights of at least three times the balance weight at full load.

### 2. People, test, and marking (paragraphs 9–12)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Workers | At least 18 years old, with a certificate from training recognised by the Commissioner for Labour or provided by the manufacturer or its local agent |
| Harness | Every person on the platform wears a safety harness with the lanyard attached to an independent lifeline or an anchorage |
| Before use | Inspection by a competent person. Load test and thorough examination by a competent examiner before use, and after substantial repair, re-erection, adjustment, failure, collapse, or weather likely to have affected stability |
| Display | The most recent certificate or report displayed prominently on the platform |
| Marking | Safe working load and the maximum number of persons, plus a mark that distinguishes the platform from similar ones |

**SD takeaway:** Mark the safe working load and the maximum number of persons on the platform, and do not put it into use without the competent examiner's load test.
