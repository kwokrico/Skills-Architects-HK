# APP-75 Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue in Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued May 1995 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: The effect of the Building (Planning) (Amendment) Regulation 1995 on regulations 41B and 41C. Detailed dimensions are in the revised Code of Practice for Means of Access for Firefighting and Rescue, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
The Amendment Regulation revises means of access for firefighting and rescue. It applies in accordance with Buildings Ordinance section 39, and by section 39(2) it does not apply to building works being carried out, or consented to commence, on or before 22 May 1995.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fireman's lifts and the firefighting and rescue stairway (paragraphs 2–3)
| Regulation | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 41B | Fireman's lifts are designed and located so that, in a fire, firemen have safe and unobstructed access to the lifts and to the floors they serve |
| 41C | Only fireman's lifts are installed in a firefighting and rescue stairway, and the access stairway in that stairway serves all floors of the building |

**SD takeaway:** Put only fireman's lifts in the firefighting and rescue stairway, run that stair to every floor, and keep the approach to those lifts unobstructed.
