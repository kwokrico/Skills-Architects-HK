# ADV-13 Application for Excavation Permit for Works on Public Road
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2009 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issued August 1996)

> Scope note: An excavation permit is required before building works excavate a public road maintained by the Highways Department. It does not design the temporary works.

## Regulatory Overview
Excavation for building works on a public road maintained by the Highways Department, including sheet piling, trial pits, ground investigation, and erection or demolition of hoardings, covered walkways, and gantries, requires an excavation permit from the Director of Highways under section 10 of the Land (Miscellaneous Provisions) Ordinance (Chapter 28). Working without the permit is an offence.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Utility circulation and trial pits (paragraphs 2–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Rejection | The Highways Department will reject an excavation-permit application that is not accompanied by evidence that advance notices have been circulated to all relevant utility undertakers and that their requirements have been responded to |
| List of undertakers | Table 1 of Appendix A to the Conditions of Excavation Permit, in the Excavation Permit Processing Manual, at http://www.hyd.gov.hk/eng/public/publications/xppm/index.htm |
| Purpose of the notice | Avoid damage, give time to disconnect services before demolition, and coordinate road works |
| Before heavy plant | Suitable investigations, for example hand-dug trial pits, to fix the position of utilities in the vicinity |

**SD takeaway:** Do not programme heavy plant on a public road until the excavation permit is in place and hand-dug trial pits have located the utilities.
