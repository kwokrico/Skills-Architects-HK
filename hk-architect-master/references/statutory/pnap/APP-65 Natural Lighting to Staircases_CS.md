# APP-65 Natural Lighting to Staircases
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 2003 | Buildings Department (first issue November 1994; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Guidelines for adequate natural lighting to a common staircase under Building (Planning) Regulation 40, and the artificial-lighting alternative the Building Authority is prepared to waive the natural-lighting requirement for. It applies to a staircase intended for common use by more than two tenants.

## Regulatory Overview
Building (Planning) Regulation 40 requires a staircase in any building intended for common use by more than two tenants to be provided with adequate natural lighting. Clear glass and a minimum glass area, opening onto a space that depends on the number of storeys, are the guidelines for satisfying that requirement.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Natural lighting (paragraph 2)
| Rule | Dimension |
| --- | --- |
| Glass | Clear glass |
| Area | Aggregate glass area of the staircase window or windows for each storey not less than one-tenth of the plan area within the staircase enclosure |
| Building not exceeding 12 storeys | Windows face an uncovered space complying with "open air" in regulation 2(1) |
| Building exceeding 12 storeys | Windows face an uncovered and unenclosed space with a base width of not less than 2.3 m and a length at right angles to the base of not less than 4.5 m. The full width of any adjoining service lane may be included |
| Scissor stair, end wall of a landing | Window centrally positioned, minimum width 1.3 m. Alternatives will be considered |
| Scissor stair, side wall of a landing or flight | Minimum width 650 mm, not less than two windows each storey, and the separating wall between flights reduced so the back flight has adequate light |

**SD takeaway:** Give each common-stair storey clear glazing of at least one-tenth of the enclosure plan area, onto open air if the building is 12 storeys or less, or onto a 2.3 m by 4.5 m uncovered space if it is taller.

### 2. Artificial lighting in place of natural light (paragraphs 3–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Normal lighting | Permanent artificial lighting of 30 lux minimum at floor level |
| Emergency | Automatically activated emergency lighting complying with the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, providing not less than 2 lux horizontal illuminance at floor level |
| Management | Maintained by a management company on occupation, with regular maintenance incorporated in the Deed of Mutual Covenant. That is what the Building Authority would normally accept as proper management |

**SD takeaway:** If the stair cannot face the required open space, provide 30 lux permanent lighting and 2 lux emergency lighting, and write the maintenance duty into the Deed of Mutual Covenant.
