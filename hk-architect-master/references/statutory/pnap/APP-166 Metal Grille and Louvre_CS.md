# APP-166 Metal Grille and Louvre
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision January 2026 | Buildings Department (first issue December 2021)

> Scope note: When a metal grille or louvre needs a structural submission, by the function it performs. Concrete or glass follows the relevant codes. An interior facing or architectural decoration fixed to interior structural elements does not need a structural submission. It is not a cladding or curtain-wall code; those cross-refer to PNAP APP-16, PNAP APP-37, PNAP APP-110, and PNAP ADV-33.

## Regulatory Overview
A grille is an openwork barrier, usually metal, covering an opening, and a louvre is an assembly of sloping overlapping blades that admits air or light and excludes rain. Structural submission depends on whether it is external cladding, part of an external wall, or a protective barrier.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When a structural submission is required (paragraphs 2–3)
| Type | Function | Submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | External cladding | Required if any part is installed above 6 m from the adjoining ground or adjoining floor. Design reference: Appendices B6 and B9 of PNAP ADV-33, and paragraph 4 of PNAP APP-16 |
| 2 | Wholly or partly an external wall | Required if the structural span exceeds 6 m, or if it is where the design wind pressure is 2.86 kPa or above and the structural opening exceeds 6 m2 or the least dimension exceeds 1.8 m. Appendices B7 and B10 of PNAP ADV-33, and paragraphs 4 to 7 of PNAP APP-37 |
| 3 | Protective barrier | Required in all circumstances, except where it also performs as Type 2 and meets the criteria under which a Type 2 submission is not required. Paragraph 5 of PNAP APP-110 |

**SD takeaway:** Submit structure for a metal screen once it is cladding above 6 m, a wall spanning over 6 m, or a barrier, unless a Type 2 screen stays under those span and wind thresholds.

### 2. What the plans contain (paragraphs 4–6)
Structural plans, or the same content embedded in the superstructure plans: framing and elements; anchors and supports; design loads, standards, and codes; material specifications; workmanship for connections and protection; and calculations covering the parent structure, adequacy and stability, element design, and deflection of major members if applicable. Location, layout, and elevation go on the general building plans. Where submission is not required, the authorized person and registered structural engineer still secure the safety standard. The checklist at Appendix B7 and samples at Appendix B10 of PNAP ADV-33 apply to the streamlined route.

**SD takeaway:** Show every grille and louvre on the general building plans, and put the structural details in the superstructure plans when a separate submission is not made.

### 3. Exempted and minor works (paragraphs 7–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Designated exempted works | Item 27 in Part 2 of Schedule 2 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation: erection, alteration, repair, or removal of a metal window security grille, or a metal wind guard, for an opening on an external wall, where the works meet the description in that regulation |
| Minor works | No specific grille or louvre item. If they are part of external cladding, part or the whole of a window or window wall, or part of a protective barrier, they may use minor-works items 3.31, 2.8 and 3.6, and 1.6 respectively. Schedule 1 and PNAP APP-147 |

**SD takeaway:** A metal window security grille or wind guard on an external-wall opening can be designated exempted works under item 27 of Part 2 of Schedule 2.
