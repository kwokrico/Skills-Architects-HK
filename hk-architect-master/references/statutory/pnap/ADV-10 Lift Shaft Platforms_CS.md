# ADV-10 Lift Shaft Platforms
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2011 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 181; first issue October 1995)

> Scope note: Safety requirements for temporary platforms inside lift shafts during construction, enforced by the Labour Department. It is not the permanent lift installation note (see PNAP APP-29).

## Regulatory Overview
Lift shaft platforms are temporary wall-supported platforms used as a rain shelter, formwork platform, mid-shaft protection, debris catcher, or crash deck, and past collapses have killed or seriously injured workers. The Labour Department will consider a suspension notice under section 10 of the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance if any of the paragraph 4 requirements is missed.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Design, supervision, and site records (paragraphs 4–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Every platform designed by an authorized person or registered structural engineer employed by the contractor. The design plan contains all details and specifications |
| Method | Construction plans and method statements for erection, alteration, maintenance, and dismantling, prepared by the contractor's engineer of TCP T4 or above, and checked and signed off by the contractor's AP or RSE |
| Erection | In accordance with the construction plan, under the full-time immediate supervision of a competent person with TCP T1, appointed in writing by the contractor. On completion, checked by the contractor's engineer |
| Alteration | Redesigned by the contractor's AP or RSE; method statement by the contractor's engineer and signed off by that AP or RSE |
| Risk assessment | Before erection, maintenance, or dismantling, by a planning team from the main contractor and subcontractors with at least one person at TCP T4 or equivalent, including a thorough inspection of stability by the TCP T1 person |
| Housekeeping | Maintain to the construction plan and remove accumulated material regularly. Do not throw, tip, or shoot material onto a platform unless the contractor's AP or RSE designed it for that |
| Dismantling | Full-time immediate supervision of the same competent person. Workers use a suitable fall-arresting system during erection, maintenance, and dismantling |
| On site for inspection | Design plan; construction plan and method statement; dates of erection, maintenance, and dismantling and the name of the supervising competent person; risk assessment report |

**SD takeaway:** Do not treat a lift-shaft platform as a site carpenter's deck; it needs a contractor's AP or RSE design, a T4 method statement, and full-time T1 supervision before anyone works on it.

### 2. Temporary occupation (paragraph 6)
If a temporary occupation permit is used, plan fire safety, worker safety, and occupant safety at design stage. Every condition of the permit, including fire separation of adequate rating between occupied and construction areas, and the safety features above, must be kept at all times, including while lift-shaft and platform works continue, until the occupation permit is issued.

**SD takeaway:** If part of the building is occupied under a temporary occupation permit, keep the fire separation in place while lift-shaft platforms are still in the shafts.
