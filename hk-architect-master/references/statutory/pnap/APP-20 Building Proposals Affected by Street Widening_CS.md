# APP-20 Building Proposals Affected by Street Widening
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2008 | Buildings Department (first issue April 1980; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: How bonus plot ratio and site coverage under Building (Planning) Regulation 22(2) are quantified when part of a lot is acquired for street widening. Cash settlement and land exchange are negotiated by the Lands Department and are not designed here.

## Regulatory Overview
Where part of a lot is acquired for street widening, compensation may be bonus plot ratio and/or site coverage under Building (Planning) Regulation 22(2), cash, an exchange of land, or a combination. If the bonus is adopted, the part required for widening may also count as site area, but only for the first building subsequently erected on the reduced site, and subject to paragraph 4 and Building (Planning) Regulation 23(2)(a).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Conditions for counting the surrender as site area (paragraphs 3–5)
| Condition | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Individual assessment | Every claim is dealt with according to the special circumstances of the case |
| Extra plot ratio under regulation 22(2)(b) | Only where the Building Authority is satisfied that the density of the development or redevelopment is not excessive |
| Block plan | A separate block plan, in quadruplicate, showing the area to be surrendered, accompanies the submission that claims the bonus |
| Endorsement | Building plans are endorsed to indicate the areas to be surrendered |
| Agreement | A formal Agreement to Surrender, to the satisfaction of the Lands Department, is executed and registered in the Land Registry |
| Open space | Under Building (Planning) Regulation 25, the setback area may count as open space where it is at the side or rear of a building |

**SD takeaway:** Claim the widening strip as site area only for the first building on the reduced site, show it on a quadruplicate block plan and on the building plans, and register an Agreement to Surrender.

### 2. Lands submission and occupation (paragraphs 6–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Parallel submission | When building plans are submitted, also send a set to the Chief Estate Surveyor/Acquisition, Lands Department |
| Occupation | A temporary occupation permit or an occupation permit will not be issued before the Agreement to Surrender, incorporating all relevant terms and conditions, is executed |

**SD takeaway:** Send the plans to Lands Acquisition at the same time as the building submission, and do not expect an occupation permit before the Agreement to Surrender is executed.
