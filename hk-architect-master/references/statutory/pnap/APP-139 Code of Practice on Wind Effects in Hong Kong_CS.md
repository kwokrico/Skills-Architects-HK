# APP-139 Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2023 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 219; first issue December 2004)

> Scope note: When foundation and superstructure plans must use the Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2019, and the December 2023 amendments to that code and its Explanatory Notes. The Explanatory Notes are not part of the code. Alteration and addition checking follows PNAP APP-117. It is not a substitute for the wind code.

## Regulatory Overview
After 31 March 2021, only foundation or superstructure plans designed to the 2019 code are accepted, unless plans for those works were submitted before that date, approved, and later submissions continue the code used in the original design. Consent may still be refused under section 16(3)(d) of the Buildings Ordinance if more than 2 years have passed since approval of the prescribed plans.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which code, and wind-tunnel procedure (paragraphs 2–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Grace period | Plans to the 2004 code were accepted from the circular letter of 30 September 2019 until the extended grace period ended on 31 March 2021. That includes plans with a loading allowance for future structures |
| Later stages | If the original foundation or superstructure plans were approved on the code used before the grace period expired, subsequent plans for those works may keep that code |
| Alteration and addition after the grace period | PNAP APP-117 |
| Wind tunnel not to section 6 of the 2019 code | Submit the methodology for prior acceptance. A determination is normally given within 45 days. A pre-submission enquiry under PNAP ADM-19 may settle principles first |
| Quality assurance | Included in the methodology. Reference may be made to ASCE Manuals and Reports on Engineering Practice No. 67, or the Australasian Wind Engineering Society quality-assurance manual. Essential contents are in Appendices C and D |
| Witness | The registered structural engineer, or an independent engineer who is not staff of the laboratory, a Corporate Member of the Hong Kong Institution of Engineers or equivalent, with at least five years’ relevant experience |
| Report | Procedures, the whole set of readings, structural and dynamic properties used, and a statement by the registered structural engineer that supervision was provided and the raw data meet the 2019 code |

**SD takeaway:** Design new foundation and superstructure wind loads to the 2019 code unless an earlier approved submission is still being followed, and have any non-standard wind-tunnel method accepted before the test.

### 2. Across-wind check and site hoardings (Appendix B, December 2023 Explanatory Notes)
Across-wind base moment need not be checked when, with natural periods taken as H/46 in both directions, H divided by the minimum of B and D is less than 5, H is less than 100 m, and N is greater than 0.5 Hz.

For several towers on one podium, the fundamental frequency for across-wind effects may assume: the tower standing alone down to the base; or the tower plus podium measured from the structural wall or column edge by not more than the least of 20 m, three podium bays, and the midline to the next tower, with no substantial floor openings in that portion; or an extent conforming to recognised engineering practice. An integrated model may use the dominant frequency of the mode mainly aligned with the across-wind direction.

Hoarding, covered walkway, contractor shed, bamboo shed, tent, or marquee that is not for residential use: wind pressures not less than 37% of the pressures in the code. For a site hoarding or covered walkway, Table 2-1 design net pressure (all-inclusive) may be used: 0.63 kPa at 2.5 m or below, 0.70 kPa at 5 m, and 0.77 kPa at 10 m. Intermediate heights may be interpolated. Cp and Ss are included. Self-weight of steel members may be considered beneficial. A topography factor is considered where local topography may worsen the wind effect.

**SD takeaway:** A building under 100 m with H/min(B,D) below 5 and frequency above 0.5 Hz need not be checked for across-wind base moment, and a site hoarding may use 0.63 to 0.77 kPa net pressure up to 10 m.
