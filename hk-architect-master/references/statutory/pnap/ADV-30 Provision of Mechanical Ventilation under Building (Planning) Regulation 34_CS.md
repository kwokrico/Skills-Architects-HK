# ADV-30 Provision of Mechanical Ventilation under Building (Planning) Regulation 34
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue January 2007 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: The fresh-air rate when an office cannot meet Building (Planning) Regulation 31 and must use mechanical ventilation under regulation 34(a). It does not redesign the natural-lighting rules in regulations 30 and 31.

## Regulatory Overview
Every room used or intended as an office must have adequate lighting and ventilation under Building (Planning) Regulations 30 and 31. Where position, level, or unsuitable surroundings mean regulation 31 cannot be met to the Building Authority's satisfaction, regulation 34(a) requires mechanical ventilation capable of supplying fresh air to all parts of the room at not less than 5 air changes per hour.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Five air changes, and who should design a central plant (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | An office room that cannot comply with regulation 31 because of position, level, or unsuitable surroundings |
| Rate | Not less than 5 changes of air per hour, to all parts of the room |
| Large or complex system | A central plant and a ducted system serving multiple rooms or floors: advisable to engage a Registered Professional Engineer in the building-services discipline |

**SD takeaway:** An office that cannot open onto regulation 31 light and air needs a mechanical supply of at least 5 air changes per hour to every part of the room.
