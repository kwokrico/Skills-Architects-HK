# ADV-35 Greening in Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2020 | Buildings Department (first issue June 2017)

> Scope note: Structural, drainage, and escape constraints when greening is added to a building. Whether a particular installation is building works depends on the facts of the case. Site coverage of greenery percentages are in PNAP APP-152, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
Greening may or may not be building works under the Buildings Ordinance, judged on fixation, permanence, nature and intended use, size, associated works and facilities, and ease of assembly and removal, taken together. Fixed planter boxes are building works; laying soil and planting vegetation in them are not.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When a submission is required (paragraphs 3–5)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Designated minor works | May use the simplified requirements under Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation and PNAP APP-147, instead of prior approval and consent |
| No new building works | No plan submission. Still check load capacity of the existing structure, drainage, fire safety, and any change in use |
| New building works or strengthening | Submit an alteration and addition proposal |

**SD takeaway:** A fixed planter is building works and needs approval or a minor-works item; soil and plants in an existing box do not, but the slab and the drains still have to take the saturated weight.

### 2. Loads, falls, and escape (paragraphs 6–13)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Loads | All combinations: drainage layer, substrate, growing medium, plant variety and weight. Use the fully saturated weight. Include the irrigation system and construction load |
| Higher risk | Inaccessible roofs, long-span roof or podium, roofs or podiums with water ponds, cantilever slabs |
| Code | Code of Practice for Dead and Imposed Loads 2011: weight of soil, waterproofing, drainage system, and plants is dead load. Imposed load is additional. Where people may congregate, use the minimum imposed load for that use |
| Existing building | Check approved records and site condition against the greening load, from a green carpet to a deep water pond. Follow PNAP APP-117 |
| Falls | Surface fall not shallower than 1:80 on the structure and the greenery. Adapt the drains to the greenery system. On an existing building, add drains if the present system is inadequate, to avoid ponding and overload |
| Waterproofing | Sound waterproofing of the roof or podium before greening, so seepage does not damage the structure |
| Refuge | Do not diminish or obstruct the extent of the refuge with greenery or any other use |
| Barriers | Protective barriers such as balustrades and railings for safe access and to prevent falling, under regulation 8 of the Building (Construction) Regulations, plus access and safety provisions for maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** Design the roof or podium for the fully saturated greening as dead load plus the imposed load, fall it at not less than 1:80, and keep greenery out of the refuge.
