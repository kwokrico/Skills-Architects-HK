# ADV-14 Facilities for External Inspection and Maintenance of Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 218; first issue April 1998)

> Scope note: Asks for permanent access to external walls at design stage, including a roof gondola. Construction rules for the platform itself are in PNAP ADV-11. Cast-in anchor dimensions are in the Construction Industry Council guidelines cited in paragraph 7.

## Regulatory Overview
External pipes, windows, finishes, and the wall fabric need maintenance at height, and the note strongly advises authorized persons and registered structural engineers to incorporate safe access at design stage. A roof gondola is the access the Construction Industry Council identified as desirable, including on residential buildings.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Gondola on the roof (paragraphs 4–5)
| Party | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | A gondola enclosure may be treated as a rooftop utility structure and exempted from the height restriction if the size is the minimum that meets the function. In general, rooftop utility structures should not exceed 15 m beyond the main roof or 10% of the building height, whichever is the less. Also follow the statutory town plan and ridgeline views |
| Lands | Comply with the lease height restriction. An enclosure may be exempted from the lease height calculation as a rooftop utility structure if the size is not excessive |
| Structure | Supports comply with the Buildings Ordinance, the Building (Construction) Regulations, and the relevant codes. Supports, members, and fixings are designed for all load combinations from the system, are approved by the Building Authority, and are corrosion resistant or protected |
| Sway | Adequate restraint against undue sway or motion on every part of the facade that can be exposed to wind |
| Access | Convenient and safe access to the system |
| Platform | Follow PNAP ADV-11 for installation, use, and maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve a roof track and enclosure within 15 m or 10% of building height, whichever is less, and design the supports for wind on every exposed part of the facade.

### 2. If there is no permanent platform (paragraphs 6–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Future davit | Consider mechanical booms or anchorages on the roof so a suspended system can be installed later |
| Temporary scaffold | Hooks, bolts, holes, or platforms for anchorage of temporary structures or scaffolding. Members, fixings, and anchorages corrosion resistant or protected |
| Cast-in anchors | For personal fall-protection on the external wall. Location, design, installation, and maintenance are in the Construction Industry Council technical guidelines, at http://www.hkcic.org |
| Lightwells and re-entrants | Pipes are often placed there; design the access for repair. Prefer access through common areas or areas the deed of mutual covenant designates for inspection and maintenance |
| Lowest level | The lowest level of a re-entrant or lightwell that houses soil and waste pipes or stacks should be a common area, with access, including a cat-ladder where appropriate, for maintenance and refuse clearance |

**SD takeaway:** Even without a gondola, cast in roof anchorages and make the bottom of every pipe lightwell a common area with a way in.
