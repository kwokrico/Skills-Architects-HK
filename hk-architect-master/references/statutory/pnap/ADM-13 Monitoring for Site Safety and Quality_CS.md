# ADM-13 Monitoring for Site Safety and Quality
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2010 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 186; first issue September 1995)

> Scope note: Describes the Site Monitoring Section's inspections of construction, demolition, and selected minor works. It does not replace the supervision duties of the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer.

## Regulatory Overview
The Site Monitoring Section inspects construction and demolition sites for divergence from approved plans, defective materials, missing precautions, lack of supervision, departure from agreed sequence, inexperienced operatives on hazardous work, non-compliance with Building Authority conditions or orders, and contraventions of the Buildings Ordinance. Inspections for plan approval, consent, permits, licences, occupation permits, and performance tests remain with the New Buildings Divisions.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the inspections look for (paragraphs 1–4 and 7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Higher frequency | Demolition, deep excavations, percussion piling, formation of steep slopes, construction of high retaining walls, works projecting over public thoroughfares, and operation of heavy powered mechanical plant |
| Powers | Warrant card to inspect premises and enter land, ascertain compliance, and carry out tests, make openings, and take reasonable samples |
| Remedies | Corrective advice to the AP, RSE, or RGE; orders under sections 23 and 24A; prosecution and disciplinary action where appropriate |
| Outside the Ordinance | Contraventions outside the Buildings Ordinance are referred to the appropriate department |

**SD takeaway:** Treat demolition, deep excavation, percussion piling, steep slopes, high retaining walls, and projections over streets as high-frequency inspection stages and keep the approved sequence on site.

### 2. What must be on site (paragraph 5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Ready access for Site Monitoring Section staff |
| Approved works | Copies of approved plans, agreed work sequence and procedures, and all Building Authority conditions, issued to the registered contractor to keep on site |
| Minor works | Copies of prescribed plans, details, and supervision plans as required, issued to the prescribed registered contractor |
| Explanation | Approved sequence, procedures, conditions, and orders explained to the contractor, with translations where necessary |
| Supervision and precautions | Supervision requirements met; precautions against falling objects |
| Notification | Any contravention from the carrying out of works notified to the Building Authority as soon as possible, and unsatisfactory aspects rectified without delay |

**SD takeaway:** Issue the approved plans, the agreed sequence, and the consent conditions to the contractor to keep on site before the high-risk operations start.

### 3. Minor works under the simplified requirements (paragraph 8)
Minor works carried out under the simplified requirements use submitted plans rather than approved plans, are small in scale, and have a short construction period. Selected cases under construction are subject to audit site-safety inspections rather than the systematic programme used for sites with approved plans. The matters of concern, remedies, and co-operation above apply to the extent of the individual case.

**SD takeaway:** For minor works, still keep the prescribed plans and supervision plan on site; selected sites are audited even though there is no systematic inspection programme.
