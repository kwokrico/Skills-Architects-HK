# APP-116 Aluminium Windows
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 248; first issue July 2001)

> Scope note: Design and installation of aluminium windows accepted as meeting section 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulation. Large windows follow PNAP APP-37. Locking devices of openable sashes follow PNAP APP-37. Repair and replacement may be minor works under PNAP APP-147. Deviations from paragraphs 4 to 9 must be substantiated. A similar note has been issued to registered contractors.

## Regulatory Overview
Aluminium windows must be of suitable quality and fixed so that they perform their function, including structural stability and resistance to water seepage. Compliance with paragraphs 4 to 9 is accepted as meeting section 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Frames, sections, and sash size (paragraphs 4–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Design attention | Horizontal imposed loads, protection of openings, protective-barrier function, locking of openable sashes and sub-frames, corrosion protection, material quality, and protection against spread of fire and smoke between floors |
| Fixing lugs | Stainless steel or hot-dip galvanised steel, minimum material thickness 1.5 mm, at 300 mm centres maximum. A greater spacing must be shown not to compromise structural stability and waterproofing |
| Aluminium thickness | Minimum 2 mm for all structural members |
| Mullion depth | Not less than 38 mm |
| Hinges | Able to withstand positive and negative design wind pressure when the window is closed, and sized for the window. Hold the weight of the sash or sub-frame |
| Side-hung sash | Width not exceeding 700 mm |
| Top-hung sash | Size not exceeding 2.5 m2 |
| Standards | Appendix A to PNAP APP-37 and PNAP APP-53 lists standards acceptable to the Building Authority |

**SD takeaway:** Limit a side-hung sash to 700 mm wide and a top-hung sash to 2.5 m2, with 2 mm aluminium and 38 mm mullions.

### 2. Four-bar hinges (paragraph 8 and Appendix A)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Stainless steel with adjustable friction shoe. Bars minimum thickness 2.5 mm. Stainless steel rivets or screws; aluminium rivets shall not be used |
| Fixings | At least 3 stainless steel rivets of diameter not less than 4.8 mm, or screws of diameter not less than 5 mm, to each bottom and top bar, to the frame and to the sash |
| Dissimilar metals | Measures to prevent contact between stainless steel and aluminium |
| Backing | A stainless steel or hot-dip galvanised plate, bar, or angle not less than 3 mm thick inside the section, or local thickening of the window section to not less than 5 mm. Otherwise the aluminium thickness is substantiated by calculation |
| Length | At least 60% of the width of the side-hung casement sash |

**SD takeaway:** A 4-bar hinge is stainless steel at least 2.5 mm thick, at least 60% of the sash width, and fixed with three rivets or screws into a 3 mm backing or a 5 mm local thickening.

### 3. Water (paragraphs 12–14 and Appendix B)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Drip | The top member of the frame should have a built-in projecting fin with a drip nose. A typical section is at Appendix B |
| Field test | Advisable. Some international methods are in Annex A of the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018 |
| Cleaning | Do not use a corrosive agent on external walls and windows unless it is thoroughly washed with clean water immediately after application |
| Guidance | Guidelines on Prevention of Water Seepage in New Buildings, circular letter dated 7 March 2005 |

**SD takeaway:** Put a drip-nose fin on the head of the frame, and allow for a field water-penetration test.
