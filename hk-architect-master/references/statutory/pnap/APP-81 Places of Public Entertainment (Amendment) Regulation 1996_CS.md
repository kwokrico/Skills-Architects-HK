# APP-81 Places of Public Entertainment (Amendment) Regulation 1996
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued June 1996 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Where design, planning, and construction rules for places of public entertainment moved when the Places of Public Entertainment (Amendment) Regulation 1996 came into operation on 28 June 1996. The amended Regulation itself focuses on licensing.

## Regulatory Overview
Most safety requirements were removed from the principal Regulation and placed in other instruments. Those instruments, and Part III of the 1996 means-of-escape code, apply under Buildings Ordinance section 39(2) and do not apply to building works being carried out, or consented to commence, on or before 28 June 1996.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Where the design rules now sit (paragraph 2)
| Instrument | What it regulates |
| --- | --- |
| Building (Construction) (Amendment) Regulation 1996 | Defines "cinema" and regulates protective barriers at the outer edge of any upper tier in a place of public entertainment |
| Building (Planning) (Amendment) Regulation 1996 | Adds Part VIA on the situation, planning, design, and other matters relevant to a place of public entertainment |
| Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996, Part III | Means of escape for places of public entertainment, transferred from the repealed Regulations, and incorporating part of Practice Note for Authorized Persons 54 |

**SD takeaway:** Design a place of public entertainment to the 1996 construction and planning amendments and to Part III of the 1996 means-of-escape code, not to the old entertainment regulations.
