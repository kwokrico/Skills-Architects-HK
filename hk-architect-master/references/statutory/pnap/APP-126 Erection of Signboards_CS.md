# APP-126 Erection of Signboards
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Planning, design, and construction of a signboard, including one that projects over a street under section 31(1)(aa) of the Buildings Ordinance. Positional limits in the body and Appendix A are below. Other departments’ rules are in Appendices C to E. Signboards that are minor works or designated exempted works follow the minor-works system instead of full approval. It is not a lease or town-plan instrument.

## Regulatory Overview
A signboard erected only to display an advertisement, announcement, or visual information is building works. It may project over a street only if it is not a danger to the building or the public, a traffic or fire hazard, or an obstruction of traffic equipment, means of escape or firefighting access, or of the lighting and ventilation required by the regulations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Clearances (Appendix A, general requirements)
| Situation | Clearance |
| --- | --- |
| Within 1 m of the outer edge of a footpath, or projecting over a carriageway | Not less than 5.8 m beneath every part |
| Over a footpath | Not less than 3.5 m, or not less than 2.5 m if the projection over the footpath is not more than 600 mm |
| Over a tramway | Not less than 7 m beneath every part |
| Street centre | No portion within 1.5 m, measured horizontally, of a vertical line from the nearest point on the centre line. If another signboard is opposite, not less than 3 m clear between them horizontally |
| Projection more than 600 mm from the external wall | Not less than 2.4 m laterally from any part of an adjacent projecting signboard, including guys, frames, and struts, throughout the height of the building, and not less than 1.2 m from the common boundary of an adjoining lot |
| Escape, access, light, and trees | Must not reduce required means of escape, emergency vehicular access, firefighting access, required natural lighting and ventilation, or open space, and must not obstruct trees |

For this appendix the external wall excludes projections under regulation 7 of the Building (Planning) Regulations, canopies, outer walls of balconies and verandahs outside the site, air-conditioning platforms under paragraph 3(iii) of PNAP APP-150, and curtain walls under paragraph 7 of PNAP APP-2.

**SD takeaway:** Keep 5.8 m under a sign over a carriageway, 3.5 m over a footpath, and 2.4 m laterally from the next projecting sign if it stands more than 600 mm off the wall.

### 2. When a signboard is treated as a building (paragraphs 4–6)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Wholly within the site | Still meet the applicable paragraph 3 requirements. A wall or projecting signboard that exceeds Appendix B on dimensions or projection is a building, including gross floor area and site coverage |
| Higher than 3 m on the ground or roof | Must not create enclosed or partly enclosed floor space unless that space is in the gross floor area and site coverage, or is occupied by machinery or ductwork |
| Not minor works or designated exempted works | Prior approval and consent. Fabrication and installation of the fixing, frame, and display surface by a registered general building contractor |

Also comply with the Hong Kong Airport (Control of Obstructions) Ordinance, the Outline Zoning Plan, railway protection, illuminated signs facing the harbour or near the airport, and monuments.

**SD takeaway:** A sign over 3 m on a roof must not enclose floor space unless that space is counted in gross floor area and site coverage.
