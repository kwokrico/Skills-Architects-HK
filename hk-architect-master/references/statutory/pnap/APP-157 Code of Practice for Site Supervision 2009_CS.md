# APP-157 Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Amendments that produce the Code of Practice for Site Supervision (2024 Edition), and the extra check on temporary works that support a tower crane. Qualifications of technically competent persons remain paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24 of the 2021 Edition, with a list of accepted courses in this note. It is not a substitute for the supervision code. Quality-supervision checklists are in PNAP APP-158.

## Regulatory Overview
Circular letters from 2015 to 2021 were incorporated in the 2021 Edition, and further amendments in Appendix A (December 2023) and Appendix B (August 2024) are incorporated in the 2024 Edition on the Buildings Department website. Temporary works supporting a tower crane need an independent check when Form BA 10 is submitted on or after 1 November 2024.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Tower-crane temporary works (paragraph 2)
In addition to paragraph 4.9 of the Supervision Code (2021 Edition), the registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor appoints a design engineer to prepare plans, construction drawings, and design justifications, and an independent checking engineer to check them, for compliance with the Buildings Ordinance, the regulations, and the relevant codes. This applies to all development projects whose specified Form BA 10 is submitted on or after 1 November 2024. Certification forms TW1, TW2, and TW3 are on the Buildings Department website.

**SD takeaway:** From a Form BA 10 dated 1 November 2024, temporary works under a tower crane need both a design engineer and an independent checking engineer.

### 2. Editions (paragraphs 1 and 3)
| Document | Status |
| --- | --- |
| 2021 Edition | Incorporates the 2015–2021 circular letters. Technically competent person qualifications are paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24 |
| Appendix A, December 2023, and Appendix B, August 2024 | Further amendments, brought into the 2024 Edition |
| Accepted courses | A list of local and non-local courses previously accepted as equivalent academic qualifications |

**SD takeaway:** Use the 2024 Edition of the supervision code, and match technically competent persons to paragraphs 8.18 to 8.24 of the 2021 Edition or to a course on the accepted list.
