# APP-38 Bridges over Streets and Lanes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2011 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 107; first issue November 1984)

> Scope note: Exemption under Buildings Ordinance section 31(1) for a bridge that projects over a street or lane. Structural design is in PNAP APP-34. Appearance is judged by the Advisory Committee on the Appearance of Bridges and Associated Structures under Environment, Transport and Works Bureau Technical Circular (Works) No. 36/2004. Planting on footbridges and flyovers is in Technical Circular (Works) No. 10/2005.

## Regulatory Overview
Exemption to project over a street or lane may be given where the bridge is wholly or partly within a private lot, or gains support from or is connected to a building on a private lot, and the Building Authority is satisfied of one of the purposes in paragraph 2(a). A free-standing bridge entirely on government land or over a public highway is exempt from the Buildings Ordinance, plans go directly to the Highways Department, and maintenance responsibility is resolved with that department before construction.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the bridge may be, and when studies are required (paragraph 2)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Required by the lease or a planning-permission condition, or consistent with the outline zoning plan, outline development plan, or layout plan; or functionally necessary for occupants to move between buildings so as to relieve street-level pedestrian traffic, and acceptable to Lands, Planning, Transport, and Highways; or an identified public benefit such as separating pedestrians from road traffic or linking to public transport, public buildings, or the public footbridge network, similarly acceptable; or other justified special circumstances |
| Use | Pedestrian circulation or vehicular links only. Commercial activities are generally not allowed unless there is planning permission and a specific justified public benefit acceptable to the relevant departments, and that floor space is accountable for gross floor area under the outline zoning plan |
| Width and storeys | Full justification against anticipated pedestrian flow. Impact assessment studies on visual and air ventilation may be required if the bridge is more than 1 storey and/or the clear width is more than 6 m |
| Appearance | Acceptable to the Advisory Committee for any bridge or associated highway structure over, under, on, or immediately next to a public road. If the committee does not accept the design, the Building Authority may not allow the section 31(1) exemption |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a footbridge to a single storey and a clear width of 6 m or less unless visual and air-ventilation studies are prepared, and do not put shops on it unless they are planned and counted as gross floor area.

### 2. Submission to the committee (paragraph 3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Copies | Ten sets, sent directly to the secretary, condensed to no larger than A3, simply stapled, plus a CD-ROM or DVD-ROM in PDF or JPG |
| Contents | General arrangement; elevations of all exposed parts with a perspective or axonometric view and enough information for form, texture, and colour; comprehensive site photographs affixed to the plans; for a footbridge, anticipated pedestrian flows on the plans to justify width, design, and location |

**SD takeaway:** Send ten A3 sets to the Advisory Committee, with pedestrian flows marked on the footbridge plans, before relying on a section 31(1) exemption.
