# ADM-11 Change of Address
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2019 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 152; first issue January 1992)

> Scope note: States the statutory notice when a registered professional changes the address at which business is carried on. It does not set design standards.

## Regulatory Overview
Regulation 45 of the Building (Administration) Regulations requires every authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered geotechnical engineer to notify the Building Authority, in the specified form, of any change in the address at which he carries on business, within 14 days of the change. The specified form is Form BA 24.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Notice of a new business address (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Any change in the address at which the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer carries on business |
| Time | Within 14 days |
| Form | Form BA 24 (see PNAP APP-60) |
| Online | Electronic Forms Submission System on www.bd.gov.hk |
| Paper | Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon |

**SD takeaway:** If the practice address changes during a project, file Form BA 24 within 14 days so correspondence and statutory notices still reach the registered professional.
