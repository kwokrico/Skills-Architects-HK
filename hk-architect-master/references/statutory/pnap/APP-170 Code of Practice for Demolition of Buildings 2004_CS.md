# APP-170 Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue October 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Amendments to the Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 in Appendix A (September 2016) and Appendix B (October 2023). A similar note has been issued to registered contractors. It is not a substitute for the demolition code. The code is not intended to cover demolition under the Minor Works Control System.

## Regulatory Overview
The 2016 amendments bring in the registered geotechnical engineer where demolition has a geotechnical concern, add scaffold and debris-net measures, and add guidance for machine demolition and for aged buildings designed to the London County Council codes. The 2023 amendments add propping for light plant, controls at the site boundary and at floor openings, and temporary platforms under cantilevers unless they are cut and lifted.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. September 2016 (Appendix A)
| Change | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Roles | Statutory role of the registered geotechnical engineer where demolition has a geotechnical concern, and the minor-works regime. The code is not intended to cover demolition works under the Minor Works Control System (Type G is named in the amended clause 1.1) |
| Scaffold | Measures to stabilise critical portions of the scaffold, including the catchfan projection, under strong wind and in a typhoon |
| Nets | An extra layer of heavy-duty net against unexpected falling debris |
| Method | Additional design guidance for demolition by machinery, and for demolition of aged buildings designed to London County Council design codes |
| Waste | Updated designated waste-disposal facilities and debris-handling procedures |

**SD takeaway:** A demolition with a geotechnical concern needs the registered geotechnical engineer, and the scaffold catchfan is designed for strong wind and typhoon.

### 2. October 2023 (Appendix B)
| Clause | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Table 3.4 | Propping requirement for light-weight mechanical plant of maximum 5,800 kg |
| 3.8.8 | Precautionary measures at the interface between two adjacent demolition or construction sites |
| 3.8.9 | Safety measures for floor openings and free edges |
| 3.8.10 | Control on conveying debris through floor openings |
| 3.10.7 | Disposal of construction and demolition material |
| 4.2.4(C) and Figure 4.5 | Method and procedures for demolition of an exterior column |
| Appendix F and G figures | Temporary platforms are required unless the cantilevered structures are demolished by cut and lift or a similar technique as stated in clause 3.5.1(B) |

**SD takeaway:** Prop for a machine up to 5,800 kg to Table 3.4, and keep a temporary platform under a cantilever unless it is removed by cut and lift.
