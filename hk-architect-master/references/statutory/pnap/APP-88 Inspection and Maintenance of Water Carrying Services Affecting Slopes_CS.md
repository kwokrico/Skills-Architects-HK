# APP-88 Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue November 1996 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Announces the Works Branch Code of Practice on Inspection and Maintenance of Water Carrying Services Affecting Slopes, to be read with PNAP 168. The inspection methods are in that Code.

## Regulatory Overview
The Code guides inspection and maintenance of buried water-carrying services affecting slopes, for government departments, professionals, contractors, and building managers. Under Buildings Ordinance section 27C, the Building Authority may require an owner to investigate and repair buried water-carrying services laid in or near a slope.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the Code is used for (paragraphs 2–3)
| Aspect | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Programme | Planning an effective inspection and maintenance programme |
| Detection | Options for detecting, inspecting, and repairing buried services affecting a slope |
| Permanent measures | Recommendations to minimize the impact of leakage on slope safety |
| Appointment | Authorized persons, registered structural engineers, and registered contractors refer to the Code when appointed for design, construction, or maintenance of buried services near slopes |

**SD takeaway:** Design buried water-carrying services near a slope to the Works Branch code, because section 27C can require investigation and repair if leakage threatens the slope.
