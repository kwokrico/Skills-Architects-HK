# APP-63 Geoguide 1 Guide to Retaining Wall Design
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision November 2020 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 166; first issue August 1994)

> Scope note: Which edition of Geoguide 1 the Building Authority will accept for permanent retaining walls. The design rules themselves are in Geoguide 1, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
Geoguide 1, published by the Geotechnical Engineering Office, is the recommended standard of good practice for permanent retaining walls in Hong Kong, including conventional reinforced concrete walls, gravity walls such as crib, gabion, and mass concrete walls, and cantilevered retaining walls. A wall designed entirely to either the first edition (1982) or the second edition (1993, as continuously updated) is acceptable; a mixture of the two editions is not.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which edition to use (paragraphs 2–3)
| Edition | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| First edition, 1982 | Limit state analysis with global factors of safety |
| Second edition, 1993, as amended | Limit state analysis with partial factors of safety, plus guidance on geotechnical parameters, groundwater pressures, and the link between design and construction. The updated text is on the Civil Engineering and Development Department website |
| Submission | State clearly which edition has been used. Designs based on the second edition are strongly recommended |

**SD takeaway:** Design the whole retaining wall to the second edition of Geoguide 1 and say so on the submission; do not mix first-edition and second-edition factors.
