# APP-138 Appointment of Authorised Signatory to Act for a Registered Contractor
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2004 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 290)

> Scope note: Who may act as the authorised signatory of a registered contractor, how a change is notified, and the limits on a temporary substitute. It is not a design note. Continuous supervision duties sit in sections 9(5) and 9(6) of the Buildings Ordinance, Part V of the Building (Administration) Regulations, the Technical Memorandum for Supervision Plans, and the Code of Practice for Site Safety Supervision.

## Regulatory Overview
Every registered contractor appoints at least one authorised signatory, and the Building Authority accepts only one authorised signatory to act for the contractor on each project, being the person who signs Form BA 10. If that person is absent and no acceptable substitute is acting, Building (Administration) Regulation 22(2) requires the works to cease until another contractor is appointed.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. One signatory per project (paragraphs 2–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Forms | The signatory of Forms BA 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 14A, 18, and 20 is the person responsible for the contractor’s duties. The signatory of Form BA 10 is deemed to be that person |
| Name on forms | From 1 July 2004, a specified form without the name is incomplete and unacceptable. If the supervision-plan name differs from Form BA 10, 13, or 14, the plan or the form is rejected unless the change has been approved or notified |
| Contractor with only one signatory | A change is not accepted unless an application to add the new signatory to the registration has been made under PNRC 38 and approved |
| Contractor with more than one | Notify on the standard form at Appendix A to PNRC 59, countersigned by the authorized person, not less than 7 days before the change. Otherwise the original signatory remains responsible |

**SD takeaway:** Name one authorised signatory on Form BA 10, and give 7 days’ notice before changing to another signatory of the same contractor.

### 2. Temporary absence (paragraphs 5–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Notice | Appendix B to PNRC 59, countersigned by the authorized person, at least 7 days before the absence. For sudden illness or accident, documentary proof within 3 days |
| Who may act | Another authorised signatory of the same contractor on the same register or sub-register, or an authorised signatory currently acting for another contractor on the same register or sub-register. A partnership or corporation needs an authorisation letter from the rest of the partners or the required quorum of directors |
| Period | Maximum 14 days. Extension only with documentary proof of special circumstances such as illness. Total temporary acting permitted is a maximum of 30 days |
| If the nominee does not qualify | The nomination is not accepted, and the works may have to cease |
| Beyond 30 days | Change to another signatory of the same company, or apply to add a new signatory, under paragraph 4 |
| What the temporary signatory may sign | Inspection records required by the accepted supervision plan. Not supervision plans or specified forms |

**SD takeaway:** A stand-in authorised signatory can supervise for at most 14 days, and 30 days in total, and cannot sign the specified forms.
