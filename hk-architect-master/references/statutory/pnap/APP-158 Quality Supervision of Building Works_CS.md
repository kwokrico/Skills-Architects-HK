# APP-158 Quality Supervision of Building Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: How quality supervision sits inside the supervision plan, and the common inspection items for superstructure, excavation and lateral support, and site formation in Appendix A. Foundation works, ground investigation, and soil nails are already guided by the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009. Appendix A is not exhaustive. A similar note has been issued to registered contractors. It is not a workmanship specification.

## Regulatory Overview
The authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered geotechnical engineer supervise in accordance with the supervision plan, and the registered contractor provides continuous supervision. Quality supervision is the part of that plan that checks the works against the Buildings Ordinance, the approved plans, and the approval or consent conditions.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Checklists (paragraphs 2–3)
The authorized person, structural engineer, geotechnical engineer, and the contractor’s authorized signatory devise checklists from the typical items in the 2009 code, plus any item essential to the project and any condition imposed on approval or consent. The code already identifies inspection items and critical stages for foundation works, ground-investigation fieldwork, and soil nailing, and guides which sections are critical activities.

Appendix A of this note adds common inspection items for superstructure, excavation and lateral support, and site formation. Other items that belong on a project checklist include curtain walls, glass walls, prestressed concrete, structural steelwork, and application and testing of waterproofing.

**SD takeaway:** Put curtain wall, waterproofing tests, and any consent condition on the quality-supervision checklist, in addition to the Appendix A items for the superstructure or excavation.

### 2. Records on site (paragraphs 4–5)
Each stream operates its own part of the supervision plan and inspects, including personally, so that the plan is fulfilled and the technically competent persons do their duties. All checklists and inspection records are kept on site for the Site Monitoring Section or other representatives of the Building Authority. A sample record is in Appendix B. The streams may adapt that record to the project.

**SD takeaway:** Keep the quality-supervision checklists and inspection records on site for audit.
