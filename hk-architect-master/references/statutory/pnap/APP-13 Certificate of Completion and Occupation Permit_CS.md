# APP-13 Certificate of Completion and Occupation Permit
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2023 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 53; first issue July 1978)

> Scope note: Certificates under regulation 25 of the Building (Administration) Regulations, occupation permits under section 21, record plans, and the schedule of materials. It does not set design standards. The checklists in Appendices F and G are the lists of documents to clear before a certificate is submitted. Fast-track demolition completion is in PNAP APP-21.

## Regulatory Overview
A new building is certified on Form BA12 or BA13, and works that do not result in a new building on Form BA14, BA14A, BA14B, or BA14C, or on Form MW02 or MW04 where minor works apply. The Buildings Department generally replies to Form BA14 or BA14A within 28 days, to Form BA14B or BA14C within 14 days if not audited and 28 days if audited, and processes Form BA12 or BA13 with the occupation-permit application within 14 days under section 21.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What must be approved and recorded before occupation (paragraphs 2–17)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Spread footing not deeper than 4.5 m, same contractor for foundation and superstructure | Completion of the footing and the superstructure may be on one Form BA12, BA13, or BA14. A separate Form BA14 for the footing is not required |
| Amendments | Secure approval of amendment plans before the occupation-permit application. The exception is a minor positional deviation that does not contravene the Ordinance or the regulations |
| Record plans | Where there are approved amendments, submit general building plans that amalgamate every approved plan, each carrying the approval chop or the authorized person's certification that it is identical to the approved plan. Reduced hard copy is acceptable at not less than 1:200, or a non-rewritable DVD-ROM. The plans contain no unapproved amendment. A further disc in AutoCAD or Microstation, marked for the Rating and Valuation Department, is encouraged |
| Materials | A schedule under regulation 44 with the occupation-permit application. The authorized person certifies performance. The contractor confirms use. Cover materials not shown on the approved plans whose use affects structural integrity or fire safety, and the cast-iron pipes and fittings in PNAP APP-133. Fire-rated doorsets and non-loadbearing partitions may use a product-conformity certificate under paragraph 4 of PNAP APP-165 |
| Fire dampers | May be installed by the general building contractor and a ventilation specialist, or as minor works. A ventilation specialist inspects and certifies them under clause E8.3 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011, including when the general contractor installed them |
| Structural documents | Testing reports, mill certificates, method statements, construction records, compliance certificates, foundation record plans, foundation reports, monitoring records, and quality documents, to the approval conditions. Summary tables at half-yearly intervals and at foundation completion and the occupation-permit application. If alteration works last less than half a year, the table goes in at completion. The foundation report includes the as-built summary in Appendix E |
| Soft copies with the occupation permit | Documents required by PNAP ADM-2, APP-126, APP-151, APP-152, and APP-156, on DVD-ROM, for the Buildings Department website |

**SD takeaway:** Approve every amendment before the occupation-permit application, and lodge the materials schedule, the structural-document summaries, and the fire-damper certificate with that application.

### 2. Fast-track alteration and addition (paragraphs 18–20)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible works | Simple alteration and addition under paragraph 29 of PNAP ADM-19; repair or replacement of curtain wall, window wall, and cladding under paragraph 32 of PNAP ADM-19; erection or alteration of a signboard under paragraphs 8 to 10 of PNAP APP-126 |
| Submission | Form BA14C with record plans, the required documents, colour photos of the works, the facades, and the components, and a mark-up of the photo directions |
| Time | Generally 14 days if not audited. If selected for audit, written notice within 14 days and a reply generally within 28 days |

**SD takeaway:** Use Form BA14C only for the three classes of simple, curtain-wall, or signboard works named in paragraphs 18(a) to (c).
