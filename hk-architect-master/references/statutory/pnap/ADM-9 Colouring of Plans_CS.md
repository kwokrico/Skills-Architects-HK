# ADM-9 Colouring of Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision August 2015 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 127; first issue March 1988)

> Scope note: Sets the preferred colours that separate existing work from proposed work on the copy submitted for approval. Non-use of the preferred colours is a processing matter; the note does not itself set structural or planning standards.

## Regulatory Overview
Regulation 14(3) of the Building (Administration) Regulations requires one copy of every plan submitted for approval to be coloured so that existing works are clearly differentiated from proposed new works, and one part of any proposed new works from other parts. The same colouring or other identification must appear on all sets of amendment plans submitted to the Buildings Department.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Preferred colours (paragraph 2 and Appendix A)
Plot screening is 100 (full intensity). RGB values are red, green, blue. AutoCAD colour index is in parentheses.

| Material or status | Preferred colour | RGB | AutoCAD index |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hardcore or dry fill | Putty | 204, 178, 102 | 43 |
| Brick | Orange red | 255, 63, 0 | 20 |
| Concrete slab (lighter wash) | Witch haze | 223, 255, 127 | 61 |
| Concrete, plain or reinforced | British racing green | 0, 76, 38 | 118 |
| Solid concrete blocks | Electric blue | 127, 223, 255 | 141 |
| Hollow concrete blocks | Purple | 191, 127, 255 | 191 |
| Lightweight partition (for example plasterboard) | Macaroni and cheese | 255, 191, 127 | 31 |
| Plaster or cement rendering | Wild willow | 204, 204, 102 | 53 |
| Impermeable or non-absorbent floor or wall | Neon pink | 255, 127, 223 | 221 |
| Glass | Electric blue | 127, 255, 255 | 131 |
| Timber | Muesli | 153, 133, 76 | 45 |
| Metal work or steel | Heliotrope | 223, 127, 255 | 201 |

| Demolition or deletion of approved works | Blue | 0, 63, 255 | 160 |

**SD takeaway:** Colour the approval copy to this legend, and repeat the same identification on every set of amendment plans.

### 2. Drawing notes (Appendix A)
| Note | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | Ducts, light-wells, and lift shafts should be left uncoloured |
| (b) | Elevations and larger plan areas may use lighter washes of the preferred colours |
| (c) | A light colour wash should identify phased development |
| (d) | A colour legend or key should be added to all sets of drawings |

**SD takeaway:** Leave shafts and light-wells uncoloured, wash large areas lightly, and put a colour key on every set.
