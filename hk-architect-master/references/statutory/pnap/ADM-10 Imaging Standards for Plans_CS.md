# ADM-10 Imaging Standards for Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision October 2013 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 135; first issue June 1990)

> Scope note: Drawing standards so approved plans can be stored as readable electronic images. Non-conformity is not a ground for disapproval under the Buildings Ordinance.

## Regulatory Overview
The Buildings Department images approved building plans, and this note asks for drawing standards that keep those images clear. The standards apply to building plans, drainage plans, site formation plans, structural plans, and the calculations referred to in paragraphs 8 and 9 of PNAP ADM-8, but failure to follow them does not by itself justify disapproval.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Sheet size, border, and stamp space (Appendix A, items 1, 2, and 4)
| Sheet | Outside edges | Minimum unused border |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A0 | 1189 mm × 841 mm | 20 mm |
| A1 | 841 mm × 594 mm | 20 mm |
| A2 | 594 mm × 420 mm | 10 mm |
| A3 | 420 mm × 297 mm | 10 mm |
| A4 | 297 mm × 210 mm | 10 mm |

Sizes larger than A0 should be avoided. Every plan needs a minimum unused space 90 mm wide by 150 mm high at the lower right corner for the Building Authority's stamps of approval and certification of copies.

**SD takeaway:** Keep sheets to A0 or smaller, hold the stated borders, and reserve 90 mm × 150 mm at the lower right of every plan for the approval stamp.

### 2. Content that must survive imaging (Appendix A, item 3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Unique drawing number; version as a suffix such as (Rev.A), (Rev.B) |
| Title | Describe contents, location, and type of works |
| Reading direction | All notes read from the same direction; this does not include dimensions |
| Scale | A numbered or graphic scale corresponding to the plan |
| Legend | At least one plan carries a legend of markings and symbols, preferably on the right-hand side |
| Colour | Colours will not be identifiable on images; identify materials by hatching or annotation (for example rc, br, ms) as well as colour |
| Lettering | Do not cover lettering or numbering with heavy hatching, dark shading, or dark colouring |
| Print | Matt surface and adequate contrast are desirable |

**SD takeaway:** Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish materials; hatch or annotate them so the imaged record remains readable.

### 3. Plan index with the occupation permit (Appendix A, item 5)
An occupation-permit application should be accompanied by a Plan Index listing all approved plans, on an A-size sheet, with a separate index for each category (building, structural, drainage, and so on). Columns are BD drawing number (for BD use), AP/RSE drawing number, description, and approval date. An asterisk marks the latest approved plans which represent the building as completed. This is in addition to the record plan system.

**SD takeaway:** Prepare a separate A-size plan index per plan category, and asterisk the latest approved sheets that match the completed building, before the occupation-permit application.
