# APP-119 Stair-well and Open Wells in School and Other Buildings Used by Youngsters
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue May 2001 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 253)

> Scope note: Advice following a coroner’s recommendation on open stair-wells in schools and other premises used by children and youngsters, plus the statutory barrier height. It is not a school means-of-escape code.

## Regulatory Overview
Open wells in staircases should be avoided in new school buildings as far as practicable, and the same consideration applies to other premises used by children and youngsters. Where an open well or stair-well is accessible to students or youngsters, additional safety measures are to be evaluated, and the barrier rules still apply.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Open wells used by youngsters (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Schools | Take the coroner’s recommendation into account as far as practicable: staircases with an open well should be avoided in new school buildings |
| Other premises | Same consideration for premises for use by children and youngsters |
| If an open well remains | Evaluate whether proper or improper use would create undue hazard, and provide additional measures such as safety nets or a higher railing where appropriate |

**SD takeaway:** Avoid an open stair-well in a new school, and if youngsters can reach an open well, add a safety net or a higher railing.

### 2. Statutory barrier height (paragraphs 3–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Open stair-well | Adequate protective measures. Handrail satisfying paragraph 17.6 of the Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996 and paragraph 8 of the Third Schedule of the Building (Planning) Regulations |
| Balustrade height | Not less than 1100 mm, as required by Building (Construction) Regulation 8 |
| Other level changes | Suitable protective barriers where the difference in levels is greater than 600 mm |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the balustrade at not less than 1100 mm, and protect every drop greater than 600 mm.
