# APP-56 Exemption Criteria for Site Formation Works in the New Territories
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 1997 | Buildings Department (first issued August 1991; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009). Appendix A was revised June 1996.

> Scope note: When the Director of Lands may certify exemption of site formation works associated with building works already exempted under the Buildings Ordinance (Application to the New Territories) Ordinance, Cap. 121. If the criteria or conditions cannot be met, site formation plans are submitted to the Buildings Department in the normal way.

## Regulatory Overview
Cap. 121 provides for a certificate of exemption from sections 4, 9, 14, 21, and 30 of the Buildings Ordinance and the Building Regulations for certain New Territories building works, and section 6(a) provides for a certificate of exemption for the associated site formation. The District Lands Officer considers that certificate only when every criterion in paragraph 3 is met.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Criteria and conditions (paragraphs 3–4 and Appendix A)
| Test | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Gradient across the lot, boundary to boundary | Not more than 15 degrees |
| Overall gradient of the area bounded by lines 10 m outside the lot boundary in any direction | Less than 15 degrees |
| Slope within 10 m outside the lot | None steeper than 30 degrees or higher than 1.5 m |
| Retaining wall or terrace wall in the lot or within 10 m outside it | None higher than 1.5 m |
| Walls that may be built under the certificate | No retaining or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m. Combined height of retaining wall and slope not exceeding 1.5 m. Retaining height to breadth ratio not greater than two. Masonry or concrete |
| Slopes that may be built | None steeper than 30 degrees or higher than 1.5 m |
| Building-works certificate | All retaining walls are excluded from that exemption and are site formation works. Any structure retaining earth or fill is a retaining wall |

**SD takeaway:** A New Territories exempted house can avoid a Buildings Department site-formation submission only if the lot and the 10 m margin are flatter than 15 degrees and no slope or wall over 1.5 m is present or proposed.
