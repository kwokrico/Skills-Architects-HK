# APP-98 Lighting and Ventilation for Bathrooms and Lavatories in Domestic Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2013 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 219; first issue December 1997)

> Scope note: Modification of Regulation 36 of the Building (Planning) Regulations so that an internal bathroom or lavatory in a domestic unit need not have a window. It applies to new and existing domestic buildings. Water-heater rules remain in Regulation 35A and PNAP APP-27.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority will give favourable consideration to a modification where the room is part of a domestic unit of accommodation, is of a reasonable size, and the modification is unlikely to compromise public health and safety. The modification is granted with the conditions in paragraph 3.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ventilation and lighting conditions (paragraph 3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanical ventilation | Artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation producing 5 air changes per hour whenever the room is in use. Air is changed with the outside. Ventilation ducting with access for cleaning is acceptable |
| Permanent ventilation | Additional permanent ventilation to the open air under Regulation 2, to the external air under Regulation 31, or to another room whose window meets the area requirement for the combined rooms. A duct with cleaning access, or an aperture in a wall or door, permanently open or protected with louvres, of minimum size 1/20 of the floor area of the room |
| Duct access, unless the duct is demountable or replaceable | At least one access door or panel every 4 metres, at an easily accessible location, at every change of direction, and adjacent to turning vanes or dampers. Minimum 150 mm by 225 mm where the duct is 150 mm by 150 mm or above; 100 mm by 225 mm where the duct is smaller |
| Water heating | Regulation 35A and PNAP APP-27, where applicable |
| System per unit | Each unit has its own artificial lighting, mechanical ventilation, and ducting. A communal system serving more than one unit needs proper management, normally a property management company on occupation or on completion of the alteration |
| Cleaning | Ducting should preferably be inspected and cleaned annually |

**SD takeaway:** An internal domestic bathroom can omit the Regulation 36 window only with 5 air changes per hour to outside air, plus a permanent opening of at least 1/20 of the room floor area, and its own fan and duct unless management is in place.
