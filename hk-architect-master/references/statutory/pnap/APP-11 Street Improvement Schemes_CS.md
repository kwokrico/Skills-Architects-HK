# APP-11 Street Improvement Schemes
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This update January 1990 | Buildings Department (first issued May 1978; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Building plans for lots affected by a street improvement scheme approved before the Roads (Works, Use and Compensation) Ordinance, Cap. 370, was enacted in 1982. It does not itself authorize resumption or compensation.

## Regulatory Overview
A street improvement scheme approved before 1982 remains valid, and the principles in this note continue to apply to those schemes and the lots they affect. A street improvement plan is only an indication of desirable improvements.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What an old street-improvement plan does and does not do (paragraphs 2–5)
| Situation | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Existence of an approved street improvement plan | Not a representation that Government will implement it, or will resume the whole or any part of a property. Not a basis for a claim for compensation or damages |
| Building plans that conform to the plan | Approval under the Buildings Ordinance does not imply that the street plan will be implemented, or that compensation or damages will be paid |
| Building plans that do not conform | Approval is entirely without prejudice to any action Government may take under any other enactment, including resumption of the whole or any part of the property |

**SD takeaway:** Do not treat a pre-1982 street improvement plan as either a right to compensation or a guarantee that the widening will happen.

### 2. Enquiry before submission (paragraph 6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| When | Development or redevelopment of a lot that appears to be affected by an approved street improvement scheme |
| Who | Enquire of the Highways Department, before formal submission of building plans, to ascertain Government's intention |

**SD takeaway:** Ask the Highways Department whether the old scheme will be implemented before drawing plans that either follow it or ignore it.
