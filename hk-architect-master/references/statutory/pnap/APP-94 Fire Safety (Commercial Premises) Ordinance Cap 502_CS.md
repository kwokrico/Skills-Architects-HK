# APP-94 Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance Cap 502
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision October 2010 | Buildings Department (first issue; the Ordinance came into operation on 2 May 1997 and the amendment on 1 June 1998)

> Scope note: Which commercial premises and buildings must be upgraded to the fire-safety standards in the Fire Safety (Commercial Premises) Ordinance, Cap. 502. Construction requirements are enforced by the Director of Buildings; fire service installations by the Director of Fire Services. Detailed construction rules are in the 1996 means-of-escape code, the 1996 fire-resisting-construction code, and the 1995 means-of-access code, as listed against Schedules 2 and 5.

## Regulatory Overview
Prescribed commercial premises are those used, or proposed to be used, for banking other than merchant banking, off-course betting, a jewellery or goldsmith's business with a security area, a supermarket, hypermarket, or department store, or a shopping arcade, where the total floor area exceeds 230 m2. A specified commercial building is one constructed or used for office, business, trade, or entertainment, whose building-works plans were first submitted for approval on or before 1 March 1987, or which was constructed on or before that date where no plans were submitted by then.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Measures that may be required (paragraphs 2–6)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Floor area of prescribed premises | Calculated under sections 3(3) to 3(6). Appendix A illustrates shopping-arcade demarcation |
| Construction | Adequate means of escape; adequate means of access for firefighting and rescue; measures to inhibit spread of fire and to ensure structural integrity |
| Installations | Automatic sprinkler system; automatic cut-off devices for a mechanical ventilation system; emergency lighting; manual fire alarms; portable fire extinguishers; fire hydrant and hose reel system; and the requirements in the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment 1994 |
| Codes for the construction requirements | Means of escape 1996; fire resisting construction 1996; means of access for firefighting and rescue 1995 |

**SD takeaway:** If a shop, arcade, or similar use exceeds 230 m2, or the commercial building's plans were first submitted on or before 1 March 1987, design the upgrade to the three 1995–1996 fire codes and allow for sprinklers and a hydrant and hose-reel system.

### 2. Directions (paragraphs 9–10)
| Instrument | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Fire safety direction under section 5(1), for prescribed commercial premises | Served on the owner by the Director of Buildings, requiring compliance with Schedule 2 |
| Fire safety improvement direction under section 5(1)(A), for a specified commercial building | Served on the owner, requiring compliance with Schedule 5 |
| After conviction for failure to comply | The Director may apply to a magistrate under section 6(1) for a fire safety compliance order or a fire safety improvement compliance order |

**SD takeaway:** Treat a direction under section 5 as a design brief for the affected owner: the works are those in Schedule 2 or Schedule 5, not a voluntary upgrade.

### 3. Alternatives and the plan submission (paragraphs 11–14)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Practical difficulty | The Director of Buildings will consider alternative improvement measures based on fire safety engineering principles that equally achieve the primary objectives of the Ordinance |
| Advisory committee | The Advisory Committee for the Fire Safety (Buildings) Ordinance and this Ordinance, established on 1 June 2010, deliberates those alternatives when necessary. Terms of reference are in Appendix A to PNAP APP-145 |
| Non-exempted works | Apply for approval under the Buildings Ordinance in the usual way, whether the works are voluntary or required by a direction. The Fire Services Department requires three sets of plans showing fire service installations and equipment, instead of two |
| Fee | No fee under Building (Administration) Regulation 42 if the proposals are entirely for improving fire safety measures pursuant to a fire safety direction or a fire safety improvement direction |

**SD takeaway:** Where the prescriptive construction items cannot be built, offer a fire-engineering alternative, and submit three sets of fire-service plans; a direction-only submission is not charged under Regulation 42.
