# APP-155 Signboard Validation Scheme
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Validation of existing unauthorized signboards erected before 2 September 2013, so they may continue in use. New signboards follow PNAP APP-126 and the minor-works items. Technical specifications and positions are in Appendix A and match the minor-works descriptions. Signboards that are not eligible, or are not validated, remain subject to enforcement.

## Regulatory Overview
The scheme has been in operation since 2 September 2013 for an existing unauthorized signboard that fits a minor-works signboard description, which may be validated by safety inspection, strengthening if needed, and certification. Validation lasts 5 years and then must be renewed or the signboard removed.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What can be validated (paragraphs 1–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Erected before 2 September 2013 |
| Types | Projecting signboards; wall signboards, including shopfront signboards; signboards on the roof; outdoor signboards fixed on-grade; outdoor signboards with a spread footing; signboards on or hung beneath the soffit of a balcony or canopy other than a cantilevered slab |
| Who certifies | Only a prescribed building professional and/or prescribed registered contractor registered for the class, type, or item that covers erection and alteration of that signboard |
| Cycle | Safety inspection and certification every 5 years, or removal |

**SD takeaway:** An unauthorized signboard erected on or after 2 September 2013 cannot use this scheme, and a validated one must be revalidated every 5 years.

### 2. Who inspects and which form (paragraph 4)
| Class of the matching minor-works item | Inspection | If strengthening is required | Notification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Authorized person, and a registered structural engineer if the signboard is not a specified construction | Prescribed registered contractor under the authorized person’s and, as necessary, the structural engineer’s supervision | Form SC01 with record photos, fixing details, plans, and structural calculations, plus the safety-inspection checklist. Strengthening: SC01 for commencement and SC01C for completion |
| II | Prescribed building professional (authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered inspector) or prescribed registered contractor | Prescribed registered contractor | Form SC02 with record photos, fixing details, and plans, plus the checklist |
| III | Same as Class II | Prescribed registered contractor | Form SC03 with record photos and fixing details, plus the checklist. Completion of any strengthening is also notified on Form SC03 |

**SD takeaway:** A large unauthorized signboard that matches Class I needs an authorized person and, unless it is a specified construction, a registered structural engineer, with structural calculations on Form SC01.
