# ADM-8 Structural Design Information
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2023 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 121; first issue July 1986)

> Scope note: How structural plans and calculations are organised so the Buildings Department can process them. Member design itself is in the structural codes.

## Regulatory Overview
Structural plans and calculations submitted for approval must be indexed and split so that a synopsis stays on the Buildings Department record and the bulk element-by-element printout can be returned. Design imposed loads shown on the structural plans must also appear on the general building plans.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What the drawing set must show (paragraphs 2–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Index | Each drawing has a title and number |
| First drawing | General notes and material specification: design codes; concrete materials including cement, aggregates, pulverised fuel ash, and ground granulated blastfurnace slag; reinforcement and other materials; grade and characteristic strength; concrete covers, corrosion protection, and fire resistance; method of construction, workmanship, and testing |
| Drawings | Key plans, floor and layout plans, sections, and elevations showing layout, dimensions, levels, and identification of all frames and members |
| Imposed loads | On the corresponding floor or layout plan, including allowance for partitions, screeds, dynamic effects, and the like. Different loads on different parts of a floor are shown diagrammatically. The same information is shown on the general building plans |
| Alteration and addition | Reinforcement or steelwork of existing members affected by the works, extracted from the approved plans of those members |
| Geotechnical content | For foundation, retaining wall, excavation and lateral support, and site formation: ground-investigation boreholes, adjacent buildings and their foundations, land, streets, utilities, slopes, nullahs, and retaining walls; precautionary and protective measures, monitoring, and contingency plans. See the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 and PNAP APP-18, APP-54, APP-57, and ADM-15 |

**SD takeaway:** Put design codes, material grades, covers, fire resistance, and imposed loads on the structural set, and repeat the imposed loads on the general building plans.

### 2. Part I calculations retained, Part II returned (paragraphs 9–11)
Part I is retained. Part II is the detailed analysis, usually bulk printout from a pre-accepted program, and is returned after processing.

| Part I content | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| (a) Synopsis | Foundation and structural system and how loads reach the ground; design method, assumptions, codes, and limits on stress and deformation |
| (b) Computer | Program name, version, developer, Buildings Department reference and validity; accepted scope and limitations; model assumptions and parameters; input graphics or sketch of frames, nodes, elements, and fixity; summary of critical moment, shear, and displacement and a compatibility check of applied forces against overall reactions; how the output is used in the design |
| (c) Lateral loads | Load-resisting system and model; diagrams of frames and members; sectional properties; lateral loads and distribution to frames at each floor; principal reactions; equilibrium check at foundation and at levels where the structural configuration changes |
| (d) Dead and imposed | Design data, and principal reactions in vertical members at foundation and all floor levels |
| (e)–(f) | Design of major transfer members, and of cantilevered canopies, balconies, corbels, and major structural appendages |
| (g) Geotechnical | Geological conditions from the investigation, adopted parameters, founding stratum and allowable bearing capacity, and a summary of effects on nearby buildings, structures, slopes, retaining walls, land, streets, and services |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the stability synopsis, computer reference, lateral-load equilibrium check, transfer members, and major cantilevers in Part I, because that is the part the Department retains.

### 3. New methods, checklists, and electronic files (paragraphs 12–16)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| New theory, material, form, or method | Consult through the pre-submission enquiry in PNAP ADM-19 before detailed design. If not done, one additional set goes to the Technical Services Unit and the principles are considered in parallel |
| Checklist | Include the completed Appendix A checklist so plans can be referred to other departments |
| Electronic Submission Hub | Encouraged in lieu of paper or CD/DVD; requirements are in PNAP ADM-17 |
| CD/DVD still accepted for Part II | Non-rewriteable CD-ROM (ISO 9660) or DVD-ROM (ISO/IEC 13346:1995). Print the Buildings Department reference, title, name of RSE or RGE, and disc number on the cover and sign the cover. Files in PDF, each page not larger than A3, with an index, searchable text, and images at not less than 300 dpi |
| Disc | Not replaceable and not returned. Supplementary Part II material goes on a further disc in the same format |

**SD takeaway:** If the structure uses a new theory, material, or form, clear it under ADM-19 before the formal structural submission, and put the program's Buildings Department reference in Part I.
