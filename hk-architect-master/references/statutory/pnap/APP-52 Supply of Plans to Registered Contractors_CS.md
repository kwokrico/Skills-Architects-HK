# APP-52 Supply of Plans to Registered Contractors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2010 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 139; first issue December 1990)

> Scope note: The authorized person's duty under Building (Administration) Regulation 36, and under section 55 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation for Class I minor works, to give the contractor the approved plans. It does not change what must be shown on those plans.

## Regulatory Overview
The appointed authorized person must supply the registered general building contractor, registered specialist contractor, and registered minor works contractor with a copy of the plans approved under Regulation 30, a copy of the structural details prepared by the registered structural engineer and approved by the Building Authority, and a copy of any supervision plan. Only a copy is required, but it must be an exact reproduction of the plans as approved, stamped, signed, and dated.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Timing and Class I minor works (paragraphs 3–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| When | The regulation sets no time limit, but copies of the latest plans must be supplied promptly so continuous supervision can be carried out properly |
| Class I minor works | Under section 55, deliver to the prescribed registered contractor a copy of the prescribed plans and details submitted under section 30(c), and, if the technical memorandum requires it, a copy of the supervision plan submitted under section 30(d) or 48(2)(b) or (3)(b) |

**SD takeaway:** Issue the contractor an exact stamped copy of the latest approved plans, structural details, and any supervision plan before work that those drawings control is carried out.
