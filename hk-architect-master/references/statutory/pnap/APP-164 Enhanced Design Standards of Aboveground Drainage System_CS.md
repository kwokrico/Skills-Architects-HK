# APP-164 Enhanced Design Standards of Aboveground Drainage System
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision November 2023 | Buildings Department (first issue April 2021)

> Scope note: Enhanced standards for floor drains, anti-syphonage pipes, branch discharge pipes, ventilating pipes, and rainwater pipes, required under section 28(1) of the Buildings Ordinance while amendment of the drainage regulations is pursued. Floor-drain traps still comply with regulation 25 of those regulations. It is not a below-ground drainage code.

## Regulatory Overview
New drainage plans, or a major revision, submitted after 31 August 2021 must comply, and where consent to superstructure works is granted after that date, paragraphs 2(b), 2(c), and 2(f) apply and amended plans are submitted, or the minor-amendment route in paragraphs 22 to 24 of PNAP ADM-19 may be used. The note also applies to aboveground drainage commenced under the minor-works system after 31 August 2021.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Floor drain and stack ventilation (paragraph 2(a)–(b))
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Kitchen, toilet, or pantry floor drain | Used water from a lavatory basin, bath, or shower is diverted to replenish the trap seal, without backflow. The trap complies with regulation 25 |
| Connection slope and size | Connecting pipes slope upwards at not more than 67.5 degrees from the stack, internal diameter not less than the main anti-syphonage pipe |
| Spacing, more than 5 and not more than 20 storeys | Connections at intervals of not more than 5 storeys, from the lowest storey with a branch discharge pipe on the stack |
| Spacing, over 20 storeys | Intervals of not more than 3 storeys |
| Storey count | Irrespective of how many storeys the stack serves. A stack that only serves a podium or basement is spaced by the storeys of that podium or basement |
| Offset | A connection below the lowest branch discharge pipe and above the bend of an offset. None is required between offsets that have no branch discharge pipes. An offset is a change from vertical to horizontal, and also where the stack discharges into a manhole |

**SD takeaway:** Feed the kitchen, toilet, and pantry floor-drain trap from a basin, bath, or shower, and vent the stack at least every 5 storeys up to 20 storeys and every 3 storeys above that.

### 2. Restricted zone and separate lowest-floor pipework (paragraph 2(c)–(e))
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| No connection in the restricted zone | Up to 600 mm above the bend for a building of not more than 5 storeys, and 1 000 mm above the bend for a building over 5 storeys, measured from the invert of the tail of the bend, and within 2 500 mm horizontally from the bend |
| Not applied to | The separate pipework required by paragraphs 2(d) and 2(e) |
| More than 3 and not more than 20 storeys | Sanitary fitments on the floor where the stack discharges to a manhole are not connected to the stack. Separate pipework discharges directly to a manhole |
| Over 20 storeys | Fitments on that floor and the floor above are not connected to the stack. The pipework for those two floors can be combined if the drainage regulations and PNAP APP-93 and APP-164 are met |

**SD takeaway:** Keep branch connections 2 500 mm clear of an offset bend, and take the lowest floor, plus the floor above on a building over 20 storeys, off the stack.

### 3. Vent terminal, rainwater pipes, and trap distance (paragraphs 2(f), 5–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Open end of a ventilating pipe on the roof | Not less than 3 m from a common boundary with an adjacent site, and from any window, opening, or fresh-air intake |
| Accessible roofs | Advisable to carry ventilation pipes not less than 3 m above the accessible roof, in addition to paragraph 2(f) |
| Hopper heads | Consider omitting them, to avoid blockage by debris and vegetation |
| Rainwater pipes of internal diameter not more than 100 mm | 40 mm serves 9 m2; 65 mm serves 33 m2; 80 mm serves 58 m2; 100 mm serves 106 m2 of horizontal roofed-over surface |
| Existing repair | Advise clients to consider these requirements, including replacing an open hopper-head system with a closed system and traps under regulations 12(2), 12(3), and 25 |
| Regulation 30(2)(b)(ii) | The Building Authority is prepared to allow the anti-syphonage pipe to connect to the branch soil or waste pipe at not more than 750 mm from the trap outlet |

**SD takeaway:** Terminate the vent at least 3 m from a boundary, window, or fresh-air intake, and size a rainwater pipe of 100 mm or less to the roof area in paragraph 7.
