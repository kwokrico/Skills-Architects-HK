# APP-169 Strength Tests for Structural Fixings in Concrete
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue October 2023 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Proof-load tests the Building Authority may impose under item 6 of section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance on drilled-in anchors and on cementitious or polymer grouted bolts, dowels, or reinforcing bars. Appendices A to C are pre-accepted method statements. Quoting the relevant appendix on the plans removes the need to submit a method statement. An alternative method must be agreed. PNAP APP-146 uses Appendix B for metal-gate anchors.

## Regulatory Overview
The manufacturer’s recommended tensile and shear loads are verified by proof tests on a sample of the installed fixings, at a test load of not less than 1.5 times the recommended load for all three pre-accepted methods. Appendix A also requires at least 80% recovery of the peak deformation, while Appendices B and C accept the fixing if it shows no separation, plastic deformation, or deleterious effect.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Which appendix (paragraph 1)
| Appendix | Fixings |
| --- | --- |
| A | Drilled-in anchors for cantilevered structure, hanger, or curtain-wall remedial works |
| B | Drilled-in anchors for works other than those |
| C | Cementitious or polymer grouted bolts, dowels, or reinforcing bars, or steel T-bolts with cast-in channels, for curtain wall or cladding |

**SD takeaway:** Use Appendix A for a remedial cantilever, hanger, or curtain-wall anchor, Appendix B for other drilled-in anchors, and Appendix C for grouted bolts or cast-in T-bolt channels on a curtain wall or cladding.

### 2. Sample, load, and acceptance
| Item | Appendix A (cantilever, hanger, curtain-wall remedial) | Appendices B and C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample | At least 5% or 5 numbers, whichever is more, of each type and size | At least 1% or 5 numbers, whichever is more. Appendix C is by type, size, and embedment, or by type and size of each T-bolt assembly |
| Load | Not less than 1.5 times the manufacturer’s recommended load, tensile and/or shear as appropriate | Same 1.5 factor |
| Hold | Continuously at 9 to 11 N/(mm2·s), or in at least 10 equal increments. Maximum load held at least one hour, readings at the start and end when stable. Released in at least 5 decrements, or at a constant rate | Loaded at a constant rate and held at least 2 minutes, then released at a constant rate |
| Pass | No separation, plastic deformation, or deleterious effect, and recovery at least 80% of the total deformation at the maximum test load | No separation, plastic deformation, or deleterious effect |
| Frame (Appendix A tensile test) | Supports equally spaced, at least 8A from the anchor axis. A is the hole diameter or one-quarter of the embedded length, whichever is greater. Displacement measured independently of the frame, at least 12A from the axis, to 0.02 mm. Load to 2%. A force-displacement graph for each test | — |

**SD takeaway:** Proof-test a remedial cantilever or curtain-wall anchor at 5% or 5 numbers to 1.5 times the recommended load for one hour, and accept it only if it recovers at least 80%; other drilled-in anchors and grouted fixings are sampled at 1% or 5 numbers and held for 2 minutes.
