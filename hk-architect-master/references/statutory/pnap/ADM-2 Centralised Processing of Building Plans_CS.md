# ADM-2 Centralised Processing of Building Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 30; first issue September 1976)

> Scope note: How the Building Authority consults other departments inside the statutory processing time, and how gross-floor-area concessions are recorded for publication after the occupation permit. It does not itself set the concession rules; those are in the APP notes named in Appendix E.

## Regulatory Overview
The centralised processing system makes the Buildings Department the point that consults interested departments and collates their comments within the time allowed for processing building plans. Comments that fall within the Buildings Ordinance and are a fundamental issue under PNAP ADM-19 can be disapproval items; other comments are passed on for information and are not raised as disapproval items.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Referral, copies, and who decides (paragraphs 2–5 and Appendices B and C)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Electronic | Submit through the Electronic Submission Hub so plans go to the Department and the consulted bodies at the same time |
| Paper | Too few sets delays referral. Authorized persons complete Appendix A before general building plans. Structural sets follow PNAP ADM-8 |
| Tung Chung Cable Car Route Protection Area | Building plans are referred to Ngong Ping 360 Limited (Appendix I) |
| Disapproval | Adverse comments within the Buildings Ordinance that are a fundamental issue under ADM-19 are disapproval items. Other comments are conveyed and are not disapproval items |
| Late comments | Sent by a separate letter, or by a notification in the Electronic Submission Hub |
| Arbitration | The Building Authority decides matters governed by the Ordinance. For other comments, approach the department direct. Divergent departmental requirements are taken to a meeting arranged by the Buildings Department |
| Fire services, paper | Copy the covering letter to the Fire Services Department. Mark the two sets (three if Cap. 502, Cap. 572, or Cap. 636 applies) in bold at the lower right, showing the installations referred to in section 16(1)(b)(ii) |
| Fire services, electronic | Upload to the folder designated for the Fire Services Department; the bold note is not required |
| Uncollected FSD plans | Collect paper plans within 3 months of the notification or the Fire Services Department may dispose of them |
| FSD comment groups | (a) reasons for refusal of a certificate under section 16(1)(b); (b) strongly recommended practice, which if ignored may lead to abatement under the Fire Services Ordinance, Cap. 95; (c) optional good practice |
| Lease, paper | A set of general building plans with the Development Schedule and coloured plans, plus CAD or BIM files on DVD-ROM where a computer is used for areas (Lands Administration Office Practice Note 3/2018), is referred to the District Lands Office |
| Lease, electronic | Upload general building plans and the CAD or BIM files to the Hub for direct referral. BIM in the predefined format replaces a separate CAD area file |
| Lease amendment | Complete the Lands Department reply slip for streamlined checking of general building plan amendments |
| Lease enquiry | Send to the District Lands Office, Building Plan Unit, or Railway Development Section, not through the Hub. A required lease modification should precede formal plan submission |

**SD takeaway:** Put fire-service installations on the sets referred to the Fire Services Department, and put the Development Schedule on the set referred to Lands, before the first general building plan.

### 2. Certainty during the 60-day period (paragraphs 6–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-submission | Discuss a specific proposition before formal submission. For a complicated site, a presentation, with a 3-D model if appropriate, may follow submission if the Chief Professional Officer requests or agrees |
| Day 45 | On a new-building submission, ask the case Building Surveyor or Structural Engineer whether the submission is fundamentally acceptable, and discuss non-conformities before a disapproval letter |
| Resubmission | Where a submission is fundamentally unacceptable and the disapproval items were notified in writing within the statutory 60 days, new disapproval items will not, in normal circumstances, be added to that proposal on resubmission |

**SD takeaway:** Use day 45 to confirm whether the submission is fundamentally acceptable, and treat the written 60-day disapproval list as the list that should not grow on a normal resubmission.

### 3. Modification and exemption (paragraphs 10–14 and Appendix E)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Each exemption or modification on Form BA16, with the special circumstances and why it is justified |
| Conditions | May be imposed under section 42 and issued on Form BD 106 |
| On the plans | Incorporate the modification conditions in later amendment plans before the certificate under regulation 25 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. Sample tables are in Appendix A3 of PNAP ADV-33 |
| At completion | Submit a checklist of valid Forms BD 106 with that regulation 25 certificate (sample at Appendix F) |
| Frequent cases | Appendix E lists the information to send with commonly applied modifications, including gross-floor-area calculations for the exempted areas, and points to the APP note that governs each case |

**SD takeaway:** Do not treat a concession as granted until Form BA16 is approved and the BD 106 conditions are written onto the amendment plans before the completion certificate.

### 4. Publishing gross-floor-area concessions (paragraphs 16–18)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| On the plans | In an application involving gross-floor-area concessions, give the detailed breakdown on the building plans as in Appendix G |
| With the occupation permit | Submit a summary of concessions granted and the number of car parks (sample at Appendix H) |
| Publication | The Buildings Department publishes the information on its website after the occupation permit |

**SD takeaway:** Tabulate every gross-floor-area concession on the general building plans, and file the Appendix H summary with the occupation-permit application.

### 5. Structural result letters and title (paragraphs 15 and 19)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Approval does not confer title to land or waive a lease or licence (Buildings Ordinance section 14(2)). Secure adjoining owners' consent before works on adjoining lots, and comply with other authorities |
| Structural results | From 2 July 2024, result letters for approval and consent of structural works are issued electronically even in reply to paper submissions, and are no longer posted. Structural works here include excavation and lateral support, ground investigation in the scheduled areas, foundation, and all structural works, excluding alteration and addition and railway consultation submissions |

**SD takeaway:** From 2 July 2024, collect structural approval and consent results electronically; do not wait for a posted hard copy.
