# APP-132 Site Coverage and Open Space Provision
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 280; first issue January 2004)

> Scope note: Case-by-case modification of regulations 20 and 25 of the Building (Planning) Regulations for a full-height street setback, or for an innovative open-space approach. It does not replace site coverage on a statutory town plan. The concessions are subject to the same prerequisites as the gross-floor-area concession in PNAP APP-151. Lease modification may attract premium; Lands Administration Office Practice Note No. 1/2004 applies.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority may allow site coverage to vary with height if a building is set back for its full height from a street boundary by the stated area and frontage. A separate open-space approach may relax regulations 20 and 25 where the unbuilt volume and gross floor area do not exceed a viable notional scheme and the stated coverage caps are kept.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Setback that unlocks the height tables (paragraph 3)
| Criterion | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Area | Not less than 8% of the site for a non-domestic building, and not less than 18% for a domestic building. A composite building shows 8% for the non-domestic portion and 18% for the domestic portion |
| Frontage | Set back for not less than half the frontage and not less than 10 m, or the full frontage if the frontage is less than 10 m |
| Control | Satisfactory arrangements for control and maintenance of the setback, any resultant flat roofs, and covered areas |
| Character | Properly landscaped and/or paved, open, uncovered, and without permanent building structures other than landscaped features and perforated boundary walls |
| Purpose | Contributes to improving the street environment |
| Dedication | Excludes areas dedicated or surrendered for public passage under regulation 22 |

**SD takeaway:** A domestic street setback of at least 18% of the site, and a non-domestic setback of at least 8%, each for half the frontage and at least 10 m, is the gate to the height tables.

### 2. Coverage if the setback criteria are met (paragraph 4 and Appendices A and B)
Total covered area must not exceed 63% of the site for a domestic building, and 80% of the site or the Appendix B percentage, whichever is the greater, for a non-domestic building. Total covered area is the vertical footprint, including features exempted from site coverage under Joint Practice Notes 1 and 2, and excluding projections under PNAP APP-19. These may then be excluded from the assessment: unexcavated or backfilled area under the footprint on a sloping site, and open-sided covered areas of non-domestic buildings that qualify as green features under Joint Practice Notes 1 and 2, are common areas, are accessible to all occupants, and have no commercial activities.

Domestic site coverage by height from street level (Appendix A), selected bands:

| Height (m) | Site coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Not exceeding 24 | 63% |
| 30 | 55% |
| 40 | 48% |
| 50 | 44% |
| 60 | 41% |
| Over 61 | 40% |

The table steps by 1 m between these bands, from 62% at 25 m down to 41% at 61 m.

Non-domestic site coverage (Appendix B):

| Height (m) | Site coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Not exceeding 24 | 92% |
| 30 | 88% |
| 40 | 82% |
| 50 | 74% |
| 60 | 66% |
| Over 61 | 65% |

The table steps by 1 m between these bands.

**SD takeaway:** Above 61 m the setback route allows 40% domestic coverage and 65% non-domestic coverage, provided the footprint stays within 63% or 80%.

### 3. Open-space approach (paragraph 5)
Modification of regulations 20 and 25, where applicable, for an innovative design with a vibrant configuration compatible with the surroundings. Using the maximum permitted coverage on every floor may not be regarded as innovative.

| Test | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Unbuilt open-space volume | Not less than that of a viable notional scheme. Both volumes are measured from the mean level of the lowest street to the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor of the notional scheme. The notional scheme complies with the Buildings Ordinance, allied regulations, and the Outline Zoning Plan |
| Gross floor area | Not exceeding the viable notional scheme |
| Domestic, any level | Maximum site coverage 63% |
| Non-domestic, not exceeding 24 m from street level | Maximum site coverage 92% |
| Non-domestic, exceeding 24 m | Maximum site coverage 80% |

**SD takeaway:** An innovative scheme may reach 63% domestic coverage at any level, and 92% non-domestic coverage below 24 m, only if gross floor area and unbuilt volume do not beat a viable notional scheme.
