# APP-83 Amendments and Clarification to Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision November 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 199; first issue August 1996)

> Scope note: Corrigenda to the Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996, including openings in compartment walls and raised floors. It does not replace the Code.

## Regulatory Overview
The January 1996 edition is corrected on paragraph 7.2, the fire-door diagram at paragraph 17.2, and Table C. A later reprint is corrected on openings in compartment walls, a cross-reference on page 14, and fixed lights on page 32.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cover, openings, and the date the opening rule applies (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Paragraph 7.2, fourth line | Delete "but in no case less than 2 hours" |
| Table C, page 24 | Concrete cover to all reinforcement required to provide 2 hours fire resistance period to continuous floors and landings of solid reinforced concrete construction is 25 mm, not 40 mm |
| Openings for communication, not combination, of adjoining compartments | Protected by a door or fire shutter |
| Total width of openings not more than 25% of the length of the compartment walls | Same fire resistance period as the walls for integrity only |
| Total width more than 25% | Same fire resistance period for integrity and insulation. Insulation may be reduced to 30 minutes if additional sprinkler heads on each side are part of the building sprinkler system, comply with the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, cover each side in full, and are spaced to the LPC Rules incorporating BS EN 12845:2003 |
| When paragraph 10.1 as revised applies | Consent to commencement on or after 18 August 2011. For a new building that consent is the consent to commence foundation works; for alterations and additions it is the consent to commence those works |
| Page 32 | "fixed light;" is replaced by "fixed light with FRP ≥ ½ FRPe;" |

**SD takeaway:** For consent on or after 18 August 2011, keep compartment-wall openings to 25% of the wall length if the door is rated for integrity only, and use 25 mm cover for 2-hour solid reinforced-concrete floors and landings.

### 2. Raised floor (paragraph 3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Raised floor not more than 600 mm above the structural floor | Not an element of construction and does not require a fire resistance period |
| Compartment walls and other fire-separation walls | Start from the structural floor. They do not rest only on the raised floor |

**SD takeaway:** Carry every compartment wall and fire-separation wall down to the structural slab, even where a raised floor is 600 mm or less and itself needs no fire resistance period.
