# ADM-17 Submission of Plans and Documents in Electronic Format
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This note as read includes Electronic Submission Hub stages to 30 June 2024 and the Registration and Minor Works Management System launched on 29 May 2026 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: File rules and the statutory clock for electronic submissions under the Buildings Ordinance. It does not change the design standards the plans must meet.

## Regulatory Overview
Electronic records and digital signatures have the same legal status as paper under the Electronic Transactions Ordinance (Cap. 553), in operation for these purposes since 7 April 2000, and the Buildings Department receives Buildings Ordinance submissions only through the designated systems in this note. A submission that does not follow the published format, manner, and procedure is invalid, and the statutory processing period is computed in the same way as for paper.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Designated systems (paragraphs 4–5 and 8–17)
| System | Use | Limit stated in the note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electronic Submission Hub, https://esh.bd.gov.hk | Plan submissions. Operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except maintenance and unexpected downtime | One set only. Sign in with a personal digital certificate or iAM Smart. Plans must meet Appendix B and be accompanied by an electronic plan index (Appendix C). Stage 1 launched 30 June 2022, Stage 2 on 31 March 2023, Stage 3 on 30 June 2024 |
| e-Counter, receipt@bd.gov.hk | Submissions that are not voluminous, not a custom program, and not large drawings | Email including attachments and signatures not exceeding 25 MB. Specified forms in PDF, signed at the specified locations. Other records in signed PDF with each page not larger than A3, or signed TIFF or JPEG at 200 dpi not greater than 3 300 × 2 340 pixels and 24-bit colour |
| Electronic Forms Submission System, www.bd.gov.hk | Certain specified forms (PNAP APP-60) | Forms and attachments digitally signed; total size not exceeding 25 MB |
| Registration and Minor Works Management System, https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk | Certain specified forms and supporting documents (PNAP APP-60). Launched 29 May 2026 | Each form not exceeding 25 MB. Sign with Hongkong Post, Digi-Sign, or iAM Smart |

Using the Hub is agreement that it is the designated system for receiving electronic records from the Department under section 19(2)(a)(i) of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance. The Building Authority may still require extra hard copies under regulation 11(2) of the Building (Administration) Regulations. After a Hub submission, later amendments should stay on the Hub. Equipment must be in place so electronic records can be supplied, kept on site, and produced under regulations 36 and 40.

**SD takeaway:** Send plans through the Electronic Submission Hub as a single signed set with an electronic plan index, and keep later amendments on the same channel.

### 2. When the statutory clock starts (paragraphs 18–19)
| Event | When counting starts |
| --- | --- |
| Any paper or Hub submission | The next day after the day of receipt, whether or not that next day is a public holiday, a gale-warning day, or a black-rainstorm day |
| Example: Saturday 23:59 via the Hub | Counting starts on Sunday |
| Example: Sunday 00:00 via the Hub | Counting starts on Monday |

**SD takeaway:** A Hub lodgement at the end of Saturday starts the statutory period on Sunday; do not assume the clock waits for the next working day.

### 3. Form BA10 on the Hub (paragraphs 20–21)
The Department will favourably consider a modification of regulation 20(1) of the Building (Administration) Regulations so that notification of appointment of the registered contractor and of commencement may be not less than 1 day before commencement, if Form BA10 is submitted via the Hub with a digital signature. Apply for that modification with the consent application. The modification will be conditioned on BA10 being submitted via the Hub. Regulations 20(2) and 20(3) still require notification of technically competent persons, the related information, and a supervision plan not less than 7 days before commencement.

**SD takeaway:** A Hub BA10 can support a 1-day commencement notice, but the supervision-plan notice remains 7 days.
