# APP-91 Maintenance and Replacement Works of Lift Installations
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued July 1997 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Fire resistance of lift landing doors under paragraph 11.2 of the Code of Practice for Fire Resisting Construction 1996, and the temporary protection required while doors are open or removed. The note says these requirements will be incorporated into that Code.

## Regulatory Overview
A door at a lift landing giving access to the car must have a fire resistance period of not less than 1 hour with regard to integrity, and should normally stay closed unless a car stops at that landing. While doors are open or removed for maintenance or replacement, the lift shaft must still be protected so fire cannot spread between floors through the shaft.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Open or removed landing doors (Appendix)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | All landing doors remain closed |
| One door open | Normally no more than one door in a shaft open at one time. An opening other than the one where the car is level with the landing is attended by lift workers |
| More than one door open | Maximum three at the same time, and no hot works or welding |
| Doors taken down | Avoid removing more than one landing door in a shaft at one time |
| More than one removed | Protect the openings with temporary hoarding of not less than one hour fire resistance period |
| Openings in the hoarding | None, except small ventilation openings and access doors. Each ventilation opening not exceeding 5,500 mm2, at the upper portion, limited to two per liftway and a maximum of four in the hoarding |
| Access door | Same fire resistance period as the hoarding, self-closing, locked against unauthorized access, and openable from the inside without a key |
| Escape | Hoarding should not, as far as reasonably practicable, obstruct or reduce the width of any escape route |
| Inside the shaft | Temporary scaffold, formwork, planking, and strutting of non-combustible materials |
| Unattended periods | Before lunch breaks or the end of the day, return opened doors to closed or enclose the openings with fire-resisting hoarding |

**SD takeaway:** Keep lift landing doors at not less than 1 hour integrity and normally closed, and if more than one is removed, hoard the openings to 1 hour without narrowing an escape route.
