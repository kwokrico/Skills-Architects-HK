# APP-143 Quality Control and Supervision of Precast Concrete Construction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
Buildings Department

> Scope note: Quality assurance, qualified supervision, and factory audit for key precast concrete elements. It does not apply to minor elements such as non-structural precast blocks, architectural features, and planters. Design and construction follow the Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016. Amendments to that code are in PNAP APP-174.

## Regulatory Overview
The authorized person and registered structural engineer give periodic supervision, and the registered contractor gives continuous supervision. Approval of plans is conditioned on an ISO 9001 factory and a quality-assurance scheme lodged before production starts.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Factory assurance (paragraph 4)
| Condition | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Item 6 of section 17(1) | Precast elements manufactured by a factory with ISO 9001 quality-assurance certification |
| Regulation 10 | A copy of the manufacturer’s quality-assurance scheme at least 14 days before production starts. The registered structural engineer confirms in writing that the scheme is adequate for compliance with the Buildings Ordinance and the approved plans |

**SD takeaway:** Do not start precast production until the factory’s ISO 9001 scheme has been submitted, at least 14 days ahead, with the structural engineer’s confirmation.

### 2. Factory supervision and audit (paragraphs 6–7)
| Role | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Contractor’s quality control co-ordinator | Continuous supervision in the factory. Minimum qualifications and experience are those of a grade T1 technically competent person in the contractor’s stream under the Code of Practice for Site Supervision |
| Records | Names and qualifications in the inspection log books, kept at the factory, with a copy at the building site office, and produced to the Buildings Department when required |
| Visits | The registered structural engineer and the contractor’s authorized signatory inspect the first batch in person, then audit at least once every month. In-person inspections at quarterly intervals. The months in between (2nd, 3rd, 5th, 6th, and so on) may be in person or by videotelephony |
| Reports | Copies of both audit reports, endorsed by the structural engineer and the authorized signatory, within 14 days after the checks, under a regulation 10 condition. Each report covers the qualified supervision of their own supervisor |

**SD takeaway:** Programme a joint first-batch visit and then a factory audit every month, in person at least every quarter, with the report in within 14 days.
