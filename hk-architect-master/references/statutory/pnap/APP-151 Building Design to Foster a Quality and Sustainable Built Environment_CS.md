# APP-151 Building Design to Foster a Quality and Sustainable Built Environment
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision applies to plans on or after 30 June 2024 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Prerequisites and the 10% cap for exempting or disregarding green and amenity features and non-mandatory or non-essential plant rooms from gross floor area and site coverage. Building separation, setback, and greenery are in PNAP APP-152. Residential energy limits are in PNAP APP-156. Features and their own practice notes are listed in Appendix A. Plans approved before this revision follow the August 2022 version at Appendix D, subject to section 16(3)(d) of the Buildings Ordinance.

## Regulatory Overview
Compliance with the sustainable building design guidelines, where applicable, is a prerequisite for those gross floor area concessions in a new development. The concessions that are capped are limited to 10% of the total gross floor area, with domestic and non-domestic parts capped separately.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The 10% cap, and what sits outside it (paragraphs 4–5)
The cap is 10% of the total gross floor area. In a development with both domestic and non-domestic buildings, or in a composite building, each part is capped at 10% of that part. Features inside the cap are listed in Appendix A.

Not subject to the cap, though they may have their own acceptance criteria:

| Feature | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory features and essential plant | Such as refuse storage chambers, and telecommunications and broadcasting rooms |
| Communal podium gardens and sky gardens | That improve permeability to the neighbourhood |
| Parking and loading | Floor space used solely for parking and for loading and unloading of motor vehicles |
| Voids | In front of cinemas or in shopping arcades, with operational needs, in non-domestic developments |
| Bonus and dedication | Bonus gross floor area and exemptions for dedication for public passage, surrender for road widening, and building setback under the sustainable building design guidelines |
| Hotels | Concessions under regulation 23A of the Building (Planning) Regulations |

**SD takeaway:** Keep green features and non-essential plant inside 10% of gross floor area; car parks, sky gardens, and dedication bonus sit outside that cap.

### 2. Prerequisites (paragraphs 6–8)
For every green or amenity feature and non-mandatory or non-essential plant room in Appendix A:

| Prerequisite | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Sustainable building design | PNAP APP-152, where applicable |
| Domestic or composite energy | PNAP APP-156, where applicable |
| BEAM Plus | Official letter from the Hong Kong Green Building Council acknowledging project registration under BEAM Plus New Buildings Version 2.0 or above |
| Target on the general building plans | Anticipated Gold, with no specific standard; or Anticipated Silver plus one specific standard on quality built environment; or Anticipated Bronze plus two. Specific standards are in Appendix C. The target may be changed on an amendment before the occupation permit. Sample drawing C042 in Appendix A4 of PNAP ADV-33 |
| Before superstructure consent | Provisional Assessment credit summary from the BEAM Society Limited, aligned with the target; estimated energy performance for common parts of a domestic development, or for the entire building if non-domestic including hotels, on Appendix B; and the provisional energy efficiency report under PNAP APP-156 where applicable. A registered professional engineer of the relevant discipline is consulted for the energy assessment |
| At occupation permit | Updated energy information; the Compliance Assessment result certifying the target rating; and the final energy report under PNAP APP-156 where applicable |
| Cap and feature rules | Paragraph 4, and the acceptance criteria for each feature |
| Revocation | The modification will be revoked if consent is applied for before the pre-consent information is submitted |

**SD takeaway:** Register BEAM Plus New Buildings 2.0 or above, state Gold or a lower rating with the required specific standards, and lodge the provisional assessment before superstructure consent.

### 3. Dates and public disclosure (paragraphs 9–10)
Paragraphs 6 to 9 apply to new general building plans, or a major revision, submitted on or after 30 June 2024, and to plans previously disapproved and resubmitted on or after that date. After the occupation permit, the Buildings Department website shows the estimated energy performance, the BEAM Plus Compliance Assessment result, and the final residential thermal transfer value and the overall thermal transfer value of residential recreational facilities.

**SD takeaway:** A general building plan submitted on or after 30 June 2024 needs the full prerequisite set before the concessions are granted.
