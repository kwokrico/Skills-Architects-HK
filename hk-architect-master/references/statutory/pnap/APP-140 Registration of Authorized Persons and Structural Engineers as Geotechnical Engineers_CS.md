# APP-140 Registration of Authorized Persons and Structural Engineers as Geotechnical Engineers
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue December 2004 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 293)

> Scope note: The transitional route, under section 53I of the Buildings Ordinance, for an authorized person on the engineers’ list or a registered structural engineer to register as a registered geotechnical engineer. The window was 12 months from 31 December 2004, that is on or before 30 December 2005. The basic qualification otherwise is a registered professional engineer in the geotechnical discipline. Read with PNAP 43, particularly paragraphs 7 and 11 to 14. It is not a geotechnical design code.

## Regulatory Overview
The Buildings (Amendment) Ordinance 2004 requires a registered geotechnical engineer for the geotechnical elements of building works, who investigates, designs, and supervises those works and is statutorily responsible for them. An authorized person or registered structural engineer who met the site-formation experience tests could apply for registration by 30 December 2005.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Who could apply, and for how long the registration lasts (paragraphs 3 and 5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who | An authorized person on the list of engineers, or a registered structural engineer |
| Window | On or before 30 December 2005 |
| Engagement | During the 7 years immediately before the application, engaged in or taking part in site formation works carried out and completed in accordance with the Buildings Ordinance |
| Appointment | He was the authorized person or registered structural engineer appointed for the building works or street works to which the site formation related |
| Competence | He satisfies the Building Authority of appropriate geotechnical experience and competence. The Building Authority may require information or documents |
| Validity | 5 years, renewable as long as his name remains on the authorized-person or registered-structural-engineer register |

**SD takeaway:** This transitional registration route closed on 30 December 2005 and lasted 5 years, renewable only while the applicant stayed on the authorized-person or structural-engineer register.

### 2. Experience the Building Authority accepted (paragraph 4)
Within the same 7 years:

| Test | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Projects, from plan preparation through approval and supervision to satisfactory completion | 5 projects requiring Category I qualified supervision, or 2 projects requiring Category II qualified supervision, or 3 Category I projects and 1 Category II project |
| What preparation includes | Supervision of his employees. It excludes preparation of plans by a geotechnical engineering sub-consultant |
| Categories | Category I and Category II qualified supervision as defined in PNAP 83 |
| No double use | The projects must not be ones claimed to support an application under section 53I(2)(a) by other applicants |
| Completion | An authorized person on the engineers’ list has certified completion under the Ordinance. A registered structural engineer has reported to the certifying authorized person that the works were completed in accordance with the approved plans and in compliance with the Ordinance |
| Record | Completed with no record of contravention of the Ordinance |

**SD takeaway:** The accepted track record was five Category I site-formation projects, or two Category II projects, or three Category I plus one Category II, all within the previous 7 years.
