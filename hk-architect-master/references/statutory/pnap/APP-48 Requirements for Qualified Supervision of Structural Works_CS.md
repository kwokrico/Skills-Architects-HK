# APP-48 Requirements for Qualified Supervision of Structural Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2005 | Buildings Department (first issue June 1990; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Buildings Ordinance section 17 conditions requiring qualified supervision of structural, foundation, and excavation works. Qualified supervision of geotechnical works is in PNAP 83, not in this note.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 17 allows conditions, when plans are approved or consent is given, requiring qualified supervision at certain stages or for particular operations. The person supervising must have technical knowledge and experience relevant to the works and must be fully conversant with the engineering basis of the design.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Operations that attract a supervision condition (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Examples or duty |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | Major excavation and foundation construction |
| (b) | Concreting |
| (c) | Compliance testing |
| (d) | Prestressing, including pre-tensioning, post-tensioning, grouting, and lock-off |
| (e) | Fabrication, erection, and examination of key structural steelwork elements on site |
| (f) | Specialist building works |
| Who must ensure it | The authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer, and registered contractor, under Buildings Ordinance sections 4(3) and 9(3) and Building (Administration) Regulations 37 and 41 |

**SD takeaway:** Allow in the programme for named, technically competent supervision at excavation, foundation, concreting, testing, prestressing, key steelwork, and specialist operations, because section 17 conditions can require it.
