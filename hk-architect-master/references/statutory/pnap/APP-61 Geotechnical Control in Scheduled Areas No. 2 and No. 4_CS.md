# APP-61 Geotechnical Control in Scheduled Areas No. 2 and No. 4
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 161; first issue November 1993)

> Scope note: Ground investigation, foundations, and wells in the north-western New Territories (Scheduled Area No. 2) and part of Ma On Shan (Scheduled Area No. 4), where marble cavities occur. It complements the Code of Practice for Foundations 2017 and PNAP APP-18. Foundation design in marble is also in GEO Technical Guidance Note No. 26.

## Regulatory Overview
Ground investigation in these areas needs approval and consent under section 41(3) before it starts, because it is building works, and the plans are submitted under regulation 8(1)(l). Drillholes should penetrate at least 20 m into sound marble where marble is met, and deeper where cavities are found in that hole or an adjacent hole, and every hole is grouted on completion.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Investigation before the building plan is frozen (paragraphs 5–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Logging | A Competent Person (Logging), to the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 and Geoguide 3. Record cavity location and size, the cavity wall and infill, and discontinuities. Show total core recovery, solid core recovery, rock quality designation, and fracture index |
| Sequence | Investigate in stages, including some deep holes, before general building plans are finalised, so structures can be moved off serious cavities. Inclined holes may help if the trend is vertical. Geophysics may fill gaps between holes |
| Flushing | Control the use of water. Excessive flushing has induced sinkholes. Triple-tube core-barrels with air foam are used for high-quality infill samples |
| After the pile layout | Further holes, including some at the edges of the main pile area, because edge cavities are more detrimental |
| Notice | At least two weeks to the district Chief Geotechnical Engineer before commencement, under regulation 20 |
| Cores | Retained on site in good condition until substantial completion of the building works |

**SD takeaway:** Prove whether marble with cavities is under the site, with at least 20 m of sound marble cored, before fixing the general building plans and the pile layout.

### 2. Piles, performance review, and wells (paragraphs 13–27)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Usual foundation | Driven or machine-bored piles for heavily loaded structures on marble. Hand-dug caissons need dewatering that can induce sinkholes, and PNAP APP-59 restricts them |
| Driven piles | Heavy section, modified or strengthened tips, so they can be driven through rock bridging shallow cavities and will not deflect on an inclined marble surface. Increase the number above the minimum so surplus piles can carry load redistributed around a cavity, and design the pile cap for that. Pre-drilling where rock is very deep or the karst zone is thick |
| Bored piles and barrettes | Consider cavities below each pile and the surrounding area when choosing founding level and bearing capacity. Drill and, where appropriate, probe the significantly stressed zone |
| Documents | Regulation 8(1)(d)(ii) documents must accompany the foundation plans, or the submission may be refused under sections 16(1)(a) and 16(1)(i) |
| Driving records | Blows per 500 mm when driving is easy, and per 100 mm when hard, plus a plot of blows per 100 mm against depth. The suggested dividing point is 10 blows per 100 mm. Survey the deviation of each pile from its initial position |
| Superstructure consent | May be refused under section 16(3)(ba) if the performance review required by clause 7.8 of the Foundation Code does not show adequate inspection, assessment of records, and any necessary design changes |
| Settlement | Agree the monitoring scheme before the foundations are completed. Submit results monthly. Report unusual settlement immediately. The Geotechnical Engineering Office may continue monitoring after the occupation permit. The same caution applies to buildings founded in weathered siltstone and sandstone above marble |
| Wells | A registered geotechnical engineer must show that extraction will not adversely affect the site and the surroundings. Flushing water will normally be supplied whenever possible in Yuen Long Town and Ma On Shan. See PNAP APP-4 |

**SD takeaway:** Use driven or bored piles, not hand-dug caissons, add spare piles for cavity loss of support, and do not start the superstructure until the foundation performance review is accepted.
