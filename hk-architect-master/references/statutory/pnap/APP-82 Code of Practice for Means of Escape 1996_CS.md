# APP-82 Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued June 1996 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Application of the 1996 means-of-escape code, which guides compliance with section 41(1) of the Building (Planning) Regulations. Escape dimensions are in the Code. The note records that the fire-engineering approach and existing buildings may need further guidelines.

## Regulatory Overview
The revised Code clarifies anomalies, upgrades some components, removes duplication with the fire-resisting-construction code, and adds a new Part III for places of public entertainment. It comes into operation on 1 December 1996, with the two exceptions below.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Effective dates (paragraph 4)
| Item | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Part III (places of public entertainment) | Effective on 28 June 1996. PNAP 194 refers |
| Rest of the revised Code | 1 December 1996 |
| Works being carried out, or consented, on or before 1 December 1996 | The earlier edition may be used as the basis for the facilities |

**SD takeaway:** Use the 1996 means-of-escape code, including Part III for a place of public entertainment from 28 June 1996, unless consent was already given on or before 1 December 1996.
