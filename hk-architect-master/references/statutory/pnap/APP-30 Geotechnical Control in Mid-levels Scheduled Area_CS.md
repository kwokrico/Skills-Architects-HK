# APP-30 Geotechnical Control in Mid-levels Scheduled Area
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 85; first issue July 1982)

> Scope note: Additional geotechnical controls on building works in Scheduled Area No. 1 (Mid-levels) under Schedule 5 to the Buildings Ordinance. The boundary plan is on display in the Geotechnical Information Unit and on the Buildings Department website. Bulk excavation limits are determined under GEO Technical Guidance Note No. 50.

## Regulatory Overview
All geotechnical aspects of works in Scheduled Area No. 1 require a registered geotechnical engineer, particularly at the early planning stage. Bulk excavation must be limited to a bulk excavation limit that minimises the cumulative adverse effect on hillside stability from excavation at two or more sites, under section 25 of the Building (Construction) Regulation.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ground investigation, demolition, and the bulk excavation limit (paragraphs 3–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Ground investigation | Approval and consent are required under section 41(3) before commencement. Plans, including laboratory testing under regulation 8(1)(l) of the Building (Administration) Regulations, conform to Geoguide 2 and Geoguide 3. A section 17(1)(6)(e) condition will require site supervision by a suitably experienced engineer or engineering geologist, and careful logging of cores. See PNAP APP-49 |
| Demolition | Plans under regulations 8(3) and (4) must be submitted for approval |
| Provisional limit | Ask the Geotechnical Engineering Office for a provisional bulk excavation limit as soon as commissioned. A plan of the tentative limit is normally issued within 10 working days |
| Agreement | Submit the assessment, with the registered structural engineer if necessary, and agree it with the Buildings Department before site formation, foundation, excavation and lateral support, or other structural plans that are constrained by the limit. Agreement is generally issued within one month and is the final determination. A limit determined to Technical Guidance Note No. 50 is deemed to satisfy section 25 |
| Drawings | Show the envelope of all bulk excavation and the limit on the site formation plans under regulation 8(1)(bb)(vii) |

**SD takeaway:** Obtain the bulk excavation limit before fixing basement depth, and do not submit formation or foundation plans until the Buildings Department has agreed that limit.

### 2. Foundations, performance review, and extra copies (paragraphs 10–13)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation plan | Submit with the site formation plan under Regulation 11A, so hillside stability from groundwater change or surcharge can be considered. Non-compliance may be refused under sections 16(1)(a), (e), or (i) |
| Performance review | May be imposed as a condition, but not on every site. It must show that the works were inspected and monitored and that the geotechnical design assumptions are valid. Otherwise an occupation permit or temporary occupation permit may be refused under section 21(6)(f) |
| Other controls | Landslip debris resistance, sequence of works, and groundwater drainage under sections 16(1)(q), 17(1)(6)(f), and 28A to 28D |
| Extra set | One extra set of general building plans; site formation plans with reports and calculations; foundation plans including pile-cap details, reports, and calculations; and excavation and lateral support plans |

**SD takeaway:** Submit the foundation plan together with the site formation plan, and keep the lowest excavation inside the agreed bulk excavation limit.
