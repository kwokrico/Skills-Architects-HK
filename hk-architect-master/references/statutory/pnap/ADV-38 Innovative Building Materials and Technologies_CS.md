# ADV-38 Innovative Building Materials and Technologies
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue April 2024 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: A pre-acceptance mechanism for non-site-specific design and construction matters of building, drainage, and structural innovative materials, products, systems, or technologies. It excludes innovative geotechnical materials and technologies, which go to the Geotechnical Engineering Office. Pre-acceptance is not approval or consent, and it is not a prerequisite for approving plans that use the innovation.

## Regulatory Overview
A supplier or manufacturer appoints an authorized person and, as appropriate, a registered structural engineer and a registered geotechnical engineer to evaluate the innovation against the Buildings Ordinance and to submit a pre-acceptance application. The Department gives a curtailed assessment of compliance with the safety, health, and environmental standards in the Ordinance and its subsidiary legislation, so that the industry can use the innovation with a stated set of requirements and limitations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What can be pre-accepted, and what the application contains (paragraphs 1–3)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| In scope | Building, drainage, and structural innovative building materials and technologies. An example in the note is concrete with an innovative cement replacement or aggregate that advances performance, or a product for which there is currently no relevant local or international standard |
| Out of scope | Anything already accepted and shown in a practice note or a code issued by the Buildings Department, for example Modular Integrated Construction. Anything already in the Central Data Bank. Innovative geotechnical materials and technologies, which are taken to the Geotechnical Engineering Office |
| Registered geotechnical engineer | Appointed if the innovation involves geotechnical matters, for example a new foundation system |
| Application | Appendix A, signed by the supplier or manufacturer and the appointed professionals; an innovation brief stating use, design principle, job reference if any, and the innovative content; building, drainage, or structural plans showing how the Ordinance is met or that performance is equivalent or not inferior; and a technical specification with catalogue, test or assessment reports from accredited laboratories, and a factory quality-assurance scheme with ISO 9001 or an equivalent certification |

**SD takeaway:** Use this route for a building, drainage, or structural product that is not already in a practice note, a code, or the Central Data Bank, and send a geotechnical innovation to the Geotechnical Engineering Office instead.

### 2. Time, effect, and use on a project (paragraphs 4–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Determination | Normally within 45 days of receipt, as a letter of in-principle acceptance or a letter of comments, if the application has sufficient information |
| Comments | Respond to every comment within 30 days or the application may be treated as abandoned. After the response, a determination follows within 30 days. A refusal requires a new application under paragraph 2 |
| Letter | States the requirements and limitations, reminds the supplier that approval and consent are still required for a project, and states whether the innovation is suitable for minor works under the simplified requirements. The letter, a brief description, and the requirements and limitations are posted on the Buildings Department website |
| On a project | Pre-acceptance is not an exemption from prior approval and consent. A plan that uses the pre-accepted innovation is accompanied by an application for modification or exemption under section 42 of the Buildings Ordinance where appropriate. The project designers remain responsible for compliance with the Ordinance, the approved plans, and the letter |
| Not a prerequisite | Plans that use an innovation may be approved without pre-acceptance |
| Validity | A maximum of 5 years. Renewal is an Appendix B application by the authorized person and, as appropriate, the registered structural engineer and registered geotechnical engineer, checked against the latest Ordinance, codes, and practice notes |

**SD takeaway:** A letter of in-principle acceptance lasts at most 5 years and still needs approval and consent, and a section 42 application where the design departs from the deemed-to-satisfy provisions.
