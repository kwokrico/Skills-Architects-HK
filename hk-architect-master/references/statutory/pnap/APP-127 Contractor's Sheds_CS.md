# APP-127 Contractor's Sheds
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision October 2007 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 273; first issue November 2002)

> Scope note: Permits under Building (Planning) Regulation 53 for contractor’s sheds used as site office, storage, staff room, or canteen, and which sheds a contractor may certify. It is not a permanent-building code. Form BA18 is the application.

## Regulatory Overview
A permit is required to erect a contractor’s shed. Sheds that meet Appendix A may be certified by a registered general building contractor or registered specialist contractor; larger sheds within the curtilage are certified by an authorized person and a registered structural engineer.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Sheds a contractor may certify (Appendix A)
| Criterion | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Single storey, storey height less than 3 m, floor area less than 230 m2 |
| Stilts | Base not more than 1.5 m above ground |
| Site gradient | Maximum gradient across the shed site not more than 15 degrees. Overall gradient of the area bounded by lines 10 m outside the footprint less than 15 degrees |
| Slopes nearby | No slope within 10 m outside the footprint steeper than 30 degrees or higher than 1.5 m |
| Walls nearby | No retaining wall or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m on the shed site or within 10 m outside the footprint |
| Siting | Not on a cantilever, and not above a hoarding or covered walkway. Within the building-site boundaries and not readily accessible to the public |

If the location has a geotechnical concern, geotechnical documents by a registered geotechnical engineer, comparable to Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1)(bb), are submitted with the authorized-person route.

**SD takeaway:** A contractor-certified shed stays under 3 m high, under 230 m2, and 10 m clear of a slope steeper than 30 degrees or a wall higher than 1.5 m.

### 2. Siting and fire (paragraphs 8–10 and Appendix B)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Over a hoarding or covered walkway | No permit if the shed is supported on that structure or if a support obstructs its clear width |
| Combustible construction | At least 3 m from any adjoining building or the site boundary, and that space kept clear |
| Contractor-certified, single storey, less than 230 m2 | One 4.5 kg carbon-dioxide or 9-litre water extinguisher at each entrance |
| Authorized-person certified, more than one storey and less than 230 m2 | That extinguisher at each entrance, and at least one open staircase |
| Single storey over 230 m2 | That extinguisher at each entrance, and fire-engine access to within 30 m travel distance of the shed |
| Other designs, or non-compliance with the table | Fire Services Department sets requirements case by case |
| Travel distance | Comply with the Code of Practice for the Provision of Means of Escape in Case of Fire 1996 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep a combustible shed 3 m off the boundary, and give every entrance a 4.5 kg carbon-dioxide or 9-litre water extinguisher.
