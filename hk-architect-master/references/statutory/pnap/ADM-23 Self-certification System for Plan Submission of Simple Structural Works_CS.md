# ADM-23 Self-certification System for Plan Submission of Simple Structural Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue June 2025 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: A fast track for structural plans of defined simple works, certified by the registered structural engineer and, for Categories C to E, by an independent checking engineer. Works outside Appendix A stay on the normal regulation 30 timetable.

## Regulatory Overview
Simple structural works that meet the Appendix A limits may be processed on the strength of the registered structural engineer's certification, with an independent checking engineer for Categories C, D, and E. The Buildings Department's curtailed check then covers document completeness, registration, compatibility with the supporting structure, fire resistance, design loads, codes, materials and workmanship, testing of materials and fixings, and corrosion resistance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. General limits for every category (Appendix A, paragraph 1)
| Rule | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Materials | Conventional only: reinforced concrete, precast reinforced concrete, structural glass, structural steel, stainless steel, structural aluminium, stone, and other materials in the Central Data Bank |
| Stability | Must not affect the overall structural stability of the building the works are attached to |
| Exclusions | No prestressed structure and no retaining structure |

**SD takeaway:** Prestressed work, a retaining structure, or anything that changes overall stability is outside this fast track.

### 2. Category limits that drive schematic design (Appendix A, paragraph 2)
| Category | Who certifies | Design lock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Registered structural engineer | Same nature, scale, and complexity as a minor-works item, in a new building; or minor works in an existing building submitted for approval and consent instead of the simplified requirements |
| B | Registered structural engineer | Elements that are not Category A and are not exempted works. Metal or glass cladding, false ceiling, louvre, and grille: highest point not more than 100 m above adjoining ground; least dimension of a rectangle around any panel not more than 2 m; panel area not more than 6 m². Plant modules on an external wall: 100 m limit, each module not more than 0.75 m in height and width, projection not more than 0.2 m, proprietary system. Metal or glass canopy: the cladding limits, and no projection more than 2 m from the column, wall, or beam. Internal frame for a building service: imposed load not more than 2 kPa and individual weight not more than 2,000 kg. Frame for a glass-reinforced-polyester tank or service on grade or on a slab: not more than 20 kPa and not more than 9,000 kg. Internal maintenance platform: imposed load not greater than 2 kPa |
| C | Registered structural engineer and independent checking engineer | Stone cladding, not Category A: 100 m height limit, panel rectangle not more than 2 m, panel area not more than 6 m² |
| D | Both | Single-storey refuse room, plant room, or covered walkway: span not more than 8 m, height not more than 5 m, no basement. Detached or semi-detached single-family house not exceeding three storeys: span not more than 8 m, storey height not more than 4.5 m, no basement. May sit on a podium, a shallow foundation, or a pile cap |
| E | Both | Shallow foundation supporting Category D: allowable pressure not more than 300 kPa, or 150 kPa if below the groundwater table; not on soft clay or mud; not founded more than 3 m below existing ground; not in Scheduled Area Numbers 1, 3, and 5; ground within 10 m downhill not steeper than 15 degrees overall and no slope steeper than 15 degrees in that area; no retaining or terrace wall higher than 1.5 m, or below a 45-degree line down from the base of the footing, in that area |

False ceiling, louvre, and grille in Category B are those subject to wind and suspended under a balcony, transfer plate, or open frame, or, for louvre and grille, attached to the exterior. Associated Category D works include curtain wall, skylight, precast element, external steel maintenance platform, external metal staircase, and external cat-ladder other than designated exempted works item 28, if they meet the general limits.

**SD takeaway:** A three-storey house on this track needs spans at or under 8 m, storey height at or under 4.5 m, no basement, and a shallow foundation no deeper than 3 m with the slope and wall clearances in Category E.

### 3. Processing times and the checking engineer (paragraphs 4–12)
| Submission | Via Electronic Submission Hub | Other than the Hub |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First submission, resubmission, or major revision | 20 days | 40 days |
| Amendment, including Type II works applied for fast-track consent | 10 days | 20 days |

The independent checking engineer is a registered structural engineer appointed by the developer or owner, with no conflict of interest, notified on or before the plan submission (Appendix E). Once a submission uses this system, later resubmissions and amendments must use it too, or the Department may treat them as a normal submission under regulation 30 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. A first submission that did not use the system may adopt it later. Concurrent approval and consent is available if the corresponding building plans are already approved, following PNAP ADM-19. Plans and calculations follow PNAP ADM-8. Audit checks are random.

**SD takeaway:** Lodge simple structural plans on the Electronic Submission Hub if the 20-day first-submission time is needed, and appoint the independent checking engineer before the submission date for Categories C to E.
