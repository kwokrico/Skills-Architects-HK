# APP-37 Curtain Wall, Window and Window Wall
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 106; first issue September 1984)

> Scope note: Structural submissions for curtain walls under sections 29 to 31 of the Building (Construction) Regulation, and for windows and window walls that are large, high, or heavily loaded. Fire separation of the wall follows section 35 and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011. Acceptable standards are in Appendix A and PNAP APP-53. Deflection limits are those in the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Glass 2018. Repair and replacement may be minor works. See PNAP APP-147.

## Regulatory Overview
Curtain wall plans include the framing, the check on the parent structure, element design, deflection of major members, elevations, connections, tolerances, the mode of support, materials, and the fire and smoke separation between floors, between compartments, and between the accommodation and a required staircase or its protected lobby. Support is by anchorage in structural concrete or by welding to structural steel, and any other mode, such as a drilled or bolted fixing, needs a modification of section 31.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When a window or window wall needs structural plans (paragraphs 5–6 and 18–21)
| Item | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| External wall, and a structural opening whose least dimension exceeds 1.8 m or whose area exceeds 6 m2, and either 100 m or more above adjoining ground or a wind reference pressure of 2.86 kPa or more | Submit framing and key details. If the 2004 wind code is used instead, the criterion is the design wind pressure qz. Include horizontal imposed load where the window is a protective barrier, to section 9 and Table 3 of the Schedule, and a deflection check to the glass code |
| Structural span of a mullion, glass fin, or similar element exceeding 6 m | The same structural information, including a glass shopfront |
| Otherwise | The authorized person and structural engineer still meet horizontal imposed load, protection of openings, barrier function, corrosion protection, material quality, and fire and smoke separation between floors. Plans are not required |
| Locking devices, on structural plans submitted on or after 1 August 2020 | Durable and non-combustible, evenly distributed. Ultimate design strength is the characteristic strength divided by a factor of safety of 1.8. Show locations and that strength. One handle bar is not connected to more than 8 locking points |
| Sash size, in general | A top-hung sash not exceeding 2.5 m2. A side-hung sash not exceeding 700 mm wide |
| Separate structural engineer | Forms BA4 and BA5, an assessment of the effect on the parent structure signed off by the project structural engineer, and certification under regulation 25(3). Cast-in anchorage installed earlier is on the project superstructure plans |

**SD takeaway:** Submit structural plans for a window once an opening exceeds 1.8 m or 6 m2 and the wall is at or above 100 m or 2.86 kPa, and keep a top-hung sash to 2.5 m2 and a side-hung sash to 700 mm wide.

### 2. Glass, sealant, and tests before occupation (paragraphs 13–17 and 23)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Tempered glass | Factory with ISO 9001 certification. The quality assurance scheme, covering clause 9.1.3 of the glass code, is submitted at least 14 days before production, with the structural engineer's statement. Heat-soak compliance reports, endorsed by the structural engineer, are submitted before the occupation permit or Form BA14 |
| Supervision of heat soak | The conditions imposed on approval include inspection covering at least 30% of the panes. The quality-assurance checklist in the appendix includes a minimum clearance of 20 mm between panels and a limit on time above 270°C |
| Structural sealant | Compatibility, adhesion, and print-review certificates to the glass code, plus a deglazing test, from the manufacturer and accepted by the structural engineer, before the occupation permit or Form BA14 |
| Spider fixing | Proof-load test to Appendix C after the works, unless that model is already in the Central Data Bank under PNAP ADM-20. The model number, properties, dimensions, and capacities are on the plans. The glass interface allows the anticipated movement |
| Curtain-wall safety test | Required under section 17(1) item 6, by a laboratory accredited for that test, on a Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme certificate or an equivalent, before the occupation permit or Form BA14. Not required for fast-track repair under PNAP ADM-19 or for minor works item 1.61. Choose representative panes and follow clause 8.3.1 of the glass code |
| Maintenance | Annex D of the glass code. Spare panes may be reserved at completion |

**SD takeaway:** Use a heat-soaked tempered glass from an ISO 9001 factory, prove sealant and spider fixings before occupation, and do not hang one handle on more than eight locks.
