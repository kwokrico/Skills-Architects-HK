# APP-99 Flushing Volume for Flushing Cisterns
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 220; first issue May 2000)

> Scope note: Acceptance of a flushing cistern discharge below the 9 to 14 litres required by Regulation 19 of the Building (Standards of Sanitary Fitments, Plumbing, Drainage Works and Latrines) Regulations. It does not set Water Authority product rules; those are in the Waterworks Ordinance and Regulations and Water Authority circulars.

## Regulatory Overview
Regulation 19 requires a watercloset flushing cistern to discharge between 9 and 14 litres, but both the Building Authority and the Water Authority will accept a smaller discharge if the flushing volume is compatible with the bowl so that one flush clears the waste, and the apparatus meets Water Authority requirements. Where the flush water comes from the waterworks, Regulation 91 means Regulations 19 to 23 do not apply, so no modification of Regulation 19 is required.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Waterworks supply and other supplies (paragraphs 2–3)
| Supply | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| From the waterworks | No modification of Regulation 19. The authorized person ensures the apparatus complies with the Waterworks Ordinance and Regulations and the Water Authority's technical circular letters |
| Other than from the waterworks | The Building Authority is prepared to modify Regulation 19 for an individual watercloset system, provided the reduced volume still clears the bowl in a single flush and the apparatus meets the Water Authority requirements in paragraph 2 |

**SD takeaway:** Specify a cistern whose flush volume matches the bowl for a single-flush clearance; a modification of Regulation 19 is needed only when the flush water is not from the waterworks.
