# APP-67 Energy Efficiency of Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 172; first issue May 1995)

> Scope note: Overall Thermal Transfer Value limits under the Building (Energy Efficiency) Regulation for the external walls and roofs of a commercial or hotel building. The calculation method is in the Code of Practice for Overall Thermal Transfer Value in Buildings 1995. Sunshade concessions are also in PNAP APP-19.

## Regulatory Overview
The regulation reduces heat transfer through the building envelope so that air-conditioning electricity is saved. For plans or a major revision submitted for approval on or after 31 December 2025, the Overall Thermal Transfer Value of a building tower should not exceed 20 W/m2, and that of a podium should not exceed 40 W/m2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is counted, and when the figures are submitted (paragraphs 4–9 and 12)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Podium height where site coverage varies under PNAP APP-132 | The notional height of the podium for this calculation can be regarded as 20 m above the mean street level. It may be demarcated lower than 20 m to simplify the computation |
| Alterations and additions | The regulation applies to a horizontal or vertical extension that constitutes a new building, and to new external walls or facades covering the entire tower or podium |
| Open-front shop on ground floor | Disregarded if usable floor area is not more than 50 m2 and any air-conditioning is separate from the main building. If usable floor area is more than 50 m2, a notional calculation based on clear-glass shopfront may be acceptable to demonstrate paragraph 3 |
| First building-plan submission | Need not include the information and calculations required by regulation 5 |
| Before consent | An Overall Thermal Transfer Value Summary Sheet in Appendix A, under regulation 10 of the Building (Administration) Regulations, calculated to the code |
| Before occupation permit, or Form BA14 for alterations | Record the final values for external walls and roofs, and the shading coefficient of glass, on the general building plans. Submit a report with calculations, Forms OTTV1 to OTTV4, record plans, test certificates or published specifications, and the final summary sheet |
| Materials not listed in the code | Obtain the properties from reliable sources and explain why they suit local conditions |
| Building types on the summary sheet | Hotel, office including industrial-office, shops, or other commercial buildings such as department stores, places of public entertainment, places of public assembly, and restaurants |

**SD takeaway:** For a commercial or hotel submission on or after 31 December 2025, design the tower envelope to not more than 20 W/m2 and the podium to not more than 40 W/m2, and lodge the summary sheet before consent.

### 2. Sunshades (paragraphs 10–11)
| Projection | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 1.5 m or less, and a genuine sunshade that assists in reducing the value | Not accountable for gross floor area, and not included in site coverage, under regulation 6 |
| Over 750 mm | Submit a quantitative assessment that the shade reduces the value |
| Not more than 750 mm | Regarded as not obstructing prescribed windows |
| Over a street | Not allowed under section 31(1), unless an exemption is justified by special circumstances |
| Other designs | May be accepted if comparable or better in energy efficiency. An innovative design that reduces the value is not penalised in plot ratio and site coverage if it is shown to be effective |

**SD takeaway:** A sunshade of not more than 1.5 m can stay out of gross floor area and site coverage if it genuinely cuts the thermal transfer value, and one over 750 mm needs a calculation to prove that.
