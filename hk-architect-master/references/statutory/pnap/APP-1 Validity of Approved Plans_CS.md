# APP-1 Validity of Approved Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 1994 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP; first issued December 1974; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: How approved plans that support an Exclusion Order under the Landlord and Tenant (Consolidation) Ordinance, Cap. 7, sit with Buildings Ordinance sections 16(3A) and 16(3)(d), and what counts as a material variation or a penalty-free extension. It does not set the ordinary two-year consent rule for projects that are not under an Exclusion Order.

## Regulatory Overview
Approved plans that form the basis of an Exclusion Order are normally taken out of Buildings Ordinance section 16(3)(d) by section 16(3A), and consent applied for more than two years after approval will be given if the work can still be completed within the Re-development Order period under the Demolished Buildings (Re-development of Sites) Ordinance, Cap. 337. A Building Authority approval does not mean the plans are accepted under the Landlord and Tenant (Consolidation) Ordinance.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Material variation of an Exclusion Order scheme (paragraph 2)
| Change | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Reduction in floor space of more than 10% | Material variation |
| Change in use of any part of the proposed building | Material variation |
| Involvement of other premises also subject to the Landlord and Tenant (Consolidation) Ordinance, for example by extending the project | Material variation |
| Consequence | The authorized person, or the client's solicitors, must apply to the Lands Tribunal for an amending Exclusion Order |

**SD takeaway:** Freeze the floor area to within 10% and freeze the use before locking the scheme, because either a larger cut or a use change needs an amending Exclusion Order from the Lands Tribunal.

### 2. Penalty-free extension of commencement (paragraphs 4–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Length | One month, granted by the Building Authority on request before the commencement period in the Exclusion Order expires |
| (a) | Inability to obtain vacant possession, if legal proceedings have been commenced and are pursued with due diligence |
| (b) | Inability to obtain entry to adjacent property for adequate shoring or other required precautionary works |
| (c) | Inability to obtain consent to commence demolition for other reasons beyond the applicant's control |

**SD takeaway:** If commencement will miss the Exclusion Order date, request the one-month penalty-free extension before that date expires, and only on one of the three listed grounds.
