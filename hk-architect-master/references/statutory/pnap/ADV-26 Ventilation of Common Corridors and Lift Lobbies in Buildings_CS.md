# ADV-26 Ventilation of Common Corridors and Lift Lobbies in Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue January 2004 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Recommends ventilation of common corridors and lift lobbies beyond the rooms for which the regulations already prescribe natural ventilation. It does not amend those prescribed room standards.

## Regulatory Overview
Prescribed natural ventilation under the building regulations applies to rooms used for habitation, as offices, or as kitchens; this note promotes ventilation of common corridors and lift lobbies, including entrance lobbies, for environmental hygiene. Where natural ventilation is not feasible, mechanical ventilation should be provided.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mechanical ventilation of corridors and lobbies (paragraphs 2–3)
| Practice | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh air | Capable of supplying sufficient fresh air for the anticipated population |
| Intake and exhaust | Designed so the fresh-air supply is not contaminated and the system does not short-circuit |
| Maintenance | The system and its ductwork, where provided, conveniently accessible for maintenance |

**SD takeaway:** At schematic design, give common corridors and entrance and lift lobbies a natural-vent path or a mechanical system sized to the anticipated population, with intake and exhaust that cannot short-circuit.
