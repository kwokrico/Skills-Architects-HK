# APP-146 Metal Gates
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision March 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 304; first issue December 2008)

> Scope note: Design, installation, and maintenance of metal gates, manual or electric, at fence walls or entrances. Gates over 3.2 m need structural plans. Certain installations in existing buildings may be minor works under PNAP APP-147. Anchor tests follow Appendix B of PNAP APP-169. It is not a boundary-wall code.

## Regulatory Overview
Installation of a metal gate is building works, and new buildings show the gates on the building plans. A gate higher than 3.2 m also needs structural details and calculations, and the same threshold applies to a new gate in an existing building.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Height that triggers structural plans (paragraphs 2–6)
| Case | Submission |
| --- | --- |
| New building | Gates on the building plans for approval and consent |
| Height exceeding 3.2 m | Structural details and design calculations as well |
| New gate in an existing building over 3.2 m | Both building and structural plans before installation |
| Performance | Section 3 of the Building (Construction) Regulation: suitable materials, recognised standards, sound construction, adequate strength |
| Structural submission, where required | Wind to the prevailing Code of Practice on Wind Effects in Hong Kong; framework, posts, and foundation stable in operation; connection to the surrounding structure and a check of that structure; hinge-pin details for a swing gate; end stoppers at both ends for a sliding gate, and between leaves of a multi-passing gate; guides against lift-off or derailment. Decorative parts need not be submitted |

**SD takeaway:** Any metal gate over 3.2 m high needs structural plans, including the wind load and the posts and foundation.

### 2. Hinges, wheels, and stoppers (Appendix A)
| Type | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Swing | Hinge fittings welded all round, able to take dead weight and wind. Minimum three hinges to each post. Over 3.2 m high: stainless steel hinges at not more than 1000 mm centres, and not more than 300 mm from the top and the bottom. One hinge pin reversed, or an equivalent restraint, so the gate cannot be lifted off |
| Sliding | Leaf and moving parts must not fall, collapse, or derail. Minimum two pairs of guiding wheels, minimum spacing 1000 mm, at the end support, overlapping the leaf. At least one stopper on the leaf, plus stoppers on the ground, column, or fence wall. Rubber cushion on the end stoppers |
| Folding | Paragraphs 1 to 6 apply to two or more hinged leaves guided or supported at top or bottom, including lift-off restraint on the hanging fittings |
| Alternative leaf | An alternative openable access may be provided so the main leaf is swung or slid less often |
| Welds | Hinge welds left uncovered for inspection |
| Maintenance | Routine inspection every three months: floor track, debris, stopper, guiding wheels and axle, welds, hinges, and lubrication |

**SD takeaway:** A swing gate needs three hinges, and a gate over 3.2 m needs stainless hinges at 1000 mm centres with one pin reversed.

### 3. Tests (paragraphs 8–11)
| Test | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Drilled-in anchors | At least 5 of each type and size, pull-out tested to Appendix B of PNAP APP-169, capacity not less than 1.5 times the manufacturer’s recommended tensile load. Satisfactory if there is no separation, plastic deformation, or deleterious effect |
| Welds | Non-destructive tests of all welded joints at the supports and of the mechanical end stoppers |
| Laboratory | Accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme, or a mutual-recognition partner, for that test |
| Direction | Authorized person or registered structural engineer if plans are approved; prescribed building professional for Class I minor works; registered general building contractor or registered minor works contractor for Class II or III without a prescribed building professional |
| Electric gates | Code of Practice for Installation of Electrically Operated Sliding Gates, Sliding Glass Doors and Rolling Shutters, Electrical and Mechanical Services Department |

**SD takeaway:** Pull-test at least five anchors of each size to 1.5 times the recommended tensile load, and non-destructively test every support weld and end stopper.
