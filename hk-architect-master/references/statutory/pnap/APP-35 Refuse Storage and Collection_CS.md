# APP-35 Refuse Storage and Collection
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2022 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 98; first issue November 1983)

> Scope note: Vehicle access to a refuse storage and material recovery chamber, and the alternative of a refuse storage room on every domestic floor plus a centralised recycling corner, under the Building (Refuse Storage and Material Recovery Chambers and Refuse Chutes) Regulations. Floor-space calculations for the chambers required by the Schedule to regulation 3 are submitted with the plans. Appendix B gives typical room layouts; the minimum dimensions in the text are those below.

## Regulatory Overview
A chamber intended to be visited by a refuse collection vehicle is on the ground floor or a podium floor, with direct vehicular access from the street that does not pass through intervening floors, and the access and exit routes are shown on the building plans. Subject to satisfactory planning, the areas of material recovery chambers, refuse storage and material recovery chambers and rooms, refuse chutes, and hopper rooms may be excluded from gross floor area under regulation 23(3)(b) of the Building (Planning) Regulations.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. The vehicle the access must fit (paragraphs 1–2)
| Item | Limit, worst case |
| --- | --- |
| Overall length | 11 m |
| Overall width, mirror to mirror | 3 m |
| Overall height | 4 m |
| Headroom for bin lifting | 4.5 m |
| Ground clearance | 0.24 m |
| Angle of departure, fully laden | 10 degrees |
| Turning circle, wall to wall | 20 m diameter |
| Gross vehicle weight | 26 tonnes |
| Maximum gradient | 1:10 |
| Access-road width | Preferably 4 m, and not less than 3.5 m |
| Parking space for the collection operation | 5 m by 12 m |
| If the vehicle cannot leave forwards | A hammer-head of 27 m by 9.5 m with an approach 10.9 m wide, or another layout based on the same vehicle |

**SD takeaway:** Give the refuse vehicle a route at least 3.5 m wide, not steeper than 1:10, with 4.5 m headroom at the lift and a 5 m by 12 m standing space, or a 27 m by 9.5 m hammer-head if it cannot drive straight out.

### 2. Rooms, fire separation, and the recycling-corner alternative (paragraphs 6–13 and Appendix C)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Every refuse storage and material recovery room | Fire alarm and sprinkler. Walls not less than -/120/120. Access door not less than -/60/60, self-closing, and not able to be held open |
| Ventilation | A central system may use one air purifier before discharge, preferably at upper roof level away from other buildings, or at the main chamber if the building is surrounded by taller buildings. Fire dampers if there are exhaust grilles and ducts at each floor. Noise to the Technical Memorandum under the Noise Control Ordinance, Cap. 400. An air scrubber, bio-oxygen generator, photo-oxidation generator, or similar device inside a refuse storage and material recovery chamber |
| Regulation 3A | A refuse storage and material recovery room on every floor of a domestic building or the domestic part of a composite building, except the cases in regulations 3A(2) to (6) |
| Alternative, total usable floor space 1,320 m2 or more | A refuse storage room on every domestic floor, minimum dimension 1 m and floor area 1.5 m2, complying with regulations 12A, 12B, and 12C, plus one centralised recycling corner per tower at the residents' main entrance level, in the common parts |
| Recycling-corner size | Total usable floor space 1,320 to 7,260 m2: 10 to 14 m2. Above 7,260 up to 13,200 m2: 14 to 16 m2. Above 13,200 m2: 16 to 21 m2. No dimension less than 1.5 m, and a 1.5 m by 1.5 m manoeuvring space. Accessible to persons with a disability |
| Shared corner | A building under 1,320 m2 may share if the buildings served together are at least 1,320 m2, each main entrance is not more than 75 m horizontally from the corner, and the size follows the table for the combined floor space |
| Recycling corner, services | Not less than 3 air changes per hour of fresh air, plus air purification of the exhaust; illumination not less than 120 lux at finished floor level and sockets for future equipment; same fire ratings as the rooms above; a water point and sink; a floor outlet with grating, connected by a pipe of internal diameter not less than 100 mm to a back-inlet trapped gully immediately outside, with an airtight cover, discharging to a foul drain |
| Gross floor area | The alternative rooms and corner may also be excluded under regulation 23(3)(b) |

**SD takeaway:** Either provide a refuse and recycling room on every domestic floor, or, where usable floor space is at least 1,320 m2, a 1.5 m2 refuse room on each floor and a recycling corner of 10 to 21 m2 at the entrance, within 75 m if it is shared.
