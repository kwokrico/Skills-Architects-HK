# APP-42 Amenity Features
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision July 2025 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Features that are not statutory requirements but may be exempted or disregarded from gross floor area and site coverage to encourage better buildings. Every concession in this note is also subject to the pre-requisites, and where stated the overall cap, in PNAP APP-151. Projections and canopies are in PNAP APP-19 and PNAP APP-68. Prestige entrances are in PNAP APP-2.

## Regulatory Overview
Floor space genuinely for air-conditioning may be disregarded under regulation 23(3)(b) of the Building (Planning) Regulations. In the absence of a detailed plant layout, plant rooms not exceeding 1% of the total gross floor area of the building, and air-handling units not exceeding 4% of the gross floor area of the floor they are on, are treated as reasonable, and a larger provision or a plant room in each domestic unit needs full justification.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Plant, management rooms, and covered links (paragraphs 6–21)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Residential air-conditioning | A permanent wall box or reinforced-concrete platform may be excluded from site coverage, including over a street. See PNAP APP-19. Commercial and industrial buildings should use a central system or internal space, without projecting package units over streets |
| Plant room inside a flat | Access is from outside the unit or from the common area unless that is justified. If access is inside the unit, the plant-room floor is at least 500 mm different in level from the main accommodation, at least one side is open to the open air, and a railing, grille, or louvre up to parapet level is acceptable. Enclosure above the parapet is allowed only to separate intake from exhaust, or to meet clause C11.1 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011 |
| Exhaust and condensate | Locate the exhaust and provide a condensate disposal so that adjoining occupants and the public are not affected. An unusually extensive system is shown in detail and is operational before the occupation permit or Form BA14 |
| Security gates | May be at unit entrances, at main staircase exits, and in a composite building between the domestic and non-domestic parts, preferably in a lobby. They must not obstruct means of escape, and locks open from the inside without a key |
| Counters, kiosks, offices, stores, guard rooms, and lavatories for watchmen and management | In the common parts, not excessive. Domestic: 0.2% of total domestic gross floor area, or 5 m2 for every 50 flats, whichever is smaller. Non-domestic: 0.2% of total non-domestic gross floor area, or 120 m2, whichever is smaller |
| Owners' corporation office and caretakers' quarters | Size by reference to the lease, or as justified |
| Horizontal screen or covered walkway | Only on a communal outdoor passageway at ground or podium, open-sided, linking the main access at the lot boundary to a main entrance and/or main entrances of buildings, not obstructing a prescribed window or open air, not part of commercial premises, in the common parts. Width not exceeding 3.5 m and height not exceeding 4.5 m. The height of the part across an emergency vehicular access may be relaxed on the merits |

**SD takeaway:** Hold air-conditioning plant to 1% of the building and air-handling units to 4% of the floor, and keep a management suite to the smaller of 0.2% or 5 m2 per 50 flats.

### 2. Trellis, shelters, and podium gardens (paragraphs 22–31)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Trellis on a private roof or garden | Not more than 5% of that roof or garden, or 20 m2, whichever is less |
| Trellis on a common roof or garden | Not more than 5% of the roof and not more than 10% of the garden, and each trellis not exceeding 20 m2 |
| Trellis form | Open-sided, not higher than the storey height of the floor it is on, or of the floor below if it is on the roof. Horizontal supports or intermediate bars not nearer than 200 mm. Not on a refuge roof. Commentary to clause B18.3 of the 2011 fire safety code |
| Shelter over outdoor fixed seats | On communal outdoor space at ground, podium, or roof. Total area not more than 10% of that space, and each shelter not exceeding 20 m2. Open-sided, width and height not more than 3.5 m, and horizontal clearance not less than 3.5 m from any other structure. Not on a refuge roof. Common parts |
| Communal podium garden or covered landscaped or play area under and within a domestic tower | Modification of regulation 23(3)(a) if the area is open and not encumbered with structure, and the exempted area is within 5% of total domestic gross floor area |
| Covered area of a public open space required by the lease or a planning condition | Resting facilities shown on the plans, not part of commercial premises, designated as common parts, and no objection from the Lands Department and the Planning Department |

**SD takeaway:** Cap a private trellis at 5% or 20 m2, a seat shelter at 20 m2 and 3.5 m wide, and a covered landscaped area under a domestic tower at 5% of domestic gross floor area.
