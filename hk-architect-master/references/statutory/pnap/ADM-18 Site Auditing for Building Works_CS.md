# ADM-18 Site Auditing for Building Works
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision April 2018 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 254; first issue April 2001)

> Scope note: The Buildings Department's surprise audit of foundation and superstructure works under section 22 of the Buildings Ordinance. It does not reduce the statutory supervision duties of the authorized person, registered structural engineer, registered geotechnical engineer, or contractor.

## Regulatory Overview
The Building Authority enters sites under section 22 to check compliance, using surprise audits whose frequency depends on the last visit's findings and which are separated from the officers who process the submission. The audit looks at structural safety and integrity; a properly carried out site is not intended to be stopped.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How often and who attends (paragraphs 3–9)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Detailed checking of foundation and superstructure works, items in Appendix I |
| Frequency | Performance-based. The first visit rates site conditions, complexity, the contractor's organisation, attendance of qualified supervisory personnel, completeness of documents on site, and quality of work to date. Later visits come sooner if the site is difficult, anomalies are found, or quality is substandard |
| Predictability | No pre-determined percentage of units, and generally no pre-arranged date with the AP, RSE, RGE, or contractor. Sites in progress or completed may be audited |
| Staff | The auditing officer is neither the officer who processes that site's submissions nor the officer who did the previous inspection |
| Focus of investigation | Anomalies that affect structural safety and integrity, or that occur frequently |

**SD takeaway:** Keep qualified supervisors and the approved documents on site at all times; the next audit is earlier if the last one found substandard work, and it will not be booked in advance.

### 2. Two-storey warehouses and minor works (paragraphs 10–11)
| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Minor works under the simplified requirements | Selected cases under construction get audit site-safety inspections rather than the systematic programme. Appendix I items that can arise (spread footing, reinforced concrete, in-situ concrete, cantilever canopy) are still audited |
| Two-storey warehouse on the ADM-19 fast track | Inspection of the foundation bearing stratum within one working day (within 8 working hours) of notification. Quality inspection of structural elements is combined with the comprehensive superstructure inspection into one joint visit |
| Warehouse not exceeding 1,500 m² gross floor area | Only one unscheduled audit unless irregularities warrant more |

**SD takeaway:** For a fast-track two-storey warehouse, notify the bearing-stratum inspection and expect it within one working day; a warehouse at or under 1,500 m² gross floor area is otherwise a single unscheduled audit.
