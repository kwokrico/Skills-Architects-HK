# APP-68 Design and Construction of Cantilevered Reinforced Concrete Structures
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2012 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 173; first issue October 1994)

> Scope note: Design, drainage, and supervision of cantilevered reinforced concrete canopies, balconies, projecting windows, air-conditioner hoods and platforms, and flower beds. A cantilever under a permanent solid cover not more than one storey above it, and at least as large in plan as that cover, may be treated as not exposed to weathering. Projections must also meet Building (Planning) Regulations 7 and 10 and PNAP APP-19. Submissions that follow Appendix A are treated as complying with regulations 4(b) and 10(3) of the Building (Planning) Regulations and regulation 4 of the Building (Construction) Regulations.

## Regulatory Overview
A cantilever with a clear span of more than 1,000 mm should use a beam-and-slab arrangement instead of a pure slab where practicable, and span-to-effective-depth ratios follow clause 7.3.4.2 of the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2004 (Second Edition). Dead load of finishes, parapets, and waterproofing, and imposed load from maintenance and from ponding if the drains fail, are included.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Beams, slabs, and exposure (Appendix A, paragraphs 1 and 5–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Cantilevered beam at the support | Overall depth at least 300 mm. Top and bottom bars held by stirrups. Top bars anchored to clause 9.4.3 of the Concrete Code. External beams designed for exposure condition 2 or higher |
| Slab thickness | 110 mm if the span is not greater than 500 mm; 125 mm if greater than 500 mm but not greater than 750 mm; 150 mm if greater than 750 mm |
| Slab reinforcement | High-yield bars in both faces and both directions. Main bars at least 10 mm diameter, spacing not greater than 150 mm, and steel area not less than 0.25% of the structural concrete section. Where the slab drops at the support, top main bars not greater than 16 mm diameter so they can be anchored. These rules do not apply to infill slabs enclosed by edge beams or to slabs that do not behave as cantilevers |
| External slab, serviceability | Crack width at the tension face limited to 0.1 mm, or stress in deformed high-yield bars not exceeding 100 N/mm2 under working load. Cover for exposure condition 2 or higher |
| Replacement | Especially where the cantilever projects over a street, detail it so it can be demolished or replaced without affecting the safety of the main structure |
| Casting | Cast monolithically with, and at the same time as, the directly supporting members. Do not place a construction joint along the external edge of the supporting members unless an alternative method is approved first. Supervision to Table 9.1 of the Code of Practice for Site Supervision 2009 |

**SD takeaway:** Use a beam and slab once the clear span exceeds 1,000 mm, keep a cantilevered slab at least 150 mm thick beyond 750 mm, and cast it monolithically with its support.

### 2. Drainage and long exposed slabs (Appendix A, paragraphs 4 and 9–10)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Waterproofing | Effective waterproofing. Membrane or tanking protected by 1:3 cement-sand mortar laid to a fall of not less than 1:75 |
| Discharge | Drain water away from the cantilever where practicable. If water is to cascade from the edge, as at a projecting window, it must not cause a nuisance under regulation 4(b), and a continuous drip or groove is provided at the bottom edge. Every canopy discharges to the building's surface-water system |
| Outlets where the top is not accessible for cleaning | Not more than 5 m apart |
| Pipes in the slab | Avoid penetration and embedment. If unavoidable, allow for the local loss of strength. See PNAP APP-105 for water-borne pipes in beams and slabs |
| Exposed cantilevered slab with span exceeding 750 mm | Waterproof concrete of characteristic strength not less than 35 MPa at 28 days. All main bars hot-dip galvanized to BS EN ISO 1461. Membrane or tanking protected by 1:3 mortar of maximum free water/cement ratio 0.65, or an equivalent. On completion, a construction report and a Form BA 14 confirming the works follow the approved plans and are structurally safe. The report includes an as-built plan of location, dimensions, and the direction and gradient of the fall |

**SD takeaway:** Fall an exposed cantilever at not less than 1:75, and for a span over 750 mm use 35 MPa waterproof concrete and galvanized main bars.
