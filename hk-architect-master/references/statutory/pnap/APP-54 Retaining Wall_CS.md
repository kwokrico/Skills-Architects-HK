# APP-54 Retaining Wall
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 142; first issue December 1990)

> Scope note: Design and construction of a permanent structure on land that retains earth or fill, under section 23 of the Building (Construction) Regulation. Anchor plates supporting a rock or earth face are not treated as a retaining wall. Rock faces are not "earth" for earth-pressure calculation. Design practice is Geoguide 1, second edition, and PNAP APP-63.

## Regulatory Overview
Other than a minor retaining wall defined in section 22, drainage and the filter comply with section 24, and the filter is clean, sound, and durable, free from clay, organic material, and other impurities. In non-aggressive conditions, geotextile filters of resistant synthetic polymers are suitable alternatives to granular filters in permanent works.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Backfill grading (paragraphs 5–7 and Appendix A, Table 1)
| Requirement | Crushed rock products | Soil |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum size | 200 mm | 75 mm. Up to 5% of rock fragments not exceeding 200 mm is allowed if compaction and the wall are not affected |
| Percentage passing 63 µm | 0 | 0 to 45. If less than 30% by weight is finer than 63 µm, liquid limit and plasticity index need not be checked |
| Coefficient of uniformity | Not less than 5 | Not less than 50 for soil from in-situ rock weathering. For alluvial sand and gravel, not less than 5, and not gap-graded |
| Liquid limit | Not applicable | Not more than 45% |
| Plasticity index | Not applicable | Not more than 20% |
| Layer thickness | Maximum particle size not exceeding two-thirds of the compacted layer | Same |
| Excluded material | Peat, vegetation, timber, organic or degradable material; dangerous or toxic material; combustible material; metal, rubber, plastic, or synthetic material; material of significant volume change such as marine mud, swelling clays, and collapsible soils; soluble material. Backfill must not be chemically aggressive, for example excessive sulphate | Same |
| Tests | Sections 6 and 8 of Geospec 3. Particle size without dispersants | Same |

**SD takeaway:** Specify backfill to Table 1, keep soil fines at or below 45% passing 63 µm, and do not use marine mud, swelling clay, or sulphate-rich fill behind the wall.

### 2. Drainage, monitoring, demolition, and minor works (paragraphs 3–4 and 8–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Filter design | Section 8.5 of Geoguide 1, and GEO Publication No. 1/93 |
| Existing wall, remedial or preventive works | Past performance assists the designer. Section 7.3.3 of the Geotechnical Manual for Slopes. Designs to those recommendations are acceptable |
| During construction | Monitor the wall and the effects on groundwater, the site, and any building, structure, land, street, or services |
| Demolition | Submit demolition plans and supporting documents under regulations 8(3) and (4) of the Building (Administration) Regulations |
| Routine maintenance | Certain items are minor works. See Schedule 1 and PNAP APP-147 |

**SD takeaway:** Detail a section 24 filter on every wall that is not a minor retaining wall, and monitor groundwater and movement while the wall is built.
