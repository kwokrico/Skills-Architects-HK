# APP-104 Exclusion of Floor Areas for Recreational Use
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision July 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 229; first issue January 1999)

> Scope note: Gross floor area (GFA) exclusion for recreational facilities in residential developments under section 42 of the Buildings Ordinance, by modification of regulation 23(3)(a) of the Building (Planning) Regulations. Covered landscaped and play areas follow paragraph 8 and PNAP APP-42. The concession is also subject to the prerequisites and overall cap in PNAP APP-151, except for those covered landscaped and play areas.

## Regulatory Overview
Recreational facilities aimed at owners and residents generally may be excluded from GFA on a case-by-case basis, within a sliding cap tied to total domestic GFA. Luxury clubs, restaurants, and commercial undertakings are outside the concession.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Sliding cap (paragraph 4, Table 1)
The cap covers the facilities and all voids, plant rooms (including a swimming-pool filtration plant room), covered courts, and similar spaces serving solely the recreational facilities. The greater of the percentage or the stated area applies.

| Total domestic GFA | Maximum concession |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 25,000 m2 | 5% |
| More than 25,000 to 50,000 m2 | 4.5% / 1,250 m2 |
| More than 50,000 to 75,000 m2 | 4.0% / 2,250 m2 |
| More than 75,000 to 100,000 m2 | 3.5% / 3,000 m2 |
| More than 100,000 to 125,000 m2 | 3.0% / 3,500 m2 |
| More than 125,000 m2 | 2.5% / 3,750 m2 |

The application must justify overall size, headroom, mix, and how exclusive use by owners, residents, and bona fide visitors will be operated and controlled.

**SD takeaway:** Size the clubhouse, including its plant and voids, inside the Table 1 band before counting it as non-accountable GFA.

### 2. What may be excluded (paragraphs 2, 6–7 and Appendix A)
| Class | Accepted items |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Swimming pool; multi-purpose ball court or squash court; basketball or football court; tennis or badminton court; children play area; games room; indoor golf room; table-tennis room; fitness room; gymnasium; weight training, aerobic or exercise room; bowling alley; skating rink; volleyball court; rock climbing room; dance or yoga room |
| Passive | Sitting area or lounge; billiard room; sauna, jacuzzi or spa; reading room, study room or library; function room; computer or video game room; music room or karaoke room |
| Ancillary, size-related | Club house management office and reception; staff room; snack bar, mini-bar or pantry (total area not exceeding 10 m2); first-aid room; kitchen not exceeding 15 m2 for sites without any commercial floor space; changing room and shower; lavatories and toilets; store rooms |
| Not accepted | Substantially luxury clubs with restaurants and other facilities meant for exclusive membership and commercial undertakings; commercial undertakings generally |

**SD takeaway:** Keep the ancillary kitchen at or below 15 m2 and the snack or pantry total at or below 10 m2, and do not put commercial dining inside the concession.

### 3. Elderly provision and covered play (paragraphs 5 and 8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Elderly | At least one elderly-friendly toilet and elderly fitness equipment complying with the Design Manual: Barrier Free Access, shown on the plans. If that toilet also serves as a required accessible toilet, provide another elderly-friendly facility in addition to the fitness equipment. Provision is commensurate with the design; examples are in Chapter 6 of the Design Manual |
| Open-sided covered landscaped or children play areas under the domestic tower footprint | Not subject to Table 1, but within 5% of total domestic GFA, as stated in PNAP APP-42, and subject to the prerequisites in PNAP APP-151 |
| Plans on or after 31 August 2025 | Paragraph 5 applies to all new building plans or major revisions submitted for approval on or after that date |

**SD takeaway:** Put an elderly-friendly toilet and fitness equipment in the recreational floor, and keep covered play under the tower within 5% of domestic GFA.

### 4. Common-area control (paragraphs 9–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Undertaking | Before plan approval, a letter designating the facilities as common area in a Deed of Mutual Covenant, with use, location, and binding terms for control, operation, financial support, and maintenance |
| Amendments | A revised undertaking and layout plan |
| Occupation permit | The undertaking and layout plan must be registered in the Land Registry before the occupation-permit application, and GFA-concession information must be on the approved building plans as in PNAP ADM-2 |
| Use lock | Exclusive use by owners, residents, and bona fide visitors as shown on the approved plans; no other purpose or persons without prior consent of the Building Authority |

**SD takeaway:** Draw the recreational areas as common areas and lock exclusive residential use on the approved plans.
