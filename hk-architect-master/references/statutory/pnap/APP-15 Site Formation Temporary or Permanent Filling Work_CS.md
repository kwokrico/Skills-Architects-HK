# APP-15 Site Formation Temporary or Permanent Filling Work
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2005 | Buildings Department (first issue December 1981; re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Compaction standards where failure of a fill slope from private building works could endanger the public. Drawings must specify Appendix A. Supervision follows the technical memorandum under section 39A and qualified supervision under section 17(1)(6)(e) as set out in PNAP 83. An occupation permit will normally be refused under section 21(6)(a) if temporary fill remains or if permanent fill that could endanger the public does not comply with Appendix A.

## Regulatory Overview
The in-situ field dry density of compacted material forming the peripheral portion of an earth fill slope must be not less than 95% of the maximum dry density. Maximum dry density, optimum moisture content, field density, and relative compaction are determined to Geospec 3, by a laboratory accredited under the Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme for the relevant tests.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Density and test frequency (Appendix A)
| Batch | Determinations of moisture content and relative compaction |
| --- | --- |
| Fill in excavations for structures, pits, and trenches, and on formations, 0 to 100 m2 | 3 |
| Same, 100 to 500 m2 | 2 for each 100 m2 or part thereof |
| Same, exceeding 500 m2 | 1 for each 100 m2 or part thereof |
| Other fill, 0 to 1 ha | 4 for each 1,000 m2 or part thereof |
| Other fill, 1 to 10 ha | 3 for each 1,000 m2 or part thereof |
| Other fill, exceeding 10 ha | 2 for each 1,000 m2 or part thereof |
| Small amount | Depth less than 1 m and total volume less than 300 m3: frequency may be reduced if in-situ density tests are supplemented by penetration tests |
| Same batch | A batch is fill of the same type with similar properties throughout. The maximum dry density test must be on the same soil type and source as the in-situ test location. Each soil type is tested when first used and again with every set of field density tests |

**SD takeaway:** Compact the outer skin of a fill slope to at least 95% of maximum dry density, and test each batch at the Table 1 rate with a HOKLAS laboratory.

### 2. Reports and loose fill (paragraphs 2–7)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Before filling | Give the Building Authority the commencement date. The registered geotechnical engineer gives periodic supervision |
| During filling | Monthly reports, signed by the registered geotechnical engineer, summarising in-situ density, laboratory compaction, and relative compaction, highlighting non-compliance, and recording corrective measures. Keep Appendix A records on site |
| Suspected loose fill | The Building Authority may check with GCO probes and trial pits. If loose fill is detected, in-situ density tests are done in the presence of Buildings Department and Geotechnical Engineering Office officers. Extensive loose fill that is a significant threat can lead to a Cease Works Order under section 23 or 24A |
| Before the completion certificate and occupation permit | As-built plans and sections showing the relationship to existing and proposed buildings, and the complete density and compaction records. If structural works are involved, the registered structural engineer signs the calculations and assessment and certifies completion of the structural works |

**SD takeaway:** Remove temporary fill before occupation, and do not seek an occupation permit until the permanent fill has as-built sections and a full compaction record.
