# APP-55 Procedure for Payment of Fees on Submission of Plans
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2024 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 143; first issue March 1991)

> Scope note: When fees under Regulation 42 of the Building (Administration) Regulations are payable on a plan submission, and what counts as a major revision that is charged again. All resubmissions of plans are free of charge. Proposals in response to a statutory order or notice, or an advisory letter on building, fire, or slope safety, are not charged.

## Regulatory Overview
For a new building, permanent or temporary, the fee on the first submission, including a major revision, is based on gross floor area and also covers the associated site formation, structural, and drainage plans. For a new building without accountable gross floor area, and for alteration and addition or other works that will not result in a new building, the fee is based on the number of plans of A1 size or smaller, and a plan larger than A1 in either dimension counts as multiples of A1.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Rates and what is not charged (paragraphs 2–7 and Appendix A, Form BD 24)
| Case | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Industrial building, gross floor area not more than 20,000 m2 | Rate 2,740 dollars, minimum 10,400 dollars. Area is divided by 100 m2 and rounded up |
| Industrial building, over 20,000 m2 | Rate 2,200 dollars, minimum 547,100 dollars |
| Non-industrial building, not more than 10,000 m2 | Rate 4,340 dollars, minimum 10,350 dollars |
| Non-industrial building, over 10,000 m2 | Rate 3,480 dollars, minimum 434,400 dollars |
| No accountable gross floor area, or works not resulting in a new building | 14,200 dollars times the number of plans not larger than A1. Buildings without accountable gross floor area include transformer stations, petrol filling stations, oil storage installations, jetties, and similar structures. Minor ancillary accommodation that would otherwise be accountable may be ignored |
| Payment | Crossed cheque to the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, with Form BD 24, on every first submission including a major revision. Cash and post-dated cheques are not accepted. Fees are not refundable if the submission is withdrawn. A wrong calculation is corrected by a supplementary fee or a refund |
| Missing or dishonoured fee, or supplementary fee not paid within 14 days of notification | Plans may be disapproved under section 16(1)(f) |
| Non-profit schools, hospitals, and similar community buildings | Exemption is generally considered favourably on Form BA16. No cheque is then required with the plans |
| Electronic Submission Hub | Faster Payment System, or a cheque with a copy of the acknowledgement receipt at the Receipt and Dispatch Counter within 14 days. See PNAP ADM-5 for the counter |

**SD takeaway:** Enclose the gross-floor-area fee with the first new-building submission, and pay any supplementary fee within 14 days, or the plans may be disapproved under section 16(1)(f).

### 2. Major revision (paragraphs 9–11)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Definition | A submission so extensively revised that it must be fundamentally reassessed and centralised processing repeated. See PNAP ADM-2. A new Form BA5 is required |
| Examples | Change in disposition or number of blocks; change in number of storeys, in particular in the podium and the number of basements; floor-plan changes that force reassessment of plot ratio, site coverage, lighting and ventilation, or means of escape; change of principal use that affects construction or planning or escape, such as office to residential or hotel to office; substantial change in site area or configuration; changes that seriously affect access; substantial modifications, exemptions, or bonuses; ground conditions found wrong in initial excavation, for site formation not resulting in a new building; changes that must be examined under new legislation or a new or amended outline zoning plan |
| Doubt | The Chief Building Surveyor of New Buildings Division 1 answers within seven days |
| Localized revision | Fee may be based on the localized gross floor area, per podium or per affected podium floors, because part of a building is also a building. Other cases may be considered under section 42 |

**SD takeaway:** A change in the number of blocks, storeys, basements, or the principal use is a major revision and is charged again, with a new Form BA5.
