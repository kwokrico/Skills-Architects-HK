# APP-19 Projections in relation to Site Coverage and Plot Ratio
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision June 2025 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 68; first issue March 1980)

> Scope note: Which projections need not be counted for site coverage and plot ratio under Building (Planning) Regulations 20 and 21, and which projecting windows and greenery features may be exempted. Site coverage is the area of the site covered by the buildings, so every component, including projections, is accountable unless this note accepts otherwise. A feature that is exaggerated, excessive, or used to defeat the Ordinance, such as a structural beam doubling as a flower box, is counted.

## Regulatory Overview
Gross floor area is measured to the external wall, including windows and parapet railings, and a projection that is not at a floor level, or a potential floor level, and does not dominate the face, need not be measured as accountable gross floor area. All other projections are included in site coverage and plot ratio unless a specific acceptance in this note applies.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Projections that need not be counted (paragraph 3)
| Feature | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Pitched-roof eaves and flat-roof overhangs | Projection and clear-height limits in regulation 7(1), and not contained within parapet walls as part of an accessible flat roof |
| Individual air-conditioner boxes and platforms | Reasonable size, projecting not more than 750 mm, with a built-in condensate disposal system |
| Air-conditioner platforms | Appendices B and C of the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance |
| Individual projections, window hoods, and porches | Complying with the regulations; porch projection not exceeding 2 m |
| Window cills and surrounds | Not more than 100 mm |
| String courses, fins, and architectural mouldings | Regulation 7(1) limits. Structural beams and columns are not included |
| Window flower boxes | Not more than 500 mm, and Diagram 1 of Appendix A |
| External drainage pipes and gutters | Regulation 7(2) |
| Sunshades solely for energy conservation | Not more than 1.5 m, and the criteria in PNAP APP-67 and PNAP APP-156 |
| Reflectors | Not more than 1.5 m. If more than 0.5 m, a quantitative assessment of environmental benefits for the Building Authority |
| Canopies | Not more than 3.5 m over an entrance |
| Drying racks and frames for light fittings, antennas, or public-telecommunications transceivers | Regulation 7(3) |
| Retractable awnings | Regulation 7(4) and minor works item 2.43 |
| Metal frames for plants | Not more than 300 mm from the external wall, within the lot boundary |

**SD takeaway:** Keep air-conditioner platforms to 750 mm, flower boxes to 500 mm, entrance canopies to 3.5 m, and plant frames to 300 mm if they are to stay out of site coverage and plot ratio.

### 2. Projecting windows and deep overhangs (paragraphs 5–8)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Projecting window | From a living room, dining room, or bedroom of domestic accommodation only. One per room, on one external wall. Elevational area not exceeding 50% of the area of that external wall. Projection not more than 100 mm from the outer face of the main external wall. Base not less than 500 mm above finished floor. Complies with regulation 3A. The 1,100 mm protective barrier is measured from floor level, and any part of the window within that height is fixed or otherwise protected under paragraph (2) of that regulation. Not a piecemeal addition to an existing building. Enclosure thickness of the window counts in the window area; enclosure walls and slabs of the room are discounted from the wall area |
| Covered area under a large projecting feature | Not a shopfront, signboard, or the like. No objection from relevant departments. Clear height more than 7.5 m above the covered area. Ratio of horizontal width of the covered area to that clear height not less than 1:8. Access not possible and abuse unlikely. Areas that are a weather-protected shelter capable of functional use are not accepted on this basis. Other forms, such as a twisted building or an inclined wall, need a merits justification; see also PNAP APP-2 |
| Over a street | No projection normally, unless justified in the public interest, except features complying with Part II. Individual air-conditioner boxes or platforms not exceeding 750 mm, and window hoods not exceeding 500 mm, may be permitted at not less than 2.5 m above ground. See also PNAP APP-42 |
| Wholesale conversion of an industrial building, or similar alteration, with air-conditioner projection more than 750 mm | Favourable consideration of exemption from section 31(1) and modification of regulations 20 and 21 only if the existing layout makes internal placement difficult. Grade 1 room air-conditioners under the Code of Practice on Energy Labeling of Products must be installed on completion |

**SD takeaway:** A domestic projecting window stays out of gross floor area only if it projects not more than 100 mm, starts at least 500 mm above the floor, and covers not more than half the wall of that room.

### 3. Projecting greenery and cantilevers (paragraphs 9–10)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Planters in common parts only | Not more than 600 mm from the external wall, within the lot. Safety provisions of the Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance, automatic irrigation, and adequate drainage. Concession is not subject to the prerequisites or the overall cap in PNAP APP-151. The developer or owner undertakes to designate them as common parts; the undertaking is registered before the occupation-permit application |
| Maintenance walkway to those planters or to the paragraph 3(n) frames | A drop of at least 150 mm from the area that leads to the walkway. Width not less than 400 mm and not more than 600 mm. Same external-maintenance code. This concession is subject to the prerequisites and the cap in PNAP APP-151. If the walkway and planter together project more than 750 mm, they obstruct prescribed windows |
| Cantilevered reinforced concrete | Show means to prevent accumulation of water, and surface drainage to paragraph 4 of Appendix A of PNAP APP-68, on the general building plans and the drainage plans |

**SD takeaway:** A common-part planter may project 600 mm without using the APP-151 concession cap, but the maintenance walkway beside it is 400 mm to 600 mm wide and does count toward that cap.
