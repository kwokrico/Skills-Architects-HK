# APP-174 Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue June 2026 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Amendments to the Code of Practice for Precast Concrete Construction 2016 in Appendix A (November 2020) and Appendix B (June 2026), uploaded to the Buildings Department website. It is not a substitute for the code. Quality supervision of precast work is in PNAP APP-143.

## Regulatory Overview
The November 2020 amendments point corrosion cover and material properties to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013, and add water seepage and further alkali-silica controls. The June 2026 amendments change bearing strength and require an on-site water test of completed panel and window joints to AAMA 501.2.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Cover, materials, and alkali (Appendix A)
| Clause | Amendment |
| --- | --- |
| 2.4.3 | Fire cover follows the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings. Corrosion cover follows the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013 |
| 2.4.4.1 | Connections filled to prevent corrosion, cracking, spalling, or water seepage |
| 2.6.1 | Use of materials follows the Building (Construction) Regulations and the 2013 concrete code. Design properties come from the 2013 code |
| 2.6.2.1 | Reactive alkali content, as equivalent sodium oxide per cubic metre, should not exceed 3.0 kg. Also: control cement content; low-alkali cement; cement replacement such as pulverised fuel ash; seek expert advice before alkali-reactive aggregates are used; use non-reactive aggregate in accordance with CS1; or restrict moisture. The supplier submits a mix design and Hong Kong Laboratory Accreditation Scheme endorsed certificates showing the alkali limit |

**SD takeaway:** Keep reactive alkali at or below 3.0 kg of equivalent sodium oxide per cubic metre, and take corrosion cover from the 2013 concrete code.

### 2. Bearing strength (Appendix B, clause 2.7.9.4)
Design ultimate bearing strength fcb, based on the weaker surface:

| Bearing | Strength |
| --- | --- |
| Dry on concrete | 0.27 m fcu |
| Bedded on concrete | 0.40 m fcu |
| Steel bearing plate cast in, each dimension not exceeding 40% of the corresponding concrete dimension | 0.80 m fcu |

m is the confinement modification factor, the square root of A2/A1, and is not more than 2. A1 is the loaded area and A2 is the load distribution area in Figure 2.5a. An intermediate value may be used for flexible bedding. The symbol fcb is added in clause 1.3.

**SD takeaway:** Size a dry precast bearing at 0.27 m fcu on the weaker face, with m not above 2.

### 3. Water test of completed joints (Appendix B, clause 2.8.2.8 and Table 2.7a)
On-site field testing of completed joints between precast panels and in-situ elements, and of completed panels and installed windows, follows AAMA 501.2.

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Samples | 100% |
| Nozzle pressure | 205 to 240 kPa |
| Hose diameter | 19 mm |
| Duration | 5 minutes in every 1.5 m of joint, nozzle moved slowly |
| Distance | 305 mm, plus or minus 25 mm |
| Failure | Dampness or leakage on interior surfaces during the test or within two hours after |
| Remedy | Remedy failed joints and retest them until all meet the criteria |

**SD takeaway:** Hose-test every completed panel and window joint at 205 to 240 kPa, 305 mm off the joint, for 5 minutes per 1.5 m.
