# ADM-22 Withdrawal and Resubmission
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issue September 2019 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Governs written withdrawal and resubmission of occupation-permit and plan-approval applications so the statutory processing period can restart. It does not change the design standards those applications must meet.

## Regulatory Overview
Withdrawal and resubmission is a long-established practice that lets an authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer restart the statutory time limit for an occupation permit, plan approval, or consent without physically taking the plans or documents back. The note limits how that practice may be used.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How withdrawal and resubmission works (paragraphs 1–2 and footnote 1)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| What it is | A letter to the Building Authority that simultaneously withdraws and resubmits an application for approval of plans, an occupation permit, or consent to commence works, without physically taking back and resubmitting the plans or documents |
| Effect | Re-commences the statutory period for processing |
| Form | Must be in writing and provided with reasons |
| Repeat limit | Withdrawal and resubmission of the same submission may generally be entertained for not more than two times |

**SD takeaway:** Use a written withdrawal and resubmission, with reasons, only to restart the statutory clock, and plan on no more than two uses for the same submission.
