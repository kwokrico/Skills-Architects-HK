# APP-111 Design of Car Parks and Loading/Unloading Facilities
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision July 2019 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 236; first issue March 2000)

> Scope note: Layout dimensions and geometry the Building Authority uses when car parks and loading bays are shown on plans. The figures incorporate Table 11, Chapter 8 of the Hong Kong Planning Standards and Guidelines and Transport Department requirements. The Commissioner for Transport still advises on road safety, run-ins, and public roads. Disregarding the floor from gross floor area under regulation 23(3)(b) of the Building (Planning) Regulations is in PNAP APP-2 and depends on the Building Authority being satisfied with the design. Private streets follow the Building (Private Streets and Access Roads) Regulations.

## Regulatory Overview
Car parks and their ancillary works are to be designed for convenience and safety. Where the desirable standard cannot be met, the minimum standard in Appendix A, or another justified standard with swept paths, may be used; the absolute minimum is only for exceptional cases with full justification.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Stall size and headroom (Appendix A, Table 1)
| Vehicle | Length (m) | Width (m) | Minimum clear headroom (m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motorcycles | 2.4 (minimum 2) | 1 | 2.4 |
| Private cars and taxis | 5 | 2.5 | 2.4 |
| Coaches and buses | 12 | 3.5 | 3.8 |
| Light goods vehicles | 7 | 3.5 | 3.6 |
| Medium and heavy goods vehicles | 11 | 3.5 | 4.7 |
| Container vehicles | 16 | 3.5 | 4.7 |
| Light buses | 8 | 3 | 3.3 |
| Car parks for persons with a disability | 5 | 3.5 | 2.4 |

Accessible bays also follow the latest Design Manual: Barrier Free Access.

**SD takeaway:** A private-car bay is 5 m by 2.5 m with 2.4 m headroom; a goods-vehicle bay needs 4.7 m headroom.

### 2. Aisles (Appendix A, Table 1)
| Use | Angle | Desirable aisle width | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private cars and taxis, one-way | 90 degrees | 6 m | 5.5 m (absolute minimum 5.0 m) |
| Private cars and taxis, two-way | 90 degrees | 7.5 m | 6.0 m (absolute minimum 5.5 m) |
| Private cars and taxis, one-way | 0 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 70 / 80 degrees | 3 / 3 / 3.6 / 4.2 / 4.7 / 5.3 m | No minimum stated |
| Light goods vehicles, one-way | 45 / 90 degrees | 7 / 10 m | — |
| Medium and heavy goods vehicles, one-way | 45 / 90 degrees | 8 / 12 m | 90 degrees minimum 10 m |
| Medium and heavy goods vehicles, two-way | 90 degrees | 16 m | 10 m |
| Container vehicles, one-way | 45 / 90 degrees | 11 / 16 m | — |

Goods vehicles are loaded from the rear. Reserve about one vehicle length in front of a perpendicular bay. A hammerhead is recommended; show the swept path. A turn-table where the constraint is severe. A vehicle should park in one manoeuvre without reversing the steering lock.

**SD takeaway:** A 90-degree private-car aisle is 6 m desirable and 5.5 m minimum, with 5.0 m only as the absolute minimum.

### 3. Driveways, ramps, and bends (Appendix A, Tables 2 and 3)
| Item | Desirable | Minimum | Absolute minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Residential one-way width | 5.5 m | 4.0 m | 3.0 m, private cars only |
| Residential two-way width | 7.3 m | 5.5 m | 5.0 m, private cars only |
| Industrial or mixed one-way | 6.0 m | 5.5 m | 3.5 m |
| Industrial or mixed two-way | 10.5 m | 7.3 m | 6.0 m |
| Straight ramp, private cars | 1:10 | 1:7 | 1:6 |
| Straight ramp, goods vehicles | 1:10 | 1:8 | — |
| Helical ramp, private cars | 1:12.5 | 1:8 | — |
| Helical ramp, goods vehicles | 1:12.5 | 1:10 | — |
| Vertical clearance over the driveway and pavement within 600 mm of its outer edge | 5.1 m minimum for all vehicles. A lower clearance may be considered if the road is limited to certain vehicle types |
| Horizontal clearance | 500 mm. If impracticable, not less than 200 mm from railings and sign posts |

Straight one-way ramps in a multi-storey car park for private cars and taxis: minimum width between kerbs 3.0 m. Helical one-way: 3.65 m. Two-way straight: 6.0 m, or 5.5 m minimum with no central kerb. Horizontal clearance 300 mm on straight ramps and 600 mm on helical ramps. Minimum inner radius at bends on straight ramps 5.5 m. Helical one-way outer radius 9.0 m. Two-way ramps are not recommended. Bend widening: for a road 6 m or less, 1.2 m if the centre-line radius is less than 18 m, 1.0 m from 18 m to 24 m, and 0.6 m over 24 m; for a road more than 6 m, 1.0 m, 0.6 m, and 0.3 m for those same radius bands.

**SD takeaway:** Keep a private-car straight ramp no steeper than 1:7, and 1:6 only as the absolute maximum, with 5.1 m clearance where all vehicles use the driveway.
