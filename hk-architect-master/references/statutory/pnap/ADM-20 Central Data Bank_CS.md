# ADM-20 Central Data Bank
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 292; first issue January 2005)

> Scope note: A historical list of materials, components, and systems some departments have accepted before. Quoting a listing does not approve the product for the current project, and the Department does not take applications from suppliers to be listed.

## Regulatory Overview
The Central Data Bank, set up in 2005, is a web reference of building materials, components, and construction systems accepted by some government departments, mainly the Buildings Department, on the basis of accredited tests or certificates of conformity assessed under PNAP APP-118 and PNAP APP-165. The list is historical data for a past project, and the authorized person, registered structural engineer, or registered geotechnical engineer who relies on it remains fully responsible for performance against the latest standard.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. How an item gets onto the list (paragraphs 4–7)
| Product | Route onto the Buildings Department list |
| --- | --- |
| Structural | Use in a particular completed project has been accepted and certified by the authorized person, registered structural engineer, and registered contractor; or the item is on the list of recognised pile types |
| Non-structural | Taken from Schedules of Building Materials and Products submitted under PNAP APP-13. Some items are randomly audited |
| Not published | Design details, test and assessment reports, and certificates of conformity are not exhibited, for copyright reasons |
| Direct application | A supplier or manufacturer cannot apply to have a product listed |

**SD takeaway:** Do not specify a product merely because a supplier asks to be listed; only a past accepted project, a recognised pile type, or an audited APP-13 schedule puts an item on the bank.

### 2. Quoting the bank instead of resubmitting a report (paragraph 8)
If the test reports or certificates are identical to those on the list, and the material has been properly tested to the latest standards or meets the relevant product-conformity scheme, the reference may be quoted in the certification instead of submitting another hard copy. Any supplementary test or assessment that is not on the list must still be submitted. On the Schedule of Building Materials and Products, the list reference may be entered in the Remarks/Comments column.

**SD takeaway:** Quote a Central Data Bank reference only when the report is identical to the listed one and still meets the current standard; attach any newer supplementary test.
