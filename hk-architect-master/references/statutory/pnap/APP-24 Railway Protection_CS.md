# APP-24 Railway Protection
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2022 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 77; first issue April 1981)

> Scope note: Building works, ground investigation, and underground drainage inside railway protection areas under the Railways Ordinance, Cap. 519, the Mass Transit Railway (Land Resumption and Related Provisions) Ordinance, Cap. 276, and Area No. 3 of the Scheduled Areas. The boundary is generally about 30 m outside the outer surface of the railway structure or fence, or from the nearest rail if there is no fence, and may take in the whole lot if any part of the lot is inside that distance. Stations may be more extensive. Plans inside the area are circulated to the MTR Corporation Limited.

## Regulatory Overview
No pile, foundation, borehole, well, soil nail, horizontal drain, rock bolt, or other geotechnical installation is placed within 3 m of any point of an underground railway structure, and piling is not permitted within 3 m on plan of the railway fence or wall or within 7 m on plan of the nearest track centreline where there is no fence, unless the MTR Corporation Limited has specially agreed. Any part of an anchor, if allowed, is more than 3 m from the railway, and the centroid of the fixed length is more than twice the fixed length away.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Clearances and pressure (Appendix B, section B)
| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Vertical or horizontal pressure change on underground railway structures | Not exceeding 20 kPa from excavation, filling, ground improvement, dewatering, field testing, and foundation loads including construction loads. The limit may be reviewed case by case if an engineering approach is accepted |
| Openings, intakes, and exhausts | Not closer than 5 m to a railway ventilation opening, or 2.5 m if the railway exhaust is directed away and is not likely to affect the opening by natural convection. The same 5 m applies to vertical greening |
| Scaffolding, signs, and projections above track level | Not within 6 m on plan of any point of the tracks without agreement. Work inside that limit may have to be outside operating hours |
| Ground investigation holes | If within 10 m on plan and sunk to within 3 m of the highest point of an underground railway structure, a method for checking verticality. If within 3 m on plan, a method for controlling depth |
| Wells | Building works under section 14(1). Approval and consent before sinking |
| Drainage | A building that adjoins or is integrated with the railway is designed so that a blocked drain or a burst pipe cannot flood the railway. Site drainage is agreed with the MTR Corporation Limited |
| Area No. 3 | Ground investigation needs approval and consent under regulation 8(1)(l). Underground drainage in a building in Area No. 3 is not exempt, except minor works that the system allows. Outside Area No. 3 but inside a protection area, send ground investigation and underground drainage proposals to the MTR Corporation Limited before they start |

**SD takeaway:** Keep piles, anchors, and boreholes more than 3 m from an underground railway, hold pressure change to 20 kPa, and keep windows and intakes at least 5 m from a railway vent.

### 2. Movement and vibration if the empirical limits are used (Appendix B, section C)
| Criterion | Alert | Alarm | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Angular distortion in any plane | 1 in 2,000 | 1 in 1,350 | 1 in 1,000 |
| Total movement in any plane | 10 mm | 15 mm | 20 mm |
| Level difference between rails of a track | 2.5 mm | 3.8 mm | 5 mm |
| Peak particle velocity, blasting | 15 mm/s | 20 mm/s | 25 mm/s |
| Peak particle velocity, prolonged vibration such as pile driving | 9 mm/s | 12 mm/s | 15 mm/s |
| Overhead power line, signalling, and telecommunications: peak particle velocity | 6 mm/s | 8 mm/s | 10 mm/s |
| Same furniture: vibration amplitude | 48 µm | 64 µm | 80 µm |

A monitoring plan for the railway is included with site formation, foundation, excavation and lateral support, and any other works that would move or vibrate the railway. Records go to the MTR Corporation Limited at bi-weekly intervals, or at an agreed frequency. Works that would affect the railway are suspended when the pre-set Action limit is reached. An engineering approach with site-specific limits may be used if Appendix C is followed and accepted. More stringent values may be required where the railway already has a settlement record. PNAP APP-137 also applies to vibration and settlement control.

**SD takeaway:** If no agreed engineering limits are used, stop at 20 mm of movement, 1 in 1,000 angular distortion, 5 mm of rail cross-level, and 15 mm/s prolonged vibration.
