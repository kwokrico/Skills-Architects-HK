# APP-96 Registration of General Building Contractors and Specialist Contractors
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision September 2020 | Buildings Department

> Scope note: Who may carry out building works and street works under sections 8 to 8F of the Buildings Ordinance. It does not set design standards. Minor works contractor registration is in PNAP APP-148. The scope of each category is in Appendix A.

## Regulatory Overview
There are three registers, for general building contractors, specialist contractors, and minor works contractors, and five categories of specialized works may be carried out only by a registered specialist contractor entered in that sub-register: demolition, foundation, ground investigation field works, site formation, and ventilation. A general building contractor may carry out general building works and street works that are not in those categories, and all minor works in Part 2 of Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation, while a specialist contractor's minor works are limited to section 28(1)(b) to (ea) of that Regulation for the categories in which the contractor is registered.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Appointment and the people the contractor must have (paragraphs 6–13)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Owner's appointment | From the register that matches the category of the works. Some general building works may be carried out by more than one category; Appendix A gives those cases |
| Registration under section 8B(2) | Adequate management structure if a corporation; appropriate experience and qualifications of personnel; access to plant and resources; and an authorized signatory who understands building works and street works and the basic statutory requirements |
| Minimum key people | One authorized signatory. A corporation also has at least one director as technical director, authorised to access plant and resources, provide technical and financial support, make decisions, and supervise the authorized signatory. If that director lacks the required qualification or experience, an other officer or an authorized signatory assists |
| Who may be the authorized signatory | The individual applicant; a partner appointed by the other partners; or, for a corporation, a person appointed by the board. The technical director must be a director under the Companies Ordinance. One person may be both if both sets of requirements are met |
| Staff | Appropriate qualified staff to execute, manage, and supervise the works, in addition to those key people |
| Finding a contractor | Names are published annually in the Gazette. The authorized signatory, registration number, and expiry date are on the Buildings Department website |

**SD takeaway:** Appoint a registered specialist contractor, not a general building contractor, for demolition, foundations, ground investigation, site formation, and ventilation.
