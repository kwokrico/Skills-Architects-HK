# ADM-16 Ground Investigation Works in Scheduled Areas — Approval and Consent
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2006 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009; first issue July 1998)

> Scope note: States that ground investigation in the Scheduled Areas is building works requiring approval and consent, and how approval and consent can be processed together. It does not set borehole spacing or test methods.

## Regulatory Overview
Ground investigation in the Scheduled Areas is building works and requires approval and consent under the Buildings Ordinance. The Buildings Department will process an application for approval and an application for consent concurrently if the submission follows the two steps in paragraph 3.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Concurrent processing (paragraphs 2–3)
| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| (a) | Submit a site supervision plan together with the ground investigation plans |
| (b) | State in the covering letter that concurrent processing is requested, and submit the application for consent after the 32nd day of the submission of plans for approval |

**SD takeaway:** In a Scheduled Area, do not start ground investigation as if it were exempt works; submit the investigation plans with a site supervision plan and ask for concurrent consent after day 32.
