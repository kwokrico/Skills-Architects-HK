# APP-86 Non-loadbearing Partition Walls
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision December 2020 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 203; first issue October 1996)

> Scope note: Safety of non-loadbearing partition walls even though they are not structural elements, including demolition and the minor-works route. Design standards are those accepted under PNAP APP-53.

## Regulatory Overview
Alteration and addition works often build or demolish non-loadbearing partition walls, and the authorized person and registered structural engineer must still achieve the required safety and performance standards. Design and construction follow the relevant standards and codes, taking account of every factor that affects stiffness and stability.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Design, demolition, and minor works (paragraphs 2–4)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Follow PNAP APP-53. Account for slenderness ratio, supports, and restraints |
| Demolition | Follow PNAP APP-21. Provide precautionary measures to suit the site, use experienced workers, and supervise throughout |
| Minor works | Certain works to these walls may use the simplified requirements. See Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation and PNAP APP-147 |

**SD takeaway:** Design a non-loadbearing partition for slenderness, support, and restraint under APP-53, and demolish it only with the precautions in APP-21.
