# APP-70 Use of Plastic Sheet to Cover Scaffolding Outside Buildings
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued May 1995 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Precautions when polyvinyl sheeting is used to cover scaffolding around a building. Screens, fans, and catch platforms remain obligatory under the provisions named in paragraph 1.

## Regulatory Overview
Screens, fans, catch platforms, or similar measures are obligatory to stop objects falling outside the building boundary and to alleviate dust, under Regulation 49(1) of the Construction Sites (Safety) Regulations, Cap. 59, Regulation 3(2)(a) of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations, Cap. 123, and section 4B(2) of the Summary Offences Ordinance, Cap. 228. Full polyvinyl sheeting on scaffolding can aggravate fire spread, affect bamboo-scaffold stability in strong winds if it is not properly fixed, and obstruct ventilation and lighting of occupied buildings.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Precautions when sheeting is used (paragraph 4)
| Measure | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Use flame-retardant sheeting |
| Wind | Reinforce the scaffolding to withstand strong winds |
| Occupied buildings under renovation | Avoid obstructing natural ventilation and lighting as far as practicable |

**SD takeaway:** If the scaffold is to be sheeted, specify flame-retardant sheet, a wind-reinforced scaffold, and a layout that does not blank the windows of an occupied building.
