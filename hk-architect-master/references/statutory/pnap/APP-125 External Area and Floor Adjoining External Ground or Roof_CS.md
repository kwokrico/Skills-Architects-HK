# APP-125 External Area and Floor Adjoining External Ground or Roof
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
This revision February 2021 | Buildings Department (previously PNAP 266; first issue December 2001)

> Scope note: Accepted ways to meet sections 33(2), 33(3), and 34(2) of the Building (Construction) Regulation on water entering from external ground or a roof, and on paving outside a building. Landscaped area is excepted from the paving rule in section 33(3). It is not a waterproofing specification.

## Regulatory Overview
Sections 33(2) and 34(2) require adequate means to prevent water entering from the ground surface outside a building, or from the roof, to the adjoining floor. Section 33(3) requires the ground surface outside a building, except in a landscaped area, to be paved to a gradient that directs water into a surface water drain.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Level difference or channel (paragraph 2)
Designs accepted as complying with sections 33(2) and 34(2):

| Option | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Raised floor | Internal floor not less than 150 mm above the adjoining external ground or roof |
| Channels | Additional drainage channels, each with at least 2 drainage outlets, and the external ground or roof laid to fall at not less than 1 in 80 away from the adjoining internal floor |
| Kerb, on merits | If those designs cannot be considered, a kerb with waterproofing construction at the access point, total height 150 mm above the adjoining external ground or roof, may be considered on individual merits |

**SD takeaway:** Set the internal floor 150 mm above outside ground or roof, or fall the outside at 1 in 80 into channels that each have two outlets.

### 2. External paving (paragraph 3)
For section 33(3), paving should be laid to fall at not less than 1 in 80 to a gully trap or to drainage channels connected to a surface water drain.

**SD takeaway:** Fall external paving at not less than 1 in 80 to a gully or a channel that connects to a surface water drain.
