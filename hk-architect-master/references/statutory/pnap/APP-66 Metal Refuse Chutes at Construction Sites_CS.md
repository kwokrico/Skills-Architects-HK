# APP-66 Metal Refuse Chutes at Construction Sites
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
First issued August 1994 | Buildings Department (re-issued under the new categorization in August 2009)

> Scope note: Temporary refuse chutes assembled from old metal barrels on the outside of a building under construction. It is a site-safety note, not a design rule for the permanent refuse system.

## Regulatory Overview
External chutes made from old metal barrels are a noise nuisance and are unsafe, because weather and the impact of debris can affect their stability, and collapse hazards both workers and passers-by. Other safer means of lowering and removing debris should be adopted.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. If a chute is still used (paragraphs 4–5)
| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Assembly | Properly assembled, secured, and installed |
| Preferred construction | Chutes of other materials of sufficient strength, installed in internal areas of the site, to reduce noise |
| Supervision | Authorized persons and registered structural engineers ensure proper safety measures are in place on sites under their control |

**SD takeaway:** Do not plan debris removal by an external barrel chute; if a chute is unavoidable, put a purpose-made chute of sufficient strength inside the site and secure it.
