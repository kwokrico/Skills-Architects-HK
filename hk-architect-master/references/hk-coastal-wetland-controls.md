# Foreshore, marine works and wetland controls

Read from `hk-spatial-planning` for site feasibility and `hk-site-establishment` for marine mobilisation. Official guidance checked **6 November 2026**. Obtain the actual boundaries, published plans and conditions; proximity alone does not identify every approval required.

## Cap. 127 — Foreshore and Sea-Bed (Reclamations) Ordinance

**Trigger:** an undertaking over or upon foreshore/sea-bed, potentially including piers, intakes/outfalls and cable works as well as land formation. **Interface:** Lands Department and the statutory authorisation process; establish the project promoter and undertaking boundary.

**Action:** locate the Gazette proposal and deposited plan; identify the objection period and the actual authorisation notice before treating the marine footprint as available. Track affected interests and compensation matters with the land/legal team. A building-plan or planning approval does not establish this authorisation.

**Evidence:** Gazette notices, plan reference, objections/disposition where relevant, authorisation and project conditions. [Lands Department notices and plans](https://www.landsd.gov.hk/en/resources/gov-notices/acq/acq_2022.html) include seawater works and cable examples; [example statutory authorisation notice](https://www.landsd.gov.hk/doc/en/acq/egazette/2011/gn2936_e.pdf). These examples explain the approval evidence, not permission for a new project.

For Victoria Harbour, also screen the separate Protection of the Harbour Ordinance (Cap. 531) and its **2025 amendments**; do not infer that Cap. 127 alone settles harbour reclamation. [Government commencement notice](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202505/16/P2025051600685p.htm).

## Cap. 313 — Shipping and Port Control Ordinance

**Trigger:** construction, reclamation, dredging, boring, cable laying or other works in Hong Kong waters likely to interfere with navigation or port facilities. **Authority:** Director of Marine; identify the works promoter and marine contractor.

**Gate:** Marine Department Notice 23/2020 explains regulation 70 of Cap. 313A: give the Director **at least 14 days' written notice** before such works and comply with safety directions. This is not a universal permission process for every marine activity; events under regulation 66 have their own permission requirement.

**Action:** coordinate work limits, duration, vessels, safe navigation and information needed for a Marine Department Notice; establish any further vessel/area-specific requirements. **Evidence:** notice submission, MD correspondence/directions, published marine notice where applicable and contractor arrangements. [MD Notice 23/2020](https://www.mardep.gov.hk/en/notices/pdf/mdn20023.pdf). This notification does not replace foreshore authorisation, dumping permits or environmental permits.

## Cap. 476 — Marine Parks Ordinance

**Trigger:** site works, temporary marine access, anchoring or loading affecting a marine park/reserve. **Authority:** Country and Marine Parks Authority, through AFCD.

**Gate:** check the park/reserve designation and Cap. 476A activity controls. Some controlled activities may receive a permit under regulation 17; do not assume that an ordinary activity permit authorises dredging, reclamation or other prohibited works. A marine reserve has different restrictions from a marine park.

**Action:** overlay permanent and temporary works, vessel routes and moorings on current boundaries. Consult AFCD on the exact activity, prohibition/exception and permit route before committing to the method. **Evidence:** designation/boundary plan, written applicability response, permit and conditions where available, and environmental mitigation arrangements. [AFCD permit framework](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/country/cou_vis/cou_vis_mar/cou_vis_mar_mpp/cou_vis_mar_mpp.html); [controlled-activity permit information](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/country/cou_app/cou_app_primp/cou_app_primp.html). Separate MD, EPD and Lands requirements still apply.

## Ramsar Convention — wetlands

**Trigger:** development affecting Mai Po/Inner Deep Bay or connected wetland habitat. Ramsar is an international convention, not a numbered Hong Kong ordinance or a stand-alone building permit. [Government Ramsar overview](https://cco.gov.hk/en/project-sites/ramsar-site).

**Action:** map the Ramsar boundary, Wetland Conservation Area (WCA), Wetland Buffer Area (WBA), statutory zoning and relevant ecological connections. Engage an ecologist early; assess temporary works, drainage, water quality, disturbance and habitat impacts alongside permanent development. Establish applicable OZP Notes and current TPB guidance and independently screen Cap. 499 designated-project triggers and any protected-area/access controls.

**Evidence:** boundary overlays, planning/EIA applicability record, ecological baseline, mitigation and management commitments, authority decisions and conditions. AFCD describes WCA/WBA as controls protecting the site's ecological integrity: a generic wetland zone summary cannot decide a site's acceptability. [AFCD local wetland conservation](https://www.afcd.gov.hk/english/conservation/con_wet/con_wet_local_pro/con_wet_local_pro.html). Do not substitute a blanket buffer distance or an assumed Ramsar approval for the actual local controls.
