# Travel-distance lookup

Establish use classification, internal corridor versus balcony approach, exit arrangement and the points being measured before giving a limit. Sprinklers alone do not select a universal domestic travel distance.

For use classifications 1 and 2 on a storey with two or more protected exits, FS Code B11.3(a)(i) limits travel from the flat/guestroom exit to the nearest required staircase to **24 m**, or **45 m along a qualifying balcony approach**. Check B10.6 for balcony conditions, B11.2 for dead ends, and the other B11 provisions for the actual arrangement. Do not apply this flat-door measurement to the whole path from the remotest point inside a room.

For other occupancies or exit arrangements, consult the applicable clause rather than extending these figures. A geometric calculator cannot establish the route's legal classification.

Source checked 7 November 2026: [BD Fire Safety Code 2011 (2024 Edition), Part B](https://www.bd.gov.hk/doc/en/resources/codes-and-references/code-and-design-manuals/fs_code2011.pdf). The supporting regulation is Building (Planning) Regulations regulation 41; the detailed travel rules are in the Code.
