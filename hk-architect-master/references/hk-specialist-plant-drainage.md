# Specialist plant and land drainage gates

Read from `hk-building-services` when the brief includes pressure equipment, radiation equipment or a main watercourse. Official guidance checked **6 November 2026**. These are applicability and coordination gates; use current consolidated legislation, specialist design and actual licence conditions for a submission.

## Cap. 56 — Boilers and Pressure Vessels Ordinance

**Trigger:** proposed steam boilers, air receivers or other pressure equipment potentially within the statutory definitions. **Authority:** Labour Department, Boilers and Pressure Vessels Authority. **Duty holder:** owner; examination and operation require the appropriate statutory specialists/personnel.

**Gate:** equipment must be registered and have a valid Certificate of Fitness before use unless a documented exemption applies. EPD fuel-burning approval is a separate Cap. 311 requirement and does not establish pressure-equipment fitness.

**Architect action:** obtain the supplier's equipment classification and exemption position before fixing the plant room; coordinate access for inspection, replacement, safe operation and maintenance with the services engineer and appointed examiner. Programme registration, examination and certification before commissioning; check operator competency requirements for the actual equipment.

**Evidence:** equipment schedule, registration/exemption, Certificate of Fitness, examination and maintenance records. [LD owner's code](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/news/pdf/CoPOwners.pdf); [LD registration form notes](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/form/bpvd/BPVD-V19d-Form3notes.pdf). Do not derive universal room clearances or examination intervals from the chapter title.

## Cap. 303 — Radiation Ordinance

**Trigger:** radioactive substances or irradiating apparatus, such as X-ray or radiotherapy installations. **Authority:** Radiation Board, supported by the Department of Health's Radiation Health Division. **Duty holder:** the person undertaking the licensed activity.

**Gate:** relevant licensing is required before possession or the controlled activity, not merely before the first patient is treated. Establish which activities and apparatus require which licence and whether an exemption applies.

**Architect action:** obtain equipment, workload and adjacent-occupancy information; engage a radiation-protection specialist for shielding, controlled access and testing. Coordinate the specialist design with the licensing submission before fixing walls, doors, penetrations and occupied spaces above/below. Do not choose a standard lead thickness from this summary. Check licence implications before relocation or fit-out changes.

**Evidence:** apparatus/source schedule, shielding design, licence conditions, surveys/testing and operator arrangements. [Radiation Board licensing](https://www.rbhk.org.hk/eng/app-intro.html); [Department of Health service](https://www.dh.gov.hk/english/main/main_rh/main_rh.html).

## Cap. 446 — Land Drainage Ordinance

**Trigger:** a rural site or works affecting a main watercourse in a Drainage Authority Area. **Authority:** Director of Drainage Services as Drainage Authority.

**Gate:** establish the applicable published plan and designated main watercourse; ordinary internal drainage design and a drainage impact assessment do not establish statutory consent. Section 26 controls engineering works, filling or obstructions in a main watercourse that impede its flow: check written Drainage Authority consent before committing to a crossing, culvert, diversion or fill.

**Architect action:** overlay the watercourse and works on the surveyed site; ask the civil/drainage engineer to confirm applicability with DSD, protect maintenance access and resolve any notices or consent conditions. Do not infer a universal setback from Cap. 446.

**Evidence:** plan reference, survey, DSD correspondence, consent and conditions. [DSD guide to consent and watercourse controls](https://www.dsd.gov.hk/EN/Flood_Prevention/Land_Use_Management_and_Legislation/A_Guide_to_Land_Drainage_Ordinance/index.html); [DSD published-plan requirements](https://www.dsd.gov.hk/EN/HTML/217.html); [Cap. 446, including section 26](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap446). Check the current text and site designation before relying on a procedural deadline.
