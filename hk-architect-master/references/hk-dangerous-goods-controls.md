# Dangerous goods — Cap. 295 coordination

Read from `hk-fire-life-safety` for dangerous-goods storage, use or plant, including fuel tanks, solvents, gases and refrigerant interfaces. FSD guidance checked **6 November 2026**. Existing Category 5 circulars and PNAP APP-72 are historical/specialist sources, not the complete current licensing test.

## Trigger, authority and current regime

Identify each substance, UN number/class where applicable, concentration/form, quantity, packaging and activity (manufacture, storage, use or conveyance). Obtain safety data sheets and the maximum concurrent inventory, including temporary contractor supplies.

FSD regulates DG on land under Cap. 295, Cap. 295E and Cap. 295G, with important divisions: gases within Cap. 51 go to EMSD; Schedule 1 DG to CEDD; DG at sea/cargo terminals to Marine Department. The regime harmonised local classification and packing/marking/labelling with the IMDG Code from **31 March 2022**. Do not translate an old Category 5 label directly into a current class or exemption. [FSD authority and regulatory framework](https://es.hkfsd.gov.hk/dg/en/administration/authority/).

## Licensing and design gate

Have the operator and competent DG specialist check the current Cap. 295E schedules and Cap. 295G controls for the actual substance, quantity, premises and activity, including applicable exempt-quantity and aggregation rules. Record the basis of an exemption; neither a small container nor a supplier's description establishes it. Do not use a single litres threshold for all goods. [FSD current DG list](https://es.hkfsd.gov.hk/dg/en/administration/dg_list/); [Cap. 295E](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap295E); [Cap. 295G](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap295G).

Before fixing rooms or tanks, obtain applicable licence/design requirements and coordinate separation, fire resistance, ventilation, containment, fire protection, delivery access and compatible storage with the specialist and authority. These are design coordination topics; this reference does not prescribe universal dimensions. Confirm required licences before starting the controlled activity; changes in substance, quantity, layout or operation need a fresh applicability/licence review.

## Evidence and interfaces

Record duty holder/licensee, inventory, data sheets, classification/exemption assessment, authority correspondence, licensed layout, licence conditions and inspection/maintenance arrangements. Retain the approved maximum inventory in the operating/handover information.

- [Environmental controls](hk-environmental-site-controls.md): Cap. 403 refrigerants, Cap. 311 emissions and Cap. 354 chemical waste remain independent controls.
