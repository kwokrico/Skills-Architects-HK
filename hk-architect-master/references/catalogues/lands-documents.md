## 7. LACO circular memoranda

Legal Advisory and Conveyancing Office circulars. Each row is the schematic-design takeaway. A circular that only records a fee, a form address, or a superseded procedure is still listed so the set is complete. Do not treat a circular as a waiver of the site lease.

| LACO Circular Memorandum No. 1B (09-12-1996) | Classify the consent package against CM No. 7 early — the ASP completion-date rule differs sharply between A1 and A2 (below). Consent can start earlier on estimates, but consent will not issue until contract/precise costs arrive — keep AP cost certificates on the consent critical path. Lease-tied DDH / MLP / car-park layout / landscaping approvals remain consent blockers on a 28-day clock once LACO has checked the rest — schedule them before relying on priority. Under CM 7 residential (A1), the contractual ASP compliance date is squeezed to BC expiry + 3 months — much tighter than the CM 1 / A2 +12 months rule. Lock BC status and construction programme before promising a pre-sale launch date. Extract every consent-related Special Condition (DDH, MLP, landscape, car park, GIC, DMC) into the SD constraints list; Checklist A will test them item-by-item. Outstanding cost figure comes from the AP certificate — keep cost-to-complete current when consent is live. |
