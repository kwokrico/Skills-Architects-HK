# F1_2A legislation — coverage register

Updated **6 November 2026**. The historical filename is retained for existing links. All **35 ordinances and the Ramsar Convention** on the supplied checklist have a topic route and scoped practical guidance. This is not a claim that every provision or subsidiary instrument is covered.

## How to read coverage

- **Existing scoped guidance:** operative topic or practice-note guidance predates this update; its limits remain stated below.
- **Workflow:** applicability, responsible party/authority, coordination action and evidence, supported by linked official guidance. It is not a full consolidated-law Critical Summary.
- **Workflow + snapshot:** current coordination workflow plus a copied, dated archive summary. Check current law before quoting snapshot limits, penalties or deadlines.

A name in PNAP ADV-2 alone does not establish coverage. Read only the workflow relevant to the question, then follow its sources if a specific legal provision, exception or current numerical limit matters.
