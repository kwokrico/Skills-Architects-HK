| ADV-19 | Construction and Demolition Waste | Balance cut and fill, combine the service trenches, and specify precast or system formwork so the volume that leaves the site is decided before tender. If the job will produce more than 300,000 m³ of construction and demolition material, obtain that advice before the waste management plan is accepted. Specify the designated public filling facility for inert material and the designated landfill for non-inert waste, and require a trip ticket for every truck. Printed figures: Over 300,000 m³: The Director of Environmental Protection is prepared to advise before the plan is accepted. The Public Fill Committee of the Civil Engineering Department is also prepared to advise on management and beneficial reuse; Hours and charge: Public filling facilities are normally open 8:30 a.m. to 12:00 noon and 1:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., and are open to the public free of charge for land delivery of inert material by vehicles |
| APP-21 | Demolition Works — Measures for Public Safety | Do not apply for demolition consent until hoarding, catch platforms, propping, sealed drains, and at least one video camera are in place, and name the plant operators in the application. Use fast-track completion only where the building stands alone, has no basement to retain, and leaves no party wall that still needs shoring. Printed figures: Before consent: Those measures, and the documents in Appendix B, are in place. Consent may be refused under section 16(3)(b), (ba), or (bc) if documents are missing, or under section 16(3)(bb) if the protective measures are inadequate; Video: At least one camera per site, at locations agreed by the authorized person and the engineers and shown on the demolition plan, recording the whole process including debris movement and sequence. The contractor keeps the records for at least 14 days; Plant operators: Meet regulation 9(3) of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations. For regulation 9(3)(b), the Hong Kong Institute of Construction course "Demolition of Building Course for Plant Operators", or an equivalent certificate. Particulars accompany the consent application under regulation 31, or consent may be refused under section 16(3)(b). A change of operator is notified within 7 days; Fire and explosion: Remove flammable goods. Regulation 6 of the Building (Demolition Works) Regulations; Completion: Form BA14A within 14 days, or Form BA14B where the fast-track criteria are met; Drains and street names: Tell the Drainage Services Department when drains are sealed (regulation 3(2)(b)). Tell the Director of Highways if street name plates are fixed to the building (section 32); How to use it: State compliance on the demolition plans. Submit Form BA14B with colour photos of the site, adjacent land or streets, and all elevations of adjoining buildings, and a mark-up of photo directions. A reply is generally within 14 days if not audited, or notice of audit within 14 days and a reply generally within 28 days; Dangerous-building shores: Preferably structural steel of Grade 250 or above, to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011 (2023 Edition) or equivalent. Agree dead-shore positions with the Existing Buildings Division before erection. Temporary shores are removed after demolition and replaced by permanent support where necessary. Adjacent buildings to be demolished together should start together; Minor works before demolition, same project team: Form MW01 not less than 7 days before those works, submitted with the demolition-plan application. The plans show the minor works and state that protective measures follow the Technical Guidelines on the Minor Works Control System. Form MW02 goes with Form BA14A or BA14B |
| APP-170 | Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings 2004 | A demolition with a geotechnical concern needs the registered geotechnical engineer, and the scaffold catchfan is designed for strong wind and typhoon. Prop for a machine up to 5,800 kg to Table 3.4, and keep a temporary platform under a cantilever unless it is removed by cut and lift. Printed figures: Roles: Statutory role of the registered geotechnical engineer where demolition has a geotechnical concern, and the minor-works regime. The code is not intended to cover demolition works under the Minor Works Control System (Type G is named in the amended clause 1.1); Table 3.4: Propping requirement for light-weight mechanical plant of maximum 5,800 kg; 3.8.8: Precautionary measures at the interface between two adjacent demolition or construction sites; 3.8.9: Safety measures for floor openings and free edges; 3.8.10: Control on conveying debris through floor openings; 3.10.7: Disposal of construction and demolition material; 4.2.4(C) and Figure 4.5: Method and procedures for demolition of an exterior column; Appendix F and G figures: Temporary platforms are required unless the cantilevered structures are demolished by cut and lift or a similar technique as stated in clause 3.5.1(B) |
