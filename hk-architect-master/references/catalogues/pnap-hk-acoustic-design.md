## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| ADV-17 | Noise Annoyance Prevention - Design of Pump Room and Ventilation System | Do not place a pump room against a flat; buffer it, give the party wall Sound Transmission Class 50, and isolate the pumps and the pipes. Keep large fans and air-cooled chillers off facades that face flats or schools, and design the system to 5 dB(A) under the Technical Memorandum acceptable level. Printed figures: Airborne, pumps: Use quieter pumps and motors. If the total sound power level of the pumps exceeds 100 dB(A), consider a higher insulating capacity than the figures below; Airborne, fabric: Walls and ceilings or floors adjoining noise-sensitive receivers (residential flats and school premises) with a minimum Sound Transmission Class of 50; Doors: Doors facing sensitive areas with a minimum Sound Transmission Class of 25; Target: 5 dB(A) below the relevant acceptable noise level in paragraph 2 of that Technical Memorandum |
