## Operative rules

| Instrument | Operative rule |
| --- | --- |
| Rules of the Institute | Membership classes, HKIA/FHKIA titles, Corporate Member 30% director or voting-rights threshold, voting limited to Fellows and Members, Council/general-meeting machinery, and disciplinary powers including ARB notification. Rule 63 includes the Code and Conditions of Engagement within “Rules.” |
| Code of Professional Conduct | Four Principles for Members (including Graduates); written appointment; taking over another Member’s work; invited consultancy submission guidelines; promotion rules (no fee publicity; no “best/largest/most”). |

These are Institute instruments, not Cap. 408 registration. Cap. 408 and the ARB Code remain in the professional-conduct subskill body.
