## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| PNAP | Subject | Operative rule |

| APP-67 | Energy Efficiency of Buildings | For a commercial or hotel submission on or after 31 December 2025, design the tower envelope to not more than 20 W/m2 and the podium to not more than 40 W/m2, and lodge the summary sheet before consent. A sunshade of not more than 1.5 m can stay out of gross floor area and site coverage if it genuinely cuts the thermal transfer value, and one over 750 mm needs a calculation to prove that. Printed figures: Podium height where site coverage varies under PNAP APP-132: The notional height of the podium for this calculation can be regarded as 20 m above the mean street level. It may be demarcated lower than 20 m to simplify the computation; Open-front shop on ground floor: Disregarded if usable floor area is not more than 50 m2 and any air-conditioning is separate from the main building. If usable floor area is more than 50 m2, a notional calculation based on clear-glass shopfront may be acceptable to demonstrate paragraph 3; First building-plan submission: Need not include the information and calculations required by regulation 5; Before consent: An Overall Thermal Transfer Value Summary Sheet in Appendix A, under regulation 10 of the Building (Administration) Regulations, calculated to the code; Before occupation permit, or Form BA14 for alterations: Record the final values for external walls and roofs, and the shading coefficient of glass, on the general building plans. Submit a report with calculations, Forms OTTV1 to OTTV4, record plans, test certificates or published specifications, and the final summary sheet; 1.5 m or less, and a genuine sunshade that assists in reducing the value: Not accountable for gross floor area, and not included in site coverage, under regulation 6; Over 750 mm: Submit a quantitative assessment that the shade reduces the value; Not more than 750 mm: Regarded as not obstructing prescribed windows; Over a street: Not allowed under section 31(1), unless an exemption is justified by special circumstances |
| APP-152 | Sustainable Building Design Guidelines | On a site of 20,000 m2 or more with a tower over 60 m, provide 20% and 33.3% permeability on the two planes, with intervening spaces at least 7.5 m, or 15 m between facades. On a street under 15 m, keep the building 7.5 m off the centreline up to 15 m above the street, or use a 4.5 m podium garden and the 45-degree line. A site of 1,000 m2 or more needs 20% greenery overall, of which 10% is in the primary zone, rising to 30% and 15% at 20,000 m2. Printed figures: Height of the tallest building: Site under 20,000 m2 with projected facade 60 m or more, each plane; 60 m or less: 20%; Over 60 m: 20%; Corridor: No part of the building within 7.5 m of the street centreline, so the ventilation section is at least 15 m by 15 m. Where the street level varies, keep that section along the full frontage; Podium garden: A cross-ventilated communal podium garden with clear height not less than 4.5 m, and no part of the building, up to 15 m above street level, protruding above a 45-degree plane whose base is at street level on the opposite boundary; Above 15 m: Structure may build over the setback. An uncovered setback may have a canopy complying with regulation 10 of the Building (Planning) Regulations. The ground-level setback under such structure or canopy may be exempt from gross floor area if it is common parts, accessible to occupants, and without commercial activities; Minor projections: Not more than 600 mm from the external wall, clear height not less than 2.5 m. Single-storey footbridges may cross the setback. Columns may stand in the setback as in Figure C2; Low building: Exempt from the whole or part of the setback to a street if building height is less than 2 times the mean width of that street. Height is from the mean street level to the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor; 1,000 m2 to 20,000 m2: 10%; 20,000 m2 or more: 15% |
| APP-156 | Design and Construction Requirements for Energy Efficiency of Residential Buildings | Hold the residential wall at or below 12.5 W/m2 and the roof at or below 3.5 W/m2, with glass transmittance at least 50% and external reflectance at most 20%. Put the compliance statement on the first general building plans, the summary sheets before consent, and the energy report with the occupation permit. Keep a sunshade within 750 mm of the wall if it is not to be assessed, and do not project it over a street without an exemption. Printed figures: Wall residential thermal transfer value: Not exceeding 12.5 W/m2 (previously 14 W/m2); Roof residential thermal transfer value: Not exceeding 3.5 W/m2 (previously 4 W/m2); Overall thermal transfer value of residents’ recreational facilities in a tower: Not exceeding 20 W/m2 (previously 21 W/m2), calculated to the 1995 code; Same, in a podium: Not exceeding 40 W/m2 (previously 50 W/m2); Visible light transmittance of envelope glass: Not less than 50%. This applies only to glass in the prescribed windows under regulations 30 and 31 of the Building (Planning) Regulations; External reflectance of that glass: Not more than 20%; Podium and tower dates: The revised overall-thermal-transfer limits in paragraph 6(b) apply to new building plans or a major revision submitted on or after 31 December 2025; After general building plan approval, before consent: Summary sheets in Appendices A and B for paragraphs 6(a), 6(b), and 6(d); With the occupation permit: An energy efficiency report: residential-thermal-transfer calculations on Appendix III of the Guidelines; overall-thermal-transfer forms OTTV 1 to 4 if applicable; record plans; test certificates or published specifications; final summary sheets; and the natural-ventilation extent in Appendix VI of the Guidelines; Over 750 mm from the external wall: Quantitative assessment for the Building Authority. Projection over 1.5 m from the external walls is not envisaged; Prescribed windows: A sunshade projecting not more than 750 mm is regarded as not obstructing a prescribed window; Over a street: Not allowed under section 31(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, unless an exemption is justified |
