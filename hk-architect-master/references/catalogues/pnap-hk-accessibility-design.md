## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| APP-41 | Barrier Free Access | Draw the barrier-free route on the general building plans, and do not omit a ramp to the common parts unless control, consent, and any ground-beam constraint are all demonstrated. For an elderly-friendly flat, keep a 1,200 mm turning circle in the bathroom, an 850 mm door, and a shower of at least 1,500 mm by 900 mm, and set common handrails between 810 mm and 900 mm. Printed figures: Bathroom: Clear turning space not less than 1,200 mm diameter. Doorway not less than 850 mm. Shower not less than 1,500 mm by 900 mm, with a folding seat. Grab bars. Slip-resistant floors. No shiny finishes such as marble or glazed tile; Common doors: Opening force not more than 28 N for an external door and 18 N for an internal door where a closer is fitted. Lever handles. Automatic doors as far as practicable; Handrails to corridors, steps, and stairs: Top of rail between 810 mm and 900 mm above finished floor. Timber or a plastic-coated surface; Lift: Clear depth not less than 1.5 m. Lobby controls or buttons at 1,050 mm above finished floor, easy to operate, with a luminous contrast of at least 30%. Buttons in the car back-lit. A folding seat or a lean rail in the car, except a fireman's lift. Audio indication |
