## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| PNAP | Subject | Operative rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| APP-65 | Natural Lighting to Staircases | Give each common-stair storey clear glazing of at least one-tenth of the enclosure plan area, onto open air if the building is 12 storeys or less, or onto a 2.3 m by 4.5 m uncovered space if it is taller. If the stair cannot face the required open space, provide 30 lux permanent lighting and 2 lux emergency lighting, and write the maintenance duty into the Deed of Mutual Covenant. Printed figures: Building not exceeding 12 storeys: Windows face an uncovered space complying with "open air" in regulation 2(1); Building exceeding 12 storeys: Windows face an uncovered and unenclosed space with a base width of not less than 2.3 m and a length at right angles to the base of not less than 4.5 m. The full width of any adjoining service lane may be included; Scissor stair, end wall of a landing: Window centrally positioned, minimum width 1.3 m. Alternatives will be considered; Scissor stair, side wall of a landing or flight: Minimum width 650 mm, not less than two windows each storey, and the separating wall between flights reduced so the back flight has adequate light; Normal lighting: Permanent artificial lighting of 30 lux minimum at floor level; Emergency: Automatically activated emergency lighting complying with the Code of Practice for Minimum Fire Service Installations and Equipment, providing not less than 2 lux horizontal illuminance at floor level |
| APP-98 | Lighting and Ventilation for Bathrooms and Lavatories in Domestic Buildings | An internal domestic bathroom can omit the Regulation 36 window only with 5 air changes per hour to outside air, plus a permanent opening of at least 1/20 of the room floor area, and its own fan and duct unless management is in place. Printed figures: Mechanical ventilation: Artificial lighting and mechanical ventilation producing 5 air changes per hour whenever the room is in use. Air is changed with the outside. Ventilation ducting with access for cleaning is acceptable; Permanent ventilation: Additional permanent ventilation to the open air under Regulation 2, to the external air under Regulation 31, or to another room whose window meets the area requirement for the combined rooms. A duct with cleaning access, or an aperture in a wall or door, permanently open or protected with louvres, of minimum size 1/20 of the floor area of the room; Duct access, unless the duct is demountable or replaceable: At least one access door or panel every 4 metres, at an easily accessible location, at every change of direction, and adjacent to turning vanes or dampers. Minimum 150 mm by 225 mm where the duct is 150 mm by 150 mm or above; 100 mm by 225 mm where the duct is smaller; Water heating: Regulation 35A and PNAP APP-27, where applicable |
| APP-130 | Lighting and Ventilation - Performance-based Approach | Size a habitable-room window for 8% vertical daylight factor and 1.5 natural air changes, and a kitchen for 4% plus a mechanical 5 air changes. The daylight cone is 100 degrees and no longer than the facade height from the lowest window head to the main-roof parapet. An office conversion that cannot daylight the floors needs 40% in Energy Use and Indoor Environmental Quality under BEAM Plus before the modification of regulations 30 and 31. Printed figures: Habitable room: 8%; Kitchen: 4% |
