## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| APP-126 | Erection of Signboards | Keep 5.8 m under a sign over a carriageway, 3.5 m over a footpath, and 2.4 m laterally from the next projecting sign if it stands more than 600 mm off the wall. A sign over 3 m on a roof must not enclose floor space unless that space is counted in gross floor area and site coverage. Printed figures: Within 1 m of the outer edge of a footpath, or projecting over a carriageway: Not less than 5.8 m beneath every part; Over a footpath: Not less than 3.5 m, or not less than 2.5 m if the projection over the footpath is not more than 600 mm; Over a tramway: Not less than 7 m beneath every part; Street centre: No portion within 1.5 m, measured horizontally, of a vertical line from the nearest point on the centre line. If another signboard is opposite, not less than 3 m clear between them horizontally; Projection more than 600 mm from the external wall: Not less than 2.4 m laterally from any part of an adjacent projecting signboard, including guys, frames, and struts, throughout the height of the building, and not less than 1.2 m from the common boundary of an adjoining lot; Wholly within the site: Still meet the applicable paragraph 3 requirements. A wall or projecting signboard that exceeds Appendix B on dimensions or projection is a building, including gross floor area and site coverage; Higher than 3 m on the ground or roof: Must not create enclosed or partly enclosed floor space unless that space is in the gross floor area and site coverage, or is occupied by machinery or ductwork |
| APP-147 | Minor Works Control System | Count 58 Class I, 68 Class II, and 61 Class III items, and take the size limits from Schedule 1 of the Building (Minor Works) Regulation. A Class I item, such as a stair between two floors, still needs an authorized person and a commencement notice; a household Class III item is a contractor’s combined notice and certificate. Printed figures: I: 58; II: 68; III: 61; Professional ceases on Class I: Standard form to the Building Authority within 7 days; Contractor ceases on Class I: Specified form to the authorized person or registered inspector within 7 days; that person submits it to the Building Authority within 7 days of receipt |
| APP-148 | Minor Works Contractors Registration | Class III items can be done by a registered individual; other classes need a registered company for that type and class. A company needs an authorized signatory, and a corporation also needs a technical director who is a director, and that person cannot hold the same key role in another firm. Printed figures: Registered Minor Works Contractor (Individual): Section 10(1)(a): an individual self-employed worker, for items of class III minor works; Registered Minor Works Contractor (Company): Section 10(1)(b): a corporation, sole proprietorship, or partnership, for types and classes; Individual, section 11(2): Appropriate qualifications and experience; ability to personally carry out the class III items; a recognised training course on class III minor works; suitability; Company, section 12(5): Qualifications and experience of key personnel; access to plant and resources; adequate management structure if a corporation; persons appointed to act understand the works and the basic statutory requirements; suitability |
| APP-149 | Authorized Signatory for Minor Works | Name one authorized signatory on Form MW01 or MW03, and give 7 days’ notice before changing to another signatory of the same contractor. A stand-in can supervise minor works for at most 14 days, and 30 days in total, and cannot sign the specified forms. Printed figures: Forms: The signatory of Forms MW01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 10, 11, and 12 is responsible for the contractor’s duties. The signatory of Form MW01 or 03 is that person; Only one signatory on the registration: A change needs an application to add the new signatory under PNRC 38 or 69, and approval; More than one: Notify on Appendix A to PNRC 72, countersigned by the authorized person if the works are Class I, not less than 7 days before the change; Notice: Appendix B to PNRC 72, countersigned by the authorized person for Class I, at least 7 days before the absence. Sudden illness or accident: documentary proof within 3 days; Period: Maximum 14 days, extendable only with proof such as illness, and 30 days in total; Beyond 30 days: Change to another signatory of the same company, or add a new one |
| APP-155 | Signboard Validation Scheme | An unauthorized signboard erected on or after 2 September 2013 cannot use this scheme, and a validated one must be revalidated every 5 years. A large unauthorized signboard that matches Class I needs an authorized person and, unless it is a specified construction, a registered structural engineer, with structural calculations on Form SC01. Printed figures: Date: Erected before 2 September 2013; Cycle: Safety inspection and certification every 5 years, or removal |
