## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| PNAP | Subject | Operative rule |

| ADM-1 | Practice Notes in Force | Cite the current ADM, APP, or ADV number from the website list, and read across parts when a design topic is split. Do not lock a submission to a printed index; confirm the note still in force on the Buildings Department website before design freeze. |
| ADM-2 | Centralised Processing of Building Plans | Put fire-service installations on the sets referred to the Fire Services Department, and put the Development Schedule on the set referred to Lands, before the first general building plan. Use day 45 to confirm whether the submission is fundamentally acceptable, and treat the written 60-day disapproval list as the list that should not grow on a normal resubmission. Do not treat a concession as granted until Form BA16 is approved and the BD 106 conditions are written onto the amendment plans before the completion certificate. Tabulate every gross-floor-area concession on the general building plans, and file the Appendix H summary with the occupation-permit application. From 2 July 2024, collect structural approval and consent results electronically; do not wait for a posted hard copy. Printed figures: Paper: Too few sets delays referral. Authorized persons complete Appendix A before general building plans. Structural sets follow PNAP ADM-8; Tung Chung Cable Car Route Protection Area: Building plans are referred to Ngong Ping 360 Limited (Appendix I); Disapproval: Adverse comments within the Buildings Ordinance that are a fundamental issue under ADM-19 are disapproval items. Other comments are conveyed and are not disapproval items; Fire services, paper: Copy the covering letter to the Fire Services Department. Mark the two sets (three if Cap. 502, Cap. 572, or Cap. 636 applies) in bold at the lower right, showing the installations referred to in section 16(1)(b)(ii); Uncollected FSD plans: Collect paper plans within 3 months of the notification or the Fire Services Department may dispose of them; FSD comment groups: (a) reasons for refusal of a certificate under section 16(1)(b); (b) strongly recommended practice, which if ignored may lead to abatement under the Fire Services Ordinance, Cap. 95; (c) optional good practice; Lease, paper: A set of general building plans with the Development Schedule and coloured plans, plus CAD or BIM files on DVD-ROM where a computer is used for areas (Lands Administration Office Practice Note 3/2018), is referred to the District Lands Office; Pre-submission: Discuss a specific proposition before formal submission. For a complicated site, a presentation, with a 3-D model if appropriate, may follow submission if the Chief Professional Officer requests or agrees; Day 45: On a new-building submission, ask the case Building Surveyor or Structural Engineer whether the submission is fundamentally acceptable, and discuss non-conformities before a disapproval letter; Resubmission: Where a submission is fundamentally unacceptable and the disapproval items were notified in writing within the statutory 60 days, new disapproval items will not, in normal circumstances, be added to that proposal on resubmission; Form: Each exemption or modification on Form BA16, with the special circumstances and why it is justified; Conditions: May be imposed under section 42 and issued on Form BD 106; On the plans: Incorporate the modification conditions in later amendment plans before the certificate under regulation 25 of the Building (Administration) Regulations. Sample tables are in Appendix A3 of PNAP ADV-33; At completion: Submit a checklist of valid Forms BD 106 with that regulation 25 certificate (sample at Appendix F); Title: Approval does not confer title to land or waive a lease or licence (Buildings Ordinance section 14(2)). Secure adjoining owners' consent before works on adjoining lots, and comply with other authorities; Structural results: From 2 July 2024, result letters for approval and consent of structural works are issued electronically even in reply to paper submissions, and are no longer posted. Structural works here include excavation and lateral support, ground investigation in the scheduled areas, foundation, and all structural works, excluding alteration and addition and railway consultation submissions |
| ADM-3 | Emergency Situations Telephone Numbers for Use Outside Office Hours | Before works start, lodge a working out-of-hours number and update it when it changes so an emergency on site can reach the responsible professional. Printed figures: How to update: Letter in Appendix A to the Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei (fax 2625 0437); Personal data: Provision is voluntary; access and correction via the Registration Unit at 2626 1616 |
| ADM-4 | Priority | If the project is a new building in one of the listed categories, ask for priority with the first plan submission; do not rely on it for alteration and addition work unless the pollution exception applies. Priority is a processing favour that can be withdrawn if the project stalls, and it does not move a land transaction. |
| ADM-5 | Submissions to the Buildings Department | Lodge paper plans at the counter that matches the submission type, within Monday-to-Friday 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, folded as in Appendix A so the statutory clock can be time-stamped. Do not take an electronic pack to these counters; follow ADM-17 and APP-60 for when the processing clock starts. Printed figures: New building developments and alteration and addition works, except works in response to a statutory order, notice, direction, or an advisory letter related to building or slope safety: Receipt and Dispatch Counter, office of New Buildings Divisions, 7/F, 14 Taikoo Wan Road, Taikoo Shing, Hong Kong; Other submissions: General Enquiry and Receipt Counter, Buildings Department Headquarters, G/F, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon |
| ADM-9 | Colouring of Plans | Colour the approval copy to this legend, and repeat the same identification on every set of amendment plans. Leave shafts and light-wells uncoloured, wash large areas lightly, and put a colour key on every set. |
| ADM-10 | Imaging Standards for Plans | Keep sheets to A0 or smaller, hold the stated borders, and reserve 90 mm × 150 mm at the lower right of every plan for the approval stamp. Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish materials; hatch or annotate them so the imaged record remains readable. Prepare a separate A-size plan index per plan category, and asterisk the latest approved sheets that match the completed building, before the occupation-permit application. Printed figures: A0: 1189 mm × 841 mm; A1: 841 mm × 594 mm; A2: 594 mm × 420 mm; A3: 420 mm × 297 mm; A4: 297 mm × 210 mm |
| ADM-11 | Change of Address | If the practice address changes during a project, file Form BA 24 within 14 days so correspondence and statutory notices still reach the registered professional. Printed figures: Time: Within 14 days; Form: Form BA 24 (see PNAP APP-60); Paper: Registration Unit, Buildings Department Headquarters, North Tower, West Kowloon Government Offices, 11 Hoi Ting Road, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon |
| ADM-14 | Minor Amendments to Plans and Specified Forms | Agree the minor amendment with the case officer first, and return substitute sheets or the taken-away set within two working days, or the submission may be disapproved. If a minor amendment changes gross floor area or a hotel back-of-house exemption, send the extra annotated set and a Development Schedule to Lands at the same time. Printed figures: Missed date: If plans or forms for routes (b), (c), or (d) are not returned or not submitted as a Revised Submission by the agreed date, disapproval may follow under BO sections 16(1)(i) and/or 16(1)(c) because prescribed plans and/or required specified forms have not been submitted |
| ADM-15 | Submission of Site Formation Proposals | Freeze slope layout, associated drainage, and the size of retaining structures as Part A before, or with, the structural details in Part B, and mark the sets clearly. Keep a small retaining structure, an exempted-house platform, or slope drainage without a major retaining structure as one submission. If the formation includes piling, add the APP-18 and APP-137 monitoring package to the submission. Printed figures: A: Layout and details of slope works and associated drainage, and the general layout and size of earth retaining structures, if any, as per Building (Administration) Regulation 8(1)(bb) |
| ADM-17 | Submission of Plans and Documents in Electronic Format | Send plans through the Electronic Submission Hub as a single signed set with an electronic plan index, and keep later amendments on the same channel. A Hub lodgement at the end of Saturday starts the statutory period on Sunday; do not assume the clock waits for the next working day. A Hub BA10 can support a 1-day commencement notice, but the supervision-plan notice remains 7 days. Printed figures: Electronic Submission Hub, https://esh.bd.gov.hk: Plan submissions. Operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except maintenance and unexpected downtime; Electronic Forms Submission System, www.bd.gov.hk: Certain specified forms (PNAP APP-60); Registration and Minor Works Management System, https://rmwms.bd.gov.hk: Certain specified forms and supporting documents (PNAP APP-60). Launched 29 May 2026; Example: Saturday 23:59 via the Hub: Counting starts on Sunday; Example: Sunday 00:00 via the Hub: Counting starts on Monday |
| ADM-19 | Building Approval Process | Lock the general building plan layout first; later structural and drainage plans must match it, and items outside the curtailed check are still the designer's duty before consent. For a new building submitted after 31 March 2023, obtain Stage II approval of the general building plan before superstructure consent, and Stage III approval before the occupation permit. Clear a novel structure or a concession strategy with a concept drawing before detailed design, and keep the area-calculation file in step with every amendment that changes area. After first consent, minor amendments listed as eligible can proceed without a fresh consent only if the regulation 33(1) modification is in place and the record is in the site office, and they still need approval before the occupation permit. A two-storey general-storage warehouse with no basement, compartments within 10,500 m² and 28,000 m³, and a complying emergency vehicular access can be lodged for approval and consent together and is undertaken in 45 days. Printed figures: Letter of assurance: A written pre-submission enquiry is normally answered within 45 days. Use it for a new structural theory, material, system, sophisticated design, or unconventional method; BIM: Follow the Guidelines for Using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plan Submission and PNAP ADV-34. Submitting the model permits Lands and Planning to extract the features listed in paragraphs 26 and 27; Alteration and addition, drainage, and secondary structural elements (paragraph 34): Concurrent application if there is no foundation other than spread footing excavated not more than 4.5 m, no significant geotechnical content, no precautionary works that must be finished before commencement, and all regulation 8 plans plus consent documents are submitted. Curtain-wall certificates under PNAP APP-37 may follow before the occupation permit; Simple alteration and addition, including simple drainage (paragraph 35): Processed within 30 days if the works do not involve the structure and do not affect fundamental issues, or only minor changes that comply are confirmed, and no referral is needed except to the Fire Services Department; Two-storey warehouse (Appendix I): Not more than two storeys, no basement, general storage, Class A, B, or C site, emergency vehicular access complying with regulation 41D and the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings, no compartment over 28,000 m³ or 10,500 m². Concurrent approval and consent within 45 days, and bearing-stratum inspection within one working day of notification. Submit via the Efficiency Office One Stop Centre or direct to the Buildings Department, including general building, structural, drainage, demolition, site supervision, and hoarding plans and the consent application; Repair of curtain wall, window wall, or cladding where the structural system is not altered (paragraphs 37–38): Within 30 days. Minor-works items 1.48, 1.60, 1.61, 2.8, 2.48, 3.6, and 3.31 may instead use the simplified requirements. Like-for-like replacement of glass panes, sealants, setting blocks, and cladding panels does not need structural analysis of the replacement piece or a check of the parent structure; Form BA8A: The consent application takes effect on the date of approval, or of deemed approval. It is not processed if the plans are not approved. If approval is given but not with consent, the 28 days under regulation 32(3) run from the date of approval |
| ADM-20 | Central Data Bank | Do not specify a product merely because a supplier asks to be listed; only a past accepted project, a recognised pile type, or an audited APP-13 schedule puts an item on the bank. Quote a Central Data Bank reference only when the report is identical to the listed one and still meets the current standard; attach any newer supplementary test. Printed figures: Non-structural: Taken from Schedules of Building Materials and Products submitted under PNAP APP-13. Some items are randomly audited |
| ADM-21 | Site Parameters - Documentary Proof | General building plans may proceed on a provisional lease or an old approved plan, but superstructure consent waits for a District Survey Office lease plan or an authorized land surveyor's plan. Round site area to 0.1 m² below 2,000 m² and to 1 m² at or above 2,000 m², and resolve any clash between the lease plan and the land survey plan before superstructure consent. Printed figures: Conflict with a weaker document: A lease plan or land survey plan is generally preferred. See Buildings Ordinance section 14(2); Coordinates and dimensions on the survey plan: To 0.001 m, under the Code of Practice issued under the Land Survey Ordinance (Cap. 473); Area rounding: 5 up, 4 down: nearest 0.1 m² if the area is under 2,000 m²; nearest 1 m² if the area is 2,000 m² or above; Copies: Two sets of a land survey plan. One is referred to the District Lands Office for the District Survey Office, with form SMF-0043, a survey report, and a survey record plan (Lands Department Practice Note 2/2013). Lands Administration Office Practice Note 4/2008 requires the authorized person to ensure the works do not encroach on government land |
| ADM-22 | Withdrawal and Resubmission | Use a written withdrawal and resubmission, with reasons, only to restart the statutory clock, and plan on no more than two uses for the same submission. |
| ADM-23 | Self-certification System for Plan Submission of Simple Structural Works | Prestressed work, a retaining structure, or anything that changes overall stability is outside this fast track. A three-storey house on this track needs spans at or under 8 m, storey height at or under 4.5 m, no basement, and a shallow foundation no deeper than 3 m with the slope and wall clearances in Category E. Lodge simple structural plans on the Electronic Submission Hub if the 20-day first-submission time is needed, and appoint the independent checking engineer before the submission date for Categories C to E. Printed figures: First submission, resubmission, or major revision: 20 days; Amendment, including Type II works applied for fast-track consent: 10 days |
| ADV-33 | Essential Information in Plan Submissions | The first general building plans carry the site class against streets of not less than 4.5 m, the plot ratio and site coverage, the exit widths, and the compartment diagram; deferral of prescribed information needs a statement on those plans. Keep curtain-wall, balcony, and cladding details, and the critical prescribed-window sums, for the amendment before superstructure consent, and say so on the first submission if they are deferred. A foundation submission names the system, the code edition, and the effect on adjoining buildings and any stream or railway, and a site in a Scheduled Area or over a nullah needs the extra sets listed in Appendix B1. Printed figures: Plans: Floor and roof plans, elevations with street information, sections with the site and street profile, a block plan, a key plan, and diagrams for plot ratio, site coverage, and open space. Foundations on these plans are indicative only. Gross-floor-area, site-coverage, usable-floor-area, usable-floor-space, and compartmentation diagrams use the colour coding in Table 1 of the Guideline for Using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plans Submission 2019, drawn over the corresponding floor plans; Site: Dimensions and area, including service lanes, streets, surrender, dedication, lease special areas, and non-building areas. For a Class B or C site, the percentage of the boundary abutting specified streets not less than 4.5 m wide, and the width of the narrowest part of those streets; Density: Accountable, disregarded, and exempted areas, areas under the 10% gross-floor-area cap, and areas subject to pre-requisites but not that cap. Overall plot ratio and site coverage, the mean street level of the lowest specified street, the mean height of the roof over the highest usable floor space, and the 15 m level for full site coverage on the elevation and section. A summary of gross-floor-area concessions. Details for PNAP APP-152. Car parking, including electric-vehicle charging locations and sizes, and any above-ground car park to be regarded as underground; Fire service access: Fireman’s lifts and firefighting and rescue stairways, with passage measurements at critical locations more than 44 m (open-plan direct line) and 59 m (actual passage). Distance from the ground-floor fire-service access point to the fireman’s lift or stairway where it is more than 17 m. An emergency vehicular access plan and the major-façade length; Means of escape: An occupant-capacity table for every room, compartment, and storey, including the ground floor, with usable floor area. A table of required and provided numbers and widths of exit doors and exit routes, and discharge-value calculations. Where staircases are less than 7 m apart, the 6 m separation at critical locations. The 48 m horizontal distance between staircases at a critical location for a situation over 47 m. Travel distance measured where it is more than 47 m horizontal. The 30° rule shown where two or more exit doors form an angle of less than 33°. For a place of public entertainment, at least one half of the perimeter of the building having those premises, with exit routes from each tier direct to two or more thoroughfares; Fire resisting construction: Compartment diagram and area and volume calculations. A table of fire resistance ratings. The 6 m separation for an unprotected external wall and opening of a required staircase and its protected lobby. Fixed-light percentage where it exceeds 24% under clause C9.7 of the Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings 2011. Smoke-outlet spacing where it exceeds 29 m, with the total area against the required area; Light, air, and open space: Prescribed-window calculations are column II, for a critical case where the surplus window area is less than 10% of the required provision. Open-space area, disposition, diagram, and calculations are column I, under regulation 25 and the Second Schedule; Before superstructure consent (column II): Standard details such as gas flue, utility platform, balcony, air-conditioning platform, curtain wall, non-structural prefabricated external wall, projecting window, cladding, sunken slab, acoustic window, acoustic fin, vertical greening, protective barrier, top-hung window, accessible toilet, and tactile warning strips. For a sunken slab, only a typical section showing the minimum storey height under Building (Planning) Regulation 24 is required. Usable-floor-area diagrams for sanitary fitments on podium and non-typical floors, and for means of escape on those floors. Designed imposed loads on the floor plans. Prescribed-window area calculations for critical situations, that is a surplus window area of less than 10%; Appendix A2: A checklist of typical modifications and exemptions, including projections over a street under section 31(1) of the Buildings Ordinance, exclusion of projections from plot ratio and site coverage under regulations 20 and 21, and the gross-floor-area exemptions the checklist ties to regulation 23(3)(a) and the related practice notes. Calculations for an area to be exempted are shown on the plans. Appendix A3 is the sample table of modifications granted or applied for, attached to Form BA16; Foundation: Appendix B1. The synopsis states the foundation type, design codes and edition, material grades, geotechnical parameters, groundwater, and the load path into the founding stratum, plus a summary of effects on adjoining buildings, land, streets, utilities, slopes, and retaining walls. Extra sets when the works involve Scheduled Areas, a railway protection area, the Designated Area of Northshore Lantau, slopes or retaining structures or deep excavation or a disused tunnel, a culvert, nullah, or stream course (3 sets of plans), Chek Lap Kok Airport, a structure in, over, under, or upon a street, a highway structure, a seawall, reclamation or a pier, or a public drain or water main; Excavation and lateral support: Appendix B2; Superstructure: Appendix B3; Curtain wall: Appendix B4, with sample embed details in Appendix B12; Glass balustrade: Appendix B5, with sample drawings in Appendix B8; Metal cladding: Appendix B6, with sample drawings in Appendix B9; Metal ceiling, grille, or louvre: Appendix B7, with sample drawings in Appendix B10; Suspended duct and fan frames: Sample drawings in Appendix B11 for supporting frames of a suspended horizontal air duct, axial fan, cabinet fan, and air handling unit inside a building; Drainage: Appendix C1, typical modifications and exemptions in Appendix C2, and sample drawings in Appendix C3 |
| ADV-34 | Building Information Modelling (BIM) | The approved document is the prescribed plan; if the model and the sheet disagree, the sheet governs, so generate the sheet from the model and do not redraw it by hand. A general building plan model follows the general-building-plan guideline and ADM-19; a foundation, drainage, or superstructure model follows the separate statutory-plan guideline. Use the model to show area sums, escape and compartments, and the load path, and add a phasing video only as a supplement to the plans. Printed figures: General building plans: PNAP ADM-19, the “Guidelines for using Building Information Modelling in General Building Plans Submission”, and the sample drawings and BIM models on the Buildings Department website; Video: A supplementary file in Windows Media Video (wmv) or Audio Video Interleave (avi), playable in Windows Media Player 11 or above |

Section 4 and regulation 23; BA5: Application for approval of plans of building works and/or street works, and certificate of preparation. Section 14(1)(a) and regulations 18A and 29; BA6: Stability certificate. Regulation 18; BA8: Consent to commence building works or street works. Section 14(1)(b) and regulation 31; BA8A: Concurrent consent. Section 14(1)(b) and regulation 31; BA10: Appointment of the registered contractor, notice of commencement, and the contractor's undertaking. Section 9 and regulation 20; BA12: Completion certificate and application for a temporary occupation permit. Section 21 and regulation 25; BA14 and BA14A or BA14B: Completion of works.
