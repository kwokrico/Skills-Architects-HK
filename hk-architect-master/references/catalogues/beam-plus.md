## Operative rules (Assessment Manuals)

| Tool | Operative rule |
| --- | --- |
| EB V3.0 Assessment Manual | Comprehensive Scheme A (one-step, optional PA) or Scheme B (step-wise within 3 years; no PA). Absolute points, max 100 + 10 IA. Rating needs overall 75/65/55/40 and minima MAN/SS/MW/WU/HWB ≥4 and EU ≥15. No Selective Scheme. Unauthorised portions not assessed. |
| NB V2.0 Assessment Manual (2025.02) | All applicable prerequisites required. Weights: IDCM 18%, SS 15%, MW 9%, EU 29%, WU 7%, HWB 22%. Grades need ≥20% in each category and overall ≥75/65/55/40. PA → CA → RFA. SS P1 greenery ≥20% on sites ≥1,000 m²; WU P1 10% water saving; EU P1 BEC-plus AC/lighting; IDCM P3 no virgin timber for temporary works. |
