## PNAP operative rules for this subject

Distilled from the Practice Notes for Authorized Persons critical summaries. The rule column is the schematic-design takeaway in that summary. Where the summary gives no separate takeaway, the overview or the printed table is used, and the note is listed as thin in the building-codes index.

| ADV-36 | Modular Integrated Construction | Tie the modules together against disproportionate collapse, detail every joint against water, and do not count a separate balcony or utility-platform module as MiC floor area. If the module is unconventional, clear it by enquiry or in-principle acceptance in the 45-day track before detailed design, and draw the module interfaces at 1:50. Name a Grade T3 weekly supervisor for each stream and a Grade T1 continuously for the contractor, and submit the supervision plan at least 14 days before the factory starts. Printed figures: Separate balcony or utility platform: Do not design a balcony or utility platform as a separate module joined in situ. If one is proposed, its area is not accepted as MiC floor area for gross floor area and site coverage concessions under Joint Practice Note No. 8; Pre-submission enquiry: PNAP ADM-19. A determination on an unconventional design or on the performance of a prototype is available within 45 days; In-principle acceptance: For the performance items in Appendix C. If the information is complete and the system meets the Buildings Ordinance, a letter of in-principle acceptance is issued within 45 days of receipt. Comments are also issued within 45 days; Response: Clarify every comment within 30 days or the application is treated as abandoned. The Department then accepts or refuses within 30 days of the response. A refusal is processed as a new application, again within 45 days; Plans with the application: General notes on the codes. Intended building height, number of storeys, and use. General building plans of all MiC floors, or of the system or prototype, at a scale not less than 1:100. Module interfaces with upper, lower, and adjacent modules and with in-situ work at a scale not less than 1:50. Structural plans at not less than 1:100. The module that represents the whole building and its height when assembled. Mirrored modules count as one type; Factory proof: A Full Certificate under the Modular Integrated Construction Manufacturer Accreditation Scheme, or ISO 9001 certification plus a quality assurance scheme as in Appendix B; Factory: A valid Full Certificate from the Building Technology Research Institute under the Modular Integrated Construction Manufacturer Accreditation Scheme, or ISO 9001 or an equivalent quality assurance certification. Imposed as a condition under section 17(1) of the Buildings Ordinance when plans are approved; Before production: Submit the Full Certificate, or the quality assurance scheme, at least 14 days before production starts in the factory, unless it is already covered by an in-principle acceptance and is unchanged. Submit the MiC supervision plan at least 14 days before production; Minimum supervision: Authorized-person stream: Grade T3, weekly. Registered-structural-engineer stream: Grade T3, weekly. Registered-contractor stream: Grade T3, weekly, and Grade T1, continuous. Grades are as in the Code of Practice for Site Supervision. The authorized person’s and structural engineer’s teams cover fire-resisting construction, drainage, and structure as in PNAP APP-158; Quality audit: The authorized person, the registered structural engineer, and the authorized signatory inspect the first batch in person, then audit at least once every month. Quarterly audits are in person. The months between may be by videotelephony, assisted by the supervisory teams, and not inferior to a personal visit. Submit the endorsed audit report within 14 days after the check |
