# Labour, compensation and construction plant legislation

Use from `hk-construction-health-safety` for employment, occupational injury, shared-workplace duties and temporary access plant. Departmental guidance checked **6 November 2026**. These are architect coordination workflows, not a complete employment-law manual. For an actual claim, confirm the applicable consolidated chapter, commencement provisions and facts; do not calculate compensation from an undated table.

For each applicable item, record the duty holder, trigger, authority, submission or action, supporting evidence, due date and closeout. The architect coordinates evidence; the employer, occupier, plant owner or statutory specialist retains the relevant duty.

## Cap. 55 — Labour Relations Ordinance

**Trigger:** a trade dispute or threatened stoppage affecting a project. **Authority:** Labour Department (LD), Labour Relations Division. Cap. 55 supplies procedures for settling trade disputes; Cap. 57 sets employment standards.

**Action:** identify the actual employer and affected subcontract tier; preserve the dispute chronology, employment terms, wage and attendance records; refer the parties to LD conciliation. Record the agreed outcome and separately assess programme and contractual notices with the contract administrator. Do not infer an extension of time or entitlement to deduct money from the existence of a labour dispute.

**Evidence:** referral, records supplied, settlement or unresolved issues, and separate contract notices. [LD explanation of the statutory framework](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/public/iprd/2024/chapter3.html).

## Cap. 57 — Employment Ordinance

**Trigger:** employment terms, wage default or subcontract labour claims. **Duty holder:** employer, with statutory wage liability potentially extending to principal and superior contractors in building and construction works. **Authority:** LD.

**Action:** check wages, payment dates, deductions, leave and termination against current LD guidance. For subcontract wage default, identify the contractual chain immediately: liability can extend to the first **two months** of unpaid wages. An employee's written notice to the principal contractor or main nominated subcontractor is normally due within **60 days** after wages become due, with an additional period of **90 days** if permitted by the Commissioner. Preserve notice receipt and refer liability questions to LD/legal advisers. A payment certificate is not proof that workers received wages; contract deductions require their own basis.

**Evidence:** payroll, attendance, payment receipts, subcontract chain and notices. [LD Employment Ordinance at a Glance](https://www.labour.gov.hk/common/public/pdf/wcp/EO_at_a_Glance_en.pdf).

## Cap. 78 — Contracts for Employment outside Hong Kong Ordinance

**Trigger:** local recruitment to work outside Hong Kong for an employer who is neither in Hong Kong nor carrying on business here. Do not apply this chapter automatically to every overseas assignment by a Hong Kong practice.

**Gate:** LD describes coverage for manual employees and non-manual employees earning no more than **HK$20,000 per month** in that employment arrangement. A written contract containing prescribed particulars must be attested by the Commissioner for Labour **before departure**.

**Action:** ask HR to establish the employer's identity, business location, work location, employee category and pay; obtain LD's scope confirmation where uncertain and programme attestation before mobilisation. **Evidence:** written contract, applicability review and attestation. [LD External Employment Service](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/service/content4_3.htm).

## Cap. 282 — Employees' Compensation Ordinance

**Trigger:** work injury or prescribed occupational disease. **Duty holder:** employer; investigate principal-contractor exposure and subcontract insurance rather than assuming the immediate employer is the only relevant party. **Authority:** LD Employees' Compensation Division.

**Gate:** employers must carry employees' compensation insurance covering statutory and common-law work-injury liabilities. LD states that work injuries are generally reported within **14 days**, and fatal cases within **7 days**, using Form 2, 2A or 2B as applicable. Report without waiting for a decision on liability. Check the precise case and current form instructions.

**Action/evidence:** verify policy period, work locations, activities and subcontract-worker cover before mobilisation; preserve incident, medical and wage records; record LD submission and separate insurer notification. Also apply Cap. 509 reporting below: the compensation-reporting clock does not replace urgent workplace notification. [LD insurance and reporting FAQ](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/faq/cap282a_whole.htm).

## Cap. 360 — Pneumoconiosis and Mesothelioma (Compensation) Ordinance

**Trigger:** suspected disease associated with dust or asbestos exposure. This chapter provides disease compensation, including relevant incapacity and death benefits. Prevention and asbestos works controls arise under other instruments, including Caps. 59 and 311.

**Action:** refer diagnosis and claim eligibility to medical specialists, LD and the Pneumoconiosis Compensation Fund Board; retain occupational and exposure histories, surveys, abatement and employment records. Do not promise an award from the presence of asbestos alone. Check the rates applicable to the case: compensation levels changed on **17 April 2025**. [LD compensation update](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/news/ECO_PMCO_ODCO_Amendment_2025.htm); [Fund Board eligibility](https://pcfb.org.hk/compensate.php?lang=en).

## Cap. 398 — Occupational Safety and Health Council Ordinance

**Trigger:** selecting safety training, consultancy or safety-promotion support. The Occupational Safety and Health Council (OSHC) is the statutory body promoting workplace safety through training, research, information and consultancy.

**Action:** use OSHC services where suitable and retain the course or consultancy scope and completion records. An OSHC course or scheme is not itself LD approval of a site, and Cap. 398 must not be substituted for employer/occupier duties under Caps. 59 and 509. Check any particular statutory training-recognition requirement separately. [OSHC functions](https://www.oshc.org.hk/eng/main/about_us/oshc_profile/).

## Cap. 469 — Occupational Deafness (Compensation) Ordinance

**Trigger:** a worker reports hearing loss after employment in specified noisy occupations. **Claim body:** Occupational Deafness Compensation Board (ODCB).

**Action:** retain noisy-work employment and exposure records; refer the worker to ODCB for occupational and medical assessment and the applicable application time limit. Eligibility and compensation depend on the statutory occupation and hearing-loss criteria, not a building's acoustic rating. Separate compensation from worker noise prevention under Cap. 59 and community/plant noise control under Cap. 400. [ODCB compensation scheme](https://odcb.org.hk/files/cms/leaflet/Leaflet%28Compensation%29_04-23-%28accessible_EN%29.pdf); [LD updated compensation levels](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/news/ECO_PMCO_ODCO_Amendment_2025.htm).

## Cap. 470 — Builders' Lifts and Tower Working Platforms (Safety) Ordinance

**Trigger:** temporary builders' lifts or mast-type tower working platforms. **Authority:** EMSD; **duty holders:** equipment owner, registered contractor and registered examiner, as applicable.

**Gate:** classify the actual equipment before choosing its regime. Permanent lifts fall under Cap. 618; material hoists under Cap. 59I and suspended working platforms under Cap. 59AC are different categories. Builders' lifts and tower working platforms have separate EMSD design codes.

**Action:** reserve foundations, mast ties, landings, enclosure and safe access using the selected equipment's engineered design. Require installation testing/examination and the required EMSD endorsement before use, plus maintenance, periodic examinations and records. Recheck after relocation or alteration. **Evidence:** registered appointments, design, logbook and endorsed test/examination certificate; Form 14 addresses examination after installation. [EMSD codes](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/en/other_regulatory_services/builders_lifts_tower_working_platforms/publications/code_of_practice/index.html); [EMSD Form 14](https://www.emsd.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_609/Form%2014_Edit_0722.pdf).

## Cap. 509 — Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance

**Trigger:** employees at a workplace, including occupied premises during alterations. **Authority:** LD. Sections 6–7 distinguish employer duties from occupier duties concerning premises, access/egress, plant and substances; appointment of a contractor does not erase the duties of an occupier who retains control.

**Action:** map actual control of shared stairs, access, landlord plant and work areas; allocate safe systems and corrective actions. An LD suspension notice remains a constraint until revoked. Use the [integrated Cap. 509 summary](statutory/legislation/cap-509.md) for duties and enforcement; detailed site precautions remain in Cap. 59I and relevant regulations.

**Reporting:** fatal or serious-injury workplace accidents require notification to LD within **24 hours**, followed by a written report within **7 days** unless the notification was already an adequate written report. Also check dangerous occurrences and other reportable injuries. Timely Cap. 282 reporting can satisfy specified written-report requirements, but do not assume it discharges every Cap. 509 notification. Record each duty and evidence separately. [LD accident-reporting guidance](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/public/os/A/accidentdo.pdf).
