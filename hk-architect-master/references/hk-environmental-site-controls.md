# Environmental controls for building and site works

Use the relevant section only: **311** air/asbestos; **354** waste; **358** runoff/discharge; **403** refrigerants; **466** marine dumping. EPD guidance checked **6 November 2026**. Treat layout recommendations below as coordination actions, rather than dimensions prescribed by a parent ordinance. Each permit controls its own activity; an EIA environmental permit does not replace separate licences.

## Cap. 311 — Air Pollution Control Ordinance

**Trigger:** specified processes, fuel-burning plant, dusty works, asbestos or air-quality assessment. **Authority:** EPD; identify the operator/owner and the particular regulated activity.

**Action:** screen the plant/process against Schedule 1 before reserving its location; coordinate emission treatment, chimney arrangement and access with the relevant approval or licence. For demolition/A&A, investigate suspect asbestos and commission the required registered specialists and submissions before disturbance. Check construction-dust requirements separately from plant approval.

**Evidence:** process/plant schedule, licensing decision, asbestos investigation/abatement records, dust-control measures and authority submissions. The [integrated Cap. 311 summary](statutory/legislation/cap-311.md) contains the archived Schedule 1 and asbestos detail; the [Cap. 311A summary](statutory/legislation/cap-311a.md) addresses furnaces, ovens and chimneys. Verify numerical tables and the applicable version before use. [EPD APCO guide](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/sites/default/files/epd/english/environmentinhk/air/guide_ref/files/apco_e.pdf); [EPD asbestos control](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/environmentinhk/air/asbestos/asbestos.html).

## Cap. 354 — Waste Disposal Ordinance

**Trigger:** construction/demolition waste, contaminated material or chemical waste. **Authority:** EPD. Identify the waste producer, collector and receiving facility separately.

**Gate:** chemical-waste producers must register before undertaking activities that generate chemical waste; packaging, labelling, safe storage, licensed collection and licensed disposal apply. Retain chemical-waste trip tickets. [EPD chemical waste controls](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/environmentinhk/waste/guide_ref/guide_cwc.html).

**Action:** classify waste streams before tender; provide segregated storage and safe collection access and verify authorised destinations. Arrange the construction-waste billing account before using government disposal facilities. For construction contracts valued at **HK$1 million or more**, the main contractor must apply for a contract-specific account within **21 days of award**; smaller contracts have different account arrangements. Do not copy chemical-waste collector licensing requirements indiscriminately onto every inert-waste movement. [EPD construction waste charging scheme](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/misc/cdm/scheme.htm).

**Evidence:** classification, producer registration where required, collector/facility checks, billing account and disposal records. Asbestos waste also needs the relevant Cap. 311 controls; sea disposal uses Cap. 466.

## Cap. 358 — Water Pollution Control Ordinance

**Trigger:** trade/process effluent, contaminated construction runoff, dewatering, treatment plant or septic-tank discharge. **Authority:** EPD. Identify the discharger and receiving environment.

**Gate:** a licence for a controlled discharge must be obtained before discharge begins. Domestic sewage to communal sewers and genuinely unpolluted water to storm drains/waters have specific exclusions; do not treat construction runoff as clean without establishing its quality. Sewage-treatment-plant and septic-tank discharges remain controlled.

**Action:** map every outfall and water control zone; coordinate sedimentation/treatment, sampling and maintenance space before works. Size treatment to the applicable Technical Memorandum and actual licence conditions, not an assumed universal limit. Check licence variation when the discharge changes.

**Evidence:** drainage/outfall plan, flow and quality assumptions, treatment design, licence, monitoring and maintenance records. [EPD discharge guidance](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/environmentinhk/water/guide_ref/guide_wpc_wpco_2.html); [integrated Cap. 358 summary](statutory/legislation/cap-358.md). DSD connection consent and Cap. 446 watercourse consent are separate checks.

## Cap. 403 — Ozone Layer Protection Ordinance

**Trigger:** specifying, importing, replacing or decommissioning refrigeration/air-conditioning equipment or handling controlled refrigerants. **Authority:** EPD; identify the importer/exporter and servicing party.

**Gate:** check the current scheduled substance and equipment controls, rather than assuming this chapter concerns only CFCs. Scheduled HFC import/export licensing and local-consumption import quotas took effect on **1 December 2025**. [EPD HFC quota guidelines](https://olpo.epd.gov.hk/Quota_Application/resource/guidelines/Guidelines%20on%20the%20Allocation%20of%20Import%20Quota%20for%20HFCs%20%28eng%29.pdf).

**Action:** record refrigerant identity and charge; have the services engineer confirm supply, servicing and recovery arrangements. Where the Controlled Refrigerants Regulation applies, recovery/recycling requires DEP-approved equipment used according to its instructions. Do not apply that narrow statutory definition automatically to every refrigerant. [EPD approved recovery equipment](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/environmentinhk/air/ozone_layer_protection/wn6_info_equipment.html).

**Evidence:** refrigerant schedule, supplier licensing/quota evidence where applicable, recovery equipment and disposal/service records. Lower ozone impact does not establish safe use: flammable/toxic alternatives need separate safety and DG assessment. [EPD alternative-refrigerant safety](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/environmentinhk/air/ozone_layer_protection/wn6_alt_refrigerant.html).

## Cap. 466 — Dumping at Sea Ordinance

**Trigger:** marine dumping and related loading operations. **Authority:** EPD. Identify the permit applicant/operator; for the private-project sediment framework in PNAP ADV-21, the contractor applies for the dumping permit.

**Gate:** obtain the applicable dumping permit before dumping and related loading; confirm material, site, vessel/method and permit conditions. Disposal-space allocation and sediment classification do not themselves constitute a dumping permit. [EPD marine-works controls](https://www.epd.gov.hk/epd/english/greenconstruction/poll_pro/popup_marine_work.html).

**Action:** first test whether sediment can remain in place; if removal is required, commission characterisation and arrange disposal allocation and permit sequencing before tender/mobilisation. Reuse [PNAP ADV-21](statutory/pnap/ADV-21%20Management%20Framework%20for%20Disposal%20of%20Dredged-Excavated%20Sediment_CS.md) for the detailed sediment workflow; it is an **April 2007 source**, so verify current sampling, facilities, fees and programme assumptions with EPD/CEDD rather than treating its tables as current permits.

When presenting this sediment workflow, state that the linked PNAP ADV-21 is dated April 2007 and distinguish its historical detail from current EPD/CEDD requirements. Carry that source-date caveat into the answer; do not leave it only in this reference.

**Evidence:** sediment reports, allocation, permit and conditions, vessel monitoring and disposal records. Cap. 358 discharge control, Cap. 313 navigation requirements and Cap. 127 foreshore authorisation remain separate gates.
