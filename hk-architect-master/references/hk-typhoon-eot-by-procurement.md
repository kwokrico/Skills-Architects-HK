# Typhoon and Weather Delay — Contract Assessment (Hong Kong)

Read with `hk-procurement-strategy`; contract administration uses `hk-tender-contract-administration` §12.8. Establish the executed form, edition, Contract Data, selected options and amendments before deciding entitlement. A procurement route alone does not determine weather relief. DBB can use an NEC Option C contract.

## 1. NEC weather: select the applicable contract first

The unamended NEC weather test must not be imposed automatically on DEVB contracts. The DEVB 9 May 2024 NEC4 amendment library replaces clause 60.1(13) with local weather choices. Under its Choice 1 and C/D clause 62.2, T8+, Black and Red warnings may support time and monetary assessment; Amber/inclement weather is generally time-only. These are examples of amendments, not findings about an unseen contract. Check the executed choice and additional conditions. The current DEVB index also distinguishes NEC ECC Hong Kong Edition from NEC4.

Sources checked 7 November 2026: [DEVB NEC4 amendments, clauses 60.1(13) and 62.2](https://www.devb.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_1002/01_Library_of_Standard_Amendments_to_NEC4_ECC_9-5-2024_clean.pdf); [DEVB current contract/practice-note index](https://www.devb.gov.hk/en/publications_and_press_releases/publications/standard_contract_documents/practice_notes_nec_engineering_construction_contract/index.html).

Where an unamended statistical-weather provision actually applies, use its specified measurements, location, calendar month and comparison data. Do not substitute a generic wind-speed test, a ten-year average, or assume a typical T8 necessarily fails.

## 2. Option C payment and pain/gain

Separate three questions: whether the event qualifies; whether it changes Completion/Key Dates and Prices; and how eligible Defined Cost plus Fee and the final share are assessed. A qualifying monetary CE may adjust the target. Do not automatically book every weather cost as pain against an unchanged target. Check Disallowed Cost, Contract Data share ranges and percentages; do not invent a 50:50 split. [NEC publisher's payment explanation](https://www.neccontract.com/news/nec4-ecc-pricing-provisions-%E2%80%93-an-introduction-for-new-nec-users).

## 3. Other procurement arrangements

| Arrangement | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Traditional GCC / SFBC | Establish the actual weather event, notices and completion impact; time relief does not automatically establish prolongation payment. Check express payment provisions separately. |
| Design and build | Check the executed allocation of design and construction risks; do not automatically equate days of closure with EOT or exclude every design consequence. |
| Management contracting / construction management | Assess each package's weather entitlement separately from access/handover obligations to subsequent packages. Do not presume a pass-through claim succeeds. |

For a GCC comparator consult the executed conditions and [GCC Building Works clause 50](https://www.devb.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_188/gf541a.pdf). Private and amended forms may differ.

## 4. Evidence and decision record

- Identify event dates, HKO warnings or contract measurements, site diary and actual affected work.
- Preserve required notices; early warning is not a substitute for a CE notification where both apply.
- Assess accepted/current programme logic, mitigation and completion impact; signal duration is not automatically the awarded extension.
- Record the governing clause, eligibility, time assessment, target/price assessment, cost records and share treatment separately.
- Coordinate hospital access, clinical operations, shutdowns and safety constraints in a hospital extension; they do not themselves establish weather entitlement.

The architect's contractual role depends on appointment. Under NEC distinguish Project Manager, Supervisor and designer; do not assume the traditional certifier role applies.
