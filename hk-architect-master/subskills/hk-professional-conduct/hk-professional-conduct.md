# HK Professional Conduct

Registration, title, and conduct rules for architectural practice in Hong Kong. This skill does not approve building plans and does not set design, fire, or structural standards.

## 1. Who may be on the register (Cap. 408 s.13)

The Board shall not register a person unless all of the following are met:

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Qualification — one of | Member of the Institute (HKIA Member or Fellow); or membership of a body the Board accepts as not less than that standard; or examinations, training, and experience the Board accepts as not less than that standard |
| Hong Kong experience | 1 year’s relevant professional experience in Hong Kong before the application |
| Residence | Ordinarily resident in Hong Kong |
| Barring order | Not subject to an inquiry or a Part IV order that precludes registration |
| Competence | Written declaration that the person is competent to practise |
| Character | A fit and proper person |

A foreign membership alone does not put the name on the register. The Board may refuse where the person was convicted of an offence that may bring the profession into disrepute and was sentenced to imprisonment (suspended or not), or has committed professional misconduct or neglect.

Registration lasts **12 months** and is renewable. Apply not earlier than 3 months and not later than **28 days** before expiry. If no renewal application is in before expiry, the person is deemed not currently on the register from the expiry date. Notify a change of registered particulars within **28 days**. Notify the Board of a likely continuous absence from Hong Kong of more than 6 months. Failure to reside in Hong Kong for 2 years or more means the Board shall not treat the person as ordinarily resident.

## 2. Title and how a practice is supervised (s.30–s.31)

A person not on the register is not entitled to describe himself as “architect” or “registered architect”, or to use “R.A.”, except:

- a description by reference to an architectural discipline not related to the design, construction, or fitting out of buildings; or
- membership of a body formed outside Hong Kong, where the description does not imply a right to practise architecture in Hong Kong.

A firm or company shall not use “architects”, “registered architects”, or “R.A.” unless, at each place where the business of architecture is carried on, the business is under the supervision of a registered architect who does not at the same time act in a similar capacity for any other person (other than a firm or company with substantially the same beneficial ownership and management). In a multidisciplinary practice, the architecture business must be under the full-time control and management of a registered architect who meets the same exclusivity test.

Drawing titles, letterheads, and consultant schedules must match the live register. False use of the title is an offence (fine at level 5 and imprisonment for 1 year).

## 3. Discipline (ss.21–24)

A registered architect commits a disciplinary offence for professional misconduct or neglect, an offence under the Ordinance, registration by fraud or misrepresentation, not having been entitled to be registered, failure without reasonable excuse to attend an inquiry when summoned, or a conviction that may bring the profession into disrepute with a sentence of imprisonment.

If the person discloses that matter when applying for registration or renewal, and the Board then accepts the application, that disclosed matter is not later treated as a disciplinary offence for registration or renewal purposes.

An inquiry committee may have regard to a code promulgated by the Board or currently in use by the Institute. Orders include removal, removal for a period, a written reprimand on the register, suspension of an order for not more than 2 years, a bar on a fresh application, and an oral admonishment. A name removed or barred cannot carry the title or the section 30 supervision role.

## 4. ARB Code of Professional Conduct (5 March 2024)

Established under section 9 as mandatory rules. Five aspects: competence, integrity, conflict of interest, statutory and regulatory compliance, and public interest. Duty to the client sits alongside a supervening duty to safeguard the safety and well-being of the public.

| Aspect | Rule as printed |
|---|---|
| Competence | Carry out duties under the Client / Architect Agreement diligently, to the standard expected of a member of the profession |
| Contract administration | Act impartially and administer building contracts and consultancy agreements under the architect’s control with fairness and integrity |
| Status | Once an undischarged bankrupt, or once renewal or registration is rejected, make that status known to persons expected to be affected by services proffered as an architect |
| Promotion | Promote honestly; do not maliciously criticise another architect; do not unduly influence another architect to breach the code |
| Conflict | No financial or personal interest inconsistent with professional obligations unless fully declared to all parties likely to be affected |
| Compliance | Do not act unlawfully or unethically so as to bring the profession into disrepute; co-operate with a Board investigation with full disclosure |
| Public interest | Proper regard for the safety and well-being of those who may use or enjoy the result of the work |

## 5. HKIA Code of Professional Conduct (18 August 2017)

Governs Members, and the code says a Member includes Fellow, Member, Associate, and Graduate. It is not Cap. 408. Critical Summary: `references/statutory/hkia/HKIA Code of Professional Conduct (18-08-2017)_CS.md`.

- A Graduate cannot carry a commission without guidance from a Member who is not a Graduate.
- Work cannot be subcommissioned without the client’s prior agreement and a definition of responsibilities.
- A financial interest in the contract has to be declared and accepted, and an arbitrator arranged early.
- Avoid a situation in which the Member could improperly influence a planning application or a statutory approval.
- Do not start on another Member’s drawings until that Member has been notified in writing and the client has the right to use the previous information, drawings, and design.
- An invited concept submission is recommended to stop at schematic site layout, principal floor plans, sections, and a design statement, at a scale compatible with that stage, with the fee agreed and recorded before the work.
- Fee rates are not to be publicised, and the practice is not to be described as the best, the largest, or the most.

## 6. HKIA Rules of the Institute (17 June 2021)

Internal membership, Council, meeting, and disciplinary rules under Cap. 1147. Not Cap. 408 and not themselves the Code — Rule 63 includes the Code and Conditions of Engagement within “Rules.” Critical Summary: `references/statutory/hkia/HKIA Rules of the Institute (17-06-2021)_CS.md`.

- Members (Rule 5A) need recognised qualification plus two years’ post-graduate experience and the HKIA/ARB Professional Assessment (or approved equivalents); Fellows need 15 years’ standing and practice (or significant contribution).
- Only Fellows and Members vote; Corporate Members do not vote or hold Institute office.
- Titles: FHKIA (Fellows), HKIA (Members); Associates and Affiliates use their own prescribed titles.
- Corporate Membership needs at least one Foundation Member/Member/Fellow director and ≥30% directors or voting rights held by those classes.
- Council may reprimand, suspend, expel, notify ARB, and/or award costs after the hearing/appeal process in Rules 24A–25C.
- CPD requirements may be set for Members, Non-Resident Members, Affiliates, and Graduate Members; failure after reminder can terminate membership.

## 7. Practice checks

1. Name the registered architect who supervises each office, and, if multidisciplinary, the registered architect in full-time control of the architecture work.
2. Diary the 28-day-before-expiry renewal cutoff before describing the person, or a practice relying on that person, as architect.
3. Disclose prior misconduct or imprisonment convictions on the application or renewal if the practice needs the Board’s acceptance.
4. Keep PI and duty-of-care wording in `hk-professional-indemnity`; this skill does not size insurance.
